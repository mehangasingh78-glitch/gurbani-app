ਅੰਗ 1
ੴ ਸਤਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਕਰਤਾ ਪੁਰਖੁ ਨਿਰਭਉ ਨਿਰਵੈਰੁ...
Ikk (Ek) Onkar Sat Naam Karta Purakh Nirbhou Nirvair Akal Moorat Ajooni Saibhang Gur Prasad.
Word Meaning
  • Ikk = One
  • Sat Naam = Eternal Name
  • Karta Purakh = Creator
  • Nirbhau = Fearless
  • Nirvair = Without Enmity
  • Akal Moorat = Timeless Being
  • Ajooni = Beyond Birth
  • Saibhang = Self-existent
  • Gur Prasad = Realized through Guru's Grace
Meaning
There is only One Supreme Being. His existence is eternal. He is the Creator, fearless, without enmity, timeless, unborn and self-existent. He can only be realized through the Guru's grace.
Note 1
Naam is also used in Guru Granth Sahib to indicate Waheguru's Hukam (Divine Command) and the Laws of Nature.
ਆਸਾ ਦੀ ਵਾਰ

ਆਪੀਨੈ ਆਪੁ ਸਾਜਿਓ ਆਪੀਨੈ ਰਚਿਓ ਨਾਉ॥
Meaning
After creating Himself, Waheguru created His Hukam (Natural Laws) and through those laws created the universe.
Note 2
Guru can be a human Guru or the Divine Word (Shabad).
