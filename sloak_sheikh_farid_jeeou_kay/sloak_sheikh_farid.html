ੴ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਪ੍ਰਸਾਦਿ॥ ਸਲੋਕ ਸੇਖ ਫਰੀਦ ਜੀ ਕੇ

Ek Onkar Satgur Persad(i):  Sloak Sheikh Farid Ji Kay 

          

ਜਿਤੁ ਦਿਹਾੜੈ ਧਨ ਵਰੀ ਸਾਹੇ ਲਏ ਲਿਖਾਇ॥ ਮਲਕੁ ਜਿ ਕੰਨੀ ਸੁਣੀਦਾ ਮੁਹੁ ਦੇਖਾਲੇ ਆਇ॥ ਜਿੰਦੁ ਨਿਮਾਣੀ ਕਢੀਐ ਹਡਾ ਕੂ ਕੜਕਾਇ॥ ਸਾਹੇ ਲਿਖੇ ਨ ਚਲਨੀ ਜਿੰਦੂ ਕੋ ਸਮਝਾਇ॥ ਜਿੰਦੁ ਵਹੁਟੀ ਮਰਣੁ ਵਰੁ ਲੈ ਜਾਸੀ ਪਰਣਾਇ॥ ਆਪਣ ਹਥੀ ਜੋਲਿ ਕੈ ਕੈ ਗਲਿ ਲਾਗੈ ਧਾਇ॥ ਵਾਲਹੁ ਨਿਕੀ ਪੁਰਸਲਾਤ ਕੰਨੀ ਨ ਸੁਣੀਆਇ॥ ਫਰੀਦਾ ਕਿੜੀ ਪਵੰਦੀਈ ਖੜਾ ਨ ਆਪੁ ਮੁਹਾਇ॥ 1॥

(PAGE 1377) conti:  Jit dihaarraiy dhun vurie, saahay luyyay likhaayay. Mulk(u) j(i) kunnie soonieda,mooh(u) daykhalay aayay. Jind(u) nimaani kuddhiyaiy, huddaan koo kurrkaayay. Saahay likhay na chullni, jindoo ko sumjhaayay. Jind(u) vuhouti, mern(u) vur(u), laiy jaasi, pernaayay. Aaapun hutheen joal(i) kaiy, kaiy gul(i) laagaiy dhaayay. Vaalhoun nikki, Purslaat, kunnie na sooniyayay. Farida, kirrie pvundyee, khurraa na aap(u) moohaayay.   1.

Word Meaning:  Jit(u) Dihaaraiy = the day.  Dhun = human bride (soul).  Vurie = is to be married.  Saahay = the date and time. Mulk(u) = the Angel of death. Jind(u) = life; soul. Nimaani = helpless.  Kurrkaayay = twisting; causing discomfort.  Chullini = moved.  Jindoo = soul.   Vuhouti = wife.   Mern(u) = death.  Vur(u) = husband.  Jaasi = will. Pernaayay = by marrying.   Joal(i) Kaiy = seeing off.  Gul(i) Laagaiy = hug; seek comfort.  Vaalhoun Nikki = thinner than hair.  Purslaat = the Siraat-named bridge in hell (a Muslim belief).  Kirrie = voices (of suffering).   Moohaayay = get robbed.   

MEANING:  The time and date of marriage (with death) of every human bride is prewritten by God, and the Angel of death, about which all of us have heard, appears at that time.  The helpless soul is extracted by causing suffering to the bones (body).  The Angel explains to the soul that the prewritten time of death cannot be moved (changed).  Death, as husband, takes the soul-bride away.  Whom can the body rush to hug for comfort (has no one) when it sees off the soul itself?   Haven’t you heard of the Siraat-named bridge in hell, which is extremely difficult to cross as it is thinner than the hair (i.e., there is very tight scrutiny of one’s deeds), oh man?  So, listen to the voices of suffering of the people, and do not wait and let yourself be robbed of good qualities (worship Allah), of Farid.   1.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਦਰ ਦਰਵੇਸੀ ਗਾਖੜੀ ਚਲਾ ਦੁਨੀਆ ਭਤਿ॥

(ਅੰਗ 1378)॥      ਬੰਨ੍ਹਿ ਉਠਾਈ ਪੋਟਲੀ ਕਿਥੈ ਵੰਞਾ ਘਤਿ॥ 2॥

Farida, Dur dervayshi gaakhrri, chullaan dooniya bhut(i). 

(PAGE 1378):  Bunnh(i) ootthaayi poatli, kithaiy vunjaan ghut(i).  2.

Word Meaning:  Dur = Allah’s Door.  Dervayshi = godliness; renouncement. Gaakhrri = difficult.  Bhut(i) = like; following the same customs.  Bunnh(i) Oothaayi = packed and carrying. Poatli = package (of worldly affairs).  Vunjaan = I go.  Ghut(i) = to drop. 

MEANING:  It is very difficult to reach Allah’s Door (by renouncing the world), oh Farid. Like the rest of the world, I am also carrying the burden of my family responsibilities; how (by what means) can I throw it away to be free (to reach Allah’s Door)?     2.

ਕਿਝੂ ਨ ਬੁਝੈ ਕਿਝੁ ਨ ਸੁਝੈ ਦੁਨੀਆ ਗੁਝੀ ਭਾਹਿ॥ ਸਾਈ ਮੇਰੇ ਚੰਗਾ ਕੀਤਾ ਨਾਹੀ ਤ ਹੰਭੀ ਦਝਾ ਆਹਿ॥ 3॥

Kijh(u) na boojhaiy, kijh(u) na soojhaiy, dooniyaa goojhi bhaah(i). Saayeen mayray chunga keeta, naahi ta humbhi dujhaan aah(i).   3.

Word Meaning:  Kijh(u) Na = nothing.  Boohaiy = understand.  Soojhaiy = think of.  Goojhi Bhaayay = hidden fire.   Naahi Ta = otherwise.  Humbhi = I too.  Dujhaan = burn. 

MEANING:  Worldly attractions are such a hidden fire (source of suffering) that one cannot understand, or think about it (of some solution to protect oneself).  My Lord did a great favour to me (that I have begun to recite His Naam), otherwise, I would also be suffering in this fire, oh Farid. 3.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਜੇ ਜਾਣਾ ਤਿਲ ਥੋੜੜੇ ਸੰਮਲਿ ਬੁਕੁ ਭਰੀ॥ ਜੇ ਜਾਣਾ ਸਹੁ ਨੰਢੜਾ ਥੋੜਾ ਮਾਣੁ ਕਰੀ॥ 4॥

Farida, jay jaana til thorr-rray sumul(i) book bhureen.  Jay jaana Shuhou nundhrra, thorra maan(u) kureen. 4.

Word Meaning:  Til = sesame seeds; breaths. Sumul(i) = carefully.  Book Bhureen = use wisely.  Shuou = husband; Allah.  Nundhrra = childlike (pure-hearted).  Maan(u) = conceit.

MEANING = When you know that the man has only a few breaths to take (short life), you should spend them wisely (recite Allah’s Naam), oh Farid.  And, when you know that Allah loves only the pure-minded people, do not show off your conceit.   4.

ਜੇ ਜਾਣਾ ਲੜੁ ਛਿਜਣਾ ਪੀਡੀ ਪਾਈ ਗੰਢਿ॥ ਤੈ ਜੇਵਡ ਮੈ ਨਾਹੀ ਕੋ ਸਭੁ ਜਗੁ ਡਿਠਾ ਹੰਢਿ॥ 5॥

Jay jaana lurr chhijjna, peedee paayeen gundh(i). Taiyn jayvud mein naahi ko, subh(u) jug(u) dittha hundh(i).  5.

Word Meaning: Lurr(u) = bond.  Chhijjna = weaken.  Peedi = very tight.  Gundh(i) = knot; bond.  Taiyn = You (Allah).  Jayvud = as great.    Hundh(i) = experiencing.

MEANING:   Experiencing the whole world, I have found out that there is nobody as great as Allah is. So, make a very strong bond with Him so that it does not weaken, oh Farid. 5.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਜੇ ਤੂ ਅਕਲਿ ਲਤੀਫੁ ਕਾਲੇ ਲਿਖੁ ਨ ਲੇਖੁ॥ ਆਪਨੜੇ ਗਿਰੀਵਾਨ ਮਹਿ ਸਿਰੁ ਨੀਵਾ ਕਰਿ ਦੇਖੁ॥ 6॥

Farida, jay tu ukl(i) luteef(u), kaalay likh(u) na laykh(u) Aapiunrray girivaan meh(i) sir(u) neevaan kur(i) daykh(u).   6.

Word Meaning:  Ukl(i) Lateef(u) = have sharp wisdom.  Kaalay Laykh = find faults of others. Girivaan = robe; heart.  Neevaan = lower, bend.

MEANING:  If you consider yourself gifted with sharp wisdom (smartness), then do not find faults of others.  Bend your head (to see your heart) to analyze it, (and see how many weaknesses you, yourself, have), oh Farid.   6.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਜੋ ਤੈ ਮਾਰਨਿ ਮੁਕੀਆ ਤਿਨ੍ਹਾ ਨਾ ਮਾਰੇ ਘੁੰਮਿ॥ ਆਪਨੜੈ ਘਰਿ ਜਾਈਐ ਪੈਰ ਤਿਨ੍ਹਾ ਦੇ ਚੁੰਮਿ॥7॥

Farida, jo taiyn maarun(i) mookkiyaan, tinha na maaray ghoomm(i). Apunrraiy ghur(i) jaayiyaiy, paiyr tinha day choom(i).  7. 

Word Meaning:  Maarun(i) Mookkiyaan = attack you with fists (point out your faults).  Ghoom(i) = in return; retaliate.   Ghur(i) Jaayiyaiy = keep your cool.  Paiyr Choom(i) = kiss feet; thank them.  7. 

MEANING:  Do not retaliate by finding the faults of those who make you aware of your faults, oh Farid. Rather, be thankful to them and keep your mind at peace.      7.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਜਾ ਤਉ ਖਟਣ ਵੇਲ ਤਾ ਤੂ ਰਤਾ ਦੁਨੀ ਸਿਉ॥ ਮਰਗ ਸਵਾਈ ਨ੍ਹੀਹਿ ਜਾ ਭਰਿਆ ਤਾ ਲਦਿਆ॥8॥

Farida, ja tuou khuttun vayl, ta tu rutta dooni siyoun. Merg suvaayi neeh(i), ja bhuriya, ta luddiya.  8. 

Word Meaning:  Tuou = yours. Khuttun = earn; acquire (spirituality).  Vayl = time. Rutta = 

engrossed.    Merg = death.  Suvaayi = more than one (more than 100% certain).  Neeh(i) = 

foundation.   Bhuriya = when life is full (reaches its end).     Luddiya = leaves.

MEANING: When it was time for you to acquire spirituality (worship Allah), you were engrossed in worldly affairs, oh Farid.  The foundation (time) of death is certain (and it grants no more opportunity to worship). When one reaches the end of his life, he leaves the world.8. 

ਦੇਖੁ ਫਰੀਦਾ ਜੁ ਥੀਆ ਦਾੜੀ ਹੋਈ ਭੂਰ॥ ਅਗਹੁ ਨੇੜਾ ਆਇਆ ਪਿਛਾ ਰਹਿਆ ਦੂਰਿ॥ 9॥

Daykh(u) Farida ju theeya, daahrrI hoyee bhoor.  Aggoun nayrra aaya, pichha ruhhiya door(i).  9.

Word Meaning:  Bhoor = brown; grey.  Aggoun = future (death).  Pichha = past. 

MEANING:  Look, what has happened to you – your beard has become grey!! Your past is left far behind, and your future (death) is approaching fast (and you still haven’t started to remember Allah), oh Farid.   9.

ਦੇਖੁ ਫਰੀਦਾ ਜੋ ਥੀਆ ਸਕਰ ਹੋਈ ਵਿਸੁ॥ ਸਾਈ ਬਾਝਹੁ ਆਪਣੇ ਵੇਦਣ ਕਹੀਐ ਕਿਸੁ॥ 10॥

Daykh Farida jo theeya, shukkur hoyee viss(u). Saayeen baajhoun aapnay, vaydun kuhhiyaiy kis(u).  10.

Word Meaning:  Shukkur = sugar (the sweet luxuries of life).  Viss(u) = poison (for spirituality).  Baajhoun = without.  Vaydun = pain; agony.

MEANING: Look, what has happened – all the sweet luxuries that you enjoyed in life have turned into poison for your spirituality – oh, Farid. But, other than your own Lord, who else is there whom to tell your agony (only His Naam can treat your suffering). 10.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਅਖੀ ਦੇਖਿ ਪਤੀਣੀਆ ਸੁਣਿ ਸੁਣਿ ਰੀਣੇ ਕੰਨ॥ ਸਾਖ ਪਕੰਦੀ ਆਈਆ ਹੋਰ ਕਰੇਦੀ ਵੰਨ॥ 11॥

Farida, ukheen daykh(i) puteeniyaan, soon(i), soon(i) reenay kunn. Shaakh pukundi aayiya, hoar kurayndi vunn.  11.

Word Meaning: Ukheen = eyes.  Puteeniyaan = have become weak.   Reeany = empty (loss of hearing).  Shaakh = branches; all parts of the body.  Pukundi Aayiya = about to ripen.  Vunn = colour.

MEANING:  Enjoying the luxuries of life, your eyes have lost their strength, and your ears have lost their hearing power. Not only these two, but all the other parts of your body have also become weak with the ripening of your age, and they have changed their colour (appearance). (Time is running out for you to worship God), oh Farid.  11. 

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਕਾਲੀ ਜਿਨੀ ਨ ਰਾਵਿਆ ਧਉਲੀ ਰਾਵੈ ਕੋਇ॥ ਕਰਿ ਸਾਈ ਸਿਉ ਪਿਰਹੜੀ ਰੰਗੁ ਨਵੇਲਾ ਹੋਇ॥12

Farida, kaali jinhi na raaviya, dhauli raavaiy koyay. Kur(i) Saayeen siyoun pirhurri, rung(u) nuvayla hoyay. 

Word Meaning:  Kaali = when hair is still black; young age.  Raaviya = recited God’s Naam.   Dhauli = grey hair; old age.  Pirhurri = Rung = love.     Nuvayla = new; fresh. 

MEANING:  If one does not remember Allah in their youth, there is a rare individual who begins to do so in old age. So, one should start loving God from an early age so that their love is always new (young), oh Farid.      12.

ਮ:3॥ ਫਰੀਦਾ ਕਾਲੀ ਧਉਲੀ ਸਾਹਿਬੁ ਸਦਾ ਹੈ ਜੇ ਕੋ ਚਿਤਿ ਕਰੇਇ॥ ਆਪਣਾ ਲਾਇਆ ਪਿਰਮੁ ਨ ਲਗਈ ਜੇ ਲੋਚੈ ਸਭੁ ਕੋਇ॥ ਇਹੁ ਪਿਰਮੁ ਪਿਆਲਾ ਖਸਮ ਕਾ, ਜੈ ਭਾਵੈ ਤੈ ਦੇਇ॥ 13॥

M:3:  Farida, kaali, dhauli, Sahib(u) sudaa haiy, jay ko chit(i) kuray. Aapna laaya pirm na lugyee, jay loachaiy subh(u) koyay. Eh(u) pirm(u) piyaala Khusm ka, jaiy bhaavaiy taiyn dayay.   13.

Word Meaning:  M:3 = the Third Sikh Guru, Sri Amar Dass Ji.  Pirm = love.  Loachaiy = yeans for.  Bhaavaiy = likes.   Taiyn = him.

MEANING:  Expanding on Farid Sahib’s Sloak above, Gur Amar Dass Ji explains that age does not matter, and one can love Waheguru any time, whether he is young or old, provided he keeps Him in mind.  However, one cannot foster love for Waheguru on one's own, although everybody yearns to do so.  Love for Waheguru is His benevolence; He bestows the cup (drink) of His love only to those whom He loves.   13.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਜਿਨ੍ਹ ਲੋਇਣੁ ਜਗੁ ਮੋਹਿਆ ਸੇ ਲੋਇਣ ਮੈ ਡਿਠੁ॥ ਕਜਲ ਰੇਖ ਨ ਸਹਿਦੀਆ ਸੇ ਪੰਖੀ ਸੂਇ ਬਹਿਠੁ॥ 14॥

Farida, jinh loyin jug(u) mohiya, say loyin mein ditth(u). Kujjul raykh ne sehindiyaan, say punkhi sooyay behitth(u).  14.

Word Meaning: Loyin = eyes.  Mohiya = charmed.  Kujjul Raykh = a touch of kohl. Sehindiyaan = endure.   Punkhi = birds.   Sooyay = offspring; eggs.  Behitth = room.

MEANING: I have seen the beautiful eyes of women who have charmed the whole world. The eyes, which were too delicate to endure even a touch of kohl, have now become the place for the birds to lay eggs in there (in the grave), oh Farid. (This is the worth of a human body.) 14.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਕੂਕੇਦਿਆ ਚਾਗੇਦਿਆ ਮਤੀ ਦੇਦਿਆ ਨਿਤ॥ ਜੋ ਸੈਤਾਨਿ ਵੰਞਾਇਆ ਸੇ ਕਿਤ ਫੇਰਹਿ ਚਿਤ॥ 15॥

Farida, kookayndiyaan chaangayndiyaan, mutti dayndiyaan nit. Jo Shaiytaan vunjaaya, say kit phayreh(i) chit(u). 15.

Word Meaning:  Kookayndiyaan = Chaangayndiyaan = calling out loudly.  Mutti = advice. Shaiytaan = the Satan (-like mind).   Vunjaaya = misled.  Phayreh(i) = turn around.

MEANING:  Despite being constantly advised aloud to do so, the people

 who have been misled by the Satan-like mind do not turn around from their evil thinking (and worship Allah), oh Farid.  15.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਥੀਉ ਪਵਾਹੀ ਦਭੁ॥ ਜੈ ਸਾਈ ਲੋੜਹਿ ਸਭੁ॥ ਇਕ ਛਿਜਹਿ ਬਿਆ ਲਤਾੜੀਅਹਿ॥ ਤਾ ਸਾਈ ਦੇ ਦਰਿ ਵਾੜੀਅਹਿ॥ 16॥

Farida, theeou puvaahi dubh(u).  Jaiy Saayeen lorreh(i) subh(u).  Ikk chhijjeh biyya lutaarriyeh(i). Taan Saayeen day Dur(i) vaarriyeh(i).   16.

Word Meaning:  Theeou = become.     Puvaahi = of the trail (path).  Dubh(u) = a kind of tall grass.  Subh(u) = everywhere.   Ikk = some. Chhijjeh = (beaten to) weaken it (reduce in size).   Biyya = other.  Lutaarriyeh(i) = crush under weight.  Vaarriyeh(u) = put in; taken to 

MEANING: You need to be very humble (to be approved by Allah), like the tall grass of a trail, some of which is beaten to make it soft, while the rest is crushed before it is taken to its master, oh Farid.   16.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਖਾਕੁ ਨ ਨਿੰਦੀਐ ਖਾਕੂ ਜੇਡੁ ਨ ਕੋਇ॥ ਜੀਵਦਿਆ ਪੈਰਾ ਤਲੈ ਮੁਇਆ ਉਪਰਿ ਹੋਇ॥ 17॥

Farida, khaak(u) na nindiyaiy, khaakoo jayd(u) na koyay. Jeevundiyaan paiyraan tulaiy, mooyiyaan oopur hoyay.  17.

Word Meaning: Khaak(u) = dust.   Nindiyaiy = look down upon.   Jayd(u) = as good. 

MEANING:  One should not look down upon dust, oh, Farid.  In fact, nothing is as good as it is because it is under our feet (humble) when we are alive, but we are humbled by it after 

death, when it goes above us.  17. 

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਜਾ ਲਬੁ ਤ ਨੇਹੁ ਕਿਆ ਲਬੁ ਤਾ ਕੂੜਾ ਨੇਹੁ॥ ਕਿਚਰੁ ਝਤਿ ਲਘਾਈਐ ਛਪਰਿ ਤੁਟੇ ਮੇਹੁ॥ 18॥

Farida, ja lub(u) ta nayhoun kiyya, lub(u) ta koorra nayhou. Kitchur jhut(i) lunghaayiyaiy, chhuppur toottaiy mayhoun.  18. 

Word Meaning:  Lub(u) = greed; selfishness. Nayhou = love.   Koorra = fake.  Kitchur = how long.  Jhut(i) Lunghaayiyaiy = pass time.  Chhuppur = thatched hut.  Mayh(u) = rain. 

MEANING:  What kind of love (for Allah) is that where selfishness is involved; in that case, such love is fake, oh Farid. It is like not taking good care of a thatched hut on regular basis which begins to leak in rain, but how long can one stay in the broken hut without fixing it?  (If one is selfish and does not look after the hut, it does not serve him well either).    18.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਜੰਗਲੁ ਜੰਗਲੁ ਕਿਆ ਭਵਹਿ ਵਣਿ ਕੰਡਾ ਮੋੜੇਹਿ॥ ਵਸੀ ਰਬ ਹਿਆਲੀਐ ਜੰਗਲੁ ਕਿਆ ਢੂਢੇਹਿ॥ 19॥

Farida, jungle, jungle, kiyya bhuveh(i), vun(i) kunda morreh(i). Vussi Rubb hiyaaliyaiy, jungle kiyya dhoondheh(i). 19.

Word Meaning:   Bhuveh(i) = roam around.  Vun(i) = in the forest.  Kunda = thorn. Morreh(i) = bend.   Vussi = resides.  Rubb = Allah. Hiyaaliyaiy = in heart. 

MEANING: (Some people leave home and live in forests in search of God). Farid Sahib advises such people, questioning them as follows:  What are you roaming around in forests for, and tread on thorns, oh man?  God resides in your heart; so, what are you searching for in the forests, oh Farid?  19.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਇਨੀ ਨਿਕੀ ਜੰਘੀਐ ਥਲ ਡੂੰਗਰ ਭਵਿਓਮਿ॥ ਅਜ ਫਰੀਦੈ ਕੂਜੜਾ ਸੈ ਕੋਹਾ ਥੀਓਮਿ॥ 20॥

Farida, innih nikki junghiyaiyn, thul doongur bhuviom(i). Ajj Faridaiy koojrra, saiy kohaan theeom(i).  20.

Word Meaning:  Junghiyaiy = thighs; legs.  Thul = land. Doongur = Bhuviom(i) = wandered around. Ajj = today (in the old age).  Koojrra = a small earthen round pot (to store water).  Saiy Kohaan = hundreds of km (1 Koh = 2.2. km). Theeom(i) = has become.

MEANING: (When I was young) I would wander around (tirelessly) on the land, and on the mountains with my these little (young) legs; but now, in my old age, even my waterpot, which is close to me, seems like it is hundreds of km away, oh Farid. (This is how old age takes its toll on the man, so one should start worshipping Allah at a young age).  20.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਰਾਤੀ ਵਡੀਆ ਧੁਖਿ ਧੁਖਿ ਉਠਨਿ ਪਾਸ॥

(ਅੰਗ 1379)॥      ਧਿਗੁ ਤਿਨ੍ਹਾ ਦਾ ਜੀਵਿਆ ਜਿਨ੍ਹਾ ਵਿਡਾਣੀ ਆਸ॥  21॥

Farida, raateen vuddiyaan, dhookh(i), dhookh(i) ootthun paas. 

(PAGE 1379):        Dhig(u) tinha da jeeviya, jinha vidaani aas.  21. 

Word Meaning:  Dhookh(i) = hurt because of tiredness. Paas = body sides.  Dhig(u) = condemnable.  Vidaani Aas = dependence (hope) on others.

MEANING: The nights are so long (in winter) that one’s sides begin to hurt due to tiredness, yet the man keeps lying in bed, and does not sit down to worship Allah.  The life of those is reprehensible who have hope in the other (wealth rather than Allah, which they believe can buy them any comfort), oh Farid.   21.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਜੇ ਮੈ ਹੋਦਾ ਵਾਰਿਆ ਮਿਤਾ ਆਇੜਿਆ॥ ਹੇੜਾ ਜਲੈ ਮਜੀਠ ਜਿਉ ਉਪਰਿ ਅੰਗਾਰਾ॥ 22॥

Farida, jay mein honda vaariya, mitta aayayrriyaan, Hayrra mulaiy mujeetth jiyoun oopur(i) ungaara.  22.

Word Meaning:  Honda Vaariya = hide something. Mitta = friend (Guru).  Hayrra = heart.  Mujeetth = madder.  Ungaara = smouldering coal.   

MEANING:  If I hide something from my dear friend (my guru) when he visits me, I feel as if my heart is burning like madder on a smouldering coal, oh Farid.   22.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਲੋੜੇ ਦਾਖ ਬਿਜਉਰੀਆ ਕਿਕਰਿ ਬੀਜੈ ਜਟੁ॥ ਹੰਢੈ ਉਨ ਕਤਾਇਦਾ ਪੈਧਾ ਲੋੜੈ ਪਟੁ॥ 23॥

Farida, loarray daakh Bijauriya, kikkur(i) beejaiy jatt(u). Hundhaiy oonn kutaayaynda, paiyndha loarraiy putt(i).   23.

Word Meaning: Loarray = wishes.  Daakh = small resins.  Bijauriya = of Bijapur (an area in Afghanistan known for its resins).  Kikkur(i) = a tree or a bush with strong, sharp thorns.  Jatt(u) = farmer.  Hundhaiy = wanders around.  Oonn Kutaayaynda = spinning wool.  Paindha = wear.  Putt(u) = silk. 

MEANING:  What an irony, the farmer plants thorny bushes, but wishes for resins of Bijaur; and wanders around spinning wool, but wishes to wear silk, oh Farid. (This is what most people do; they wish for something better in return for their poor efforts.    23.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਗਲੀਏ ਚਿਕੜੁ ਦੂਰਿ ਘਰਿ ਨਾਲ ਪਿਆਰੇ ਨੇਹੁ॥ ਚਲਾ ਤ ਭਿਜੈ ਕੰਬਲੀ ਰਹਾ ਤ ਤੁਟੈ ਨੇਹੁ॥ 24॥

Farida guliyain chikkurr(u), door(i) ghur(i), mayra naal(i) Piyaray nayh(u). Chullaan ta bhijjaiy kumblee, ruhhaan ta toottaiy nayh(u). 24. 

Word Meaning: Guliyain = in streets; in the path. Chikkurr(u) = mud (of worldly attachments, ego, and selfishness, etc.).   Piyaray = Dear Allah.  Chullaan = if I walk on the path.  Bhijjaiy Kumblee = the blanket (of my heart) becomes drenched (heavy) with worldly attachments. 

MEANING:  The house of my Dear (Allah) is far away (spiritually - and not physically - as He resides in our heart), and the path of union with Him is very difficult as there is the obstacle of the mud of worldly attachments and of sensual pleasures.  If I follow the path to connect with Him, the blanket of my heart is drenched with the mud of worldly attachments (and pulls me back), but if I stay, my love for my Dear (Allah) is terminated, oh Farid.

ਭਿਜਉ ਸਿਜਉ ਕੰਬਲੀ ਅਲਹ ਵਰਸੈ ਮੇਹੁ॥ ਜਾਇ ਮਿਲਾ ਤਿਨ੍ਹਾ ਸਜਣਾ ਤੁਟਉ ਨਾਹੀ ਨੇਹੁ॥ 25॥

Bhijjhou, sijjou kumblee, Allah versaiy mayh(u). Jaayay milaan tinha Sujjna, tootaiy naahi nayh(u).

MEANING:  I pray to my Dear (Allah) that He causes the rain of His love on my heart, so that blanket of my heart gets fully drenched with His love, and the filth of the mud of worldly attachments, and of sensual pleasures is washed away completely with the result that I may connect with Him, and my love for Him is not terminated, oh Farid.   25. 

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਮੈ ਭੋਲਾਵਾ ਪਗ ਦਾ ਮਤੁ ਮੈਲੀ ਹੋਇ ਜਾਇ॥ ਗਹਿਲਾ ਰੂਹੁ ਨ ਜਾਣਈ ਸਿਰੁ ਭੀ ਮਿਟੀ ਖਾਇ॥26॥

Farida, mein bholaava pugg da, mut(u) maiyli hoyay jaayay. Gehla rooh na jaanyee, sir(u) bhee mitti khaayay.  26.

Word Meaning:  Bholaava = worried about.  Mut(u) = lest.  Gehla = unaware.  Rooh(u) = soul; mind. 

MEANING:  I was worried about my turban lest it should get dirty; but my unaware mind does not realize that not only the turban, but the earth eats away even the head, after death,   oh Farid. 26.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਸਕਰ ਖੰਡੁ ਨਿਵਾਤ ਗੁੜੁ ਮਾਖਿਓੁ ਮਾਝਾ ਦੁਧੁ॥ ਸਭੇ ਵਸਤੂ ਮਿਠੀਆ ਰਬ ਨ ਪੁਜਨਿ ਤੁਧੁ॥ 27॥

Farida, shukkur, khund(u), nivaat goorr(u), maakhiyon, maajha doodh(u). Subbhay vustoo mitthiyaan, Rubb na poojun(i) Toodh(u).  27.

Word Meaning:  Shukkur = raw sugar.  Nivaat = crystallized sugar.   Goorr(u) = jaggary.  Maakhiyon = honey.   Maajha Doodh(u) = buffalo milk.  Pujjun = match. 

MEANING:  Raw sugar, sugar, crystallized sugar, jaggery, honey, and buffalo’s milk are all sweet, but they cannot match the sweetness of (union with) Allah, oh Farid.  27.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਰੋਟੀ ਮੇਰੀ ਕਾਠ ਕੀ ਲਾਵਣੁ ਮੇਰੀ ਭੁਖ॥ ਜਿਨਾ ਖਾਧੀ ਚੋਪੜੀ ਘਣੇ ਸਹਿਨਗੇ ਦੁਖ॥ 28॥

Farida, roti mayri kaatth kee, laavun(u) mayri bhookh.  Jinha khaadhi choaprri, ghunay sehngay dookh.  28.

Word Meaning: Roti = roti; meal.  Kaatth = (hard and dry like) wood; very poor.  Laavun = salty dishes.  Bhookh = appetite.   Choaprri = buttered (rich food).   Ghunay = immense. 

MEANING: (Like any poor person) my meal consists of unbuttered roti, which is hard and dry like wood, and my appetite itself serves as the salty dishes. Those who eat rich foods will suffer immensely (on two fronts – first, usually one has to use dishonest means and commit sins to eat rich foods for which they will be punished in Allah’s Court, and second, rich foods usually cause diseases), oh Farid.  28.

ਰੁਖੀ ਸੁਖੀ ਖਾਇ ਕੇ ਠੰਡਾ ਪਾਣੀ ਪੀਉ॥ ਫਰੀਦਾ ਦੇਖਿ ਪਰਾਈ ਚੋਪੜੀ ਨ ਤਰਸਾਏ ਜੀਉ॥ 29॥

Rookkhi, sookkhi khaaykay, tthunda paani peeou. Farida, daykh(i) puraayi choaprri, na tersaayay jeeou.   29.

Word Meaning = Ookkhi = without any salty dish.  Sookkhi = unbuttered.  Puraayi = of others.   Tersaayay = suffer in desire. 

MEANING:  Do not let your mind suffer in the desire to have rich food whenever you see someone else having it. Feel contented to eat unbuttered roti without any salty dish and by drinking cold water, oh Farid.   29.

ਅਜੁ ਨ ਸੁਤੀ ਕੰਤ ਸਿਉ ਅੰਗੁ ਮੁੜੇ ਮੁੜਿ ਜਾਇ॥ ਜਾਇ ਪੁਛਹੁ ਡੋਹਾਗਣੀ ਤੁਮ ਕਿਉ ਰੈਣਿ ਵਿਹਾਇ॥30॥

Ajj(u) na sootti Kunt siyoun, ung(u) moorray, moor(i) jaayay. Jaayay poochhou dohaagni, toom kiyoun raiyn(i) vihaayay.  30.

Word Meaning: Sootti = sleep with; connected with.  Kunt = Husband (Allah).  Ung = body part (mind).  Moorray = is restive.  Dohaagni = separated.   Kiyon = how. 

MEANING:  It was only today that I did not connect with my Allah, and I already feel miserable in my body and my mind. Ask those who are disconnected from Allah how they spend their nights (of life) without Him, oh Farid.  30.

ਸਾਹੁਰੈ ਢੋਈ ਨ ਲਹੈ ਪੇਈਐ ਨਾਹੀ ਥਾਉ॥ ਪਿਰੁ ਵਾਤੜੀ ਨ ਪੁਛਈ ਧਨ ਸੋਹਾਗਣਿ ਨਾਉ॥ 31॥

Sahooraiy dhoyee na luhhaiy, payiyaiy naahi thaoun. Pir(u) vaatrri n poochhyee, dhun sohaagun(i) naaoun.  31.

Word Meaning:  Sahooraiy = in-laws’ house.  Dhoyee = shelter.   Payiyaiy = parents’ house.  Thaaoun = room.  Dhun = woman. Sohaagun(i) = married. 

MEANING:  What kind of woman is that who calls herself “married”, but has no support (love) in her husband’s home, and has no room (is not welcome) in her parents’ home, oh Farid?31. 

ਸਾਹੁਰੈ ਪੇਈਐ ਕੰਤੁ ਕੀ ਕੰਤੁ ਅਗੰਮੁ ਅਥਾਹੁ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਸੋ ਸੋਹਾਗਣੀ ਜੁ ਭਾਵੈ ਬੇਪਰਵਾਹੁ॥ 32॥

Sauhuraiy, payiyaiy Kunt(u) kee, Kunt(u) agumm(u) athaah(u).  Nanak, so sohaagni, ju bhaavaiy Baypervaah(u).  32.

MEANING:  The inaccessible, and the unfathomable Waheguru is the Husband (Lord) of every human soul in both the worlds – on the earth, and after death – but only that human bride gets married to (connected with) Him, whom He likes, oh Nanak. (So, one should recite Waheguru’s Naam with diligence to be respected in both worlds).  32.

NOTE: This Sloak has been written by Guru Nanak Sahib, and is enshrined in Sri Guru Granth Sahib on page 1088.

ਨਾਤੀ ਧੋਤੀ ਸੰਬਹੀ ਸੁਤੀ ਆਇ ਨਚਿੰਦੁ॥ ਫਰੀਦਾ ਰਹੀ ਸੁ ਬੇੜੀ ਹਿੰਙੁ ਦੀ ਗਈ ਕਥੂਰੀ ਗੰਧੁ॥ 33॥

Nahati, dhoti sumbhee, sootti aayay nachind(u). Farida ruhhi so bayhrri hing dee, guyyi kathoori gundh(u).   33.

Word Meaning: Sumbhee = adorned.   Sootti =  became negligent.  Nachind(u) = worry free.  Bayhrri = smeared with.    Hing = asafetida (a spice with awful smell).     Kuthoori = musk.

MEANING:  A person who washes his body well (to purify it), and adorns himself with religious symbols with the belief that God will be pleased with his symbols, and then shows negligence in remembering Him (does not please Him). His efforts become like an object which loses its musk-like pleasant fragrance, and is left with the repulsive smell of asafetida, oh Farid.   33.

ਜੋਬਨ ਜਾਦੇ ਨ ਡਰਾ ਜੇ ਸਹਿ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਿ ਨ ਜਾਇ॥ ਫਰੀਦਾ ਕਿਤੀ ਜੋਬਨ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਿ ਬਿਨੁ ਸੁਕਿ ਗਏ ਕੁਮਲਾਇ॥34

Jobun jaanday na duraan, jay Sheh(i) preet(i) na jaayay. Farida, kitti jobun preet(i) bin(u), sook(i) guyyay koomlaayay.  34.

Word Meaning:  Jobun = youthfulness.  Sheh(i) = Husband (God).  Preet(i) = love.  Kitti = very many. Sook(i) = dried up.  Koomlaayay = wilted.

MEANING:  I am not afraid to lose my youthfulness if my love for Allah is not lost.  The hearts of countless people have wilted and dried up (have experienced spiritual death) even in the prime of their youth because of their lack of love for Allah, oh Farid.  34. 

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਚਿੰਤ ਖਟੋਲਾ ਵਾਣੁ ਦੁਖੁ ਬਿਰਹਿ ਵਿਛਾਵਣ ਲੇਫੁ॥ ਏਹੁ ਹਮਾਰਾ ਜੀਵਣਾ ਤੂ ਸਾਹਿਬ ਸਚੇ ਵੇਖੁ॥35॥

Farida, chint khutoala, vaan(u) dookh(u), bireh(i) vichhaavun layf. Eh(u) humaara jeevna, Tu Sahib Sachay vaykh(u).  35.

Word Meaning:  Chint = worries.  Khutoala = cot.  Vaan = the strings of jute with which the cot is woven.   Bireh(i) = the pain of separation (from God).  Vichhaavun = to make bed; the soft pad to lie on.   Layf = mattress. 

MEANING: Because of my separation from You, my worries have become my cot (bed) which is woven with the strings of discomforts (suffering), and which has the pain of separation from you, as the mattress, oh my dear Allah. Farid prays to You to look at the pathetic life that he is living without You, oh, the eternal Allah.   35.

ਬਿਰਹਾ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਆਖੀਐ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਤੂ ਸੁਲਤਾਨੁ॥ ਫਰੀਦਾ ਜਿਤੁ ਤਨਿ ਬਿਰਹੁ ਨ ਊਪਜੈ ਸੋ ਤਨੁ ਜਾਣੁ ਮਸਾਨੁ॥ 36॥

Birha, birha aakhiyaiy, birha tu sultan. Farida, jit(u) tun(i) birhou na oopjaiy, so tun jaan(u) musaan(u).  36.

Word Meaning:  Birha = pain of separation.  Sultan = king; great.  Oopjaiy = is born; feels.  Jaan(u) = consider.   Musaan(u) = graveyard. 

MEANING:  Everybody talks about (and hates) the pain of separation from their loved ones, but I feel that the pain of separation (from Allah) is great.  The body (man) which does not feel any pain of separation from Allah is like a graveyard (where ghosts reside), oh Farid.    36.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਏ ਵਿਸੁ ਗੰਦਲਾ ਧਰੀਆ ਖੰਡੁ ਲਿਵਾੜਿ॥ ਇਕਿ ਰਾਹੇਦੇ ਰਹਿ ਗਏ ਇਕਿ ਰਾਧੀ ਗਏ ਉਜਾੜਿ॥37

Farida, eh viss(u) gundlaan, dhuriyaan khund(u) livaarr(i). Ikk(i) raahaynday reh(i) guyyay, ikk(i) raadhi guyyay oojaarr(i). 

Word Meaning:  Viss(u) = poison.  Gundlaan = soft stems of leafy vegetables; attractions of Maya.   Dhuriyaan = are presented.  Livaarr(u) = coated with.  Raahaynday = planting. Reh(i) Guyyay = exhausted.  Raadhi = planted.   Oojaarr(i) = destroy.

MEANING:  The attractions of Maya are like a sugar-coated stalk of some poisonous plant.   Some get exhausted planting them (earning more and more wealth to enjoy the attractions, but never truly enjoy them), while others destroy (waste away) the wealth saved by their ancestors, oh, Farid. (Some die working hard to earn money, and never get to enjoy, while others use the hard-earned wealth they inherit to enjoy sensual pleasures).   37.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਚਾਰਿ ਗਵਾਇਆ ਹੰਢਿ ਕੈ ਚਾਰਿ ਗਵਾਇਆ ਸੰਮਿ॥ ਲੇਖਾ ਰਬ ਮੰਗੇਸੀਆ ਤੂੰ ਆਹੋ ਕੇਰ੍ਹੇ ਕੰਮਿ॥38

Farida, chaar(i) guvaaya hundh(i) kaiy, chaar(i) guvaaya summ(i). Laykha Rubb mungaysiya,tu aahon kayrray kumm(i).  38.

Word Meaning:  Chaar(i) = four (pehrs) = 12 hours of day.  Hundh(i) = wandering around in worldly affairs.  Summ(i) = sleeping.  Laykha = account.  Aahon = came into the world.

MEANING: (Forgetting Allah), the man spends the 12 hours of the day working for his living, and spends the 12 hours of the night sleeping (has no time to remember Allah). But Allah will demand the account of his deeds, and will question him what the purpose of his birth was (to earn wealth, or have union with God), oh Farid.    38. 

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਦਰਿ ਦਰਵਾਜੈ ਜਾਇ ਕੈ ਕਿਉ ਡਿਠੋ ਘੜੀਆਲੁ॥ ਇਹੁ ਨਿਦੋਸਾ ਮਾਰੀਐ ਹਮ ਦੋਸਾ ਦਾ ਕਿਆ ਹਾਲੁ॥ 39॥

Farida, dur(i) durvaajaiy jaayay kaiy, kiyoun dittho ghurriyaal(u). Eh(u) nidosha maariyaiy, hum doashaan ka kiyya haal(u)?  39.

Word Meaning:  Dur(i) = Durvaajaiy = at the door (of some official building).  Ghurriyaal(u) = gong; bell.  Nidosha = not guilty.  Doashaan = guilty. 

MEANING:  Have you ever seen a gong being beaten at regular intervals (to announce time) at some office, oh, Farid?  If that guilt-free gong is beaten so hard, guess what will happen to us, who are so guilty (sinners), in Allah’s Court.   39.

ਘੜੀਐ ਘੜੀਐ ਮਾਰੀਐ ਪਹਰੀ ਲਹੈ ਸਜਾਇ॥ਸੋ ਹੇੜਾ ਘੜੀਆਲ ਜਿਉ ਡੁਖੀ ਰੈਣਿ ਵਿਹਾਇ॥ 40॥

Ghurriyaiy, ghurriyaiy maariyaiy, pehreen luhhaiy sujaayay. So, hayrra ghurriyaal jiyoun, dookheen raiyn(i) vihaayay.   40.

Word Meaning:  Ghurriyaiy = every 24 minutes. Pehreen = every three hours. Sujaayay = punishment; beaten much harder.  Hayrra = heart.  Dookheen = in suffering.  Raiyn(i) = night (of life). Vihaayay = is spent.

MEANING: The gong is beaten (albeit not very hard) every twenty-four minutes, and is beaten much harder (as if being punished) every three hours.  The heart (without Allah’s Naam) is like the gong, and its life is spent in suffering, oh Farid.  40.

(ਅੰਗ 1380)॥   ਬੁਢਾ ਹੋਆ ਸੇਖ ਫਰੀਦੁ ਕੰਬਣਿ ਲਗੀ ਦੇਹ॥ ਜੇ ਸਉ ਵਰ੍ਹਿਆ ਜੀਵਣਾ ਭੀ ਤਨੁ ਹੋਸੀ ਖੇਹ॥ 41॥

(PAGE 1380):              Booddha hoa Sheikh Farid(u), kumbun luggi dayh(u).  Jay suou vurihaan jeevna, bhee tun(u) hoasi khayh. 

Word Meaning:  When a man gets old, his body begins to shake uncontrollably.  Even if the man lives for hundreds of years, his body will still turn into dust. Oh, Farid (this is the fate of the human body, and all his enjoyments are left behind. So, worship Allah).  41.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਬਾਰਿ ਪਰਾਇਐ ਬੈਸਣਾ ਸਾਈ ਮੁਝੈ ਨ ਦੇਹਿ॥ ਜੇ ਤੂੰ ਏਵੈ ਰਖਸੀ ਜੀਉ ਸਰੀਰਹੁ ਲੇਹਿ॥ 42॥

Farida, baar(i) puraayiyain baiysna, Saayeen moojhaiy na dayh(i). Jay tu avaiyn rukhsi, jeeou sureeroun layh(i).   42.

Word Meaning: Baar(i) = door (shelter).  Puraayiyaiy = of others.  Baiysna = sit; depend upon.  Rukhsi = keep.   Jeeou = life; soul.

MEANING: Do not leave me to the dependence of others (including spiritual dependence of others than Yours), begs Farid.  If You must keep me in that condition, kindly take my soul out of my body, oh Allah.  42.

ਕੰਧਿ ਕੁਹਾੜਾ ਸਿਰਿ ਘੜਾ ਵਣਿ ਕੈਸਰੁ ਲੋਹਾਰੁ॥ ਫਰੀਦਾ ਹਉ ਲੋੜੀ ਸਹੁ ਆਪਣਾ ਤੂ ਲੋੜਹਿ ਅੰਗਿਆਰ॥ 43॥

Kundh(i) koohaarra, sir(i) ghurra, vun(i) kaiser(u) lohaar(u). Farida, huoun loarreen Shuhou aapna, tu loorreh(i) ungiyaar.  43.

Word Meaning: Kundh(i) = on the shoulder.  Koohaarra = ax.   Ghurra = pitcher (of water).  Vun(i) = forest; trees.  Kaiser(u) = king (conceit).  Lohaar(u) = blacksmith.   Loarreen = wish for.  Shuhou = husband.  Ungiyaar = smoldering coal (ash); sensual pleasures. 

MEANING:  [Using the example of a blacksmith, who feels conceited in the forest (that he can cut down any tree - and the trees in the forest are symbols of humbleness and sacrifice- see Sloak 60 in this section), and contrasting the nature of the two, Farid Sahib explains how one can realize Allah].  With a water pitcher on his head and an axe on his shoulder, the blacksmith feels conceited like a king in the forest, that he can attack any tree.  [But, the trees in the forest advise him that with our humbleness and sacrifice (we do not show disdain for you when you cut us; we are rather very useful for humans like you. Not only do we provide wood for you to build houses, furniture, boats, doors, windows, and so on, but we also provide fruit, shade, and shelter from rain and cold, along with many other services. We are hoping to be approved by God)]. But you, on the other hand, are conceited and would use us for your enjoyment and even burn us for fuel.  God is not realized by such an attitude and pleasure-seeking; He likes humility like trees, oh Farid.    43. 

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਇਕਨਾ ਆਟਾ ਅਗਲਾ ਇਕਨਾ ਨਾਹੀ ਲੋਣੁ॥ ਅਗੈ ਗਏ ਸਿੰਞਾਪਸਨ੍ਹਿ ਚੋਟਾ ਖਾਸੀ ਕਉਣੁ॥ 44॥

Farida, ikkna aata ugla, ikkna naahi loan(u). Uggaiy guyyay sinyaapsun(i) choataan khaasi kaun(u). 

Word Meaning:  Ikkna = some.  Atta = flour (wealth).  Ugla = too much.  Naahi Loan = not even salt (no wealth at all).  Sinyaapsun(i) = recognized.  Choataan = hits; punishment.

MEANING:  Some people have too much wealth (which they normally accumulate by dishonest means), while others are so poor that they cannot afford even table salt (which is very inexpensive).  When they reach Allah’s Court, the wealthy will be recognized and punished severely, oh Farid.   44.

ਪਾਸਿ ਦਮਾਮੇ ਛਤੁ ਸਿਰਿ ਭੇਰੀ ਸਡੋ ਰਡ॥ ਜਾਇ ਸੁਤੇ ਜੀਰਾਣ ਮਹਿ ਥੀਏ ਅਤੀਮਾ ਗਡ॥ 45॥

Paas(i) Dumaamay, chhut(u) sir(i), bhayri, suddo rud. Jaayay soottay jeeraan meh(i), theeyay ateema gudd.  45.

Word Meaning:  Paas(i) = in possession; have.  Dumaamay = drums (sign of authority). Chhut(u) = royal canopy.  Bhayri = bugles.   Suddo = call; singing.  Rud = a lyric sung in praise.  Jeeraan = graveyard.  Theeyay = Ateema = orphans.  Gudd = buried.

MEANING:  Even the rajahs for whom drums are beaten in their honour, have royal canopies above their heads, the sound of bugles announces their arrival, and songs are sung in their praise, are eventually buried in the graveyard, and become orphans (powerless), oh Farid.  45.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਕੋਠੇ ਮੰਡਪ ਮਾੜੀਆ ਉਸਾਰੇਦੇ ਭੀ ਗਏ॥ ਕੂੜਾ ਸਉਦਾ ਕਰਿ ਗਏ ਗੋਰੀ ਆਇ ਪਏ॥ 46॥

Farida, koatthay, mundup, maarriyaan, oosaaranday bhee guyyay. Koorra sauda kur(i) guyyay, goareen aayay puyyay.  46.

Word Meaning:  Koatthay = houses.  Mundup = decorated.  Maarriyaan = mansions.  Oosaaraynday = building.  Koorra Sauda = false deals (of perishables).  Goareen = in grave.

MEANING:  Even those who built big houses and decorated their mansions left this world. They made false deals (of perishables with nothing to accompany), and at the end were laid down in small graves, oh, Farid.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਖਿੰਥੜਿ ਮੇਖਾ ਅਗਲੀਆ ਜਿੰਦੁ ਨ ਕਾਈ ਮੇਖ॥ ਵਾਰੀ ਆਪੋ ਆਪਣੀ ਚਲੇ ਮਸਾਇਕ ਸੇਖ॥ 47॥

Farida, khinthurr(i) maykhaan ugliyaan, jind(u) na kaayee maykh. Vaari aapo, aaoni, chullay musaayik sheikh.  47.

Word Meaning:  Khinthurr(i) = quilt.  Maykhaan = nails, stitches (to keep quilts falling apart). Vaari = turn.  Sheikh = a learned or a wealthy Muslim.  Musaayik = many sheikhs.

MEANING: Even a quilt has many stitches (to keep it from falling apart), but a human soul has none. Even all the sheikhs leave this world when it is their turn, oh Farid.   47.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਦੁਹੁ ਦੀਵੀ ਬਲੰਦਿਆ ਮਲਕੁ ਬਹਿਠਾ ਆਇ॥ ਗੁੜੁ ਲੀਤਾ ਘਟੁ ਲੁਟਿਆ ਦੀਵੜੇ ਗਇਆ ਬੁਝਾਇ॥

Farida, doohoun deeveen bulundiyaa, Mulk(u) behttha aayay. Gurrh(u) leeta, ghut(u) lootiya, deevrray guyya boojhaayay.  48.

Word Meaning: Doohoun = both.  Deeveen = lamps (eyes).  Bulundiyaa = lighting. Mulk(u) = Angel of death.  Gurrh(u) = fort; body.   Leeta = controlled.   Ghut(u) = heart. 

MEANING: The Angel of death arrives even when both the eyes of the man are still lit (living).  He takes possession of his body fort, robs his heart (soul), and shuts down his eyes, oh Farid.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਵੇਖੁ ਕਪਾਹੈ ਜਿ ਥੀਆ ਜਿ ਸਿਰਿ ਥੀਆ ਤਿਲਾਹ॥ ਕਮਾਦੈ ਅਰੁ ਕਾਗਦੈ ਕੁੰਨੇ ਕੋਇਲਿਆਹ॥ ਮੰਦੇ ਅਮਲ ਕਰੇਦਿਆ ਏਹ ਸਜਾਇ ਤਿਨਾਹ॥ 49॥

Farida, vaykh(u) kupaahaiy j(i) theeya, j(i) sir(i) theeya tilaahn. Kmaadaiy, ur(u) kaagdaiy, koonnaiy koyiliyaan.  Munday uml kurayndiyaan, eh sujaayay tinhaan.  49.

Word Meaning:  Kupaahaiy = cotton.  J(i) = whatever. Theeya = happened.  Tilaah = sesame seeds.  Kmaadaiy = to sugarcane.  Kaagdaiy = paper.  Koonnaiy = cooking vessels.  Koyiliyaan = to coals.   Munday Uml = bad deeds. Sujssyay = punishment.

MEANING: Look what happens to cotton (it is ginned); to the sesame seeds (which are crushed to get oil); to the sugarcane (which is squeezed tightly in mills); to paper (when it is prepared by crushing wood to prepare pulp); to cooking vessels (they are put on open flame), and the coals (which are burned). They are punished for their only crime (bad deed) which is that they each hide (contain) things which are very useful to the man.  (So, imagine what will your punishment be in Allah’s Court, who has committed many bad deeds in hiding), oh Farid.   49.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਕੰਨਿ ਮੁਸਲਾ ਸੂਫੁ ਗਲਿ ਦਿਲਿ ਕਾਤੀ ਗੁੜੁ ਵਾਤਿ॥ ਬਾਹਰਿ ਦਿਸੈ ਚਾਨਣਾ ਦਿਲਿ ਅੰਧਿਆਰੀ ਰਾਤਿ॥ 50॥

Farida, kunnh(i) moosulla, soof(u) gul(i), dil(i) kaati, goorr(u) vaat(i). Baahur(i) disaiy chaan-na, dil(i) undhiyaari raat(i).  50.

Word Meaning:  Kunnh(i) = on the shoulder. Moosulla = a small rug to sit on when they offer nivaj.  Soof(u) Gul(i) = a black outfit that the Sufis (a Muslim sect) wear.  Kaati = knife.   Goorr(u) = jaggary; sweetness.   Vaat(i) = in mouth; on tongue.   Chaan-na = enlightenment.    Undhiyaari = darkness.

MEANING: Muslims, who wear all the religious symbols including a small soft rug on the shoulder, wear a black robe of Sufism, and are very sweet in conversation; but have knife at heart (to fleece people), may look highly enlightened outwardly, but their minds (thinking) have darkness of night, oh Farid.  50.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਰਤੀ ਰਤੁ ਨ ਨਿਕਲੇ ਜੇ ਤਨੁ ਚੀਰੈ ਕੋਇ॥ ਜੋ ਤਨ ਰਤੇ ਰਬ ਸਿਉ ਤਿਨ ਤਨਿ ਰਤੁ ਨ ਹੋਇ॥51॥

Farida, rutee, rutt(u) na niklay, jay tun(u) cheeraiy koyay. Jo tun tuttay Rubb siyoun, tinh tun(i) rutt(u) na hoyay.  51.

Word Meaning:  Rutee = a miniscule amount. Rutt(u) = blood.  Cheeraiy = lacerate; slash.   Ruttay = imbued in love. 

MEANING: Not even  a droplet of blood comes out if someone slashes the body of a devotee of Allah, as the devotees, who are imbued with Allah’s love, have no blood in their bodies, oh Farid.  51.

ਮ:3॥ ਇਹੁ ਤਨੁ ਸਭੋ ਰਤੁ ਹੈ ਰਤੁ ਬਿਨੁ ਤੰਨੁ ਨ ਹੋਇ॥ ਜੋ ਸਹ ਰਤੇ ਆਪਣੇ ਤਿਤੁ ਤਨਿ ਲੋਭੁ ਰਤੁ ਨ ਹੋਇ॥ ਭੈ ਪਇਐ ਤਨੁ ਖੀਣੁ ਹੋਇ ਲੋਭੁ ਰਤੁ ਵਿਚਹੁ ਜਾਇ॥ ਜਿਉ ਬੈਸੰਤਰਿ ਧਾਤੁ ਸੁਧੁ ਹੋਇ ਤਿਉ ਹਰਿ ਕਾ ਭਉ ਦੁਰਮਤਿ ਮੈਲੁ ਗਵਾਇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਤੇ ਜਨ ਸੋਹਣੇ ਜਿ ਰਤੇ ਹਰਿ ਰੰਗੁ ਲਾਇ॥ 52॥

M:3: Eh(u) tun(u) subbho rutt(u) haiy, bin(u) rutt(u), tunn(u) na hoyay. Joruttay, Sheh aapnay, tit(u) tun(i) loabh(u) rutt(u) na hoyay. Bhaiy puyyiyain tun(u) kheen(u) hoyay, loabh(u) rutt(u) vichoun jaayay. Jiyoun baiysuntar(i), dhaat(u) shoodh(u) hoyay, tiyoun Hur(i) ka bhuou doormat(i) maiyl(u) guvaayay.  Nanak, tay jun sohnay, j(i) ruttay Hur(i) rung(u) laayay.   52.

NOTE:   Lest the previous Sloak by Farid Sahib may mislead some people about the lack of blood in the bodies of the devotees, Guru Amar Dass Ji clarifies it in this Sloak.

Word Meaning:  Sheh = Husband; Waheguru; Allah. Loabh = greed.  Bhaiy = fear of God.    Kheen = weak.   Baiysuntar(i) = fire.  Dhaat(u) = metals; gold.   Doormut(i) = evil thinking.  Maiyl(u) = filth.   Sohnay = beautiful (by mind). 

MEANING: The human body has blood in it, and it cannot exist without blood. Yes, the devotees, who are imbued in the love of their Dear (Waheguru) do not have blood of greed.  With the fear of Waheguru, their bodies become weak (in greed), and the blood of greed leaves the body.  Just like fire purifies gold, the fear of Allah, removes the filth of evil thinking. They are beautiful by mind who are imbued in God’s love, oh Nanak.   52.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਸੋਈ ਸਰਵਰ ਢੂਢਿ ਲਹੁ ਜਿਥਹੁ ਲਭੀ ਵਥੁ॥ ਛਪੜਿ ਢੂਢੈ ਕਿਆ ਹੋਵੈ ਚਿਕੜਿ ਡੁਬੈ ਹਥੁ॥ 53॥

Farida, soyee survur dhoondh(i) luhhou, jithoun lubbhi vuth(u). Chhuppurr(i) dhoondhaiy kiyya hovaiy, chikkurr(i) doobaiy huth(u).  53. 

Word Meaning:  Survur = pool (of Amrit); Guru. Vuth(u) = the thing one needs (Allah’s Naam).   Chhuppur = pond of dirty water (a fake guru).  Chikkurr(u) = mud. 

MEANING:  Find the true Guru from whom you can acquire Allah’s Naam, oh Farid.  What can you gain by searching a pond of dirty water (an unenlightened guru), other than that, your hand will be smeared with mud when you put it in that?  (One gets only ill advice from a fake guru).   53.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਨੰਢੀ ਕੰਤੁ ਨ ਰਾਵਿਓ ਵਡੀ ਥੀ ਮੁਈਆ ਸੁ॥ ਧਨ ਕੂਕੇਦੀ ਗੋਰ ਮੇ ਤੈ ਸਹ ਨਾ ਮਿਲੀਆ ਸੁ॥ 54॥

Farida, nundhi Kunt(u) na raaviyo, vuddi thee mooyiya su. Dhun kookayndi goar main, taiy Sheh na miliya su.  54.

Word Meaning:  Nundhi = a young girl (human bride), Raaviyo = recited the Naam.  Vuddi = old.  Su = that. Dhun = woman; human soul. Kookayndi = laments aloud.  Goar = grave.

MEANING:  If a human bride (man) does not recite Allah’s Naam in young age, she dies the same way (without reciting the Naam) in old age.  Then the human soul laments in the grave that she did not acquire (get connected with) Allah, oh Farid.   54.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਸਿਰੁ ਪਲਿਆ ਦਾੜੀ ਪਲੀ ਮੁਛਾ ਭੀ ਪਲੀਆ॥ ਰੇ ਮਨ ਗਹਿਲੇ ਬਾਵਲੇ ਮਾਣਹਿ ਕਿਆ ਰਲੀਆ॥55

Farida,sir(u) puliya, daahrri pulie, moochhaan bhee puliyaan. Re mun gehlay baavlay, maaneh(i) kiyya ruliyaan.   55.

Word Meaning:  Puliya = grew old (hair turned grey). Daahrri = beard.  Moochhaan = moustache.  Gehlay = unaware.  Baavlay = foolish.  Ruliyaan = worldly enjoyments. 

MEANING: Your hair, beard, and moustache have all turned grey (you have become old), oh, Farid. But (forgetting Waheguru), how can you still enjoy worldly luxuries, oh, my unaware and foolish mind?   55.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਕੋਠੇ ਧੁਕਣੁ ਕੇਤੜਾ ਪਿਰ ਨੀਦੜੀ ਨਿਵਾਰਿ॥ ਜੋ ਦਿਹ ਲਧੇ ਗਾਣਵੇ ਗਏ ਵਿਲਾੜਿ ਵਿਲਾੜਿ॥ 56॥

Farida, koatthay dhookun(u) kaytrra, Pir neendrri nivaar(i).  Jo dinh luddhay gaanvay, guyyay vilaar(i), vilaarr(i). 56.

Word Meaning:  Koattahy = (on the roof of a) house. Dhookun(u) = running.     Kaytrra = how far.   Pir = Dear (Allah).  Neendrri = bad sleep; unacceptable ignoring.  Novaar(i) = remove.   Dinh = days. Ludhay = received.   Gaanvay = limited.  Vilaarr(i) = jumping.

MEANING: The span of life is short, like running on the roof of a house, where you cannot go too far.  So, get rid of your unacceptable ignoring of Allah (and start worshipping Him). The limited days of life you received are passing very fast, as if they were jumping, oh, Farid.  56.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਕੋਠੇ ਮੰਡਪ ਮਾੜੀਆ ਏਤੁ ਨ ਲਾਇ ਚਿਤੁ॥ ਮਿਟੀ ਪਈ ਅਤੋਲਵੀ ਕੋਇ ਨ ਹੋਸੀ ਮਿਤੁ॥ 57॥

Farida, koatthay mundup maarriyaan, ait na laayay chit(u). Mitti puyyi atoalveen, koyay na hoasi mit(u).  57. 

Word Meaning:  Mundup Maarriyaan = decorated mansions. Atoalveen = immeasurable.

MEANING:  Do not get attached to your big house and the decorated mansion, oh Farid. You may have used an unlimited amount of soil (wealth) to build them, but nothing will help you when your death comes.   57.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਮੰਡਪ ਮਾਲੁ ਨ ਲਾਇ ਮਰਗ ਸਤਾਣੀ ਚਿਤਿ ਧਰਿ॥

(ਅੰਗ 1381)॥   ਸਾਈ ਜਾਇ ਸਮ੍ਹਾਲਿ ਜਿਥੈ ਹੀ ਤਉ ਵੰਞਣਾ॥ 58॥

Farida, mundup maal(u) na laayay, merg sutaani chit(i) dhur(i).

(PAGE 1381): Saayee jaayay smaahl(i), jithaiy hee tuou vunjna.   58.

Word Meaning:  Maal(u) = property; wealth.  Merg = death.     Sutaani = powerful.   Saayee = that.    Jaayay = place.      Smaahl(i) = remember.    Vunjna = to go. 

MEANING:  Do not get attached to your decorations (decorated mansions) and property; keep the powerful death in mind, all the time, oh Farid.  Remember the place (Allah’s court)  where you have to go at the end.  58.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਜਿਨ੍ਹੀ ਕੰਮੀ ਨਾਹਿ ਗੁਣ ਤੇ ਕੰਮੜੇ ਵਿਸਾਰਿ॥ ਮਤੁ ਸਰਮਿੰਦਾ ਥੀਵਹੀ ਸਾਈ ਦੈ ਦਰਬਾਰਿ॥ 59॥

Farida, jinhi kummeen naah(i) goon, tay kummrray visaar(i). Mut(u) sherminda theevyee, Saayeen day Durbaar(i).  59.

Word Meaning:  Kummeen = tasks.  Tay = those.   Kummrray = (spiritually) meaningless tasks.  Visaar(i) = forget.  Mut(u) = lest.  Sherminda = ashamed.  Theevyee = become.

MEANING:  Forget the worldly tasks which have no (spiritual) worth, and are (spiritually) meaningless so that you do not have to feel ashamed in Allah’s Court, oh Farid.  59.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਸਾਹਿਬ ਦੀ ਕਰਿ ਚਾਕਰੀ ਦਿਲ ਦੀ ਲਾਹਿ ਭਰਾਦਿ॥ ਦਰਵੇਸਾ ਨੋ ਲੋੜੀਐ ਰੁਖਾ ਦੀ ਜੀਰਾਦਿ॥60॥

Farida, Sahib dee kur(i) chaakri, dil dee laah(i) bhuraand(i). Dervayshaan nu loarriyaiy, rookhaan dee jeeraand(i). 

Word Meaning: Sahib = Lord; Allah. Chaakri = free service (without any reward). Laah(i) = get rid of.  Bhuraand(i) = doubts.  Dervaysh = faqir.   Jeeraand = endurance; patience.

MEANING: Serve the Lord without any expectation of any reward and get all your doubts clarified, oh Farid.  The faqirs need to be as patient and as tolerant as the trees.   60.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਕਾਲੇ ਮੈਡੇ ਕਪੜੇ ਕਾਲਾ ਮੈਡਾ ਵੇਸੁ॥ ਗੁਨਹੀ ਭਰਿਆ ਮੈ ਫਿਰਾ ਲੋਕੁ ਕਹੈ ਦਰਵੇਸੁ॥ 61॥

Farida, kaalay maiynday kupprray, kaala maiynda vays(u).  Goonhee bhuriya mein phiran, loak(u) kuhhaiy dervaysh(u).  61.

Word Meaning: Maiynday = mine.  Vays(u) = garb.  Goonhee Bhuriya = full of faults. 

MEANING: Looking at my black clothes and my black garb, people think that I am a Sufi faqir (a holy man), but they do not know that I am full of faults (sins), oh Farid.  61.

ਤਤੀ ਤੋਇ ਨ ਪਲਵੈ ਜੇ ਜਲਿ ਟੁਬੀ ਦੇਇ॥ ਫਰੀਦਾ ਜੋ ਡੋਹਾਗਣਿ ਰਬ ਦੀ ਝੂਰੇਦੀ ਝੂਰੇਇ॥ 62॥

Tutti toyay na pulvaiy, jay jul(i) toobi dayay. Farida, so dohaagun(i) Rubb dee, jhooranydi, jhooray. 62.

Word Meaning: Tutti = hot; burned (plants).  Pulvaiy = grow.  Toobi = dip. Dohaagun(i) = disconnected.   Jhoorayndi = repents and regrets. 

MEANING: Just like a burned plant does not grow (come to life again) even if it is dipped (kept) in water, the (Maya-engrossed man), disconnected with Allah, cannot grow (spiritually), simply by repenting and regretting, oh Farid.  62.

ਜਾ ਕੁਆਰੀ ਤਾ ਚਾਉ ਵੀਵਾਹੀ ਤਾ ਮਾਮਲੇ॥ ਫਰੀਦਾ ਏਹੋ ਪਛੋਤਾਉ ਵਤਿ ਕੁਆਰੀ ਨ ਥੀਐ॥ 63॥

Ja kooaari ta chaaou, veevaahi ta maamlay. Farida, eho puchhotaou, vut(i) kooaari na theeyay.  63.

Word Meaning:  Kooaari = unmarried.  Chaaou = excitement.   Maamlay = problems. Puchhotaou = regret.   Vut(i) = again.

MEANING:  Like an unmarried young girl, who is excited to get married, but faces marital problems after marriage, regrets that she cannot be unmarried again, the young people get excited at the promised luxuries of the paradise that along with many other enjoyments, they will also get 84 beautiful damsels when they reach there after death, they begin to follow the tough restraints of the religion.  But when they find the restrictions too tough to follow, they wish they had never made up their mind to accept the conditions; however, they cannot reverse their decision now, thinking they are already closer to their goal and must hang in there to reach paradise. (People who follow the tough path of Allah’s worship with the expectation that they will be rewarded in some ways are not true devotees), oh Farid.  63.

ਕਲਰ ਕੇਰੀ ਛਪੜੀ ਆਇ ਉਲਥੇ ਹੰਝ॥ ਚਿੰਜੂ ਬੋੜਨਿ ਨ ਪੀਵਹਿ ਉਡਣ ਸੰਦੀ ਡੰਝ॥ 64॥

Kullur kayri chhupprri, aayay ooluthay hunjh. Chinjoo borrun(i) na peeveh(i). ooddun sundi dunjh.  64. 

Word Meaning: Kullur = saltpetre; bad qualities.   Kayri = of.   Chhupprri = a small pond (a self-styled guru of very low spirituality).   Ooluthay = land on.  Hunjh = swans;  enlightened devotees.   Chinjhoo = beak.   Sundi = of.   Dunjh = strong desire. 

MEANING:  If swans land on a small pond with saltpetre suspended in its water to have a drink, they quickly have a strong desire to leave the place when they find the water dirty after they put their beak in it. Likewise, if some enlightened devotee of God mistakenly visits the gathering of a fake guru with very little spiritual knowledge, expecting to learn something new, gets disappointed, and leaves the place right away, oh, Farid.  64.

ਹੰਸੁ ਉਡਰਿ ਕੋਧ੍ਰੈ ਪਇਆ ਲੋਕੁ ਵਿਡਾਰਣ ਜਾਇ॥ ਗਹਲਾ ਲੋਕੁ ਨ ਜਾਣਦਾ ਹੰਸੁ ਨ ਕੋਧ੍ਰਾ ਖਾਇ॥  65॥

Huns(u) ooder(i) kodhraiy puyya, loak(u) vidaarun jaayay. Gehla loak(u) na jaanda, huns(u) na kodhra khaayay.  65.

Word Meaning:  Ooder(i) = flew into.   Kodhra = millet (a much less desired food grain). Loak = people.   Vidaarun = to kill; to scare away.    Gehla = unaware; foolish.

MEANING: If a swan flies down to land in a field of millet, people rush to scare it away. The ignorant people do not know that the swans do not eat millet. (Similarly, if a saint visits an ill-reputed (disgraced) place, people rush to slander him. The foolish people do not realize that if a saint visits such a place, it must be for some noble cause), oh Farid.  65.

ਚਲਿ ਚਲਿ ਗਈਆ ਪੰਖੀਆ ਜਿਨੀ ਵਸਾਏ ਤਲ॥ ਫਰੀਦਾ ਸਰੁ ਭਰਿਆ ਭੀ ਚਲਸੀ ਥਕੇ ਕਵਲ ਇਕਲ॥ 66॥

Chull(i), chull(i) guyyiyaan punkhiyaan, jinhi vusaayay tul. Farida, sur(u) bhuriya bhee chullsi, thuckay kuvul ikull.  66. 

Word Meaning:  Punkhiyaan = birds; birdlike humans (which are only visitors in the world.  Vusaayay = populated.  Tul = pools, lakes. Sur(u) = pool.  Kuvul = lotus flower.    Thuckay = exhausted; dried up.  Ikull = left alone. 

MEANING:  Like the waterbirds which populate the pools, but eventually leave this world, the humans, which populate cities (the world), leave as their turn comes up.  Even the full water sources (pools, lakes, etc.) eventually dry up, leaving the lotus flowers behind, which also die without their source (nothing lasts forever in this world). Like the lotus flowers in a dry pool which also die without water, the man dies spiritually without his life-source, God), oh Farid. 

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਇਟ ਸਿਰਾਣੇ ਭੁਇ ਸਵਣੁ ਕੀੜਾ ਲੜਿਆ ਮਾਸਿ॥ ਕੇਤੜਿਆ ਜੁਗ ਵਾਪਰੇ ਇਕਤੁ ਪਇਆ ਪਾਸਿ॥ 67॥

Farida, itt sirhaanay bhooyayn suvun(u), keerra lurriya maas(i). Kayturriyaan, jog vaapray, ikut(u) puyyiyaan paas(i).  67.

Word Meaning: = Itt = brick; hard surface. Sirhaany = on pillow. Bhooyayn = ground.  Suvun(u) = sleeping.  Keeraa = bugs.  Lurriya = bites.  Kayturriyaan = how many; countlss.  Joog = eons; long times. Ikutt(u) Paas(i) = on one side (back). 

MEANING: At the end, when the man dies, he is buried in ground where hard place serves as his pillow, and bugs bite his flesh; countless years pass by with him lying only on his back, oh Farid.   67.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਭੰਨੀ ਘੜੀ ਸਵੰਨਵੀ ਟੁਟੀ ਨਾਗਰ ਲਜੁ॥ ਅਜਰਾਈਲੁ ਫਰੇਸਤਾ ਕੈ ਘਰਿ ਨਾਠੀ ਅਜੁ॥ 68॥

Farida, bhunni ghurri soovunvee, tooti naagur lujj.  Israel farishta, kaiy ghur(i) naatthi ajj(u)? 68. 

Word Meaning:  Bhunni = is broken.  Ghuree = an earthen water container; human body. Soovunvee = of beautiful colour. Naagur = beautiful. Lujj(u) = the rope used to pull up the water container from the open well; the thread of breaths.  Israel Farishta = Israel, the Angel of death.  Naatthi = visitor.  Ajj(u) = today; at present; always.

MEANING:  The Angel of Death constantly visits people's houses when their beautiful bodies, along with the threads of their breath, are broken. Nobody knows whose house he will visit next, oh, Farid.  68.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਭੰਨੀ ਘੜੀ ਸਵੰਨਵੀ ਟੁਟੀ ਨਾਗਰ ਲਜੁ॥ ਜੋ ਸਜਣ ਭੁਇ ਭਾਰੁ ਥੇ ਸੇ ਕਿਉ ਆਵਹਿ ਅਜੁ॥  69॥

Farida, bhunni ghurri soovunvee, tooti naagur lujj(u). Jo sujjun bhooyayn bhaar(u) thay, say kiyoun aaveh(i) ajj(u).  69.

Word Meaning:  Sujjun = friends; men.  Bhooyayn Bhaar(u) = burden on earth (who perform immoral deeds and forget God).  Aaveh(i) = come to earth (be born as humans).

MEANING: Everybody’s body and their breath die out eventually, but the individuals who were just a burden on the earth (because forgetting God, they only committed bad deeds) will not be born as humans again, oh Farid.  69.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਬੇਨਿਵਾਜਾ ਕੁਤਿਆ ਏਹ ਨਾ ਭਲੀ ਰੀਤਿ॥ ਕਬ ਹੀ ਚਲਿ ਨ ਆਇਆ ਪੰਜੇ ਵਕਤ ਮਸੀਤਿ॥70॥

Farida, baynivaaja kootiya, eh na bhulee reet(i). Kub hee chull(i) na aaya, punjay vukt museet(i).  70.

Word Meaning:  Baynivaaja = without regular Muslim prayers.   Bhulee Reet(i) = good practice.  Punjay Vukt = all five times (Muslims offer prayer five times a day). 

MEANING: The man is like a dog that never goes to the mosque to offer the five daily required prayers.  It is not a good practice (because it will not be approved by Allah), oh Farid.  70.

ਉਠੁ ਫਰੀਦਾ ਉਜੂ ਸਾਜਿ ਸੁਬਹ ਨਿਵਾਜ ਗੁਜਾਰਿ॥ ਜੋ ਸਿਰੁ ਸਾਈ ਨ ਨਿਵੈ ਸੋ ਸਿਰੁ ਕਪਿ ਉਤਾਰਿ॥ 71॥

Ootth(u) Farida, oojoo saaj(i), soobeh(i) nivaaj goozaar(i). Jo sir(u) Saayeen na nivaiy, so sir(u) kupp(i) ootaar(i).  72.

Word Meaning:  Oojoo Saaj(i) = to wash hands, face, and feet before the prayer (Nivaaj).  Kupp(i) = slash; sever. 

MEANING:  Get up, and wash your face, hands, and feet, and offer the morning prayer, oh Farid. The head that does not bow to God deserves to be severed.   71.

ਜੋ ਸਿਰੁ ਸਾਈ ਨ ਨਿਵੈ ਸੋ ਸਿਰੁ ਕੀਜੈ ਕਾਇ॥ ਕੁੰਨੇ ਹੇਠਿ ਜਲਾਈਐ ਬਾਲਣ ਸੰਦੇ ਥਾਇ॥ 72॥

Jo sir(u) Saayeen na nivaiy, so sir(u) keejaiy kaayay. Koonnay haytth(i) julaayiyaiy, baalun Sunday thaayayn.  72.

Word Meaning:  Keejaiy Kaayay = what should be done of? Koonnay Hatth(i) = under the cooking vessel.     Baalun = fuel wood.  Sunday Thaayayn = in pace of. 

MEANING:  What should be done to the head that does not bow before God, oh Farid.  Such a head deserves to be used as fuel in place of wood under the cooking vessel, oh Farid.   72.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਕਿਥੈ ਤੈਡੇ ਮਾਪਿਆ ਜਿਨੀ ਤੂ ਜਣਿਓਹਿ॥ ਤੈ ਪਾਸਹੁ ਉਹ ਲਦਿ ਗਏ ਤੂੰ ਅਜੈ ਨ ਪਤੀਣੋਹਿ॥73॥

Farida, kithaiy taiynday maapiyaa, jinhi tu juniyohi. Taiyn paasoun ooh lud(i) guyyay, tu ajaiy na pteenohi.  73.

Word Meaning: Taiyday = yours.   Juniyohi = gave birth to.   Lud(i) Guyyay = left.  Pteenohi = convinced; changed your mind.

MEANING:  Where are your parents, who gave birth to you, oh Farid? They have left you, and you still are not convinced that you are mortal (and are still attached to worldly things), oh, Farid. 73.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਮਨੁ ਮੈਦਾਨੁ ਕਰਿ ਟੋਏ ਟਿਬੇ ਲਾਹਿ॥ ਅਗੈ ਮੂਲਿ ਨ ਆਵਸੀ ਦੋਜਕ ਸੰਦੀ ਭਾਹਿ॥ 74॥

Farida, mun(u) maiydaan(u) kur(i), toyay, tibbay laah(i). Uggaiy mool(i) na aavsi, dojuk sundi bhaah(i).  74

Word Meaning:  Maiydaan = level.   Toyay Tibbay = ditches and mounds (prejudices and biases). Uggaiy = next world.  Dojuk = hell.     Bhaah(i) = fire. 

MEANING: Level the field of your heart by removing all the ditches and the mounds (get rid of all your prejudices and biases, and treat everybody the same way), oh Farid. If you do so, you will not have to face the fire of hell in the next world (after death).   74.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਖਾਲਕੁ ਖਲਕ ਮਹਿ ਖਲ਼ਕ ਵਸੈ ਰਬ ਮਾਹਿ॥ ਮੰਦਾ ਕਿਸ ਨੋ ਆਖੀਐ ਜਾ ਤਿਸੁ ਬਿਨੁ ਕੋਈ ਨਾਹਿ॥ 75॥

Farida, Khaaluk(u), khulk meh(i), khulk vussaiy Rubb maah(i). Munda kis nu aakhiyaiy, ja Tis(u) bin(u) koyee naah(i).  75.

Word Meaning:  Khaaluk = the creator.  Khulk = the creation; the people.  Munda = bad. 

MEANING:  The Creator resides in His creation, and the people reside in Him (everybody has a part of His Soul, and is supported by Him).  So, whom can we call bad when nobody is without Him, of Farid?   75.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਜਿ ਦਿਹਿ ਨਾਲਾ ਕਪਿਆ ਜੇ ਗਲੁ ਕਪਹਿ ਚੁਖ॥ ਪਵਨਿ ਨ ਇਤੀ ਮਾਮਲੇ ਸਹਾ ਨ ਇਤੀ ਦੁਖ॥76

Farida, j(i) dih(i) naala kuppiyam jay gul(u) kuppeh(i) chookh. Puvun(i) na itee maamlay, suhaan na itee dookh.  76.

Word Meaning:  J(i) Dih(i) = the day.  Naala = umbilical chord.  Kuppiya = cut.  Chookh = a little.   Itee = these many.  Maamlay = problems.  Suhaan = endure; face.

MEANING:  (It would have been much better) if on the day, when the midwife (nurse) cut my umbilical cord, she had cut a little of my neck too, because then I would not have had to face the problems of (prejudices and biases) in the world, oh, Farid.  76. 

ਚਬਣ ਚਲਣ ਰਤੰਨ ਸੇ ਸੁਣੀਅਰ ਬਹਿ ਗਏ॥ ਹੇੜੇ ਮੁਤੀ ਧਾਹ ਸੇ ਜਾਨੀ ਚਲਿ ਗਏ॥  77॥

Chubbun, chullun, ruttan say, sooniur beh(i) guyyay. Hayrray mooti dhah, say jaani chull(i) guyyay.  77.

Word Meaning:  Chubbun = to chew (teeth). Chullun = to walk (legs).    Ruttan = eyes. Sooniur = to hear (ears).                 Beh(i) Guyyay = sat down; quit working.            Hayrray = heart.      Mooti Dhaah = cried loudly.     Jaani = dear like life.

MEANING: When the man gets old, his teeth, legs, eyes, and ears quit working. His heart cries aloud that the dear parts of his body have left him.  (The same body that the man was very proud of in his young age, becomes helpless in his old age (and one will have no energy to worship Allah. So, start worshipping Allah in the young age), oh Farid.   77.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਬੁਰੇ ਦਾ ਭਲਾ ਕਰਿ ਗੁਸਾ ਮਨਿ ਨ ਹਢਾਇ॥

(ਅੰਗ 1382)॥  ਦੇਹੀ ਰੋਗੁ ਨ ਲਗਈ ਪਲੈ ਸਭੁ ਕਿਛੁ ਪਾਇ॥ 78॥

Farida, booray da bhulaa kur(i), goossa mun(i) na hundhaayay.

(1382):        Dayhi roag(u) na luggyee, pullaiy subh(u) kichh(u) paayay.  78.

Word Meaning:  Goossa = anger.  Hundaayay = carry with you.   Dayhi = body. Roag(i) = disease.  Pullaiy = acquire. 

MEANING:  Do not carry your anger with you all the time if you are mad at some bad person, oh Farid. Actually, do him some good (he may change because of your kindness), as a result of which you will not suffer mentally, and acquire everything (satisfaction of doing good to someone).   78.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਪੰਖ ਪਰਾਹੁਣੀ ਦੁਨੀ ਸੁਹਾਵਾ ਬਾਗੁ॥ ਨਉਬਤਿ ਵਜੀ ਸੁਬਹ ਸਿਉ ਚਲਣ ਕਾ ਕਰਿ ਸਾਜੁ॥ 79॥

Farida, punkh praauhni, dooni doohaava bag(u).  Nuoubut vujji soobeh siyoun, chullun ka kur(i) saaj(u). 79.

Word Meaning:   Punkh = birds.  Praauhni = visitors.  Soohaava = comforting.  Baag(u) = orchard.  Nuoubut Vujji = the drum has been beaten (the call has come).  Soobeh Siyoun = from the early morning.   Saaj(u) = readiness. 

MEANING:  The world is like a beautiful orchard, and the man is a visitor in the world, like the birds which visit the trees only for the night.  And, like birds, which leave the trees in the morning, man’s call to leave the world comes in the morning (at the end of the night of his life), and he needs to get ready to leave at that time.  79. 

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਰਾਤਿ ਕਥੂਰੀ ਵੰਡੀਐ ਸੁਤਿਆ ਮਿਲੈ ਨ ਭਾਉ॥ ਜਿੰਨ੍ਹਾ ਨੈਣ ਨੀਦ੍ਰਾਵਲੇ ਤਿੰਨਾ ਮਿਲਣੁ ਕੁਆਉ॥80॥

Farida, raat(i) kuthoori vundiyaiy, sootiyaan milaiy na bhaaou. Jinha naiyn neendraavlay, tinha milun kooaaou.  80. 

Word Meaning:  Kuthoori = musk (like pleasant smelling Allah’s Naam). Bhaaou = share.  Neendraavlay = full of sleep.    Kooaaou = from where? 

MEANING:  Allah’s Naam is distributed (can be acquired) only in the late hours of the night (early hours of the morning). Where can those get their share whose eyes are full of sleep, and they keep sleeping in, oh Farid?   80.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਮੈ ਜਾਨਿਆ ਦੁਖੁ ਮੁਝ ਕੂ ਦੁਖੁ ਸਬਾਇਐ ਜਗਿ॥ ਊਚੇ ਚੜਿ ਕੇ ਦੇਖਿਆ ਘਰਿ ਘਰਿ ਏਹਾ ਅਗਿ॥ 81॥

Farida, mein jaaniya dookh moojh koo, dookh subayaiy jug(i). Oochay churrh(i) kay daykhiya, ghur(i), ghur(i) eha ugg(i). 81.

MEANING: I had thought that the worries and suffering in life were limited to me only, but when I rose above my selfishness, I found out that every household was burning in the same fire, oh, Farid. (The only way to have a peaceful mind is to worship Allah).  81.

ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਫਰੀਦਾ ਭੂਮਿ ਰੰਗਾਵਲੀ ਮੰਝਿ ਵਿਸੂਲਾ ਬਾਗ॥ ਜੋ ਜਨ ਪੀਰਿ ਨਿਵਾਜਿਆ ਤਿੰਨ੍ਹਾ ਅੰਚ ਨ ਲਾਗ॥ 82॥

Mehla 5:  Farida, bhoom(i) rungaavli, munjh(i) visoola bag. Jo jun Peer(i) nivaajiya, tinha unch na laag.  82. 

Word Meaning:  Bhoom(i) = earth; world. Runggavli = attractive and colourfu.  Munjh(i) = in the middle.  Visoola = poisonous.   Baag = garden.  Nivaajiya = honoured (bestowed with the Naam),  Unch = heat; suffering.  Laag = touches.

MEANING: No doubt that the world is very colourful and attractive, but there is a poisonous garden of different flowers (the fire of worldly attractions which kills the spirituality of the man) in its middle.  However, those who are blessed with Waheguru’s (God’s) Naam by the Guru are not affected by this fire at all, oh Farid. (This Sloak is by the Fifth Master).  82

ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਫਰੀਦਾ ਉਮਰ ਸੁਹਾਵੜੀ ਸੰਗਿ ਸੁਵੰਨੜੀ ਦੇਹ॥ ਵਿਰਲੇ ਕੇਈ ਪਾਈਅਨਿ ਜਿੰਨ੍ਹਾ ਪਿਆਰੇ ਨੇਹ॥ 83॥

Mehla 5:  Farida oomesoohaavrri, sung(i) soovunnrri dayh. Virlay kayee paayiun, jinha Piyaray nayh. 83.

MEANING: There are very few people who love Waheguru, oh Farid. Their life and their bodies both become very beautiful (pleasant) with Waheguru-like noble qualities.  83. 

ਕੰਧੀ ਵਹਣ ਨ ਢਾਹਿ ਤਉ ਭੀ ਲੇਖਾ ਦੇਵਣਾ॥ ਜਿਧਰ ਰਬ ਰਜਾਇ ਵਹਣ ਤਿਦਾਊ ਗੰਉ ਕਰੇ॥  84॥

Kundhi, vehn na dhaayay, tuou bhee laykha dayvna.  Jidher Rubb rujaayay, vehn tidaaoun guoun kuray.  84.

Word Meaning:  Kundhi = bank. Vehn = flow of river.  Dhaah(i) = demolish.  Rujaayay = Will.  Tidaaoun = in that direction.  Guoun Kuray = goes; follows. 

MEANING:  Like the flow of a river, which erodes its own banks, which protect it (keep it on its path), man’s evil deeds rob him of his noble qualities which provide him with peace of mind.  But the man cannot be fully blamed, because everything happens according to the Will of God (His Laws).    84.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਡੁਖਾ ਸੇਤੀ ਦਿਹੁ ਗਇਆ ਸੂਲਾ ਸੇਤੀ ਰਾਤਿ॥ ਖੜਾ ਪੁਕਾਰੈ ਪਾਤਣੀ ਬੇੜਾ ਕਪਰ ਵਾਤਿ॥ 85॥

Farida, dookhaan sayti dihoun guyya, soolaan sayti raat(i).  Khurra pookaaraiy paatni, bayrra kuppur vaat(i).  85.

Word Meaning: Dookhaan Sayti = in suffering.  Soolaan Sayti = thorny.  Paatni = boatman (Peer).  Bayrra = (the) boat (of life).  Kuppur Vaat(i) = in the mouth of a whirlpool.

MEANING:  Despite the loud warnings of the Peer (that due to his overindulgence in worldly luxuries), the boat of life of the man is in the mouth of the whirlpool of the world-sea (the man ignores to remember Allah) as a result of which, his days are spent in suffering, and nights are spent as if he is lying on thorns.  85.

ਲੰਮੀ ਲੰਮੀ ਨਦੀ ਵਹੈ ਕੰਧੀ ਕੇਰੈ ਹੇਤਿ॥ ਬੇੜੇ ਨੋ ਕਪਰੁ ਕਿਆ ਕਰੇ ਜੇ ਪਾਤਣ ਰਹੈ ਸੁ ਚੇਤਿ॥ 86॥

Lummi, lummi, nude vuhhaiy, kundhi kayraiy hayt(i). Bayrray nu kuppur kiyya kuray, jay Paatun ruhhaiy su chayt(i).  86.

Word Meaning: Lummy =  (a) long (list).  Nudee = river (of worldly attractions).  Kundhi = banks (of spirituality).  Kayraiy = to erode.  Hayt(i) = for the reason.  Su Chayt = in mind.

MEANING: The river of worldly attractions, with its long list (of attractions), flows in the world, which erodes the spirituality of people, and leads them into the deep world-sea where their boat of life can sink in its whirlpool. But the whirlpool can cause no damage to the boat of those who keep the Boatman (the Guru) in their mind.   86.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਗਲੀ ਸੁ ਸਜਣ ਵੀਹ ਇਕ ਢੂਢੇਦੀ ਨ ਲਹਾ॥ ਧੁਖਾ ਜਿਉ ਮਾਲੀਹ ਕਾਰਣਿ ਤਿੰਨ੍ਹਾ ਮਾ ਪਿਰੀ॥87॥

Farida, gulleen su sujjun veeh, ikk dhoondhandi na luhaan. Dhookhaan jiyoun maaleenh, kaarun(i) tinha ma Piree. 87.

Word Meaning: Gulleen = just to say.  Sujjun = true friends. Veeh = twenty; many.  Luhaan = acquire; have.  Dhookhaan = smoder.   Maleenh = a coarse powder of cow dung (a common fuel to cook at that time). Kaarun(i) = for the sake of. Piree = Dear (Allah). 

MEANING:  I may have many friends to say, but I cannot find a real one (one that connects me with Allah). So, my mind is smouldering like the coarse powder of cow dung due to non-connection with my dear Allah, oh Farid.  87.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਇਹੁ ਤਨੁ ਭਉਕਣਾ ਨਿਤ ਨਿਤ ਦੁਖੀਏ ਕਉਣੁ॥ ਕੰਨੀ ਬੁਜੇ ਦੇ ਰਹਾ ਕਿਤੀ ਵਗੈ ਪਉਣੁ॥ 88॥

Farida, eh(u) tun(u) bhaunkna, nit, nit dookhiyay kaun(u). Kunnee boojjay day ruhaan, kitti vugaiy paun.  88. 

Word Meaning: Bhaunkna = which barks; makes fresh demands.   Boojay = plugs.  Paun = wind; noise (of demands).

MEANING:  It has become a habit of my body to demand more and more things all the time, but who can afford (I cannot afford) to suffer meeting its demands. I have decided to plug my ears (not to listen), no matter how much noise my body makes, oh, Farid.  88.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਰਬ ਖਜੂਰੀ ਪਕੀਆ ਮਾਖਿਅ ਨਈ ਵਹੰਨਿ॥ ਜੋ ਜੋ ਵੰਞੈ ਡੀਹੜਾ ਸੋ ਉਮਰ ਹਥ ਪਵੰਨ੍ਹਿ॥ 89॥

Farida, Rubb khujooreen pukkiyaan, maakhiyaa nuyyi vuhunn(i). Jo, jo, vunjaiy deehrra, so oomer nuth puvunn(i).  89.

Word Meaning:  Khujooreen = dates (the fruit).     Pukkiyaan = are ripe.    Maakhiyaa = honey.    Vunjaiy = goes by.    Deehrra = day.     Huth Puvunn(i) = taking away. 

MEANING: The sweet fruit of dates (of Allah’s Naam) is ripe, and the rivers of honey (of His Naam) are flowing (human life is meant to connect with Allah, and acquire His dates-like and honey-like sweet Naam), oh Farid.  Every day that goes by without reciting His Naam is being taken away worthlessly from your life, oh man.  89.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਤਨੁ ਸੁਕਾ ਪਿੰਜਰੁ ਥੀਆ ਤਲੀਆ ਖੂੰਡਹਿ ਕਾਗ॥ ਅਜੈ ਸੁ ਰਬੁ ਨ ਬਾਹੁੜਿਓ ਦੇਖੁ ਬੰਦੇ ਕੇ ਭਾਗ॥ 90॥

Farida, tun(u) sookka, pinjur(u) theeya, tuliyaan khoondeh(i) kaag. Ajaiy su Rubb(u) na bahourriyo, daykh(u) bunday kay bhaag. 90.

Word Meaning: Sookka = withered; become frail.  Pinjur(u) = skeleton.   Tuliyaan = soles. Khoondeh(i) = peck at.  Kaag = crows (vices; sensual pleasures).  Bahourriyo = came to help.  Bhaag = fate.

ਪਹਿਲੈ ਪਹਿਰੈ ਫੁਲੜਾ ਫਲੁ ਭੀ ਪਛਾ ਰਾਤਿ॥ ਜੋ ਜਾਗੰਨ੍ਹਿ ਲਹੰਨਿ ਸੇ ਸਾਈ ਕੰਨੋ ਦਾਤਿ॥ 112॥

Pehlaiy pehiraiy phoollrra, ful(u) bhe puchhaa raat(i).  Jo jaagun(i) lehun(i) say, Saayeen kunnon data(i). 112.

Word Meaning:  Pehlaiy Pehraiy =  first quarter of night.    Puchha Raat(i) = last quarter of night.  Phoolrra = flower; partial benefit.  Ful = fruit; full benefit.  Lehun(i) = acquire.  Say = those. Kunnon = from.   Daat(i) = gift. 

MEANING:  Worship of Allah is useful even in the first quarter of the night, but only with partial benefit.  One gets the full benefit in the last quarter. Those who stay awake receive this gift (His worship) from Allah.   112.

ਦਾਤੀ ਸਾਹਿਬ ਸੰਦੀਆ ਕਿਆ ਚਲੈ ਤਿਸੁ ਨਾਲਿ॥ ਇਕਿ ਜਾਗੰਦੇ ਨ ਲਹਨਿ ਇਕਨਾ ਸੁਤਿਆ ਦੇਇ ਉਠਾਲਿ॥ 113॥

Dateen Sahib sundiyaan, kiyya chullaiy Tis(u) naal(i).  Ikk(i) jaagunday na lehn(i), ikkna soottiyaan dayay ootthaal(i). 

MEANING:  Waheguru’s worship is a gift to the man which He Himself gives to the individuals of His choice. No one  can force Him to give this gift to him.   He may not give it to those who stay awake, but may awaken some who are asleep (and give them the gift). 

NOTE: This Sloak is by Guru Nanak Sahib, and is also enshrined on page 83 of SGGS.

ਢੂਢੇਦੀਏ ਸੁਹਾਗੁ ਕੂ ਤਉ ਤਨਿ ਕਾਈ ਕੋਰ॥ ਜਿਨ੍ਹਾ ਨਾਉ ਸੁਹਾਗਣੀ ਤਿਨਾ ਝਾਕ ਨ ਹੋਰ॥ 114॥

Dhoondhaydiyay Soohaag(u) koo, tuou tun(i) kaayi koar. Jinha naaoun soohaagni, tinha jhaak na hoar.  114.

Word Meaning:  Dhoondhayndiyay = searching for.  Soohaag(u) = husband; Allah.             Koar = shortcoming; weakness. Naaoun = known as.   Soohaagni = married.   Jhaak = expectation; look for.

MEANING:  If you are still searching for Allah, you must have some shortage (of faith in Allah).  Those who are known as married (who are truly connected with Allah), do not look somewhere else (for the fulfilment of their wishes).    114.

ਸਬਰ ਮੰਝ ਕਮਾਣ ਏ ਸਬਰੁ ਕਾ ਨੀਹਣੋ॥ ਸਬਰ ਸੰਦਾ ਬਾਣੁ ਖਾਲਕੁ ਖਤਾ ਨ ਕਰੀ॥  115॥

Subur munjh kmaan eh, subur(u) ka neehno. Subur sunda baan(u), Khaaluk khutaa na kureen.  115. 

Word Meaning:  Subur = patience.   Munjh = in mind.   Kmaan = bow.  Neehno = force applied.    Sunda = of.   Baan(u) = arrow.   Khutaa Na Kureen = does not let one make any mistake, and makes his efforts successful. 

MEANING:  If the man uses the bow of his patience (contentment), applies the force of his patience, and his patience serves as the arrow too, Allah never lets such a devotee make any mistake (and makes him successful in achieving union with Him). 115.

ਸਬਰ ਅੰਦਰਿ ਸਾਬਰੀ ਤਨੁ ਏਵੈ ਜਾਲੇਨ੍ਹਿ॥ ਹੋਨਿ ਨਜੀਕ ਖੁਦਾਇ ਦੈ ਭੇਤੁ ਨ ਕਿਸੈ ਦੇਨ੍ਹਿ॥ 116॥

Subur undar(i) saabri, tun(u) aivaiyn jaalayn(i). Hoan(i) nujeek Khoodaay daiy, bhaytu) na kisaiy dayn(i). 116.

Word Meaning: Saabri = patient; contented.  Tun(u) Jaalayn(i) = endure hardships (overcome obstacles in the path of worship).  Nujeek = close to.  Bhayt(u) = disclose.

MEANING:  The devotees of Allah are always patient and suffer hardships (like overcoming obstacles to worship Him), but wait for Allah’s benevolence.  They are close to God, but do not disclose (publicize) this secret to others.   116.

ਸਬਰ ਏਹੁ ਸੁਆਉ ਜੇ ਤੂੰ ਬੰਦਾ ਦਿੜੁ ਕਰਹਿ॥ ਵਧਿ ਥੀਵਹਿ ਦਰੀਆਉ ਟੁਟਿ ਨ ਥੀਵਹਿ ਵਾਹੜਾ॥ 117॥

Subur eh(u) sooaaou, jay toon bunda dirr(u) kureheh. Vudh(i) theeveh(i) duriaaou, toott(i) na theeveh(i) vaahrra.  117. 

Word Meaning: Sooaaou = objective. Dirr(u) Kureh(i) = reinforce in mind. Theeveh(i) = become.  Duriyaaou = river.  Vaahrra = a creek.  117.

MEANING:  Man’s main objective is to find contentment, which he should reinforce in his mind so that it enlarges to become like a river, and not shrink to become a creek.   117.

ਫਰੀਦਾ ਦਰਵੇਸੀ ਗਾਖੜੀ ਚੋਪੜੀ ਪਰੀਤਿ॥ ਇਕਨਿ ਕਿਨੈ ਚਾਲੀਐ ਦਰਵੇਸਾਵੀ ਰੀਤਿ॥ 118॥

Farida dervayshi gaakhrri, choaprri preet(i). Ikun(i) kinaiy chaaliyaiy, dervayshaaveen reet(i). 119.

Word Meaning:  Choaprri = (only visibly) shiny (for show off).   Ikun(i) Kinaiy = some rare. 

MEANING: The journey of life of a faqir (who breaks worldly attachments) is very, very tough; but your love for Allah is only outwardly (fake), oh Farid. Very few people follow the customs (path) of a faqir.  118. 

ਤਨੁ ਤਪੈ ਤਨੂਰ ਜਿਉ ਬਾਲਣੁ ਹਡ ਬਲੰਨ੍ਹਿ ॥ ਪੈਰੀ ਥਕਾ ਸਿਰਿ ਜੁਲਾ ਜੇ ਮੂੰ ਪਿਰੀ ਮਿਲੰਨਿ॥ 119॥

Tun tupaiy tunoor jiyoun, balun(u) hudd bulun(i). Paiyreen thukkaan sir(i) joolaan, jay moon Piree milun(i).  119. 

Word Meaning:  Tupaiy = may become hot like a furnace.  Baalun(u) = fuel.  Hudd = bones.  Bulun(i) = may burn.  Joolaan = walk.   Moon = me; I. 

MEANING: My body may become hot like a furnace in which my bones may burn as fuel. If my feet get tired, I will gladly walk on my head to have union with my Dear Lord. 119.

ਤਨੁ ਨ ਤਪਾਇ ਤਨੂਰ ਜਿਉ ਬਾਲਣੁ ਹਡ ਨ ਬਾਲਿ॥ ਸਿਰਿ ਪੈਰੀ ਕਿਆ ਫੇੜਿਆ ਅੰਦਰਿ ਪਿਰੀ ਨਿਹਾਲਿ॥ 120॥

Tun(u) na tupaayay tunoor jiyoun, balun(u) hudd na baal(i). Sir(i) paiyreen kiyya phayrria, undar(i) Piree nihaa(i). 

Word Meaning:  Phayrriya = commit a crime, or a sin. Nihaal(i) = see; look for.

MEANING:  Do not heat up your body like a furnace, and do not burn your bones as the fuel.  What sin have your head or feet committed (for which you are punishing them)? Look for your Dear  (Waheguru) inside your heart.   120.

NOTE: This holy Sloak has been written by Guru Nanak Sahib and is enshrined on page 1412 of SGGS.

ਹਉ ਢੂਢੇਦੀ ਸਜਣਾ ਸਜਣੁ ਮੈਡੇ ਨਾਲਿ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਅਲਖੁ ਨ ਲਖੀਐ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਦੇਇ ਦਿਖਾਲਿ॥  121॥

Huoun dhoondhayndi Sujjna, Sujjun maiynday naal(i). Nanak, Alukh(u) na lukhiyaiy. Gurmukh(i) dayay dikhaal(i).  121.

Word Meaning:  Maiynday Naal(i) = with me. Alukh(u) = incomprehensible. 

MEANING:  I had been searching for my Dear (Waheguru), not realizing that He was always with me (in my heart, oh Nanak.  Being incomprehensible, Waheguru cannot be comprehended, but the Guru shows Him inside you.   121. 

NOTE: This Sloak is the composition of Guru Ram Dass Ji, Mehla 4.

ਹੰਸਾ ਦੇਖਿ ਤਰੰਦਿਆ ਬਗਾ ਆਇਆ ਚਾਉ॥ ਡੁਬਿ ਮੁਏ ਬਗ ਬਪੁੜੇ ਸਿਰੁ ਤਲਿ ਉਪਰਿ ਪਾਉ॥ 122॥

Hunsaan daykh(i) terndiyaan, buggaan aaya chaaou. Doob(i) mooyay bug bupurray, sir(u) tul(i) oopur(i) paaoun.  122.

Word Meaning:  Huns = swan; a holy saint. Terndiyaan = swimming.  Bugga = Heron (which resembles swans, but cannot swim);  a fake devotee.   Chaaou = zestful; excited.  Bupurray = poor; pitiful.   Tul(i) = under. Oopur(i).

MEANING: Watching the swans swim, the herons also felt zestful and tried to swim, but the poor herons drowned, with their heads under water and their feet above (herons cannot swim).  (Listening to the amazing stories of the holy saints (their praise because of their teachings), the hypocrites also begin to preach (act like gurus), but they die in shame when they cannot answer the questions of the people on spiritualism). 122.  

ਮੈ ਜਾਨਿਆ ਵਡਹੰਸ ਹੈ ਤਾ ਮੈ ਕੀਤਾ ਸੰਗੁ॥ ਜੇ ਜਾਣਾ ਬਗੁ ਬਪੁੜਾ ਜਨਮਿ ਨ ਭੇੜੀ ਅੰਗੁ॥  123॥

Mein jaaniya vudhuns haiy, ta mein keeta sung(u).  Jay jaana bug(u) bupurra, junum(i) na bhayrreen ung(u).  123. 

Word Meaning:  Vudhuns = a great swan; a holy saint. Bug(u) = heron; an imposter.  Junum(i) = the whole life.   Bhayrreen = touch; go near.  Ung(u) = body.

MEANING: I thought it (he) was a great swan (holy saint); so, that’s why I joined its (his) company.  Had I known that it (he) was only a useless heron (a fake guru), I would not have gone near him in my entire life.   123.

ਕਿਆ ਹੰਸੁ ਕਿਆ ਬਗੁਲਾ ਜਾ ਕਉ ਨਦਰਿ ਧਰੇ॥ ਜੇ ਤਿਸੁ ਭਾਵੈ ਨਾਨਕਾ ਕਾਗਹੁ ਹੰਸੁ ਕਰੇ॥ 124॥

Kiyya huns(u), kiyya bugula, ja kuou nudr(i) dhuray. Jay Tis(u) Bhaavaiy Nanaka, kaagoun huns(u) kuray.  124.

Word Meaning:  Nudr(i) Dhuray = cast kind Sight. Kaag = crow; an immoral person. 

MEANING: It does not matter whether a person is noble or immoral; if Waheguru casts His kind Sight, he can transform even an immoral person into a holy saint, oh Nanak.   124.

NOTE:  This holy Sloak has been written by Guru Nanak Sahib, and is enshrined on page  91 of SGGS.

ਸਰਵਰ ਪੰਖੀ ਹੇਕੜੋ ਫਾਹੀਵਾਲ ਪਚਾਸ॥ ਇਹੁ ਤਨੁ ਲਹਰੀ ਗਡੁ ਥਿਆ ਸਚੇ ਤੇਰੀ ਆਸ॥  125॥

Survur punkhi haykrro, phaahiwaal puchaas. Eh(u) tun(u) lehreen gudd(u) theeya, Sachay Tayri aas.  125.

Word Meaning:  Survur = pool.  Punkhi = bird; man.  Haykrro = single.  Phaahiwal = trappers.   Puchaas = fifty (many).  Lehreen = waves of sensual enjoyments. Gudd(u) Theeya = is deeply engrossed.  Sachay = oh, God.

MEANING:  Like a single bird on a pool, I am alone in this world-pool, and there are many trappers (like lust, greed, rage, etc.) to attract my mind, oh God.  My body and my mind are deeply engrossed in the waves of sensual enjoyments; You are my only hope (to protect me from their attacks), oh Lord.  125.

ਕਵਣੁ ਸੁ ਅਖਰੁ ਕਵਣੁ ਗੁਣ ਕਵਣੁ ਸੁ ਮਣੀਆ ਮੰਤੁ॥ ਕਵਣੁ ਸੁ ਵੇਸੋ ਹਉ ਕਰੀ ਜਿਤੁ ਵਸਿ ਆਵੈ ਕੰਤੁ॥ 126॥

Kuvun(u) su ukkhur(u), kuvun(u) goon, kuvun(u) su muniyaa munt(u). Kuvun(u) su vayso huoun kureen, jit(u) vuss(u) aavaiy kunt(u).  126.

Word Meaning:   Kuvun(u) = which; what. Ukkhur(u) = word; mantra.   Muniyaa Munt(u) = supreme mantra.   Vayso = attire.  Vuss(i) = control; charm.  Kunt(u) = husband.

MEANING:  Which are the words, which is the quality, which is the supreme mantra, and which is the attire for me with which I can charm my husband (or God)? 126.

ਨਿਵਣੁ ਸੁ ਅਖਰੁ ਖਵਣੁ ਗੁਣੁ ਜਿਹਬਾ ਮਣੀਆ ਮੰਤੁ॥ ਏ ਤ੍ਰੈ ਭੈਣੇ ਵੇਸ ਕਰਿ ਤਾ ਵਸਿ ਆਵੀ ਕੰਤੁ॥  127॥

Nivun(u) su ukkhur(u), khuvun(u) goon(u), jihba muniyaa munt(u). Eh traiy bhaiynay vays kur(i), ta vuss(i) aavi kunt(u).  127.

Word Meaning:  Nivun = bowing down; accepting his (His) will.  Khuvun = endurance.  Jihba = tongue; sweet talking. 

MEANING: Accepting the wish of your husband (Will of God) is the word (mantra), enduring 

the hardships of life is the quality, and using sweet language with him (Him) is the three things that constitute the attire of the wife to charm her husband.  127.

ਮਤਿ ਹੋਦੀ ਹੋਇ ਇਆਣਾ॥ ਤਾਣ ਹੋਦੇ ਹੋਇ ਨਿਤਾਣਾ॥ ਅਣਹੋਦਾ ਆਪੁ ਵੰਡਾਏ॥ ਕੋਈ ਐਸਾ ਭਗਤੁ ਸਦਾਏ॥ 128॥

Mut(i) hoandi, hoyay iyaana. Taan hoanday, hoyay nitaana. Unhoanda aap(u) vundaayay. 

Koyee aiysa bhagat sudaayay.  128. 

Word Meaning:  Mut(i) = (sharp) wisdom.  Iyaana = pretend to be ignorant.  Taan = strength; force.    Nitaana = weak; powerless.   Unhoanda = which one cannot afford.  Vundaayay = share.  Koyee = some rare. 

MEANING: There is a rare individual in the world who acts like an ignoramus even though he has sharp wisdom; shows no force when he has lots of power; shares his earnings with others even when it is difficult for him to afford.  Such an individual is the true devotee of God. 128.

ਇਕੁ ਫਿਕਾ ਨ ਗਾਲਾਇ ਸਭਨਾ ਮੈ ਸਚਾ ਧਣੀ॥ ਹਿਆਉ ਨ ਕੈਹੀ ਠਾਹਿ ਮਾਣਕ ਸਭ ਅਮੋਲਵੇ॥ 129॥

Ikk(u) fikka na galaayay, subhna main Sacha Dhunee. Hiyaaou na kaiyhee tthaah(i), maanuk subh amoalvaiyn.  129.

Word Meaning:  Fikka = impolite.  Galaayay = say.  Sacha Dhunee = eternal God. Hiyaaou = heart.   Kaiyhee = of no one.  Tthaahay = break; hurt.  Maanuk = pearls (symbol of purity).    Amoalvaiyn = priceless. 

MEANING:  Do not say even a single impolite word which could hurt the feelings of someone and break their heart because God resides in every heart; for that reason, everyone has a pearl-like, priceless heart, oh man.   129.

ਸਭਨਾ ਮਨ ਮਾਣਿਕ ਠਾਹਣੁ ਮੁਲਿ ਮਚਾਗਵਾ॥ ਜੇ ਤਉੇ ਪਿਰੀਆ ਦੀ ਸਿਕ ਹਿਆਉ ਨ ਠਾਹੇ ਕਹੀਦਾ॥ 130॥

Subhna mun maanik, tthaahun(u) mool(i) muchaangvaan. Jay tuou Piriya dee sikk, hiyaaou na tthaahay kuheenda.  130. 

Word Meaning:  Tthaahun(u) = break; hurt. Mool(u) = not at all. Muchaangvaan = not good.  Piriya = of the Dear (God).  Sikk = longing.  Hiyaaou = heart.

MEANING:  Everybody has a pearl-like (pure) heart; it is not good at all to hurt or break someone’s heart. So, do not break or hurt anyone’s heart if you wish to meet with (get connected with) your dear Allah, oh man.   130.
