ੴ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਪ੍ਰਸਾਦਿ

ਸਲੋਕ ਭਗਤ ਕਬੀਰ ਜੀਉ ਕੇ

Ek Onkar Satgur Persad(i) 

Sloak Bhagat Kabir Jeeou Kay


ਕਬੀਰ ਮੇਰੀ ਸਿਮਰਨੀ ਰਸਨਾ ਊਪਰਿ ਰਾਮੁ॥ ਆਦਿ ਜੁਗਾਦੀ ਸਗਲ ਭਗਤ ਤਾ ਕੋ ਸੁਖ ਬਿਸ੍ਰਾਮੁ॥ 1॥

(PAGE 1364) Conti:  Kabir mayri simerni, rusna oopur(i) Ram(u). Aad(i) joogaadi, sugul bhagat, ta ko sookh bisraam(u).    1.

Word Meaning:  Simerni = rosary.  Rusna = tongue.  Aad(i) = from the beginning; Joogaad(i) = before the beginning of the yoogas (eons).   Bisraam = eternal peace (meger with God, and no rebirths). 

MEANING: (People use a rosary to show their religiosity), but the Naam of God on my tongue is the rosary for me because of which all God’s devotees from the beginning of humanity, and even before the beginning of the eons, have acquired the bliss of merger with God, and do not take rebirths, oh Kabir.   1.

ਕਬੀਰ ਮੇਰੀ ਜਾਤਿ ਕਉ ਸਭੁ ਕੋ ਹਸਨੇ ਹਾਰੁ॥ ਬਲਿਹਾਰੀ ਇਸ ਜਾਤਿ ਕਉ ਜਿਹ ਜਪਿਓ ਸਿਰਜਨਹਾਰੁ॥2

Kabir mayri jaat(i) kuou subh(u) ko hussnay haar(u). Bulihaari iss jaat(i) kuou, jih juppiyo Sirjunhaar(u).    2.

Word Meaning:  Jaat(i) = caste (Kabir Sahib belonged to one of the lowest castes – weavers.   Bulihaari = love to death.      Sirjunhaar(u) = the Creator; God.

MEANING:  Everybody makes fun of my low caste, but I love my low caste to death because, even being a low caste, I have worshipped the Creator, God (which very few people do), oh Kabir.    2.

ਕਬੀਰ ਡਗਮਗ ਕਿਆ ਕਰਹਿ ਕਹਾ ਡੁਲਾਵਹਿ ਜੀਉ॥ ਸਰਬ ਸੂਖ ਕੋ ਨਾਇਕੋ ਰਾਮ ਨਾਮ ਰਸੁ ਪੀਉ॥3॥

Kabir dug mug kiyya kureh(i), kuhha doolaaveh(i) jeeyo. Serb sookh ko Naayiko, Ram Naam rus(u) peeyo.    3.

Word Meaning:  Dugmug = flip-flopping of the mind.  Kuhhan = what for?  Doolaaveh Jeeou = feel depressed; have low spirits.  Naayiko = Lord.     Rus(u) = sweet essence.

MEANING: Why is your mind flip-flopping, and why are you feeling depressed, oh Kabir?  Drink the sweet essence of the Naam of the Lord, which is the source of all comforts.  3.

ਕਬੀਰ ਕੰਚਨ ਕੇ ਕੁੰਡਲ ਬਨੇ ਊਪਰਿ ਲਾਲ ਜੜਾਉ॥ ਦੀਸਹਿ ਦਾਧੇ ਕਾਨ ਜਿਉ ਜਿਨ ਮਨ ਨਾਹੀ ਨਾਉ॥4॥

Kabir, kunchun kay koondul bunay, oopur(i) laal jurraaou. Deeseh(i) daadhay kaan jiyoun, jinh mun naahi Naaoun.  4.

Word Meaning:  Kunchun = gold.   Koondul = large earrings.  Laal Kurraaou = inlaid rubies.  Daadhay = burned.  Kaan = reed. 

MEANING:  Rich people may wear large gold earrings with inlaid rubies, but they look (in God’s Court) like a burned reed if they do not have His Naam in their mind, oh Kabir.     4.   

ਕਬੀਰ ਐਸਾ ਏਕ ਆਧੁ ਜੋ ਜੀਵਤੁ ਮਿਰਤਕੁ ਹੋਇ॥ ਨਿਰਭੈ ਹੋ ਕੇ ਗੁਨ ਰਵੈ ਜਤ ਪੇਖਉ ਤਤ ਸੋਇ॥5॥

Kabir aiysa aik aadh(u), jo jeevut(u) mirtuk(u) hoyay. Nirbhaiy ho kay Goon ruvaiy, jut paykhou, tut Soyay.

Word Meaning:  Aik Aadh = one or two; rare. Jeevut(u) Mirtuk(u) = alive dead; who has killed his worldly desires while living in the family.  Goon Ruvaiy = sings God’s praise.  Paykhou = sees. Jut = where.  Tut = there.  Soyay = God.

MEANING:  There is a rare individual who kills his worldly bonds while still living in the family. Such a person sings God’s praise fearlessly and sees Him wherever he looks, oh Kabir.    5.

ਕਬੀਰ ਜਾ ਦਿਨ ਹਉ ਮੂਆ ਪਾਛੈ ਭਇਆ ਅਨੰਦੁ॥ ਮੋਹਿ ਮਿਲਿਓ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਆਪਨਾ ਸੰਗੀ ਭਜਹਿ ਗੁੋਬਿੰਦੁ॥6॥

Kabir, ja din huoun mooaa, paachhaiy bhuyya anund(u). Moh(i) miliyo Prubh(u) aapna, sungee bhujeh(i) Gobind(u).     6.

Word Meaning:  Huoun Mooaa = killed my selfishness and ego.   Pacchaaiy = after that.  Anund(u) = blissfulness.      Sungee = companions; senses. 

MEANING:  Since the day I overcame my selfishness and my ego, I have been enjoying blissfulness.  I have been connected with my Lord, and my senses too are worshipping Him, oh Kabir.   6.

ਕਬੀਰ ਸਭ ਤੇ ਹਮ ਬੁਰੇ ਹਮ ਤਜਿ ਭਲੋ ਸਭ ਕੋਇ॥ ਜਿਨਿ ਐਸਾ ਕਰਿ ਬੂਝਿਆ ਮੀਤੁ ਹਮਾਰਾ ਸੋਇ॥  7॥

Kabir, subh tay hum booray, hum tuj(i) bhulow subh koyay. Jinh(i) aiysa kur(i) boojhiya, meet humaara soyay.   7.

Word Meaning:  Tuj(i) = excepting.  Boojhiya = realized.

MEANING:  The individual who believes that he is the worst of all, and everybody else except him is good, is my friend, oh Kabir.    7.

ਕਬੀਰ ਆਈ ਮੁਝਹਿ ਪਹਿ ਅਨਿਕ ਕਰੇ ਕਰਿ ਭੇਸ॥ ਹਮ ਰਾਖੇ ਗੁਰ ਆਪਨੇ ਉਨਿ ਕੀਨੋ ਆਦੇਸੁ॥  8॥

Kabir aayi moojheh(i) peh(i), anik kuray kur(i) bhays.  Hum raakhay Gur aapnay, oonn(i) keeno aadays(u).  8.

Word Meaning:  Aayi = came (to captivate me). Bhays = forms.   Oon(i) = that; Maya. Aadays(u) = salute; bowing to; surrender.

MEANING:  Maya came (tried) to captivate me many times in different forms, but I was protected by the Guru, and Maya had to surrender before me, oh Kabir.    8.

ਕਬੀਰ ਸੋਈ ਮਾਰੀਐ ਜਿਹ ਮੂਐ ਸੁਖੁ ਹੋਇ॥ ਭਲੋ ਭਲੋ ਸਭੁ ਕੋ ਕਹੈ ਬੁਰੋ ਨ ਮਾਨੈ ਕੋਇ॥  9॥

Kabir soyee maariyaiy, jih mooyaiy sookh(u) hoyay. Bhulow, bhulow subh(u) ko kuhhaiy, boorow na maanaiy koyay.   9.

Word Meaning: Soyee = that; haumein (selfishness and ego).  Bhulow = good; welcomes. 

MEANING: One’s haumein is the only thing to kill, oh Kabir. Everybody welcomes it (when you do it), and nobody calls it bad.    9.

ਕਬੀਰ ਰਾਤੀ ਹੋਵਹਿ ਕਾਰੀਆ ਕਾਰੇ ਊਭੇ ਜੰਤ॥

(ਅੰਗ 1365)॥        ਲੈ ਫਾਹੇ ਉਠਿ ਧਾਵਤੇ ਸਿ ਜਾਨਿ ਮਾਰੇ ਭਗਵੰਤ॥ 10॥

Kabir, raateen hoveh(i) kaariyaan, kaaray oobhay junt.

(PAGE 1365):       Laiy phaahay ootth(i) dhaavtay, s(i) jaan(i) maaray Bhugvunt. 10.

Word Meaning:  Kaariyaan = black, dark; evil-minded. Oobhay = stand up.   Phaahay = traps.  Dhaavtay = go out to attack.   S(i) = those.  Maaray = kill (spiritually). Jaan(i) = believe.

MEANING:  The individuals who wake up on dark nights, and go out to trap (rob) other people, are evil-minded; and believe it, that they are spiritually killed by God, oh Kabir.10. 

ਕਬੀਰ ਚੰਦਨ ਕਾ ਬਿਰਵਾ ਭਲਾ ਬੇੜ੍ਹਿਓ ਢਾਕ ਪਲਾਸ॥ ਓਇ ਭੀ ਚੰਦਨੁ ਹੋਇ ਰਹੇ ਬਸੇ ਜੁ ਚੰਦਨ ਪਾਸਿ॥1  11॥

Kabir, chundun ka birva bhulaa, bayhrriyo dhaak,pulaas.  Oye bhee chundun(u) hoyay 

ruhhay, bussay juchundun paas(i).  11.

Word Meaning: Chundun = sandal.   Birva = tree.  Bayhrriyo = surrounded by.  Dhaak, Pulaas = trees that are not of much worth. 

MEANING:  The sandal tree is noble because even worthless trees acquire its sweet fragrance, which surrounds it, oh Kabir. (People who join the company of a holy saint acquire noble qualities too).   11.

ਕਬੀਰ ਬਾਸੁ ਬਡਾਈ ਬੂਡਿਆ ਇਉ ਮਤ ਡੂਬਹੁ ਕੋਇ॥ ਚੰਦਨ ਕੈ ਨਿਕਟੇ ਬਸੈ ਬਾਸੁ ਸੁਗੰਧੁ ਨ ਹੋਇ॥  12॥

Kabir, baans budaayi boodiya, iyyoun mut doob-hou koyay. Chundun kaiy nicketay bussaiy, baans soogundh na hoyay.  12.

Word Meaning:  Baans(u) = a bamboo tree.  Budaayi = conceited of its height. Boodiya = lost; intoxicated.   Mut = do not.  Nicketay = near.  Soogundh(u) = fragrance.

MEANING:  Let nobody be lost in the conceit of his greatness like the bamboo tree, which is intoxicated with the conceit of its height; and does not acquire the fragrance of the sandal tree even though it may be growing close to it, oh Kabir. (An egoist does not acquire noble qualities even though he may be living next door to a holy saint).   12.

ਕਬੀਰ ਦੀਨੁ ਗਵਾਇਆ ਦੁਨੀ ਸਿਉ ਦੁਨੀ ਨ ਚਾਲੀ ਸਾਥਿ॥ ਪਾਇ ਕੁਹਾੜਾ ਮਾਰਿਆ ਗਾਫਲਿ ਅਪੁਨੈ ਹਾਥਿ॥ 13॥

Kabir, deen(u) guvvaya dooni siyoun, dooni na chaali saath(i). Paayayn koohaarra maariya, gaafil(i) apunaiy haath(i).    13.

Word Meaning:  Deen(u) = religiosity; spirituality. Dooni = world.  Siyoun = with; because of.   Chaali Saath(i) = accompany.    Paayayn = feet.    Koohaarra = ax.   Gaafil(i) = unaware; ignorant.   13.

MEANING:  The ignorant man loses his spirituality because of his involvement in worldly affairs, which do not accompany him at the time of his death.  It is like hitting one’s foot with an axe (shooting in the foot) with his own hands, oh Kabir.   13.

ਕਬੀਰ ਜਹ ਜਹ ਹਉ ਫਿਰਿਓ ਕਉਤਕ ਠਾਓ ਠਾਇ॥ ਇਕ ਰਾਮ ਸਨੇਹੀ ਬਾਹਰਾ ਊਜਰੁ ਮੇਰੇ ਭਾਇ॥14॥

Kabir jeh, jeh huoun phiriyo, kautuk tthaaoun, tthaayayn. Ikk Ram sunayhi baahra, oojur(u) mayrya bhaayayn.   14.

Word Meaning:  Jeh, Jeh = wherever.   Huoun = I.   Kautuk = natural wonders.  Tthaaoun Thaayayn = everywhere.  Sunayhi = lover; devotee.  Baahra = without.   Oojur(u) = wilderness.    Mayray Bhaayay = for me.

MEANING:  I find natural wonders everywhere I go, oh Kabir. But every lovely place is like wilderness to me if there is no devotee of God there, oh Kabir.   14.

ਕਬੀਰ ਸੰਤਨ ਕੀ ਝੁੰਗੀਆ ਭਲੀ ਭਠਿ ਕੁਸਤੀ ਗਾਉ॥ ਆਗਿ ਲਗਉ ਤਿਹ ਧਉਲਹਰ ਜਿਹ ਨਾਹੀ ਹਰਿ ਕੋ ਨਾਉ॥15॥

Kabir, suntun kee  jhoongiya bhulee, bhutth(i) koosutee gaaoun. Aag(i) lugou tih dhaulhur, jih naahi Hur(i) ko Naaoun. 

Word Meaning:  Jhoongiya = a hut.  Bhulee = good.   Bhutth(i) = a hot kiln.  Koosutee = without truth (God’s Naam).   Tih = that.    Dhaulhur = big mansion. 

MEANING:  A small hut of a saint is much better than a whole village, which is like a hot furnace to me if God’s Naam is missing there.  Let the huge mansion burn in fire if there is no God’s Naam in there, oh Kabir.  15.

ਕਬੀਰ ਸੰਤ ਮੂਏ ਕਿਆ ਰੋਈਐ ਜੋ ਅਪੁਨੇ ਗ੍ਰਿਹਿ ਜਾਇ॥ ਰੋਵਹੁ ਸਾਕਤ ਬਾਪੁਰੇ ਜੁ ਹਾਟੈ ਹਾਟ ਬਿਕਾਇ॥16

Kabir, sunt mooyay kiyya royiyaiy, jo apunay greh(i) jaayay. Rovhou shaakut baapuray, ju haataiy, haat bikaayay.  16. 

Word Meaning:  Apunay Greh(i) = own house (merger with God).  Shaakut = Maya-worshipper.   Baapuray = poor; helpless.  Haataiy Haat = from shop to shop (from one life form to another).     Bikaayay = is sold (taken through). 

MEANING: Why should anyone cry over the physical death of a saint who merges with God; we should cry (at the miserable fate) at the death of a Maya-worshipper who will be taken through many life forms after his death, oh Kabir.  16.

ਕਬੀਰ ਸਾਕਤੁ ਐਸਾ ਹੈ ਜੈਸੀ ਲਸਨ ਕੀ ਖਾਨਿ॥ ਕੋਨੇ ਬੈਠੇ ਖਾਈਐ ਪਰਗਟ ਹੋਇ ਨਿਦਾਨ॥  17॥

Kabir, saakut aiysa haiy, jaiysi lusn kee khaan(i).  Koanay baiytthay khaayiyaiy, pergut hoyay nidaan. 17.

Word Meaning:  Lusn = garlic. Khaan(i) = eating. Konay = in a corner.  Pergut = appears. Nidaan = at the end; eventually. 

MEANING:  A Maya-worshipper cannot hide his immoral deeds for a long time, like eating garlic.  Even if a man eats garlic in a far corner (in hiding), eventually others can get its (offensive) smell, oh Kabir.  18.

ਕਬੀਰ ਮਾਇਆ ਡੋਲਨੀ ਪਵਨੁ ਝਕੋਲਨਨਹਾਰੁ॥ ਸੰਤਹੁ ਮਾਖਨੁ ਖਾਇਆ ਛਾਛਿ ਪੀਐ ਸੰਸਾਰੁ॥ 18॥

Kabir, Maya doalni, puvun jhukoalunhaar(u).  Sunt-houn maakhun(u) khaaya, Chhaachh(i) peeyay sunsaar(u).  18.

Word Meaning:  Doalni = the vessel in which yogurt is churned.  Puvun = air; breaths.  Jhukaolunhaar(u) = churner.   Maakhun(u) = the essence (God’s Naam).  Chhaachh(i) = buttermilk. 

MEANING:  Maya (wealth) has good and bad qualities; its limited use for survival is helpful, but indulgence in it is spiritually very harmful.  People churn yogurt to extract butter; once butter is fully extracted, the remaining liquid, called buttermilk, is of little use.  The same thing goes for Maya; if it is churned (used) with God’s Naam with every breath, it is useful like extracting butter, and that is what the holy saints do.  The rest of the world churns (uses) it to enjoy sensual pleasures,  so it is drinking the (spiritually) useless buttermilk (wasting away its precious human life), oh Kabir.    18.

ਕਬੀਰ ਮਾਇਆ ਡੋਲਨੀ ਪਵਨੁ ਵਹੈ ਹਿਵਧਾਰ॥ ਜਿਨਿ ਬਿਲੋਇਆ ਤਿਨਿ ਖਾਇਆ ਅਵਰ ਬਿਲੋਵਨਹਾਰ॥ 19॥

Kabir, Maya doalni, puvun(u) vuhhaiy hivdhaar. Jinh(i) biloyaa, tinh(i) khaaya, uver bilovunhaar.    19.

Word Meaning:  Hivdhaar = cold air; (with) cool (peaceful) mind.     Biloyaa = churned.  Uver = others.       Bilovunhaar = still churning.

MEANING: If a devotee churns Maya with a peaceful mind (uses it with the mind focused on Waheguru), he takes in its essence (acquires the Naam); while some others are still churning it (with a wavering mind), oh Kabir.     19

ਕਬੀਰ ਮਾਇਆ ਚੋਰਟੀ ਮੁਸਿ ਮੁਸਿ ਲਾਵੈ ਹਾਟਿ॥ ਏਕੁ ਕਬੀਰਾ ਨ ਮੁਸੈ ਜਿਨਿ ਕੀਨੀ ਬਾਰਹ ਬਾਟ॥ 20॥

Kabir, Maya choarti, moos(i), moos(i) laavaiy haat(i).  Aik Kabira na moosaiy, jinh(i) keeni baarah baant. 20. 

Word Meaning:  Choarti = thief; robber.  Moos(i), Moos(i) = captivates to rob.  Laavaiy Haat(i) = decorates its shop; captivates one imperceptibly.   Moosaiy = captivated; robbed.  Baarh Baat = divide (cut) into 12 (many) pieces. 

MEANING:  Maya is such a robber which captivates people imperceptibly by its attractions (and robs them of their noble qualities. But, I, for one, who has cut it into many pieces (shattered its effects) has not been robbed by it, oh Kabir.  20. 

ਕਬੀਰ ਸੂਖੁ ਨ ਏਹ ਜੁਗਿ ਕਰਹਿ ਜੁ ਬਹੁਤੇ ਮੀਤ॥ ਜੋ ਚਿਤੁ ਰਾਖਹਿ ਏਕ ਸਿਉ ਤੇ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਾਵਹਿ ਨੀਤਿ॥21

Kabir sookh(u) na eh joog(i), kureh(i) ju buhoutay meet. Jo chit(u) raakheh(i) Aik siyoun, tay sookh(u) paaveh(i) neet(i).  21.

Word Meaning:  Joog(i) = in the world.   Neet(i) = everyday; always.

MEANING:  Making too many friends is no source of comfort in this world.  The individual, who keeps only the “One” (God) in mind, always feels blissful, oh Kabir.    21.

ਕਬੀਰ ਜਿਸ ਮਰਨੇ ਤੇ ਜਗੁ ਡਰੈ ਮੇਰੇ ਮਨਿ ਅਨੰਦੁ॥ ਮਰਨੇ ਹੀ ਤੇ ਪਾਈਐ ਪੂਰਨੁ ਪਰਮਾਨੰਦੁ॥ 22॥

Kabir, jis mernay tay jug(u) duraiy, mayray mun(i) anund(u). Mernay hee tay paayiyaiy, Poorun(u) Permanund(u).    22.

MEANING:  People are afraid of death, but I feel blissful at the thought of death because it is after death that one attains supreme bliss if he merges with God, oh Kabir. (Even though the supreme devotees are always spiritually connected with God when they are still alive, they are physically distinct, and have to see the suffering of the world; so, they do not enjoy complete bliss. See Kabir Sahib Sloak 181).

ਰਾਮ ਪਦਾਰਥੁ ਪਾਇਕੈ ਕਬੀਰਾ ਗਾਠਿ ਨ ਖੋਲ੍ਹ॥ ਨਹੀ ਪਟਣੁ ਨਹੀ ਪਾਰਖੂ ਨਹੀ ਗਾਹਕ ਨਹੀ ਮੋਲੁ॥  23॥

Ram Pudaaruth(u) paayaykaiy, Kabira gaantth(i) na khohl. Nuhhi puttun, nuhhi paarkhoo, nuhhi gaahuk, nuhhi moal(u).    23.

Word Meaning:  Ram Pudaaruth = Waheguru’s Naam.  Gaantth(i) Na Khohl = open the bag; make it public.   Puttun(u) = city; market.   Paarkhoo = evaluator; judge.  Gaahuk = customer; purchaser.  Moal(u) = full worth.

MEANING:  Do not make it public if you acquire God’s Naam because there is no market, no judge, and no right purchaser of the Naam who knows its full worth and value, oh Kabir. 23. (Waheguru’s bhagats were rarely given the rightful respect. In fact, people used to taunt them.  Even Kabir Sahib’s mother and his wife complained about his worship of Waheguru (See Bilawal Kabir Ji, Shabad 4, page 856 SGGS).

ਕਬੀਰ ਤਾ ਸਿਉ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਿ ਕਰਿ ਜਾ ਕਉ ਠਾਕੁਰੁ ਰਾਮੁ॥ ਪੰਡਿਤ ਰਾਜੇ ਭੂਪਤੀ ਆਵਹਿ ਕਉਨੇ ਕਾਮ॥ 24॥

Kabir, ta siyoun preet(i) kur(i), ja kuou Tthaakur(u) Raam(u). Pundit, rajay, bhooputee, aaveh(i) kaunay kaam.   24.

Word Meaning:  Tthaakur(u) = lord.   Bhooputee = landlords.  Kaam = purpose.

MEANING:  Make friendship only with those whose Lord is God (they are His devotees). What spiritual purpose can the pundits, rajahs, or the landlords serve, oh Kabir?   24.

ਕਬੀਰ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਿ ਇਕ ਸਿਉ ਕੀਏ ਆਨ ਦੁਬਿਧਾ ਜਾਇ॥ ਭਾਵੈ ਲਾਬੇ ਕੇਸ ਕਰੁ ਭਾਵੈ ਘਰਰਿ ਮੁਡਾਇ॥25॥

Kabir, preet(i) Ikk siyoun keeyay, aan doobidha jaayay.  Bhaanvaiyn laambay kays kur(u), Bhaavaiyn ghurur(i) moondaayay.  25. 

Word Meaning:  Aan = other. Doobidha = indecisiveness; dithering.  Ghuurur(u) Moondaayay = shave head.

MEANING:  (Some people grow long tangled hair, while others shave their heads to please God, but He is not pleased with any of such ritualistic beliefs). So, Kabir Sahib advises them to overcome their indecisiveness and love only the “One” God.  In that case, it does not matter whether they grow long hair or shave their heads.    25.

ਕਬੀਰ ਜਗੁ ਕਾਜਲ ਕੀ ਕੋਠਰੀ ਅੰਧ ਪਰੇ ਤਿਸੁ ਮਾਹਿ॥ ਹਉ ਬਲਿਹਾਰੀ ਤਿਨ੍ਹ ਕਉ ਪੈਸਿ ਜੁ ਨੀਕਸਿ ਜਾਹਿ॥ 26॥

Kabir, jug(u) kaajul kee koatthri, undh puray tis(u) maah(i). Huoun bulihaari tinh kuou, paiys(i) ju neekus(i) jaah(i).  26.

Word Meaning:  Jug(u) = world.  Kaajul = kohl; a dark powder that women put in eyes to beautify them.   Undh = blind; ignorant.   Paiys(i) = fall in; enter.  Neekus(i) = get out. 

MEANING: The world is like a very glamorous room full of worldly attractions, which are like a dark powder (one earns disgrace by indulging in them); the ignorant are attracted to the room, and get trapped in it.  I am a sacrifice (all praise for) those who enter the room, but come out of it (are not trapped, and use it very wisely), oh Kabir.   26.

ਕਬੀਰ ਇਹੁ ਤਨੁ ਜਾਇਗਾ ਸਕਹੁ ਤ ਲੇਹੁ ਬਹੋਰਿ॥ ਨਾਗੇ ਪਾਵਹੁ ਤੇ ਗਏ ਜਿਨ ਕੇ ਲਾਖ ਕਰੋਰਿ॥ 27॥

Kabir, eh(u) tun(u) jaayayga, suk-hou ta layhou buhoar(i). Naangay paavhoun tay guyyay jinh kay laakh crore.  27.

Word Meaning:  Jaayayga = will die.  Buhoar(i) = recover; save from (spiritual) death. 

MEANING: The human body has to die, no matter how much wealth one has.  Even those who had millions and billions left the world barefoot.  However, one can save oneself from spiritual death if one tries.   27.

NOTE: Kabir Sahib suggests the ways to save one from spiritual death in the next Sloak.

ਕਬੀਰ ਇਹੁ ਤਨੁ ਜਾਇਗਾ ਕਵਨੈ ਮਾਰਗਿ ਲਾਇ॥ ਕੈ ਸੰਗਤਿ ਕਰਿ ਸਾਧ ਕੀ ਕੈ ਹਰਿ ਕੇ ਗੁਨ ਗਾਇ॥28॥

Kabir, eh(u) tun(u) kaayayga, kuvnaiy maarug(i) laayay. Kaiy sungut(i) kur(i) Sadh kee, kaiy Hur(i) kay Goon gaayay.  28.

Word Meaning:  Kuvnaiy = whichever.  Maarug(i) = journey (of life).  Kaiy = or.

MEANING:  Death is certain for the human body, no matter what worldly journey one chooses.  However, to save himself from spiritual death, one needs to seek the shelter of the Guru to sing God’s praise, oh Kabir.    28.

ਕਬੀਰ ਮਰਤਾ ਮਰਤਾ ਜਗੁ ਮੂਆ ਮਰਿ ਭੀ ਨ ਜਾਨਿਆ ਕੋਇ॥

(ਅੰਗ 1366)॥        ਐਸੇ ਮਰਨੇ ਜੋ ਮਰੈ ਬਹੁਰਿ ਨ ਮਰਨਾ ਹੋਇ॥ 29॥

Kabir, merta, merta jug(u) mooaa, mer bhee na jaaniya koyay.

(PAGE 1366):         Aiysay mernay jo muraiy, buhour(i) na merna hoyay.   29.

Word Meaning:  The world has been dying ever since, but nobody has learned how to die in a dignified way (to die from worldly desires, meaning killing them).  The individual, who dies this way (by killing his desires), never dies again (his birth-death cycle is terminated as he merges into God).  29.

ਕਬੀਰ ਮਾਨਸ ਜਨਮੁ ਦੁਲੰਭੁ ਹੈ ਹੋਇ ਨ ਬਾਰੈ ਬਾਰ॥ ਜਿਉ ਬਨ ਫਲ ਪਾਕੇ ਭੁਇ ਗਿਰਹਿ ਬਹੁਰਿ ਨ ਲਾਗਹਿ ਡਾਰ॥30॥

Kabir, maanus junum(u) doolumbh(u) haiy, hoyay na baaro baar. Jiyoun bun ful paakay bhooyayn gireh(i) buhour(i) na laageh(i) daar.  30.

Word Meaning:  Maanus = human.   Doolumbh(u) = not easily available; priceless.  Baaraiy Baar = again, and again.   Paakay = ripe.   Bhooyayn = on the ground.  Daar = branch; tree. 

MEANING:  Like the ripe fruit. which falls to the ground in a forest and cannot be reattached to the branches, one does not get the opportunity (in human form) to get connected with God, oh Kabir.   30.

ਕਬੀਰਾ ਤੁਹੀ ਕਬੀਰੁ ਤੂ ਤੇਰੋ ਨਾਉ ਕਬੀਰੁ॥ ਰਾਮ ਰਤਨੁ ਤਬ ਪਾਈਐ ਜਉ ਪਹਿਲੇ ਤਜਹਿ ਸਰੀਰੁ॥31॥

Kabira, Toohi Kabir(u) Tu, Tayro naaoun Kabir(u). Ram ruttan(u) tub paayiyaiy, juou pehlay tujeh(i) sureer(u). 

Word Meaning:  Kabir(u) = greatest of all; God. Ruttan(u) =jewellike (Naam of God). Tujeh(i) = gives up.  Sureer(u) =  (love for) the body. 

MEANING:  You are the greatest, and so is Your Naam, oh God.  But one acquires His jewel-like precious Naam only when he gives up the love for his body (gives up enjoying sensual pleasures), oh Kabir.  31.

ਕਬੀਰ ਝੰਖ ਨ ਝੰਖੀਐ ਤੁਮਰੋ ਕਹਿਓ ਨ ਹੋਇ॥ ਕਰਮ ਕਰੀਮ ਜੁ ਕਰਿ ਰਹੇ ਮੇਟਿ ਨ ਸਾਕੈ ਕੋਇ॥  32॥

Kabir, jhunkh, na jhunkhiyaiy, toomro kuhhiyo na hoyay. Kerm Kareem ju kur(i) ruhhay, mayt(i) na saakaiy koyay.   32.

Word Meaning:  Jhunkh = worthless talk.   Jhunkhiyaiy = useless complaining.    Kerm = favours; gifts.        Kareem = the Provider.   Mayt(i) =  erase; stop.

MEANING: Quit complaining and using worthless words because nothing will change by your complaints, oh Kabir. The gifts that the Provider, God, has already planned for us cannot be stopped by anyone.     32.

ਕਬੀਰ ਕਸਉਟੀ ਰਾਮ ਕੀ ਝੂਠਾ ਟਿਕੈ ਨ ਕੋਇ॥ ਰਾਮ ਕਸਉਟੀ ਸੋ ਸਹੈ ਜੋ ਮਰਿ ਜੀਵਾ ਹੋਇ॥  33॥

Kabir, kasauti Ram kee, jhoottha tikaiy na koyay.  Ram kasauti so suhhaiy, jo mer(i) jeeva hoyay.   33.

Word Meaning:  Kasauti = testing device. Jhoottha = fake.   Tikaiy = stay; pass the test.   Suhhaiy = endures; becomes successful.  Mer(i) Jeeva = who kills his worldly desires.   

MEANING:  The testing device of God is such that a fake devotee cannot face it (pass the test).  Only that individual can pass the test who has killed his worldly desires while living in the family, oh Kabir.   33.

ਕਬੀਰ ਊਜਲ ਪਹਿਰਹਿ ਕਾਪਰੇ ਪਾਨ ਸੁਪਾਰੀ ਖਾਹਿ॥ ਏਕਸ ਹਰਿ ਕੇ ਨਾਮ ਬਿਨੁ ਬਾਧੇ ਜਮਪੁਰਿ ਜਾਹਿ॥  34॥

Kabir, oojul pehreh(i) kaapray, paan, soopaari khaah(i). Aikus Hur(i) kay Naam bin(u), baandhay Jumpur(i) jaah(i).  34.

Word Meaning:  Oojul = clean; shiny; expensive. Kaapray = clothes.  Paan = wrap of betel leaces.  Soopaari = areca nut.      Baandhy = chained.   Jum = Angel of justice.

MEANING:  Without God’s Naam, all those Maya-worshippers who wear expensive clothes, and chew on betel leaves and on areca nuts (signs of richness), are chained and taken to the court of the Angel of justice, oh Kabir.    34.

ਕਬੀਰ ਬੇੜਾ ਜਰਜਰਾ ਫੂਟੇ ਛੇਕ ਹਜਾਰ॥ ਹਰੂਏ ਹਰੂਏ ਤਿਰਿ ਗਏ ਡੂਬੇ ਜਿਨ ਸਿਰਿ ਭਾਰ॥ 35॥

Kabir, bayrra jurjuraa, phootay chhayk huzaar. Hurrooyay, hurooyay tir(i) guyyay, doobay jinh sir(i) bhaar.   35.

Word Meaning:  Bayrra = boat.  Jurjuraa = old.  Phootay = has.  Chhayk = holes.  Hurroyay = light weight.  Tir(i) Guyyay = swim across.  Bhaar = burden (of sins). 

MEANING: Just as an old boat, which has thousands of holes in it, sinks in the river, the boat of human life has many weaknesses, and is certain to sink in the world sea. Only those will swim across who have no weight of any sins and vices; but those who have a heavy burden of immoral deeds on them will drown in the world-sea, oh Kabir.  35.

ਕਬੀਰ ਹਾਡ ਜਰੇ ਜਿਉ ਲਾਕਰੀ ਕੇਸ ਜਰੇ ਜਿਉ ਘਾਸੁ॥ ਇਹੁ ਜਗੁ ਜਰਤਾ ਦੇਖਿ ਕੈ ਭਇਓ ਕਬੀਰ ਉਦਾਸੁ॥ 36॥

Kabir, haad  juray jiyoun laalri, kays juray jiyoun ghaas. Eh(u) jug(u) jerta daykh(i) kaiy, bhuyyo Kabir oodaas(u). 

Word Meaning:  Haad = bones.   Juray = burn.  Laakri = wood.   Kays = hair. 

MEANING: During cremation of one’s dead body, his bones burn like wood, and his hair like dry grass.  To watch this gloomy way in which the world burns, I feel depressed, oh Kabir (so, one should recite God’s Naam to attain a high spiritual state).   36.

ਕਬੀਰ ਗਰਬੁ ਨ ਕੀਜੀਐ ਚਾਮ ਲਪੇਟੇ ਹਾਡ॥ ਹੈਵਰ ਊਪਰਿ ਛਤ੍ਰ ਤਰ ਤੇ ਫੁਨਿ ਧਰਨੀ ਗਾਡ॥ 37॥

Kabir, gerb(u) na keejiyaiy, chaam lupaytay haad. Haiyvur oopur(i), chhutur tur, tay phoonn(i) dherni gaad.   37.

Word Meaning:  Gerb(u) = conceit. Chaam = skin. Haivur = best horses.  Chhuttar = royal canopy.  Tur = beneath.  Phoonn(i) = too.  Dherni = ground.  Gaad = bury in. 

MEANING: One must not feel conceited about one's beautiful body, which is nothing more than bones wrapped in skin. Even those who sit on (ride) the best horses and under the royal canopy are buried in the ground, oh Kabir.   37.

ਕਬੀਰ ਗਰਬੁ ਨ ਕੀਜੀਐ ਊਚਾ ਦੇਖਿ ਅਵਾਸੁ॥ ਆਜੁ ਕਾਲਿ ਭੁਇ ਲੇਟਣਾ ਊਪਰਿ ਜਾਮੈ ਘਾਸੁ॥ 38॥

Kabir, gerb(u) na keejiyaiy, oocha daykh(i) avaas(u). Aaj(u), kaal(i) bhooyay laytna, oopur(i) jaamaiy ghaas(u).   38.

Word Meaning:  Oocha = high; tall.  Avaas(u) = residence.  Aaj(u) Kaal(i) = today or tomorrow; anytime.   Bhooyayn = ground.  Laytna = lie down.  Jaamaiy = grows.

MEANING: One should not feel conceited to see his high (tall) residence because ultimately, he has to lie down on the (low) ground on which grows grass, oh Kabir.  38.

ਕਬੀਰ ਗਰਬੁ ਨ ਕੀਜੀਐ ਰੰਕੁ ਨ ਹਸੀਐ ਕੋਇ॥ ਅਜਹੁ ਸੁ ਨਾਉ ਸਮੁੰਦ੍ਰ ਮਹਿ ਕਿਆ ਜਾਨਉ ਕਿਆ ਹੋਇ॥ 39॥

Kabir, gerb(u) na keejiyaiy, runk(u) na hussiyaiy koyay. Ajhoun su naaou sumoondar meh(i), kiyya jaanoun, kiyya hoyay.    39.

Word Meaning:  Runk(u) = pauper. Ajhoun Su = still.  Naaou = boat. 

MEANING:  Do not be proud of your riches, and do not feel happy to see the fate of a pauper, oh Kabir.  The boat of your life is still in the (world) sea, who knows what will happen next. 39.   

ਕਬੀਰ ਗਰਬੁ ਨ ਕੀਜੀਐ ਦੇਹੀ ਦੇਖਿ ਸੁਰੰਗ॥ ਆਜੁ ਕਾਲਿ ਤਜਿ ਜਾਹੁਗੇ ਜਿਉ ਕਾਚੁਰੀ ਭੁਯੰਗ॥ 40॥

Kabir, gerb(u) na keejiyaiy, dayhi daykh(i) soorung. Aaj(u), kaal(i) tuj(i) jaahougay, jiyoun kaanchoori bhooyung.   40.

Word Meaning:  Dayhi = body.  Soorung = beautiful.   Tuj(i) = leave.  Kaanchuri = skin.

Bhooyung = snake.

MEANING: Do not feel conceited to see your beautiful body, oh Kabir.  Like a snake sheds its skin, you too will leave your body today or tomorrow (sooner or later).  40.

ਕਬੀਰ ਲੂਟਨਾ ਹੈ ਤ ਲੂਟਿ ਲੈ ਰਾਮ ਨਾਮ ਹੈ ਲੂਟਿ॥ ਫਿਰਿ ਪਾਛੈ ਪਛੁਤਾਹੁਗੇ ਪ੍ਰਾਨ ਜਾਹਿਗੇ ਛੂਟਿ॥ 41॥

Kabir, lootna haiy ta loot(i) laiy, Ram Naam haiy loot(i). Phir(i) paachhaiy puchhtaahugay, praan jaahingay chhoot(i).   41.

Word Meaning:  Lootna = to rob; to acquire; to enjoy.  Pucchtaahugay = repent.

MEANING: The most important thing to acquire in life is the Naam of God; so, acquire that if you have to acquire something; otherwise, you will repent after you lose your life, oh Kabir.41.   

ਕਬੀਰ ਐਸਾ ਕੋਈ ਨ ਜਨਮਿਓ ਅਪਨੈ ਘਰਿ ਲਾਵੈ ਆਗਿ॥ ਪਾਚਉ ਲਰਿਕਾ ਜਾਰਿ ਕੈ ਰਹੈ ਰਾਮ ਲਿਵ ਲਾਗਿ॥ 42॥

Kabir, aiysa koyee na junmiyo, apnaiy ghur(i) laavaiy aag(i). Paanchoun lurika jaar(i) kaiy, ruhhaiy Ram liv laag(i).   42.

Word Meaning:  Aiysa = such.  Ghur(i) = heart.  Aag(i) = fire (of spiritual enlightenment); 

God’s Naam.   Larika = male (in Punjabi and Hindi, every object is given a gender for 

grammatical purposes. The five demons are all males.  Jaar(i) = burn.  Liv = meditate on. 

MEANING: There is a rare individual born in this world, who enlightens his mind with the fire of Divine Knowledge, burns all the five demons in that fire, and stays focused on (connected with) God, oh Kabir.   42.

ਕੋ ਹੈ ਲਰਿਕਾ ਬੇਚਈ ਲਰਿਕੀ ਬੇਚੈ ਕੋਇ॥ ਸਾਝਾ ਕਰੈ ਕਬੀਰ ਸਿਉ ਹਰਿ ਸੰਗਿ ਬਨਜੁ ਕਰੇਇ॥ 43॥

Ko haiy lurika baychyee, luriki baychaiy koyay.  Saanjha kuraiy Kabir siyoun, Hur(i) sung(i) bunuj kuray.  43.

Word Meaning:  Baychyee = sells; trades for. Saanjha = partnership; sharing with. Sung(i) = with.  Bunuj(u) = trading. 

MEANING: There are some rare individuals who are willing to trade off their bad qualities (male or female, grammatically).  I want to have a partnership with them so that we could trade off our bad qualities together for God’s noble qualities, oh Kabir.   43.

ਕਬੀਰ ਇਹ ਚੇਤਾਵਨੀ ਮਤ ਸਹਸਾ ਰਹਿ ਜਾਇ॥ ਪਾਛੈ ਭੋਗ ਜੁ ਭੋਗਵੈ ਤਿਨ ਕੋ ਗੁੜੁ ਲੈ ਖਾਹਿ॥ 44॥

Kabir, eh chaytaavni, mut sehsa reh(i) jaayay.  Paachhaiy bhoag ju bhoagvaiy, tinh ko goorr(u) laiy khaah(i). 

Word Meaning:  Chaytaavni = warning.  Mut = lest.  Sehsa = doubt.  Pacchaiy = in the past.  Bhoag Ju Bhogvaiy = the luxuries which one has enjoyed.   Goorr(u) = jaggery (something of very low value); non-luxurious. 

MEANING:  Lest there be any doubt in anybody’s mind, I want to warn people before they repent for their deeds, that the worldly luxuries that one enjoys in life by forgetting God are spiritually so worthless that they would wish that they had lived a very simple (non-luxurious) life (but had remembered God), oh Kabir.   44.

ਕਬੀਰ ਮੈ ਜਾਨਿਓ ਪੜਿਬੋ ਭਲੋ ਪੜਿਬੇ ਸਿਉ ਭਲ ਜੋਗੁ॥ ਭਗਤਿ ਨ ਛੋਡਉ ਰਾਮ ਕੀ ਭਾਵੈ ਨਿੰਦਉ ਲੋਗੁ॥ 45॥

Kabir, mein jaaniyo purrhbo bhulow, purrhibay siyoun bhul jaog(u). Bhagat na chhoadoun Ram kee, bhaanvaiyn nindou loag(u).   45.

Word Meaning:  Jaaniyo = thought.  Purrhibo = to read holy books to acquire knowledge. Bhul = better.  Joag(u) = connecting with God (reciting His Naam). Nindou = condemn. 

MEANING: (Watching the Brahmans, who enjoy respect in society for their religious knowledge), I had thought that reading the religious books was a great thing to do, but I realized that reciting God’s Naam to connect with Him is much better than that.  I will never quit worshiping God, even if the people condemn me (disapprove of my practice), oh Kabir.  (Any type of worship other than the ritualistic worship of idols was strongly disapproved by society in those days. 

ਕਬੀਰ ਲੋਗੁ ਕਿ ਨਿੰਦੈ ਬਪੁੜਾ ਜਿਹ ਮਨਿ ਨਾਹੀ ਗਿਆਨੁ॥ ਰਾਮ ਕਬੀਰਾ ਰਵਿ ਰਹੈ ਅਵਰ ਤਜੇ ਸਭ ਕਾਮ॥  46॥

Kabir, loag k(i) nindaiy buppurra, jih mun(i) naahi Giyaan(u). Ram, Kabira ruv(i) ruhhaiy, uver tujay subh kaam.   46.

Word Meaning:  Bupurraa = poor (in Divine Knowledge); ignorant.     Ruv(i) = recite.  Tujay = giving up.     Kaam = tasks; rituals.

MEANING:  How can the ignorant people who have no Divine Knowledge condemn you (what difference does it make even if they condemn you)?  Giving up all other tasks (rituals), I am just reciting God’s Naam, oh Kabir.    46.

ਕਬੀਰ ਪਰਦੇਸੀ ਕੈ ਘਾਘਰੈ ਚਹੁ ਦਿਸਿ ਲਾਗੀ ਆਗਿ॥ ਖਿੰਥਾ ਜਲਿ ਕੋਇਲਾ ਭਈ ਤਾਗੇ ਆਚਿ ਨ ਲਾਗਿ॥  47॥

Kabir, perdaysi kaiy ghaaghraiy, chuhoun dis(i) laagi aag(i). Khintha jul(i) koyila bhuyyi, taagay aanch na laag(i).   47.

Word Meaning:  Perdaysi = foreigner; visitor (the man who is only a visitor in the world).   Ghaaghra = robe; body.     Khintha = a quilt of rags; human body.   Taaga = the thread (of soul that connects the man with God). 47.

MEANING: The fire of death surrounds the man from every direction when his time comes.  His body is burned to ashes, but the thread of the devotee's soul (which is a part of the Supreme Soul) remains intact (it is immortal), oh Kabir.   47.

                                                                                                         

ਕਬੀਰ ਖਿੰਥਾ ਜਲਿ ਕੋਇਲਾ ਭਈ ਖਾਪਰੁ ਫੂਟ ਮਫੂਟ॥ ਜੋਗੀ ਬਪੁੜਾ ਖੇਲਿਓ ਆਸਨਿ ਰਹੀ ਬਿਭੂਤਿ॥  48॥

Kabir, khintha jul(i) koyila bhuyyi, khaapur(u) phoot, muphoot. Yogi buppurra khayliyo, aasun(i) ruhhi bibhoot(i)..   48. 

Word Meaning:  Khaapur(u) = the begging dish of the yogi (worldly desires of man for which he begs for).    Buppurra = poor; ignorant.    Khayliyo = played his part.  Aasun = the place of his cremation.

MEANING: The man is a visitor on the earth like a yogi who wanders from place to place,  and begs. The man also begs for his worldly desires - and this is the role of the ignorant in the drama of life.  When death comes, his body gets burned, and all his desires are shattered.  Only ash is left behind at his cremation place, oh Kabir.   48.

(ਅੰਗ 1367)॥    ਕਬੀਰ ਥੋਰੈ ਜਲਿ ਮਾਛੁਲੀ ਝੀਵਰ ਮੇਲਿਓ ਜਾਲੁ॥ ਇਹ ਟੋਘਨੈ ਨ ਛੂਟਸਹਿ ਫਿਰਿ ਕਰਿ ਸਮੁੰਦ ਸਮਾਲਿ॥ 49॥

(PAGE 1367):      Kabir, thoraiy jul(i) maachhuli, jheevur mayliyo jaal(u). Eh toghnaiy na chhootsehn phir(i) kur(i) sumoond smaahl(i).   49.

Word Meaning:  Thoraiy = shallow.  Jheevur = fisheman (worldly attractions).   Mayliyo Jaal = castes his net. Toghnaiy = a small pond (a guide with little spiritual knowledge).    Smoond = ocean; Perfect Guru.

MEANING:  Just like a fish in shallow water can easily be caught in the net of the fisherman, worldly attractions easily trap the man when he is guided by a leader with very little spiritual knowledge, and he cannot find emancipation.  So, he needs to look for and find the Perfect Guru for emancipation, oh Kabir.    49.

ਕਬੀਰ ਸਮੁੰਦ ਨ ਛੋਡੀਐ ਜਉ ਅਤਿ ਖਾਰੋ ਹੋਇ॥ ਪੋਖਰਿ ਪੋਖਰਿ ਢੂਢਤੇ ਭਲੋ ਨ ਕਹਿ ਹੈ ਕੋਇ॥ 50॥

Kabir, sumoond na chhoadiyaiy, juou ut(i) khaara hoyay. Pokhur(i), pokhur(i) dhoondhtay, bhulow na keh(i) haiy koyay  50.

Word Meaning:  Smoond = ocean; Perfect Guru. Ut(i) = extremely.   Khaaro = salty (uncomfortable).   Pokhur(i) = pond (guru with no Knowledge).   Bhulow = good; meaningful.

MEANING:  One should never leave shelter of the Perfect Guru even if it may be very difficult to act on His Advice. Nobody gives you any meaningful spiritual advice if you visit the gurus with almost no Divine Knowledge, oh Kabir. 50. 

ਕਬੀਰ ਨਿਗੁਸਾਏ ਬਹਿ ਗਏ ਥਾਘੀ ਨਾਹੀ ਕੋਇ॥ ਦੀਨ ਗਰੀਬੀ ਆਪੁਨੀ ਕਰਤੇ ਹੋਇ ਸੁ ਹੋਇ॥ 51॥

Kabir nigoosaayayn beh(i) guyyay, thaanghi naahi koyay. Deen greebi aapuni, Kertay hoyay su hoyay. 

Word Meaning: Nogoosaayayn = having no guru.   Beh(i) Guyyay = carried away.       Thaanghi = boatman.    Deen Greebi = humbleness.   Kertay = by God. 

MEANING: Those who have no guru to spiritually guide them are left with no captain to steer the ship of their lives through the world sea and are carried away uncontrollably by the waves of worldly desires.  Those (who have the Guru to guide them) stay humble, and believe in God for whatever He does, oh Kabir.   51.

ਕਬੀਰ ਬੈਸਨਉ ਕੀ ਕੂਕਰਿ ਭਲੀ ਸਾਕਤ ਕੀ ਬੁਰੀ ਮਾਇ॥ ਓਹੁ ਨਿਤ ਸੁਨੈ ਨਾਮ ਜਸੁ ਉਹ ਪਾਪ ਬਿਸਾਹਨ ਜਾਇ॥52॥

Kabir, Baiyshno kee kookur(i) bhulee, shaakut kee boorie maayay. Oh(u) nit soonaiy Naam, Jus, oh paap bisaahun jaayay.    52.

Word Meaning: Baiyshno = devotee of God.  Kookur(i) = she dog.   Shaakut = Maya-worshipper (disconnected with God).  Bisaahun = to buy; to commit.

MEANING: Even the dog of a devotee of God is better (luckier) than the mother of a Maya-worshipper because when the devotee recites God’s Naam, his dog listens to that, while the mother of the Maya-worshipper is responsible to a great degree (for not guiding him properly) for the sins of her son, who commits them, oh Kabir.

ਕਬੀਰ ਹਰਨਾ ਦੂਬਲਾ ਇਹੁ ਹਰੀਆਲਾ ਤਾਲੁ॥ ਲਾਖ ਅਹੇਰੀ ਏਕੁ ਜੀਉ ਕੇਤਾ ਬੰਚਉ  ਕਾਲੁ॥ 53॥

Kabir, herna doobla, eh(u) huriyaala taal(u).  Laakh ahayri, aik(u) jeeou, kayta bunchou kaal(u).   53.

Word Meaning:  Herna = deer; man.       Doobla = (mentally) weak.  Huriyaala Taal(u) = green pool (of worldly attractions. Laakh = countless.            Aik Jeeou = single soul (of the man).     Ahayri = hunters; worldly attractions which attack one’s resistance to them.    Bunchou = protect; avoid.     Kaal(u) =  (spiritual) death.

MEANING: The world is full of countless attractions which are very difficult for the weak-minded man to resist by himself.  So, for how long can he resist before he is killed spiritually (gets trapped in them), oh Kabir?    53.

ਕਬੀਰ ਗੰਗਾ ਤੀਰ ਜੁ ਘਰੁ ਕਰਹਿ ਪੀਵਹਿ ਨਿਰਮਲ ਨੀਰੁ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਹਰਿ ਭਗਤਿ ਨ ਮੁਕਤਿ ਹੋਇ ਇਉ

ਕਹਿ ਰਮੇ ਕਬੀਰ॥ 54॥

Kabir, Ganga teer ju ghur(u) kureh(i), peeveh(i) nirmul neer(u). Bin(u) Hur(i) bhagat(i) na mookt hoyay, iyoun keh(i) rumay Kabir.  54.

Word Meaning:  Teer = bank. Nirmul = clean.  Neer(u) = water. Rumay = remembers. 

MEANING:  A man who builds his home on the bank of the river Ganga (which is believed to be holy and the source of emancipation) may drink its clean water, but he cannot attain emancipation there, which can be attained only by worshipping God.  So, I recite God’s Naam and advise people to do the same, oh Kabir.    54.

ਕਬੀਰ ਮਨੁ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਭਇਆ ਜੈਸਾ ਗੰਗਾ ਨੀਰੁ॥ ਪਾਛੈ ਲਾਗੋ ਹਰਿ ਫਿਰੈ ਕਹਤ ਕਬੀਰ ਕਬੀਰ॥ 55॥

Kabir, mun nirmul(u) bhuyya, jaiysa Ganga neer(u). Paachhaiy laago Hur(i) phiraiy, keht Kabir, Kabir. 55.

MEANING: (It is not the piousness of the Ganga-water), but, when one’s mind becomes pious like the water of Ganga, God follows him, calling him by the name, oh Kabir (God becomes slave of His devotee).      55.

ਕਬੀਰ ਹਰਦੀ ਪੀਅਰੀ ਚੂਨਾ ਊਜਲ ਭਾਇ॥ ਰਾਮ ਸਨੇਹੀ ਤਉ ਮਿਲੈ ਦੋਨਉ ਬਰਨ ਗਵਾਇ॥ 56॥

Kabir, herdi peeuri, choona oojul bhaayay.  Ram sunayhi tuou milaiy, donou bern guvaayay.   

Word Meaning:  Herdi = turmeric.  Peeuri = yellow (a dull colour).  Choona = quicklime (a white chemical used to whitewash buildings).  Oojul = shiny; white (a preferred colour).   Bhaayay = quality.     Ram Sunahyi = one who loves God.      Bern = colours.

MEANING:  Kabir Sahib compares low-caste people to turmeric, which has a yellow colour (which is considered to be less pure), and the high-caste people to quicklime (a chemical), which has a white colour (considered to be pure).  Kabir Sahib says that a person who loves God can be “one” with Him only if he loses (rises above) both the colours, i.e., makes no distinction between the high and the low castes.      56.

ਕਬੀਰ ਹਰਦੀ ਪੀਰਤਨੁ ਹਰੈ ਚੂਨ ਚਿਹਨ ਨ ਰਹਾਇ॥ ਬਲਿਹਾਰੀ ਇਹ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਿ ਕਉ ਜਾਤਿ ਬਰਨੁ ਕੁਲੁ ਜਾਇ॥ 57॥

Kabir, herdi peertun huraiy, choon chihn na ruhaayay. Bulihari eh preet(i) kuou, jaat(i) bern(u), kool(u) jaayay.  57.

Word Meaning: Peertun = yellowness.   Huraiy = lose.   Chihn = looks.  Ruhaayay = keep.  Bern(u) = skin colour.  Kool(u) = clan.

MEANING: I am a sacrifice for (praise a great deal) the love (for God) because of which the devotees do not feel ashamed of, or proud of their low or high castes, and make no distinction between people based on their castes, skin colours, and clans, oh Kabir. 57.

ਕਬੀਰ ਮੁਕਤਿ ਦੁਆਰਾ ਸੰਕੁਰਾ ਰਾਈ ਦਸਵੇ ਭਾਇ॥ ਮਨੁ ਤਉ ਮੈਗਲੁ ਹੋਇ ਰਹਿਓ ਨਿਕਸੋ ਕਿਉ ਕੈ ਜਾਇ॥ 58॥

Kabir, mookt dooaara sunkoora, rye dusvayn bhaayay. Mun(u) tuou maiygul hoyay ruhhiyo, nikso kiyoun kaiy jaayay.  58.

Word Meaning:  Mookt Dooaara = the door of emancipation (the stringent requirements). Rye = a very small size.  Dusvayn Bhaayay = one-tenth part.  Maiygul = (conceited like) the elephant.  Nikso = go through.

MEANING:  The door of emancipation is so narrow that it is like one-tenth the size of a rye seed (has very stringent requirements for approval by Waheguru, and only a few people can go through that); but the man has an inflated ego because of which it feels like a huge 

elephant; so how can he go through that door, oh Kabir.   58.

ਕਬੀਰ ਐਸਾ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਜੇ ਮਿਲੈ ਤੁਠਾ ਕਰੇ ਪਸਾਉ॥ ਮੁਕਤਿ ਦੁਆਰਾ ਮੋਕਲਾ ਸਹਜੈ ਆਵਉ ਜਾਉ॥ 59॥

Kabir, aiysa Satgur jay milaiy, toottha kuray pusaaou. Mookt dooaara moakla, sehjaiy aavoun jaaoun.   59.

Word Meaning:  Toottha = overpleased to be kind.  Pusaaou = shows benevolence. Moakla = wide.     Sehjaiy = easily.   

MEANING:  Answer to the above Question:  If one is lucky enough to seek shelter of the Perfect Guru who is overpleased to be benevolent to him, the door of emancipation becomes big enough that a devotee can easily come and go through that, oh Kabir.   59.

ਕਬੀਰ ਨਾ ਮੁੋਹਿ ਛਾਨਿ ਨ ਛਾਪਰੀ ਨਾ ਮੁੋਹਿ ਘਰ ਨਹੀ ਗਾਉ॥ ਮਤ ਹਰਿ ਪੂਛੈ ਕਉਨੁ ਹੈ ਮੇਰੇ ਜਾਤਿ ਨ ਨਾਉ॥ 60॥

Kabir, na moh(i) chhaan(i), na chhaapri, na moh(i) ghur nuhhi gaaoun. Mut Hur(i) poochhaiy kaun(u) haiy, mayray jaat(i) na naaoun.   60.

Word Meaning:  Chhaan = Chhaapri = a thatched hut.  Gaaoun = village.   Mut = maybe.  Naaoun = fame.

MEANING:  I am so humble (poor) that, let alone owning a big mansion, or a whole village, I do not claim to have even a thatched hut as my home; and I do not claim to belong to a high caste, or enjoy any fame.  Maybe, by being so humble, God will ask about me, oh Kabir.  60.

ਕਬੀਰ ਮੁਹਿ ਮਰਨੇ ਕਾ ਚਾਉ ਹੈ ਮਰਉ ਤ ਹਰਿ ਕੈ ਦੁਆਰ॥ ਮਤ ਹਰਿ ਪੂਛੈ ਕਉਨੁ ਹੈ ਪਰਾ ਹਮਾਰੈ ਬਾਰ॥ 61॥

Kabir, mooh(i) mernay ka chaaou haiy, meroun ta Hur(i) kaiy Dooaar. Mut Hur(i) poochhaiy kaun haiy, puraa humaaraiy baar.   61.

Word Meaning:  Chaaou = zest; excitement.   Baar = door.

MEANING:  I feel very excited about my death, provided it happens at the Door of God, where He may ask about me, who this man is lying on His Door, oh Kabir.    61.

ਕਬੀਰ ਨ ਹਮ ਕੀਆ ਨ ਕਰਹਿਗੇ ਨਾ ਕਰਿ ਸਕੈ ਸਰੀਰੁ॥ ਕਿਆ ਜਾਨਉ ਕਿਛੁ ਹਰਿ ਕੀਆ ਭਇਓ ਕਬੀਰੁ ਕਬੀਰੁ॥ 62॥

Kabir, na hum keeya, na kurhengay, na kur(i) sukaiy sureer(u). Kiyya jaanoun, kichh(u) Hur(i) keeya, bhuyyo Kabir(u), Kabir(u).    62.

Word Meaning:  Sureer(u) = human body.   Bhuyyo Kabir(u), Kabir(u) = everybody talks about Kabir (Kabir has become very famous)

MEANING:  I have no ability to do any such thing, nor can anyone in a human body do it; but I do not know what God did for me that I have become so famous that everybody is talking about me, oh Kabir. 62.

ਕਬੀਰ ਸੁਪਨੈ ਹੂ ਬਰੜਾਇ ਕੈ ਜਿਹ ਮੁਖਿ ਨਿਕਸੇ ਰਾਮੁ॥ ਤਾ ਕੈ ਪਗ ਕੀ ਪਾਨਹੀ ਮੇਰੇ ਤਨ ਕੋ ਚਾਮੁ॥63॥

Kabir, soopnaiy hoon bur-rraayay kaiy, jih mookh(i) niksaiy Ram(u). Ta kaiy pug kee paanhi, mayray tun ko chaam(u).   63.

Word Meaning:  Bur-rrayay Kaiy = talk in one’s dream.   Pug = feet. Paanhi = shoes. 

MEANING:  If someone utters God from his mouth even when he is dreaming, I will gladly offer my skin for him to make his shoes, oh Kabir.   63.

ਕਬੀਰ ਮਾਟੀ ਕੇ ਹਮ ਪੂਤਰੇ ਮਾਨਸੁ ਰਾਖਿਓ ਨਾਉ॥ ਚਾਰਿ ਦਿਵਸ ਕੇ ਪਾਹੁਨੇ ਬਡ ਬਡ ਰੂੰਧਹਿ ਠਾਉ॥ 64॥

Kabir, maati kay hum pootray, maanus(u) raakhiyo naaoun. Chaar(i) divus kay paahunay, bud, bud roondheh(i) tthaaoun.   64.

Word Meaning:   Maati = earth.  Pootray = puppets.  Maanus = humans.   Chaar Divus = of four days (very short period of time).     Paahunay = visitors.   Bud Bud = covering more, and more.  Roondheh(i) = claim to own.   Tthaaoun = area; space. 

MEANING: We, called humans, are just puppets of earth (are perishable), and not realizing that we are only visitors here for a short time, we begin to claim more and more space as ours, oh Kabir.  64.

ਕਬੀਰ ਮਹਿਦੀ ਕਰਿ ਘਾਲਿਆ ਆਪੁ ਪੀਸਾਇ ਪੀਸਾਇ॥ ਤੈ ਸਹ ਬਾਤ ਨ ਪੁਛੀਐ ਕਬਹੂ ਨ ਲਾਈ ਪਾਇ॥ 65॥

Kabir, mehndi kur(i) ghaaliya, aap(u) peesaayay, peesaayay. Taiyn Sheh baat na poochhiyaiy, kubhoon na laayi paayayn.   65.

Word Meaning:  Mehndi = henna.  Ghaaliya = worked hard.  Peesaayay = grinded; endure hardships.   Baat Na Poochhiyaiy = did not pay any attention. 

MEANING:  (People apply henna paste to their soles in the summertime to have a cooling effect), but before henna is made into a paste, it needs to be ground to be approved by the wearer.  Kabir Sahib complains to God that some people suffer many personal hardships, like henna, to be approved by Him, but God does not even pay them any attention.   65.

NOTE: Guru Amar Dass Ji explains in a Sloak on page (947 of SGGS) that hard work (to acquire noble qualities) is certainly a good step in the direction to union with Waheguru, but hard work does not give them the right to unite with Him. It is with His benevolence that one merges into Him.

ਕਬੀਰ ਜਹ ਦਰਿ ਆਵਤ ਜਾਤਿਅਹੁ ਹਟਕੈ ਨਾਹੀ ਕੋਇ॥ ਸੋ ਦਰੁ ਕੈਸੇ ਛੋਡੀਐ ਜੋ ਦਰੁ ਐਸਾ ਹੋਇ॥66॥

Kabir, jeh dur(i) aavut, jaatihoun, hutkaiy naahi koyay. So Dur(u) kaiysay chhoadiyaiy, jo dur(u) aiysa hoyay.   66.

Word Meaning:  Hutkaiy = stops; prevents. 

MEANING: Door to God’s Residence is such that nobody can prevent (an enlightened)  devotee from going there. So, why would anyone leave such a door, oh Kabir?  (So, recite Waheguru’s Naam to attain that level of spirituality).    66.

ਕਬੀਰ ਡੂਬਾ ਥਾ ਪੈ ਉਬਰਿਉ ਗੁਨ ਕੀ ਲਹਿਰ ਝਬਕਿ॥

(ਅੰਗ 1368)॥    ਜਬ ਦੇਖਿਓ ਬੇੜਾ ਜਰਜਰਾ ਤਬ ਉਤਰਿ ਪਰਿਓ ਹਉ ਫਰਕਿ॥ 67॥

Kabir, dooba thaa, paiy oobriyo, goon kee lehr jhubuk(i). 

(PAGE 1368):       Jub daykhiyo bayrra jurjuraa, tub ootur(i) puriyo huoun phuruk(i). 67.

Word Meaning:  Dooba = drowned.   Paiy = but.   Oobriyo = was saved.  Goon = God’s praise.  Lehr = wave.  Jhubuk(i) = push.   Jurjura = old.   Phuruk(i) = by jumping. 

MEANING:  I was submerged (about to drown) in the world-sea when a push by the waves of God’s praise saved me.   When I realized that the boat of my life had become weak (had many leaks for bad qualities to affect me), I quickly jumped out of that with God’s grace (and changed my lifestyle of immoral thinking) oh Kabir.  67.

ਕਬੀਰ ਪਾਪੀ ਭਗਤਿ ਨ ਭਾਵਈ ਹਰਿ ਪੂਜਾ ਨ ਸੁਹਾਇ॥ ਮਾਖੀ ਚੰਦਨੁ ਪਰਹਰੈ ਜਹ ਬਿਗੰਧ ਤਹ ਜਾਇ॥ 68॥

Kabir, paapi bhagat(i) na bhaavyee, Hur(i) pooja na soohaayay. Maakhi chundun perhuraiy, jeh bigundh teh jaayay.  68.

Word Meaning:  Na Bhaavyee = does not like. Bhagat(i) = Pooja = worship.  Soohaayay suits (his taste).    Maakhi = housefly. Chundun = (fragrance of) sandalwood.  Perhuraiy = pushes away; stays away. Bigundh = foul smell. 

MEANING:  Like a housefly, which stays away from the (pleasant) fragrance of sandalwood and lands on the foul-smelling objects, a sinner does not like the fragrance of God’s worship, which does not suit his taste, oh Kabir.  68. 

ਕਬੀਰ ਬੈਦੁ ਮੂਆ ਰੋਗੀ ਮੂਆ ਮੂਆ ਸਭ ਸੰਸਾਰੁ॥ ਏਕੁ ਕਬੀਰਾ ਨ ਮੂਆ ਜਿਹ ਨਾਹੀ ਰੋਵਨਹਾਰੁ॥ 69॥

Kabir, baiyd mooaa, roagi mooaa, mooaa subh sunsaar(u).  Aik(u) Kabira na mooaa, jih naahi rovunhaar(u).   69.

Word Meaning:  Baiyd(u) = physician (spiritual guide). Roagi = patient (of worldly desires). Aik(u) = one (one’s selfishness and ego).  Kabira = oh, Kabir. 

MEANING: Everybody in this world including the spiritual guides and the common man suffers from mental sickness because of worldly desires, and the whole world eventually dies; but one thing – man’s selfishness, and his ego - does not die (till the end) which does not have anyone to cry at its death (everybody becomes happy at its death), oh Kabir.    69.

ਕਬੀਰ ਰਾਮ ਨ ਧਿਆਇਓ ਮੋਟੀ ਲਾਗੀ ਖੋਰਿ॥ ਕਾਇਆ ਹਾਡੀ ਕਾਠ ਕੀ ਨਾ ਓਹੁ ਚਰ੍ਹੈ ਬਹੋਰਿ॥ 70॥

Kabir, Ram na dhiaayo, moati laagi khor(i). Kaaya haandi kaatth keen a oh(u) churhaiy buhoar(i).   70.

Word Meaning:  Moati = big.  Khoar(i) = erosion (of good qualities).   Haandi = cooking vessel.   Kaatth = (of) wood.    Churhaiy = put on fire to use as the cooking vessel.  Buhoar(i) = again.

MEANING:  The people, who do not recite God’s Naam (sing His praise), suffer from internal erosion of their good qualities; and like the wooden vessel which cannot be put on fire to cook the second time, (as it gets burned the first time), the human body which gets burned by the fire of its worldly desires (and the erosion of his good qualities), does not get a second chance (human life to worship God), oh Kabir.   70.

ਕਬੀਰ ਐਸੀ ਹੋਇ ਪਰੀ ਮਨ ਕੋ ਭਾਵਤੁ ਕੀਨੁ॥ ਮਰਨੇ ਤੇ ਕਿਆ ਡਰਪਨਾ ਜਬ ਹਾਥਿ ਸਿਧਉਰਾ ਲੀਨ॥ 71॥

Kabir, aiysi hoyay purie, mun ko bhaavut(u) keen(u). Mernay tay kiyya derpna, jub haath(i) sindhaura leen.   71.

Word Meaning:  Aiysi Hoyay Purie = it so happened. Mun Ko Bhaavut = what the mind liked. Derpna = be afraid.  Sidhaura = a vermilion-coated coconut that a recent widow holds in her hand to burn herself with the dead husband.  71.

MEANING:  Like a recent widow, who holds a vermillion-coated coconut in her hand (is ready) to burn herself on the pyre of her husband, when a man (with the grace of God) follows his convinced mind, and decides to sacrifice all his worldly desires, he is not afraid to do so, oh Kabir. 71.

ਕਬੀਰ ਰਸ ਕੋ ਗਾਡੋ ਚੂਸੀਐ ਗੁਨ ਕਉ ਮਰੀਐ ਰੋਇ॥ ਅਵਗੁਨੀਆਰੇ ਮਾਨਸੈ ਭਲੋ ਨ ਕਹਿ ਹੈ ਕੋਇ॥72॥

Kabir, rus ko gaando choosiyaiy, goon kuou muriyaiy royay. Uvguniaaray maansaiy, bhulow na keh(i) haiy koyay.   72.

Word Meaning:  Gaando = a piece of sugarcane.   Choosiyaiy = suck on.  Goon = (the bad) habits (which are dear to an individual). Muriyaiy Royay = kill them painfully.  Uvguniaaray = having bad habits. 

MEANING:  (The sugarcane stores its sweet juice in it, and does not want to lose it).   People have to crush, chew, and suck on it to enjoy its sweet juice (which causes pain to the cane).  Similarly, one finds it very painful when he has to kill (quit) the bad habits which are dear to him.  But he has to quit them, because nobody calls a man a gentleman if he has greed-like bad habits, oh Kabir.    72.

ਕਬੀਰ ਗਾਗਰਿ ਜਲ ਭਰੀ ਆਜੁ ਕਾਲਿ ਜੈ ਹੈ ਫੂਟਿ॥ ਗੁਰੁ ਜੁ ਨ ਚੇਤਹਿ ਆਪਨੋ ਮਾਝ ਲੀਜਹਿਗੇ ਲੂਟਿ॥73

Kabir, gaagur jul bhurie, aaj(u) kaal(i) jaiy haiy phoot(i). Gur(u) j una chayteh(i) aapno, maanjh leejehngay loot(i).  73.

Word Meaning:  Gaagur(i) = an earthen vessel to carry and store water.   Aaj(u) Kaal(i) = today or tomorrow (any time).  Phoot(i) = break.  Maanjh = in the middle of the journey.

ਕਬੀਰ ਬਿਰਹੁ ਭੁਯੰਗਮੁ ਮਨਿ ਬਸੈ ਮੰਤੁ ਨ ਮਾਨੈ ਕੋਇ॥ ਰਾਮ ਬਿਓਗੀ ਨ ਜੀਐ ਜੀਐ ਤਾ ਬਉਰਾ ਹੋਇ॥76

MEANING: The elixir stone and the sandalwood have unique qualities.  Iron and wood, which have no unique quality, become superior when they come in contact with each other in the same order, oh Kabir. (Even an immoral person acquires noble qualities when he takes shelter of the elixir-stone-like Guru).    77.

MEANING:  Human life is only for ten days (a very short period of time), and people play their drum (enjoy luxuries and spend their life) as they wish.  But they must realize that like the same passengers on the boat on the river, which rarely meet again, one does not get human life easily again (so, he must not waste it away in luxuries), oh Kabir.  80.

ਕਬੀਰ ਸਾਤ ਸਮੁੰਦਹਿ ਮਸੁ ਕਰਉ ਕਲਮ ਕਰਉ ਬਨਰਾਇ॥ ਬਸੁਧਾ ਕਾਗਦੁ ਜਉ ਕਰਉ ਹਰਿ ਜਸੁ ਲਿਖਨੁ ਨ ਜਾਇ॥ 81

MEANING:  Sitting on the pyre of her husband, the widow,(who is forced by the society to burn herself with her dead husband), talks to the cremation ground that the people who forced her to burn, have left the cremation ground for now satisfied that they have done a “noble” deed; but they do not realize that eventually all of them have to come to you (the cremation ground),  oh Kabir. 85.

MEANING:  One’s personal efforts to make a connection with God are successful only if he has His support.  Without Waheguru’s support, all his efforts fail, like bending a tree branch on which one sets foot for support, oh Kabir. (One must take shelter of the Guru to have union with God).   97.

ਕਬੀਰ ਅਵਰਹ ਕਉ ਉਪਦੇਸਤੇ ਮੁਖ ਮੈ ਪਰਿ ਹੈ ਰੇਤੁ॥ ਰਾਸਿ ਬਿਰਾਨੀ ਰਾਖਤੇ ਖਾਯਾ ਘਰ ਕਾ ਖੇਤੁ॥ 98॥

Kabir, uvrah kuou oopdayshtay, mookh mein pur(i) haiy rayt(u). Raas(i) biraani raakhtay, khaaya ghur ka khayt(u).  98.

Word Meaning:  Uvrah = others.  Oopdayshtay = advise.   Rayt(u) =  dirt. Raas(i) = (spiritual) wealth. Raakhtay = protect.   Ghur Ka = own.  Khayt = field; crop.

MEANING:  People who advise others (only to boost their ego and to earn wealth) get dirt in their mouths (filth on their minds).  They protect the spiritual wealth of others, but their sins rob them of their own spiritual crop (wealth), oh Kabir.   98.

ਕਬੀਰ ਸਾਧੂ ਕੀ ਸੰਗਤਿ ਰਹਉ ਜਉ ਕੀ ਭੂਸੀ ਖਾਉ॥ ਹੋਨਹਾਰੁ ਸੋ ਹੋਇ ਹੈ ਸਾਕਤ ਸੰਗਿ ਨ ਜਾਉ॥ 99॥

Kabir, Sadhu kee sungut(i) ruhhoun, juoun kee bhoosi khaaoun. Hoanhaar(u) so hoyay haiy, shaakut sung(i) na jaaoun. 

Word Meaning:  Juoun Kee bhoosi =  Grinded shells of barley.  Hoanhoar(u) = whatever is to happen.   Shaakut = disconnected with God.

MEANING:  I shall stay in the company of the holy saints even if I have to live on the ground 

shells of barley (spend a very poor life).  I shall never join the company of the individuals who are disconnected from God; I will rather gladly accept the pending outcome of whatever is to happen, oh Kabir.  99.

   

ਕਬੀਰ ਸੰਗਤਿ ਸਾਧ ਕੀ ਦਿਨ ਦਿਨ ਦੂਨਾ ਹੇਤੁ॥ ਸਾਕਤ ਕਾਰੀ ਕਾਬਰੀ ਧੋਏ ਹੋਇ ਨ ਸੇਤੁ॥ 100॥

Kabir, sungut(i) Sadh kee, din, din, doona hayt(u).  Shaakut kaari kaambri, dhoyay hoyay na sayt(u).  100. 

Word Meaning:  Hayt = love.  Kaari Kaambri = black blanket.  Sayt(u) = white.

MEANING:  One’s love for Waheguru doubles (increases) every day in the company of the Guru.  A Maya-worshipper is like a black blanket (full of bad deeds) which does not turn white on washing (his sins do not come off), oh Kabir.  100.

ਕਬੀਰ ਮਨੁ ਮੂੰਡਿਆ ਨਹੀ ਕੇਸ ਮੁੰਡਾਏ ਕਾਇ॥ ਜੋ ਕਿਛੁ ਕੀਆ ਸੋ ਮਨ ਕੀਆ ਮੂੰਡਾ ਮੂੰਡੁ ਅਜਾਇ॥101॥

Kabir, mun(u) moondiyaa nuhhi, kays moondaayay kaayay. Jo kichh(u) keeya, so mun keeya, moonda, moond(u) ajaayayn.  101.

Word Meaning: Moondiya = shaved; cleaned.  Kays = hair.  Kaayay = what for? of what use?  Moond(u) = head.  Ajaayay = to no use.

MEANING:  (Some people shave their heads as a symbol of their faith); addressing such people, Kabir Sahib questions them: You have not shaved (cleaned) your mind, so what is the use of shaving your head when it is one’s mind, and not the head, that does (controls) everything, oh Kabir.    101.

ਕਬੀਰ ਰਾਮੁ ਨ ਛੋਡੀਐ ਤਨੁ ਧਨੁ ਜਾਇ ਤ ਜਾਉ॥ ਚਰਨ ਕਮਲ ਚਿਤੁ ਬੇਧਿਆ ਰਾਮਹਿ ਨਾਮਿ ਸਮਾਉ॥  102॥

Kabir, Ram(u) na chhoadiyaiy, tun(u), dhu(u), jaayay tuou jaaou. Churn Kumul chit(u) baudhiya, Rameh(i) Naam(i) smaaoun.  102.

MEANING:  I will not quit reciting God’s Naam even if I have to lose my body or my wealth. My mind has been pierced (stitched) with His lotus-like Feet (His Naam), and I will stay absorbed in His Naam, oh Kabir. 102.     

ਕਬੀਰ ਜੋ ਹਮ ਜੰਤੁ ਬਜਾਵਤੇ ਟੁਟਿ ਗਈ ਸਭ ਤਾਰ॥ ਜੰਤੁ ਵਿਚਾਰਾ ਕਿਆ ਕਰੈ ਚਲੇ ਬਜਾਵਨਹਾਰ॥103॥

Kabir, jo hum junt(u) bujaavtay, toot(i) guyyeen subh taar. Junt(u) vichaara kiyya kuraiy, chullay bujaavunhaar.   103.

Word Meaning:  Junt(u) = musical instrument; worldly desires.  Taar = strings (of worldly attachments.   Bujaavunhaar = musician (mind).

MEANING:  (With the recitation of God’s Naam), all the wires of my instrument of worldly attractions, which my mind used to play at, have been broken. (I have been left with no worldly desires). What can the poor instrument (worldly desires) do now, (it cannot bother) when the mind, which used to play on it, is no longer the same, oh Kabir?    103. 

ਕਬੀਰ ਮਾਇ ਮੂੰਡਉ ਤਿਹ ਗੁਰੂ ਕੀ ਜਾ ਤੇ ਭਰਮੁ ਨ ਜਾਇ॥

(ਅੰਗ 1370)॥         ਆਪ ਡੁਬੇ ਚਹੁ ਬੇਦ ਮਹਿ ਚੇਲੇ ਦੀਏ ਬਹਾਇ॥  104॥

Kabir, maaya moondou tih guru kee, ja tay bherm(u) na jaayay.

(PAGE 1370):        Aap doobay chuhoun Bayd meh(i), chaylay deeyay buhaayay. 104.

Word Meaning:  Maayay = mother; wisdom; the teachings (of a guru) which give birth to the character of the disciples.   Moondou = shave head; shame.    Bherm(u) = doubts.   Doobay = drowned; got lost.  Chaylay = disciples.   Buhaayay = drowned in the sea of Maya.

MEANING: Shame on the teachings of such a guru who cannot dispel (remove) the doubts of his disciples.  Such Brahman gurus are lost in (the teachings of) the four Vedas, and drown their disciples in the world-sea, oh Kabir. 104

ਕਬੀਰ ਜੇਤੇ ਪਾਪ ਕੀਏ ਰਾਖ ਤਲੈ ਦੁਰਾਇ॥ ਪਰਗਟ ਭਏ ਨਿਦਾਨ ਸਭ ਜਬ ਪੂਛੇ ਧਰਮਰਾਇ॥ 105॥

Kabir jaytay paap keeyay, raakh tulaiy dooraayay. Pergut bhuyyay nidaan subh, jub poochhaiy Dhermraayay.   105.

Word Meaning:  Tulaiy = under.  Dooraayay = in hiding.   Nidaan = at the end. 

MEANING:  All those sins that the man commits in hiding are fully exposed at the end when the Angel of justice enquires about them (as he has all the records), oh Kabir.   105.

ਕਬੀਰ ਹਰਿ ਕਾ ਸਿਮਰਨੁ ਛਾਡਿ ਕੈ ਪਾਲਿਓ ਬਹੁਤੁ ਕੁਟੰਬੁ॥ ਧੰਧਾ ਕਰਤਾ ਰਹਿ ਗਇਆ ਭਾਈ ਰਹਿਆ ਨ ਬੰਧੁ॥ 106॥

Kabir, Hur(i) ka simrun(u) chhaad(i) kaiy, paaliyo buhout kootumb(u). Dhundha kerta reh(i) guyya, bhaayi ruhhiya na bundh(u).  106.

Word Meaning:  Paaliyo = raises.  Kootumb(u) = family.  Dhundha = worldly affairs.  Bundh(u) = support (to stop loss of something). 

MEANING:  Giving up (forgetting) worship of Waheguru, the man raises his big family; but in the attachment of earning more and more wealth, he loses his brothers and his relatives (nobody can save him from any punishment after death), oh Kabir. 106.

ਕਬੀਰ ਹਰਿ ਕਾ ਸਿਮਰਨੁ ਛਾਡਿ ਕੈ ਰਾਤਿ ਜਗਾਵਨ ਜਾਇ॥ ਸਰਪਨਿ ਹੋਇ ਕੈ ਅਉਤਰੈ ਜਾਏ ਅਪੁਨੇ ਖਾਇ॥ 107॥

Kabir, Hur(i) ka simrun(u) chhaad(i) kaiy, raat(i) jugaavun jaayay. Serpun hoyay kaiy aouturaiy, jaayay apunay khaayay.   107.

Word Meaning:  Raat Jugaavun = stay awake at night (under the guidance of some unscrupulous guru). Some highly ignorant childless women go to the cremation ground, sit next to a burning pyre, and perform some witchery with two objectives in mind: all the children of the dead man would die, and she would have her own children.  After reading some mantra given by the guru, she takes a nude bath, bakes roti, and eats that).   Serpun = female snake.  Aouturaiy = takes birth.  Jaayay = offspring.   

MEANING:  Ignoring God’s worship, a childless woman who performs some witchery at the cremation ground at night, takes birth in her next life as a female snake, and eats her own offspring, oh Kabir.    107.

ਕਬੀਰ ਹਰਿ ਕਾ ਸਿਮਰਨੁ ਛਾਡਿ ਕੈ ਅਹੋਈ ਰਾਖੈ ਨਾਰਿ॥ ਗਦਹੀ ਹੋਇ ਕੈ ਅਉਤਰੈ ਭਾਰ ਸਹੈ ਮਨ ਚਾਰਿ॥ 108॥

Kabir, Hur(i) ka simrun chhaad(i) kaiy, Ahoyee raakhaiy naar(i). Gudhee hoyay kaiy aouturaiy, bhaar suhhaiy mun chaar(i).  108.

Word Meaning:  Ahoyee = worship of the goddess of smallpox – Seetla – which is responsible for the disease of small pox, and rides a donkey.  Gudhee = female donkey.   Mun Chaar(i) = about 150 kg. 

MEANING:  Ignoring God’s Naam, the woman who worships Seetla, the goddess of smallpox, to please her, is born as a female donkey in her next incarnation (to become a ride for the goddess), and has to carry a weight of hundreds of kg, oh Kabir.    108.

NOTE:  As you can see from all these sloaks, disapproving of all other practices, Kabir Sahib puts emphasis only on God’s Naam.

ਕਬੀਰ ਚਤੁਰਾਈ ਅਤਿ ਘਨੀ ਹਰਿ ਜਪਿ ਹਿਰਦੈ ਮਾਹਿ॥ ਸੂਰੀ ਊਪਰਿ ਖੇਲਨਾ, ਗਿਰੈ ਤਾ ਠਾਹਰ ਨਾਹਿ॥ 109॥

Kabir, chuturaayee ut(i) ghunee, Hur(i) jup(i) hirdaiy maah(i). Soolie oopur khaylna, giraiy ta tthaahur naah(i). 

Word Meaning: Chuturaayee = wisdom.  Ut(i) Ghunee = of highest level.  Soori = cross.  Giraiy = drop.    Tthaahur = support; place to safety. 

MEANING:  The best wisdom for the man to attain peace of mind is to keep God’s Naam at heart, oh Kabir. However, love for Waheguru is very difficult to maintain because there are so many attractions in the world that captivate one’s mind. It is a risky game, like playing on a tall cross with no support once you fall from it.  Once a devotee’s mind becomes weak, and he falls for any sensual pleasure, all his previous hard labour to please Waheguru becomes null and void, and it will almost be impossible for him to earn God’s trust again.    109.

ਕਬੀਰ ਸੁੋਈ ਮੁਖੁ ਧੰਨਿ ਹੈ ਜਾ ਮੁਖਿ ਕਹੀਐ ਰਾਮੁ॥ ਦੇਹੀ ਕਿਸ ਕੀ ਬਾਪੁਰੀ, ਪਵਿਤ੍ਰ ਹੋਇਗੋ ਗ੍ਰਾਮੁ॥110॥

Kabir, soyee mookh(u) dhunn(i) haiy, ja mookh(i) kuhhiyaiy Ram(u). Dayhi kis kee baapuri, puvittar hoyaygo graam(u).   110.

Word Meaning:  Dhunn(i) = fortunate; great. Dayhi = body. Baapuri = poor. Graam(u) = village. 

MEANING:  The mouth, which recites God’s Naam, is highly fortunate because not only the poor body of the devotee, but the whole village (all its residents who listen to the Naam) also gets purified, oh Kabir.  110. 

ਕਬੀਰ ਸੋਈ ਕੁਲ ਭਲੀ ਜਾ ਕੁਲ ਹਰਿ ਕੋ ਦਾਸੁ॥ ਜਿਹ ਕੁਲ ਦਾਸੁ ਨ ਊਪਜੈ ਸੋ ਕੁਲ ਢਾਕੁ ਪਲਾਸੁ॥  111॥

Kabir, soyee kool bhulie ja kool hur(i) ko daas(u).  Jih kool daas(u) na oopjaiy, so kool dhaak(u), pulaas(u). 111.

Word Meaning:  Dhaak(u) Pulaas(u) = trees which are not of much worth, and they consume some other useful trees. 

MEANING: Only that clan is good (useful for others) which has a devotee of God born in it.  The clan which has produced no devotee of God is as worthless as the dhaak and the pulaas trees, oh Kabir.   111.

ਕਬੀਰ ਹੈ ਗੈ ਬਾਹਨ ਸਘਨ ਘਨ ਲਾਖ ਧਜਾ ਫਹਰਾਹਿ॥ ਇਆ ਸੁਖ ਤੇ ਭਿਖ੍ਹਾ ਭਲੀ ਜਉ ਹਰਿ ਸਿਮਰਤ ਦਿਨ ਜਾਹਿ॥ 112॥

Kabir, haiy, gaiy, baahun sughun ghun, laakh dhujaa phehraayay. Iyya sookh tay bhikhiya bhulie, juou hur(i) simrut din jaah(i).   112.

Word Meaning:  Haiy = horses.    Gaiy = elephants.    Baahun = vehicles (chariots).  Sughun Ghun = many, many.         Dhujaa = flags (a sign of richness and authority – the greater the number of flags, the richer and the more authoritative the owner is).    Bhikhiya = begged food. 

MEANING:  A man, who is so wealthy and has so much authority that he enjoys all the luxuries of life such as owning many, many horses, elephants, and chariots, and has thousands of flags fluttering on the top of his house, but does not remember God, is no better than the devotee who remembers God everyday even if he is so poor that he has to beg for his food, oh Kabir.   112.

ਕਬੀਰ ਸਭੁ ਜਗੁ ਹਉ ਫਿਰਿਓ ਮਾਦਲ ਕੰਧ ਚਢਾਇ॥ ਕੋਈ ਕਾਹੂ ਕੋ ਨਹੀ ਸਭ ਦੇਖੀ ਠੋਕਿ ਬਜਾਇ॥ 113॥

Kabir, subh(u) jug(u) huoun phiriyo, maandul kundh chudhaayay. Koyee kaahooko nuhhi, subh daykhi tthoak bujaayay.  113.

Word Meaning:   Maandul = drum.  Kundh chudhaayay = carrying on the shoulder.          Tthoak Bujaayay = examined very carefully.

MEANING:  I have travelled all over the world (country) and examined it carefully.  Now, I can 

say with the beating of the drum (loudly and publicly) that (other than God), there is no real friend of anybody in this world, oh Kabir.   113.

ਮਾਰਗਿ ਮੋਤੀ ਬੀਥਰੇ ਅੰਧਾ ਨਿਕਸਿਓ ਆਇ॥ ਜੋਤਿ ਬਿਨਾ ਜਗਦੀਸ ਕੀ ਜਗਤੁ ਉਲੰਘੇ ਜਾਇ॥ 114॥

Maarug(i) moti beethray, undha niksiyo aayay. Jot(i) binaa Jugdeesh ke jugut(u) oolunghay jaayay.  114.

Word Meaning:  Maarug(i) = on the journey of life. Moti = pearl-like (glorious) noble qualities of God.   Beethray = spread over.   Niksiyo Aayay = came on to the path.  Jot(i) = light; enlightenment.  Jugdeesh = Lord of the world; God.  Oolunghay = jumps over.

MEANING:  God’s glorious qualities are spread all over the man’s journey of life (His captivating beauty is visible in nature everywhere), and the (spiritually) blind man walks over it (without appreciating it). Without the enlightenment of their mind, the whole world is jumping over them (passing over without acquiring them), oh Kabir.  114.

ਬੂਡਾ ਬੰਸੁ ਕਬੀਰ ਕਾ ਉਪਜਿਓ ਪੂਤੁ ਕਮਾਲੁ॥ ਹਰਿ ਕਾ ਸਿਮਰਨੁ ਛਾਡਿ ਕੈ ਘਰ ਲੇ ਆਯਾ ਮਾਲੁ॥  115॥

Booda buns(u) Kabir ka, oopjiyo poot(u) kmaal(u). Hur(i) ka simrun(u) chhaaf(i) kaiy, ghur lay aaya maal(u).   115.

Word Meaning:  Booda = drowned; has brought disgrace. Buns(u) = family.  Oopjiyo =  taken birth; came into being.  Poot(u) = mind.  Kmaal(u) = amazing; intriguing.  Maal(u) = wealth.

MEANING:  I am ashamed of my amazing (fickle) mind, which has brought disgrace to my family because giving up God’s worship, it has given birth to ideas which think of only earning wealth, oh Kabir.   115.

ਕਬੀਰ ਸਾਧੂ ਕਉ ਮਿਲਨੇ ਜਾਈਐ ਸਾਥਿ ਨ ਲੀਜੈ ਕੋਇ॥ ਪਾਛੈ ਪਾਉ ਨ ਦੀਜੀਐ ਆਗੈ ਹੋਇ ਸੁ ਹੋਇ॥ 116॥

Kabir, Sadhu kuou milnay jaayiyaiy, saath(i) na leejaiy koyay. Paachhaiy paaoun na deejiyaiy, aagaiy hoyay su hoyay.  116.

Word Meaning:  Paachhaiy Paaoun = step backwards.  Aagaiy = next; in future.

MEANING:  Do not take anyone with you when you go to see the Guru, because your companion may advise you otherwise, because of which you may have to take backward steps (reverse your path).  So, go alone, and wait for the result of whatever happens, oh Kabir.    116.

ਕਬੀਰ ਜਗੁ ਬਾਧਿਓ ਜਿਹ ਜੇਵਰੀ ਤਿਹ ਮਤ ਬੰਧਹੁ ਕਬੀਰ॥ ਜੈ ਹੈ ਆਟਾ ਲੋਨ ਜਿਉ ਸੋਨ ਸਮਾਨਿ ਸਰੀਰੁ॥ 117॥

Kabir, jug(u) baandhiyo jih jayvri, tih mut bundhou Kabir. Jaiy, haiy aata, laon jiyoun, soan smaan(i) sureer(u).   117.

Word Meaning:  Baandhiyo = tied up.  Jayvri = chains (of worldly attachments).  Aata Loan = cheap stuff like flour and salt.

MEANING: Your body (mind) is pure like gold at the time of your birth, oh man. So, unlike the world, do not get trapped in the chains of worldly attachments; your precious gold-like human life will be reduced to flour and salt-like common things, oh Kabir.   117. 

ਕਬੀਰ ਹੰਸ ਉਡਿਓ ਤਨੁ ਗਾਡਿਓ ਸੋਝਾਈ ਸੈਨਾਹ॥ ਅਜਹੂ ਜੀਉ ਨ ਛੋਡਈ ਰੰਕਾਈ ਨੈਨਾਹ॥ 118॥

Kabir, huns oodiyo, tun(u) gaadiyo, sojhaayi saiynaah. Ujhoon jeeou na chhoadyee, runkaayee naiynah.   118.

Word Meaning:  Huns = soul.   Gaadiyo = buried.  Sojhaayi Saiynaah = talks in sign language.   Jeeou = soul; mind.     Runkaayi = poverty; meanness.  Naiynah = eyes.

MEANING:  Even when the man is so old and weak that his soul is about to leave the body, which is about to be buried in the ground, and he communicates only with signs, his eyes still do not quit his mean, immoral thinking (casting evil eyes on others’ belongings), oh Kabir.  118.

ਕਬੀਰ ਨੈਨ ਨਿਹਾਰਉ ਤੁਝ ਕਉ ਸ੍ਰਵਨੁ ਸੁਨਊ ਤੁਅ ਨਾਉ॥ ਬੈਨ ਉਚਰਉ ਤੁਅ ਨਾਮ ਜੀ ਚਰਨ ਕਮਲ ਰਿਦ ਠਾਉ॥119॥

Kabir, naiyn nihaaroun Toojh kuou, servun(u) soonoun Toou Naaoun. Baiyn oochroun Toou Naam Ji, Churn Kumul rid tthaaoun.   119.

Word Meaning: Nihaaroun = look at (with affection).  Servun(u) = ears. Baiyn = words. Tthaaaoun = room. 

MEANING:  I pray to God that I should look only at Him with affection, listen to His Naam with my ears, say only the words to recite His Naam, and give room only to His lotus-like Feet (His Naam) in my heart, oh Kabir.    119.

ਕਬੀਰ ਸੁਰਗ ਨਰਕ ਤੇ ਮੈ ਰਹਿਓ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਕੇ ਪਰਸਾਦਿ॥ ਚਰਨ ਕਮਲ ਕੀ ਮਉਜ ਮਹਿ ਰਹਉ ਅੰਤਿ ਅਰੁ ਆਦਿ॥ 120॥

Kabir, soorg, nerk tay mein rhhhiyo, Satgur kay persad(i).  Churn Kumul kee mauj meh(i) ruhhoun unt(i) ur(u) aad(i).   120.

Word Meaning:  Ruhhiyo = unconcerned.   Mauj = enjoy blissfulness.   Aad(i) = beginning.

MEANING:  With the grace of Satguru, I have become totally unconcerned about the comforts of paradise and the punishments in hell; I have been enjoying the blissfulness of His Naam from the beginning, and will continue to do so till the end, oh Kabir.   120.

ਚਰਨ ਕਮਲ ਕੀ ਮਉਜ ਕੋ ਕਹਿ ਕੈਸੇ ਉਨਮਾਨ॥ ਕਹਿਬੋ ਕਉ ਸੋਭਾ ਨਹੀ ਦੇਖਾ ਹੀ ਪਰਵਾਨੁ॥  121॥

Churn Kumul kee mauj ko. Keh(i) kaiysay oonmaan. Kehibo kuou sobha nuhhi, daykha hee pervaan(u).  121.

ਊਚ ਭਵਨ ਕਨਕਾਮਨੀ ਸਿਖਰਿ ਧਜਾ ਫਹਰਾਇ॥ ਤਾ ਤੇ ਭਲੀ ਮਧੂਕਰੀ ਸੰਤ ਸੰਗ ਗੁਨ ਗਾਇ॥ 150॥

ਕਬੀਰ ਪਾਟਨ ਤੇ ਊਜਰੁ ਭਲਾ ਰਾਮ ਭਗਤ ਜਿਹ ਠਾਇ॥ ਰਾਮ ਸਨੇਹੀ ਬਾਹਰਾ ਜਮਪੁਰੁ ਮੇਰੇ ਭਾਇ॥151॥

ਕਬੀਰ ਗੰਗ ਜਮੁਨ ਕੇ ਅੰਤਰੇ ਸਹਜ ਸੁੰਨ ਕੇ ਘਾਟ॥ ਤਹਾ ਕਬੀਰੇ ਮਟੁ ਕੀਆ ਖੋਜਤ ਮੁਨਿ ਜਨ ਬਾਟ॥152॥

Moon(i) = hermits; sages.   Baat = path.

MEANING:  (With God’s Naam in my heart), my mind has become a holy place like the city of Allahabad, which is at the confluence of the Rivers Ganga and Yamuna.  Now my mind has attained the high spiritual state where no thoughts about Maya originate, and it has become the resting place for my mind. It is the state which the sages and the hermits seek the path for, oh Kabir.  152.

ਕਬੀਰ ਜੈਸੀ ਉਪਜੀ ਪੇਡ ਤੇ ਜਉ ਤੈਸੀ ਨਿਭਹੈ ਓੜਿ॥ ਹੀਰਾ ਕਿਸ ਕਾ ਬਾਪੁਰਾ ਪੁਜਹਿ ਨ ਰਤਨ ਕਰੋੜਿ॥ 153॥

Word Meaning:  Payd = (childlike) young plant which bends easily and has no ego. Nibhaiy = to last.  Oarr(i) = end.   Heera = diamond. Ruttan = jewels.  Crore = millions.

MEANING:  Like a young plant and a newborn baby (who has no ego), if the man’s purity of mind lasts till the end (and he loves God), let alone a single diamond, even millions of precious stones cannot match the worth of his spiritual state. Oh Kabir.    153.

ਕਬੀਰਾ ਏਕੁ ਅਚੰਭਉ ਦੇਖਿਓ ਹੀਰਾ ਹਾਟ ਬਿਕਾਇ॥ ਬਨਜਨਹਾਰੇ ਬਾਹਰਾ ਕਉਡੀ ਬਦਲੇ ਜਾਇ॥  154॥

MEANING: I witnessed an amazing situation where diamonds were being sold in a market; without the right buyer (true judge), they were offered cowrie shells in trade, oh Kabir.  (Similarly, without a true devotee, nobody knows the worth of God’s Naam).  154.

ਕਬੀਰਾ ਜਹਾ ਗਿਆਨੁ ਤਹ ਧਰਮੁ ਹੈ ਜਹਾ ਝੂਠੁ ਤਹ ਪਾਪ॥ ਜਹਾ ਲੋਭੁ ਤਹ ਕਾਲੁ ਹੈ ਜਹਾ ਖਿਮਾ ਤਹ ਆਪਿ॥ 155॥

MEANING:  High moral principles prevail where there is enlightenment, and sins prevail where lies are (dishonesty is) the rule.  Likewise, there is spiritual death where greed is the rule, but God Himself is present where there is forgiveness, oh Kabir.   155. 

ਕਬੀਰ ਮਾਇਆ ਤਜੀ ਤ ਕਿਆ ਭਇਆ ਜਉ ਮਾਨੁ ਤਜਿਆ ਨਹੀ ਜਾਇ॥ ਮਾਨੁ ਮੁਨੀ ਮੁਨਿਵਰ ਗਲੇ ਮਾਨੁ ਸਭੈ ਕਉ ਖਾਇ॥ 156॥

ਕਬੀਰ ਸਾਚਾ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਮੈ ਮਿਲਿਆ ਸਬਦੁ ਜੁ ਬਾਹਿਆ ਏਕੁ॥ ਲਾਗਤ ਹੀ ਭੁਇ ਮਿਲਿ ਗਇਆ ਪਰਿਆ ਕਲੇਜੇ ਛੇਕੁ॥ 157॥

MEANING:  With the support and encouragement of Satguru, I have bought (acquired) the diamond-like Waheguru’s Naam in His shelter. Now my mind has acquired stability (has quit wavering), and has become peaceful, oh Kabir.   161.

ਕਬੀਰ ਜਿਹ ਮਾਰਗਿ ਪੰਡਿਤ ਗਏ ਪਾਛੈ ਪਰੀ ਬਹੀਰ॥ ਇਕ ਅਵਘਟ ਘਾਟੀ ਰਾਮ ਕੀ ਤਿਹ ਚੜਿ ਰਹਿਓ ਕਬੀਰ॥165॥

MEANING:  The pundits have taught the (false) path of rituals for spirituality to the people, which everybody follows for its simplicity.  But the true path to unite with God is very tough, and is like climbing a mountain. I am following that spiritual path, oh Kabir.   165.

ਕਬੀਰ ਦੁਨੀਆ ਕੇ ਦੋਖੇ ਮੂਆ ਚਾਲਤ ਕੁਲ ਕੀ ਕਾਨਿ॥ ਤਬ ਕੁਲੁ ਕਿਸ ਕਾ ਲਾਜਸੀ ਜਬ ਲੇ ਧਰਹਿ ਮਸਾਨਿ॥ 166॥

MEANING: Some wise people may disapprove of the ritualistic path of spirituality as advised by pundits, but they cannot give up their family traditions out of fear that they may be blamed (ridiculed).  But they do not realize who will be there from the family to protect them from being shamed (by the Angel of justice) when they are taken to the cremation ground after death, oh Kabir.  166.

ਕਬੀਰ ਡੂਬਹਿਗੋ ਰੇ ਬਾਪੁਰੇ ਬਹੁ ਲੋਗਨ ਕੀ ਕਾਨਿ॥ ਪਾਰੋਸੀ ਕੇ ਜੋ ਹੂਆ ਤੂ ਅਪਨੇ ਭੀ ਜਾਨੁ॥ 167॥

Word Meaning:   Baapuray = poor; low in wisdom.     Kaan(i) = mine; most(people). 

MEANING:  Following the traditions of most people, you will drown in the world-sea (experience spiritual death), oh foolish man. Believe it, that whatever happened to your neighbour (he drowned in the world-sea), will happen to you as well, oh Kabir.   167.

ਕਬੀਰ ਭਲੀ ਮਧੂਕਰੀ ਨਾਨਾ ਬਿਧਿ ਕੋ ਨਾਜੁ॥ ਦਾਵਾ ਕਾਹੂ ਕੋ ਨਹੀ ਬਡਾ ਦੇਸੁ ਬਡ ਰਾਜੁ॥  168॥

Also, there is a variety of kinds in the begged food because one begs from many different houses; moreover, there is no worry (about buying and cooking food), and the country is large, and so is your rule (because you can collect food from any house), oh Kabir.  168.

ਕਬੀਰ ਪਾਲਿ ਸਮੁਹਾ ਸਰਵਰੁ ਭਰਾ ਪੀ ਨ ਸਕੈ ਕੋਈ ਨੀਰੁ॥ ਭਾਗ ਬਡੈ ਤੈ ਪਾਇਓ ਤੂ ਭਰਿ ਭਰਿ ਪੀਉ ਕਬੀਰ॥170॥

MEANING: The world-pool is full of God’s Naam-water, but the unlucky people cannot drink it (acquire the Naam).  You are highly fortunate (to have access to it), so drink to your fill, oh Kabir. 170. 

ਕਬੀਰ ਪਰਭਾਤੇ ਤਾਰੇ ਖਿਸਹਿ ਤਿਉ ਇਹੁ ਖਿਸੇ ਸਰੀਰੁ॥ ਏ ਦੁਇ ਅਖਰ ਨਾ ਖਿਸਹਿ ਸੋ ਗਹਿ ਰਹਿਓ ਕਬੀਰੁ॥ 171॥

Kabir, perbhaatay taaray khiseh(i), tiyoun eh(u) khisay sureer(u). Eh dooyay ukkhur na khiseh(i), so geh(i) ruhhiyo Kabir(u).  171.

MEANING: Just like the shine of stars in the sky, which begins to diminish towards the end of the night, the strength of the human body also diminishes with old age (when the night of life is about to end).  But the strength of God never diminishes with time, and that is what I am keeping in my mind, oh Kabir.  171.

ਕਬੀਰ ਕੋਠੀ ਕਾਠ ਕੀ ਦਹਦਿਸਿ ਲਾਗੀ ਆਗਿ॥ ਪੰਡਿਤ ਪੰਡਿਤ ਜਲਿ ਮੂਏ ਮੂਰਖ ਉਬਰੇ ਭਾਗਿ॥ 172॥

MEANING:  Like a wooden room which is flammable and burns down quickly in fire, the human body (mind) is also highly flammable, which is surrounded all around by the fire of worldly desires. Those who consider themselves “wise” burn in the fire, but others (who are not attracted to worldly attractions) and are considered “foolish” by the “wise” are saved as they run (stay) away from the fire, oh Kabir.    172.

MEANING:  Ponder over the knowledge of religious books, and get rid of those (papers) on which only rituals are written and which cause meaningless anxieties. The only thing to do is to attach your mind to God and recite His Naam, oh Kabir.   173.

MEANING:  Like the sandal tree, which does not give up its sweet and cooling fragrance even though it is wrapped around by snakes, a holy saint does not give up his saintliness even though millions of nonbelievers may visit (and debate with) him, oh Kabir.  174.

MEANING: It is great when a man realizes the fear of God, because of which he forgets about thinking of worldly attractions.  Like a hailstone, which melts with warmth and flows into the nearby stream (which is its destination), the stone-like, merciless heart of the man melts with the warmth of God’s Naam, and he merges into God, oh Kabir.    177.

MEANING:  Making a wrap (body) of the dust (earth), God creates the human body for him to see (enjoy) the world for a short period of time. But the man must not forget that at the end, it (the human body) changes into dust again (from which it was created), oh Kabir.   178.

One does not cry, but the pain becomes more and more intense (as one longs for Waheguru’s Sight).   When I was hurt by the mind-piercing pain of separation from my God, I was so stunned that I froze at the spot (could not accept that), oh Kabir.   182.

ਕਬੀਰ ਚੋਟ ਸੁਹੇਲੀ ਸੇਲ ਕੀ ਲਾਗਤ ਲੇਇ ਉਸਾਸ॥ ਚੋਟ ਸਹਾਰੈ ਸਬਦ ਕੀ ਤਾਸੁ ਗੁਰੂ ਮੈ ਦਾਸ॥  183॥

(See the Sloak above).  So, I will be the slave of the devotee who can endure the hurt of Guru’s Shabad (before getting connected with Him).   183.

ਕਬੀਰ ਜਾ ਘਰ ਸਾਧ ਨ ਸੇਵੀਅਹਿ ਹਰਿ ਕੀ ਸੇਵਾ ਨਾਹਿ॥ ਤੇ ਘਰ ਮਰਹਟ ਸਾਰਖੇ ਭੂਤ ਬਸਹਿ ਤਿਨ ਮਾਹਿ॥ 192॥

ਕਬੀਰ ਮੇਰਾ ਮੁਝ ਮਹਿ ਕਿਛੁ ਨਹੀ ਜੋ ਕਿਛੁ ਹੈ ਸੋ ਤੇਰਾ॥ ਤੇਰਾ ਤੁਝ ਕਉ ਸਉਪਤੇ ਕਿਆ ਲਾਗੈ ਮੇਰਾ॥  203॥

Kabir, juou tooh(i) saadh Pirum kee, paakay sayti khayl(u). Kaachi sersoan payl(i) kaiy, na khul(i) bhuyyi, na tayl(u).   240.

Word Meaning:  Paakay = ripe; perfect. Sayti = with. Kaachi = unripe.  Sersoan = 

 mustard.   Paiyl(i) Kaiy =  by crushing.   Khul(i) = oilcake. 

MEANING:  If you have a longing to love God, then play the game of love with (seek shelter of) the Perfect (fully enlightened) Guru. Like crushing unripe (premature) mustard when you get neither oilcake nor oil, if you become a disciple of an unenlightened guru (like a ritualistic one), you will not gain anything spiritual at all, oh Kabir. 240. 

ਢੂੰਢਤ ਡੋਲਹਿ ਅੰਧ ਗਤਿ ਅਰੁ ਚੀਨਤੁ ਨਾਹੀ ਸੰਤ॥ ਕਹਿ ਨਾਮਾ ਕਿਉ ਪਾਈਐ ਬਿਨੁ ਭਗਤਹੁ ਭਗਵੰਤੁ॥ 241॥

Dhoondhut, doaleh(i) undh gut(i), ur(u) cheenut(u) naahi sunt. Keh(i) Naama kiyoun paayiyaiy, bin(u) bhagatoun Bhugvunt.  241.

Word Meaning:  Doaleh(i) = get astray.   Gut(i) = condition.  Cheenut(u) = recognize. 

MEANING:  The condition of those who search for God, but cannot recognize the true saints, is like the blind who get astray from the real path.  How can one connect with God without His worship, oh Namdev?  241.

ਹਰਿ ਸੋ ਹੀਰਾ ਛਾਡਿ ਕੈ ਕਰਹਿ ਆਨ ਕੀ ਆਸ॥ ਤੇ ਨਰ ਦੋਜਕ ਜਾਹਿਗੇ ਸਤਿ ਭਾਖੈ ਰਵਿਦਾਸੁ॥  242॥

Hur(i) so heera chhaad(i) kaiy, kureh(i) aan kee aas. Tay nur dojuk jaahingay, sut(i) bhaakhaaiy Ravidass(u).  242.

Word Meaning:  Heera = diamond.   Dojuk = hell.  Sat(i) Bhaakhaiy = tells the truth.

MEANING:   Bhagat Ravidass Ji tells the truth when he says that ignoring the diamondlike God, those who depend upon (seek shelter of) others, will go to hell,  oh Kabir.   242.

ਕਬੀਰ ਜਉ ਗ੍ਰਿਹੁ ਕਰਹਿ ਤ ਧਰਮੁ ਕਰੁ ਨਾਹੀ ਤ ਕਰੁ ਬੈਰਾਗੁ॥ ਬੈਰਾਗੀ ਬੰਧਨੁ ਕਰੈ ਤਾ ਕੋ ਬਡੋ ਅਭਾਗੁ॥ 243॥

Kabir, juou greh(u) kureh(i), ta dherm(u) kur(u), naahi ta kur(u) baiyraag(u). Baiyraagi, bundhun kuraiy, ta ko budo abhaag(u).   243.

Word Meaning:  Juou = if.    Greh(u) Kureh = choose family life.   Dherm(u) = life of high morality.  Baiyaag(u) = love for God, and detachment from worldly attachments. Bundhun = bonds of attachment. Budo = big; huge.  Abhaag(u) = misfortune. 

MEANING: If you want to choose family life, then follow it with high morality (spirituality); otherwise, break all bonds of worldly attachments (renounce the world), oh man.  But if a renouncer gets bonded with worldly attachments, it will be his big misfortune, oh Kabir. 243.
