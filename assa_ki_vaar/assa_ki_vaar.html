RAAG ASSA , VAAR, MEHLA 1                                                 

                                                            .

ੴ ਸਤਿਨਾਮੁ ਕਰਤਾ ਪੁਰਖੁ ਨਿਰਭਉ ਨਿਰਵੈਰ ਅਕਾਲ ਮੂਰਤਿ ਅਜੂਨੀ ਸੈਭੰ ਗੁਰ ਪ੍ਰਸਾਦਿ॥

Ek Onkar Sat(i) Naam, Kerta, Purakh(u), Nirbhuou, Nirvair(u), Akal Moorut(i) Ajooni Saibhunn(g) Gur Persad(i). 

MEANING:  There is only one Supreme Being whose existence is (Laws are) eternal. He is the creator and is omnipresent (living power).  He is fearless (because He has no superior and is above all natural laws). (See Sloak 1 after Paurri 3).  He bears no enmity (because He has no rival) for anyone (and is perfectly just).  Time has no effect on His Persona, and He never takes any life form.  He is self-illuminated and can be realized only with the grace of the Guru (Guru is a manifestation of Waheguru).

ਆਸਾ ਮਹਲਾ 1॥ ਵਾਰ ਸਲੋਕਾ ਨਾਲਿ ਸਲੋਕ ਭੀ ਮਹਲੇ ਪਹਿਲੇ ਕੇ ਲਿਖੇ॥

ਟੁੰਡੇ ਅਸਰਾਜੈ ਕੀ ਧੁਨੀ॥

        Assa Mehla 1: Vaar Sloakaan Naal(i), sloak bhee Mehlay Pehlay kay Likhay. 

                                           Toonday Asraajay kee dhooni.

NOTE:  A Vaar is a type of lyric which used to be sung by bards on stages to narrate some story of bravery (or a short epic) to inspire people.  Vaars did inspire people. So, Guru Nanak Sahib thought of vaars as a medium for singing the praise of Waheguru and used them for this sole purpose.  He wrote three Vaars, one each in Raag Maajh, Raag Assa, and Raag Mulhaar.  The other Gurus also wrote vaars in praise of Waheguru. There are 22 Vaars in Guru Granth Sahib as follows.

Guru Nanak Sahib =  3

Guru Amar Dass Ji =  4

Guru Ram Dass Ji  =  8

Guru Arjun Dev Ji   =  6

Satta and Bulvund =  1

Total                       = 22

The Vaars were written in verses, called paurris, and were intended to have some sloaks with them, but the sloaks were not necessarily written at the same time as the vaars.  The appropriate sloaks were selected by Guru Arjun Sahib later to match the paurris (have similar meanings).   Guru Arjun Sahib advised that, wherever possible, the vaars be sung to the tunes of popular folk vaars so that people can memorize them easily and sing Waheguru’s praise.  Assa Dee Vaar is advised to be sung on the tune of the vaar about “Toonda Asraaj”.   “Toonda or Tunda” means a person with severed hand(s).  Asraaj was a handsome young son of an aged rajah who married a young and beautiful girl. The new wife of the rajah was attracted to Asraaj who did not approve the new queen’s advances.  When Asraaj told the new queen in no uncertain terms that he would not yield to her immoral advances, the queen complained to the Rajah, blaming Asraaj of sexual assault.  The Rajah became so furious that he did not listen to any appeal of Asraaj, and ordered that his hands be severed and later be killed.   However, the executioner believed Asraaj’s story and did not kill him. He did sever his hands and threw him in an open well in a deserted place.  By Providence, a passerby heard cries from the well and pulled Asraaj out. Asraaj healed completely and was made the rajah of a neighbouring state.   The bards began to sing vaars about him. 

Assa Dee Vaar, which has 24 paurris (verses) and 59 sloaks, is a very popular composition of Guru Nanak Sahib, and is recited in every Gurdwara early in the morning as a regular feature.  In addition to Waheguru’s praise on many fronts written in a mesmerizing language, the Vaar also attacks the common misbeliefs and superstitions prevalent at the time, and enlightens the masses, showing them the true path of life.  It also illuminates the role of the Guru in our spiritual life.

ਸਲੋਕੁ ਮਹਲਾ 1॥ ਬਲਿਹਾਰੀ ਗੁਰ ਆਪਣੇ ਦਿਉਹਾੜੀ ਸਦ ਵਾਰ॥ ਜਿਨਿ ਮਾਣਸ ਤੇ ਦੇਵਤੇ ਕੀਏ ਕਰਤ ਨ ਲਾਗੀ ਵਾਰ॥ 1॥

Sloak(u) Mehla1:  Bulihaari Gur aapnay, diyohaarri sud vaar. Jin(i) maanus tay dayvtay keeyay, kert na laagi vaar. 1.

Word Meaning:   Bulihaari = offer to sacrifice life.   Diyohaarri = day.  Sud = hundreds.      Maanus = human being.   Kert = in doing so.   No Vaar = No time.      1.

MEANING:  I would love to sacrifice my life (be His slave) hundreds of times a day for my Guru, who transforms humans into gods (holy men) and takes no time to do so. 1.

ਮ:1॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਗੁਰੂ ਨ ਚੇਤਨ੍ਹੀ ਮਨਿ ਆਪਣੈ ਸੁਚੇਤ॥ ਛੂਟੇ ਤਿਲ ਬੂਆੜ ਜਿਉ ਸੁੰਞੇ ਅੰਦਰਿ ਖੇਤ॥ ਖੇਤੈ ਅੰਦਰਿ ਛੁਟਿਆ ਕਹੁ ਨਾਨਕ ਸਉ ਨਾਹ॥ ਫਲੀਅਹਿ ਫੁਲੀਅਹਿ ਬਪੁੜੇ ਭੀ ਤਨ ਵਿਚਿ ਸੁਆਹ॥3॥

M:1: Nanak Guru na chaytni, mun(i) aapnaiy soochayt.  Chhootay til booaarr jiyoun soonjay undar(i) khayt. Khaytaiy undar(i) chhoottiyaan kuhou Nanak suou naah.  Fuliyeh(i) fooliyeh(i) buppurray, bhee tun vich(i) sooaah.   3.

Word Meaning:  Chaytni = think of; remember.   Soochayt = alert; wise.   Chhootay = are left behind.   Til Booaarr = a sesame plant which has only ash in its pods and no sesame seed, as if it were burned (rotten).   Soonjaiy = deserted.    Khayt = field.  Naah = master; owner.   Fuliyeh(i) Fooliyeh(i) = flourish.      Bupurray = poor; helpless.   3.

MEANING:  Some of the people think themselves to be very clever (in the realm of Maya), but never think of Waheguru, oh Nanak.  They are left ignored by Waheguru like a rotten sesame plant, which is ignored (not harvested) by the owner, and is left behind in the deserted field.  Left behind in the deserted field, the rotten sesame plant may have many owners who could use it for different purposes (for example as fuel or as a sweeper), but it will have no real owner for the purpose it was cultivated for. Similarly, the Maya-engrossed individual will have no owner (Guru) who could lead him to union with Waheguru which is the real objective of human life.  Like the rotten sesame plant, such individuals may flourish in the world of Maya, but will have only ash (ignoble qualities) in their bodies (minds).    3.

ਪਉੜੀ॥ ਆਪੀਨੈ ਆਪੁ ਸਾਜਿਓ ਆਪੀ੍ਹਨੈ ਰਚਿਓ ਨਾਉ॥ ਦੁਯੀ ਕੁਦਰਤਿ ਸਾਜੀਐ ਕਰਿ ਆਸਣੁ ਡਿਠੋ ਚਾਉ॥ ਦਾਤਾ ਕਰਤਾ ਆਪਿ ਤੂੰ ਤੁਸਿ ਦੇਵਹਿ ਕਰਹਿ ਪਸਾਉ॥ ਤੂੰ ਜਾਣੋਈ ਸਭਸੈ ਦੇ ਲੈਸਹਿ ਜਿੰਦੁ ਕਵਾਉ॥ ਕਰਿ ਆਸਣੁ ਡਿਠੋ ਚਾਉ॥ 1॥

Paurri:  Aapinaiy Aap(u) saajiyo, Aapinaiy ruchiyo Naaoun.  Dooyee koodrut(i) saajiyaiy, kur(i) aasun(u) dittho chaaou.  Daata, Kerta Aap(i) Tu, toos(i) dayveh(i) kureh(i) pusaaou.  Toon jaanoyee subhsaiy, day laiyseh(i) jind(u) kuvaaou.  Kur(i) aasun(u dittho chaaou.    1.

Word Meaning:   Saajiyo = Ruchiyo = created.    Naaoun = Laws.    Dooyee = the other; second.   Koodrut(i) = nature.   Toos(i) = pleasingly.   Pusaao = spreading; giving.   Jaanoyee = know-all; omniscient.  Kuvaaou = word; Huqam.  Aasun(u) = place to sit (to manage); throne.    Chaaou = with zest.      1.

MEANING:  Waheguru created Himself, and also the Laws to govern the universe. Next, He created the nature (the visible world), and now sitting on His throne, He is watching it with zest.   He is the Creator and the Provider, and because of His kind nature, He gives His gifts pleasingly to the living beings, and keeps giving them (never stops giving them) He knows everything about His creation, and takes away life of living beings after giving it to them with His Huqam.  He is watching his creation with zest from His throne.        1.

ਸਲੋਕੁ ਮ:1॥ ਸਚੇ ਤੇਰੇ ਖੰਡ ਸਚੇ ਬ੍ਰਹਮੰਡ॥ ਸਚੇ ਤੇਰੇ ਲੋਅ ਸਚੇ ਆਕਾਰ॥ ਸਚੇ ਤੇਰੇ ਕਰਣੇ ਸਰਬ ਬੀਚਾਰ॥ ਸਚਾ ਤੇਰਾ ਅਮਰ ਸਚਾ ਦੀਬਾਣੁ॥ ਸਚਾ ਤੇਰਾ ਹੁਕਮ ਸਚਾ ਫੁਰਮਾਣੁ॥ ਸਚਾ ਤੇਰਾ ਕਰਮ ਸਚਾ ਨੀਸਾਣੁ॥ ਸਚੇ ਤੁਧੁ ਆਖਹਿ ਲਖ ਕਰੋੜਿ॥ ਸਚੈ ਸਭਿ ਤਾਣਿ ਸਚੈ ਸਭਿ ਜੋਰਿ॥ ਸਚੀ ਤੇਰੀ ਸਿਫਤਿ ਸਚੀ ਸਾਲਾਹ॥ ਸਚੀ ਤੇਰੀ ਕੁਦਰਤਿ ਸਚੇ ਪਾਤਿਸਾਹ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਸਚੁ ਧਿਆਇਨਿ ਸਚੁ॥ ਜੋ ਮਰਿ ਜੰਮੇ ਸੁ ਕਚੁ ਨਿਕਚੁ॥  1॥

Slaok M:1:  Suchay Tayray khund, Sachay brahmund.  Suchay Tayray loe, suchay aakaar.  Sachay Tayray kernay, serb beechaar.  Sacha Tayra Amar, Sacha Deebaan(u).  Sacha Tayra Huqam, Sacha Tayra Phoormaan(u).  Sacha Tayra kerm, sacha neeshaan(u).  Sachay Toodh(u) aakheh(i) lukh kroarr(i). Sachaiy subh(i) taan(i), sachaiy subh(i) joar(i).  Sachi Tayri sift(i), sachi salaah.  Sachi Tayri koodrut(i) Sachay Patshah.  Nanak Sach(u) dhiyaayin(i) Sach(u). Jo mur(i) jummay, su kuch(u) nikuch(u).   

Word Meaning:  Sachay = for real; everlasting.   Khund = Parts of the universe; planets.  Brahmund = universe.     Loe = worlds.    Aakaar = heavenly bodies.   Kernay = creations.   Amar = Huqam; Will.   Deebaan(u) = Court; justice.     Foormaan = Order; Will.   Kerm = benevolence; largesse.   Neeshaan = identity; stamp of approval.  Taan(i) = strengths.   Joar(i) = forces.   Salaah = Sift(i) = praise.  Sach(u) = immortal.   Kuch(u) Nikuch(u) = absolutely weak (vey low) in noble qualities; will take rebirths.   

MEANING:  (Contrary to the belief of some people that the world is not real and is only a dream), Your planets and Your universe are all real, oh Waheguru.  All Your worlds and all heavenly bodies are for real. Your Reign and Your Court (Justice) are everlasting, and so are Your Huqam (Laws) and Your Command.  Your benevolence and Your stamp of approval (of individuals) are also eternal.  Millions of people describe You (Your Virtues such as Your gifts, and strengths, etc.) as being everlasting, oh, the eternal Waheguru. Your praise is everlasting (it surely connects the devotee with You). Your (system of) creation is also everlasting. Those who worship the eternal Waheguru also become immortal by merging into Him, but those who take births and keep dying have no noble qualities (no significant spirituality) and will keep taking births and dying, oh Nanak.     1.

ਮ:1॥ ਵਡੀ ਵਡਿਆਈ ਜਾ ਵਡਾ ਨਾਉ॥ ਵਡੀ ਵਡਿਆਈ ਜਾ ਸਚੁ ਨਿਆਉ॥ ਵਡੀ ਵਡਿਆਈ ਜਾ ਨਿਹਚਲ ਥਾਉ॥ ਵਡੀ ਵਡਿਆਈ ਜਾਣੈ ਆਲਾਉ॥ ਵਡੀ ਵਡਿਆਈ ਬੂਝੈ ਸਭਿ ਭਾਉ॥ ਵਡੀ ਵਡਿਆਈ ਜਾ ਪੁਛਿ ਨ ਦਾਤਿ॥ ਵਡੀ ਵਡਿਆਈ ਜਾ ਆਪੇ ਆਪਿ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਕਾਰ ਨ ਕਥਨੀ ਜਾਇ॥ ਕੀਤਾ ਕਰਣਾ ਸਰਬ ਰਜਾਇ॥ 2॥

M:1:  Vuddi vudiyaayee, jaa vudda Naaoun. Vuddi vudiyaayee, jaa Sach(u) Niyaayoun. Vuddi vudiyaayee, jaa nihchul thaaoun. Vuddi viduyaayee, jaanaiy aalaaou. Vuddi vudiyaayee boojhaiy subh(i) bhaaou.  Vuddi vudiyaayee, pooch(i) na daat(i).  Vuddi vudiyaayee, ja  Aapay Aap(i). Nanak kaar na kuthnee jaayay. Keeta kerna serb rujaayay.   2. 

Word Meaning:  Vudiyaayee = greatness; magnificence.  Vuddi = transcends.   Naaoun = Laws; nature.  Niyaayoun = justice.   Nihchul = permanent.  Aalaaou = requests of the living beings.   Boojhaiy = knows.   Bhaaou = love; feelings.   Daat(i) = largesse.  Kaar = tasks.   Kuthnee = describe.    Rujaayay = Will.

MEANING:  Waheguru’s magnificence is great because His system of laws is great (looking after His creation well, delivering full justice, and keeping the universe in order). His magnificence is great because He delivers full justice, and because His throne is eternal (He will be the only Supreme Being ever). His magnificence is great because He knows the feelings and the needs of His creation without them (the living beings) saying anything.  His magnificence is great, because He grants His gifts without being asked.   His magnificence is great because He is the sovereign (and has no rival or superior). His deeds cannot be described, and whatever is happening in the universe, happens by His own Will, oh Nanak.2.   

ਮਹਲਾ 2॥ ਇਹੁ ਜਗੁ ਸਚੈ ਕੀ ਹੈ ਕੋਠੜੀ ਸਚੇ ਕਾ ਵਿਚਿ ਵਾਸੁ॥ ਇਕਨ੍ਹਾ ਹੁਕਮਿ ਸਮਾਇ ਲਏ ਇਕਨ੍ਹਾ ਹੁਕਮੇ ਕਰੇ ਵਿਣਾਸੁ॥ ਇਕਨ੍ਹਾ ਭਾਣੈ ਕਢਿ ਲਏ ਇਕਨ੍ਹਾ ਮਾਇਆ ਵਿਚਿ ਨਿਵਾਸੁ॥ ਏਵ ਭੀ ਆਖਿ ਨ ਜਾਪਈ ਜਿ ਕਿਸੈ ਆਣੈ ਰਾਸਿ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਜਾਣੀਐ ਜਾ ਕਉ ਆਪਿ ਕਰੇ ਪਰਗਾਸੁ॥ 3॥

Mehla 2:  Eh(u) jug(u) Sachaiy kee haiy kotthrri, Sachay ka vich(i) vaas(u).  Ikknah Huqam(i) smaayay luyyay, ikknah Huqamay kuray vinaas(u). Ikknah bhaanaiy kuddh(i) luyyay. Ikknah Maya vich(i) nivaas(u).  Aiyv bhee aakh(i) na jaapuyyee, j(i) kisaiy aanaiy raas(i). Nanak Gurmukh(i) jaaniyaiy, ja kuou Aap(i) kuray pergaas(u). 3.

Word Meaning:   Kotthrri = a small room to dwell.    Vaas(u) = dwelling.   Ikknah = some.  Smaayay = absorbs (in Him).   Vinaas(u) = destruction.   Bhaanaiy = with His Will.  Kuddh(i) Luyyay = pulls out.    Nivaas(u) = live in; deeply engrossed.   Raas(i) = suits the needs; serves the purpose. Gurmukh(i) = devotee. Pergaas(u) = enlightenment.  3.

MEANING:  This world is the dwelling of Waheguru, and He resides in it.  He absorbs some of the human beings in Himself (connects them with Him) while He destroys the spirituality (noble qualities) of others (by keeping them engrossed in Maya) by His Huqam (judgment).  He pulls some out of their deep involvement in Maya while keeping the others engrossed.  However, it cannot be predicted which situation will suit an individual (meet his objective) (for example, it is possible for a very rich person to spend his wealth on noble causes to serve humanity).   Only that lucky individual can be considered a real devotee, who is enlightened by Waheguru Himself, oh Nanak.   3.

ਪਉੜੀ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਜੀਅ ਉਪਾਇ ਕੈ ਲਿਖਿ ਨਾਵੈ ਧਰਮੁ ਬਹਾਲਿਆ॥ ਓਥੈ ਸਚੇ ਹੀ ਸਚਿ ਨਿਬੜੈ ਚੁਣਿ ਵਖ ਕਢੇ ਜਜਮਾਲਿਆ॥ ਥਾਉ ਨ ਪਾਇਨਿ ਕੂੜਿਆਰ ਮੁਹ ਕਾਲੈ ਦੋਜਕਿ ਚਾਲਿਆ॥ ਤੇਰੈ ਨਾਇ ਰਤੇ ਸੇ ਜਿਣਿ ਗਏ ਹਾਰਿ ਗਏ ਸਿ ਠਗਣ ਵਾਲਿਆ॥ ਲਿਖਿ ਨਾਵੈ ਧਰਮੁ ਬਹਾਲਿਆ॥ 2॥

Paurri:  Nanak jea oopaayay kaiy, likh(i) naavaiy Dherm(u) buhaaliya.  Oathaiy sachay hee such(i) nibrraiy, choon(i) vukh kuddhay jujmaaliya.  Thaaoun na paayin koorriaar mooh kaalaiy dojuk(i) chaaliya.  Tayraiy Naayay ruttay say jin(i) guyyay, haar(i) guyyay s(i) tthuggun vaaliya. Likh(i) naavaiy Dherm(u) buhaaliya.  2. 

Word Meaning:   Oppaayay kaiy = having created.   Likh(i) Naavaiy = to record accounts of deeds.  Dherm(u) = (mythical) Angel of justice.   Nibbrraiy = final decision. Choon(i) Vukh Kuddhay = sorts out.  Jujmaaliya = (mentally) sick (sinners). Koorriyaar = dealers in falsehood (deceitful).   Dojuk(i) = hell.   Ruttay = imbued.   Jin(i) guyyay = winners (in the game of life).  Tthuggun Vaalay = deceitful. 

MEANING:  After creating the living beings, Waheguru appointed the Angel of justice to record the accounts of deeds performed by them, oh Nanak.  There (in Waheguru’s Court), the decisions are based purely on truth; the sinners are sorted out and separated.  The dealers of falsehood (fraudulent) find no room there, and are sent to hell with their faces blackened (disgraced).  The devotees, who are imbued in Your Naam, win the battle of life (achieve the objective of union with You) while the deceitful lose the game of life (and are sent to be reborn), oh Waheguru. Waheguru has appointed the Angel of Justice to record the accounts of deeds, oh Nanak.     2.

Sloak M:1:  Vismaad(u) naad, vismaad(u) Vayd. Vismaad(u) jea, vismaad(u) bhayd. Vismaad(d) roop, vismaad(u) rung. Vismaad(u) naangay phireh(i) junt. 

(PAGE 464):     Vismaad(u) paun(u), vismaad(u) paani. Vismaad(u) ugnee khaydeh(i) vidaani. Vismaad(u) dherti, vismaad(u) khaani. Vismaad(u) saad(i) lugeh(i) praani. Vismaad(u) sunjoag(u), vismaad(u) vijoag(u).  Vismaad(u) bhookh, vismaad(u) bhoag(u).  Vismaad(u) sift(i), vismaad(u) salaah.  Vismaad(u) oojhurr, vismaad(u) raah.  Vismaad(u) nayrray, vismaad(u) door(i). Vismaad(u) daykhaiy haajra, hujoor(i). Vaykh(i) vidaan(u) ruhhiya vismaad(u). Nanak boojhun(u) pooraiy bhaag(i).  1.

Word Meaning:  Vismaad(u) = mesmerizing.    Naad = music.  Vayd = the Vedas; knowledge. Bhayd = differences.   Roop = forms.  Rung = colours.   Khadeh(i) = displays its different effects and forms.  Vidaani = amazing.   Khaani = sources of reproduction.   Saad = tastes.   Sunjoag(u) = union.   Vijoag = separation.   Bhoag = enjoying food.   Sift(i) = Salaah = praise.   Oojhurr = wilderness; lost from the right path.    Raah = the clear (right) path.   Haajra Hujoor(i) = present close by.   Vidaan(u) = amazing feats.     1.

NOTE:  Guru Nanak Sahib sees Waheguru everywhere and in everything, and is mesmerized by the ways He presents (shows) Himself in nature.  Everything in nature mesmerizes Guru Sahib. But one has to have a high spiritual level to appreciate this holy Shabad. 

MEANING:  I am mesmerized by the many different varieties of sounds produced by living and nonliving things, as well as by those of music.  The Vedas and the other sources of knowledge are also mesmerizing. I also get mesmerized by the variety of living beings and by the differences among them in terms of their forms and colours.  It is also mesmerizing to note how the animals survive (in extreme weather conditions) without any protection from clothes. The importance of air and water, and their easy availability, is also mesmerizing.  The way fire (energy) displays its power in different forms (appearing as lightning, dispelling cold, changing landscapes around volcanoes, and even under the surface of water in oceans, and so on), and how it can also be highly destructive, is mesmerizing. It is amazing to see the earth as the source of infinite things, and the different ways in which living beings reproduce.  How the living beings, especially humans, enjoy so many different tastes with the same tongue is mesmerizing.  The effects of union and separation are mesmerizing. I am mesmerized by the system of appetite (some animals can go without food for days or even for months, while some others keep eating), and by the different ways living beings are fed. The different ways to sing Waheguru’s praise are also mesmerizing.  It is also mesmerizing to see how, living in the same world, different men follow different paths – some the path of righteousness, while others are completely misled. It is also mesmerizing to note that some people find Waheguru far away, while others find Him very close.  I am completely mesmerized to see amazing things in nature.  But only some very lucky people attain this spiritual state to experience such mesmerization (to appreciate Waheguru’s nature), oh Nanak.    1.

ਮਹਲਾ 1॥ ਕੁਦਰਤਿ ਦਿਸੈ ਕੁਦਰਤਿ ਸੁਣੀਐ ਕੁਦਰਤਿ ਭਉ ਸੁਖ ਸਾਰੁ॥ ਕੁਦਰਤਿ ਪਾਤਾਲੀ ਆਕਾਸੀ ਕੁਦਰਤਿ ਸਰਬ ਆਕਾਰੁ॥ ਕੁਦਰਤਿ ਵੇਦ ਪੁਰਾਣ ਕਤੇਬਾ ਕੁਦਰਤਿ ਸਰਬ ਵੀਚਾਰੁ॥ ਕੁਦਰਤਿ ਖਾਣਾ ਪੀਣਾ ਪੈਨ੍ਹਣੁ ਕੁਦਰਤਿ ਸਰਬ ਪਿਆਰੁ॥ ਕੁਦਰਤਿ ਜਾਤੀ ਜਿਨਸੀ ਰੰਗੀ ਕੁਦਰਤਿ ਜੀਅ ਜਹਾਨ॥ ਕੁਦਰਤਿ ਨੇਕੀਆ ਕੁਦਰਤਿ ਬਦੀਆ ਕੁਦਰਤਿ ਮਾਨ ਅਭਿਮਾਨੁ॥ ਕੁਦਰਤਿ ਪਉਣੁ ਪਾਣੀ ਬੈਸੰਤਰੁ ਕੁਦਰਤਿ ਧਰਤੀ ਖਾਕੁ॥ ਸਭ ਤੇਰੀ ਕੁਦਰਤਿ ਤੂੰ ਕਾਦਿਰੁ ਕਰਤਾ ਪਾਕੀ ਨਾਈ ਪਾਕੁ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਹੁਕਮੈ ਅੰਦਰਿ ਵੇਖੈ ਵਰਤੈ ਤਾਕੋ ਤਾਕ॥ 2॥

Mehla 1:  Koodrut(i) disaiy, koodrut(i) sooniyaiy, koodrut(i) bhuou sookh saar(u)).  Koodrut(i) pataaleen aakaasheen, koodrut(i) serb aakaar(u). Koodrut(i) Vayd, Puran Kutaybaan, koodrut(i) serb veechaar(u).  Koodrut khaana, peena, pehnun, koodrut(i) serb piyaar(u). Koodrut(i) jaateen, jinseen, rungeen, koodrut(i) jea juhaan. Koodrut(i) naykiyaan, koodrut(i) budiyaan, koodrut(i) maan, abhimaan(u).  Koodrut(i) paun(u) paani, baiysuntar(u), koodrut(i) dherti khaak(u).  Subh Tayri koodrut(i), Toon Qadir(u) Kerta, paaki Naayee paak(u).  Nanak Huqamaiy undar(i) vaykhaiy, vertaiy taako taak.2. 

Word Meaning: Koodrut(i) = nature; Waheguru’s presence; His Laws.  Bhuou = (respectful) fear; obeying His Will. Saar(u) = supreme.   Pataaleen = in the underworlds.   Aakaar = universe.  Kutaybaan = the religious books of the West (Quran, Bible, Torah etc.).  Jinseen = different kinds.  Naykiyaan = noble deeds.  Budiyaan = ignoble deeds.   Abimaan(u) = disrespect; insult.  Baiysuntar(u) = fire.   Khaak(u) = dust.   Qadir(u) = designer.   Kerta = creator.   Paaki = holy; pious.  Nayee = praise; greatness.   Taako Taak = alone; solely.       2.

MEANING:  Waheguru is visible and can be heard through His nature (in which He is present). Living in His respectful fear (obeying His Will) is a source of supreme comfort.  He is present in nature everywhere - in the underworlds, in the skies, and in the whole universe.  Waheguru’s nature is discussed in the Vedas, in the Puranas, in the religious books of the Western world, and in discussions. All the processes of eating, drinking, and wearing (what we can eat, drink, and wear) as well as the natural feeling of love, are all governed by natural laws. Waheguru’s nature shows in different forms of life on earth (in different kinds, and in different colours). Nature is also reflected in noble and ignoble deeds, and in respect or disrespect of the man.   Nature also shows itself in the air, in water, in fire and in the dust of the earth (they are all regulated by natural laws).  It is all Your nature, and You are its designer and the creator, oh Waheguru. Your magnificence lies in Your piousness and in Your purity.  Waheguru looks after His creation using His laws, and He solely governs the universe, oh Nanak.   

ਪਉੜੀ॥ ਆਪ੍ਹੀਨੈ ਭੋਗ ਭੋਗਿ ਕੈ ਹੋਇ ਭਸਮੜ ਭਉਰੁ ਸਿਧਾਇਆ॥ ਵਡਾ ਹੋਆ ਦੁਨੀਦਾਰੁ ਗਲਿ ਸੰਗਲ ਘਤਿ ਚਲਾਇਆ॥ ਅਗੈ ਕਰਣੀ ਕੀਰਤਿ ਵਾਚੀਐ ਬਹਿ ਲੇਖਾ ਕਰਿ ਸਮਝਾਇਆ॥ ਥਾਉ ਨ ਹੋਈ ਪਉਦੀਈ ਹੁਣਿ ਸੁਣੀਐ ਕਿਆ ਰੂਆਇਆ॥ ਮਨਿ ਅੰਧੈ ਜਨਮੁ ਗਵਾਇਆ॥ 3॥

Paurri:  Aapinaiy bhog bhog(i) kaiy, hoyay bhusmurr, bhaur sidhaaya. Vudda hoa doonidaar(u), gul(i) sungul ghut(i) chulaaya. Uggaiy kerni keerut(i) vaachiyaiy, beh(i) laykha kur(i) sumjhaaya. Thaaoun na hoyee puoudeeyee, hoon(i) sooniyaiy kiya roovaaya. Mun(i) undhaiy junum(u) guvaaya.    3.

Word Meaning:  Bhoag = (enjoyment of) worldly materials.   Bhusmurr = pile of ash.   Bhaur = soul.   Sidhaaya = went away.  Vudda Hoa = beyond old age (died).   [Until about 1980s, the use of the word “death” was avoided as far as possible. Even when a lamp was to be put out, the elderly women used to say “lamp vudda kur(i) diyou”].  Doonidaar = family man.   Sungul = chain.   Ghut(i) = by putting.   Chulaaya = taken away.  Uggaiy = in the next world (Waheguru’s Court).  Kerni Keerut(i) = deeds performed.   Vaachiyaiy = reviewed.   Laykha = account.   Thaaoun = room.   Puoudeeyee = being hit with shoes; taking punishment.   Roovaaya = cries.     3.

MEANING:  Having enjoyed the worldly materials that were destined to be in his life (fate), the soul of the man leaves his body, which turns into dust.  After the death of the family man (who stayed engrossed in Maya), the agents of the Angel of death put chains around his neck, and take him away to produce him before Waheguru. The accounts of his deeds are reviewed there, and explained to him.  He does not find any room (to hide) when he is given the punishment he deserves due to his ignoble deeds, and there is nobody to listen to his cries.  The blind man wastes away his precious human life.   

ਸਲੋਕ ਮ:1॥ ਭੈ ਵਿਚਿ ਪਵਣੁ ਵਹੈ ਸਦਵਾਉ॥ ਭੈ ਵਿਚਿ ਚਲਹਿ ਲਖ ਦਰੀਆਉ॥ ਭੈ ਵਿਚਿ ਅਗਨਿ ਕਢੈ ਵੇਗਾਰਿ॥ ਭੈ ਵਿਚਿ ਧਰਤੀ ਦਬੀ ਭਾਰਿ॥ ਭੈ ਵਿਚਿ ਇੰਦੁ ਫਿਰੈ ਸਿਰ ਭਾਰਿ॥ ਭੈ ਵਿਚਿ ਰਾਜਾ ਧਰਮ ਦੁਆਰੁ॥ ਭੈ ਵਿਚਿ ਸੂਰਜੁ ਭੈ ਵਿਚਿ ਚੰਦੁ॥ ਕੋਹ ਕਰੋੜੀ ਚਲਤ ਨ ਅੰਤੁ॥ ਭੈ ਵਿਚਿ ਸਿਧ ਬੁਧ ਸੁਰਨਾਥ॥ ਭੈ ਵਿਚਿ ਆਡਾਣੇ ਆਕਾਸ॥ ਭੈ ਵਿਚਿ ਜੋਧ ਮਹਾਬਲ ਸੂਰ॥ ਭੈ ਵਿਚਿ ਆਵਹਿ ਜਾਵਹਿ ਪੂਰ॥ ਸਗਲਿਆ ਭਉ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਸਿਰਿ ਲੇਖੁ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਿਰਭਉ ਨਿਰੰਕਾਰੁ ਸਚੁ ਏਕ॥ 1॥

Sloak M:1:  Bhaiy vich(i) puvun(u) vuhaiy sudvaaou. Bhaiy vich(i) chulleh(i) lukh duriyaayo. Bhaiy vich(i) ugun(i) kuddhaiy vaygaar(i).  Bhaiy vich(i) dherti dubbi bhaar(i).  Bhaiy vich(i) Ind(u) phiraiy sir bhaar(i).  Bhaiy vich(i) Raja Dherm Dooaar(u).  Bhaiy vich(i) sooruj(u), bhaiy vich(i) chund(u).  Koh kroarri chulut na unt(u).  Bhaiy vich(i) Sidh, Budh Soornaath.  Bhaiy vich(i) aadaanaiy akaash.  Bhaiy vich(i) jodh muhaa bul soor.  Bhaiy vich(i) aaveh(i) jaaveh(i) poor. Sugliyaan bhuou likhiya sir(i) laykh(u). Nanak nirbhuou Nirunkaar Sach(u) Aik.    1.

Word Meaning:  Bhaiy = respectful fear; Waheguru’s laws.   Sudvaaou = always.  Duriyaayo = rivers.  Vaygaar(i) = service without any reward.   Dubbi Bhaar(i) = pressed (constrained) under some weight; force of gravitation.   Ind(u) = the god of rain; clouds.  Sir Bhaar(i) = on its head (upside down) (because the rain falls downwards and not upwards).   Raja Dherm = Dherm Raj = the Angel of justice.  Koh = a unit of length or distance; (koh = 2.2 km).   Soornath = the king of the gods.  Aadaanaiy = stretched.  Jodh Muha Bul Soor = gallants; valiant (who have conquered their mind and the five demons).     Aaveh(i) Jaaveh(i) = are born and die.  Poor = large groups of people.    1.

MEANING:  The air always moves, and millions of rivers flow according to the laws of nature (Waheguru).  The fire performs its duties without any reward as directed by Waheguru’s laws, and the earth is suppressed under some weight to keep it in position (in its orbit).  (Here, Guru Sahib alludes to the force of gravitation; otherwise, what will the term “weight” imply? Similarly, what will the word “constrained ” imply other than the force of gravity? He did not want to use the word for gravity directly, because He was not here to teach us science. 

The clouds also float upside down (the water drops of rain are at the bottom, and not on the top of the clouds) in the sky in keeping with Waheguru’s laws.  Even the Angel of Justice (actually there is none) serves at the door of Waheguru (he has no independent portfolio).   Even the moon and the sun obey Waheguru’s laws when they move millions of kilometres with no end.  All the Sidh yogis, the Buddhas, and the king of the gods are governed by Waheguru’s laws, and so are the stretched skies.   All the gallant and the valiant (those who have conquered their minds) obey Waheguru’s Command, and huge groups of people come and go (take births and die) governed by His laws.   All the living beings have their fortunes written on their foreheads as per the laws of Waheguru. It is the eternal Waheguru alone which is not subject to any laws, oh Nanak.     1.

(ਅੰਗ 465)॥   ਗਿਆਨੁ ਨ ਗਲੀਈ ਢੂਢੀਐ ਕਥਨਾ ਕਰੜਾ ਸਾਰੁ॥ ਕਰਮਿ ਮਿਲੈ ਤਾ ਪਾਈਐ ਹੋਰ ਹਿਕਮਤਿ ਹੁਕਮੁ ਖੁਆਰੁ॥ 2॥

Word Meaning:   Nirunkaar(u) = the formless God.   Kaytay = many; countless.   Ram = god Rama.  Ruvaal = dust of feet.   Kunnh = Krishna.   Kuhaaniyaan = stories.   Mungtay = beggars.  Girr Moorr(i) = repeatedly.  Pooreh Taal = dance steps matching the music.   Bazaari = stage performers.  Kudheh(i) Bazaar = give performance.   Aal pataal = meaningless talk.  Tukiyaan (Tuka) = a rupee (or commonly a 1/32 rupee).   Moondrray = large earrings.  Haar = necklace.   Chhaar = ash.   Hikmut(i) = cleverness.    Huqam = claim as a right.   Khooaar(u) = discomfiture.    2.

MEANING:  Only Waheguru is fearless (above any laws) while countless individuals like god Rama, are the dust of His feet (are His slaves and obey His Command), oh Nanak. There are many stories associated with Krishna, and many ideas from the Vedas (which all obey Waheguru’s Command, meaning one cannot find salvation or connect with God by worshiping them).  Countless beggars dance to music in the streets and repeatedly try to match their dancing steps with the music, with the belief that they are pleasing their deity.  Similarly, many street performers give performances, play the roles of kings and queens, and talk about meaningless things to earn money and please their deity.  All this happens in keeping with the laws of nature.  Then, there are some rich people who flaunt their wealth by wearing large earrings and necklaces worth millions of rupees, but they do not realize that the bodies they love to adorn will eventually become dust.   One cannot acquire Divine Knowledge (make a connection with Waheguru) simply by talking about Him; it is extremely difficult, like chewing iron, even to describe (what things or deeds please God).   It is only by God’s benevolence that one can connect with Him; all other means of shrewdness and claims as a right are fruitless and lead to discomfiture.       2.

ਪਉੜੀ॥ ਨਦਰਿ ਕਰਹਿ ਜੇ ਆਪਣੀ ਤਾ ਨਦਰੀ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਪਾਇਆ॥ ਏਹੁ ਜੀਉ ਬਹੁਤੇ ਜਨਮ ਭਰੰਮਿਆ ਤਾ ਸਤਿਗੁਰਿ ਸਬਦੁ ਸੁਣਾਇਆ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਜੇਵਡ ਦਾਤਾ ਕੋ ਨਹੀ ਸਭਿ ਸੁਣਿਅਹੁ ਲੋਕ ਸਬਾਇਆ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰਿ ਮਿਲਿਐ ਸਚੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਜਿਨ੍ਹੀ ਵਿਚਹੁ ਆਪੁ ਗਵਾਇਆ॥ ਜਿਨਿ ਸਚੋ ਸਚੁ ਬੁਝਾਇਆ॥ 4॥

Paurri:  Nudr(i) kureh(i) jay Aapni, ta nudri Satgur(u) paaya.  Eh(u) jeaou buhoutay junum bhermiya, ta Satgur(i) Shabad(u) soonaaya. Satgur jayvud daata ko nuhee, subh(i) sooniyo loak subayaa. Satgur(i) miliyaiy Sach(u) paaya, jinhi vichoun aap(u) guvaaya. Jin(i) Sacho Sach(u) boojhaaya.    2.

Word Meaning:   Nudr(i) = kind sight.   Jeaou = man.   Bhermiya = went through.   Jayvud = as great.     Aap(u) = selfishness and ego.   Guvaaya = lost; got rid of.      4.

MEANING:   If Waheguru casts a kind sight on some of His devotees, he gets connected with Satguru. When the man goes through many life forms, Waheguru connects him with Satguru to receive the proper advice. There is no other benefactor as great as Satguru in this world, listen, oh, the people of the world. It is the best idea (act) to apply your mind to Waheguru (always remember Him) so as to get connected with Him, which is the life (soul or basis) of the whole world.    6.

Word Meaning:  Huoun = Haumein = selfishness and ego.  Khuttiya = earned.    Guyya = lost.   Sachiyaar = having noble qualities; truthful (spiritual).   Koorriyaar = dealer in falsehood; deceitful.   Bhuriyaiy = accumulate filth (of evil deeds).   Dhovaiy = wash away the filth.   Jaati = high caste.   Jinsee = kind; humanity.  Khovaiy = loses.   Mokh Mookut(i) = emancipation.   Saar = worth; value.   Chhaya = darkness (of ignorance).   Boojhaiy = understands.   Dur(u) = (Waheguru’s) Door.    Soojhaiy = thinks of.   Vihoona = empty; devoid of.   Kuth(i) Kuth(i) = in discussions.   Loojhaiy = burning of heart.   Vakheh(i) = examines; evaluates.   Vaykh(u) = appearances; character.     1.

MEANING:  The man comes to this world full of haumein (selfishness and ego), and he leaves the world with haumein.  He is born with haumein, and he dies in haumein (haumein is the basic character of the man).  He makes donations in haumein (to get lot more in return, and to earn praise of the people), and he accepts things from others in haumein (thinking that it was his right to receive, or, he was obliging others by accepting their humble gifts).  He earns in haumein and loses in haumein too. It is mostly because of his haumein (selfishness) that he shows honesty in his dealings, and again, it is his selfishness because of which he cheats others.  One’s haumein is the underlying cause of his noble and ignoble deeds, and it is because of his deeds (noble or ignoble) which he commits under his haumein that he is born either in paradise-like conditions (born to enjoy luxuries) or in hellish conditions (born to suffer). He is happy or sad due to his haumein, and it is because of his haumein that he accumulates filth of evil deeds, or washes it away. 

He loses his high caste (respect in society), and his degree of humanity because of his haumein.  He performs acts of wisdom or of foolishness due to his haumein, and has no idea about the worth of emancipation and freedom from the bonds of Maya.  He is under the influence of Maya, and is in darkness of ignorance because of his haumein. Man’s haumein is the basis of his rebirths.   When he understands haumein, he will think of Waheguru’s Door;  but without that knowledge, he suffers from heart burning in useless discussions.  But we cannot entirely blame the individuals, because they acquire the character, and follow the path which Waheguru had carved for them after examining their previous deeds according to His laws, oh Nanak.      1.

Janeyoo Ceremony:  When the males of the upper two classes – the Brahmans and the Khatris – were about 10 years old, they were required to wear a cotton thread at a special ceremony.  The thread, called janeyoo, was considered sacred, helping the wearer in Waheguru’s Court after death, and was also a status symbol, as it showed the wearer was a member of a high caste.  All relatives, friends and dignitaries were invited for the special ceremony at which the family Brahman, called the Pandha, would recite some mantras sitting next to a fire built for the purpose.  Meals, including goat meat, were served after the ceremony.

When Guru Nanak Sahib was about 9 years old, His father Mehta Kalu Ji, in consultation with the family Brahman, Hardyal Ji, decided to hold the janeyoo ceremony for his young son. Nanak Ji waited happily for the ceremony so that He could dispel the meaningless significance of the custom. When all the guests had arrived, and Hardyal Ji had finished all the formalities and was about to put the thread around Nanak Ji’s neck, He stopped the Pandha from doing so and, to everybody’s surprise, asked the Brahman about the significance of the thread.  The Brahman was shocked, and simply narrated the usual story that it was a centuries old custom.  Nanak Ji said that He did not see any useful purpose of wearing such a thread which would wear out with time, get lost, or get burned. Moreover, it will not accompany Him to the next world.  The Brahman had no convincing response. Nanak Ji seized the opportunity, and surprised and mesmerized everybody when He described the kind of thread He wanted to wear. His main logic is explained in this holy Shabad.  Imagine a young boy of 9, explaining the qualities of the real Janeyoo!!   Enjoy the holy Shabad. 

MEANING:  I will gladly wear the sacred thread if you have one which is made from the cotton of compassion, the thread of contentment, the knots of high moral character, and is spun with the willingness to serve humanity, oh Pundit Ji.  Such a janeyoo, which is designed for the soul, will never break, will not get dirty, will not burn, and will not leave you any time (will always stay with you).  Those men are lucky (and praise-worthy) who wear such a janeyoo (which accompanies them to the next world), oh Nanak.   The thread, which you prepare for the young people, is bought for a very low price, and you put around the neck of your clients in the kitchen yard, which you claim to have been “purified”.  You load up the ears of the newly baptized child with your preaching, and proclaim to be his guru.  When the wearer dies, the janeyoo falls off, and he goes to the next world without your sacred thread.     1.

(ਅੰਗ 475)॥        ਨਾਨਕ ਸਾ ਕਰਮਾਤਿ ਸਾਹਿਬ ਤੁਠੈ ਜੋ ਮਿਲੈ॥ 1॥

Sloak Mehla 2:  Eh kinayhi daat(i), aapus tay jo paayiyaiy.

(PAGE 475):       Nanak sa kurmaat(i), Sahib tootthaiy jo milaiy.    1.

Word Meaning:   Kinayhi = what kind.   Daat(i) = gift; donation.   Aapus = with one's own efforts.   Kurmaat(i) = great gift.    Tootthaiy = pleased to be kind. 

MEANING:  What kind of gift is that which a servant receives from self?  The best gift is the one which the Lord bestows on His attendant (devotee) with His pleasure and kindness, oh Nanak.      1.

ਮਹਲਾ 2॥ ਏਹ ਕਿਨੇਹੀ ਚਾਕਰੀ ਜਿਤੁ ਭਉ ਖਸਮ ਨ ਜਾਇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਸੇਵਕੁ ਕਾਢੀਐ ਜਿ ਸੇਤੀ ਖਸਮ ਸਮਾਇ॥ 2॥

Mehla 2:  Eh kinayhi chaakri, jit(u) bhuou khusm na jaayay.  Nanak sayvuk(u) kaandhiyaiy, j(i) sayti khusm smaayay.   2.

Word Meaning:   Chaakri = service.   Bhuou = fear.   Kaandhiyaiy = is called. 

MEANING:  What kind of service is that where the worker is afraid of his master all the time (so as not to displease him).  Only that worker is the real servant who becomes one with his master, oh Nanak. (Such a situation is possible only between a devotee and Waheguru).  So, Waheguru is the only Master to serve, advises Guru Nanak.      2.

ਪਉੜੀ॥  ਨਾਨਕ ਅੰਤ ਨ ਜਾਪਨੀ ਹਰਿ ਤਾ ਕੇ ਪਾਰਾਵਾਰ॥ ਆਪਿ ਕਰਾਏ ਸਾਖਤੀ ਫਿਰਿ ਆਪਿ ਕਰਾਏ ਮਾਰ॥ ਇਕਨ੍ਹਾ ਗਲੀ ਜੰਜੀਰੀਆ ਇਕਿ ਤੁਰੀ ਚੜੇ ਬਿਸੀਆਰੁ॥ ਆਪਿ ਕਰਾਏ ਕਰੇ ਆਪਿ ਹਉ ਕੈ ਸਿਉ ਕਰੀ ਪੁਕਾਰ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਕਰਣਾ ਜਿਨਿ ਕੀਆ ਫਿਰਿ ਤਿਸ ਹੀ ਕਰਣੀ ਸਾਰ॥ 23॥

Paurri:  Nanak unt na jaapni Hur(i) Ta kay paaravaar.  Aap(i) kuraayay saakhti, phir(i) Aap(i) kuraayay maar. Ikknah guleen junjeeriyaan, ikk(i) toori churray bisiyaar(u). Aap(i) kuraayay, kuray Aap(i),  huoun kaiy siyoun kureen pookaar.  Nanak kerna jin(i) keeya, phir(i) Tis hee kerni saar. 23. 

Word Meaning:   Unt = limits.   Paaravaar = limitless.   Saakhti = creation.   Maar = death.  Junjeeriyaan = chains.    Toori = horses.   Bisiyaar = large; expensive (smart).  Pookaar = appeal.  Kerna Keeya = created the universe.  Saar = care; look after. 23. 

MEANING:  Nobody can find the limits of the limitless Waheguru, oh Nanak.  He creates the world, then destroys it (raising some to very high levels while throwing others to the ground).  There is so much disparity in this world that some have chains around their necks (are treated like slaves), while others are so rich that they ride very expensive horses.  Who can the man appeal to when Waheguru does everything Himself and makes others do whatever He wishes?   Still, Waheguru, who has created the universe, looks after His creation, oh Nanak.  23.

ਸਲੋਕੁ ਮ: 1॥ ਆਪੇ ਭਾਡੇ ਸਾਜਿਅਨੁ ਆਪੇ ਪੂਰਣੁ ਦੇਇ॥ ਇਕਨ੍ਹੀ ਦੁਧੁ ਸਮਾਈਐ ਇਕਿ ਚੁਲ੍ਹੇ ਰਹਨਿ ਚੜੇ॥ ਇਕਿ ਨਿਹਾਲੀ ਪੈ ਸਵਨ੍ਹਿ ਇਕਿ ਉਪਰਿ ਰਹਨਿ ਖੜੇ॥ ਤਿਨ੍ਹਾ ਸਵਾਰੇ ਨਾਨਕਾ ਜਿਨ੍ਹ ਕਉ ਨਦਰਿ ਕਰੇ॥ 1॥

Sloak M:1:  Aapay bhaanday saajiun(u), aapay poorun(u) dayay.  Ikkni doodh(u) smaayiyaiy, ikk(i) choolleh rehn(i) churrhay.  Ikk(i) nihaaleen paiy suvun(i), ikk(i) oopur(i) rehn(i) khurray. Tinha suvaaray Nanaka, jinh kuou nudr(i) kuray.  1.

Word Meaning:  Bhaanday = vessels; human bodies.   Saajiun(u) = creates.  Poorun dayay = perfection.    Doodh(u) = milk; good qualities.   Smaayiyaiy = add culture to make yogurt; implant.   Choolleh = on the hearth; earthen stove.  Churray = stay there (and suffer from heat).  Nihaaleen = on comfortable mattresses (beds). Khurray = (guard) with alertness.    Suvaaray = adorns; grooms (with noble qualities).      1.

MEANING:  Waheguru creates human vessels (human beings) and adorns (some of them) to perfection by implanting noble qualities in them.  There are some vessels used to make yogurt when milk is placed in them, and culture is added (they are treated nicely), while others suffer heat on the stove all the time (receive bad treatment).  Similarly, there are some humans which enjoy long sleeps in comfortable beds, while there are some who guard them with alertness.  Waheguru grooms some with noble qualities whom He is kind to, oh Nanak.   1. 

ਮਹਲਾ 2॥ ਆਪੇ ਸਾਜੇ ਕਰੇ ਆਪਿ ਜਾਈ ਭਿ ਰਖੈ ਆਪਿ॥ ਤਿਸੁ ਵਿਚਿ ਜੰਤ ਉਪਾਇ ਕੈ ਦੇਖੈ ਥਾਪਿ ਉਥਾਪਿ॥ ਕਿਸ ਨੋ ਕਹੀਐ ਨਾਨਕਾ ਸਭੁ ਕਿਛੁ ਆਪੇ ਆਪਿ॥ 2॥

Mehla 2:  Aapay saajay, kuray Aap(i), jaayee bh(i) rukhaiy Aap(i).  Tis(u) vich(i) junt oopaayay kaiy, daykhaiy thaap(i) oothaap(i).  Kis no kuhiyaiy Nanka, subh(u) kichh(u) Aapay Aap(i).  2.

Word Meaning:  Saajay = creates.   Jaayee = creation.   Rukhaiy = looks after.   Junt = living beings.   Thaap(i) = after creating.  Oothaap(i) = destroys.     2.

MEANING:  Waheguru creates the universe, and looks after His creation.  He creates living beings in the world, and watches as He creates and destroys them.  Who can we appeal to when Waheguru is everything Himself, oh Nanak?     2.

ਪਉੜੀ॥ ਵਡੇ ਕੀਆ ਵਡਿਆਈਆ ਕਿਛੁ ਕਹਣਾ ਕਹਣੁ ਨ ਜਾਇ॥ ਸੋ ਕਰਤਾ ਕਾਦਰੁ ਕਰੀਮੁ ਦੇ ਜੀਆ ਰਿਜਕੁ ਸੰਬਾਹਿ॥ ਸਾਈ ਕਾਰ ਕਮਾਵਣੀ ਧੁਰਿ ਛੋਡੀ ਤਿੰਨੈ ਪਾਇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਏਕੀ ਬਾਹਰੀ ਹੋਰ ਦੂਜੀ ਨਾਹੀ ਜਾਇ॥ ਸੋ ਕਰੇ ਜਿ ਤਿਸੈ ਰਜਾਇ॥ 24॥1॥ ਸੁਧੁ॥

Paurri:  Vudday keeyaan vudiyaayiyaan, kichh(u) kehna, kehn(u) na jaayay.  So Kerta, Kaadur(u), Kreem(u), day jeeaan rijk(u) sumbaah(i).  Saayee kaar kmaavuni, Dhoor(i) chhoadi Tinnaiy paayay. Nanak Aikee baahuri, hoar dooji naahi jaayay. So kuray, j(i) Tisaiy rujaayay.  24.1.  Shoodh(u). 

Word Meaning:  Vudda = great.   Vudiyaayiyaan = good qualities; magnificence.    Kerta = Creator.  Kaadur(u) = designer (master) of the nature.   Kreem(u) = provider.   Rijk(u) = necessities of life.  Sumbaah(i) = delivers.   Sayee = that.    Kaar Kmaavuni = performs deeds.   Dhoor(i) = right from the beginning.   Tinnaiy = that Waheguru.   Aikee = Waheguru.  Jaayay = place; shelter.   Rujaayay = (His) Will.    24. 

Shoodh(u) = corrected, verified.   

MEANING:  The magnificence (virtues) of the Great Waheguru cannot be described even if one tries very hard.  He is the creator, the designer of nature, and the provider who delivers the necessities of life to His living beings.  Everybody performs the kind of deeds for which he is predestined by Waheguru right from his birth.  There is no place beyond Waheguru’s control.   He does whatever He wishes, oh Nanak.    24.   Verified.
