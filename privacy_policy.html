Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Your privacy is important to us. It is our policy to respect your privacy regarding any information we may collect while operating our mobile application.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect personal identification information such as your name, phone number, location, or contacts. The app functions strictly locally on your device for reading Gurbani shabads offline.

2. Local Data Storage

Bookmarks, user font settings, and offline cached collection files are stored entirely locally on your device using encrypted SharedPreferences. You can clear this data at any time through the app settings.

3. Third-Party Services

The app does not contain third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or data analytics that sell or share your usage data with external third parties.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the Contact Us section in the application.
