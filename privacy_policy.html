This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which Mehanga Singh ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our") handles user information and privacy for the mobile application Reflections Of Gurbani (package name: com.leoford.reflections_of_gurbani, also referred to as "Reflections of Sri Guru Granth Sahib").

We are dedicated to safeguarding your personal privacy. Our app is designed to provide spiritual reading, collections, translations, audio, and video resources without compromising or exploiting your personal data.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We believe strongly in user data minimization. Reflections Of Gurbani does not collect, record, harvest, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII). Specifically:

  • We do not collect your real name, physical address, or phone number.
  • We do not access your contacts, SMS messages, phone logs, or calendar.
  • We do not track or log your precise or approximate physical GPS location.
  • We do not collect biometric identifiers, photos, audio microphone recordings, or camera feeds.
  • We do not require user registration, account creation, passwords, or social media logins to use the app.

2. Device Permissions and Usage

The application requests only minimal, standard permissions necessary for its intended functionality:

  • INTERNET (android.permission.INTERNET): Used solely to fetch Gurbani texts, collection updates, and stream spiritual audio and video content from our secure content distribution servers.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used to detect whether an active internet connection is available to determine whether to load remote media or serve cached offline content.

No sensitive, dangerous, or runtime privacy-invading permissions (such as Location, Camera, Microphone, or Storage access) are requested or utilized.

3. Local Data Storage & Preferences

The application stores certain user preferences locally on your physical device using Flutter's encrypted SharedPreferences mechanism:

  • Bookmarks & History: Saved bookmarks and reading history for your personal convenience.
  • Display Settings: User preferences such as font size, theme mode, and reading layout.
  • Offline Cache: Downloaded Gurbani collections, Shabads, and chapters for offline reading.

All of this data remains strictly stored on your local device. It is never uploaded to external servers, sold, or shared with third parties.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we do not collect or maintain user accounts or personal profiles on remote servers, we do not store any personal data about you on our backend.

You have absolute control over your locally stored data at any time:

  • You can clear all app preferences and cached data through the app settings or via your Android device's Settings > Apps > Reflections Of Gurbani > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Cache.
  • Uninstalling the application will immediately remove all locally stored preferences, bookmarks, and cached files from your device.

5. Third-Party Services & Links

The application does not include third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob or Unity Ads), behavioural trackers, or commercial analytics SDKs that profile users.

The app may display references or links to external media content (such as hosted audio or video streams). When interacting with external content, please be aware that third-party streaming services have their own privacy policies governing data handling.

6. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Reflections Of Gurbani is a general audience, family-friendly spiritual application suitable for users of all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction), in full compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

7. Security of Your Information

We take appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure any network communications (such as fetching content files over HTTPS) are encrypted and secure against unauthorized interception.
