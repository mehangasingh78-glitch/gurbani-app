(ਅੰਗ 56)॥    ਮੁਖਿ ਝੂਠੈ ਝੂਠੁ ਬੋਲਣਾ ਕਿਉ ਕਰਿ ਸੂਚਾ ਹੋਇ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਅਭ ਸਬਦ ਨ ਮਾਜੀਐ ਸਾਚੇ ਤੇ ਸਚੁ ਹੋਇ॥ 1॥ ਮੁੰਧੇ ਗੁਣਹੀਣੀ ਸੁਖੁ ਕੇਹਿ॥ ਪਿਰੁ ਰਲੀਆ ਰਸਿ ਮਾਣਸੀ ਸਾਚਿ ਸਬਦਿ ਸੁਖੁ ਨੇਹਿ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਪਿਰੁ ਪਰਦੇਸੀ ਜੇ ਥੀਐ ਧਨ ਵਾਢੀ ਝੂਰੇਇ॥ ਜਿਉ ਜਲਿ ਥੋੜੈ ਮਛੁਲੀ ਕਰਣ ਪਲਾਵ ਕਰੇਇ॥ ਪਿਰ ਭਾਵੈ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਾਈਐ ਜਾ ਆਪੇ ਨਦਰਿ ਕਰੇਇ॥ 2॥ ਪਿਰੁ ਸਾਲਾਹੀ ਆਪਣਾ ਸਖੀ ਸਹੇਲੀ ਨਾਲਿ॥ ਤਨਿ ਸੋਹੈ ਮਨੁ ਮੋਹਿਆ ਰਤੀ ਰੰਗਿ ਨਿਹਾਲਿ॥ ਸਬਦਿ ਸਵਾਰੀ ਸੋਹਣੀ ਪਿਰੁ ਰਾਵੈ ਗੁਣ ਨਾਲਿ॥ 3॥ ਕਾਮਣਿ ਕਾਮਿ ਨ ਆਵਈ ਖੋਟੀ ਅਵਗਣਿਆਰਿ॥ ਨਾ ਸੁਖੁ ਪੇਈਐ ਸਾਹੁਰੈ ਝੂਠਿ ਜਲੀ ਵੇਕਾਰਿ॥ ਆਵਣੁ ਵੰਞਣੁ ਡਾਖੜੋ ਛੋਡੀ ਕੰਤਿ ਵਿਸਾਰਿ॥ 4॥ ਪਿਰ ਕੀ ਨਾਰਿ ਸੁਹਾਵਣੀ ਮੁਤੀ ਸੋ ਕਿਤੁ ਸਾਦਿ॥ ਪਿਰ ਕੈ ਕਾਮਿ ਨ ਆਵਈ ਬੋਲੇ ਫਾਦਿਲੁ ਬਾਦਿ॥ ਦਰਿ ਘਰਿ ਢੋਈ ਨਾ ਲਹੈ ਛੂਟੀ ਦੂਜੈ ਸਾਦਿ॥ 5॥ ਪੰਡਿਤ ਵਾਚਹਿ ਪੋਥੀਆ ਨ ਬੂਝਹਿ ਵੀਚਾਰੁ॥ ਅਨ ਕਉ ਮਤੀ ਦੇ ਚਲਹਿ ਮਾਇਆ ਕਾ ਵਾਪਾਰੁ॥ ਕਥਨੀ ਝੂਠੀ ਜਗੁ ਭਵੈ ਰਹਣੀ ਸਬਦੁ ਸੁ ਸਾਰੁ॥ 6॥ ਕੇਤੇ ਪੰਡਿਤ ਜੋਤਕੀ ਬੇਦਾ ਕਰਹਿ ਬੀਚਾਰੁ॥ ਵਾਦਿ ਵਿਰੋਧਿ ਸਲਾਹਣੇ ਵਾਦੇ ਆਵਣੁ ਜਾਣੁ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਗੁਰ ਕਰਮ ਨ ਛੁਟਸੀ ਕਹਿ ਸੁਣਿ ਆਖਿ ਵਖਾਣੁ॥ 7॥ ਸਭਿ ਗੁਣਵੰਤੀ ਆਖੀਅਹਿ ਮੈ ਗੁਣੁ ਨਾਹੀ ਕੋਇ॥ ਹਰਿ ਵਰੁ ਨਾਰਿ ਸੁਹਾਵਣੀ ਮੈ ਭਾਵੈ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਸੋਇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਸਬਦਿ ਮਿਲਾਵੜਾ ਨਾ ਵੇਛੋੜਾ ਹੋਇ॥ 8॥5॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 1:  Mun(i) jootthaiy tun(i) jootth(i) haiy, jihva jootthi hoyay.

Moondhay goonheeni sookh(u) kayh(i).  Pir(u) ruliyaan rus(i) maansi Saach(i) Shabad(i) sookh(u) nayh(i). 1. Ruhaou. Pir(u) purdaysi jay theeyaiy, dhun vaandhi jhooray.  Jiyoun jul(i) thorraiy muchhuli, kern plaav kuray. Pir bhaavaiy sookh(u) paayiyaiy, ja Aapay nudr(i) kuray. 2. Pir(u) salaaheen aapna sukhi suhayli naal(i). Tun(i) sohaiy mun(i) mohiya, rutti rung(i) nihaal(i). Shabad(i) suvaari sohni, Pir(u) raavaiy goon naal(i). 3. Kaamun(i) kaam(i) na aavuyee khoti uvgunaar(i).  Na sookh(u) payiyaiy sahooraiy, jhootth(i) juli vaykaar(i). Aavun vunjun daakhrro, chhoadi kunt(i) visaar(i). 4.  Pir kee naar(i) soohaavni, mooti so kit(u) saad(i). Pir kaiy kaam(i) na aavuyee, boalay faadil(u) baad(i).  Dur(i) ghur(i) dhoyee na luhaiy, chhooti doojaiy saad(i). 5.  Pundit vaacheh(i) pothiyaan, na boojhay veechaar(i). Un kuou muteen day chuleh(i) Maya ka vapaar(u).  Kuthni jhootthi jug bhuvaiy, rehni Shabad su saar(u). 6.  Kaytay pundit jotki Baydaan kureh(i) beechaar(u). Vaad(i) virodh(i) slaahnay, vaaday aavun(u) jaan(u).  Bin(u) Gur kerm na chootsi keh(i) soon(i) aakh(i) vukhaan(u). 7.  Subh(i) goonvunti aakhiyaiy mein goon(u) naahi koyay. Hur(i) vur(u) naar(i) soohaavni, mein bhaavaiy Prubh(u) soyay. Nanak Shabad(i) milaavrra na vaychhorra hoyay.  8.5.
Word Meaning:  Joottha = unclean; dirty.  Jihva = tongue; language.   Kiyoun Kur(i) = how?     Soocha = clean; pure.   Ubh = heart; mind.    Maanjiyaiy = clean by washing (or by scrubbing).     1.                  Moondhay = oh, young woman (human).   Goonheeni = without good qualities.  Kayh(i) = which; how?    Ruliyaan = mixed with; connected.    Rus(i) = tastefully.   Maansi = enjoy.    Nayh(i) = in love.               Pause.        Pir(u) = husband (Waheguru).   Theeyaiy = happen, become.   Dhun = woman.   Vaandhi = separated.   Jhooray = laments; grieves.   Kern Plaav = implore; supplicate; feel restless.   Bhaavaiy = likes.      2.
Sukhi = suhayli = (female) friends.    Mohiya = enchanted; having magical effect.  Rutti = imbued.   Rung(i) = in love.   Nihaal(i) = blessed.   Svaari = adorned (with noble qualities).     3.     Kaamun(i) = woman.   Kaam(i) = of use.   Khoti = fake; dishonest.   Uvgunyaar(i) = having bad qualities.    Payiyaiy = (in) parents house (this world).   Sahooraiy = (in) in-laws' house (next world). (In Sri Guru Granth Sahib the relation between humans and Waheguru is thought of as between a wife and her husband. All humans are thought of as God’s wives with God as the husband).   Vaykaar(i) = in sensual desires or acts.    Aavun = coming; birth.   Vunjun = leave; death.   Daakhrro = very difficult; painful.   Kunt(i) = husband.   Visaar(i) = forget.     4.
Pir(u) = dear; husband.   Naar(i) = woman; wife.     Mooti = separated; disconnected.  Kit(u) = what?     Saad(i) = taste; interest.   Faadil(u) = Baad(i) = meaningless.  Dhoyee = shelter.   Luhaiy = get; obtain.   Doojaiy Saad(i) = in interst of the other (Maya).    5.
Vaacheh(i) = read casually.   Pothiyaan = religious books.   Boojhaiy = understand.  Veechaar(i) = ponder; think deeply.    Un-kuou = to others.   Mutti = advice.   Vaapaar(u) = trade.   Kuthni = sayings.   Bhuvaiy = wander; straggle.  Rehni = lead life.    Saar(u) = supreme.      6.   Jotki = fortune tellers.    Vaad(i) = (fruitless) discussion.   Viroddh(i) = in opposition.   Gur Kerm = Guru’s Kerm = deeds as per Guru’s Advice.   Vukhaan(u) = explain.     7.
Goonvunti = possessing good qualities.    Vur(u) = husband.             8.
MEANING:  When the mind is dirty, the body is also dirty (with ignoble deeds), and so is the tongue (because it criticizes others maliciously, and tells lies).  A liar tells lies with his mouth (tongue), so how can he be clean (pure)?  The mind can be cleansed with the water of Guru’s Shabad; only Waheguru is the source of Truth.       1.
What kind of comfort can a young woman (human) enjoy when she lacks all the noble qualities?  Only those wives (individuals) enjoy the love of the husband (Waheguru) which are connected with Him by Guru’s eternal Shabad, and by devotion.       Pause. 
If the husband becomes foreigner (leaves his wife), the separated wife grieves in separation, and feels restless just like a fish in less water.  But she feels perfect comfort when her husband casts a kind eye and begins to like her.           2.
One needs to praise the husband (Waheguru) in the company of the devotee-friends.   When the mind of a human bride is enchanted by Waheguru, and she is imbued in His love, she feels blessed, and her body looks pretty (she does noble deeds with her body). Adorned by Guru’s Shabad and good qualities, she becomes very attractive and enjoys union with Waheguru.         3.         The human bride, who is devoid of good qualities and fakes love for Him, is not worthy of Waheguru (and is ignored by Him) and enjoys no comfort either in this world or the next. She burns (suffers badly) in her false show off.  She is forgotten, and disowned by the husband (Waheguru), and is trapped in the painful cycle of rebirths.    4.
Only that human bride is pretty who is loved by the husband (Waheguru); but, the one who has been disowned by Him, spends a tasteless life.  She talks useless (about her mundane wishes and desires), and is unworthy for the husband (Waheguru).  She finds no support at Waheguru’s door, and is ignored because of her interest in the other (Maya).         5.
Pundits do study religious books (for debates only), but do not understand the depth of ideas. They preach others as a business to earn wealth only. Their preaching is false, and misguides the world.  Living as per the Shabad of the Guru, is supreme.         6.
There are many pundits and fortunate tellers who ponder on the Vedas. They earn praise for their ability to debate and oppose others, but they keep taking birth again, and again because of their misguided approach to attain high spirituality. They narrate, and explain the ideas to others, and listen to them too, but they can never cut the bonds of Maya without the grace of the Guru.     7.        Everybody is thought to have some noble qualities, but I do not have any such quality.  Waheguru’s wife (the devotee connected to Him) is pretty (has beautiful mind); I wish Waheguru liked me too. Once one is connected to Waheguru by Guru’s Shabad (Teachings), she will not suffer any separation again, oh Nanak.    8.5.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 1॥ 

ਜਪੁ ਤਪੁ ਸੰਜਮੁ ਸਾਧੀਐ ਤੀਰਥਿ ਕੀਚੈ ਵਾਸੁ॥ ਪੁੰਨ ਦਾਨ ਚੰਗਿਆਈਆ ਬਿਨੁ ਸਾਚੇ ਕਿਆ ਤਾਸੁ॥ ਜੇਹਾ ਰਾਧੇ ਤੇਹਾ ਲੁਣੈ ਬਿਨੁ ਗੁਣ ਜਨਮੁ ਵਿਣਾਸੁ॥ 1॥ਮੁੰਧੇ ਗੁਣ ਦਾਸੀ ਸੁਖੁ ਹੋਇ॥ ਅਵਗਣ ਤਿਆਗੁ ਸਮਾਈਐ ਗੁਰਮਤਿ ਪੂਰਾ ਸੋਇ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਵਿਣੁ ਰਾਸੀ ਵਾਪਾਰੀਆ ਤਕੇ ਕੁੰਡਾ ਚਾਰਿ॥ ਮੂਲੁ ਨ ਬੁਝੈ ਆਪਣਾ ਵਸਤੁ ਰਹੀ ਘਰ ਬਾਰਿ॥ ਵਿਣੁ ਵਖਰ ਦੁਖੁ ਅਗਲਾ ਕੂੜਿ ਮੁਠੀ ਕੂੜਿਆਰਿ॥ 2॥ ਲਾਹਾ ਅਹਿਨਿਸਿ ਨਉਤਨਾ ਪਰਖੇ ਰਤਨੁ ਵੀਚਾਰਿ॥ ਵਸਤੁ ਲਹੈ ਘਰਿ ਆਪਣੇ ਚਲੈ ਕਾਰਜੁ ਸਾਰਿ॥ ਵਣਜਾਰਿਆ ਸਿਉ ਵਣਜੁ ਕਰਿ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਬ੍ਰਹਮੁ ਬੀਚਾਰਿ॥ 3॥ ਸੰਤਾ ਸੰਗਤਿ ਪਾਈਐ ਜੇ ਮੇਲੇ ਮੇਲਣਹਾਰੁ॥ ਮਿਲਿਆ ਹੋਇ ਨ ਵਿਛੁੜੈ ਜਿਸੁ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਜੋਤਿ ਅਪਾਰ॥ ਸਚੈ ਆਸਣਿ ਸਚਿ ਰਹੈ ਸਚੈ ਪ੍ਰੇਮ ਪਿਆਰ॥ 4॥ ਜਿਨੀ ਆਪੁ ਪਛਾਣਿਆ ਘਰ ਮਹਿ ਮਹਲੁ ਸੁਥਾਇ॥ ਸਚੇ ਸੇਤੀ ਰਤਿਆ ਸਚੋ ਪਲੈ ਪਾਇ॥
