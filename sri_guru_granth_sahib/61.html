(ਅੰਗ 60)॥      ਮਨ ਰੇ ਕਿਉ ਛੂਟਹਿ ਬਿਨੁ ਪਿਆਰ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਰਵਿ ਰਹਿਆ ਬਖਸੇ ਭਗਤਿ ਭੰਡਾਰ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਰੇ ਮਨ ਐਸੀ ਹਰਿ ਸਿਉ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਿ ਕਰਿ ਜੈਸੀ ਮਛੁਲੀ ਨੀਰ॥ ਜਿਉ ਅਧਿਕਉ ਤਉ ਸੁਖੁ ਘਣੋ ਮਨਿ ਤਨਿ ਸਾਤਿ ਸਰੀਰ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਜਲ ਘੜੀ ਨ ਜੀਵਈ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਜਾਣੈ ਅਭ ਪੀਰ॥ 2॥ ਰੇ ਮਨ ਐਸੀ ਹਰਿ ਸਿਉ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਿ ਕਰਿ ਜੈਸੀ ਚਾਤ੍ਰਿਕ ਮੇਹ॥ ਸਰ ਭਰਿ ਥਲ ਹਰੀਆਵਲੇ ਇਕ ਬੂੰਦ ਨ ਪਵਈ ਕੇਹ॥ ਕਰਮਿ ਮਿਲੈ ਸੋ ਪਾਈਐ ਕਿਰਤੁ ਪਇਆ ਸਿਰਿ ਦੇਹ॥ 3॥ ਰੇ ਮਨ ਐਸੀ ਹਰਿ ਸਿਉ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਿ ਕਰਿ ਜੈਸੀ ਜਲ ਦੁਧ ਹੋਇ॥ ਆਵਟਣੁ ਆਪੇ ਖਵੈ ਦੁਧ ਕਉ ਖਪਣਿ ਨ ਦੇਇ॥ ਆਪੇ ਮੇਲਿ ਵਿਛੁੰਨਿਆ ਸਚਿ ਵਡਿਆਈ ਦੇਇ॥ 4॥ ਰੇ ਮਨ ਐਸੀ ਹਰਿ ਸਿਉ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਿ ਕਰਿ ਜੈਸੀ ਚਕਵੀ ਸੂਰ॥ਖਿਨੁ ਪਲੁ ਨੀਦ ਨ ਸੋਵਈ ਜਾਣੈ ਦੂਰਿ ਹਜੂਰਿ॥ ਮਨਮੁਖਿ ਸੋਝੀ ਨਾ ਪਵੈ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਸਦਾ ਹਜੂਰਿ॥ 5॥ ਮਨਮੁਖਿ ਗਣਤ ਗਣਾਵਣੀ ਕਰਤਾ ਕਰੇ ਸੁ ਹੋਇ॥ ਤਾ ਕੀ ਕੀਮਤਿ ਨਾ ਪਵੈ ਜੇ ਲੋਚੈ ਸਭ ਕੋਇ॥ ਗੁਰਮਤਿ ਹੋਇ ਤ ਪਾਈਐ ਸਚਿ ਮਿਲੈ ਸੁਖੁ ਹੋਇ॥ 6॥ ਸਚਾ ਨੇਹੁ ਨ ਤੁਟਈ ਜੇ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਭੇਟੈ ਸੋਇ॥ ਗਿਆਨੁ ਪਦਾਰਥੁ ਪਾਈਐ ਤ੍ਰਿਭਵਣ ਸੋਝੀ ਹੋਇ॥ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਨ ਵੀਸਰੈ ਜੇ ਗੁਣ ਕਾ ਗਾਹਕੁ ਹੋਇ॥ 7॥ ਖੇਲਿ ਗਏ ਸੇ ਪੰਖਣੂੰ ਜੋ ਚੁਗਦੇ ਸਰ ਤਲਿ॥ ਘੜੀ ਕਿ ਮੁਹਤਿ ਕਿ ਚਲਣਾ ਖੇਲਣੁ ਅਜੁ ਕਿ ਕਲਿ॥ ਜਿਸੁ ਤੂੰ ਮੇਲਹਿ ਸੋ ਮਿਲੈ ਜਾਇ ਸਚਾ ਪਿੜੁ ਮਲਿ॥8॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਗੁਰ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਿ ਨ ਊਪਜੈ ਹਉਮੈ ਮੈਲੁ ਨ ਜਾਇ॥ ਸੋਹੰ ਆਪੁ ਪਛਾਣੀਐ ਸਬਦਿ ਭੇਦਿ ਪਤੀਆਇ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਆਪੁ ਪਛਾਣੀਐ ਅਵਰ ਕਿ ਕਰੇ ਕਰਾਇ॥ 9॥ ਮਿਲਿਆ ਕਾ ਕਿਆ ਮੇਲੀਐ ਸਬਦਿ ਮਿਲੇ ਪਤੀਆਇ॥ ਮਨਮੁਖਿ ਸੋਝੀ ਨਾ ਪਵੈ ਵੀਛੁੜਿ ਚੋਟਾ ਖਾਇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਦਰੁ ਘਰੁ ਏਕੁ ਹੈ ਅਵਰੁ ਨ ਦੂਜੀ ਜਾਇ॥ 10॥11

(PAGE 60):         Mun ray kiyoun chhooteh(i) bin(u) piyaar. Gurmukh(i) untar(i) ruv(i) ruhiya, bukhshay bhugat(i) bhundaar. 1. Ruhaou. Ray mun, aisi Hur(i) siyoun preet(i) kur(i), jaisi muchhuli neer. Jiyoun udhkou tiyoun sookh(u) ghuno, mun(i) tun(i) shaant(i) sreer. Bin(u) jul ghurri na jeevuyee, Prubh(u) jaanaiy ubh peer. 2. Ray mun aisi Hur(i) siyoun preet(i) kur(i), jaisi chaatrik mayh. Sur bhur(i) thul huriyavlay, ikk boond na puvyee kayh. Kerm(i) milaiy so paayiyaiy kirt(u) puyya sir(i) dayh. 3. Ray mun aisi Hur(i) siyoun preet(i) kur(i) jaisi jul doodh hoyay. Aavtun(u) aap khuvaiy, doodh kuou khupan(i) na dayay. Aapay mayl(i) vichhooniyaan, Sach(i) vudiyaayee dayay. 4. Ray mun aisi Hur(i) siyoun preet(i) kur(i), jaisi chukvee soor. Khin(u) pul(u) neend na sovuyee, jaaniay door(i) hujoor(i). Munmukh(i) sojhi na puvaiy Gurmukh(i) suda hujoor(i). 5. Bin Gur preet(i) na oopjaiy haumein maiyl(u) na jaayay. Sohun(g) aap(u) puchhaniyaiy, Shabad(i) bhayd(i) putiyayay. Gurmukh(i) aap(u) puchhaaniyaiy uver k(i) kuray kraayay. 9. Miliya ka kiya mayliyaiy, Shabad(i) milay putiyayay. Munmukh(i) sojhi na puvaiy, veechhurr(i) chotaan khaayay. Nanak dur(u) ghur(u) aik(u) haiy, uver(u) na dooji jaayay. 10.11.

Shaant(i) = peaceful (satisfied).  Ghurri = 24 minutes; a short period of time.   Ubh = heart.   Peer = pain.     2.
Chaatrik = rainbird; a bird that is believed to drink the first drop of rain water which falls during a mythical Swanti sub-planet.    Mayh = rain; cloud.   Sur = bodies of water like lakes, ponds etc.   Bhur(i) = full.  Thul = ground.   Kayh = to what use?   Kirt(i)  Puyya = pre=written destiny.   3.         Aavtun(u) = boiling.    Khuvaiy = endures.   Khupan = suffer.   Vichhooniyaan = the separated.     Vudiyayee = honour.  4.

So chukvee waits all night for the sun to rise and keeps calling her companion throughout the night.    Soor = sun.  Khin(u) Pul(u) = a single moment. Hudoor(i) = Hujoor(i) = present close by.   Sojhi = understanding.    5.

MEANING:  One cannot break the bonds of Maya without the love for God, oh my mind. By Guru’s grace, Waheguru takes residence in one’s heart and is present there all the time. The Guru confers the treasures of Waheguru’s worship on the devotee.      Pause.
So, love God just as the lotus flower loves water; even though the flower is hit by the water waves, it thrives even more.  Other living beings, which have been created by God to live in water, die without it.                1.              Love Waheguru the same way as the fish love water.  The more water there is, the more comfort the fish get, and their mind and body feel at peace. They do not survive without water for even a moment.  So, one should love Waheguru, who knows the pain of our heart (provides comfort) the same way (as fish does for water), oh my mind.          2.             Love Waheguru the same way as the rainbird loves the rain drop during the Swanti sub-planet.  Even though all the water bodies may be full to the brim, and the land may be green due to rain, all these waters mean nothing to the rainbird if he does not get that particular raindrop. But everybody is limited by the consequences of his deeds in previous lives; one only receives that which is given to him by the kindness of Waheguru. (Even union with Him depends on His kindness).             3.           Love God the same way as water loves milk, oh my mind.  Water endures the heat of boiling and protects milk from the heat (water boils off while the milk stays behind).  (Like water, if someone yearns to connect with God), Waheguru Himself unites such separated individuals with Him and honours them.     4. 
Love Waheguru the same way as the Chukvee loves the sun, oh my mind.  She cannot sleep even for a moment and keeps calling her companion all night, thinking he is far away, even though he is still close by. She loves the sunlight and waits for the sun to rise.  Likewise, the follower of their mind thinks God to be somewhere far away, while for a Gurmukh, Waheguru is always around him.           5.         The followers of their mind try to be included among the religious people by showing off, but only what the Creator does (or makes people do) happens. However, nobody can find the limits of God’s qualities - no matter how hard they try or wish for that.  But when one comes to Guru’s shelter, he can appreciate Waheguru’s qualities and enjoy bliss by connecting with Him.            6.            If God connects one with the Guru, his true affection for Waheguru never fades. (In Guru’s shelter), One acquires the invaluable Divine Knowledge (connection with Waheguru) and realizes that Waheguru is present in the entire three-layered universe. When one begins to deal in the Divine qualities by taking the Guru’s shelter, he never forgets the holy Naam of Waheguru.      7.
The birds which used to pick food by the pool of water have completed their enjoyable games (people have completed their lives and are dead).  Everybody has to leave this world shortly – maybe today or tomorrow – but only those who are connected with Waheguru are connected with Him through His own kindness.  Such lucky people leave this world achieving the true objective of life (union with Waheguru).            8.           Love for Waheguru does not develop in people’s mind, and the filth of selfishness does not wash away, until they come to Guru’s shelter, whereby their mind is pierced with Guru’s Shabad, and they are convinced about the existence of God (and love for Him), and one recognizes self, and realizes that he is a part of Waheguru.  One recognizes oneself only by taking the Guru’s shelter; nobody can recognize oneself on their own, or with help from others.               9.
Those who are convinced by the Guru’s Shabad about Waheguru become connected to Him and do not need to be united again because they never get separated from Him. But the followers of their minds do not attain enlightenment about Waheguru and remain separated from Him, and as a result, they suffer greatly. There is only one door and one support – Waheguru –, and there is no other place in the universe for human support, oh Nanak.    10.11.
