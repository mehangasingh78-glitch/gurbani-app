Word Meaning:  Bherm(i) Bhoolaayay = misguided by internal conflicts.   Kerm = deeds (tasks).  Boojhay = understand; solve riddle.   Birtha = uselessly.   Chaanun = light.      Kerm(i) = benevolence.     1.      Sehj(i) = mental state above the effects of Maya.      Pause.

Daiynt Poot(u) = son of a demon (king Hernakush or Hernkushyup) = Prehlad.  Prehlad was a devotee of God and recited His Naam constantly. On the other hand, his father, Hernakush, who had acquired some supernatural powers with the blessing of a god, wanted his subjects to worship him alone. So, he was gravely offended by his son’s worship of God. He first tried to persuade Prehlad to desist from worshipping God, but when Prehlad refused to comply, the king tried to punish him in many different ways, even to kill him; but nothing worked, and Prehlad continued to recite God’s Naam. Finally, Henakush tried to kill his son, himself. As he was about to attack Prehlad with a sword, a lion-headed god with a human body appeared, and tore Hernakush apart with his lion-like, sharp paws, and saved Prehlad.

MEANING:  If you want to enjoy comfort, recite Waheguru’s Naam, oh my mind. Praise the Perfect Guru, and you will realize Waheguru in the higher spiritual state above the effects of Maya.        Pause.          One needs to realize that Waheguru Himself has created attraction for Maya in the mind of humans, thereby misguiding them (from the path of righteousness) by creating doubts. The follower of his mind does not realize it and performs deeds (under the influence of Maya) and wastes away his precious human life.  Gurbani is the real light (to guide the world) in this darkness (of ignorance) in the world, and takes residence in one’s heart (enlightens his mind) by the grace of the Guru.         1.       When one connects with God’s Feet (His Naam), all his doubts are cleared, and he becomes fearless (of death and of any other suffering).  When a devotee acts on the Guru’s advice, Waheguru takes residence in his mind, and the devotee becomes “one” with Him, and his mind finds peace; no fear of death comes near him.          2.          (God loves his devotees no matter what social status they have in society).  Remember well-known Bhagats Naam Dev Ji, and Kabir Ji - Naam Dev Ji was a washerman, while Kabir Ji was a weaver (both were from low classes in the Indian system of castes) – but both achieved the spiritual state above the effects of Maya by taking shelter of the Guru. They acquired Divine Knowledge (got connected with Waheguru) through the Guru’s Shabad, and destroyed (overcame) their haumein.  Now, humans and even gods sing their Bani, and nobody can erase this honour of theirs.        3.         Prehlad, the son of the demon king Hernaksh, did not know any prevailing religious rituals or practices, or even ways to control sensual desires, but did not have love for anyone else (Maya).   But when he got connected to the Guru, his mind became pure, and he recited Waheguru’s Naam day and night.  He recited only One (Naam), and realized the only One (God), and acknowledged nobody other than God.           4.         With the guidance of the Guru, all six main faiths of ascetics, the Yogis, and the Sanyasis (who leave their homes in search of God), are misled in their pursuit of God. Like anyone else, they can achieve a higher spiritual level by serving the Guru (acting on His Advice), thereby making their heart a place for Waheguru to reside. Thus, they imbibe the True Bani of the Guru and are able to break the cycle of rebirth.        5.
Without the guidance of the Guru, the pundits study the Vedas with a view to taking part in religious debates.  This approach does not result in any enhancement of their spirituality; rather, they suffer from their haumein and go through the cycle of 8.4 million life forms. They cannot break the bonds of Maya (and thus cannot get out of the rebirth cycle) without the Guru’s Shabad. With Guru’s kindness, when they get connected with Waheguru, they keep Him in mind all the time and attain a high spiritual state.          6. 
With love for the Guru, when one gets connected with Him in the company of the devotees, Waheguru’s Naam takes root in his heart.  I will lead my life according to the Guru’s advice, and will offer my body and mind to the Guru.  I am a sacrifice to my Guru who connects my mind with Waheguru, oh Nanak.        7.           Only he deserves to be called a Brahman who gets connected with Braham (God), and is imbued in His Naam.  Waheguru is close by and resides in everybody’s heart, but a rare person realizes Him.  One is honoured if he bears Waheguru Naam in his heart by acting on Guru’s Shabad, oh Nanak.   8. 5. 22

MEANING:  Without the shelter of the Guru, one cannot attain the high spiritual level (Sehj) where Maya becomes ineffective, oh my friends. Guru’s Shabad enables one to attain the Sehj stage and have union with Waheguru.        Pause.
Everybody longs to attain the Sehj stage, but it is not achievable without taking shelter of the Guru.  The Pundits and the fortune tellers exhaust themselves reading their holy books (without any success). Likewise, the ascetics of different faiths are misguided with belief in the symbols of their faiths (as they believe that just wearing the symbols makes them holy). When Waheguru is kind to someone, He connects the individual with the Guru on His own Will, whereby the individual can attain the Sehj state by acting on the advice of the Guru.      1.
Singing His praise is recognized by Waheguru only if it is done in the Sehj state; otherwise, the simple uttering of words of praise is worthless (as it is only ritualistic).  The real worship of Waheguru arises from the Sehj state when an individual becomes indifferent to Maya and fosters love for God.  Also, one attains comfort and peace only in the Sehj state, and without it, life goes to waste.            2.              One can meditate with full devotion to Waheguru in the Sehj state and sing His praise.  Waheguru’s noble qualities are sung (to be approved by Him) in the Sehj state only, and full concentration (on His Naam) is also attained only in the Sehj state. (In the Sehj state) man’s tongue relishes the taste of Waheguru’s Naam, and Waheguru takes residence in the devotee’s heart.         3.
One attains the Sehj state by taking shelter of the Guru, and his fear of death (or of rebirth) is dispelled in that state.  One performs noble deeds (as per the Guru's guidance) in the Sehj state, as Waheguru makes one's heart His residence.  They are fortunate who realize Waheguru in the Sehj state, as they remain merged with Him.         4.
The Sehj state cannot be attained as long as one is attached to Maya, because attachment with Maya is contrary to the Guru’s Teachings, and takes one farther away from Waheguru.  The deeds of a Maya-gripped man revolve around his haumein, as a result of which, he suffers in its fire; his rebirth cycle never ends, and he takes births again, and again.       5.
As long as a man is under the influence of the three effects of Maya (Tumo, Rujo, and Suto), he cannot attain the Sehj state, because the three effects misguide the man, and he is unsure about the right path (for union with God). What can a man read, ponder on, or describe anything (about Waheguru), when he has missed the opportunity right from the beginning (to serve Waheguru)?  As far as the Sehj state (also called the fourth level of spirituality) is concerned, one attains it by rising above the three effects of Maya, in the shelter of the Guru.        6.               When one realizes that the Naam of the formless Waheguru is the source of all comforts, (he works hard to connect with Him), and rises above the three effects of Maya to attain the Sehj state.  The bearers of noble qualities (true devotees) praise the eternal Waheguru, and earn eternal praise for themselves as they become “one” with Him. When the misguided lucky individuals connect with the Guru’s Shabad, they attain the Sehj state and become “one” with Waheguru.            7.            Without attaining the Sehj state, there is ignorance all around because Maya creates a wall of complete darkness (between the man and God). By following on Guru’s Shabad, one attains the Sehj state, and understands (connects with) the incomprehensible Waheguru.  The Creator Waheguru’s kindness connects the individual with Him through the Perfect Guru.       8.
The invisible light of the fearless and formless Waheguru is realized in the Sehj state. There is only one Provider for everybody who connects individuals (who are a part of His Light) with Himself (the source of all Light).  The incomprehensible Waheguru can be praised by the Shabad of the Perfect Guru.            9.           The true wealth of a devotee is Waheguru’s Naam, and he deals in it in the Sehj state. He earns the benefit (wealth) to recite Waheguru’s Naam day and night, of which Waheguru has inexhaustible treasures.  There is no shortage of Waheguru’s Naam, of which the Provider has His treasures full, oh Nanak.  10.6. 23.
