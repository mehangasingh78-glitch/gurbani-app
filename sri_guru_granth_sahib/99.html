Majh Mehla 5:  Jit(u) ghur(i) Pir(i) sohaag(u) bunaaya. Tit(u) ghur(i) sukhiyay mungal(u) gaaya. Anund binoad titaiy ghur(i) sohay, jo dhun kunt(i) shingaari jeeo. 1. Sa goonvunti, sa vudbhaagun.  Pootarvunti, sealvunt(i) sohaagun(i). Roopvunt(i) sa sooghurr(i) bichukkhun, jo dhun kunt piyari jeeo.  2. Achaarvunt(i) sayee purdhanay. Subh shingaar bunay tis(u) Giyanay. Sa koolvunti sa subhrayee, jo Pir(i) kaiy rung(i) suvaari jeeo.  3.  Mehma tis kee kehn(u) na jaayay. Jo Pir(i) mayl(i) luyyee ung(i) laayay. 
(PAGE 98):      Thir(u) soohaag(u), Vur(u) Agumm(u) Agochar(u), jun Nanak praym sadhaari jeeo.    4.4.11
Word Meaning:   Jit(u) = in which.   Ghur(i) = heart.   Pir(i) = husband (Waheguru).   Sohaag(u) = (performed the) marriage (got connected with the individual); enlightened the heart.   Sukhiyay = oh friend!  Mungal Gaaya = sang songs of happiness (became blissful).   Anund = bliss.   Binoad = enjoyment.   Titaiy = in that. Sohay = look very good.   Dhun = human bride.  Kunt(i) = husband (Waheguru).    Shingaari = beautified; adorned.     1. Goonvunti = having good qualities.   Sealvunt(i) = calm; sweet-natured.   Sohaagun = beloved.   Roopvunt(i) = pretty.    Sooghurr(i) = wise.    Bichukkhun = wise; a leaned person.     2. Achaarvunt(i) = having good manners; cultured.   Purdhanay = leader.   Shingaar = adornments.    Giyaanay = due to (Divine) knowledge.     Koolvunti = of good clan.   Subhrayee = having good brothers.    Rung(i) = in love.        3.      Mehma = praise.    Ung(i) = with heart.   Thir(u) = eternal.   Vur(u) = husband.  Agumm = (physically) unreachable.  Agochar(u) = incomprehensible.  Sadhaari = having support.       4.
MEANING:  The heart, which has been enlightened by Waheguru, feels as if the music of divine happiness is playing in it, oh my friend.  Such a heart, which has been adorned (with noble qualities) by Waheguru-husband, enjoys bliss and happiness.         1. 

That human bride is very lucky, acquires all the virtues, and is regarded highly in society like a woman who has many sons (has enlightened many others), is wise, and a learned person, and is loved by her husband.  She is considered gorgeous, good-hearted, wise, and beloved by her husband.          2.          She is considered cultured and a leader.  All these adornments are due to her Divine Knowledge.  The human bride, adorned with her husband's (Waheguru's) love, is considered to belong to a good clan and to have many brothers (which is a great support).         3.         The praise of the human bride, which has been connected to her husband (Waheguru) by heart, is beyond description.  She has an eternal husband who is physically inaccessible and incomprehensible, and she always has His support, oh Nanak.  4.4.11.
    
ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਖੋਜਤ ਖੋਜਤ ਦਰਸਨ ਚਾਹੇ॥ ਭਾਤਿ ਭਾਤਿ ਬਨ ਬਨ ਅਵਗਾਹੇ॥ ਨਿਰਗੁਣੁ ਸਰਗੁਣੁ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਮੇਰਾ ਕੋਈ ਹੈ ਜੋ ਆਣਿ ਮਿਲਾਵੈ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਖਟੁ ਸਾਸਤ ਬਿਚਰਤ ਮੁਖਿ ਗਿਆਨਾ॥ ਪੂਜਾ ਤਿਲਕੁ ਤੀਰਥ ਇਸਨਾਨਾ॥ ਨਿਵਲੀ ਕਰਮ ਆਸਨ ਚਉਰਾਸੀਹ ਇਨ ਮਹਿ ਸਾਤਿ ਨ ਆਵੈ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਅਨਿਕ ਬਰਖ ਕੀਏ ਜਪ ਤਾਪਾ॥ ਗਵਨੁ ਕੀਆ ਧਰਤੀ ਭਰਮਾਤਾ॥ ਇਕੁ ਖਿਨੁ ਹਿਰਦੈ ਸਾਤਿ ਨ ਆਵੈ ਜੋਗੀ ਬਹੁੜਿ ਬਹੁੜਿ ਉਠਿ ਧਾਵੈ ਜੀਉ॥ 3॥ ਕਰਿ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਮੋਹਿ ਸਾਧੁ ਮਿਲਾਇਆ॥ ਮਨੁ ਤਨੁ ਸੀਤਲੁ ਧੀਰਜੁ ਪਾਇਆ॥ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਅਬਿਨਾਸੀ ਬਸਿਆ ਘਟ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਮੰਗਲੁ ਨਾਨਕੁ ਗਾਵੈ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥5॥12॥
Majh Mehla 5:  Khojut khojut dershun chaahay. Bhaant(i) bhaant(i) bun bun uvgaahay. Nirgoon(u) Surgoon(u) Hur(i) Hur(i) mayra, koyee haiy jo aan(i) milaavaiy jeeo. 1. Khut(u) Shast bichrut mookh(i) giyaana. Pooja tiluk(u) teeruth ishnaana. Nivli kerm aasun chaurasee, in meh(i) shaant(i) na aavaiy jeeo. 2. Anik burkh keeyay jup taapa. Guvun(u) keeya dherti bhurmaata. Ikk(u) khin(u) hirdaiy shaant(i) na aavaiy, yogi buhoorr(i) buhoorr(i) ootth(i) dhaavaiy jeeo.  3. Ker(i) kirpa moh(i) Sadh(u) milaaya. Mun(u) tun(u) seetul(u) dheeruj(u) paaya. Prubh Abinashi busiya ghut untar(i), Hur(i) mungul(u) Nanak(u) gaavaiy jeeo.   4.5.12 
Word Meaning:  Khojut Khojut = searching for.    Bhaant(i) Bhaant(i) = of many different kinds.       Uvgaahay = treaded.   Nirgoon(u) = formless.    Surgoon(u) = present in nature (the entire visible universe is His Surgoon form).     1.
Khut(u) Shast = the six Shastras of the Hindus.   Bichrut = ponder on.   Tiluk = anointment.   Nivli kerm = an exercise of yoga.    Aasun Chaurasee = 84 different sitting positions in yoga.  Shaant(i) = peace.     2.    Jup Taapa = Hindu rituals of reciting mantras and of self-torture.  Guvun(u) = travel; journey.  Khin(u) = moment.    Buhoorr(i) Buhoorr(i) =   again and again.  Dhaavay = rushes to.     3.      Sadh(u) = Guru.   Seetul = peaceful; calm.   Dheeruj(u)  = patience.   Abinashi =         non-perishable; eternal.    Ghut = heart.    Mungal(u) = song of happiness.     4.
MEANING:  Many people tread many different forests in search of Waheguru for His holy sight.  (I am also searching for the same Waheguru), Is there someone who can connect me with the formless Waheguru, which is otherwise visible in His creation (the whole universe)?   1.  Some (Pundits) ponder on the six Shastras, and deliver their knowledge by explaining to people. Besides, they also anoint their foreheads, worship idols, and travel to their holy places.  But like the yogis, who perform their yogic exercises and sit in eighty-four different positions to meditate, they never find peace of mind.        2..
Some others recite mantras and torture themselves to please gods for years, while some others travel around the world in search of God, but do not find mental peace even for a moment, and then again run to do the same.          3.
But Waheguru was benevolent to me, connecting me with the Guru, as a result of which my mind and body have become peaceful, and I have acquired patience (no evil thoughts bother me anymore).  The eternal Waheguru has taken residence in my heart, and my heart is singing the song of happiness (I feel blissful), oh Nanak. 4.5.12.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਪਾਰਬ੍ਰਹਮ ਅਪਰੰਪਰ ਦੇਵਾ॥ ਅਗਮ ਅਗੋਚਰ ਅਲਖ ਅਭੇਵਾ॥ ਦੀਨ ਦਇਆਲ ਗੋਪਾਲ ਗੋਬਿੰਦਾ ਹਰਿ ਧਿਆਵਹੁ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਗਾਤੀ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਮਧਸੂਦਨ ਨਿਸਤਾਰੇ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਸੰਗੀ ਕ੍ਰਿਸਨ ਮੁਰਾਰੇ॥ ਦਇਆਲ ਦਮੋਦਰ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਪਾਈਐ ਹੋਰਤ ਕਿਤੈ ਨ ਭਾਤੀ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਨਿਰਹਾਰੀ ਕੇਸਵ ਨਿਰਵੈਰਾ॥ ਕੋਟਿ ਜਨਾ ਜਾ ਕੈ ਪੂਜੈ ਪੈਰਾ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਹਿਰਦੇ ਜਾ ਕੈ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਸੋਈ ਭਗਤੁ ਇਕਾਤੀ ਜੀਉ॥ 3॥ ਅਮੋਘ ਦਰਸਨ ਬੇਅੰਤ ਅਪਾਰਾ॥ ਵਡ ਸਮਰਥ ਸਦਾ ਦਾਤਾਰਾ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਜਪੀਐ ਤਿਤੁ ਤਰੀਐ ਗਤਿ ਨਾਨਕ ਵਿਰਲੀ ਜਾਤੀ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥6॥13॥
Majh Mehla 5:  Parbraham Uprumpur Dayva.  Agumm Agochar Alukh Abhayva.  Deen Duyaal Gopal Gobinda, Hur(i) dhiyavhou Gurmukh(i) gaati jeeo.  1. Gurmukh(i) Mudhsoodun nistaaray. Gurmukh(i) sungi Krishan Mooraray. Duyaal Damodar Gurmukh(i) paayiyaiy, horut kitaiy na bhaanti jeeo. 2.  Nirhaari, Kayshav Nirvaira.  Kot(i) junaa ja kaiy poojaiy paiyra. Gurmukh(i) hirday ja kaiy Hur(i) Hur(i), soyee bhugat(u) ikaati jeeo.  3.  Amogh dershun bayunt apaara. Vud Sumruth suda Dataara.  Gurmukh(i) Naam(u) jupiyaiy tit(u) turiyaiy, gut(i) Nanak virleen jaati jeeo. 4
Word Meaning:  Uprumpur = limitless.    Dayva = source of light.    Agumm = physically inaccessible.   Alukh = which cannot be understood (described).    Abhayva = whose secrets are not known.      Deen = powerless.    Gopal = Gobind, which nourishes the earth; (Go = earth).   Gaati = higher spiritual state.       1.     Mudgsoodun = Waheguru.   Nistaaray = salvaged.    Sungi = companion.   Diyaal = kind.    Damodar = Waheguru (another name for Krishna).   Horut = other.   Bhaanti = way; means.         2.             Nirhaari = which does not need food.           Kayshav = having long hair (Waheguru).           Kot(i) = millions.        3.
Amogh = which never fails (which fulfills all wishes).    Bayunt = Apaara = limitless.    Sumruth = fully capable.   Dataara = provider.   Tit(u) = by which.  Virleen = some rare person.    4.
MEANING:  Waheguru is limitless and is the source of enlightenment. He is beyond our physical reach, incomprehensible, indescribable, and unknown in terms of His deep secrets.  The nourisher of all living beings is very kind to the powerless (humble); let us remember Him at all times, in the shelter of the Guru, to enhance our spiritual level, oh my friends!           1.
Waheguru salvages (protects from evil attractions) all the devotees of the Guru, and He becomes a permanent companion of the Guru’s devotees. The only way to realize the kind Waheguru is to recite His Naam in the shelter of the Guru; there is no other way (to realize Him).         2.         The long-haired Waheguru does not need any food; He does not bear any enmity with anyone, and countless people worship His feet (Him). Taking Guru’s shelter, a person who bears Waheguru’s Naam in his heart, is the real devotee of only a single power – Waheguru.        3.         The limitless Waheguru’s sight results in the fulfillment of all one’s wishes as He is capable of achieving any and everything.  He is a great provider.  We should recite His Naam in the shelter of the Guru by which we can swim across the sea of Maya (find salvation).  But some rare devotee attains that level of spirituality, oh Nanak.    4.6.13. 
NOTE: Since Waheguru is formless, He cannot have long hair, nor can we see Him with our physical eyes. These are just expressions of love and faith, or the flight of ideas, of Guru Sahib as a poet.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਕਹਿਆ ਕਰਣਾ ਦਿਤਾ ਲੈਣਾ॥ ਗਰੀਬਾ ਅਨਾਥਾ ਤੇਰਾ ਮਾਣਾ॥ ਸਭ ਕਿਛੁ ਤੂੰ ਹੈ ਤੂੰ ਹੈ ਮੇਰੇ ਪਿਆਰੇ ਤੇਰੀ ਕੁਦਰਤਿ ਕਉ ਬਲਿ ਜਾਈ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਭਾਣੈ ਉਝੜ ਭਾਣੈ ਰਾਹਾ॥ ਭਾਣੈ ਹਰਿ ਗੁਣ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਗਾਵਾਹਾ॥ ਭਾਣੈ ਭਰਮਿ ਭਵੈ ਬਹੁ ਜੂਨੀ ਸਭ ਕਿਛੁ ਤਿਸੈ ਰਜਾਈ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਨਾ ਕੋ ਮੂਰਖ ਨ ਕੋ ਸਿਆਣਾ॥ ਵਰਤੈ ਸਭ ਕਿਛੁ ਤੇਰਾ ਭਾਣਾ॥ ਅਗਮ ਅਗੋਚਰ ਬੇਅੰਤ ਅਥਾਹਾ ਤੇਰੀ ਕੀਮਤਿ ਕਹਣੁ ਨ ਜਾਈ ਜੀਉ॥ 3॥ ਖਾਕੁ ਸੰਤਨ ਕੀ ਦੇਹੁ ਪਿਆਰੇ॥ ਆਇ ਪਇਆ ਹਰਿ ਤੇਰੈ ਦੁਆਰੈ॥ ਦਰਸਨੁ ਪੇਖਤ ਮਨੁ ਆਘਾਵੈ ਨਾਨਕ ਮਿਲਣੁ ਸੁਭਾਈ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥7॥14॥
Majh Mehla 5:  Kuhhya kerna ditta laiyna.  Greebaan anathaan Tayra maana. Sub kichh(u) Tu haiyn, Tu haiyn, mayray piyaaray, Tayri koodrut(i) kuou bul(i) jaayeen jeeo. 1. Bhaanaiy oojhurr bhaanaiy raha. Bhaanaiy Hur(i) goon Gurmukh(i) gavaaha. Bhaanaiy bherm(i) bhuvaiy buhou jooni,  subh kichh(u) Tisaiy rujayee jeeo. 2.  Na ko moorukh, na ko siyaana. Vertaiy subh kichh(u) Tayra bhaana.  Agumm Agoachur Bayunt Athaaha, Tayri keemut(i) kehn(u) na jaayee jeeo. 3.  Khaak(u) suntan kee dayhou Piyaaray.  Aayay puyya Hur(i) Tayraiy dooaaraiy.  Dershun(u) paykhut mun(u) aghaavaiy, Nanak milun(u) soobhaayee jeeo.  4.7.14. 
Word Meaning:  Kuhhiya = whatever (we are) told (ordered).    Anathaan = orphans (without any support).   Koodrut(i) = nature; magnanimity.    Bul(i) = offer life.   1.
Bhaanaiy = as per (Waheguru’s) wish.   Oojhurr = misled from the path; directionless. 
Bherm(i) = misled; no clear direction.  Bhuvaiy = travels (goes) through.   Buhou Jooni = many life forms.    Tisaiy = His (Waheguru’s).    Rujayee = Will.  2.
Vertaiy = operates; happens.   Agumm = (physically) inaccessible. Agochur = incomprehensible.   Athaaha = unfathomable.    Keemut(i) = value; magnanimity.     3.
Khaak(u) = dust (of feet); humbleness.   Paykhut = on seeing.   Aghaavaiy = feels contented.   Milun(u) = union.    Soobhaayee = with noble intentions.     4.
MEANING:  Living beings obey Your Huqam (Will), and receive whatever You provide, oh Waheguru!  (The rich and powerful may use unfair means to get what they want), But the poor and the powerless take pride only in You (You are their sole support). You are everything for us, oh my dear Waheguru! I gladly offer my life for sacrifice for Your magnanimity.      1.
As per Your Will, some people are misled from the path of righteousness, while some others tread the right path.  As per Your will, You make some people sing Your praise in Guru’s shelter.  Again, it is Your Will that some people are misled by the attractions of Maya, and suffer rebirth in many life forms.           2.          There is no foolish or wise individual by his choice; all that happens is Your Will.  You are incomprehensible, physically unreachable, limitless, and unfathomable; nobody can describe Your worth (magnanimity).        3.
I have come to Your door, and wish to have the dust of the holy people to put on my forehead (obey their Huqam like a slave).  The “sight” of Waheguru causes complete contentment as far as the worldly attractions are concerned, but such sights happen only to the devotees who have noble intentions in their mind for Waheguru.     4. 7.14.
