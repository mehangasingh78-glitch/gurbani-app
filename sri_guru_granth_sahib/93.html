(ਅੰਗ 92)॥  ਐਸਾ ਤੈ ਜਗੁ ਭਰਮਿ ਲਾਇਆ॥ ਕੈਸੇ ਬੂਝੈ ਜਬ ਮੋਹਿਆ ਹੈ ਮਾਇਆ ॥1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਕਹਤ ਕਬੀਰ ਛੋਡਿ ਬਿਖਿਆ ਰਸ ਇਤੁ ਸੰਗਤਿ ਨਿਹਚਉ ਮਰਣਾ॥ ਰਮਈਆ ਜਪਹੁ ਪ੍ਰਾਣੀ ਅਨਤ ਜੀਵਨ ਬਾਣੀ ਇਨਿ ਬਿਧਿ ਭਵ ਸਾਗਰੁ ਤਰਣਾ॥ 2॥ ਜਾ ਤਿਸੁ ਭਾਵੈ ਤਾ ਲਾਗੈ ਭਾਉ॥ ਭਰਮੁ ਭੁਲਾਵਾ ਵਿਚਹੁ ਜਾਇ॥ ਉਪਜੈ ਸਹਜੁ ਗਿਆਨ ਮਤਿ ਜਾਗੈ॥ ਗੁਰ ਪ੍ਰਸਾਦਿ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਲਿਵ ਲਾਗੈ॥ 3॥ ਇਤੁ ਸੰਗਤਿ ਨਾਹੀ ਮਰਣਾ॥ ਹੁਕਮੁ ਪਛਾਣਿ ਤਾ ਖਸਮੈ ਮਿਲਣਾ॥1॥ ਰਹਾਉ ਦੂਜਾ॥
Jun-ni jaanut soot(u) buda hoat(u) haiy, itna ku na jaanaiy j(i) din din avdh ghutut(u) haiy. Moar moar kur(i), adhik laad(u) dhur(i), paykhut hee Jumrao husaiy.  1. 
(PAGE 92):                               Aisa taiyn jug(u) bherm(i) laaya.  Kaisay boojhaiy, jub mohiya hai Maya? 1. Ruhaou. Keht Kabir chhoad(i) bikhiya rus, it(u) sungut(i) nihchou merna. Rummuyiya jup-hou praani, anunt jeevun bani, in(i) bidh(i) bhuv saagur(u) terna. 2.  Ja Tis(u) bhaavaiy ta laagaiy bhaaou. Bherm(u) bhoolaava vichoun jaayay. Oopjaiy sehj(u) Giyan, mut(i) jaagaiy. Gur Persad(i) untar(i) liv laagaiy. 3.  It(u) sungut(i) naahi merna. Huqam puchhaan(i) ta Khusmaiy milna.  Pause 2.
Word Meaning:  Aik Sooaan Kaiy Ghur(i) Gaavna = We are advised by Guru Arjun Sahib to sing this Shabad in the same tune as Guru Nanak Sahib’s Shabad in this Raag(u): Aik sooaan doyay sooaani naal(i) page 24.  
Jun-ni = mother.   Soot(u) = son.  Avdh = age (life).   Moar = mine.   Paykhut = on seeing.   Jumraou = the powerful Angel of death.        1.           Bherm(i) = doubts; misbelief.  Mohiya = enchanted; deceived.   Bikhiya = poison; Maya.       Pause.
Nihchou = for sure.  Rummuyiya = pervading the universe (Waheguru).   Anunt Jeevun = immortal.   Bidh(i) = way.       Bhuv Saagur = the world sea (attractions of Maya which cause the man to be born again, and again).        2.      Bhoolaava = misleading.   Sehj(u) = spiritual state where the mind does not wander.    Liv = full concentration.     3.
MEANING: The mother thinks that her son is growing big, but she does not realize that his life span is actually getting shorter by the day. She loves him a lot by saying, “he is mine”, but, watching all this, the Angel of death smiles (sarcastically).        1.
This is how You have led the world to misbelieve the reality, oh God!  But how can the world realize the fact when it is under the spell of Maya?          Pause.
Quit enjoying the tastes of poisonous Maya, because you will experience spiritual death for sure in their company (while enjoying them), oh man, says Kabir. Worship Waheguru, which is pervading the whole universe, for immortal life, because this is the way to swim across the sea of Maya, and being “one” with Him, oh man.           2.
However, man’s love for God is possible only when He wishes it. (With love for Waheguru) A man clears his doubts and misbeliefs. His wisdom (mind) regarding Divine Knowledge is enlightened, and he attains a high spiritual state in which Maya has no effect. By Guru’s grace, he connects with God with full concentration.          3.
Following this path, one does not experience spiritual death as one recognizes Waheguru’s Will and becomes “one” with Him.  Second pause. 4.1.

                                                    ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਤ੍ਰਿਲੋਚਨ ਕਾ
                                  Siri Raag(u) of Tirlochan Ka
ਮਾਇਆ ਮੋਹੁ ਮਨਿ ਆਗਲੜਾ ਪ੍ਰਾਣੀ ਜਰਾ ਮਰਣੁ ਭਉ ਵਿਸਰਿ ਗਇਆ॥ ਕੁਟੰਬੁ ਦੇਖਿ ਬਿਗਸਹਿ ਕਮਲਾ ਜਿਉ ਪਰ ਘਰਿ ਜੋਹਹਿ ਕਪਟ ਨਰਾ॥ 1॥ ਦੂੜਾ ਆਇਓਹਿ ਜਮਹਿ ਤਣਾ॥ ਤਿਨ ਆਗਲੜੈ ਮੈ ਰਹਣੁ ਨ ਜਾਇ॥ ਕੋਈ ਕੋਈ ਸਾਜਣੁ ਆਇ ਕਹੈ॥ ਮਿਲੁ ਮੇਰੇ ਬੀਠੁਲਾ ਮੈ ਬਾਹੜੀ ਵਲਾਇ॥ਮਿਲੁ ਮੇਰੇ ਰਮਈਆ ਮੈ ਲੇਹਿ ਛਡਾਇ॥1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਅਨਿਕ ਅਨਿਕ ਭੋਗ ਰਾਜ ਬਿਸਰੇ ਪ੍ਰਾਣੀ ਸੰਸਾਰ ਸਾਗਰ ਪੈ ਅਮਰੁ ਭਇਆ॥ ਮਾਇਆ ਮੂਠਾ ਚੇਤਸਿ ਨਾਹੀ ਜਨਮੁ ਗਵਾਇਓ ਆਲਸੀਆ॥ 2॥ ਬਿਖਮ ਘੋਰ ਪੰਥਿ ਚਾਲਣਾ ਪ੍ਰਾਣੀ ਰਵਿ ਸਸਿ ਤਹ ਨ ਪ੍ਰਵੇਸੰ॥ ਮਾਇਆ ਮੋਹੁ ਤਬ ਬਿਸਰਿ ਗਇਆ ਜਾ ਤਜੀਅਲੇ ਸੰਸਾਰੰ॥ 3॥ ਆਜੁ ਮੇਰੈ ਮਨਿ ਪ੍ਰਗਟੁ ਭਇਆ ਹੈ ਪੇਖੀਅਲੇ ਧਰਮਰਾਓ॥ ਤਹ ਕਰ ਦਲ ਕਰਨਿ ਮਹਾਬਲੀ ਤਿਨ ਆਗਲੜੈ ਮੈ ਰਹਣੁ ਨ ਜਾਇ॥ 4॥ ਜੇ ਕੋ ਮੂੰ ਉਪਦੇਸੁ ਕਰਤੁ ਹੈ ਤਾ ਵਣਿ ਤ੍ਰਿਣਿ ਰਤੜਾ ਨਾਰਾਇਣਾ॥ ਐ ਜੀ ਤੂੰ ਆਪੇ ਸਭ ਕਿਛੁ ਜਾਣਦਾ ਬਦਤਿ ਤ੍ਰਿਲੋਚਨੁ ਰਮਈਆ॥ 5॥2॥
                                  
Maya moh(u) mun(i) aaglurra praani, juraa mern(u) bhuou vissur(i) guyya.  Kootumb(u) daykh(i) bigseh(i) kumla jiyoun, pur ghur(I) johay kupt nura. 1. Doorra aayo jumeh(i) tuna. Tin aaglurraiy mein rehn(u) na jaayay. Koyee, koyee saajun aayay kuhaiy. Mil(u) mayray Beetthula mein baanhrri vulaayay.  Mil(u) mayray Rummuyiya, mein layh(i) chhudaayay. 1. Ruhaou.  Anik, anik bhog raaj bisray praani, sunsaar saagur paiy amar(u) bhuyya. Maya moottha chaytus(i) naahi, junum(u) guvaayo aalsiya. 2. Bikhum ghor punth(i) chaalna praani, Ruv(i) Sus(i) teh na pervayshun(g). Maya moh(u) tub bisur(i) guyya, ja tujilay sunsaarun(g). 3. Aaj(u) mayraiy mun(i) pergut(u) bhuyya haiy, paykhiulay Dhermrao. Teh kur dul kern(i) mahabuli, tin aaglurraiy mein rehn(u) na jaayay. 4. Jay ko moon oopdaysh(u) kert(u) haiy, ta vun(i) trin(i) ruttrra Narayana. Aiy jee, Tu aapay subh kichh(u) jaanda, budut(i) Tirlochan Rumuyyiya. 5.2. 
Word Meaning:  Aaglurra = too much.  Juraa = old age.   Bhuou = fear.   Kootumb = family.   Bigseh(i) = overjoyed; blooms.  Kumla = lotus flower.   Johay = watches with evil eye.  Kupt = deceitful.            1.
Jumeh(i) tuna = sons of the Angel of death (his agents).    Aaglurraiy = before; in front of.   Koyee, Koyee = someone rare.   Beetthula = God.    Baanhrri Vulaayay = with arms spread.        Rummuyiya = pervading the universe (God).      Pause.
Anik = countless.  Bhog = enjoyments.   Bisray = forgotten.  Amar = immortal.   Moottha = deceived; cheated.   Chaytus(i) = remember.   Aalsiya = lazy.       2.
Ghor Bikhum = very difficult; tough.  Punth(i) = path.   Ruv(i) = sun.  Sus(i) = moon.  Pervayshun(g) = enter.    Tujilay = quit.       3.          Pergut(u) = appeared.  Bhuyya = happened.   Paykhiulay = saw.   Dul = crush.     Mahabuli = great warrior.      4.
Moon = to me.  Oopdaysh = advice.   Vun = forest.  Trin = vegetation. . Ruttrra = imbued; saturated with.   Narayana = God.   Budut(i) = says.     5. 
MEANING: The man has so much love for Maya, and is so engrossed in it, that he has lost all fear of old age and death. He is overjoyed, and his mind blooms like a lotus flower to see his family.  And the deceitful man keeps an evil eye on the houses of others.         1.           Some rare devotee realizes that the agents of death are coming running towards him and he prays, “I am helpless before them, come and protect me from them, oh my God. I am waiting for you with my arms spread.”        Pause.
Among the countless enjoyments of Maya, the man forgets Waheguru and acts as if he is immortal in this world.  Captivated by Maya, he forgets Waheguru, and the lazy man wastes away his precious human life.              2.              The journey to Waheguru’s Court is very difficult, and you will not even have the necessities of daily life - like the sun (warmth and light) and the moon (for comfort).  And when you leave the world, your love for Maya will be gone too (Maya will not be with you).           3.
I have realized now that when you are face-to-face with the Angel of death, you cannot survive before him, as he is so powerful that he can crush you with his hands in a moment.         4.           When some holy man (like the Guru) enlightens me, I can see that You are present everywhere – even in the forest and the creeping grass – oh Waheguru.  You are omniscient, oh omnipresent Waheguru, says Tirlochan. 5.2.

                         
                                              ਸ੍ਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਭਗਤ ਕਬੀਰ ਜੀਉ ਕਾ॥
                                 Siri Raag(u) Bhagat Kabir Jeeo ka:
ਅਚਰਜੁ ਏਕੁ ਸੁਨਹੁ ਰੇ ਪੰਡੀਆ ਅਬ ਕਿਛੁ ਕਹਨੁ ਨ ਜਾਈ॥ ਸੁਰਿ ਨਰ ਗਣ ਗੰਧ੍ਰਬ ਜਿਨਿ ਮੋਹੇ ਤ੍ਰਿਭਵਣ ਮੇਖੁਲੀ ਲਾਈ॥ 1॥ ਰਾਜਾ ਰਾਮ ਅਨਹਦ ਕਿੰਗੁਰੀ ਬਾਜੈ॥ ਜਾ ਕੀ ਦਿਸਟਿ ਨਾਦ ਲਿਵ ਲਾਗੈ॥ 1।ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਭਾਠੀ ਗਗਨੁ ਸਿੰਙਿਆ ਅਰੁ ਚੁੰਙਿਆ ਕਨਕੁ ਕਲਸੁ ਇਕੁ ਪਾਇਆ॥ ਤਿਸੁ ਮਹਿ ਧਾਰ ਚੁਐ ਅਤਿ ਨਿਰਮਲ ਰਸ ਮਹਿ ਰਸਨ ਚੁਆਇਆ॥ 2॥ ਏਕ ਜੁ ਬਾਤ ਅਨੂਪ ਬਨੀ ਹੈ ਪਵਨ ਪਿਆਲਾ ਸਾਜਿਆ॥ ਤੀਨਿ ਭਵਨ ਮਹਿ ਏਕੋ ਜੋਗੀ ਕਹਹੁ ਕਵਨੁ ਹੈ ਰਾਜਾ॥ 3॥ ਐਸੇ ਗਿਆਨੁ ਪ੍ਰਗਟਿਆ ਪੁਰਖੋਤਮ ਕਹੁ ਕਬੀਰ ਰੰਗਿ ਰਾਤਾ॥ ਅਉਰ ਦੁਨੀ ਸਭ ਭਰਮਿ ਭੁਲਾਨੀ ਮਨੁ ਰਾਮ ਰਸਾਇਨ ਮਾਤਾ॥ 4॥3॥                               .
Achurj(u) aik soonhou ray pundiya, ub kichh(u) kehn(u) na jaayee. Soor(i) nur Gun Gundherb jin(i) mohay, tirbhuvan maykhuli laayee. 1. Raja Ram, unhud kinguree baajaiy. Ja kee disht(i) naad liv laagaiy. 1. Ruhaou.  Bhaatthi gugun(u) singiya ur(u) choongiya, kunuk(u) kulus(u) ikk(u) paaya. Tis meh(i) dhaar chooyay ut(i) nirmul, rus meh(i) rusn chooaya. 2. Aik ju baat anoop buni haiy, puvun piyala saajiya. Teen(i) bhuvun meh(i) aiko yogi, kuhou kuvun(u) haiy raja. 3. Aisay Giyan(u) pergutiya Poorkhotum, kuhou Kabir rung(i) raata.  Aour dooni subh bherm(i) bhoolaani, mun(u) 
Ram rusayin maata.  4.3.
Word Meaning:  Achurj = strange; amazing.    Soor(i) = gods.  Gun = assistants of gods.  Gundherb = musicians of the gods (there are more definitions).  Mohay = bewitched. Tirbhuvan = three-layered universe.  Maykhuli = string (tied with string) = made prisoner (slaves).       1.      Kinguree = a single-wire musical instrument.   Naad = Shabad.  Liv = focus.       Pause.      Bhaatthi = kiln; still (to distil).   Gugun(u) = sky; brain (higher level of awareness).  Singiya and choongiya = the two pipes for distilling - one to collect the distillate, and the other to reject the excess water (to collect virtues and to reject bad qualities).  Kunuk = gold.  Kulus = container for the distillate (heart).  Kunuk Kulus = heart of gold (pure heart).  Chooyay = dropping (collecting).  Nirmul = pure.  Rus = extract.      2.         Anoop = beautiful; incomparable.   Puvun Piyala = cup of air (made the breath the cup to drink).  Yogi = connected (with God).    Raja = greater.         3.   Poorkhotum = sureme human (Waheguru).  Dooni = world.  Bherm(i) bhoolaani = lost in misbeliefs.   Rusayin = source of all the extracts (God).  Maata = intoxicated.  4.

MEANING:  With full concentration on Him, a devotee gets connected with Waheguru, with His kind sight, as a result of which, a uniform and sweet melody of His Naam begins to play automatically in his mind, oh pundit.           Pause.
The amazing thing about Waheguru, which has made the whole universe (living 
beings) slaves of Maya (they have been bewitched by it), is that nothing can be said about Him. (He is indescribable).           1.
(The only thing I can say about Him) is that I have made my brain (my higher spiritual 
state, and concentration on His Naam) the still, to get the essence of His Naam (like in a distillation process to get the extract of some herb, or even to make alcohol, in which two pipes are used – one to receive the essence and the other to reject water), I am 
getting rid of my bad qualities on one hand, and collecting the Amrit of Waheguru’s 
Naam, on the other hand, is in my pure heart. A thin stream of the best among all the essences is now dripping in my mind.           2          Another beautiful thing that has happened is that my every breath has become the cup to drink the Naam Amrit, and now I see Waheguru everywhere in the three-layered universe, and it is not difficult to say that Waheguru is the greatest.           3.          Imbued in His Naam, Kabir can only say that such Divine Knowledge has sprouted in my mind with which  I have realized that the whole world is misguided in doubts, but I am intoxicated with the Naam-Amrit of Waheguru, which is the source of all essences.        4.
