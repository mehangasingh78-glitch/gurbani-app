(ਅੰਗ 114)॥  ਅਨਦਿਨੁ ਸਦਾ ਰਹੈ ਭੈ ਅੰਦਰਿ ਭੈ ਮਾਰਿ ਭਰਮੁ ਚੁਕਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 5॥ ਭਰਮੁ ਚੁਕਾਇਆ ਸਦਾ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਾਇਆ॥ ਗੁਰ ਪਰਸਾਦਿ ਪਰਮ ਪਦੁ ਪਾਇਆ॥ ਅੰਤਰੁ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਨਿਰਮਲ ਬਾਣੀ ਹਰਿ ਗੁਣ ਸਹਜੇ ਗਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 6॥ ਸਿਮ੍ਰਿਤਿ ਸਾਸਤ ਬੇਦ ਵਖਾਣੈ॥ ਭਰਮੇ ਭੂਲਾ ਤਤੁ ਨ ਜਾਣੈ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਸੇਵੇ ਸੁਖੁ ਨ ਪਾਏ ਦੁਖੋ ਦੁਖੁ ਕਮਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 7॥ ਆਪਿ ਕਰੇ ਕਿਸੁ ਆਖੈ ਕੋਈ॥ ਆਖਣ ਜਾਈਐ ਜੇ ਭੂਲਾ ਹੋਈ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਆਪੇ ਕਰੇ ਕਰਾਏ ਨਾਮੇ ਨਾਮਿ ਸਮਾਵਣਆ॥ 8॥7॥8॥
Majh Mehla 3: Amrit Bani Gur kee meetthi. Gurmukh(i) virlaiy kinaiy chukh(i) deetthi. Untar(i)s pergaas(u) maha rus(u) peevaiy, Dur(i) Sachaiy Shabad(u) vujaavniya.  1. Huoun vaari jeeo vaari, Gur Churni chit(u) laavniya. Satgur haiy Amritsar Saacha, mun(u) naavaiy maiyl(u) chookaavniya. 1. Ruhaou.  Tayra Sachay kinaiy unt(u) na paaya. Gur persad(i) kinaiy virlaiy chit(u) laaya. Toodh(u) salaaheh(i) na rujjaan kubhoon, Sachay Naavaiy kee bhookh laavniya. 2.  Aiko vaykhaan, uver na beeya. Gur persadi Amrit peeya. Gur kay Shabad(i) tikha nivaari, sehjay sookh(i) smaavniya. 3. Ruttan pdaruth pullar(i) tiyaagaiy. Munmukh(u) undha doojay bhaayay laagaiy. Jo beejaiy soyee ful(u) paayay, soopnay sookh(u) na paavniya. 4.  Apni kirpa kuray, soyee jun(u) paayay. Gur ka Shabad(u) munn(i) vusaayay. 
(PAGE114):                     Undin(u) sudaa ruhaiy Bhaiy undar(i), Bhaiy maar(i) bherm(u) chookaavniya.  5. Bherm(u) chookaaya, suda sookh(u) paaya. Gur persad(i) perm Pud(u) paaya. Untar(u) nirmul(u), nirmul Bani, Hur(i) goon sehjay gaavniya.  6. Simrit(i) Shast Bayd vukhaanaiy. Bhermay bhoola Tut(u) na jaanaiy. Bin(u) Satgur sayvay sookh(u) na paayay, dookho dookh(u) kmaavniya.  7. Aap(i) kuray, kis(u) aakhaiy koyee.  Aakhun jaayiyaiy jay bhoola hoyee. Nanak Aapay kuray kuraayay, Naamay Naam(i) smaavniya.  8.7.8.
Word Meaning:   Virlaiy = some rare.   Chukh(i) = by tasting.   Deetthi = saw; observed.   Pergaas(u) = enlightenment.   Maha Rus(u) = great essence (taste).   Dur(i) = door.   Shabad(u) Vujaavniya = (music) of Shabad plays (captivates).         1. Amritsar = pool (source) of Amrit.   Mayl(u) = filth of evil thoughts.    Chookaavniya = is removed; washed away.        Pause.      Unt(u) = limit.   Rujjaan = (I am) fully satisfied.    Na Kubhoon = never.    Bhookh = appetite.        2.      Uver(u) = other.   Beeya = second.    Tikha = thirst.    Nivaari = removed.        3.      Ruttan(u) Pdaruth(u) = highly valuable article like precious stones = Waheguru’s Naam.     Tiyaagaiy = give up; let go; trade in.    Pullar(i) = crushed dried stems of certain crops like canola (a worthless thing).   Doojaiy Bhaayay = in the love for the other (Maya).   Beejaiy = sows.    Ful(u) = result.        4.      Soyee Jun(u) = that devotee.   Bhaiy = fear.    Bherm(u) = misbelief.         5.
Perm Pud = the supreme (spiritual) level.    Nirmul(u) = pure; clean.        6. Simrit Shast = Hindu holy books.   Bayd = the Vedas.    Vukhaanaiy = utter; discuss.   Tut(u) = reality.   Dookho Dookh(u) = suffering, and suffering only.        7.Kis(u) = to whom.   Kuraayay = makes others do.   Naamay = by Naam.    Naam(i) = in Naam (Waheguru).       8.
MEANING:  The Amit (immortalizing) Bani of the Guru (in Waheguru’s praise) is very sweet.  But some rare devotee of the Guru tastes it. Whoever drinks the great essence of Bani (gets imbued in it) is enlightened, and the Divine music of Guru’s Shabad seems to play continuously in his mind at the Door of Waheguru (Waheguru’s Naam is always fresh in his mind).           1.           I am a sacrifice to those who focus their mind with full devotion with Guru’s Feet (Shabad).  Satguru is the eternal pool of Amrit; if the mind takes a dip in the pool (recites Waheguru’s Naam), all its filth (of evil thoughts) is washed away.           Pause.           Some rare individual applies his mind to Your Feet (makes connection with You) by the grace of the Guru; but nobody has ever been able to find Your limits, oh Waheguru!  I pray to You that I may always have an appetite for Your Naam, and should never be tired of singing Your praise, oh Waheguru.            2.
By the Guru’s grace, I have tasted the Amrit of Your Naam, and now I “see” only You everywhere, and nobody else, oh Waheguru.  I have killed my thirst for worldly desires, and now I am enjoying blissfulness without any special effort.         3.
(On the other hand), a follower of his mind is blind (of wisdom about Waheguru) and indulges in Maya; he trades the precious Waheguru’s Naam for worthless short-lived things involving Maya, which are like the crushed stems of dried crops.   He reaps what he sows, and does not get mental peace even in his dreams.         4.
Only that individual acquires Waheguru’s Naam, and enshrines Guru’s Shabad in his mind to whom He is kind.   Such an individual always fears God, but gets rid of all other worldly fears and also his misbeliefs.           5.           An individual who has all his misbeliefs cleared is always blissful and attains the higher spiritual state (above Maya) with the Guru’s grace.  With the Pure (Holy) Bani (of the Guru), his mind is also purified, and he always sings praise of Waheguru in the high spiritual state.          6.
Those who explain the Hindu holy Granths, such as the Shastras, the Vedas, and the Upanishads, to these people are lost in misbelief; they do not know the truth. They enjoy no comfort without serving the Guru, and suffer in discomfort.          7.
Waheguru Himself makes the living beings do whatever they are doing (Waheguru guides or misguides them based on their deeds of previous lives).  So, nobody can be fully blamed for doing immoral things.   Waheguru Himself does as He pleases and makes others act on His Will. However, one becomes “one” with Waheguru only by reciting His Naam, oh Nanak.    8.7.8.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ ਆਪੇ ਰੰਗੇ ਸਹਜਿ ਸੁਭਾਏ॥ ਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਸਬਦਿ ਹਰਿ ਰੰਗੁ ਚੜਾਏ॥ ਮਨੁ ਤਨੁ ਰਤਾ ਰਸਨਾ ਰੰਗਿ ਚਲੂਲੀ  ਭੈ ਭਾਇ ਰੰਗੁ ਚੜਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਹਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਜੀਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਨਿਰਭਉ ਮੰਨਿ ਵਸਾਵਣਿਆ॥ ਗੁਰ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਤੇ ਹਰਿ ਨਿਰਭਉ ਧਿਆਇਆ ਬਿਖੁ ਭਉਜਲੁ ਸਬਦਿ ਤਰਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਮੁਗਧ ਕਰਹਿ ਚਤੁਰਾਈ॥ ਨਾਤਾ ਧੋਤਾ ਥਾਇ ਨ ਪਾਈ॥ ਜੇਹਾ ਆਇਆ ਤੇਹਾ ਜਾਸੀ ਕਰਿ ਅਵਗਣ ਪਛੋਤਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 2॥ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਅੰਧੇ ਕਿਛੂ ਨ ਸੂਝੈ॥ ਮਰਣੁ ਲਿਖਾਇ ਆਏ ਨਹੀ ਬੂਝੈ॥ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਕਰਮ ਕਰੇ ਨਹੀ ਪਾਏ ਬਿਨੁ ਨਾਵੇ ਜਨਮੁ ਗਵਾਵਣਿਆ ॥3॥ ਸਚੁ ਕਰਣੀ ਸਬਦੁ ਹੈ ਸਾਰੁ॥ ਪੂਰੈ ਗੁਰਿ ਪਾਈਐ ਮੋਖ ਦੁਆਰੁ॥ ਅਨਦਿਨੁ ਬਾਣੀ ਸਬਦਿ ਸੁਣਾਏ ਸਚਿ ਰਾਤੇ ਰੰਗਿ ਰੰਗਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 4॥ ਰਸਨਾ ਹਰਿ ਰਸਿ ਰਾਤੀ ਰੰਗੁ ਲਾਏ॥ ਮਨੁ ਤਨੁ ਮੋਹਿਆ ਸਹਜਿ ਸੁਭਾਏ॥ ਸਹਜੇ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਮੁ ਪਿਆਰਾ ਪਾਇਆ ਸਹਜੇ ਸਹਜਿ ਮਿਲਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 5॥ ਜਿਸੁ ਅੰਦਰਿ ਰੰਗ ਸੋਈ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਵੈ॥ ਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਸਬਦਿ ਸਹਜੇ ਸੁਖਿ ਸਮਾਵੈ॥ ਹਉ ਬਲਿਹਾਰੀ ਸਦਾ ਤਿਨ ਵਿਟਹੁ ਗੁਰ ਸੇਵਾ ਚਿਤੁ ਲਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 6॥ ਸਚਾ ਸਚੋ ਸਚਿ ਪਤੀਜੈ॥ ਗੁਰ ਪਰਸਾਦੀ ਅੰਦਰੁ ਭੀਜੈ॥ ਬੈਸਿ ਸੁਥਾਨਿ ਹਰਿ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਵਹਿ ਆਪੇ ਕਰਿ ਸਤਿ ਮਨਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 7॥ ਜਿਸ ਨੋ ਨਦਰਿ ਕਰੇ ਸੋ ਪਾਏ॥ ਗੁਰ ਪਰਸਾਦੀ ਹਉਮੈ ਜਾਏ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮੁ ਵਸੈ ਮਨ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਦਰਿ ਸਚੈ ਸੋਭਾ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 8॥8॥9॥
Majh Mehla 3:  Aapay rungay sehj(i) soobhayay. Gur kaiy Shabad(i) Hur(i) rung(u) churrayay. Mun(u) tun(u) rutta, rusna rung(i) chulooli, bhaiy bhaayay rung(u) churraavniya.  1. Huoun vaari jeeo vaari, Nirbhuou munn(i) vusaavniya. Gur kirpa tay Hur(i) Nirbhuou dhiyaya, bikh(u) bhuoujul(u) Shabad(i) traavniya. 1. Ruhaou.   Munmukh moogdh kureh(i) chaturaayee. Naata dhota thaayayn na paayee.  Jayha aaya tayha jaasi, kur(i) uvgun puchhotaavniya.  2. Munmukh undhay, kichhoo na soojhaiy. Mern(u) likhaayay aayay, nuhee boojhaiy. Munmukh kerm kuray, nuhee paayay, bin(u) Naavay junum(u) guvaavniya. 3. Sach(u) kerni Shabad(u) haiy saar(u). Pooraiy Gur(i) paayiyaiy mokh dooaar(u).  Undin(u) Bani Shabad(i) soonaayay, Sach(i) raatay rung(i) rungaavniya. 4. Rusna Hur(i) rus(i) raati rung(u) laayay. Mun(u) tun(u) mohiya sehj(i) soobhaayay. Sehjay Preetum(u) piyara paaya, sehjay sehj(i) milaavniya.  5. Jis(u) undar rung, soyee goon gaavaiy. Gur kaiy Shabad(i) sehjay sookh(i) smaavaiy. Huoun bulihaari suda tin vitoun, Gur sayva chit(u) laavniya. 6. Sacha, Sacho Sach(i) puteejaiy. Gur persadi undar(u) bheejaiy. Baiys(i) soothaan(i) Hur(i) goon gaaveh(i), aapay kur(i) Sat(i) munaavniya.  7. Jis no nudr(i) kuray so paayay. Gurpersadi haumein jaayay. Nanak Naam(u) vusaiy mun untar(i) Dur(i) Sachaiy sobha paavniya. 8.8.9.
Word Meaning:   Sehj(i) Soobhaayay = higher spiritual state and love.    Rung churraayay = dyes.    Rutta = imbued.    Rusna = tongue.   Chlooli = thickly dyed.   Bhaayay = (with) love.     1.     Nirbhuou = fearless (Waheguru).    Vusaavniya = is secured (stored) in memory.  Bikh(u) = poisonous.   Bhuoujul(u) = the world sea (of Maya).   Traavniya = ferry across.      Pause.
Munmukh = follower of his own mind.     Moogdh = foolish.   Naata Dhota = bathed and dressed (religious rituals to show off).    Thaayayn Na Paayee = does not get any (respectable) place in Waheguru’s Court; is not considered worthy.    Jayha = as.   Tahya = the same.   Uvgun = evil deeds.   Puchhotaavniya = repents.       2.
Soojhaiy = think of.    Likhayay Aayay = born with pre-destiny.    Boojhaiy = understand.   Kerm = deeds.    Nuhee Paayay = gains nothing (spiritually).         3.
Sach Kerni = performance of True (noble) deeds.    Saar(u) = supreme.    Mokh = freedom from Maya.    Raatay = imbued.    Rung(i) = (in) love.       4.
Rung Laayay = is dyed.   Mohiya = captivated.    Sehj Soobhaayay = higher spiritual state and love.   Sehjay = easily; without any special effort.      5.
Bulihaari = sacrifice life.   Vitoun = upon; for.    Chit(u) = mind.     6.
Sacho Sach(i) = pure Truth (Waheguru’s Naam).     Pteejaiy = is pleased; brings faith.      Undar(u) = inside; mind.    Bheejaiy = is imbued.     Baiys = sit (in meditation).   Soothaan = a clean, attractive place; a pure mind.   Aapay = Waheguru Himself.   Sat(i) = True; right.       Munaavniya = convinces; inspires.        7.
Nudr(i) = kind sight.   Paayay = receives; acquires (Naam).   Vusaiy = makes home; stored in memory.     Sobha = praise.     8.
MEANING:  Waheguru Himself dyes the hearts of His devotees with love for Him by the Guru’s Shabad.  The devotee’s mind, body, and tongue are deeply imbued (in His love) because of his fear and love for Waheguru.           1.         I am a sacrifice for those who make their hearts a place for the fearless Waheguru to reside (enlighten their mind). The fearless Waheguru is worshipped by the grace of the Guru, and the 
Guru’s Shabad ferries the devotee across the sea of poisonous Maya.          Pause.  
The foolish follower of his mind considers him very clever.  (In trying to fool others) He adorns himself with ritualistic symbols and pretends to be religious, but such acts do not earn him recognition from Waheguru. He leaves this world with the same bad qualities with which he was born, and repents at the end.          2.
The blind (ignorant) follower of his mind cannot think of anything right in the realm of spirituality, and does not understand that a man is born to die eventually.  He performs only the rituals which cannot connect him with God, and wastes his precious human life without Waheguru’s Naam in mind.          3.         The real, and the supreme thing for a man to do, is to act on the Guru’s Shabad.  The freedom from the bonds of Maya can be achieved only by taking shelter of the Perfect Guru.  The Perfect Guru preaches the Shabad of Waheguru’s praise day and night, by which the devotees get imbued in His love (and are salvaged).            4.           The tongue, imbued in Waheguru’s Naam, also dyes one’s mind with Waheguru’s love; as a result, his peaceful mind and the body are captivated by Waheguru’s love.  The dear Waheguru is acquired (realized) in the high spiritual state (above the effects of Maya), and the devotee always stays connected with Waheguru.           5.           The individual, who has love for Waheguru at heart, sings His praise by Guru’s Shabad, and enjoys the bliss of a high spiritual state.  I offer my life to those who apply their mind to the service of the Guru (recite and obey His Shabad).         6.         Waheguru is pleased only by the true devotees (who obey His Will), and whose mind has been imbued with His love by the Guru’s grace.  Such devotees sing Waheguru’s praise with a pure and focused mind.  Waheguru Himself inspires and guides them that (obeying His Huqam) is the right approach for union with Him.          7.            Only that individual acquires Waheguru’s Naam, to whom He is kind.  With Guru’s grace, he overcomes his haumein, and Waheguru comes to reside in his heart (his heart is enlightened) and such a devotee is honoured in Waheguru’s Court.   8.8.9.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਸੇਵਿਐ ਵਡੀ ਵਡਿਆਈ॥ ਹਰਿ ਜੀ ਅਚਿੰਤੁ ਵਸੈ ਮਨਿ ਆਈ॥ ਹਰਿ ਜੀਉ ਸਫਲਿਓ ਬਿਰਖੁ ਹੈ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਜਿਨਿ ਪੀਤਾ ਤਿਸੁ ਤਿਖਾ ਲਹਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਹਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਜੀਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਸਚੁ ਸੰਗਤਿ ਮੇਲਿ ਮਿਲਾਵਣਿਆ॥ ਹਰਿ ਸਤਿਸੰਗਤਿ ਆਪੇ ਮੇਲੈ ਗੁਰ ਸਬਦੀ ਹਰਿ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥
