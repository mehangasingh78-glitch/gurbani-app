ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 4 ਘਰੁ 1॥
                                          Siri Raag(u) Mehla 4 Ghur(u) 1:  
ਮੈ ਮਨਿ ਤਨਿ ਬਿਰਹੁ ਅਤਿ ਅਗਲਾ ਕਿਉ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਮੁ ਮਿਲੈ ਘਰਿ ਆਇ॥ ਜਾ ਦੇਖਾ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਆਪਣਾ ਪ੍ਰਭਿ ਦੇਖਿਐ ਦੁਖੁ ਜਾਇ॥ ਜਾਇ ਪੁਛਾ ਤਿਨ ਸਜਣਾ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਕਿਤੁ ਬਿਧਿ ਮਿਲੈ ਮਿਲਾਇ॥1॥ ਮੇਰੇ ਸਤਿਗੁਰਾ ਮੈ ਤੁਝ ਬਿਨੁ ਅਵਰੁ ਨ ਕੋਇ॥ ਹਮ ਮੂਰਖ ਮੁਗਧ ਸਰਣਾਗਤੀ ਕਰਿ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਮੇਲੇ ਹਰਿ ਸੋਇ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਦਾਤਾ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮ ਕਾ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਆਪਿ ਮਿਲਾਵੈ ਸੋਇ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਬੁਝਿਆ ਗੁਰ ਜੇਵਡੁ ਅਵਰੁ ਨ ਕੋਇ॥ ਹਉ ਗੁਰ ਸਰਣਾਈ ਢਹਿ ਪਵਾ ਕਰਿ ਦਇਆ ਮੇਲੇ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਸੋਇ॥2॥ ਮਨਿ ਹਠਿ ਕਿਨੈ ਨ ਪਾਇਆ ਕਰਿ ਉਪਾਵ ਥਕੇ ਸਭੁ ਕੋਇ॥
(ਅੰਗ 40)॥   ਸਹਸ ਸਿਆਣਪ ਕਰਿ ਰਹੇ ਮਨਿ ਕੋਰੈ ਰੰਗੁ ਨ ਹੋਇ॥ ਕੂੜਿ ਕਪਟਿ ਕਿਨੈ ਨ ਪਾਇਓ ਜੋ ਬੀਜੈ ਖਾਵੈ ਸੋਇ॥3॥ ਸਭਨਾ ਤੇਰੀ ਆਸ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਸਭ ਜੀਅ ਤੇਰੇ ਤੂ ਰਾਸਿ॥ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਤੁਧਹੁ ਖਾਲੀ ਕੋ ਨਹੀ ਦਰਿ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਾ ਨੋ ਸਾਬਾਸਿ॥ ਬਿਖ ਭਉਜਲ ਡੁਬਦੇ ਕਢਿ ਲੈ ਜਨ ਨਾਨਕ ਕੀ ਅਰਦਾਸਿ॥ 4॥1॥65॥
Mein mun(i) tun(i) birhou ut(i) ugla, kiyoun Preetum(u) milaiy ghur(i) aayay. Ja daykhaan Prubh(u) aapna, Prubh(i) daykhiyaiyn dookh(u) jaayay. Jaayay poochhaan tin sujjnaan, Prubh(u) kit(u) bidh(i) milaiy milaayay. 1. Mayray Satgura, mein Toojh bin(u) uver(u) na koyay. Hum moorukh moogdh shernaagti, kur(i) kirpa maylay hur(i) soyay. 1. Ruhaou. Satgur(u) daata Hur(i) Naam ka, Prubh(u) aap(i) milaavaiy soyay. Satgur(i) Hur(i) Prubh(u) boojhiya, Gur  jayvud(u) uver(u) na koyay. Houn Gur shernaayee dheh(i) puvaan kur(i) duyya maylay Prubh(u) soyay. 2.  Mun(i) hutth(i) kinaiy na paaya kur(i) opaav thukkay subh(u) koyay. 
(PAGE 40):       Sehs siyaanup kur(i) ruhay, mun(i) koraiy rung(u) na hoyay. Koorr(i) kupt(i) kinaiy na paayo, jo beejaiy, khaavaiy soyay. 3. Subhna Tayri aas Prubh(u) subh jea Tayray Tu raas(i). Prubh(u) toodhoun khaali ko nuhi dur(i) Gurmukhaan nu shaabash(i).  Bikh bhoujul doobday kudh(i) laiy,  jun Nanak kee ardaas(i).  4.1.65.
Word Meaning:  Birhou = pain of separation.   Ugla = very much.    Kiyoun =   how?  Preetum(u) = dear (Waheguru).    Ghur(i) = in the heart.      Kit(u) = which.     Bidh(i) = way; process.     1.         Moogdh = fool; ignorant.   Shernaagti = refugee; in Your shelter.      Pause.
Daata = source.    Boojhiya = realized.   Dheh(i) Puvaan = drop at the feet.        2.
Hutth(i) = stubbornness; obstinacy.    Opaav = means; arrangements.  Thukkay = exhausted.   Sehs = thousand.   Koraiy = undyed; blank.    Koorr(i) = deceits; dishonesty; show off.   Kupt(i) = cheating; fraud.          3.        Raas(i) = capital; source of everything.    Shaabash(i) = accolades; receive praise.   Bikh Bhoujul = sea of Maya.   Jun = slave; devotee.     4.
MEANING:  I do not have anybody else (as my support) without You, oh my Satguru!  I am an ignorant fool, and have come to Your shelter. Please, be kind, and connect me with Waheguru.            Pause.           I am suffering mentally and physically from the pain of separation; how can I have my darling (Waheguru) come and stay in my heart? All my pain goes away when I see (spiritually) my dear Waheguru.  I will approach my devotee friends and seek their advice on how to get connected with Waheguru.         1            Satguru is the source of Waheguru’s Naam and can connect you with Waheguru. Satguru has realized Waheguru (is “one” with Him); no one else is as great as Satguru. I will (humbly) drop at the feet of the Satguru, and He will kindly connect me with Waheguru.             2. 
Many people exhaust themselves with different approaches, but no one has ever realized Waheguru through their own stubborn efforts.  Thousands have tried their wisdom, but a blank mind cannot be dyed in Waheguru’s love (just as fabric requires a base before it can be dyed, the human mind also needs Guru’s Shabad before it can be dyed in Waheguru’s love). Also, nobody has ever realized Waheguru by deceitful methods and by cheating; they reap the rewards of their own sowings.    3.
Everybody has their hopes on You, as You are the source to fulfil all their needs, oh Waheguru.  Nobody is without You (You are present in everybody), but only those who follow the Guru’s teachings earn respect in Your Court.  I, Nanak, a devotee of Yours, pray that we human beings, drowning in the sea of Maya, be kindly saved, oh Waheguru.    4.1.65.    

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 4॥ ਨਾਮੁ ਮਿਲੈ ਮਨੁ ਤ੍ਰਿਪਤੀਐ ਬਿਨੁ ਨਾਮੈ ਧ੍ਰਿਗੁ ਜੀਵਾਸੁ॥ ਕੋਈ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਸਜਣੁ ਜੇ ਮਿਲੈ ਮੈ ਦਸੇ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਗੁਣਤਾਸੁ॥ ਹਉ ਤਿਸੁ ਵਿਟਹੁ ਚਉਖੰਨੀਐ ਮੈ ਨਾਮ ਕਰੇ ਪਰਗਾਸੁ॥ 1॥ ਮੇਰੇ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਮਾ ਹਉ ਜੀਵਾ ਨਾਮੁ ਧਿਆਇ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਨਾਵੈ ਜੀਵਣੁ ਨ ਥੀਐ ਮੇਰੇ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਨਾਮੁ ਦ੍ਰਿੜਾਇ॥1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਨਾਮੁ ਅਮੋਲਕੁ ਰਤਨੁ ਹੈ ਪੂਰੇ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਪਾਸਿ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਸੇਵੈ ਲਗਿਆ ਕਢਿ ਰਤਨੁ ਦੇਵੈ ਪਰਗਾਸਿ॥ ਧੰਨੁ ਵਡਭਾਗੀ ਵਡਭਾਗੀਆ ਜੋ ਆਇ ਮਿਲੇ ਗੁਰ ਪਾਸਿ॥2॥ ਜਿਨਾ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਪੁਰਖੁ ਨ ਭੇਟਿਓ ਸੇ ਭਾਗਹੀਣ ਵਸਿ ਕਾਲ॥ ਓਇ ਫਿਰਿ ਫਿਰਿ ਜੋਨਿ ਭਵਾਈਅਹਿ ਵਿਚਿ ਵਿਸਟਾ ਕਰਿ ਵਿਕਰਾਲ॥ ਓਨਾ ਪਾਸਿ ਦੁਆਸਿ ਨ ਭਿਟੀਐ ਜਿਨ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਕ੍ਰੋਧੁ ਚੰਡਾਲ॥3॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਪੁਰਖੁ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤਸਰੁ ਵਡਭਾਗੀ ਨਾਵਹਿ ਆਇ॥ ਉਨ ਜਨਮ ਜਨਮ ਕੀ ਮੈਲੁ ਉਤਰੈ ਨਿਰਮਲ ਨਾਮੁ ਦ੍ਰਿੜਾਇ॥ ਜਨ ਨਾਨਕ ਉਤਮ ਪਦੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਕੀ ਲਿਵ ਲਾਇ॥ 4॥2॥66॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 4:  Naam(u) milaiy mun(u) triptiyaiy, bin(u) Naamaiy dhrig(u) jeevaas(u). Koyee Gurmukh(i) sujjan(u) jay milaiy, mein dusaay Prubh(u) goontaas(u).  Houn tis(u) vitoun chaukhunniyaiy mein Naam kuray purgaas(u). 1. Mayray Preetuma houn jeevaan Naam(u) dhiyaayay.  Bin(u) Naavaiyn jeevun(u) na theeyay, mayray Satguru Naam(u) drirraayay. 1. Ruhaou. Naam(u) amoluk(u) ruttun(u) haiy, pooraiy Satgur paas(i). Satguru sayvaiy luggiyaan, kudh(i) ruttan(u) dayvaiy purgaas(i). Dhunn(u) vudbhaagi vudbhaagiyaan, jo aayay milay Gur paas(i). 2. Jinha Satgur(u) Purakh(u) na bhaytiyo, say bhaagheen vus(i) kaal. Oyay phir(i) phir(i) joan(i) bhuvaayeh(i), vich(i) vishta kur(i) vikraal. Ohna paas(i) dooaas(i) na bhitiyaiy, jin untar(i) krodh(u) chundaal. 3. Satgur(u) Purakh(u) Amritsar(u), vudbhaagi naaveh(i) aayay. Oon junum junum kee maiyl ootraiy, nirmul Naam(u) drirrayay. Jun Nanak ootum pud(u) paaya Satgur kee liv laayay.  4.2.66.
Word Meaning:  Triptiyaiy = is contented.  Dhrig(u) = condemnable.   Jeevaas(u) = life.   Goontaas(u) = treasure of noble qualities (Waheguru).   Chaukhunniyaiy = cut into pieces.        1.        Preetuma = darling.  Theeyaiy = happen; exist.   Drirraayay = reinforce.            Pause.           Amoluk(u) = priceless.   Ruttun(u) = precious stone.   Purgaas(i) = enlighten.  Dhunn(u) = worthy of respect.   Vudbhaagi = very lucky.    2.
Bhaytiyo = met; connected with.    Vus(i) Kaal = die spiritual death.   Bhuvaayeh(i) = made to go through.  Vishta = crap (Maya).   Vikraal = scary; frightening.  Paas(i) dooaas(i) = near; close.   Bhitiyaiy = meet; go near.   Chundaal = butcher; vicious.       3.
Amritsar(u) = pool of life-sustaining water (ambrosia).  Ootum Pud(u) = highest spiritual state.   Liv = meditate with full concentration. 4.
MEANING: I survive only if I recite (remember) Your Naam, oh my darling Waheguru. There is no life without Your Naam; my Satguru reiterates Your Naam.       Pause.
My mind finds contentment when I acquire Waheguru’s Naam; life is condemnable without Naam.  I am looking for a devotee friend who can lead me to Waheguru, the treasure of all noble qualities. I will cut myself into pieces (be an unconditional slave) for him who enlightens me with Waheguru’s Naam.       1.            The perfect Satguru possesses (is the source of) Waheguru’s Naam, which is priceless like a jewel.  When one serves Satguru, He confers Naam-Rattan on him and enlightens his mind.  Those who get connected with Satguru are the luckiest of the lucky.         2.            (On the other hand, those who do not get connected with Satguru) are prisoners of (spiritual) death.  (Disconnected from the Guru), They are trapped in the crappy Maya and are made to suffer in the frightening cycle of reincarnation.  One should never get even close to those who harbour the butcher-like rage.       3.
Satguru, who is a manifestation of Waheguru, is a pool of life-sustaining water (Naam-Amrit); the lucky ones take a dip in it.  Their filth of many lives is washed away, and they recite Waheguru’s Naam repeatedly with a clean mind.  I, a devotee of the Satguru, have also attained the highest spiritual level by concentrating on the Naam, oh, Nanak.    4.2.66.          

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 4॥ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਵਾ ਗੁਣ ਵਿਥਰਾ ਗੁਣ ਬੋਲੀ ਮੇਰੀ ਮਾਇ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਸਜਣੁ ਗੁਣਕਾਰੀਆ ਮਿਲਿ ਸਜਣ ਹਰਿ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਇ॥ ਹੀਰੈ ਹੀਰੁ ਮਿਲਿ ਬੇਧਿਆ ਰੰਗਿ ਚਲੂਲੇ ਨਾਇ॥1॥ ਮੇਰੇ ਗੋਵਿੰਦਾ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਵਾ ਤ੍ਰਿਪਤਿ ਮਨਿ ਹੋਇ॥ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਪਿਆਸ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮ ਕੀ ਗੁਰੁ ਤੁਸਿ ਮਿਲਾਵੈ ਸੋਇ॥1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਮਨੁ ਰੰਗਹੁ ਵਡਭਾਗੀਹੋ ਗੁਰ ਤੁਠਾ ਕਰੇ ਪਸਾਉ॥ ਗੁਰ ਨਾਮ ਦ੍ਰਿੜਾਏ ਰੰਗ ਸਿਉ ਹਉ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਬਲਿ ਜਾਉ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਨ ਲਭਈ ਲਖ ਕੋਟੀ ਕਰਮ ਕਮਾਉ॥2॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਭਾਗਾ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਨ ਮਿਲੈ ਘਰਿ ਬੈਠਿਆ ਨਿਕਟਿ ਨਿਤ ਪਾਸਿ॥ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਅਗਿਆਨ ਦੁਖੁ ਭਰਮੁ ਹੈ ਵਿਚਿ ਪੜਦਾ ਦੂਰਿ ਪਈਆਸਿ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਭੇਟੇ ਕੰਚਨੁ ਨਾ ਥੀਐ ਮਨਮੁਖੁ ਲੋਹੁ ਬੂਡਾ ਬੇੜੀ ਪਾਸਿ॥3॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਬੋਹਿਥੁ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਵ ਹੈ ਕਿਤੁ ਬਿਧਿ ਚੜਿਆ ਜਾਇ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਭਾਣੈ ਜੋ ਚਲੈ ਵਿਚਿ ਬੋਹਿਥ ਬੈਠਾ ਆਇ॥ ਧੰਨੁ ਧੰਨੁ ਵਡਭਾਗੀ ਨਾਨਕਾ ਜਿਨਾ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਲਏ ਮਿਲਾਇ॥ 4॥3॥67॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 4:  Goon gaavaan, goon vithraan, goon boaleen mayri maayay. Gurmukh(i) sujjan(u) goonkaariya mil(i) sujjan Hur(i) goon gaayay. Heeraiy heer(u) mil(i) baydhiya, rung(i) chloolay naayay. 1. Mayray Govinda, goon gaavaan triput(i) mun(i) hoyay. Untar(i) piyaas Hur(i) Naam kee, Gur(u) toos(i) milaavaiy soyay.1. Ruhaou.  Mun(u) rungou vudbhaagiho Gur toottha kuray pusaaou. Gur Naam drirrayay rung siyoun, huoun Satgur kaiy bul(i) jaaoun. Bin(u) Satgur Hur(i) Naam(u) na lubhyee, lukh koti kerm kmaoun. 2. Bin(u) bhaagaan Satgur(u) na milaiy ghur(i) baitthiyaan nikt(i) nit paas(i). Untar(i) ugiyaan dookh(u) bherm(u) haiy, vich(i) purrdah door(i) puyiaas(i). Bin(u) Satguru bhaytay kunchan(u) na theeyaiy, munmukh(u) loh(u), booda bayrri paas(i). 3. Satgur bohith(u) Hur(i) Naav haiy, kit(u) bidh(i) churriyaa jaayay. Satgur kaiy bhaanaiy jo chullaiy, vich(i) bohith baittha aayay. Dhunn(u) dhunn(u) vudbhaagi Nanaka jinha Satgur(u) luyyay milaayay.  4.3.67.
Word Meaning:  Vithraan = explain with detail; preach.  Goonkaariya = possessing noble qualities.   Sujjan = (devotee) friends.  Heeraiy = diamond (Satguru).  Heera = human mind.  Baydhiya = pierced (affected deeply).    Chloolay = deep red shiny colour (red colour represents deep devotion for God).  Naayay = with Waheguru’s Naam.     1.
Govinda = Waheguru.   Triput(u) = content.    Toos(i) = pleased to be kind.     Pause. 
Toottha = pleased to be kind.   Psaaou = blesses.   Drirrayay = reinforces.   Rung Siyoun = with love.   Bul(i) = sacrifice self.   Lukh Koti = millions.  Kerm = religious rituals.    Kmaaoun = perform.         2.       Ghur(I) = in the heart.  Nikt(i) = near; close by.  Ugiyaan = ignorance.   Bherm = doubts; wavering mind.  Purrdah = curtain.   Puyiaas = is drawn.  Kunchan = (pure like) gold.  Theeyaiy = becomes.  Loh = (like) iron; stone-hearted.        3.   
Bohith(u) = ship.   Naav = boat.   Kit(u) bidh(i) = in what way; how.   Bhaanaiy = as per His Will.  Chullaiy = leads his life.       4.
MEANING:  My mind finds contentment when I sing praise of Your noble qualities, oh my Waheguru!  There is a thirst in my mind for Waheguru’s Naam; the Guru kindly connects me with Him (thereby quenching my thirst).       Pause.        I sing Waheguru’s noble qualities, explain them to others, and my routine conversation is also about His noble qualities, oh my mother!  My friend is a devotee of the Satguru and possesses all the noble qualities; I sing Waheguru’s qualities in his company. After connecting with the Guru, who is like a diamond, my mind has been pierced (deeply moved) and has also become like a diamond, imbued with the deep red, shiny colour (which never fades) of the Naam.          1.       The Guru is very kind and pleased (to connect you with Waheguru).  Be fortunate,  and take Guru’s shelter to be imbued in the deep red colour of His love, oh my friend!  I am a sacrifice for the Guru who fosters and reinforces Waheguru’s Naam with affection. Without taking Guru’s shelter, no one can acquire Waheguru’s Naam on their own, no matter how many ritualistic deeds they perform.          2.          Even though Waheguru is always close to you, residing in your heart, without good fortune, you cannot realize Him.  Normally, there is a curtain of ignorance and doubts drawn between the individual and Waheguru, as a result of which, his mind stays away from Waheguru. Without connecting to the Guru, the mind of the individual is hard like iron, and never becomes soft and pure) like gold; (with the mind being hard like iron, it does not melt in Waheguru’s love), and the individual drowns (in the sea of Maya) even though the boat (Waheguru) is close by on the shore.            3.      Satguru is the ship, and Waheguru’s Naam is the boat, but one does not know how to get on to the Naam-boat (to be ferried across the sea of Maya). Those who lead their lives according to the Guru’s Will get to ride on the boat and earn respect when connected with the Guru (and Waheguru), oh Nanak!   4.3.67.
