Hur(i) Hur(i) Naam(u) jupay aaradhay, vich(i) ugni Hur(i) jup(i) jeeviya. Bahur(i) junum(u) bhuyya, mookh(i) laaga, sursay pita maat theeviya. Jis kee vust(u) tis(u) chaytou praani, kur(i) hirdaiy Gurmukh(i) beechaar(i). Kuhou Nanak praani pehlaiy pehraiy, Hur(i) jupiyaiy kirpa dhaar(i). 1. Word Meaning:  Oodur = womb.   Munjhar(i) = inside.   Oochraiy = utters.  Smaar(i) = remember.   Aaradhay = worship.   Ugni = fire; (warmth of womb).   Bhuyya = happened.  Mookh Laaga = came in sight.  Sursay = felt joyed.  Theeviya = became.   Vust(u) = article.  Chaytou = remember.    Praani = oh man.  Hirdaiy = in the heart. 1. MEANING:   The first quarter of life begins with Waheguru implanting the seed of life in the mother’s womb, oh, the trader of Waheguru’s Naam. (According to the Hindu belief) The fetus survives and matures by remembering and reciting Waheguru’s Naam in the womb. The fetus endures the high temperature of the womb and survives by reciting Waheguru’s Naam and worshiping Him.  When the baby is born, the parents are overjoyed to see him.   (However, they ignore Waheguru, who gave them the baby), But one must keep Waheguru in mind (who gave them the baby), and taking Guru’s shelter, remember Him.   (Guru) Nanak says that in the first quarter of life, the man should recite Waheguru’s Naam with His kindness.    1. Doojaiy pehraiy raiyn(i) kaiy vunjaariya mittra, mun(u) laaga doojaiy bhaayay. Mayra, mayra kur(i) paaliyaiy vunjaariya mittra lay maat pita gul(i) laayay. Laavaiy maat pita suda gul saytee, mun(i) jaanaiy khut(i) khuvayaiy. Jo dayvaiy tisaiy na jaanaiy moorra, ditay nu luptaayay. Koyee Gurmukh(i) hovaiy su kuraiy veechaar(u), Hur(i) dhiyavaiy mun(i) liv laayay.  Kuhou Nanak doojaiy pehraiy praani tis(u) kaal(u) na kubhoon khaayay.  2. Word Meaning:  Doojaiy = the other (Maya).    Bhaayay = (in) love.   Paaliyaiy = raise. Saytee = with.   Khut(i) = earn.    Moorra = foolish; ignorant.    Luptaayay = clings.     Liv Laayay = wh full concentration.     Kaal(u) = (spiritual) death.      2. MEANING:  In the second quarter of life, man’s mind is attracted to Maya (family, 
wealth, etc.), oh, the trader of Waheguru’s Naam!  The parents hug their child and raise him, thinking that he is theirs, and with the expectation that he will earn and feed them (when they get old).  The ignorant man does not remember the Provider but clings to the gifts given by Him.   Some rare individual thinks about this reality, and taking the Guru’s shelter, recite Waheguru’s Naam with full concentration.  Such a devotee never suffers spiritual death, oh, Nanak. (PAGE 77): Ehou dhun(u) sumpaiy Maya jhootthi unt(i) chhoad(i) chuliya puchhutaayee.  Jis nu kirpa kuray Gur(u) maylay, so Hur(i) Hur(i) Naam(u) small(i). Kuhou Nanak teejaiy pehraiy praani, say jaayay milay Hur(i) naal(i).   3. Word Meaning:  Aal(i) junjaal(i) = routine family matters (which bind him more with Maya).   Chitvaiy = thinks about.     Sunchvaiy = accumulates.    J(i) = jo =which. Sukhaayee = friend (helpful).    Sumpaiy = wealth; property.    Jhootthi = does not last for ever.     Puchutaayee = repenting.    3. MEANING:  In the third quarter of life, man’s mind gets busy in worldly affairs (which do not support spirituality), oh trader in Waheguru’s Naam.  Man’s mind thinks about wealth, and about accumulating it, and does not remember Waheguru’s Naam. He never remembers Waheguru and His Naam, which is of great help at the end.  The wealth and property are not real (do not last forever), and the man leaves them behind repenting.  Waheguru connects that individual with the Guru whom He is kind to, and the individual then recites Waheguru’s Naam. Such individuals merge into Waheguru, says (Guru) Nanak.      3. Chauthaiy pehraiy raiyn(u) kaiy vunjaariya mittra, Hur(i) chullun vayla aandi.  Kur(i) sayvhou Poora Satguru vunjaariya mittra,subh chulli raiyn(u) vihaandi. Hur(i) sayvhou khin(u) khin(u), dhill mool(i) na kurhou, jit(u) asthir(u) joog(u) joog(u) hovhou. Hur(i) saytee sud maanhou ruliyaan, junum mern dookh khovhou. Gur Satgur Soami bhayd(u) na jaanhou Jit(u) mil(i) Hur(i) bhugut(i) sohaandi.  Kuhou Nanak chauthaiy pehraiy, sufliou raiyn(i) bhugutaan di. 4.Word Meaning:  Cullun Vayla = time to leave.   Aandi = brings.   Vihaandi = running out.   Khin(u) Khin(u) = every moment.  Dhill = delay.  Mool Na = not at all.    Asthir(u) = immortal.   Saytee = with.  Maanhou Ruliyaan = enjoy (happiness of union with Him).   Khovhou = get rid.  Soaami  = Lord (God).   Bhayd(u) = distinction.   Sufli ou = successful.     4.MEANING:  In the fourth quarter of the life of the human trader, who comes to this world to trade in Waheguru’s Naam, the time to leave this world arrives by Waheguru’s Huqam.  So (before death), the man should serve the Perfect Guru (without any delay) because life runs out quickly.  Serve Waheguru every moment without any delay at all, to be immortal through the eons.  Enjoy union with Waheguru all the time, and get rid of the suffering of the rebirth cycle.  Do not make any distinction between the Lord (God) and the Satguru, in whose company Waheguru’s worship becomes attractive (successful). (By doing so), The life of the devotees becomes blissful, oh Nanak.  4.1.3.

ਸਿਰੀਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਪਹਿਲੈ ਪਹਰੈ ਰੈਣਿ ਕੈ ਵਣਜਾਰਿਆ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਧਰਿ ਪਾਇਤਾ ਉਦਰੈ ਮਾਹਿ॥ ਦਸੀ ਮਾਸੀ ਮਾਨਸੁ ਕੀਆ ਵਣਜਾਰਿਆ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਕਰਿ ਮੁਹਲਤਿ ਕਰਮ ਕਮਾਹਿ॥ ਮੁਹਲਤਿ ਕਰਿ ਦੀਨੀ ਕਰਮ ਕਮਾਣੈ ਜੈਸਾ ਲਿਖਤੁ ਧੁਰਿ ਪਾਇਆ॥ ਮਾਤ ਪਿਤਾ ਭਾਈ ਸੁਤ ਬਨਿਤਾ ਤਿਨ ਭੀਤਰਿ ਪ੍ਰਭੂ ਸੰਜੋਇਆ॥ ਕਰਮ ਸੁਕਰਮ ਕਰਾਏ ਆਪੇ ਇਸੁ ਜੰਤੈ ਵਸਿ ਕਿਛੁ ਨਾਹਿ॥ ਕਹੁ ਨਾਨਕ ਪ੍ਰਾਣੀ ਪਹਿਲੈ ਪਹਰੈ ਧਰਿ ਪਾਇਤਾ ਉਦਰੈ ਮਾਹਿ॥ 1॥
ਦੂਜੈ ਪਹਰੈ ਰੈਣਿ ਕੈ ਵਣਜਾਰਿਆ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਭਰਿ ਜੋਬਨੁ ਲਹਰੀ ਦੇਇ ॥ ਬੁਰਾ ਭਲਾ ਨ ਪਛਾਣਈ ਵਣਜਾਰਿਆ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਮਨੁ ਮਤਾ ਅਹੰਮੇਇ॥  ਬੁਰਾ ਭਲਾ ਨਾ ਪਛਾਣੈ ਪ੍ਰਾਣੀ ਆਗੈ ਪੰਥੁ ਕਰਾਰਾ॥ ਪੂਰਾ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਕਬਹੂੰ ਨ ਸੇਵਿਆ ਸਿਰਿ ਠਾਢੇ ਜਮ ਜੰਦਾਰਾ॥ ਧਰਮ ਰਾਇ ਜਬ ਪਕਰਸਿ ਬਵਰੇ ਤਬ ਕਿਆ ਜਬਾਬੁ ਕਰੇਇ॥ਕਹੁ ਨਾਨਕ ਦੂਜੈ ਪਹਰੈ ਪ੍ਰਾਣੀ ਭਰਿ ਜੋਬਨੁ ਲਹਰੀ ਦੇਇ॥ 2॥ ਤੀਜੈ ਪਹਰੈ ਰੈਣਿ ਕੈ ਵਣਜਾਰਿਆ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਬਿਖੁ ਸੰਚੈ ਅੰਧੁ ਅਗਿਆਨੁ॥ ਪੁਤ੍ਰਿ ਕਲਤ੍ਰਿ ਮੋਹਿ ਲਪਟਿਆ ਵਣਜਾਰਿਆ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਲਹਰਿ ਲੋਭਾਨੁ॥ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਲਹਰਿ ਲੋਭਾਨੁ ਪਰਾਨੀ ਸੋ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਚਿਤਿ ਨ ਆਵੈ॥ ਸਾਧਸੰਗਤਿ ਸਿਉ ਸੰਗੁ ਨ ਕੀਆ ਬਹੁ ਜੋਨੀ ਦੁਖੁ ਪਾਵੈ॥ ਸਿਰਜਨਹਾਰੁ ਵਿਸਾਰਿਆ ਸੁਆਮੀ ਇਕ ਨਿਮਖ ਨ ਲਗੋ ਧਿਆਨੁ॥ ਕਹੁ ਨਾਨਕ ਪ੍ਰਾਣੀ ਤੀਜੈ ਪਹਰੈ ਬਿਖੁ ਸੰਚੇ ਅੰਧੁ ਅਗਿਆਨੁ॥ 3॥ ਚਉਥੈ ਪਹਰੈ ਰੈਣਿ ਕੈ ਵਣਜਾਰਿਆ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਦਿਨੁ ਨੇੜੈ ਆਇਆ ਸੋਇ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਸਮਾਲਿ ਤੂੰ ਵਣਜਾਰਿਆ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਤੇਰਾ ਦਰਗਹ ਬੇਲੀ ਹੋਇ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਸਮਾਲਿ ਪਰਾਣੀ ਅੰਤੇ ਹੋਇ ਸਖਾਈ॥
