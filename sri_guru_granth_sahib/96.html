(ਅੰਗ 95)॥       ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 4॥ ਹਰਿ ਗੁਣ ਪੜੀਐ ਹਰਿ ਗੁਣ ਗੁਣੀਐ॥ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮ ਕਥਾ ਨਿਤ ਸੁਣੀਐ॥ ਮਿਲਿ ਸਤਿਸੰਗਤਿ ਹਰਿ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਏ ਜਗੁ ਭਉਜਲੁ ਦੁਤਰੁ ਤਰੀਐ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਆਉ ਸਖੀ ਹਰਿ ਮੇਲੁ ਕਰੇਹਾ॥ ਮੇਰੇ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਮ ਕਾ ਮੈ ਦੇਇ ਸੁਨੇਹਾ॥ ਮੇਰਾ ਮਿਤ੍ਰੁ ਸਖਾ ਸੋ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਮੁ ਭਾਈ ਮੈ ਦਸੇ ਹਰਿ ਨਰਹਰੀਐ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਮੇਰੀ ਬੇਦਨ ਹਰਿ ਗੁਰੁ ਪੂਰਾ ਜਾਣੈ॥ ਹਉ ਰਹਿ ਨ ਸਕਾ ਬਿਨੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਵਖਾਣੈ॥ ਮੈ ਅਉਖਧੁ ਮੰਤ੍ਰੁ ਦੀਜੈ ਗੁਰ ਪੂਰੈ ਮੈ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮਿ ਉਧਰੀਐ ਜੀਉ॥ 3॥ ਹਮ ਚਾਤ੍ਰਿਕ ਦੀਨ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਸਰਣਾਈ॥ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਬੂੰਦ ਮੁਖਿ ਪਾਈ॥ ਹਰਿ ਜਲਨਿਧਿ ਹਮ ਜਲ ਕੇ ਮੀਨੇ ਜਨ ਨਾਨਕ ਜਲ ਬਿਨੁ ਮਰੀਐ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥3॥
(PAGE 95):       Majh Mehla 4: Hur(i) goon purriyaiy Hur(i) goon gooniyaiy. Hur(i) Hur(i) Naam kutha nit sooniyaiy. Mil(i) Satsungat(i) Hur(i) goon gaayay, jug(u) bhuojul dootar(u) turiyaiy jeeo. 1. Aao sukhi Hur(i) mayl(u) kurayha. Mayray Preetum ka mein dayay soonayha. Mayra mittar(u) sukha so preetum(u) bhaayee, mein dussay Hur(i) Nurhuriyaiy jeeo. 2. Mayri baydun Hur(i) Gur(u) poora jaanaiy. Huoun reh(i) na sukaan bin(u) Naam(u) vukhanaiy.   Mein aoukhudh(u) Mantar(u) deejaiy Gur Pooraiy, mein Hur(i) Hur(i) Naam(i) oodhariyaiy jeeo. 3. Hum chaatrik deen, Satgur shernaayee. Hur(i) Hur(i) Naam(u) boond mookh(i) payee. Hur(i) julnidh(i), hum jul kay meenay, jun Nanak jul bin(u) muriyaiy jeeo.   4.3.
Word Meaning:  Gooniyaiy = ponder on.  Kutha = story; explanations (of virtues).  Bhuojul = sea of life; the world-sea.   Dootar(u) = difficult; almost impossible to swim across.     1.Sukhee = female friends; devotees.   Kurayha = (let us) do (make the connection).  Soonayha = message.  Nurhuriyaiy = Waheguru.      2.  Baydun = pain (of separation).  Poora = perfect.     Vukhanaiy = utter; sing.   Aoukhudh = medication.   Mantar(u) = a collection of some special words (called mantra) the recitation of which is believed to cure some specific diseases (a Hindu belief).    Oodhariyaiy = find salvation.       3.Chaatrik = (rainbird); a mythical bird who survives only if he drinks the first raindrop on a special occasion.   Deen = powerless (pride-less).   Boond = drop.  Julnidh(i) = treasure of water – sea.   Meenay = fish.     4.
MEANING:  Let us read and ponder over the virtues of Waheguru as a congregation, oh my friends!  Let us listen to the explanations of Waheguru’s Naam.  Let us sing the virtues of Waheguru in the congregation, which will enable us to swim across the world-sea, which is almost an impossible task to perform.        1. Let us join together to plan ways to connect with Waheguru, oh my friends!  Maybe we will get some message (advice) about our dear Waheguru. He will be my dear friend, my darling brother, who tells me (the way to connect with) Waheguru.            2. The Perfect Guru knows my pain (of separation from Waheguru) that I cannot survive without reciting His Naam.  Please, dear Perfect Guru, give me the Mantra as medication so that I may recite Waheguru’s Naam by means of which one can find salvation.            3. We, human beings, are powerless like the mythical bird, chaatrik, and have come to Your shelter, oh Satguru.  Please give us a drop of Waheguru’s Naam-water so we can survive (spiritually). Waheguru is like the ocean, and we are like fish, and cannot live without Waheguru-ocean, oh Nanak.  4.2.
NOTE:  As discussed under word meanings, Sikhs do not believe in any mantra – the only Mantra is Waheguru’s Naam, which is the panacea for all diseases.  Even as a chaatrik, Guru Ram Dass Ji asks for a drop of Waheguru’s Naam-water.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 4॥ ਹਰਿ ਜਨ ਸੰਤ ਮਿਲਹੁ ਮੇਰੇ ਭਾਈ॥ ਮੇਰਾ ਹਰਿ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਦਸਹੁ ਮੈ ਭੂਖ ਲਗਾਈ॥ ਮੇਰੀ ਸਰਧਾ ਪੂਰਿ ਜਗਜੀਵਨ ਦਾਤੇ ਮਿਲਿ ਹਰਿ ਦਰਸਨਿ ਮਨੁ ਭੀਜੈ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਮਿਲਿ ਸਤਸੰਗਿ ਬੋਲੀ ਹਰਿ ਬਾਣੀ॥ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਕਥਾ ਮੇਰੈ ਮਨਿ ਭਾਣੀ॥ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਹਰਿ ਮਨਿ ਭਾਵੈ ਮਿਲਿ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਪੀਜੈ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਵਡਭਾਗੀ ਹਰਿ ਸੰਗਤਿ ਪਾਵਹਿ॥ ਭਾਗਹੀਨ ਭਰਮਿ ਚੋਟਾ ਖਾਵਹਿ॥  ਬਿਨੁ ਭਾਗਾ ਸਤਸੰਗੁ ਨ ਲਭੈ ਬਿਨੁ ਸੰਗਤਿ ਮੈਲੁ ਭਰੀਜੈ ਜੀਉ॥ 3॥ ਮੈ ਆਇ ਮਿਲਹੁ ਜਗਜੀਵਨ ਪਿਆਰੇ॥ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਦਇਆ ਮਨਿ ਧਾਰੇ॥ ਗੁਰਮਤਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਮੀਠਾ ਮਨਿ ਭਾਇਆ ਜਨ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮਿ ਮਨੁ ਭੀਜੈ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥4॥
Majh Mehla 4:  Hur(i) jun sunt milhou mayray bhaayee.  Mayra Hur(i) Prubh(u) dus-hou, mein bhookh lugayee. Mayri sherdha poor(i) Jugjeevun Daatay, mil(i) Hur(i) dershun(i) mun(u) bheejaiy jeeo. 1. Mil(i) Satsung(i) boleen Hur(i) Bani. Hur(i) Hur(i) kutha mayraiy mun(i) bhaani. Hur(i) Hur(i) Amrit(u) Hur(i) mun(i) bhaavaiy, mil(i) Satgur Amrit peejaiy jeeo. 2. Vudbhaagi Hur(i) sungat(i) paaveh(i). Bhaag-heen bherm(i) choataan khaaveh(i).  Bin(u) bhaagaan Satsung(u) na lubbhaiy, bin(u) Sungat(i) maiyl(u) bhureejaiy jeeo. 3.  Mein aayay milhou Jugjeevun piyaray. Hur(i) Hur(i) Naam(u) duyya mun(i) dhaaray. Gurmat(i) Naam(u) meettha mun(i) bhaaya, jun Nanak Naam(i) mun(u) bheejaiy jeeo.  4.4.
Word Meaning:  Hur(i) Jun Sunt = devotees of Waheguru (holy men).   Sherdha = faith; wish (to connect with God).   Jugjeevun = (source of) life of the world (God).  Hur(i) Dershun =   sight of Waheguru.   Bheejaiy = gets imbued.     1.        Bani = words of praise.   Kutha = explanation (of virtues); story.  Bhaani = I cherish; revere.  Bhaavaiy = to my liking.       2.
Bhaagheen = unlucky.  Bherm(i) = in doubts; hesitations.  Choataan Khaaveh(i) = suffer.   3.
MEANING:  I wish to meet with some holy devotees of Waheguru who would guide me to connect with Waheguru, as I have a big appetite (longing) to connect with Him. Please, fulfill my wish and show me Your kind sight so that I may be imbued in Your Naam, oh the source of (spiritual) life, Waheguru.         1.I wish to sing Your praise in the holy gathering, oh Waheguru. I like and revere explanations of Your virtues. Waheguru is the real Amrit (the mythical liquid of immortality), and I love it, which can be tasted only in the company of the Satguru.          2. Only a lucky few are able to join the holy congregation, while the unlucky ones suffer greatly from doubt and hesitation (because of their love for Maya).  One cannot access the holy congregation without luck, and the mind gathers filth without the holy congregation.  3.Please, Waheguru, kindly connect me with You, and give me the gift of Your Naam.  With the advice of the Guru, Waheguru’s Naam tastes very sweet, and I love it. The mind of Your slave, Nanak, has been imbued in Your Naam, oh Waheguru.     4. 4.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 4॥ ਹਰਿ ਗੁਰ ਗਿਆਨੁ ਹਰਿ ਰਸੁ ਹਰਿ ਪਾਇਆ॥ ਮਨੁ ਹਰਿ ਰੰਗਿ ਰਾਤਾ ਹਰਿ ਰਸੁ ਪੀਆਇਆ॥ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਮੁਖਿ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਬੋਲੀ ਮਨੁ ਹਰਿ ਰਸਿ ਟੁਲਿ ਟੁਲਿ ਪਉਦਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਆਵਹੁ ਸੰਤ ਮੈ ਗਲਿ ਮੇਲਾਈਐ॥ ਮੇਰੇ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਮ ਕੀ ਮੈ ਕਥਾ ਸੁਣਾਈਐ॥ ਹਰਿ ਕੇ ਸੰਤ ਮਿਲਹੁ ਮਨੁ ਦੇਵਾ ਜੋ ਗੁਰਬਾਣੀ ਮੁਖਿ ਚਉਦਾ ਜੀਉ॥2॥ ਵਡਭਾਗੀ ਹਰਿ ਸੰਤੁ ਮਿਲਾਇਆ॥ ਗੁਰਿ ਪੂਰੈ ਹਰਿ ਰਸੁ ਮੁਖਿ ਪਾਇਆ॥ ਭਾਗਹੀਨ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਨਹੀ ਪਾਇਆ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਗਰਭ ਜੂਨੀ ਨਿਤ ਪਉਦਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 3॥ ਆਪਿ ਦਇਆਲ ਦਇਆ ਪ੍ਰਭਿ ਧਾਰੀ॥ ਮਲੁ ਹਉਮੈ ਬਿਖਿਆ ਸਭ ਨਿਵਾਰੀ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਹਟ ਪਟਣ ਵਿਚ ਕਾਇਆ ਹਰਿ ਲੈਦੇ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਸਉਦਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥5॥
Majh Mehla 4:  Hur(i) Gur Giyan(u) Hur(i) rus(u) Hur(i) paaya. Mun(u) Hur(i) rung(i) raata, Hur(i) rus(u) peeyaya. Hur(i) Hur(i) Naam(u) mookh(i) Hur(i) Hur(i) boaleen, mun(u) Hur(i) rus(i) tooll(i) tooll(i) paunda jeeo. 1. Aavhou Sunt mein gul(i) maylaayiyaiy. Mayray Preetum kee mein kutha soonaayiyaiy. Hur(i) kay Sunt milhou mun(u) dayvaan, jo Gurbani mookh(i) chaunda jeeo. 2. Vudbhaageen Hur(i) Sunt(u) milaaya. Gur(i) Pooraiy Hur(i) rus(u) mookh(i) paaya. Bhaag-heen Satgur(u) nuhee paaya munmukh gerbh jooni nit paunda jeeo.  3.  Aap(i) duyaal duya Prubh(i) dhaari. Mul(u) haumein bikhiya subh nivaari. Nanak hut puttan vich(i) kayaan Hur(i) laiynday Gurmukh(i) sauda jeeo.    4.5. 
Word Meaning:  Giyan(u) = (Divine) Knowledge.  Rus = essence. Rung(i) Raata = imbued in His love.      Tooll(i) Tooll(i) = overflowing with zest.      1.       Kutha = story (of virtues).     Chaunda = utters; recites.        2.        Vudbhaageen = highly lucky.    Sunt = Guru.   Gerbh Jooni = different life forms.        3.          Duyaal = kind; benevolent.    Mul(u) = filth.    Bikhiya = poisonous (Maya).   Nivaari = dispelled.  Hut = bazaar.   Puttan = cities.  Kayaan = human body; mind.  Sauda = merchandise (of Waheguru’s Naam).      4.
MEANING: I have realized the Knowledge delivered by the Guru, who is a manifestation of Waheguru, and have tasted Waheguru’s Naam–essence. Now my mind is completely imbued in Waheguru’s Naam.  Now I recite Waheguru’s Naam with my mouth (tongue), and it has become my routine conversation, and my mind is overflowing with the zest of Waheguru’s Naam.        1.        Now I wish to hug the holy men tightly and listen to the virtues of my dear Waheguru from them.  I wish to surrender my mind to the holy man who recites Gurbani (Waheguru’s praise) by his tongue.              2.              With great luck, Waheguru has connected me with the Guru, and the Perfect Guru has given me the dose of Waheguru Naam-essence in my mouth (so that my tongue recites Waheguru’s Naam).  The unfortunate followers of their mind do not connect with the Guru, and as a result, they go through all the different forms of life.            3.           The benevolent Waheguru has kindly washed away all my filth of haumein and of poisonous Maya.   The followers of the Guru deal in Waheguru’s Naam in the bazaars and cities of their mind, oh Nanak.      4.5.
