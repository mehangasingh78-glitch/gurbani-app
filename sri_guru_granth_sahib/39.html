(ਅੰਗ 38)॥      ਮੁੰਧੇ ਕੂੜਿ ਮੁਠੀ ਕੂੜਿਆਰਿ॥ ਪਿਰੁ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਸਾਚਾ ਸੋਹਣਾ ਪਾਈਐ ਗੁਰ ਬੀਚਾਰਿ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਮਨਮੁਖਿ ਕੰਤੁ ਨ ਪਛਾਣਹੀ ਤਿਨ ਕਿਉ ਰੈਣਿ ਵਿਹਾਇ॥ ਗਰਬਿ ਅਟੀਆ ਤ੍ਰਿਸਨਾ ਜਲਹਿ ਦੁਖੁ ਪਾਵਹਿ ਦੂਜੈ ਭਾਇ॥ਸਬਦਿ ਰਤੀਆ ਸੋਹਾਗਣੀ ਤਿਨ ਵਿਚਹੁ ਹਉਮੈ ਜਾਇ॥ਸਦਾ ਪਿਰੁ ਰਾਵਹਿ ਆਪਣਾ ਤਿਨਾ ਸੁਖੇ ਸੁਖਿ ਵਿਹਾਇ॥ 2॥ ਗਿਆਨ ਵਿਹੂਣੀ ਪਿਰ ਮੁਤੀਆ ਪਿਰਮੁ ਨ ਪਾਇਆ ਜਾਇ॥ ਅਗਿਆਨਮਤੀ ਅੰਧੇਰੁ ਹੈ ਬਿਨੁ ਪਿਰ ਦੇਖੇ ਭੁਖ ਨ ਜਾਇ॥ਆਵਹੁ ਮਿਲਹੁ ਸਹੇਲੀਹੋ ਮੈ ਪਿਰੁ ਦੇਹੁ ਮਿਲਾਇ॥ ਪੂਰੈ ਭਾਗਿ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਮਿਲੈ ਪਿਰੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਸਚਿ ਸਮਾਇ॥ 3॥ ਸੇ ਸਹੀਆ ਸੋਹਾਗਣੀ ਜਿਨ ਕਉ ਨਦਰਿ ਕਰੇਇ॥ ਖਸਮੁ ਪਛਾਣਹਿ ਆਪਣਾ ਤਨੁ ਮਨੁ ਆਗੈ ਦੇਇ॥ ਘਰਿ ਵਰੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਆਪਣਾ ਹਉਮੈ ਦੂਰਿ ਕਰੇਇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਸੋਭਾਵੰਤੀਆ ਸੋਹਾਗਣੀ ਅਨਦਿਨੁ ਭਗਤਿ ਕਰੇਇ॥ 4॥28॥61॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 3:  Soon(i) soon(i) kaam(i) guhayliyay, kiyya chuleh(i) baanh loodayay. Aapna Pir(u) na puchhaanhi, kiyya moonhu dayseh(i) jaayay. Jinhi sukheen Kunt(u) puchhaniya houn tin kaiy laagoun paayay. Tin hee jaisi thee ruhhaan Satsungat(i) mayl(i) milaayay. 1.
(PAGE 38):       Moondhay koorr(i) mootthi koorriaar(i). Pir(u) Prubh(u) Saacha sohna paayiyaiy Gur beechaar(i). 1. Ruhaou.   Munmukh(i) kunt(i) na puchhanhi, tin kiyoun raiyn(i) vihaayay. Gerb(i) uttiyaan trishna juleh(i), dookh(u) paaveh(i) doojaiy bhaayay. Shabad(i) ruttiyaan sohaagni, tin vichoun haumein jaayay. Suda Pir(u) raaveh(i) aapna, tinha sookhay sookh(i) vihaayay. 2.  Giyaan-vihooni Pir mootiyaan, Pirm(u) na paaya jaayay. Ugiyaanmutee undhayr(u) haiy, bin Pir daykhay, bhookh na jaayay. Aavhou milhou suhaliyou, mein Pir(u) dayhou milaayay. Pooraiy bhaag(i) Satgur(u) milaiy Pir(u) paaya Such(i) smaayay. 3. Say suhiyaan sohaagni jin kuou nudr(i) kuray. Khusm(u) puchhaneh(i) aapna, tun(u) mun(u) aagaiy dayay. Ghur(i) vur(u) paaya aapna, haumein door(i) kuray. Nanak sobhavunteeyaan sohagni undin(u) bhugut(i) kuray. 4.28.61.
Word Meaning: Kaam(i) Guhayliyay = overpowered by lust.   Baanh loodayay = with arm swinging (with conceit).   Pir(u) = husband (Waheguru).    Puchhaanhi = recognize.  Kunt(u) = husband.    Thee Ruhhaan = become.       1.         Kiyoun = why; how.   Vihaayay = spends.  Gerb(i) Uttiyaan = trapped in conceit.   Trishna = desires.   Doojaiy Bhaayay = in love with another (Maya).   Ruttiyaan = imbued.  Sohaagni = loved by husband.  Raaveh(i) = enjoy.  Tinha = theirs.       2.          Vihooni = without.    Mootiyaan = condemned.   Pirm(u) = (union with) husband.   Ugiyaanmuti = without knowledge.   Undhayr(u) = darkness.   Smaayay= merge.       3.           Say = those.  Suhiyaan = female friends (women).    Khusm = master; husband (God)
Vur(u) = bridegroom.   Sobhavunteeyaan = who earn praise (with their virtues).      4.
MEANING: Listen carefully, oh young woman gripped by lust!  Why are you walking with your arms swinging (what are you so conceited about)? You do not recognize your own (real) husband (Waheguru); what kind of face will you show to Him?  I touch the feet (show respect for) of those friends who recognize their husband (Waheguru). I, too, can be like them when I connect with Waheguru in the company of such devotees.         1.         Those who deal in the mundane (are engrossed in Maya), are robbed (of their spirituality) by their false (short-lived) dealings in Maya, oh, young woman.  The true and attractive husband is Waheguru, who can be realized by pondering on the Guru’s Shabad.           Pause.           What kind of life do the followers of their mind (Maya-engrossed) have who do not recognize their real husband?  They are gripped by conceit and are full of worldly desires; consequently, they suffer because of their love for Maya. But those who are imbued in Guru’s Shabad are the true brides (loved by Waheguru-husband) as they have overcome their selfishness and ego.  They always enjoy union with their Husband, and their lives are spent in full comfort (bliss).           2.             The ignorant (without any spiritual awareness) are condemned by their Husband (Waheguru), and cannot enjoy His Company. The ignorant are in darkness; they cannot find contentment without seeing (realizing) the Husband.  Please, let me join your company, oh my devotee friends, and connect me too with Waheguru. One gets connected with the Guru only because of his good fortune, and once the Guru connects the devotee with Waheguru, he attains complete union with Him.           3.           Those, on whom Waheguru casts His kind sight, are the ones who are truly married (to Waheguru). They recognize their true Husband and offer their bodies and minds to Him. They overcome their selfishness and ego, and realize their Groom in their own heart. Such married women worship Waheguru day and night, and their fame spreads around in the world, oh Nanak. 4.28.61.  

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ ਇਕਿ ਪਿਰੁ ਰਾਵਹਿ ਆਪਣਾ ਹਉ ਕੈ ਦਰਿ ਪੂਛਉ ਜਾਇ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਸੇਵੀ ਭਾਉ ਕਰਿ ਮੈ ਪਿਰੁ ਦੇਹੁ ਮਿਲਾਇ॥ ਸਭੁ ਉਪਾਏ ਆਪੇ ਵੇਖੈ ਕਿਸੁ ਨੇੜੈ ਕਿਸੁ ਦੂਰਿ॥ ਜਿਨਿ ਪਿਰੁ ਸੰਗੇ ਜਾਣਿਆ ਪਿਰੁ ਰਾਵੈ ਸਦਾ ਹਦੂਰਿ॥ 1॥ ਮੁੰਧੇ ਤੂ ਚਲੁ ਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਭਾਇ॥ ਅਨਦਿਨੁ ਰਾਵੈ ਪਿਰੁ ਆਪਣਾ ਸਹਜੇ ਸਚਿ ਸਮਾਇ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਸਬਦਿ ਰਤੀਆ ਸੋਹਾਗਣੀ ਸਚੈ ਸਬਦਿ ਸੀਗਾਰਿ॥ ਹਰਿ ਵਰੁ ਪਾਇਨਿ ਘਰਿ ਆਪਣੈ ਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਹੇਤਿ ਪਿਆਰਿ॥ ਸੇਜ ਸੁਹਾਵੀ ਹਰਿ ਰੰਗਿ ਰਵੈ ਭਗਤਿ ਭਰੇ ਭੰਡਾਰ॥ ਸੋ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਮੁ ਮਨਿ ਵਸੈ ਜਿ ਸਭਸੈ ਦੇਇ ਆਧਾਰੁ॥2॥ ਪਿਰੁ ਸਾਲਾਹਨਿ ਆਪਣਾ ਤਿਨ ਕੈ ਸਦ ਬਲਿਹਾਰੈ ਜਾਉ॥ ਮਨੁ ਤਨੁ ਅਰਪੀ ਸਿਰੁ ਦੇਈ ਤਿਨ ਕੈ ਲਾਗਾ ਪਾਇ॥ ਜਿਨੀ ਇਕੁ ਪਛਾਣਿਆ ਦੂਜਾ ਭਾਉ ਚੁਕਾਇ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਪਛਾਣੀਐ ਨਾਨਕ ਸਚਿ ਸਮਾਇ॥ 3॥29॥62॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 3:  Ikk(i) Pir(u) raaveh(i) aapna houn kaiy dur(i) poochhoon jaayay. Satgur(u) sayveen bhaaou kur(i), mein Pir(u) dayhou milaayay. Subh(u) oopaayay Aapay vaykhaiy, kis(u) nayrraiy kis(u) door(i). Jin(i) Pir(u) sungay jaaniya, Pir(u) raavaiy suda hudoor(i). 1. Moondhay, tu chul(u) Gur kaiy bhaayay. Undin(u) raavaiy Pir(u) aapna, sehjay Such(i) smaayay. 1. Ruhaou.  Shabad(i) ruttiyaan sohagni, Sachaiy Shabad(i) seegaar(i).  Hur(i) vur(u) paayin ghur(i) aapnaiy, Gur kaiy hayt(i) piyaar(i). Sayj soohaavi Hur(i) rung(i) ruvaiy, bhugut(i) bhuray bhundaar. So Prubh Preetum(u) mun(i) vusaiy j(i) subhsaiy dayay aadhaar(u). 2. Pir(u) salaahun(i) aapna tin kaiy sud bulihaaraiy jaaoun. Mun(u) tun(u) urpeen sir(u) dayeen, tin kaiy laagaan paayay. Jini ikk(u) puchhaniya dooja bhaaou chookaayay. Gurmukh(i) Naam(u) puchhniyaiy Nanak Such(i) smaayay. 3.29.62.
Word Meaning:   Ikk(u) = many.   Raaveh(i) = enjoy.  Bhaaou = with love.   Pir(u) = husband (Waheguru).   Oopaayay = created.    Sungay = with; in the company.       1.       Moondhay = pretty young woman.   Bhaayay = in love (as per His will).   Sehjay = in the blissful spiritual state.   Smaayay = merge; become one with.         Pause.           Ryttiyaan = imbued.  Seegaar(i) = make up; adornment.   Vur(u) = bridegroom.  Ghur(i) = in the house (heart).   Hayt(i) = love.  Sayj = bed (heart).   Rung(i) = in love.   Ruvaiy = enjoys.   Bhundaar = treasures.   Preetum(u) = dear.   Aadhaar = food.        2.        Sud = always.   Bulihaaray = be a sacrifice.   Urpeen = offer.   Dooja  Bhaou = love for (Maya).   Chookaayay = finished.   Sach(i) = in Waheguru.   Smaayay = merge with.    3.
MEANING:   Lead your life as per the Will of the Guru, oh beautiful young woman (human being). If you do that, you will enjoy the love of your husband (Waheguru), and will be “one” with Him in the blissful state of mind.         Pause.           Which door should I go to (whom should I ask) to find out how some women (devotees) enjoy union with their husband (Waheguru).  (Eventually, I find out) That if I serve the Guru with dedication, He will connect me with God. Waheguru Himself looks after everybody whom He has created, but for some, He is very close to, while for some others, He is far away.  Whoever gets connected with Him, enjoys His union all the time.         1.           Adorned by the Guru’s eternal Shabad, they are imbued in the Guru’s love, and are truly married to (loved by) God.  With love and affection for the Guru, they realize Waheguru as the bridegroom in their own hearts.  The treasures of worship are full for them, and imbued in Waheguru’s love, their heart-beds become very enjoyable when they enjoy Waheguru’s Love. Waheguru, who provides food to everybody, takes residence in their heart.             2.             I would gladly sacrifice my life for those who sing the praises of their husband (Waheguru) and have recognized the only “One” by terminating their love for Maya.  I will offer my body, my mind, and my head (will get rid of my wisdom to accept theirs), and touch their feet.  (But remember), Waheguru can be recognized (realized) only by the grace of the Guru, when one becomes “one” with Waheguru, oh Nanak.   3.29.62.
