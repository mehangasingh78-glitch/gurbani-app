(ਅੰਗ 67)॥       ਬਿਨੁ ਸਬਦੇ ਜਗੁ ਦੁਖੀਆ ਫਿਰੈ ਮਨਮੁਖਾ ਨੋ ਗਈ ਖਾਇ॥ ਸਬਦੇ ਨਾਮੁ ਧਿਆਈਐ ਸਬਦੇ ਸਚਿ ਸਮਾਇ॥ 4॥ ਮਾਇਆ ਭੂਲੇ ਸਿਧ ਫਿਰਹਿ ਸਮਾਧਿ ਨ ਲਗੈ ਸੁਭਾਇ॥ ਤੀਨੇ ਲੋਅ ਵਿਆਪਤ ਹੈ ਅਧਿਕ ਰਹੀ ਲਪਟਾਇ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਗੁਰ ਮੁਕਤਿ ਨ ਪਾਈਐ ਨਾ ਦੁਬਿਧਾ ਮਾਇਆ ਜਾਇ॥ 5॥ ਮਾਇਆ ਕਿਸ ਨੋ ਆਖੀਐ ਕਿਆ ਮਾਇਆ ਕਰਮ ਕਮਾਇ॥ ਦੁਖਿ ਸੁਖਿ ਏਹੁ ਜੀਉ ਬਧੁ ਹੈ ਹਉਮੈ ਕਰਮ ਕਮਾਇ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਸਬਦੇ ਭਰਮਿ ਨ ਚੁਕਈ ਨ ਵਿਚਹੁ ਹਉਮੈ ਜਾਇ॥ 6॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਪ੍ਰੀਤੀ ਭਗਤਿ ਨ ਹੋਵਈ ਬਿਨੁ ਸਬਦੈ ਥਾਇ ਨ ਪਾਇ॥ ਸਬਦੇ ਹਉਮੈ ਮਾਰੀਐ ਮਾਇਆ ਕਾ ਭ੍ਰਮੁ ਜਾਇ॥ ਨਾਮੁ ਪਦਾਰਥੁ ਪਾਈਐ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਸਹਜਿ ਸੁਭਾਇ॥ 7॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਗੁਰ ਗੁਣ ਨ ਜਾਪਨੀ ਬਿਨੁ ਗੁਣ ਭਗਤਿ ਨ ਹੋਇ॥ ਭਗਤਿ ਵਛਲੁ ਹਰਿ ਮਨਿ ਵਸਿਆ ਸਹਜਿ ਮਿਲਿਆ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਸੋਇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਸਬਦੇ ਹਰਿ ਸਾਲਾਹੀਐ ਕਰਮਿ ਪਰਾਪਤਿ ਹੋਇ॥ 8॥4॥21॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 3:  Gurmukh(i) Naam(u) dhiyayiyaiy munmukh(i) boojh na paayay. Gurmukh(i) suda mookh oojlay, Hur(i) vusiyaa mun(i) aayay. Sehjay hee sookh paayiyaiy, sehjay ruhaiy smaayay. 1. Bhayee ray, daasun(i) daasaan hoyay. Gur kee sayva Gur bhugut(i) haiy, virla paayay koyay.  1. Ruhaou.   Suda soohaag(u) soohaagni, jay chuleh(i) Satgur bhaayay. Suda Pir(u) nihchul(u) paayiyaiy,  na ohu muraiy na jaayay. Shabad(i) mili na veechhurraiy, Pir kaiy unk(i) smaayay. 2. Hur(i) nirmul(u) ut(i) oojla, bin(u) Gur paaya na jaayay.  Paatth(u) purraiy na boojhyee, bhaykheen bherm(i) bhoolaayay.  Gurmut(i) Hur(i) suda paaya, rusna Hur(i) rus(u) smaayay.  3. Maya moh(u) chookaaya Gurmut(i) sehj(i) soobhaayay.

Shabaday Naam(u) dhiyayiyaiy, Shabaday Sach(i) smaayay. 4.  Maya bhoolay Sidh phireh(i), smaadh(i) na lugaiy soobhaayay. Teenay loe viyaput haiy, udhik ruhee luptaayay. Bin(u) Gur mookt(i) na paayiyaiy, na doobhidha Maya jaayay. 5. Maya kis no aakhiyaiy, kiya Maya kerm kmaayay. Dookh(i) sookh(i) eho jeeo budh(u) haiy, haumein kerm kmaayay. Bin(u) Shabadaiy bherm(i) na chookyee, na vichoun haumein jaayay. 6.  Bin(u) preeti bhugut(i) na hovyee, bin(u) Shabadaiy thaayayn na paayay. Shabday haumein maariyaiy Maya ka bherm(u) jaayay. Naam(u) pdaruth(u) paayiyaiy Gurmukh(i) sehj(i) soobhaayay. 7. Bin(u) Gur goon na jaapni, bin(u) goon bhugut(i) na hoyay.  Bhugut(i) vuchhul(u) Hur(i) mun(i) vusiya, sehj(i) miliya Prubh(u) soyay.  Nanak Shabaday Hur(i) salaahiyaiy kerm(i) praput(i) hoyay.    8.4.21.
Word Meaning:  Naam(u) dhiyayiyaiy = recite Naam (diligently).    Boojh = understanding.    Oojlaiy = shining; clean (honourable).   Sehjay (mental state above the effects of Maya).    Smaayay= fully absorbed (in Naam).       1.    Daasun(i) Daasaan = slave of slaves.   Virla = rare.    Pause.      Soohaag(u) = (company of) husband.    Bhaayay = (with) affection.  Pir(u) = dear; husband.     Nihchul(u) = immovable; stable.   Unk(i) = (in the) heart.    2.
Nirmul(u) = clean; pure.   Oojla = shining.    Bhaykheen = (with) religious symbols.  Bherm(i) Bhoolaayay = lost (misguided) in doubts.   Rusna = tongue (which tastes).    3.
Chookaayiya = cleared; got rid of.  Soobhaayay= (with) affection.       4.   Sidh = yogis who hich seek to acquire supernatural powers.   Smaadh(i) = sit in concentration.   Teenay Loe = three-layered universe.   Viyaput = affecting.   Udhik = much.   Luptaayay = clinging.     Doobhidha = duality; conflicting views.      5.    Budh(u) = is shackled.    Bherm(i) = misguidance.      6.     Thaayayn na paayay = does not get respect; not recognized.   Pdaruth(u) = (valuable) 
article (Waheguru’s Naam).   Soobhayay = good intentions.   7.
Jaapni = realization.    Vuchhul (u) = loving; lover.    Kerm(i) = benevolence.       8.
MEANING: It is a rare individual who realizes that the true service to the Guru is worshipping Him (obeying His Teachings).  So (to earn Waheguru’s approval), act like a slave of His slaves (serve the devotees of the Guru), oh my brother.    Pause.   
The follower of his mind does not realize that Waheguru’s Naam can be worshipped only in the shelter of the Guru.  The faces of the devotees of the Guru are always shiny (honourable with the confidence of noble qualities) because they have made their heart a residence for Waheguru. With the spiritual state above the effects of Maya, a devotee remains connected to Waheguru and enjoys all comforts.           1.          If a human bride leads her life as per the Will of the Guru, she always enjoys the company of the Waheguru-husband and stays married (connected with Him) all the time. Waheguru-husband is eternal, and He never dies or leaves.  Once connected with Waheguru through the Guru’s Shabad, the devotee never experiences separation from Him and clings to His heart (his soul merges into the Supreme Soul).        2.
Waheguru is pure and cannot be realized without the Guru.  Ritualistic recitation of mantras does not enlighten anyone; they wander, misguided, wearing symbols of different faiths. All those who take Guru’s advice (act on it), realize Waheguru, and their tongue is always imbued in the taste (love) of His Naam.            3.          Acting on Guru’s advice, they routinely overcome the attractions of Maya without any special effort.  Without Guru’s Shabad, people are in pain (they suffer), and Maya swallows the followers of their mind (it kills their spirituality, robbing them of good qualities).  We can recite Waheguru’s Naam and become “one” with Waheguru only through the Guru’s Shabad.           4.          Even the Sidhs (yogis who meditate to acquire supernatural powers) are misguided by Maya, and cannot concentrate on any power with dedication.   Maya is affecting people in all three worlds (earth, the underworld and the sky) in a big way.  One cannot overcome the fickleness of the mind and get freedom from the bonds of Maya without the Guru.           5.            What is called Maya, and what kinds of deeds does one perform under the influence of Maya?  (Under the influence of Maya), A man commits deeds of selfishness and ego, including the acquisition of comforts and freedom from discomforts.  But man’s misgivings are not cleared without the shelter of the Guru, and he is not able to overcome his selfishness and ego.         6.
Also, one cannot worship Waheguru without love for Him, and his efforts are not recognized without the Guru’s Shabad.  Man’s haumein can be eradicated by Guru’s Shabad, and his doubts about the futility of Maya are cleared. By taking Guru’s shelter, one attains the spiritual state where one feels no effects of Maya and acquires invaluable Naam.       7.
Without the Guru, one does not recognize the noble qualities without which one cannot worship God.  With the high spiritual state (above the effects of Maya), Waheguru, which loves his worship (devotees), takes residence in the devotee’s mind, and the latter becomes “one” with Him. Waheguru can be praised by Guru’s Shabad - that too only by the benevolence of Guru, oh Nanak.   8. 4. 21

ਸਿਰੀਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ 

ਮਾਇਆ ਮੋਹੁ ਮੇਰੈ ਪ੍ਰਭਿ ਕੀਨਾ ਆਪੇ ਭਰਮਿ ਭੁਲਾਏ॥ ਮਨਮੁਖਿ ਕਰਮ ਕਰਹਿ ਨਹੀ ਬੂਝਹਿ ਬਿਰਥਾ ਜਨਮੁ ਗਵਾਏ॥ ਗੁਰਬਾਣੀ ਇਸੁ ਜਗ ਮਹਿ ਚਾਨਣੁ ਕਰਮਿ ਵਸੈ ਮਨਿ ਆਏ॥ 1॥ ਮਨ ਰੇ ਨਾਮੁ ਜਪਹੁ ਸੁਖੁ ਹੋਇ॥ ਗੁਰੁ ਪੂਰਾ ਸਾਲਾਹੀਐ ਸਹਜਿ ਮਿਲੈ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਸੋਇ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਭਰਮੁ ਗਇਆ ਭਉ ਭਾਗਿਆ ਹਰਿ ਚਰਣੀ ਚਿਤੁ ਲਾਇ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਸਬਦੁ ਕਮਾਈਐ ਹਰਿ ਵਸੈ ਮਨਿ ਆਇ॥ ਘਰਿ ਮਹਲਿ ਸਚਿ ਸਮਾਈਐ ਜਮਕਾਲੁ ਨ ਸਕੈ ਖਾਇ॥ 2॥ ਨਾਮਾ ਛੀਬਾ ਕਬੀਰੁ ਜੁੋਲਾਹਾ ਪੂਰੇ ਗੁਰ ਤੇ ਗਤਿ ਪਾਈ॥ ਬ੍ਰਹਮ ਕੇ ਬੇਤੇ ਸਬਦੁ ਪਛਾਣਹਿ ਹਉਮੈ ਜਾਤਿ ਗਵਾਈ॥ ਸੁਰਿ ਨਰ ਤਿਨ ਕੀ ਬਾਣੀ ਗਾਵਹਿ ਕੋਇ ਨ ਮੇਟੈ ਭਾਈ॥ 3॥ ਦੈਤ ਪੁਤੁ ਕਰਮ ਧਰਮ ਕਿਛੁ ਸੰਜਮ ਨ ਪੜੈ ਦੂਜਾ ਭਾਉ ਨ ਜਾਣੈ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਭੇਟਿਐ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਹੋਆ ਅਨਦਿਨੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਵਖਾਣੈ॥ ਏਕੋ ਪੜੈ ਏਕੋ ਨਾਉ ਬੂਝੈ ਦੂਜਾ ਅਵਰੁ ਨ ਜਾਣੈ॥ 4॥ ਖਟੁ ਦਰਸਨ ਜੋਗੀ ਸੰਨਿਆਸੀ ਬਿਨੁ ਗੁਰ ਭਰਮਿ ਭੁਲਾਏ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਸੇਵਹਿ ਤਾ ਗਤਿ ਮਿਤਿ ਪਾਵਹਿ ਹਰਿ ਜੀਉੇ ਮੰਨਿ ਵਸਾਏ॥ ਸਚੀ ਬਾਣੀ ਸਿਉ ਚਿਤੁ ਲਾਗੈ ਆਵਣੁ ਜਾਣੁ ਰਹਾਏ॥ 5॥ ਪੰਡਿਤ ਪੜਿ ਪੜਿ ਵਾਦੁ ਵਖਾਣਹਿ ਬਿਨੁ ਗੁਰ ਭਰਮਿ ਭੁਲਾਏ॥ ਲਖ ਚਉਰਾਸੀਹ ਫੇਰੁ ਪਇਆ ਬਿਨੁ ਸਬਦੈ ਮੁਕਤਿ ਨ ਪਾਏ॥ ਜਾ ਨਾਉ ਚੇਤੈ ਤਾ ਗਤਿ ਪਾਏ ਜਾ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਮੇਲਿ ਮਿਲਾਏ॥ 6॥ ਸਤਸੰਗਤਿ ਮਹਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਹਰਿ ਉਪਜੈ ਜਾ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਮਿਲੈ ਸੁਭਾਏ॥
