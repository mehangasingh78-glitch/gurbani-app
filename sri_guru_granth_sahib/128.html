Majh Mehla 3:   Hur(i) aapay maylay sayv kuraayay.  Gur kaiy Shabad(i) bhaao dooja jaayay. Hur(i) nirmul(u) suda goondaata, Hur(i) goon meh(i) Aap(i) smaavniya. 1. Huoun vaari jeeo vaari, Sach(u) Sacha hirdaiy vusaavniya.  Sacha Naam(u) suda haiy nirmul(u), Gur Shabadi munn(i) vusaavniya. 1. Ruhaou. Aapay Gur Daata, kerm bidhaata. Sayvuk sayveh(i), Gurmukh(i) Hur(i) jaata.  Amrit Naam(i) suda jun soheh(i), Gurmut(i) Hur(i) rus(u) paavniya. 2.  Iss(u) goofa meh(i) ikk(u) thaan(u) soohaayiya.  Pooraiy Gur(i) haumein bherm(u) chookaaya. Undin(u) Naam(u) slaahun(i) rung(i) raatay, Gur kirpa tay paavniya. 3.  
(PAGE 127):        Gur kaiy Shabad(i) eho goofa veechaaray. Naam(u) Nirunjun(u) untar(i) vusay Mooraray.  Hur(i) goon gaavaiy Shabad(i) soohaayay, mil(i) Preetum sookh(u) paavniya. 4. Jum(u) jagaati doojaiy bhaayay kur(u) laayay. Naavoun bhoolay dayay sujaayay. Ghurri moohut ka laykha layvaiy, ruttioun maasa toal kudaavniya.  5. Payiurraiy Pir(u) chaytay naahi. Doojaiy mootthi, rovaiy dhaahi. Khuri kooaaliyo kuroop(i) koolukhani, soopnay Pir(u) nuhee paavniya. 6. Payiurraiy Pir(u) munn(i) vusaaya. Pooraiy Gur(i) hudoor(i) dikhaaya.  Kaamun(i) Pir(u) raakhiya kuntth(i) laayay, Shabaday Pir(u) raavaiy sayj soohaavniya. 7.  Aapay dayvaiy sud(i) boolaayay. Aaapna Naoun munn(i) vusaayay. Nanak Naam(u) milaiy vudiyaayee undin(u) suda goon gaavniya.     8.28.29.
Word Meaning:  Bhaao Dooja = love for Maya.     Nirmul(u) = clean; pure.   Goondaata = source of virtues.   Smaavniya = is mixed with (present).       1.
Hirdaiy = in the heart.    Munn(i) Visaavniya = is embedded in mind.        Pause.
Kerm Bidhaara = writer of fate.   Jaata = recognized; connected with.   Jun = devotees.    Soheh(i) = look dignified; are adorned.    Rus(u) = taste; essence.         2. 
Goofa = cave (heart).  Thaan(u) = place (heart).  Bherm = misbeliefs.        3.
Nirunjun(u) = above the effects of Maya (unjun); Waheguru.    Mooraray = God.       4.
Jum = angel of death.    Jagaati = the tax collector (punishes).    Kur(u) = tax (punishment for bad deeds).   Ghurri Moohut = every moment.   Laykha = account.  Rutti Maasa = very small units of weight for precious metals.    Toal =weight.        5.
Payiurraiy = parents’ home (this life).     Pir(u) = husband (Waheguru).   Chaytay = remember.  Mootthi = deceived.   Rovaiy Dhaahi = wail; cry aloud.   Khuri = much.   Kooaaliyo = of bad clan.  Kuroop(i) = ugly.   Koolukhni = having bad qualities (vices). Soopnay = (in) dreams.        6.     Hudoor(i) = right in front.   Kuntth Laayay = hold tightly to the neck (remember always).      Raavaiy = enjoys.   Sayj = bed (of heart).         7.
Sud Boolaayay = call in.     Vudiyaayee = praise; respect.         8.
MEANING:  Waheguru, Himself, connects some devotees with Him and makes them serve Him (recite His Naam).  Such devotees lose their love for Maya with the Guru’s Shabad.  God is always pure (Perfect), and is the source of all virtues – actually, He is present in virtues (where there is virtue, there is God).        1.      
I am a sacrifice for all those who have embedded His eternal Naam in their heart.  Waheguru’s eternal Naam is always pure and is embedded in the mind by the Guru’s Shabad.        Pause.         Waheguru, Himself is the Guru, and the writer of the fate of living beings.  The devotees serve the Guru and come to recognize Waheguru (get connected with Him).   The devotees always look dignified with Waheguru’s Naam at heart, and enjoy the essence of His Naam in the shelter of the Guru (with His guidance).         2.          The human mind in the body is adorned when the Guru dispels the doubts and misbeliefs of the individual.  Imbued in the love of Waheguru, such devotees sing His praise day and night with the grace of the Guru.         3.        One analyzes his mind with Guru’s Shabad, when Waheguru’s Naam comes to dwell in his mind.  With the pleasing (comforting) Shabad of the Guru, he sings Waheguru’s virtues and feels blissful to connect with God.        4.          The individuals who love Maya and have forgotten Waheguru’s Naam are heavily taxed (given punishment) by the tax collector, the Angel of justice. He strictly demands the account of every moment like a goldsmith who weighs gold to the last rutti.         5.       A human bride, who does not remember her husband (Waheguru) in the present life, cries loudly as she is robbed of good qualities by her love for Maya. In the judgment of Waheguru, as husband, she is like a wife of a very low class, ugly, and with bad qualities, and who can never enjoy the company of her husband, even in her dreams.        6.
The Guru “shows” the presence of Waheguru clearly to the devotee who enshrines Waheguru’s Naam in his heart. With Guru’s Shabad, such a human bride keeps Waheguru close to her heart all the time, and enjoys the company of the husband (Waheguru) on the bed of her mind which is adorned by virtues now.       7. 
Waheguru calls such a devotee in and gives His Naam for the devotee to embed in his mind.  Waheguru’s Naam wins respect when a devotee sings His virtues day and night, oh Nanak!      8.28.29.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 3: ਊਤਮ ਜਨਮੁ ਸੁਥਾਨਿ ਹੈ ਵਾਸਾ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਸੇਵਹਿ ਘਰ ਮਾਹਿ ਉਦਾਸਾ॥ ਹਰਿ ਰੰਗਿ ਰਹਹਿ ਸਦਾ ਰੰਗਿ ਰਾਤੇ ਹਰਿ ਰਸੁ ਮਨੁ ਤ੍ਰਿਪਤਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਹਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਜੀਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਪੜਿ ਬੁਝਿ ਮੰਨਿ ਵਸਾਵਣਿਆ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਪੜਹਿ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਸਾਲਾਹਹਿ ਦਰਿ ਸਚੈ ਸੋਭਾ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਅਲਖ ਅਭੇਉ ਹਰਿ ਰਹਿਆ ਸਮਾਏ॥ ਉਪਾਇ ਨ ਕਿਤੀ ਪਾਇਆ ਜਾਏ॥ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਕਰੇ ਤਾ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਭੇਟੈ ਨਦਰੀ ਮੇਲਿ ਮਿਲਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 2॥ ਦੂਜੈ ਭਾਇ ਪੜੈ ਨਹੀ ਬੂਝੈ॥ ਤ੍ਰਿਬਧਿ ਮਾਇਆ ਕਾਰਣਿ ਲੂਝੈ॥ ਤ੍ਰਿਬਧਿ ਬੰਧਨ ਤੂਟੈ ਗੁਰ ਸਬਦੀ ਗੁਰ ਸਬਦੀ ਮੁਕਤਿ ਕਰਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 3॥ ਇਹੁ ਮਨ ਚੰਚਲੁ ਵਸਿ ਨ ਆਵੈ॥ ਦੁਬਿਧਾ ਲਾਗੈ ਦਹਦਿਸਿ ਧਾਵੈ॥ ਬਿਖੁ ਕਾ ਕੀੜਾ ਬਿਖੁ ਮਹਿ ਰਾਤਾ ਬਿਖੁ ਹੀ ਮਾਹਿ ਪਚਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 4॥ ਹਉ ਹਉ ਕਰੇ ਤੈ ਆਪੁ ਜਣਾਏ॥ ਬਹੁ ਕਰਮ ਕਰੈ ਕਿਛੁ ਥਾਇ ਨ ਪਾਏ॥ ਤੁਝ ਤੇ ਬਾਹਰਿ ਕਿਛੂ ਨ ਹੋਵੈ ਬਖਸੇ ਸਬਦਿ ਸੁਹਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 5॥ ਉਪਜੈ ਪਚੈ ਹਰਿ ਬੂਝੈ ਨਾਹੀ॥ ਅਨਦਿਨੁ ਦੂਜੈ ਭਾਇ ਫਿਰਾਹੀ॥ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਜਨਮੁ ਗਇਆ ਹੈ ਬਿਰਥਾ ਅੰਤਿ ਗਇਆ ਪਛੁਤਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 6॥ ਪਿਰੁ ਪਰਦੇਸਿ ਸਿਗਾਰੁ ਬਣਾਏ॥ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਅੰਧੁ ਐਸੇ ਕਰਮ ਕਮਾਏ॥ ਹਲਤਿ ਨ ਸੋਭਾ ਪਲਤਿ ਨ ਢੋਈ ਬਿਰਥਾ ਜਨਮੁ ਗਵਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 7॥ ਹਰਿ ਕਾ ਨਾਮੁ ਕਿਨੈ ਵਿਰਲੈ ਜਾਤਾ॥ ਪੂਰੈ ਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਸਬਦਿ ਪਛਾਤਾ॥ ਅਨਦਿਨੁ ਭਗਤਿ ਕਰੇ ਦਿਨੁ ਰਾਤੀ ਸਹਜੇ ਹੀ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 8॥ ਸਭ ਮਹਿ ਵਰਤੈ ਏਕੋ ਸੋਈ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਵਿਰਲਾ ਬੂਝੈ ਕੋਈ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮਿ ਰਤੇ ਜਨ ਸੋਹਹਿ ਕਰਿ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਆਪਿ ਮਿਲਾਵਣਿਆ॥  9॥29॥30॥
Majh Mehla 3:  Ootum junum(u) soothaan(i) haiy vaasa. Satgur(u) sayveh(i) ghur maah(i) oodaasa. Hur(i) rung(i) ruheh(i) suda rung(i) raatay, Hur(i) rus(u) mun(u) triptaavniya. 1. Huoun vaari jeeo vaari, purrh(i) boojh(i) munn(i) vusaavniya. Gurmukh(i) purreh(i), Hur(i) Naam salaaheh(i), Dur(i) Sachaiy sobha paavniya. 1. Ruhaou.  Alukh Abhayo Hur(i) ruhiya smaayay. Oopaayay na kiti paaya jaayay. Kirpa kuray ta Satgur(u) bhaytaiy, nudri mayl(i) milaavniya. 2. Doojaiy bhaayay purreh(i), nuhee boojhay. Tribidh(i) Maya kaarun(i) loojhaiy. Tribidh(i) bundhun tootaiy Gur Shabadi, Gur Shabadi mookt(i) kraavniya. 3.  Eho mun chunchul(u) vus na aavaiy. Doobidha laagaiy dehdis(i) dhaavaiy. Bikh(u) ka keerra bikh(u) meh(i) raata, bikh(u) hee maah(i)  puchaavniya. 4. Huoun, huoun kuray taiy aap(u) junaayay. Buhou kerm kuraiy, kichh(u) thaayayn na paayay. Toojh tay baahur(i) kichhoo na hovaiy, bukhshay Shabad(i) soohaavniya.  5. Oopjaiy puchaiy, Hur(i) boojhaiy naahi. Undin(u) doojaiy bhaaya phiraahi. Munmukh junum(u) guyya haiy birtha, unt(i) guyya puchhutaavniya. 6.  Pir(u) perdays(i), sigaar(u) bunaayay. Munmukh undh(u) aisay kerm kmaayay. Hult(i) na sobha, pult(i) na dhoyee, birtha junum(u) guvaavniya.  7. Hur(i) ka Naam(u) kinaiy virlaiy jaata. Pooraiy Gur kaiy Shabad(i) puchhaata. Undin(u) bhagat(i) kuray din(u) raati, sehjay hee sookh(u) paavniya. 8.  Subh meh(i) vertaiy Aiko soyee.  Gurmukh(i) virla boojhaiy koyee. Nanak Naam(i) rutay jun soheh(i), kur kirpa Aap(i) milaavniya.   9.29.30.
Word Meaning:  Ootum = superior.  Soothaan(i) = beautiful place; clean (pure) heart.
Ghur Maah(i) = in the house (heart).    Oodaasa = detachment from Maya.  Rung(i) = in love.  Raatay = imbued.   Triptaavniya = is contented.       1. 
Boojh(i) = understand; unlock secrets.   Munn(i) Vusaavniya = embed in mind.     Dur(i) Sachay = in Waheguru’s Court.       Pause.       Alukh = inconceivable.   Abhayo = whose secrets cannot be known.   Opaayay = means.   Na Kiti = not by any.    Bhaytaiy = connect with.       2.        Doojaiy Bhaayay = love for Maya.   Tribidh(i) = having three (effects).   Kaarun(i) = because of.  Loojhaiy = struggles.   Bundhun = bonds.  Tootaiy = are broken.   Mookt(i) = free (from Maya).      3.      Chunchul = fickle; unsettling; wandering.   Vus = control.   Doobidha = caught between two choices (spiritual and Maya); indecisiveness.   Dehdis = in ten (every) directions.   Dhaavaiy = runs fast.   Bikh = poison (Maya).   Keerra = worm.  Raata = imbued.   Puchaavniya = suffer.      4.
Houn Houn = I am, I am (self-praise); egoist.   Junaayay = shows off.    Kerm = rituals.   Thaayayn Na Paayay = finds no support; is not approved; not even acknowledged.      5.         Oopjaiy = is born.  Puchaiy = suffers; dies.  Birtha = empty.   Unt(i) = in the end.     6.        Pir(u) = husband.    Perdays = in other country; far away.   Sigaar = adorn.   Kerm = deeds.  Hult(i) = this world.  Pult(i) = the next world.   Dhoyee = shelter, support.     7.     Virlaiy = rare. Puchhaata = recognized; made a connection.  Sehjay = without effort.     8.      Vertaiy = operates.  Aiko Soyee = only one Waheguru.      9.
MEANING:  A devotee, who serves (obeys) the Guru, becomes detached from Maya.   His heart becomes a beautiful (virtuous) place, and his life becomes much superior. Such devotees are always imbued in Waheguru’s love, and are contented enjoying the taste of Waheguru’s Naam.        1.          I offer my life to those who enshrine Waheguru’s Naam in their heart by reading about Him, and getting enlightened.   A devotee of the Guru, who reads about Waheguru and sings His praise, finds respect in His Court.      Pause.         The inconceivable Waheguru, whose secrets cannot be known, is omnipresent, but cannot be realized by any means at the personal level.    The only way to realize Him is by taking the Guru’s shelter, that too, if Waheguru casts a kind sight on an individual, and kindly connects him with the Guru, who then connects the individual with Him.         2.         If a money-minded individual does read about Waheguru, he cannot realize Him (because his mind is not pure), and he still struggles against his love for Maya.  The bonds of the Maya, which have three effects, are broken only with the Guru’s Shabad.          3. The human mind is highly fickle and difficult to control.  It wanders very fast in every possible direction.  Imbued in the love of poisonous Maya, the individual suffers like a worm in poison, which jumps in pain.       4.
A money-minded person shows himself off everywhere, and the egoist indulges in self-praise.  He does many deeds to show off, but finds no recognition.  Nothing happens outside of Waheguru, who forgives and refines His devotees with Guru’s Shabad.     5.
A Maya-engrossed individual is born and dies without recognizing (making a connection with) Waheguru; he wanders day and night in the love for Maya.  The spiritual life of the money-minded person goes to waste, and he leaves this world repenting.          6.    
The follower of his mind is ignorant and performs some worthless rituals. His efforts are like those of a married woman who beautifies herself when her husband is far away (for whom does she beautify herself?)    Such a person gets no praise in this world, and no shelter (support) in the next; his life goes completely to waste.       7.
A rare person makes a connection with Waheguru’s Naam by the Guru’s Shabad. He worships Waheguru day and night, and enjoys bliss without any extra effort.      8.
Taking Guru’s shelter, some rare devotee realizes that it is Waheguru alone which operates everywhere. The devotees imbued in Waheguru Naam find respect, and Waheguru Himself connects them with Him by His Grace.     9.29.30.
