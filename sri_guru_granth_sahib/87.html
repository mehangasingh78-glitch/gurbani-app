(ਅੰਗ 86)॥   ਮ:3॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਸੇਵਿ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਸਚੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਗੁਣਤਾਸੁ॥ ਗੁਰਮਤੀ ਆਪੁ ਪਛਾਣਿਆ ਰਾਮ ਨਾਮ ਪਰਗਾਸੁ॥ ਸਚੋ ਸਚੁ ਕਮਾਵਣਾ ਵਡਿਆਈ ਵਡੇ ਪਾਸਿ॥ ਜੀਉ ਪਿੰਡੁ ਸਭਿ ਤਿਸ ਕਾ ਸਿਫਤਿ ਕਰੇ ਅਰਦਾਸਿ॥ ਸਚੈ ਸਬਦਿ ਸਾਲਾਹਣਾ ਸੁਖੇ ਸੁਖਿ ਨਿਵਾਸੁ॥ ਜਪੁ ਤਪੁ ਸੰਜਮੁ ਮਨੈ ਮਾਹਿ ਬਿਨੁ ਨਾਵੈ ਧ੍ਰਿਗੁ ਜੀਵਾਸਿ॥ ਗੁਰਮਤੀ ਨਾਉ ਪਾਈਐ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਮੋਹਿ ਵਿਣਾਸੁ॥ ਜਿਉ ਭਾਵੈ ਤਿਉ ਰਾਖੁ ਤੂ ਨਾਨਕ ਤੇਰਾ ਦਾਸੁ॥ 2॥
(PAGE 86):          M:3: Satgur(u) sayv sookh(u) paaya, Sach(u) Naam(u) Goontaas(u).  Gurmati aap(u) puchhaniya, Ram Naam pergaas(u). Sacho Sach(u) kmaavna, vudiyayee Vuday paas(i). Jeeo pind(u) subh(i) Tis ka sift(i) kuray ardaas(i). Sachaiy Shabad(i) salahna, sookhay sookh(i) nivaas(u).  Jup(u) tup(u) sunjum(u) munaiy maah(i), bin(u) Naavaiy dhrig(u) jeevaas(i). Gurmatee Naoun paayiyaiy, munmukh moh(i) vinaas(u). Jiyoun bhaaviy tiyoun raakh(u) Tu, Nanak Tayra daas(u).  2.
Word Meaning:  Goontaas(u) = treasure of all virtues (God).  Puchhaniya = recognized; realized.   Pergaas(u) = enlightenment.   Vuday = the Supreme (Waheguru) Jeeo = life.    Pind(u) = body.   Sift(i) = praise.   Nivaas(u) = residence.   Sunjum(u) = control on desires.   Dhrig(u) = condemnable.   Vinaas(u) = destruction.      2.
MEANING:  Waheguru’s eternal Naam is the treasure of all virtues, and can be acquired by serving (obeying) Satguru. One recognizes oneself by following the advice of the Guru when his mind is enlightened with Waheguru’s Naam.  Man’s duty is to lead an honest life, keeping Waheguru in mind all the time; however, the recognition of one’s deeds is in the hands of the supreme Waheguru. Waheguru has given life and body to us; our main objective should be just to sing His praise, which can be sung by Satguru’s Shabad only, and because of which the individual’s life is spent in comfort.  Such an individual needs no ritualistic rendering of any mantras, self-torture, or need to put in any special effort to control his desires, as the devotee is already exercising them in his mind (they are subsumed in Waheguru’s Naam). Life is condemnable without Waheguru’s Naam, which can be realized by following the advice of the Guru, but the spirituality of the follower of his mind is crushed in worldly attachments.  Kindly protect me from worldly attachments as You please, oh Waheguru, prays Your slave, (Guru) Nanak.    2.

ਪਉੜੀ॥ ਸਭੁ ਕੋ ਤੇਰਾ ਤੂੰ ਸਭਸੁ ਦਾ ਤੂੰ ਸਭਨਾ ਰਾਸਿ॥ ਸਭਿ ਤੁਧੈ ਪਾਸਹੁ ਮੰਗਦੇ ਨਿਤ ਕਰਿ ਅਰਦਾਸਿ॥ ਜਿਸੁ ਤੂੰ ਦੇਹਿ ਤਿਸੁ ਸਭੁ ਕਿਛੁ ਮਿਲੈ ਇਕਨਾ ਦੂਰਿ ਹੈ ਪਾਸਿ॥ ਤੁਧੁ ਬਾਝਹੁ ਥਾਉ ਕੋ ਨਾਹੀ ਜਿਸੁ ਪਾਸਹੁ ਮੰਗੀਐ ਮਨਿ ਵੇਖਹੁ ਕੋ ਨਿਰਜਾਸਿ॥ ਸਭਿ ਤੁਧੈ ਨੋ ਸਾਲਾਹਦੇ ਦਰਿ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਾ ਨੋ ਪਰਗਾਸਿ॥ 9॥
Paurri:  Subh(u) ko Tayra, Toon subhs(u) da, Tu subhna raas(i). Subh(i) Toodhaiy paasoun mungday, nit kur(i) ardaas(i). Jis Tu dayh(i), tis(u) subh(u) kichh(u) milaiy, ikkna door(i) haiy paas(i).  Toodh(u) baajhoun thaaoun ko naahi, jis paasoun mungiyaiy, mun(i) vaykhou ko nirjaas(i). Subh(i) Toodhaiy nu salaahnday dur(i) Gurmukhaan nu pergaas(i).   9.
Word Meaning:  Raas(i) = capital; wealth.   Baajhoun = without.   Nirjaas(u) = verify.
MEANING:  Everybody is Yours, and You belong to everybody, and Your Naam is the capital for investment (to acquire noble qualities), oh Waheguru.  Everybody prays to You all the time for the fulfillment of their needs. You are close to some and far away from others; the individual whom You choose to give to receives everything. There is no place other than You from where one can ask for (the fulfilment of their needs); if someone does not believe it, he can verify it himself. Everybody praises You, but only the true devotees are enlightened at Your Door (in Your shelter), oh Waheguru.   9.

ਸਲੋਕ ਮ:3॥ ਪੰਡਿਤੁ ਪੜਿ ਪੜਿ ਉਚਾ ਕੂਕਦਾ ਮਾਇਆ ਮੋਹੁ ਪਿਆਰੁ॥ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਬ੍ਰਹਮ ਨ ਚੀਨਈ ਮਨਿ ਮੂਰਖੁ ਗਾਵਾਰੁ॥ ਦੂਜੈ ਭਾਇ ਜਗਤੁ ਪਰਬੋਧਦਾ ਨ ਬੂਝੈ ਬੀਚਾਰੁ॥ ਬਿਰਥਾ ਜਨਮੁ ਗਵਾਇਆ ਮਰਿ ਜੰਮੈ ਵਾਰੋ ਵਾਰ॥ 1॥
Sloak M:3:  Pundit purrh(i) purrh(i) oocha kookda, Maya moh(u) piyaar(u). Untar(i) Braham na cheenyee, mun(i) moorukh(u) gaavaar(u).  Doojay bhayay jugat(u) perbodh-da, na boojhaiy beechaar(u). Birtha junum(u) guvaaya, mer(i) jummaiy vaaro vaar. 1.
Word Meaning:   Kookda = reads in very loud voice.   Cheenyee = recognizes.    Gaavaar = unrefined.   Perbodh-da = preaches.   Birtha = empty; useless.
MEANING: In the love for money, the pundit reads the Vedas to the people in a very loud voice.  The foolish and the unrefined do not recognize Waheguru inside them.  In the love for Maya, he preaches the world, but does not realize the reality (Truth) himself.  He wastes away his precious human life and is reborn again and again. 1.

ਮ:3॥ ਜਿਨੀ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਸੇਵਿਆ ਤਿਨੀ ਨਾਉ ਪਾਇਆ ਬੂਝਹੁ ਕਰਿ ਬੀਚਾਰੁ॥ ਸਦਾ ਸਾਂਤਿ ਸੁਖੁ ਮਨਿ ਵਸੈ ਚੂਕੈ ਕੂਕ ਪੁਕਾਰ॥ ਆਪੈ ਨੋ ਆਪੁ ਖਾਇ ਮਨੁ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਹੋਵੈ ਗੁਰ ਸਬਦੀ ਵੀਚਾਰੁ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਸਬਦਿ ਰਤੇ ਸੇ ਮੁਕਤੁ ਹੈ ਹਰਿ ਜੀਉ ਹੇਤਿ ਪਿਆਰੁ॥ 2॥
M:3: Jinhi Satguru sayviya, tinhi Naoun paaya, boojhou kur(i) beechaar(u).  Suda shaant(i), sookh(u) mun(i) vusaiy, chookaiy kook pookaar.  Aapaiy nu aap(u) khaayay, mun(u) nirmul(u) hovaiy, Gur Shabadi veechaar(u). Nanak Shabad(i) ruttay say mookt(u) haiy, Hur(i) jeeo hayt(i) piyaar(u).  2.
Word Meaning:  Boojhou = find out.  Shaant(i) = peaceful.   Chookaiy = ends.   Kook Pookaar = beg loudly for help.    Ruttay = imbued.    Mookt(u) = emancipation.   Hayt(i) = for.
MEANING: Careful searching reveals that only those who realize Waheguru’s Naam serve Satguru.  They are always at peace and are blissful in mind; all their loud prayers and begging are over.  By pondering (and acting) on the Guru’s Shabad, one is able to kill his selfishness when his mind becomes pure.  Those who are imbued with Guru’s Shabad and have love for Waheguru attain freedom from the bonds of Maya (attain emancipation), oh Nanak.   2.

ਪਉੜੀ॥ ਹਰਿ ਕੀ ਸੇਵਾ ਸਫਲ ਹੈ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਪਾਵੈ ਥਾਇ॥ ਜਿਸੁ ਹਰਿ ਭਾਵੈ ਤਿਸੁ ਗੁਰੁ ਮਿਲੈ ਸੋ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਧਿਆਇ॥ ਗੁਰ ਸਬਦੀ ਹਰਿ ਪਾਈਐ ਹਰਿ ਪਾਰਿ ਲਘਾਇ॥ ਮਨ ਹਠਿ ਕਿਨੈ ਨ ਪਾਇਓ ਪੂਛਹੁ ਵੇਦਾ ਜਾਇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਹਰਿ ਕੀ ਸੇਵਾ ਸੋ ਕਰੇ ਜਿਸੁ ਲਏ ਹਰਿ ਲਾਇ॥ 10॥
Paurri: Hur(i) kee sayva suful haiy, Gurmukh(i) paavaiy thaayayn. Jis(u) Hur(i) bhaavaiy tis(u) Gur(u) milaiy, so Hur(i) Naam(u) dhiyayay.  Gur Shabadi Hur(i) paayiyaiy, Hur(i) paar(i) lunghaayay.  Mun hutth(i) kinaiy na paayo,  poochhou Vedaan jaayay. Nanak Hur(i) kee sayva so kuray, jis(u) luyay Hur(i) laayay.  10.
Word Meaning:  Suful = bears fruit; results in success.    Paavaiy Thaayayn = is recognized (rewarded).   Mun Hutth(i) = obstinacy of mind; stubbornness.    
MEANING: Service to Waheguru in the shelter of the Guru bears fruit and is rewarded. Those who love Waheguru are connected to the Guru and recite Waheguru’s Naam.  Waheguru is realized by acting on Guru’s Shabad, who (Waheguru) ferries them across the sea of Maya.  Nobody has ever realized Waheguru with the stubbornness of his mind (with his own efforts), even the Vedas testify to this fact.  Only those serve Waheguru who are prompted by Him to do so, oh Nanak.  10.

ਸਲੋਕ ਮ:3॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਸੋ ਸੂਰਾ ਵਰੀਆਮੁ ਜਿਨਿ ਵਿਚਹੁ ਦੁਸਟੁ ਅਹੰਕਰਣੁ ਮਾਰਿਆ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਸਾਲਾਹਿ ਜਨਮੁ ਸਵਾਰਿਆ॥ ਆਪਿ ਹੋਆ ਸਦਾ ਮੁਕਤੁ ਸਭੁ ਕੁਲੁ ਨਿਸਤਾਰਿਆ॥ ਸੋਹਨਿ ਸਚਿ ਦੁਆਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਪਿਆਰਿਆ॥ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਮਰਹਿ ਅਹੰਕਾਰਿ ਮਰਣੁ ਵਿਗਾੜਿਆ॥ ਸਭੋ ਵਰਤੈ ਹੁਕਮੁ ਕਿਆ ਕਰਹਿ ਵਿਚਾਰਿਆ॥ ਆਪਹੁ ਦੂਜੈ ਲਗਿ ਖਸਮੁ ਵਿਸਾਰਿਆ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਬਿਨੁ ਨਾਵੈ ਸਭੁ ਦੁਖੁ ਸੁਖੁ ਵਿਸਾਰਿਆ॥ 1॥
Sloak M:3: Nanak so soora vuriyam(u) jin(i) vichoun doosht(u) ahunkern(u) maariya. Gurmukh(i) Naam(u) salaah(i), junum(u) suvaariya. Aap(i) hoa suda mookt(u), subh(u) kool(u) nistaariya. Sohun(i) Sach(i) dooaar(i), Naam(u) piyaariya. Munmukh mureh(i) ahunkaa(ri) mern(u) vigarriya. Subho vertaiy Huqam(u), kiya kureh(i) vichaariya. Aap-houn doojaiy lug(i), Khusm(u) visariya. Nanak bin(u) Naavaiy subh(u) dookh(u), sookh(u) visariya.  1.
Word Meaning:  Soora = Vuriyam(u) = valiant; gallant.   Doosht(u) = evil.  Ahunkern(u) = conceit; ego.  Suvaariya = made virtuous.   Kool(u) = clan; accompanying devotees.    Nistaariya = salvaged.   Sohun(i) = look attractive; look dignified.    Khusm(u) = Lord (Waheguru).   Visaariya = forgotten. 
MEANING: The real valiant is the one who eradicates (overcomes) his evil ego, oh Nanak.  The devotee of the Guru makes his life beautiful by praising Waheguru’s Naam.  Not only does he become free from the bonds of Maya, but he also salvages all those devotees who join his company.  In love with Waheguru’s Naam, they are highly exalted in Waheguru’s Court (honoured there).  The followers of their mind die in conceit, thereby defiling (ruining) their death too (because they will be punished).  But such individuals cannot be solely blamed for their acts because they are helpless against Waheguru’s Huqam which is prevailing everywhere (they are predestined to follow the path of love for Maya).  They indulge in Maya, and forget Waheguru completely.  It is all discomfort without Waheguru’s Naam; the comfort forgets (by-passes) them, oh Nanak.  1.

ਮ:3॥ ਗੁਰਿ ਪੂਰੈ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਦ੍ਰਿੜਾਇਆ ਤਿਨ ਵਿਚਹੁ ਭਰਮੁ ਚੁਕਾਇਆ॥ ਰਾਮ ਨਾਮੁ ਹਰਿ ਕੀਰਤਿ ਗਾਈ ਕਰਿ ਚਾਨਣੁ ਮਗੁ ਦਿਖਾਇਆ॥ ਹਉਮੈ ਮਾਰਿ ਏਕ ਲਿਵ ਲਾਗੀ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ
ਵਸਾਇਆ॥
