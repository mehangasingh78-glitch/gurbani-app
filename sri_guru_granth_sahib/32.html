(ਅੰਗ 31)॥   ਸਿਰੀਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਛੋਡਿ ਦੂਜੇ ਲਗੈ ਸੇਵਾ ਕਰਹਿ ਵਿਡਾਣੀ॥ ਆਪਣਾ ਧਰਮੁ ਗਵਾਵਹਿ ਬੂਝਹਿ ਨਾਹੀ ਅਨਦਿਨੁ ਦੁਖਿ ਵਿਹਾਣੀ॥ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਅੰਧੁ ਨ ਚੇਤਹੀ ਡੂਬਿ ਮੁਏ ਬਿਨੁ ਪਾਣੀ॥1॥ ਮਨ ਰੇ ਸਦਾ ਭਜਹੁ ਹਰਿ ਸਰਣਾਈ॥ ਗੁਰ ਕਾ ਸਬਦੁ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਵਸੈ ਤਾ ਹਰਿ ਵਿਸਰਿ ਨ ਜਾਈ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਇਹੁ ਸਰੀਰੁ ਮਾਇਆ ਕਾ ਪੁਤਲਾ ਵਿਚਿ ਹਉਮੈ ਦੁਸਟੀ ਪਾਈ॥ ਆਣਾ ਜਾਣਾ ਜੰਮਣੁ ਮਰਣਾ ਮਨਮੁਖਿ ਪਤਿ ਗਵਾਈ॥ ਸਤਗੁਰੁ ਸੇਵਿ ਸਦਾ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਜੋਤੀ ਜੋਤਿ ਮਿਲਾਈ॥2॥ ਸਤਗੁਰ ਕੀ ਸੇਵਾ ਅਤਿ ਸੁਖਾਲੀ ਜੋ ਇਛੇ ਸੋ ਫਲੁ ਪਾਏ॥ ਜਤੁ ਸਤੁ ਤਪੁ ਪਵਿਤੁ ਸਰੀਰਾ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਮੰਨਿ ਵਸਾਏ॥ ਸਦਾ ਅਨੰਦਿ ਰਹੈ ਦਿਨੁ ਰਾਤੀ ਮਿਲਿ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਮ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਾਏ॥ 3॥ ਜੋ ਸਤਗੁਰ ਕੀ ਸਰਣਾਗਤੀ ਹਉ ਤਿਨ ਕੈ ਬਲਿ ਜਾਉ॥ ਦਰਿ ਸਚੈ ਸਚੀ ਵਡਿਆਈ ਸਹਜੇ ਸਚਿ ਸਮਾਉ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਦਰੀ ਪਾਈਐ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਮੇਲਿ ਮਿਲਾਉ॥ 4॥12॥45॥
(PAGE 31):                          Siri Raag(u) Mehla 3:  Amrit(u) chhoad(i) doojay luggaiy, sayva kureh(i) vidaani. Aapna dherm(u) guvaaveh(i), boojhaiyy naahi, undin(u) dookh(i) vihaani. Manmukh undh(u) na chayt-hee, doob(i) mooyay bin(u) paani. 1. Mun ray, suda bhujou Hur(i) shernaayee. Gur ka Shabad(u) untar(i) vusaiy, ta Hur(i) vissar(i) na jaayee. 1. Ruhaou. Eho sreer(u) Maya ka pootla, vich(i) haumein dooshti paayee. Aana jaana, jumun(u) murna, manmukh(i) put(i) guvaayee. Satgur(u) sayv(i) suda sookh(u) paaya joti Jot(i) milaayee. 2. Satgur kee sayva ut(i) sookhaali, jo ichhaiy so ful(u) paayay. Jut(u) sut(i) tup(i) pvit(u) sreera, Hur(i) Hur(i) mun(i) vusaayay. Suda anund(i) ruhhaiyy din(u) raati, mil(i) Preetum sookh(u) paayay. 3. Jo Satgur kee shernaagtee, houn tin kaiy bul(i) jaoun. Dur(i) Suchaiyy Suchee vudiyaayee, sehjay Such(i) smaaoun. Nanak nudri paayiyaiy Gurmukh(i) mayl(i) milaou.  4.12.45. 
Word Meaning:  Amrit = life-sustaining water (Waheguru’s Naam).  Doojay = the other (Maya).  Vidaani = of a stranger (others).    Dherm(u) = religion (principles, obligations).   Boojhaiyy = realize.   Undin(u) = day and night.   Vihaani = passes by.  Chayt-hee = remembers; cites (Naam).   Doob(i) Mooyay = die by drowning.      1.
Bhujou=worship Waheguru. Shernaayee = in the shelter.  Vissar(i) = forgotten.            Pause. 
Pootla = puppet.   Dooshti = evil.   Put(i) = honour.   Joti = Waheguru’s light in man.  Jot(i) = the source of light (Waheguru).       2.        Ut(i) = extreme.   Sookhaali = comforting.   Ichhaiy = wishes.    Ful(u) = fruit (result).  Jut(u) = control on lust.  Sut(u) = high moral character.  Tup = self-torture to please some god.  Pvit(u) = pure.   Anund(i) = in bliss.   Preetum = dear (Waheguru).         3.       Sheenaagtee = in the shelter of.   Bul(i) = sacrificial.   Dur(i) = door.   Vudiyaayee = honour.   Nudri = kind sight.    4. 
MEANING: Those who forget Waheguru’s Naam, and are engaged in Maya, are serving a stranger (from whom they will draw no spiritual benefit).  They do not realize that they are losing their religiosity (spirituality or noble qualities). They spend their entire life in suffering.  The follower of his mind is blind (ignorant) and drowns without water in the sea of Maya.       1.
Seek Waheguru’s shelter and worship Him all the time, oh my mind.  (As a result) Waheguru will take dwelling in your heart, and you will never forget Him.          Pause. 
The human body is a puppet of Maya and harbours evil, selfishness, and ego in the mind.  (Consequently), He is caught in the birth-death cycle and loses his honour (in Waheguru’s Court).  Serving the Satguru provides the eternal comfort and connects man’s light (soul) with the Super-Soul (Waheguru.        2.        Service to the Satguru (obeying Him) is extremely comforting, and one is conferred with whatever one wishes.  When Waheguru’s Naam takes residence in one’s heart, his mind and body are purified, and he does not need to torture himself or do ritualistic hard labour to control his sensual pleasures.  Being one with God, one is always blissful and enjoys all comforts.         3.
I will gladly sacrifice my life for those who come to Waheguru’s shelter.  In the court of the True Lord, one receives real honour and becomes “one” with the dear Lord without any special effort.  However, you get connected with Waheguru only through the Guru, and that too only when He casts His kind sight on you, oh Nanak.        4.12.45.

ਸਿਰੀਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਕਰਮ ਕਮਾਵਣੇ ਜਿਉ ਦੋਹਾਗਣਿ ਤਨਿ ਸੀਗਾਰੁ॥ ਸੇਜੈ ਕੰਤੁ ਨ ਆਵਈ ਨਿਤ ਨਿਤ ਹੋਇ ਖੁਆਰੁ॥ ਪਿਰ ਕਾ ਮਹਲੁ ਨ ਪਾਵਈ ਨਾ ਦੀਸੈ ਘਰੁ ਬਾਰੁ॥1॥ ਭਾਈ ਰੇ ਇਕ ਮਨਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਧਿਆਇ॥ ਸੰਤਾ ਸੰਗਤਿ ਮਿਲਿ ਰਹੈ ਜਪਿ ਰਾਮ ਨਾਮੁ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਾਇ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਸਦਾ ਸੋਹਾਗਣੀ ਪਿਰੁ ਰਾਖਿਆ ਉਰ ਧਾਰਿ॥ ਮਿਠਾ ਬੋਲਹਿ ਨਿਵਿ ਚਲਹਿ ਸੇਜੈ ਰਵੈ ਭਤਾਰੁ॥ ਸੋਭਾਵੰਤੀ ਸੋਹਾਗਣੀ ਜਿਨ ਗੁਰ ਕਾ ਹੇਤੁ ਅਪਾਰੁ॥ 2॥ ਪੂਰੈ ਭਾਗਿ ਸਤਗੁਰੁ ਮਿਲੈ ਜਾ ਭਾਗੈ ਕਾ ਉਦਉ ਹੋਇ॥ ਅੰਤਰਹੁ ਦੁਖੁ ਭ੍ਰਮੁ ਕਟੀਐ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਰਾਪਤਿ ਹੋਇ॥ ਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਭਾਣੈ ਜੋ ਚਲੈ ਦੁਖੁ ਨ ਪਾਵੈ ਕੋਇ॥3॥ ਗੁਰ ਕੇ ਭਾਣੇ ਵਿਚਿ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਹੈ ਸਹਜੇ  ਪਾਵੈ ਕੋਇ॥ ਜਿਨਾ ਪਰਾਪਤਿ ਤਿਨ ਪੀਆ ਹਉਮੈ ਵਿਚਹੁ ਖੋਇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਧਿਆਈਐ ਸਚਿ ਮਿਲਾਵਾ ਹੋਇ॥ 4॥13॥46॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 3: Munmukh kerm kmaavnay jiyoun dohaagun(i) tun(i) seegaar(u). sayjaiy kunt(u) na aavyee nit nit hoyay khooar(u). Pir ka mehl(u) na paavyee na deesaiy ghur(u) baar(u). 1. Bhaayee ray, ikk mun(i) Naam(u) dhiyayay. Suntaan sungat(i) mil(i) ruhaiy, jup(i) Ram Naam(u) sookh(u) paayay. 1. Ruhaou.  Gurmukh(i) suda sohaagni, pir(u) raakhiya oordhaar(i). MIttha boleh(i) niv(i) chuleh(i) sayjaiy ruvaiy bhutaar(u). Sobhavuntee sohaagni, jin Gur ka hayt(u) apaar(u). 2. Pooraiy bhaag(i) Satgur(u) milaiy, ja bhaagaiy ka oodou hoyay. Untroun dookh(u) bherm(u) kuttiyaiy sookh(u) praput(i) hoyay. Gur kaiy bhaanaiy jo chulaiy, dookh(u) na paavaiy koyay. 3. Gur kay bhaanay vich(i) Amrit haiy, sehjay paavaiy koyay. Jina praput(i) tin peeya, haumein vichoun khoyay. Nanak Gurmukh(i) Naam(u) dhiyayaiy, Such(i) milavaa hoyay. 4.13.46.
Word Meaning:  Dohaagun(i) = condemned by the husband.   Seegaar(u) = adornment.  Sajaiy = on the bed.  Kunt(u) = husband.   Khooar(u) = suffer humiliation.  Pir = husband.   Mehl(u) = (God’s) palace; residence (union with).       1.       Ikk mun(i) = with a focused mind.      Pause.            Sohaagni = married and loved by her husband.  Oor = heart.   Dhaar(i) = acquired.   Niv(i) = kneel down (humble).  Ruvaiy = enjoys.   Bhutaar(u) = husband.   Sobhavuntee = praise worthy.   Hayt(u) = affection.   Apaar(u) = limitless.       2.      Bhaag(i) = fortune.   Oodou = lights up.    Bherm(u) = skepticism.  Bhaanaiy = as per the wish.  Chulaiy = walks (leads life).        3.          Amrit(u) = life-sustaining water.   Sehjay = (in the high spiritual state above all desires).    Praput(i) = acquired.        4. 
MEANING:  The deeds performed by the follower of his mind are (spiritually) as worthless as the body adornment of the woman who has been condemned by her husband, because of which, he will not come to her bed, and she will suffer humiliation every day. Similarly, the follower of his mind will never get close to Waheguru’s residence or His door, let alone have union with Him.       1.            Recite Waheguru’s Naam with full dedication in the company of the true devotees, and acquire all the comforts, oh my friend.        Pause.
Those who obey the Satguru acquire Waheguru’s Naam in their heart, and stay always married (enjoy Waheguru’s union).  They always use sweet language and are humble.  They enjoy union with Waheguru just as a woman enjoys it with her husband in bed.  Those who have unlimited affection for the Guru earn praise and are always married to Waheguru (enjoy His union).            2.         When a person's good fortune lights up (shows its arrival), he meets the Guru; all his doubts in mind disappear, his discomforts disappear, and he acquires all comforts.  Those who lead their life as preached by the Guru suffer no discomfort.        3.
Obeying the Guru leads to the acquisition of the immortalizing holy water (Waheguru’s Naam-Amrit), which can be tasted only by those who have attained the highest spiritual level (above all the effects of Maya). Those who are able to taste it, have their haumein destroyed. To have union with Waheguru, we should recite Waheguru’s Naam in Guru’s shelter, oh Nanak.      4.13.46.
