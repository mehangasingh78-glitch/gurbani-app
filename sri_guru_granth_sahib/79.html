(ਅੰਗ 78)॥          ਇਹੁ ਮੋਹੁ ਮਾਇਆ ਤੇਰੈ ਸੰਗਿ ਨ ਚਾਲੈ ਝੂਠੀ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਿ ਲਗਾਈ॥ ਸਗਲੀ ਰੈਣਿ ਗੁਦਰੀ ਅੰਧਿਆਰੀ ਸੇਵਿ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਚਾਨਣੁ ਹੋਇ॥ ਕਹੁ ਨਾਨਕ ਪ੍ਰਾਣੀ ਚਉਥੈ ਪਹਰੈ ਦਿਨੁ ਨੇੜੈ ਆਇਆ ਸੋਇ॥ 4॥ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਆਇਆ ਗੋਵਿੰਦ ਕਾ ਵਣਜਾਰਿਆ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਉਠਿ ਚਲੇ ਕਮਾਣਾ ਸਾਥਿ॥ ਇਕ ਰਤੀ ਬਿਲਮ ਨ ਦੇਵਨੀ ਵਣਜਾਰਿਆ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਓਨੀ ਤਕੜੇ ਪਾਏ ਹਾਥ॥ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਆਇਆ ਪਕੜਿ ਚਲਾਇਆ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਸਦਾ ਦੁਹੇਲੇ॥ ਜਿਨੀ ਪੂਰਾ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਸੇਵਿਆ ਸੇ ਦਰਗਹ ਸਦਾ ਸੁਹੇਲੇ॥ ਕਰਮ ਧਰਤੀ ਸਰੀਰੁ ਜੁਗ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਜੋ ਬੋਵੈ ਸੋ ਖਾਤਿ॥ ਕਹੁ ਨਾਨਕ ਭਗਤ ਸੋਹਹਿ ਦਰਵਾਰੇ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਸਦਾ ਭਵਾਤਿ॥ 5॥1॥4॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 5:  Pehlaiy Pehraiy raiyn(i) kaiy vunjaariya mittra, dhur(i) paayayta oodraiy maah(i).  Duseen maaseen maanus(u) keeya vunjaariya mittra, kur(i) moohlut(i) kerm kmaahay.  Moohlut(i) kur(i) deeni kerm kmaanaiy, jaiysa likhut(u) dhoor(i) paaya. Maat pita bhaayee bunita, tin bheeter(i) Prubh(u) sunjoya. Kerm sookerm kraayay Aapa,y is(u) juntaiy vus(i) kichh(u) naah(i). Kuhou Nanak praani pehlaiy pehraiy, dhur(i) paayayta oodraiy maah(i).     1. Word Meaning:  Oodraiy Maah(i) = in the womb.   Maanus(u) = human.   Moohlut(i) = time limit.   Kerm = deeds.   Dhoor(i) = from the beginning.   Soot = son (children).  Bunita = wife (spouse).   Sunjoya = mixed; bonded.   Sookerm = noble deeds.   Juntaiy = living being.    Vus(i) = in control.    1. MEANING:  The first quarter of the life of the trader of Waheguru’s Naam starts with the conception in the mother’s womb as per the Will of Waheguru.  In ten months, the fetus is born a human, given a time limit to perform deeds. Within that time limit, the man performs deeds as per his predetermined nature (character) based on the deeds of his previous lives.  As per his predetermined destiny, Waheguru bonds the child among his parents, children, and spouse.   Waheguru Himself makes the individual perform good or bad deeds; human beings have no control on the nature of deeds they perform.   The first quarter of a man's life starts with conception in the mother’s womb, oh Nanak.   1.Doojaiy pehraiy raiyn(i) kaiy vunjaariya mittra, bhur(i) jooaani lehreen dayay.   Boora bhula na puchhanyee vunjaariya mittra, mun(u) mutta ahumay. Boora bhula na puchhaanaiy praani, aagaiy punth(u) kraara. Poora Satgur kubhoon na sayviya, sir(i) tthandhay Jum jundaara. Dherm Rai jub pukrus(i) buvray, tub kiyya jubaab(u) kuray. Kuhou Nanak doojaiy pehraiy praani, bhur(i) jobun(u) lehreen dayay   2. Word Meaning:   Bhur(i) Jooaani = in the prime of youth.  Lehreen = waves of desires.   Puchhaanyee = recognize.    Mutta = intoxicated.   Ahumay = ego.  Punth = journey.    Kraara = tough; difficult.   Kubhoon na = never.   Tthandhay = standing; watching.   Jundaara = unkind.   Dhermroy = justice of deeds.   Buvray = ignorant.   2. MEANING: In the second quarter of life, one is in the prime of his youth, and the waves of sensual desires rise strongly in his mind.  Intoxicated with ego, the man does not make any distinction between good and evil.  One performs deeds indiscriminately, unaware that the journey to the next world is very difficult. One never worships the Perfect Guru and does not realize that the unkind Angel of death is looming over his head (watching him closely).  How will the ignorant respond when the Justice of deeds questions him (about his evil deeds)?  (Guru) Nanak says that in the second quarter of one’s life, the waves of sensual desires arise strongly.     2.Teejaiy pehraiy raiyn(i) kaiy vunjaariya mittra, bikh(u) sunchaiy undh(u) ugiyan(u). Pootar(i) klutar(i) moh(i) luptiya vunjaariya mittra, untar(i) lehr(i) lobhaan(u). Untar(i) lehr(i) lobhaan(u) praani, so Prubh(u) chit(i) na aavaiy. Sadhsungat siyoun sung(u) na keeya, buhou joani dookh(u) paavaiy.  Sirjunhaar(u) visariya Soami, ikk nimukh na lugo dhiyaan(u).  Kuhou Nanak teejaiy pehraiy praani, bikh(u) sunchay undh(u) ugiyaan(u).      3. Word Meaning:  Bikh(u) = poisonous (Maya).   Sunchaiy = accumulates.    Ugiyaan(u) = ignorant.     Klutar(i) = wife (spouse).  Luptiya = clung to.   Lehr(i) = wave.    Lobhaan(u) = greed.     Chit(i) = in mind.   Sadhsungat = holy congregation.  Sung(u) = company.   Joani = life forms.    Sirjunhaar(u) = the Creator.   Visariya = dropped from memory.   Soaami = Lord.  Nimukh = moment.    Dhiyaan(u) = mind.  3. MEANING:  In the third quarter of life, the blind and the ignorant trader of Waheguru’s Naam, accumulates poisonous Maya. With the waves of worldly attachment in his mind, the greedy clings to his children and the spouse.   With waves of desires in mind, Waheguru does not come in his thoughts.  He does not join any holy congregation, as a result of which, he suffers through many life forms.  The Creator and the Lord (Waheguru) has been forgotten, and he does not focus his mind on Him even for a short moment.  In the third quarter, the blind and ignorant accumulates (only) wealth, oh Nanak.  3. Chauthaiy pehraiy raiyn(i) kaiy vunjaariya mittra, din(u) nayrraiy aaya soyay. Gurmukh(i) Naam(u) smaal(i) tu vunjaariya mittra, tayra Durgeh bayli hoyay. Gurmukh(i) Naam(u) smaal(i) praani, untay hoyay sukhayee. 
(PAGE78):    Ehou moh(u) Maya tayraiy sung(i) na chaalaiy, jhootthee preet(i) lugayee.  Sugli raiyn(u) goodri undhiyaari, sayv(i) Satgur(u) chaanun(u) hoyay. Kuhou Nanak praani chauthaiy pehraiy din(u) nayrraiy aaya soyay.  4. Word Meaning:  Din = the day (of death).   Bayli = friend.   Sukhayee = friend (supporter).   Moh(u) = attachment.   Sung(i) = with.    Sugli = whole.  Channun(u) = light (enlightenment).  MEANING:  In the fourth quarter of the life of man, the day of death approaches near. So, you should recite Waheguru’s Naam by taking shelter of the Guru, which will be your supporter and your friend in Waheguru’s Court, oh, the trader of Waheguru’s Naam.   Love for Maya will not accompany you, which is purely temporary. The whole night of your life is spent in darkness (ignorance); serve the Satguru to enlighten your mind.  (Guru) Nanak says that the day of death approaches near in the fourth quarter of life, oh man. 4.Likhiya aaya Govind ka vunjaariya mittra, ootth(i) chulay kmaana saath(i).  Ikk rutti bilum na dayvni vunjaariya mittra, ohni tukrray paayay haath.  Likhiya aaya pukkurr(i) chulaya, munmukh suda doohaylay.  Jinhi Poora Satgur(u) sayvia, say Durgeh suda soohaylay.   Kerm dherti sreer(u) joog untar(i), jo bovaiy so khaat(i). Kuhou Nanak bhugut sohay Durvaaray munmukh suda bhuvaat(i).  5. Word Meaning:  Govind = (a name for) Waheguru.   Kmaana = deeds done.  Rutti = a very small unit of weight or time.    Bilum = delay.    Doohaylay = suffer in discomfort.    Soohaylay = blissful.   Bovaiy = sows.    Khaat(i) = eats; reaps.   Bhuvaat(i) = taken through (various life forms). MEANING: When Waheguru’s written Huqam (about a man’s death) is implemented, a man’s deeds done during his life accompany him (to the next world).  The agents of the Justice of deeds grab the man with powerful hands and give him no time at all before taking him away.  As per the written orders of Waheguru, the agents grab the man and take him away; the followers of their mind always suffer discomfort.  On the other hand, the devotees, who serve the Perfect Guru, are blissful in Waheguru’s Court. The human body is the ground for man to plant deeds in this world; the man reaps the results of what he sows (the kinds of deeds he performs).   The devotees look dignified (are honoured) in Waheguru’s Court, while the followers of their mind are cycled through various life forms, oh Nanak.   5.1.4.  
NOTE: There are no documents written by Waheguru. His Will is everything.  Such language is used for our understanding only. 
NOTE: CHHUNT: Chhunt or Chhund is a form of lyric which follows a specific metre. Even though there may be exceptions, a typical chhunt has four verses, each with six sentences. The above four Pehrays are also chhunts. They are given a special name, Pehray, simply because they describe the four quarters (Pehray) of life.


ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 4 ਘਰੁ 2 ਛੰਤ॥ ੴ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਪ੍ਰਸਾਦਿ॥
            Siri Raag(u) Mehla 4 Ghur(u) 2 Chhunt:  Ek Onkar Satguru Persad(i)
ਪ੍ਰਸਾਦਿ॥ ਮੁੰਧ ਇਆਣੀ ਪੇਈਅੜੇ ਕਿਉ ਕਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਦਰਸਨੁ ਪਿਖੈ॥ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਅਪਨੀ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਕਰੇ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਸਾਹੁਰੜੈ ਕੰਮ ਸਿਖੈ॥ ਸਾਹੁਰੜੈ ਕੰਮ ਸਿਖੈ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਸਦਾ ਧਿਆਏ॥ ਸਹੀਆ ਵਿਚਿ ਫਿਰੈ ਸੁਹੇਲੀ ਹਰਿ ਦਰਗਹ ਬਾਹ ਲੁਡਾਏ॥ ਲੇਖਾ ਧਰਮਰਾਇ ਕੀ ਬਾਕੀ ਜਪਿ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਕਿਰਖੈ॥ ਮੁੰਧ ਇਆਣੀ ਪੇਈਅੜੈ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਹਰਿ ਦਰਸਨੁ ਦਿਖੈ॥ 1॥ ਵੀਆਹੁ ਹੋਆ ਮੇਰੇ ਬਾਬੁਲਾ ਗੁਰਮੁਖੇ ਹਰਿ ਪਾਇਆ॥ ਅਗਿਆਨੁ ਅੰਧੇਰਾ ਕਟਿਆ ਗੁਰ ਗਿਆਨੁ ਪ੍ਰਚੰਡੁ ਬਲਾਇਆ॥ ਬਲਿਆ ਗੁਰ ਗਿਆਨੁ ਅੰਧੇਰਾ ਬਿਨਸਿਆ ਹਰਿ ਰਤਨੁ ਪਦਾਰਥੁ ਲਾਧਾ॥ ਹਉਮੈ ਰੋਗੁ ਗਇਆ ਦੁਖੁ ਲਾਥਾ ਆਪੁ ਆਪੈ ਗੁਰਮਤਿ ਖਾਧਾ॥ ਅਕਾਲ ਮੂਰਤਿ ਵਰੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਅਬਿਨਾਸੀ ਨਾ ਕਦੇ ਮਰੈ ਨ ਜਾਇਆ॥ ਵੀਆਹੁ ਹੋਆ ਮੇਰੇ ਬਾਬੋਲਾ ਗੁਰਮੁਖੇ ਹਰਿ ਪਾਇਆ॥2॥ ਹਰਿ ਸਤਿ ਸਤੇ ਮੇਰੇ ਬਾਬੁਲਾ ਹਰਿ ਜਨ ਮਿਲਿ ਜੰਞ ਸੁਹੰਦੀ॥ ਪੇਵਕੜੈ ਹਰਿ ਜਪਿ ਸੁਹੇਲੀ ਵਿਚਿ ਸਾਹੁਰੜੈ ਖਰੀ ਸੋਹੰਦੀ॥ ਸਾਹੁਰੜੈ ਵਿਚਿ ਖਰੀ ਸੋਹੰਦੀ ਜਿਨਿ ਪੇਵਕੜੈ ਨਾਮੁ ਸਮਾਲਿਆ॥ ਸਭੁ ਸਫਲਿਓ ਜਨਮੁ ਤਿਨਾ ਦਾ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਜਿਨਾ ਮਨੁ ਜਿਣਿ ਪਾਸਾ ਢਾਲਿਆ॥ ਹਰਿ ਸੰਤ ਜਨਾ ਮਿਲਿ ਕਾਰਜੁ ਸੋਹਿਆ ਵਰੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਪੁਰਖੁ ਅਨੰਦੀ॥ ਹਰਿ ਸਤਿ ਸਤਿ ਮੇਰੇ ਬਾਬੋਲਾ ਹਰਿ ਜਨ ਮਿਲਿ ਜੰਞ ਸੁੋਹੰਦੀ॥ 3॥ ਹਰਿ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਮੇਰੇ ਬਾਬੁਲਾ ਹਰਿ ਦੇਵਹੁ ਦਾਨੁ ਮੈ ਦਾਜੋ॥
