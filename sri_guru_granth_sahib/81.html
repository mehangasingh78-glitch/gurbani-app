(ਅੰਗ 80)॥     ਪੁਰਬੇ ਕਮਾਏ ਸ੍ਰੀਰੰਗ ਪਾਏ ਹਰਿ ਮਿਲੇ ਚਿਰੀ ਵਿਛੁੰਨਿਆ॥ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਬਾਹਰਿ ਸਰਬਤਿ ਰਵਿਆ ਮਨਿ ਉਪਜਿਆ ਬਿਸੁਆਸੋ॥ ਨਾਨਕੁ ਸਿਖ ਦੇਇ ਮਨ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਮ ਕਰਿ ਸੰਤਾ ਸੰਗਿ ਨਿਵਾਸੋ॥ 4॥ ਮਨ ਪਿਆਰਿਆ ਜੀਉ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਹਰਿ ਪ੍ਰੇਮ ਭਗਤਿ ਮਨੁ ਲੀਨਾ॥ ਮਨ ਪਿਆਰਿਆ ਜੀਉ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਹਰਿ ਜਲ ਮਿਲਿ ਜੀਵੇ ਮੀਨਾ॥ ਹਰਿ ਪੀ ਆਘਾਨੇ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਬਾਨੇ ਸ੍ਰਬ ਸੁਖਾ ਮਨ ਵੁਠੇ॥ ਸ੍ਰੀਧਰ ਪਾਏ ਮੰਗਲ ਗਾਏ ਇਛ ਪੁੰਨੀ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਤੁਠੇ॥ ਲੜਿ ਲੀਨੇ ਲਾਏ ਨਉਨਿਧਿ ਪਾਏ ਨਾਉ ਸਰਬਸੁ ਠਾਕੁਰਿ ਦੀਨਾ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਸਿਖ ਸੰਤ ਸਮਝਾਈ ਹਰਿ ਪ੍ਰੇਮ ਭਗਤਿ ਮਨੁ ਲੀਨਾ॥ 5॥1॥2॥
Mun piyariya jeeo mittra, Gobind Naam(u) smaalay. Mun piyariya jee mittra Hur(i) nibhaiy tayraiy naalay. Sung(i) suhayee Hur(i) Naam(u) dhiyaayeen, birtha koyay na jaayay. Mun chinday sayee ful paaveh(i), churn kumal chit(u) laayay. Jul(i) thul(i) poor(i) ruhhiya Bunvaari, ghut(i) ghut(i) nudr(i) nihaalay. Nanak sikh dayay mun preetum sadhsung(i) bherm(u) jaalay.    1. -----------
Word Meaning:   Gobind = a name for Krishna = Waheguru.  Smaalay = remember.  Nibhaiy = accompanies; supports all the way.   Suhaayee Sung(i) = supports and accompanies.   Birtha = without; empty.   Chinday = desired.   Ful = (reap) consequences.  Churn Kumal = lotus-like soft feet (of Waheguru).   Chit(u) = mind.   Thul = land.  Bunvaari = another name for Krishna = Waheguru.   Ghut = heart.   Nihaalay = nihaaray = sees.   Sikh = advice.   Bherm = doubts; skepticism.    1.
MEANING: Remember Waheguru’s Naam, oh my dear friend, my mind.  It is Waheguru’s Naam that accompanies you (supports you) all the way.  Nobody goes without support if he recites Waheguru’s Naam, which always supports him.   Whosoever focuses his mind on the Naam of Waheguru, which has Feet as soft as a lotus flower, has all his wishes fulfilled.  Waheguru, which is present in waters and on the land, casts his kind sight on every heart (body).  My advice to you, oh my dear mind, is to seek the company of the Guru (or his devotees) to clear (dispel) your doubts, says (Guru) Nanak.    1.
 Mun piyariya jee mittra, Hur(i) bin(u) jhootth(u) pusaaray.  Mun piyariya jeeo mittra, bikh(u) saagur(u) sunsaaray. Churn kumal kur(i) bohith(u) Kertay, sehsa dookh(u) na biyapaiy.  Gur Poora bhaytaiy vudbhaageen, aatth pehr Prubh(u) jaapaiy. Aad(i) joogadi sayvuk soaami, bhugtaan Naam(u) adharay. Nanak(u) sikh dayay mun preetum bin(u) Hur(i) jhootth pusaaray.   2. 
Word Meaning:  Jhootthay = not real; perishable.   Pusaaray = expansions; developments.    Bikh = poison.    Sunsaaray = world.    Bohith = ship.   Sehsa = worry due to unknown outcome.   Biyapaiy = happen; affects.    Bhaytaiy = meets.  Vudbhageen = with great luck.  Aad(i) Joogadi = from the beginning of time.   Sayvuk = personal attendant; devotee.   Adharay = support.   2.
MEANING:  Without Waheguru’s Naam, all expansions (extensions) involving Maya are perishable (therefore are useless in the spiritual world), oh my dear mind.  The world is a sea of poisonous (Maya); and Waheguru, which is the Creator, and has lotus-like Feet, is that ship which ferries you across the sea of Maya, oh my friend.  if you board the Waheguru-ship, no discomfort and worry will haunt you. When one connects with the Perfect Guru, with great fortune, he worships Waheguru for all twenty-four hours. Waheguru’s Naam is the spiritual food for all His devotees in each eon (and the Lord loves His devotees).   (Guru) Nanak advises the man that without Waheguru Naam in mind, all expansions involving Maya are meaningless as they are perishable.     2.
 Mun piyariya jeeo mittra, Hur(i) ludday khayp suvulli.  Mun piyariya jeeo mittra, Hur(i) dur(u) nihchul mulli. Hur(i) dur(u) sayvay Alukh Abhayvay, nihchul(u) aasun(u) paaya. Teh junum na mern(u) na aavun jaana, sunsa dookh(u) mitaya. Chittar Goopt ka kaagud phaarriya, jumdootaan kuchhoo na chulli. Nanak sikh dayay mun preetum Hur(i) luddday khayp svulli.    3. -------------
Word Meaning:   Ludday = load up.  Khayp = merchandise.   Svulli = resulting in success.    Nihchul = everlasting.   Alukh = incomprehensible.   Abhayvay = unknowable.    Aasun = resting place.   Sunsa = doubts.   Chitter Goopt = according to Hidu Granths, these are two invisible bodies which record the deeds of each individual.   Kaagud = the paper on which the account is kept.   Jumdoot = the agents of the angel of death.   Kuchoo Na Chulli = cannot do anything.
MEANING:  Load up your mind with the merchandise of Waheguru’s Naam to be successful (in achieving union with God), oh my dear mind.  (By doing so) You will be able to occupy an eternal spot at Waheguru’s Door (achieve eternal union with Him).  Whoever worships the incomprehensible and unknowable Waheguru at His Door (focusing fully on His Naam), acquires an eternal seat (becomes a permanent part of Waheguru).  Such an individual gets rid of his doubts and discomforts, as well as his birth-death cycle.  For him, all accounts of his deeds kept by the invisible agents - Chitter and Goopt - are erased, and the agents of the Angel of death are left with no power to hurt him in any way.  (Guru) Nanak advises the dear mind that it should load up the merchandise of Waheguru’s Naam to achieve success.        3.
 Mun piyariya jeeo mittra, kur(i) suntan sung(i) nivaaso. Mun piyariya jeeo mittra, Hur(i) Naam(u) jupt pergaaso. Simer(i) Soami sookheh gaami, ichh sugli pooniyaan. 
(PAGE 80):      Poorbay kmaayay Sri Rung paayay, Hur(i) milay chireen vichhooniyaan. Untar(i) baahur(i) serbut(i) ruviya, mun(i) oopjiya bisuaaso. Nanak(u) sikh dayay mun preetum kur(i) suntan sung(i) nivaaso.    4. 
Word Meaning:  Sung(i) = with.    Nivaaso = live with; keep company.    Simer = recite (Waheguru’s Naam).    Sookheh Gaami = provider of comforts.   Ichh = wishes.  Sugli = all.    Pooniyaan = fulfilled.    Poorbay Kmaayay = earned in previous lives.  Sri = Maya    Siri Rung = (Maya’s husband) Waheguru.   Chireen = for along time.  Vichhooniyaan = separated.   Ryviya = present.   Oopjiya = sprouted; develops.    Bisuaaso = faith.   4.
MEANING:  Stay in the company of the holy devotees (of Waheguru), oh my dear friend, my mind.  Mind is enlightened by recitation of Waheguru’s Naam.  By remembering the Lord, who is the source of all comforts, all your wishes are fulfilled.  Because of the (noble) deeds performed in the previous lives, one realizes Waheguru, from whom he had been separated for a very long time.  At that spiritual stage, one realizes that Waheguru is present within (the heart) and without (the universe), and one develops full faith in Him.  (Guru) Nanak advises that one should keep company with holy devotees.     4.
Mun piyariya jeeo mittra, Hur(i) praym bhagat(i) mun(u) leena.  Mun piyariya jeeo mittra, Hur(i,) jul mil(i) jeevay meena.   Hur(i) pee aaghanay, Amrit baanay, serb sookha mun(i) vootthay.   Sridhar paayay, mungul gaayay, ichh pooni Satgur tootthay.  Lurr(i) leenay naoun nidh(i) paayay, Naoun serbus(u) Tthaaku(i) deena.  Nanak sikh sunt sumjhaayee, Hur(i) praym bhugut(i) mun(u) leena.         5.1.2.
Word Meaning:   Praym bhagat(i) = love-filled devotion.   Leena = fully absorbed.   Meena = fish.  Aaghanay = satisfied; contented.   Baanay = Bani; Shabad.   Vootthay = settled; took residence. Sridhar = support (creator) of Maya (Waheguru).  Mungul = songs of happiness.  Ichh = wish.  Pooni = fulfilled.   Tootthay = pleased to be kind.  Lurr(i) leenay = taking refuge (of Satguru).  Naoun Nidh(i) = the nine treasures (Hindu Ggranths believe that there are nine different treasures of god, Kuber).   Serbus(u) = of all.     Tthakur = Lord.   5.
MEANING:  My dear friend's mind is fully absorbed in the affectionate worship of Waheguru (and like the fish, which cannot live without water, my mind cannot live without Him).  Whoever drinks the Shabad-Amrit of Waheguru’s Naam gets fully content, and all comforts come to stay in his mind.  When Satguru is pleased, all one’s wishes are fulfilled with His kindness, and one feels blissful as if the music of happiness is playing in one's heart.  If one seeks Satguru’s shelter, the Lord of all (Waheguru) is pleased and gives His Naam to him, which is the source of all happiness, and one feels as if he has acquired all the nine mythical treasures of Kuber.   (Guru) Nanak says that Waheguru’s saints advise that Waheguru takes residence in one’s heart by worshiping Him with affection and devotion.  5. 1.2.

ਸਿਰੀਰਾਗ ਕੇ ਛੰਤ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ੴ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਪ੍ਰਸਾਦਿ॥ ਡਖਣਾ॥ ਹਠ ਮਝਾਹੂ ਮਾ ਪਿਰੀ ਪਸੇ ਕਿਉ ਦੀਦਾਰ॥ ਸੰਤ ਸਰਣਾਈ ਲਭਣੇ ਨਾਨਕ ਪ੍ਰਾਣ ਅਧਾਰ॥ 1॥
ਛੰਤੁ॥ ਚਰਨ ਕਮਲ ਸਿਉ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਿ ਰੀਤਿ ਸੰਤਨ ਮਨਿ ਆਵਏ ਜੀਉ॥ ਦੁਤੀਆ ਭਾਉ ਬਿਪਰੀਤਿ ਅਨੀਤਿ ਦਾਸਾ ਨਹ ਭਾਵਏ ਜੀਉ॥ ਦਾਸਾ ਨਹ ਭਾਵਏ ਬਿਨੁ ਦਰਸਾਵਏ ਇਕ ਖਿਨੁ ਧੀਰਜੁ ਕਿਉ ਕਰੈ॥ ਨਾਮ ਬਿਹੂਨਾ ਤਨੁ ਮਨੁ ਹੀਨਾ ਜਲ ਬਿਨੁ ਮਛੁਲੀ ਜਿਉ ਮਰੈ॥ ਮਿਲੁ ਮੇਰੇ ਪ੍ਰਾਨ ਅਧਾਰੇ ਗੁਣ ਸਾਧਸੰਗਿ ਮਿਲਿ ਗਾਵਏ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਕੇ ਸੁਆਮੀ ਧਾਰਿ ਅਨੁਗ੍ਰਹੁ ਮਨਿ ਤਨਿ ਅੰਕਿ ਸਮਾਵਏ॥ 1॥
Siri Raag Kay Chhunt Mehla 5: Ek Onkar Satgur Persad(i):  
Dukkhna:  Hutth mujhaahoo ma Piri, pusay kiyoun deedar. Sunt shernaayee lubhnay, Nanak pran adhaar. 1. 
Chhunt:  Churn kumal siyoun preet(i) reet(i), suntun mun(i) aavay jeeo.  Dootiya bhaao bipreet(i), uneet(i), dasaan neh bhaavay jeeo.  Dasaan neh bhaavay, bin(u) dersaavay, ikk khin(u) dheeruj kiyoun kuraiy.  Naam bihoona tun(u) mun(u) heena, jul bin(u) muchhuli jiyoun muraiy.  Mil(u) mayray praan adhaaray, goon Sadhsung(i) mil(i)  gaavay.  Nanak kay Soami dhaar(i) anugreh(u), mun(i) tun(i) unk(i) smaavay.  1.
Word Meeting:  Dukknna = Southern (dialect).   Hutth = heart.  Mujhaahoo = inside.  Piri = Husband (Waheguru).       Pusay Deedar = to have Sight.   Lubhnay = find.   Praan Adhaar = basis of life (God).
MEANING:  Sloak in southern dialect:  My dear husband (Waheguru) resides in my heart invisibly; so, how I can have his sight (see him).  (The only way) to “see” the basis of my life (Waheguru), is to take refuge of the holy devotees (or Satguru). 
Chhunt: Word Meaning:  Chhunt = a type of lyric (see the beginning note).  Churn Kumal = lotus-like Feet.  Reet(i) = (noble) custom; lifestyle.    Dootiya Bhaao = Love for the other (Maya).  Bipreet(i) = against custom.   Dasaan = slaves (humble devotees).  Bhaavay = to the liking.  Desaavay = sight; visitation.  Khin(u) = moment.    Dheeruj = patience; peace.  Bihoona = without.   Heena = weak.   Anugreh(u) = kindness.  Unk(i) = heart.    1.
MEANING:  As their natural custom (routine), the devotees have love in their mind for Waheguru, who has Feet which are soft like a lotus flower.  Love for anyone else (Maya) is against their customary routine and is not to their liking.  Without the holy sight of Waheguru, they do not like anything else, and cannot have peace (comfort) even for a moment.  Like a fish, which does not survive without water, the body and mind of the devotees become weak (lifeless) without Waheguru’s Naam.  They sing praise of Waheguru’s kind virtues and request that they be connected with Him.  (Guru) Nanak also requests Waheguru to be kind to Him so that He becomes a part of His mind and body.    1.

ਡਖਣਾ॥ ਸੋਹੰਦੜੋ ਹਭ ਠਾਇ ਕੋਇ ਨ ਦਿਸੈ ਡੂਜੜੋ॥ ਖੁਲ੍ਹੜੇ ਕਪਾਟ ਨਾਨਕ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਭੇਟਤੇ॥1॥
ਛੰਤੁ॥ ਤੇਰੇ ਬਚਨ ਅਨੂਪ ਅਪਾਰ ਸੰਤਨ ਆਧਾਰ ਬਾਣੀ ਬੀਚਾਰੀਐ ਜੀਉ॥ ਸਿਮਰਤ ਸਾਸ ਗਿਰਾਸ ਪੂਰਨ ਬਿਸੁਆਸ ਕਿਉ ਮਨਹੁ ਬਿਸਾਰੀਐ ਜੀਉ॥ ਕਿਉ ਮਨਹੁ ਬਿਸਾਰੀਐ ਨਿਮਖ ਨਹੀ ਟਾਰੀਐ ਗੁਣਵੰਤ ਪ੍ਰਾਨ ਹਮਾਰੇ॥ ਮਨ ਬਾਂਛਤ ਫਲ ਦੇਤ ਹੈ ਸੁਆਮੀ ਜੀਅ ਕੀ ਬਿਰਥਾ ਸਾਰੇ॥ ਅਨਾਥ ਕੇ ਨਾਥੇ ਸ੍ਰਬ ਕੈ ਸਾਥੇ ਜਪਿ ਜੂਐ ਜਨਮੁ ਨ ਹਾਰੀਐ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਕੀ ਬੇਨੰਤੀ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਪਹਿ ਕ੍ਰਿਪਾ ਕਰਿ ਭਵਜਲੁ ਤਾਰੀਐ॥ 2॥
(Sloak) Dukkna:  Sohundrro hubh ttaayay, koyay na disaiy doojrro. Khoollhurray kpaat Nanak Satgur bhayt-tay. 
Word Meaning:  Sohundrro = looks very pretty (majestic).  Hubh = subh = all.  Tthaayay = places.  Kpaat = doors (of mind).     Bhayt-tay = (upon) meeting.
MEANING:  Connecting with Satguru, the doors of my mind have opened (my mind is enlightened). Now I find You looking very majestic everywhere, and I do not see anybody without You (You are present in everybody), oh Waheguru.   
Chhunt: Tayray Buchan anoop apaar, suntan aadhar, Bani beechaariyaiy jeeo. Simrut saas giraas, poorun bisooaas, kiyoun munhoun bisaariyaiy jeeo. Kiyoun munhoun bisaariyaiy, nimukh nuhee taariyaiy, Goonvunt praan humaaray.  Mun baanchhut ful dayt haiy Soami, jee kee birtha saaray.  Anaath kay Naathay, serb kaiy saathay, jup(i), jooyay junum na haariyaiy. Nanak kee baynti Prubh peh(i) kirpa kur(i) bhuvjul(u) taariyaiy.  2. 

Word Meaning: Anoop = unparalleled.  Apaar = without limits.  Giraas = (with every) nibble.  Bisooaas = faith.  Bisaariyaiy = drop from mind (memory).  Nimukh = moment.  Taariyaiy = put off; delay.  Goonvunt = source of virtues.  Baanchhut = desired.   Birtha = condition.  Saaray = looks after.  Anaath = orphan.  Naathay = Master.   Jooyaiy = in gamble.  Bhuvjul = world sea (sea of Maya). Taariyaiy = take across (have different pronunciation).
MEANING:   Guru’s words in Your praise are of unparalleled beauty, oh the limitless, and the basis (support) of the spiritual life of Your devotees, Waheguru.   One must ponder on Guru’s Shabad (to worship) Waheguru.  Waheguru should never be forgotten from one’s mind; He should rather be remembered with every breath and with every bite with full faith.  The treasure of virtues (God) is the support of our life, and one cannot afford to forget Him even for a moment; remembering Him should not be delayed.  Waheguru fulfills all wishes and desires and looks after everybody according to their needs and according to their mind.   He is the Lord of the spiritually orphans (who have no guru to guide them), and one should recite His Naam for spiritual life; and should not waste this (precious human) life in gambling (mental struggle between Maya and God).  (Guru) Nanak requests the Lord that He should kindly carry Him (Guru Nanak) across the sea of Maya.       2.
