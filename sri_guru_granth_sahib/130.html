(ਅੰਗ 129)॥ ਅਹਿਨਿਸਿ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਿ ਸਬਦਿ ਸਾਚੈ ਹਰਿ ਸਰਿ ਵਾਸਾ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 5॥ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਸਦਾ ਬਗੁ ਮੈਲਾ ਹਉਮੈ ਮਲੁ ਲਾਈ॥ ਇਸਨਾਨੁ ਕਰੈ ਪਰੁ ਮੈਲੁ ਨ ਜਾਈ॥ ਜੀਵਤੁ ਮਰੈ ਗੁਰ ਸਬਦੁ ਬੀਚਾਰੈ ਹਉਮੈ ਮੈਲੁ ਚੁਕਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 6॥ ਰਤਨੁ ਪਦਾਰਥੁ ਘਰ ਤੇ ਪਾਇਆ॥ ਪੂਰੈ ਸਤਿਗੁਰਿ ਸਬਦੁ ਸੁਣਾਇਆ॥ ਗੁਰ ਪਰਸਾਦਿ ਮਿਟਿਆ ਅੰਧਿਆਰਾ ਘਟਿ ਚਾਨਣੁ ਆਪੁ ਪਛਾਨਣਿਆ॥ 7॥ ਆਪਿ ਉਪਾਏ ਤੈ ਆਪੇ ਵੇਖੈ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਸੇਵੈ ਸੋ ਜਨੁ ਲੇਖੈ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮੁ ਵਸੈ ਘਟ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਗੁਰ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਤੇ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥    8॥31॥32॥
Majh Mehla 3:  Nirgoon(u) Surgoon(u) aapay Soyee. Tut(u) puchhaanaiy so pundit hoyee. Aap(i) turaiy suglay kool taaraiy Hur(i) Naam(u) munn(i) vusaavniya. 1. Huoun vaari jeeo vaari, Hur(i) rus(u) chukh(i) saad(u) paavniya.  Hur(i) rus(u) chaakheh(i) say jun nirmul, nirmul Naam(u) dhiyaavniya. 1. Ruhaou.  So nihkermi jo Shabad(u) beechaaray. Untar(i) Tut(u) Giyan(i), haumein maaray. Naam(u) Pdaruth(u) nau nidh(i) paayay, traiy goon mayt(i) smaavniya. 2. Haumein kuraiy, nihkermi na hovaiy. Gur persadi haumein khovaiy. Untar(i) bibayk(u), suda aap(u) veechaaray, Gur Shabadi goon gaavniya. 3. Hur(i) sur(i) saagar(u) nirmul Soyee. Sunt choogeh(i) nit Gurmukh(i) hoyee. Ishnaan(u) kureh(i) suda din(u) raati, haumein maiyl(u) chookaavniya. 4. Nirmul hunsa praym piyaar(i). Hur(i) sur(i) vusaiy haumein maar(i). 
(PAGE 129):                   Ehinis(i) preet(i) Shabad(i) Sachaiy, Hur(i)-Sur(i) vaasa paavniya. 5. Munmukh suda bug(u) maiyla, haumein mul(u) laayee. Ishnaan(u) kuraiy, pur(u) maiyl(u) na jaayee. Jeevut(u) muraiy, Gur Shabad beechaaraiy, haumein maiyl(u) chookaavniya. 6.  Ruttan pdaruth ghur tay paaya. Pooraiy Satgur(i) Shabad(u) soonaaya. Gur persad(i) mitiya undhiara, ghut(i) chaanun(u) aap(u) puchhanuniya.  7. Aap(i) oopaayay taiy Aapay vaykhaiy. Satgur sayvaiy so jun(u) laykhaiy. Nanak Naam(u) vusaiy ghut untar(i), Gur kirpa tay paavniya.     8.31.32.
Word Meaning:  Nirgoon = formless (no visible traits); above the three effects of Maya.   Surgoon = manifesting (hidden) in nature, with the three effects of Maya.  Tut(u) = reality.   Puchhaanay = recognize; have a deep understanding.   Pundit = knowledgeable.   Turaiy = find salvation.   Kool = clan (associates).       1.
Rus(u) = essence; taste.  Chukh(i) = to taste. Saad(u) = taste. Jun= devotee.     Pause.
Nihkermi = one who does not indulge (unaffected).   Giyaan(i) = having Divine Knowledge.   Pdaruth = highly valuable article (Waheguru’s Naam).   Nau Nidh(i) = the nine mystical treasures.     Mayt(i) = erase.      2.     Bibayk(u) = knowledge; ability to tell right from wrong.    Aap(u) veechaaray = self-analysis.        3.  
Sur(i) = body of water.  Soyee = the same (Waheguru).   Sunt = devotee.   Choogeh(i) = pick up food (for soul).   Ishnaan = bathe (cleanse mind in Naam-water).       Haumein Maiyl(u) = filth of selfishness and ego).        4.      Hunsa = swan-like (clean soul).  (The swan is considered to be a clean animal in the Hindu Granths).  Vusaiy = reside.       Ehinis(i) = day and night.           5.      Bug(u) = bugula = heron (imposter).  Bugula is considered a mean imposter because he looks like a swan but catches fish or toads, giving the impression of a swan which does not eat fish.  Jeevut Muraiy = lives in the world but kills his worldly desires.      6.     Ruttan Pdaruth = invaluable article like precious stones (Waheguru’s Naam).   Undhiyara = darkness (ignorance).     Ghut = heart.    Chaanun = light (enlightenment).    Aap(u) = self.        7.
Oopaayay = created.   Vakhaiy = looks after.   Lakhaiy = in the record (of successful people); approved (by Waheguru).        8.
MEANING:  Waheguru manifests Himself in two forms – (Nirgoon) (above the effects of Maya), and (Surgoon) in nature with the effects of Maya.  Only that individual deserves to be called a pundit who understands (internalizes) this reality. By embedding Waheguru’s Naam in mind, not only does such an individual find salvation for himself, but he also gets salvation for his clan (associates).         1.
I offer my life to all those who enjoy (union with) Waheguru by tasting His Naam.  Those who taste Waheguru’s Naam have a pure mind as they recite Waheguru’s pure Naam.       Pause.         Only that individual is considered not to indulge in Maya (is above its effects) who ponders on the Guru’s Shabad, and overcomes his selfishness and ego with the enlightenment of his mind.  He acquires the invaluable Naam, which is like having all the nine treasures, and merges with Waheguru by overcoming the effects of Maya.           2.           A man, who is gripped by haumein, cannot be above the effects of Maya.  He has to get rid of his haumein by the grace of the Guru.  A man who analyzes his mind all the time acquires the wisdom to tell the right from the wrong and sings Waheguru’s praise by Guru’s Shabad.         3.        Waheguru is pure and is like the sea of all virtues; the holy devotees of the Guru feed on these virtues all the time.   They take a dip in Naam-Amrit day and night, and thereby wash away the filth of their haumein.         4.         With dedicated love for God, man’s soul is purified. Such an individual gets rid of his haumein, and always resides in the ocean of Waheguru (stays absorbed in Him). With day and night love for Waheguru’s Shabad; he resides in Waheguru-sea (stays connected with Him).        5.       The follower of his mind is an imposter, like a heron who is very different inside from his (innocent) looks, and always carries the filth of haumein.  He may take dips in the waters of holy places, but the filth of haumein never goes away.  Of course, if he kills his sensual desires and leads his life as per the Guru’s Shabad, his filth of haumein is washed away.        6.
If one “listens” to Guru’s Shabad with dedication, he can acquire invaluable Waheguru’s Naam in his mind.  With Guru’s grace, the darkness of his mind disappears, and he recognizes himself with the enlightened mind.          7.
Waheguru creates the world Himself, and looks after it too.  A person who serves 
Waheguru gets the account of his bad deeds obliterated, and is honoured because Waheguru’s Naam enlightens his mind by the grace of the Guru, oh Nanak!  8.31.32.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ ਮਾਇਆ ਮੋਹੁ ਜਗਤੁ ਸਬਾਇਆ॥ ਤ੍ਰੈ ਗੁਣ ਦੀਸਹਿ ਮੋਹਿਆ ਮਾਇਆ॥ ਗੁਰ ਪਰਸਾਦੀ ਕੋ ਵਿਰਲਾ ਬੂਝੈ ਚਉਥੈ ਪਦਿ ਲਿਵ ਲਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਹਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਜੀਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਮਾਇਆ ਮੋਹੁ ਸਬਦਿ ਜਲਾਵਣਿਆ॥ ਮਾਇਆ ਮੋਹੁ ਜਲਾਏ ਸੋ ਹਰਿ ਸਿਉ ਚਿਤੁ ਲਾਏ ਹਰਿ ਦਰਿ ਮਹਲੀ ਸੋਭਾ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਦੇਵੀ ਦੇਵਾ ਮੂਲੁ ਹੈ ਮਾਇਆ॥ ਸਿੰਮ੍ਰਿਤਿ ਸਾਸਤ ਜਿਨਿ ਉਪਾਇਆ॥ ਕਾਮੁ ਕ੍ਰੋਧੁ ਪਸਰਿਆ ਸੰਸਾਰੇ ਆਇ ਜਾਇ ਦੁਖੁ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 2॥ ਤਿਸੁ ਵਿਚਿ ਗਿਆਨੁ ਰਤਨੁ ਇਕੁ ਪਾਇਆ॥ ਗੁਰ ਪਰਸਾਦੀ ਮੰਨਿ ਵਸਾਇਆ॥ ਜਤੁ ਸਤੁ ਸੰਜਮੁ ਸਚੁ ਕਮਾਵੈ ਗੁਰਿ ਪੂਰੈ ਨਾਮੁ ਧਿਆਵਣਿਆ॥ 3॥ ਪੇਈਅੜੈ ਧਨ ਭਰਮਿ ਭੁਲਾਣੀ॥ ਦੂਜੈ ਲਾਗੀ ਫਿਰਿ ਪਛੋਤਾਣੀ॥ ਹਲਤੁ ਪਲਤੁ ਦੋਵੈ ਗਵਾਏ ਸੁਪਨੈ ਸੁਖੁ ਨ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 4॥ ਪੇਈਅੜੈ ਧਨ ਕੰਤੁ ਸਮਾਲੇ॥ ਗੁਰ ਪਰਸਾਦੀ ਵੇਖੈ ਨਾਲੇ॥ ਪਿਰ ਕੈ ਸਹਜਿ ਰਹੈ ਰੰਗਿ ਰਾਤੀ ਸਬਦਿ ਸਿੰਗਾਰੁ ਬਣਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 5॥ ਸਫਲੁ ਜਨਮੁ ਜਿਨਾ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਪਾਇਆ॥ ਦੂਜਾ ਭਾਉ ਗੁਰ ਸਬਦਿ ਜਲਾਇਆ॥ ਏਕੋ ਰਵਿ ਰਹਿਆ ਘਟ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਮਿਲਿ ਸਤਸੰਗਤਿ ਹਰਿ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 6॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਨ ਸੇਵੇ ਸੋ ਕਾਹੇ ਆਇਆ॥ ਧ੍ਰਿਗੁ ਜੀਵਣੁ ਬਿਰਥਾ ਜਨਮੁ ਗਵਾਇਆ॥ ਮਨਮੁਖਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਚਿਤਿ ਨ ਆਵੈ ਬਿਨੁ ਨਾਵੈ ਬਹੁ ਦੁਖੁ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 7॥ ਜਿਨਿ ਸਿਸਟ ਸਾਜੀ ਸੋਈ ਜਾਣੈ॥ ਆਪੇ ਮੇਲੈ ਸਬਦਿ ਪਛਾਣੈ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮੁ ਮਿਲਿਆ ਤਿਨ ਜਨ ਕਉ ਜਿਨ ਧੁਰਿ ਮਸਤਕਿ ਲੇਖੁ ਲਿਖਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 8॥1॥32॥33॥
Majh Mehla 3:  Maya moh(u) jugut(u) subaaya. Traiy goon deeseh(i) mohiya Maya. Gur persadi ko virla boojhaiy, chauthaiy pud(i) liv laavniya. 1.  Huoun vaari jeeo vaari, maya moh(u) Shabad(i) julaavniya. Maya moh(u) julaayay, so Hur(i) siyoun chit(u) laayay, Hur(i) Dur(i) Mehli sobha paavniya. 1. Ruhaou. Dayvi (Devi) Dayva (Deva) mool(u) haiy Maya. Simrit(i) Shaast jin(i) oopaaya. Kaam(u) krodh(u) pusriya sunsaaray, aayay jaayay dookh(u) paavniya.  2. Tis(u) vich(i) Giyaan ruttan ikk(u) paaya. Gur persadi munn(i) vusaaya. Jut(u) sut(u) sunjum(u) Sach(u) kumaavaiy, Gur(i) Pooraiy Naam(u) dhiyaavniya. 3. Payiurraiy dhun bherm(i) bhoolaani. Doojaiy laagi phir(i) puchhotaani. Hult(u) pult(u) dovaiy guvaayay, soopnay sookh(u) na paavniya. 4. Payiurraiy dhun kunt(u) smaalay. Gur pesadi vaykhaiy naalay. Pir kaiy sehj(i) ruhaiy rung(i) raati, Shabad(i) shingaar(u) bunaavniya. 5. Sufl(u) junum(u) jinha Satgur(u) paaya.  Dooja bhaao Gur Shabad(i) julaaya. Aiko ruv(i) ruhiya ghut untar(i) mil(i) Sutsungat(i) Hur(i) goon gaavniya.  6. Satgur na sayvay so kaahay aaya. Dhrig(u) jeevun(u), birtha junum(u) guvaaya. Munmukh(i) Naam(u) chit(i) na aavaiy, bin(u) Naavaiy buhou dookh(u) paavniya.  7. Jin(i) sisht saaji, soyee jaanaiy. Aapay maylaiy Shabad(i) puchhaanaiy. Nanak Naam(u) miliya, tin jun kuou jin Dhoor(i) mustuk(i) laykh(u) likhaavniya.     8.1.32.33.
Word Meaning:  Maya Moh(u) = attachment for Maya.   Subaaya = the whole world.   Traiy goon = affected by the three effects of Maya (living beings).    Mohiya = captivated.    Virla = some rare.   Chauthaiy Pud(i) = the fourth (highest) level of spirituality above the effects of Maya.   Liv = focus; concentration; connecting with Waheguru.         1.
Julaavniya = is burnt.  Siyoun = with.    Chit(u) = mind.   Dur(i) = door (court).   Pause.
Mool(u) = basis.    Simrit Shast = Hindu Granths.     Kaam(u) = lust.    Pusriya = is spread (has gripped).   Aavay Jaayay = takes birth and dies (rebirths).          2.
Tis(u) Vich(i) = in that (heart).  Ruttan = precious like a precious stone.   Jut(u) = celibacy.    Sat(u) = service to man kind.   Sunjum(u) = contro on sensual desires.   Sach(u) = truthfulness.   Kmaavaiy = works for.      Pooraiy = perfect.        3.
Payiurraiy = in paternal house (this world).   Dhun = woman (human being).   Bherm(i) Bhoolaani = lost in doubts.   Hult(u) = this world.   Pult(u) = the next world.     4.
Kunt(u) = husband (Waheguru).    Smaalay = look after; remember.  Sehj(i) = in the spiritual state where Maya has no effect (mind quits wandering).    Rung(i) Raati = imbued in love.   Shingaar(u) = adornment.       5.       Sufl(u) = successful.   Dooja Bhaao = love for the other (Maya).    Aiko = only God.  Ruv(i) Ruhiya = is present.   Ghut = heart.       6.      Kaahay = what for?   Dhrig(u) = condemnable.    Birtha = empty; uselessly.       7.       Sisht = Srishti = world.   Saaji = created.   Puchhaanaiy = recognize (get connected with).   Dhoor(i) = from the root (source).  Mustuk = forehead.   Laykh(u) = destiny.  8. 
MEANING:   The whole world is in the grip of mind-captivating Maya.  All the living beings are affected by the three qualities of Maya, and are captivated by it.  A rare person realizes this when he attains the fourth level of spiritual awareness (above the effects of Maya) by the grace of the Guru.           1.         I am a sacrifice for all those who burn down (overcome) their attachment to Maya by the Guru’s Shabad.  An individual who overcomes his attachment to Maya applies his mind to Waheguru (gets connected with Him) and wins the praise of Waheguru at His door, in His court, and in His mansion (they all mean the same).          Pause.           The basis of all the gods and goddesses is Maya, as discussed in the Hindu Granths, the Simritis, and the Shastras.  Lust and rage are rampant in the world, and as a result of that, living beings take birth, die, and are born again.           2.          Waheguru has placed a precious thing - Divine knowledge (His Naam) - in the human mind, but it lies dormant. It appears in the mind of the devotee only with the grace of the Guru.  Some people try to observe celibacy, to have their sensual desires under control, and to have high moral character by the stubbornness of their mind, but fail to make any connection with God, even if they are able to control their desires. On the other hand, a devotee of Waheguru does not need to put in any special efforts to control his desires because they are controlled automatically when he recites Waheguru’s Naam in the shelter of the Guru.          3. 
In love with Maya, a human bride is misled by her misbeliefs, and forgets her husband, Waheguru, and has to repent at the end.  She wastes away both the worlds – the present, and the next – and gets no comfort even in her dreams.         4.
If a human bride remembers her husband (Waheguru) in the present life, and “sees” him around by the grace of the Guru, she adorns her life (with virtues), and stays always imbued in the love of her husband – Waheguru.        5.
Those who have made a connection with Satguru and burn down their love for Maya with Guru’s Shabad make their life a success.  Waheguru alone is present in their heart, and they sing His praise in the company of the holy congregation.        6.
What is the use of taking birth as a human if one does not serve Satguru?  His life is condemnable because he wastes away his precious human life.  The follower of his mind does not remember Waheguru’s Naam, and suffers big time without it.          7. 
Only Waheguru, who has created the world, knows all this (how to connect people with Him).  He connects them with Him by making them recognize (love) the Guru’s Shabad.  However, only that individual gets to embed Waheguru’s Naam in his heart who has had this (union with Waheguru) prewritten on his forehead, right from the beginning, oh Nanak.    1.32.33.
