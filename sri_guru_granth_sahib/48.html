(ਅੰਗ 47)॥     ਮਾਇਆ ਮੋਹ ਪਰੀਤਿ ਧ੍ਰਿਗੁ, ਸੁਖੀ ਨ ਦੀਸੈ ਕੋਇ॥1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਦਾਨਾ ਦਾਤਾ ਸੀਲਵੰਤੁ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਰੂਪੁ ਅਪਾਰੁ॥ ਸਖਾ ਸਹਾਈ ਅਤਿ ਵਡਾ ਊਚਾ ਵਡਾ ਅਪਾਰੁ॥ ਬਾਲਕੁ ਬਿਰਧਿ ਨ ਜਾਣੀਐ ਨਿਹਚਲੁ ਤਿਸੁ ਦਰਵਾਰੁ॥ ਜੋ ਮੰਗੀਐ ਸੋਈ ਪਾਈਐ ਨਿਧਾਰਾ ਆਧਾਰੁ॥ 2॥ ਜਿਸੁ ਪੇਖਤ ਕਿਲਵਿਖ ਹਿਰਹਿ ਮਨਿ ਤਨਿ ਹੋਵੈ ਸਾਤਿ॥ ਇਕ ਮਨਿ ਏਕੁ ਧਿਆਈਐ ਮਨ ਕੀ ਲਾਹਿ ਭਰਾਤਿ॥ ਗੁਣ ਨਿਧਾਨੁ ਨਵਤਨੁ ਸਦਾ ਪੂਰਨ ਜਾ ਕੀ ਦਾਤਿ॥ ਸਦਾ ਸਦਾ ਆਰਾਧੀਐ ਦਿਨੁ ਵਿਸਰਹੁ ਨਹੀ ਰਾਤਿ॥ 3॥ ਜਿਨ ਕਉ ਪੂਰਬਿ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਤਿਨ ਕਾ ਸਖਾ ਗੋਵਿੰਦੁ॥ ਤਨੁ ਮਨੁ ਧਨੁ ਅਰਪੀ ਸਭੋ ਸਗਲ ਵਾਰੀਐ ਇਹੁ ਜਿੰਦੁ॥ ਦੇਖੈ ਸੁਣੈ ਹਦੂਰਿ ਸਦ ਘਟਿ ਘਟਿ ਬ੍ਰਹਮੁ ਰਵਿੰਦੁ॥ ਅਕਿਰਤਘਣਾ ਨੋ ਪਾਲਦਾ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਨਾਨਕ ਸਦ ਬਖਸਿੰਦੁ॥ 4॥13॥83॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 5:  Preet(i) luggy Tis(u) Sach(u) siyoun, murraiy na aavaiy jaayay. Na vaychhorriyan vicchurray, subh meh(i) ruhiya smaayay. Deen durd dookh(u) bhunjna sayvuk kaiy sut bhaayay.  Achurj roop(u) Nirunjno, Gur(i) maylaaya maayay. 1.  Bhaayee ray meet(u) kurhou Prubh(u) soyay. 
(PAGE 47):  Maya moh preet(i) dhrig(u), sookhi na deesaiy koyay. 1. Ruhaou. Daana daata seelvunt(u), nirmul(u) roop(u) apaar(u).  Sukha suhayee ut(i) vuda, oocha vuda apaar(u).  Baaluk(u) birdh(i) na jaaniyaiy, nihchul tis(u) Durvaar(u). Jo mungiyaiy so paayiyaiy nidharaan aadhaar(u). 2. Jis(u) paykhut kilvikh hireh(i) mun(i) tun(i) hovaiy shaant(i). Ikk mun(i) Aik(u) dhiyayiyaiy, mun kee laahay bhraant(i). Goon nidhaan(u) nuvtun(i) suda, poorun ja kee daat(i).  Suda suda aaradhiyaiy, din(u) vissrou nuhi raat(i). 3. Jin kou poorub(i) likhiya, tin ka sukha Govind(u). Tun(u) mun(u) dun(u) urpeen subho, sugul vaariyaiy eho jind(u).  Daykhaiy, soonaiy ,hudoor(i) sud, ghut(i) ghut(i) Braham ruvind(u). Akritghuna nu paalda Prubh Nanak sud bukhshind(u).  4.13.83.
Word Meaning:  Tis(u) Sach(u) = that eternal Truth (God).   Vaychhorriyaan = separating.   Deen = powerless; humble.   Bhunjna = destroyer.  Sut Bhaayay = honest intent (respectful love).   Achurj = amazing.     Nirunjno = of the One on which Maya has no effect (God).      1.        Dhrig(u) = condemnable.         Pause.        Daana = wise; know-all.   Seelvunt(u) = calm (never gets agitated).   Nirmul(u) = pure.   Apaar(u) = whose ends are not known (limitless).   Sukha = close friend.  Nihchul(u) = eternal.    Nidharaan = without support.  Aadhaar(u) = support.      2.         Paykhut = on seeing (connecting).     Kilvikh = sins.    Hireh(i) = destroyed.   Shaant(u) = peaceful.    Bhraant(i) = wandering of the mind.    Nuvtun(i) = new; fresh (never tired).   Daat(i) = largess.         3.        Poorub(i) = right from the beginning (predestined).   Urpeen = offer.   Vaariyaiy = sacrifice.  Hudoor(i) = present around.  Ghut(i) = in the heart.   Braham = Waheguru.      Ruvind(u) = pervading; present.    Akritghun = ungrateful; thankless.     4.
MEANING:  Make Waheguru your friend, oh my brothers. Love for Maya is condemnable because no one seems happy in it (it does not last, and its loss causes grief).      Pause.
I have developed a love for the eternal Waheguru because (unlike Maya) he never dies or leaves you. He is an integral part of everyone, and (once connected), He never separates from you, even if some people may try to do just that (to weaken your faith in Him).  If a devotee has pure love for Waheguru, He eradicates all his troubles as He is kind to the humble (who overcome their ego to seek His shelter).  He appears in many amazing forms, and the Guru has connected me with Him, oh my mother.        1.          God knows everyone’s needs and diligently looks after His creation. He is the provider, very calm, perfectly pure, and of limitless beauty (in His nature).   He is one’s real friend and helper, extremely expansive, and very high, beyond reach (by our senses).  He is never-changing (he was never a child, young and will never be old), and has eternal Court (will never cease to be the Lord).  He is the support for all those who are otherwise without support, and with His kindness, one acquires whatever one wishes.        2.               The man needs to worship the “One” (Waheguru) with full dedication as He destroys all his sins as soon as he gets connected with Him; and He removes all one’s doubts, as a result of which one’s mind and body become pure, and find peace.  The treasure of all noble qualities (God) is always fresh, and His largess is perfect. One should worship Him all the time and should never forget Him.     3.            Waheguru is the friend of all those who are predestined (to have Him as their friend). One should offer Him all - one’s body, mind (wisdom), and wealth, etc. - and should sacrifice one's life for Him. Waheguru pervades every heart; He is always close to us, and He sees and listens to everything.  He always forgives us for our sins and nourishes even the ungrateful, oh Nanak.  4.13.83.  

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ 

ਮਨੁ ਤਨੁ ਧਨੁ ਜਿਨਿ ਪ੍ਰਭਿ ਦੀਆ ਰਖਿਆ ਸਹਜਿ ਸਵਾਰਿ॥ ਸਰਬ ਕਲਾ ਕਰਿ ਥਾਪਿਆ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਜੋਤਿ ਅਪਾਰ॥ ਸਦਾ ਸਦਾ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਸਿਮਰੀਐ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਰਖੁ ਉਰਧਾਰਿ॥ 1॥ ਮੇਰੇ ਮਨ ਹਰਿ ਬਿਨੁ ਅਵਰੁ ਨ ਕੋਇ॥ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਸਰਣਾਈ ਸਦਾ ਰਹੁ ਦੂਖੁ ਨ ਵਿਆਪੈ ਕੋਇ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਰਤਨ ਪਦਾਰਥ ਮਾਣਕਾ ਸੁਇਨਾ ਰੁਪਾ ਖਾਕੁ॥ ਮਾਤ ਪਿਤਾ ਸੁਤ ਬੰਧਪਾ ਕੂੜੇ ਸਭੇ ਸਾਕ॥ ਜਿਨਿ ਕੀਤਾ ਤਿਸਹਿ ਨ ਜਾਣਈ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਪਸੁ ਨਾਪਾਕ॥2॥ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਬਾਹਰਿ ਰਵਿ ਰਹਿਆ ਤਿਸ ਨੋ ਜਾਣੈ ਦੂਰਿ॥ ਤ੍ਰਿਸਨਾ ਲਾਗੀ ਰਚਿ ਰਹਿਆ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਹਉਮੈ ਕੂਰਿ॥ ਭਗਤੀ ਨਾਮ ਵਿਹੂਣਿਆ ਆਵਹਿ ਵੰਞਹਿ ਪੂਰ॥ 3॥ ਰਾਖਿ ਲੇਹੁ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਕਰਣਹਾਰੁ ਜੀਅ ਜੰਤ ਕਰਿ ਦਇਆ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਕੋਇ ਨ ਰਖਣਹਾਰੁ ਮਹਾ ਬਿਕਟ ਜਮ ਭਇਆ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮੁ ਨ ਵੀਸਰਉ ਕਰਿ ਅਪੁਨੀ ਹਰਿ ਮਇਆ॥ 4॥14॥84॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 5:  Mun(u) tun(u) dhun(u) jinh(i) Prubh(i) deeya, rukhiya sehj(i) svaar(i). Serb kula kur(i) thaapiya, untar(i) jot(i) appar. Suda suda Prubh(u) simriyaiy untar(i) rukh(u) oordhaar(i). 1. Mayray mun, Hur(i) bin(u) uver(u) na koyay. Prubh shernayee suda ruhou dookh(u) na viyapaiy koyay. 1. Ruhaou.  Ruttan pdaruth maanka, soyina roopa khaak(u).  Maat pita soot bundhpa, koorray subhay saak. Jin(i) keeta tiseh(i) na jaanyee, munmukh pus(u) napaak. 2.  Untar(i) baahur(i) ruv(i) ruhiya, Tis nu jaanaiy door(i).  Trishna laagi ruch(i) ruhiya, untar(i) haumein koorr(i).  Bhugti Naam vihooniya, aaveh(i) vunjeh(i) poor.  3. Raakh(i) layhou Prubh Kernhaar(u), jea junt kur(i) duyya. Bin(u) Prubh koyay na rukhanhaar(u), maha bicket Jum bhuyya. Nanak Naam na visrou, kur(i) aapni Hur(i) muyya. 4.14.84.
Word Meaning:   Sehj(i) = without any special effort.    Svaar(i) = groomed (with noble qualities).   Serb Kulaa = flawless art.   Thaapiya = created. Jot(i) = light.   Untar(i) = in mind.   Oordhaar(i) = keeping in mind.   Oor = mind.    Viyapaiy = happen; affect.     1.
Pdaruth = (valuable) article; objective of life (union with Waheguru).  Ruttan = precious stones.   Maanka = pearls.   Roopa = silver.   Khaak(u) = dust.   Soot = sons.    Bundhpa = relatives.    Koorray = false; temporary.    Pus(u) = animal.   Napaak = unholy.    2.
Ruv(i) = pervading; present.   Trishna = worldly desires.   Ruch(i) = absorbed; indulged.     Haumein = ego and selfishness.    Vihooniya = devoid of.    Vunjay = goes.   Poor = a group of passengers (people).          3.          Kernhaar(u) = creator.   Junt = living beings.   Duyya = kindness.   Bin(u) = without.    Maha = huge.   Bicket = tough.    Jum = agent of death.   Bhuyya = bhai = fear.  Visrou = forget; lose from mind.   Muyya = ruth; kindness.   4.
MEANING:  There is nobody other than God (to protect you), oh my mind. No trouble or discomfort can even come near if one stays in His shelter all the time.       Pause. 
One should always worship and keep in mind Waheguru, who has given him mind, body, wealth, and has groomed him well (kept him in good shape) without any special effort. The creation of man, and placing His light in him, is a perfect art of God.           1.
The animal-like, unholy man does not recognize Waheguru, who has created him and given him everything.  The animal-like (ignorant) man does not realize that all the wealth, including the precious stones, gold, and silver, is like dust (which has no value in Waheguru’s Court), and all the relations like mother, father, and other relatives are false (temporary).           2.
(The ignorant man does not realize) that Waheguru is pervading inside and out (everywhere); he rather thinks Him to be somewhere far, far away.  The man is full of ego and is absorbed in fulfilling his desires involving Maya, all of which are temporary.  Without devotion to Waheguru, the load of passengers on the shore of the sea of Maya keeps coming and going (the world is caught in the cycle of reincarnation).           3.         Please show kindness and save the world (from the suffering of Maya, and of reincarnation), oh, the Creator of the world (Waheguru).  Without Waheguru, no one can save a man from the fear of the harsh agent of death.  I pray to Waheguru to be kind so that I never forget His Naam, oh Nanak.     4.14.84.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ 

ਮੇਰਾ ਤਨੁ ਅਰੁ ਧਨੁ ਮੇਰਾ ਰਾਜ ਰੂਪ ਮੈ ਦੇਸੁ॥ ਸੁਤ ਦਾਰਾ ਬਨਿਤਾ ਅਨੇਕ ਬਹੁਤੁ ਰੰਗ ਅਰੁ ਵੇਸ॥ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਰਿਦੈ ਨ ਵਸਈ ਕਾਰਜਿ ਕਿਤੇ ਨ ਲੇਖਿ॥ 1॥ ਮੇਰੇ ਮਨ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਧਿਆਇ॥ ਕਰਿ ਸੰਗਤਿ ਨਿਤ ਸਾਧ ਕੀ ਗੁਰ ਚਰਣੀ ਚਿਤੁ ਲਾਇ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਨਾਮੁ ਨਿਧਾਨੁ ਧਿਆਈਐ ਮਸਤਕਿ ਹੋਵੈ ਭਾਗੁ॥ ਕਾਰਜ ਸਭਿ ਸਵਾਰੀਅਹਿ ਗੁਰ ਕੀ ਚਰਣੀ ਲਾਗੁ॥ ਹਉਮੈ ਰੋਗੁ ਭ੍ਰਮੁ ਕਟੀਐ ਨਾ ਆਵੈ ਨਾ ਜਾਗੁ॥ 2॥ ਕਰਿ ਸੰਗਤਿ ਤੂ ਸਾਧ ਕੀ ਅਠਸਠਿ ਤੀਰਥ ਨਾਉ॥ ਜੀਉ ਪ੍ਰਾਣ ਮਨੁ ਤਨੁ ਹਰੇ ਸਾਚਾ ਏਹੁ ਸੁਆਉ॥
