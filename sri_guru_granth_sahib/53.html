MEANING:  It is easy to say, and to listen, to things about You, oh my Waheguru. But it is very difficult to do things to Your liking (to obey Your Huqam).        Pause.
I have been playful because of my deep faith, and my deep love for You, oh my dear Waheguru. We are Your children who make many mistakes and get astray from the right path, but You are our father and our mother (and are forgiving).       1.  
I consider You as my own, and take pride in You, and feel very strong because of my faith in You.  You are present in everybody, yet you are out of our reach (because of our ignorance). ਸਿਰੀਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 5 ਘਰੁ 1॥ 

ਸੰਤ ਜਨਾ ਮਿਲਿ ਭਾਈਆ ਕਟਿਅੜਾ ਜਮਕਾਲੁ॥ ਸਚਾ ਸਾਹਿਬੁ ਮਨਿ ਵੁਠਾ ਹੋਆ ਖਸਮੁ ਦਇਆਲੁ॥ ਪੂਰਾ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਭੇਟਿਆ ਬਿਨਸਿਆ ਸਭੁ ਜੰਜਾਲੁ॥ 1॥ ਮੇਰੇ ਸਤਿਗੁਰਾ ਹਉ ਤੁਧੁ ਵਿਟਹੁ ਕੁਰਬਾਣੁ॥ ਤੇਰੇ ਦਰਸਨ ਕਉ ਬਲਿਹਾਰਣੈ ਤੁਸਿ ਦਿਤਾ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਨਾਮੁ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਜਿਨ ਤੂ ਸੇਵਿਆ ਭਾਉ ਕਰਿ ਸੇਈ ਪੁਰਖ ਸੁਜਾਨ॥ ਤਿਨਾ ਪਿਛੈ ਛੁਟੀਐ ਜਿਨ ਅੰਦਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਨਿਧਾਨੁ॥ ਗੁਰ ਜੇਵਡ ਦਾਤਾ ਕੋ ਨਹੀ ਜਿਨਿ ਦਿਤਾ ਆਤਮ ਦਾਨੁ॥ 2॥ ਆਏ ਸੇ ਪਰਵਾਣੁ ਹਹਿ ਜਿਨ ਗੁਰੁ ਮਿਲਿਆ ਸੁਭਾਇ॥ ਸਚੇ ਸੇਤੀ ਰਤਿਆ ਦਰਗਹ ਬੈਸਣੁ ਜਾਇ॥ ਕਰਤੇ ਹਥਿ ਵਡਿਆਈਆ ਪੂਰਬਿ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਪਾਇ॥ 3॥ ਸਚੁ ਕਰਤਾ ਸਚੁ ਕਰਣਹਾਰੁ ਸਚੁ ਸਾਹਿਬੁ ਸਚੁ ਟੇਕ॥ ਸਚੋ ਸਚੁ ਵਖਾਣੀਐ ਸਚੋ ਬੁਧਿ ਬਿਬੇਕ॥ ਸਰਬ ਨਿਰੰਤਰਿ ਰਵਿ ਰਹਿਆ ਜਪਿ ਨਾਨਕ ਜੀਵੈ ਏਕ॥ 4॥28॥98॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 5:  Sunt junaa mil(i) bhaayiyaan, kutiyurra Jumkaal(u).  Sacha Sahib(u) mun(i) voottha hoa Khusm duyaal(i).  Poora Satgur(u) bhaytiya binsiya subh(u) junjaal(u). 1. Mayray Satgura, huoun toodh(u) vitoun koorbaan.  Tayray dershun kuou bulihaarnaiy toos(i) ditta Amrit Naam(u). 1. Ruhaou.  Jin Tu sayviya bhaaou kur(i) sayee purakh soojaan. Tinha pichhaiy chhootiyaiy, jin undar(i) Naam(u) nidhaan(u). Gur jayvud daata ko nuhi jin(i) ditta aatum daan(u). 2.  Aayay say purvaan(u) heh(i) jin Gur(u) miliya soobhaayay. Sachay saytee ruttiyaan Durgeh baisun(u) jaayay. Kurtay huth(i) vudiyayeeyaan, poorub(i) likhiya paayay. 3. Sach(u) Kerta Sach(u) kernhaar(u), Such Sahib(u), Such tayk. Sucho Sach(u) vukhaaniyaiy Sucho boodh(i) bibayk.  Serb niruntar ruv(i) ruhhiya jup(i) Nanak jeevaiy Aik. 4.28.98.
Word Meaning:    Kutiyurra = cut; finish off.    Jumkaal(u) = Angel of death.  Voottha = came to reside.   Khusm(u) = husband; Master.   Bhaytiya = met (took shelter).   Binsiya = vanished.   Junjaal(u) = undesired responsibilities.       1.           Toodh(u) = you.  Vitoun = on; for.   Koorbaan(u) = Bulihaarnaiy = sacrifice life; be a slave.  Toos(i) = pleasingly; kindly.     Pause.
Bhaao = respectful affection.   Purakh = men.   Soojaan = wise.   Pichhaiy = behind; by following; because of.    Chhootiyaiy = become free (from all bonds).   Nidhaan(u) = treasure.   Jayvud(u) = as great.   Daata = donor; provider.    Aatum = for soul.     2. 
Purvaan(u) = approved; successful.    Soobhaayay = (because of) affection and dedication.   Ruttiyaan = imbued.   Durgeh = (Waheguru’s) Court.    Jaayay = place; room.   Kertay = Creator.   Poorub(i)  Likhiya = pre-written; predestined.       3.
Sach(u) = eternal.    Kernhaar(u) = Creator.    Vukhaaniyaiy = say; describe.  Boodh(i) Bibayk = wisdom to make distinction (between good and bad).   Niruntar(i) = without any gap; uniformly.     Ruv(i) = (is) present.    Aik = One (Waheguru).     4.
MEANING:  I feel zestful to sacrifice my life for You, oh my Satguru. I will gladly sacrifice my life to have a holy sight of Yours, as You have delightfully gifted me Waheguru’s immortalizing (Amrit-like) Naam.          Pause.         My saintly brothers (devotee friends) have dispelled my fear of the Angel of death.  The True Lord (Waheguru) has been kind to me and has made my heart His home.  I have internalized the Perfect Guru, and all my undesired responsibilities have ended.         1.          Those who serve You with respectful affection are the real wise people.  Because of those individuals who harbour Waheguru’s Naam in their hearts, many people find salvation in their company (because they follow their footsteps). Nobody else equals the Guru who gives the gift (of Waheguru’s Naam) for our souls.        2. 
Those who have realized the Guru by their respectful affection are respected in this world.  Those who are imbued in Waheguru’s Naam find a respectful position in Waheguru’s Court.  However, such honour is in the hands of the Creator, and only those receive it who are predestined to do so.         3.            True (eternal) is the Creator, True is the Lord, and True is His support. True (everlasting) is the description of the Lord, and so is the wisdom that can differentiate between the good and the bad.  I survive on reciting the Naam of the “One” who is present everywhere without any exception, oh Nanak.    4.28.98

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥  

ਗੁਰੁ ਪਰਮੇਸੁਰੁ ਪੂਜੀਐ ਮਨਿ ਤਨਿ ਲਾਇ ਪਿਆਰੁ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਦਾਤਾ ਜੀਅ ਕਾ ਸਭਸੈ ਦੇਇ ਅਧਾਰੁ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਬਚਨ ਕਮਾਵਣੇ ਸਚਾ ਏਹੁ ਵੀਚਾਰੁ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਸਾਧੂ ਸੰਗਤਿ ਰਤਿਆ ਮਾਇਆ ਮੋਹੁ ਸਭੁ ਛਾਰੁ॥1॥ ਮੇਰੇ ਸਾਜਨ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਸਮਾਲਿ॥ ਸਾਧੂ ਸੰਗਤਿ ਮਨਿ ਵਸੈ ਪੂਰਨ ਹੋਵੈ ਘਾਲੁ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥   ਗੁਰੁ ਸਮਰਥੁ ਅਪਾਰੁ ਗੁਰੁ ਵਡਭਾਗੀ ਦਰਸਨੁ ਹੋਇ॥ ਗੁਰੁ ਅਗੋਚਰੁ ਨਿਰਮਲਾ ਗੁਰ ਜੇਵਡੁ ਅਵਰੁ ਨ ਕੋਇ॥ ਗੁਰੁ ਕਰਤਾ ਗੁਰੁ ਕਰਣਹਾਰੁ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਸਚੀ ਸੋਇ॥ ਗੁਰ ਤੇ ਬਾਹਰਿ ਕਿਛੁ ਨਹੀ ਗੁਰੁ ਕੀਤਾ ਲੋੜੇ ਸੁ ਹੋਇ॥2॥ ਗੁਰੁ ਤੀਰਥੁ ਗੁਰੁ ਪਾਰਜਾਤੁ ਗੁਰੁ ਮਨਸਾ ਪੂਰਣਹਾਰੁ॥ ਗੁਰੁ ਦਾਤਾ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਦੇਇ ਉਧਰੈ ਸਭੁ ਸੰਸਾਰ॥ ਗੁਰੁ ਸਮਰਥੁ ਗੁਰੁ ਨਿਰੰਕਾਰੁ ਗੁਰੁ ਊਚਾ ਅਗਮ ਅਪਾਰੁ॥ ਗੁਰ ਕੀ ਮਹਿਮਾ ਅਗਮ ਹੈ ਕਿਆ ਕਥੈ ਕਥਨਹਾਰੁ॥ 3॥ ਜਿਤੜੇ ਫਲ ਮਨਿ ਬਾਛੀਅਹਿ ਤਿਤੜੇ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਪਾਸਿ॥ ਪੂਰਬਿ ਲਿਖੇ ਪਾਵਣੇ ਸਾਚੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਦੇ ਰਾਸਿ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਸਰਣੀ ਆਇਆ ਬਾਹੁੜਿ ਨਹੀ ਬਿਨਾਸੁ॥ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਨਕ ਕਦੇ ਨ ਵਿਸਰਉ ਏਹੁ ਜੀਉ ਪਿੰਡੁ ਤੇਰਾ ਸਾਸੁ॥ 4॥29॥99॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 5:  Gur(u) Permayshur(u) poojiyaiy, mun(i) tun(i) laayay piyaar(u). Satgur(u) daata jea ka subhsaiy dayay adhaar(u).  Satgur Buchun kmaavnay Sacha eho veechaar(i). Bin(u) Sadhu sungat(i) ruttiyaan, Maya moh(u) subh(u) chhaar(u). 1. Mayray saajun, Hur(i) Hur(i) Naam(u) smaal(i).  Sadhu sungat(i) mun(i) vusaiy, poorun hovaiy ghaal. 1. Ruhao. Gur(u) sumruth(u) apaar(u) Gur(u), vudbhaagi dershun(u) hoyay. Gur(u) agochur(u) nirmula, Gur jayvud(u) uver(u) na koyay. Gur(u) kerta, Gur(u) kernhaar(u), Gurmukh(i) suchi soyay. Gur tay baahur(i)  kichh(u) nuhi, Gur(u) keeta lorray su hoyay. 2.Gur(u) teeruth(u), Gur(u) paarjaat(u), Gur(u) munsa poorunhaar(u).  Gur(u) daata Hur(i) Naam(u) dayay, oodhraiy subh(u) sunsaar. Gur(u) sumruth(u), Gur(u) nirunkaar(u), Gur(u) oocha agum apaar(u). Gur kee mehma agum haiy kiyya kuthaiy kuthunhaar(u). 3. Jitrray ful mun(i) baanchhiyeh(I) titrray Satgur paas(i). Poorub(i) likhay paavnay Saach(u) Naam(u) day raas(i). Satgur sherni aayaan, bahoorr(i) nuhi binaas(u).  Hur(i) Nanak kuday na visrou, eho jeao pind(u) tayra saas(u).    4.29.99.
Word Meaning:  Permayshur(u) = the Supreme Being.   Daata = donor; provider.   Adhaar(u) = support; food.   Buchan = words (Shabad; Teachings).   Kmaavnay = act on.  Sacha = everlasting.    Ruttiyaan = imbued.     Chhaar(u) = (like) dust.         1.
Saajun = friend.   Smaal(i) = take care; keep in mind.   Ghaal(u) = hard work.          Pause.
Sumruth(u) = capable of doing anything.    Apaar(u) = limitless; incomprehensible.  Agochur(u) = intangible; ethereal.    Soyay = fame.    Lorray = wishes.          2.
Paarjaat = a mythical tree that fulfills all your wishes. It is one of the five trees in the Paradise. The other four are: Kulp, Mundaar, Suntaan, and Hari-Chundan.  Paarjaat was one of the 14 precious articles retrieved from the Kheer Sea when it was churned by the gods and the demons.   Munsa = wish.  Poorunhaar(u) = fulfiller.   Oodhraiy = is salvaged.    Nirunkaar(u) = formless.   Agum = physically unreachable.    Mehma = praise; greatness.     Kuthunhaar(u) = describer.       3.       Ful = results (wishes).    Baanchhiyeh(i) = wish for.   Titrray = all those.   Poorub(i) Likhay = pre-written; predestined.    Raas(i) = capital; wealth.  Sherni = shelter.    Bahoorr(i) = again.  Binaas(u) = destruction (death; rebirth).    Visrou = forgotten.   Pind(u) = body.     4.
MEANING:  Keep Waheguru’s Naam in mind all the time, oh my friend.  He comes to reside in your heart when you take shelter of the Guru, and all your hard labour is rewarded.      Pause.              Worship the Guru, who is a manifestation of Waheguru, with deep love from your mind and body, oh my friend.  Satguru is the provider for the body and soul, and provides food to everybody.  To act on the advice of the Guru is the True (everlasting path) to unite with Waheguru.  Without getting imbued in the love of Satguru’s Teachings, all Attachments with Maya are useless (in the spiritual realm) and are just like ash.        1.
The Guru (a manifestation of Waheguru) is fully capable of doing anything but is not comprehensible; only a few lucky people get to “see” Him (with mental eyes).   The Guru is unreachable physically. He is pure, and nobody can equal Him. (Being a manifestation of Waheguru), He is the Creator, and also the source (provider) of everything. His devotees, who realize this, have everlasting fame. There is nothing beyond the control of the Guru, and whatever He wishes, happens (right away).         2.
The Guru Himself is the holy place, the Parijaat Tree, and the fulfiller of all the wishes.  The Guru delivers Waheguru’s Naam, because of which the whole world is salvaged.  The Guru is omnipotent; He is formless, above Maya, intangible, and limitless (unreachable physically).  The greatness of the Guru is beyond description, so what can a person say about Him?       3.
The Guru has all those rewards that a person wishes for. He gives Waheguru’s Naam as the capital to His devotees, but the fulfillment of the wishes and connection with Him are predetermined by Waheguru.  When a person comes to the Guru’s shelter, he becomes immortal (is never born again).  I beg the Guru that I should never forget Waheguru, who controls the life, the body, and our breath (life), oh Nanak.   4.29. 99.
