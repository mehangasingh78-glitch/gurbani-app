(ਅੰਗ 45)॥       ਮੇਰੇ ਮਨ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਧਿਆਇ॥ ਨਾਮੁ ਸਹਾਈ ਸਦਾ ਸੰਗਿ ਆਗੈ ਲਏ ਛਡਾਇ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਦੁਨੀਆ ਕੀਆ ਵਡਿਆਈਆ ਕਵਨੈ ਆਵਹਿ ਕਾਮਿ॥ ਮਾਇਆ ਕਾ ਰੰਗੁ ਸਭੁ ਫਿਕਾ ਜਾਤੋ ਬਿਨਸਿ ਨਿਦਾਨਿ॥ ਜਾ ਕੈ ਹਿਰਦੈ ਹਰਿ ਵਸੈ ਸੋ ਪੂਰਾ ਪਰਧਾਨੁ॥ 2॥ ਸਾਧੂ ਕੀ ਹੋਹੁ  ਰੇਣੁਕਾ ਆਪਣਾ ਆਪੁ ਤਿਆਗਿ॥ ਉਪਾਵ ਸਿਆਣਪ ਸਗਲ ਛਡਿ ਗੁਰ ਕੀ ਚਰਣੀ ਲਾਗੁ॥ ਤਿਸਹਿ ਪਰਾਪਤਿ ਰਤਨੁ ਹੋਇ ਜਿਸੁ ਮਸਤਕਿ ਹੋਵੈ ਭਾਗੁ॥ 3॥ ਤਿਸੈ ਪਰਾਪਤਿ ਭਾਈਹੋ ਜਿਸੁ ਦੇਵੈ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਆਪਿ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਕੀ ਸੇਵਾ ਸੋ ਕਰੇ ਜਿਸੁ ਬਿਨਸੈ ਹਉਮੈ ਤਾਪੁ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਕਉ ਗੁਰੁ ਭੇਟਿਆ ਬਿਨਸੇ ਸਗਲ ਸੰਤਾਪ॥ 4॥8॥78॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 5:  Naam(u) dhiyayay so sookhi, jis(u) mookh(u) oojul(u) hoyay. Pooraiy Gur tay paayiyaiy pergut(u) subhnee loyay. Sadh Sungat(i) kaiy ghur(i) vusaiy aiko Sacha soyay. 1.
(PAGE 45):       Mayray mun, Hur(i) Hur(i) Naam(u) dhiyayay. Naam(u) suhaayee suda sung(I, aagaiy luyay chhudayay. 1. Ruhaou.   Dooniya keeyaan vudiyayiyaan kuvnaiy aaveh(i) kaam(i). Maya ka rung(u) subh(u) fikka, jaato binus(i) nidaan(i).  Ja kaiy hirdaiy Hur(i) vusaiy, so poora purdhaan. 2.  Sadhu kee hohou raynuka, aapna aap(u) tiyaag(i). Oopaav siyaanup sugul chhud(i), Gur kee churni laag(u). Tiseh(i) praaput(i) ruttan(u) hoyay jis(u) mustuk(i) hovaiy bhaag(u). 3. Tisaiy praaput(i) bhaayeeo jis(u) dayvaiy Prubh(u) Aap(i). Satguru kee sayva so kuray jis(u) binsaiy haumein taap(u). Nanak kuou Gur(u) bhaytiya binsay sugul suntaap.  4.8.78. 
Word Meaning:  Dhiyayay = remember.  Oojul(u) = clean; pious.   Pergut(u) = appears; shows self.      Loyay = worlds.        1.         Suhayee = helpful.   Sung(i) = accompanies.   Aagaiy = next world.       Pause.        Vudiyaayiyaan = praise; honours.   Kuvnaiy = which; what.   Fikka = tasteless; light (colour) which wears off fast.    Binus(i) = vanishes.  Nidaan(i) = at the end.        2.         Raynuka = dust (of the feet); ego-free.     Oopaav = efforts; arrangements.    Praaput(i) = aquire.  Ruttan = valuable like a jewel (Waheguru’s Naam).  Mustuk(i) = (on the) forehead.     Bhaag(u) = fortune.         3.       Binsaiy = vanish.   Taap = heat; suffering.   Bhaytiya = met.   Suntaap = suffering.    4.
MEANING:  Recite Waheguru’s Naam all the time, oh my mind.  Waheguru’s Naam is always a great support that accompanies you into the next world and frees you from the clutches of the Angel of Justice.        Pause.           The person who always remembers Waheguru’s Naam, enjoys comfort, and his face glows (with the purity of his mind), and is known (respected) all over the world.  However, Waheguru’s Naam can be acquired only from the Guru, as the eternal Waheguru resides in the Guru (and His devotees).        1.
The honours conferred by the world on a man are of no use in the spiritual world. The colour of (love for) different attractions of Maya is short-lived and wears off eventually, but a man who harbours Waheguru’s Naam in his heart is respected everywhere.        2.
Overcome your ego and be humble as if you are the dust of the Guru’s feet, and, quitting all your wisdom, take the shelter of the Guru, oh my mind. The individual, who has good fortune on his forehead, acquires Waheguru’s Naam, which is invaluable like a precious stone.        3.        However, only that individual acquires His Naam whom Waheguru confers on with His own volition.  And only that individual serves the Guru who has overcome the illness of ego. Luckily, I got connected with the Guru, and all my suffering has gone, oh Nanak.    4.8.78.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ 

ਇਕੁ ਪਛਾਣੂ ਜੀਅ ਕਾ ਇਕੋ ਰਖਣਹਾਰੁ॥ ਇਕਸ ਕਾ ਮਨਿ ਆਸਰਾ ਇਕੋ ਪ੍ਰਾਣ ਅਧਾਰੁ॥ ਤਿਸੁ ਸਰਣਾਈ ਸਦਾ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਾਰਬ੍ਰਹਮੁ ਕਰਤਾਰੁ॥ 1॥ ਮਨ ਮੇਰੇ ਸਗਲ ਉਪਾਵ ਤਿਆਗੁ॥ ਗੁਰੁ ਪੂਰਾ ਆਰਾਧਿ ਨਿਤ ਇਕਸੁ ਕੀ ਲਿਵ ਲਾਗੁ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਇਕੋ ਭਾਈ ਮਿਤੁ ਇਕੁ ਇਕੋ ਮਾਤ ਪਿਤਾ॥ ਇਕਸ ਕੀ ਮਨਿ ਟੇਕ ਹੈ ਜਿਨਿ ਜੀਉ ਪਿੰਡੁ ਦਿਤਾ॥ ਸੋ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਮਨਹੁ ਨ ਵਿਸਰੈ ਜਿਨਿ ਸਭੁ ਕਿਛੁ ਵਸਿ ਕੀਤਾ॥ 2॥ ਘਰਿ ਇਕੋ ਬਾਹਿਰ ਇਕੋ ਥਾਨ ਥਨੰਤਰਿ ਆਪਿ॥ ਜੀਅ ਜੰਤ ਸਭਿ ਜਿਨਿ ਕੀਏ ਆਠ ਪਹਰ ਤਿਸੁ ਜਾਪਿ॥ ਇਕਸੁ ਸੇਤੀ ਰਤਿਆ ਨ ਹੋਵੀ ਸੋਗ ਸੰਤਾਪੁ॥ 3॥ ਪਾਰਬ੍ਰਹਮੁ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਏਕੁ ਹੈ ਦੂਜਾ ਨਾਹੀ ਕੋਇ॥ ਜੀਉ ਪਿੰਡੁ ਸਭੁ ਤਿਸ ਕਾ ਜੋ ਤਿਸੁ ਭਾਵੈ ਸੁ ਹੋਇ॥ ਗੁਰਿ ਪੂਰੈ ਪੂਰਾ ਭਇਆ ਜਪਿ ਨਾਨਕ ਸਚਾ ਸੋਇ॥  4॥9॥79॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 5: Ikk(u) puchhaanoo jea ka, ikko Rukhunhaar(u). Ikus ka mun(i) aasra Ikko praan adhaar(u). Tis(u) shernayee suda sookh(u), Parbrahm Kartar(u). 1. Mun mayray, sugul oopaav tiyaag(u). Gur(u) poora aaraadh(i) nit Ikus kee liv laag(u). 1. Ruhaou.  Ikko bhaayee mit(u) ikk(u), Ikko maat pita. Ikus kee mun(i) tayk haiy,  jin(i) jeo pind(u) ditta. So Prubh(u) munou na vissray jin(i) subh(u) kichh(u) vus(i) keeta. 2. Ghur(i) Ikko, baahir Ikko, thaan thnuntar(i) Aap(i). Jea junt subh(i) jin(i) keeyay, aatth pehr Tis(u) jaap(i). Ikus(u) saytee ruttiyaan na hovee soag suntaap(u). 3. Parbrahm Prubh(u) aik(u) haiy, dooja naahi koyay. Jeo pind(u) subh(u) Tis ka, jo Tis(u) bhaavaiy su hoyay. Gur(i) Pooraiy, poora bhuyya, jup(i) Nanak Sacha soyay.  4.9.79. 
Word Meaning: Puchhaanoo = one who recognizes (knows well).   Rukhunhaar(u) = saviour.  Adhaar(u) = support; provider.  Shernayee = in shelter of.  Parbrahm = God. Kartar(u) = the Creator (Waheguru).      1.            Sugul = all.   Oopaav = efforts; approaches (like rituals).  Tiyaag(u) = quit; discard. Aaraadh(i) = worship.   Nit = every day.  Liv = concentration.     Pause.            Mit(u) = friend.   Tayk = support.   Jeo = soul.   Pind(u) = body.  Vus(i) = control.  2.           Thaan Thnuntar = different places (everywhere).  Aatth Pehr = day and night.  Jaap(i) = recite (Naam).  Ruttiyaan = being imbued.   Soag = grief.   Suntaap  = suffering.       3.
Bhaavaiy = as per Will.   Poora bhuyya = became complete or perfect (like God).       4.
MEANING:  Discarding all other efforts and approaches (all prevalent religious rituals), worship (recite the Naam with full dedication to) only the Perfect Guru (Waheguru) day and night, oh my mind!       Pause.         Waheguru is the only Supreme Being who knows your needs and wishes, and who is your saviour. Remember, He is the only support of mind and life (fulfills your spiritual and worldly needs).  There are all comforts in Waheguru’s (who is also the Creator) shelter, oh my mind.       1.            Only He is your real brother, friend, mother and father.  Only He, who has given us soul and body, is the real support of your mind (to fulfill its needs and wishes).  The Waheguru, which controls everything, should never be forgotten, oh man.      2.          Only He is present in one’s heart, and everywhere outside in the whole world. Recite His Naam day and night, who has created all the living beings. When one is imbued in His Naam, he does not experience any grief or suffering (his mental level becomes so high spiritually that he does not feel any suffering).           3.
There is only one Supreme Being, and none other (He has no rival).  Our body and mind belong to Him (are given by Him), and only those things happen in the world which He wishes.  The Perfect Guru connects one with Waheguru, and one merges with Him by worshipping Him (by reciting His Naam and obeying His Command), oh Nanak.   4.9.79.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ 

ਜਿਨਾ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਸਿਉ ਚਿਤੁ ਲਾਇਆ ਸੇ ਪੂਰੇ ਪਰਧਾਨ॥ ਜਿਨ ਕਉ ਆਪਿ ਦਇਆਲੁ ਹੋਇ ਤਿਨ ਉਪਜੈ ਮਨਿ ਗਿਆਨੁ॥ ਜਿਨ ਕਉ ਮਸਤਕਿ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਤਿਨ ਪਾਇਆ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ॥1॥ ਮਨ ਮੇਰੇ ਏਕੋ ਨਾਮੁ ਧਿਆਇ॥ ਸਰਬ ਸੁਖਾ ਸੁਖ ਊਪਜਹਿ ਦਰਗਹ ਪੈਧਾ ਜਾਇ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਜਨਮ ਮਰਣ ਕਾ ਭਉ ਗਇਆ ਭਾਉ ਭਗਤਿ ਗੋਪਾਲੁ॥ ਸਾਧੂ ਸੰਗਤਿ ਨਿਰਮਲਾ ਆਪਿ ਕਰੇ ਪ੍ਰਤਿਪਾਲ॥ ਜਨਮ ਮਰਣ ਕੀ ਮਲੁ ਕਟੀਐ ਗੁਰ ਦਰਸਨੁ ਦੇਖਿ ਨਿਹਾਲ॥ 2॥ ਥਾਨ ਥਾਨੰਤਰਿ ਰਵਿ ਰਹਿਆ ਪਾਰਬ੍ਰਹਮ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਸੋਇ॥ ਸਭਨਾ ਦਾਤਾ ਏਕੁ ਹੈ ਦੂਜਾ ਨਾਹੀ ਕੋਇ॥ ਤਿਸੁ ਸਰਣਾਈ ਛੁਟੀਐ ਕੀਤਾ ਲੋੜੇ ਸੁ ਹੋਇ॥ 3॥ ਜਿਨ ਮਨਿ ਵਸਿਆ ਪਾਰਬ੍ਰਹਮ ਸੇ ਪੂਰੇ ਪਰਧਾਨ॥ ਤਿਨ ਕੀ ਸੋਭਾ ਨਿਰਮਲੀ ਪਰਗਟੁ ਭਈ ਜਹਾਨ॥ ਜਿਨੀ ਮੇਰਾ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਧਿਆਇਆ ਨਾਨਕ ਤਿਨ ਕੁਰਬਾਨ॥ 4॥10॥80॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 5:  Jinha Satgur siyoun chit(u) laaya, say pooray perdhaan. Jin kuuou aap(i) duyaal hoyay tin oopjaiy mun(i) Giyan(u). Jin kou mustuk(i) likhiya, tin paaya Hur(i) Naam(u). 1. Mun mayray, aiko Naam(u) dhiyayay. Serb sookha sookh oopjeh(i) Durgeh paidha jaayay. 1. Ruhaou. Junum mern ka bhuou guyya, bhaao bhugat(i) Gopal(u). Sadhu Sungat(i) nirmula aap(i) kuray pritpaal. Junum mern kee mul(u) kuttiyaiy, Gur dershun(u) daykh(i) nihaal. 2. Thaan thnuntar(i) ruv ruhhiya, Parbrahm Prubh(u) soyay. Subhna daata Aik(u) haiy, dooja naahi koyay. Tis(u) shernaayee chhootiyaiy keeta lorraiy su hoyay. 3. Jinh mun(i) vusiya Parbrahm, say pooray purdhaan. Tin kee sobha nirmuli, purgut(u) bhuyee jahaan. Jinhi mayra Prubh(u) dhiyaaya, Nanak tin koorbaan.  4.10.80.
Word Meaning:   Chit(u) = mind.   Perdhaan = honourable.  Duyaal = kind.   Oopjaiy = sprouts.  Giyan = Divine Knowledge (enlightenment).   Mustuk(i) = forehead.      1.
Dhiyayay = recite (with love and concentration). Serb Sookhaan Sookh = supreme comfort.  Oopjeh(i) = sprouts.   Paidha = wears (robe of honour).        Pause.
Bhuou = fear.  Bhaao = (respectful) love.  Gopal = who nourishes the earth (God).  Nirmula = clean; pure.   Pritpaal = nourish and nurture.   Mul(u) = filth.  Gur dershan(u) = the holy sight of the Guru (internalization).   Nihaal = blessed.              2.          Thaan Thnunar(i) = everywhere.     Ruv(i) = pervades.     Lorraiy = wishes for.           3.           Vusiya = takes residence.  Sobha = fame; praise.  Nirmuli = pure.  Purgut(i) = appear; spread.   Bhuyyi = 
came into being.      4.
MEANING:  Recite the Naam of the only “One” (Waheguru), as a result of which you will experience the supreme comfort, and will wear the robe of honour (be honoured) in the Court of Waheguru, oh my mind.       Pause.           Those whose minds are connected to Satguru are truly honourable.  Divine Knowledge (enlightenment) sprouts in the minds of those whom Waheguru is kind to. But only those who acquire Waheguru’s Naam (get connected with Him) who have good fortune on their foreheads (are predestined for).           1.
Those who worship Waheguru with love (and dedication) lose the fear of birth and death. Their mind becomes pure in the company of the Guru, and Waheguru Himself nourishes and nurtures them (fosters noble qualities in them).  One’s filth of sins of many lives is washed away, and he is blessed when he internalizes Satguru.       2.       Waheguru is present everywhere, and He is the only provider for everybody - there is none other. One gets freedom from the bonds of Maya (is salvaged), and he acquires whatever he wishes for when he comes under the shelter of Waheguru.      3.          Those are truly honourable who have made their hearts a residence for Waheguru. Their holy fame spreads all over the world.  I am a sacrifice for those who recite Waheguru’s Naam, oh Nanak.     4.10.80.
