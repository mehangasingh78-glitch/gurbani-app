Word Meaning:  Mujun(u) = bathing.    Sung(i) = with.   Dhoorri = dust of the feet.   Daan(u) = donation.    Kerm = deeds.    Mohiyaiy = captivates.  Binsaiy = is destroyed.    Loabh(u) = greed.    Sooaan(u) = dog.   Maarug(i) = path.   Oostut(i) = praise.  Utth(i) Sutth(i) = 8+60 = 68 (holy places of the Hindus.   Poonn(u) = noble deeds, including donations.    Pervaan(u) = approved.   Soojaan = wise.   Soochay = clean (minded).    Kaandhieh(i) = are called.  
NOTE: The month of Maagh is considered very holy by Hindus. They consider it a great virtuous deed to take a dip at holy places, especially, Prayag (Allahabad, which is on the confluence of the Rivers Ganga and Jamuna), on the first day of this month, and give donations to Brahmans.
MEANING: (Rather than taking a dip at the so-called holy places), one should take a dip (in Waheguru’s Naam-Amrit) in the company of, and in the dust of the feet of holy men (their Shabad; an expression for deep reverence).  Recite and listen to Waheguru’s Naam, and donate it to others (motivate them to recite the Naam). By taking a dip in the Naam-Amrit, one’s filth of misdeeds gathered over many lives is washed away, and one's ego is eradicated.  Lust and rage cannot captivate (attract) the devotee, and his dog-like greed is destroyed.  The world praises those who follow the path of truth.  (The Hindus consider pilgrimage to the sixty-eight holy places as a highly virtuous deed), But taking pity on a needy human being (helping him) is much more acceptable to Waheguru.  That individual is really wise who is blessed with virtuous qualities by the benevolence of God.  I would love to offer my life to those who are connected with Waheguru, oh Nanak.  Those are considered pure (without any vice) in the month of Maagh, to whom God is kind.   12.

MEANING: I wish to serve my Satguru and recite his Shabad day and night.  Overcoming my selfishness, I wish to drop on His feet, talk sweetly with my tongue, and request that He connect me with the real friend – Waheguru - from whom I have been separated for many lives.  Those who are disconnected from Waheguru do not enjoy real comfort (bliss), oh my friend!   I have searched all the skies (wandered everywhere) for peace of mind, but there is no peace (blissfulness) without dear Waheguru.  However, I cannot blame anyone for my separation, because it is the result of my own evil deeds.  I beg Waheguru to kindly protect me (from the attractions of Maya so that I may connect with Him) as there is no one else who can do that for me, or make someone else do the same.  It is like getting degraded to dust without You, oh Waheguru!
