(ਅੰਗ 41)॥    

 ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 4॥ 

ਹਉ ਪੰਥੁ ਦਸਾਈ ਨਿਤ ਖੜੀ ਕੋਈ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਦਸੇ ਤਿਨਿ ਜਾਉ॥ ਜਿਨੀ ਮੇਰਾ ਪਿਆਰਾ ਰਾਵਿਆ ਤਿਨ ਪੀਛੈ ਲਾਗਿ ਫਿਰਾਉ॥ ਕਰਿ ਮਿੰਨਤਿ ਕਰਿ ਜੋਦੜੀ ਮੈ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਮਿਲਣੇ ਕਾ ਚਾਉ॥1॥ ਮੇਰੇ ਭਾਈ ਜਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਮੋ ਕਉ ਹਰਿ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਮੇਲਿ ਮਿਲਾਇ॥ ਹਉ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਵਿਟਹੁ ਵਾਰਿਆ ਜਿਨਿ ਹਰਿ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਦੀਆ ਦਿਖਾਇ॥1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਹੋਇ ਨਿਮਾਣੀ ਢਹਿ ਪਵਾ ਪੂਰੈ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਪਾਸਿ॥ ਨਿਮਾਣਿਆ ਗੁਰੁ ਮਾਣੁ ਹੈ ਗੁਰੁ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਕਰੇ ਸਾਬਾਸਿ॥ ਹਉ ਗੁਰੁ ਸਾਲਾਹਿ ਨ ਰਜਉ ਮੈ ਮੇਲੇ ਹਰਿ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਪਾਸਿ॥2॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਨੋ ਸਭੁ ਕੋ ਲੋਚਦਾ ਜੇਤਾ ਜਗਤੁ ਸਭੁ ਕੋਇ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਭਾਗਾ ਦਰਸਨੁ ਨਾ ਥੀਐ ਭਾਗਹੀਣ ਬਹਿ ਰੋਇ॥ ਜੋ ਹਰਿ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਭਾਣਾ ਸੋ ਥੀਆ ਧੁਰਿ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਨ ਮੇਟੈ ਕੋਇ ॥3॥ ਆਪੇ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਆਪਿ ਹਰਿ ਆਪੇ ਮੇਲਿ ਮਿਲਾਇ॥ ਆਪਿ ਦਇਆ ਕਰਿ ਮੇਲਸੀ ਗੁਰ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਪੀਛੈ ਪਾਇ॥ ਸਭੁ ਜਗਜੀਵਨੁ ਜਗਿ ਆਪਿ ਹੈ ਨਾਨਕ ਜਲੁ ਜਲਹਿ ਸਮਾਇ॥ 4॥4॥68॥
(PAGE 41): Siri Raag(u) Mehla 4: Houn punth(u) dusaayeen nit khurri, koyee Prubh(u) dussay tin(i) jaaoun. Jini mayra Piyara raaviya, tin peechhaiy laag(i) phiraoun.  Kur(i) minut(i) kur(i) joadrri, mein Prubh(u) milnay ka chaaou. 1. Mayray bhaayee juna, koyee mo kuou Hur(i) Prubh(u) mayl(i) milaayay. Houn Satgur vitoun vaariya jin(i) Hur(i) Prubh(u) deeya dikhayay. 1. Ruhaou. Hoyay nimaani dheh(i) puvaan, pooraiy Satgur paas(i). Nimaniyaan Gur(u) maan(u) haiy, Gur(u) Satgur(u) kuray shabaash(i). Houn Gur(u) saalahay na rujoun, mein maylay Hur(i) Prubh(u) paas(i). 2. Satgur nu subh(u) ko loachda jayta jugat(u) subh(u) koyay. Bin(u) bhaagaan dershun(u) na theeyaiy, bhaagheen beh(i) royay. Jo Hur(i) Prubh(i) bhaana so theeya, dhoor(i) likhiya na maytaiy koyay. 3. Aapay Satgur(u) aap(i) Hur(i) aapay mayl(i) milaayay. Aap(i) duyya kur(i) maylsi Gur Satgur peechhaiy paayay. Subh(u) Jugjeevun(u) jug(i) Aap(i) haiy, Nanak jul(u) juleh(i) smaayay.  4.4.68.
Word Meaning:  Punth(u) = (spiritual) path.  Dusaayeen = I enquire.   Nit = every day; always. Raaviya = enjoyed (His union).  Laag(i) = following.  Jodrri = beg; request.  Chaaou = zest: gusto; enthusiasm.       1.         Bhaayee Juna = brothers and friends.   Vitoun = for.   Vaariya = sacrifice.         Pause.       Nimaana = without ego; humble; powerless.   Dheh(i) = drop on feet.   Maan(u) = confers honour.  Shabaash(i) = applauds.   Rujoun = satisfied; tired.     2.
Loachda = strongly wishes for.  Jayta = however much; all.   Jugat(u) = world.   Bhaagaan = luck.  Theeyaiy = happens.   Bhaana = Wish; liking.   Dhoor(i) = from the source.   Maytaiy = erases; stops.        3.         Duyya = kindness; mercy.  Peechaiy = behind; follow.  Jugjeevun(u) = God.  Jul = water.  Juleh(i) = in water.   Smaayay = merges.       4.
MEANING:  Would one of you, please, connect me with Waheguru, oh my dear devotee friends!  I am a sacrifice to my Satguru, whom I know shows (connects with) You, oh Waheguru.        Pause.         I am always waiting to ask for the path (to union with Waheguru); I will humbly approach the individual who can show it to me.  I will follow those who have enjoyed union with the dear Waheguru. I am very zealous to connect with Waheguru, so I will request them, beg them, to connect me with Him.         1.
Shedding all my ego, I will drop at the feet of the Satguru (surrender myself).  The Guru applauds and confers dignity on those who have no ego and are humble.  I never get tired of singing the praises of my Guru, who connects me to Waheguru.        2.
Everybody in the world wishes (yearns) to connect with Satguru but cannot do so without good fortune. The unlucky simply laments over his helplessness. (But even such people should not be blamed) because only God’s will prevails, and nobody can erase what has been predestined for them.   (So, until Waheguru is kind, they cannot connect with Him with their own efforts).           3.          Satguru is a manifestation of God, who Himself connects individuals with Him through Satguru.  When Waheguru is kind, He connects the individuals with Satguru (for them to follow His Teachings).  Waheguru is the life of everybody in the world, and He is blended with everyone indiscernibly just as a small body of water mixes with a large body, oh Nanak.    4.4.68.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 4॥ ਰਸੁ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਰਸੁ ਅਤਿ ਭਲਾ ਕਿਤੁ ਬਿਧਿ ਮਿਲੈ ਰਸੁ ਖਾਇ॥ ਜਾਇ ਪੁਛਹੁ ਸੋਹਾਗਣੀ ਤੁਸਾ ਕਿਉ ਕਰਿ ਮਿਲਿਆ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਆਇ॥ ਓਇ ਵੇਪਰਵਾਹ ਨ ਬੋਲਨੀ ਹਉ ਮਲਿ ਮਲਿ ਧੋਵਾ ਤਿਨ ਪਾਇ॥ 1॥ ਭਾਈ ਰੇ ਮਿਲਿ ਸਜਣੁ ਹਰਿ ਗੁਣ ਸਾਰਿ॥ ਸਜਣੁ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਪੁਰਖੁ ਹੈ ਦੁਖੁ ਕਢੈ ਹਉਮੈ ਮਾਰਿ॥1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀਆ ਸੋਹਾਗਣੀ ਤਿਨ ਦਇਆ ਪਈ ਮਨਿ ਆਇ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਵਚਨੁ ਰਤੰਨੁ ਹੈ ਜੋ ਮੰਨੇ ਸੋ ਹਰਿ ਰਸੁ ਖਾਇ॥ ਸੇ ਵਡਭਾਗੀ ਵਡ ਜਾਣੀਐ ਜਿਨ ਹਰਿ ਰਸੁ ਖਾਧਾ ਗੁਰ ਭਾਇ॥2॥ ਇਹੁ ਹਰਿ ਰਸੁ ਵਣਿ ਤਿਣਿ ਸਭਤੁ ਹੈ ਭਾਗਹੀਣ ਨਹੀ ਖਾਇ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਪਲੈ ਨ ਪਵੈ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਰਹੇ ਬਿਲਲਾਇ॥ ਓਇ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਆਗੈ ਨ ਨਿਵਹਿ ਓਨਾ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਕ੍ਰੋਧੁ ਬਲਾਇ॥3॥ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਰਸੁ ਆਪਿ ਹੈ ਹਰਿ ਆਪੇ ਹਰਿ ਰਸੁ ਹੋਇ॥ ਆਪਿ ਦਇਆ ਕਰਿ ਦੇਵਸੀ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਚੋਇ॥ ਸਭੁ ਤਨੁ ਮਨੁ ਹਰਿਆ ਹੋਇਆ ਨਾਨਕ ਹਰਿ ਵਸਿਆ ਮਨਿ ਸੋਇ॥ 4॥5॥69॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 4: Rus(u) Amrit(u), Naam(u) rus(u) ut(i) bhula, kit(u) bidh(i) milaiy rus(u) khaayay. Jaayay poochhou sohaagni toosaan kiyoun kur(i) miliyaa Prubh(u) aayay. Oye vaypervaah na boalni, houn mul(i) mul(i) dhovaan tin paayay. 1. Bhaayee ray, mil(i) sujjan Hur(i) goon saar(i). Sujjan(u) Satgur(u) Purakh(u) haiy, dookh(u) kuddhay houmein maar(i). 1. Ruhaou. Gurmukhiyaan sohaagni, tin duyya puyee mun(i) aayay. Satgur Vuchun(u) ruttan(u) haiy, jo munnay so Hur(i) rus(u) khaayay. Say vudbhaagi vud jaaniyaiy, jin Hur(i) rus(u) khaadha Gur Bhaayay. 2. Aiyho Hur(i) rus(u) vun(i) tin(i) subht(u) haiy, bhaag-heen nuhi khaayay. Bin(u) Satgur pullaiy na puvaiy, munmukh ruhhay bil-laayay.  Oye Satgur aagaiy na niveh(i) ohna untar(i) krodh(u) blaayay. 3.  Hur(i) Hur(i) Hur(i) rus(u) aap(i) haiy, Hur(i) aapay Hur(i) rus(u) hoyay. Aap(i) duyya kur(i) dayvsi Gurmukh(i) Amrit(u) choayay. Subh(u) tun(u) mun(u) huriya hoya, Nanak Hur(i) vussiya mun(i) soyay.  4.5.69.
Word Meaning:  Rus(u) = a sweet extract to enjoy. Ut(i) Bhula = extremely beneficial. Kit(u) Bidh(i) = how; in what way.  Sohaagni = married (connected with God).  Vaypervaah = indifferent; do not care (about publicity).    Mul(i) = rubbing well (affectionately).      1.
Sujjan(u) = (devotee) friend.   Saar(i) = remember; sing.   Purakh = manifestation of God.    Haumein = selfishness and ego.         Pause.         Vuchun(u) = Shabad; Teaching.   Ruttan(u) = precious stone.  Bhaayay = in love.        2.        Vun(i) Tin(i) = in forest and in vegetation.   Bhaag-heen = unlucky.  Pullaiy = to have (in possession).   Bil-laayay = desperate to have.   Niveh(u) = kneel down; surrender.   Blaayay = demon; uninvited (trouble or problem).      3. 
Choayay = drips.   Huriya = becomes green (blooms or feels blissful).        4.
MEANING:  Sing praise of (remember) Waheguru’s qualities in the company (shelter) of the friend - Satguru.  Satguru is a manifestation of Waheguru who dispels one’s discomfort by eradicating one's haumein.              Pause.          Waheguru’s Naam is the real Amrit – an immortalizing sweet extract as a mental food to enjoy – and is highly beneficial.  But, how to acquire it to enjoy (there is no simple way)?   We need to ask the married human brides (who have been connected with Waheguru) how Waheguru came to reside in their hearts (how they got connected).  But they do not tell you easily (because they do not want to make their relationship with Waheguru public to avoid any publicity or public praise).   So, I will need to wash their feet by rubbing them well (gently and with affection), i.e. I need to overcome my ego and serve them to please them (to seek their guidance).        1.
The devotees of Satguru are the real brides (connected with Waheguru); they take pity on you (and advise you) that Satguru’s Shabad is priceless like a jewel; whoever obeys it, enjoys the taste (of Waheguru’s union). They are lucky who enjoy this extract (Waheguru’s Naam-Amrit) by obeying the Will of Satguru.         2.          This extract (Waheguru’s Naam) is present in the forests, in the vegetation, and in the whole world, but the unfortunate cannot enjoy it. They cannot acquire it without Satguru, and the followers of their mind (engrossed in Maya) simply keep lamenting.  They have the demon of rage (or ego) in them, and do not surrender to Satguru.        3.         Waheguru’s Naam is the real Amrit (immortalizing liquid), and He makes it available to the people through Satguru when He is kind.  The body and mind of the recipient bloom when Waheguru takes residence in his heart, oh Nanak.    4.5.69.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 4॥ ਦਿਨਸੁ ਚੜੈ ਫਿਰਿ ਆਥਵੈ ਰੈਣਿ ਸਬਾਈ ਜਾਇ॥ ਆਵ ਘਟੈ ਨਰੁ ਨਾ ਬੁਝੈ ਨਿਤਿ ਮੂਸਾ ਲਾਜੁ ਟੁਕਾਇ॥ ਗੁੜੁ ਮਿਠਾ ਮਾਇਆ ਪਸਰਿਆ ਮਨਮੁਖੁ ਲਗਿ ਮਾਖੀ ਪਚੈ ਪਚਾਇ॥1॥ ਭਾਈ ਰੇ ਮੈ ਮੀਤੁ ਸਖਾ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਸੋਇ॥ ਪੁਤੁ ਕਲਤੁ ਮੋਹੁ ਬਿਖੁ ਹੈ ਅੰਤਿ ਬੇਲੀ ਕੋਇ ਨ ਹੋਇ॥1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਗੁਰਮਤਿ ਹਰਿ ਲਿਵ ਉਬਰੇ ਅਲਿਪਤੁ ਰਹੇ ਸਰਣਾਇ॥
