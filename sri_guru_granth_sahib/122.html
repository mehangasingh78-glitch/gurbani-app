(ਅੰਗ 121)॥        ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮਿ ਰਤੇ ਵੀਚਾਰੀ ਸਚੋ ਸਚੁ ਕਮਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 8॥18॥19॥
Majh Mehla 3:  Vern roop verteh(i) subh Tayray. Mer(i) mer(i) jummeh(i) fayr puveh(i) ghunayray. Tu Aiko nihchul(u) Agumm Apaara, Gurmuti  boojh boojhaavniya.  1. Houn vaari jeeo vaari, Ram Naam(u) Munn(i) vusaavniya.  Tis(u) roop na raykhiya, vern(u) na koyee, Gurmuti Aap(i) boojhaavniya.  1. Ruhaou.  Subh aika Jot(i) jaanaiy jay koyee. Satgur(u) sayviyaan pergut(u) hoyee. Gupt(u) pergut(u) vertaiy subh thaayeen joti Jot(i) milaavniya.  2.  Tishna ugun(i) julaiy sunsaara.  Lobh(u) abhimaan(u) buhout(u) ahunkaara.  Mur(i) mur(i) junmaiy put(i) guvaayay aapni,  birtha junum(u) guvaavniya.  3.  Gur ka Shabad(u) ko virla boojhaiy. Aap(u) maaray ta tribhuvun soojhaiy. Phir(i) oho muraiy na murna hovaiy, sehjay Sach(i) smaavniya.  4.  Maya meh(i) phir(i) chit(u) na laayay.  Gur kaiy Shabad(i) sud ruhaiy smaayay. Sach(u) salaahay subh ghut untar(i) Sacho Sach(u) soohaavniya.   5. Sach(u) salaaheen suda huzooray.  Gur kaiy Shabad(i) ruhiya bhurpooray. Gur persad(i) Sach(u) nudri aavaiy, Sachay hee sookh(u) paavniya.  6. Sach(u) mun undar(i) ruhhiya smaayay. Suda Sach(u) nihchul(u) aavaiy na jaayay. Sachay laagaiy so mun(u) nirmul(u) Gurmuti Sach(i) smaavniya. 7.  Sach(u) salaaheen uver(u) na koyee. Jit(u) sayviyaiyn suda sookh(u) hoyee. 
(PAGE 121):   Nanak Naam(i) ruttay veechaari, Sacho, Sach(u) kumaavniya.  8.18.19.
Word Meaning:  Vern = skin colour.   Roop = beauty; looks.     Verteh(i) = operates   Fayr = rebirth.   Nihchul = stable; never changing.   Agumm = inaccessible.   Apaara = limitless.     Boojh = understand; unlock the secret.        1.    Munn(i) vusaavniya = embed in mind.     Raykhiya = physical features.       Pause.      Jot(i) = Waheguru’s light.     Pergut(u) = appears; becomes visible.    Gupt = hidden.   Joti = human soul.          2.         Tishna = desires.    Ugun(i) = fire.     Abhimaan = Ahunkaara = conceit.    Put(i) = dignity.     Birtha = empty.        3.        Virla = rare.    Boojhay = unravel; unlock. Tribhuyun = three-layered universe.     Soojhaiy = enlightenment.   Sehjay = in a higher spiritual state.        4.      Chit(u) = mind.   Smaayay = absorbed in.   Salaahay = praises.    Ghut = heart.   Sacho Sach = only God.    Soohaavniya = looking pretty (praise worthy).        5.      Huzooray = in the presence of (believing God to be present there).      Bhurpooray = filling the whole space; omnipresent.       6.      Sachay Laagaiy = working to connect with Waheguru.        7.       Uver(u) = other; else.   Jit(u) = whom.    Ruttay = imbued.   Veechaari = thoughtful; who can distinguish between good and bad.      Kmaavniya = deal in.     8.
MEANING: There are countless living beings of different colours and different looks (physical features) in the world, oh Waheguru!  But, in reality, they are all Your colours and features because You are present in each one of them, oh Waheguru.  All these living beings have fixed life spans – they are born, and they die as their turn comes, and the cycle continues indefinitely.  On the other hand, You are immortal, physically inaccessible, and limitless, oh Waheguru.  (Not everybody understands this fact. You enlighten people through the Guru’s Teachings.           1.            I offer my life to those who make their hearts home for Waheguru (and embed His Naam in their hearts).   You have no physical features or looks, but You use Guru’s Shabad to enlighten people, oh Waheguru.         Pause.            It becomes evident to the devotees who serve the Guru that it is Waheguru’s Light that is operating, hidden and visible, everywhere.  His Light is present in every living being.         2.           The whole world is burning (suffering) in the desire for Maya, and yet it has greed, conceit, and too much ego.  The man loses his respect by being caught in the birth-death cycle, and is wasting away the precious human life (which is an opportunity to unite with God).         3. 
A rare individual, who is able to overcome his selfishness, gets enlightened by the Guru’s Shabad and can unlock the secrets of the universe.  Then he does not die to be reborn; he merges with Waheguru in a higher spiritual state (above Maya).         4.
He loses all interest in Maya and stays absorbed in Waheguru with Guru’s Shabad.  He sings praise of Waheguru, which is present in every heart, and looks adorable by becoming like Waheguru (by acquiring virtues like Him).        5.
I sing Waheguru’s praise with the firm belief that He is around.  When one concentrates on Guru’s Shabad, he can “see” that Waheguru is filling the whole space.  With Guru’s grace, one can “see” Waheguru and enjoy the bliss when connected with Him.        6.
Waheguru is present in our body; He is immortal, and neither comes nor goes (is not born, nor does He die).  Those who work hard to connect with Waheguru are absorbed in Him through the Guru’s advice.          7.        I praise Waheguru and nobody else, because one gets the eternal bliss by serving Him alone.  The thoughtful people are imbued in Waheguru’s Naam, and deal only in His Naam, oh Nanak!     8.18.19.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ ਨਿਰਮਲ ਸਬਦੁ ਨਿਰਮਲ ਹੈ ਬਾਣੀ॥ ਨਿਰਮਲ ਜੋਤਿ ਸਭ ਮਾਹਿ ਸਮਾਣੀ॥ ਨਿਰਮਲ ਬਾਣੀ ਹਰਿ ਸਾਲਾਹੀ ਜਪਿ ਹਰਿ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਮੈਲੁ ਗਵਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਹਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਜੀਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਸੁਖਦਾਤਾ ਮੰਨਿ ਵਸਾਵਣਿਆ॥ ਹਰਿ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਗੁਰ ਸਬਦਿ ਸਲਾਹੀ ਸਬਦੋ ਸੁਣਿ ਤਿਸਾ ਮਿਟਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਨਿਰਮਲ ਨਾਮੁ ਵਸਿਆ ਮਨਿ ਆਏ॥ ਮਨੁ ਤਨੁ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਮਾਇਆ ਮੋਹੁ ਗਵਾਏ॥ ਨਿਰਮਲ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਵੈ ਨਿਤੁ ਸਾਚੇ ਕੇ ਨਿਰਮਲ ਨਾਦੁ ਵਜਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 2॥ ਨਿਰਮਲ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਗੁਰ ਤੇ ਪਾਇਆ॥ ਵਿਚਹੁ ਆਪੁ ਮੁਆ ਤਿਥੈ ਮੋਹੁ ਨ ਮਾਇਆ॥ ਨਿਰਮਲ ਗਿਆਨੁ ਧਿਆਨੁ ਅਤਿ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਨਿਰਮਲ ਬਾਣੀ ਮੰਨਿ ਵਸਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 3॥ ਜੋ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਸੇਵੇ ਸੁ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਹੋਵੈ॥ ਹਉਮੈ ਮੈਲੁ ਗੁਰ ਸਬਦੇ ਧੋਵੈ॥ ਨਿਰਮਲ ਵਾਜੈ ਅਨਹਦ ਧੁਨਿ ਬਾਣੀ ਦਰਿ ਸਚੈ ਸੋਭਾ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 4॥ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਤੇ ਸਭ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਹੋਵੈ॥ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਮਨੂਆ ਹਰਿ ਸਬਦਿ ਪਰੋਵੈ॥ ਨਿਰਮਲ ਨਾਮਿ ਲਗੇ ਬਡਭਾਗੀ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਨਾਮਿ ਸੁਹਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 5॥ ਸੋ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਜੋ ਸਬਦੈ ਸੋਹੈ॥ ਨਿਰਮਲ ਨਾਮਿ ਮਨੁ ਤਨੁ ਮੋਹੈ॥ ਸਚਿ ਨਾਮਿ ਮਲੁ ਕਦੇ ਨ ਲਾਗੈ ਮੁਖੁ ਊਜਲੁ ਸਚੁ ਕਰਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 6॥ ਮਨੁ ਮੈਲਾ ਹੈ ਦੂਜੈ ਭਾਇ॥ ਮੈਲਾ ਚਉਕਾ ਮੈਲੈ ਥਾਇ॥ ਮੈਲਾ ਖਾਇ ਫਿਰਿ ਮੈਲੁ ਵਧਾਏ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਮੈਲੁ ਦੁਖੁ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 7॥ ਮੈਲੇ ਨਿਰਮਲ ਸਭਿ ਹੁਕਮਿ ਸਬਾਏ॥ ਸੇ ਨਿਰਮਲ ਜੋ ਹਰਿ ਸਾਚੇ ਭਾਏ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮੁ ਵਸੈ ਮਨ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਮੈਲੁ ਚੁਕਾਵਣਿਆ॥  8॥19॥20॥
Majh Mehla 3:  Nirmul Shabad(u) nirmul haiy Bani. Nirmal Jot(i) subh mah(i) smaani.  Nirmul Bani Hur(i) salaaheen, jup(i) Hur(i) nirmul(u) maiyl(u) guvaavniya. 1. Huoun vaari jeeo vaari, Sookhdaata munn(i) vusaavniya. Hur(i) nirmul(u) Gur Shabad(i) salaahee, Shabado soon(i) tisa mitaavniya. 1. Ruhaou.   Nirmul Naam(u) vusiya mun(i) aayay. Mun(u) tun(u) nirmul(u), Maya moh(u) guvaayay. Nirmul goon gaavaiy nit(u) Sachay kay, nirmul naad(u) vujaavniya.  2. Nirmul Amrit(u) Gur tay paaya. Vichoun aap(u) mooa, tithaiy moh(u) na Maya. Nirmul Giyan(u) dhiyan(u) ut(i) nirmul(u), nirmul Bani munn(i) vusaavniya. 3.  Jo Nirmul(u) sayvay, su nirmul(u) hovaiy. Haumein maiyl(u) Gur shabaday dhovaiy. Nirmul vaajaiy unhud dhoon(i) Bani, dur Sachaiy sobha paavniya.  4.  Nirmul(u) tay subh nirmul(u) hovaiy. Nirmul(u) munooa Hur(i) Shabad(i) provaiy. Nirmul Naam(i) luggay vudbhaagi, Nirmul(u) Naam(i) soohaavniya.  5.  So nirmul(u) jo Shabadaiy sohaiy. Nirmul Naam(i) mun(u) tun(i) mohaiy.  Sach(i) Naam(i) mul(u) kuday na laagaiy, mookh(u) oojul(u) Sach(u) kuraavniya. 6. Mun(u) maiyla haiy doojaiy bhaayay. Maiyla chaunka maiylaiy thaayayn. Maiyla khaayay, phir(i) maiyl(u) vudhaayay, munmukh maiyl(u) dookh(u) paavniya. 7.  Maiylay nirmul subh(i) Huqam(i) subaayay. Say Nirmul jo Hur(i) Sachay bhaayay. Nanak Naam(u) vusaiy mun untar(i) , Gurmukh(i) maiyl(u) chookaavniya.   8.19.20. 
Word Meaning:  Nirmul = pure; purifying.   Jot(i) = God’s Light (Soul).   Smaani = mixed with; present.    Salaaheen = (I) sing praise.     Jup(i) = by reciting (Naam).   Nirmul(u) = pure; holy (Waheguru).         1.      Tisa = thirst (for Maya).       Mitaavniya = is eradicated.         Pause.     Vusiya = takes residence; embeds.  Moh(u) = attachment.   Nit(u) = every day.   Naad = auto-playing music which the yogis believe is heard in a higher spiritual state.       2.     Aap(u) = selfishness.  Tithaiy = there.  Dhiyan(u) = concentration.   Ut(i) = extreme.        3.       Nirmul(u) = Pure (Waheguru).  Unhud Dhoon(i) = Divine Shabad (Music).     Dur Sachaiy = in the Court of Waheguru.     Sobha = praise.       4.     Munooa = mind.    Provaiy = stitch together.    Soohaavniya = look dignified.       5.    Mohaiy = captivates.    Mul(u) = filth (of evil desires).   Oojul(u) = clean; pure.      6.      Doojaiy Bhaayay = in the love for Maya (sensual desires).  Chaunka = kitchen; mind (where thoughts are cooked).     Maiyla = filthy (cooked with dishonest means).        7.     Huqam = Waheguru’s Will.      Chookaavniya = removes.       8.
MEANING:  Guru’s Shabad and His Bani are pure (holy), and they purify the mind.  Waheguru’s holy Light is present in everybody.  I sing praise of Waheguru by reciting the holy Bani, and thus washing away the filth of my mind (purifying it).       1.  I offer my life to those who embed the source of all comfort, Waheguru’s Naam, in their mind.  I sing praise of the holy Waheguru by Guru’s Shabad, and listening to Guru’s Shabad quenches all my thirst (desires) for Maya.        Pause.
The mind-purifying Naam has made my heart its dwelling, as a result of which, my attachment for Maya has been killed, and my mind and body have been purified now.  My mind now sings the virtues of the holy Waheguru, and it feels as if I am listening to the auto-playing Divine music which the yogis believe in.       2. I have acquired the holy Amrit (Waheguru’s Naam) by the grace of the Guru; now my selfishness is gone, and I have no attachment to Maya.  Ever since Waheguru’s holy Naam has been embedded in my mind, I have acquired the holy Divine Knowledge, and the concentration of my mind has been purified.         3.          Whoever serves the Holy (Waheguru) becomes holy too, as he washes away the filth of his mind by the Guru’s Shabad.   The so-believed, auto-playing Divine music is produced by the holy Bani, and the devotee listening to it is respected in Waheguru’s Court.       4. Anybody who gets connected with the Holy (Waheguru) becomes holy too.   The purified mind stitches itself with Waheguru’s Shabad (connects his mind with Waheguru).  Those connected with the holy Naam are lucky and look respectful everywhere.         5.         An individual adorned with the Guru’s Shabad leads a holy life.  His body and his mind are captivated by the purifying Naam (are fully imbued in Naam).   With the eternal Naam in mind, the individual’s mind never catches the filth of sensual desires, and the eternal Waheguru makes his face shiny (pure; holy).            6.  The human mind is filthy because of its love for Maya.  His kitchen (mind where evil ideas are cooked) is filthy, and so is his whole body (thaayayn).  He feeds on filthy ideas, which add more filth to his character.  The followers of their filthy minds suffer badly.         7. Whether one is filthy or pure, it is all by the Huqam of Waheguru.  Only those are pure who are liked by Waheguru.  The devotees of the Guru wash away their filth, and Waheguru’s Naam gets enshrined in their mind, oh Nanak!     8.19.20.          

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ ਗੋਵਿੰਦੁ ਊਜਲੁ ਊਜਲ ਹੰਸਾ॥ ਮਨੁ ਬਾਣੀ ਨਿਰਮਲ ਮੇਰੀ ਮਨਸਾ॥ ਮਨਿ ਊਜਲ ਸਦਾ ਮੁਖ ਸੋਹਹਿ ਅਤਿ ਊਜਲ ਨਾਮੁ ਧਿਆਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਹਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਜੀਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਗੋਬਿੰਦ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਵਣਿਆ॥ ਗੋਬਿਦੁ ਗੋਬਿਦੁ ਕਹੈ ਦਿਨੁ ਰਾਤੀ ਗੋਬਿਦ ਗੁਣ ਸਬਦਿ ਸੁਣਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਗੋਬਿਦੁ ਗਾਵਹਿ ਸਹਜਿ ਸੁਭਾਏ॥ ਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਭੈ ਊਜਲ ਹਉਮੈ ਮਲੁ ਜਾਏ॥ ਸਦਾ ਅਨੰਦਿ ਰਹਹਿ ਭਗਤਿ ਕਰਹਿ ਦਿਨੁ ਰਾਤੀ ਸੁਣਿ ਗੋਬਿਦ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 2॥ ਮਨੂਆ ਨਾਚੈ ਭਗਤਿ ਦ੍ਰਿੜਾਏ॥ ਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਸਬਦਿ ਮਨੈ ਮਨੁ ਮਿਲਾਏ॥ ਸਚਾ ਤਾਲੁ ਪੂਰੇ ਮਾਇਆ ਮੋਹੁ ਚੁਕਾਏ ਸਬਦੇ ਨਿਰਤਿ ਕਰਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 3॥ ਊਚਾ ਕੂਕੇ ਤਨਹਿ ਪਛਾੜੈ॥ ਮਾਇਆ ਮੋਹਿ ਜੋਹਿਆ ਜਮਕਾਲੇ॥
