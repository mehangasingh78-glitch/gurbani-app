(ਅੰਗ 84)॥              ਵਖਤੁ ਵੀਚਾਰੇ ਸੁ ਬੰਦਾ ਹੋਇ॥ ਕੁਦਰਤਿ ਹੈ ਕੀਮਤਿ ਨਹੀ ਪਾਇ॥ ਜਾ ਕੀਮਤਿ ਪਾਇ ਤ ਕਹੀ ਨ ਜਾਇ॥ ਸਰੈ ਸਰੀਅਤਿ ਕਰਹਿ ਬੀਚਾਰੁ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਬੂਝੈ ਕੈਸੇ ਪਾਵਹਿ ਪਾਰੁ॥ ਸਿਦਕੁ ਕਰਿ ਸਿਜਦਾ ਮਨੁ ਕਰਿ ਮਖਸੂਦੁ॥ ਜਿਹ ਧਿਰਿ ਦੇਖਾ ਤਿਹ ਧਿਰਿ ਮਉਜੂਦੁ ॥1॥
Sloak M:1:  Koodrut(i) kur(i) kaiy vusiya soyay. 
(PAGE 84):               Vukht (u) veecharay su bunda hoyay. Koodrut(i) haiy, keemut(i) nuhee paayay. Ja keemut(i) paayay, ta kuhee na jaayay. Shureh Shreeut(i) kureh(i) beechaar(u) . Bin boojhaiy kaisay paaveh(i) paar(u).  Siduk(u) kur(i) sijda mun(u) kur(i) mukhdood(u). Jih dhir(i) daykhaan tih dhir(i) moujood(u).  1.
Word Meaning:  Vusiya = is present.  Vukht(u) = time; opportunity.    Veechaaray = makes good use of.   Bunda = servant (devotee).  Shureh Shreeut(i) = Muslim way of life.   Bin(u) Boojhaiy = without understanding (enlightenment).  Siduk(u) = faith.   Sijda = bowing.   Mukhsood(u) = aim.   Dhir(i) = direction.   Moujood(u) = present.
MEANING: Having created the universe, God makes it His home (is present everywhere).  The man, who realizes that human life is an opportunity to be “one” with Waheguru (and recites His Naam), is the real devotee (successful in his objective).  Nature is certainly there, but we do not value (fully appreciate) it. Even if someone values it, they cannot describe it.  A Muslim who simply follows the rituals of the Religion (does not gain anything spiritually); so, how can he swim across the sea of Maya?  Rather than simply bowing to Him in a ritualistic way, the real Muslim is the one who has full faith in Allah, and who also is able to control his mind - the aim of his life.  At that point, he will find Allah in every direction wherever he looks.   1.  

ਮ:3॥ ਗੁਰ ਸਭਾ ਏਵ ਨ ਪਾਈਐ ਨਾ ਨੇੜੈ ਨਾ ਦੂਰਿ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਤਾ ਮਿਲੈ ਜਾ ਮਨੁ ਰਹੈ ਹਦੂਰਿ॥2॥
M:3: Gur subha ayve na paayiyaiy, na nayrraiy, na door(i). Nanak Satgur ta milaiy jay mun(u) ruhay hudoor(i). 
Word Meaning:  Gur Subha = Guru’s company (shelter).    Hudoor(i) = close to. 
MEANING: One does not realize Guru’s shelter  (connection with Him) by simply being physically close to Him (physical nearness or separation makes no difference). One realizes the Guru only by being mentally close to Him (focusing the mind on Him).

ਪਉੜੀ॥ ਸਪਤ ਦੀਪ ਸਪਤ ਸਾਗਰਾ ਨਵ ਖੰਡ ਚਾਰਿ ਵੇਦ ਦਸ ਅਸਟ ਪੁਰਾਣਾ॥ ਹਰਿ ਸਭਨਾ ਵਿਚਿ ਤੂੰ ਵਰਤਦਾ ਹਰਿ ਸਭਨਾ ਭਾਣਾ॥ ਸਭਿ ਤੁਝੈ ਧਿਆਵਹਿ ਜੀਅ ਜੰਤ ਹਰਿ ਸਾਰਗ ਪਾਣਾ॥ ਜੋ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਹਰਿ ਆਰਾਧਦੇ ਤਿਨ ਹਉ ਕੁਰਬਾਣਾ॥ ਤੂੰ ਆਪੇ ਆਪਿ ਵਰਤਦਾ ਕਰਿ ਚੋਜ ਵਿਡਾਣਾ॥4
Paurri:  Supt deep, supt saagra, nuv khund, chaar(i) Vayd, dus-asht Purana. Hur subhna vich(i) Toon vertda Hur(i) subhna bhaana.  Subh(i) Toojhaiy dhiaaveh(i) jea junt, Hur(i) Saarungpaana.  Jo Gurmukh(i) Hur(i) aaradhday, tin huoun koorbaana. Toon aapay aap(i) vertda kur(i) choaj vidaana.  4.
Word Meaning: Supt = seven.  Nuv Khund = the nine regions of the earth.   Dus-asht = ten and eight = 18.   Vertda = operates.  Bhaana = is liked.   Saarungpaana = who holds a bow in his hands (a name for Waheguru).   Choaj Vidaana = amazing feats.  
MEANING:  You are operating in all the seven continents, the seven oceans, the nine regions of the earth, in the four Vedas, and the eighteen Puranas; and are liked by everyone, oh Waheguru.  All the living beings worship You.   I am a sacrifice to all those who worship You under the guidance of the Guru. It is an amazing feat of Yours that You operate imperceptibly everywhere.       4.

ਸਲੋਕ ਮ:3॥ ਕਲਉ ਮਸਾਜਨੀ ਕਿਆ ਸਦਾਈਐ ਹਿਰਦੈ ਹੀ ਲਿਖਿ ਲੇਹੁ॥ ਸਦਾ ਸਾਹਿਬ ਕੈ ਰੰਗਿ ਰਹੈ ਕਬਹੂੰ ਨ ਤੂਟਸਿ ਨੇਹੁ॥ ਕਲਉ ਮਜਾਸਨੀ ਜਾਇਸੀ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਭੀ ਨਾਲੇ ਜਾਇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਸਹ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਿ ਨ ਜਾਇਸੀ ਜੋ ਧੁਰਿ ਛੋਡੀ ਸਚੈ ਪਾਇ॥ 1॥
Sloak M:3: Kulou musaajni kiya sudayiyaiy, hirdaiy hee likh(i) layhou. Suda Sahib kaiy rung(i) ruhaiy, kubhoon na tootus(i) nayhou. Kulou musaajni jaayaysi, likhiya bhee naalay jaayay. Nanak Sheh preet(i) na jaayaysi jo dhoor(i) chhoadi Sachaiy paayay. 1.
Word Meaning:  Kulou = pen.   Musaajni = inkpot.  Sudayiyaiy = call for. 
MEANING:  Do not call for a pen and an inkpot; write (Waheguru’s Naam) on your heart, oh man.  Love for Waheguru never fades if one leads their life imbued in His love. Pen and inkpot are perishable, and so is the writing on it; but love for Waheguru is never lost that He has written on someone’s mind right from the beginning, oh Nanak.

ਮ:3॥ ਨਦਰੀ ਆਵਦਾ ਨਾਲਿ ਨ ਚਲਈ ਵੇਖਹੁ ਕੋ ਵਿਉਪਾਇ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰਿ ਸਚੁ ਦ੍ਰਿੜਾਇਆ ਸਚਿ ਰਹਹੁ ਲਿਵ ਲਾਇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਸਬਦੀ ਸਚੁ ਹੈ ਕਰਮੀ ਪਲੈ ਪਾਇ॥ 2॥
M:3: Nudri aanvda naal(i) na chulyee, vaykhou ko viopaayay. Satgur(i) Sach(u) drirraya Sach(i) ruhou liv laayay. Nanak Shabadi Sach(u) haiy kermi pullaiy paayay. 2.
Word Meaning:  Viopaayay = verify. Drirraya = reiterate.    Pullaiy Paayay = receives. 
MEANING: You can confirm by testing that nothing visible accompanies you (when you leave the world).  (To be accepted in Waheguru’s Court), One should focus one's mind on Waheguru’s Naam as reiterated by Satguru.  Sach(u) (Waheguru) can be realized by the Guru’s Shabad, which one receives (implants in mind) by His kindness. 2.

ਪਉੜੀ॥ ਹਰਿ ਅੰਦਰਿ ਬਾਹਰਿ ਇਕੁ ਤੂੰ ਤੂੰ ਜਾਣਹਿ ਭੇਤੁ॥ ਜੋ ਕੀਚੈ ਸੋ ਹਰਿ ਜਾਣਦਾ ਮੇਰੇ ਮਨ ਹਰਿ ਚੇਤੁ॥ ਸੋ ਡਰੈ ਜਿ ਪਾਪ ਕਮਾਵਦਾ ਧਰਮੀ ਵਿਗਸੇਤੁ॥ ਤੂੰ ਸਚਾ ਆਪਿ ਨਿਆਉ ਸਚੁ ਤਾ ਡਰੀਐ ਕੇਤੁ॥ ਜਿਨਾ ਨਾਨਕ ਸਚੁ ਪਛਾਣਿਆ ਸੇ ਸਚਿ ਰਲੇਤੁ॥ 5॥
Paurri:  Hur(i) undar(i) baahur(i) ikk(u) Toon, Toon jaaneh(i) bhayt(u).  Jo keechaiy so Hur(i) jaanda, mayray mun Hur(i) chayt(u).  So duraiy j(i) paap kmaanvda, dhermi vigsayt(u). Toon Sacha aap(i), niyayoun Sach(u), ta duriyaiy kayt(u).  Jinha Nanak Sach(u) puchhaaniya, say Sach(i) rulayt(u). 
Word Meaning:  Bhayt(u) = secrets.  Vigsayt(u) = blissful.  Sacha = just.  Niyayoun = judgment.   Kayt(u) = why; what for.  Sach(i) = in the True (Waheguru).  Rulayt(u) = mix completely; become one with. 
MEANING: You are present outside (in the world), and inside the minds of people; so. You know all the secrets of their minds, oh Waheguru.  Whatever a man does (even thinks of doing) is known to Waheguru, so recite His Naam, oh my mind.  The sinner has to be fearful of Waheguru; a devotee on the righteous path is blissful.  When Waheguru is just and delivers justice, why should one be afraid? Those who recognize the Just Waheguru (connect with Him) become “one” with Him, oh Nanak. 5.

ਸਲੋਕ ਮ:3॥ ਕਲਮ ਜਲਉ ਸਣੁ ਮਸਵਾਣੀਐ ਕਾਗਦੁ ਭੀ ਜਲਿ ਜਾਉ॥ ਲਿਖਣ ਵਾਲਾ ਜਲਿ ਬਲਉ ਜਿਨਿ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਦੂਜਾ ਭਾਉ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਪੂਰਬਿ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਕਮਾਵਣਾ ਅਵਰੁ ਨ ਕਰਣਾ ਜਾਇ॥ 1॥
Sloak M:3: Kulm julou sun(u) musvaaniyaiy, kaagud(u) bhee jul(i) jaao.  Likhun wala jul(i) bulou, jin(i) likhiya dooja bhao. Nanak poorub(i) likhiya kumaavna, uver(u) na kerna jaayay. 1.
Word Meaning:   Sun(u) = including; along with.  Dooja Bhao = love for the other (Maya).  Poorub(i) Likhiya = prewritten.   Uver(u) = other; else.
MEANING: The pen, the inkpot, and the paper should all burn down, including the writer who writes love for anything else (Maya) (other than Waheguru).  (But no one can be blamed for his bad deeds) because he does only what he is predestined to do, oh Nanak.  1.
NOTE: A man’s thinking (mind-set) is predetermined based on his deeds of previous lives. One cannot change that on his own. Only the Guru can change that. 
ਮ:3॥ ਹੋਰੁ ਕੂੜੁ ਪੜਣਾ ਕੂੜੁ ਬੋਲਣਾ ਮਾਇਆ ਨਾਲਿ ਪਿਆਰੁ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਵਿਣੁ ਨਾਵੈ ਕੋ ਥਿਰ ਨਹੀ ਪੜਿ ਪੜਿ ਹੋਇ ਖੁਆਰੁ॥ 2॥
M:3:  Hoar(i) koorr(u) purrhna, koorr(u) boalna, Maya naal(i) piyaar(u).  Nanak vin(u) Naanvaiy ko thir nuhee, purrh(i) purrh(i) hoyay khooaar(u). 
Word Meaning: Koorr(u) = worthless (for spirituality).  Vin(u) = without; except.  Thir = stable; immortal.  Khooaar(u) = suffer without any achievement. 
MEANING:  Whatever one reads or talks about is useless if it involves love for Maya. Nothing lives forever except Waheguru’s Naam.  Reading about anything else causes only humiliation for the reader (in Waheguru’s Court), oh Nanak.  2.  

ਪਉੜੀ॥ ਹਰਿ ਕੀ ਵਡਿਆਈ ਵਡੀ ਹੈ ਹਰਿ ਕੀਰਤਨੁ ਹਰਿ ਕਾ॥ਹਰਿ ਕੀ ਵਡਿਆਈ ਵਡੀ ਹੈ ਜਾ ਨਿਆਉ ਹੈ ਧਰਮ ਕਾ॥ ਹਰਿ ਕੀ ਵਡਿਆਈ ਵਡੀ ਹੈ ਜਾ ਫਲੁ ਹੈ ਜੀਅ ਕਾ॥ ਹਰਿ ਕੀ ਵਡਿਆਈ ਵਡੀ ਹੈ ਜਾ ਨ ਸੁਣਈ ਕਹਿਆ ਚੁਗਲ ਕਾ॥ ਹਰਿ ਕੀ ਵਡਿਆਈ ਵਡੀ ਹੈ ਅਪੁਛਿਆ ਦਾਨੁ ਦੇਵਕਾ॥ 6॥
Paurri:  Hur(i) kee vudiyaayee vudi haiy, Hur(i) keertun(u) Hur(i) ka. Hur(i) kee vudiyaayee vudi haiy,  ja niyayoun haiy Dherm ka. Hur(i) kee vudiyayee vudi haiy,  ja ful(u) haiy jea ka. Hur(i) kee vudiyayee vudi haiy, ja na soonyee kuhiya choogal ka. Hur(i) kee vudyaayee vudi haiy,  apoochhiya daan(u) dayvka.  6.
Word Meaning:  Vudiyaayee = adulation; magnanimity.  Dherm Ka = based on noble principles (righteousness).  Jea Ka Ful(u) = result of one’s deeds (or thoughts).  Choogul = slanderer.      Apoochhiya = unasked. 
MEANING: Adulating Waheguru and singing His praise are supreme.  Waheguru’s magnanimity is supreme because His justice is based on noble principles.  Waheguru’s magnanimity is supreme because He rewards one based on his deeds or thoughts (and there is no injustice).  Waheguru’s magnanimity is supreme because He does not listen to a slanderer.  Waheguru’s magnanimity is supreme, because He provides (fulfils the needs of everybody) even without asking.      6.

ਸਲੋਕ ਮ:3॥ ਹਉ ਹਉ ਕਰਤੀ ਸਭ ਮੁਈ ਸੰਪਉ ਕਿਸੈ ਨ ਨਾਲਿ॥ ਦੂਜੈ ਭਾਇ ਦੁਖੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਸਭ ਜੋਹੀ ਜਮਕਾਲਿ॥
