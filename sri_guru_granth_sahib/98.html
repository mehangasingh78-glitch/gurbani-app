Majh Mehla 5 Chaupuday Ghur(u) 1:  Mayra mun(u) loachaiy Gur dershan taayeen. Bilp kuray chaatrik kee niyayeen. Trikha na ootraiy shaant(i) na aavaiy, bin dershan Sunt piyaray jeeo. 1. Huoun gholi jeeo ghol(i) ghoomayee, Gur dershan Sunt piyaray jeeo. 1. Ruhaou.  Tayra mookh(u) soohaava jeeo sehj dhoon(i) bani. Chir(u) hoya daykhay Saringpaani. Dhunn su days(u) juhaan Tu vussiya, mayray sujjan meet Mooraray jeeo. 2. Huoun gholi jeeo ghol(i) ghoomayee, Gur sujjan meet Mooraray jeeo. 1. Pause. Ikk ghurri na miltay ta Kalyug(u) hota. Hoon(i) kud miliyaiy Priyay Toodh(u)  Bhugvunta.                                                                                          
(PAGE 97):  Mohay raiyn(i) na vihavaiy, neend na aavaiy, bin(u) daykhay Gur Durbaray jeeo.    3.  Huoun gholi jeeo ghol(i) ghoomayee tis(u) Sachay Gur Durbaaray jeeo. 1. Ruhaou.   Bhaag(u) hoya Gur(i) Sunt(u) milaya. Prubh(u) abinashi ghur meh(i) paaya. Sayv kureen pul(u) chusa na vichhurraan, jun Nanak Daas Toomharay jeeo.  4.  Huoun gholi jeeo ghol(i) ghoomayee, jun Nanak Dass Toomharay jeeo. Ruhaou.  1.8. 
Word Meaning:  Lochaiy = longs; pines.    Bilp = cries due to the pain of separation.  Chaatrik = rainbird; a mythical bird which drinks only the first drop of rainwater during a special period.    Niyayeen = like.    Trikha = thirst.     1.
Gholi Jeeo Ghol(i) Ghoomayee= (wish) to offer life.    Sunt = Guru.       Pause.
Soohaava = beautiful; comforting.     Sehj = a higher spiritual state where the mind quits wandering.  Bani = words of praise.     Saringpaani = bow-bearing (Waheguru) (a name for Vishnu).     Mooraray = Waheguru (enemy of the demon, moor; a name for Krishna because he killed the demon).      2.     Ghurri = a very short period of time.   Kalyug = dark (very bad and hostile) time.  Bhugvunta = God; Waheguru.      Raiyn(i) = night.     Vihaavaiy = spend.    3.    Bhaag hoya = luck has come to being.   Sunt = source of peace (Waheguru).   Abinashi = non-perishable (eternal).   Ghur(i) = Meh(i) = in my own heart.   Pul, Chusa = both denote very small periods of time; a moment.      4.
MEANING: My mind craves for, and cries like a rainbird to have a sight of the Guru. My mind does not find peace, and my thirst for His sight is not quenched until I see Him. 1. 
I am gladly willing to sacrifice my life for the sight of the dear Guru.           Pause.  
Your face (sight) is highly comforting and Your sweet praise leads me to higher spiritual state (where Maya has no effect), oh dear Guru Ji! The place (heart) is worthy of respect where You are residing, oh my dear friend, Waheguru!          2. 
I wish to sacrifice my life for my dear friend, Waheguru.        Pause.
It was a dark time for me whenever I was away from You; when will I be able to see You, now, oh my dear God (Satguru)!  I cannot fall asleep, and my night does not seem to be over without having a sight of Your Court (You), oh Guru Ji!          3.
I wish to sacrifice my life for the eternal Court of the Guru.       Pause.
My good luck has materialized, and the Guru has connected me with Waheguru, and I have realized the eternal Waheguru in my heart.  Now, I will serve Him like a slave and will never separate from Him, oh Nanak.      4.
I am glad to sacrifice my life for Waheguru, oh Nanak.     Pause.
NOTE: This is the only Shabad in this set which has “Ruhaou” in it – and not just once, but as many times as the number of stanzas, 4. Each stanza is followed by “Ruhaou,” implying that there is a different idea in every stanza.  That way, this holy Shabad is unique in the set. There is a story associated with this Shabad that Guru Ram Dass Sahib sent His youngest son, Arjun Dev Ji (later the Fifth Guru), to Lahore to attend a family marriage and told Arjun Dev Ji not to return to Amritsar to the Guru until he was invited back.  Arjun Dev Ji had always treated his father, Guru Ram Dass Ji, more as his Guru. Quite some time passed, but Guru Sahib sent no message to Arjun Dev Ji inviting him back to Him in Amritsar.  The long separation from the Guru caused unrest in Arjun Dev Ji’s mind, so he began writing letters to Guru Sahib expressing his anguish over it.  His letters were spiritual in nature, and each letter contained only one stanza.  The first three letters were intercepted by Arjun Dev Ji’s selfish elder brother, Pirthi Chand Ji. The fourth letter was delivered directly to Guru Sahib. When Guru Sahib read the affectionate letter, He called Arjun Dev Ji back immediately and passed on the Guruship to Him.  Even though the Shabad's uniqueness suggests the story is likely true, it still requires further investigation.

ਰਾਗੁ ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਸਾ ਰੁਤਿ ਸੁਹਾਵੀ ਜਿਤੁ ਤੁਧੁ ਸਮਾਲੀ॥ ਸੋ ਕੰਮ ਸੁਹੇਲਾ ਜੋ ਤੇਰੀ ਘਾਲੀ॥ ਸੋ ਰਿਦਾ ਸੁਹੇਲਾ ਜਿਤੁ ਰਿਦੈ ਤੂ ਵੁਠਾ ਸਭਨਾ ਕੇ ਦਾਤਾਰਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਤੂ ਸਾਝਾ ਸਾਹਿਬੁ ਬਾਪ ਹਮਾਰਾ॥ ਨਉ ਨਿਧਿ ਤੇਰੈ ਅਖੁਟ ਭੰਡਾਰਾ॥ ਜਿਸੁ ਤੂ ਦੇਹਿ ਸੁ ਤ੍ਰਿਪਤਿ ਅਘਾਵੈ ਸੋਈ ਭਗਤੁ ਤੁਮਾਰਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਸਭੁ ਕੋ ਆਸੈ ਤੇਰੀ ਬੈਠਾ॥ ਘਟ ਘਟ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਤੂ ਹੈ ਵੁਠਾ॥ ਸਭੇ ਸਾਝੀਵਾਲ ਸਦਾਇਨਿ ਤੂ ਕਿਸੈ ਨ ਦਿਸਹਿ ਬਾਹਰਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 3॥ ਤੂ ਆਪੇ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਮੁਕਤਿ ਕਰਾਇਹਿ॥ ਤੂ ਆਪੇ ਮਨਮੁਖਿ ਜਨਮਿ ਭਵਾਇਹਿ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਦਾਸ ਤੇਰੈ ਬਲਿਹਾਰੈ ਸਭੁ ਤੇਰਾ ਖੇਲੁ ਦਸਾਹਰਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥2॥9॥
Raag Majh Mehla 5: Sa root(i) soohavee jit(u) Toodh(u) smaaleen. So kumm soohayla jo tayri ghaali. So rida soohayla jit(u) ridaiy Tu voottha, subhna kay Dataara jeeo. 1. Ghut ghut untar(i) Tu haiy voottha. Subhay saanjhivaal sudayin Tu kisaiy na dis-hay bahra jeeo. 3.  Tu aapay Gurmukh(i) mookt(i) kurahayn.  Tu aapay munmukh(i) junum(i) bhuvahayn. Word Meaning:  Sa = that.   Root(i) = season.   Smalleen = I remember.   Soohayla = fruitful; comforting.     Tayri Ghaali = in Your service.   Rida = heart.    Voottha = reside.    1.
Saanjha = common; shared.   Naou Nidh(i) = the mythical nine treasures.   Ukhoot = inexhaustible.   Bhundara = treasures.    Triput Aghaavaiy = completely contented.     2.
Tayri Aasaiy = have hopes on You.   Ghut Ghut = every heart.  Voottha = reside.   Saanjhivaal = having a share of You.     Sudayin = are called.   Bahra = outsider.         3.    
Mookt(i) = salvage.   Junum(i) Bhuvahayn = put through rebirths.  Bulihaaray = a sacrifice.    Dusahra = visible.    4.
MEANING:  Only that season is worthwhile (comforting) in which I remember You, and only that task is comforting (successful) which is in Your service; likewise, that heart is beautiful (peaceful and helpful) in which You reside, oh, the provider for all, Waheguru!           1.
You are the common father of us all.  All your nine treasures are inexhaustible; whoever You provide is fully contented, and only he deserves to be called Your devotee, oh Waheguru!     2.             Everybody is hopeful of You (depends upon Your support) as You are residing in every heart.  Everybody claims to be part of You, and You do not seem separate from anyone.      3.                  You, Yourself, salvage the devotees of the Guru, and You put the followers of their own minds through different life forms.  The whole visible world is Your drama (creation).  Your slave, Nanak, is always a sacrifice for You, oh Waheguru.       4.2.9.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਅਨਹਦੁ ਵਾਜੈ ਸਹਜਿ ਸੁਹੇਲਾ॥ ਸਬਦਿ ਅਨੰਦ ਕਰੇ ਸਦ ਕੇਲਾ॥ ਸਹਜ ਗੁਫਾ ਮਹਿ ਤਾੜੀ ਲਾਈ ਆਸਣੁ ਊਚ ਸਵਾਰਿਆ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਫਿਰਿ ਘਿਰਿ ਅਪੁਨੇ ਗ੍ਰਿਹ ਮਹਿ ਆਇਆ॥ ਜੋ ਲੋੜੀਦਾ ਸੋਈ ਪਾਇਆ॥ ਤ੍ਰਿਪਤਿ ਅਘਾਇ ਰਹਿਆ ਹੈ ਸੰਤਹੁ ਗੁਰਿ ਅਨਭਉ ਪੁਰਖੁ ਦਿਖਾਰਿਆ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਆਪੇ ਰਾਜਨ ਆਪੇ ਲੋਗਾ॥ ਆਪਿ ਨਿਰਬਾਣੀ ਆਪੇ ਭੋਗਾ॥ ਆਪੇ ਤਖਤ ਬਹੈ ਸਚੁ ਨਿਆਈ ਸਭ ਚੂਕੀ ਕੂਕ ਪੁਕਾਰਿਆ ਜੀਉ॥ 3॥ ਜੇਹਾ ਡਿਠਾ ਮੈ ਤੇਹੋ ਕਹਿਆ॥ ਤਿਸੁ ਰਸੁ ਆਇਆ ਜਿਨਿ ਭੇਦੁ ਲਹਿਆ॥ ਜੋਤੀ ਜੋਤਿ ਮਿਲੀ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਜਨ ਨਾਨਕ ਇਕੁ ਪਸਾਰਿਆ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥3॥10॥
Majh Mehla 5:  Unhud vaajaiy sehj(i) soohayla. Shabad(i) anund kuray sud kayla.  Sehj goofa meh(i) taarri laayee, aasun(u) ooch suvaariya jeeo. 1. Phir(i) ghir(i) apunay greh(i) meh(i) aaya. Jo lorreenda soyee paaya. Triput aghaayay ruhiyya haiy Sunt-hou, Gur(i) unbhuou Purakh(u) dikhaariya jeeo. 2. Aapay raajun aapay loga. Aap(i) nirbani Aapay bhoga. Aapay tukhut buhaiy Sach(u) niyayeen, subh chooki kook pookaariya jeeo. 3. Jayha dittha mein tayho kuhiyya. Tis(u) rus(u) aaya jin(i) bhayd(u) luhiyya. Joti Jot(i) mili sookh(u) paaya,  jun Nanak ikk(u) pusaariya jeeo.     4.3.10
Word Meaning:  Unhud = automatic and uniform divine (music).    Sehj(i) = higher spiritual state.   Kayla = blissful.   Goofa = cave (mind).   Taarri = meditate with concentration.    Aasun = sitting (position, place, pose).   Ooch = transcendental (Waheguru).   1.
Phir(i) Ghir(i) = returns again.   Greh(i) = mind.    Lorreenda = needed.  Triput Aghaayay = fully contented.    Unbhou Purakh(u) = fearless and omnipresent Waheguru.   2.
Raajun = ruler; king.   Loga = public; subjects.    Nirbani = non-existence of any desire.    Bhoga = one who enjoys.   Niyayeen = judge.    Chooki = disappeared.  Kook Pookaariya = cries and imploring; complaining.     3.      Dittha = saw; noticed.   Tayho = the same.   Rus(u) = enjoyed the taste.   Bhayd(u) = secret.   Joti = light (of Wheguru) in individuals.   Jot(i) = Waheguru’s light.    Pusaariya = spread out (present).       4.
MEANING: (By Guru’s benevolence) I am feeling as if an automatic and uniform divine music is playing in my mind, which is producing comfort, and I am feeling blissful in my higher spiritual state.  Adorned (with noble qualities) by Waheguru, I have found a high place (at His feet) where I am completely imbued in His Naam.          1. 
After wandering for a long time, my mind has returned to its real home, having realized whatever it needed (i.e., Waheguru’s Naam). Now it is completely content as the Guru has shown it the fearless and the omnipresent Waheguru, oh holy men!          2.
Waheguru, Himself is the King, and Himself His subjects.  He is completely without any desire, but being present in everybody, he enjoys all the luxuries of life too.  A person who believes that Waheguru is the True (impartial) Judge who sits on His eternal throne, finds all his complaints to be baseless (his doubts are cleared).             3.
I have described exactly what I have experienced, and whoever has uncovered this secret enjoys the essence of Waheguru.  When the light of an individual merges with the light of Waheguru, his life becomes very blissful, and he realizes that it is solely Waheguru that pervades the whole universe.  4. 3. 10.
