(ਅੰਗ 44)॥   ਸਾਧੂ ਸੰਗ ਮਸਕਤੇ ਤੂਠੈ ਪਾਵਾ ਦੇਵ॥ ਸਭੁ ਕਿਛੁ ਵਸਗਤਿ ਸਾਹਿਬੈ ਆਪੇ ਕਰਣ ਕਰੇਵ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਬਲਿਹਾਰਣੈ ਮਨਸਾ ਸਭ ਪੂਰੇਵ॥ 3॥ ਇਕੋ ਦਿਸੈ ਸਜਣੋ ਇਕੋ ਭਾਈ ਮੀਤੁ॥ ਇਕਸੈ ਦੀ ਸਾਮਗਰੀ ਇਕਸੈ ਦੀ ਹੈ ਰੀਤਿ॥ ਇਕਸ ਸਿਉ ਮਨੁ ਮਾਨਿਆ ਤਾ ਹੋਆ ਨਿਹਚਲੁ ਚੀਤੁ॥ ਸਚੁ ਖਾਣਾ ਸਚੁ ਪੈਨਣਾ ਟੇਕ ਨਾਨਕ ਸਚੁ ਕੀਤੁ॥ 4॥5॥75॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 5: Subhay gullaan visrun(u), ikko visur(i) na jaao.  Dhundha subh(u) jlaayay kaiy, Gur(i) Naam(u) deeya Sach(u) sooaao. Aasa subhay laahay kaiy, ikka aas kumaaou. Jinhi Satgur(u) sayviya, tin aagaiy miliya thaaon. 1.  Mun mayray Kertay nu saalahay. Subhay chhudd(i) siyaanpaa Gur kee paiyreen paahay.1. Ruhaou. Dookh bhookh neh viyapyee, jay Sookhdaata mun(i) hoyay. Kit hee kumm(i) na chhijjiyaiy ja hirdaiy Sacha Soyay. Jis(u) Tu rukhay huth day, tis(u) maar(i) na sukaiy koyay. Sookhdaata Gur(u) saviyaiy, subh(i) uvgoon kuddhaiy dhoyay. 2. Sayva mungaiy sayvko laayiyaan apuni sayv. 
(PAGE 44):      Saadhu sung(u) mushuktay, tootthaiy paavaan Dayv. Subh(u) kichh(u) vusgut(i) Sahibaiy, aapay kern krayv. Satgur kaiy bulihaarnaiy munsa subh poorayv. 3. Ikko disaiy sujjno ikko bhaayee meet(u). Ikksaiy dee samugree, ikksaiy dee haiy reet(i). Ikus siyoun mun(u) maaniya , ta hoa nihchul(u) cheet(u). Sach(u) khaana Sach(u) pehnuna, tayk Nanak Sach(u) keet(u). 4.5.75. 
Word Meaning:  Visrun(u) = slip from mind; forget.  Ikko = the one and the only (Waheguru).  Dhundha = routine work (of rituals).  Sooaao = taste; objective.  Kmaaou = earn.    Aagaiy = in the next world.         1.    Kertay = Creator (Waheguru).   Siyaanup = wisdom.   Pause.          Viyapyee = affect; act upon.  Chhijjiyaiy = weakens; feels stress.  Uvgoon = bad qualities.    2.            Sayvko = slaves; servants.   Mushuktay = hard work (in Guru’s service).   Tootthaiy = at (Your) pleasure.  Vusgut(i) = in control.   Sahibaiy = the Lord.  Krave = does.   Bulihaarnaiy = glad to sacrifice myself.   Munsa = wishes of the mind.        3.
Samugree = all materials.  Reet(i) = tradition; rules.   Siyoun = with.  Maaniya = convinced; content.  Nihchul Cheet(u) = stable mind.     Tayk = support.          4.
MEANING:  Sing praise of the Creator (God), oh my mind. Keeping all your wisdom (ego) aside, fall on the Guru’s feet (obey Him with humbleness).       Pause. 
My Guru has enlightened me and explained to me the real objective of life – (by singing Waheguru’s praise to be “one” with Him); so, forgetting everything about daily routine, I am reciting Waheguru’s Naam. My only wish is that I may forget everything else, but not the One - (Waheguru’s Naam).  All other hopes (wishes) are unimportant; the only important wish that one needs to achieve is (Waheguru’s Naam), and one should work hard for that. Those who serve the Guru get the eternal seat (union with Waheguru) in the next world.          1.        The suffering of discomforts and of hunger (for Maya), does not impact an individual who keeps the source of all comfort – Waheguru – in mind.  No job (worldly attraction) causes any stress on you if you have enshrined Waheguru’s Naam in your heart.  No one can kill you (or no worldly attraction can rob you of your spirituality), when Waheguru is protecting you and providing His coverage.  We should serve Satguru, who is the source of all comfort; He washes away (the filth of your ignoble deeds), and roots out all your ill qualities.        2.
I beg to serve those who serve You, oh Waheguru. If you please, allow me to serve You in the company of Your devotees. Everything is under Your control; in fact, You do everything Yourself and get others to do as You please, oh Waheguru. I wish to sacrifice my life for Satguru, who fulfils all my needs (and wishes).          3.
I see only one true friend, one brother, and one pal (Waheguru).  He owns the whole universe, and it is only His tradition (laws) that operate everywhere. My mind found contentment when it was convinced (that only Waheguru is the source of all comforts). Now, He is my support, and I remember Him while eating and wearing (all the time), oh Nanak. 4.5.75.
            
ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 5: 

ਸਭੇ ਥੋਕ ਪਰਾਪਤੇ ਜੇ ਆਵੈ ਇਕੁ ਹਥਿ॥ ਜਨਮੁ ਪਦਾਰਥੁ ਸਫਲੁ ਹੈ ਜੇ ਸਚਾ ਸਬਦੁ ਕਥਿ॥ ਗੁਰ ਤੇ ਮਹਲੁ ਪਰਾਪਤੇ ਜਿਸੁ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਹੋਵੈ ਮਥਿ॥ 1॥ ਮੇਰੇ ਮਨ ਏਕਸ ਸਿਉ ਚਿਤੁ ਲਾਇ॥ ਏਕਸ ਬਿਨੁ ਸਭ ਧੰਧੁ ਹੈ ਸਭ ਮਿਥਿਆ ਮੋਹੁ ਮਾਇ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਲਖ ਖੁਸੀਆ ਪਾਤਿਸਾਹੀਆ ਜੇ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਨਦਰਿ ਕਰੇਇ॥ ਨਿਮਖ ਏਕ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਦੇਇ ਮੇਰਾ ਮਨੁ ਤਨੁ ਸੀਤਲ ਹੋਇ॥ ਜਿਸ ਕਉ ਪੂਰਬਿ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਤਿਨਿ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਚਰਨ ਗਹੇ॥ 2॥ ਸਫਲ ਮੂਰਤੁ ਸਫਲਾ ਘੜੀ ਜਿਤੁ ਸਚੇ ਨਾਲਿ ਪਿਆਰੁ॥ ਦੂਖੁ ਸੰਤਾਪੁ ਨ ਲਗਈ ਜਿਸੁ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਅਧਾਰੁ॥ ਬਾਹ ਪਕੜਿ ਗੁਰਿ ਕਾਢਿਆ ਸੋਈ ਉਤਰਿਆ ਪਾਰਿ॥ 3॥ ਥਾਨੁ ਸੁਹਾਵਾ ਪਵਿਤੁ ਹੈ ਜਿਥੈ ਸੰਤ ਸਭਾ॥ ਢੋਈ ਤਿਸ ਹੀ ਨੋ ਮਿਲੈ ਜਿਨਿ ਪੂਰਾ ਗੁਰੂ ਲਭਾ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਬਧਾ ਘਰੁ ਤਹਾ ਜਿਥੈ ਮਿਰਤੁ ਨ ਜਨਮੁ ਜਰਾ॥ 4॥6॥76॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 5:  Subhay thoak praaptay jay aavaiy Ikk(u) huth(i). Junum(u) pdaaruth(u) suful(u) haiy, jay Sacha Shabad(u) kuth(i). Gur tay Mehl(u) praaptay jis(u) likihiya hovaiy muth(i). 1. Mayray mun, Aikus siyoun chit(u) laayay. Aikus bin(u) subh dhundh(u) haiy, subh mithiya moh(u) Maayay. 1. Ruhaou. Lukh khooshiyaan paatshahiyaan jay Satgur(u) nudr(i) kuray. Nimkh aik Hur(i) Naam(u) dayay, mayra mun(u) tun(u) seetul hoyay. Jis kuou poorub(i) likhiya tin(i) Satgrur churn guhhay. 2. Suful moorut(u) sufla ghurri, jit(u) Sachay naal(i) piyar(u).  Dookh(u) suntaap(u) na luggyee jis(u) Hur(i) Naam(u) adhaar(u). Baanh puckurr(i) Gur(i) kaadhiya soyee ootriya paar(i). 3. Thaan soohaava pvit(u) haiy, jithaiy Sunt subha. Dhoyee tis hee nu milaiy jin(i) poora Guru lubha. Nanak buddha ghur(u) tuhaan, jithaiy mirt(u), na junum, juraa. 4.6.76.
Word Meaning:  Thoak = things.   Praaptay = acquire.   Ikk(u) = one (Waheguru).    Junum(u) Pdaruth = (valuable) article for life; objective of life (union with Waheguru).  Suful(u) = successful; is achieved.  Kuth(i) = recite.   Mehl(u) = God’s abode.  Likhiya hovaiy Muth(i) = written on the forehead (fortune); predestined.         1.        Aikus  =  One (Waheguru).   Dhundh(u) = Maya related.  Mithiya = false.          Pause.        Nimkh = a moment.  Seetal = calm; peaceful.    Poorub(i) = predestined.         2.           Moorut(u) = Ghurree= moment.   Suntaap(u) = suffering.   Adhaar = support.  Ootriya Paar(i)= made to the far end of the sea of Maya (salvaged).         3.        Soohaava = pleasing; comforting.  Pvit(u) = holy.  Sunt Subha = holy congregation.    Dhoyee = shelter.  Buddha Ghur(u) = built residence; controlled mid; attained the highest spiritual level.   Mirt(u) = death.  Juraa = old age.       4.
MEANING: Concentrate (meditate) only on Waheguru, oh my mind!  Other than the One (Waheguru), everything else is just daily routine work involving Maya, and attachment with Maya is short-lived (and hence, of no spiritual value).     Pause. 
If one is able to acquire (Waheguru’s Naam), he automatically acquires everything else he wishes for. The objective of life is achieved if one recites the True Shabad (of the Guru) (with dedication). If one is predestined, he will achieve union with Waheguru by the grace of the Guru.       1.           If Satguru casts His kind sight on someone, he enjoys the bliss which is better than millions of happiness and kingdoms.  My mind finds peace (feels blissful) if Satguru fosters Waheguru’s Naam in my mind – even if only for a short moment.  But one seeks the shelter of Satguru only if one is predestined to do so.           2.                 That moment, that time, is successful when you love the eternal Waheguru. The individual who makes Waheguru his support does not suffer from any discomfort or painful situations.  (His mind becomes so strong that he does not feel any discomfort.  Whoever is held by the arm by Waheguru (is supported by Him), swims across the sea of Maya.            3.
The place where a holy congregation is held becomes very comforting and pious. Only that individual finds shelter (attains relief from all sorts of miseries) who connects with the Guru. With Guru’s kindness, I have built the place (attained the spiritual level) where there is no death, rebirth, or old age (have become “one” with Waheguru), oh Nanak.   4.6.76.

ਸ੍ਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ 

ਸੋਈ ਧਿਆਈਐ ਜੀਅੜੇ ਸਿਰਿ ਸਾਹਾ ਪਾਤਿਸਾਹੁ॥ ਤਿਸ ਹੀ ਕੀ ਕਰਿ ਆਸ ਮਨ ਜਿਸ ਕਾ ਸਭਸੁ ਵੇਸਾਹੁ॥ ਸਭਿ ਸਿਆਣਪਾ ਛਡਿ ਕੈ ਗੁਰ ਕੀ ਚਰਣੀ ਪਾਹੁ॥ 1॥ ਮਨ ਮੇਰੇ ਸੁਖ ਸਹਜ ਸੇਤੀ ਜਪਿ ਨਾਉ॥ ਆਠ ਪਹਰ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਧਿਆਇ ਤੂੰ ਗੁਣ ਗੋਿੰਬੰਦ ਨਿਤ ਗਾਉ॥1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਤਿਸ ਕੀ ਸਰਨੀ ਪਰੁ ਮਨਾ ਜਿਸ ਜੇਵਡੁ ਅਵਰੁ ਨ ਕੋਇ॥ ਜਿਸੁ ਸਿਮਰਤ ਸੁਖੁ ਹੋਇ ਘਣਾ ਦੁਖੁ ਦਰਦੁ ਨ ਮੂਲੇ ਹੋਇ॥ 2॥ ਸਾਧ ਸੰਗਤਿ ਹੋਇ ਨਿਰਮਲਾ ਕਟੀਐ ਜਮ ਕੀ ਫਾਸ॥ ਸੁਖਦਾਤਾ ਭੈ ਭੰਜਨੋ ਤਿਸੁ ਆਗੈ ਕਰਿ ਅਰਦਾਸਿ॥ ਮਿਹਰ ਕਰੇ ਜਿਸੁ ਮਿਹਰਵਾਨੁ ਤਾ ਕਾਰਜੁ ਆਵੈ ਰਾਸਿ॥ 3॥ ਬਹੁਤੋ ਬਹੁਤੁ ਵਖਾਣੀਐ ਊਚੋ ਊਚਾ ਥਾਉ॥ ਵਰਨਾ ਚਿਹਨਾ ਬਾਹਰਾ ਕੀਮਤਿ ਕਹਿ ਨ ਸਕਾਉ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਕਉ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਮਇਆ ਕਰਿ ਸਚੁ ਦੇਵਹੁ ਆਪਣਾ ਨਾਉ॥  4॥7॥77
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 5:  Soyee dhiyayiyaiy jeeurray sir(i) shaahaan Patshah(u). Tis hee kee kur(i) aas mun, jis ka subhs(u) vaysaah(u). Subh(i) siyaanpaan chhudd(i) kaiy Gur kee churni paah(u). 1. Mun mayray, sookh sehj saytee jup(i) Naaoun. Aatth pehr Prubh(u) dhiyayay tu, goon Gobind nit gaao. 1. Ruhao. Tis kee sherni pur(u) muna jis jayvud(u) uver(u) na koyay. Jis simrut sookh(u) hoyay ghuna, dookh(u) durd(u) na moolay hoyay. 2. Sadh Sungut(i) hoyay nirmula, kuttiyaiy jum kee phaas. Sookhdaata bhaiy bhunjno, tis(u) aagaiy kur(i) ardaas(i). Mehr kuray jis(u) mehrvaan(u) ta kaaruj aavaiy raas(i). 3. Buhutou buhout(u) vukhaaniyaiy, oocho oocha Thaaoun. Verna chihna baahra, keemut(i) keh(i) na skaaoun. Nanak kuou Prubh(u) muyya kur(i) Such(u) dayvhou aapna Naaoun. 4.7.77.
Word Meaning:  Dhiyayiyaiy = remember; worship.  Jeeurray = oh, mind.  Sir(i) = above.  Vaysaah(u) = trust; faith.    Siyaanupaan = all wisdom.        1.
Sehj = stable or peaceful mind.    Saytee = with.  Aatth Pehr = 24 hours; day and night. (pehr is a unit of time and is equal to three hours).  Gobind = a name for Waheguru.  Nit = every day (all the time).        Pause.          Jayvud = as expansive.   Uver(u) = else.   Simrut = remembering.  Ghuna = very much.    Na Moolay = not at all.      2.
Sadh Sungat(i) = holy congregation (of the Guru or the devotees).  Nirmula = clean; purefied.    Jum Kee Phaas = noose of the angel of death.  Bhaiy = fear.  Bhunjno = the destroyer (Waheguru).   Mehrvaan(u) = kind; munificent.   Kaaruj = task.  Raas(i) = suitable; successful (the objective is achieved).             3.        Vkhaaniyaiy = say; explain.  Ooho oocha = higher and higher (unreachable). Thaaon = place; residence.   Verna = colours (of the skin).   Chihna = physical features.   Baahra = beyond.    Muyya = kindness; mercy.            4.
MEANING:  Remember (keep in mind) only (Waheguru) who is the King of all kings, and seek His shelter who has won the faith of everybody, oh my mind.  Forgetting all your wisdom, drop on the feet of (surrender to) the Guru.          1.         Recitation of Waheguru’s Naam with a peaceful mind (above the effects of Maya) is the only source of comfort, oh my mind. So, recite His Naam 24 hours, and sing His praise all the time.        Pause.
Take the refuge of only the “One” which has no equal (rival), oh, my friend. Worshipping Him provides great comfort, and one suffers no pain or discomfort.          2.
Mind is purified in the company of the Satguru, and the noose of the Angel of death is cut free.  Pray before the source of all comforts and the destroyer of all fears (God), oh my mind.  The objective (of being “one” with Waheguru) is achieved if the kind (Waheguru) shows His benevolence.        3.              People say that Waheguru is more and more expansive, and He is higher and higher; but nobody can evaluate Him fully (describe Him) because He has no skin colour and no physical features.  I only pray to Him that He kindly implants His Naam in my heart, oh Nanak.   4. 7. 77.
