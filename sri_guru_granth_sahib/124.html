(ਅੰਗ 123)॥         ਹਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਜੀਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਨਾਮੁ ਸੁਣਿ ਮੰਨਿ ਵਸਾਵਣਿਆ॥ ਹਰਿ ਜੀਉ ਸਚਾ ਊਚੋ ਊਚਾ ਹਉਮੈ ਮਾਰਿ ਮਿਲਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਹਰਿ ਜੀਉ ਸਾਚਾ ਸਾਚੀ ਨਾਈ॥ ਗੁਰ ਪਰਸਾਦੀ ਕਿਸੈ ਮਿਲਾਈ॥ ਗੁਰ ਸਬਦਿ ਮਿਲਹਿ ਸੇ ਵਿਛੁੜਹਿ ਨਾਹੀ ਸਹਜੇ ਸਚਿ ਸਮਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 2॥ ਤੁਝ ਤੇ ਬਾਹਰਿ ਕਛੂ ਨ ਹੋਇ॥ ਤੂੰ ਕਰਿ ਕਰਿ ਵੇਖਹਿ ਜਾਣਹਿ ਸੋਇ॥ ਆਪੇ ਕਰੇ ਕਰਾਏ ਕਰਤਾ ਗੁਰਮਤਿ ਆਪਿ ਮਿਲਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 3॥ ਕਾਮਣਿ ਗੁਣਵੰਤੀ ਹਰਿ ਪਾਏ॥ ਭੈ ਭਾਇ ਸੀਗਾਰੁ ਬਣਾਏ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਸੇਵਿ ਸਦਾ ਸੋਹਾਗਣਿ ਸਚ ਉਪਦੇਸਿ ਸਮਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 4॥ ਸਬਦੁ ਵਿਸਾਰਨਿ ਤਿਨਾ ਠਉਰੁ ਨ ਠਾਉ॥ ਭ੍ਰਮਿ ਭੂਲੇ ਜਿਉ ਸੁੰਞੈ ਘਰਿ ਕਾਉ॥ ਹਲਤੁ ਪਲਤੁ ਤਿਨੀ ਦੋਵੈ ਗਵਾਏ ਦੁਖੇ ਦੁਖਿ ਵਿਹਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 5॥ ਲਿਖਦਿਆ ਲਿਖਦਿਆ ਕਾਗਦ ਮਸੁ ਖੋਈ॥ ਦੂਜੈ ਭਾਇ ਸੁਖੁ ਨ ਪਾਏ ਕੋਈ॥ ਕੂੜੁ ਲਿਖਹਿ ਤੈ ਕੂੜੁ ਕਮਾਵਹਿ ਜਲਿ ਜਾਵਹਿ ਕੂੜਿ ਚਿਤੁ ਲਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 6॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਸਚੋ ਸਚੁ ਲਿਖਹਿ ਵੀਚਾਰੁ॥ ਸੇ ਜਨ ਸਚੇ ਪਾਵਹਿ ਮੋਖ ਦੁਆਰੁ॥ ਸਚੁ ਕਾਗਦੁ ਕਲਮ ਮਸਵਾਣੀ ਸਚੁ ਲਿਖਿ ਸਚਿ ਸਮਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 7॥ ਮੇਰਾ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਬੈਠਾ ਵੇਖੈ॥ ਗੁਰ ਪਰਸਾਦੀ ਮਿਲੈ ਸੋਈ ਜਨੁ ਲੇਖੈ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮੁ ਮਿਲੈ ਵਡਿਆਈ ਪੂਰੇ ਗੁਰ ਤੇ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥  8॥22॥23॥
Majh Mehla 3: Tayray bhagat soheh(i) Sachaiy Derbaaray. Gur kaiy Shabad(i) Naam(i) suvaaray. Suda anund(i) ruheh(i) din(u) raati goon keh(i) Gooni smaavniya.  1.
(PAGE 123):              Houn vaari jeeo vaari, Naam(u) soon(i) munn(i) vusaavniya. Hur(i) jeeo Sacha oocho oocha, haumein maar(i) milaavniya. 1. Ruhaao.  Hur(i) jeeo Saacha Saachi naayee. Gur persadi kisaiy milaayee. Gur Shabad(i) mileh(i) say vichhurreh(i) naahi, sehjay Sach(i) smaavniya.  2. Toojh tay baahur(i) kuchhoo na hoyay. Tu kur(i) kur(i) vaykheh(i) jaaneh(i) soyay.  Aapay kuray kuraayay Kerta, Gurmut(i) Aap(i) milaavniya. 3. Kaamun(i) goonvunti Hur(i) paayay. Bhaiy bhaayay seegaar(u) bunaayay. Satgur(u) sayv(i) suda sohaagun(i) Sach oopdaysh(i) smaavniya.  4. Shabad(u) visaarun(i) tinha tthaur(u) na tthaaoun. Bherm(i) bhoolay jiyoun soonjaiy ghur(i) kaaoun. Hult(u) pult(u) tinhi dovaiy guvaayay, dookhay dookh(i) vihaavniya. 5. Likhdiyaan likhdiyaan kaagud mus(u) khoyee. Doojaiy sookh(u) na paayay koyee. Koorr(u) likheh(i) taiy koorr(u) kumaaveh(i), jul(i) jaaveh(i) koorr(i) chit(u) laavniya.  6. Gurmukh(i) Sacho Sach(u) likheh(i) veechar(u). Say jun sachay, paaveh(i) moakh dooaar(u). Sach(u) kaagud(u) kulm musvaani, Sach(u) likh(i) Sach(i) smaavniya. 7.  Mayra Prubh(u) untar(i) baiyttha vaykhaiy. Gur persadi milaiy soyee jun(u) laykhaiy. Nanak Naam(u) milaiy vudiyaayee Poray Gur tay paavniya.    8.22.23.
Word Meaning:  Soheh(i) = look dignified.     Svaaray = adorned (with virtues).  Anund(i) = blissful.     Gooni = the source of all comfort (Waheguru).             1. Mun(i) Vusaavniya = Make mind home for Waheguru (embed in mind).    Oocha Oocha = higher and higher above Maya.    Haumein Maar(i) = overcome ego.   Pause.
Naayee = greatness; magnanimity.   Kisaiy = some rare.  Vichhurreh(i) = separated.      2.         Baahur(i) = outside of.     Kerta = Creator.      3.    Kaamun(i) = woman (human being).   Goonvunti = virtuous.   Bhaiy Bhaayay = love-filled fear.   Seegar = adornment.    Sohaagun = married; loved by husband.    Oopdaysh = Teachings.      4.           Visaarun(i) = forget; ignore.   Thaur(u) Tthaaoun = shelter; support.    Bherm(i) = misbeliefs.     Soonjaiy Ghur(i) = in an uninhabited house.   Kaaoun = crow.   Hult(u) = this world.   Pult(u) = next world (after death).   Tinhi = them; those.   Vihaavniya = (life) is spent.      5.     Kaagud = paper.    Mus = ink.    Khoyee = ran out.     Doojaiy = Maya.    Koorr(u) = lies; perishable stuff.    Kmaaveh(i) = deals in.    Jul(i) = burn.           6. Veechaar = thoughts.  Moakh Dooaar(u) = door of salvation.    Musvaani = ink pot.  7. Untar(i) = inside (the body).    Laykhaiy = in record (approved).   Vudiyaayee = respect.8        
MEANING:  Adorned with Your Naam by Guru’s Shabad, Your devotees look exalted in Your Court, oh Waheguru.   They merge into You by singing Your virtues, and stay blissful day and night.             1. I offer my life to those who listen to Your Naam and embed it in their mind.  Eternal Waheguru is very, very high (way above the effects of Maya), and can be reached only by those who have gotten rid of their haumein.         Pause.              Waheguru is eternal, and so is His magnanimity. A rare person connects with Him by the Guru’s grace.  Those who connect with Him through the Guru’s Shabad never get separated from Him again and merge with Him by attaining the high spiritual state above the effects of Maya.            2. Nothing happens outside Your Huqam; You create bodies, and know what You do about them, oh Waheguru!  You do things Yourself, and make living beings do (whatever You wish), and connect with some of them by making them act on Guru’s advice.            3. The virtuous woman (human being) gets connected with Waheguru.  She adorns her mind with the noble qualities of fear and love for Waheguru, and by serving the Guru, she always stays married (loved by Waheguru – husband). She merges with Waheguru by acting on Guru’s eternal Teachings.             4. Those who ignore Waheguru find no shelter and no support in the spiritual world. They are misled by misbeliefs, and, like a crow that goes to an uninhabited house (and finds nothing to eat), they do not achieve anything spiritually.   They waste away (spiritually ruin) both the worlds – the present and the next – and their life is spent in deep suffering.           5.The money-minded people may write something in praise of Waheguru, but do not gain anything spiritually blissful just by writing, even if they write so much that they run out of paper and ink, because all their writings are rooted in Maya, and they look for) material things in their writings.  They write about material things, deal in them, and eventually get heart- burning (suffer gravely) because their mind always thinks only about money.           6. 
(On the other hand), A devotee writes only those thoughts that relate to Waheguru. Such pure-minded devotees find the door of salvation. Their paper, their pen, and their ink pot are all about Waheguru, and they merge with Him by writing His praise.         7.
Sitting in every heart, my God is watching everything that people do.  Only that individual’s account (balance sheet of good and bad deeds) is approved (rewarded) who gets connected with Waheguru by the grace of the Guru.  And, only those people are honoured who have Waheguru’s Naam at heart, which is acquired only through the Perfect Guru, oh Nanak.           8.22.23.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ ਆਤਮ ਰਾਮ ਪਰਗਾਸੁ ਗੁਰ ਤੇ ਹੋਵੈ॥ ਹਉਮੈ ਮੈਲੁ ਲਾਗੀ ਗੁਰ ਸਬਦੀ ਖੋਵੈ॥ ਮਨੁ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਅਨਦਿਨੁ ਭਗਤੀ ਰਾਤਾ ਭਗਤਿ ਕਰੇ ਹਰਿ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਹਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਜੀਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਆਪਿ ਭਗਤਿ ਕਰਨਿ ਅਵਰਾ ਭਗਤਿ ਕਰਾਵਣਿਆ॥ ਤਿਨਾ ਭਗਤ ਜਨਾ ਕਉ ਸਦ ਨਮਸਕਾਰੁ ਕੀਜੈ ਜੋ ਅਨਦਿਨੁ ਹਰਿ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਆਪੇ ਕਰਤਾ ਕਾਰਣੁ ਕਰਾਏ॥ ਜਿਤੁ ਭਾਵੈ ਤਿਤੁ ਕਾਰੈ ਲਾਏ॥ ਪੂਰੇ ਭਾਗਿ ਗੁਰ ਸੇਵਾ ਹੋਵੈ ਗੁਰ ਸੇਵਾ ਤੇ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥2॥ ਮਰਿ ਮਰਿ ਜੀਵੈ ਤਾ ਕਿਛੁ ਪਾਏ॥ ਗੁਰ ਪਰਸਾਦੀ ਹਰਿ ਮੰਨਿ ਵਸਾਏ॥ ਸਦਾ ਮੁਕਤੁ ਹਰਿ ਮੰਨਿ ਵਸਾਏ ਸਹਜੇ ਸਹਜਿ ਸਮਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 3॥ ਬਹੁ ਕਰਮ ਕਮਾਵੈ ਮੁਕਤਿ ਨ ਪਾਏ॥ ਦੇਸੰਤਰੁ ਭਵੈ ਦੂਜੈ ਭਾਇ ਖੁਆਏ॥ ਬਿਰਥਾ ਜਨਮੁ ਗਵਾਇਆ ਕਪਟੀ ਬਿਨੁ ਸਬਦੈ ਦੁਖੁ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 4॥ ਧਾਵਤੁ ਰਾਖੈ ਠਾਕਿ ਰਹਾਏ॥ਗੁਰ ਪਰਸਾਦੀ ਪਰਮ ਪਦੁ ਪਾਏ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਆਪੇ ਮੇਲਿ ਮਿਲਾਏ ਮਿਲਿ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਮ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 5॥
