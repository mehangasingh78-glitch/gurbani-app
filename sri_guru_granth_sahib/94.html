(ਅੰਗ 93)॥  ਸ੍ਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਬਾਣੀ ਭਗਤ ਬੇਣੀ ਜੀਉ ਕੀ॥ ਪਹਰਿਆ ਕੈ ਘਰਿ ਗਾਵਣਾ॥
ੴ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਪ੍ਰਸਾਦਿ॥
(PAGE 93):  Siri Raag(u) Bani Bhagat Bayni Jeeo Kee. Pehriya kaiy ghur(i) gaavna
                                                  Ek Onkar Satgur Persaad(i):

ਰੇ ਨਰ ਗਰਭ ਕੁੰਡਲ ਜਬ ਆਛਤ ਉਰਧ ਧਿਆਨ ਲਿਵ ਲਾਗਾ॥ ਮਿਰਤਕ ਪਿੰਡਿ ਪਦ ਮਦ ਨਾ ਅਹਿਨਿਸਿ ਏਕੁ ਅਗਿਆਨ ਸੁ ਨਾਗਾ॥ ਤੇ ਦਿਨ ਸੰਮਲੁ ਕਸਟ ਮਹਾ ਦੁਖ ਅਬ ਚਿਤੁ ਅਧਿਕ ਪਸਾਰਿਆ॥ ਗਰਭ ਛੋਡਿ ਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਮੰਡਲ ਆਇਆ ਤਉ ਨਰਹਰਿ ਮਨਹੁ ਬਿਸਾਰਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਫਿਰਿ ਪਛੁਤਾਵਹਿਗਾ ਮੂੜਿਆ ਤੂੰ ਕਵਨ ਕੁਮਤਿ ਭ੍ਰਮਿ ਲਾਗਾ॥ ਚੇਤਿ ਰਾਮੁ ਨਾਹੀ ਜਮਪੁਰਿ ਜਾਹਿਗਾ ਜਨੁ ਬਿਚਰੈ ਅਨਰਾਧਾ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਬਾਲ ਬਿਨੋਦ ਚਿੰਦ ਰਸ ਲਾਗਾ ਖਿਨੁ  ਖਿਨੁ ਮੋਹਿ ਬਿਆਪੈ॥ ਰਸੁ ਮਿਸੁ ਮੇਧੁ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਬਿਖੁ ਚਾਖੀ ਤਉ ਪੰਚ ਪ੍ਰਗਟ ਸੰਤਾਪੈ॥ ਜਪੁ ਤਪੁ ਸੰਜਮੁ ਛੋਡਿ ਸੁਕ੍ਰਿਤ ਮਤਿ ਰਾਮ ਨਾਮੁ ਨ ਅਰਾਧਿਆ॥ ਉਛਲਿਆ ਕਾਮੁ ਕਾਲ ਮਤਿ ਲਾਗੀ ਤਉ ਆਨਿ ਸਕਤਿ ਗਲਿ ਬਾਧਿਆ॥ 2॥ ਤਰੁਣ ਤੇਜੁ ਪਰ ਤ੍ਰਿਅ ਮੁਖੁ ਜੋਹਹਿ ਸਰੁ ਅਪਸਰੁ ਨ ਪਛਾਣਿਆ॥ ਉਨਮਤ ਕਾਮਿ ਮਹਾ ਬਿਖੁ ਭੂਲੈ ਪਾਪੁ ਪੁੰਨੁ ਨ ਪਛਾਨਿਆ॥ ਸੁਤ ਸੰਪਤਿ ਦੇਖਿ ਇਹੁ ਮਨੁ ਗਰਬਿਆ ਰਾਮੁ ਰਿਦੈ ਤੇ ਖੋਇਆ॥ ਅਵਰ ਮਰਤ ਮਾਇਆ ਮਨੁ ਤੋਲੇ ਤਉ ਭਗ ਮੁਖਿ ਜਨਮੁ ਵਿਗੋਇਆ॥ 3॥ ਪੁੰਡਰ ਕੇਸ ਕੁਸਮ ਤੇ ਧਉਲੇ ਸਪਤ ਪਾਤਾਲ ਕੀ ਬਾਣੀ॥ ਲੋਚਨ ਸ੍ਰਮਹਿ ਬੁਧਿ ਬਲ ਨਾਠੀ ਤਾ ਕਾਮੁ ਪਵਸਿ ਮਾਧਾਣੀ॥ ਤਾ ਤੇ ਬਿਖੈ ਭਈ ਮਤਿ ਪਾਵਸਿ ਕਾਇਆ ਕਮਲੁ ਕੁਮਲਾਣਾ॥ ਅਵਗਤਿ ਬਾਣਿ ਛੋਡਿ ਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਮੰਡਲਿ ਤਉ ਪਾਛੈ ਪਛੁਤਾਣਾ॥ 4॥ ਨਿਕੁਟੀ ਦੇਹ ਦੇਖਿ ਧੁਨਿ ਉਪਜੈ ਮਾਨ ਕਰਤ ਨਹੀ ਬੂਝੈ॥ ਲਾਲਚੁ ਕਰੈ ਜੀਵਨ ਪਦ ਕਾਰਨ ਲੋਚਨ ਕਛੂ ਨ ਸੂਝੈ॥ ਥਾਕਾ ਤੇਜੁ ਉਡਿਆ ਮਨੁ ਪੰਖੀ ਘਰਿ ਆਗਨਿ ਨ ਸੁਖਾਈ॥ ਬੇਣੀ ਕਹੈ ਸੁਨਹੁ ਰੇ ਭਗਤਹੁ ਮਰਨ ਮੁਕਤਿ ਕਿਨਿ ਪਾਈ॥ 5॥
Ray nur gerbh koondul jub aachhut, oordh dhiyan liv laaga. Mirtuk pind(i), pud mud na, ehinis(i) Aik,ugiyan, su naanga. Tay din summul(u), kusht maha dookh, ub chit(u) adhik pusaariya. Gerbh chhoad(i) mirt mundal aaya, tuou Nurhur(i) munhoun bisaariya. 1. Phir(i) puchhutaavehinga moorriya, tu kuvun koomut(i) bherm(i) laaga. Chayt(i) Ram(u) naahi Jumpur(i) jaahaynga, jun(u) bichraiy unraadha. 1. Ruhaou.  Baal binoad chind rus laaga, khin(u), khin(u) moh(i) biyaapaiy. Rus(u) mis(u) maydh(u) amrit bikh(u) chaakhi, tuou punch pergut suntaapaiy. Jup(u) tup(u) sunjum(u) chhoad(i), sookirt mut(i), Ram Naam(u) na araadhiya. Oochhliya kaam(u) kaal mut(i) laagi, tuou aan(i) shakut(i) gul(i) baandhiya. 2. Troon tayj(u), pur triya mookh(u) johay, sur(u) upsur(u) na puchhniya. Oonmut kaam(i) muha bikh(u) bhoolaiy, paap(u) poonn(u) na puchhaniya. Soot sumput(i) daykh(i) eho mun(u) gerbiya, Ram(u) ridaiy tay khoya. Uver mert Maya mun(u) tolay, tuou bhug mookh(i) junum(u) vigoya. 3. Poondur kays koosum tay dhaulay, supt pataal kee bani. Lochun  sermeh(i) boodh(i) bul naatthi, ta kaam(u) puvus(i) madhaani. Ta tay bikhaiy bhuyyee mut(i) paavus(i), kaaya kumul(u) koomlaana. Uvgut(i) baan(i) chhoad(i) mirt mundul(i), tuou paachhaiy puchhutaana. 4. Nikooti dayh daykh(i) dhoon(i) oopjaiy, maan kert nuhee boojhaiy.  Laaluch(u) kuraiy jeevun pud kaarun, lochun kuchhoo na soojhaiy. Thaaka tayj(u), oodiya mun(u) punkhi, ghur(i) aangun(i) na sookhayee. Bayni kuhaiy soonhou ray bhugtou mern mookt(i) kin(i) paayee?   5. 
Word Meaning: Pehriya Kaiy Ghur(i) Gaavna = to sing with the same tune as the Pehray in the same Raag starting on page 74.  
Gerbh Koondul = in the mother’s womb.  Aachhut = (you) were.  Oordh = upside down.  Mirtuk Pind(i) = half-alive body.  Pud = existence.  Mud = intoxication; conceit.  Naanga = without any bad quality.   Summul(u) = remember.   Maha Kusht = big discomfort.   Pusaariya = expansion.  Mirt Mundul = mortal world.   Nurhur(i) = Waheguru.     1. 
Moorriya = oh foolish.   Koomut(i) = bad thinking.   Chayt(i) = remember.   Jumpur = home of the angel of death. Bichraiy = live in the world. Unraadha = uncontrolled.      Pause.     Binoad = (children’s) games.  Chind = worry; attention.  Khin(u) Khin(u) = every small moment.  Mis(u) = excuse.   Maydhu = pious; alcohol (which is a more appropriate use in the present context).      Bikh = poison.   Punch = the five (demons – lust, rage, etc.).  Pergut = clearly.   Suntaapiy = begin to bother (cause trouble).   Sookirt = noble deeds.    Oochhliya Kaam(u) = the lust sprouted.   Kaal Mut(i) = wisdom that blackens one's face (earns him disgrace).     Shakut(i) = woman.    2.
Troon = youth; beauty.  Tayj(u) = forceful impression; glamour.    Triya = woman.   Johay = keeps an evil sight.       Sur(u) Upsur(u) = appropriate and inappropriate time.  Oomut Kaam(i) = under the influence of lust.   Maha Bikh(u) = great poison (Maya).  Soot = sons.   Sumput(i) = wealth.   Gerbiya = conceited.   Uver mert = at the death of others.   Bhug Mookh(i) = with good fortune.   Vigoya = wasted away.        3.
Poondar Kays = white hair like a lotus flower.   Koosum = flower.   Dhaulay = grey hair.   Supt Pataal = (as if coming from) the seventh underworld.    Loachun = eyes.  Sermeh(i) = watery.   Kaam Madhaani = mind churner (inciting for lust).   Paavus(i) = rain.   Koomlana = wilted.   Uvgut(i) = invisible; incomprehensible.  Baan(i) = Shabad (of Waheguru’s praise).   Mirt Mundul(i) = mortal world.    Paachhaiy = after; later.   4.
Nikooti dayh = small body (children).   Dhoon(i) = attention; affection.  Maan Kert = feeling proud.   Jeevun Pud Kaarun = existence for (longer) life.  Tayj(u) = energy.  Aangun(i) = in the yard.   Na Sookhayee = becomes uncomfortable.    5. 
MEANING:  Remember the day (time) when you were in a big (troublesome) uncomfortable position in the womb of your mother where you were suspended upside down, had no conceit of the existence of your half-alive body, and were fully connected with the “One”, God, day and night, and had no awareness or evil quality, oh man!  Now that you are out of your mother’s womb and are in the mortal world, you have forgotten Waheguru completely and have plans for big expansions.    1.
Under what ill-advice are you leading a life full of doubts and misbeliefs, oh foolish man?  Remember Waheguru’s Naam, you will repent later because, as a result of your bad deeds under the influence of your uncontrolled mind, you will be taken to the court of the Angel of death.            Pause.        As a child, the man is drawn to the joy of games and grows increasingly attached to the world with every moment. As life goes on, he begins to use Maya as if it were amrit, and abuses the poisonous Maya to enjoy other tastes of life, including alcohol, when all the five demons (lust, rage, greed, etc.) begin to visibly trouble (overpower) him.  He does not practise even the ritualistic recitation of mantras, and self-torture to have control over sensual pleasures; and totally forgetting the wisdom of noble deeds, he does not remember Waheguru’s Naam.  When lust sprouts in his mind, and his poor thinking begins to prompt him to evil deeds, he brings in a woman to hug her tightly.        2.
Under the forceful impact of youth, the man begins to keep a close eye on other women as well, with total disregard for the appropriateness of the time.  Under compete intoxication of lust, and completely misguided by poisonous Maya, the man cannot tell sin or crime from noble deeds.  Seeing his sons and the wealth, the man feels conceited and forgets Waheguru’s Naam completely.  At the death of a relative, he begins to guess how much wealth he will inherit, and (because of such greed and lack of empathy), he wastes the human life he was given by his good fortune.        3.
Even when the hair of the man turns white like a lotus flower, and his voice becomes so soft (weak) as if it is coming from the seventh underworld, and even when his eyes are watery all the time, and his energy and his conniving wisdom have left him, lust still churns his mind i.e., he is still lustful.  The lotus-like body has wilted away, but evil ideas are still dropping in his mind like rain. Quit thinking about the mortal world, and recite the Naam of Waheguru; otherwise, you will repent later, oh man!        4.
Seeing the little grandchildren, his heart fills with affection. And he still does not realize the reality (of the imminent death).  He has the greed to live longer, even though he can hardly see with his eyes.  When his energy is exhausted, and his soul leaves his body, his dead body causes discomfort in the house and in the yard (among the family members).  Listen, hey devotees, who gets salvation after death (nobody does), says Bayni. 5.                                


ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ॥
                                                  Siri Raag(u):
ਤੋਹੀ ਮੋਹੀ ਮੋਹੀ ਤੋਹੀ ਅੰਤਰੁ ਕੈਸਾ॥ ਕਨਕ ਕਟਿਕ ਜਲ ਤਰੰਗ ਜੈਸਾ॥ 1॥ ਜਉਪੈ ਹਮ ਨ ਪਾਪ ਕਰੰਤਾ ਅਹੇ ਅਨਂੰਤਾ॥ ਪਤਿਤ ਪਾਵਨ ਨਾਮੁ ਕੈਸੇ ਹੁੰਤਾ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਤੁਮ੍ਹ ਜੁ ਨਾਇਕ ਆਛਹੁ ਅੰਤਰਜਾਮੀ॥ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਤੇ ਜਨੁ ਜਾਨੀਜੈ ਜਨ ਤੇ ਸੁਆਮੀ॥ 2॥ ਸਰੀਰੁ ਆਰਾਧੈ ਮੋ ਕਉ ਬੀਚਾਰੁ ਦੇਹੂ॥ ਰਵਿਦਾਸ ਸਮ ਦਲ ਸਮਝਾਵੈ ਕੋਊ॥ 3॥
Tohi mohi, mohi tohi, untar(u) kaisa. Kunuk kutick jul trung jaiysa. 1. Juoupaiy hum na paap krunta, uhay Ununta. Putit paavun Naam(u) kaisay hoonta? 1. Ruhaou. Toom ju nayak aachhou untarjaami. Prubh tay jun(u) jaanijaiy, jun tay Soami. 2. Sreer(u) aaradhaiy, mo kuou beechaar(u) dayhoo. Ravidaas sum dul sumjhaavaiy koyoo. 3.
NOTE: The heading of this holy Shabad does not reveal the author's name, but the last line makes it clear that Sri Ravidass Ji is the author. 
Word Meaning:  Tohi = you.   Mohi = me.   Untar(u) = difference.  Kunuk = gold.   Kutick = bracelet.  Trung = wave.     1.     Ununta = limitless (Waheguru).  Putit = degraded.   Paavun = purifier.    Pause.     Nayak = leader; guide.   Aachhou = (you) are.  Untarjaami = all-knowing; omniscient.      Jun = servant (devotee).  Jaanijaiy = is known.   Soami = Master; Lord.  2.    Aaradhaiy = recite (Naam).  Sum Dul = equal everywhere (omnipresent).   3. 
MEANING: Now, (that I am connected with You), the difference between You and me is just like the difference between gold and a bracelet made from gold, or between water in the ocean and a wave on its surface (i.e., there is no difference), oh Lord!     1.
If we (human beings) had not committed any sin, how would You have been called the Purifier?           Pause.        You are omniscient, and our guide (to show us the path of righteousness).  Also, a devotee is known by God, and God is recognized by his devotees. (God and His devotees are important to each other).           2.
Please, enlighten me so that my body may recite Your Naam.  I wish someone could educate (enlighten) me that You are omnipresent, begs Ravidass.      3.
