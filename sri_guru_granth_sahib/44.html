 ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥

ਭਲਕੇ ਉਠਿ ਪਪੋਲੀਐ ਵਿਣੁ ਬੁਝੇ ਮੁਗਧ ਅਜਾਣਿ॥ ਸੋ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਚਿਤਿ ਨ ਆਇਓ ਛੁਟੈਗੀ ਬੇਬਾਣਿ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਸੇਤੀ ਚਿਤੁ ਲਾਇ ਸਦਾ ਸਦਾ ਰੰਗੁ ਮਾਣਿ॥ 1॥ ਪ੍ਰਾਣੀ ਤੂ ਆਇਆ ਲਾਹਾ ਲੈਣਿ॥ ਲਗਾ ਕਿਤੁ ਕੁਫਕੜੇ ਸਭ ਮੁਕਦੀ ਚਲੀ ਰੈਣਿ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਕੁਦਮੁ ਕਰੇ ਪਸੁ ਪੰਖੀਆ ਦਿਸੈ ਨਾਹੀ ਕਾਲੁ॥ ਓਤੇ ਸਾਥਿ ਮਨੁਖੁ ਹੈ ਫਾਥਾ ਮਾਇਆ ਜਾਲਿ॥ ਮੁਕਤੇ ਸੇਈ ਭਾਲੀਅਹਿ ਜਿ ਸਚਾ ਨਾਮੁ ਸਮਾਲਿ॥ 2॥ ਜੇ ਘਰੁ ਛਡਿ ਗਵਾਵਣਾ ਸੋ ਲਗਾ ਮਨ ਮਾਹਿ॥ ਜਿਥੈ ਜਾਇ ਤੁਧੁ ਵਰਤਣਾ ਤਿਸ ਕੀ ਚਿੰਤਾ ਨਾਹਿ॥ ਫਾਥੇ ਸੇਈ ਨਿਕਲੇ ਜਿ ਗੁਰ ਕੀ ਪੈਰੀ ਪਾਹਿ॥ 3॥ ਕੋਈ ਰਖਿ ਨ ਸਕਈ ਦੂਜਾ ਕੋ ਨ ਦਿਖਾਇ॥ ਚਾਰੇ ਕੁੰਡਾ ਭਾਲਿ ਕੈ ਆਇ ਪਇਆ ਸਰਣਾਇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਸਚੈ ਪਾਤਿਸਾਹਿ ਡੁਬਦਾ ਲਇਆ ਕਢਾਇ॥ 4॥3॥73॥
(PAGE 43): Siri Raag(u) Mehla 5:  Bhulkay ootth(i) pupoliyaiy vin(u) boojhay moogdh ujaan(i). So Prubh(u) chit(i) na aayo, chootaiygi baybaan(i).  Satgur saytee chit(u) laayay suda suda rung(u) maan(i). 1. Praani, tu aaya laaha laiyn(i). Lugga kit(u) koophukkrray subh mookdi chulli raiyn(i). 1. Ruhaou. Koodum(u) kuray pus(u) punkhiyaan, disaiy naahi kaal(u). Otay saath(i) munookh(u) haiy, phaatha Maya jaal(i). Mooktay sayee bhaaliyeh(i), j(i) Sacha Naam(u) smaal(i). 2. Jay ghur(u) chhudd(i) guvaavna, so lugga mun maah(i). Jithaiy jaayay toodh(u) vertna tis kee chinta naah(i). Phaathay sayee niklay, j(i) Gur kee paiyreen paah(i). 3. Koyee rukh(i) na sukyee, dooja ko na dikhaayay. Chaaray koondaan bhaal(i) kaiy aayay puyiya shernayay. Nanak Sachaiy Paatshaah(i) doobda luyya kudhaayay.  4.3.73. 
Word Meaning:  Pupoliyaiy = nourish.  Moogdh = foolish.  Ujaan(i) = ignorant.   Baybaan(i) = deserted place (graveyard).   Saytee = with.   Rung = colour (colourful life); blissfulness.   Maan(i) = enjoy.          1.         Laaha = earn profit (achieve the objective of spirituality and be “one” with God).  Koophukkrray = worthless (ignoble) deeds.       Pause.         Koodum(u) = jump around carelessly; have fun.  Kaal(u) = death.  Otay = the same.  Phaatha = trapped.   Mooktay = free from the bonds of Maya.  Bhaaliyeh(i) = search; are considered.     Smaal(i) = take care of; remember.         2.        Jay = Jo = which.  Ghur = house; life.   Vertna = have to deal with.       3.         Koondaan = directions.   Bhaal(i) = search.    Shernayay = (Guru’s) shelter.      4.
MEANING:  You came to this earth to achieve an objective (of being “one” with Waheguru), oh man. But you are busy pursuing worthless deeds without realizing that the night of your life is slipping away.     Pause.         Without the realization (of what the real objective of life is), the foolish and the ignorant man is only nourishing his body, starting early in the morning.  He never thinks of Waheguru (despite the fact that eventually), his dead body will be thrown in the graveyard.  To enjoy eternal happiness (blissfulness), the man should focus his mind on Waheguru.        1.           Just like animals and birds, which jump around (have fun) carefreely unaware of the impending death, the ignorant man also enjoys life trapped in the net of Maya.  Only those are free from the bonds of Maya who are fostering Waheguru’s Naam in their hearts.           2.             The man has only the household's interests in mind, which he has to leave behind anyway, but shows no concern (completely unaware) about his future home, where he will go (after death).  Trapped in the net of Maya, only those get free from it who take shelter at the Guru’s feet.         3.          Nobody else (other than the Guru) can guide (enlighten) us and save us from sins.  So, after searching in all four directions (everywhere), I have come to the refuge of the Guru. The True King (Waheguru) has saved me from drowning in the sea of Maya (and connected me with Him), oh Nanak.   4.3.73.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਘੜੀ ਮੁਹਤ ਕਾ ਪਾਹੁਣਾ ਕਾਜ ਸਵਾਣਹਾਰੁ॥ ਮਾਇਆ ਕਾਮਿ ਵਿਆਪਿਆ ਸਮਝੈ ਨਾਹੀ ਗਾਵਾਰੁ॥ ਉਠਿ ਚਲਿਆ ਪਛੁਤਾਇਆ ਪਰਿਆ ਵਸਿ ਜੰਦਾਰ॥ 1॥ ਅੰਧੇ ਤੂ ਬੈਠਾ ਕੰਧੀ ਪਾਇ॥ ਜੇ ਹੋਵੀ ਪੂਰਬਿ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਤਾ ਗੁਰ ਕਾ ਬਚਨੁ ਕਮਾਹਿ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਹਰੀ ਨਾਹੀ ਨਹ ਡਡੁਰੀ ਪਕੀ ਵਢਣਹਾਰ॥ ਲੈ ਲ਼ੈ ਦਾਤ ਪਹੁਤਿਆ ਲਾਵੈ ਕਰਿ ਤਈਆਰੁ॥ ਜਾ ਹੋਆ ਹੁਕਮੁ ਕਿਰਸਾਣ ਦਾ ਤਾ ਲੁਣਿ ਮਿਣਿਆ ਖੇਤਾਰੁ॥ 2॥ ਪਹਿਲਾ ਪਹਰੁ ਧੰਧੈ ਗਇਆ ਦੂਜੈ ਭਰਿ ਸੋਇਆ॥ ਤੀਜੈ ਝਾਖ ਝਖਾਇਆ ਚਉਥੈ ਭੋਰੁ ਭਇਆ॥ ਕਦ ਹੀ ਚਿਤਿ ਨ ਆਇਓ ਜਿਨਿ ਜੀਉ ਪਿੰਡੁ ਦੀਆ॥3॥ ਸਾਧ ਸੰਗਤਿ ਕਉ ਵਾਰਿਆ ਜੀਉ ਕੀਆ ਕੁਰਬਾਣੁ॥ ਜਿਸ ਤੇ ਸੋਝੀ ਮਨਿ ਪਈ ਮਿਲਿਆ ਪੁਰਖੁ ਸੁਜਾਣੁ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਡਿਠਾ ਨਾਲਿ ਹਰਿ ਅੰਤਰਜਾਮੀ ਜਾਣੁ॥ 4॥4॥74॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 5:  Ghurri mohut ka paahouna kaaj svarunhaar(u). Maya kaam(i) viyapiya, sumjhaiy naahi gavaar(u).  Ootth(i) chulliya puchhutaaya puriya vus(i) jundaar. 1. Undhay, tu baittha kundhee paayay. Jay hovuyee poorub(i) likhiya ta Gur ka Buchun(u) kmaahay. 1. Ruhaou.  Hurie naahi, neh dudduri, puckee vudhanhaar. Laiy laiy daat puhoutiya, laavaiy kur(i) tuyeeyar(u). Ja hoa huqam(u) kirsaan da, ta loon(i) miniya khaytaar(u). 2. Pehla pehr(u) dhundhaiy guyya, doojaiy bhur(i) soyaa.  Teejaiy jhaakh jhkhaaya, chauthaiy bhor(u) bhuyya. Kud hee chit(i) na aayo jin(i) jeo pind(u) deeya. 3. Saadh sungat(i) kuou vaariya, jeo keeya koorbaan(u). Jis tay sojhee mun(i) puyee miliya Purakh(u) Soojaan(u). Nanak dittha naal(i) Hur(i), Untarjaami Jaan(u). 4.4.74
Word Meaning: Mohut = a very short period of time.  Paahouna = guest.  Svarunhaar = finish the task.  Kaam(i) = in lust.  Viyapiya = trapped; indulged.   Gavaar(u) = unrefined (uninformed).   Vus(i) = in the control.    Jundaar = unkind.     1.
Kundhee Paayay = near the bank of a river (which can cave in at any time).   Poorub(i) = predestined.  Buchan = Word (Shabad).    Kmaayay = deal in; act on.          Pause.
Dudduri = half-ripe.   Vudhanhaar = ready for cutting (harvesting).   Daat = sickles; a curved and toothed blade with a wooden handle to cut crops.  Laavaiy = farm helpers.  Kirsaan = farmer (landlord).  Loon(i) = cut; harvested.   Khaytaar = field.         2.
Pehr = one quarter of (night or life).   Dhundhaiy = routine work; daily affairs.   Jhaakh Jhkhaaya = fruitless conversation; enjoying sensual pleasures.  Bhor(u) = early morning; time of grey hair (old age).  Kud Hee Na = never.   Chit(i) = in mind or memory.  Pind(u) = body.    3.            Vaariya = offer life to sacrifice.   Jeo = mind.  Sojhee = enlightenment.  Purakh = (Waheguru).  Soojaan = Wise.  Dittha = saw; realized.  Untarjaami = omniscient.        4.  
MEANING:  (You do not realize that) your life is very short, like a tree near the bank of a river (which can cave in any time), oh, blind (ignorant) man!  However, those who are predestined act on the Guru’s advice (to make life worthwhile).         Pause.        (It is ridiculous) For a guest visiting for a short time, to start finishing the house he is visiting, as if he owned it.  Gripped in lust, the uninformed man does not realize that he is here on the earth as a visitor only.  Upon death, he repents his actions when he becomes a prisoner of the unkind (Angel of death).       1.          (Human life is like a crop) which can be cut whenever the landlord wants, regardless of whether it is still green, half-ripe, or fully ripe. On his orders, his farm helpers arrive at the field with sickles in hand, cut the entire field, and measure it.       2.
(After spending the day in his routine business of earning wealth), The man spends the first quarter of the night eating and chatting with the family, and enjoys deep sleep in the second quarter. He enjoys sensual pleasures in the third quarter, and then morning approaches in the fourth quarter (to start a new day).  Thus, He gets no time to remember Waheguru, who has given him the soul and the body.           3.            I would gladly offer my life to (in the service of) the Sadh-sangat (congregation of the holy devotees) where my mind was enlightened, and I became “one” with the Super-wise Waheguru.  Now I find the omniscient Waheguru always with me, oh Nanak.     4.4.74.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਸਭੇ ਗਲਾ ਵਿਸਰਨੁ ਇਕੋ ਵਿਸਰਿ ਨ ਜਾਉ॥ ਧੰਧਾ ਸਭੁ ਜਲਾਇ ਕੈ ਗੁਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਦੀਆ ਸਚੁ ਸੁਆਉ॥ ਆਸਾ ਸਭੇ ਲਾਹਿ ਕੈ ਇਕਾ ਆਸ ਕਮਾਉ॥ ਜਿਨੀ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਸੇਵਿਆ ਤਿਨ ਆਗੈ ਮਿਲਿਆ ਥਾਉ॥ 1॥ ਮਨ ਮੇਰੇ ਕਰਤੇ ਨੋ ਸਾਲਾਹਿ॥ ਸਭੇ ਛਡਿ ਸਿਆਣਪਾ ਗੁਰ ਕੀ ਪੈਰੀ ਪਾਹਿ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਦੁਖ ਭੁਖ ਨਹ ਵਿਆਪਈ ਜੇ ਸੁਖਦਾਤਾ ਮਨਿ ਹੋਇ॥ ਕਿਤ ਹੀ ਕੰਮਿ ਨ ਛਿਜੀਐ ਜਾ ਹਿਰਦੈ ਸਚਾ ਸੋਇ॥ ਜਿਸੁ ਤੂ ਰਖਹਿ ਹਥ ਦੇ ਤਿਸੁ ਮਾਰਿ ਨ ਸਕੈ ਕੋਇ॥ ਸੁਖਦਾਤਾ ਗੁਰੁ ਸੇਵੀਐ ਸਭਿ ਅਵਗਣ ਕਢੈ ਧੋਇ॥ 2॥ ਸੇਵਾ ਮੰਗੈ ਸੇਵਕੋ ਲਾਈਆ ਅਪੁਨੀ ਸੇਵ॥
