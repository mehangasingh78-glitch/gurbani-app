(ਅੰਗ 63)॥              ਮਨਮੁਖੁ ਜਾਣੈ ਆਪਣੇ ਧੀਆ ਪੂਤ ਸੰਜੋਗੁ॥ ਨਾਰੀ ਦੇਖਿ ਵਿਗਾਸੀਅਹਿ ਨਾਲੇ ਹਰਖੁ ਸੁ ਸੋਗੁ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਸਬਦਿ ਰੰਗਾਵਲੇ ਅਹਿਨਿਸਿ ਹਰਿ ਰਸੁ ਭੋਗੁ॥ 3॥ ਚਿਤੁ ਚਲੈ ਵਿਤੁ ਜਾਵਣੋ ਸਾਕਤ ਡੋਲਿ ਡੋਲਾਇ॥ ਬਾਹਰਿ ਢੂੰਢਿ ਵਿਗੁਚੀਐ ਘਰ ਮਹਿ ਵਸਤੁ ਸੁਥਾਇ॥ ਮਨਮੁਖਿ ਹਉਮੈ ਕਰਿ ਮੁਸੀ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਪਲੈ ਪਾਇ॥ 4॥ ਸਾਕਤ ਨਿਰਗੁਣਿਆਰਿਆ ਆਪਣਾ ਮੂਲੁ ਪਛਾਣੁ॥ ਰਕਤੁ ਬਿੰਦੁ ਕਾ ਇਹੁ ਤਨੋ ਅਗਨੀ ਪਾਸਿ ਪਿਰਾਣੁ॥ ਪਵਣੈ ਕੈ ਵਸਿ ਦੇਹੁਰੀ ਮਸਤਕਿ ਸਚੁ ਨੀਸਾਣੁ॥ 5॥ ਬਹੁਤਾ ਜੀਵਣੁ ਮੰਗੀਐ ਮੁਆ ਨ ਲੋੜੈ ਕੋਇ॥ ਸੁਖ ਜੀਵਣੁ ਤਿਸੁ ਆਖੀਐ ਜਿਸੁ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਵਸਿਆ ਸੋਇ॥ ਨਾਮ ਵਿਹੂਣੇ ਕਿਆ ਗਣੀ ਜਿਸੁ ਹਰਿ ਗੁਰ ਦਰਸੁ ਨ ਹੋਇ॥ 6॥ ਜਿਉ ਸੁਪਨੈ ਨਿਸਿ ਭੁਲੀਐ ਜਬ ਲਗ ਨਿਦ੍ਰਾ ਹੋਇ॥ ਇਉ ਸਰਪਨਿ ਕੈ ਵਸਿ ਜੀਅੜਾ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਹਉਮੈ ਦੋਇ॥ ਗੁਰਮਤਿ ਹੋਇ ਵੀਚਾਰੀਐ ਸੁਪਨਾ ਇਹੁ ਜਗੁ ਲੋਇ॥7॥ ਅਗਨਿ ਮਰੈ ਜਲੁ ਪਾਈਐ ਜਿਉ ਬਾਰਿਕ ਦੂਧੈ ਮਾਇ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਜਲ ਕਮਲ ਸੁ ਨਾ ਥੀਐ ਬਿਨੁ ਜਲ ਮੀਨੁ ਮਰਾਇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਹਰਿ ਰਸਿ ਮਿਲੈ ਜੀਵਾ ਹਰਿ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਇ॥ 8॥15॥

Word Meaning:  Chittay = artfully painted.    Dhaulhur = mansions.   Buggay = white- washed.   Bunk = beautiful; strong.     Oosaariya = built.   Doojay = the other (Maya).  Dheh Dhayri = collapse into a pile of rubble.    Chhaar(u) = ash.      1.
Nirmulo = clean; pure.    Daat(i) = gift.   Dayvunhaar(u) = the provider.    Aagaiy = next world; Waheguru’s Court.   Poochh = checking.    Bayli = friend.      2.

Chit(u) chullay = mind suffers depression.   Vit(u) = wealth.    Sakut = money - minded.   Doal(i) = weakening of mental strength.   Dhoondh(i) = search.   Vigoochiyaiy = suffer in vain.  Vust = the article (Waheguru’s Naam).  Soothayayn = the same place (his heart).  Moosi = deceived.   Pullaiy Paayay = acquires.       4.     Nirgoonaariya = devoid of noble qualities.   Mool(u) = origin; roots.   Rukt(u) = blood (egg of mom).  Bind(u) = sperm (of dad).   Ugni Paas(i) = in possession of fire (to burn the body).   Piraan(u) = understand (do not forget).  Vus(i) = in control.  Dayhoori = body.   Mustuk(i) = (on) forehead.   Neeshaan(u) = stamp (Huqam).       5.    Lorraiy = wishes.    Vihoonaiy = without; empty.   Gunni = consider.       6.
Nis(i) = (at) night.   Nindra = sleep.   Surpun(i) = a female snake used as a metaphor for Maya.  Doyay = prejudice; feelings of the higher and lower classes.   Loyay = world.      7.
Baarik = child.    Theeyaiy = happen; exist.   Meen(u) = fish.       8.   
MEANING:  Nothing – not even the body and the wealth – accompanies a man to the next world, oh my brothers. Only Waheguru’s Naam is the true wealth (that accompanies the man), which is gifted by the Guru by the benevolence of God.      Pause.
Artfully painted houses with beautiful white gates, which are built with great zest in the love for Maya, look very pretty from the outside.  But if they are empty inside, they collapse into rubble.   Just like such houses, if a mind is empty of the love for Waheguru, it turns into dust upon death.       1.            Just like God is pure, His Naam-wealth is also pure, and its bearer can easily become “one” with Him. But, Waheguru’s Naam can be acquired only by the grace of the Guru.   The bearers of Waheguru’s Naam go through no checking (scrutiny) in Waheguru’s Court, as the Guru and Waheguru are his friends (supporters). One can find salvation only if Waheguru Himself helps him, because He is the only one who is the source of such gifts.        2.         The follower-of- his-mind considers his daughters and sons to be his own, not realizing that he got them only because of his prewritten destiny (Will of Waheguru).  His mind blossoms to see his wife, but along with this joy, he also feels depressed because of the possible fear of losing them.  But the devotees of the Guru are always imbued in His Shabad, and enjoy the delicious food of Waheguru’s Naam (and never feel sad or overjoyed).           3.         When a money-minded man loses some wealth, he also loses peace of mind and his mental strength. He tries to find peace of mind in the outside world, and does not realize that the peace lies in his heart (in the form of Waheguru’s Naam), which is occupied with the desire for wealth (so, the Naam lies dormant in his heart).  The follower of his mind is defrauded by his haumein, whereas the devotee of the Guru acquires Waheguru’s Naam.       4.            Recognize your roots (origin), oh, devoid of noble qualities, and money-minded, man.  (You are conceited of your body, oh man), But remember, that it is made up of the egg of your mother and the sperm of your father (by Waheguru’s Huqam) and eventually has to burn down (die). It is the eternal Huqam of Waheguru on the forehead of every man that the body functions only as long as he breathes (which God controls).          5.
Everybody wishes for a long life, and nobody wants to die.  However, only that life is considered comfortable (worth living) which has taken Guru’s shelter, and which has made its mind a home for Waheguru to live in. (Without Waheguru’s Naam), an individual, who has not had a holy “sight” of the Guru and Waheguru, is not worth even as a statistic.        6.
Just as a sleeping man is misled by various situations in his dream, which lasts only for as long as his sleep, (and sees the reality upon waking up), a man who ponders on Guru’s advice, realizes that this world is also just like a dream (and not a reality).  Under the influence of Maya, the human mind considers this world to be real, as a result of which, he has both haumein and prejudice in his mind.     7.
Just as the fire of the appetite of a child is put out when his mother feeds him milk, the fire of desires is put out by the Naam-water. Just as a lotus does not live, and a fish dies without water, a Waheguru’s devotee cannot live without Him, and survives only when he sings praise of Waheguru, and enjoy its taste, oh Nanak.  8.15.  

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 1॥ ਡੂੰਗਰੁ ਦੇਖਿ ਡਰਾਵਣੋ ਪੇਈਅੜੈ ਡਰੀਆਸੁ॥ ਊਚਉ ਪਰਬਤੁ ਗਾਖੜੋ ਨਾ ਪਉੜੀ ਤਿਤੁ ਤਾਸੁ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਜਾਣਿਆ ਗੁਰਿ ਮੇਲੀ ਤਰੀਆਸੁ॥ 1॥ ਭਾਈ ਰੇ ਭਵਜਲੁ ਬਿਖਮੁ ਡਰਾਉ॥ ਪੂਰਾ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਰਸਿ ਮਿਲੈ ਗੁਰੁ ਤਾਰੇ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਉ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਚਲਾ ਚਲਾ ਜੇ ਕਰੀ ਜਾਣਾ ਚਲਣਹਾਰੁ॥ ਜੋ ਆਇਆ ਸੋ ਚਲਸੀ ਅਮਰੁ ਸੁ ਗੁਰੁ ਕਰਤਾਰੁ॥ਭੀ ਸਚਾ ਸਾਲਾਹਣਾ ਸਚੈ ਥਾਨਿ ਪਿਆਰੁ॥2॥ ਦਰ ਘਰ ਮਹਲਾ ਸੋਹਣੇ ਪਕੇ ਕੋਟ ਹਜਾਰ॥ ਹਸਤੀ ਘੋੜੇ ਪਾਖਰੇ ਲਸਕਰ ਲਖ ਅਪਾਰ॥ ਕਿਸ ਹੀ ਨਾਲਿ ਨਾ ਚਲਿਆ ਖਪਿ ਖਪਿ ਮੁਏ ਅਸਾਰ॥ 3॥ ਸੁਇਨਾ ਰੁਪਾ ਸੰਚੀਐ ਮਾਲੁ ਜਾਲੁ ਜੰਜਾਲੁ॥ ਸਭ ਜਗ ਮਹਿ ਦੋਹੀ ਫੇਰੀਐ ਬਿਨੁ ਨਾਵੈ ਸਿਰਿ ਕਾਲੁ॥ ਪਿੰਡੁ ਪੜੈ ਜੀਉ ਖੇਲਸੀ ਬਦਫੈਲੀ ਕਿਆ ਹਾਲ॥ 4॥ ਪੁਤਾ ਦੇਖਿ ਵਿਗਸੀਐ ਨਾਰੀ ਸੇਜ ਭਤਾਰ॥ ਚੋਆ ਚੰਦਨੁ ਲਾਈਐ ਕਾਪੜੁ ਰੂਪੁ ਸੀਗਾਰੁ॥ ਖੇਹੂ ਖੇਹ ਰਲਾਈਐ ਛੋਡਿ ਚਲੈ ਘਰ ਬਾਰੁ॥5॥ ਮਹਰ ਮਲੂਕ ਕਹਾਈਐ ਰਾਜਾ ਰਾਉ ਕਿ ਖਾਨੁ॥ ਚਉਧਰੀ ਰਾਉ ਸਦਾਈਐ ਜਲਿ ਬਲੀਐ ਅਭਿਮਾਨ॥ ਮਨਮੁਖਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਵਿਸਾਰਿਆ ਜਿਉ ਡਵਿ ਦਧਾ ਕਾਨੁ॥ 6॥ ਹਉਮੈ ਕਰਿ ਕਰਿ ਜਾਇਸੀ ਜੋ ਆਇਆ ਜਗ ਮਾਹਿ॥
