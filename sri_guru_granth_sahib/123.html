(ਅੰਗ 122)॥      ਮਾਇਆ ਮੋਹੁ ਇਸੁ ਮਨਹਿ ਨਚਾਏ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਕਪਟੁ ਦੁਖੁ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 4॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਭਗਤਿ ਜਾ ਆਪਿ ਕਰਾਏ॥ ਤਨੁ ਮਨੁ ਰਾਤਾ ਸਹਜਿ ਸੁਭਾਏ॥ ਬਾਣੀ ਵਜੈ ਸਬਦਿ ਵਜਾਏ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਭਗਤਿ ਥਾਇ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 5॥ ਬਹੁ ਤਾਲ ਪੂਰੈ ਵਾਜੇ ਵਜਾਏ॥ ਨਾ ਕੋ ਸੁਣੇ ਨ ਮੰਨਿ ਵਸਾਏ॥ ਮਾਇਆ ਕਾਰਣਿ ਪਿੜ ਬੰਧਿ ਨਾਚੈ ਦੂਜੈ ਭਾਇ ਦੁਖੁ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 6॥ ਜਿਸੁ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਿ ਲਗੈ ਸੋ ਮੁਕਤਾ॥ ਇੰਦ੍ਰੀ ਵਸਿ ਸਚ ਸੰਜਮਿ ਜੁਗਤਾ॥ ਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਸਬਦਿ ਸਦਾ ਹਰਿ ਧਿਆਏ ਏਹਾ ਭਗਤਿ ਹਰਿ ਭਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 7॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਭਗਤਿ ਜੁਗ ਚਾਰੇ ਹੋਈ॥ ਹੋਰਤਿ ਭਗਤਿ ਨ ਪਾਏ ਕੋਈ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮੁ ਗੁਰ ਭਗਤੀ ਪਾਈਐ ਗੁਰ ਚਰਣੀ ਚਿਤੁ ਲਾਵਣਿਆ॥  8॥20॥21॥
Majh Mehla 3:  Govind(u) oojul(u) oojul hunsa. Mun(u) Bani, nirmul mayri munsa. Mun(i) oojul suda mookh soheh(i) ut(i) oojul Naam(u) dhiyaavniya. 1. Huoun vaari jeeo vaari, Gobind goon gaavniya. Gobind(u) Gobind(u) kuhaiy din(u) raati, Gobind goon Shabad(i) soohaavniya.  1. Ruhaou.   Gobind(u) gaaveh(i) sehj(i) soobhaayay. Gur kaiy bhaiy oojul, haumein mul(u) jaayay. Suda anund(i) ruheh(i), bhugat(i) kureh(i) din(u) raati, soon(i) Gobind goon gaavniya.  2. Munooa naachaiy bhugat(i) drirraayay. Gur kaiy Shabad(i) munaiy mun(u) milaayay. Sacha taal(u) pooray, Maya moh(u) chookaayay, Shabaday nirt(i) kraavniya.  3. Oocha kookay tuneh(i) puchhaarraiy. Maya moh(i) johiya jum kaalay. 
(PAGE 122):       Maya moh(u) is(u) muneh(i) nuchaayay, untar(i) kupt(u) dookh(u) paavniya.  4. Gurmukh(i) bhagat(i) ja Aap(i) kuraayay. Tun(u) mun(u) raata sehj(i) soobhaayay. Bani vujaiy Shabad(i) vujaayay, Gurmukh(i) bhagat(i) thaayayn paavniya. 5.  Buhou taal pooraiy vaajay vujaayay. Na ko soonay, na munn(i) vusaayay. Maya kaarun(i) pirr bundh(i) naachaiy, doojaiy bhaayay dookh(u) paavniya. 6.  Jis(u) untar(i) preet(i) lugaiy so mookta. Inderi vus(i) sach sunjum(i) joogta. Gur kaiy Shabad(i) suda Hur(i) dhiyayay, eha bhagat(i) Hur(i) bhaavniya.  7. Gurmukh(i) bhagat(i) joog chaaray hoyee. Horut(u) bhagat(i) na paayay koyee. Nanak Naam(u) Gur bhugti paayiyaiy, Gur Churni chit(u) laavniya. 8.20.21.
Word Meaning: Govind(u) = the provider for the earth (Waheguru).    Oojul(u) = clean; pure.   Hunsa = human soul (mind).   Nirmul = clean; pure.    Munsa = wishes. 
Soheh(i) = dignified; respectful.   Ut(i) = extreme.      1.        Vaari = sacrifice.   Gobind = Waheguru.         Pause.      Sehj(i) Soobhaayay = (in) higher spiritual state and with love.   Anund(i) = in bliss.      2.     Munooa = mind.   Naachay = dances (in high spirits).  Drirraayay = reinforce.      Saacha = True.   Taal pooray = move steps synchronously.       Nirt(i) = dance.       3.      Oocha Kookay = make a high-pitched sound.   Tuneh(i) = (with, to) body.    Puchhaarray = hit against; push back.   Johiya = watched closely.   Jum Kaalay = angel of death.   Muneh(i) = mind.    Kupt = deceit.        4. Vujaiy = plays     Thaayayn paavniya = gets full value; is rewarded.         5. Vaajay vujaayay = plays musical instruments.      Pirr Bundh = build a (dancing) ring.  
Doojaiy Bhaayay = for the love of the other (Maya).         6.       Mookta = free from the bonds of Maya (is salvaged).   Inderi = senses.     Vus(i) = to control.   Sunjum(i) = efforts to control senses.  Joogta = technique.   Bhaavniya = is liked.      7. Joog Charay = the four accepted eons (Hindu belief).    Horut(u) = somewhere else.  
Gur Churni = Guru’s feet (His Shabad).          8.
MEANING:  Since God is pure, the individuals who serve Him (recite His Naam and obey Him) also become pure-minded (no evil thoughts originate there).  With the recitation of holy Bani, my mind has also been purified of its desires.  Those. Those who recite the Naam have extremely pure minds, and they look exalted everywhere.           1.           I am a sacrifice for those who sing the virtues of Waheguru.  They recite His Naam day and night, and look dignified by singing Waheguru’s praise by Guru’s Shabad.       Pause.        They recite Waheguru’s Naam with love and in a high spiritual state; with a love-filled fear of Waheguru, they rid themselves of their haumein and become pure in mind.  They worship Waheguru day and night, and sing and listen to His praise, and stay blissful.           2.          They reinforce Waheguru’s worship to the extent that their mind begins to dance (in bliss), and with Guru’s Shabad, they keep their mind under control so that it does not wander. Just like a professional dancer who synchronizes her moves, the devotee synchronizes his mind with the Naam, and his mind dances with the Shabad, whereby it overcomes his attraction for Maya.       3. The hypocritical devotees of gods worship them publicly by dancing, just to impress people. They actually are strongly attached to Maya, and the Angel of Death watches them closely.  They dance just for the love of Maya, recite mantras loudly, and fall to the ground (as if kneeling before the deity), all as a show-off to receive money.  But, because of deceit in their mind, they suffer badly at the end of the day.   4. On the other hand, the devotees of Waheguru worship Him when He inspires them. Their body and mind are imbued with His love, and their mind feels blissful. Guru’s Shabad, Guru’s Bani, produce heavenly music in their mind (they attain the state of blissfulness), as such worship of Waheguru in Guru’s shelter is appropriately rewarded by Waheguru.  5.  The hypocrites dance in synchronicity with music to please some gods, but nobody honestly listens to them or gives room to their mantras in their hearts.  For the love of money, the fake devotees dance among people who form a circle around them just to enjoy their dances, which they perform for the sake of money. Such fake devotees suffer deeply because of their love for money. 6.
But a devotee, who fosters love for God, finds salvation. He controls his sensual desires, and rather than putting in efforts to control sensual pleasures by mind’s sheer stubbornness, his efforts are directed to worship Waheguru.  He always recites Waheguru’s Naam with Guru’s Shabad, and this is the kind of worship which Waheguru likes (and approves).            7.          (Hindu Granths espouse that the worship of gods takes different forms in different eons) (But Guru Sahiban preach) that it does not matter what eon it is – the only kind of worship that Waheguru likes, is to recite Waheguru’s Naam in Guru’s shelter; and no other kind is acceptable.   Waheguru’s Naam can be realized only by taking Guru’s shelter and worshipping Him, oh Nanak!    8.20.21.
NOTE: Guru is a manifestation of Waheguru; so, Waheguru’s worship is the same as Guru’s worship.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ ਸਚਾ ਸੇਵੀ ਸਚੁ ਸਾਲਾਹੀ॥ ਸਚੈ ਨਾਇ ਦੁਖੁ ਕਬ ਹੀ ਨਾਹੀ॥ ਸੁਖਦਾਤਾ ਸੇਵਨਿ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਾਇਨਿ ਗੁਰਮਤਿ ਮੰਨਿ ਵਸਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਹਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਜੀਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਸੁਖ ਸਹਜਿ ਸਮਾਧਿ ਲਗਾਵਣਿਆ॥ ਜੋ ਹਰਿ ਸੇਵਹਿ ਸੇ ਸਦਾ ਸੋਹਹਿ ਸੋਭਾ ਸੁਰਤਿ ਸੁਹਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਸਭੁ ਕੋ ਤੇਰਾ ਭਗਤੁ ਕਹਾਏ॥ ਸੇਈ ਭਗਤ ਤੇਰੈ ਮਨਿ ਭਾਏ॥ ਸਚੁ ਬਾਣੀ ਤੁਧੈ ਸਾਲਾਹਨਿ ਰੰਗਿ ਰਾਤੇ ਭਗਤਿ ਕਰਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 2॥ ਸਭੁ ਕੋ ਸਚੇ ਹਰਿ ਜੀਉ ਤੇਰਾ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਮਿਲੈ ਤਾ ਚੂਕੈ ਫੇਰਾ॥ ਜਾ ਤੁਧੁ ਭਾਵੈ ਤਾ ਨਾਇ ਰਚਾਵਹਿ ਤੂੰ ਆਪੇ ਨਾਉ ਜਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 3॥ ਗੁਰਮਤੀ ਹਰਿ ਮੰਨਿ ਵਸਾਇਆ॥ ਹਰਖੁ ਸੋਗੁ ਸਭੁ ਮੋਹੁ ਗਵਾਇਆ॥ ਇਕਸੁ ਸਿਉ ਲਿਵ ਲਾਗੀ ਸਦ ਹੀ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਮੰਨਿ ਵਸਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 4॥ ਭਗਤ ਰੰਗਿ ਰਾਤੇ ਸਦਾ ਤੇਰੈ ਚਾਏ॥ ਨਉ ਨਿਧਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਵਸਿਆ ਮਨਿ ਆਏ॥ ਪੂਰੈ ਭਾਗਿ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਸਬਦੇ ਮੇਲਿ ਮਿਲਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 5॥ ਤੂੰ ਦਇਆਲੁ ਸਦਾ ਸੁਖਦਾਤਾ॥ ਤੂ ਆਪੇ ਮੇਲਹਿ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਜਾਤਾ॥ ਤੂੰ ਆਪੇ ਦੇਵਹਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਵਡਾਈ ਨਾਮਿ ਰਤੇ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 6॥ ਸਦਾ ਸਦਾ ਸਾਚੇ ਤੁਧੁ ਸਾਲਾਹੀ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਜਾਤਾ ਦੂਜਾ ਕੋ ਨਾਹੀ॥ ਏਕਸੁ ਸਿਉ ਮਨੁ ਰਹਿਆ ਸਮਾਏ ਮਨਿ ਮੰਨਿਐ ਮਨਹਿ ਮਿਲਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 7॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਹੋਵੈ ਸੋ ਸਾਲਾਹੇ॥ ਸਾਚੇ ਠਾਕੁਰ ਵੇਪਰਵਾਹੇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮੁ ਵਸੈ ਮਨ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਗੁਰ ਸਬਦੀ ਹਰਿ ਮੇਲਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 8॥21॥22॥
Majh Mehla 3:  Sacha sayveen Sach(u) salaaheen. Sachaiy Naayay dookh(u) kub hee naahi. Sookhdaata sayvun(i) sookh(u) paayin, Gurmut(i) munn(i) vusaavniya.  1. Huoun vaari jeeo vaari, sookh sehj(i) smaadh(i) lugaavniya.  Jo Hur(i) saveh(i) say suda soheh(i), sobha soorut(i) soohaavniya. 1. Ruhaou.   Subh(u) ko Tayra bhugat(u) kuhaayay. Sayee bhugat Tayraiy mun(i) bhaayay. Sach(u) Bani Toodhaiy sallahun(i), rung(i) raatay bhugat(i) kraavniya. 2. Subh(u) ko Sachay Hur(i) jeeo Tayra. Gurmukh(i) milaiy ta chookaiy phayra. Ja Toodh(u) bhaavaiy ta Naayayn ruchaveh(i) Tu Aapay Naoun jupaavniya.  3. Gurmuti Hur(i) munn(i) vusaaya. Hurkh(u) soag(u) subh(u) moh(u) guvaaya. Ikkus(u) siyoun liv laagi sud hee, Hur(i) Naam(u) munn(i) vusaavniya. 4. Bhugat rung(i) raatay suda Tayraiy chaayay. Nau nidh(i) Naam(u) vusiya mun(i) aayay. Pooraiy bhaag(i) Satgur(u) paaya, Shabaday mayl(i) milaavniya. 5.  Tu Duyyal(u) suda sookhdaata. Tu Aapay mayleh(i) Gurmukh(i) jaata. Tu Aapay dayveh(i) Naam(u) vudayee, Naam(i) ruttay sookh(u) paavniya.  6. Suda, suda Sachay Toodh(u) salaaheen. Gurmukh(i) jaata dooja ko naahi. Aikus(u) siyoun mun(u) ruhiya smaayay, mun(i) munniyaiyn muneh(i) milaavniya. 7. Gurmukh(i) hovaiy so salaahay. Sachay Tthaakur(u) vaypervaahay. Nanak Naam(u) vusaiy mun untar(i), Gur Shabadi Hur(i) maylaavniya.   8.21.22. 
Word Meaning:   Sacha = the eternal (Waheguru).  Sayveen = (I) dedicate myself.  Salaaheen = (I) sing praise.   Sayvun(i) = serve (obey Command).    Munn(i) Vusaavniya = is embedded in the mind.      1.      Sehj(i) Smaadh(i) = concentrate with a peaceful mind.     Soheh(i) = looks respectable.  Soorut(i) = attention; concentration.      Pause.       Sach(u) Bani = eternal Shabad (Waheguru’s praise).  Toodhay = You (Waheguru).   Rung(i) Raatay = imbued in love.        2. Chookay = ends.    Phayra = rebirth.      Naayayn Ruchaveh(i) = get them connected with Naam.     Jupaavniya = make them recite.      3.     Hurkh(u) = joy; happiness.   Soag = sadness; grief.   Ikkus(u) = only One (God).   Liv = concentration of mind.      4. Chaayay = zest; gusto.   Nau Nidh(i) = the nine mythical treasures.    5. Duyyal(u) = kind.     Jaata = recognized.     Vudayee = praise; respect.       6. Aikus siyoun = just with One (God).      Smaayay = absorbed.    Munniyaiyn = convinced; satisfied.     7.        Tthaakur(u) = the Lord.   Vaypervaahay = carefree; indifferent.        8.
MEANING: I serve (obey His Huqam), and sing praise of the eternal Waheguru.  One never suffers if he keeps Waheguru’s Naam in mind.  Those who serve Waheguru, which is the source of all comfort, and embed His Naam in their mind by Guru’s grace, enjoy comfort.      1.        I offer my life (services) to those who concentrate on Waheguru’s Naam and attain the blissful state of mind in which Maya has no effect.  Those who serve Waheguru with full dedication are respected and praised.      Pause.
Everybody calls himself Your devotee, but only those are the real devotees who accept Your Will, oh Waheguru!  They sing Your praise by the eternal Bani, and imbued in Your love, recite Your Naam.         2.  Everybody is Yours (has been created by You), but they merge into You only by the grace of the Guru; at which time, their reincarnation comes to an end.  You inspire some select people whom You like to recite Your Naam.         3. They embed Your Naam in their mind by the advice of the Guru, and get rid of their overjoy, sadness, and worldly attachments.  With only Your Naam enshrined in their minds, they always remain focused on Your Naam (connected to You), oh Waheguru.        4. Imbued in Your love, Your devotees are always zestful. Your Naam, which is like acquiring all the nine mythical treasures, gets enshrined in their heart.  Only the highly fortunate get connected with the Guru, and then get connected with You by the Guru’s Shabad, oh Waheguru. 5.
You are always very kind, and a source of all comforts, and connect some fortunate individuals with the Guru so that they may recognize You (connect with You).  You, Yourself, honour people for reciting Your Naam, and those who are imbued in Your Naam, enjoy blissfulness, oh Waheguru!           6. I wish I could sing Your praise all the time. By Guru’s grace, I have realized that there is none other like You. When one’s mind is focused only on Waheguru, he is convinced about Him being the Supreme power, and his soul gets united with Him. 7.
One sings praise of Waheguru, who is otherwise carefree (indifferent). only in the shelter of the Guru.   With Guru’s Shabad, Waheguru’s Naam gets embedded in one’s mind, and he becomes “one” with Him, oh Nanak.    8.21.22
