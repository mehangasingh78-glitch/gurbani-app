(ਅੰਗ 81)॥    ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਹਰਿ ਪੀਵਤੇ ਸਦਾ ਥਿਰੁ ਥੀਵਤੇ ਬਿਖੈ ਬਨੁ ਫੀਕਾ ਜਾਨਿਆ॥ ਭਏ ਕਿਰਪਾਲ ਗੋਪਾਲ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਮੇਰੇ ਸਾਧਸੰਗਤਿ ਨਿਧਿ ਮਾਨਿਆ॥ ਸਰਬਸੋ ਸੂਖ ਆਨੰਦ ਘਨ ਪਿਆਰੇ ਹਰਿ ਰਤਨੁ ਮਨ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਸੀਵਤੇ॥ ਇਕੁ ਤਿਲੁ ਨਹੀ ਵਿਸਰੈ ਪ੍ਰਾਨ ਆਧਾਰਾ ਜਪਿ ਜਪਿ ਨਾਨਕ ਜੀਵਤੇ॥ 3॥
(Sloak) Dukkhna:  Dhoorri mujun(u) Sadh khay, Saayeen theeyay kirpal. Luddhay hubhay thoakrro, Nanak Hur(i) dhun(u) maal.    1.
Word Meaning:   Dhoorri = dust (of the feet).   Mujun = bath.   Khay = of.   Saayeen = Lord.   Theeyay = became.  Kirpal = kind.   Luddhay = found; acquired.  Hubbay = all.   Thoakrro = articles (of use).   Maal = wealth. 
MEANING:   God becomes very kind when one takes a bath in the dust of Satguru’s Feet (obeys His advice).  As a result, one acquires everything useful to him because the real wealth is Waheguru’s Naam.  1.
Chhunt(u):  Soondar Soami dhaam, bhugtah bisraam, aasa lug(i) jeevtay jeeo. Mun(i) tunay gultaan, simrut Prubh Naam, Hur(i) Amrit(u) peevtay jeeo. 
(PAGE 81):          Amrit Hur(i) peevtay suda thir(u) theevtay bikhaiy bun(u) feeka jaaniya. Bhuyay kirpal, Gopal Prubh mayray, Sadh sungat(i) nidh(i) maaniya.  Serbso sookh aanund ghun piyaray, Hur(i) ruttan(u) mun untar(i) seevtay. Ikk(u) til(u) nuhee visraiy praan aadhara jup(i) jup(i) Nanak jeevtay. 
Word Meaning:  Soami = God.   Dhaam = residence.    Bisraam = peace.  Aasa = hope; faith.     Gultaan = inebriated.   Suda Thir(u) = never changing; immortal.  Theevtay = became.      Bikhaiy = of poison (Maya).     Bun(u) = forest; world.  Feeka = tasteless.   Gopal = God.  Nidh(i) = Treasure (God).  Serbso = all.   Ghun = very much.   Til(u) = (the size of a) sesame seed (tiny amount of time).      3.
MEANING:  The devotees find peace of mind while reciting Waheguru’s Naam, and live with the faith that they will be “one” with Him who has a beautiful Residence.  Remembering Waheguru’s Naam, they drink the life-sustaining Amrit and feel inebriated in mind and body. By drinking Amrit of Waheguru’s Naam, they find the world of Maya to be tasteless, and as a result, they attain emancipation and become immortal.  With the grace of the kind Waheguru, one connects with Satguru and realizes Waheguru, the treasure of everything.   Such individuals, stich precious Waheguru’s Naam in their minds, thereby acquiring great comfort and bliss.  They do not forget Waheguru even for a tiny moment of time, and live by reciting the Naam of Waheguru which is the basis of their life, oh Nanak.  3.

ਡਖਣਾ॥ ਜੋ ਤਉ ਕੀਨੇ ਆਪਣੇ ਤਿਨਾ ਕੂੰ ਮਿਲਿਓਹਿ॥ ਆਪੇ ਹੀ ਆਪਿ ਮੋਹਿਓਹਿ ਜਸੁ ਨਾਨਕ ਆਪਿ ਸੁਣਿਓਹਿ॥ 1॥ ਛੰਤੁ॥ ਪ੍ਰੇਮ ਠਗਉਰੀ ਪਾਇ ਰੀਝਾਇ ਗੋਬਿੰਦ ਮਨੁ ਮੋਹਿਆ ਜੀਉ॥ ਸੰਤਨ ਕੈ ਪ੍ਰਸਾਦਿ ਅਗਾਧਿ ਕੰਠੇ ਲਗਿ ਸੋਹਿਆ ਜੀਉ॥ ਹਰਿ ਕਿੰਠ ਲਗਿ ਸੋਹਿਆ ਦੋਖ ਸਭਿ ਜੋਹਿਆ ਭਗਤ ਲਖ੍ਹਣ ਕਰਿ ਵਸਿ ਭਏ॥ ਮਨਿ ਸਰਬ ਸੁਖ ਵੁਠੇ ਗੋਵਿਦ ਤੁਠੇ ਜਨਮ ਮਰਣਾ ਸਭਿ ਮਿਟਿ ਗਇਆ॥ ਸਖੀ ਮੰਗਲੋ ਗਾਇਆ ਇਛ ਪੁਜਾਇਆ ਬਹੁੜਿ ਨ ਮਾਇਆ ਹੋਹਿਆ॥ ਕਰੁ ਗਹਿ ਲੀਨੇ ਨਾਨਕ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਪਿਆਰੇ ਸੰਸਾਰੁ ਸਾਗਰੁ ਨਹੀ ਪੋਹਿਆ॥ 4॥
(Sloak) Dukkhna:  Jo Tuou keenay aapnay, tinha koon miliohay. Aapay hee Aap(i) mohiyo, jus(u) Nanak Aap(i) sooniohay.  1.
Word Meaning:  Koon = to; with.   Mohiohay = captivated.   Jus(u) = praise.  1.
MEANING:  God connects with them whom he has made His own. (Being present in everyone) He captivates Himself (other living beings) and listens to His own praise. 
Chhunt(u): Praym ttugaouri paayay reejhaayay, Gobind mun(u) mohiya jeeo. Suntun kaiy persaad(i), aghaad(i), kuntthay lug(i) sohiya jeeo. Hur(i) kuntth(i) lug(i) sohiya, doakh subh(i) johiya, bhagat lukhan kur(i) vus(i) bhuyay. Mun(i) serb sookh vootthay, Govind tootthay, junum merna subh(i) mit(i) guyya. Sukheen mungalo gaaya, ichh poojaaya, bahoorr(i) na Maya hoya. Ker(u) geh(i) leenay, Nanak Prubh piyaray, sunsaar saagar nuhee pohiya.     4.
Word Meaning:  Tthugaouri = charm.   Reejhaayay = charms.   Mohiya = enchanted.  Aghaadh(i) = beyond reach.   Kuntth = neck.   Sohiya = looks pretty.  Doakh = opposing (the five demons).  Johiya = warned.   Lukhan = qualities.  Vus(i) = controlled.    Vootthay = take residence.   Govind = Krishna = Waheguru.  Tootthay = pleased to be kind.   Sukheen = female friends (senses).   Mungalo = song of happiness.  Ichh = wish.  Bahoorr(i) = again.  Kur(u) = hand.  Geh(i) = by holding.   Na Pohiya = do not even come close. 
MEANING:  Waheguru enchants our mind by captivating us with the charm of His Love. By the kindness of His beloved devotees, the otherwise unreachable Waheguru hugs His devotees to the neck, and they look very pretty (exalted) (because of their virtues). When a devotee is adorned (honoured) by being hugged by Waheguru, all his opposing demons (evil qualities) are warned (to stay away) – actually, they come under control because of one’s newly acquired noble qualities. With Waheguru pleased, His kindness causes all comforts to settle in the devotee’s heart, and his birth-death cycle comes to an end.  All the wishes of the devotee are fulfilled, and he feels as if all his senses are singing a song of happiness; and he is not deceived by Maya again.  Dear Waheguru holds the devotee by the hand, and the sea of Maya does not affect him at all.     4.

ਡਖਣਾ॥ ਸਾਈ ਨਾਮੁ ਅਮੋਲੁ ਕੀਮ ਨ ਕੋਈ ਜਾਣਦੋ॥ ਜਿਨਾ ਭਾਗ ਮਥਾਹਿ ਸੇ ਨਾਨਕ ਹਰਿ ਰੰਗੁ ਮਾਣਦੋ॥ 1॥         ਛੰਤੁ॥ ਕਹਤੇ ਪਵਿਤ੍ਰ ਸੁਣਤੇ ਸਭਿ ਧੰਨੁ ਲਿਖਤੀ ਕੁਲੁ ਤਾਰਿਆ ਜੀਉ॥ ਜਿਨ ਕਉ ਸਾਧੂ ਸੰਗੁ ਨਾਮ ਹਰਿ ਰੰਗੁ ਤਿਨੀ ਬ੍ਰਹਮੁ ਬੀਚਾਰਿਆ ਜੀਉ॥ ਬ੍ਰਹਮੁ ਬੀਚਾਰਿਆ ਜਨਮੁ ਸਵਾਰਿਆ ਪੂਰਨ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਪ੍ਰਭਿ ਕਰੀ॥ ਕਰੁ ਗਹਿ ਲੀਨੇ ਹਰਿ ਜਸੋ ਦੀਨੇ ਜੋਨਿ ਨਾ ਧਾਵੈ ਨਹ ਮਰੀ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਦਇਆਲ ਕਿਰਪਾਲ ਭੇਟਤ ਹਰੇ ਕਾਮੁ ਕ੍ਰੋਧੁ ਲੋਭੁ ਮਾਰਿਆ॥ ਕਥਨੁ ਨ ਜਾਇ ਅਕਥੁ ਸੁਆਮੀ ਸਦਕੈ ਜਾਇ ਨਾਨਕੁ ਵਾਰਿਆ॥ 5॥1॥3॥
(Sloak) Dukkhna:  Saayeen Naam amoal(u), keem na koyee jaando.  Jinnah bhaag muthahay, say Nanak Hur(i) rung(u) maando. 1.
Word Meaning: Saayeen = Lord.    Amoal = invaluable.   Keem = value.   Bhaag = luck.   Muthahay = on the forehead.  Rung(u) = love.   Maando = enjoy.   1.
MEANING:  Waheguru’s Naam is invaluable, and nobody knows its true worth.  The individuals who have good fortune on their forehead enjoy His love (but even they do not know the worth of His Naam). 
Chhunt(u) : Kehtay puvittar soontay subh(i), dhunn(u) likhteen kool(u) taariya jeeo. Jin kuou Sadhu sung(u), Naam Hur(i) rung(u), tinhi Braham beechaariya jeeo. Braham beechaariya, junum(u) suvaariya, poorun kirpa Prubh(i) kurie. Ker(u) geh(i) leenay Hur(i) juso deenay,  joan(i) na dhaavaiy, neh murie. Satguru duyaal kirpal bhaytut Huray, kaam(u) krodh(u) loabh(u) maariya. Kuthun na jaayay Akuth(u) Soami, sudkaiy jaayay Nanak(u) vaariya. 
Word Meaning:  Puvittar = pious.   Dhunn(u) = worthy of respect.   Likhteen = writers.   Kool(u) = clan (devotees around).    Sadhu Sung(i) = company of the Guru.  Braham = Waheguru.  Svaariya = made successful.   Poorun = full.   Kur(u) = hand.  Geh(i) = by holding.   Juso = praise.    Joan(i) = rebirth.   Bhaytut = meeting; connection.  Kaam(u) = lust.   Kuthna = describe.  Akuth = indescribable = Waheguru.      5.
MEANING: The devotees who recite, and all those who listen to Waheguru’s Naam, are pious (holy); and those who write His praise are worthy of respect. Not only do they find salvation, but they salvage their whole clan (those who come in contact with them) too. Those who stay in the company (shelter) of the Guru enjoy Waheguru’s love and ponder on Waheguru Naam (make a connection with Him).  Because of their connection with Waheguru and His kindness, they make their lives a success. Waheguru holds their hand, and gifts His praise (Naam) to them, as a result of which they (become “one” with Waheguru), and never suffer death again, and do not have to take birth again.  The kind and the munificent connect the devotees with God, and their lust, rage, and greed are eradicated. Nothing can be said about the indescribable Waheguru (no one can say anything); I am a sacrifice to Waheguru, oh Nanak.   5.1.3.

ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਉਤਮੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਹੈ ਜਿਨਿ ਸਿਰਿਆ ਸਭੁ ਕੋਇ ਜੀਉ॥ ਹਰਿ ਜੀਅ ਸਭੇ ਪ੍ਰਤਿਪਾਲਦਾ ਘਟਿ ਘਟਿ ਰਮਈਆ ਸੋਇ॥ ਸੋ ਹਰਿ ਸਦਾ ਧਿਆਈਐ ਤਿਸੁ ਬਿਨੁ ਅਵਰੁ ਨ ਕੋਇ॥ ਜੋ ਮੋਹਿ ਮਾਇਆ ਚਿਤੁ ਲਾਇਦੇ ਸੇ ਛੋਡਿ ਚਲੇ ਦੁਖੁ ਰੋਇ॥ ਜਨ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮੁ ਧਿਆਇਆ ਹਰਿ ਅੰਤਿ ਸਖਾਈ ਹੋਇ॥ 1॥ ਮੈ ਹਰਿ ਬਿਨੁ ਅਵਰੁ ਨ ਕੋਇ॥ ਹਰਿ ਗੁਰ ਸਰਣਾਈ ਪਾਈਐ ਵਣਜਾਰਿਆ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਵਡਭਾਗਿ ਪਰਾਪਤਿ ਹੋਇ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥
