(ਅੰਗ 48)॥     ਐਥੈ ਮਿਲਹਿ ਵਡਾਈਆ ਦਰਗਹਿ ਪਾਵਹਿ ਥਾਉ॥ 3॥ ਕਰੇ ਕਰਾਏ ਆਪਿ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਸਭੁ ਕਿਛੁ ਤਿਸ ਹੀ ਹਾਥਿ॥ ਮਾਰਿ ਆਪੇ ਜੀਵਾਲਦਾ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਬਾਹਰਿ ਸਾਥਿ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਸਰਣਾਗਤੀ ਸਰਬ ਘਟਾ ਕੇ ਨਾਥ॥ 4॥15॥85॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 5:  Mayra tun(u) ur(u) dhun(u) mayra, raj roop mein days(u).  Soot, daara, bunita anayk, buhout(u) rung ur(u) vays. Hur(i) Naam(u) ridaiy na vusyee, kaaruj(i) kitay na laykh(i). 1. Mayray mun, Hur(i) Hur(i) Naam(u) dhiyayay. Kur(i) sungat(i) nit Sadh kee Gur churni chit(u) laayay. 1. Pause.  Naam(u) nidhaan(u) dhiyayiyaiy mustuk(i) hovaiy bhaag(u). Kaaruj subh(i) svaariyeh(i) Gur kee churni laag(u). Haumein roag bherm(u) kuttiyaiy na aavaiy na jaag(u). 2.. Kur(i) sungat(i) tu Sadh kee utth-sutth(i) teeruth Naaoun. Jeo praan mun(u) tun(u) huray sacha eho sooaao. 
(PAGE 48):            Aithaiy mileh(i) vudaayiyaan Durgeh(i) paaveh(i) thaaoun. 3. Kuray kurayay aap(i) Prubh(u), subh(u) kichh(u) Tis(u) hee haath(i).  Maar(i) Aapay jeevalda untar(i) baahur(i) saath(i).  Nanak Prubh(u) shernaagti, serb ghutaan kay Naath. 4.15.85. 
Word Meaning:   Soot = sons.   Dara = Bunita = wife.   Rung = colours (enjoyments). 
Vays = dresses.   Kaaruj(i) = of some use.   Laykh(i) = in account (credit).       1.
Dhiyayay = recite; remember.    Sadh = Guru.    Chit(u) = mind.       Pause.
Nidhaan(u) = treasure.    Mustuk(i) = on the forehead.     Bhaag(u) = fortune.    Kaaruj  = tasks.   Svaariyeh(i) = complete with a nice finish.     Bherm(u) = doubts; non-clarity in mind.    Aavaiy = comes.   Jaag(u) = goes.          2.         Sadh = Guru.   Utth-sutth(i)  Teeruth = the sixty-eight holy places of the Hindus.    Jeo Praan = life.   Huray = green (healthy).  Sooaao = taste; objective (of life).   Aithaiy = in this world.   Durgeh(i) = Waheguru’s Court.   Thaaon = is seated (honoured).          3.           Haath(i) = in His hand (control).  Shernaagti = in shelter.  Ghutaan = minds.  Naath = master; Lord.    4.
MEANING:  Worship Waheguru’s Naam in the company  (shelter) of the Guru, and focus on His Feet (His Shabad), oh my mind.        Pause.           (The ignorant) A man thinks that he owns his body, mind, wealth, the state, his good looks, and the whole country.  He also believes that he owns his sons, his wife, and various kinds of clothes, and that he enjoys many kinds of pleasures. What he does not realize is that if he has not fostered Waheguru’s Naam in his heart, all these things are worthless, and of no account (consideration) (in Waheguru’s Court).         1.           One should worship the treasure of Naam (God), so as to have good fortune on his forehead for his future, and one should seek the Guru’s shelter to achieve his important objective (to be united with Waheguru). When one worships Waheguru, all his doubts and sufferings are gone, and he does not come and go in this world (is not reincarnated).         2.          Come to the shelter of the Guru to enjoy His Company, and you will not need to take a dip in any of the sixty-eight Hindu holy places, oh my friend.  (In Guru’s company), your soul, mind, and body, will all be healthy (will be purified), which is the real objective of life.  You will earn praise in this world and will be given a seat (will be honoured) in Waheguru’s Court.        3.     Waheguru controls everything; He does, and gets things done by others, without any assistance from anybody else.  He is with us inside and outside, and it is He who gives and takes life. I seek the shelter of Waheguru, who is the master of all hearts, oh Nanak.   4.15.85.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥

 ਸਰਣਿ ਪਏ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਆਪਣੇ ਗੁਰੁ ਹੋਆ ਕਿਰਪਾਲੁ॥ ਸਤਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਉਪਦੇਸਿਐ ਬਿਨਸੇ ਸਰਬ ਜੰਜਾਲ॥ ਅੰਦਰੁ ਲਗਾ ਰਾਮ ਨਾਮਿ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਨਦਰਿ ਨਿਹਾਲੁ॥ 1॥ ਮਨ ਮੇਰੇ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਸੇਵਾ ਸਾਰੁ॥ ਕਰੇ ਦਇਆ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਆਪਣੀ ਇਕ ਨਿਮਖ ਨ ਮਨਹੁ ਵਿਸਾਰੁ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਗੁਣ ਗੋਵਿੰਦ ਨਿਤ ਗਾਵੀਅਹਿ ਅਵਗੁਣ ਕਟਣਹਾਰ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਨ ਸੁਖੁ ਹੋਇ ਕਰਿ ਡਿਠੇ ਬਿਸਥਾਰ॥ ਸਹਜੇ ਸਿਫਤੀ ਰਤਿਆ ਭਵਜਲੁ ਉਤਰੇ ਪਾਰਿ॥ 2॥ ਤੀਰਥ ਵਰਤ ਲਖ ਸੰਜਮਾ ਪਾਈਐ ਸਾਧੂ ਧੂਰਿ॥ ਲੂਕਿ ਕਮਾਵੈ ਕਿਸ ਤੇ ਜਾ ਵੇਖੈ ਸਦਾ ਹਦੂਰਿ॥ ਥਾਨ ਥਨੰਤਰਿ ਰਵਿ ਰਹਿਆ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਮੇਰਾ ਭਰਪੂਰਿ॥ 3॥ ਸਚੁ ਪਾਤਿਸਾਹੀ ਅਮਰੁ ਸਚੁ ਸਚੇ ਸਚਾ ਥਾਨੁ॥ ਸਚੀ ਕੁਦਰਤਿ ਧਾਰੀਅਨੁ ਸਚਿ ਸਿਰਜਿਓਨੁ ਜਹਾਨੁ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਜਪੀਐ ਸਚੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਹਉ ਸਦਾ ਸਦਾ ਕੁਰਬਾਨੁ॥  4॥16॥86॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 5:  Shern(i) puyay Prubh aapnay, Gur(u) hoa kirpal(u). Satgur kaiy oopdaysiyaiyn, binsay serb junjaal. Undar(u) lugga Ram Naam(i), Amrit nedr(i) nihaal(u). 1. Mun mayray, Satgur sayva saar(u). Kuray duyya Prubh aapni, ikk nimkh na munhou visaar(u). 1. Ruhaou. Goon Govind nit gaaviyay uvgoon kuttunhaar. Bin(u) Hur(i) Naam(u) na sookh(u) hoyay, kur(i) ditthay bisthaar. Sehjay sifteen ruttiyaan bhuvjul(u) ootray paar(i). 2. Teeruth, vert, lukh sunjma, paayiyaiy Sadhu dhoor(i). Look(i) kmaavaiy kis tay, ja vaykhaiy suda hudoor(i). Thaan thnuntar(i) ruv(i) ruhiya, Prubh(u) mayra bhurpoor(i). 3. Sach(u) paatshaahi, amur(u) Sach(u), Sachay Sacha thaan(u). Suchi koodrut(i) dhaariun, Such(i) sirjioun jahaan(u).  Nanak jupiyaiy Sach(u) Naam(u) huoun suda suda koorbaan(u). 4.16.86.
Word Meaning:  Shern(i) = shelter.    Kirpal(u) = kind.   Oopdaysiyaiyn = with Teachings.   Binsay = vanish.   Serb = all.    Junjaal = responsibilities rooted in Maya.    Amrit Nedr(i) = sweet and kind sight.   Nihaal(u) = blissful.       1.             Saar(u) = supreme.    Duyya = kindness.   Nimkh = a short moment.              Pause.          Govind = God.   Uvgoon = bad traits.  Ditthay = saw; found out.  Bisthhar =  developments; achieve many things.  Sehjay = with a peaceful mind.  Ruttiyaan = imbued.  Bhuvjul(u) = sea of life.  Ootray Paar(i) = landed on the other side; swam across (salvaged).        2.          Vert = vows to undergo bodily hardships to please some god.    Sunjma = control over sensual pleasures.    Look(i) = secretly.   Hudoor(i) = (present) close by.  Thaan Thnuntar = everywhere.  Ruv(i) = pervades (present).  Bhurpoor(i) = filling the whole space.          3.
Sach(u) = True (everlasting).   Amur(u) = rule; command.   Koodrut(i) = nature.   Dhaariun = is supporting.   Sirjioun = He created.   Koorbaan(u) = sacrifice.     4.  
MEANING:  Service to the Guru (obeying His Teachings) is supreme, oh my mind.  I pray to Waheguru that, with His kindness, I should not lose Him from my mind for even a single moment.           Pause.            With His kindness, I took shelter of the Guru, whose Teachings (my mind was enlightened), and all the bonds of Maya were broken.  My mind got connected with Waheguru, and with His sweet and kind sight, I felt blessed.         1.
We should sing Waheguru’s praise all the time, who destroys all our ill qualities. I have achieved many things with money, but I have found that there is no real comfort without Waheguru’s Naam.  One can easily swim across the sea of Maya when one’s wandering mind finds peace and is imbued with Waheguru’s praise.        2. 
When one overcomes his ego to become humble like the dust of the Guru’s feet (shows deep respect and obeys Him), one does not need any pilgrimage, undertakings of any hardships, or thousands of tough body controls.  God is present everywhere, and is filling the whole space; so how can a man commit some misdeed when Waheguru is watching him all the time from close by?            3.             Waheguru’s Kingship is eternal, and so are His Huqam (Command, or Will), and His Throne.  The nature He is supporting is also true (eternal), and the eternal Waheguru has created the whole universe.  We should recite His Naam all the time, and I am always a sacrifice for Him, oh Nanak.    4.16.86.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ 

ਉਦਮੁ ਕਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਜਾਪਣਾ ਵਡਭਾਗੀ ਧਨੁ ਖਾਟਿ॥ ਸੰਤ ਸੰਗਿ ਹਰਿ ਸਿਮਰਣਾ ਮਲੁ ਜਨਮ ਜਨਮ ਕੀ ਕਾਟਿ॥ 1॥ ਮਨ ਮੇਰੇ ਰਾਮ ਨਾਮੁ ਜਪਿ ਜਾਪੁ॥ ਮਨ ਇਛੇ ਫਲ ਭੁੰਚਿ ਤੂ ਸਭੁ ਚੂਕੈ ਸੋਗੁ ਸੰਤਾਪੁ॥1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਜਿਸੁ ਕਾਰਣਿ ਤਨੁ ਧਾਰਿਆ ਸੋ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਡਿਠਾ ਨਾਲਿ॥ ਜਲਿ ਥਲਿ ਮਹੀਅਲਿ ਪੂਰਿਆ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਆਪਣੀ ਨਦਰਿ ਨਿਹਾਲਿ॥ 2॥ ਮਨੁ ਤਨੁ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਹੋਇਆ ਲਾਗੀ ਸਾਚੁ ਪਰੀਤਿ॥ ਚਰਨ ਭਜੇ ਪਾਰਬ੍ਰਹਮ ਕੇ ਸਭਿ ਜਤ ਤਪ ਤਿਨ ਹੀ ਕੀਤਿ॥ 3॥ ਰਤਨ ਜਵੇਹਰ ਮਾਣਿਕਾ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਹਰਿ ਕਾ ਨਾਉ॥ ਸੁਖ ਸਹਜ ਆਨੰਦ ਰਸ ਜਨ ਨਾਨਕ ਹਰਿ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਉ॥ 4॥17॥87॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 5:  Oodum(u) kur(i) Hur(i) jaapna vudbhaagi dhun(u) khaat(i). Sunt sung(i) Hur(i) simerna mul(u) junum junum kee kaat(i). 1. Mun mayray, Ram Naam(u) jup(i) jaap(u). Mun ichhay ful bhoonch(i) tu subh(u) chookaiy soag(u) suntaap(u). 1. Ruhaou. Jis(u) kaarun(i) tun(u) dhariya, so Prubh(u) dittha naal(i). Jul(i) thul(i) muhhiul pooriya, Prubh(u) aapni nudr(i) nihaal(i). 2. Mun(u) tun(u) nirmul(u) hoya, laagi Saach(u) preet(i). Churn bhujay Parbraham kay, subh(i) jut, tup tin hee keet(i). 3. Ruttan juvayhur maanika Amrit(u) Hur(i) ka Naaoun. Sookh sehj aanund rus jun Nanak Hur(i) goon gaaoun.  4.17.87.
Word Meaning:  Oodum(u) = put in effort; take initiative.  Jaapna = recite (Naam).  Khaat(i) = earn.  Sunt = Guru.  Mul(u) = filth.        1.          Ichhay = desired.   Ful = results.    Bhoonch(i) = enjoy.    Chookay = dispel.   Soag = grief.   Suntaap = sufferings.       Pause.       Kaarun(i) = purpose; objective.   Dittha = saw; realized.     Muhhiul = sky.   Pooriya = filled; present everywhere.          2.              Nirmul(u) = clean; pure.    Bhujay = worshipped.   Jut = control on lust.   Tup = self-hardship (to please some god).   Keet(i) = did; practised.           3.
Ruttan = Juvayhur = Maanika = precious stones and pearls.     Sehj Aanund = bliss.
Rus = taste; enjoyment.     4.
MEANING:  Recite Waheguru’s Naam, oh my mind.  (When you do that) you will reap the desired results, and all your griefs and sufferings will be eradicated.         Pause. 
Make an effort to recite Waheguru’s Naam, and be fortunate to earn the wealth of His  Naam.  Recite Waheguru’s Naam in the company of the Guru, and all the filth of your mind accumulated over many, many incarnations, will be washed away.          1.
With the recitation of Waheguru’s Naam, the purpose for which man has been given the human life (to be “one” with Waheguru) is achieved, and you see Him (with the eyes of your mind) within you. (When you recite His Naam), Waheguru, which is present in water, on the land, and in the sky (meaning everywhere), casts His kind sight on you to “see” Him, and you feel blissful.          2.       When one gets attached to the eternal Waheguru, one’s mind and body get purified, and one does not need to recite some ritualistic mantras or suffer the hardships of forcibly controlling one’s sensual pleasures - the hardships that some people endure to please some god.           3.           Waheguru’s Naam is the real wealth, including precious stones, pearls, and other valuables. There are all sorts of comforts like peace of mind, blissfulness, and enjoyment of different tastes, in His Naam. So, Waheguru’s devotee, Nanak, affectionately recites His Naam.    4.17.87.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥

ਸੋਈ ਸਾਸਤੁ ਸਉਣੁ ਸੋਇ ਜਿਤੁ ਜਪੀਐ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਉ॥ ਚਰਨ ਕਮਲੁ ਗੁਰਿ ਧਨੁ ਦੀਆ ਮਿਲਿਆ ਨਿਥਾਵੇ ਥਾਉ॥ ਸਾਚੀ ਪੂੰਜੀ ਸਚੁ ਸੰਜਮੋ ਆਠ ਪਹਰ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਉ॥ ਕਰਿ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਭੇਟਿਆ ਮਰਣੁ ਨ ਆਵਣੁ ਜਾਉ॥ 1॥ ਮੇਰੇ ਮਨ ਹਰਿ ਭਜੁ ਸਦਾ ਇਕ ਰੰਗਿ॥ ਘਟ ਘਟ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਰਵਿ ਰਹਿਆ ਸਦਾ ਸਹਾਈ ਸੰਗਿ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਸੁਖਾ ਕੀ ਮਿਤਿ ਕਿਆ ਗਣੀ ਜਾ ਸਿਮਰੀ ਗੋਵਿੰਦੁ॥ ਜਿਨ ਚਾਖਿਆ ਸੇ ਤ੍ਰਿਪਤਾਸਿਆ ਉਹ ਰਸੁ ਜਾਣੈ ਜਿੰਦੁ॥
