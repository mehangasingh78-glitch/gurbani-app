(ਅੰਗ 12)॥   ਆਪੇ ਕਰਤਾ ਤੇਰਾ ਕੀਆ ਸਭੁ ਹੋਇ॥ ਤੁਧੁ ਬਿਨੁ ਦੂਜਾ ਅਵਰੁ ਨਾ ਕੋਇ॥ ਤੂ ਕਰਿ ਕਰਿ ਵੇਖਹਿ ਜਾਣਹਿ ਸੋਇ॥ ਜਨ ਨਾਨਕ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਪਰਗਟੁ ਹੋਇ॥ 4॥2॥
Assa Mehla 4. Tu Kerta Sachiyaar(u) mainda saayeen, jo Tuou bhaavaiy soyee theesee jo Tu deh(i) soyee huoun paayeen.  Ruhaou. Subh Tayri, Tu subhnee dhiyaya.  Jis nu kirpa kureh(i) tin(i) Naam ruttan paaya. Gurmukh(i) laadha, munmukh(i) guvaaya. Toodh(u) aap(i) vichhorriya Aap(i) milaaya.      1.  Tu duriyaou, subh toojh hee maah(i). Toojh bin(u) dooja koyee naah(i). Jea junt subh(i) Tayra khayl. Vijoag(i) mil(i) vichhurriyaan, sunjoagi mayl(u).  2. Jis nu Tu jaanayeh(i) soyee jun(u) jaanaiy. Hur(i) goon sud hee aakh(i) vukhaanaiy. Jin(i) Hur(i) sayviya, tin(i) sookh(u) paaya. Sehjay hee Hur(i) Naam smaaya.       3.

Word Meaning:  Kerta = creator.  Sachiyaar = the home of Truth (noble qualities); everlasting.  Mainda = my; mine.  Saayeen = Lord.   Bhaavaiy = like; wish.  Soyee = the same.  Theesee = will happen; takes place. Houn = I.      Paayeen = I receive.     
Pause. 
Subhnee = everybody.  Dhiyaya = remembered; recited Your Naam; worshiped.  Kirpa = kindness.  Naam Ruttan = valuable as a precious stone (invaluable Naam).  Gurmukh(i) = devotee; fully dedicated to the Guru.  Laadha = found; achieved.  
Munmukh(i) = follower of one’s mind as opposed to Guru’s teachings (non-believer)  Guvaaya = lost. Vichhorriya = separated; disconnected.      1. 
Duriyaaou = (like a) river. Toojh Maah(i) = contained in you.  Bin(u) = without.  Jea Junt = living beings.  Khayl = enjoyment (creation).  Vijoag(i) = (due to) separation.  
Sunjoagi = (as per the destiny of) union.  Mayl(u) = union.       2. 
Jaanayeh(i) = enable.   Soyee = that (person).  Hur(i) Goon = noble traits of Waheguru.  Vukhaanaiy = describes.  Sayviya = served; remembered; kept in mind. Sehjay = without much effort.  Smaaya = got absorbed; mixed completely.      3. 
Uver(u) = somebody else.  Kur(i) Kur(i) Vaykheh(i) = create and watch (look after). Gurmukh(i) = through the Guru. Purgut(u) = appear; reveal; unfold.      4.2. 
MEANING:  You are the source of noble qualities, are everlasting and the creator of the universe, oh my Lord, Waheguru!  Only that happens that you wish; and I receive only that which You provide.              Pause.         The whole creation is Yours (You created everything), and everybody worships You. But only that individual realizes Your invaluable Naam, to whom You are kind.  Your faithful devotees realize Your Naam while the non-believers lose it (the opportunity to realize).  But it is Your own Will that some are disconnected from You while You connect with some others.     1. 
You are like a river of life, oh my Lord!  Everybody is contained in You (is Your part). There is nobody without You. All the living beings are Your creation. As per Your law of separation (disconnection), those who are disconnected are reconnected with you as per their destiny for reunion.             2. 
Only those people know You (Your secrets) who are enabled by You. Such people always describe (disseminate) Your noble traits. Those who serve You are always absorbed in Your Naam without any special effort and lead comfortable lives.       3. 
You alone are the creator, and everybody is Your creation.  There is nobody other than You (as the creator and the provider).  You create, and watch Your creation (look after it). You reveal Your existence through the Guru.   4.2. 

ਆਸਾ ਮ:1॥ ਤਿਤੁ ਸਰਵਰੜੈ ਭਈਲੇ ਨਿਵਾਸਾ ਪਾਣੀ ਪਾਵਕੁ ਤਿਨਹਿ ਕੀਆ॥ ਪੰਕਜੁ ਮੋਹ ਪਗੁ ਨਹੀ ਚਾਲੈ ਹਮ ਦੇਖਾ ਤਹ ਡੂਬੀਅਲੇ॥1॥ ਮਨ ਏਕੁ ਨ ਚੇਤਸਿ ਮੂੜ ਮਨਾ ਹਰਿ ਬਿਸਰਤ ਤੇਰੇ ਗੁਣ ਗਲਿਆ॥1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਨਾ ਹਉ ਜਤੀ ਸਤੀ ਨਹੀ ਪੜਿਆ ਮੂਰਖ ਮੁਗਧਾ ਜਨਮੁ ਭਇਆ॥ ਪ੍ਰਣਵਤਿ ਨਾਨਕ ਤਿਨ ਕੀ ਸਰਣਾ ਜਿਨ ਤੂ ਨਾਹੀ ਵੀਸਰਿਆ॥2॥3॥
Assa Mehla 1. Titt(u) surver-rraiy bhuyeelay nivaasa, paani paavuk(u) Tineh(i) kiya. Punkuj(u) moh pug(u) naahi chaalaiy, hum daykhaa teh doobiulay.1. Mun Aik(u) na chaytis(i) moorr munah, Hur(i) bisrut tayray goon gulliya. Ruhaou.  Na houn jutee sutee naahi purriyah, moorukh mooghdha junum(u) bhuyya. Prunvut(i) Nanak tin kee sherna jin Tu naahi vissuriya. 2.3. 
Word Meaning:  Titt(u) = that.  Surve-rrraiy = pool.  Bhuyeelay = taken place. Nivaasa = residence.  Paavuk(u) = fire.  Tineh(i) =  Him (Waheguru).  Punkuj(u) = mud.  Moh = attachment.    Pug(u) = foot; step. Doobiyaylay = is drowning.        1. Aiyk(u) =  One (Waheguru; God).  Moorr Munah = stupid (ignorant) mind.  Bisrut = forgetting; ignoring.  Gulliya = rotted.      Pause. 
Juttee = celibate.  Suttee = of high morals.  Moorukh = Moogdha = foolish; ignorant.  Bhuyya = taken palce.   Prunvut(i) =  humble request.  Shurna = refuge (in the service of). Vissuriya = forgotten; ignored.   
MEANING:  We have taken birth in such a pool (of life) which Waheguru has filled with the fire (of wanton desires such as lust, greed, attachment, etc.) rather than water.  Living beings cannot take a step out of the mud of attachment, as a result of which, they are drowning in the pool (they are getting entangled more and more in their desires) as I am watching.            1.              All this is happening because you have forgotten the kind Waheguru, as a result of which all your good qualities are rotting away, oh my stupid, and ignorant mind!           Pause. 
Neither do I have any good qualities, such as celibacy and good moral character, nor am I an educated person.  My life is just like that of an ignorant, stupid person.  I (Guru Nanak) humbly request my Lord to kindly give me the refuge of the holy men who have not forgotten Him.      2.3. 

ਆਸਾ ਮ:5॥ ਭਈ ਪਰਾਪਤਿ ਮਾਨੁਖ ਦੇਹੁਰੀਆ॥ਗੋਬਿੰਦ ਮਿਲਣ ਕੀ ਇਹ ਤੇਰੀ ਬਰੀਆ॥ ਅਵਰਿ ਕਾਜ ਤੇਰੈ ਕਿਤੈ ਨ ਕਾਮ॥ ਮਿਲੁ ਸਾਧਸੰਗਤਿ ਭਜੁ ਕੇਵਲ ਨਾਮੁ॥ 1॥ ਸਰੰਜਾਮਿ ਲਾਗੁ ਭਵਜਲ ਤਰਨ ਕੈ॥ ਜਨਮੁ ਬ੍ਰਿਥਾ ਜਾਤ ਰੰਗਿ ਮਾਇਆ ਕੈ॥1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਜਪੁ ਤਪੁ ਸੰਜਮੁ ਧਰਮੁ ਨ ਕਮਾਇਆ॥ ਸੇਵਾ ਸਾਧ ਨਾ ਜਾਨਿਆ ਹਰਿ ਰਾਇਆ॥ ਕਹੁ ਨਾਨਕ ਹਮ ਨੀਚ ਕਰੰਮਾ॥ ਸਰਣਿ ਪਰੇ ਕੀ ਰਾਖਹੁ ਸਰਮਾ॥2॥4॥
Assa Mehla 5.  Bhuyee praput(i) manookh dayhuriya. Gobind milun kee eh tayri buriya. Uver(i) kaaj tayraiy kitaiy na kaam. Mil(u) Saadh sungat(i) bhuj(u) kayvul Naam. 1. Srunjaam(i) laag(u) bhuvjul turn kaiy. Junum(u) birthaa jaat rung(i) Maya kaiy. Ruhaou.  Jup(u) tup(u) sunjum(u), dherm(u) na kmaaya. Sayva saadh, na, jaaniya Hur(i) raaya. Kuhou Nanak hum neech kerma. Shern(i) purray kee rakhou sherma.2.4 
Word Meaning:  Bhuyee Praput(i) = have acquired (been given).  Manookh dayhuriya = human body.  Gobind = Waheguru; God.  Buriya = turn, chance, opportunity.  Uver(i) = other.  Kaaj = tasks.  Kitaiy na Kaam = of no use.               Saadh Sungat(i) = holy congregation.   Bhuj(u) = recite; sing the praise.  Kayvul Naam = only Waheguru’s Naam.        1.          Srunjaam(i) = make effort (arrangements).  Bhuvjul = sea of Maya.  Birthaa = empty (wasting away).  Rung Maya kaiy = love for the colourful world of Maya.     Pause. 
Jup(u) = recitation of Naam.  Tup(u) = hard labour (to realize Waheguru).  Sunjum(u) self-restraints (on sensual desires).  Dherm(u) = noble deeds.  Kmaaya = earned.  Sayva Saadh = served the holy men.  Raaya = remembered (recite Naam).  Neech Kerma = ignoble deeds.     Shern(i) = in refuge.     Shermaa = dignity.  2. 
MEANING:  You have been given the body of a human being, which is your opportunity to realize Waheguru, oh my friend!  No other task (rituals and the like) is of any use to you (in the path of spirituality). So, sing Waheguru’s praise in the company of holy men.    1.                   You are wasting away your time enjoying the colourful world of Maya, oh my friend.  Make arrangements to swim across the sea of Maya (break the worldly bonds to attain emancipation), oh my mind.    Pause. 
I have not recited Waheguru’s Naam, or done any hard labour to realize Him. Neither have I any control over my sensual desires, nor have I performed any noble deed. I have not even learned to serve the holy men, and have not worshiped Waheguru.  I (Guru Nanak) admit that all my deeds are ignoble; I humbly request You (my Lord, Waheguru), to please save my dignity as I have come to seek Your refuge.      2.4.

Jaiy ghur(i) keerut(i) aakhiyaiy Kertay ka hoyay beechaaro. Titt(u) ghur(i) gaavhou Sohila siverhou Sirjunhaaro.1.  Toom gaavhou mayray Nirbhuou ka sohila. Huoun vaari jitt(u) sohilaiy suda sookh(u) hoyay. 1. Ruhaou.  Nit nit jearray smaaliun(i) daykhaiyga Dayvunhaar. Tayray daanaiy keemut(i) na puvaiy tis(u) Daatay kuvun(u) shumaar(u). 2. Sumbut(i) saaha likhiya mil(i) kur(i) paavhou tayl(u). Dayhou sujjan asseesrriyaan jioun hovaiy Sahib sioun mayl(u).3. Ghur(i) ghur(i) aiho paahucha sudrray nit puvun(i).  Suddunhaara simriyaiy Nanak say dayh aavun(i). 4.1. 
Word Meaning:  Sohila = a song of joy that uplifts the spirit. 
NOTE: It is also the name of a new composition comprising of five holy Shabads. 
Jaiy = in which.  Ghur(i) = house (holy congregation; holy book). Keerut(i) = praise.  
Beechaaro =  (noble qualities and kindness) are discussed.  Titt(u) = in that.  
Siverihou = meditate on; worship.  Sirjunhaaro = the creator.      1. 
Nirbhuou = the fearless (Waheguru). Houn vaari = wish to sacrifice myself.     Pause.  
Nit nit = everyday (all the time). Jearray = living beings.  Smalliun(i) = looked after.  Dayvunhaar = the provider.   Tayray = you.   Daanaiy = of gifts (largesse).  Shumaar = count; evaluation.         2.          Sumbut Saaha = the year and date (of some event). Paavhou tayl = perform a custom or ritual at a wedding ceremony.    Sujjan = friends.  Asseesrriyaan = blessings.     Jioun = by which.      Mayl(u)  = union.         3. 
Paahucha = (a wedding) message. Sudrray Nit puvun(i)= invites (calls) are sent everyday.    Suddunhaara = one who calls.  Say deh = that day (time). 4. 
MEANING:  Sing the song of joy and worship the Lord in the congregation of the holy men where the praise of the Lord is sung, and the acts of kindness of the Creator are discussed, oh, my friend.      1.  
Sing the song of joy that uplifts your spirit (so as to) worship the fearless Lord, oh my friends!  I will gladly sacrifice my life for such a holy song which provides eternal happiness (bliss).        Pause.     The Provider looks after all His creation and fulfills all its needs. You cannot even count His blessings; so, how can you find His limits?          2.  
The day and the year of your wedding with death is predetermined (which is also the opportunity to be one with Waheguru). Perform the rituals of the wedding ceremony, and bless me so that I may be connected to Waheguru; oh, my holy friends.      3. 
The invitations of the wedding of the human bride with death (as the groom) are sent (by the Lord) all the time to the people (when their time comes, and that time will come to all of us.  So, we should remember (and worship) the Lord to be one with Him when our time comes.     4.1.
