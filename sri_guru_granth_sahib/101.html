(ਅੰਗ 100)॥  ਰੇਨੁ ਸੰਤਨ ਕੀ ਮੇਰੈ ਮੁਖਿ ਲਾਗੀ॥ ਦੁਰਮਤਿ ਬਿਨਸੀ ਕੁਬੁਧਿ ਅਭਾਗੀ॥ ਸਚ ਘਰਿ ਬੈਸਿ ਰਹੇ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਏ ਨਾਨਕ ਬਿਨਸੇ ਕੂਰਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥11॥18॥
Majh Mehla 5:  Sugal Suntan peh(i) vust(u) ikk maangoun.  Kuroun baynti maan(u) tiyaagoun. Vaar(i) vaar(i) jaayeen lukh vuriyaan, dayho Suntan kee dhoora jeeo. 1. Toom Daatay Toom Purakh Bidhaatay.  Toom(u) sumruth suda sookh Daatay. Subh ko Toom hee tay versaavaiy, aousur(u) kurhou hmaara poora jeeo. 2.  Dershun(i) Tayray bhuvan pooneeta. Aatum gurrh(u) bikhum(u) tinha hee jeeta. Toom Daatay Toom Purakh Bidhaatay, Toom(u) jayvud uver(u) na soora jeeo. 3. 
(PAGE 100):   Rayn(u) Suntan kee mayraiy mookh(i) laagi. Doormut(i) binsee kooboodh(i) abhaagi. Sach ghur(i) baiys(i) ruhay goon gaayay Nanak binsay koorra jeeo. 4.11.18.
Word Meaning: Sugal = all.   Peh(i) = from.   Vust(u) = article; thing.   Tiyaagoun = (I) give up.    Vaar(i) = offer to sacrifice.   Lukh vuriyaan = countless times.  Dhoora = dust of the feet. (an expression of love and respect).      1.      Daatay = provider.  Bidhatay = carver of fate.   Sumruth = capable of doing anything.  Sookh Daatay = source of comfort.    Versaavaiy = blessed; profits from.   Aousur(u) = time; opportunity; objective of human life.       2.
Bhuvan = mansions; hearts.     Pooneeta = holy; free of all evil.  Atum Gurrh = the fort of soul (human mind).    Bikhum(u) = very difficult.   Jayvud = as great as.   Uver(u) = other; else.   Soora = gallant; hero.         3.        Rayn(u) = dust (of feet).    Doormut(i) = evil wisdom.    Binsi = ended; destroyed.  Koobudh(i) = evil mind.    Abhaagi = unlucky.   Koorra = mundane distractions.      4. 
MEANING:  With all the humbleness, I beg only one thing from all the devotees of Waheguru that please, donate me the dust of your feet (I surrender all my pride and wisdom, and wish to act on your advice).  I am gladly willing to offer my life to you (serve you as your slave) (if you connect me with Waheguru).         1.          You are the provider, and you are the carver of the fate of everybody, oh omnipresent Waheguru!  You are capable of doing everything (are omnipotent) and a source of comfort.  Everybody is blessed by You; kindly help me achieve the objective of my human life, oh Waheguru.         2.
Your sight purifies the hearts of individuals, and they are able to conquer the fort of the human soul (mind), which otherwise is impossible to conquer.  You are the provider, and the carver of peoples’ fate; nobody else is as great a gallant (hero, who looks after everyone) as You are!              3.                  I am lucky to put the dust of the feet of Your devotees on my forehead (serve them that they have connected me with You) as a result of which all my evil thoughts have been eradicated, and the unlucky bad wisdom has run away from me.  When a person’s mind quits wandering, and he sings the virtues of Waheguru, all his mundane distractions leave him alone, oh Nanak.   4.11.18.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਵਿਸਰੁ ਨਾਹੀ ਏਵਡ ਦਾਤੇ॥ ਕਰਿ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਭਗਤਨ ਸੰਗਿ ਰਾਤੇ॥ ਦਿਨਸੁ ਰੈਣਿ ਜਿਉ ਤੁਧੁ ਧਿਆਈ ਏਹੁ ਦਾਨੁ ਮੋਹਿ ਕਰਣਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਮਾਟੀ ਅੰਧੀ ਸੁਰਤਿ ਸਮਾਈ॥ ਸਭ ਕਿਛੁ ਦੀਆ ਭਲੀਆ ਜਾਈ॥ ਅਨੰਦ ਬਿਨੋਦ ਚੋਜ ਤਮਾਸੇ ਤੁਧੁ ਭਾਵੈ ਸੋ ਹੋਣਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਜਿਸ ਦਾ ਦਿਤਾ ਸਭੁ ਕਿਛੁ ਲੈਣਾ॥ ਛਤੀਹ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਭੋਜਨੁ ਖਾਣਾ॥ ਸੇਜ ਸੁਖਾਲੀ ਸੀਤਲੁ ਪਵਣਾ ਸਹਜ ਕੇਲ ਰੰਗ ਕਰਣਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 3॥ ਸਾ ਬੁਧਿ ਦੀਜੈ ਜਿਤੁ ਵਿਸਰਹਿ ਨਾਹੀ॥ ਸਾ ਮਤਿ ਦੀਜੈ ਜਿਤੁ ਤੁਧੁ ਧਿਆਈ॥ ਸਾਸ ਸਾਸ ਤੇਰੇ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਵਾ ਓਟ ਨਾਨਕ ਗੁਰ ਚਰਣਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥12॥19॥
Majh Mehla 5:  Vissar(u) naahi  aivud Daatay. Kur(i) kirpa bhugtan sung(i) raatay.  Dinus raiyn(i) jiyoun Toodh(u) dhiyayeen, eho daan(u) moh(i) kerna jeeo. 1. Maati undhi soorut(i) smaayee. Subh kichh(u) deeya bhuliya jaayee. Anund binoad choaj tmaashay, Toodh(u) bhaavaiy so hona jeeo. 2. Jis da ditta subh(u) kichh(u) laiyna. Chhuttee amrit(u) bhojun khana.  Sayj sookhali seetul(u) puvna, sehj kayl rung kerna jeeo. 3. Sa boodh(i) deejaiy jit(u) visreh(i) naahi. Sa mut(i) deejaiy jit(u) Toodh(u) dhiyayeen.  Saas saas Tayray goon gaavaan, oat Nanak Gur churna jeeo.  4.12.19.
Word Meaning:   Vissar(u) naahi = (please) do not be forgotten.   Aivud = that great. 
Raatay = united completely.  Dhiyaayeen = (I) remember (You).     1.
Maati = soil; earth (the body of a fetus).   Undhi = completely ignorant.  Soorut(i) = awareness.   Bhuliya Jaayee = comfortable place.   Anund = enjoyment.  Binod, Choj, Tmaashay = activities for entertainment.    Bhaavaiy = as per (Waheguru’s Will).         2.       Chhuttee Amrit Bhojun = old Indian granths recognize thirty-six (amrit-like) delicious foods.   Sayj = bed.  Seetul(u) Puvna = cool air.   Sehj = peaceful mind.  Kayl, Rung = entertainment; enjoyment.      3.     
Sa = that.  Boodh(i) = wisdom.   Visreh(i) = forget.  Oat = support.     4.
MEANING: Please, do not allow me to forget You, oh great Waheguru!  It is Your kindness that Your devotees are imbued in Your Naam.  I, too, beg You that kindly give me the gift (enable me) that I recite Your Naam day and night.          1.
(You are so kind) that You grant the awareness to the fetus, which is nothing more than an ignorant piece of mud (earth), and You put him in a comfortable position (in the womb), and provide all the necessities for his survival and growth. (For the future), All enjoyment and activities of entertainment are there for him as per Your Will.         2.        Whatever the living beings receive in life is all provided by You. Your kindness provides 36 kinds of delicious food, comfortable beds, cool air (a comfortable environment), a peaceful life, and other amenities for the man to enjoy.          3.          Please give me the wisdom so that I may never forget You, and always recite Your Naam, oh Waheguru!   I wish that I may sing Your virtues with every breath in the shelter of the Guru, which is my support, oh, Nanak!     4.12.19.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਸਿਫਤਿ ਸਾਲਾਹੁਣ ਤੇਰਾ ਹੁਕਮੁ ਰਜਾਈ॥ ਸੋ ਗਿਆਨੁ ਧਿਆਨੁ ਜੋ ਤੁਧੁ ਭਾਈ॥ ਸੋਈ
ਜਪੁ ਜੋ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਜੀਉ ਭਾਵੈ ਭਾਣੈ ਪੂਰ ਗਿਆਨਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਤੇਰਾ ਸੋਈ ਗਾਵੈ॥ ਜੋ ਸਾਹਿਬ ਤੇਰੇ ਮਨਿ ਭਾਵੈ॥ ਤੂ ਸੰਤਨ ਕਾ ਸੰਤ ਤੁਮਾਰੇ ਸੰਤ ਸਾਹਿਬ ਮਨੁ ਮਾਨਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਤੂ ਸੰਤਨ ਕੀ ਕਰਹਿ ਪ੍ਰਤਿਪਾਲਾ॥ ਸੰਤ ਖੇਲਹਿ ਤੁਮ ਸੰਗਿ ਗੋਪਾਲਾ॥ ਅਪੁਨੇ ਸੰਤ ਤੁਧੁ ਖਰੇ ਪਿਆਰੇ ਤੂ ਸੰਤਨ ਕੇ ਪ੍ਰਾਨਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 3॥ ਉਨ ਸੰਤਨ ਕੈ ਮੇਰਾ ਮਨੁ ਕੁਰਬਾਨੇ॥ ਜਿਨ ਤੂ ਜਾਤਾ ਜੋ ਤੁਧੁ ਮਨਿ ਭਾਨੇ॥ ਤਿਨ ਕੈ ਸੰਗਿ ਸਦਾ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਹਰਿ ਰਸ ਨਾਨਕ ਤ੍ਰਿਪਤਿ ਅਘਾਨਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥13॥20॥
Majh Mehla 5:  Sift(i) salaahun Tayra Huqam rujaayee.  So Giyan dhiyan(u) jo Toodh(u) bhaayee. Soyee jup(u) jo Prubh jeeo bhaavaiy, bhaanaiy poor Giyana jeeo. 1. Amrit(u) Naam(u) Tayra soyee gaavaiy. Jo Sahib Tayray mun(i) bhaavaiy. Tu Suntan ka, Sunt Toomhaaray, Sunt Sahib mun(u) maana jeeo. 2. Tu Santan kee kureh(i) pritpaala. Sunt khayleh(i) Toom sung(i) Gopaala. Apunay Sunt Toodh(u) khuray piyaray Tu Suntan kay praana jeeo. 3. Oon Suntan kaiy mayra mun(u) koorbaanay. Jin Tu jaata jo Toodh(u) mun(i) bhaanay. Tin kaiy sung(i) suda sookh(u) paaya, Hur(i) rus Nanak triput(i) aghaana jeeo. 4.13. 
Word Meaning:   Sift(i) Salaahun = sing praise.    Rujayee = (to obey Waheguru’s) Will.  Bhaayee = to (Your) liking.   Jup(u) = recitation (of some mantras).   Bhaanaiy = Will.   Poor Giyana = complete Divine knowledge (unity with God).      1.       Mun(u) Maana = mutual liking for each other.       2.         Pritpaala = raising and nourishing (looking after).   Gopaala = Waheguru.   Khuray = very much.  Praana = life.       3.       Jaata = understood (made a connection).   Bhaanay = liked.   Triput(i) Aghaana = complete contentment.    4.
MEANING:  To obey Your Huqam gladly is You real praise, oh Waheguru!  Only that knowledge and meditation are meaningful that are to Your liking.  Only that recitation of mantras is worth which You approve, and only that knowledge is perfect which You provide, oh Waheguru.       1.          Only that individual sings Your Amrit-Naam whom You like, oh Waheguru! The devotees (who are fully connected with You) belong to You, and You belong to them; You and Your devotees like (enjoy) each other.         2.            You look after Your devotees, and the devotees enjoy Your company (shelter), oh Waheguru.  Your devotees are very dear to You, and You are the life of the devotees (they cannot live without You).       3.
I wish to sacrifice my life for the devotees who have fully recognized You (are fully connected with You), and whom You love.  Their company is always comforting, and I am fully content with Your Naam-essence in their company, oh Nanak!   4.13.20.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਤੂੰ ਜਲਨਿਧਿ ਹਮ ਮੀਨ ਤੁਮਾਰੇ॥ ਤੇਰਾ ਨਾਮ ਬੂੰਦ ਹਮ ਚਾਤ੍ਰਿਕ ਤਿਖਹਾਰੇ॥ ਤੁਮਰੀ ਆਸ ਪਿਆਸਾ ਤੁਮਰੀ ਤੁਮ ਹੀ ਸੰਗਿ ਮਨੁ ਲੀਨਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਜਿਉ ਬਾਰਿਕੁ ਪੀ ਖੀਰੁ ਅਘਾਵੈ॥ ਜਿਉ ਨਿਰਧਨੁ ਧਨੁ ਦੇਖਿ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਾਵੈ॥ ਤ੍ਰਿਖਾਵੰਤੁ ਜਲੁ ਪੀਵਤ ਠੰਢਾ ਤਿਉ ਹਰਿ ਸੰਗਿ ਇਹੁ ਮਨੁ ਭੀਨਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਜਿਉ ਅੰਧਿਆਰੈ ਦੀਪਕੁ ਪਰਗਾਸਾ॥ ਭਰਤਾ ਚਿਤਵਤ ਪੂਰਨ ਆਸਾ॥ ਮਿਲਿ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਮ ਜਿਉ ਹੋਤ ਅਨੰਦਾ ਤਿਉ ਹਰਿ ਰੰਗਿ ਮਨੁ ਰੰਗੀਨਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 3॥ ਸੰਤਨ ਮੋ ਕਉ ਹਰਿ ਮਾਰਗਿ ਪਾਇਆ॥ ਸਾਧ ਕ੍ਰਿਪਾਲਿ ਹਰਿ ਰੰਗਿ ਗਿਝਾਇਆ॥ ਹਰਿ ਹਮਰਾ ਹਮ ਹਰਿ ਕੇ ਦਾਸੇ ਨਾਨਕ ਸਬਦੁ ਗੁਰੂ ਸਚੁ ਦੀਨਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥14॥21॥
Majh Mehla 5:  Tu julnidh(i) hum meen Toomhaaray. Tayra Naam boond hum chaatrik tikh-haaray. Toomri aas piyasa Toomri, Toom hee sung(i) mun(u) leena jeeo. 1.  Jiyoun baarik(u) pee kheer(u) aghaavaiy. Jiyoun nirdhun(u) dhun(u) daykh(i) sookh(u) paavaiy. Trikhavunt jul peevut tthundha, tiyoun Hur(i) sung(i) eho mun(u) bheena jeeo. 2. Jiyoun undhiyaaraiy deepuk(u) pergaasa. Bherta chitvut poorun aasa. Mil(i) preetum jiyoun hoat anunda, tiyoun Hur(i) rung(i) mun(u) rungeena jeeo. 3. Suntan mo kuou Hur(i) Maarug(i) paaya. Sadh kirpaal(i) Hur(i) rung(i) gijhaaya. Hur humra, hum Hur(i) kay daasay, Nanak Shabad Guru Sach(u) deena jeeo.  4.14.21.
Word Meaning:  Julnidh(i) = source of water (ocean).  Meen = fish.  Boond = drop (the mythical first drop of the rain water for which the chaatrik laments for).   Tikh-haaray = thirsty.   Leena = fully absorbed.        1.        Kheer(u) = milk.   Aghaavaiy = contented.  Trikhvunt = 
thirsty.   Bheena = imbued.      2.      Undhiyaaray = in darkness.   Bherta = husband.   Chitvut  = having thoughts about.    Preetum = beloved.   Anunda = blissful.    Rungeena = dyed.      3.
Kirpal(i) = kindly.   Rung(i) = in love.   Gijhaaya = got used to; instilled.     4.
MEANING: If You are like the ocean, then we are like fish (who cannot survive without water), oh Waheguru!  We are like the mythical rainbird, chaatrik, who is thirsty for- Your Naam, which is like the first rain drop for us.  We are thirsty for You, and You are our hope, and our mind is imbued in Your Naam.          1.            Just like a hungry child who feels satisfied after drinking milk; and just like a poor man who is overjoyed to receive money; and just like a thirsty man who is happy (satisfied) to drink cold water (on a hot day), we feel blissful to be imbued in Your Naam!           2.           Just like the light of a lamp is a boon in the darkness, and just like the arrival of the husband fulfils the wish of a waiting wife, and just like the bliss that a person feels on meeting his (long separated) dear friend, my mind is completely permeated with the love of God.          3.           The holy men put me on the path to Waheguru, and the kind Guru instilled love for Waheguru in me.  Now, God belongs to me, and I am His slave; Guru’s Shabad has connected me with the Truth (eternal Waheguru), oh Nanak.      4.14.21
