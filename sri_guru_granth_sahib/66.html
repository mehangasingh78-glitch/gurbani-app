(ਅੰਗ 65)॥           ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਸੇਵ ਗੁਣ ਨਿਧਾਨੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਤਿਸ ਕੀ ਕੀਮ ਨ ਪਾਈ॥ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਸਖਾ ਹਰਿ ਜੀਉ ਮੇਰਾ ਅੰਤੇ ਹੋਇ ਸਖਾਈ॥ 3॥ ਪੇਈਅੜੇ ਜਗਜੀਵਨੁ ਦਾਤਾ ਮਨਮੁਖਿ ਪਤਿ ਗਵਾਈ ॥ਬਿਨੁ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਕੋ ਮਗੁ ਨ ਜਾਣੈ ਅੰਧੇ ਠਉਰ ਨ ਕਾਈ॥ ਹਰਿ ਸੁਖ ਦਾਤਾ ਮਨਿ ਨਹੀ ਵਸਿਆ ਅੰਤਿ ਗਇਆ ਪਛੁਤਾਈ॥ 4॥ ਪੇਈਅੜੇ ਜਗਜੀਵਨੁ ਦਾਤਾ ਗੁਰਮਤਿ ਮੰਨਿ ਵਸਾਇਆ॥ ਅਨਦਿਨੁ ਭਗਤਿ ਕਰਹਿ ਦਿਨੁ ਰਾਤੀ ਹਉਮੈ ਮੋਹੁ ਚੁਕਾਇਆ॥ਜਿਸੁ ਸਿਉ ਰਾਤਾ ਤੈਸੋ ਹੋਵੈ ਸਚੇ ਸਚਿ ਸਮਾਇਆ॥ 5॥ ਆਪੇ ਨਦਰਿ ਕਰੇ ਭਾਉ ਲਾਏ ਗੁਰ ਸਬਦੀ ਬੀਚਾਰਿ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਸੇਵਿਐ ਸਹਜੁ ਊਪਜੈ ਹਉਮੈ ਤ੍ਰਿਸਨਾ ਮਾਰਿ॥ ਹਰਿ ਗੁਣ ਦਾਤਾ ਸਦਾ ਮਨਿ ਵਸੈ ਸਚੁ ਰਖਿਆ ਉਰਧਾਰਿ॥ 6॥ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਮੇਰਾ ਸਦਾ ਨਿਰਮਲਾ ਮਨਿ ਨਿਰਮਲਿ ਪਾਇਆ ਜਾਇ॥ ਨਾਮੁ ਨਿਧਾਨੁ ਹਰਿ ਮਨਿ ਵਸੈ ਹਉਮੈ ਦੁਖੁ ਸਭੁ ਜਾਇ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਸਬਦੁ ਸੁਣਾਇਆ ਹਉ ਸਦ ਬਲਿਹਾਰੈ ਜਾਉ॥ 7॥ਆਪਣੈ ਮਨਿ ਚਿਤਿ ਕਹੈ ਕਹਾਏ ਬਿਨੁ ਗੁਰ ਆਪੁ ਨ ਜਾਈ॥ ਹਰਿ ਜੀਉ ਭਗਤਿ ਵਛਲੁ ਸੁਖਦਾਤਾ ਕਰਿ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਮੰਨਿ ਵਸਾਈ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਸੋਭਾ ਸੁਰਤਿ ਦੇਇ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਆਪੇ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਦੇ ਵਡਿਆਈ॥  8॥1॥18॥

Gurmukh(i) kirpa kuray bhugut(i) keejaiy, bin(u) Gur bhugut(i) na hoyay. Aapaiy aap(u) milaayaiy boojhaiy, ta nirmul(u) hovaiy koyay. Hur(i) Jeao Sacha, Sachi Bani Shabad(i) milaava hoyay. 1. Bhaayee ray bhugut-heen(u) kaahay jug(i) aaya. Pooray Gur kee sayv na keeni, birtha junum(u) guvaaya. 1. Ruhaou.  Aapay Hur(i) jugjeevun(u) daata, Aapay bukhsh(i) milaayay. Jea junt eh kiya vaychaaray, kiya ko aakh(i) soonaayay. Gurmukh(i) Aapay day vudiyaayee, Aapay sayv kuraayay. 2. Daykh(i) kootumb(u) moh(i) lobhaana chuldiyaan naal(i) na jaayee.

Bin(u) Satgur ko mug(u) na jaanaiy, undhay tthaur na kaayee. Hur(i) sookhdaata mun(i) nuhee vusiya, unt(i) guyaa puchhutayee. 4. Payiurray Jugjeevun Daata Gurmut(i) munn(i) vusaayaa. Undin(u) bhugut(i) kureh(i) din(u) raati, haumein moh(u) chookaaya. Jis(u) siyoun raata taiso hovaiy, Sachay Sach(i) smaaya. 5.  Aapay nudr(I) kuray bhaaou laayay, Gur Shabadi beechaar(i).  Satgur(u) sayviyaiy sehj(u) oopjaiy, haumein trishna maar(i). Hur(i) goon daata sud mun(i) vusaiy, Sach(u) rukhiya oordhaar(i). 6.  Prubh(u) mayra suda nirmula, mun(i) nirmul(i) paaya jaayay. Naam(u) nidhaan(u) Hur(i) mun(i) vusaiy haumein dookh(u) subh(u) jaayay. Satgur(i) Shabad(u) soonaaya, houn sud bulihaaraiy jaoun. 7. Aapnaiy mun(i) chit(i) kuhaiy kuhaayay, bin(u) Gur aap(u) na jaayee. Hur(i) Jeao bhugat(i) vuchhul(u) sookh daata, kur(i) kirpa munn(i) vusayee. Nanak sobha soort(u) dayay Prubh(u) aapay, Gurmukh(i) dayay vudiyayee.  8.1.18.
Word Meaning:  Bin(u) = without.  Boojhay = understand; realize.  Milaava = unite.  1.
Heen(u) = without; empty.    Birtha = useless.    Guvaaya = lost; wasted.   Pause.
Jug Jeevun(u) = life (support) of the world.   Bukhsh(i) = by being kind.  Junt = living beings.   Vaychaaray = poor; powerless.  Vudiyayee = honour.     2.     Kootumb(u) = family.   Moh(i) = attachment.   Lobhaana = is entangled.  Goon Nidhaan(u) = treasure of noble qualities (Waheguru).   Sukha = friend.       3.      Payiurray = paternal home (this world).   Put(i) = dignity.   Mug(u) = path (to spirituality).    Tthaur = shelter; support.  Sookh Daata = source of comfort.      4.       Undin(u) = day and night.    Chookaaya = ended; got rid of.   Raata = imbued.     5.      Nudr(i) = kind sight.   Bhaao = affection.   Sehj(u) = state of mind above the attractions of Maya.  Trishna = desires.     6.
Nirmula = clean; pure.   Nirmul(i) = through purity.  Nidhaan(u) = treasure.   7.
Aap(u) = self (selfishness).   Bhugut(i) Vuchhul(u) = lover of devotion (Waheguru) Sobha = praise.   Soort(i) = concentration of mind; ability.   8.
MEANING:  (The real objective of human life is to be “one” with Waheguru by worshipping Him). So, what for did the individual, who is devoid of Waheguru’s worship, come in this world, oh my brother? He does not serve the Perfect Guru and wastes away his precious life for nothing.        Pause.         (But we cannot entirely blame such an individual because), A man cannot worship Waheguru on his own. Only those serve (worship) Him whom He kindly connects with the Guru. And only that individual recognizes this fact whose mind has been purified by Waheguru after he has been connected with Him (through the Guru).  God is Pure, and so is Bani or His praise, (which can be sung by) Guru’s Shabad only, and which connects the individual with God.        1.            Waheguru Himself is the life (support of all kinds) of the world, and it is because of His kindness that He connects people with Him; otherwise, the poor living beings have no power (to connect with Him) and are unable to explain anything about Him.  Waheguru Himself honours people (by connecting with Him) and enables them to serve Him.         2.         On seeing his family, the man is entangled in (possessed by) its affection, which does not accompany him at the moment of his death.  He does not recognize the value of union with Waheguru, the source of all noble qualities, which can be realized only by serving Satguru. God is the only friend who can help one at his death.       3.
While living in this world, the follower of his mind does not remember Waheguru (which is the source of life of the whole world), and loses his respect (in Waheguru’s Court).  Without Satguru, the blind man does not know the real path (of union with Waheguru) and does not find shelter (peace of mind).  The source of all comfort, Waheguru, does not take residence in his heart, and he leaves the world repenting.        4.         (On the other hand) A man who takes Guru’s advice in this life gets Waheguru to make home in his heart. By worshipping Waheguru day and night, he gets rid of his haumein and worldly attachments.  Being imbued in His Naam, he acquires the same traits as Waheguru, and thus becoming pure, he merges with the Pure God.           5.             When God is kind to someone, He connects the individual with the Guru, who ponders on the Guru’s Shabad, and develops affection for Waheguru.  By serving Satguru, one can overcome one's haumein and sensual desires, thereby attaining the spiritual level (Sehj) where Maya has no effect.  In that state, Waheguru, which is the source of all the noble qualities, takes residence in his heart, and the individual always preserves His Naam in his heart.          6.          Waheguru is always pure and can be realized only with a pure mind. The treasure of Naam (Waheguru) begins to reside in the pure heart, and all the pain (suffering) of haumein of the individual is gone. Satguru kindly guides people with His Shabad; I love to sacrifice my life for the Satguru.     7.
One may think to himself and may make people believe he has overcome his selfishness, but nobody can overcome selfishness without the Guru. Waheguru, the source of all comforts, who loves being worshipped (and loves His worshippers), kindly implants His Naam in the hearts of His devotees. Waheguru gifts the wisdom to praise Him, and honours individuals by connecting them with the Guru, oh Nanak.   8.1.18

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ ਹਉਮੈ ਕਰਮ ਕਮਾਵਦੇ ਜਮ ਡੰਡੁ ਲਗੈ ਤਿਨ ਆਇ॥ ਜਿ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਸੇਵਨਿ ਸੇ ਉਬਰੇ ਹਰਿ ਸੇਤੀ ਲਿਵ ਲਾਇ॥1॥ ਮਨ ਰੇ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਧਿਆਇ॥ ਧੁਰਿ ਪੂਰਬਿ ਕਰਤੈ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਤਿਨਾ ਗੁਰਮਤਿ ਨਾਮਿ ਸਮਾਇ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਵਿਣੁ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਪਰਤੀਤਿ ਨ ਆਵਈ ਨਾਮਿ ਨ ਲਾਗੋ ਭਾਉ॥ ਸੁਪਨੈ ਸੁਖੁ ਨ ਪਾਵਈ ਦੁਖ ਮਹਿ ਸਵੈ ਸਮਾਇ॥ 2॥ ਜੇ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਕੀਚੈ ਬਹੁਤੁ ਲੋਚੀਐ ਕਿਰਤੁ ਨ ਮੇਟਿਆ ਜਾਇ॥ ਹਰਿ ਕਾ ਭਾਣਾ ਭਗਤੀ ਮੰਨਿਆ ਸੇ ਭਗਤ ਪਏ ਦਰਿ ਥਾਇ॥3॥ ਗੁਰੁ ਸਬਦੁ ਦਿੜਾਵੈ ਰੰਗ ਸਿਉ ਬਿਨੁ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਲਇਆ ਨ ਜਾਇ॥ ਜੇ ਸਉ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਨੀਰੀਐ ਭੀ ਬਿਖੁ ਫਲੁ ਲਾਗੈ ਧਾਇ॥4॥ ਸੇ ਜਨ ਸਚੇ ਨਿਰਮਲੇ ਜਿਨ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਨਾਲਿ ਪਿਆਰੁ॥ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਕਾ ਭਾਣਾ ਕਮਾਵਦੇ ਬਿਖੁ ਹਉਮੈ ਤਜਿ ਵਿਕਾਰੁ॥ 5॥ ਮਨ ਹਠਿ ਕਿਤੈ ਉਪਾਇ ਨ ਛੁਟੀਐ ਸਿਮ੍ਰਿਤਿ ਸਾਸਤ੍ਰ ਸੋਧਹੁ ਜਾਇ॥ ਮਿਲਿ ਸੰਗਤਿ ਸਾਧੂ ਉਬਰੇ ਗੁਰ ਕਾ ਸਬਦੁ ਕਮਾਇ॥ 6॥ ਹਰਿ ਕਾ ਨਾਮੁ ਨਿਧਾਨੁ ਹੈ ਜਿਸੁ ਅੰਤੁ ਨ ਪਾਰਾਵਾਰੁ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਸੇਈ ਸੋਹਦੇ ਜਿਨ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਕਰੇ ਕਰਤਾਰੁ॥ 7॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਦਾਤਾ ਏਕੁ ਹੈ ਦੂਜਾ ਅਉਰੁ ਨ ਕੋਇ॥ ਗੁਰ ਪਰਸਾਦੀ ਪਾਈਐ ਕਰਮਿ ਪਰਾਪਤਿ ਹੋਇ॥ 8॥2॥19
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 3: Haumein kerm kmaavnday, Jum dund(u) lugaiy tin aayay. J(i) Satgur(u) sayvun(i) say oobray, Hur(i) saytee liv laayay. 1. Mun ray, Gurmukh(i) Naam(u) dhiyaayay. Dhoor(i) poorub(i) Kertaiy likhiya, tinha Gurmut(i) Naam(i) smaayay. 1. Ruhaou. Vin(u) Satgur purteet(i) na aavuyee, Naam(i) na laago bhaaou. Soopnaiy sookh(u) na paavyee, dookh meh(i) suvaiy smaayay. 2. Jay Hur(i) Hur(i) keechaiy buhout(u) lochiyaiy, kirt(u) na maytiya jaayay. Hur(i) ka bhaana bhugteen munniya, say bhugat puyay dur(i) thaayayn. 3. Gur(u) Sahabad(u) dirraavaiy rung siyoun, bin(u) kirpa luyya na jaayay. Jay suou amrit neeriyaiy, bhee bikh(u) ful(u) laagaiy dhaayay. 4. Say jun sachay nirmulay jin Satgur naal(i) piyaar(u). Satgur ka bhaana kmaanvday, bikh(u) haumein tuj(i) vikaar(u). 5. Mun hutth(i) kitaiy oopaayaiy na chhootiyaiy, Simrit Shastar sodhou jaayay. Mil(i) sungut(i) Sadhu oobray, Gur ka Shabad(u) kmaayay. 6. Hur(i) ka Naam(u) Nidhaan haiy, jis(u) unt(i) na paaravaar(u). Gurmukh(i) sayee sohnday jin kirpa kuray Kartar(u). 7. Nanak daata Aik(u) haiy, dooja aur(u) na koyay. Gur persadi paayiyaiy kerm(i) praput(i) hoyay.  8.2.19.
Word Meaning:  Haumein = selfishness and ego.  Kerm = deeds.    Dund(u) = punishment.    Oobray = rose above (the attractions of Maya); salvaged.   Saytee = with.    Liv = concentrate.   1.       Naam(u) Dhiyayaiy = recite the Naam with devotion.   Dhoor(i) = from the source (God). Poorub(i) Likhiya = pre-written. Kertaiy = the Creator. Smaayay= merged with.     Pause
Bin(u) = without.  Purteet(i) = have faith.   Bhaao = affection.  Suvaiy = sleeps.      2.
Keechaiy = do; utter.  Lochiyaiy = long for.   Kirt(u) = result of previous deeds.   Maytiya = erased.   Bhaana = Will (Huqam).   Thaayayn = room in Waheguru’s Court (approved by Waheguru).     3.       Dirraavaiy = reinforce.   Rung Siyoun = with love.   Suou = hundreds.   Neeriyaiy = irrigate; feed.   Bhee = still.  Bikh(u) = poison.    Dhaayay = approach aggressively   4.       Kmaanvday = earn.    Tuj(i) = relinquish.   Vikaar(u) = immoral indulgence.      5.
Mun hutth(i) = to try to control natural body needs by obstinacy.    Oopaayaiy = ways to control.   Simrit Shastar = Hindu holy books.   Sod-hou = pondering.      6.
Nidhaan(u) = treasure.  Paaravaar(u) = limitless; this end or the other.   Sohnday = look attractive.    Kartar(u) = Waheguru.       7.
Aur = other    Kerm(i) = by the kindness.   Praput(i) = acquire.  8.
MEANING:  Those who perform tasks to satisfy their ego suffer the punishment of the Justice of deeds. Those who serve Satguru and concentrate on Waheguru’s Naam are salvaged.       1.            So, take Guru’s shelter. and recite Waheguru’s Naam (with dedication), oh my mind. Those who are predestined by the Creator act on the Guru’s advice and stay imbued in Waheguru’s Naam.         Pause.            Without Satguru, one does not develop faith and love for God’s Naam. They do not find comfort even in their dreams, and spend their night (life) gripped in discomfort.          2.        Even if someone wants to recite Waheguru’s Naam with his own efforts (he is not successful), because his bad nature due to the results of his deeds of previous lives, cannot be changed (on his own).  However, the devotees who obey Waheguru’s Huqam (Will) are recognized for their dedication in God’s Court.         3.
No one can worship Waheguru without the grace of the Guru, who reinforces His Shabad in praise of Waheguru in the heart of the Sikhs.  The follower of his mind is like a poisonous tree which aggressively bears poisonous fruit, even if it is watered with amrit (of hundreds of noble qualities).         4.          Those who have honest affection for Satguru are truthful and pure.  They obey Satguru’s Will by relinquishing all their selfishness, ego, and evil deeds.     5.
Nobody can overcome the attractions of Maya by exercising self-control with his own efforts; even the Simritis and the Shastras will tell you that.  The only way (to break the bonds of Maya), is to follow the Guru’s Shabad in the shelter of the Guru, or His devotees.         6.
Waheguru’s Naam is an inexhaustible treasure, but one can acquire the Naam only by taking shelter of the Guru with His kindness.  Such devotees look honourable (are honoured) in God’s Court.         7.        There is only one Provider (Waheguru) and none else, oh Nanak.  One can realize Him only by the benevolence of the Guru and of Waheguru.    8.2.19
