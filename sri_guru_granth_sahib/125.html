ਗਵਾਏ॥ ਆਪਿ ਡੁਬੇ ਸਗਲੇ ਕੁਲ ਡੋਬੇ ਕੂੜੁ ਬੋਲਿ ਬਿਖੁ ਖਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 6॥ ਇਸੁ ਤਨ ਮਹਿ ਮਨੁ
ਕੋ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਦੇਖੈ॥ ਭਾਇ ਭਗਤਿ ਜਾ ਹਉਮੈ ਸੋਖੈ॥ ਸਿਧ ਸਾਧਿਕ ਮੋਨਿਧਾਰੀ ਰਹੇ ਲਿਵ ਲਾਇ ਤਿਨ ਭੀ ਤਨ ਮਹਿ ਮਨੁ ਨ ਦਿਖਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 7॥ ਆਪਿ ਕਰਾਏ ਕਰਤਾ ਸੋਈ॥ ਹੋਰੁ ਕਿ ਕਰੇ ਕੀਤੈ ਕਿਆ ਹੋਈ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਜਿਸੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਦੇਵੈ ਸੋ ਲੇਵੈ ਨਾਮੋ ਮੰਨਿ ਵਸਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 8॥23॥24॥
Majh Mehla 3:  Aatum Ram pergaas(u) Gur tay hovaiy. Haumein maiyl(u) laagi Gur Shabadi khovaiy. Mun(u) nirmul(u) undin(u) bhugti raata, bhagat(i) kuray Hur(i) paavniya. 1. Houn vaari jeeo vaari, aap(i) bhagat(i)  kurn(i) uvraa bhagat(i) kraavniya.  Tinah bhagat junaa kuou sud numskaar(u) keejaiy, jo undin(u) Hur(i) goon gaavniya. 1. Ruhaou. Aapay Kerta kaarun(u) kuraayay. Jit(u) bhaavaiy tit(u) kaaraiy laayay. Pooraiy bhaag(i) Gur sayva hovaiy, Gur sayva tay sookh(u) paavniya.  2. Mur(i) mur(i) jeevaiy, ta kichh(u) paayay. Gur perasadi Hur(i) munn(i) vusaayay. Suda mookt(u) Hur(i) munn(i) vusaayay, sehjay sehj(i) smaavniya.  3. Buhou kerm kmaavaiy mookt(i) na paayay. Daysuntar(u) bhuvaiy doojaiy bhaayay khooaayay. Birtha junum(u) guvaaya kupti, bin(u) Shabadaiy dookh(u) paavniya. 4. Dhaavut(u) raakhaiy tthaak(i) ruhaayay. Gur persadi Perm Pud(u) paayay. Satgur(u) aapay maiyl(i) milaayay, mil(i) Preetum sookh(u) paavniya. 5.  
(PAGE 124):             Ikk(i) koorr(i) laagay koorray ful paayay.  Doojaiy bhaayay birtha junum(u) guvaayay. Aap(i) doobay suglay kool doabay koorr(u) boal(i) bikh(u) khaavniya. 6.  Is(u) tun meh(i) mun(u) ko Gurmukh(i) daykhaiy. Bhaayay bhagat(i) ja haumein sokhaiy. Sidh Sadhik Moandhaari ruhay liv laayay, tin bhee tun meh(i) mun(u) na dikhaavniya. 7.  Aap(i) kuraayay Kerta soyee. Hoar(u) k(i) kuray keetaiy kiya hoyee. Nanak jis(u) Naam(u) dayvaiy so layvaiy, Naamo munn(i) vusaavniya.  8.23.24.
Word Meaning: Atum Ram= Lord of soul (Waheguru).    Pergaas(u) = enlightenment. Haumein maiyl(u) = filth of selfishness and ego.   Khovaiy = lose; get rid of.    Nirmul = clean; pure.   Bhugti Raata = imbued in worship.       1.    Uveraan = others.    Tinah = them; those.  Numskaar = bow to.       Pause.       Kerta = Creator.   Kaarun(u) = creation.   Jit(u) = whomever.    Kaaraiy = in the task (of worship of Waheguru).    Pooraiy Bhaag(i) = complete luck.       2.     Mur(i) Jeevaiy = live after killing sensual desires.   Kichh(u) = something (Naam).    Mookt(u) = free from the bonds of Maya.   Sehj(i) = in the higher spiritual state.       3.       Kerm = rituals.    Daysuntar(i) Bhuvaiy = wander around in foreign countries.    Khooaayay = suffers without any achievement.     Kupti = deceitful.         4.         Dhaavut = running (wandering) fast.    Raakhaiy = control (mind).    Tthaak(i) = restraint; stop.   Perm Pud(u) = the highest spiritual state.     Preetum = beloved.      5.      Koor(i) = in false (perishable) stuff.    Ful = result.    Suglay Kool = all clans (associates).    Bikh(u) = poison (anything involving Maya).         6. Ko = some rare.  Bhaayay Bhagat(i) = in the love for worship.    Sokhaiy = absorb; destroy.   Sadhik = those who purify their body by yogic exercises.   Maondhaari = hermits (who stay quiet for a very, very long time); sages.    Liv laayay = meditate.      7.  Naamo = from Naam.          8.
MEANING:  The enlightenment About Waheguru is achieved by the grace of the Guru, as the filth of haumein is washed away by Guru’s Shabad.   Imbued with the spirit of worship day and night, one’s mind is purified, and he internalizes Waheguru.       1. I am a sacrifice for those who worship God themselves and cause others to do the same. Those devotees who sing the virtues of Waheguru day and night deserve to be bowed to (respected).            Pause.             The Creator creates everything Himself and puts the individuals He likes to work (to recite His Naam).   One serves the Guru only with perfect fortune, and enjoys all sorts of comfort as a result.             2. A person achieves something (spiritually) only if he kills his sensual desires and enshrines Waheguru’s Naam in his heart by the grace of the Guru.  When he gets Waheguru’s Naam embedded in his heart, he becomes permanently free from the bonds of Maya, and easily attains the spiritual level above the effects of Maya.         3. (A money-minded) A person performs rituals (to give the impression of being religious), but never achieves emancipation.  He wanders through foreign lands (for wealth), but suffers greatly without achieving anything spiritually.  The deceitful wastes away his precious human life and suffers without the Guru’s Shabad.         4. 
(On the other hand), A devotee controls his fast-wandering mind, restrains it, and attains the highest spiritual state by the grace of the Guru.   Satguru Himself connects the devotee with Him, and then with Waheguru; at that spiritual state, he enjoys the bliss of union with his beloved Waheguru.              5.           Some are engaged in material things and reap the false (short-lived) material rewards.  With love for Maya, they waste away their human life.  They consume poison (of worldly attractions) by telling lies (and by deceit), and as a result, drown in the sea of Maya, taking down their associates with them as well.             6.               Some rare devotee of the Guru gets rid of his haumein by worshipping Waheguru with devotion, and is able to “see” (analyze) his mind in the body.  Accomplished Sidh yogis, sages, yogis, do meditate, but they too are unable to “see” their mind in the body.         7.
The Creator Himself prompts His devotees (to sing His praise), and nobody else can do that. Only those individuals acquire Waheguru’s Naam in their mind who are prompted to do so by Waheguru Himself, Oh Nanak!        8.23.24. 
                                    
ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ ਇਸੁ ਗੁਫਾ ਮਹਿ ਅਖੁਟ ਭੰਡਾਰਾ॥ ਤਿਸੁ ਵਿਚਿ ਵਸੈ ਹਰਿ ਅਲਖ ਅਪਾਰਾ॥ ਆਪੇ ਗੁਪਤੁ ਪਰਗਟੁ ਹੈ ਆਪੇ ਗੁਰ ਸਬਦੀ ਆਪੁ ਵੰਞਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਹਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਜੀਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਨਾਮੁ ਮੰਨਿ ਵਸਾਵਣਿਆ॥ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਨਾਮੁ ਮਹਾ ਰਸੁ ਮੀਠਾ ਗੁਰਮਤੀ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਪੀਆਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਹਉਮੈ ਮਾਰਿ ਬਜਰ ਕਪਾਟ ਖੁਲਾਇਆ॥ ਨਾਮੁ ਅਮੋਲਕੁ ਗੁਰ ਪਰਸਾਦੀ ਪਾਇਆ॥ਬਿਨੁ ਸਬਦੈ ਨਾਮੁ ਨ ਪਾਵੈ ਕੋਈ ਗੁਰ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਮੰਨਿ ਵਸਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 2॥ ਗੁਰ ਗਿਆਨ ਅੰਜਨੁ ਸਚੁ ਨੇਤ੍ਰੀ ਪਾਇਆ॥ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਚਾਨਣੁ ਅਗਿਆਨੁ ਅੰਧੇਰ ਗਵਾਇਆ॥ ਜੋਤੀ ਜੋਤਿ ਮਿਲੀ ਮਨੁ ਮਾਨਿਆ ਹਰਿ ਦਰਿ ਸੋਭਾ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 3॥ ਸਰੀਰਹੁ ਭਾਲਣਿ ਕੋ ਬਾਹਰਿ ਜਾਏ॥ ਨਾਮੁ ਨ ਲਹੈ ਬਹੁਤੁ ਵੇਗਾਰਿ ਦੁਖੁ ਪਾਏ॥ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਅੰਧੇ ਸੂਝੈ ਨਾਹੀ ਫਿਰਿ ਘਿਰਿ ਆਇ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਵਥੁ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 4॥ ਗੁਰ ਪਰਸਾਦੀ ਸਚਾ ਹਰਿ ਪਾਏ॥ ਮਨਿ ਤਨਿ ਵੇਖੈ ਹਉਮੈ ਮੈਲੁ ਜਾਏ॥ ਬੈਸਿ ਸੁਥਾਨਿ ਸਦ ਹਰਿ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਵੈ ਸਚੈ ਸਬਦਿ ਸਮਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 5॥ ਨਉ ਦਰ ਠਾਕੇ ਧਾਵਤੁ ਰਹਾਏ॥ਦਸਵੈ ਨਿਜ ਘਰਿ ਵਾਸਾ ਪਾਏ॥ ਓਥੈ ਅਨਹਦ ਸਬਦ ਵਜਹਿ ਦਿਨੁ ਰਾਤੀ ਗੁਰਮਤੀ ਸਬਦੁ ਸੁਣਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 6॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਸਬਦੈ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਆਨੇਰਾ॥ ਨ ਵਸਤੁ ਲਹੈ ਨ ਚੂਕੈ ਫੇਰਾ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਹਥਿ ਕੁੰਜੀ ਹੋਰਤੁ ਦਰੁ ਖੁਲੈ ਨਾਹੀ ਗੁਰ ਪੂਰੈ ਭਾਗਿ ਮਿਲਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 7॥ ਗੁਪਤੁ ਪਰਗਟੁ ਤੂੰ ਸਭਨੀ ਥਾਈ॥ ਗੁਰ ਪਰਸਾਦੀ ਮਿਲਿ ਸੋਝੀ ਪਾਈ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮੁ ਸਲਾਹਿ ਸਦਾ ਤੂੰ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਮੰਨਿ ਵਸਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 8॥24॥25॥
Majh Mehla 3:  Iss(i) goofa meh(i) akhoot bhundara. Tis(u) vich(i) vusaiy Hur(i) alukh apaara. Aapay goopt(u) pergut(u) haiy Aapay, Gur Shabadi aap(u) vunjaavniya. 1. Huoun vaari jeeeo vaari, Amrit Naam(u) munn(i) vusaavniya. Amrit Naam(u) maha rus(u) meettha, Gurmat(i) Amrit peaavniya. 1. Ruhaou.  Haumein maar(i) bujjar kpaat khoolaaya. Naam(u) amoluk(u) Gur Persadi paaya. Bin(u) Shabadaiy Naam(u) na paavaiy koyee, Gur kirpa munn(i) vusaavniya.  2. Gur Giyan unjun(u) naytreen paaya. Untar(i) chaanun(u), ugiyaan(u) undhayr guvaaya. Joti Jot(i) mili mun(u) maaniya, Hur(i) Dur(i) sobha paavniya. 3.  Sreeroun bhaalun(i) ko baahur(i) jaayay. Naam(u) na luhaiy, buhout(u) vaygaar(i), dookh(i) paayay. Munmukh undhay soojhaiy naahi, phir(i) ghir(i) aayay, Gurmukh(i) Vuth(u) paavniya. 4.  Gur Persadi Sacha Hur(i) paayay. Mun(i) tun(i) vaykhaiy haumein maiyl(u) jaayay. Baiys(i) soothaan(i) sud Hur(i) goon gaavaiy, Sachaiy Shabad(i) smaavniya. 5. Naun dur tthaakay dhaavut(u) ruhaayay. Dusvaiy nij ghur(i) vaasa paayay. Oathaiy unhud shabad vujeh(i) din(u) raati, Gurmati Shabad(u) soonaavniya. 6.  Bin(u) Shabadaiy untar(i) aanayra. Na Vust(u) luhaiy, na chookaiy fayra. Satgur huth(i) koonji, hoarut(u) Dur(u) khoolay naahi, Gur pooraiy bhaag(i) milaavniya. 7. Goopt(u) pergut(u) Tu subhni thaayeen. Gur Persadi mil(i) sojhi paayee. Nanak Naam(u) slaaheh(i) suda Tu, Gurmukh(i) munn(i) vusaavniya.   8.24.25.
Word Meaning:  Goofa = cave; human body.   Akhoot = inexhaustible.   Bhundaara = treasures.    Tis(u) = that.    Vusaiy = resides.   Alukh = inconceivable.     Apaara = limitless.    Goopt(u) = hidden.   Pergut(u) = become visible.   Aap(u) = selfishness.  Vunjaavniya = to destroy.      1.     Munn(i) Vusaavniya = embed in mind. Maha Rus(u) = great juice (essence).       Pause.         Bujjar = stone-like.   Kpaat = doors.   Amoluk = priceless.       2.     Unjun(u) = kohl (mascara).   Naytreen = in eyes.   Joti = light (human soul).   Jot(i) = Waheguru’s light.    Sobha = praise; respect.        3. Bhaalun = search.  Luhaiy = acquire.   Vaygaar(i) = to perform somebody else’s task without any compensation.   Soojhaiy = cannot think of.   Phir(i) Ghir(i) = return (after getting tired).   Vuth = valuable article (Waheguru’s Naam).        4. Vaykhaiy = examine.    Haumein Maiyl(u) = filth of selfishness and ego.    Baiys = sit down (meditate).    Soothaan(i) = clean place (with pure mind).       5.Nau dur = nine doors of the human body (sensual pleasures).    Tthaakay = restrain.   Dhaavut = running uncontrolled (mind).     Dusvaiy = the tenth door (spiritual state of high awareness).   Nij ghur(i) = own mind.  Unhud Shabad = auto-playing Divine music that the yogis believe they hear in a higher mental state.   Vujeh(i) = plays.     6. Aanayra = hunayra = darkness.   Vust = invaluable item (Waheguru’s Naam).  Chookay = ends.   Fayra = revisit (in the world); rebirth.    Koonji = key.   Hoarut = some other.     7.  Sojhi = understanding; enlightenment.       8.
MEANING:  The inconceivable and the limitless Waheguru, which is the source of inexhaustible treasures, resides in everybody’s heart.  Waheguru is present, hidden as well as visible (in nature), but can be realized only when one kills (overcomes) his selfishness by obeying the Guru’s Shabad.          1. I offer myself for sacrifice to those who embed the immortalizing (Amrit)-Naam of Waheguru in their heart.  The Naam-Amrit is very, very sweet, but can be tasted (realized) only by the Advice of the Guru.         Pause.            When one overcomes his haumein (selfishness and ego), he gets his stone-like hard door of a higher spiritual state (called the Tenth Door, where one merges with Waheguru) opened, and realizes Waheguru’s invaluable Naam by the grace of the Guru.  Nobody can realize Waheguru’s Naam without Guru’s Shabad; it is only by the grace of the Guru that one can enshrine the Naam in his heart.            2. 
When one applies the kohl of the Guru’s Divine knowledge to his mental eyes, his mind is enlightened, and the darkness of his ignorance is gone.  He is convinced of Waheguru’s existence, and his soul merges with Waheguru, and he is honoured in His Court.        3.       (Note: women apply kohl to their eyes not only to beautify them, but also because they believe that it enhances their eyesight. Similarly, Guru’s Advice enhances devotees' spiritual awareness.          
If an ignorant person searches for Waheguru (which resides in the body) outside (in the wilderness), not only will he not realize the Naam, but he will also suffer like a person who suffers for doing a fag task (somebody else’s task without any compensation).  The ignorant fool does not realize what he is doing, but after getting exhausted, returns to the shelter of the Guru, where he can realize Naam.         4.    
One can realize Waheguru only by the Guru’s grace when he examines his body and mind (what kind of deeds does the body do and what the mind thinks), and gets the filth of haumein washed away.  Then, with a clean mind, he sings praise of Waheguru with full concentration and stays connected with Him by the Guru’s eternal Shabad.       5.               When one controls all the nine doors of the body (sensual pleasures), he attains the spiritual level where his mind stays in itself.  With Guru’s advice, at that level, he feels as if the auto-playing Divine music is playing in his mind day and night.         6.             Without the Guru’s Shabad, the mind is in complete darkness. Such an individual neither connects with the Guru’s Shabad nor has his cycle of rebirths broken.  Only Satguru has the key to the door of Waheguru’s Naam, and there is no other way to open the door. Also, one gets connected with the Guru only if one has great fortune.        7.             You are everywhere – hidden or visible – oh Waheguru!  But one realizes You only by Guru’s grace.  Sing Waheguru’s praise, and have His Naam embedded in your mind in Guru’s shelter, oh Nanak!    8.24.25.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਮਿਲੈ ਮਿਲਾਏ ਆਪੇ॥ ਕਾਲੁ ਨ ਜੋਹੈ ਦੁਖੁ ਨ ਸੰਤਾਪੇ॥ ਹਉਮੈ ਮਾਰਿ ਬੰਧਨ ਸਭ ਤੋੜੈ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਸਬਦਿ ਸੁਹਾਵਣਿਆ॥1॥ ਹਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਜੀਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮਿ ਸੁਹਾਵਣਿਆ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਗਾਵੈ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਨਾਚੈ ਹਰਿ ਸੇਤੀ ਚਿਤੁ ਲਾਵਣਿਆ॥1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥
