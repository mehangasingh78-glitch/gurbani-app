(ਅੰਗ 109)॥     ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਝੂਠਾ ਮੰਗਣੁ ਜੇ ਕੋਈ ਮਾਗੈ॥ ਤਿਸ ਕਉ ਮਰਤੇ ਘੜੀ ਨ ਲਾਗੈ॥ ਪਾਰਬ੍ਰਹਮੁ ਜੋ ਸਦ ਹੀ ਸੇਵੈ ਸੋ ਗੁਰ ਮਿਲਿ ਨਿਹਚਲੁ ਕਹਣਾ॥ 1॥ ਪ੍ਰੇਮ ਭਗਤਿ ਜਿਸ ਕੈ ਮਨਿ ਲਾਗੀ॥ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਵੈ ਅਨਦਿਨੁ ਨਿਤਿ ਜਾਗੀ॥ ਬਾਹ ਪਕੜਿ ਤਿਸੁ ਸੁਆਮੀ ਮੇਲੈ ਜਿਸ ਕੈ ਮਸਤਕਿ ਲਹਣਾ॥ 2॥ ਚਰਨ ਕਮਲ ਭਗਤਾ ਮਨਿ ਵੁਠੇ॥ ਵਿਣੁ ਪਰਮੇਸਰ ਸਗਲੇ ਮੁਠੇ॥ ਸੰਤ ਜਨਾ ਕੀ ਧੂੜਿ ਨਿਤ ਬਾਛਹਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਸਚੇ ਕਾ ਗਹਣਾ॥ 3॥ ਊਠਤ ਬੈਠਤ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਗਾਈਐ॥ ਜਿਸੁ ਸਿਮਰਤ ਵਰੁ ਨਿਹਚਲੁ ਪਾਈਐ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਕਉ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਹੋਇ ਦਇਆਲਾ ਤੇਰਾ ਕੀਤਾ ਸਹਣਾ॥ 4॥43॥50॥
(PAGE 109):                Majh Mehla 5:  Jhoottha mungun(u) jay koyee maangaiy.  Tis kuou mertay ghurree na laagaiy. Parbraham(u) jo sud hee sayvaiy, so Gur mil(i) nihchul(u) kehna. 1.  Praym bhugut(i) jis kaiy mun(i) laagi.  Goon gaavaiy undin(u), nit(i) jaagi. Baanh pukarr(i) tis(u) Soami maylaiy, jis kaiy mustuk(i) lehna. 2. Churn kumul bhugutaan mun(i) vootthay. Vin(u) Permayshur suglay mootthay. Sunt junaa kee dhoorr(i) nit baanchheh(i), Naam(u) Sachay ka gehna. 3.  Ootthut baitthut Hur(i) Hur(i) gaayiyaiy.  Jis simrut vur(u) nihchul(u) paayiyaiy.  Nanak kuou Prubh hoyay duyyala, Tayra keeta sehna.  4.43.50.
Word Meaning:  Jhoottha = perishable; mundane.    Mungun = thing wished for.   Sayvaiy = serves (obeys Huqam).   Nihchul(u) = eternal (Waheguru).      1.Undin(u) = day and night.  Nit(i) = every day.   Jaagi = awake (alert; watchful).   Mustuk(i) = on the forehead.   Lehna = to receive.        2. Churn Kumul = lotus-like feet of (Waheguru).   Vootthay = took residence; stored in memory.  Vin(u) = without.    Mootthay = got deceived.  Baanchheh(i) = long for.   Gehna = ornament; adorned with (virtues).       3. Vur(u) = husband (Waheguru).    Nihchul(u) = eternal.   Sehna = endure (gladly).   4. MEANING:  It is of no use for a person to wish for mundane things because they are perishable, and can be gone in no time.  (On the other hand), A person who serves Waheguru all the time will ask only for things which last forever (Waheguru’s Naam) in the shelter of the Guru.         1. A person who has developed love for Waheguru’s worship, sings His virtues day and night, and is alert (against all evils) all the time.   A person whose fortune is engraved on his forehead to receive (Waheguru’s Naam) is held by the arm by the Lord and connected with Him.       2. Waheguru’s lotus-like feet (His Naam) take residence in the heart of the devotees (is stored in their memory).  Without Waheguru’s Naam, all get deceived (by the captivating Maya). I long for the dust of the feet of the devotees (wish to serve them because they connect one with Waheguru).  Waheguru’s Naam is the real wealth of virtues which adorn a person (spiritually) just like ornaments adorn a woman.       3. We should recite Waheguru’s Naam whether standing or sitting (all the time) because by reciting His Naam, one acquires the eternal husband (Master) – Waheguru.  Waheguru has been very kind to me, who has enabled me to accept His Will, oh Nanak!   4.43.50.

 ਰਾਗੁ ਮਾਝ ਅਸਟਪਦੀਆ ਮਹਲਾ 1 ਘਰੁ 1॥ ੴ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਪਰਸਾਦਿ॥
  Raag Majh Ashtpudiaan Mehla 1 Ghur(u) 1:   Ek Onkar Satgur Persad(i):

ਸਬਦਿ ਰੰਗਾਏ ਹੁਕਮਿ ਸਬਾਏ॥ ਸਚੀ ਦਰਗਹ ਮਹਲਿ ਬੁਲਾਏ॥ ਸਚੇ ਦੀਨ ਦਇਆਲ ਮੇਰੇ ਸਾਹਿਬਾ ਸਚੇ ਮਨੁ ਪਤੀਆਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਹਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਜੀਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਸਬਦਿ ਸੁਹਾਵਣਿਆ॥ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਨਾਮੁ ਸਦਾ ਸੁਖਦਾਤਾ ਗੁਰਮਤੀ ਮੰਨਿ ਵਸਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਨਾ ਕੋ ਮੇਰਾ ਹਉ ਕਿਸੁ ਕੇਰਾ॥ ਸਾਚਾ ਠਾਕੁਰੁ  ਤ੍ਰਿਭਵਣਿ ਮੇਰਾ॥ ਹਉਮੈ ਕਰਿ ਕਰਿ ਜਾਇ ਘਣੇਰੀ ਕਰਿ ਅਵਗਣ ਪਛੋਤਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 2॥ ਹੁਕਮੁ ਪਛਾਣੈ ਸੁ ਹਰਿ ਗੁਣ ਵਖਾਣੇ ॥ ਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਸਬਦਿ ਨਾਮਿ ਨੀਸਾਣੈ॥ ਸਭਨਾ ਕਾ ਦਰਿ ਲੇਖਾ ਸਚੈ ਛੂਟਸਿ ਨਾਮਿ ਸੁਹਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 3॥ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਭੂਲਾ ਠਉਰੁ ਨ ਪਾਏ॥ ਜਮ ਦਰਿ ਬਧਾ ਚੋਟਾ ਖਾਏ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਨਾਵੈ ਕੋ ਸੰਗਿ ਨ ਸਾਥੀ ਮੁਕਤੇ ਨਾਮੁ ਧਿਆਵਣਿਆ॥ 4॥ ਸਾਕਤ ਕੂੜੇ ਸਚੁ ਨ ਭਾਵੈ॥ ਦੁਬਿਧਾ ਬਾਧਾ ਆਵੈ ਜਾਵੈ॥ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਲੇਖੁ ਨ ਮੇਟੈ ਕੋਈ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਮੁਕਤਿ ਕਰਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 5॥ ਪੇਈਅੜੈ ਪਿਰੁ ਜਾਤੋ ਨਾਹੀ॥ ਝੂਠਿ ਵਿਛੁੰਨੀ ਰੋਵੈ ਧਾਹੀ॥ ਅਵਗਣਿ ਮੁਠੀ ਮਹਲੁ ਨ ਪਾਏ ਅਵਗੁਣ ਗੁਣਿ ਬਖਸਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 6॥ ਪੇਈਅੜੈ ਜਿਨਿ ਜਾਤਾ ਪਿਆਰਾ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਬੂਝੈ ਤਤੁ ਬੀਚਾਰਾ॥ ਆਵਣੁ ਜਾਣਾ ਠਾਕਿ ਰਹਾਏ ਸਚੈ ਨਾਮਿ ਸਮਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 7॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਬੂਝੈ ਅਕਥੁ ਕਹਾਵੈ॥ ਸਚੇ ਠਾਕੁਰੁ ਸਾਚੋ ਭਾਵੈ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਸਚੁ ਕਹੈ ਬੇਨੰਤੀ ਸਚੁ ਮਿਲੈ ਗੁਣ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 8॥1॥

(PAGE 109 ) : Shabad(i) rungaayay Huqam(i) subaayay.  Sachi Durgeh Mehl(i) boolaayay. Sachay deen duyyaal mayray Sahiba, Sachay mun(u) putiaavniya.  1.  Huoun vaari jeeo vaari, Shabad(i) soohaavniya. Amrit Naam(u) suda Sookhdaata, Gurmuti munn(i) vusaavniya. 1. Ruhaou. Na ko mayra houn kis(u) kayra. Sacha Tthaakur(u) tirbhuvun mayra.  Haumein kur(i) kur(i) jaayay ghunayri, kur(i) uvgun puchhotaavniya. 2. Huqam(u) puchhaanaiy su Hur(i) goon vukhaanay. Gur kaiy Shabad(i) Naam(i) nisaanaiy. Subhna ka Dur(i) laykha Sachaiy, chhootus(i) Naam(i) soohaavniya. 3. Munmukh bhoola tthaur na paayay. Jum dur(i) buddha choataan khaayay. Bin(u) Naavaiy ko sung(i) na saathi, mooktay Naam(u) dhiyavuniya. 4. Saakut koorray Sach(u) na bhaavaiy. Doobidha bandha aavaiy jaavaiy. Likhiya laykh(u) na maytaiy koyee, Gurmukh(i) mookut(i) kraavniya. 5.  Payiurraiy Pir(u) jaato naahi. Jhootth(i) vichhoonni rovaiy dhaaheen. Uvgun(i) mootthi Mehl(u) na paayay, uvgoon goon(i) bukhshavniya. 6. Payiurraiy jin(i) jaata Piyara. Gurmukh(i) boojhaiy tut(u) beechaara. Aavun jaana tthaak(i) ruhayay, Sachaiy Naam(i) smaavniya. 7.  Gurmukh(i) boojhaiy Akuth(u) kuhaavaiy. Sachay Tthaakur(u) Saacho bhaavaiy.  Nanak Sach(u) kuhaiy baynti, Sach(u) milaiy goon gaavniya.  8.1.
Word Meaning:  Rungaayay = imbued.  Subaayay = all.  Durgeh = Mehl(u) = Waheguru’s Court.   Deen = humble.   Duyaal = kind.  Sachay = with Waheguru’s Naam; with pure heart.     Putiaavniya = is pleased.        1.    Vaari = sacrifice.  Soohaavniya = become pretty (with virtuous qualities).   Sookhdaata = source of comfort.    Vusaavniya = stay in memory.       Pause.      Tirbhuvun = in the three-layered universe.   Ghunayri = many, many.   Uvgoon = vices; ignoble qualities.       Puchhotaavniya = repents.        2.       Puchhaanay = recognizes.      Vukhaanay = utters; explains.   Nishaanaiy = gets recognition.  Laykha = account (good and bad deeds).   Chhootus(i) = gets freedom from the bonds of Maya; finds salvation. 
Soohaavniya = pretty; providing comfort.       3.       Bhoola = lost (from the true path).   Tthaur = shelter; peace.   Jum dur(i) = at the door of the angel of death.   Choataan = gets beaten up; suffers.   Bin(u) = without.    Sung =    company.     Mooktay = free from the bonds of Maya.     4. Saakut = worshipper of Maya; money-minded.   Koorray = who indulges in mundane things.   Doobidha = caught between Naam and Maya.   Aavaiy jaavaiy = is born again and again.    Likhiya laykh(u) = pre-written destiny.   Maytay = erase.       5.      Payiurraiy = in paternal house (this life).   Pir(u) = husband (Waheguru).   Jhootth(i) = engaged in worldly attractions.   Vichhoonni = disconnected (from Waheguru).   Dhaheen = wail.    Mootthi = deceived.    Mehl(u) = Waheguru’s residence.       6.       Boojhaiy = understands.   Tut(u) = reality; essence.   Beechaara = by pondering on.   Tthaak(i) = stop; put an end to.   Smaavniya = can merge.     7.
Akuth = indescribable (Waheguru).  Kuhaavaiy = makes others recite.   Bhaavaiy = likes.  Baynti = request.    Sach(u) = Waheguru.    8.  
MEANING:  By obeying Waheguru’s Huqam, those who have imbued their minds in the love of Waheguru are called to the Residence of Waheguru (are connected with Him). My Lord, Waheguru, is always kind to the humble, and His eternal Naam instils faith among the devotees (whose thinking turns from Maya to Waheguru).        1.  
I will gladly offer my life for the blissful Shabad in Waheguru’s praise.  Waheguru’s immortalizing Naam is always a source of comfort, but can be embedded in the mind only by the advice of the Guru.         Pause.         Nobody is mine, and I belong to nobody but Waheguru, which is pervading the whole universe.  (Only Waheguru is the real companion of man; all other relations are short-lived).  Countless people leave this world after performing deeds of haumein, and then repent for their ignoble deeds.       2.
An individual who recognizes Waheguru’s Huqam recites His virtues (Naam), and he is recognized (in Waheguru’s Court) for having the Guru’s Shabad at heart.  Everybody’s account of his deeds is maintained in Waheguru’s Court; only those who have Waheguru’s Naam at heart go unchecked (become “one” with God).            3.
The follower of his mind is misled and can find no shelter; he is punished in the prison of the Angel of Death.  Without Waheguru’s Naam, there is no friend or companion; only those are above all misdeeds who recite Waheguru’s Naam.  4. A money-minded person deals in perishable goods and does not like the Truth (Waheguru’s Naam).  Caught between Waheguru’s Naam and Maya, he is born again, and again.   The prewritten destiny (based on his deeds in previous lives) cannot be erased by anyone (he continues to commit evil deeds); only by acting on the advice of the Guru can a person be set free from the bonds of Maya.           5. An individual who does not recognize Waheguru (makes no connection with Him) in this life, and is disconnected with Him because of his dealings in mundane things, wails profusely (suffers badly).  Having been cheated by vices, he cannot access Waheguru’s Residence (make a connection with Him).  Vices can be forgiven only by acquiring virtues (by reciting Waheguru’s Naam).              6. Those who realize dear Waheguru in this life by pondering on Guru’s Shabad in His shelter are not subjected to rebirth; they become “one” with Waheguru.         7. Taking Guru’s shelter, the individual who realizes the indescribable Waheguru, makes others recite His Naam too. The eternal Lord likes Truth (recitation of His Naam). I make an honest submission that Waheguru can be realized only by singing His virtues, oh Nanak!    8.1.
