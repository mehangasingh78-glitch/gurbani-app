(ਅੰਗ 73)॥     ਤੁਧੁ ਆਪੇ ਆਪੁ ਉਪਾਇਆ॥ਦੂਜਾ ਖੇਲੁ ਕਰਿ ਦਿਖਲਾਇਆ॥ ਸਭੁ ਸਚੋ ਸਚੁ ਵਰਤਦਾ ਜਿਸੁ ਭਾਵੈ ਤਿਸੈ ਬੁਝਾਇ ਜੀਉ॥ 20 ॥ ਗੁਰ ਪਰਸਾਦੀ ਪਾਇਆ॥ ਤਿਥੈ ਮਾਇਆ ਮੋਹੁ ਚੁਕਾਇਆ॥ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਕਰਿ ਕੈ ਆਪਣੀ ਆਪੇ ਲਏ ਸਮਾਇ ਜੀਉ॥ 21॥ ਗੋਪੀ ਨੈ ਗੋਆਲੀਆ॥ ਤੁਧੁ ਆਪੇ ਗੋਇ ਉਠਾਲੀਆ॥ ਹੁਕਮੀ ਭਾਡੇ ਸਾਜਿਆ ਤੂੰ ਆਪੇ ਭੰਨਿ ਸਵਾਰਿ ਜੀਉ॥ 22॥ ਜਿਨ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਸਿਉ ਚਿਤੁ ਲਾਇਆ॥ ਤਿਨੀ ਦੂਜਾ ਭਾਉ ਚੁਕਾਇਆ॥ ਨਿਰਮਲ ਜੋਤਿ ਤਿਨ ਪ੍ਰਾਣੀਆ ਓਇ ਚਲੇ ਜਨਮੁ ਸਵਾਰਿ ਜੀਉ॥ 23॥ ਤੇਰੀਆ ਸਦਾ ਸਦਾ ਚੰਗਿਆਈਆ॥ ਮੈ ਰਾਤਿ ਦਿਹੈ ਵਡਿਆਈਆ॥ ਅਣਮੰਗਿਆ ਦਾਨੁ ਦੇਵਣਾ ਕਹੁ ਨਾਨਕ ਸਚੁ ਸਮਾਲਿ ਜੀਉ॥ 24॥1॥

MEANING:  (Being omnipresent) You are a Yogi among the yogis, and a family man among the family men, oh Waheguru.  Nobody has ever found Your limits in the sky, on the earth, and in the underworld.           1.          I wish to sacrifice myself for Your Naam (magnanimity).         Pause.         You created the whole world and gave everyone responsibility (putting everyone in worldly affairs) according to their suitability.  Now, You are watching your creation, in which humans are acting like pawns in a game of dice.          2.         You are visible in the world through Nature, and everybody longs for Your Naam.  However, the whole world is enchanted by the gimmicks of Maya, and no one can acquire Waheguru’s Naam without the Guru’s benevolence.         3.         One should offer one's life to the Guru with whom one connects and attains a high spiritual level. Satguru enlightens the individuals to realize Waheguru, whom the gods, men, and the Moanies long for.           4.          (If someone asks) What a holy congregation should be like, (then Guru Sahib’s response is): it is a gathering where only Waheguru’s Naam is explained and discussed.  Satguru has declared that only Waheguru’s Naam is the command (of Waheguru) to be followed (for union with Him).         5.
This world has been misled in doubts (caught between Maya and Waheguru), and Waheguru Himself has misguided it.  The unfortunate human-wives (souls) who have no good fortune inscribed on their foreheads and have been discarded by the Husband (Waheguru) suffer gravely.        6.        How can a dohaagun(i) be recognized?  They are disconnected from their Waheguru- Husband, and spend their lives in disrespect.  Their dresses (bodies) are dirty (they are unattractive because of their ignoble deeds), and the night of their life is spent in suffering.         7.         Q:  What kind of deeds do the individuals perform to be a sohaagun(i) (to enjoy the love of the husband)? A:  They reap the rewards of their virtuous deeds of previous lives, as a result of which, Waheguru Himself connects them with Him by casting His kind sight on them, and they are loved by Him.         8.
Waheguru fosters His Naam in the hearts of the human wives whom He makes to obey His Will.  Those friends (devotees) who win Waheguru’s love are sohaaguns.       9.
Those who enjoy the taste of Waheguru’s Will have their doubts cleared.   The Satguru is the one who connects everybody with Waheguru.        10.
As a result of connecting with Satguru, the individual's ego disappears.  Good fortune appears on his forehead, and his suffering from the ill-advice is destroyed.     11.
Guru’s Shabad in Your praise is Amrit, and it takes dwelling in the heart of Your devotees, oh Waheguru.   I feel great comfort when You keep me in Your service; You salvage people by Your kind sight, oh Lord.            12.         An individual’s connection with Satguru is meaningful only if he worships Waheguru’s Naam in the shelter of Satguru. Nobody can realize Waheguru without the shelter of Satguru, no matter how many other rituals he performs, or even exhausts himself performing accepted deeds of kindness.       13. 
I am a sacrifice to the Satguru who clears all our doubts and guides us on the right path (to union with Waheguru). Waheguru Himself connects some lucky individuals with Him if He casts His kind sight on them.        14.          Even though the Creator, Waheguru, is present in everyone, He is invisible.  He appears in the heart of only those fortunate individuals who come to Satguru’s shelter and in whom Waheguru places His light.          15.
The Lord Himself creates humans, neatly giving them body and life, and Himself confers honour on His devotees. He protects their dignity (against Maya's attacks) and rescues them by placing His kind hands on their foreheads.         16.
NOTE:  God is formless. “Placing both His hands” has been used by Guru Sahib for our understanding only.
All wisdom and all efforts to control sensual feelings become insignificant for the devotees because Waheguru knows their intentions and protects them.  Waheguru spreads the fame of His devotees in the world, and everybody sings their praise.         17.
Waheguru did not consider my virtues or vices; He simply exercised His eternal nature of kindness. He took me in His embrace and did not let me suffer even a little.        18.
I worshipped Waheguru with my mind and body, and reaped the fruit of my wishes.  God is the Lord of all kings and emperors; I survive only by reciting His Naam.      19. 
God creates Himself, and Maya (to sustain living beings' interest in the world).  It is Waheguru, which is operating everywhere, but only those few people of His choosing understand this fact, whom He enlightens.         20.        Waheguru is realized only by Satguru’s benevolence; Satguru enlightens individuals so that they lose all interest in Maya.  Waheguru then unites those individuals with Him by His kindness.          21.        (People worship Sri Krishna, who used to graze cows by the banks of the River Yamuna, in the company of cowgirls, and who later lifted Mount Govardhan on his little finger to use that as an umbrella to protect the masses from incessant rain), but (being omnipresent), it was You, oh Lord, who did all that. The Lord Waheguru creates all bodies and destroys them, reshaping them for their spiritual improvement.         22.           Those who fall in love with Satguru lose all interest in Maya. Their souls become pure, and they leave this world clean.         23.
You are always very kind and forgiving, oh Waheguru. I sing Your praise day and night for Your noble qualities.  Waheguru fulfils all your needs even without you asking for them, so man must remember Him at all times, oh Nanak.    24.

                                           Siri Raag(u) Mehla 5:  
ਪੈ ਪਾਇ ਮਨਾਈ ਸੋਇ ਜੀਉ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਪੁਰਖਿ ਮਿਲਾਇਆ ਤਿਸੁ ਜੇਵਡੁ ਅਵਰੁ ਨ ਕੋਇ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਗੋਸਾਈ ਮਿਹੰਡਾ ਇਠੜਾ॥ ਅੰਮ ਅਬੇ ਥਾਵਹੁ ਮਿਠੜਾ॥ ਭੈਣ ਭਾਈ ਸਭਿ ਸਜਣਾ ਤੁਧੁ ਜੇਹਾ ਨਾਹੀ ਕੋਇ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਤੇਰੇ ਹੁਕਮੈ ਸਾਵਣੁ ਆਇਆ॥ ਮੈ ਸਤ ਕਾ ਹਲੁ ਜੋਆਇਆ॥ ਨਾਉ ਬੀਜਣ ਲਗਾ ਆਸ ਕਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਬੋਹਲ ਬਖਸ ਜਮਾਇ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਹਉ ਗੁਰ ਮਿਲਿ ਇਕੁ ਪਛਾਣਦਾ॥ ਦੁਯਾ ਕਾਗਲੁ ਚਿਤਿ ਨ ਜਾਣਦਾ॥ ਹਰਿ ਇਕਤੈ ਕਾਰੈ ਲਾਇਓਨੁ ਜਿਉ ਭਾਵੈ ਤਿਵੈ ਨਿਬਾਹਿ ਜੀਉ॥ 3॥ ਤੁਸੀ ਭੋਗਿਹੁ ਭੁੰਚਹੁ ਭਾਈਹੋ॥ ਗੁਰਿ ਦੀਬਾਣਿ ਕਵਾਇ ਪੈਨਾਈਓ॥ ਹਉ ਹੋਆ ਮਾਹਰੁ ਪਿੰਡ ਦਾ ਬੰਨਿ ਆਦੇ ਪੰਜਿ ਸਰੀਕ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥ ਹਉ ਆਇਆ ਸਾਮ੍ਹੈ ਤਿਹੰਡੀਆ॥ ਪੰਜਿ ਕਿਰਸਾਣ ਮੁਜੇਰੇ ਮਿਹੰਡਿਆ॥ ਕੰਨੁ ਕੋਈ ਕਢਿ ਨ ਹੰਘਈ ਨਾਨਕ ਵੁਠਾ ਘੁਘਿ ਗਿਰਾਉ ਜੀਉ॥ 5॥  ਹਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਘੁੰਮਾ ਜਾਵਦਾ॥ ਇਕ ਸਾਹਾ ਤੁਧੁ ਧਿਆਇਦਾ॥ ਉਜੜੁ ਥੇਹੁ ਵਸਾਇਓ ਹਉ ਤੁਧੁ ਵਿਟਹੁ ਕੁਰਬਾਣੁ ਜੀਉ॥ 6॥  ਹਰਿ ਇਠੈ ਨਿਤ ਧਿਆਇਦਾ॥ ਮਨਿ ਚਿੰਦੀ ਸੋ ਫਲੁ ਪਾਇਦਾ॥ ਸਭੇ ਕਾਜ ਸਵਾਰਿਅਨੁ ਲਾਹੀਅਨੁ ਮਨ ਕੀ ਭੁਖ ਜੀਉ॥ 7॥  ਮੈ ਛਡਿਆ ਸਭੋ ਧੰਧੜਾ॥ ਗੋਸਾਈ ਸੇਵੀ ਸਚੜਾ॥ ਨਉ ਨਿਧਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਨਿਧਾਨੁ ਹਰਿ ਮੈ ਪਲੈ ਬਧਾ ਛਿਕਿ ਜੀਉ॥ 8॥ ਮੈ ਸੁਖੀ ਹੂੰ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਾਇਆ॥ ਗੁਰ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਸਬਦੁ ਵਸਾਇਆ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰਿ ਪੁਰਖਿ ਵਿਖਾਲਿਆ ਮਸਤਕਿ ਧਰਿ ਕੈ ਹਥੁ ਜੀਉ॥ 9॥ ਮੈ ਬਧੀ ਸਭ ਧਰਮਸਾਲ ਹੈ॥ ਗੁਰਸਿਖਾ ਲਹਦਾ ਭਾਲਿ ਕੈ॥ ਪੈਰ ਧੋਵਾ ਪਖਾ ਫੇਰਦਾ ਤਿਸੁ ਨਿਵਿ ਨਿਵਿ ਲਗਾ ਪਾਇ ਜੀਉ॥ 10॥
