(ਅੰਗ 24)॥           ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 1 ਘਰੁ 3॥ ਅਮਲੁ ਕਰਿ ਧਰਤੀ ਬੀਜੁ ਸਬਦੋ ਕਰਿ ਸਚ ਕੀ ਆਬ ਨਿਤ ਦੇਹ ਪਾਣੀ॥ ਹੋਇ ਕਿਰਸਾਣੁ ਈਮਾਨੁ ਜੰਮਾਇ ਲੈ ਭਿਸਤੁ ਦੋਜਕੁ ਮੂੜੇ ਏਵ ਜਾਣੀ॥ 1॥ ਮਤੁ ਜਾਣਸਹਿ ਗਲੀ ਪਾਇਆ॥ ਮਾਲ ਕੈ ਮਾਣੈ ਰੂਪ ਕੀ ਸੋਭਾ ਇਤੁ ਬਿਧੀ ਜਨਮੁ ਗਵਾਇਆ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਐਬ ਤਨਿ ਚਿਕੜੋ ਇਹੁ ਮਨੁ ਮੀਡਕੋ ਕਮਲ ਕੀ ਸਾਰ ਨਹੀ ਮੂਲਿ ਪਾਈ॥ ਭਉਰੁ ਉਸਤਾਦੁ ਨਿਤ ਭਾਖਿਆ ਬੋਲੇ ਕਿਉ ਬੂਝੈ ਜਾ ਨਹ ਬੁਝਾਈ॥ 2॥ ਆਖਣੁ ਸੁਨਣਾ ਪਉਣ ਕੀ ਬਾਣੀ ਇਹੁ ਮਨ ਰਤਾ ਮਾਇਆ॥ ਖਸਮ ਕੀ ਨਦਰਿ ਦਿਲਹਿ ਪਸਿੰਦੇ ਜਿਨੀ ਕਰਿ ਏਕੁ ਧਿਆਇਆ॥ 3॥ ਤੀਹ ਕਰਿ ਰਖੇ ਪੰਜ ਕਰਿ ਸਾਥੀ ਨਾਉ ਸੈਤਾਨੁ ਮਤੁ ਕਟਿ ਜਾਈ॥ ਨਾਨਕੁ ਆਖੈ ਰਾਹਿ ਪੈ ਚਲਣਾ ਮਾਲੁ ਧਨੁ ਕਿਤ ਕੂ ਸੰਜਿਆਹੀ॥ 4॥27॥
(PAGE 24):               Siri Raag(u) Mehla 1, Ghur(u) 3: Uml(u) kur(i) dherti, beej(u) Shabado kur(i), Sach kee aab nit dayh paani. Hoyay kirsan(u), eemaan(u) jummayay laiy, bhisht, dojuk(u) moorray aiv jaani.  1.  Mut(u) jaanseh(i) gulleen paaya. Maal kaiy maanaiy, roop kee sobha, it(u) bidhi junum(u) guvaaya. Ruhaou. Aiyb tun(i) chikkrro, eh(u) mun(u) meendko, kumul kee saar nuhhi mool(i) paayi.  Bhaur Ustad(u) nit bhaakhiya boalay, kiyoun boojhaiy ja neh boojhaayi.  2.  Aakhun(u) soon-na paun kee baani, eh(u) mun rutta Maya. Khusm nudr(i) dileh(i) pusinday, jinhi kur(u) Aik(u) dhiyaaya. 3. Teeh kur(i) rukhay, punj kur(I saathi, naaoun Shaitan(u) mut(u) kut(i) jaayi. Nanak(u) aakhaiy raah(i) paiy chullna, maal(u) dhun(u) kit koo sunjiaahi. 4-27.
Word Meaning: Uml(u) = deeds.   Aab = shine; splendour.   Eemaan = faith; reverence.  Jummayay Laiy = cultivate.    Bhist = paradise.     Dojuk = hell.     Aiv = like this.      1.
Mut Jaanseh(i) = do not think.     Maal = wealth.    Maanaiy = in pride.  Roop = beauty.   Sobha = praise; fame.   It(u) Bidh(i) = in this way.          Pause.        Aib(u) = vices.   Chikkrro = mud.    Meendko = frog.      Kumul = lotus flower.   Saar = worth.  Mool(i) NUhhi = not at all.    Bhaur = bumblebee.   Ustad(u) = Guru.   Bhaakhiya Boalay = preaches.   Boojhaiy = comprehend.        2.           Paun Kee Bani = words in the air. Dileh(i) = in heart.  Pusinday = to like.     3.
Teeh = 30 (Rozsa).   Punj = five (Nivajes; Muslim prayers).    Mut = maybe.   Sunjiaahi = 
accumulate.    4.
MEANING: Like a farmer, if you make your deeds (thinking; mind) the field, plant the seeds of Satguru’s Shabad (your deeds should be according to the Guru’s Teachings), in the mind filed, and irrigate the field with the splendour of Waheguru with full faith, His Naam will sprout in your mind field, and you will know what paradise and the hell (connection with, and separation from Waheguru) are, oh my friends.         1.         
Never, ever believe that you can realize Waheguru just by talking (about Him).  (With this misbelief), The man wastes away his precious human life in his conceit of Maya and the fame of his handsomeness.           Pause.
The human body is full of the mud of vices, and the mind is like the frog in that mud, which does not understand the worth of the lotus growing in that mud (Waheguru, which is present in the same body).  The bumblebee hovers around the lotus flower with the musical sound of its flapping wings, but the frog does not recognize the worth of the lotus flower (Satguru delivers His Teachings, but the frog-like mind does not listen to that).  Actually, how can the Maya-engrossed human mind understand that until Waheguru exhorts him to do so?              2.             With his mind imbued in his love for Maya, Satguru’s Shabad is simply words in the air for the man. But those who remember the “One” (Waheguru) with devotion, love simply a kind sight of Waheguru.         3.         A Muslim observes the thirty Rozas, and offers prayers five times a day with the belief that his name will be dropped from Satan’s list (he will be a good human in the eyes of Allah, and will be approved by Him); but all such holy deeds are spiritually worthless because) he is still imbued in Maya, and accumulates it.  The only way to realize Waheguru (Allah) is to tread the path of righteousness, oh Nanak.        4.27.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 1 ਘਰੁ 4॥ ਸੋਈ ਮਉਲਾ ਜਿਨਿ ਜਗੁ ਮਉਲਿਆ ਹਰਿਆ ਕੀਆ ਸੰਸਾਰੋ॥ ਆਬੁ ਖਾਕੁ ਜਿਨਿ ਬੰਧਿ ਰਹਾਈ ਧੰਨੁ ਸਿਰਜਣਹਾਰੋ॥ 1॥ ਮਰਣਾ ਮੁਲਾ ਮਰਣਾ॥ ਭੀ ਕਰਤਾਰਹੁ ਡਰਣਾ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਤਾ ਤੂ ਮੁਲਾ ਤਾ ਤੂ ਕਾਜੀ ਜਾਣਹਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਖੁਦਾਈ॥ ਜੇ ਬਹੁਤੇਰਾ ਪੜਿਆ ਹੋਵਹਿ ਕੋ ਰਹੈ ਨ ਭਰੀਐ ਪਾਈ॥ 2॥ ਸੋਈ ਕਾਜੀ ਜਿਨਿ ਆਪੁ ਤਜਿਆ ਇਕੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਕੀਆ ਆਧਾਰੋ॥ ਹੈ ਭੀ ਹੋਸੀ ਜਾਇ ਨ ਜਾਸੀ ਸਚਾ ਸਿਰਜਣਹਾਰੋ॥ 3॥ ਪੰਜ ਵਖਤ ਨਿਵਾਜ ਗੁਜਾਰਹਿ ਪੜਹਿ ਕਤੇਬ ਕੁਰਾਣਾ॥ ਨਾਨਕੁ ਆਖੈ ਗੋਰ ਸਦੇਹੀ ਰਹਿਓ ਪੀਣਾ ਖਾਣਾ॥  4॥28॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 1, Ghur(u) 4: Soyi maula, Jinh(i) jug(u) mauliya, huriya keeya sunsaro. Aab(u), khaak(u), Jinh(i) bundh(i) ruhaayi, dhunn(u) Sirjunhaaro.  1. Merna, Moolah merna. Bhee Kartaroun derna. Ruhaou. Ta tu moolah, ta tu qazi, jaaneh(i) Naam(u) Khudaayi. Jay buhoutayra purrhiya hoveh(i), ko ruhhaiy na bhuriyaiy paayi. 2. Soyi qazi, jin(i) aap(u) tujjiya, Ikk(u) Naam(u) keeya adhaaro. Haiy bhee, hosi, jaayay na jaasi, Sacha Sirjunhaaro. 3. Punj vukht nivaj goojaareh(i), purreh(i) Kutayb, Qurana. Nanak(u) aakhaiy goar sudayhi, ruhhiyo peena, khana. 4.28.  
Word Meaning: Maula = bloom; Lord.    Mauliya = cause to bloom; energize.    Aab(u) = water.   Khaak = dust.    Dhunn(u) = great; praiseworthy.        1.        Kartaroun = from Waheguru.      Pause.      Bhuriyaiy Paayi = when the time is up.  Paayi = an old device to measure time. A standard bowl with holes at the bottom was placed on the surface of water.  Water would enter the bowl through the holes, and when the device was full, it would sink. The time for the bowl to sink was called a “Ghurri” which was equal to 24 minutes.         2.     
Aap(u) = one’s selfishness and ego.    Tujiya = abandoned.  Adhaaro = made the basis.     Sirjunhaaro = the Creator (Waheguru).         3.        Goar = grave.        4.
MEANING: Everybody has to die one day; so, you must be afraid of Waheguru (Allah), oh Moolah (because only His Naam can destroy the fear of death).       Pause.
The Lord (Waheguru), who has bonded water and dust together to create the world, which is the source of all life, and which causes the world to bloom and energizes it, is great. (So, sing His praise).        1.       Only then you are a true Moolah or a Qazi, if you enshrine Allah’s Naam in you heart.  Nobody stays in this world after his allotted time is up – no matter how highly literate he is.            2.         The eternal Creator (Waheguru) is here, will always be here, and will never go away. So, only that individual is a true qazi who has rid himself of selfishness and ego and has made Allah the basis of his life.          3.         The graveyard (death) is calling everybody, when all his eating and drinking will end even if he offers nivaj five times a day, and reads the Quran or the other holy books of the Western religions, oh Nanak.    4.28.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 1 ਘਰੁ 4॥ ਏਕੁ ਸੁਆਨੁ ਦੁਇ ਸੁਆਨੀ ਨਾਲਿ॥ ਭਲਕੇ ਭਉੇਕਹਿ ਸਦਾ ਬਇਆਲਿ॥ ਕੂੜੁ ਛੂਰਾ ਮੁਠਾ ਮੁਰਦਾਰੁ॥ ਧਾਣਕ ਰੂਪ ਰਹਾ ਕਰਤਾਰ॥ 1॥ ਮੈ ਪਤਿ ਕੀ ਪੰਦਿ ਨ ਕਰਣੀ ਕੀ ਕਾਰ॥ ਹਉ ਬਿਗੜੈ ਰੂਪ ਰਹਾ ਬਿਕਰਾਲ॥ ਤੇਰਾ ਏਕੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਤਾਰੇ ਸੰਸਾਰੁ॥ ਮੈ ਏਹਾ ਆਸ ਏਹੋ ਆਧਾਰੁ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਮੁਖਿ ਿਂਨੰਦਾ ਆਖਾ ਦਿਨੁ ਰਾਤਿ॥ ਪਰ ਘਰੁ ਜੋਹੀ ਨੀਚ ਸਨਾਤਿ॥ ਕਾਮੁ ਕ੍ਰੋਧ ਤਨਿ ਵਸਹਿ ਚੰਡਾਲ॥ ਧਾਣਕ ਰੂਪ ਰਹਾ ਕਰਤਾਰ॥ 2॥ ਫਾਹੀ ਸੁਰਤਿ ਮਲੂਕੀ ਵੇਸੁ॥ ਹਉ ਠਗਵਾੜਾ ਠਗੀ ਦੇਸੁ॥ ਖਰਾ ਸਿਆਣਾ ਬਹੁਤਾ ਭਾਰੁ॥ ਧਾਣਕ ਰੂਪ ਰਹਾ ਕਰਤਾਰ॥ 3॥ ਮੈ ਕੀਤਾ ਨ ਜਾਤਾ ਹਰਾਮਖੋਰੁ॥ ਹਉ ਕਿਆ ਮੁਹੁ ਦੇਸਾ ਦੁਸਟੁ ਚੋਰੁ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨੀਚੁ ਕਹੈ ਬੀਚਾਰੁ॥ ਧਾਣਕ ਰੂਪ ਰਹਾ ਕਰਤਾਰ॥ 4॥29॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 1, Ghur(u) 4: Aik sooaan, dooyay sooaani naal(i). Bhulkay bhaunkeh(i), sudaa buyyal(i). Koorr(u) chhoora, moottha moordaar(u). Dhaanuk roop, ruhha Kartar. 1. Mein Put(i) kee pund(i), na kerni kee kaar. Huoun bigrraiy roop ruhha bikraal. Tayra aik(u) Naam(u) taaray sunsaar(u). Mein eha aas, eho adhaar(u).  Ruhaou. Mookh(i) ninda aakhaan din(u) raat(i). Pur ghur(u) joheen, neech, sunaat(i). Kaam(u) kroadh tun(i) vuseh(i) chundaal. Dhaanuk roop ruhha Kartar. 2. Phaahi soort(i), malooki vays(u). Huoun tthugwaarra, tthuggeen days(u). Khuraa siyaana, buhouta bhaar(u). Dhaanuk roop ruhha Kartar. 3. Mein keeta na jaata, haraamkhor(u). Huoun kiyya moonh daysaan, doosht(u) choar(u). Nanak, neech(u) kuhhaiy beechaar(u). Dhaanuk roop ruhha Kartar. 4.29.
Word Meaning: Sooaan = dog (greed).   Sooaani = female dog (hopes and desires).   Bhulkay = everyday.  Bhaunkeh(i) = bark; make loud demands.  Buyyal(i) = early morning.   Koorr(u) = falsehood; lies.  Chhoora = big knife.   Moottha = robbed.    Moordaar(u) = belongings of others.   Dhaanuk Roop = contemptuous lifestyle.         1.        
Put(i) = Master.    Pund(i) = advice.    Kerni Kee Kaar = deeds worth performing.      Biggraiy Roop = with twisted thinking.  Bikraal = frightening.  Adhaar(u) = support; basis.         Pause.
Ninda = vilification.   Joheen = keep an evil eye.   Neech Sunaat(i) = ignoble; of low caste.  Chundaal(u) = ruthless.        2.       Phaahi Soort(i) = (have) thinking to trap (people to deceive them).     Malooki Yays = garb of holy people.  Tthugwaarra = home of deception.   Khuraa = too much.      Buouhta Bhaar(u) = heavy burden of sins.          3.         Keeta Na Jaata = did not acknowledge favours.      Haraamkhor(u) = hugely ungrateful.    Moonh Daysaan = show my face.    Doosht(u) = evil. Choar(u) = thief.         4. 
MEANING: (Please be kind to me; I have to make the following confession, oh my Waheguru.) I lead a contemptuous lifestyle as I have doglike greed, and female-doglike hopes and desires in my mind, which make loud demands starting early in the morning, every day.  I have the knife of deceits and lies in my hand (to defraud others), and am being robbed (of my good qualities, if I am left with any) by my evil deeds of robbing others.           1.              I do not listen to the advice of the Master (Satguru) and do not perform the noble deeds as per His Advice.  I have become frighteningly twisted in my thinking. My only hope and my only basis are Your Naam, which salvages the whole world.            Pause.
I am so ignoble and so low-caste that I maliciously criticize (vilify) others day and night, and keep an evil eye on the homes of others. (On top of those), Ruthless lust and fury keep occupying my mind. Such is my contemptuous lifestyle, oh my Waheguru.          2.
Even though I don an outfit of holy-looking men, trapping others in my net of deceit, is always on my mind. Indeed, my mind is a home of treachery, and I deceive the world. I consider myself very smart, but carry a heavy load of sins, oh my Waheguru.         3.        I am hugely ungrateful to You as I do not acknowledge Your favours and benevolence for me. I am such an evil thief, that I cannot show you my ugly face. After deep thinking, I, Nanak, have realized that I am an ignoble, and such contemptuous is my lifestyle, oh Waheguru.    4.29.
