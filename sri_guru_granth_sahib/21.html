(ਅੰਗ 20)॥        ਪੰਚ ਭੂਤ ਸਚਿ ਭੈ ਰਤੇ ਜੋਤਿ ਸਚੀ ਮਨ ਮਾਹਿ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਅਉਗਣ ਵੀਸਰੇ ਗੁਰਿ ਰਾਖੇ ਪਤਿ ਤਾਹਿ॥ 4॥15॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 1:  Tun jul(i), bul(i) maati bhuyya, mun(u) Maya moh(i), munoor. Augun phir laagoo bhuyyay, koor(i) vujaavaiy toor. Bin(u) Shabadaiy, bhermaayiyaiy, doobidha doabay poor(u).  1. Mun ray, Shabad(i) turhou chit(u) laayay. Jinh(i) Gurmukh(i) Naam(u) na boojhiya, mur(i) junmaiy, aavaiy, jaayay. Ruhaou. Tun(u) soocha so aakhiyaiy, jis meh(i) Saacha Naaoun. Bhaiy, Sach(i) raati dayhoori, jihva Sach(u) sooaaou. Sachi nedr(i) nihaliyaiy, buhourr na paavaiy taaou. 2. Saachay tay puvna bhuyya, puvnaiy tay jul hoyay. Jul tay tirbhuvun saajiya, ghut(i), ghut(i) Jot(i) smoyay. Nirmul maiyla na theeyaiy, Shabad ruttay put(i) hoyay. 3. Eh(u) mun(u) Saach(i) suntokhiya, nedr(i) kuray Tis(u) maah(i). 
(PAGE 20):           Punch bhoot Sach(i) bhaiy ruttay, Jot(i) Sachi mun maah(i). Nanak, augun veesray, raakhay put(i) taah(i).  4.15.
Word Meaning:  Munoor = burned (brittle) iron.     Augun = ignoble qualities.   Laagoo Bhuyyay = on the attack.  Vujaavaiy Toor = play loud music; make much noise; begin to prevail.    Bhermaayiyaiy = misled in misbeliefs.   Doobhida = indecisiveness (to choose between Waheguru and Maya).  Poor = groups.        1.       Turhou = swim across.     Boojhiya = realized; made a connection with.       Pause.         Soocha = pure.    Raati = imbued.   Dayhoori = body.   Sooaaou = taste.   Nihaaliyaiy = see; cast.    Taaou = heat of the womb.           2.            Puvna = air; gases.     Tirbhuvun = three-dimensional (solid); universe.    Nirmul = clean; pure.   Theeyaiy = becomes.    Put(i) = dignity.        3.       Saach(i) = with the Naam.    Suntokhiya = contented.    Punch Bhoot = the five demons.      Bhaiy Ruttay =   imbued in love-filled fear.    Taah(i) = of those.       4.   
MEANING:  Without the Guru’s Shabad, huge groups of people (countless people) are misled by the attractions of Maya and drown in its sea because of their indecisiveness.  Man’s body burns in the fire of desires (which are not always fulfilled) to become cheap like dirt, and his mind becomes like burnt (brittle) iron (he loses his morality and patience).   His ignoble qualities attack him like an enemy, and the falsehood (lies and deceit) begin to prevail.         1.      Swim across the sea of worldly attractions by focusing on Guru’s Shabad, oh my mind.   Those who do not realize Waheguru’s Naam in the shelter of the Guru die and are reborn again, and again.         Pause.             The human body is considered pure, with Waheguru’s eternal Naam enshrined within it.  Such a body (man) is imbued in Waheguru’s love-filled fear, and the tongue enjoys the taste of His Naam.   Waheguru casts kind sight on such an individual, who never suffers the heat of the womb again (is never reborn).         2.         Waheguru created gases from Himself, which were converted into water, and then into solids to create the universe. Waheguru’s Jot(i) (Soul) is present in every heart.   The minds of those who are imbued with the love of the Guru’s Shabad get purified, never to gather filth (of immoral deeds) again, and they earn respect everywhere.            3.           Such devotees find full contentment with Waheguru’s Naam, who always casts a kind sight on them.  With their minds enlightened with Waheguru, their five demons also get imbued with the fear of Waheguru (and stay within their limits).  All their ignoble qualities disappear, and the Guru protects their dignity, oh Nanak.    4.15.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 1॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਬੇੜੀ ਸਚ ਕੀ ਤਰੀਐ ਗੁਰ ਵੀਚਾਰਿ॥ ਇਕਿ ਆਵਹਿ ਇਕਿ ਜਾਵਹੀ ਪੂਰ ਭਰੇ ਅਹੰਕਾਰਿ॥ ਮਨ ਹਠ ਮਤੀ ਬੂਡੀਐ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਸਚੁ ਸੁ ਤਾਰਿ॥ 1॥ ਗੁਰ ਬਿਨੁ ਕਿਉ ਤਰੀਐ ਸੁਖੁ ਹੋਇ॥ ਜਿਉ ਭਾਵੈ ਤਿਉ ਰਾਖੁ ਤੂ ਮੈ ਅਵਰੁ ਨ ਦੂਜਾ ਕੋਇ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਆਗੈ ਦੇਖਉ ਡਉ ਜਲੈ ਪਾਛੈ ਰਹਿਓ ਅੰਗੂਰ॥ ਜਿਸ ਤੇ ਉਪਜੈ ਤਿਸ ਤੇ ਬਿਨਸੈ ਘਟਿ ਘਟਿ ਸਚੁ ਭਰਪੂਰਿ॥ ਆਪੇ ਮੇਲਿ ਮਿਲਾਵਹੀ ਸਾਚੈ ਮਹਲਿ ਹਦੂਰਿ॥ 2॥ ਸਾਹਿ ਸਾਹਿ ਤੁਝੁ ਸੰਮਲਾ ਕਦੇ ਨ ਵਿਸਾਰੇਉ॥ ਜਿਉ ਜਿਉ ਸਾਹਿਬ ਮਨਿ ਵਸੈ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਪੇਉ॥ ਮਨੁ ਤਨੁ ਤੇਰਾ ਤੂ ਧਣੀ ਗਰਬੁ ਨਿਵਾਰਿ ਸਮੇਉ॥ 3॥ ਜਿਨਿ ਏਹੁ ਜਗਤ ਉਪਾਇਆ ਤ੍ਰਿਭਵਣੁ ਕਰਿ ਆਕਾਰੁ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਚਾਨਣ ਜਾਣੀਐ ਮਨਮੁਖਿ ਮੁਗਧੁ ਗੁਬਾਰੁ॥ ਘਟਿ ਘਟਿ ਜੋਤਿ ਨਿਰੰਤਰੀ ਬੂਝੈ ਗੁਰਮਤਿ ਸਾਰੁ॥ 4॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਜਿਨੀ ਜਾਣਿਆ ਤਿਨ ਕੀਚੈ ਸਾਬਾਸਿ॥ ਸਚੇ ਸੇਤੀ ਰਲਿ ਮਿਲੇ ਸਚੇ ਗੁਣ ਪਰਗਾਸਿ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮਿ ਸੰਤੋਖੀਆ ਜੀਉ ਪਿੰਡ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਪਾਸਿ॥ 5॥16॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 1: Nanak, bayrri Sach kee, turiyaiy Gur veechaar(i). Ikk(i) aaveh, ikk(i) jaavhi, poor bhuray uhunkaar(i). Mun hutth mutee boodiyaiy, Gurmukh(i) Sach(u) su taar(i). 1. Gur bin(u) kiyoun turiyaiy, sookh hoyay. Jiyoun bhaavaiy, tiyoun raakh Tu, mein uver(u) na dooja koyay. Ruhaou. Aagaiy daykhoun duou julaiy, paachhaiy ruhhiyo angoor. Jis tay oopjaiy, Tis tay binsaiy, ghut(i), ghut(i) Sach(u) bherpoor. Aapay mayl(i) milaavhi, Saachaiy Mehl(i) hudoor(i). 2. Saah(i), saah(i) Toojh summlaan, kuday na visaarayoun. Jiyoun, jiyoun Sahib mun(i) vusaiy, Gurmukh(i) Amrit payoun. Mun(u), tun(u) Tayra, Tu Dhunee, gerb nivaar(i) smayoun. 3. Jinh(i) eh(u) jugut oopaaya, tirbhuvun kur(i) aakaar. Gurmukh(i) Chaanun jaaniyaiy, munmukh(i) moogudh goobaar.  Ghut(i), ghut(i) Jot(i) Niruntri, boojhaiy Gurmut(i) saar(u).  4. Gurmukh(i) jinhi jaaniya, tinh keechaiy shabaash. Sachay sayti rul(i) milay, Sachay goon pergaas(i). Nanak Naam(i) suntokhiya, jeeou, pind Prubh paas(i).  5.16.
Word Meaning:  Sach = Waheguru (His Naam).  Poor = large groups; countless people.  Mun Hutth Mutee = thinking with persistence of mind.    Boodiyaiy = drown        1.         Aagaiy = future; death.  Duou Julaiy = burning of fire; heat of womb; rebirths.   Angoor = new sprouting (births).    Oopjaiy = comes into being.   Binsay = is destroyed.    Bherpoor = fully present.   MIllavhi = connects.    Hudoor(i) = near; present.        2.        Summlaan = remember.    Visaarayoun = forget.    Dhunee = Lord.  Nivaar(i) = removing.       3.       Tirbhuvun = three-layered universe.   Aakaar = size.    Chaanun = enlightenment.   Moogudh = foolish.   Goobaar = darkness.   Niruntri = of the omnipresent (God).   Saar(u) = supreme.       4.       Shabaash = accolades.   Sayti = with.  Pergaas(i) = enlightenment.           5.
Suntokhiya = made content.  Jeeou = soul; life.   Pind = body.    Paas(i) = in; with.      5.
MEANING:  Waheguru’s Naam, which is realized by acting on the Advice of the Guru (by pondering on His Shabad), is the ship to ferry across the sea of Maya, which one should recite to find (attain) salvation.  But, because of their ego, countless people are born, and countless of them die. Those who use the persistence of their mind (have no love for God, but rely on rituals), drown in the sea of Maya, while those who realize Waheguru with the grace of the Guru, swim across.        1.       
One cannot swim across the world-sea and attain blissfulness without the Guru.   I pray to the Guru that He protect me (against the attacks of Maya) in whatever way He deems appropriate, as I have no one else to go to.         Pause.       When I look into the future, I see the heat of the womb (rebirths) for most, while the new births are following them.  Waheguru is present in everybody, and it is He from whom everybody comes into being, and who destroys them.  Waheguru is not far away; He is close to us (lives in every heart, which is His dwelling). He Himself connects people to Him through the Guru.           2.
I wish to remember You with every breath, and never forget You, oh Waheguru. In the shelter of the Guru, as the Lord appears more and more, in my mind, I drink the Amrit of His Naam.        3.     Waheguru, who has created this world in the form of a three-layered universe, can be realized when the Guru enlightens one’s mind, while it is all darkness of Maya for the Maya-engrossed foolish.     Even though the Supreme Light (Waheguru’s Soul) is present in every heart, one realizes it only with the advice of the Guru.                 4.        Those who make a connection with Waheguru in the shelter of the Guru must be praised. One’s mind is enlightened with the noble qualities of Waheguru, because of which, he merges into Him.  One’s life and his body are controlled by Waheguru, and His Naam is the source of all contentment, oh Nanak.   5.16.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 1॥ ਸੁਣਿ ਮਨ ਮਿਤ੍ਰ ਪਿਆਰਿਆ ਮਿਲੁ ਵੇਲਾ ਹੈ ਏਹ॥ ਜਬ ਲਗੁ ਜੋਬਨ ਸਾਸੁ ਹੈ ਤਬ ਲਗੁ ਇਹੁ ਤਨ ਦੇਹ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਗੁਣ ਕਾਮ ਨ ਆਵਈ ਢਹਿ ਢੇਰੀ ਤਨੁ ਖੇਹ॥ 1॥ ਮੇਰੇ ਮਨ ਲੈ ਲਾਹਾ ਘਰਿ ਜਾਹਿ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਸਲਾਹੀਐ ਹਉਮੈ ਨਿਵਰੀ ਭਾਹਿ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਸੁਣਿ ਸੁਣਿ ਗੰਢਣੁ ਗੰਢੀਐ ਲਿਖਿ ਪੜਿ ਬੂਝਹਿ ਭਾਰੁ॥ ਤ੍ਰਿਸਨਾ ਅਹਿਨਿਸਿ ਅਗਲੀ ਹਉਮੈ ਰੋਗੁ ਵਿਕਾਰੁ॥ ਓਹੁ ਵੇਪਰਵਾਹੁ ਅਤੋਲਵਾ ਗੁਰਮਤਿ ਕੀਮਤਿ ਸਾਰੁ। 2॥ ਲਖ ਸਿਆਣਪ ਜੇ ਕਰੀ ਲਖ ਸਿਉ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਿ ਮਿਲਾਪੁ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਸੰਗਤਿ ਸਾਧ ਨ ਧ੍ਰਾਪੀਆ ਬਿਨੁ ਨਾਵੈ ਦੂਖ ਸੰਤਾਪੁ॥ ਹਰਿ ਜਪਿ ਜੀਅ ਰੇ ਛੁਟੀਐ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਚੀਨੈ ਆਪੁ॥ 3॥ ਤਨੁ ਮਨੁ ਗੁਰ ਪਹਿ ਵੇਚਿਆ ਮਨੁ ਦੀਆ ਸਿਰੁ ਨਾਲਿ॥ ਤ੍ਰਿਭਵਣੁ ਖੋਜਿ ਢੰਢੋਲਿਆ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਖੋਜਿ ਨਿਹਾਲਿ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰਿ ਮੇਲਿ ਮਿਲਾਇਆ ਨਾਨਕ ਸੋ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਨਾਲਿ॥ 4॥17॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 1: Soon(i) mun mittar piyaariya, mil(u) vayla haiy eh.  Jub lug jobun saas haiy, tub lug eh(u) tun dayh. Bin(u) goon kaam na aavyee, dheh dhayri, tun(u) khayh. 1. Mayray mun, laiy laaha ghur(i) jaah(i). Gurmukh(i) Naam(u) slaahiyaiy, haumein nivri bhaah(i). Ruhaou.  Soon(i), soon(i) gundhun gundhiyaiy, likh(i) purrh(i) boojheh bhaar(u). Trishna ehinis(i) uglee, haumein roag(u) vikaar(u). Oh(u) Vaypervaah atoalvaan, Gurmut(i) keemut(i) saar(u). 2.  Lakh siyaanup jay kureen, lakh siyoun preet(i), milaap(u). Bin(u) sungut(i) Sadh na dhuraapiya, bin(u) Naavaiyn dookh, suntaap.  Hur(i) jup(i), jea ray chhoottiyaiy, Gurmukh(i) cheenaiy aap(u). 3. Tun(u), mun(u) Gur peh(i) vaychiya, mun(u) deeya, sir(u) naal(i).  Tirbhuvun khoaj(i) dhundholiya, Gurmukh(i) khoaj(i) nihaal. Satgur(i) mayl(i) milaaya, Nanak, so Prubh(u) naal(i).  4.17.
Word Meaning:  Vayla = time; opportunity.   Eh = this; human life.     Jub Lug = as long as.  Jobun = youthfulness.  Tub Lug = till then.   Dayh = body.   Dheh(i) Dhayri = collapses into a pile.  Khayh = dust.       1.        Laiy Laaha = earn the benefit.   Ghur(i) = Waheguru’s Palace. Nivri = is removed.      Haumein Bhaah(i) = fire of selfishness and ego.         Pause.      Gundhun Gundhiyaiy = try to build a bond (with Waheguru).   Bhaar(u) = load of books.  Trishna = appetite for (fire of) worldly attractions.   Ehinis = day and night.   Uglee = very much.    Vikaar(u) = of vices.   Oh(u) = God.   Atoalvaan = immeasurable.    Keemut(i) Saar(u) = find worth.           2.              Lakh = hundred thousand.   Siyaanup = smartness.   Milaap = meeting with people.     Dhuraapiya = find contentment.    Suntaap = suffering.    Chhoottiyaiy = get freedom from Maya; emancipation.   Cheenaiy = analyze.   Aap(u) = self.        3.      Vaychiya = sold.    Dhundholiya = searched.   Khoaj(i) = search.  Nihaal = blissful.    4.
MEANING: This (the human life) is your opportunity to meet (get connected) with Waheguru, oh my mind-friend.  Your body (and mind to realize Waheguru) is with you only as long as you are young and have breath in it.  Without acquiring virtues (by reciting Waheguru’s Naam), your body will be worthless, as it will eventually collapse, and become dust, oh man.       1.      Acquire the benefit (wealth of Waheguru’s Naam), and go to your eternal home (merge into Waheguru), oh my mind.   Sing praise of Waheguru’s Naam (recite it with devotion) in the shelter of the Guru, whereby the fire of your haumein (selfishness and ego) will be put out.         Pause.           Many people try to comprehend and bond with Waheguru by listening to others and by reading and writing tons of books (without any love for Him). But they deeply suffer from their boosted ego, which is a serious disease, and have even greater desires for worldly attractions and immoral thoughts.  Let alone bonding with Waheguru, by acquiring any bookish knowledge, such individuals cannot even comprehend the true worth of Waheguru, who is immeasurable, and who does not care about such knowledge.   So, try to understand His true worth in the shelter of the Guru, oh my mind.             2.       
If one uses millions of different kinds of smartness and is a part of a circle of millions of learned people, he cannot find contentment without the company (shelter) of the Guru, and suffers serious discomforts without Waheguru’s Naam. One can attain emancipation only when one recites Waheguru’s Naam and analyzes oneself (as to what one's real objective is and what type of qualities one has) in the shelter of the Guru.        3.             I have sold my body and my mind, along with my head (wisdom), to the Guru (have lost claim on all my rights).  I searched for Waheguru in the whole universe, but was blessed only to acquire Him when I searched for Him in Guru’s shelter.   My Satguru connected me with Waheguru, who is with me now (has appeared in my heart), oh Nanak.   4.17.
