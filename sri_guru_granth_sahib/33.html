(ਅੰਗ 32)॥      ਹਉ ਹਉ ਕਰਤੀ ਜਗੁ ਫਿਰੀ ਨਾ ਧਨੁ ਸੰਪੈ ਨਾਲਿ॥ ਅੰਧੀ ਨਾਮੁ ਨ ਚੇਤਈ ਸਭ ਬਾਧੀ ਜਮਕਾਲਿ॥ ਸਤਗੁਰਿ ਮਿਲਿਐ ਧਨੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮਾ ਰਿਦੈ ਸਮਾਲਿ॥ 3॥ ਨਾਮਿ ਰਤੇ ਸੇ ਨਿਰਮਲੇ ਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਸਹਜਿ ਸੁਭਾਇ॥ ਮਨੁ ਤਨੁ ਰਾਤਾ ਰੰਗ ਸਿਉ ਰਸਨਾ ਰਸਨ ਰਸਾਇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਰੰਗੁ ਨ ਉਤਰੈ ਜੋ ਹਰਿ ਧੁਰਿ ਛੋਡਿਆ ਲਾਇ॥ 4॥14॥47॥

Bhaayee ray, Gur bin(u) bhugut(i) na hoyay. Bin(u) Gur bhugut(i) na paayiyaiy jay loachaiy subh(u) koyay.1. Ruhaou. Lukh-chauraasee fayr(u) puyya kaamun(i) doojaiy bhaayay. Bin(u) Gur neend na aavyee, dookhi raiyn(i) vihaayay. Bin(u) Shabadaiy Pir(u) na paayiyay birtha junum(u) guvaayay. 2.

Naam(i) ruttay say nirmlay, Gur kaiy sehj(i) soobhayay. Mun(u) tun(u) raata rung siyoun, rusna rusn rusaayay. Nanak rung na ootraiy jo dhoor(i) chhodiya laayay.  4.14.47.
Word Meaning:  Ja = if.  Uggaiy Dhuray = offer.  Sohaagni = married.  Kerm = deeds.  Sayee = same.   Sehjay = (in a high spiritual state).     Vudaayee = honour.       1.        Loachaiyy = wishes; longs.       Pause.          Lukh-Chauraasee = 8.4 million life forms (a Hindu belief).   Fayr(u) = rebirth-cycle.  Kaamun(i) = human bride.  Doojaiy Bhaayay = affection for the other (Maya).  Neend = sleep; peace of mind.  Raiyn = night (of life).    Pir(u) = husband (Waheguru).  Birtha = useless (without any gain).        2.       Houn = selfishness.   Sumpaiy = amassed wealth.  Undhi = blind (ignorant).   Chatyee = keeps in mind (recite Naam).   Baandhi = shackled.   Jum Kaal(i) = angel of death.  Dhun(u) = wealth (Waheguru’s Naam).  Ridaiy = in the heart.         3.           Nirmulay = clean; pure.   Sehj(i) = in a high spiritual level (with pure mind).   Soobhayay = in affection.   Raata = imbued.  Rung = affection.  Rusna = tongue.   Rusn Rusaayay = enjoys the sweet taste.  Dhoor(i) = from the source (Waheguru).      4.
MEANING:  If a man thinks Waheguru as his own (has full faith in Him), he offers his body and mind to Him, and performs the same deeds as the other married women do (accept His Will, and lead a life of high morality).  With a pure mind, he can achieve union with the True Lord, who confers great honour on him.        1.         No one can worship God without the Guru’s grace, oh my friend.   Even though everybody longs for it, one cannot attain spiritual transcendence without the Guru.             Pause.                If a human bride (a person) is engrossed in the love of the other (Maya), she goes through the cycle of 8.4 million life forms.  Without the guidance of the Guru, she cannot sleep peacefully (cannot find peace of mind), and her life is spent in discomfort.  Without Guru’s Shabad, one cannot have union with the Husband (Waheguru), and his life goes to waste (without any spiritual gain).           2.
People spend their lives pursuing their selfish interests, but at the time of death, no wealth or property accompanies them.  The ignorant do not remember Waheguru’s Naam; they are all the prisoners of the Angel of death.  But when someone connects with the Guru, he carefully saves (enshrines) Waheguru’s Naam in his heart, which is his true wealth.           3.
In Pure (holy) Guru’s love, those, who are imbued in Waheguru’s Naam, become clean and pure.  Their mind and body are imbued in Waheguru’s Naam, and their tongue enjoys the sweetness of His Naam.  The colour of their love for Waheguru, in which they were dyed right from the beginning, never fades, oh Nanak.    4.14.47.

ਸਿਰੀਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਕ੍ਰਿਪਾ ਕਰੇ ਭਗਤਿ ਕੀਜੈ ਬਿਨੁ ਗੁਰ ਭਗਤਿ ਨ ਹੋਈ॥ ਆਪੈ ਆਪੁ ਮਿਲਾਏ ਬੂਝੈ ਤਾ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਹੋਵੈ ਸੋਈ॥ ਹਰਿ ਜੀਉ ਸਾਚਾ ਸਾਚੀ ਬਾਣੀ ਸਬਦਿ ਮਿਲਾਵਾ ਹੋਈ॥ 1॥ ਭਾਈ ਰੇ ਭਗਤਿਹੀਣੁ ਕਾਹੇ ਜਗਿ ਆਇਆ॥ ਪੂਰੇ ਗੁਰ ਕੀ ਸੇਵ ਨ ਕੀਨੀ ਬਿਰਥਾ ਜਨਮੁ ਗਵਾਇਆ॥1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਆਪੇ ਜਗਜੀਵਨੁ ਸੁਖਦਾਤਾ ਆਪੇ ਬਖਸਿ ਮਿਲਾਏ॥ ਜੀਅ ਜੰਤ ਏ ਕਿਆ ਵੇਚਾਰੇ ਕਿਆ ਕੋ ਆਖਿ ਸੁਣਾਏ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਆਪੇ ਦੇਇ ਵਡਾਈ ਆਪੇ ਸੇਵ ਕਰਾਏ॥ 2॥ ਦੇਖਿ ਕੁਟੰਬੁ ਮੋਹਿ ਲੋਭਾਣਾ ਚਲਦਿਆ ਨਾਲਿ ਨ ਜਾਈ॥ਸਤਗੁਰੁ ਸੇਵਿ ਗੁਣ ਨਿਧਾਨੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਤਿਸ ਕੀ ਕੀਮ ਨ ਪਾਈ॥ ਹਰਿ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਸਖਾ ਮੀਤੁ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਮੇਰਾ ਅੰਤੈ ਹੋਇ ਸਖਾਈ ॥3॥ ਆਪਣੈ ਮਨਿ ਚਿਤਿ ਕਹੈ ਕਹਾਏ ਬਿਨੁ ਗੁਰ ਆਪੁ ਨ ਜਾਈ॥ ਹਰਿ ਜੀਉ ਦਾਤਾ ਭਗਤਿ ਵਛਲੁ ਹੈ ਕਰਿ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਮੰਨਿ ਵਸਾਈ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਸੋਭਾ ਸੁਰਤਿ ਦੇਇ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਆਪੇ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਦੇ ਵਡਿਆਈ॥ 4॥15॥48॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 3:  Gurmukh(i) kirpa kuray bhagat(i) keejaiy, bin(u) Gur bhagat(i) na hoyee. Aapaiy aap(u) milayay boojhaiyy, ta nirmul hovaiy soyee.  Hur jeo Saacha Saachi baani; Shabad(i) milava hoyee. 1. Bhaayee ray, bhugut(i) - heen(u) kaahay jug(i) aayaa. Pooray Gur kee sayv na keeni, birtha junum(u) guvaaya. 1. Ruhaou. Aapay Jugjeevun(u) sookhdaata, aapay bukhsh(i) milayay. Jea junt eh kiyya vaychaaray, kiyya ko aakh(i) soonayay.  Gurmukh(i) aapay daye vudayee, aapay sayv kurayay. 2.  Daykh(i) kootumb(u) moh(i) lobhaana, chuldiyaan naal(i) na jaayee. Satgur sayv(i) goon nidhaan(u) paaya, tis kee keem na paayee. Hur(i) Prubh(u) sukha meet(u) Prubh(u) mayra, untaiy hoyay sukhayee. 3.  Aapnaiy mun(i) chit(i) kuhaiyy kuhaayay, bin(u) Gur aap(u) na jaayee. Hur(i) jeo daata bhugut(i) vuchhul(u) haiy, kur(i) kirpa mun(i) vusaayee. Nanak sobha soort(i) dayay Prubh(u) aapay, Gurmukh(i) dayay vudiyayee. 4.15.48.
Word Meaning:  Gurmukh(i) = through devotion of the Guru.  Bhagat(i) = worship; devotion.  Boojhaiyy = recognizes.    Nirmul(u) = clean; pure.   Sacha = eternal.          1.
Bhugut(i)-heen(u) = devoid of worship.  Kaahay = for what?   Birtha = useless.       Pause.
Jugjeevun = the source of life in the world (Waheguru).  Sookhdaata = the source of all comforts (God).  Bukhsh(i) = forgive; gift; kindness.   Jea Junt = living beings.   Vachhaaray = powerless.  Vudiyaayee = honour.   Sayv = service.         2.         Kootumb(u) = family.   Lobhaana = captivated.    Chuldiyaan = at the time of leaving (death).    Goon Nidhaan(u) = treasure of noble qualities (God).  Keem = keemut = value.   Sukha = Meet(u) = friend.   Untaiy = in the end.   Sukhayee = helpful.        3.        Chit(i) = in the mind.   Aap(u) = self (selfishness).   Bhgut(i) Vuchhul(u) = lover of worship (devotion).    Sobha = fame; praise.   Soort(i) = understanding; wisdom.      4. 
MEANING:  When Waheguru is kind to someone, He connects him with the Guru, and the devotee begins to worship Waheguru (recite His Naam). No one can worship Waheguru without the Guru’s grace.  Waheguru connects the individual to Him who becomes pure, and then realizes Him. Waheguru is eternal, and so is His Bani (Shabad or Huqam); an individual gets connected with Him by obeying His Huqam (Command or Will).        1.         What is the purpose of a person coming to this world if he is devoid of Waheguru’s worship, oh my friend?  Without serving the perfect Guru, he simply wastes away his precious human life.      Pause.
The source of life, Waheguru Himself, is the provider of all comforts, and He Himself connects people with Him by His kindness.  What can the powerless living beings say to, or tell Him?  (He provides without asking).  God Himself honours some by connecting them with the Guru, and Himself makes them obey His Will.         2.         Living in the family, one is captivated by the affection of the family members, but none of them accompany him when he leaves this world.  One’s real friend is Waheguru, who becomes his true saviour (helper) at the time, but the man does not recognize the worth of His friendship, and does not take Guru’s shelter to realize Him, which is the source of all noble qualities.           3.          A man may sing Waheguru’s praise in his mind (in his own way) and may also make others do the same, but he cannot get rid of his selfishness without the Guru’s advice.  Waheguru loves His devotees, and He connects them with Him by His own kindness. God Himself confers the wisdom of praising Him (obeying His Will) on his devotees, and honours them through the Guru, oh Nanak.    4.15.48.

ਸਿਰੀਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ ਧਨੁ ਜਨਨੀ ਜਿਨਿ ਜਾਇਆ ਧੰਨੁ ਪਿਤਾ ਪਰਧਾਨੁ॥ ਸਤਗੁਰੁ ਸੇਵਿ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਵਿਚਹੁ ਗਇਆ ਗੁਮਾਨੁ॥ ਦਰਿ ਸੇਵਨਿ ਸੰਤ ਜਨ ਖੜੇ ਪਾਇਨਿ ਗੁਣੀ ਨਿਧਾਨੁ॥ 1॥ ਮੇਰੇ ਮਨ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਧਿਆਇ ਹਰਿ ਸੋਇ॥ ਗੁਰ ਕਾ ਸਬਦੁ ਮਨਿ ਵਸੈ ਮਨੁ ਤਨੁ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਹੋਇ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਕਰਿ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਘਰਿ ਆਇਆ ਆਪੇ ਮਿਲਿਆ ਆਇ॥ ਗੁਰ ਸਬਦੀ ਸਾਲਾਹੀਐ ਰੰਗੇ ਸਹਜਿ ਸੁਭਾਇ॥ ਸਚੈ ਸਚਿ ਸਮਾਇਆ ਮਿਲਿ ਰਹੈ ਨ ਵਿਛੁੜਿ ਜਾਇ॥2॥ ਜੋ ਕਿਛੁ ਕਰਣਾ ਸੁ ਕਰਿ ਰਹਿਆ ਅਵਰੁ ਨ ਕਰਣਾ ਜਾਇ॥ ਚਿਰੀ ਵਿਛੁੰਨੇ ਮੇਲਿਅਨੁ ਸਤਗੁਰ ਪੰਨੈ ਪਾਇ॥ ਆਪੇ ਕਾਰ ਕਰਾਇਸੀ ਅਵਰੁ ਨ ਕਰਣਾ ਜਾਇ॥ 3॥ ਮਨੁ ਤਨੁ ਰਤਾ ਰੰਗ ਸਿਉ ਹਉਮੈ ਤਜਿ ਵਿਕਾਰ॥ ਅਹਿਨਿਸਿ ਹਿਰਦੈ ਰਵਿ ਰਹੈ ਨਿਰਭਉ ਨਾਮੁ ਨਿਰੰਕਾਰ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਆਪਿ ਮਿਲਾਇਅਨੁ ਪੂਰੈ ਸਬਦਿ ਅਪਾਰ॥ 4॥16॥49॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 3:  Dhunn(u) jun-nee jinh(i) jaaya, dhunn(u) pitaa perdhan(u). Satgur(u) sayv(i) sookh(u) paaya, vichoun guyya goomaan(u). Dur(i) sayvun(i) sunt jun khurray, paayin(i) Gooni-Nidhaan. 1.  Mayray mun, Gurmukh(i) dhiyayay Hur(i) soyay. Gur ka Shabad(u) mun(i) vusaiy, mun(u) tun(u) nirmul(u) hoyay. 1. Ruhaou.   Kur(i) kirpa ghur(i) aaya, Aapay miliya aayay. Gur Shabadi salaahiyaiy rungay sehj(i) soobhaayay.  Suchaiy such(i) smaayaa, mil(i) ruhhaiy na vichhurr(i) jaayay. 2. Jo kichh(u) kurna su kur(i) ruhhiya, uver(u) na kurna jaayay. Chireen vichhoonnay mayliun, Satgur punnaiy paayay. Aapay kaar kurayaysi uver(u) na kurna jaayay. 3.  Mun(u) tun(u) rutta rung siyoun, haumein tuj(i) vikaar. Ehinis(i) hirdaiy ruv(i) ruhaiy, nirbhuou Naam(u) Nirunkaar.  Nanak aap(i) milaayayun(u) pooraiy Shabad(i) apaar.  4.16.49.
Word Meaning:  Dhunn(u) = worthy of respect.    Jun-nee = mother.    Jaaya = gave birth.   Purdhaan(u) = leader.   Goomaan(u) = ego. Conceit.   Dur(i) = at the door (always in service)).    Gooni-Nidhaan(u) = treasure of noble qualities (God).         1.         Gurmukh(i) = through the Guru (taking His shelter).   Nirmul(u) = clean; pure.           Pause.          Ghur(i) = house; heart.   Rungay = imbue.     Sehj Soobhaayay = with pure mind and love.  Such(i) = through God (His Naam or praise).  Vichhurr(u) = separation.          2.           Uver(u) = else.   Chirreen = since long.   Vichhoonnay = separated.    Punnaiy = in His account; through Him.   Kaar = (desired) deeds.        3.            Rutta = imbued.   Rung = love.   Tuj(i) = by giving up.    Vikaar = evil thoughts or deeds.  Ehinis(i) = day and night.   Ruv(i) = is present; appears.   Nirbhou = fearless.   Nirunkar = formless (Waheguru).    Appar = beyond reach.  
MEANING:  Worthy of respect is the mother who gives birth to, and worthy of respect is the leading father of the devotee, who serves the Guru, and enjoys all the comforts (blissfulness) by getting rid of his ego and selfishness. Such devotees are always in the service of the Lord and acquire Him, which is the treasure of all noble qualities.          1.        Worship Waheguru by taking shelter of the Guru, oh my mind!  (As a result), your mind and body will become pure, and Guru’s Shabad will take up dwelling in your heart, oh my mind.          Pause. 
He (Waheguru) kindly appears in the heart, and connects the devotees of His liking (those who deserve) with Him.   This happens when one sings Waheguru’s praise using Guru’s Shabad, and is imbued in His Naam with his love for Him (Waheguru) in his purified mind.  One becomes “one” with Waheguru by singing His praise, and once connected with Him, one never suffers from His separation.            2.         Waheguru does what He wishes, and nothing can be done to thwart it (by anyone else). He connects the ones with Him who had been separated from Him for a long time through the Guru, and makes them do the deeds He likes, and nothing can be done (against His Will).          3.        At that stage, one conquers his haumein and evil thoughts (and deeds), and his mind and body get completely imbued in Waheguru’s Naam.  The fearless and formless Waheguru’s Naam appears in the mind of the devotee forever. Waheguru, who is beyond our reach, Himself connects the devotees with Him through the Shabad of the Guru, oh Nanak.   4.16.49.
