(ਅੰਗ 135)॥     ਮਨਿ ਤਨਿ ਪਿਆਸ ਦਰਸਨ ਘਣੀ ਕੋਈ ਆਣਿ ਮਿਲਾਵੈ ਮਾਇ॥ ਸੰਤ ਸਹਾਈ ਪ੍ਰੇਮ ਕੇ ਹਉ ਤਿਨ ਕੈ ਲਾਗਾ ਪਾਇ॥ ਵਿਣੁ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਕਿਉ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਾਈਐ ਦੂਜੀ ਨਾਹੀ ਜਾਇ॥ ਜਿਨ੍ਹੀ ਚਾਖਿਆ ਪ੍ਰੇਮ ਰਸੁ ਸੇ ਤ੍ਰਿਪਤਿ ਰਹੇ ਆਘਾਇ॥ ਆਪੁ ਤਿਆਗਿ ਬਿਨਤੀ ਕਰਹਿ ਲੇਹੁ ਪ੍ਰਭੂ ਲੜਿ ਲਾਇ॥ ਜੋ ਹਰਿ ਕੰਤਿ ਮਿਲਾਈਆ ਸਿ ਵਿਛੁੜਿ ਕਤਹਿ ਨ ਜਾਇ॥ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਵਿਣੁ ਦੂਜਾ ਕੋ ਨਹੀ ਨਾਨਕ ਹਰਿ ਸਰਣਾਇ॥ ਅਸੂ ਸੁਖੀ ਵਸੰਦੀਆ ਜਿਨਾ ਮਇਆ ਹਰਿ ਰਾਇ॥   8॥
Assun(i) praym oomaharra kiyoun miliyaiy Hur(i) jaayay. 
(PAGE 135):         Mun(i) tun(i) piyaas dershun ghuni, koyee aan(i) milaavaiy maayay. Sunt suhaayee praym kay, houn tin kaiy laagaan paayay.  Vin(u) Prubh kiyoun sookh(u) paayiyaiy, dooji naahi jaayay. Jinhi chaakhiya praym rus(u) say triput(i) ruhay aaghaayay. Aap(u) tiyaag(i) binti kureh(i) layhou Prubhoo lurr(i) laayay. Jo Hur(i) kunt(i) milaayiyaan, s(i) vichhurr(i) kuteh(i) na jaayay. Prubh vin(u) dooja ko nuhi Nanak Hur(i) shernaayay. Assoo sookhi vusundiyaan jinha muyya Hur(i) Raayay.     8.
Word Meaning:   Oomaaharra = zestful; dances.   Kiyoun = how.    Piyaas = thirst; longing.     Ghuni = much.    Suhaayee = helpful.    Vin(u) = Bin(u) = without.    Jaayay = place.   Chaakhiya = tasted.     Triput(i) = Aaghaayay = fully satisfied; contented.    Aap(u) tiyaag(i) = relinquishing selfishness.     Lurr(i) Laayay = (please) make your own; provide support.    Vichhurr(i) = separate from.    Kuteh(i) Na = nowhere.   Shernaayay = in the shelter of.    Muyya = kindness.         8.
MEANING: (Normally, the month of Assoo (Assun) is very pleasant in Punjab as it is neither hot nor cool yet, and every living being seems to enjoy it).  In this pleasant climate, the hearts of devotees begin to feel a zest for the love of Waheguru, and they look for ways to connect with Him.  There is a great thirst (longing) in my body and my mind to “see” Waheguru, and I wish that someone could connect me with Him.   I know that the holy men would be helpful in my love for Waheguru, so I would better drop on their feet (request them) to connect me with Waheguru, because there is no other place than Waheguru where my mind can find peace.  Those who have tasted the essence of Waheguru’s love (in His Naam) become fully satisfied and contented. Relinquishing selfishness, we too, should pray to Waheguru that He kindly makes us His own (patronizes us, or connects us with Him). Those women (human brides) who are once connected to Waheguru-Husband never get separated to seek support from anyone else.  There is none other than Waheguru (for support).  So, I (Nanak) too, have sought the shelter of Waheguru.  For those who are kind to Waheguru, the month of Assoo is very delightful.       8.

ਕਤਿਕਿ ਕਰਮ ਕਮਾਵਣੇ ਦੋਸੁ ਨ ਕਾਹੂ ਜੋਗੁ॥ ਪਰਮੇਸਰ ਤੇ ਭੁਲਿਆ ਵਿਆਪਨਿ ਸਭੇ ਰੋਗ॥ ਵੇਮੁਖ ਹੋਇ ਰਾਮ ਤੇ ਲਗਨਿ ਜਨਮ ਵਿਜੋਗ॥ ਖਿਨ ਮਹਿ ਕਉੜੇ ਹੋਇ ਗਏ ਜਿਤੜੇ ਮਾਇਆ ਭੋਗ॥ ਵਿਚੁ ਨ ਕੋਈ ਕਰਿ ਸਕੈ ਕਿਸ ਥੈ ਰੋਵਹਿ ਰੋਜ॥ ਕੀਤਾ ਕਿਛੂ ਨ ਹੋਵਈ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਧੁਰਿ ਸੰਜੋਗ॥ ਵਡਭਾਗੀ ਮੇਰਾ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਮਿਲੈ ਤਾ ਉਤਰਹਿ ਸਭਿ ਬਿਓਗ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਕਉ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਰਾਖਿ ਲੇਹਿ ਮੇਰੇ ਸਾਹਿਬ ਬੰਦੀ ਮੋਚ॥ ਕਤਿਕ ਹੋਵੈ ਸਾਧਸੰਗੁ ਬਿਨਸਹਿ ਸਭੇ ਸੋਚ॥ 9॥
Kutik(i) kerm kumaavnay, doash(u) na kaahoo joag. Permayshur tay bhoolliyaan viyaapun(i) subhay roag. Vaymookh hoyay Ram tay, luggun(i) junum vijoag. Khin meh(i) kaurray hoyay guyyay, jitrray Maya bhoag. Vich(u) na koyee kur(i) sukaiy, kis thaiy roveh(i) roaj. Keeta kichhoo na hovuyee, likhiya dhoor(i) sunjoag. Vudbhaagi mayra Prubh milaiy, ta ootreh(i) subh(i) biyoag. Nanak kuou Prubh raakh(i) layh(i), mayray Sahib bundi moach. Kutik hovaiy Sadhsung(u), binseh(i) subhay soach.     9.
Word Meaning:  Kerm = deeds; rituals.   Doash(u) = blame.   Na Kahoo = nobody.   Viyaapun(i) = happen; attack.   Vaymookh = turn face away from (ignore ).    Vijoag = (pain of) separation.   Bhoag = enjoyments.  Vich(u) = the role of the middle man.   Roaj = personal problems.   Likhiya Dhoor(i) Sunjoag = the relation between the body and the character (soul), is written right from the very beginning.    Ootreh(i) = go away.   Bundi Moach = the destroyer of all bonds.    Binseh(i) = are destroyed; come to the end.    Soach = worries.        9.  
MEANING: (Kutik is a month of rituals for the Hindus. They perform many spiritually worthless rituals which, as you will find soon, are not approved by Guru Sahiban).  The deeds you perform in the month of Kutik are worthless, but you cannot blame anyone else for your inability to deliver the expected results, because you invite many troubles by forgetting Waheguru.   When one ignores Waheguru, he gets disconnected from Him for many lives (and suffers).   Even the enjoyments of Maya, for which one forgets Waheguru, become painful in a moment (due to their loss or other issues).   And, the sad thing is, that there is nobody to share the troubles with, as nobody can help.  Moreover, one’s character is prewritten, and nobody can change that with his own efforts.  However, if by good fortune one connects with Waheguru, things can change, and all the troubles caused by disconnection from Waheguru are eradicated.   I (Nanak) request You, please save me from the attacks of Maya, oh Waheguru - the terminator of all bonds of Maya (emancipator).  If one can join the company of the Guru in the month of Kutik, all his worries are gone.      9.

ਮੰਘਿਰਿ ਮਾਹਿ ਸੋਹੰਦੀਆ ਹਰਿ ਪਿਰ ਸੰਗਿ ਬੈਠੜੀਆਹ॥ ਤਿਨ ਕੀ ਸੋਭਾ ਕਿਆ ਗਣੀ ਜਿ ਸਾਹਿਬਿ ਮੇਲੀੜੀਆਹ॥ ਤਨੁ ਮਨੁ ਮਉਲਿਆ ਰਾਮ ਸਿਉ ਸੰਗਿ ਸਾਧ ਸਹੇਲੜੀਆਹ॥ ਸਾਧ ਜਨਾ ਤੇ ਬਾਹਰੀ ਸੇ ਰਹਨਿ ਇਕੇਲੜੀਆਹ॥ ਤਿਨ ਦੁਖੁ ਨ ਕਬਹੂ ਉਤਰੈ ਸੇ ਜਮ ਕੈ ਵਸਿ ਪੜੀਆਹ॥ ਜਿਨੀ ਰਾਵਿਆ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਆਪਣਾ ਸੇ ਦਿਸਨਿ ਨਿਤ ਖੜੀਆਹ॥ ਰਤਨ ਜਵੇਹਰ ਲਾਲ ਹਰਿ ਕੰਠਿ ਤਿਨਾ ਜੜੀਆਹ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਬਾਛੈ ਧੂੜਿ ਤਿਨ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਸਰਣੀ ਦਰਿ ਪੜੀਆਹ॥ ਮੰਘਿਰਿ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਆਰਾਧਣਾ ਬਹੁੜਿ ਨ ਜਨਮੜੀਆਹ॥10॥
Munghir(i) maah(i) sohundiyaan Hur(i) Pir sung(i) baitthrriaan.  Tin kee sobha kiya guni, J(i) Sahib(i) maylrriaan. Tun(u) mun(u) mauliya Ram siyoun, sung(i) Sadh suhaylurriaan.  Sadh juna tay baahri, say rehn(i) ikaylrriaan. Jinhi raaviya Prubh(u) aapna, say dissun(i) nit khurriyaan. Ruttan juvayhur laal Hur(i), kuntth(i) tinha jurriyaan. Nanak baanchhay dhoorr(i) tin, Prubh sherni Dur(i) purriyaan. Munghir(i) Prubh(u) aaraadhna, buhourr(i) na junumurriyaan.        10.
Word Meaning:  Munghir(i) = in the month of Mugghur.    Sohundiyaan = look pretty; are successful.  Pir = dear; husband.    Sung(i) = in the company of.   Sobha = praise; level of bliss.   Mauliya = bloomed.   Suhaylurriyaan = friends (female).     Bahhri = without.   Jum kaiy vus(i) = under the control of the Angel of death.    Raaviya = enjoyed.   Nit = everyday; always.   Khurriyaan = alert; watchful.   Kuntth(i) = around the neck.    Baanchhaiy = wishes for; longs for.   Dhoorr(i) = dust of the feet.    Buhourr(i) = anymore; again.    Junumurriyaan = (re) birth.     10.
MEANING: Like a married woman wants to sit with her husband (enjoy his company) in the cold month of Mugghar (Munghir), a human bride (devotee) loves to have the comfort of the company of her Waheguru-Husband in this month.  Their praise (fortune) cannot be measured who have been connected with Waheguru.  In the company of the 
holy friends, their body and mind bloom (enjoying the love of Waheguru).  But those, who stay away from the company of the holy devotees, remain alone (disconnected from Waheguru).   Those who have enjoyed the love of Waheguru are always watchful against Maya's attacks.  Rather than a necklace of precious stones like rubies, around their neck for beautification, the devotees have the necklace of the priceless Naam of Waheguru around their neck (heart) (which keeps all the evils away).  (Guru) Nanak says that he longs for the dust of the feet of the devotees (a sign of extreme respect) who have taken shelter of Waheguru.  If one worships Waheguru in the month of Mugghar, he never takes birth again. 10.

ਪੋਖਿ ਤੁਖਾਰੁ ਨ ਵਿਆਪਈ ਕੰਠਿ ਮਿਲਿਆ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਹੁ॥ ਮਨੁ ਬੇਧਿਆ ਚਰਨਾਰਬਿੰਦ ਦਰਸਨਿ ਲਗੜਾ ਸਾਹੁ॥ ਓਟ ਗੋਵਿੰਦ ਗੋਪਾਲ ਰਾਇ ਸੇਵਾ ਸੁਆਮੀ ਲਾਹੁ॥ ਬਿਖਿਆ ਪੋਹਿ ਨ ਸਕਈ ਮਿਲਿ ਸਾਧੂ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਹੁ॥ ਜਹ ਤੇ ਉਪਜੀ ਤਹ ਮਿਲੀ ਸਚੀ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਿ ਸਮਾਹੁ॥ ਕਰੁ ਗਹਿ ਲੀਨੀ ਪਾਰਬ੍ਰਹਮਿ ਬਹੁੜਿ ਨ ਵਿਛੁੜੀਆਹੁ॥ ਬਾਰਿ ਜਾਉ ਲਖ ਬੇਰੀਆ ਹਰਿ ਸਜਣੁ ਅਗਮ ਅਗਾਹੁ॥ ਸਰਮ ਪਈ ਨਾਰਾਇਣੈ ਨਾਨਕ ਦਰਿ ਪਈਆਹੁ॥ ਪੋਖੁ ਸੁੋਹੰਦਾ ਸਰਬ ਸੁਖ ਜਿਸੁ ਬਖਸੇ ਵੇਪਰਵਾਹੁ॥11॥
Pokh(i) tookhaar(u) na viyaapyee, kuntth(i) miliya Hur(i) Naah(u). Mun(u) baydhiya Churnaarbind, dershun luggrra Shah(u). Oat Govind Gopaal Raayay, sayva Soami laah(u). Bikhiya poh(i) na sukyee mil(i) Sadhu goon gaah(u). Jeh tay oopji teh mili, sachi preet(i) smaah(u).  Kur(u) geh(i) leeni Parbraham(i), buhourr(i) na vichhurriahou. Baar(i) jaaoun lukh bayriaan, Hur(i) Sujjan agumm agaahou.  Sherm puyee Naraiyanaiy, Nanak Dur puyeeahou. Pokh soohunda serb sookh(u) jis(u) bukhshay vaypervahou.   11.
Word Meaning:  Tookhaar = extreme cold.    Viyaapyee = affects.   Naah(u) = Lord; Master.   Baydhiya = pierced.   Churnaarbind = (Churn +Aarbind) = lotus-like feet.    Shah(u) = king; Waheguru.   Oat = shelter.  Govind, Gopaal, Raayay = all names for Waheguru.    Laah(u) = benefit; in the bargain.    Bikhiya = poisonous (Maya).   Poh(i) Na Sukyee = cannot even touch.    Sadhu = Guru.   Oopji = came into being.   Smaah(u) = Absorbed.   Ker(u) = hand.   Geh(i) = by holding.   Buhourr(i) = again.   Bayriaan = times.  Agumm = unreachable.  Agaahou = unfathomable.  Sherm = accepts responsibility.   Naraayin = Waheguru.  Dur(i) Puyeeahou = shelter seeker.   Soohunda= comforting; enjoyable.  Bukhshay = to gift.  Vaypervaah(u) = indifferent; carefree.          11.
MEANING:  Even though the month of Poh (Pokh) is the coldest month in northern India, yet the individuals who have Lord Waheguru’s Naam on their tongue do not feel any effect of the extreme cold.  Their minds are pierced (stitched) with the lotus-like feet (expression of love and respect) of Waheguru (His Naam), and they long to have a sight of the King – Waheguru.  They take the shelter of Waheguru and get to serve the Lord as an added benefit (spiritual wealth). They sing Waheguru’s praise in the company of the Guru, as a result of which the poisonous Maya cannot even come near them.  Because of their pure love (devotion), they merge into the same Waheguru who instilled that love for Him in them.  Waheguru holds them in His hand and connects them to Him so they never get separated again.  I wish to sacrifice my life millions of times for Waheguru, who is otherwise unreachable and unfathomable.  When an individual seeks Waheguru’s shelter with a pure mind, Waheguru accepts His innate responsibility and absorbs the individual in Him, oh Nanak(u).   The otherwise cold month of Poh becomes enjoyable for those individuals who are given the Naam by the benevolence of Waheguru, who is indifferent to Maya.    11.
NOTE:  Nanak is the common name used by all Guru Sahiban in Guru Granth Sahib, which means that their writings are in complete agreement with Guru Nanak Sahib’s philosophy. But Nanak(u) is used for the specific Guru.
