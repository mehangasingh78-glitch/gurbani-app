(ਅੰਗ 54)॥        ਗਣਤ ਗਣਾਵਣਿ ਆਈਆ ਸੂਹਾ ਵੇਸੁ ਵਿਕਾਰੁ॥ ਪਾਖੰਡਿ ਪ੍ਰੇਮੁ ਨ ਪਾਈਐ ਖੋਟਾ ਪਾਜੁ ਖੁਆਰੁ॥ 1॥ ਹਰਿ ਜੀਉ ਇਉ ਪਿਰੁ ਰਾਵੈ ਨਾਰਿ॥ ਤੁਧੁ ਭਾਵਨਿ ਸੋਹਾਗਣੀ ਆਪਣੀ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਲੈਹਿ ਸਵਾਰਿ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਗੁਰ ਸਬਦੀ ਸੀਗਾਰੀਆ ਤਨੁ ਮਨੁ ਪਿਰ ਕੈ ਪਾਸਿ॥ ਦੁਇ ਕਰਿ ਜੋੜਿ ਖੜੀ ਤਕੈ ਸਚੁ ਕਹੈ ਅਰਦਾਸਿ॥ ਲਾਲਿ ਰਤੀ ਸਚ ਭੈ ਵਸੀ ਭਾਇ ਰਤੀ ਰੰਗਿ ਰਾਸਿ॥2॥ ਪ੍ਰਿਅ ਕੀ ਚੇਰੀ ਕਾਢੀਐ ਲਾਲੀ ਮਾਨੈ ਨਾਉ॥ ਸਾਚੀ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਿ ਨ ਤੁਟਈ ਸਾਚੇ ਮੇਲਿ ਮਿਲਾਉ॥ ਸਬਦਿ ਰਤੀ ਮਨੁ ਵੇਧਿਆ ਹਉ ਸਦ ਬਲਿਹਾਰੈ ਜਾਉ॥ 3॥ ਸਾਧਨ ਰੰਡ ਨ ਬੈਸਈ ਜੇ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਮਾਹਿ ਸਮਾਇ॥ ਪਿਰੁ ਰੀਸਾਲੂ ਨਉਤਨੋ ਸਾਚਉ ਮਰੈ ਨ ਜਾਇ॥ ਨਿਤ ਰਵੈ ਸੋਹਾਗਣੀ ਸਾਚੀ ਨਦਰਿ ਰਜਾਇ॥ 4॥ ਸਾਚੁ ਧੜੀ ਧਨ ਮਾਡੀਐ ਕਾਪੜੁ ਪ੍ਰੇਮ ਸੀਗਾਰੁ॥ ਚੰਦਨੁ ਚੀਤਿ ਵਸਾਇਆ ਮੰਦਰੁ ਦਸਵਾ ਦੁਆਰੁ॥ ਦੀਪਕੁ ਸਬਦਿ ਵਿਗਾਸਿਆ ਰਾਮੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਉਰਹਾਰੁ॥ 5॥ ਨਾਰੀ ਅੰਦਰਿ ਸੋਹਣੀ ਮਸਤਕਿ ਮਣੀ ਪਿਆਰੁ॥ ਸੋਭਾ ਸੁਰਤਿ ਸੁਹਾਵਣੀ ਸਾਚੈ ਪ੍ਰੇਮਿ ਅਪਾਰ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਪਿਰ ਪੁਰਖੁ ਨ ਜਾਣਈ ਸਾਚੇ ਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਹੇਤਿ ਪਿਆਰਿ॥6॥ ਨਿਸਿ ਅੰਧਿਆਰੀ ਸੁਤੀਏ ਕਿਉ ਪਿਰ ਬਿਨੁ ਰੈਣਿ ਵਿਹਾਇ॥ ਅੰਕੁ ਜਲਉ ਤਨੁ ਜਾਲੀਅਉ ਮਨੁ ਧਨੁ ਜਲਿ ਬਲਿ ਜਾਇ॥ ਜਾ ਦਿਨ ਕੰਤਿ ਨ ਰਾਵੀਆ ਤਾ ਬਿਰਥਾ ਜੋਬਨੁ ਜਾਇ॥7॥ ਸੇਜੈ ਕੰਤ ਮਹੇਲੜੀ ਸੂਤੀ ਬੂਝ ਨ ਪਾਇ॥ ਹਉ ਸੁਤੀ ਪਿਰੁ ਜਾਗਣਾ ਕਿਸ ਕਉ ਪੂਛਉ ਜਾਇ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰਿ ਮੇਲੀ ਭੈ ਵਸੀ ਨਾਨਕ ਪ੍ਰੇਮੁ ਸਖਾਇ॥ 8॥2॥
Mehla 1:  Subhay kunt muhayliyaan sugliyaan kureh(i) sheengar(u).

NOTE: This holy Shabad does not have “Siri Raag(u) before Mehla 1.
Word Meaning:  Kunt = husband (Waheguru).     Muhayliyaan = (human) wives.   Sugliyaan = all.    Sheengaar(u) = make up; beautify (wear religious symbols to show their false spirituality).  Gunut gnaavun(i) = to be counted (included) or considered. (They are not honest and their makeup is just a show off).   Sooha vays = pink dress (pink colour is representative of Maya).  Vikaar(u) = sensual pleasures.   Paakhund(i) = dishonest act.    Khota = false; counterfeit.    Paaj(u) = show off.   Khooaar(u) = discomfiture; suffering because of no achievement.      1.
Iyoun = like this (as follows).   Raavaiy = enjoys (the union).  Naar(i) = woman.   Toodh(u) = You.    Bhaavun(i) = are liked.    Sohaagni = married (loved by husband). 
Svaar(i) = grooming; embellish with noble qualities.      Pause.
Doyay Ker(i) jorr(i) = (with) both hands folded.  Laal(i) Rutti = imbued in the love of the dear.    Bhaiy = fear.   Bhaayay = affection.   Rung(i) Raas(i) = filled with the love of.     2.       Chayri = disciple; obedient.    Kaandhiyaiy = is considered.   Laali = (like a) slave.    Vaydhiya = pierced (affected deeply).     Bulihaaraiy = be a sacrifice.        3.
Sa Dhun = that woman.   Rund = widow (devoid of husband’s love).   Baiysyee = stays; lives.   Pir(u) = husband (Waheguru).   Resaaloo = full of love.  Noutuno = new (whose love never lessens).  Saachou = eternal.   Nit = every day.   Ruvaiy = enjoys (the union).    Rujaayay= with obedience.      4

MEANING:  All humans are Waheguru’s brides, and all of them adorn themselves (by wearing religious symbols) to be approved by Waheguru.  However, their true dress is pink (they are all engrossed in Maya; the pink colour has been used as a metaphor for Maya in Guru Granth Sahib, because pink, though very attractive, fades away quickly like Maya), and they are indulging in sensual pleasures. So, their adornments are a farce, and they just hope to be approved by Waheguru. But they cannot win Waheguru’s affection through hypocrisy, and their false show-off brings them shame alone.         1.
Only those human brides enjoy Your union to whom You are kind (who have deep respect and affection for You), oh Waheguru. You can adorn them with noble qualities by Your kindness, and that is how the Husband (Waheguru) enjoys the lucky human brides.        Pause.
Such devotees have been adorned by the Guru’s Shabad and have offered their body and mind to the Husband (Waheguru).  They are waiting for Waheguru’s Command with folded hands, and pray before Him.  Imbued in the love of the dear (Waheguru), such devotees always keep respectful fear of Waheguru in mind, and being imbued in His love, they are fully content.          2.          That devotee is considered a true disciple of the Lord (God), who obeys His Command like a slave.  Once Waheguru has connected with an individual through the Guru, their bond of True love is never broken.  I am always a sacrifice for the individuals whose minds have been pierced by Guru’s Shabad and are imbued with His love.       3.
Such a bride (devotee) who has become “one” with the Guru is never a widow again (never loses His love).  The source of all tastes, Waheguru, is always young, and He neither dies nor goes away (is not separated).  With Waheguru’s kind sight, such a devotee always stays married and enjoys the union with Waheguru.            4.         Such a devotee is always adorned with the braids and beautiful clothes of Waheguru’s love.  She has fostered Waheguru’s Naam in her heart, and Waheguru, with the fragrance of Sandalwood (of noble qualities), resides in the temple of her Tenth Door (mind).  Enlightened by the lamp of Guru’s Shabad, the heart of such a devotee blooms, and she wears the necklace of Waheguru’s Naam in her heart.       5.
Such a human bride has a beautiful (pious) heart, and she wears an anointment of a shiny stone (a pleasant glow) on her forehead.  Because of her love for Waheguru, she is spiritually very wise and enjoys a holy fame.  And because of her love for the Perfect Guru, she does not acknowledge any other person than Waheguru as her master.       6.      Sleeping in the darkness of night (ignorance), how does your night (of life) spend without the Husband (Waheguru), oh my friend? Let such a heart burn; set such a body, as well as the mind, and the wealth, on fire, which is without Waheguru’s Naam.  If a human bride is not loved by her Waheguru-Husband, then her youthfulness is wasted.            7.
If a bride always stays sleeping (engrossed in Maya, and separated from her Waheguru)-Husband, she does not realize that He is not with her. Who can she ask about the joy of union with the Husband who is awake all the time (who fends off the attacks of Maya) while the former is always sleeping?  However, those who are connected with Waheguru by Satguru lead their life under respectful fear of their Husband and enjoy their life in the love of Waheguru- riend, oh Nanak.      8.2.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 1॥ ਆਪੇ ਗੁਣ ਆਪੇ ਕਥੈ ਆਪੇ ਸੁਣਿ ਵੀਚਾਰੁ॥ ਆਪੇ ਰਤਨ ਪਰਖਿ ਤੂ ਆਪੇ ਮੋਲੁ ਅਪਾਰੁ॥ ਸਾਚਉ ਮਾਨੁ ਮਹਤੁ ਤੂ ਆਪੇ ਦੇਵਣਹਾਰੁ॥ 1॥ ਹਰਿ ਜੀਉ ਤੂ ਕਰਤਾ ਕਰਤਾਰੁ॥ ਜਿਉ ਭਾਵੈ ਤਿਉ ਰਾਖੁ ਤੂ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਮਿਲੈ ਆਚਾਰੁ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਆਪੇ ਹੀਰਾ ਨਿਰਮਲਾ ਆਪੇ ਰੰਗੁ ਮਜੀਠ॥ ਆਪੇ ਮੋਤੀ ਊਜਲੋ ਆਪੇ ਭਗਤ ਬਸੀਠੁ॥ ਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਸਬਦਿ ਸਲਾਹਣਾ ਘਟਿ ਘਟਿ ਡੀਠੁ ਅਡੀਠੁ॥ 2॥ ਆਪੇ ਸਾਗਰੁ ਬੋਹਿਥਾ ਆਪੇ ਪਾਰੁ ਅਪਾਰੁ॥ ਸਾਚੀ ਵਾਟ ਸੁਜਾਣ ਤੂ ਸਬਦਿ ਲੰਘਾਵਣਹਾਰੁ॥ ਨਿਡਰਿਆ ਡਰੁ ਜਾਣੀਐ ਬਾਝੁ ਗੁਰੂ ਗੁਬਾਰੁ॥ 3॥ ਅਸਥਿਰੁ ਕਰਤਾ ਦੇਖੀਐ ਹੋਰ ਕੇਤੀ ਆਵੈ ਜਾਇ॥ ਆਪੇ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਏਕੁ ਤੂੰ ਹੋਰ ਬੰਧੀ ਧੰਧੈ ਪਾਇ॥ ਗੁਰਿ ਰਾਖੇ ਸੇ ਉਬਰੇ ਸਾਚੇ ਸਿਉ ਲਿਵ ਲਾਇ॥ 4॥
