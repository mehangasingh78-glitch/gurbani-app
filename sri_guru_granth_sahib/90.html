(ਅੰਗ 89)॥    ਜਿਨ ਕਉ ਹੋਆ ਕ੍ਰਿਪਾਲੁ ਹਰਿ ਸੇ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਪੈਰੀ ਪਾਹੀ॥ ਤਿਨ ਐਥੈ ਓਥੈ ਮੁਖ ਉਜਲੇ ਹਰਿ ਦਰਗਹ ਪੈਧੇ ਜਾਹੀ॥ 14॥
Paurri: Satgur(u) jinhi dhiyaya, say kurrh(i) na svaahi. Satgur(u) jinhi dhiyaya, say triput(i) aghaayee. Satgur(u) jinhi dhiyaya, tin jum dur(u) naahi. Jin kuou hoa kirpal(u) Hur(i), say Satgur paireen paahi. Tin aithaiy oathaiy mookh oojlay, Hur(i) Durrgeh paindhay jaahi.   14.
Word Meaning:  Dhiyaya = worship; recited (the Naam); obeyed.  Kurrh(i) = grumble; get irritated; complain in dissatisfaction.   Na Svaahi = Na Subaahi = not in early morning; never.  Triput(i) Aghaayee = contented.   Kirpal(u) = kind.   Aithaiy = here (in this world).  Oathaiy = there (next world).   Paindhay = are robed (honoured). 
MEANING:  Those who worship Satguru do not grumble (get irritated) in the early mornings of the day (any time).  Those who worship Waheguru are content and have no fear of the Angel of Death. Those who take shelter of Satguru are kind to Waheguru.  Their faces shine (with piety of mind) in this world and the next. They are honoured in the next world (Waheguru’s Court) upon arrival.  14.

ਸਲੋਕ ਮ:2॥ ਜੋ ਸਿਰੁ ਸਾਈ ਨਾ ਨਿਵੈ ਸੋ ਸਿਰੁ ਦੀਜੈ ਡਾਰਿ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਜਿਸੁ ਪਿੰਜਰ ਮਹਿ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਨਹੀ ਸੋ ਪਿੰਜਰੁ ਲੈ ਜਾਰਿ॥ 1.
Sloak M:2:  Jo sir(u) Saayeen na nivaiy, so sir(u) deejaiy daar(i). Nanak, jis pinjur meh(i) birha nuhee, so pinjur(u) laiy jaar(i).  1.
Word Meaning: Saayeen = Lord (Waheguru).   Nivaiy = bow; kneel down.  Daar(i) = cut and throw.   Pinjur = skeleton; body.  Birha = pain of separation.  Jaar(i) = burn.
MEANING: The head that does not bow to the Lord deserves to be cut and thrown away. The human body, that has no pain of separation (from Waheguru), deserves to be burned down, oh Nanak.   1.
ਮ:5॥ ਮੁੰਢਹੁ ਭੁਲੀ ਨਾਨਕਾ ਫਿਰਿ ਫਿਰਿ ਜਨਮਿ ਮੁਈਆ ਸੁ॥ ਕਸਤੂਰੀ ਕੇ ਭੋਲੜੈ ਗੰਦੇ ਡੁੰਮਿ
ਪਈਆ ਸੁ॥ 2॥
M:5: Moondhoun bhooli Nanaka, phir(i) phir(i) junum(i) mooya su. Kustoori kay bholrray gunday doom(i) puyaa su.  2.
Word Meaning:  Moondhoun = from the very start.  Bhooli = misled.  Su = that.   Kustoori = musk.   Bholrray = mistakenly.   Gunday = dirty.  Doom(i) = deep water pit.   
MEANING: A man, who is misled right from the start (birth) (loves Maya rather than Waheguru), is like the one who jumps into a pit of dirty water, mistaking it for musk, and takes birth, and dies, again and again.  2.

ਪਉੜੀ॥ ਸੋ ਐਸਾ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਧਿਆਈਐ ਮਨ ਮੇਰੇ ਜੋ ਸਭਨਾ ਉਪਰਿ ਹੁਕਮੁ ਚਲਾਏ॥ ਸੋ ਐਸਾ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਜਪੀਐ ਮਨ ਮੇਰੇ ਜੋ ਅੰਤੀ ਅਉਸਰਿ ਲਏ ਛਡਾਏ॥ ਸੋ ਐਸਾ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਜਪੀਐ ਮਨ ਮੇਰੇ ਜੁ ਮਨ ਕੀ ਤ੍ਰਿਸਨਾ ਸਭ ਭੁਖ ਗਵਾਏ॥ ਸੋ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਜਪਿਆ ਵਡਭਾਗੀ ਤਿਨ ਨਿੰਦਕ ਦੁਸਟ ਸਭਿ ਪੈਰੀ ਪਾਏ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮੁ ਅਰਾਧਿ ਸਭਨਾ ਤੇ ਵਡਾ ਸਭਿ ਨਾਵੈ ਅਗੈ ਆਣਿ ਨਿਵਾਏ॥ 15॥
Paurri:  So aisa Hur(i) Naam(u) dhiyayiyaiy mun mayray, jo subhna oopur(i) Huqam(u) chulayay. So aisa Hur(i) Naam(u) jupiyaiy mun mayray, jo untee ausur(i) luyay chhudayay. So aisa Naam(u) jupiyaiy mun mayray, ju mun kee trishna subh bhookh guvayay. So Gurmukh(i) Naam(u) jupiyaa vudbhageen, tin ninduk doosht subh(i) paireen paayay. Nanak Naam(u) araadh(i) subhna tay vudda ,subh(i) Naavay uggaiy aan(i) nivayay.  15.
Word Meaning: Aisa = such; that.  Dhiyayiyaiy = recite; worship.  Untee= last; at the end.  Ausur(i) = time.  Chhudayay = gets free.   Trishna = desires; appetite to acquire more and more.  Ninduk = malicious critics.  Doosht = evil people.  Aaradh(i) = worship.  
MEANING: Worship the Naam(u) of such Waheguru who rules over everybody, oh my mind.  Worship the Naam (sing praise) of that Waheguru which gets you free (from the bonds of Maya) at the end, and which kills your appetite for the worldly desires (discontentment), oh my mind.  The highly lucky individuals worship the Naam of such Waheguru in the shelter of the Guru, which forces all the malicious critics and the evil to drop on their (devotees’) feet.  Worship the Naam of the Waheguru, which is the greatest of all, and which forces everybody to bow to the Naam, oh Nanak.    15.  

ਸਲੋਕ ਮ:3॥ ਵੇਸ ਕਰੈ ਕੁਰੂਪਿ ਕੁਲਖਣੀ ਮਨਿ ਖੋਟੈ ਕੂੜਿਆਰਿ॥ ਪਿਰ ਕੈ ਭਾਣੇ ਨਾ ਚਲੈ ਹੁਕਮੁ ਕਰੇ ਗਾਵਾਰਿ॥ ਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਭਾਣੈ ਜੋ ਚਲੈ ਸਭਿ ਦੁਖ ਨਿਵਾਰਣਹਾਰਿ॥ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਮੇਟਿ ਨ ਸਕੀਐ ਜੋ ਧੁਰਿ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਕਰਤਾਰਿ॥ ਮਨੁ ਤਨੁ ਸਉਪੈ ਕੰਤ ਕਉ ਸਬਦੇ ਧਰੇ ਪਿਆਰੁ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਨਾਵੈ ਕਿਨੈ ਨ ਪਾਇਆ ਦੇਖਹੁ ਰਿਦੈ ਬੀਚਾਰਿ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਸਾ ਸੁਆਲਿਉ ਸੁਲਖਣੀ ਜਿ ਰਾਵੀ ਸਿਰਜਨਹਾਰਿ॥ 1॥
Sloak M:3: Vayse kuraiy kuroop(i) kulukhni mun(i) khotaiy koorriaar(i). Pir kaiy bhaanay na chullaiy, Huqam(u) kuray gaavaar(i). Gur kaiy bhaanaiy jo chullaiy subh(i) dookh nivaarunhaar(i). Likhiya mayt(i) na sukiyaiy jo dhoor(i) likhiya Kartaar(i). Mun(u) tun(u) sounpaiy kunt kuou Shabday dhuray piyaar(u). Bin Naavaiy kinaiy na paaya daykhou ridaiy beechaar(i). Nanak sa sooaliyo sulukhni j(i) raavi Sirjunhaar.  1.
Word Meaning:  Vays Kuraiy = dons different religious garbs.  Kuroop(i) = ugly.  Kulukhni = having evil qualities.  Khotaiy = deceitful; devious.  Koorriaar(i) = liars; dishonest.  Bhaanaiy = Will.    Nivaarunhaar(i) = remover; dissipater.  Mayt(i) = erase; remove.  Dhoor(i) = from the beginning.  Sounpaiy = offer.  Ridaiy = in heart (mind).   Sooaliyo = pretty.   Kunt = husband (Waheguru).   Sulukhni = virtuous.   Raavi = enjoyed union; became connected.   Sirjunhaar(i) = the Creator (Waheguru).  
MEANING:  The individuals who have deceit in their mind, but don a religious garb (to fool) people, are in fact ugly (unattractive), have evil qualities, and are liars and dishonest.  They do not lead their lives according to the Will of Waheguru, and the unrefined order leads others to follow their own minds.    But everybody’s actions are controlled by their predestined fate as written by Waheguru; nobody can erase His Will.  Those who follow the Guru's advice have all their troubles eradicated.  They offer their mind and body to the husband, Waheguru, and develop love for the Guru’s Shabad.  Anyone who ponders deeply can realize that no one can connect with Waheguru without reciting His Naam.  Only that human being is pretty and virtuous, whose dedication is enjoyed by (is connected to) Waheguru, oh Nanak.     1.

ਮ:3॥ ਮਾਇਆ ਮੋਹੁ ਗੁਬਾਰੁ ਹੈ ਤਿਸ ਦਾ ਨ ਦਿਸੈ ਉਰਵਾਰੁ ਨ ਪਾਰੁ॥ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਅਗਿਆਨੀ ਮਹਾ ਦੁਖੁ ਪਾਇਦੇ ਡੁਬੇ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਵਿਸਾਰਿ॥ ਭਲਕੇ ਉਠਿ ਬਹੁ ਕਰਮ ਕਮਾਵਹਿ ਦੂਜੈ ਭਾਇ ਪਿਆਰੁ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਸੇਵਹਿ ਆਪਣਾ ਭਉਜਲੁ ਉਤਰੇ ਪਾਰਿ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਸਚਿ ਸਮਾਵਹਿ ਸਚੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਉਰਧਾਰਿ॥ 2॥
M:3: Maya moh(u) goobaar(u) haiy, tis da na disaiy oorvaar(u) na paar(u). Munmukh ugiyani maha dookh(u) paayaynday, doobay Hur(i) Naam(u) visaar(i). Bhulkay ootth(i) buhou kerm kmaaveh(i), doojaiy bhaayay piyar(u).  Satgur sayveh(i) aapna, bhoujul(u) ootray paar(i). Nanak Gurmukh(i) Sach(i) smaaveh(i), Sach(u) Naam(u) oordhaar(i).   2
Word Meaning:  Moh(u) = attachment.    Goobaar(u) = darkness.  Oorvaar(u) = the closer side.  Paar(u) = the farther side.   Ugiyani = ignorant; without knowledge.  Maha = big.   Visaar(i) = by forgetting.  Bhulkay = early morning (everyday).  Kerm = rituals.   Kmaaveh(i) = perform.  Doojaiy Bhaayay = in the love for the other (Maya).      Bhoujul(u) = the world sea.  Sach(i) = in the eternal.   Smaaveh(i) = get absorbed; merge into.     Oordhaar(i) = by acquiring in mind. 
MEANING:  The attachment with Maya is such darkness that neither its closer side, nor its far side, is visible (has no end to it).  By forgetting Waheguru (His Naam), the ignorant followers of their mind suffer a great deal, and drown in the world sea.  Starting early in the morning, they perform a number of rituals, but all in the name of Maya (which pushes them even farther from God).   But those who serve their Satguru get ferried across (the sea of Maya).  The Gurmukhs acquire Waheguru’s Naam in their mind, and become “one” with Him, oh Nanak. 2.

ਪਉੜੀ॥ ਹਰਿ ਜਲਿ ਥਲਿ ਮਹੀਅਲਿ ਭਰਪੂਰਿ ਦੂਜਾ ਨਾਹੀ ਕੋਇ॥ ਹਰਿ ਆਪਿ ਬਹਿ ਕਰੇ ਨਿਆਉ ਕੂੜਿਆਰ ਸਭ ਮਾਰਿ ਕਢੋਇ॥ ਸਚਿਆਰਾ ਦੇਇ ਵਡਿਆਈ ਹਰਿ ਧਰਮ ਨਿਆਉ ਕੀਓਇ॥ ਸਭ ਹਰਿ ਕੀ ਕਰਹੁ ਉਸਤਤਿ ਜਿਨਿ ਗਰੀਬ ਅਨਾਥ ਰਾਖਿ ਲੀਓਇ॥ ਜੈਕਾਰੁ ਕੀਓ ਧਰਮੀਆ ਕਾ ਪਾਪੀ ਕਉ ਡੰਡੁ ਦੀਓਇ॥ 16॥
Paurri: Hur(i) jul(i) thul(i) muhhiul(i) bhurpoor(i), dooja naahi koyay. Hur(i) aap(i) beh(i) kuray niyaoun, koorriaar subh maar(i) kudhoyay.  Sachiyaraan dayay vudiyayee, Hur(i), Dherm niyaoun keeoyay. Subh Hur(i) kee kurhou oostut(i), jin(i) greeb anaath raakh(i) leeoyay. Jaikaar keeyo dhermiya ka, paapi kuou dund(u) deeoyay.  16.
Word Meaning:  Muhiul(i) = sky.  Bhurpoor(i) = filling completely.   Koorriaar = lover of Maya; dishonest; deceitful.  Kudhoyay = took out.  Sachiyaar = pure-hearted; followers of the Guru.  Niyaoun = justice.   Dherm = based on high morals.  Oostut(i) = praise.  Anaath = orphans.   Jaikaar = hail to.  Dund(u) = punishment. 
MEANING:  Waheguru is filling all the oceans, the earth, and the sky with His presence (He is omnipresent), and there is none other like Him.  Waheguru Himself does justice, and all dishonest people are killed spiritually and pulled out.  (On the other hand), The honest people (Waheguru’s devotees) are praised (honoured) because this is His justice based on the high moral principles. Everybody should sing Waheguru’s praise, which protects the powerless and the orphans (who have no support). The virtuous are hailed, and the sinners are punished.  16.

ਸਲੋਕ ਮ:3॥ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਮੈਲੀ ਕਾਮਣੀ ਕੁਲਖਣੀ ਕੁਨਾਰਿ॥ ਪਿਰੁ ਛੋਡਿਆ ਘਰਿ ਆਪਣਾ ਪਰ ਪੁਰਖੈ ਨਾਲਿ ਪਿਆਰੁ॥ ਤ੍ਰਿਸਨਾ ਕਦੇ ਨ ਚੁਕਈ ਜਲਦੀ ਕਰੇ ਪੁਕਾਰ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਬਿਨੁ ਨਾਵੈ ਕੁਰੂਪਿ ਕੁਸੋਹਣੀ ਪਰਹਰਿ ਛੋਡੀ ਭਤਾਰਿ॥ 1॥
Sloak M:3: Munmukh maiylee kaamni, kulukhni koonaar(i).  Pir(u) chhodiya ghur(i) aapna, pur poorkhaiy naal(i) piyaar(u).  Trishna kuday na chookyee, juldee kuray pookaar. Nanak bin(u) Naavaiy kuroop(i) kusohni, purhur(i) chhodi Bhutaar(i).  1.
Word Meaning:  Mailee = filthy.  Kaamni = woman.   Kulukhni = having evil qualities.  Koonaar(i) = bad (unfaithful) wife.  Pir(u) = husband; Lord.  Pur = other.  Poorkhaiy = male; master.  Trishna = appetite for desires.  Juldi = burning; suffering.  Kuroop(i) = Kusohni = ugly; unattractive; not worthy of love or liking.  Bhataar(i) = husband. 
NOTE: All human beings (male or female) are considered females, with Waheguru being the only male (Master or Lord) to express human love for God, or vice versa. 
MEANING:  A follower of his mind is like an unfaithful wife who is filthy with evil qualities, and who has left her own husband in love with some other male.  Her desires are never fulfilled, as a result of which, she has a burning heart and raises hue and cry in suffering.  Without Waheguru’s Naam, she is unattractive and unworthy of Waheguru’s love, and is like a wife who has been condemned by her husband.  1.
