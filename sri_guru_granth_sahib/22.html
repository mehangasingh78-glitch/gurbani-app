(ਅੰਗ 21)॥ ਅੰਤਰ ਕੀ ਗਤਿ ਜਾਣੀਐ ਗੁਰ ਮਿਲੀਐ ਸੰਕ ਉਤਾਰਿ॥ ਮੁਇਆ ਜਿਤੁ ਘਰਿ ਜਾਈਐ ਤਿਤੁ ਜੀਵਦਿਆ ਮਰੁ ਮਾਰਿ॥ ਅਨਹਦ ਸਬਦਿ ਸੁਹਾਵਣੇ ਪਾਈਐ ਗੁਰ ਵੀਚਾਰਿ॥ 2॥ ਅਨਹਦ ਬਾਣੀ ਪਾਈਐ ਤਹ ਹਉਮੈ ਹੋਇ ਬਿਨਾਸੁ॥ ਸਤਗੁਰ ਸੇਵੇ ਆਪਣਾ ਹਉ ਸਦ ਕੁਰਬਾਣੈ ਤਾਸੁ॥ ਖੜਿ ਦਰਗਹ ਪੈਨ੍ਹਾਈਐ ਮੁਖਿ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮ ਨਿਵਾਸੁ॥ 3॥ ਜਹ ਦੇਖਾ ਤਹ ਰਵਿ ਰਹੇ ਸਿਵ ਸਕਤੀ ਕਾ ਮੇਲੁ॥ ਤ੍ਰਿਹੁ ਗੁਣ ਬੰਧੀ ਦੇਹੁਰੀ ਜੋ ਆਇਆ ਸੋ ਖੇਲੁ॥ ਵਿਜੋਗੀ ਦੁਖਿ ਵਿਛੁੜੇ ਮਨਮੁਖਿ ਲਹਹਿ ਨ ਮੇਲੁ॥ 4॥ ਮਨੁ ਬੈਰਾਗੀ ਘਰਿ ਵਸੈ ਸਚ ਭੈ ਰਾਤਾ ਹੋਇ॥ ਗਿਆਨ ਮਹਾ ਰਸੁ ਭੋਗਵੈ ਬਾਹੁੜਿ ਭੂਖ ਨ ਹੋਇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਇਹੁ ਮਨੁ ਮਾਰਿ ਮਿਲੁ ਭੀ ਫਿਰਿ ਦੁਖੁ ਨ ਹੋਇ॥ 5॥18॥ Siri Raag(u) Mehla 1: Mernaiy kee chinta nuhhi, jeevun kee nuhhi aas. Tu serb jeeyaan pritpaalhi, laykhaiy saas giraas. Untar(i) Gurmukh(i) Tu vuseh(i), jiyoun bhaavaiy tiyoun nirjaas(i). 1. Jea ray, Ram juput mun maan(u). Untar laagi jul(i) boojhi, paaya Gurmukh(i) Giyan(u). Ruhaou. (PAGE 21): Untar kee gut(i) jaaniyaiy, Gur miliyaiy shunk ootaar(i). Mooyaan jit(u) Ghur(i) jaayiyaiy, tit(u) jeevundiyaan mur(u) maar(i). Unhud Shabad(i) soohaavnay, paayiyaiy Gur veechaar(i). 2. Unhud Bani paayiyaiy, teh haumein hoyay binaas(u). Satgur sayvay aapna, huoun sud koorbaanaiy taas(u). Khurr(i) Durgeh pehnayiyaiy, mookh(i) Hur(i) Naam nivaas(u). 3. Jeh daykhaan teh ruv(i) ruhhay, Shiv shakti ka mayl(u). Trihu goon bundhi dayhoori, jo aaya so khayl(u). Vijogi dookh(i) vichhurray, munmukh(i) luhheh(i) na mayl(u). 4. Mun baiyraagi, ghur(i) vussaiy, Sach bhaiy raata hoyay. Giyan muhha rus bhogvaiy, bahourr(i) bhookh na hoyay. Nanak, eh(u) mun maar(i) mil(u), bhee phir(i) dookh(u) na hoyay. 5.18. Word Meaning: Chinta = worry. Aas = wish. Pritpaalhi = nourish and raise. Laykhaiy = in account. Giraas = bite. NIrjaas(i) = test; decide. 1. Untar Laagi = fire of desires. Jul(i) Boojhi = burned to the end. Pause. Gut(i) = situation; condition. Shunk = doubts. Mur(u) = death. Unhud Shabad(i) = with the Divine Shabad = Waheguru’s Naam. 2. Taas(u) = his; for him. Khurr(i) = take to. Pehnaayiyaiy = to honour. 3. Ruv(i) Ruhhiya = is present. Shiv = Waheguru. Shakti = Maya. Trihu Goon = the three effects of Maya. Bundhi = bonded to. Dayhoori = body. Khayl(u) = plays; enjoys. Vijogi = separation; disconnection. Munmukh(i) = Maya-engrossed. Luhhaiy = acquires. 4. Bairaagi = with love (for Waheguru). Ghur(i) = in its own place; peaceful. Muhha Rus = supreme taste. Bhogvaiy = enjoy. Bhookh = appetite; desire. Bhee = again. 5. MEANING: One’s mind gets convinced (about the omnipresence of Waheguru) when he recites His Naam, oh my soul (mind). By acquiring the Divine Knowledge in the shelter of the Guru, one’s internal fire (of worldly desires) is put out. Pause. (Being connected with Waheguru), Such a devotee has no worries about death, and does not care if he lives any longer (to enjoy worldly attractions). He is confident that Waheguru nourishes all living beings and that everyone’s number of breaths and their needs for food are in His account (He ensures all of that). When Waheguru dwells in one’s heart with Guru’s grace, one becomes confident that Waheguru makes His decisions as He chooses. 1. Divine Knowledge, which is highly blissful, is acquired by pondering on the Guru’s Shabad. One realizes his internal situation (the presence of Waheguru within) when he surrenders to the Guru, clears all his doubts (with full faith), and kills the fear of death. 2. One’s haumein is destroyed by acquiring the Divine Knowledge (Waheguru’s Naam). Those who have Waheguru’s Naam on their tongue are taken to Waheguru’s Court, where they receive the robe of honour. I am always a sacrifice for those who serve the Satguru. 3. I see the combination of Waheguru and Maya (worldly attractions) everywhere (Maya is the creation of Waheguru). The human body is bound to the three effects of Maya, so whoever comes to this world plays their part to enjoy the worldly attractions. (With the love for Maya), Those who are disconnected from Waheguru suffer from His separation and cannot unite with Him. 4. But, the mind, which is imbued with the love-filled fear of Waheguru, stays in itself (does not wander any more), and enjoys the great taste of the Divine Knowledge (union with Waheguru) and never experiences the appetite for worldly desires. When a devotee kills his mind (his desires), he merges into Waheguru, and never suffers again, oh Nanak. 5.18. ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 1॥ ਏਹੁ ਮਨੋ ਮੂਰਖੁ ਲੋਭੀਆ ਲੋਭੇ ਲਗਾ ਲੁੋਭਾਨੁ॥ ਸਬਦਿ ਨ ਭੀਜੈ ਸਾਕਤਾ ਦੁਰਮਤਿ ਆਵਨੁ ਜਾਨੁ॥ ਸਾਧੂ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਜੋ ਮਿਲੈ ਤਾ ਪਾਈਐ ਗੁਣੀ ਨਿਧਾਨੁ॥ 1॥ ਮਨ ਰੇ ਹਉਮੈ ਛੋਡਿ ਗੁਮਾਨੁ॥ ਹਰਿ ਗੁਰੁ ਸਰਵਰੁ ਸੇਵਿ ਤੂ ਪਾਵਹਿ ਦਰਗਹ ਮਾਨੁ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਰਾਮ ਨਾਮੁ ਜਪਿ ਦਿਨਸੁ ਰਾਤਿ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਹਰਿ ਧਨੁ ਜਾਨੁ॥ ਸਭਿ ਸੁਖ ਹਰਿ ਰਸ ਭੋਗਣੇ ਸੰਤ ਸਭਾ ਮਿਲਿ ਗਿਆਨੁ॥ ਨਿਤਿ ਅਹਿਨਿਸ ਹਰਿ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਸੇਵਿਆ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਦੀਆ ਨਾਮੁ॥ 2॥ ਕੂਕਰ ਕੂੜੁ ਕਮਾਈਐ ਗੁਰ ਨਿੰਦਾ ਪਚੈ ਪਚਾਨੁ॥ ਭਰਮੇ ਭੂਲ਼ਾ ਦੁਖੂ ਘਣੋ ਜਮੁ ਮਾਰਿ ਕਰੈ ਖੁਲਹਾਨੁ॥ ਮਨਮੁਖਿ ਸੁਖੁ ਨ ਪਾਈਐ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਸੁਖੁ ਸੁਭਾਨੁ॥ 3॥ ਐਥੈ ਧੰਧੁ ਪਿਟਾਈਐ ਸਚੁ ਲਿਖਤੁ ਪਰਵਾਨੁ॥ ਹਰਿ ਸਜਣੁ ਗੁਰੁ ਸੇਵਦਾ ਗੁਰ ਕਰਣੀ ਪਰਧਾਨੁ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮੁ ਨ ਵੀਸਰੈ ਕਰਮਿ ਸਚੈ ਨੀਸਾਣੁ॥ 4॥19॥ Siri Raag(u) Mehla 1: Eh(u) muno moorukh lobhiya, lobhay lugga lobhaan(u). Shabad(i) na bheejaiy saaktaa, doormut(i) aavun(u), jaan(u). Sadhu Satgur(u) jo milaiy, ta paayiyaiy Gooni-Nidhaan(u). 1. Mun ray, haumein chhoad(i) goomaan(u). Hur(i), Gur(u) servur(u) sayv(i) tu, paaveh(i) Durgeh maan(u). Ruhaou. Ram Naam(u) jup(i) dinus(u) raat(i), Gurmukh(i) Hur(i) dhun(u) jaan(u). Subh(i) sookh Hur(i) rus bhognay, sunt subha mil(i) Giyan(u). Nit(i) ehinis Hur(i) Prubh(u) sayviya, Satgur deeya Naam(u). 2. Kookur koorr(u) kmaayiyaiy, Gur ninda puchaiy, puchaan(u). Bhemay bhoola dookh(u) ghuno, Jum(u) maar(i) kuraiy khoolhaan(u). Munmukh(i) sookh(i) na paayiyaiy, Gurmukh(i) sookh(u) soobhaan(u). 3. Aiythaiy dhundh pitaayiyaiy, Sach(u) likhut(u) pervaan(u). Hur(i) sujjun(u) Gur(u) saynvda, Gur kerni perdhaan(u). Nanak, Naam(u) na veesraiy, kerm(i) Sachaiy neeshaan(u). 4.19. Word Meaning: Lobhiya = greedy. Lobhaan(u) = engrossed in greed. Bheejaiy = get imbued. Saaktaa = Maya-worshippers. Doomut(i) = immoral thinking. Aavun Jaan = coming and going; rebirths. Gooni-Nidhaan(u) = the source of all virtues; Waheguru. 1. Goomaan = pride. Servur(u) = pool (of Naam-Amrit). Pause. Kookur = dog; doglike. Koorr(u) = falsehood. Puchaiy Puchaa(u) = burns (suffer badly). Ghuno = immense. Khoolhaan = pulverize. Soobhaan = amazing. 3. Aiythaiy = here; in this world. Dhundh Pitaayiyaiy = perform worldly affairs. Sach(u) Likhut(u) = Waheguru’s written Huqam. Saunvda = serves. Kerni = performance of (noble) deeds. Perdhaan(u) = main. Neeshaan = identification; approval. 4. MEANING: The minds of the Maya-engrossed people do not get imbued in the Guru’s Shabad, and due to their immoral thinking, they continue to take births and die. (Like those people) you are also engrossed in Maya out of greed, oh, my foolish mind. But one can realize Waheguru, which is the source of all virtues, in Satguru’s shelter. 1. To earn respect in Waheguru’s Court, get rid of your selfishness, ego, and your pride, and serve the Guru, who is a manifestation of Waheguru, and is a pool of all virtues, oh my mind. Pause. Recognize the wealth (great worth) of Waheguru in the shelter of Satguru, and recite His Naam day and night. Acquire (enshrine) Waheguru’s Naam in your heart in the holy congregation of the saints (Satguru), and enjoy all the comforts and all the great tastes of life. Those who are given Waheguru’s Naam by Satguru serve Waheguru (recite His Naam) day and night. 2. The vilifiers of Satguru lie about Him, and are like dogs (which bark at everyone), as a result of which they suffer badly in the fire (of jealousy). Lost in their misbeliefs, they suffer immensely, and the Angel of justice pulverizes them (punishes them severely). The vilifiers of Satguru never enjoy peace, while Guru’s Sikhs enjoy amazing bliss. 3. The man performs Maya-related tasks here (in this world), but only they are approved by Waheguru, who obeys His Huqam (Will) (even while performing worldly affairs). Performing deeds as per the Teachings of the Guru is the main objective for the man, and such a devotee serves the Guru and Waheguru and becomes their friend. With the benevolence of Waheguru, the devotee who never forgets Waheguru’s Naam receives the stamp of His approval, oh Nanak. 4.19. ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 1॥ ਇਕੁ ਤਿਲੁ ਪਿਆਰਾ ਵੀਸਰੈ ਰੋਗੁ ਵਡਾ ਮਨ ਮਾਹਿ॥ ਕਿਉ ਦਰਗਹ ਪਤਿ ਪਾਈਐ ਜਾ ਹਰਿ ਵਸੈ ਮਨ ਮਾਹਿ॥ ਗੁਰਿ ਮਿਲਿਐ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਾਈਐ ਅਗਨਿ ਮਰੈ ਗੁਣ ਮਾਹਿ॥ 1॥ ਮਨ ਰੇ ਅਹਿਨਿਸਿ ਹਰਿ ਗੁਣ ਸਾਰਿ॥ ਜਿਨ ਖਿਨੁ ਪਲੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਨ ਵੀਸਰੈ ਤੇ ਜਨ ਵਿਰਲੇ ਸੰਸਾਰਿ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਜੋਤੀ ਜੋਤਿ ਮਿਲਾਈਐ ਸੁਰਤੀ ਸੁਰਤਿ ਸੰਜੋਗੁ॥ ਹਿੰਸਾ ਹਉਮੈ ਗਤੁ ਗਏ ਨਾਹੀ ਸਹਸਾ ਸੋਗੁ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਜਿਸੁ ਹਰਿ ਮਨਿ ਵਸੈ ਤਿਸੁ ਮੇਲੇ ਗੁਰੁ ਸੰਜੋਗੁ॥ 2॥ ਕਾਇਆ ਕਾਮਣਿ ਜੇ ਕਰੀ ਭੋਗੇ ਭੋਗਣਹਾਰੁ॥ ਤਿਸੁ ਸਿਉ ਨੇਹੁ ਨ ਕੀਜਈ ਜੋ ਦੀਸੈ ਚਲਣਹਾਰੁ॥ ਗੁਰਮਖਿ ਰਵਹਿ ਸੋਹਾਗਣੀ ਸੋ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਸੇਜ ਭਤਾਰ॥ 3॥
