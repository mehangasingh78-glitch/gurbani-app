(ਅੰਗ 22)॥     ਚਾਰੇ ਅਗਨਿ ਨਿਵਾਰਿ ਮਰੁ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਹਰਿ ਜਲੁ ਪਾਇ॥ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਕਮਲੁ ਪ੍ਰਗਾਸਿਆ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਭਰਿਆ ਅਘਾਇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਮੀਤੁ ਕਰਿ ਸਚੁ ਪਾਵਹਿ ਦਰਗਹ ਜਾਇ॥ 4॥20॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 1: Ikk(u) til(u) Piyara veesraiy, roag(u) vudda, mun maah(i). Kiyoun Durgeh put(i) paayiyaiy, ja Hur(i) vussaiy mun maah(i).  Gur(i) miliyaiyn sookh paayiyaiy, ugun(i) muraiy goon maah(i). 1. Mun ray, ehinis Hur(i) goon saar(i). Jinh khin(u), pul(u) Naam(u) na veesraiy, tay jun virlaiy sunsaar(i). Ruhaou. Joti, Jot(i) milaayiyaiy, soorti, soort(i) sunjog(u). Hinsa, haumein gut(u) guyyay, naahi sehsa soag(u). Gurmukh(i) jis(u) Hur(i) mun(i) vussaiy, tis(u) maylay Gur(u) sunjog(u).  2. Kaaya kaamun(i) jay kureen, bhogay bhogunhaar(u). tis(u) siyoun nayh(u) na keejyee, jo deesaiy chullunhaar(u).  Gurmukh(i) ruveh(i) sohaagni, so Prubh sayj bhutaar.   3.
(PAGE 22):         Chaaray ugun(i) nivaar(i) mur(u), Gurmukh(i) Hur(i) jul(u) paayay. Untar(i) kumul pergaasiya, Amrit(u) bhuriya aghaayay. Nanak, Satgur meet(u) kur(i) Sach(u) paaveh(i) Durgeh Jaayay.  4.20.
Word Meaning: Til(u) = very short time.   Kiyoun = how.   Put(i) = respect.  Ja = with which.   Ugun = fire (of desires).        1.      Ehinis(i) = day and night.   Saar(i) = remember. Khin = Pul = very short moment.    Tay Jun = those.   Virlaiy = rare; very few.        Pause.     Joti = human soul.   Jot(i) = Supreme Soul.   Soorti = the subject of focus (God).   Soort(i) = focused mind.     Sunjog(u) = union.   Hinsa = cruelty.   Gut(u) Guyyay = ran away.   Sehsa = worries.   Soag(u) = grief.        2.       Kaaya = body.   Kaamun(i) = (loyal) wife.             Bhogunhaar = one who enjoys (Waheguru).  Nayh = love.      Chullunhaar = perishable. Ruveh(i) = enjoys.   Sohaagni = married; beloved.   Sayj = bed.    Bhutaar = husband.           3.        Chaaray Ugun(i) = the four fires: cruelty; attachment; greed; and rage.    Nivaar(i) = remove.   Mur(u) = kill worldly desires.  Pergasiya = blooms.  Aghaayay = contentment. 4.
MEANING:  I feel a deep affliction of the mind if I forget my Dear (Waheguru) for even a short moment. To earn respect in Waheguru’s Court, one needs to enshrine His Naam in one's mind.   One acquires noble qualities in the shelter of the Guru due to which the fire of his worldly desires is killed, and he enjoys blissfulness.          1.
So, remember Waheguru’s Virtues (sing His praise) day and night, oh my mind.   But there are very few such individuals who do not forget Waheguru for even a moment.       Pause.        If a devotee focuses his mind on Waheguru (His Naam) with full devotion, he gets united with Waheguru, and his soul merges into the Supreme Soul; his cruelty and haumein vanish, and he never suffers from worries and any grief. Satguru unites (connects) the devotee with Waheguru, who gets Waheguru’s Naam enshrined in his heart with the grace of Satguru.           2.              One should not have love for the perishable to satisfy one's sensual pleasures. (So, rather than enjoying worldly pleasures), if I make my body an obedient wife of Waheguru, then Waheguru will enjoy union with me (His Naam will be enshrined in my heart).  With Guru’s grace, such a devotee becomes married to Waheguru (gets connected with him), and the Waheguru-Husband comes to the bed of the devotee’s heart.            3.          Make Satguru your friend, and with His grace, sprinkle the water of Naam-Amrit on the four fires (of cruelty, attachments, greed, and fury) smouldering in your mind, and kill your desires. Then you will notice that the lotus of your heart will bloom (your mind will be enlightened), you will be fully contented with the Naam Amrit, and will be united with Waheguru, oh Nanak.     4.20.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 1॥ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਜਪਹੁ ਪਿਆਰਿਆ ਗੁਰਮਤਿ ਲੇ ਹਰਿ ਬੋਲਿ॥ ਮਨੁ ਸਚ ਕਸਵਟੀ ਲਾਈਐ ਤੁਲੀਐ ਪੂਰੈ ਤੋਲਿ॥ ਕੀਮਤਿ ਕਿਨੈ ਨ ਪਾਈਐ ਰਿਦ ਮਾਣਕ ਮੋਲਿ ਅਮੋਲਿ॥ 1॥ ਭਾਈ ਰੇ ਹਰਿ ਹੀਰਾ ਗੁਰ ਮਾਹਿ॥ ਸਤ ਸੰਗਤਿ ਸਤਗੁਰੁ ਪਾਈਐ ਅਹਿਨਿਸਿ ਸਬਦਿ ਸਲਾਹਿ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਸਚੁ ਵਖਰੁ ਧਨੁ ਰਾਸਿ ਲੈ ਪਾਈਐ ਗੁਰ ਪਰਗਾਸਿ॥ ਜਿਉ ਅਗਨਿ ਮਰੈ ਜਲਿ ਪਾਈਐ ਤਿਉ ਤ੍ਰਿਸਨਾ ਦਾਸਨਿ ਦਾਸਿ॥ ਜਮ ਜੰਦਾਰੁ ਨ ਲਗਈ ਇਉ ਭਉਜਲੁ ਤਰੈ ਤਰਾਸਿ॥ 2॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਕੂੜੁ ਨ ਭਾਵਈ ਸਚਿ ਰਤੇ ਸਚ ਭਾਇ॥ ਸਾਕਤ ਸਚ ਨ ਭਾਵਈ ਕੂੜੈ ਕੂੜੀ ਪਾਇ॥ ਸਚਿ ਰਤੇ ਗੁਰ ਮੇਲੀਐ ਸਚੇ ਸਚਿ ਸਮਾਇ॥3॥ ਮਨ ਮਹਿ ਮਾਣਕੁ ਲਾਲੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਰਤਨ ਪਦਾਰਥ ਹੀਰੁ॥ ਸਚੁ ਵਖਰੁ ਧਨੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਹੈ ਘਟਿ ਘਟਿ ਗਹਿਰ ਗੰਭੀਰੁ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਪਾਈਐ ਦਇਆ ਕਰੇ ਹਰਿ ਹੀਰੁ॥  4॥21॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 1: Hur(i) Hur(i) jupou piyariya Gurmut(i) lay Hur(i) boal(i). Mun(u) Sach kusvutti laayiyaiy, tooliyaiy pooraiy toal(i). Keemut(i) kinaiy na paayiyaiy, rid maanuk moal(i) amoal(i). 1. Bhaayi ray, Hur(i) heera, Gur maah(i). Sat-sungut(i) Satgur paayiyaiy, ehinis(i) Shabad(i) salaah(i). 1. Ruhaou. Sach(u) Vukkhur(u) dhun(u) raas(i) laiy, paayiyaiy Gur pergaas(i). Jiyoun ugun(i) muraiy jul paayiyaiy, tiyoun trishna  daasun(i) daas(i). Jum jundaar(u) na luggyee, iyoun bhuoujul(u) turaiy turaas(i). 2. Gurmukh(i) koorr(u) na bhaavyee, Sach(i) ruttay, Sach bhaayay. Saakut Sach na bhaavyee, koorraiy, koorri paayayn. Sach(i) ruttay Gur mayliyaiy, Sachay Sach(i) smaayay. 3. Mun meh(i) maanuk(u), laal(u) Naam(u), ruttan pdaaruth heer(u). Sach(u) vukkhur dhun(u) Naam(u) haiy, ghut(i), ghut(i) Gehr, Gumbheer(u). Nanak, Gurmukh(i) paayiyaiy, duyya kuray Hur(i) heer(u).  4.21.
Word Meaning: Jupou = recite (the Naam).   Boal(i) = utter; say.   Kusvutti = touchstone; yardstick.   Tooliyaiy = weighed.       Toal(i) = standard weight.  Rid = heart.  Maanuk = pearls.   Amoal(i) = priceless.         1.         Maah(i) = with; in possession.    Salaah(u) = praise.      Pause.          Vukkhur = merchandise.  Dhun(u) Raas(i) = capital; wealth.    Pergaas(i) = enlightenment. Trishna = fire of desires.      Jundaar(u) = evil-spirited.    Bhoujul(u) = the world-sea.    Tyraiy Turaas(i) = swim across with confidence.            2.          Koorr(u) = falsehood; hypocrisy.   Bhaayay = with love.   Saakut = Maya-engrossed.    Sach = Waheguru.    Koorri = false.    Paayayn = ability; thinking power.  Sach(i) = with the Naam.        3.         Laal(u) = ruby.       Ruttan Pdaaruth = valuable jewels.     Heer(u) = diamond.   Gehr = unfathomable.    Gumbheer = never changing (Waheguru).         4.
MEANING: Recite Waheguru’s Naam and sing His praise with the Advice (Shabad) of the Guru, oh my dear friend.  One’s mind is tested on the scale, which assesses one’s virtues against those required for union with Waheguru, and one is approved for the union only if one meets the required standard. Nobody can determine the spiritual worth (transcendence) of such a devotee who has met the requirement of pearl-like noble qualities for the union.            1.              The diamond-like Waheguru’s Naam is in the control of the Guru (only He can implant the Naam in the devotees’ hearts).  One can meet with the Guru in the gathering of the holy saints when he needs to sing Waheguru’s praise day and night.          Pause.
When a devotee’s mind is enlightened by the Guru, he acquires the wealth of Waheguru’s Naam, and the desire of his fires is put out like water puts out the common fires; also, the devotee becomes so humble as if he were the slave of Waheguru’s slaves.  The evil Angel of death cannot touch him, as he swims across the world-sea (to merge into Waheguru).          2.            Imbued in the love of Waheguru, the Sikhs of the Guru do not like falsehood (hypocrisy and love for the perishables) while the Maya-engrossed hypocrites do not like Waheguru (His praise), because their thinking power is maligned by their hypocrisy.   The devotees, who are imbued with Waheguru’s love, get connected with Satguru and get absorbed in Waheguru with His Naam.          3.         The unfathomable, and the never-changing Waheguru is present in every heart, and His Naam is the devotee’s eternal wealth.  With the Naam at heart, the devotee acquires all the noble qualities like those of pearls, rubies, jewels, and diamonds.  When the diamond-like Waheguru becomes benevolent to someone, he acquires His Naam in his heart in the shelter of Satguru, oh Nanak.   4.21.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 1॥ ਭਰਮੈ ਭਾਹਿ ਨ ਵਿਝਵੈ ਜੇ ਭਵੈ ਦਿਸੰਤਰ ਦੇਸੁ॥ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਮੈਲੁ ਨ ਉਤਰੈ ਧ੍ਰਿਗੁ ਜੀਵਣੁ ਧ੍ਰਿਗੁ ਵੇਸੁ॥ ਹੋਰੁ ਕਿਤੈ ਭਗਤਿ ਨ ਹੋਵਈ ਬਿਨੁ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਕੇ ਉਪਦੇਸ॥ 1॥ ਮਨ ਰੇ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਅਗਨਿ ਨਿਵਾਰਿ॥ ਗੁਰ ਕਾ ਕਹਿਆ ਮਨਿ ਵਸੈ ਹਉਮੈ ਤ੍ਰਿਸਨਾ ਮਾਰਿ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਮਨੁ ਮਾਣਕੁ ਨਿਰਮੋਲੁ ਹੈ ਰਾਮ ਨਾਮਿ ਪਤਿ ਪਾਇ॥ ਮਿਲਿ ਸਤ ਸੰਗਤਿ ਹਰਿ ਪਾਈਐ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਹਰਿ ਲਿਵ ਲਾਇ॥ ਆਪੁ ਗਇਆ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਮਿਲਿ ਸਲਲੈ ਸਲਲ ਸਮਾਇ॥ 2॥ ਜਿਨਿ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮਿ ਨ ਚੇਤਿਓ ਸੁ ਅਉਗੁਣਿ ਆਵੈ ਜਾਇ॥ ਜਿਸੁ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਪੁਰਖ ਨ ਭੇਟਿਓ ਸੁ ਭਉਜਲਿ ਪਚੈ ਪਚਾਇ॥ ਇਹੁ ਮਾਣਕੁ ਜੀਉ ਨਿਰਮੋਲੁ ਹੈ ਇਉ ਕਉਡੀ ਬਦਲੈ ਜਾਇ॥3॥ ਜਿਨਾ ਸਤਗੁਰੁ ਰਸਿ ਮਿਲੈ ਸੇ ਪੂਰੇ ਪੁਰਖ ਸੁਜਾਣ॥ ਗੁਰ ਮਿਲਿ ਭਉਜਲੁ ਲੰਘੀਐ ਦਰਗਹ ਪਤਿ ਪਰਵਾਣੁ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਤੇ ਮੁਖ ਉਜਲੇ ਧੁਨਿ ਉਪਜੈ ਸਬਦੁ ਨੀਸਾਣੁ॥ 4॥22॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 1: Bhermaiy, bhaayay na vijhvaiy, jay bhuvvaiy disuntar days(u). Untar maiyl(u) na ootraiy, dhrig(u) jeevun(u), dhrig(u) vays(u). Hoar(u) kitaiy bhugut(i) na hovyee, bin(u) Satgur kay Oopdays. 1. Mun ray, Gurmukh(i) ugun(i) nivaar(i). Gur ka kuhhiya mun(i) vussaiy, haumein trishna maar(i). Ruhaou.  Mun(u) maanuk(u), nirmoal(u) haiy, Ram Naam(i) put(i) paayay. Mil(i) Sat-sungut(i) Hur(i) paayiyaiy, Gurmukh(i) Hur(i) liv laayay. Aap(u) guyya, sookh(u) paaya, mil(i) sul-laiy sulul smaayay. 2. Jinh(i) Hur(i), Hur(i) Naam(i) na chaytiyo, su augun(i) aavaiy jaayay. Jis(u) Satgur(u) Purakh na bhaytiyo, su bhuoujul(i) puchaiy, puchaayay. Eh(u) maanuk(u) jeeou nirmoal(u) haiy, iyoun kaudi budlaiy jaayay. 3. Jinha Satgur(u) rus(i) milaiy, say pooray purakh soojan. Gur mil(i) bhuoujul lunghiyaiy, Durgeh put(i) pervaan. Nanak, tay mookh oojlay, dhoon(i) oopjaiy, Shabad(u) neeshaan(u).   4.22.
Word Meaning: Bhermaiy = by roaming around.      Bhaayay = fire (of desires).  Vijhvaiy = put out.      Disuntar Days(u) = faraway lands.   Dhrig(u) = reprehensible.   Vays(u) = religious outfit.        1.       Nivaar(i) = remove.   Kuhhiya = Words; Shabad.  Maar(i) = kill.  
Pause.          Nirmoal(u) = priceless.   Put(i) = respect.   Liv Laayay = meditate.  Aap(u) = haumein (selfishness and ego).   Sulul = water.         2.             Chatiyo = remember.  Su = that.  Augun(i) = bad qualities.  Bhaytiyo = meet with.   Puchaiy Puchaayay = suffers badly.  Kaudi = a worthless cowrie shell.  Budlay = in exchange.          3.          Rus(i) = with affection.  Pooray = perfect.  Soojaan = wise.   Oojlay = shiny; unblemished.  Dhoon(i) = sound (of Waheguru’s praise).      Neeshaan(u) = identification.         4.
MEANING: (Some people leave their homes and roam around with the belief that they will get detached from worldly attractions. But Guru Nanak Sahib has the firm belief that one’s fire of desires is never put out, no matter how far away lands he roams around.  Leading life with such misleading beliefs is reprehensible, and so is wearing such religious garbs.  No other worship than the Teachings of Satguru is approved (by Waheguru).        1.  
Put out the fire of your desires in the shelter of the Guru, oh my mind.  One’s haumein (selfishness and ego) are destroyed when Satguru’s Shabad is cultivated in his mind. 
Pause.                 The mind, which has Waheguru’s Naam enshrined in it, becomes invaluable and pure like a pearl and earns respect everywhere.  One acquires Waheguru’s Naam in the holy gathering when he meditates on Guru’s Shabad; (by doing so, he gets rid of his selfishness and ego, attains blissfulness and merges in Waheguru like water in one container mixes fully with water in another container.           2.           Those who do not remember Waheguru cannot rid themselves of their ignoble qualities, and as a result, they continue to take rebirths. Likewise, those who do not seek Satguru’s shelter suffer badly in the world-sea.   The human life of such individuals, which is otherwise priceless, is wasted, as if traded for a worthless cowrie shell.          3.        Those who seek the shelter of Satguru with love and respect are truly wise (acquire His wisdom). One swims across the sea of Maya in the shelter of Satguru and is respected in Waheguru’s Court.  The sound of Waheguru’s praise arises in the mind of such devotees, which becomes their identification to be honoured in Waheguru’s Court, where their faces radiate due to their purity of mind, oh Nanak.        4.22.
