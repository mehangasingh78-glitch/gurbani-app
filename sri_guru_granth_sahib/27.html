(ਅੰਗ 26)॥     ਸਭ ਦੁਨੀਆ ਆਵਣ ਜਾਣੀਆ॥ 3॥ ਵਿਚਿ ਦੁਨੀਆ ਸੇਵ ਕਮਾਈਐ॥ ਤਾ ਦਰਗਹ ਬੈਸਣੁ ਪਾਈਐ॥ ਕਹੁ ਨਾਨਕ ਬਾਹ ਲੁਡਾਈਐ॥ 4॥33॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 1, Ghur(u) 5: Achhull Chhalaayi neh chhulaiy, neh ghaaou kataara kur(i) sukaiy. Jiyoun Sahib(u) raakhaiy tiyoun ruhhaiy. Iss lobhi ka jeeou tulpulaiy. 1.   Bin(u) tayl, deeva kiyoun julaiy. Ruhaou.  Pothi Puran kmaayiyaiy. Bhuou vutti it(u) tun(i) paayiyaiy. Sach(u) boojhun(u) aan(i) julaayiyaiy. 2. Eh(u) tayl(u) deeva iyoun julaiy. Kur(i) chaanun(u) Sahib(u), tuou milaiy.  Ruhaou. It(u) tun(i) laagaiy Baania. Sookh(u) hovaiy sayv kmaaniyaan. 
(PAGE 26):               Subh dooniya aavun jaaniya.  3. Vich(i) dooniya sayv kmaayiyaiy. Ta Durgeh baisun(u) paayiyaiy. Kuhhou Nanak, baanh loodaayiyaiy.  4.33.
Word Meaning: Achhull = undeceivable.     Chhalaayi = try to deceive.   Ghaaou = wound.  Kataara = big knife.   Lobhi = greedy.  Jeeou = mind.   Tulpulaiy = gets tempted.    1.
Tayl = oil (Waheguru’s Naam).    Deeva = lamp (to enlighten the mind).         Pause.
Pothi = book of Teachings of the Guru.   Kmaayiyaiy = lead life by.  Bhuou = fear of God.  Vutti = wick.    Sach(u) Boojhun(u) = comprehending Waheguru (making a connection with Him).  Aan(i)= by bringing.   Julaayiyaiy = light up.          2.        Baania = Guru’s Shabad.     3.
Baisun(u) = seat.   Baanh Loodaayiyaiy = walk with confidence; feel blissful.       4. 
MEANING:  Maya is so undeceivable (its attractions are so strong) that no one can deceive it (resist the temptations of its attractions) even if he tries very hard to do so.  No knife can cause any wound to it (weaken its effects). Actually, it is Waheguru’s Will that man’s greedy mind becomes weak in its temptations.  So, we have to live by His will.         1.
Just like an oil lamp cannot be lighted without oil, one’s mind cannot be enlightened without the oil of Waheguru’s Naam.           Pause.        To light up the lamp of mind, one should lead his life according to the Teachings of the Guru, which should be his reading of the Puranas; and a love-filled fear of Waheguru in his mind should serve as the wick of the lamp.  Comprehending Waheguru (making a connection with Him) enlightens the lamp-like mind of the devotee.            2.         (Waheguru’s Naam) Is the oil to light up the lamp of the mind? One connects with Waheguru when one's mind is illuminated by the lamp described above.         Pause.           Everybody comes and goes in this temporary world. One feels blissful only when one enshrines the Guru’s Bani (His Shabad) in one's mind.         3.
One should serve humanity in the world, to earn a seat in Waheguru’s Court (to be approved by Him) when he can walk with pride (feel blissful), oh Nanak.   4.33.

 

                         ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 3 ਘਰੁ 1॥ ੴ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਪ੍ਰਸਾਦਿ॥ 
             Siri Raag(u) Mehla 3 Ghur(u) 1:  Ek Onkar Sat Gur Pursaad(i).

ਹਉ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਸੇਵੀ ਆਪਣਾ ਇਕ ਮਨਿ ਇਕ ਚਿਤਿ ਭਾਇ॥ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਮਨ ਕਾਮਨਾ ਤੀਰਥੁ ਹੈ ਜਿਸ ਨੋ ਦੇਇ ਬੁਝਾਇ॥ ਮਨ ਚਿੰਦਿਆ ਵਰੁ ਪਾਵਣਾ ਜੋ ਇਛੈ ਸੋ ਫਲੁ ਪਾਇ॥ ਨਾਉ ਧਿਆਈਐ ਨਾਉ ਮੰਗੀਐ ਨਾਮੇ ਸਹਜਿ ਸਮਾਇ॥1॥ ਮਨ ਮੇਰੇ ਹਰਿ ਰਸੁ ਚਾਖੁ ਤਿਖ ਜਾਇ॥ ਜਿਨੀ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਚਾਖਿਆ ਸਹਜੇ ਰਹੇ ਸਮਾਇ॥1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਜਿਨੀ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਸੇਵਿਆ ਤਿਨੀ ਪਾਇਆ ਨਾਮੁ ਨਿਧਾਨੁ॥ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਰਸੁ ਰਵਿ ਰਹਿਆ ਚੂਕਾ ਮਨਿ ਅਭਿਮਾਨੁ॥ ਹਿਰਦੈ ਕਮਲੁ ਪ੍ਰਗਾਸਿਆ ਲਾਗਾ ਸਹਜਿ ਧਿਆਨੁ॥ ਮਨੁ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਹਰਿ ਰਵਿ ਰਹਿਆ ਪਾਇਆ ਦਰਗਹਿ ਮਾਨੁ॥ 2॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਸੇਵਨਿ ਆਪਣਾ ਤੇ ਵਿਰਲੇ ਸੰਸਾਰਿ॥ ਹਉਮੈ ਮਮਤਾ ਮਾਰਿ ਕੈ ਹਰਿ ਰਾਖਿਆ ਉਰਧਾਰਿ॥ ਹਉ ਤਿਨ ਕੈ ਬਲਿਹਾਰਣੈ ਜਿਨਾ ਨਾਮੇ ਲਗਾ ਪਿਆਰੁ॥ ਸੇਈ ਸੁਖੀਏ ਚਹੁ ਜੁਗੀ ਜਿਨਾ ਨਾਮੁ ਅਖੁਟੁ ਅਪਾਰੁ॥ 3॥ ਗੁਰ ਮਿਲਿਐ ਨਾਮੁ ਪਾਈਐ ਚੂਕੈ ਮੋਹ ਪਿਆਸ॥ ਹਰਿ ਸੇਤੀ ਮਨੁ ਰਵਿ ਰਹਿਆ ਘਰ ਹੀ ਮਾਹਿ ਉਦਾਸੁ॥ ਜਿਨਾ ਹਰਿ ਕਾ ਸਾਦੁ ਆਇਆ ਹਉ ਤਿਨ ਬਲਿਹਾਰੈ ਜਾਸੁ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਦਰੀ ਪਾਈਐ ਸਚੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਗੁਣਤਾਸੁ॥ 4॥1॥34॥
Houn Satgur(u) sayveen aapna, ikk mun(i) ikk chit(i), bhaayay. Satgur(u) mun kaamna teeruth(u) haiy jis nu dayay boojhayay. Mun chindya vur(u) paavna, jo ichhaiyy so ful(u) paayay. Naoun dhiyayaiy Naoun mungiyaiy, Naamay sehj(i) smaayay. 1. Mun mayray Hur(i) rus(u) chaakh(u) tikh jaayay. Jini Gurmukh(i) chaakhiya, sehjay ruhay smaayay. 1. Ruhaou. Jinhi Satgur(u) saviya, tinhi paaya Naam(u) nidhaan(u). Untar(i) Hur(i) rus(u) ruv(i) ruhhiya chooka mun(i) abhimaan(u). Hirdaiy kumul(u) purgaasiya, laaga sehj(i) dhiyaan(u). Mun(u) nirmul(u) Hur(i) ruv(i) ruhhiya, paaya Durgeh(i) maan(u). 2. Satgur(u) sayvun(i) aapna tay virlay sunsaar(i). Haumein mumta maar(i) kaiy Hur(i) raakhiya oordhaar(i). Houn tinh kaiy bulihaarnaiy jinha Naamay lugga piyaar(u). Sayee sookhiyay ch-houn joogeen, jinha Naam(u) ukhoot(u) upaar(u). 3. Gur miliyaiy Naam(u) paayiyaiy, chookay moh piyaas. Hur(i) saytee mun(u) ruv(i) ruhhiya ghur hee maah(i) oodaas(u).  Jinha Hur(i) ka saad(u) aayaa, houn tin bulihaaraiy jaas(u). Nanak nudri paayiyaiy Sach(u) Naam(u) Goontaas(u).  4. 1.34.
Word Meaning: Houn = I.   Sayveen= serve (obey Him).  Bhaayay = with affection (respect).  Mun Kaamna = mind’s wishes.  Boojhayay = enlightens.  Mun Chindya = as wished.    Vur(u) = blessing.    Sehj = stage of mental contentment.        1.        Chaakh(u) = to taste.  Tikh = thirst (of desires).        Pause.        Nidhaan(u) = treasure.    Ruv(i) ruhhiya = present.  Chooka = ends.  Hirdaiy     Kumul(u) Purgaasiya = lotus of mind blooms (state of bliss).     Nirmul(u) = clean.       2.          Virlay = rare (only a few).   Mumta = attachment with family.   Oordhaar(i) = acquire (embed) in mind.   Bulihaarnaiy = offer to sacrifice life.  Ch-houn joogeen = in all the four eons (in all times).  Ukhoot(u) = inexhaustible.  Upaar(u) = limitless.        3.
Moh piyaas.   = thirst of attachment.   Oodaas(u) = detached.      Nudri = kind sight.  Goontaas(u) = source of all noble qualities (Waheguru).        4.
MEANING: There is only one Supreme Being who can be realized with the Guru’s grace. 
Taste the sweet juice of Waheguru’s Naam to quench your thirst of desires, oh my mind!  Those who have tasted it by the grace of the Guru acquire the state of bliss where the mind wanders no more.          Pause.          I serve (obey) my Satguru with undivided attention, and with respectful love for Him.  The one who has been enlightened by the Guru realizes that the Guru is the real holy place where all one’s wishes are fulfilled, and one receives the blessings of one's choice. He can have any of his wishes and desires fulfilled.  (However, when one has that kind of faith in the Guru), he requests begs) for Waheguru’s Naam only; and recites the Naam, whereby he acquires mental contentment.           1.
Those who serve Satguru acquire the Naam-treasure. The sweet taste of Waheguru’s Naam fills their mind, and their selfishness and ego are eradicated.  The lotus of their mind blossoms, and they find contentment.  Waheguru appears in their clean (pure) mind, and they are honoured in His Court.          2.           There are only a few who serve Satguru in this world. They overcome their selfishness, ego, and affection for their family, and cleanse their hearts so that Waheguru may reside there.   I am a sacrifice for those who keep Waheguru’s Naam in their hearts (minds).  Those, who make Waheguru’s Naam - which is an inexhaustible and unlimited treasure - their sole support, find happiness and contentment in all the four eons (no matter what the eon is).             3.
Waheguru’s Naam can be acquired only by the grace of the Guru, as a result of which one’s thirst for worldly attachment is lost.  When one’s mind is imbued with Waheguru’s Naam, he becomes detached from worldly attractions while still living in the family. I will gladly offer my life to those who keep Waheguru’s Naam in their hearts.  However, Waheguru’s Naam, which is the source of all noble qualities, can be acquired only by Guru’s grace, oh Nanak.   4.1.34.
    
ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥  ਬਹੁ ਭੇਖ ਕਰਿ ਭਰਮਾਈਐ ਮਨਿ ਹਿਰਦੈ ਕਪਟੁ ਕਮਾਇ॥ ਹਰਿ ਕਾ ਮਹਲੁ ਨ ਪਾਵਈ ਮਰਿ ਵਿਸਟਾ ਮਾਹਿ ਸਮਾਇ॥ 1॥ ਮਨ ਰੇ ਗ੍ਰਿਹ ਹੀ ਮਾਹਿ ਉਦਾਸੁ॥ ਸਚੁ ਸੰਜਮੁ ਕਰਣੀ ਸੋ ਕਰੇ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਹੋਇ ਪਰਗਾਸੁ॥1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਸਬਦਿ ਮਨੁ ਜੀਤਿਆ ਗਤਿ ਮਤਿ ਘਰੈ ਮਹਿ ਪਾਇ॥ ਹਰਿ ਕਾ ਨਾਮੁ ਧਿਆਈਐ ਸਤਸੰਗਤਿ ਮੇਲਿ ਮਿਲਾਇ॥ 2॥ ਜੇ ਲਖ ਇਸਤਰੀਆ ਭੋਗ ਕਰਹਿ ਨਵ ਖੰਡ ਰਾਜ ਕਮਾਹਿ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਸਤਗੁਰ ਸੁਖੁ ਨ ਪਾਵਈ ਫਿਰਿ ਫਿਰਿ ਜੋਨੀ ਪਾਹਿ॥ 3॥ ਹਰਿ ਹਾਰੁ ਕੰਠਿ ਜਿਨੀ ਪਹਿਰਿਆ ਗੁਰ ਚਰਣੀ ਚਿਤੁ ਲਾਇ॥ ਤਿਨਾ ਪਿਛੈ ਰਿਧਿ ਸਿਧਿ ਫਿਰੈ ਓਨਾ ਤਿਲੁ ਨ ਤਮਾਇ॥ 4॥ ਜੋ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਭਾਵੈ ਸੋ ਥੀਐ ਅਵਰੁ ਨ ਕਰਣਾ ਜਾਇ॥ ਜਨੁ ਨਾਨਕੁ ਜੀਵੈ ਨਾਮੁ ਲੈ ਹਰਿ ਦੇਵਹੁ ਸਹਜਿ ਸੁਭਾਇ॥ 5॥2॥35॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 3:  Buhou bhaykh kur(i) bhurmaayiyaiy, mun(i) hirdaiy kupt(u) kmaayay. Hur(i) ka mehl(u) na paavyee, mur(i) vishta maah(i) smaayay. Mun ray, greh hee maahay oodaas(u). Sach(u) sunjum(u) kurni so kuray, Gurmukh(i) hoyay purgaas(u). 1. Ruhaou.  Gur kaiy Shabad(i) mun(u) jeetiya, gut(i) mut(i) ghuraiy meh(i) paayay. Hur(i) ka Naam(u) dhiyayiyaiy,  Sutsungat(i) mayl(i) milaayay. 2. Jay lukh isturiyaan bhog kureh(i) nuv khund raaj kmaahay. Bin(u) Satgur sookh(u) na paavyee phir(i) phir(i) joani paahay. 3.  Hur(i) haar(u) kuntth(i) jinhi pehriya Gur churni chit(u) laayay. Tina pichhaiyy ridh(i) sidh(i) phiraiy, ohna til(u) na tmaayay. 4. Jo Prubh bhaavaiy so theeyaiy uver(u) na kurna jaayay. Jun(u) Nanak(u) jeevaiy Naam(u) laiy, Hur(i) dayvhou sehj(i) soobhaayay.    5.2.35.
Word Meaning:  Bhaykh = religious garbs.    Bhurmaayiyaiy = wander around (with the misbelief that one’s mind gets rid of evil thoughts by running away from home).   Kupt(u) = dishonesty.      Mehl(u) = palace (residence).   Vishta = crap (Maya).         1.
Oodaas(u) = state of no desires.  Sach(u) Sunjum(u) = control on desires.       Pause.
Jeetiya = won (controlled).  Gut(i) = high spiritual state.  Mut(i) = wisdom; thinking.   Ghuraiy meh(i) = inside the house (mind).    Mayl(i) = union.         2.         Isturiyaan = women.    Nuv Khund = the nine regions of earth (the whole earth).   Phir(i) phir(i) = again and again.   Joni paahay = takes different life forms.         3.         Haar(u) = necklace.   Kuntth(i) = (around the) neck; on the tongue.   Ridh(i) Sidh(i) = supernatural powers.  Til(u) = (the size of) a sesame seed.    Tmmayay = desire.         4.         Bhaavaiy = likes.     Theeyaiy = happens.  Uver(u) = anything else. 5.
MEANING: If a man dons religious garbs and wanders around (to give the impression of an ascetic), but harbours dishonesty, and other evil thoughts in mind, never has access to Waheguru’s residence (cannot be “one” with Him); to the contrary, he dies a spiritual death, and gets engaged again in the crap (Maya).         1.          So, rather than leaving home, get detached from worldly attractions while living with the family, oh my mind!  Such a person remembers Waheguru all the time, has control over his senses, and is enlightened by the Guru.         Pause.             He controls his mind by the Guru’s Shabad and attains a high spiritual level and noble wisdom while still living with his family.  If we remember Waheguru all the time, He Himself connects us with the Guru, who in turn connects us with Waheguru.      2.           If an individual enjoys millions of women and rules the whole earth, he will not have real happiness without the Guru and will keep taking rebirths.            3.        The individuals who wear Waheguru’s Naam necklace around their neck (recite Waheguru’s Naam all the time) by taking refuge in the Guru, have absolutely no worldly desire left in their mind, and they do not care for supernatural powers even though they beg them for acceptance.        4.
Only those things take place in the world which Waheguru wishes, and nothing else can be done (to thwart them). (Guru) Nanak says that He is a staunch devotee of Waheguru and survives only by reciting His Naam. I beg Waheguru to kindly give me His Naam so that I may have mental contentment in His affection, oh Nanak.  5.2.35.
