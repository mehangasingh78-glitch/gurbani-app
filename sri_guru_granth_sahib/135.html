(ਅੰਗ 134)॥   ਨਾਨਕ ਕੀ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਬੇਨਤੀ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਮਿਲਹੁ ਪਰਾਪਤਿ ਹੋਇ॥ ਵੈਸਾਖੁ ਸੁਹਾਵਾ ਤਾ ਲਗੈ ਜਾ ਸੰਤੁ ਭੇਟੈ ਹਰਿ ਸੋਇ॥ 3॥
Vaisaakh(i) dheerun kiyoun vaandhiyaan jinha praym bichhoh(u). Hur(i) saajun Purakh(u) visaar(i) kaiy luggi Maya dhoh(u).  Poottar kluttar na sung(i) dhunaa, Hur(i) avinaashi Oh(u). Pulch(i), pulch(i), sugli mooyee, jhootthaiy dhundhaiy moh(u). ikkus(u) Hur(i) kay Naam bin ugaaiy luyieh(i) khoh(i).  Duyyou visaar(i) vigoochna Prubh bin(u) uver(u) na koyay. Preetum churni jo lugay, tin kee nirmul soyay. 
(PAGE 134):              Nanak kee Prubh bayntee, Prubh milhou praaput(i) hoyay. Vaisaakh(u) soohaava ta lugaiy ja Sunt(u) bhaytaiy Hur(i) soyay.    3.
Word Meaning: Dheerun = pick up courage; find peace.   KIyoun = how?    Vaandhiyaan = disconnected with Waheguru.   Bichhoh(u) = pain of separation.    Visaar(i) = forget.   Dhoh(u) = deceit.     Kluttar = wife.    Sung(i) = with.  Avinaashi = non-perishable; eternal.    Pulch(i) = entangled.    Jhootthaiy Dhundhay = false (spiritually worthless) tasks.    Uggaiy = next world.   Khoh(i) = snatch.   Duyyou = loving Waheguru.   Vigoochna = suffer without any achievement.   Biin(u) = without.    Uver(u) = other.       Soyay = praise.    Soohaava = pleasant; providing comfort.    Praaput(i) Hoyay = is acquired (get connected with).      3. 
MEANING:  How can those human-brides find peace in the month of Vaisaakh who are disconnected from their Waheguru-Husband, and are suffering from the pain of separation?  By forgetting Waheguru, they are getting attached to the deceitful Maya.  However, they should realize that the sons, the wife, and the wealth are all perishable (will not live with him all the time); the only real friend is Waheguru, which is eternal.  The whole world is dying (spiritually) by being entangled in the false (short-lived) tasks of worldly attachments (Maya).  The world does not realize that when a man has to appear before God after death, only His Naam accompanies him, and he is stripped of everything else before that.  The man suffers greatly from forgetting Waheguru, as no one else can help him.  Those who serve Waheguru earn real praise.  I, Nanak, pray to You that You connect me to You, so that I may enjoy Your union.   Even though the month of Vaisaakh is very pretty in terms of the fresh beauty of nature (it is springtime), the devotees of Waheguru enjoy it only if the Guru connects them with Waheguru.      3.

ਹਰਿ ਜੇਠਿ ਜੁੜੰਦਾ ਲੋੜੀਐ ਜਿਸੁ ਅਗੈ ਸਭਿ ਨਿਵੰਨਿ॥ ਹਰਿ ਸਜਣ ਦਾਵਣਿ ਲਗਿਆ ਕਿਸੈ ਨ ਦੇਈ ਬੰਨਿ॥ ਮਾਣਕ ਮੋਤੀ ਨਾਮੁ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਉਨ ਲਗੈ ਨਾਹੀ ਸੰਨਿ॥ ਰੰਗ ਸਭੇ ਨਾਰਾਇਣੈ ਜੇਤੇ ਮਨਿ ਭਾਵੰਨਿ॥ ਜੋ ਹਰਿ ਲੋੜੇ ਸੋ ਕਰੇ ਸੋਈ ਜੀਅ ਕਰੰਨਿ॥ ਜੋ ਪ੍ਰਭਿ ਕੀਤੇ ਆਪਣੇ ਸੇਈ ਕਹੀਅਹਿ ਧੰਨਿ॥ ਆਪਣ ਲੀਆ ਜੇ ਮਿਲੈ ਵਿਛੁੜਿ ਕਿਉ ਰੋਵੰਨਿ॥ ਸਾਧੂ ਸੰਗੁ ਪਰਾਪਤੇ ਨਾਨਕ ਰੰਗ ਮਾਣੰਨਿ॥ ਹਰਿ ਜੇਠੁ ਰੰਗੀਲਾ ਤਿਸੁ ਧਣੀ ਜਾ ਕੈ ਭਾਗੁ ਮਥੰਨਿ॥ 4॥
Hur(i) Jaytth(i) joorrunda lorriyaiy, jis(u) uggaiy subh(i) nivunn(i).  Hur(i) sujjan daavun(i) luggiyaan kisaiy na dayee bunnh(i). Maanuk moti Naam(u) Prubh(u), oonn luggaiy naahi sunnh(i). Rung subhay Naraayinaiy jaytay mun(i) bhaavunn(i).  Jo Hur(i) lorray so kuray, soyee. Jea krunn(i).  Jo Prubh(i) keetay aapnay sayee kuhiyaiy dhunn(i).  Aapun leeya jay milaiy, vichhurr(i) kiyoun rovunn(i). Sadhu sung(u) praaptay Nanak rung maanunn(i). Hur(i) Jaytth(u) rungeela tis(u) dhuni ja kaiy bhaag(u) muthunn(i).    4.
Word Meaning:  Joorunda = connect with.   Lorriyaiy = wish; need to.    Daavun(i) = seeking support; taking shelter of.    Bunnh(i) = hurdle.   Maanak Moti = pearls.   Sunnh(i) = break-in.  (Until about 60 years ago, it was quite common for thieves to drill a big hole in some outside wall of a house to gain entry to steal. Drilling of the hole was called sunn).  Rung = colour; love; amazing feats.   Naraayinaiy = of Waheguru.  Bhaavunn(i) = mind-captivating.   Lorraiy = wishes.   Dhunn(i) = worthy of praise.   Mannunn(i) = enjoy.  Rungeela = colourful; blissful.    Dhuni = rich; lucky.   Muthunn(i) = on the forehead.          4.
MEANING: In the month of Jaytth(u), one needs to connect with Waheguru before everyone kneels down.  If one takes shelter of Waheguru, who is a friend of everybody, nobody (including the Angel of death) can erect any hurdles in his way to salvation (unite with God).  Pearls and other costly items can be stolen, but Waheguru’s Naam is such a pearl which nobody can steal.  Waheguru manifests Himself in many different ways (does some amazing feats), which captivate our minds when we have His Naam at heart.  Waheguru does whatever He wishes to do, and the living beings also do what He wants them to do.  Those are worthy of our respect whom Waheguru connects with Him. But nobody can achieve anything (including Waheguru’s Naam) on their own (without Waheguru’s Will); if people were able to acquire whatever they wished for on their own, they would not need to cry when they lost it (because they should be able to get it back).  Waheguru’s Naam can be realized only in the company of the Guru, and the devotees enjoy the bliss of union with Him.  The month of Jaytth(u) is blissful for individuals who have good fortune on their foreheads (to connect with Waheguru).   4.

ਆਸਾੜੁ ਤਪੰਦਾ ਤਿਸੁ ਲਗੈ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਹੁ ਨ ਜਿੰਨਾ ਪਾਸਿ॥ ਜਗਜੀਵਨ ਪੁਰਖੁ ਤਿਆਗਿ ਕੈ ਮਾਣਸ ਸੰਦੀ ਆਸ॥ ਦੁਯੈ ਭਾਇ ਵਿਗੁਚੀਐ ਗਲਿ ਪਈਸੁ ਜਮ ਕੀ ਫਾਸ॥ ਜੇਹਾ ਬੀਜੈ ਸੋ ਲੁਣੈ ਮਥੈ ਜੋ ਲਿਖਿਆਸੁ॥ ਰੈਣਿ ਵਿਹਾਣੀ ਪਛੁਤਾਣੀ ਉਠਿ ਚਲੀ ਗਈ ਨਿਰਾਸ॥ ਜਿਨ ਕੌ ਸਾਧੂ ਭੇਟੀਐ ਸੋ ਦਰਗਹ ਹੋਇ ਖਲਾਸੁ॥ ਕਰਿ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਆਪਣੀ ਤੇਰੇ ਦਰਸਨ ਹੋਇ ਪਿਆਸ॥ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਤੁਧੁ ਬਿਨੁ ਦੂਜਾ ਕੋ ਨਹੀ ਨਾਨਕ ਕੀ ਅਰਦਾਸਿ॥ ਆਸਾੜੁ ਸੁਹੰਦਾ ਤਿਸੁ ਲਗੈ ਜਿਸੁ ਮਨਿ ਹਰਿ ਚਰਣ ਨਿਵਾਸ॥5॥
Aasaarr(u) tupunda tis(u) luggaiy Hur(i) Naah(u) na jinna paas(i).  Jugjeevun Purakh(u) tiyaag(u) kaiy, maanus sundi aas. Dooyaiy bhaayay vigoochiyaiy gul(i) puyyee s(u) jum kee phaas. Jayha beejaiy so loonaiy muthaiy jo likhiyas(u). Raiyn(i) vihaani puchhutaani, ootth(i) chulli guyee niraas. Jin kuou Sadhu bhaytiyaiy so Durgeh hoyay khulaas(u). Kur kirpa prubh aapni, Tayray dershun hoyay piyaas. Prubh Toodh(u) bin(u) dooja ko nuhee, Nanak kee ardaas(i).  Aasaarr(u) soohunda tis(u) lugaiy jis(u) mun(i) churn nivaas.    5.
Word Meaning:  Tupunda = sizzling hot. (Aassarr(u) is the hottest month in India).   
Naah(u) = Lord; Master. Jugjeevun = Life of the world (Waheguru).   Purakh(u) = Living Waheguru.   Maanus = man.    Sundi = of.   Dooyaiy Bhaayay = in the love for the other (Maya).   Vigoochiyaiy = suffer without any achievement; discomfit.   Phaas = noose.   Loonaiy = cuts; reaps.    Raiyn(i) = night (life).   Vihaani = is spent.   Ootth(i) Chulli = leaves the world.   Niraas = disappointed.    Sadhu = Guru.   Bhaytiyaiy = met; got connected.   Khulass = (declared) free of any sins.  Soohunda = pleasant.           5.
MEANING:   The intense heat of the month of Asaarr(u) causes suffering to those who do not have Waheguru’s Naam at heart, and, ignoring Waheguru, they seek the support of someone else.   They suffer in vain because of their love for Maya, and the noose of the Angel of Death awaits them (they are caught in the rebirth cycle).  As per their pre-written fortune, they reap the (bad) results of what they had sown.   When their life is over, they leave the world disappointed and in repentance.  On the other hand, those who connect with the Guru are declared free from any sins in the Court of Waheguru (are salvaged).  I pray to You that, please, be kind to me so that I (Nanak) too, may have longing for Your sight (get connected with You), because there is none other like You, oh Waheguru!    Even though the month of Asaarr(u) is sizzling hot, those who have embedded Waheguru’s Naam in their heart find it very comforting, oh Nanak.      5.

ਸਾਵਣਿ ਸਰਸੀ ਕਾਮਣੀ ਚਰਨ ਕਮਲ ਸਿਉ ਪਿਆਰੁ॥ ਮਨੁ ਤਨੁ ਰਤਾ ਸਚ ਰੰਗਿ ਇਕੋ ਨਾਮੁ ਅਧਾਰੁ॥ ਬਿਖਿਆ ਰੰਗ ਕੂੜਾਵਿਆ ਦਿਸਨਿ ਸਭੇ ਛਾਰੁ॥ ਹਰਿ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਬੂੰਦ ਸੁਹਾਵਣੀ ਮਿਲਿ ਸਾਧੂ ਪੀਵਣਹਾਰੁ॥ ਵਣੁ ਤਿਣੁ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਸੰਗਿ ਮਉਲਿਆ ਸੰਮ੍ਰਥ ਪੁਰਖ ਅਪਾਰੁ॥ ਹਰਿ ਮਿਲਣੈ ਨੋ ਮਨੁ ਲੋਚਦਾ ਕਰਮਿ ਮਿਲਾਵਣਹਾਰੁ॥ ਜਿਨੀ ਸਖੀਏ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਹੰਉ ਤਿਨ ਕੈ ਸਦ ਬਲਿਹਾਰ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਹਰਿ ਜੀ ਮਇਆ ਕਰਿ ਸਬਦਿ ਸਵਾਰਣਹਾਰੁ॥ ਸਾਵਣੁ ਤਿਨਾ ਸੁਹਾਗਣੀ ਜਿਨ ਰਾਮ ਨਾਮੁ ਉਰਿ ਹਾਰੁ॥6॥
Saavun(i), sersi kaamni, Churn Kumul siyoun piyaar(u). Mun(u) tun(u) rutta Sach rung(i), ikko Naam(u) adhaar(u).  Bikhiya rung koorraviya, disun(i) subhay chhaar(u). Hur(i) Amrit boond soohaavni, mil(i) Sadhu peevunhaar(u). Vun(u) trin(u) Prubh sung(i) mauliya, sumruth Purukh Apaar(u).  Hur(i) milnaiy nu mun(u) loachda, kerm(i) milaavunhaar(u). Jinhi sukhiyayn Prubh(u) paaya, houn tin kaiy sud bulihaar. Nanak Hur(i) ji muyya kur(i) Shabad(i) svaarunhaar(u).  Saavun(u) tinha soohaagni jin Ram Naam(u) oor(i) haar(u).      6.
Word Meaning:   Sersi = blissful; blooming of heart.    Kaamni = woman (human bride).    Siyoun = with.      Rutta = imbued.      Sach Rung(i) = in the love of God.   Adhaar = support.   Bikhiya = poisonous (Maya).    Koorraviya = false (short-lived).    Chhar(u) = ash.   Boond = drop.   Soohaavni = pretty; comforting; soothing.   Trin(u) = vegetation.   Mauliya = bloomed; zestful.    Sumruth = omnipotent; capable of doing everything.    Loachda = longs for.    Kerm(i) = kindness; benevolence.    Sukhiyaiyn = (female) friends.    Muyya Kur(i) = be kind; be benevolent.    Svaarunhaar = complete successfully.   Sohaagni = married woman, (loved by her husband).    Oor = heart.       6.
MEANING: Like the rainy months of Saavun(u) and Bhaadon, which bring respite from the sizzling heat after the hot and dry months of Jaytth and Asaarr, and infuse new life in nature, and among the living beings, the heart of the woman (human being), who has love for Waheguru with lotus-like soft Feet, begins to bloom in bliss.  Her body and mind are imbued with the love of God, and Naam alone is her support in life.  She realizes that the enjoyments of Maya are temporary and no more worthy than ash.  The really worthy thing is the drop of Naam-Amrit, which can be tasted in the company of the Guru.  The limitless Waheguru is omnipotent, and all the forests and vegetation bloom because of Him.  My mind also longs to connect with Waheguru, but only His benevolence can do so.  I wish to offer my life any time to those friends who have realized Him.  I (Nanak) pray to Waheguru that He be kind, and adorn my life with Guru’s Shabad, so that I may connect with Him to make my life successful.  Women wear necklaces of gold or other precious metals around their neck to look pretty, but the month of Saavun is blissful to those who have the necklace of Waheguru’s Naam in their heart.      6.

ਭਾਦੁਇ ਭਰਮਿ ਭੁਲਾਣੀਆ ਦੁਜੈ ਲਗਾ ਹੇਤੁ॥ ਲਖ ਸੀਗਾਰ ਬਣਾਇਆ ਕਾਰਜਿ ਨਾਹੀ ਕੇਤੁ॥ ਜਿਤੁ ਦਿਨਿ ਦੇਹ ਬਿਨਸਸੀ ਤਿਤੁ ਵੇਲੈ ਕਹਸਨਿ ਪ੍ਰੇਤ॥ ਪਕੜਿ ਚਲਾਇਨਿ ਦੂਤ ਜਮ ਕਿਸੈ ਨ ਦੇਨੀ ਭੇਤੁ॥ ਛਡਿ ਖੜੋਤੇ ਖਿਨੈ ਮਾਹਿ ਜਿਨ ਸਿਉ ਲਗਾ ਹੇਤੁ॥ ਹਥ ਮਰੋੜੈ ਤਨੁ ਕਪੇ ਸਿਆਹਹੁ ਹੋਆ ਸੇਤੁ॥ ਜੇਹਾ ਬੀਜੈ ਸੋ ਲੁਣੈ ਕਰਮਾ ਸੰਦੜਾ ਖੇਤੁ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਸਰਣਾਗਤੀ ਚਰਣ ਬੋਹਿਥ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਦੇਤੁ॥ ਸੇ ਭਾਦੁਇ ਨਰਕਿ ਨ ਪਾਈਅਹਿ ਗੁਰੁ ਰਖਣ ਵਾਲਾ ਹੇਤੁ॥ 7॥
Bhaadonyay bherm(i) bhoolaaniya, doojaiy lugga hayt(u). Lukh seegaar bunaaya, kaaruj(i) naahi kayt(u).  Jit(u) din(i) dayh binus-si, tit(u) vaylaiy kehsun(i) prayt(u).  Pukurr(i) chulaayin doot Jum, kisaiy na dayni bhayt(u).  Chhudd(i) khurrotay khinaiy maah(i) jin siyoun lugga hayt(u). Huth murorraiy tun kupay, siahou hoya sayt(u).  Jayha beejaiy so loonaiy, kerma sundrra khayt(u).  Nanak Prubh shernaagti Churn bohith Prubh dayt(u). Say Bhaadonyay nerk(i) na paayiyaiy Gur(u) rukhunvaala hayt(u).   7.
Word Meaning:   Bherm(i) Bhoolaaniya = misled by misbeliefs.    Doojaiy = Maya.    Hayt(u) = attachment; love.   Seegaar = beautification.   Kaaruj(i) = useful; helpful.   Kayt(u) = nowhere.    Dayh = body.   Binus-si = will be destroyed.    Tit(u) vaylay = at that time.    Prayt = belonging to past; dead.    Doot Jum = agents of the Angel of death.   Bhayt(u) = secret.   Khinaiy Maah(i) = in a moment.    Murorraiy = twist.    Kupay = trembles.   Siahou = from black.    Sayt(u) = white.     Loonaiy = cuts; reaps.   Sundrra = of.    Khayt(u) = field.     Shernaagti = seeking shelter.     Bohith = ship; boat.   Hayt(u) = love.        7.
MEANING:  Those who follow their minds and get attached to Maya in the month of Bhaadon (or any other month for that matter) are misled by their misbeliefs (in the realm of spirituality). No matter how hard they try to look attractive to Waheguru by adorning themselves (by wearing religious garb and performing rituals), it is of no use. (Rather than getting any recognition in Waheguru’s Court after death), Their relatives call them “dead” when their soul leaves their body.  The agents of the Angel of Death take control, and take them away, and disclose no secret about their destination.  All the loved ones leave them alone in a moment (and move away).  With the body trembling, the dying individual twists his hands, and his colour changes from dark to white (as if there is no blood there). Human life is like a field; one reaps only what one sows in the form of deeds.  I (Nanak), am in the shelter of Waheguru whose “Feet” (Naam) serve as the ship to carry us across the sea of Maya.  People who come to Waheguru’s shelter (connect with Him) never see hell (in the month of Bhaadon or any other time) because the loving Guru becomes their guard.      7.

ਅਸੁਨਿ ਪ੍ਰੇਮ ਉਮਾਹੜਾ ਕਿਉ ਮਿਲੀਐ ਹਰਿ ਜਾਇ॥
