Majh Mehla 5:  Aithaiy Tu haiy, aagaiy Aapay.  Jea juntar subh(i) Tayray thaapay.  Toodh(u) bin(u) uver(u) na koyee Kurtay, mein dhur oat Toomhaari jeeo. 1.  Rusna jup(i) jup(i) jeevaiy Soami.  Parbraham Prubh untarjaami.  Jin(i) sayviya tin hee sookh(u) paaya, so junum na jooaiy haari jeeo.  2.  Naam(u) uvkhudh(u) jin(i) jun Tayraiy paaya. 
(PAGE 108):         Junum junum ka roag guvaaya.  Hur(i) keertun(u) gaavhou din(u) raati, suful eha haiy kaari jeeo.  3.  Drisht(i) dhaar(i) Apuna daas suvaariya.  Ghut ghut untar(i) Parbraham(u) numskaariya.  Ikus(u) vin(u) hoar(u) dooja naahi, baba eh mut(i) saari jeeo.  4.39.46.
Word Meaning:  Aithaiy = here (in this world).  Aagaiy = ahead (in the next world after death).  Jea Juntar = living beings.  Thaapay = created.   Uver(u) = other.   Kurtray = oh Creator!    Dhur = Oat = support.       1.     Rusna = tongue.    Parbrahm = Prubh = God.    Untarjaami = omniscient.  Jooaiy = in gamble (earn wealth at the cost of Waheguru’s Naam).      Haari = lose.       2.     Uvkhudh(u) = cure; medication.   Jun = devotee.   Kaari = Kaaruj = deed.     3.
Drisht(i) = (kind) sight.   Dhaar(i) = cast.    Svaariya = made perfect (flawless).    Ghut = heart.    Vin(u) = without.    Saari = supreme.      4.
MEANING:   It is Your Huqam that is working in this world, as well as in the next, oh Waheguru!   All the living beings have been created by You.  There is none other (like You); I survive with Your support and in Your shelter, oh, Waheguru.        1.  
I survive spiritually by reciting Your Naam with my tongue; (and You know that) as You are omniscient, oh Waheguru.  Whoever serves You (obeys Your Huqam) lives in comfort (has mental peace), and he does not lose the precious human life in a gamble (risking Waheguru’s displeasure while evil deeds).           2.         The devotee, who implants Your Naam in the heart as the panacea for all diseases, gets the suffering of rebirth eliminated.  Everybody should sing praise of Waheguru day and night because this is the only deed to perform for success (to find salvation).       3. 
Waheguru makes His devotee flawless with His kind sight. Such a devotee salutes (obeys) Waheguru, which is present in every heart.  This (to obey Waheguru) is the supreme wisdom to follow because there is none other than Him (who deserves praise), oh Nanak.   4.39.46.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਮਨੁ ਤਨੁ ਰਤਾ ਰਾਮ ਪਿਆਰੇ॥ ਸਰਬਸੁ ਦੀਜੈ ਅਪਨਾ ਵਾਰੇ॥ ਆਠ ਪਹਰ ਗੋਵਿੰਦ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਈਐ ਬਿਸਰੁ ਨ ਕੋਈ ਸਾਸਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਸੋਈ ਸਾਜਨ ਮੀਤੁ ਪਿਆਰਾ॥ ਰਾਮ ਨਾਮੁ ਸਾਧ ਸੰਗਿ ਬੀਚਾਰਾ॥ ਸਾਧੂ ਸੰਗਿ ਤਰੀਜੈ ਸਾਗਰੁ ਕਟੀਐ ਜਮ ਕੀ ਫਾਸਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਚਾਰਿ ਪਦਾਰਥ ਹਰਿ ਕੀ ਸੇਵਾ॥ ਪਾਰਜਾਤੁ ਜਪਿ ਅਲਖ ਅਭੇਵਾ॥ ਕਾਮ ਕ੍ਰੋਧੁ ਕਿਲਬਿਖ ਗੁਰਿ ਕਾਟੇ ਪੂਰਨ ਹੋਈ ਆਸਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 3॥ ਪੂਰਨ ਭਾਗ ਭਏ ਜਿਸੁ ਪ੍ਰਾਣੀ॥ ਸਾਧ ਸੰਗਿ ਮਿਲੇ ਸਾਰਿੰਗਪਾਣੀ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮੁ ਵਸਿਆ ਜਿਸੁ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਪਰਵਾਣ ਗਿਰਸਤ ਉਦਾਸਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 4.40.47.
MajhMehla 5:  Mun(u) tun(u) rutta Ram piyaray.  Serbus(u) deejaiy apuna vaaray.  Aatth pehr Govind goon gaayiyaiy, bisur(u) na koyee saasa jeeo. 1.  Soyee saajun meet(u) piyara.  Ram Naam(u) Sadh sung(i) beechaara.  Sadhu sung(i) treejaiy saagur(u), kuttiyaiy jum kee phaasa jeeo.  2.  Chaar(i) pdaruth Hur(i) kee sayva.  Paarjaat(u) jup(i) Alukh Abhayva.  Kaam krodh(u) kilbikh Gur(i) kaatay, poorun hoyee aasa jeeo. 3.  Poorun bhaag bhuyay jis(u) praani. Sadh sung(i) milay Saarigpaani.  Nanak Naam(u) vusiya jis(u) untar(i), pervaan girust  oodaasa jeeo.  4.40.47.
Word Meaning:  Rutta = imbued.   Serbus(u) = everything.    Vaaray = sacrifice.   Aatth Pehr = twenty-four hours.    Bisur(u) = forget.   Saasa = breath; moment.       1.
Saajun = Meet(u) = friend.   Sung(i) = with.   Beechaara = ponder on.    Treejaiy = swim across.   Saagur = (world) ocean.        Phaasa = noose.      2.
Chaar(i) = four.   Pdaruth = invaluable articles: (1) Dherm (high morals), (2) Arth = wealth, (3) Kaam = wishes, success. (4) Moakh = salvation (according to Hindu Granths).
Paarjaat = a mythical tree in paradise which fulfills all one’s wishes.  Alukh = inconceivable.  Abhayva = enigmatic; whose secrets cannot be known.   Kilbikh = sins.    3.
Poorun Bhaag = maturation of luck.   Bhuyay = became.   Saaringpaani = Waheguru (a name for Vishnu for holding a bow in his hand).   Girust = in family.   Oodaasa = to renounce the world and live away from the family in some forest, etc.       4.
MEANING:  An individual, whose body and mind are imbued with dear Waheguru’s love, is gladly willing to sacrifice his life and all his belongings for Him.  We should also sing Waheguru’s virtues day and night (keep Him in mind), and should not forget Him even for a moment.         1.           Only that individual is my friend, my pal, who ponders on the virtues of Waheguru in the company (shelter) of the Guru. It is only in the company of the Guru that one can swim across the sea of Maya (find salvation) and cut the noose of the angel of death.     2.            Serving Waheguru (obeying His Huqam and reciting His Naam) is like having acquired all four invaluable articles (see above).  Worshipping the inconceivable and enigmatic Waheguru is like having the mythical Paarjaat tree in your possession. The Guru rids the devotee of his lust, rage, and all sins, and all his wishes are granted.      3. 
An individual whose luck matures is connected with Waheguru in the company of the Guru.  An individual who has stored Waheguru’s Naam in his heart is approved by Waheguru, regardless of whether he is a family man or has renounced the world to live a lonely life, oh Nanak.    4. 40.47
ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 5: ਸਿਮਰਤ ਨਾਮੁ ਰਿਦੈ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਾਇਆ॥ ਕਰਿ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਭਗਤੀ ਪ੍ਰਗਟਾਇਆ॥ ਸੰਤ ਸੰਗਿ ਮਿਲਿ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਜਪਿਆ ਬਿਨਸੇ ਆਲਸ ਰੋਗਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਜਾ ਕੈ ਗ੍ਰਿਹਿ ਨਵ ਨਿਧਿ ਹਰਿ ਭਾਈ॥ ਤਿਸੁ ਮਿਲਿਆ ਜਿਸੁ ਪੁਰਬ ਕਮਾਈ॥ ਗਿਆਨ ਧਿਆਨ ਪੂਰਨ ਪਰਮੇਸੁਰ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਸਭਨਾ ਗਲਾ ਜੋਗਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਖਿਨ ਮਹਿ ਥਾਪਿ ਉਥਾਪਨਹਾਰਾ॥ ਆਪਿ ਇਕੰਤੀ ਆਪਿ ਪਸਾਰਾ॥ ਲੇਪੁ ਨਹੀ ਜਗਜੀਵਨ ਦਾਤੇ ਦਰਸਨ ਡਿਠੇ ਲਹਨਿ ਵਿਜੋਗਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 3॥ ਅੰਚਲਿ ਲਾਇ ਸਭ ਸਿਸਟਿ ਤਰਾਈ॥ ਆਪਣਾ ਨਾਉ ਆਪਿ ਜਪਾਈ॥ ਗੁਰ ਬੋਹਿਥੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਤੇ ਨਾਨਕ ਧੁਰਿ ਸੰਜੋਗਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥41॥48॥

Majh Mehla 5:  Simrut Naam(u) ridaiy sookh(u) paaya. Kur(i) kirpa bhugtee pergtaaya. Sunt sung(i) mil(i) Hur(i) Hur(i) jupiya, binsay aalus roaga jeeo. 1.  Ja kaiy greh(i) nuv nidh(i) Hur(i) bhaayee. Tis(u) miliya jis(u) poorb kmaayee.  Giyan dhiyaan poorun Permayshur, Prubh subhna gullaan joga jeeo.  2.  Khin meh(i) thaap(i) oothaapunhaara. Aap(i) ikunti Aap(i) pusaara. Layp(u) nuhee Jugjeevun Daatay, dershun ditthay lehn(i) vijoga jeeo.  3.  Unchul(i) laayay subh sisht(i) traayee. Apuna Naoun Aap(i) jupaayee.  Gur bohith(u) paaya kirpa tay, Nanak dhoor(i) sunjoga jeeo.  4.41.48.
Word Meaning:   Pergtaaya = revealed; made visible.    Binsay = dispelled.    Aalus = laziness; indolence.   Roaga = ailments; bad habits.     1.      Greh(i) = in the house (heart).    Nav Nidh(i) = the nine mythical treasures of Kuber.  Poorb = in a previous life.   Giyaan = knowledge.   Dhiyaan = full concentration; meditation.    Joga = capable.      2.   Khin = moment.   Thaap(i) = after creating.  Oothaapunhaara = destroyer.   Ikunti = all by Himself.   Psaara = expansion.   Layp(u) = effect of Maya.    Jugjeevun = (source of) life of the world.   Lehn(i) = remove; come off.      Vijoga = (pain of) separation.    3.     Unchul(i) Laayay = taking under wings (in Indian Culture a custom to bond a couple together by a piece of cloth (scarf) to indicate their unwavering love.   Sisht = world.    Traayee = salvaged.  Bohith = ship.   Dhoor(i) = from the very beginning.   Sunjoga = predestined union.     4.
MEANING:  When one remembers Waheguru’s Naam, he feels comfort at heart (feels blissful).  By Waheguru’s grace, such devotees, who worship Him, become well known in the world.  They sing Waheguru’s praise in the company of the Guru, as a result of which their bad habit of laziness is gone.           1.           A person who has made his heart the residence for Waheguru (has stored Him in his memory), feels as if he has acquired all the nine mythical treasures. But Waheguru takes residence only in that heart which had worked hard to connect with Him in his previous life.  Waheguru Himself is the perfect knowledge and the concentration for his devotees (a person who has been connected with Waheguru acquires all the Knowledge and concentration without any special effort), and He is capable of doing anything (is omnipotent).            2.            Waheguru is capable of creating anything and destroying it in an instant.  He is all by Himself (aloof from the universe), and yet pervading the whole universe.   The source of life of the world, Waheguru, is totally unaffected by Maya, and all the pain of separation disappears when one “sees” Him.          3.
He can salvage the whole world by taking it under His wings (with His support).  He Himself motivates and enables people to recite His Naam.   Guru is the ship (to ferry people across the sea of Maya), but only those who are connected with Him with Waheguru’s benevolence get to be predestined for union with Him, oh Nanak.   4.41.48.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਸੋਈ ਕਰਣਾ ਜਿ ਆਪਿ ਕਰਾਏ॥ ਜਿਥੈ ਰਖੈ ਸਾ ਭਲੀ ਜਾਏ॥ ਸੋ ਸਿਆਣਾ ਸੋ ਪਤਿਵੰਤਾ ਹੁਕਮੁ ਲਗੈ ਜਿਸੁ ਮੀਠਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਸਭ ਪਰੋਈ ਇਕਤੁ ਧਾਗੈ॥ ਜਿਸੁ ਲਾਇ ਲਏ ਸੋ ਚਰਣੀ ਲਾਗੈ॥ ਊਧ ਕਵਲੁ ਜਿਸੁ ਹੋਇ ਪ੍ਰਗਾਸਾ ਤਿਨਿ ਸਰਬ ਨਿਰੰਜਨੁ ਡੀਠਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਤੇਰੀ ਮਹਿਮਾ ਤੂਹੈ ਜਾਣਹਿ॥ ਅਪਣਾ ਆਪੁ ਤੂ ਆਪਿ ਪਛਾਣਹਿ॥ ਹਉ ਬਲਿਹਾਰੀ ਸੰਤਨ ਤੇਰੇ ਜਿਨਿ ਕਾਮੁ ਕ੍ਰੋਧੁ ਲੋਭੁ ਪੀਠਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 3॥ ਤੂ ਨਿਰਵੈਰੁ ਸੰਤ ਤੇਰੇ ਨਿਰਮਲ॥ ਜਿਨ ਦੇਖੇ ਸਭ ਉਤਰਹਿ ਕਲਮਲ॥ ਨਾਨਕੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਧਿਆਇ ਧਿਆਇ ਜੀਵੈ ਬਿਨਸਿਆ ਭ੍ਰਮੁ ਭਉ ਧੀਠਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥42॥49॥
Majh Mehla 5:  Soyee Kur(i)na j(i) Aap(i)  Kuraayay. Jithaiy rukhaiy, sa bhulee jaayay. So siyaana, so putivunta, Huqam(u) lugaiy jis(u) meattha jeo. 1. Subh proyee ikutt(u) dhaagaiy. Jis(u) laayay luyyay so churnee laagaiy.  Oondh kuvul(u) jis(u) hoyay pergaasa, tin(i) serb Nirunjun deettha jeeo.  2.  Tayri mehma Tuhain jaaneh(i). Apuna aap(u) Tu Aap(i) puchhaaneh(i). Huoun bulihaari Suntan Tayray, jin(i) kaam(u) krodh(u) loabh(u) peettha jeeo.  3. Tu nirvaiyr, Sunt Tayray nirmul. Jin daykhay subh ootreh(i) kulmul. Nanak(u) Naam(u) dhiyaayay dhiyaayay jeevaiy, binsiya bherm(u) bhuou dheettha jeeo.  4.42.49.
Word Meaning:  J(i) = Jo = whatever.    Jithaiy = wherever.   Bhulee = comfortable.   Jaayay = place (situation).      Putivunta = respectable.       1.     Proyee = attached to; stitched to.   Ikutt(u) = one and the same.   Dhaagaiy = thread.   Churnee Laagaiy = touches feet (makes connection).  Oondh = upside down (away from Maya and towards God).  Kuvul = lotus (heart).   Oondh Kuvul = the heart which is turned away from Maya and is attracted to Waheguru.  Pergaasa = enlightened.   Serb = everywhere.   Nirunjun = Unaffected by Maya (God).       Deettha = sees.      2.     Mehma = greatness; magnanimity.   Tuhaiyn = only You.     Puchhaaneh(i) = recognize.    Bulihaari = sacrifice.   Peettha = grinded; crushed.        3.
Nirvaiyr = bear no enmity (because You have no rival) and are perfectly just.  Nirmul = clean (at heart).   Kulmul = sins.    Binsiya = destroyed.     Dheettha = stubborn.   4.
MEANING: To obey Waheguru’s Huqam, and do whatever He wants us to do, and to accept gladly in whatever situation He puts us in, is in the best interest of human beings.   Only that individual is wise and respectable (in the eyes of Waheguru) who accepts Waheguru’s Huqam as being sweet.           1.            (Just like the beads in a necklace have a common thread), All the living beings (beads) are connected to the same thread, Waheguru. However, only that individual makes a connection with Waheguru, who is prompted to do so by Waheguru Himself.  A person who has turned his mind away from Maya and towards Waheguru is enlightened by the Divine Light, and he sees (finds) Waheguru everywhere.        2.
Only You know Your own magnanimity, and recognize Yourself, oh Waheguru!   I (do not know anything about Your greatness), but will gladly offer my life to Your devotees who have crushed (overcome) the evils like lust, rage, and greed.         3.           Because You bear no animosity against anyone (You deliver perfect justice), Your devotees too, have clean hearts (bear no enmity for anyone); whosoever “sees” them (joins their company), has his sins washed away.  I survive (spiritually) only by reciting Waheguru’s Naam as a result of which, all my stubborn misbeliefs and fears have been dispelled, oh Nanak!     4.42.49.
