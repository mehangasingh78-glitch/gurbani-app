(ਅੰਗ 33)॥   ਸਤਗੁਰਿ ਮਿਲਿਐ ਸਦ ਭੈ ਰਚੈ ਆਪਿ ਵਸੈ ਮਨਿ ਆਇ॥1॥ ਭਾਈ ਰੇ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਬੂਝੈ ਕੋਇ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਬੂਝੇ ਕਰਮ ਕਮਾਵਣੇ ਜਨਮੁ ਪਦਾਰਥੁ ਖੋਇ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਜਿਨੀ ਚਾਖਿਆ ਤਿਨੀ ਸਾਦੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਬਿਨੁ ਚਾਖੇ ਭਰਮਿ ਭੁਲਾਇ॥ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਸਾਚਾ ਨਾਮੁ ਹੈ ਕਹਣਾ ਕਛੂ ਨ ਜਾਇ॥ ਪੀਵਤ ਹੂ ਪਰਵਾਣੁ ਭਇਆ ਪੂਰੈ ਸਬਦਿ ਸਮਾਇ॥ 2॥ ਆਪੇ ਦੇਇ ਤ ਪਾਈਐ ਹੋਰੁ ਕਰਣਾ ਕਿਛੂ ਨ ਜਾਇ॥ ਦੇਵਣ ਵਾਲੈ ਕੈ ਹਥਿ ਦਾਤਿ ਹੈ ਗੁਰੂਦੁਆਰੈ ਪਾਇ॥ ਜੇਹਾ ਕੀਤੋਨੁ ਤੇਹਾ ਹੋਆ ਜੇਹੇ ਕਰਮ ਕਮਾਇ॥ 3॥ ਜਤੁ ਸਤੁ ਸੰਜਮੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਹੈ ਵਿਣੁ ਨਾਵੈ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਨ ਹੋਇ॥ ਪੂਰੈ ਭਾਗਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਮਨਿ ਵਸੈ ਸਬਦਿ ਮਿਲਾਵਾ ਹੋਇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਸਹਜੇ ਹੀ ਰੰਗਿ ਵਰਤਦਾ ਹਰਿ ਗੁਣ ਪਾਵੈ ਸੋਇ॥ 4॥17॥50॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 3:  Govind(u) gooni-nidhaan(u) haiy, unt(u) na paaya jaayay. Kuthni budni na paayiyaiy, haumein vichoun jaayay.
  (PAGE 33):     Satgur(i) miliyaiy sud bhaiy ruchaiy, aap(i) vusaiy mun(i) aaayay. 1. Bhaayee ray, Gurmukh(i) boojhaiy koyay. Bin boojhay kerm kmaavnay junum(u) pudaaruth(u) khoyay. 1. Ruhaou.   Jinhi chaakhiya tinhi saad(u) paaya, bin chaakhay bherm(i) bhoolayay. Amrit Sacha Naam haiy, kehna kuchhoo na jaayay.  Peevut hee purvaan(u) bhuyya, pooray Shabad(i) smaayay. 2. Aapay dayay ta paayiyaiy, hoar(u) kurna kichhoo na jaayay. Dayvunvaalaiy kaiy huth(i) daat(i) haiy, Gurudwaraiy paayay. Jayha keeton(u) tayha hoya, jayhay kerm kmaayay. 3. Jut(u) sut(u) sunjum(u) Naam(u) haiy, vin(u) Naavaiy nirmul(u) na hoyay. Pooraiy bhaag(i) Naam(u) mun(i) vusaiy Shabad(i) milaava hoyay. Nanak sehjay hee rung(i) vertda, Hur(i) goon paavaiy soyay.  4.17.50.
Word Meaning: Govind(u)= a name for God.  Gooni Nidhaan(u) = treasure of noble qualities.  Unt(u) = limits.   Kuthni = by saying (describing).  Budni = with mouth.     Haumein = selfishness and ego.   Bhaiy = (respectful) fear.  Ruchaiyy = engaged.        1.      Boojhaiyy = recognizes. Koyay = rare. Junum(u) Pudaruth = objective of human life.           Pause.
Chaakhiya = tasted.   Saad(u) = taste; enjoy.   Bherm(i) = in superstitions.  Bhoolayay = lost.   Peevut Hoo = right after drinking.   Purvaan(u) = approved.   Smaayay = becomes one with.     2.            Daat(i) = gift.    Gurudwaraiy = through the Guru.        3.          Jut(u) = celibacy.   Sut(u) = high morals; service to mankind.  Sunjum(u) = control over desires.   Vin(u) = without.   Nirmul(u) = pure.   Rung(i) = in love.       4. 
MEANING:  Waheguru is the treasure of all noble qualities, but nobody can find His limits.  He cannot be realized simply by describing, or by words of praise by mouth (without real love for Him), unless one gets rid of his haumein.  However, when one connects with the Satguru and acts upon His advice gladly all the time, (his haumein is eradicated) and Waheguru Himself makes the devotee’s heart His residence.         1.          But, only some rare devotee of the Guru realizes this fact, oh my friend.  A person who just performs the accepted rituals without any spiritual knowledge wastes his precious human life.         Pause.
Waheguru’s True (everlasting) Naam is the Amrit (immortalizing holy water); no words can describe its taste. Only those who have tasted it enjoy it; without tasting it, one is lost in doubts and superstitions. Those who taste it are approved (by Waheguru) as soon as they finish drinking it, and they become “one” with Him.          2.         We can acquire Waheguru’s Naam only if He bestows it on us; otherwise, no one acquires it on their own. This special gift is in the hands of the Provider – Waheguru only - and can be acquired only through the Guru. But one only reaps the rewards of what he sows (what type of deeds he performs).           3.
Celibacy, high moral character, and control over desires are all subsumed (included) in Waheguru’s Naam, without which the mind never becomes pure.  But Naam takes up residence in one’s heart only if one has good fortune, as a result of which one becomes connected with Waheguru.  Only that individual acquires Waheguru’s noble qualities who leads his life with a pure heart, and with love for Him, oh Nanak.     4.17.50
ਸਿਰੀਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ ਕਾਇਆ ਸਾਧੈ ਉਰਧ ਤਪੁ ਕਰੈ ਵਿਚਹੁ ਹਉਮੈ ਨ ਜਾਇ॥ ਅਧਿਆਤਮ ਕਰਮ ਜੇ ਕਰੇ ਨਾਮੁ ਨ ਕਬ ਹੀ ਪਾਇ॥ ਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਸਬਦਿ ਜੀਵਤੁ ਮਰੈ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਵਸੈ ਮਨਿ ਆਇ॥ 1॥ ਸੁਣਿ ਮਨ ਮੇਰੇ ਭਜੁ ਸਤਗੁਰ ਸਰਣਾ॥ ਗੁਰ ਪਰਸਾਦੀ ਛੁਟੀਐ ਬਿਖੁ ਭਵਜਲੁ ਸਬਦਿ ਗੁਰ ਤਰਣਾ॥1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਤ੍ਰੈ ਗੁਣ ਸਭਾ ਧਾਤੁ ਹੈ ਦੂਜਾ ਭਾਉ ਵਿਕਾਰੁ॥ ਪੰਡਿਤੁ ਪੜੈ ਬੰਧਨ ਮੋਹ ਬਾਧਾ ਨਹ ਬੂਝੈ ਬਿਖਿਆ ਪਿਆਰਿ॥ ਸਤਗੁਰਿ ਮਿਲਿਐ ਤ੍ਰਿਕੁਟੀ ਛੂਟੈ ਚਉਥੈ ਪਦਿ ਮੁਕਤਿ ਦੁਆਰੁ॥ 2॥ ਗੁਰ ਤੇ ਮਾਰਗੁ ਪਾਈਐ ਚੂਕੈ ਮੋਹੁ ਗੁਬਾਰੁ॥ ਸਬਦਿ ਮਰੈ ਤਾ ਉਧਰੈ ਪਾਏ ਮੋਖ ਦੁਆਰੁ॥ ਗੁਰ ਪਰਸਾਦੀ ਮਿਲਿ ਰਹੈ ਸਚੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਕਰਤਾਰੁ॥ 3॥ ਇਹੁ ਮਨੂਆ ਅਤਿ ਸਬਲ ਹੈ ਛਡੇ ਨ ਕਿਤੈ ਉਪਾਇ॥ ਦੂਜੈ ਭਾਇ ਦੁਖੁ ਲਾਇਦਾ ਬਹੁਤੀ ਦੇਇ ਸਜਾਇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮਿ ਲਗੇ ਸੇ ਉਬਰੇ ਹਉਮੈ ਸਬਦਿ ਗਵਾਇ॥ 4॥18॥51॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 3:  Kaayan saadhaiy oordh tup(u) kuraiy, vichoun haumein na jaayay. Adhiyatum kerm jay kuray, Naam(u) na kub hee paayay.  Gur kaiy Shabad(i) jeevut(u) muraiy, Hur(i) Naam(u) vusaiy mun(i) aayay. 1.  Soon(i) mun mayray, bhuj(u) Satgur sherna. Gur pursaadi chhootiyaiy, bikh(u) bhuvjul(u) Shabad(i) Gur turna. 1. Ruhaou. Traiy goon subha dhaat(u) haiy, dooja bhaao vikaar(u). Pundit(u) purraiy bundhun moh baadha, neh boojhaiy bikhiya piyaar(i). Satgur(i) miliyaiy tirkooti chhootaiy, chauthaiy pud(i) mookt(i) dooaar(u). 2. Gur tay maarug(u) paayiyaiy, chookaiy moh(u) goobaar(u) . Shabad(i) muraiy ta oodhraiy, paayay mokh dooaar(u). Gur pursaadi mil(i) ruhaiy Sach(u) Naam(u) Kartar. 3. Eho munooa ut(i) subul haiy, chhudday na kitaiy oopaayay. Doojaiy bhaayay dookh(u) laayaynda, buhouti dayay sujaayay. Nanak Naam(i) lugay say oobray, haumein Shabad(i) guvaayay. 4.18.51.
Word Meaning:  Kaayan = body.   Saadhaiy = cleanses.   Oordh = upside down.    Tup(u) = torture oneself to please some god.    Adhiyatum = spiritual.  Jeevut(u) Muraiy = overcome selfishness and Maya.        1.       Bhuj = run quickly. Bikh(u) = poison (Maya).  Bhuvjul(u) = sea of life (Maya).          Pause.            Traiy goon = three effects of Maya.   Dhaat(u) = pursuit; efforts.  Dooja Bhaao = love for the other (Maya).   Vikaar(u) = evil thoughts (or deeds).   Bundhun = bonds.    Trikooti = the three effects of Maya.  Chautha Pud(i) = the fourth spiritual stage (above the effects of Maya).    Mookt(i) = freedom from Maya (union with God).      2.
Maarug(u) = (true) path.   Moh(u) = worldly attachments.    Goobaar(u) = darkness; 
ignorance.    Oodhraiy = is salvaged.        3.        Munooa = mind.    Ut(i) = extremely.    Subl = strong; overwhelming.      4.   
MEANING: Rush to take shelter of Satguru’s feet, oh my mind.  The only way to overcome your haumein and swim across the sea of Maya is to obey Satguru’s Shabad with His benevolence.         Pause.        Some people cleanse their bodies (by taking a dip at holy places or by yogic techniques), and meditate by suspending themselves upside down (torture themselves to please some god), but cannot get rid of their haumein by any of such means.  They may also perform some spiritual deeds simply as a show off to impress people, but they will never cultivate Waheguru’s Naam in their mind.  However, if they overcome their selfishness and love for Maya by obeying the Guru’s Shabad, Waheguru will take up dwelling in their hearts.       1.         Pursuit of Maya is a worthless effort, and one achieves no spiritual gain in its love.  Pundit reads his religious books but is still bound to Maya and does not understand the real objective of life.  But when one connects to Satguru, he conquers the three effects of Maya and attains the fourth level of spirituality, where he achieves union with God.           2.          It is the Guru who guides one on the right path (to connect with God), whereby one’s darkness caused by worldly attractions is dispelled.  If he kills (overcomes) his selfishness by Guru’s Shabad, he is able to swim across the sea of Maya, and attain freedom from it to be “one” with Waheguru.  By Guru’s benevolence, he stays connected with the True Naam of the Creator.       3.        The desires of the mind are highly overwhelming, and the mind cannot control them on its own. As a result of his weak mind, the man receives punishment (from Waheguru) and suffers immensely because of his love for Maya.  Only those individuals who overcome their haumein who recite Waheguru Naam by the Guru’s Shabad. Such individuals are able to swim across the sea of Maya, oh Nanak. 4.18.51.
