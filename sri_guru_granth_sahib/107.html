Word Meaning:  Meenh(u) = rain.  Jea Junt = living beings.   Sookhi Vusaaya = settled in comfort.   Klaysh(u) = hostility; bitterness.     1.    Pritpaaray = raised; looked after.   Rukhvaaray = guard.   Ghaali = hard work.      2.     Jul Thul Muhhiul = water, land, and sky = the whole world.    Triptaanay = contented.   Churn Pukhaali = wash feet (an expression of profound love and respect).        3.    Ichh = wish; desire.    Poojaavunhaara = fulfiller.   Bulihaara = offer life for sacrifice.    Rusaalee = the source of all essences (Waheguru).   4.
MEANING:  With the kindness of Waheguru, the rain of His Naam is coming down as a result of which all the living beings are having comfort in their life.  By remembering Waheguru’s Naam, all the troubles, and hostility against one another go away, and the everlasting comfort is achieved.         1.          Waheguru, to whom all the living beings belong, looks after them, and He becomes their guard.  The Lord has answered my prayers, and my efforts have been successful (in achieving the objective of human life).         2.          Waheguru is the provider for all the living beings, and can be “seen” (realized) only by the grace of the Guru. I wish to wash the feet of the Guru (serve Him by obeying His advice gladly) with whose grace all the living beings in the waters, on the land, and in the sky get fully satisfied.       3.
Waheguru fulfills the wishes of the mind.  I wish to sacrifice my life for Him all the time. The destroyer of all the discomforts, Waheguru, has bestowed me with the gift (of union with Him); so now I am fully imbued in the love of the source of all essences (Waheguru), oh Nanak!     4.32.39.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਮਨੁ ਤਨੁ ਤੇਰਾ ਧਨੁ ਭੀ ਤੇਰਾ॥ ਤੂ ਠਾਕੁਰੁ ਸੁਆਮੀ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਮੇਰਾ॥ ਜੀਉ ਪਿੰਡੁ ਸਭੁ ਰਾਸਿ ਤੁਮਾਰੀ ਤੇਰਾ ਜੋਰੁ ਗੋਪਾਲਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਸਦਾ ਸਦਾ ਤੂ ਹੈ ਸੁਖਦਾਈ॥ ਨਿਵਿ ਨਿਵਿ ਲਾਗਾ ਤੇਰੀ ਪਾਈ॥ ਕਾਰ ਕਮਾਵਾ ਜੇ ਤੁਧੁ ਭਾਵਾ ਜਾ ਤੂ ਦੇਹਿ ਦਇਆਲਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਤੁਮ ਤੇ ਲਹਣਾ ਤੂ ਮੇਰਾ ਗਹਣਾ॥ ਜੋ ਤੂ ਦੇਹਿ ਸੋਈ ਸੁਖੁ ਸਹਣਾ॥ ਜਿਥੈ ਰਖਹਿ ਬੈਕੁੰਠੁ ਤਿਥਾਈ ਤੂ ਸਭਨਾ ਕੇ ਪ੍ਰਤਿਪਾਲਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 3॥ ਸਿਮਰਿ ਸਿਮਰਿ ਨਾਨਕ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਾਇਆ॥ ਆਠ ਪਹਰ ਤੇਰੇ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਇਆ॥ ਸਗਲ ਮਨੋਰਥ ਪੂਰਨ ਹੋਏ

Majh Mehla 5:  Mun(u) tun(u) Tayra, dhun(u) bhee Tayra.  Tu Tthaakur(u) Soami Prubh mayra.  Jeeo pind(u) subh(u) raas(i) Toomaari, Tayra joar Gopala jeeo. 1. Suda suda Tu haiy sookhdaayee.  Niv(i) niv(i) laagaan Tayri paayeen. Kaar kmaavaan jay Toodh(u) bhaavaan, jaa Tu  dayh(i) Duyyaala jeeo. 2. MEANING:  You are my Master and my Lord, oh Waheguru!  My mind, my body, and my wealth all belong to You (are given by You). My life and by body are Your capital for me (I can use my mind and my body for noble or ignoble deeds), and You are the force behind me, oh the Provider for the whole world!         1.          You are always the source of comfort; I wish to kneel down to touch Your feet (obey Your Command or Will, gladly).  I wish to do only those deeds that You kindly ask me to do so that You may like me.        2.
I have to receive everything from You, and You are my wealth, oh Waheguru.  Whatever you bestow on me, is for my comfort.  You look after everybody, and in whatever situation You keep me in, is paradise for me.        3.
I sing Your virtues day and night, and I get all the comfort when I recite Your Naam.  All my objectives in life (including the one to be “one” with You) have been fulfilled, and I will never experience any source of discomfort, oh Nanak!    4.33.40.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਪਾਰਬ੍ਰਹਮਿ ਪ੍ਰਭਿ ਮੇਘੁ ਪਠਾਇਆ॥ ਜਲਿ ਥਲਿ ਮਹੀਅਲਿ ਦਹ ਦਿਸਿ ਵਰਸਾਇਆ॥ ਸਾਤਿ ਭਈ ਬੁਝੀ ਸਭ ਤ੍ਰਿਸਨਾ ਅਨਦੁ ਭਇਆ ਸਭ ਠਾਈ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਸੁਖਦਾਤਾ ਦੁਖ ਭੰਜਨਹਾਰਾ॥ ਆਪੇ ਬਖਸਿ ਕਰੇ ਜੀਅ ਸਾਰਾ॥ ਅਪਨੇ ਕੀਤੇ ਨੋ ਆਪਿ ਪ੍ਰਤਿਪਾਲੇ ਪਇ ਪੈਰੀ ਤਿਸਹਿ ਮਨਾਈ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਜਾ ਕੀ ਸਰਣਿ ਪਇਆ ਗਤਿ ਪਾਈਐ॥ ਸਾਸਿ ਸਾਸਿ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਧਿਆਈਐ॥ ਤਿਸੁ ਬਿਨੁ ਹੋਰੁ ਨ ਦੂਜਾ ਠਾਕਰੁ ਸਭ ਤਿਸੈ ਕੀਆ ਜਾਈ ਜੀਉ॥ 3॥ ਤੇਰਾ ਮਾਣੁ ਤਾਣੁ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਤੇਰਾ॥ ਤੂ ਸਚਾ ਸਾਹਿਬੁ ਗੁਣੀ ਗਹੇਰਾ॥ ਨਾਨਕੁ ਦਾਸੁ ਕਹੈ ਬੇਨੰਤੀ ਆਠ ਪਹਰ ਤੁਧੁ ਧਿਆਈ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥34॥41॥
Majh Mehla 5:  Parbraham(i) Prubh(i)  maygh(u) putthaaya.  Jul(i) thul(i) muhhiul(i)  deh dis(i) versaaya. Shaant(i) bhuyyi boojhi subh trishna, anund(u) bhuyya subh tthaayeen jeo.1. Sookhdaata dookh bhunjunhaara. Aapay bukhsh(i) Kuray jea saara. Apunay keetay nu aap(i) pritpaalay, paiy paiyreen tiseh(i) munaayee jeeo. 2. Ja kee shern(i) puyyiyaan gut(i) paayiyaiy. Saas(i) saas(i) Hur(i) Naam(u) dhiyayiyaiy. Tis(u) bin(u) hoar(u) na dooja Tthaakur(u), subh Tisaiy keeya jaayee jeeo. 3.  Tayra maan(u), taan(u) ,Prubh Tayra. Tu Sacha Sahib(u) gooni guhayra.  Nanak daas(u) kuhaiy baynti, aatth pehr Toodh(u) dhiyayeen jeeo.   4.34.41.
Word Meaning:  Maygh(u) = cloud (Guru).   Putthaaya = sent.   Muhiul = sky.    Deh Dis(i) = ten directions (in every direction).   Versaaya = dropped.   Shaant = calmness; peace of mind.   Trishna = worldly desires.   Anund = bliss.  Bhuyya = happened; became.   Subh Tthaayeen = everywhere.     1.    Bhunjunhaara = destroyer.   Bukhsh(i) = kindly forgive.   Saara = looks after.   Pritpaalay = raises and nourishes.   Munaayeen = I ask for forgiveness; win over.    2. 
Gut(i) = higher spiritual state.  Tthaakur(i) = Master; Lord.     Jaayee = places.      3. 
Maan(u) = pride.  Taan(u) = force; capability.   Gooni Guhayra = limitless (source of) virtues.   Aatth Pehr = twenty-four hours (all the time).      4.
NOTE: India, being a hot country, welcomes clouds, unless people have very poor shelters or the rain is damaging to crops.  In addition to providing respite from hot days and alleviating water shortages, thereby spurring crop growth, the rains also wash the trees and other vegetation clean, and as a result, they begin to look very pretty. There is a lot of poetry written about clouds and rain.  People need rain for food to satisfy their appetite, but there is another kind of rain they also need badly for their mental food: the rain of Waheguru’s Naam, caused by the Guru-cloud.  In this, and many more Shabads, you will find the rain to mean Naam-rain and the cloud to mean Guru-cloud.
MEANING:  Just as all the living beings in the water, on the land, and in the sky are satisfied when it rains, Waheguru has sent another cloud in the form of Guru, which causes rain on the hearts of all human beings everywhere, as a result of which calmness and peace have prevailed, and all their desires for worldly possessions are satisfied.          1.
Not only is Waheguru the source of all comfort and the destroyer of all troubles, but He is also forgiving.  He ignores our weaknesses, looks after His creation, and raises and nourishes them all.  I, too, drop on His feet to seek forgiveness and win His favour.       2.  
We should worship Waheguru (recite His Naam) with each breath whose shelter raises our spiritual level to the degree where Maya has no effect.  There is no other lord to rival Waheguru, who has created all the places (the whole universe).          3.
All my pride and capability are Your gifts, oh Waheguru.   You are the real Lord, and the limitless source of all virtues.  Your slave (Guru) Nanak prays to You that “I should recite Your Naam all the time.”    4.34.41

Majh Mehla 5:  Subhaiy sookh bhuyyay Prubh tootthay.  Gur Pooray kay churn mun(i) vootthay.  Sehj smaadh(i) lugi liv untar(i), so rus(u) soyee jaanaiy jeeo. 1.  Agumm agoachur Sahib(u) mayra. Ghut ghut untar(i), vertaiy nayra.  Suda aliput(u) jeeaan ka Daata, ko virla aap(u) puchhaanaiy jeeo.  2.  Prubh milnaiy kee eh nishaani.  Mun(i) ikko Sacha, Huqam(u) puchhani.   Sehj(i) suntokh(i) suda triptaasay, anund Khusm kaiy bhaanaiy jeeo.  3. Word Meaning:   Tootthay = pleased to be kind.    Churn = feet (Shabad).  Vootthay = took residence.   Smaadh(i) = meditate with full concentration.  Liv = make connection.    Rus(u) = taste; bliss.     1.     Agumm = (physically) unreachable.    Agoachur = incomprehensible.      Ghut = heart.   Nayra = nearby.   Aliput = unaffected by Maya.   Virla = rare.    Aap(u) = self.      2.      Nishaani = recognition.    Sehj(i) = in a higher spiritual state.     Suntokh = contentment.  
Triptaasay = fully satisfied.   Anund = bliss.    Bhaanaiy = as per the Will.       3.
Hutheen Ditti = gives Himself (Naam).     Nivaaray = dispels.    Rung = enjoyments; bliss.   Maanay = enjoy.     4.
MEANING:  When Waheguru is pleased to be kind, all the comforts are realized, and the holy Shabad of the Perfect Guru takes residence in the heart (appears in the heart).  As a result, the individual attains the higher spiritual level (above the effects of Maya), and with full concentration, he is connected to Waheguru; and only he enjoys that blissful feeling.       1.
My Lord, Waheguru, is physically unreachable and incomprehensible.   He is present in every heart and is close by.  The provider of all living beings, God, is above the effects of Maya, but some rare individuals recognize Him within themselves.          2.
If one wants to know how to recognize a devotee who has realized Waheguru, the answer is: that individual has only Waheguru at heart and recognizes His Will.  He is always satisfied and content, and Maya has no effect on Him.  He is blissful to accept Waheguru’s Will.        3.
The provider Waheguru bestows (His Naam) Himself, and kills the discomfort of reincarnation.  The devotees, who have been adopted (connected to Him) by Waheguru, enjoy the bliss of singing His praise, oh Nanak!   4.35.42.
