MEANING: I drop on the feet (of Satguru) to win His affection. Satguru connected me with the Primal Being  (Waheguru), who has no equal.   Pause.    My Lord of the world is very sweet – sweeter than even my mother and father.  Nobody - sister, brother or other relative (friend) - is like Him.        1.        It is like the month of June-July (the sowing season) for me.  So, (to sow the seed of Waheguru’s Naam), I am ploughing the land of my heart with the tiller of high moral character. I am sowing the seed of His Naam with the hope that with His kindness, I will have a bumper crop (of His Naam).          2.   In the shelter of the Guru, I recognize only the “One” (Waheguru), and I will never think of any other power as my Guru.  Waheguru has sent me (given me human life) for only one business (of His Naam); now I request Him to please finish this business successfully the way He pleases.         3.          Satguru has conferred the robe of honour on me in His Court. Now, I am the ruler of my body-village and have made the five opposing demons (lust, rage, greed, attachment, and rage) my prisoners. I ask my devotee brothers to enjoy the taste of Waheguru’s Naam –juice.           4.           I have come to Your shelter, oh Waheguru.  Now the five demons are my farm assistants (they help me in growing the Naam-crop in my heart).  None of them can raise their head (to harm the Naam-crop), and the village of my heart has been densely inhabited with noble qualities (virtues).          5.        I am a sacrifice to You, oh my Lord, and recite Your Naam continuously.  You have re-inhabited the ruins of my deserted heart-village (with virtues), oh Waheguru.  I wish to lay down my life for You.         6. I recite Your Naam with love and respect, and reap the fruit of my wishes (Your Naam).  You have fulfilled all my wishes and satisfied the appetite of my mind, oh Waheguru.  7. I have dropped all other worthless deeds and worship only the True Lord of the world. I have realized that the real treasure is Waheguru’s Naam, and have firmly entrenched this fact in my mind.  8.  I have fostered Guru’s Shabad in my mind, and have acquired the best of all comforts. Satguru has shown me Waheguru by placing His kind hand on my forehead.   9.  The Guru has founded a place (holy congregation) for the Sikhs to attain a high level of spirituality. He asks His Sikhs to look around to find other Gur Sikhs.  He washes their feet, fans them to provide comfort from the heat, and bends down to touch their feet (to set an example of humbleness).      10. Hearing such great things about the Guru, I, too, went to His shelter. Satguru reinforces The importance of Waheguru’s Naam, service to the masses, and purity of mind.  (Following the Guru’s advice), The whole world has achieved freedom from the bonds of Maya, because the Guru enabled them to board the ship (of Waheguru’s Naam)  which carries them (across the sea of Maya) to Waheguru, oh Nanak.         11.The entire world is serving You day and night; pay attention to their prayers, oh kind Waheguru.  Everybody has examined thoroughly from every aspect, and found out that when You are pleased with someone, You cut him free from the bonds of Maya on Your own, oh Waheguru.        12.          It is Your Law (that when people are cut loose from the bonds of Maya), no demon (of sensual feelings) troubles them, oh Waheguru.   Such people have a life of blissfulness, and this is what you may call the kingdom of humbleness. 13.        (In this state of spirituality) There is a drizzle of Amrit in their minds; they speak what they are told to (by the Lord), meaning they obey Waheguru’s Command.  They have full pride (faith) in Waheguru, which values their dedication and connects them with Him.        14.Waheguru’s devotees are always hungry for (desirous of) His sight.  He fulfils all their wishes.  Kindly grant me Your sight, and take me in Your embrace, oh, the source of all comforts!     15.I have searched, and have not found anybody as magnanimous as You are, oh Waheguru!  You are present in the islands (earth), in all fourteen worlds, and in the underworld. You are present everywhere, oh Lord!  Waheguru is the support for all His devotees, oh Nanak!  16.       I am a wrestler of the Lord of the world, and fight against my evil sensual pleasures. With the Guru's support, I am the winner (against the five demons).   Everybody is a wrestler In the world-rink, and the kind Waheguru Himself is watching them fight against the five demons.  17. NOTE:  Guru Sahib has used “Chhinjh or Wrestling Match” as a metaphor for the 
struggle humans are facing against the five demons (lust, rage, greed, attachment and ego). (Like music is played in traditional matches where scores of wrestlers take part), different types of musical instruments of sensual pleasures - like the ones that are played with the mouth, small and big drums, etc. – are being played in the human mind. The human-mind and the five demons have entered the wrestling ring for the match, and each is displaying its confidence. Devotees of Waheguru are able to disarm the five demons, and the Guru pats their backs in admiration!      18. Everybody is fighting this battle in the world (some will win and some will lose), and following different paths, they will leave this world to go to the next world to face the consequences!   The Gurmukhs will leave this world with profit (higher spiritual levels) while the followers of their mind will leave with the loss of even their capitals (the virtues they were born with, if they had any).       19.You are without any colour or facial features, yet you are still visibly present (in nature), oh Waheguru!  Hearing about Your wonderful qualities, Your devotees worship You, and are imbued in You Naam, oh the treasure of all virtues, Waheguru.        20.I have been in the service of the kind Lord through the eons (there have been devotees of God in all the eons). The Guru has cut my bonds of Maya; I got this opportunity (human life) to serve Waheguru, and as a result, I won’t have to struggle against the demons any more (I won’t be born again), oh Nanak.  21.2.29.
