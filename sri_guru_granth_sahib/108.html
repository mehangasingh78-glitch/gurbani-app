(ਅੰਗ 107)॥      ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਕੀਨੀ ਦਇਆ ਗੋਪਾਲ ਗੁਸਾਈ॥ ਗੁਰ ਕੇ ਚਰਣ ਵਸੇ ਮਨ ਮਾਹੀ॥ ਅੰਗੀ ਕਾਰੁ ਕੀਆ ਤਿਨਿ ਕਰਤੈ ਦੁਖ ਕਾ ਡੇਰਾ ਢਾਹਿਆ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਮਨਿ ਤਨਿ ਵਸਿਆ ਸਚਾ ਸੋਈ॥ ਬਿਖੜਾ ਥਾਨੁ ਨ ਦਿਸੈ ਕੋਈ॥ ਦੂਤ ਦੁਸਮਣ ਸਭਿ ਸਜਣ ਹੋਏ ਏਕੋ ਸੁਆਮੀ ਆਹਿਆ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਜੋ ਕਿਛੁ ਕਰੇ ਸੁ ਆਪੇ ਆਪੈ॥ ਬੁਧਿ ਸਿਆਣਪ ਕਿਛੂ ਨ ਜਾਪੈ॥ ਆਪਣਿਆ ਸੰਤਾ ਨੋ ਆਪਿ ਸਹਾਈ  ਪ੍ਰਭਿ ਭਰਮ ਭੁਲਾਵਾ ਲਾਹਿਆ ਜੀਉ॥ 3॥ ਚਰਣ ਕਮਲ ਜਨ ਕਾ ਆਧਾਰੋ॥ ਆਠ ਪਹਰ ਰਾਮ ਨਾਮੁ ਵਾਪਾਰੋ॥ ਸਹਜ ਅਨੰਦ ਗਾਵਹਿ ਗੁਣ ਗੋਵਿੰਦ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਨਾਨਕ ਸਰਬ ਸਮਾਹਿਆ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥36॥43॥
(PAGE 107):           Majh Mehla 5:  Keeni duyya Gopal Gosaayeen.  Gur kay churn vusay mun(i) maaheen. Ungikaar(u) keeya tin(i) Kurtaiy, dookh ka dayra dhaaya jeeo.  1. Mun(i) tun(i) vusiya Sacha soyee. Bikhrra thaan(u) na disaiy koyee. Doot dooshmun subh(i) sujjan hoyay, aiko Soami aahiya jeeo. 2.  Jo kichh(u) kuray, so Aapay Aapaiy. Boodh(i) siyanup kichhoo na jaapaiy. Aapniyaan suntaan nu Aap suhaayee, Prubh(i) bherm bhoolaava laahiya jeeo.  3.Churn Kumal jun ka aadhaaro.  Aatth pehr Ram Naam(u) vaapaaro.  Sehj anund gaaveh(i) goon Govind, Prubh Nanak serb smaahiya jeeo. 4.36.43.
Word Meaning:   Duyya = benevolence; compassion.   Gopal = Gosaayeen = the provider for the earth = Waheguru.   Churn = feet (Shabad).   Maaheen = in.   Ungikaar(u) = hugged (expressed love and kindness).   Dayra = home.    Dhaaya = demolished.      1.Vusiya = took residence.   Bikhrra = difficult; insurmountable.    Doot = demons (lust, rage etc.)   Aahiya = wished for.       2.    Boodh(i) = wisdom.   Jaapaiy = visible; evident.   Bherm Bhoolaava = misled by doubts and misbeliefs.     Laahiya = removed.         3.Churn Kumal = lotus-like feet (Shabad).    Aadhaaro = support; prop.    Aatth Pehr = twenty-four hours (all the time).  Vaapaar = deal in.  Sehj = higher spiritual state (above the effects of Maya).  Anund = bliss.    Smaahiya = mixed with; present.     4.
MEANING:  Because of the kindness of Waheguru - the provider for the earth – Guru’s feet (His Shabad) has flourished (ingrained) in my heart.  The Creator did me a huge favour and demolished the home of all my discomforts.          1. Waheguru has taken residence in my body and mind, and nothing seems to be insurmountable now.   By wishing only for Waheguru, all the demons (lust, rage, greed, etc.) have become my friends.        2. Whatever Waheguru does, He does it alone, and nobody else’s wisdom or intelligence is needed.  He supports His devotees and clears all their doubts and misbeliefs. 3. Waheguru’s lotus-like feet are (His Naam is) the basis of the life of the devotees, and they deal in His Naam all the time.  By singing the virtues of Waheguru, they enjoy a blissful state in which Maya has no effect.   Waheguru is omnipresent, oh Nanak!          4.36.43.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਸੋ ਸਚੁ ਮੰਦਰੁ ਜਿਤੁ ਸਚੁ ਧਿਆਈਐ॥ ਸੋ ਰਿਦਾ ਸੁਹੇਲਾ ਜਿਤੁ ਹਰਿ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਈਐ॥ ਸਾ ਧਰਤਿ ਸੁਹਾਵੀ ਜਿਤੁ ਵਸਹਿ ਹਰਿ ਜਨ ਸਚੇ ਨਾਮ ਵਿਟਹੁ ਕੁਰਬਾਣੋ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਸਚੁ ਵਡਾਈ ਕੀਮ ਨ ਪਾਈ॥ ਕੁਦਰਤਿ ਕਰਮੁ ਨ ਕਹਣਾ ਜਾਈ॥ ਧਿਆਇ ਧਿਆਇ ਜੀਵਹਿ ਜਨ ਤੇਰੇ ਸਚੁ ਸਬਦ ਮਨਿ ਮਾਣੋ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਸਚੁ ਸਾਲਾਹਣੁ ਵਡਭਾਗੀ ਪਾਈਐ॥ ਗੁਰ ਪਰਸਾਦੀ ਹਰਿ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਈਐ॥ ਰੰਗਿ ਰਤੇ ਤੇਰੈ ਤੁਧੁ ਭਾਵਹਿ ਸਚੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਨੀਸਾਣੋ ਜੀਉ॥ 3॥ ਸਚੇ ਅੰਤੁ ਨ ਜਾਣੈ ਕੋਈ॥ ਥਾਨਿ ਥਨੰਤਰਿ ਸਚਾ ਸੋਈ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਸਚੁ ਧਿਆਈਐ ਸਦ ਹੀ ਅੰਤਰਜਾਮੀ ਜਾਣੋ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥37॥44॥
Majh Mehla 5:  So Sach(u) mundar(u) jit(u) Sach(u) dhiyayiyaiy.  So rida soohayla jit(u) Hur goon gaayiyaiy. Sa dhert(i) soohaavi jit(u) vuseh(i) Hur(i) jun, Sachay Naam vit-houn koorbaano jeeo. 1.  Sach(u) vudaayee keem na payee.  Koodrut(i) kerm(u) na kehna jaayee.  Dhiyayay dhiyayay jeeveh(i) jun tayray, Sach(u) Shabad mun(i) maano jeeo. 2.  Sach(u) saalahun(u) vudbhaageen paayiyaiy.  Gur persadi Hur(i) goon gaayiyaiy. Rung(i) ruttay Tayraiy, Toodh(u) bhaaveh(i), Sach(u) Naam(u) nishaano jeeo.  3.  Sachay unt(u) na jaanaiy koyee.  Thaan(i) thanuntar(i) Sacha soyee. Nanak Sach(u) dhiyayiyaiy sud hee, Untarjaami jaano jeeo.  4.37.44. 
Word Meaning:   Mundar = temple; mansion.    Rida = heart.  Soohayla = blissful; peaceful.    Soohaavi = providing comfort; pretty.   Vit-houn = from; for.        1.    Sach(u) = Waheguru.    Vudaayee = greatness; magnanimity.   Keem = value.  Koodrut(i) = nature; capability.   Kurm(u) = deeds; tasks.     Maano = enjoy.       2.     Saalahun(u) = praise.   Vudbhaageen = with great luck.   Rung(i) Ruttay = imbued in love.    Bhaaveh(i) = are liked.   Nishaano = recognition.      3.     Unt(u) = limits.   Thaan(i) Thanuntar(i) = (in) every place.    Untarjaami = omniscient.   4.
MEANING: Only that temple is real where only Waheguru is worshipped.  Only that heart is blissful which sings the virtues of Waheguru.  That piece of land is a source of comfort where His devotees reside, who are always ready to sacrifice their lives for Waheguru.      1. 
Waheguru’s magnanimity is eternal, but nobody can evaluate it.  His capability and His deeds cannot be described. However, His devotees experience bliss by reciting His Naam and meditating on His Shabad (Huqam)  2.   One can sing Waheguru’s praise only if one is very lucky, and sing Waheguru’s virtues only by the grace of the Guru. Waheguru likes those who are imbued in His love.  They are recognized by the fact that they always recite Waheguru’s Naam (sing His praise).   3.  Nobody can find the limits of Waheguru as He is present in all the countries and beyond.  We should recite His Naam all the time (no matter how isolated in some faraway place we are) because the omniscient Waheguru is everywhere, and knows everything about us, oh Nanak.  4.37.44.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਰੈਣਿ ਸੁਹਾਵੜੀ ਦਿਨਸੁ ਸੁਹੇਲਾ॥ ਜਪਿ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਨਾਮੁ ਸੰਤਸੰਗਿ ਮੇਲਾ॥ ਘੜੀ ਮੂਰਤ ਸਿਮਰਤ ਪਲ ਵੰਞਹਿ ਜੀਵਣੁ ਸਫਲੁ ਤਿਥਾਈ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਸਿਮਰਤ ਨਾਮੁ ਦੋਖ ਸਭਿ ਲਾਥੇ॥ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਬਾਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਸਾਥੇ॥ ਭੈ ਭਉ ਭਰਮੁ ਖੋਇਆ ਗੁਰਿ ਪੂਰੈ ਦੇਖਾ ਸਭਨੀ ਜਾਈ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਸਮਰਥੁ ਵਡ ਊਚ ਅਪਾਰਾ॥ ਨਉ ਨਿਧਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਭਰੇ ਭੰਡਾਰਾ॥ ਆਦਿ ਅੰਤਿ ਮਧਿ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਸੋਈ ਦੂਜਾ ਲਵੈ ਨ ਲਾਈ ਜੀਉ॥ 3॥ ਕਰਿ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਮੇਰੇ ਦੀਨ ਦਇਆਲਾ॥ ਜਾਚਿਕੁ ਜਾਚੈ ਸਾਧ ਰਵਾਲਾ॥ ਦੇਹਿ ਦਾਨੁ ਨਾਨਕੁ ਜਨੁ ਮਾਗੈ ਸਦਾ ਸਦਾ ਹਰਿ ਧਿਆਈ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥38॥45॥
Majh Mehla 5:  Raiyn(i) soohaavrri dinus(u) soohayla.  Jup(i) Amrit Naam Sunt sung(i) mayla.  Ghurree moorut simrut pul vunjeh(i), jeevun sufu(u) tithaayeen jeeo.  1. Simrut Naam(u) doakh subh(i) laathay. Untar(i) baahur(i) Hur(i) Prubh(u) saathay.  Bhaiy bhuou bherm(u) khohiya Gur Pooraiy,  daykhaan subhnee jaayeen jeeo.  2.  Prubh(u) sumruth(u) vud ooch apaara. Naun nidh(i) Naam(u) bhuray bhundaara. Aad(i) unt(u) mudh(i) Prubh soyee, dooja luvaiy na laayee jeeo.  3.  Kur(i) kirpa mayray deen duyaala. Jaachik(u) jaachaiy Sadh ruvaala.  Dayh(i) daan(u) Nanak jun maangaiy, suda suda Hur(i) dhiyayeen jeeo.  4.38.45.
Word Meaning:   Raiyn(i) = night.    Soohaavrri = Soohayla = pleasant; providing comfort.  Mayla = together; in the company.   Ghurree = Moorut = moment.   Vunjeh(i) = spend; go by.    Tithaayee = (only) that.      1.       Doakh = allegations; sins.   Laathay = removed.   Saathay = with.   Bhaiy = bhuou = fear.   Bherm = doubts; misbeliefs.   Khohiya = lost; removed.  Jaayeen = places.       2.          Sumruth = fully capable; omnipotent.   Naun Nidh(i) = the nine mythical treasures of god Kuber.   Luvaiy na Laayee = equals; matches.       3.
Deen Duyaala = kind to the powerless.   Jaachik(u) = beggar.   Jaachaiy = begs.   Sadh Ruvaala = dust of Guru’s feet (an expression of love and respect); favour of the Guru.    Jun(u) = devotee.    4.
MEANING:  When a person recites Waheguru’s life-sustaining Naam in the company of the Guru, both his night and day become very pretty (comforting) for him.  When every moment of life is spent in reciting Waheguru’s Naam, it becomes successful (one achieves the objective of life, i.e. becomes “one” with Waheguru).            1.         All the sins of the individual are washed away by reciting Waheguru’s Naam, and Waheguru is always with Him inside and out.  The perfect Guru dispels all his fears and misbeliefs, and the individual “sees” Waheguru all around.           2.         God is omnipotent, great, transcendent (above the effects of Maya), and limitless.  The treasure of Waheguru’s Naam is like having all the nine mythical treasures of Kuber.  Waheguru has been here since the beginning, is here now, and will always be here till the end, and no one else can rival Him.        3.
Please be kind to this beggar (me) as You are always kind to the powerless (humble), and grant me the dust of the feet of the Guru (His Shabad) which I beg of You. Your slave, Nanak, begs for Your Naam, which He wishes to recite all the time.    4.38.45.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਐਥੈ ਤੂ ਹੈ ਆਗੈ ਆਪੇ॥ ਜੀਅ ਜੰਤ੍ਰ ਸਭਿ ਤੇਰੇ ਥਾਪੇ॥ ਤੁਧੁ ਬਿਨੁ ਅਵਰੁ ਨ ਕੋਈ ਕਰਤੇ ਮੈ ਧਰ ਓਟ ਤੁਮਾਰੀ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਰਸਨਾ ਜਪਿ ਜਪਿ ਜੀਵੈ ਸੁਆਮੀ॥ ਪਾਰਬ੍ਰਹਮ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਅੰਤਰਜਾਮੀ॥ ਜਿਨਿ ਸੇਵਿਆ ਤਿਨ ਹੀ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਸੋ ਜਨਮੁ ਨ ਜੂਐ ਹਾਰੀ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਨਾਮੁ ਅਵਖਧੁ ਜਿਨਿ ਜਨ ਤੇਰੈ ਪਾਇਆ॥
