MEANING:  The human bride, who possesses the qualities of a slave (obeying orders of Waheguru, without questioning), enjoys the real comfort of life, oh young woman.  Only that bride is perfect who gets rid of all the bad qualities by acting on Guru’s advice, and gets connected with Waheguru as a result.       Pause.
(To have their wishes fulfilled) Some people recite some mantras, or torture themselves physically, or live in some holy place (to please some god); but no such act, or even accepted noble deeds, donations (to Brahmans), or other acts of kindness, result in any enhancement in their spiritual level without singing praise of) Waheguru. One reaps rewards only for what one sows; without acquiring noble qualities, one’s life goes to waste.       1.
Like a trader who has no capital but looks for profit everywhere, most pundits are looking for spiritual gain outside (without any devotion to Waheguru), and do not realize that the invaluable object they are looking for (source of life - Waheguru) is inside them (in their mind). Without this merchandise (Waheguru’s Naam), they experience heavy suffering. The fraudsters are robbed (of spirituality) by the false show of their religiosity - their knowledge of the Vedas and their ability to debate.                  2.        However, if one always examines the Rattan (precious Waheguru, which is always fresh) by pondering on the Guru’s Shabad, he earns a huge spiritual gain.  He can achieve the real objective (Waheguru’s Naam) in his own heart, and will thus leave the world having achieved the purpose of life.  One should come to Guru’s shelter, and deal in the (Naam) with the traders (real devotees) by discussing Waheguru’s noble qualities (sing Waheguru’s praise in their company).        3.
Waheguru can be realized in the company of the Guru (or saintly devotees), provided He wants to connect someone with His kindness.  Once one is connected to Waheguru and has been enlightened, he will never experience disconnection. With true devotion and love, a man can be “one” with Waheguru, and enjoy the eternal Residence of Waheguru as an integral component (part) of His.        4.          Those who have recognized the self (as a component of Waheguru) and have cleansed their minds to be a place for Waheguru to reside are imbued with Waheguru’s Naam, receiving it as a gift. With His eternal Naam in mind, one “sees” Waheguru everywhere in the three-layered universe.          5. 
That woman (the bride) becomes very beautiful when she realizes Waheguru within her.  She is invited to His Palace by the Master (Waheguru), and she enjoys His love. She is a truly married bride (loved by the Husband), and a pretty woman (with noble qualities), who has been enchanted by Waheguru because of His noble qualities.       6.   
(Not realizing that God lives in their heart) Many people, rather than searching Him in their hearts, leave their homes in search of Him.  They wander on earth, on mountains, and in forests, but still cannot find Him because only the Guru can lead them on the right path.  Forgetting Waheguru’s Naam, if one keeps wandering unguided, he continues to be born again, and again.         7.            (To know the real path so as to be “one” with Waheguru) Ask the travellers for guidance who have actually walked the path for union with Him, oh my friend.  They will tell you that (to connect with God) one has to have the qualities of a personal attendant (full devotion with faith).  He should recognize the King (Waheguru) as his own Master (serve Him diligently), when he will experience no hurdle or barrier to reach Waheguru’s Palace.  Then he will also realize that there is only “one” God pervading the whole universe, and no one else, oh Nanak.   8.6.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 1॥ 

ਗੁਰ ਤੇ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਜਾਣੀਐ ਨਿਰਮਲ ਦੇਹ ਸਰੀਰੁ॥ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਸਾਚੋ ਮਨਿ ਵਸੈ ਸੋ ਜਾਣੈ ਅਭ ਪੀਰ॥ ਸਹਜੈ ਤੇ ਸੁਖੁ ਅਗਲੋ ਨ ਲਾਗੈ ਜਮ ਤੀਰੁ॥ 1॥ਭਾਈ ਰੇ ਮੈਲੁ ਨਾਹੀ ਨਿਰਮਲ ਜਲਿ ਨਾਇ॥ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਸਾਚਾ ਏਕੁ ਤੂੰ ਹੋਰੁ ਮੈਲੁ ਭਰੀ ਸਭ ਜਾਇ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਹਰਿ ਕਾ ਮੰਦਰੁ ਸੋਹਣਾ ਕੀਆ ਕਰਣੈਹਾਰੁ॥ ਰਵਿ ਸਸਿ ਦੀਪ ਅਨੂਪ ਜੋਤਿ ਤ੍ਰਿਭਵਣਿ ਜੋਤਿ ਅਪਾਰ॥ ਹਾਟ ਪਟਣ ਗੜ ਕੋਠੜੀ ਸਚ ਸਉਦਾ ਵਾਪਾਰ॥ 2॥ ਗਿਆਨ ਅੰਜਨੁ ਭੈ ਭੰਜਨਾ ਦੇਖੁ ਨਿਰੰਜਨ ਭਾਇ॥ ਗੁਪਤੁ ਪ੍ਰਗਟੁ ਸਭ ਜਾਣੀਐ ਜੇ ਮਨੁ ਰਾਖੈ ਠਾਇ॥ ਐਸਾ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਜੇ ਮਿਲੈ ਤਾ ਸਹਜੇ ਲਏ ਮਿਲਾਇ॥ 3॥ ਕਸਿ ਕਸਵਟੀ ਲਾਈਐ ਪਰਖੇ ਹਿਤੁ ਚਿਤੁ ਲਾਇ॥ ਖੋਟੇ ਠਉਰ ਨ ਪਾਇਨੀ ਖਰੇ ਖਜਾਨੈ ਪਾਇ॥ ਆਸ ਅੰਦੇਸਾ ਦੂਰਿ ਕਰਿ ਇਉ ਮਲੁ ਜਾਇ ਸਮਾਇ॥ 4॥ ਸੁਖ ਕਉ ਮਾਗੈ ਸਭੁ ਕੋ ਦੁਖੁ ਨ ਮਾਗੈ ਕੋਇ॥ ਸੁਖੈ ਕਉ ਦੁਖੁ ਅਗਲਾ ਮਨਮੁਖਿ ਬੂਝ ਨ ਹੋਇ॥ ਸੁਖ ਦੁਖ ਸਮ ਕਰਿ ਜਾਣੀਅਹਿ ਸਬਦਿ ਭੇਦਿ ਸੁਖੁ ਹੋਇ॥ 5॥ ਬੇਦੁ ਪੁਕਾਰੇ ਵਾਚੀਐ ਬਾਣੀ ਬ੍ਰਹਮ ਬਿਆਸੁ॥ ਮੁਨਿ ਜਨ ਸੇਵਕ ਸਾਧਿਕਾ ਨਾਮਿ ਰਤੇ ਗੁਣਤਾਸੁ॥ ਸਚਿ ਰਤੇ ਸੇ ਜਿਣਿ ਗਏ ਹਉ ਸਦ ਬਲਿਹਾਰੈ ਜਾਸੁ॥ 6॥ ਚਹੁ ਜੁਗਿ ਮੈਲੇ ਮਲੁ ਭਰੇ ਜਿਨ ਮੁਖਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਨ ਹੋਇ॥ ਭਗਤੀ ਭਾਇ ਵਿਹੂਣਿਆ ਮੁਹੁ ਕਾਲਾ ਪਤਿ ਖੋਇ॥ ਜਿਨੀ ਨਾਮੁ ਵਿਸਾਰਿਆ ਅਵਗਣ ਮੁਠੀ ਰੋਇ॥ 7॥ ਖੋਜਤ ਖੋਜਤ ਪਾਇਆ ਡਰੁ ਕਰਿ ਮਿਲੈ ਮਿਲਾਇ॥ ਆਪੁ ਪਛਾਣੈ ਘਰਿ ਵਸੈ ਹਉਮੈ ਤ੍ਰਿਸਨਾ ਜਾਇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਿਰਮਲ ਊਜਲੇ ਜੋ ਰਾਤੇ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਇ॥ 8॥7॥

Word Meaning:  Nirmul(u) = clean; pure (Waheguru).   Dayh = body; mind.   Ubh = heart.   Peer = peerr = pain.    Sehjaiy Tay = with control on mind (mind at peace).   Uglo = very much.   Jum = Angel of death.   Teer = arrow; attack.      1.      Maiyl(u) = filth.   Nirmul Jul(u) = clean (pure) water (Waheguru’s Naam-water).  Jaayay = place.    Pause.

MEANING: Just like a dip in clean water cleanses your body, a dip in the holy water of Waheguru’s Naam cleanses the filth of sensual pleasures from your mind, oh my brother.  Only Waheguru is eternally clean; all other places (human hearts) are full of filth of evil desires.       Pause.          However, one gets connected with the Pure Waheguru only by the grace of the Guru, whereby one’s mind and body also get pure. (Body becomes pure when no evil deed is done by it). When the mind becomes pure, Waheguru, who knows the pain of your heart (because of separation from Him), takes residence in your heart.  When the mind is pure, one feels great comfort (blissfulness), and the fear of death is gone.        1.
Even though the Creator (Waheguru) is present in every heart imperceptibly, He enlightens the hearts of His devotees only and makes their hearts a place to reside. In such beautiful (pure) hearts, the sun of Divine Knowledge destroys the darkness of ignorance, and the moon of coolness puts out the fire of burning desires. The devotee then “sees” God everywhere in the three-layered universe. The heart of such a devotee becomes a shop, a city, or a fort wherein only the “True” merchandise of Waheguru’s Naam is traded (the devotee spends most of his time in the company of other devotees, where only Waheguru’s praise is sung).       2.             If one is fortunate to connect with the Guru who imparts Divine Knowledge in his mind which acts like kohl for the eyes to “see” the destroyer of all fears – Waheguru- who is above the effects of Maya, and fosters love for Waheguru in the mind of the individual, he attains the spiritual state where mind finds peace, and can “see” Waheguru which is present visibly and invisibly (in nature).         3.       Like a goldsmith who tests gold for its purity by grazing it on a touchstone, Waheguru examines His humans very carefully, but with kindness. Like counterfeit money, the fake (hypocrite) finds no place in Waheguru’s Court, while the true devotees (like real money) become part of Waheguru’s treasure. The filth of all the doubts and of the worldly hopes of such devotees is removed, and they become “one with” God.        4.            Of course, everybody wishes comforts, and nobody wants discomforts; but they do not realize that comforts are followed by huge discomforts. The follower of his mind does not recognize this fact. The true comfort is attained when a man’s mind is pierced with the Guru’s Shabad, as a result of which, he makes no distinction between comforts and discomforts.       5.          Sage Biyas kept reading the Vedas loudly (preaching them with vigour), but to connect with Waheguru, one needs to ponder on the Guru’s Shabad (which praises God). The sages who stay quiet for a long time, the devotees, and the ones who try different ways to purify their minds, are successful only if they are imbued with Waheguru’s Naam. I am a sacrifice for all those who are successful in their objective (swim across the sea of Maya) by being imbued in Waheguru’s Love.       6.        Those who do not recite Waheguru’s Naam by their tongue have the filth of Maya, no matter what eon it is.  Those who are devoid of the love for worship of God, lose respect in (Waheguru’s Court), and their faces are blackened (they are humiliated). The individuals who ignore Waheguru’s Naam are robbed (of spirituality) by their ignoble qualities, and they have to repent.        7.
Individuals who have Waheguru’s respectful fear in mind and search for Him are connected with the Guru by Waheguru’s kindness and are guided by the Guru to be connected with Waheguru. They recognize themselves, overcome their selfishness, ego, and worldly desires, and find peace in their minds. Those who are imbued in Waheguru’s Naam are pure (in every way), oh Nanak.   8.7.
