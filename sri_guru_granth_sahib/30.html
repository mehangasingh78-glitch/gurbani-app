(PAGE 29):        Lakh Chauraasee tursday, jis(u) maylay, so milaiy Hur(i) aayay. Nanak, Gurmukh(i) Hur(i) Paaya, suda Hur(i) Naam(i) smaayay. 4.6.39.
Word Meaning:  Raas(i) = capital; wealth.    Vaapaar(u) = trading.   Vukkhur(u) = merchandize.    Adhaar(u) = support.    Drirryaaya = reinforced.   Utoot(u) = inexhaustible.    Bhundaar(u) = treasure.       1.          Aalus(u) = laziness; indolence.   Dhiyayay = ponder on, and recite (with concentration).          Pause.        Pakhund(i) = by faking.    Doobidha = difficulty in choosing between Maya and Waheguru. Khooaar(u) = suffer in vain; be discomfited.   Bibayk = knowledge; ability to analyze.     Beechaar(u) = thinking.      2.
Sayvuk = devotee.    Ooridhaar(i) = implant in mind.     Sounpay = offer.   Dhuray = place.  Haumein = selfishness; ego.    Dhun(u) = praise worthy.   Purvaan(u) = acceptable.  J(i) = if.   Kuday na = never.         3.        Kerm(i) = through kindness (as a favour).   Vin(u) = without.  Lukh Chauraasee = 8.4 million (life forms according to Hindu belief).     Tursday = dying to have something. 4.
MEANING: For the devotees, Waheguru’s Naam is their wealth and capital; they seek the advice of the Guru to trade in the business (of Waheguru’s Naam).  They always praise Waheguru, which is their merchandize, and His Naam is their support. The perfect Guru makes sure that the Sikhs recite Waheguru’s Naam repeatedly, which is an inexhaustible treasure.       1.          Question your mind as to why it is so indolent, oh my friends!   It should be reciting Waheguru Naam as advised by the Guru.       Pause.         If you seek Guru’s advice, you will learn that unconditional love for Waheguru is His real worship.  One cannot worship Waheguru simply by faking (hypocrisy). Duality of mind (indecisiveness to choose between Waheguru and Maya) leads one to worthless suffering (humiliation).  An individual who has the ability to think and analyze will never be part of the group that engages in Maya and will never put on a false show of love for Waheguru.          2.          Only a person who always keeps Waheguru in mind can be called a real devotee.  He kills his selfishness and ego, and offers his mind and body to Waheguru.  Such a devotee of the Guru, who never loses battle against the five demons (lust, rage, greed, attachment, and ego), is praiseworthy and is acceptable to Waheguru.         3.          One cannot realize Waheguru’s Naam with his own efforts, no matter how hard he tries; only that individual internalizes it, whom God is kind to.  All the 8.4 million types of living beings kill themselves (try very hard) to acquire Waheguru’s Naam, but only those connect to Him whom He connects, Himself.  You realize Waheguru only by Guru’s grace, but once connected, you become “one” with Him, oh Nanak!  4.6.39.

 

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ ਸੁਖ ਸਾਗਰੁ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਹੈ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਪਾਇਆ ਜਾਇ॥ ਅਨਦਿਨੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਧਿਆਈਐ ਸਹਜੇ ਨਾਮਿ ਸਮਾਇ॥ ਅੰਦਰੁ ਰਚੈ ਹਰਿ ਸਚ ਸਿਉ ਰਸਨਾ ਹਰਿ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਇ॥ 1॥ ਭਾਈ ਰੇ ਜਗੁ ਦੁਖੀਆ ਦੂਜੈ ਭਾਇ॥ ਗੁਰ ਸਰਣਾਈ ਸੁਖੁ ਲਹਹਿ ਅਨਦਿਨੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਧਿਆਇ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਸਾਚੇ ਮੈਲੁ ਨ ਲਾਗਈ ਮਨੁ ਨਿਰਮਲ ਹਰਿ ਧਿਆਇ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਸਬਦੁ ਪਛਾਣੀਐ ਹਰਿ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਨਾਮਿ ਸਮਾਇ॥ ਗੁਰ ਗਿਆਨੁ ਪ੍ਰਚੰਡ ਬਲਾਇਆ ਅਗਿਆਨੁ ਅੰਧੇਰਾ ਜਾਇ॥ 2॥ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਮੈਲੇ ਮਲੁ ਭਰੇ ਹਉਮੈ ਤ੍ਰਿਸਨਾ ਵਿਕਾਰੁ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਸਬਦੈ ਮੈਲੁ ਨ ਉਤਰੈ ਮਰਿ ਜੰਮਹਿ ਹੋਇ ਖੁਆਰੁ॥ ਧਾਤੁਰ ਬਾਜੀ ਪਲਚਿ ਰਹੇ ਨਾ ਉਰਵਾਰੁ ਨਾ ਪਾਰੁ॥3॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਜਪ ਤਪ ਸੰਜਮੀ ਹਰਿ ਕੈ ਨਾਮਿ ਪਿਆਰੁ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਸਦਾ ਧਿਆਈਐ ਏਕੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਕਰਤਾਰੁ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮੁ ਧਿਆਈਐ ਸਭਨਾ ਜੀਆ ਕਾ ਆਧਾਰੁ॥ 4॥7॥40॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 3:  Sookh saagur(u) Hur(i) Naam(u) haiy, Gurmukh(i) paaya jaayay. Undin(u) Naam(u) dhiyayiyaiy sehjay Naam(i) smaayay. Undar(u) ruchaiy Hur(i) Sach siyoun, rusna Hur(i) goon gaayay. 1. Bhaayee ray, jug(u) dookhiya doojaiy bhaayay. Gur surnayee sookh(u) luhay undin(u) Naam(u) dhiyayay. 1. Ruhaou.  Saachay maiyl(u) na laaguyee, mun(u) nirmul Hur(i) dhiyayay.  Gurmukh(i) Shabad(u) puchhaaniyaiy Hur(i) Amrit Naam(i) smaayay. Gur giyan(u) purchund bulaaya, ugiyaan undhayra jaayay. 2. Munmukh maiylay mul(u) bhuray, haumein trishna vikaar(u). Bin(u) Shabdaiy maiyl(u) na ootraiy mur(i) jummeh(i) hoyay khooaar(u). Dhaatur baaji pulch(i) ruhay, na oorvaar(u) na paar(u). 3. Gurmukh(i) jup tup sunjmi, Hur(i) kaiy Naam(i) piyaar(u). Gurmukh(i) suda dhiyayiyaiy aik Naam(u) Kartaar(u). Nanak Naam(u) dhiyayiyaiy subhna jeeaan ka aadhaar(u). 4.7.40. 
Word Meaning: Sookh Saagur(u) = the sea (source) of comforts.  Undin(u) = day and night.   Sehjay = in the state of a stable mind.   Smaayay = is absorbed.   Ruchaiyy = get absorbed.   Siyoun = with.     Rusna = tongue.        1.       Doojaiy Bhaayay = in love with the other (Maya).   Surnayee = refuge.   Luhhay = get; acquire.          Pause.        Maiyl(u) = filth (of evil thoughts).   Nirmul = clean.    Puchhaaniyaiy = recognize.   Giyan = Divine knowledge; connection with God.    Purchund = bright light.  Ugiyaan = ignorance.       2.            Munmukh = followers of their own mind (who have turned away from the Guru).   Trishna = desires.    Vikaar(u) = evil deeds.   Bin(u) = without.    Dhaatur = running around for Maya.   Baaji = game (not reality).  Pulch(i) = entangled.   Oorvaar(u) = this (the near) side of the bank or shore.   Paar(u) = the other side of the bank or shore.               3.         Jup = recitation of some mantra to please some god.  Tup = self-suffering to please some god.    Sunjmi = control over sensual pleasures.     4.
MEANING:  Waheguru is the sea (source) of all comforts, but can be realized only by the benevolence of the Guru.  Remember Waheguru’s Naam day and night and enjoy the bliss of being “one” with Him. One should be internally connected to Waheguru and sing His praise with the tongue.          1.        The world is suffering because of its love for Maya, oh my friend!   But, by taking the refuge of the Guru, and by reciting Waheguru’s Naam day and night, one can acquire all comforts (real blissfulness).          Pause.          A mind which has become pure by reciting Waheguru’s Naam never gets filthy again. By taking refuge in the Guru, one recognizes the importance of the Shabad and is soaked in the life-sustaining holy water (Amrit) of Waheguru’s Naam.  The Guru lights up the bright lamp of Divine Knowledge in the devotee’s mind by which the darkness of ignorance is dispelled.          2.
The followers of their mind are filled with the filth of haumein, desires, and other evil thoughts.  Such filth cannot be washed away without Waheguru’s Shabad, and the individuals suffer even after death.  They are entangled in the short-lived attractions of Maya, and are not happy either on this side of the sea of Maya (this world) or on the other (next world).        3.
A person who seeks the Guru’s shelter to remember Waheguru with full devotion is fully content and does not need any jup, tup or any special effort to control his senses. A real devotee of the Guru recites the Naam of Waheguru alone all the time.  (Guru) Nanak also recites Waheguru’s Naam, which is the support (provider) for all the living beings.  4.7.40.

ਸ੍ਰੀਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ ਮਨਮੁਖੁ ਮੋਹਿ ਵਿਆਪਿਆ ਬੈਰਾਗੁ ਉਦਾਸੀ ਨ ਹੋਇ॥ ਸਬਦੁ ਨ ਚੀਨੈ ਸਦਾ ਦੁਖੁ ਹਰਿ ਦਰਗਹਿ ਪਤਿ ਖੋਇ॥ ਹਉਮੈ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਖੋਈਐ ਨਾਮਿ ਰਤੇ ਸੁਖੁ ਹੋਇ॥ 1॥ ਮੇਰੇ ਮਨ ਅਹਿਨਿਸਿ ਪੂਰਿ ਰਹੀ ਨਿਤ ਆਸਾ॥ ਸਤਗੁਰੁ ਸੇਵਿ ਮੋਹੁ ਪਰਜਲੈ ਘਰ ਹੀ ਮਾਹਿ ਉਦਾਸਾ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉੇ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਕਰਮ ਕਮਾਵੈ ਬਿਗਸੈ ਹਰਿ ਬੈਰਾਗੁ ਅਨੰਦੁ॥ਅਹਿਨਿਸਿ ਭਗਤਿ ਕਰੇ ਦਿਨੁ ਰਾਤੀ ਹਉਮੈ ਮਾਰਿ ਨਿਚੰਦੁ॥ ਵਡੈ ਭਾਗਿ ਸਤਸੰਗਤਿ ਪਾਈ ਹਰਿ ਪਾਇਆ ਸਹਜਿ ਅਨੰਦੁ॥2॥ ਸੋ ਸਾਧੂ ਬੈਰਾਗੀ ਸੋਈ ਹਿਰਦੈ ਨਾਮੁ ਵਸਾਏ॥ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਲਾਗਿ ਨ ਤਾਮਸੁ ਮੂਲੇ ਵਿਚਹੁ ਆਪੁ ਗਵਾਏ॥ ਨਾਮੁ ਨਿਧਾਨੁ ਸਤਗੁਰੂ ਦਿਖਾਲਿਆ ਹਰਿ ਰਸੁ ਪੀਆ ਅਘਾਏ॥ 3॥ ਜਿਨਿ ਕਿਨੈ ਪਾਇਆ ਸਾਧਸੰਗਤੀ ਪੂਰੈ ਭਾਗਿ ਬੈਰਾਗਿ॥ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਫਿਰਹਿ ਨ ਜਾਣਹਿ ਸਤਗੁਰੁ ਹਉਮੈ ਅੰਦਰਿ ਲਾਗਿ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਸਬਦਿ ਰਤੇ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮਿ ਰੰਗਾਏ ਬਿਨੁ ਭੈ ਕੇਹੀ ਲਾਗਿ॥ 4॥8॥41॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 3:  Munmukh(u) moh(i) viyapiya, bairaag(u) oodaasi na hoyay. Shabad(u) na cheenaiy suda dookh(u), Hur(i) Durgeh(i) put(i) khoyay. Haumein Gurmukh(i) khoyiyay, Naam(i) ruttay sookh(u) hoyay. 1. Mayray mun, ehinis(i) poor(i) ruhhi nit aasa. Satgur(u) sav(i) moh(u) purjulaiy, ghur hee maah(i) oodaasa. 1. Ruhaou. Gurmukh(i) kerm kmaavaiy bigsaiy, Hur(i) bairaag(u) anund(u). Ehinis(i) bhugut(i) kuray din(u) raati, haumein maar(i) nichund(u). Vuddaiy bhaag(i) Satsungat paayee, Hur(i) paaya sehj(i) anund(u).  2. So sadhu bairaagi soyee, hirdaiy Naam(u) vusaayay. Untar(i) laag(i) na taamus(u) moolay, vichoun aap(u) guvaayay. Naam(u) nidhaan(u) Satguru dikhaaliya Hur(i) rus(u) peeya aghaayay. 3.  Jin(i) kinaiy paaya Sadhsungtee, pooraiy bhaag(i) bairaag(i).  Munmukh phireh(i) na jaaneh(i) Satgur(u), haumein undar(i) laag(i).  Nanak Shabad(i) ruttay Hur(i) Naam(i) rungaayay bin(u) bhaiy kayhee laag(i). 4.8.41.
Word Meaning:  Munmukh(u) = follower of his mind.   Viyapiya = under the influence.
Bairaag(u) = smitten by love.    Oodaasi = detached.    Cheenaiy = recognize; evaluate.     Khoyay = lose.    Durgeh(i) = Court.    Ruttay = imbued.     1.
Ehinis(i) = day and night.  Purjulaiy = burned (killed) completely. Ghur = heart.  Pause
Kerm = (noble) deeds.   Bigsaiy = blooms.   Anund(u) = bliss.   Nichund(u) = worry-free.   Vuddaiy Bhaag(i) = with great luck.   Sehj(i) = stability (purity) of mind.       2.
Sadhu = devotee.   Soyee = that (person).    Taamus(u) = ignorance; fire of desires.   Moolay = even a bit.  Nidhaan(u) = treasure.  Aghaayay = fully satisfied.     3.
Jin(i) Kinaiy = whoever.   Phireh(i) = with mind turned away (not having faith in Guru).  Rungaayay = coloured their mind (imbued).     Laag(i) = attachment; affection.      4.
MEANING:  The follower of his mind is so much under the influence of Maya that he does not foster any love for Waheguru, and cannot detach himself from Maya.  He does not ponder on Guru’s Shabad, as a result of which, he always suffers and loses respect in Waheguru’s Court.  One can get rid of his haumein by taking Guru’s shelter, and once he is imbued in Waheguru’s Naam, he enjoys eternal bliss.            1.           You are wishing for material things day and night, oh, my mind.  You can kill (get rid of) your worldly attachments completely by serving the Guru (obeying Him), whereby you will be completely detached from Maya while still living in the family.         Pause.        When one performs deeds as per the Will of the Guru, his mind blooms, and he enjoys bliss in Waheguru’s (God’s) love.  He worships Waheguru day and night, and becomes worry-free by killing his haumein (selfishness).  With good luck, he joins the company of holy men and realizes God with a pure mind, and enjoys bliss.         2.        One is the real devotee, and has real affection for Waheguru, who has made his heart a residence for God.  An individual who has overcome his selfishness does not suffer even a bit from the fire of desires.  The Guru shows him the treasure of Naam-Amrit, which he drinks with great taste to his complete satisfaction.         3.        Whoever realizes Waheguru does so only in the company of the devotees, which he finds by his good fortune and with his deep affection for Waheguru.  But, the follower of his mind does not recognize the Guru, and spends his life in ignorance with haumein in mind.  But without some respectful fear of God, there is no affection for Him to colour one’s mind with Guru’s Shabad; and his mind cannot be imbued in the love of the Guru and Waheguru, oh Nanak.  4. 8.41.
