(ਅੰਗ 116)॥   ਮਨਮੁਖ ਖੋਟੀ ਰਾਸਿ ਖੋਟਾ ਪਾਸਾਰਾ॥ ਕੂੜੁ ਕਮਾਵਨਿ ਦੁਖੁ ਲਾਗੈ ਭਾਰਾ॥ ਭਰਮੇ ਭੂਲੇ ਫਿਰਨਿ ਦਿਨ ਰਾਤੀ ਮਰਿ ਜਨਮਹਿ ਜਨਮੁ ਗਵਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 7॥ ਸਚਾ ਸਾਹਿਬੁ ਮੈ ਅਤਿ ਪਿਆਰਾ॥ਪੂਰੈ ਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਸਬਦਿ ਅਧਾਰਾ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮਿ ਮਿਲੈ ਵਡਿਆਈ ਦੁਖੁ ਸੁਖੁ ਸਮ ਕਰਿ ਜਾਨਣਿਆ॥ 8॥10॥11॥
Majh Mehla 3:  Aap(u) vunjaayay ta subh kichh(u) paayay. Gur Shabadi Sachi liv laayay. Sach(u) vunjeh(i), Sach(u) sunghreh(i), Sach(u) vapaar(u) kraavniya.  1. Huoun vaari jeeo vaari, Hur(i) goon undin(u) gaavniya. Huoun Tayra Tu Tthaakur(u) mayra, Shabad(i) vudiyayee dayvniya. 1. Ruhaou. Vayla vukht subh(i) soohaaya. Jit(u) Sacha mayray mun(i) bhaaya. Sachay sayviyaiyn Sach(u) vudiyayee, Gur kirpa tay Sach(u) paavniya. 2.  Bhaaou bhojun(u) Satgur(i) tootthaiy paayay.  Un rus(u) chookaiy, Hur(i) rus(u) munn(i) vusaayay. Sach(u) suntokh(u) sehj sookh(u) Bani, pooraiy Gur tay paavniya. 3.  Satgur(u) na sayveh(i) moorukh undh guvaara. Phir(i) oye kithoun paayin mokh dooara. Mer(i) mer(i) jummeh(i) phir(i) phir(i) aaveh(i), Jum dur(i) choataan khaavniya. 4.  Shabaday saad jaaneh(i) ta aap(u) puchhaaneh(i).  Nirmul Bani Shabad(i) vukhaaneh(i). Sachay sayv(i) suda sookh(u) paayin, nau nidh(i) Naam(u) munn(i) vusaavniya. 5.  So thaan(u) soohaaya jo Hur(i) mun(i) bhaaya. Sat Sungat(i) beh(i) Hur(i) goon gaaya. Undin(u) Hur(i) salaaheh(i) Sacha nirmul naad(u) vujaavniya. 6. 
(PAGE 116):     Munmukh khoti raas(i) khota pasaara. Koorr(u) kumaavun(i) dookh(u) laagaiy bhaara. Bhermay bhoolay phirn(i) din raati, mur(i) junmeh(i), junum(u) guvaavniya.  7.  Sacha Sahib(u) mein ut(i) piyara. Pooraiy Gur kaiy Shabad(i) adhaara. Nanak Naam(i) milaiy vudiyayee, dookh(u) sookh(u) sum kur(i) jaan-niya. 8.10.11.   
Word Meaning:  Aap(u) = selfishness.   Vunjaayay = get rid of.   Vunjeh(i) = Vapaar = (do business (deal in).    Sungreh(i) = gather; accumulate; acquire.        1.Tthaakur(u) = Lord.   Vudiyayee = respect.      Pause.       Vayla Vukht(u) = time.   Soohaava = pleasant; comforting.   Jit(u) = at which.   Bhaaya = liked.   Sayviyaiyn = (by) serving.        2.       Bhaaou = respectful love.      Bhojun = food.   Tootthaiy = (when) pleased.   Un = other.   Rus = enjoyable tasty essences.    Chookaiy = ended.   Suntokh(u) = contentment.  Sehj Sookh = bliss of high spiritual state.            3.            
Undh = blind; ignorant.    Guvaara = unrefined.    Phir(i) = then.  Kithoun = from where.     Jum Dur(i) = at the door (in the Court) of the angel of death.   Choataan = beatings; punished.       4.        Saad(u) = taste.   Puchhaaneh(i) = recognize.   Vukhaneh(i) = utter; explain to others.  Nau Nidh(i) = the nine mythical treasures.         5. Thaan(u) = heart.   Soohaaya = became pretty.   Naad(u) = Shabad.       6. Khoti = fake; unworthy.    Raas(i) = investment (deeds).   Pasaara = expanse.   Koorr(u) = perishable; worthless.   Bhaara = heavy; huge.    Bhermaiy = in misbeliefs.  
Bhoolay = lost.    Junum(u) = (precious human) life.      7.       Sahib(u) = Lord; God.   Ut(i) = extremely.   Adhaara = support; dependence.    Sum = the same; equal.     8.
MEANING:  One can achieve something worthwhile (Waheguru’s Naam) only when he gets rid of his selfishness and focuses his mind on Waheguru with Guru’s Shabad.  
He then deals in Truth (Waheguru’s Naam) and accumulates the wealth of His eternal Naam as his main business (daily routine).           1.          I am a sacrifice for those who sing Waheguru’s virtues day and night.  You are my Lord, and I am Your servant, oh Waheguru.  You honour those who recite and act on the Guru’s Shabad.        Pause.
All those times or occasions were pleasant (auspicious) when I began to like Waheguru.  One gets true respect when he serves the eternal Waheguru. However, one gets connected with Waheguru only by the grace of the Guru.        2. One acquires the food (for the mind) of love for Waheguru only when the Guru is pleased to be kind.  At that time, all worldly tastes (enjoyments) become meaningless, and the bliss of Waheguru’s Naam settles down in the devotee’s heart.  Guru’s eternal Bani is the source of contentment and is the bliss of a high spiritual state, but can be acquired only by the grace of the Guru.         3.         The foolish, unrefined, and the blind (ignorant follower of his mind) do not serve the Guru; so, how can he find the door to salvation?   Such people take birth, and die again, and again, and are punished in the prison of the Angel of death.           4.         An individual who enjoys (obeys) the Guru’s Shabad is able to recognize (analyze) himself. He sings Waheguru’s virtues by discussing the holy Bani and Guru’s Shabad.  He makes his heart a place for Waheguru’s Naam to reside, which is like having the nine mythical treasures, and he stores Waheguru’s Naam in his mind and stays blissful all the time.        5. That heart becomes pretty (with good qualities), which pleases Waheguru.  Such a heart (the individual) sings Waheguru’s praise in the company of the holy devotees. He praises the eternal Waheguru day and night, as a result of which, he enjoys the blissful melody of the holy Shabad all the time.            6. (On the other hand), The follower of his mind makes investments in fake (perishable) goods (unworthy in the realm of spirituality), and his huge wealth expansions are also false (short-lived). He earns only short-lived comforts, but suffers greatly in the future (the next world). The money-minded people are lost in misdirected investments, waste away their precious human life, and are reborn again and again.      7. The True Lord (Waheguru) is very dear to me, and I have earned His support by acting on the Guru’s Shabad.  One earns respect if he is imbued in the Naam, and accepts comfort and discomfort the same way, oh Nanak!    8.10.11.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ ਤੇਰੀਆ ਖਾਣੀ ਤੇਰੀਆ ਬਾਣੀ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਨਾਵੈ ਸਭ ਭਰਮਿ ਭੁਲਾਣੀ॥ ਗੁਰ ਸੇਵਾ ਤੇ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਬਿਨੁ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਕੋਇ ਨ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਹਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਜੀਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਹਰਿ ਸੇਤੀ ਚਿਤੁ ਲਾਵਣਿਆ॥ ਹਰਿ ਸਚਾ ਗੁਰ ਭਗਤੀ ਪਾਈਐ ਸਹਜੇ ਮੰਨਿ ਵਸਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਸੇਵੇ ਤਾ ਸਭ ਕਿਛੁ ਪਾਏ॥ਜੇਹੀ ਮਨਸਾ ਕਰਿ ਲਾਗੈ ਤੇਹਾ ਫਲੁ ਪਾਏ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਦਾਤਾ ਸਭਨਾ ਵਥੂ ਕਾ ਪੂਰੈ ਭਾਗਿ ਮਿਲਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 2॥ ਇਹੁ ਮਨੁ ਮੈਲਾ ਇਕੁ ਨ ਧਿਆਏ॥ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਮੈਲੁ ਲਾਗੀ ਬਹੁ ਦੂਜੈ ਭਾਏ॥ਤਟਿ ਤੀਰਥਿ ਦਿਸੰਤਰਿ ਭਵੈ ਅਹੰਕਾਰੀ ਹੋਰੁ ਵਧੇਰੈ ਹਉਮੈ ਮਲੁ ਲਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 3॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਸੇਵੇ ਤਾ ਮਲੁ ਜਾਏ॥ ਜੀਵਤੁ ਮਰੈ ਹਰਿ ਸਿਉ ਚਿਤੁ ਲਾਏ॥ ਹਰਿ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਸਚੁ ਮੈਲੁ ਨ ਲਾਗੈ ਸਚਿ ਲਾਗੈ ਮੈਲੁ ਗਵਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 4॥ ਬਾਝੁ ਗੁਰੂ ਹੈ ਅੰਧ ਗੁਬਾਰਾ॥ਅਗਿਆਨੀ ਅੰਧਾ ਅੰਧੁ ਅੰਧਾਰਾ॥ਬਿਸਟਾ ਕੇ ਕੀੜੇ ਬਿਸਟਾ ਕਮਾਵਹਿ ਫਿਰਿ ਬਿਸਟਾ ਮਾਹਿ ਪਚਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 5॥ ਮੁਕਤੇ ਸੇਵੇ ਮੁਕਤਾ ਹੋਵੈ॥ ਹਉਮੈ ਮਮਤਾ ਸਬਦੇ ਖੋਵੈ॥ ਅਨਦਿਨੁ ਹਰਿ ਜੀਉ ਸਚਾ ਸੇਵੀ ਪੂਰੈ ਭਾਗਿ ਗੁਰੁ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 6॥ ਆਪੇ ਬਖਸੇ ਮੇਲਿ ਮਿਲਾਏ॥ ਪੂਰੈ ਗੁਰ ਤੇ ਨਾਮੁ ਨਿਧਿ ਪਾਏ॥ਸਚੈ ਨਾਮਿ ਸਦਾ ਮਨੁ ਸਚੁ ਸਚਾ ਸੇਵੇ ਦੁਖੁ ਗਵਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 7॥ਸਦਾ ਹਜੂਰਿ ਦੂਰਿ ਨ ਜਾਣਹੁ॥ ਗੁਰ ਸਬਦੀ ਹਰਿ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਪਛਾਣਹੁ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮਿ ਮਿਲੈ ਵਡਿਆਈ ਪੂਰੈ ਗੁਰ ਤੇ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥  8॥11॥12॥
Majh Mehla 3:  Tayriya khaani Tariya bani.  Bin(u) Naavaiy subh bherm(i) bhoolaani. Gur sayva tay Hur(i) Naam(u) paaya, bin(u) Satgur koyay na paavniya.  1. Huoun vaari jeeo vaari, Hur(i) sayti chit(u) laavniya. Hur(i) Sacha Gur bhugti paayiyaiy, sehjay munn(i) vusaavniya. 1. Ruhaou. Satgur(u) sayvay ta subh kichh(u) paayay. Jayhi munsa kur(i) laagaiy, tayha ful(u) paayay. Satgur(u) daata subhna vuthoo ka, pooraiy bhaag(i) milaavniya.  2. Eho mun(u) maiyla Ikk(u) na dhiyayay. Untar(i) maiyl(u) laagi buhou doojaiy bhaayay.  Tut(i) teeruth(i) disuntar(i) bhuvaiy ahunkaari, hoar(u) vudhayraiy haumein mul(u) laavniya.  3. Satgur sayvay ta mul(u) jaayay. Jeevut(u) muraiy Hur(i) siyoun chit(u) laayay. Hur(i) nirmul(u) Sach(u) maiyl(u) na laagaiy, Sach(i) laagaiy maiyl(u) guvaavniya.  4. Baajh(u) Guru haiy undh goobaara. Ugiyani undha, undh(u) undhaara.  Bishta kay keerray bishta kumaaveh(i), phir(i) bishta maaheh(i) puchaavniya. 5. Mooktay sayvay, mookta hovaiy. Haumein mumta Shabaday khovaiy. Undin(u) Hur(i) jeeo Sacha sayveen, pooraiy bhaag(i) Gur(u) paavniya. 6.  Aapay bukhshay mayl(i) milaayay.  Pooraiy Gur tay Naam(u) nidh(i) paayay. Sachaiy Naam(i) suda mun(u) Sach(u), Sacha sayvay dookh(u) guvaavniya. 7. Suda huzoor(i) door(i) na jaanhou. Gur Shabadi Hur(i) untar(i) puchhaanhou. Nanak Naam(i) milaiy vudiyayee, Pooraiy Gur tay paavniya.   8.11.12. 
Word Meaning: Khaani = sources of reproduction for all sorts of life.    Bani = creation.    Bherm(i) Bhoolaani = lost in misbeliefs.       1.      Hindu granths believe that there are four sources of reproduction – live births, eggs, vegetation, and conducive conditions for certain kinds of bacteria and small creatures to grow and flourish.  But Guru Nanak Sahib professes that there are many more ways for reproduction.  Sayti = with.      Sehjay = in a higher spiritual state.       Pause.       Munsa = wish; desire.     Laagaiy = engage; get connected.    Vutthoo = things.       2.         Ikk(u) = one Waheguru.   Doojaiy Bhaayay = in the love for the other (Maya).   Tut(u) = banks of a (holy) river.  Teeruth = holy places.   Disuntar(i) = foreign countries.    Mul(u) = filth (of evils).       3.      
Jeevut(u) Muraiy = kill desires (when alive).   Siyoun = with.   Nirmul = pure; clean.  4.Baajh(u) = without.  Undh Goobaara = complete darkness.  Ugiyani = ignorant.    Bishta = feces.    Keerray = worms.    Puchaavniya = burn; suffer.        5. Mooktay = one who is free from Maya (emancipated).     Mumta = love for the family (and the mundane).      Khovaiy = loses; gets rid of.     Undin(u) = day and night.     6. Bukhshay = forgives.     Nidh(i) = treasure.         7. Huzoor(i) = present close by.     Puchhaanhou = recognize.  Vudiyayee = respect.   8.
MEANING:  All the sources of reproduction of life, and everything else, have been created by You, oh Waheguru!  Those who do not realize this (and think that many things have been created by them, and not by God) suffer from misbeliefs. Waheguru’s Naam can be realized only by serving the Guru; no one has ever realized Naam without the shelter of the Guru.            1.           I am a sacrifice for those who focus their mind on Waheguru’s Naam.  The eternal Waheguru can be realized by worshipping the Guru who enables the devotee to attain the highest spiritual state when Waheguru becomes a permanent resident in his heart.         Pause.              If one serves the Satguru, he can acquire whatever he chooses.  Satgur is the provider (source of everything), but one can connect with Him only if one has perfect fortune (based on the deeds of one's previous life).          2. The human mind is filthy (with evil thoughts), and (for lack of faith), does not worship a single power (Waheguru).  In love with the other (Maya), his mind is very filthy (wishes to enjoy sensual pleasures).   He is full of conceit, and (in search for the fulfilment of his desires) he visits holy river banks, holy places, and even foreign countries, which, rather than doing any good to him spiritually, further boost his ego, and he gets more filth on his mind.           3.The filth of one’s mind can be washed away only by serving the Guru, whereby his mind gets connected with Waheguru, and he overcomes his evil desires.   Waheguru is pure Truth, and is perfectly clean because Truth is never filthy; so, one can also wash away his filth by connecting with the Pure Waheguru.               4. It is complete darkness without the guidance of the Guru.  The ignorant (follower of his mind) is blind to Truth and is in complete darkness.  The followers of their mind are like the worms in feces, which live in filth, enjoy filth, and earn filth (bad qualities) and suffer there.           5.              (It is believed that a devotee becomes just like his deity), So if one serves (Waheguru), which is above the effects of Maya, the devotee will also 
achieve that spiritual level (above the effects of Maya) where he will get rid of his selfishness, ego, and his affection for the family. A perfectly lucky person comes to the shelter of the Guru and serves Waheguru day and night.            6. Waheguru, Himself, forgives his devotees and connects them to the Guru to be connected with Him.   The devotees acquire Waheguru’s Naam, which is like acquiring all the nine mythical treasures, by the grace of the Perfect Guru.  A devotee, who acquires the eternal Naam, has the eternal Waheguru in his mind all the time, whom he serves, and thus gets rid of his misery.           7. Waheguru is always with you (in your heart); do not think that He is far away from you, oh my friend.  The only thing you need to do is to recognize Him inside you by acting on the Guru’s Shabad. A man gets resect if he has Waheguru’s Naam in his heart; but Waheguru can be acquired only by the grace of the Perfect Guru, oh Nanak.   8.11.12
