(ਅੰਗ 118)॥       ਹਰਿ ਚੇਤਹੁ ਅੰਤਿ ਹੋਇ ਸਖਾਈ॥ ਹਰਿ ਅਗਮੁ ਅਗੋਚਰੁ ਅਨਾਥੁ ਅਜੋਨੀ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਭਾਇ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਹਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਜੀਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਆਪੁ ਨਿਵਾਰਣਿਆ॥ ਆਪੁ ਗਵਾਏ ਤਾ ਹਰਿ ਪਾਏ ਹਰਿ ਸਿਉ ਸਹਜਿ ਸਮਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਪੂਰਬਿ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਸੁ ਕਰਮੁ ਕਮਾਇਆ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਸੇਵਿ ਸਦਾ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਾਇਆ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਭਾਗਾ ਗੁਰੁ ਪਾਈਐ ਨਾਹੀ ਸਬਦੈ ਮੇਲਿ ਮਿਲਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 2॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਅਲਿਪਤੁ ਰਹੈ ਸੰਸਾਰੇ॥ ਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਤਕੀਐ ਨਾਮਿ ਅਧਾਰੇ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਜੋਰੁ ਕਰੇ ਕਿਆ ਤਿਸ ਨੋ ਆਪੇ ਖਪਿ ਦੁਖੁ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 3॥ ਮਨਮੁਖਿ ਅੰਧੇ ਸੁਧਿ ਨ ਕਾਈ॥ ਆਤਮਘਾਤੀ ਹੈ ਜਗਤ ਕਸਾਈ॥ ਨਿੰਦਾ ਕਰਿ ਕਰਿ ਬਹੁ ਭਾਰੁ ਉਠਾਵੈ ਬਿਨੁ ਮਜੂਰੀ ਭਾਰੁ ਪਹੁਚਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 4॥ ਇਹੁ ਜਗੁ ਵਾੜੀ ਮੇਰਾ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਮਾਲੀ॥ ਸਦਾ ਸਮਾਲੇ ਕੋ ਨਾਹੀ ਖਾਲੀ॥ ਜੇਹੀ ਵਾਸਨਾ ਪਾਏ ਤੇਹੀ ਵਰਤੈ ਵਾਸੂ ਵਾਸੁ ਜਣਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 5॥ ਮਨਮੁਖੁ ਰੋਗੀ ਹੈ ਸੰਸਾਰਾ॥ ਸੁਖਦਾਤਾ ਵਿਸਰਿਆ ਅਗਮ ਅਪਾਰਾ॥ ਦੁਖੀਏ ਨਿਤਿ ਫਿਰਹਿ ਬਿਲਲਾਦੇ ਬਿਨੁ ਗੁਰ ਸਾਤਿ ਨ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 6॥ ਜਿਨਿ ਕੀਤੇ ਸੋਈ ਬਿਧਿ ਜਾਣੈ॥ ਆਪਿ ਕਰੇ ਤਾ ਹੁਕਮਿ ਪਛਾਣੈ॥ ਜੇਹਾ ਅੰਦਰਿ ਪਾਏ ਤੇਹਾ ਵਰਤੈ ਆਪੇ ਬਾਹਰਿ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 7॥ ਤਿਸੁ ਬਾਝਹੁ ਸਚੇ ਮੈ ਹੋਰੁ ਨ ਕੋਈ॥ ਜਿਸੁ ਲਾਇ ਲਏ ਸੋ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਹੋਈ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮੁ ਵਸੈ ਘਟ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਜਿਸੁ ਦੇਵੈ ਸੋ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 8॥14॥15॥
Majh Mehla 3:  Satgur Saachi sikh soonaayee. 
(PAGE 118):            Hur(i) chaytou unt(i) hoyay sukhaayee. Hur(i) agumm(u) agoachar(u) anaath(u) ajoni, Satgur(i) kaiy bhaayay paavniya. 1. Huoun vaari jeeo vaari, aap(u) nivaarniya. Aap(u) guvaayay ta Hur(i) paayay, Hur siyoun sehj(i) smaavniya. 1. Ruhaou. Poorub(i) likhiya su kerm kumaaya. Satgur(u) sayv(i) suda sookh(u) paaya. Bin(u) bhaagaan Gur(u) paayiyaiy naahi, Shabadaiy mayl(i) milaavniya. 2.  Gurmukh(i) aliput(u) ruhaiy sunsaaray.  Gur kaiy tukiyaiy Naam(i) adhaaray. Gurmukh(i) zoar(u) kuray kiya tis nu, aapay khup(i) dookh(u) paavniya. 3. Munmukh(i) undhay soodh(i) na kaayee. Atumghaati haiy jugut ksaayee. Ninda kur(i) kur(i),  buhou bhaar(u) ootthaavaiy, bin mujoori bhaar(u) puhouchaavniya.  4. Eho jug(u) vaarri mayra Prubh(u) maali.  Suda smaalay ko naahi khaali.  Jayhi vaasna paayay tayhi vertaiy, vaasoo vaas(u) junaavniya.  5. Munmukh(u) roagi haiy sunsaara. Sookhdaata visriya Agumm Apaara.  Dookhiyay nit(i) phireh(i) bil-laanday, bin(u) gur shaant(i) na paavniya.  6. Jin(i) keetay soyee bidh(i) jaanaiy. Aap(i) kuray ta Huqam(i) puchhaanaiy. Jayha undar(i) paayay tayha vertaiy, aapay baahur(i) paavniya. 7. Tis(u) baajhoun Sachay mein hoar(u) na koyee.  Jis(u) laayay luyay so nirmul(u) hoyee. Nanak Naam(u) vusaiy ghut untar(i) jis(u) dayvaiy so paavniya.  8.14.15. 
Word Meaning:  Saachi = eternal.  Sikh = advice (teaching).   Chaytoh = remember (keep in mind).   Sukhaayee = friend; supporter.   Agumm =   beyond (physical) reach.     Agoachar(u) = incomprehensible.   Anaath = orphan (because nobody creates Him).   Ajoni = who does not take any life form.     Bhaayay = love for.       1.
Aap(u) = self; selfishness.    Nivaarniya = get rid of.     Sehj(i) = spiritual state above Maya.       Pause.      Poorub(i) Likhiya = pre-written destiny (based on the deeds of previous lives).    Kerm = deeds.    Shabadaiy Maiyl(i) = by connecting with the Guru’s Shabad.        2.          Aliput(u) = disconnected (with Maya).   Tukiyaiy = Adhaaray = with the support of (on the basis of).      Zoar(u) = (external) force or pressure.    Khup(i) = much effort without any gain; meet frustration; feel humiliation.           3.
Soodh(i) = awareness; knowledge.   Atumghaati = suicidal.    Ksaayee = butcher.  
Ninda = malicious criticism; vilification.    Mujoori = fare charges.          4.     
Vaarri = garden.   Maali = gardener.     Smaalay = looks after.    Vaasna = desire.  
Vertaiy = operates.   Vaasoo = the outcome of the desired deeds.          5.
Visriya = is forgotten.   Apaara = limitless.    Bil-laanday = crying (complaining) because of unfulfilled desires.    Shaant(i) = peace of mind.           6. 
Keetay = created.     Bidh(i) = situation; condition.   Aap(i) Kuray = (when) Waheguru Himself guides.       Baahur(i) = outside world (world of Maya).       7.
Baajhoun = without.     Nirmul(u) = pure.    Ghut = heart.        8.
MEANING:  Satguru has delivered the eternal Advice (Teaching) that remember Waheguru all the time, which will be your friend (supporter) in the end, oh my friends!   Waheguru is physically unreachable, incomprehensible, an orphan (because nobody creates Him), and never takes any life form.  Such a Waheguru can be realized only with love for Satgur(u).            1.            I offer my life to those who get rid of their selfishness.  One can realize Waheguru only by getting rid of one's selfishness and becoming “one” with Waheguru in the high spiritual state (above Maya).       Pause.
One performs only those deeds for which his character has been prewritten (based on the deeds of his previous lives) by Waheguru.   Those who serve (obey) Satguru are always blissful.  However, one can connect with the Guru only with great fortune. One achieves union with God only by obeying the Shabad of the Guru.         2.
With the support of the Guru and Waheguru’s Naam, the devotee of the Guru remains disconnected from Maya in the world.   Nobody can force the devotee of the Guru to yield to his wishes (make him do something that the devotee does not like); on the contrary, the aggressor suffers humiliation (as a result of his failure).        3.
The blind follower of his mind has no awareness; actually, he is a butcher who commits (spiritual) suicide.  With malicious criticism of others, he adds to his own burden; he is like a labourer who carries the burden of others for free and gets no payment.          4.
This world is like a garden, with God as the gardener who always looks after every plant (human being), and none is left without His care.  However, like every plant is different in character (different flowers, different vegetables, fruits, and so on), He puts in different characters in each human being. Human beings do things according to their individual character, and are recognized as such by their deeds.           5.
Because he has forgotten the inaccessible and the limitless source of comforts, Waheguru, the follower of his mind in the world is (mentally) sick.  He finds no peace of mind without the Guru, and roams around (spends his life) complaining and crying in suffering.            6.           Waheguru, who has created the world, knows the mental state of the people (and knows how to deal with that).   The individual, guided by Waheguru, realizes His Huqam (and connects with Him).  However, individuals are guided by their behaviour and traits as designed by Waheguru, who Himself has created an attraction for Maya among living beings.       7.
I do not believe in anybody else, oh Waheguru!   An individual, whom You motivate to recite Your Naam, gets purified, and Your Naam stays in his mind. But only those do so whom Waheguru is kind to, oh Nanak!      8.14.15.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਨਾਮੁ ਮੰਨਿ ਵਸਾਏ॥ ਹਉਮੈ ਮੇਰਾ ਸਭੁ ਦੁਖੁ ਗਵਾਏ॥ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਬਾਣੀ ਸਦਾ ਸਲਾਹੇ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤਿ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਹਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਜੀਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਬਾਣੀ ਮੰਨਿ ਵਸਾਵਣਿਆ॥ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਬਾਣੀ ਮੰਨਿ ਵਸਾਏ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਧਿਆਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਬੋਲੈ ਸਦਾ ਮੁਖਿ ਵੈਣੀ॥ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਵੇਖੈ ਪਰਖੈ ਸਦਾ ਨੈਣੀ॥ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਕਥਾ ਕਹੈ ਦਿਨੁ ਰਾਤੀ ਅਵਰਾ ਆਖਿ ਸੁਨਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 2॥ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਰੰਗਿ ਰਤਾ ਲਿਵ ਲਾਏ॥ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਗੁਰ ਪਰਸਾਦੀ ਪਾਏ॥ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਰਸਨਾ ਬੋਲੈ ਦਿਨੁ ਰਾਤੀ ਮਨਿ ਤਨਿ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਪੀਆਵਣਿਆ॥3॥ ਸੋ ਕਿਛੁ ਕਰੈ ਜੁ ਚਿਤਿ ਨ ਹੋਈ॥ ਤਿਸ ਦਾ ਹੁਕਮੁ ਮੇਟਿ ਨ ਸਕੈ ਕੋਈ॥ ਹੁਕਮੈ ਵਰਤੈ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਬਾਣੀ ਹੁਕਮੈ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਪੀਆਵਣਿਆ॥ 4॥ ਅਜਬ ਕੰਮ ਕਰਤੇ ਹਰਿ ਕੇਰੇ॥ ਇਹੁ ਮਨੁ ਭੂਲਾ ਜਾਦਾ ਫੇਰੇ॥ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਬਾਣੀ ਸਿਉ ਚਿਤੁ ਲਾਏ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਸਬਦਿ ਵਜਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 5॥
