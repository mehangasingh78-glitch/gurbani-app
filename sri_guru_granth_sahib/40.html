(ਅੰਗ 39)॥    ਤਿਨ ਕੀ ਸੇਵਾ ਧਰਮਰਾਇ ਕਰੈ ਧੰਨੁ ਸਵਾਰਣਹਾਰੁ॥ 2॥ ਮਨ ਕੇ ਬਿਕਾਰ ਮਨਹਿ ਤਜੈ ਮਨਿ ਚੁਕੈ ਮੋਹੁ ਅਭਿਮਾਨੁ॥ ਆਤਮਰਾਮੁ ਪਛਾਣਿਆ ਸਹਜੇ ਨਾਮਿ ਸਮਾਨੁ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਮੁਕਤਿ ਨ ਪਾਈਐ ਮਨਮੁਖਿ ਫਿਰੇ ਦਿਵਾਨੁ॥ ਸਬਦੁ ਨ ਚੀਨੈ ਕਥਨੀ ਬਦਨੀ ਕਰੇ ਬਿਖਿਆ ਮਾਹਿ ਸਮਾਨੁ॥ 3॥ ਸਭੁ ਕਿਛੁ ਆਪੇ ਆਪਿ ਹੈ ਦੂਜਾ ਅਵਰੁ ਨ ਕੋਇ॥ ਜਿਉ ਬੋਲਾਏ ਤਿਉ ਬੋਲੀਐ ਜਾ ਆਪਿ ਬੁਲਾਏ ਸੋਇ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਬਾਣੀ ਬ੍ਰਹਮੁ ਹੈ ਸਬਦਿ ਮਿਲਾਵਾ ਹੋਇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮੁ ਸਮਾਲਿ ਤੂ ਜਿਤੁ ਸੇਵਿਐ ਸੁਖੁ ਹੋਇ॥ 4॥30॥63॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 3: Hur(i) Ji Sacha Sach(u) Tu, subh(u) kichh(u) tayraiy cheeraiy. Lukh chauraasee tursday firay, bin(u) Gur bhaytay Peeraiy.  Hur(i) Jeo bukhshay bukhsh(i) luyay, sookh suda sreeraiy. Gur persadi sayv kureen, Sach(u) gehir gumbheeraiy. 1. Mun mayray, Naam(i) ruttay sookh(u) hoyay. Gurmutee Naam(u) slaheeyaiy, dooja uver(u) na koyay. 1. Ruhaou.   Dherm Raayay nu huqam haiy, beh(i) Sacha dherm(u) beechaar(i). Doojay bhaayay doosht(u) aatma, ohou tayri surkaar. Adhiaatmi Hur(i) goontaas(u) mun(i), jupeh(i) Aik(u) Mooraar(i).
(PAGE 39):  Tin kee sayva Dherm Rayan kuraiy dhunn(u) Svaarunhaar(u). 2. Mun kay bikaar muneh(I) tujaiy, mun(i) chookaiy moh(u) abhimaan(u).  Aatamraam(u) puchhaniya, sehjay Naam(i) smaan(u).  Bin(u) Satgur mookt(i) na paayiyaiy munmukh(i) firay divaan(u). Shabad(u) na cheenaiy, kuthni budni kuray, bikhiya maaheh smaan(u). 3. Subh(u) kichh(u) aapay aap(i) haiy, dooja uver(u) na koyay. Jiyoun bolaayay tiyoun boliyaiy, ja Aap(i) bolaayay soyay. Gurmukh(i) Bani Braham(u) haiy, Shabad(i) milaava hoyay. Nanak Naam(u) smaal(i) tu, jit(u) sayiyaiy sookh(u) hoyay. 4.30.63.
Word Meaning:  Sacha = eternal.   Cheeraiy = in His Huqam (Command).  Lukh Chauraasee = 8.4 million (according to Hindu belief, there are 8.4 million life forms).  Tursday = long for; wish for badly.   Bin(u) = without.    Bhaytay = have a meeting with.  Bukhshay = endows.    Bukhsh(i) = forgive.  Gehir = deep (unfathomable).   Gumbheeraiy = never changing; unemotional.     1.        Ruttay = imbued.   Gurmuti = taking Guru’s advice.  Uver(u) = else.  Pause.               Dherm raayay = (mythical) angel of justice.  Doosht(u) = sinner; merciless.   Surkaar = subject.     Adhiaatmi = devotees.   Goontaas(u) =   treasure of noble qualities. Mooraar(i) = another name for Waheguru (the killer of the demon Mooraar - Krishna). Dhunn(u) = worthy of respect.   Svaarunhaar = one who embellishes; grooms (Waheguru).      2.             Bikaar = ignoble deeds or thoughts.   Tujaiy = quit.   Chookaiy = ends.   Moh(u) = attachment.  Aatamraam(u) = God.   Sehjay = blissful state of mind.    Smaan(u) = merge with; union.     Mookt(i) = freedom from the bonds of Maya.   Divaan(u) = with lost mind; insane.    Cheenaiy = recognize.  Kuthni Budni = fruitless talk.  Bikhiya = poisonous Maya.
3.       Braham(u) = (about) Waheguru.   Smaal(i) = take care; remember always.         4.
MEANING:  One enjoys the real comfort (bliss) when his mind is imbued in the Naam of Waheguru, oh my mind.  However, one can sing praise of the Naam (Waheguru) by taking refuge only in the Guru, and nobody else’s.        Pause.         You are the only eternal Truth, and everybody is under Your Huqam (Command), oh my Waheguru. But nobody can realize You without taking the Guru’s shelter, and for that reason, even though living beings of 8.4 million life forms are dying to see You, they are not successful.  At the same time, when Waheguru forgives our shortcomings and grants us His sight (connects us with Him), we enjoy all the comforts.  However, the unfathomable and never-changing God can be served (His Naam is enshrined in the heart) only by the grace of the Guru.         1.
Waheguru has ordered the Angel of justice to deliver pure justice (as per the Divine Principles). The lovers of Maya are sinners and are the Angel’s subjects (punishable).   (On the other hand), The devotees always keep Waheguru, which is the treasure of noble qualities, in mind, and worship Him alone.  Waheguru, which embellishes his devotees with noble qualities, is great and worthy of worship; His embellished devotees are accorded respect by the Angel of justice.           2            When one gets rid of his desires, of his worldly attachments, and his conceit, he recognizes (realizes) Waheguru, and gets imbued in His Naam in the blissful state of mind.    (On the other hand), The follower of his mind does not seek the Guru’s refuge to achieve freedom from Maya, and spends his life like an insane person.  He does not realize the importance of the Guru’s Shabad, and engrossed in Maya, he engages in fruitless conversations.           3.           God Himself controls everything and has no rival.  We speak (and act) as He wishes us to do, and He Himself makes us sing His praise.  The routine conversation of a devotee is about God and His praise.  He connects us with Him by Guru’s Shabad.  So, foster Waheguru’s Naam in your heart, because one achieves comfort (blissfulness) by serving Him alone, oh Nanak.    4.30.63.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ ਜਗਿ ਹਉਮੈ ਮੈਲੁ ਦੁਖੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਮਲੁ ਲਾਗੀ ਦੂਜੈ ਭਾਇ॥ ਮਲੁ ਧੋਤੀ ਕਿਵੈ ਨ ਉਤਰੈ ਜੇ ਸਉ ਤੀਰਥ ਨਾਇ॥ ਬਹੁ ਬਿਧਿ ਕਰਮ ਕਮਾਵਦੇ ਦੂਣੀ ਮਲੁ ਲਾਗੀ ਆਇ॥ ਪੜਿਐ ਮੈਲੁ ਨ ਉਤਰੈ ਪੂਛਹੁ ਗਿਆਨੀਆ ਜਾਇ॥1॥ ਮਨ ਮੇਰੇ ਗੁਰ ਸਰਣਿ ਆਵੈ ਤਾ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਹੋਇ॥ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਕਰਿ ਥਕੇ ਮੈਲੁ ਨ ਸਕੇ ਧੋਇ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਮਨਿ ਮੈਲੈ ਭਗਤਿ ਨ ਹੋਵਈ ਨਾਮੁ ਨ ਪਾਇਆ ਜਾਇ॥ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਮੈਲੈ ਮੈਲੈ ਮੁਏ ਜਾਸਨਿ ਪਤਿ ਗਵਾਇ॥ ਗੁਰ ਪਰਸਾਦੀ ਮਨਿ ਵਸੈ ਮਲੁ ਹਉਮੈ ਜਾਇ ਸਮਾਇ॥ ਜਿਉ ਅੰਧੇਰੈ ਦੀਪਕੁ ਬਾਲੀਐ ਤਿਉ ਗੁਰ ਗਿਆਨਿ ਅਗਿਆਨੁ ਤਜਾਇ॥ 2॥ ਹਮ ਕੀਆ ਹਮ ਕਰਹਗੇ ਹਮ ਮੂਰਖ ਗਾਵਾਰ॥ ਕਰਣੈ ਵਾਲਾ ਵਿਸਰਿਆ ਦੂਜੈ ਭਾਇ ਪਿਆਰੁ॥ ਮਾਇਆ ਜੇਵਡੁ ਦੁਖੁ ਨਹੀ ਸਭ ਭਵਿ ਥਕੇ ਸੰਸਾਰੁ॥ ਗੁਰਮਤੀ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਾਈਐ ਸਚੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਉਰਧਾਰਿ॥ 3॥ ਜਿਸ ਨੋ ਮੇਲੇ ਸੋ ਮਿਲੈ ਹਉ ਤਿਸੁ ਬਲਿਹਾਰੈ ਜਾਉ॥ ਏ ਮਨ ਭਗਤੀ ਰਤਿਆ ਸਚੁ ਬਾਣੀ ਨਿਜ ਥਾਉ॥ ਮਨਿ ਰਤੇ ਜਿਹਵਾ ਰਤੀ ਹਰਿ ਗੁਣ ਸਚੇ ਗਾਉ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮੁ ਨ ਵੀਸਰੈ ਸਚੇ ਮਾਹਿ ਸਮਾਉ॥4॥33॥31॥64॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 3:  Jug(i) haumein maiyl(u) dookh(u) paaya, mul(u) laagi doojaiy bhaayay. Mul(u) dhoti kivaiy na ootraiy, jay suou teeruth naayay. Buhou bidh(i) kerm kmaanvday, dooni mul(u) laagi aayay. Purriyaiyn maiyl(u) na ootraiy, poochhou giyaniyaan jaayay. 1. Mun mayray, Gur shern(i) aavaiy ta nirmul(u) hoyay. Munmukh(i) Hur(i) Hur(i) kur(i) thukkay, maiyl(u) na sukay dhoyay. 1. Ruhaou.   Mun(i) maiylaiy bhugut(i) na hovyee Naam(u) na paaya jaayay. Munmukh maiylaiy, maiylaiy mooyay, jaasun(i) put(i) guvaayay. Gur Persaadi mun(i) vusaiy, mul(u) haumein jaayay, smaayay. Jiyoun undhayraiy deepuk(u) baaliyaiy tiyoun Gur Giyaan(i) ugiyaan(u) tujaayay. 2. Hum keeya, hum kurehngay, hum moorakh gavaar. Kurnaiy vaala vissriya, doojaiy bhaayay piyaar(u).  Maya jayvud dookh(u) nuhee, subh bhuv(i) thukkay sunsaar(u).  Gurmutee sookh(u) paayiyaiy, Sach(u) Naam(u) oordhaar(i).  3.  Jis nu maylay so milaiy, houn tis(u) bulihaaraiy jaoun. Eh mun bhugti ruttiya Sach(u) Bani nij thaoun. Mun(i) ruttay jihva rutti, Hur(i) goon Sachay gaoun. Nanak Naam(u) na visraiy Sachay maahay smaaoun.  4.33.31.64.
Word Meaning:   Jug(i) = world.    Maiyl(u) = filth.   Doojaiy Bhaayay = love for the other (Maya).  Mul(u) = Maiyl(u) = filth.     Buhou Bidh(i) = in many various ways.  Kerm = ritualistic religious deeds.        1.         Gur shern(i) = in the shelter of the Guru.   Nirmul(u) = clean; pure.    Pause.         Jaasun(i) = will leave.  Put(i) = dignity.    Mun(i) Vusaiy = (Waheguru) takes residence in the heart (of the devotee).  Smaayay = merges; becomes one with.  Jioun = just as.   Tioun = like that.  Ugiyaan = lack of knowledge.   Tujaayay = quits.      2.
Gavaar = unrefined; foolish.   Oordhaar(i) = acquire in mind.        3.
Bulihaaraiy = sacrifice life.  Ruttiya = imbued.  Nij = personal.   Thaoun = place (at peace).   Jihva = tongue.   Smaaoun = merge in; get absorbed.          4.
MEANING:  The only way to cleanse your mind is to take the Guru’s protection, oh my mind.  The followers of their mind exhaust themselves by uttering Waheguru’s Naam, but only to give the false impression of their religiosity to the people. They cannot wash away the filth of their mind by simply pretending to be what they really are not.        Pause.
The world is suffering from the filth of selfishness and from the love for Maya. The filth of haumein does not go away, no matter how many dips at holy places one takes. To wash away the filth, people perform various ritualistic religious deeds, but they do not realize that they actually add twice the filth they believe they washed away, because such deeds further boost their ego.  The learned (enlightened) people will also tell you that this filth does not go away simply by reading religious books.            1.          (It is important to cleanse your mind because) One cannot worship Waheguru with a filthy mind or realize Him.  The followers of their mind are filthy; they lose their dignity, and they die filthy. (However), By Guru’s grace, the filth of haumein can be washed away, and the person becomes “one” with Waheguru.  Just as a lamp is lighted to dispel darkness, the wisdom of a devotee, who is connected to Waheguru, dispels the darkness of ignorance.             2.         In his love for Maya, the ignorant forgets Waheguru’s Naam, and the idiot boasts of his achievements and takes credit for everything.  There is no other discomfort as big as worrying about Maya, because the lover of Maya exhausts himself while pursuing it around the world.  The real comfort is attained when one enshrines Waheguru’s Naam in his heart by taking shelter of the Guru.        3.
Only those individuals are connected with Waheguru whom He connects Himself to; I would gladly sacrifice my life for such lucky people. When the mind is imbued with Waheguru’s Naam, the eternal Shabad of the Guru takes residence in the heart, which attains peace. By singing Waheguru’s praise, one’s mind and tongue get imbued with Waheguru’s Naam.  One should never forget Waheguru so as to stay connected with Him, oh Nanak!   4.33.31.64.
