ਰਾਗੁ ਮਾਝ ਚਉਪਦੇ ਘਰੁ 1 ਮਹਲਾ 4      
                 RAAG(u) MAJH CHAUPDAY GHUR 1 MEHLA  4

(ਅੰਗ 94)॥           ੴ ਸਤਿਨਾਮੁ ਕਰਤਾ ਪੁਰਖੁ ਨਿਰਭਉ ਨਿਰਵੈਰੁ ਅਕਾਲ
ਮੂਰਤਿ ਅਜੂਨੀ ਸੈਭੰ ਗੁਰ ਪ੍ਰਸਾਦਿ॥
ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਮੈ ਹਰਿ ਮਨਿ ਭਾਇਆ॥ ਵਡਭਾਗੀ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਧਿਆਇਆ॥ ਗੁਰਿ ਪੂਰੈ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮ ਸਿਧਿ ਪਾਈ ਕੋ ਵਿਰਲਾ ਗੁਰਮਤਿ ਚਲੈ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਮੈ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਖਰਚੁ ਲਇਆ ਬੰਨਿ ਪਲੈ॥ ਮੇਰਾ ਪ੍ਰਾਣ ਸਖਾਈ ਸਦਾ ਨਾਲਿ ਚਲੈ॥ ਗੁਰਿ ਪੂਰੈ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਦਿੜਾਇਆ ਹਰਿ ਨਿਹਚਲੁ ਹਰਿ ਧਨੁ ਪਲੈ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਸਜਣੁ ਮੇਰਾ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਮੁ ਰਾਇਆ॥ ਕੋਈ ਆਣਿ ਮਿਲਾਵੈ ਮੇਰੇ ਪ੍ਰਾਣ ਜੀਵਾਇਆ॥ ਹਉ ਰਹਿ ਨ ਸਕਾ ਬਿਨੁ ਦੇਖੇ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਮਾ ਮੈ ਨੀਰੁ ਵਹੈ ਵਹਿ ਚਲੈ ਜੀਉ॥ 3॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਮਿਤ੍ਰੁ ਮੇਰਾ ਬਾਲ ਸਖਾਈ॥ ਹਉ ਰਹਿ ਨ ਸਕਾ ਬਿਨੁ ਦੇਖੇ ਮੇਰੀ ਮਾਈ॥ ਹਰਿ ਜੀਉ ਕ੍ਰਿਪਾ ਕਰਹੁ ਗੁਰੁ ਮੇਲਹੁ ਜਨ ਨਾਨਕ ਹਰਿ ਧਨੁ ਪਲੈ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥1॥
(PAGE 94): Raag(u) Majh Chaupuday Ghur(u) 1, Mehla 4:  Ek Onkar Sat(i) Naam(u) Kerta Purakh(u) Nirbhaou Nirvair(u) Akal Moorat(i) Ajooni Saibhun(g) Gur Persad(i)                           
Hur(i) Hur(i) Naam(u) mein Hur(i) mun(i) bhaaya. Vudbhaageen Hur(i) Naam(u) dhiyaya. Gur(i) Pooraiy Hur(i) Naam sidh(i) paayee, ko virla Gurmat(i) chullaiy jeeo. 1. Mein Hur(i) Hur(i) khurch(u) luyya bunnh(i) pullaiy. Mayra Praan Sukhayee suda naal(i) chullaiy. Gur(i) Pooraiy Hur(i) Naam(u) dirraya, Hur(i) nihchul(u) Hur(i) dhun(u) pullaiy jeeo. 2. Hur(i) Hur(i) sujjan(u) mayra preetum(u) Raaya. Koyee aan(i) milaavaiy myaray praan jeevaaya. Huoun reh(i) na sukaan bin(u) daykhay Preetuma, mein neer(u) vuhaiy veh(i) chullaiy jeeo. 3. Satgur mittar mayra baal sukhayee. Huoun reh(i) na sukaan bin(u) daykhay mayri maayee. Hur(I) Jeeo kirpa kurhou Gur(u) maylhou, jun Nanak Hur(i) dhun(u) pullaiy jeeo.  4.
Word Meaning:  Chaupuday = a Shabad with four stanzas. 
Sat = eternal.   Naam(u) = Waheguru’s existence (Waheguru and His Naam are synonymous). (See the first sentence of the following Shabad); His Laws.   Bhaaya = cherish; revere.   Vudbhaageen = highly lucky.  Sidh(i) = success; perfection.   Virla = rare.  Gurmat(i) = Guru’s advice.    1.     Khurch(u) = expenses.  Bunnh(i) Pullaiy = garnered; gathered.   Sukhayee = close friend.   Dirraya = reinforced.  Nihchul = everlasting.      2.      Preetum(u) = dear.   Raaya = royal; king.  Praan Jeevaaya = basis of (spiritual) life.  Neer(u) = water (tears).  Vuhaiy = flows.     3.       Baal sukhayee = friend from childhood (life-long friend).     4.
NOTE:  Jeeo is simply an expression of respect; it is used very frequently in the Shabads of this Raag.
MEANING:  There is only one Supreme Being and His existence is eternal; He is a living power (Purakh); the creator of the whole universe and is pervading it; He is fearless (is above all the Laws of nature); He bears no enmity with anyone (his justice is perfect); He is not affected by time (always stays the same). He never takes any life form; is Self-Illuminating and is realized only through the Guru (The Guru is a manifestation of God).

I cherish Waheguru and His Naam, but only the very lucky recite His Naam. One can be successful in reciting Waheguru’s Naam (connecting with Him) only by taking the shelter of the Perfect Guru. But, again, only a rare devotee follows the Guru’s advice.       1. 
I have garnered Waheguru’s Naam as the expenses for the journey of my life.  Waheguru’s Naam is my life-long friend and will always be with me (even in the next world).  The Perfect Guru has reinforced Waheguru’s Naam in me, which has become my everlasting possession.  2.               Waheguru King is my darling friend; I am looking for some holy man (Guru) who can connect me with Him, who sustains my (spiritual) life.  I cannot survive without “seeing” 
Him and my tears are flowing uncontrollably (in His separation).         3.
Satguru is also my lifelong friend. I cannot live without “seeing” Him, oh my mother! Please, Waheguru, be kind and connect me with the Guru so that I may acquire Waheguru’s Naam-wealth, oh Nanak.   4.1.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 4॥ ਮਧੁਸੂਦਨ ਮੇਰੇ ਮਨ ਤਨ ਪ੍ਰਾਨਾ॥ ਹਉ ਹਰਿ ਬਿਨੁ ਦੂਜਾ ਅਵਰੁ ਨ ਜਾਨਾ॥ ਕੋਈ ਸਜਣੁ ਸੰਤੁ ਮਿਲੈ ਵਡਭਾਗੀ ਮੈ ਹਰਿ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਪਿਆਰਾ ਦਸੈ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਹਉ ਮਨੁ ਤਨੁ ਖੋਜੀ ਭਾਲਿ ਭਾਲਾਈ॥ ਕਿਉ ਪਿਆਰਾ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਮੁ ਮਿਲੈ ਮੇਰੀ ਮਾਈ॥ ਮਿਲਿ ਸਤਸੰਗਤਿ ਖੋਜੁ ਦਸਾਈ ਵਿਚਿ ਸੰਗਤਿ ਹਰਿ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਵਸੈ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਮੇਰਾ ਪਿਆਰਾ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਮੁ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਰਖਵਾਲਾ॥ ਹਮ ਬਾਰਿਕ ਦੀਨ ਕਰਹੁ ਪ੍ਰਤਿਪਾਲਾ॥ ਮੇਰਾ ਮਾਤ ਪਿਤਾ ਗੁਰ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਪੂਰਾ ਗੁਰ ਜਲ ਮਿਲਿ ਕਮਲੁ ਵਿਗਸੈ ਜੀਉ॥3॥ ਮੈ ਬਿਨੁ ਗੁਰੁ ਦੇਖੇ ਨੀਦ ਨ ਆਵੈ॥ ਮੇਰੇ ਮਨਿ ਤਨਿ ਵੇਦਨੁ ਗੁਰ ਬਿਰਹੁ ਲਗਾਵੈ॥ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਦਇਆ ਕਰਹੁ ਗੁਰੁ ਮੇਲਹੁ ਜਨ ਨਾਨਕ ਗੁਰੁ ਮਿਲਿ ਰਹਸੈ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥2॥
Majh Mehla 4:  Madhusoodun mayray mun tun praana. Huoun Hur(i) bin(u) dooja uver(u) na jaana. Koyee sujjan(u) Sunt(u) milaiy vudbhaageen, mein Hur(i) Prubh(u) piyara dussaiy jeeo.  1. Huoun mun(u) tun(u) khojeen bhaal(i) bhulayeen. Kiyoun piyara preetum(u) milaiy mayri maayee. Mil Sutsungat(i) khoj(u) dusayeen, vich(i) sungat(i) Hur(i) Prubh(u) vussaiy jeeo. 2. Mayra piyara preetum(u) Satgur(u) rukhvaala. Hum baarik deen, kurhou pritpaala. Mayra maat pita Gur Satgur Poora, Gur jul mil(i) kumal(u) vigsaiy jeeo. 3.  Mein bin(u) Gur daykhay neend na aavaiy. Mayray mun(i) tun(i) vaydun(u) Gur birhoun lugaavaiy. Hur(i) Hur(i) duyya kurhou Gur(u) maylhou, jun Nanak Gur(u) mil(i) rehsaiy jeeo.  4.2.
Word Meaning: Madhusoodun = Waheguru (a name for Krishna because he killed a demon named Madhu).  Uver(u) = other; else.  Sujjan = friend.  Sunt(u) = saint.  1.
Khojeen = search for.    Bhaal(i) Bhulayeen = search and have searched.    Kiyoun = how?    Khoj(u) dusayeen = I ask for His location.       2.      Rukhvaala = protector; guard.     Baarik = children.   Deen = powerless; ignorant.  Kumal(u) = lotus flower, which thrives in water.     Vigsaiy = blooms.     3.       Vaydun = pain.     Birhoun = pain of separation.   Rehsaiy = feels blissful.   4.
MEANING: Waheguru is my mind, body, and soul.  I do not acknowledge anybody else other than my Waheguru. I wish I were very lucky so as to meet a holy friend who could advise me on how to reach Waheguru.        1.        I search for Him in my mind and body, and beg others to search Him for me.  How can I find my dear and loving (Waheguru), oh my mother!   I am advised to go to the congregation of holy devotees and seek their advice, as Waheguru is believed to be present there.           2.           My dear and loving Satguru is my guard.  We are Your ignorant and powerless children; please nourish us, oh Satguru!  My Perfect Satguru is my mother and my father; He is like a pool of water, and we are like lotus flowers that thrive in that water.         3.          I cannot sleep without “seeing” the Satguru. His separation is becoming painful for my mind and my body.  Please, Waheguru, connect me with the Guru - meeting with whom my mind blooms, oh Nanak.     4.2.
