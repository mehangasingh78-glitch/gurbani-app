(ਅੰਗ 99)॥          ਜੀਇ ਸਮਾਲੀ ਤਾ ਸਭੁ ਦੁਖੁ ਲਥਾ॥ ਚਿੰਤਾ ਰੋਗੁ ਗਈ ਹਉ ਪੀੜਾ ਆਪਿ ਕਰੇ ਪ੍ਰਤਿਪਾਲਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਬਾਰਿਕ ਵਾਗੀ ਹਉ ਸਭ ਕਿਛੁ ਮੰਗਾ॥ ਦੇਦੇ ਤੋਟਿ ਨਾਹੀ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਰੰਗਾ॥ ਪੈਰੀ ਪੈ ਪੈ ਬਹੁਤ ਮਨਾਈ ਦੀਨ ਦਇਆਲ ਗੋਪਾਲਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 3॥ ਹਉ ਬਲਿਹਾਰੀ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਪੂਰੇ॥ ਜਿਨਿ ਬੰਧਨ ਕਾਟੇ ਸਗਲੇ ਮੇਰੇ॥ ਹਿਰਦੇ ਨਾਮੁ ਦੇ ਨਿਰਮਲ ਕੀਏ ਨਾਨਕ ਰੰਗਿ ਰਸਾਲਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥8॥15॥
Majh Mehla 5:  Dookh(u) tuday ja vissar(i) jaavaiy. Bhookh viyapaiy buhou bidh(i) dhaavaiy. Simrut Naam(u) suda soohayla, jis(u) dayvaiy deen Diyaala jeeo. 1. Satgur mayra vud sumrutha. Jeeyay smaaleen ta subh(u) dookh(u) lutha. Chinta roag(u) guyee Huoun peerra, Aap(i) kuray pritpaala jeeo.  2. Baarik vaangeen Huoun subh kichh(u) mungaan.  Daynday toat(i), naahi Prubh rungaan.  Paiyreen paiy paiy buhout munaayeen, deen Diyaal Gopala jeeo.  3.  Huoun bulihaari Satgur pooray. Jin(i) bundhan kaatay suglay mayray.  Hirday Naam(u) day nirmul keeyay, Nanak rung(i) rusaala jeeo.  4.8.15. 
Word Meaning:  Vissar(i) = forgotten.  Bhookh(u) = appetite (for worldly attractions) Viyapaiy = exerts; affects strongly.   Bhidh(i) = ways; means.  Dhaavaiy = moves quickly.     Soohayla = comfortable.    1.Vud = great.   Sumrutha = capable.   Jeeyay = in mind.   Smaleen = remember.  Huoun = haumein (selfishness and ego).    Pritpaala = nourishes; looks after.      2.
Baarik Vaangi = like a child.   Toat(i) = shortage.   Munayeen = (I) bring around; win over.     Deen Diyaal = kind to the powerless.     Gopala = Waheguru.   3. Bulihaari = sacrifice.   Bundhan = bonds (of Maya).    Nirmul = clean (of all evil thoughts).    Rung(i) = in love.    Rusaala = sources of all tastes (enjoyment).     4.

MEANING: When a person forgets Waheguru’s Naam, he begins to suffer (spiritually).  Worldly desires begin to overpower him, and he tries many different means to satisfy them as quickly as possible.  On the contrary, a person to whom the Kind Waheguru inspires to recite His Naam enjoys comfort all the time.         1. My Waheguru is perfectly capable (of doing everything); all my troubles go away when I remember Him (keep Him in mind) with love, all my worries and the discomfort of my haumein are all dispelled because He looks after me in every which way.        2.
I ask Him for everything like a child; He keeps giving me, and there is never a shortage in his bounty. Countless people beg at the feet of the Kind and the Provider Waheguru, and try to win Him over for His gifts.        3.        I offer my life to the Perfect Guru who has cut all my bonds to Maya and who has cleansed my mind by giving Waheguru’s Naam; now I am enjoying the bliss of Waheguru’s love, oh Nanak.  4. 8.15.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਲਾਲ ਗੋਪਾਲ ਦਇਆਲ ਰੰਗੀਲੇ॥ ਗਹਿਰ ਗੰਭੀਰ ਬੇਅੰਤ ਗੋਵਿੰਦੇ॥ ਊਚ ਅਥਾਹ ਬੇਅੰਤ ਸੁਆਮੀ ਸਿਮਰਿ ਸਿਮਰਿ ਹਉ ਜੀਵਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਦੁਖ ਭੰਜਨ ਨਿਧਾਨ ਅਮੋਲੇ॥ ਨਿਰਭਉ ਨਿਰਵੈਰ ਅਥਾਹ ਅਤੋਲੇ॥ ਅਕਾਲ ਮੂਰਤਿ ਅਜੂਨੀ ਸੰਭੌ ਮਨਿ ਸਿਮਰਤ ਠੰਡਾ ਥੀਵਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਸਦਾ ਸੰਗੀ ਹਰਿ ਰੰਗ ਗੋਪਾਲਾ॥ ਊਚ ਨੀਚ ਕਰੇ ਪ੍ਰਤਿਪਾਲਾ॥ ਨਾਮੁ ਰਸਾਇਣੁ ਮਨੁ ਤ੍ਰਿਪਤਾਇਣੁ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਪੀਵਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 3॥ ਦੁਖਿ ਸੁਖਿ ਪਿਆਰੇ ਤੁਧੁ ਧਿਆਈ॥ ਏਹ ਸੁਮਤਿ ਗੁਰੂ ਤੇ ਪਾਈ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਕੀ ਧਰ ਤੂ ਹੈ ਠਾਕੁਰ ਹਰਿ ਰੰਗਿ ਪਾਰਿ ਪਰੀਵਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥9॥16॥
Majh Mehla 5:  Laal Gopal Duyaal Rungeelay. Gehr gumbheer bayunt Govinday.  Ooch athaah bayunt Soami, simar(i) simar(i) Huoun jeevaan jeeo. 1. Dookh bhunjun nidhaan amolay. Nirbhou, Nirvair, Athaah, Atolay. Akal Moorat(i) Ajooni Sumbhou, mun(i) simrut tthunda theevaan jeeo.  2. Suda sungi Hur(i) rung Gopaala. Ooch neech kuray pritpaala. Naam(u) rusaayin(u) mun triptaayin(u), Gurmukh(i) Amrit peevaan jeeo. 3. Dookh(i) sookh(i) piyaaray Toodh(u) dhiyayeen. Eh soomut(i) Guru tay payee.  Nanak kee dhur Tu haiyn Tthaakur, Hur(i) rung(i) paar(i) preevaan jeeo. 4.9.16. 
Word Meaning:  Laal = ruby; dear.   Gopal = Waheguru.   Rungeelay = performer of uncanny feats; source of bliss.   Gehr = deep; unknowable.   Gumbheer = unemotional; never changing.   Govinday = Waheguru.   Ooch = higher (above the effects of Maya).   Athaah = unfathomable.  1.       Dookh Bhunjun = destroyer of discomforts (troubles).   Nidhaan = treasure (of). Amolay = priceless.   Akal Moorut(i) = unaffected by time.  Ajooni = which never takes a life form.    Sumbhou = self-illuminated.      2.       Sungi = companion.   Rung = situation.  Pritpaala = nourishing.   Rusaayin = source of all tastes (comforts).   Triptaayin = providing contentment.    3.           Dhiyayeen = I worship.   Soomut(i) = noble advice.   Dhur = support.   Rung(i) = in love.  Paar(i) preevaan = swim across (the sea of Maya).        4.
MEANING: Dear Waheguru is invaluable like a ruby. He is kind, a performer of uncanny feats, unknowable, never-changing, and limitless.  The Lord is above the effects of Maya, unfathomable, and limitless.  I survive only by reciting His Naam.        1.He is the destroyer of all troubles, and His treasures are priceless.  He is without fear, bears no enmity to anyone, is unfathomable, and immeasurable.  He is unaffected by time, does not take on life forms, and is self-illuminating.  I become completely peaceful when I recite His Naam.          2.                 The source of bliss, Waheguru, is always our companion who nourishes (looks after) all the low and high-class people. His Naam is the source of all enjoyment and of contentment; I drink His Naam-Amrit in the shelter of the Guru.     3. I worship Him in comfort and in discomfort as advised by the guru.  You are my support, and I can swim across the sea of Maya in Your love, oh Nanak.  4.9.16

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਧੰਨੁ ਸੁ ਵੇਲਾ ਜਿਤੁ ਮੈ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਮਿਲਿਆ॥ ਸਫਲੁ ਦਰਸਨੁ ਨੇਤ੍ਰ ਪੇਖਤ ਤਰਿਆ॥ ਧੰਨੁ ਮੂਰਤ ਚਸੇ ਪਲ ਘੜੀਆ ਧੰਨਿ ਸੁ ਓਇ ਸੰਜੋਗਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਉਦਮੁ ਕਰਤ ਮਨੁ ਨਿਰਮਲ ਹੋਆ॥ ਹਰਿ ਮਾਰਗਿ ਚਲਤ ਭ੍ਰਮੁ ਸਗਲਾ ਖੋਇਆ॥ ਨਾਮੁ ਨਿਧਾਨੁ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੂ ਸੁਣਾਇਆ ਮਿਟਿ ਗਏ ਸਗਲੇ ਰੋਗਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਬਾਹਰਿ ਤੇਰੀ ਬਾਣੀ ॥ ਤੁਧੁ ਆਪਿ ਕਥੀ ਤੈ ਆਪਿ ਵਖਾਣੀ॥ ਗੁਰਿ ਕਹਿਆ ਸਭੁ ਏਕੋ ਏਕੋ ਅਵਰੁ ਨ ਕੋਈ ਹੋਇ ਗਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 3॥ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਰਸੁ ਹਰਿ ਗੁਰ ਤੇ ਪੀਆ॥ ਹਰਿ ਪੈਨਣੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਭੋਜਨੁ ਦੀਆ॥ ਨਾਮਿ ਰੰਗਿ ਨਾਮਿ ਚੋਜ ਤਮਾਸੇ ਨਾਉ ਨਾਨਕ ਕੀਏ ਭੋਗਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥10॥17॥
Majh Mehla 5:  Dhunn(u) su vayla jit(u) mein Satgur(u) miliya. Suful(u) dershun(u), naytur paykhut turiya. Dhunn(u) moorut chusay pul ghurriyaan, dhunn(i) su oye sunjoga jeeo. 1. Oodum(u) kert mun(u) nirmul hoya. Hur(i) maarug(i) chult bherm(u) sugla khoya. Naam(u) nidhaan(u) Satguru soonaaya, mit(i) guyay suglay roga jeeo. 2.  Untar(i) baahur(i) Tayri Bani. Toodh(u) Aap(i) kuthi taiy Aap(i) vukhaani. Gur(i) kuhhiya subh(u) Aiko Aiko, uver(u) na koyee hoyay ga jeeo.  3.  Amrit rus(u) Hur(i) Gur tay peeya.  Hur(i) pehnun(u), Naam(u) bhojun deeya. Naam(i) rung(i) Naam(i)  choaj tmaashay, Naoun Nanak keeyay bhoga jeeo.  4.10.17.
Word Meaning:  Dhunn(u) = good luck (for me).   Suful(u) = blissful; comforting.  Paykhut = on seeing.   Moorut, Chusay, Pul, Ghurriyaan = they denote very short periods of time.  Sunjoga = predetermined union.       1.        Oodum(u) = enthusiasm; vivacity.    Bherm(u) = misbeliefs; doubts.   Khoya = lost; went away.   Nidhaan(u) = (source of all) treasures.    Mit(i) = erased; are removed.        2.        Bani = Shabad; Huqam.   Kuthi = uttered; declared.  Vukhaani = explained.   Uver = other.         3.          Rus(u) = essence; taste.   Pehnan(u) = clothes (to wear).   Bhojun = meals.   Rung(i) = enjoyment.    Choaj Tmaashay = entertaining activities.   Bhoga = source of enjoyment.        4. 
MEANING:  I feel very lucky for the moment when I got connected with Satguru. His sight is blissful and I got freedom from the bonds of Maya at His sight. That moment, that time was highly lucky for me, and so was the predetermined union with Him.      1.
My vivacity (to act on Guru’s advice) resulted in cleaning my mind (of any evil thoughts), and all my misbeliefs were cleared when I began to follow the path advised by the Guru. As Satguru narrated the treasure of Waheguru’s Naam to me, all my mental troubles were gone.
2             Satguru explained to me that Your Huqam is prevailing everywhere – inside and out.  You have declared Your Huqam and explained it too.   Satguru explained that there is only one Supreme Being, and that is You, and there will be none other, oh Waheguru.         3.
I acquired the essence of Naam-Amrit from Satguru.  He advised me that Waheguru’s Naam is like wearing costly dresses and enjoying rich foods. Not only that, Waheguru’s Naam brings you the fun and enjoyment of all the activities of entertainment.  Waheguru’s Naam is like all the tastes of life for me, oh Nanak.  4.10.17

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਸਗਲ ਸੰਤਨ ਪਹਿ ਵਸਤੁ ਇਕ ਮਾਗਉ॥ ਕਰਉ ਬੇਨੰਤੀ ਮਾਨੁ ਤਿਆਗਉ॥ ਵਾਰਿ ਵਾਰਿ ਜਾਈ ਲਖ ਵਰੀਆ ਦੇਹੁ ਸੰਤਨ ਕੀ ਧੂਰਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਤੁਮ ਦਾਤੇ ਤੁਮ ਪੁਰਖ ਬਿਧਾਤੇ॥ ਤੁਮੁ ਸਮਰਥ ਸਦਾ ਸੁਖਦਾਤੇ॥ ਸਭ ਕੋ ਤੁਮ ਹੀ ਤੇ ਵਰਸਾਵੈ ਅਉਸਰੁ ਕਰਹੁ ਹਮਾਰਾ ਪੂਰਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਦਰਸਨਿ ਤੇਰੇ ਭਵਨ ਪੁਨੀਤਾ॥ ਆਤਮ ਗੜੁ ਬਿਖਮੁ ਤਿਨਾ ਹੀ ਜੀਤਾ॥ ਤੁਮ ਦਾਤੇ ਤੁਮ ਪੁਰਖੁ ਬਿਧਾਤੇ ਤੁਮੁ ਜੇਵਡ ਅਵਰੁ ਨ ਸੂਰਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 3॥
