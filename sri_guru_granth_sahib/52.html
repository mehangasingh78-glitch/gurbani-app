(ਅੰਗ 51)॥   ਨਾਨਕ ਧੰਨੁ ਸੋਹਾਗਣੀ ਜਿਨ ਸਹ ਨਾਲਿ ਪਿਆਰੁ॥ 4॥23॥93॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 5:  Tichur(u) vuseh(i) soohaylurri, jichur(u) saathi naal(i). Ja saathi ootthi chuliya, ta dhun khaakoo raal(i).  1. Mun(i) bairaag(u) bhuyya, dershun daykhnay ka chaaou. Dhunn(u) su tayra thaan(u). 1. Ruhaou.  Jichur(u) vusiya kunt(u) ghur(i) jeo jeo subh(i) kuhaat(i). Ja ootthi chulsi kuntrra, ta koyay na poochhaiy tayri baat. 2. Payiurray shuhou sayv tu, sahururray sookh(i) vus(u). Gur mil(i) chujj achaar(u) sikh(u), toodh(u) kuday na luggaiy dookh(u). 3. Subhna sahauraiy vunjnaa subh(i) mooklaavunhaar.
(PAGE 51):             Nanak dhunn(u) sohaagni jin sheh naal(i) piyar(u).   4.23.93.
Word Meaning: Tichur(u) = till then.    Soohaylurri = with comfort; while enjoying.  Jichur(u) = as long.  Saathi = companion (soul).  Dhun = woman (human body).   Khaakoo = dust.   Raal(i) = mix with.        1.          Bairaag(u) = lack of attachment with the mundane (and love for Waheguru).  Bhuyya = taken place.   Chaaou = zest; excitement. Dhunn(u) = worthy of respect.  Tayra = yours (Waheguru’s).     Pause.       Vusiya = resided.   Kunt(u) = husband (soul).   Kuhaat(i) = called (with love).  Otthi chulsi = leave (this world).    Poochhaiy Baat = care about.           2.         Payiurray = in this world. (In Guru Granth Sahib, the present world is compared to the parental house, while the next world is compared to the in-laws’ house).  Shuhou = husband (Waheguru).   Sahururray = in-laws’ house (where God is the husband).  Chujj Achaar(u) = desirable manners.    Toodh(u) = you.         3.
Vunjunaa = (have) to go.   Mooklaavunhaar = (waiting) to join the husband. (It was the custom in India to marry off girls at a very young age, but they were sent to their in-laws after a few years. (That sending off was called mooklaava).   Sohaagni = truly married.     4.
MEANING: The human body enjoys comforts (and affection) only as long as its companion (soul) is with it.  Once the soul leaves the body, it becomes a part of dust.      1                      (Realizing this bitter fact), I have come to love Waheguru so much so that I have a great yearning to “see” Him now. Even the place where He resides has become worthy of worship for me.          Pause.             As long as the husband (soul) resides in the body, everybody shows respect and affection; but as soon as the soul leaves the body, nobody cares about the corpse.          2.             If you serve your real husband (Waheguru) in your parental home (this world), you will enjoy all the comforts in your in-laws’ house (the next world), where Waheguru is the husband, oh my friend.  To do that, you need to seek the shelter of the Guru to learn the desired manners (values) for the next world, and you will never suffer any discomfort, oh my friend.           3.         Everybody has to go to the in-laws’ home (Waheguru’s Court), and everybody is in line for the next world.  (Some may not be able to enjoy the love of Waheguru because of their ill deeds); but those married human brides, who earn Waheguru’s love, are truly “one” with Him, and are worthy of our respect, oh Nanak.   4.23. 93.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 5 ਘਰੁ 6॥ 

ਕਰਣ ਕਾਰਣ ਏਕ ਓਹੀ ਜਿਨਿ ਕੀਆ ਆਕਾਰੁ॥ ਤਿਸਹਿ ਧਿਆਵਹੁ ਮਨ ਮੇਰੇ ਸਰਬ ਕੋ ਆਧਾਰੁ॥ 1॥ ਗੁਰ ਕੇ ਚਰਨ ਮਨ ਮਹਿ ਧਿਆਇ॥ ਛੋਡਿ ਸਗਲ ਸਿਆਣਪਾ ਸਾਚਿ ਸਬਦਿ ਲਿਵ ਲਾਇ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਦੁਖੁ ਕਲੇਸੁ ਨ ਭਉ ਬਿਆਪੈ ਗੁਰ ਮੰਤ੍ਰੁ ਹਿਰਦੈ ਹੋਇ॥ ਕੋਟਿ ਜਤਨਾ ਕਰਿ ਰਹੇ ਗੁਰ ਬਿਨੁ ਤਰਿਓ ਨਾ ਕੋਇ॥ 2॥ ਦੇਖਿ ਦਰਸਨੁ ਮਨੁ ਸਾਧਾਰੈ ਪਾਪ ਸਗਲੇ ਜਾਹਿ॥ ਹਉ ਤਿਨ ਕੈ ਬਲਿਹਾਰਣੈ ਜਿ ਗੁਰ ਕੀ ਪੈਰੀ ਪਾਹਿ॥ 3॥ ਸਾਧ ਸੰਗਤਿ ਮਨਿ ਵਸੈ ਸਾਚੁ ਹਰਿ ਕਾ ਨਾਉ॥ ਸੇ ਵਡਭਾਗੀ ਨਾਨਕਾ ਜਿਨਾ ਮਨਿ ਇਹੁ ਭਾਉ॥ 4॥24॥94॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 5:  Kern kaarun aik Ohi jin(i) keeya aakaar(u). Tiseh(i) dhiyavou mun mayray, serb ko aadhaar(u). 1. Gur kay churn mun meh(i) dhiyayay.  Chhoad(i) sugul siyanpaan Sach(i) Shabad(i) liv laayay. 1. Ruhaou.  Dookh(u) klaysh(u) na bhuou biyaapaiy, Gur Muntar(u) hirdaiy hoyay. Kot(i) jutna kur(i) ruhay, Gur bin(u) turiyo na koyay. 2. Daykh(i) dershun(u) mun(u) saadharaiy, paap suglay jaah(i). Huoun tin kaiy bulihaarnaiy j(i) Gur kee paiyreen paah(i). 3. Sadh sungat(i) mun(i) vusaiy Saach(u) Hur(i) ka Naoun. Say vudbhaagi Nanaka, jinha mun(i) eho bhaaou.  4.24.94.
Word Meaning:  Kern Kaarun = The Creator of the universe (God).  Aakaar(u) = creation.   Dhiyavou = worship (keep in mind).   Aadhaar(u) = support; provider.       1.  
Gur kay Churn = feet of the Guru (a metaphor for the Shabad or the Teachings of the Guru).  Siyanpaan = wisdom.     Liv = fully focused.         Pause.         Klaysh(u) = unnecessary disputes.   Bhuou = fear.  Biyaapaiy = affects.    Muntar = Shabad; Teaching.   Hirdaiy = in the heart.  Kot(i) = millions.         2.            Sadhaaraiy = finds blissful support.   Bulihaarnaiy = sacrifice life.   J(i) = who          3.          Sadh Sungat(i) = in the company of the Guru.  Bhaao = affection.      4.
MEANING:  Only He (Waheguru) is the creator of the whole universe, oh my mind.  Worship Him alone (remember and recite only His Naam all the time), as He alone is the support and the provider for all.        1.           Worship Guru’s Feet (act on His Teachings), oh my mind-friend.  Surrendering all your wisdom to the Guru, focus on His Shabad with full dedication.    Pause.          When you internalize Guru’s Shabad, no trouble (discomfort), unnecessary disputes, and fear of any kind can affect you (your spiritual level gets so high that you do not feel them).  Millions of people have tried different ways, but nobody has ever been able to swim across the sea of Maya (cut the bonds of Maya) without the Guru’s shelter.      2. 
When one has a sight of the Guru (sees Him with mental eyes), he attains a blissful state, and all his sins are washed away.  I wish to offer my life to those (serve them humbly) who take Guru’s shelter, oh Nanak.        3.           The eternal Waheguru’s Naam takes residence in one’s heart in the company of the Guru (or His devotees). Those who have such deep, respectful affection for the Guru are highly fortunate.    4.24.94.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ 

ਸੰਚਿ ਹਰਿ ਧਨੁ ਪੂਜਿ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਛੋਡਿ ਸਗਲ ਵਿਕਾਰ॥ ਜਿਨਿ ਤੂੰ ਸਾਜਿ ਸਵਾਰਿਆ ਹਰਿ ਸਿਮਰਿ ਹੋਇ ਉਧਾਰੁ॥ 1॥ ਜਪਿ ਮਨ ਨਾਮੁ ਏਕੁ ਅਪਾਰੁ॥ ਪ੍ਰਾਨ ਮਨੁ ਤਨੁ ਜਿਨਹਿ ਦੀਆ ਰਿਦੇ ਕਾ ਆਧਾਰੁ॥1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਕਾਮਿ ਕ੍ਰੋਧਿ ਅਹੰਕਾਰਿ ਮਾਤੇ ਵਿਆਪਿਆ ਸੰਸਾਰੁ॥ ਪਉ ਸੰਤ ਸਰਣੀ ਲਾਗੁ ਚਰਣੀ ਮਿਟੈ ਦੂਖੁ ਅੰਧਾਰੁ॥ 2॥ ਸਤੁ ਸੰਤੋਖੁ ਦਇਆ ਕਮਾਵੈ ਏਹ ਕਰਣੀ ਸਾਰ॥ ਆਪੁ ਛੋਡਿ ਸਭ ਹੋਇ ਰੇਣਾ ਜਿਸੁ ਦੇਇ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਨਿਰੰਕਾਰੁ॥3॥ ਜੋ ਦੀਸੈ ਸੋ ਸਗਲ ਤੂ ਹੈ ਪਸਰਿਆ ਪਾਸਾਰੁ॥ ਕਹੁ ਨਾਨਕ ਗੁਰ ਭਰਮੁ ਕਾਟਿਆ ਸਗਲ ਬ੍ਰਹਮ ਬੀਚਾਰੁ॥ 4॥25॥95॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 5:  Sunch(i) Hur(i) dhun(u) pooj(i) Satgur(u), chhoad(i) sugul vikaar. Jin(i) tu saaj(i) svaariya, Hur(i) simer(i) hoyay oodhaar(u). 1. Jup(i) mun Naam(u) Aik(u) Apaar(u).  Praan mun(u) tun(u) jineh(i) deeya, riday ka aadhaar(u). 1. Pause.  Kaam(i) krodh(i) ahunkaar(i) maatay, viyapiya sunsaar(u).  Puou Sunt sherni, laag(u) churni, mitaiy dookh(u) undhaar(u). 2.  Sut(u) suntokh(u) duyya kmaavaiy eh kurni saar.  Aap(u) choad(i) subh hoyay rayna, jis dayay Prubh(u) Nirunkaar(u). 3. Jo deesaiy so sugul Tu haiy, pusriya paasaar(u). Kuhou Nanak Gur bherm(u) kaatiya sugul Braham beechaar(u).  4.25.95.
Word Meaning:  Sunch(i) = accumulate.    Pooj(i) = worship.    Vikaar = sensual desires or ignoble deeds.   Saaj(i) = created.   Svaariya = groomed with noble qualities.     Simer = remember.   Oodhaar(u) = salvation.      1.       Jup(i) = recite.   Apaar(u) = limitless.    Praan = life.    Riday = heart; mind.   Aadhaar(u) = support; basis.          Pause.          Kaam(i) = lust.   Maatay = intoxicated; inebriated.     Viyapiya = influenced.          2. 
Sut(u) = high moral character.   Suntokh(u) = contentment.   Kurni = worth doing.  Saar = supreme.   Aap(u) = self (selfishness).   Rayna = dust (of the feet) – a metaphor for humbleness.    Nirunkaar(u) = formless (Waheguru).          3.
Sugul = all.   Pusriya = spread out.     Paasaar(u) = expansion; creation.     4.
MEANING: Recite the Naam of the “One” and the limitless Waheguru, who has given you the gift of life, mind, and the body; and is also the support for your heart (soul), oh my mind.           Pause.            Conquering your sensual desires, worship Satguru, and accumulate the wealth of Waheguru’s Naam, oh my mind.  To be salvaged, keep Waheguru always in mind, who has created you and instilled noble qualities in you, oh man.       1.            The whole world is intoxicated under the influence of lust, rage, and conceit. Come to the Guru’s shelter, and drop on His feet, because of which, all your discomforts and the darkness of ignorance will be eliminated.          2.          Developing High moral character, contentment, and kindness is the supreme business in the world. Those who the formless Waheguru is kind to, overcome their selfishness, and become humble as if they were the dust of the feet of everybody.        3.
Whatever is visible is all Your creation, oh Waheguru. The Guru has dispelled all my doubts, making it clear that the whole universe has been created by the planning of Waheguru, oh Nanak.   4.25.95

ਸਿਰੀਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ 

ਦੁਕ੍ਰਿਤ ਸੁਕ੍ਰਿਤ ਮੰਧੇ ਸੰਸਾਰੁ ਸਗਲਾਣਾ॥ ਦੁਹਹੂੰ ਤੇ ਰਹਤ ਭਗਤੁ ਹੈ ਕੋਈ ਵਿਰਲਾ ਜਾਣਾ॥ 1॥ ਠਾਕੁਰੁ ਸਰਬੇ ਸਮਾਣਾ॥ ਕਿਆ ਕਹਉ ਸੁਣਉ ਸੁਆਮੀ ਤੂ ਵਡ ਪੁਰਖੁ ਸੁਜਾਣਾ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਮਾਨ ਅਭਿਮਾਨ ਮੰਧੇ ਸੋ ਸੇਵਕੁ ਨਾਹੀ॥ ਤਤ ਸਮਦਰਸੀ ਸੰਤਹੁ ਕੋਈ ਕੋਟਿ ਮੰਧਾਹੀ॥ 2॥ ਕਹਨ ਕਹਾਵਨ, ਇਹੁ ਕੀਰਤਿ ਕਰਲਾ॥ ਕਥਨ ਕਹਨ ਤੇ ਮੁਕਤਾ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਕੋਈ ਵਿਰਲਾ॥ 3॥ ਗਤਿ ਅਵਿਗਤਿ ਕਛੁ ਨਦਰਿ ਨ ਆਇਆ॥ ਸੰਤਨ ਕੀ ਰੇਣੁ ਨਾਨਕ ਦਾਨੁ ਪਾਇਆ॥ 4॥26॥96॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 5:  Dookirt sookirt mundhay, sunsaar(u) suglaana.  Doohoon tay reht bhagat(u) haiy, koyee virla jaana. 1.  Tthakur(u) serbay smaana. Kiyya kuhoun soonou Soami, Tu vud Purakh(u) soojaana. 1. Ruhaoiu.  Maan abhimaan mundhay, so sayvuk naahi. Tut sumdersi Suntou, koyee kot(i) mundhahee. 2.  Kehn kuhaavun, eho keerut(i) kurla. Kuthun kehn tay mookta, Gurmukh(i) koyee virla. 3.  Gut(i) uvgut(i) kuchh(u) nudr(i) na aaya.  Suntun kee raiyn, Nanak daan paaya. 4.26.96.
Word Meaning:  Dookirt = immoral and unethical deeds.    Sookirt = noble deeds.  Mundhay = in the middle.    Suglana = whole.   Reht = without; free from.   Bhagat(u) = devotee.    Virla = rare.    Jaana = recognized; considered.         1.         Tthakur(u) = Lord; Master.   Smaana = pervading; present.   Soami = Master.   Vud = great.   Purakh(u) = Waheguru.   Soojaana = wise; great.         Pause.          Abhimaan = disrespect.   Tut = basis; root cause (Waheguru.    Sumdersi = views without distinction.     Kot(i) = in millions.     Mundhahe = between; among.       2.              Keerut(i) = praise.   Kurla = path.   Kuthun = saying; uttering.  Mookta = free from.    3.           Gut(i) = go. Uvgut(i) = come.  Nudr(i) = in sight.   Raiyn = dust (of the feet); serving the Guru and His devotees.    Daan = donation.          4.
MEANING:  The functioning of the whole world is being carried out under the influence of Maya, and it ranges between bad and noble deeds. There is a rare individual who is above these two kinds of deeds (above the influence of Maya); such an individual is recognized as the real devotee of Waheguru.           1.         The Lord, Waheguru, is present everywhere. What can I say, or listen to, about Him?  He is the wise Supreme Being.         Pause.
An individual who is sensitive to respect and disrespect is not a devotee.  There is only one among millions who views everything as created by Waheguru.            2.
To say things about Waheguru, and have others say something about Him (debating) has become the only accepted - albeit fruitless - path of praising Waheguru; only some rare individual is free from such worthless discussions.           3.          Nobody ever sees Waheguru coming or going (taking birth or dying) because He never does.  (I was lucky) that I got the gift of serving the Guru (and devotees of Waheguru), oh Nanak.     4.26.96.
