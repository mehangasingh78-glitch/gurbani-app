(ਅੰਗ 119)॥      ਖੋਟੇ ਖਰੇ ਤੁਧੁ ਆਪਿ ਉਪਾਏ॥ ਤੁਧੁ ਆਪੇ ਪਰਖੇ ਲੋਕ ਸਬਾਏ॥ ਖਰੇ ਪਰਖਿ ਖਜਾਨੈ ਪਾਇਹਿ ਖੋਟੇ ਭਰਮਿ ਭੁਲਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 6॥ ਕਿਉ ਕਰਿ ਵੇਖਾ ਕਿਉ ਸਾਲਾਹੀ॥ ਗੁਰ ਪਰਸਾਦੀ ਸਬਦਿ ਸਲਾਹੀ॥ ਤੇਰੇ ਭਾਣੇ ਵਿਚਿ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਵਸੈ ਤੂ ਭਾਣੈ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਪੀਆਵਣਿਆ॥ 7॥ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਸਬਦੁ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਹਰਿ ਬਾਣੀ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰਿ ਸੇਵਿਐ ਰਿਦੇ ਸਮਾਣੀ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਨਾਮੁ ਸਦਾ ਸੁਖਦਾਤਾ ਪੀ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਸਭ ਭੁਖ ਲਹਿ ਜਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 8॥15॥16॥
Majh Mehla 3:  Amrit Naam(u) munn(i) vusaayay.  Haumein mayra subh(u) dookh(u) guvaayay. Amrit Bani suda salaahay Amrit(i), Amrit(u) paavinya. 1. Houn vaari jeeo vaari, Amrit Bani munn(i) vusaavniya. Amrit Bani munn(i) vusaayay, Amrit(u) Naam(u) dhiyaavniya. 1. Ruhaou.  Amrit(u) bolaiy suda mookh(i) vaiyni. Amrit(u) vaykhaiy, purkhaiy; suda naiyni.  Amrit kutha kuhaiy din(u) raati, uvraan aakh(i) soonaavniya. 2. Amrit rung(i) rutta liv laayay. Amrit Gur persadi paayay. Amrit(u) rusna bolaiy din(u) raati mun(i) tun(i) Amrit(u) peeaavniya. 3.  So kichh(u) kuraiy ju chit(i) na hoyee. Tis da Huqam(u) mayt(i) na sukaiy koyee.  Huqamaiy vertaiy Amrit Bani, Huqamaiy Amrit(u) peeaavniya. 4. Ujab kumm Kertay Hur(i) kayray. Eho mun(u) bhoola jaanda phayray.  Amrit Bani siyoun chit(u) laayay, Amrit Shabad(i) vujaavniya. 5.
(PAGE 119):                 Khotay khuray Toodh(u) Aap(i) oopaayay. Toodh(u) Aapay perkhay loak subaayay.  Khuray perkh(i) khujaanay paayayn, khotay bherm(i) bhoolaavniya. 6. Kiyoun kur(i) vaykhaan, kiyoun salaaheen. Gur Persadi Shabad(i) salaaheen. Tayray bhaanay vich(i) Amrit(u) vusaiy, Tu bhaanaiy Amrit(u) peeaavniya.  7. Amrit Shabad(u) Amrit Hur(i) Bani. Satgur(i) sayviyaiyn riday smaani.  Nanak Amrit Naam(u) suda sookhdaata, pea Amrit(u) subh bhookh leh(i) jaavniya.   8.15.16.  
Word Meaning:   Munn(i) Vusaayay = embed (enshrine) in heart.     Mayra = mine.   Amrit Bani = the immortalizing (eternal) Shabad (in God’s praise).   Amrit(i) = through Amrit (Gurbani).       1.      Amrit Naam(u) dhiyaavniya = recite Waheguru’s Naam which makes one immortal.  Vainy = Bani.   Purkhaiy = tests.    Naiyni = with eyes.    Kutha = story (Waheguru’s virtues).    Uveraan = others.       2.      Rung(i) Rutta = imbued in love.   Live Laayay = meditates with full concentration.   Rusna = tongue.       3. So Kichh(u) = that.   Chit(i) = (in) mind.     Mayt(i) = erase; remove.  Vertaiy = operates.        4.         Ujab = strange; unbelievable.    Kertay Kayray = of the Creator.   Pharay = turns around.     Siyoun = with.  Vujaavniya = has a big impact.          5. Khotay = fake; impure.    Khuray = pure.    Perkhay = tested.   Khuzanay = in the treasure (Waheguru Himself).     Bherm(i) = in doubt (inability to choose).        6. Kiyoun = why; how?     Bhaanay = Will.       Vusaiy = resides; is controlled.         7. Sayviyaiyn = by serving.     Riday = (in the) heart.    Bhookh = appetite (desires).     8.
MEANING:  All the discomforts due to haumein and selfishness disappear when one  enshrines Waheguru’s immortal Naam in the heart.  Waheguru’s immortal Naam is acquired by singing the Guru’s immortalizing Bani (Shabad) with dedication.       1.I am a sacrifice to all those who enshrine Waheguru’s immortal Bani in their heart.  They enshrine the immortal Bani in their minds and recite His Amrit Naam all the time.    Pause.          They sing Waheguru’s praise all the time, see Waheguru in everything, and assess His worth (admire Him) in them. They discuss Waheguru’s immortal virtues day and night, and share them with others.          2.  
The individual, who is imbued in Waheguru’s immortal Naam, focuses his attention on His Naam, which he acquires with Guru’s grace.  His tongue utters His immortal Naam day and night, and he drinks the Naam Amrit with his body and mind.       3.
Waheguru does things which a man can never even think about, and nobody can erase His Will.   His immortal Shabad (Huqam) is prevailing everywhere, and He implants   His Amrit-Naam in His devotees’ hearts by His own sweet Will.       4.
Waheguru’s deeds are amazing.  He turns around the misguided minds of some so that they apply their minds to Waheguru’s immortal Bani, and the immortal Shabad has a deep impact on them.          5.            You, Yourself, have created the pure and the impure minds of people, and Yourself test (evaluate) them, oh Waheguru!   The pure are deposited in Your treasure (are approved by You and merge in You), while the impure stay misguided in doubts (and are subjected to reincarnation).         6.
How can I “see” You and how can I appreciate You, oh Waheguru? (The answer is): 
One can appreciate Him and “see” Him by singing His praise with the Guru’s Shabad.
Your Naam–Amrit is in Your control, and You can serve it to anybody by Your Will, oh Waheguru!           7.              Waheguru’s Shabad (Huqam) and His Bani are immortal.  His Naam can be enshrined in the mind by serving (obeying) Satguru.  Waheguru’s immortal Naam is the source of all bliss, and all one’s appetite (wishes and desires for Maya) is satisfied by drinking the Amrit of His Naam, oh Nanak!    8.15.16.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਵਰਸੈ ਸਹਜਿ ਸੁਭਾਏ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਵਿਰਲਾ ਕੋਈ ਜਨੁ ਪਾਏ॥ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਪੀ ਸਦਾ ਤ੍ਰਿਪਤਾਸੇ ਕਰਿ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਤ੍ਰਿਸਨਾ ਬੁਝਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਹਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਜੀਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਪੀਆਵਣਿਆ॥ ਰਸਨਾ ਰਸੁ ਚਾਖਿ ਸਦਾ ਰਹੈ ਰੰਗਿ ਰਾਤੀ ਸਹਜੇ ਹਰਿ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਗੁਰ ਪਰਸਾਦੀ ਸਹਜੁ ਕੋ ਪਾਏ॥ ਦੁਬਿਧਾ ਮਾਰੇ ਇਕਸੁ ਸਿਉ ਲਿਵ ਲਾਏ॥ ਨਦਰਿ ਕਰੇ ਤਾ ਹਰਿ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਵੈ ਨਦਰੀ ਸਚਿ ਸਮਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 2॥ ਸਭਨਾ ਉਪਰਿ ਨਦਰਿ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਤੇਰੀ॥ ਕਿਸੈ ਥੋੜੀ ਕਿਸੈ ਹੈ ਘਣੇਰੀ॥ ਤੁਝ ਤੇ ਬਾਹਰਿ ਕਿਛੁ ਨ ਹੋਵੈ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਸੋਝੀ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 3॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਤਤੁ ਹੈ ਬੀਚਾਰਾ॥ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤਿ ਭਰੇ ਤੇਰੇ ਭੰਡਾਰਾ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਸੇਵੇ ਕੋਈ ਨ ਪਾਵੈ ਗੁਰ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਤੇ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 4॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਸੇਵੈ ਸੋ ਜਨੁ ਸੋਹੈ॥ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਨਾਮਿ ਅੰਤਰੁ ਮਨੁ ਮੋਹੈ॥ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤਿ ਮਨੁ ਤਨੁ ਬਾਣੀ ਰਤਾ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਸਹਜਿ ਸੁਣਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 5॥ ਮਨਮੁਖੁ ਭੂਲਾ ਦੂਜੈ ਭਾਇ ਖੁਆਏ॥ ਨਾਮੁ ਨ ਲੇਵੈ ਮਰੈ ਬਿਖੁ ਖਾਏ॥ ਅਨਦਿਨੁ ਸਦਾ ਵਿਸਟਾ ਮਹਿ ਵਾਸਾ ਬਿਨੁ ਸੇਵਾ ਜਨਮੁ ਗਵਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 6॥ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਪੀਵੈ ਜਿਸ ਨੋ ਆਪਿ ਪੀਆਏ॥ ਗੁਰ ਪਰਸਾਦੀ  ਸਹਜਿ ਲਿਵ ਲਾਏ॥ਪੂਰਨ ਪੂਰਿ ਰਹਿਆ ਸਭ ਆਪੇ ਗੁਰਮਤਿ ਨਦਰੀ ਆਵਣਿਆ॥ 7॥ ਆਪੇ ਆਪਿ ਨਿਰੰਜਨੁ ਸੋਈ॥ ਜਿਨਿ ਸਿਰਜੀ ਤਿਨਿ ਆਪੇ ਗੋਈ॥ ਨਾਨਕੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਸਮਾਲਿ ਸਦਾ ਤੂ ਸਹਜੇ ਸਚਿ ਸਮਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 8॥16॥17॥
Majh Mehla 3:  Amrit versaiy sehj(i) soobhaayay.  Gurmukh(i) virla koyee jun(u) paayay. Amrit(u) pea suda triptaaasay, kur(i) kirpa trishna boojhaavniya.  1. Huoun vaarri jeeo vaari, Gurmukh(i) Amrit(u) piaavniya. Rusna rus(u) chaakh(i) suda ruhaiy rung(i) raati, sehjay Hur(i) goon gaavniya. 1. Ruhaou.  Gur persadi sehj(u) ko paayay. Doobidha maaray Ikkus(u) siyoun liv laayay. Nudr(i) kuray ta Hur(i) goon gaavaiy, nudri Sach(i) smaavniya. 2. Subhna ooper(i) nudr(i) Prubh Tayri. Kisaiy thorri kisaiy haiy ghunayri.  Toojh tay baahur(i) kichh(u) na hovaiy, Gurmukh(i) sojhi paavniya.  3. Gurnukh(i) Tut(u) haiy beechaara. Amrit(i) bhuray Tayray bhundaara.  Bin(u) Satgur sayvay koyee na paavaiy, Gur kirpa tay paavniya.  4. Satguru(u) sayvaiy so jun(u) sohaiy.  Amrit Naam(i) untar(i) mun(u) mohaiy. Amrit(i) mun(u) tun(i) Bani rutta, Amrit(u) sehj(i) smaavniya.   5. Munmukh(u) bhoola doojaiy bhaayay khooyayay. Naam(u) na layvaiy, muraiy bikh(u) khaayay.  Undin(u) suda vishta meh(i) vaasa, bin(u) sayva junum(u) guvaavniya.  6. Amrit(u) peevaiy jis nu Aap(i) piaayay   Gur persadi sehj(i) liv laayay. Poorun poor(i) ruhiya subh Aapay, Gurmut(i) nudri aavniya.  7. Aapay aap(i) Nirunjun(u) soyee. Jin(i) sirjee, Tin(i) aapay goyee.  Nanak(u) Naam(u) smaal(i) suda tu, sehjay Sach(i) smaavniya.  8.16.17.
Word Meaning:  Amrit = (Waheguru’s) eternal (Naam).  Versaiy = rains (is available).    Sehj(i) Soobhaayay = with love (for the Guru) and in high spiritual state; naturally.     Virla = rare.   Jun = devotee.    Triptaasay = is contented.     Trishna = (sensual) desires; hunger for Maya.       Boojhaavniya = is quenched.        1.      Rusna = tongue.    Chaakh(i) = by tasting.    Rung(i) Raati = imbued in love.      Pause.       Doobidha = indecisiveness because one is caught between two options.    Ikkus(u) Siyoun =  with One (Waheguru).            2.         Ghunayri = very much.     Sojhi = understanding; awareness.           3.   Tut(u) = reality.   Beechaara = pondered on.    Bhundaara = treasures.       4.Sohaiy = looks impressive (honourable); shiny.    Smaavniya = merges in.         5.Doojaiy Bhaayay = in love for Maya.    Khooyayay = suffers without any gain.   Bikh(u) = poison (Maya).     Vishta = feces (evil deeds and thoughts).         6. Amrit(u) = Waheguru’s Naam.    Liv Laayay = concentrate on (Waheguru’s Naam).     Poorun = perfect.  Poor(i) = is filling completely.  Nudri Aavniya = is “seen”.           7. Nirunjun = unaffected by Maya (God).    Sirjee = created.    Goyee = destroyed.       8.
MEANING: When an individual meditates on Waheguru with pure love and in a high spiritual state, His Amrit-Naam rains on his mind (his mind gets fully imbued with love for Waheguru). However, some rare devotees attain this state in the shelter of the Guru. Such devotees are fully contented, and all their desires for Maya are satisfied (go away) with the grace of the Guru.       1. I am a sacrifice for all those devotees who drink Waheguru’s Naam-Amrit in the shelter of the Guru. By tasting the Naam-Amrit, their tongue is always imbued with Waheguru’s love, and they sing His praise with a peaceful mind.            Pause.           A rare person attains a high spiritual state with the grace of the Guru.  His indecisiveness goes away, and he concentrates his mind only on Waheguru. With Waheguru’s benevolence, he sings His praise and merges into Him.            2.            Waheguru casts His kind sight on everyone; it is a different matter that the degree is low for some and very high for some others.   A devotee realizes this fact in the shelter of the Guru that nothing happens without the Will of Waheguru.        3. A devotee realizes the fact that Waheguru’s treasures are full of His Naam-Amrit, but nobody has access to them without serving the Guru, and one can access them only with His grace.         4. Those devotees who serve Satguru shine (look dignified) everywhere; the Amrit-Naam inside them captivates people. Their mind and body are imbued with the Guru’s Shabad, and they recite it to others with a blissful mind.       5.The follower of his mind is lost in the love for Maya, and suffers (has no peace of mind).  He does not recite Waheguru’s Naam and deals in activities involving the poisonous 
Maya.  He lives in the filth of Maya (leads a lustful life) day and night, and wastes away 
his precious human life without serving the Guru.        6. Only those who drink the Naam-Amrit (are imbued with Naam) who are motivated by Waheguru Himself.  With the Guru’s grace, they attain the spiritual state above the effects of Maya. The Perfect Waheguru is pervading everywhere, but can be “seen” only in the shelter of the Guru (obeying Him).         7. 
Waheguru alone is operating everywhere.  He creates the universe, and then destroys it (at His own Will).  You'd better take care of Waheguru’s Naam (always remember Him) to attain the high spiritual state, oh Nanak!    8.16.17.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ ਸੇ ਸਚਿ ਲਾਗੇ ਜੋ ਤੁਧੁ ਭਾਏ॥ ਸਦਾ ਸਚੁ ਸੇਵਹਿ ਸਹਜ ਸੁਭਾਏ॥ ਸਚੈ ਸਬਦਿ ਸਚਾ ਸਾਲਾਹੀ ਸਚੈ ਮੇਲਿ ਮਿਲਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਹਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਜੀਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਸਚੁ ਸਾਲਾਹਣਿਆ॥ ਸਚੁ ਧਿਆਇਨਿ ਸੇ ਸਚਿ ਰਾਤੇ ਸਚੇ ਸਚਿ ਸਮਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਜਹ ਦੇਖਾ ਸਚੁ ਸਭਨੀ ਥਾਈ॥ ਗੁਰ ਪਰਸਾਦੀ ਮੰਨਿ ਵਸਾਈ॥ ਤਨੁ ਸਚਾ ਰਸਨਾ ਸਚਿ ਰਾਤੀ ਸਚੁ ਸੁਣਿ ਆਖਿ ਵਖਾਨਣਿਆ॥2॥
