One should teach (educate) their mind to sing Waheguru’s praise with the Guru’s Shabad. Those who get imbued in the love of Waheguru’s Naam have no burden of immorality and no misbeliefs, oh Nanak.   With the recitation of Waheguru’s Naam, one earns the great wealth that the fearless Waheguru takes dwelling in his heart.      4.23.
