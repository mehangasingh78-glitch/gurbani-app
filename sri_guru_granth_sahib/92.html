(ਅੰਗ 91)॥ ਹਰਿ ਭਗਤਾ ਨੋ ਦੇਇ ਅਨੰਦੁ ਥਿਰੁ ਘਰੀ ਬਹਾਲਿਆਨੁ॥ ਪਾਪੀਆ ਨੋ ਨ ਦੇਈ ਥਿਰੁ ਰਹਣਿ ਚੁਣਿ ਨਰਕ ਘੋਰਿ ਚਾਲਿਅਨੁ॥ ਹਰਿ ਭਗਤਾ ਨੋ ਦੇਇ ਪਿਆਰੁ ਕਰਿ ਅੰਗੁ ਨਿਸਤਾਰਿਅਨੁ॥
Paurri: Hur(i) aapni bhugut(i) kuraayay, vudiyayee vaykhaaliun(u).  Aapni Aap(i) kuray purteet(i), Aapay sayv ghaaliun.
(PAGE 91):       Hur(i) bhugtaan nu dayay anund, thir(u) ghureen buhaaliun. Paapiyaan nu na dayee thir(u) rehn(i), choon(i) nerk ghor(i) chaaliun.  Hur(i) bhugtaan nu dayay piyaar(u), kur(i) ung(u) nistaariun(u).   19.
Word Meaning:  Vaykhaaliun = shows.  Purteet(i) = faith.  Sayv Ghaaliun = performs tough service.     Anund(u) = blissfulness.  Thir(u) = stable.  Ghureen = in hearts.  Ghor(i) Nerk = torturous Hell.    Ung(u) = favour.   Nistaariun = salvages.  
MEANING:  Waheguru reveals His grandeur when He motivates people to worship Him.  Being present in everybody, it is in fact Waheguru Himself, who shows faith in Him, and performs tough service (of worshiping Waheguru). He enables His devotees to control their minds, whereby they enjoy blissfulness.  He does not provide the sinners the opportunity to stabilize their minds, as a result of which they are taken to a torturous Hell.  Waheguru favours His devotees with love and salvages them.  19.

ਸਲੋਕ ਮ:1॥ ਕੁਬੁਧਿ ਡੂਮਣੀ ਕੁਦਇਆ ਕਸਾਇਣਿ ਪਰ ਨਿੰਦਾ ਘਟ ਚੂਹੜੀ ਮੁਠੀ ਕ੍ਰੋਧਿ ਚੰਡਾਲਿ॥ ਕਾਰੀ ਕਢੀ ਕਿਆ ਥੀਐ ਜਾ ਚਾਰੇ ਬੈਠੀਆ ਨਾਲਿ॥ ਸਚੁ ਸੰਜਮੁ ਕਰਣੀ ਕਾਰਾ ਨਾਵਣੁ ਨਾਉ ਜਪੇਹੀ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਅਗੈ ਊਤਮ ਸੇਈ ਜਿ ਪਾਪਾ ਪੰਦਿ ਨ ਦੇਹੀ॥ 1॥
Sloak M:1:  Kooboodh(i) doomni, kooduyya ksaayan(i), per ninda ghut choohrri, mootthi krodh(i) chundaal(i).  Kaari kuddhi kiyya theeyaiy, ja chaaray baitthiyaan naal(i). Sach(u) sunjum(u), kerni kaaraan, naavun(u) Naoun jupayhi.  Nanak uggaiy ootum sayee, j(i) paapaan pund(i) na dayhi.  1.
Word Meaning:  Kooboodh(i) = evil thinking.  Doomni = low caste.  Kooduyya = ruthlessness; cruelty. Kusaayan(i) = butcher.   Ninda = malicious criticism; to vilify.   Ghut = heart.   Choohrri = lowest caste.  Mootthi = deceived; robbed.     Krodh(i) = rage.  Chundaal(i) = butcher; savage.  Kaari = lines drawn.  Sunjum = effort to control sensual pleasures.   Naavun(u) = dip in holy waters.  Ootum = superior.   Pund(i) = advice. 
NOTE: Brahmans, who considered themselves to be the elite (highest) class in society, observed some meaningless rituals to impress people with their cleanliness.  Such rituals included pilgrimage, cleaning some area for their own cooking, drawing boundary lines for the area where nobody was allowed to enter, and so on.  Guru Nanak Sahib ridiculed such hollow beliefs and advised them about what was really meaningful.  
MEANING: Evil thinking is the true low caste, and cruelty is like being a butcher. Likewise, unwarranted, malicious criticism (vilification) of others is the lowest class in society, while rage is like a butcher who robs one of good qualities. So, what is the significance of rituals like taking a dip in holy places, and drawing boundary lines around the kitchen area when all four (above-mentioned) evil qualities accompany you, oh Brahman?  (Guru Sahib advises that) Living a life of high morality is the real effort to control your sensual pleasures; noble deeds should serve as the boundary lines, and recitation of Waheguru’s Naam is the true holy dip. Only that individual is considered superior (holy) in Waheguru’s Court, who, unlike Brahmans, does not give evil advice to people, oh Nanak.  1.
ਮ:1॥ ਕਿਆ ਹੰਸੁ ਕਿਆ ਬਗੁਲਾ ਜਾ ਕਉ ਨਦਰਿ ਕਰੇਇ॥ ਜੇ ਤਿਸੁ ਭਾਵੈ ਨਾਨਕਾ ਕਾਗਹੁ ਹੰਸੁ ਕਰੇਇ॥ 2॥
M:1:  Kiyya huns(u), kiyya bugula, ja kuou nudr(i) kuray. Jay Tis(u) bhaavaiy Nanaka, kaag-houn huns(u) kuray.  2.
Word Meaning:  Huns = swan (an individual who follows the Guru’s advice).   Bugula = a heron (an imposter) because a heron looks like a swan from a distance.  Kaag = a crow (a munmukh, who only follows his mind like a crow that makes no distinction between filth and good food). 
MEANING:  It does not make any difference whether it is a Gurmukh (a swan) or an imposter (a heron); if Waheguru wishes, He can transform even a crow (a manmukh) into a swan (a Gurmukh), oh Nanak.  2.

ਪਉੜੀ॥ ਕੀਤਾ ਲੋੜੀਐ ਕੰਮੁ ਸੁ ਹਰਿ ਪਹਿ ਆਖੀਐ॥ ਕਾਰਜੁ ਦੇਇ ਸਵਾਰਿ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਸਚੁ ਸਾਖੀਐ॥ ਸੰਤਾ ਸੰਗਿ ਨਿਧਾਨੁ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਚਾਖੀਐ॥ ਭੈ ਭੰਜਨ ਮਿਹਰਵਾਨ ਦਾਸ ਕੀ ਰਾਖੀਐ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਹਰਿ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਇ ਅਲਖੁ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਲਾਖੀਐ॥ 20॥
Paurri:  Keeta lorriyaiy kumm(u) su Hur(i) peh(i) aakhiyaiy.  Kaaruj(u) dayay svaar(i) Satguru Sach(u) sakhiyaiy.  Suntaan sung(i) nidhaan(u) Amrit(u) chaakhiyaiy. Bhaiy bhunjun mehrvaan daas kee rakhiyaiy.  Nanak Hur(i) goon gaayay ulukh(u) Prubh(u) laakhiyaiy.  20.
Word Meaning:  Lorriyaiy = wish.  Aakhiyaiy = say to; pray to.  Kaaruj = task.  Svaar(i) = complete to perfection.  Sakhiyaiy = advice.   Bhaiy Bhunjun = destroyer of fears (God).    Mehrvaan = kind (Waheguru).   Ulukh(u) = incomprehensible.  
MEANING: If you wish to have a task successfully completed, pray to Waheguru for it. Satguru’s perfect advice leads to a perfect completion of the task.  Taste Waheguru’s Naam, which is the treasure of Amrit, in the company of the holy men, and pray to Waheguru as follows: protect your slave (from the evil bonds of Maya), oh the destroyer of all fears, Waheguru. One can realize the incomprehensible Waheguru by singing His virtuous qualities, oh Nanak.  20.

ਸਲੋਕ ਮ:3॥ ਜੀਉ ਪਿੰਡੁ ਸਭੁ ਤਿਸ ਕਾ ਸਭਸੈ ਦੇਇ ਅਧਾਰੁ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਸੇਵੀਐ ਸਦਾ ਸਦਾ ਦਾਤਾਰੁ॥ ਹਉ ਬਲਿਹਾਰੀ ਤਿਨ ਕਉ ਜਿਨਿ ਧਿਆਇਆ ਹਰਿ ਨਿਰੰਕਾਰੁ॥ ਓਨਾ ਕੇ ਮੁਖ ਸਦ ਉਜਲੇ ਓਨਾ ਨੋ ਸਭੁ ਜਗਤੁ ਕਰੇ ਨਮਸਕਾਰੁ॥ 1॥
Sloak M:3: Jeeo pind(u) subh(u) Tis ka, subhsaiy dayay adhaar(u). Nanak Gurmukh(i) sayviyaiy, suda suda Aataar(u). Houn bulihari tin kuou, jin(i) dhiyaaya Hur(i) 
Nirunkaar(u). Ohna kay mookh sud oojlay, ohna nu subh(u) jugut(u) kuray numskaar. 
Word Meaning:   Jeeo = life.   Pind(u) = body.   Adhaar(u) = food; support.   Dataar(u) = provider.  Bulihari = sacrifice.   Nirunkaar(u) = formless.   Oojlaiy = shiny.
MEANING:  The life (soul) and the body are all His (Waheguru’s) gifts; He provides food to all living beings.  So, we must serve (worship and obey) the eternal provider Waheguru, in the shelter (guidance) of the Guru, oh Nanak.  I wish to sacrifice my life for those who worship the formless Waheguru.  Their faces are always shiny (they are unblemished) and the whole world bows to them.  1.

ਮ:3॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਮਿਲਿਐ ਉਲਟੀ ਭਈ ਨਵ ਨਿਧਿ ਖਰਚਿਉ ਖਾਉ॥ ਅਠਾਰਹ ਸਿਧੀ ਪਿਛੈ ਲਗੀਆ ਫਿਰਨਿ ਨਿਜ ਘਰਿ ਵਸੈ ਨਿਜ ਥਾਇ॥ ਅਨਹਦ ਧੁਨੀ ਸਦ ਵਜਦੇ ਉਨਮਨਿ ਹਰਿ ਲਿਵ ਲਾਇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਹਰਿ ਭਗਤਿ ਤਿਨਾ ਕੈ ਮਨਿ ਵਸੈ ਜਿਨ ਮਸਤਕਿ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਧੁਰਿ ਪਾਇ॥ 2॥
M:3: Satgur miliyaiy oolti bhuyee, nuv nidh(i) khurchiyo khaao. Attharah sidhi pichhaiy luggiyaan phirin(i), nij ghur(i) vusaiy nij thayayn.  Unhud dhooni sud vujday, oonmun(i) Hur(i) liv laayay.  Nanak Hur(i) bhugut(i) tinha kaiy mun(i) vusaiy, jin mustuk(i) likhiya dhoor(i) paayay. 2.
Word Meaning:  Oolti bhuyee = (mind) is turned around.  Nuv Nidh(i) = the nine mythical treasures of Kuber (a mythical Hindu god).  Attharah Sidhi = the 18 believed supernatural powers.  Nij Ghur(i) = in one's own mind.  Unhud Dhooni = spiritual state in which a special type of uniform music is believed to be heard.  Oonmun(i) = high spiritual state.  Liv = complete focus.   Dhoor(i) = from the beginning. 
MEANING: When one is connected with Satguru, his mind is turned around (from Maya to Waheguru), as a result of which, all the nine (mythical) treasures of Kuber become available to the Sikh for spending and eating.  All eighteen supernatural powers follow him (become his slaves), but he does not care for them as his mind stays within itself (quits wandering after worldly luxuries).  In the high spiritual state, he can “listen” to the auto-playing divine music when fully connected with Waheguru. However, only those who are predestined to do so acquire Waheguru’s Naam in their hearts, oh Nanak.  2.

ਪਉੜੀ॥ ਹਉ ਢਾਢੀ ਹਰਿ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਖਸਮ ਕਾ ਹਰਿ ਕੈ ਦਰਿ ਆਇਆ॥ ਹਰਿ ਅੰਦਰਿ ਸੁਣੀ ਪੁਕਾਰ ਢਾਢੀ ਮੁਖਿ ਲਾਇਆ॥ ਹਰਿ ਪੁਛਿਆ ਢਾਢੀ ਸਦਿ ਕੈ ਕਿਤੁ ਅਰਥਿ ਤੂ ਆਇਆ॥ ਨਿਤ ਦੇਵਹੁ ਦਾਨੁ ਦਇਆਲ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਧਿਆਇਆ॥ ਹਰਿ ਦਾਤੈ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਜਪਾਇਆ ਨਾਨਕੁ ਪੈਨਾਇਆ॥ 21॥ ਸੁਧੁ॥
Paurri:  Houn dhaadhi Hur(i) Prubh Khusm ka Hur(i) kaiy Dur(i) aaya. Hur(i) undar(i) sooni pookaar, dhaadhi mookh(i) laaya. Hur(i) poochhiya dhaadhi sud(i) kaiy kit(u) urth(i) tu aaya. Nit dayvhou daan(u) duyaal Prubh, Hur Naam(u) dhiyaya.  Hur(i) daataiy Hur(i) Naam(u) jupayaya, Nanak(u) pehnaya.  21. Soodh(u). 
Word Meaning:  Dhaadhi = bard.    Khusm = Master; Lord.   Pookaar = loud request.  Mookh(i) Laaya = allowed to “see”.  Urth(i) = purpose.   Pehnaaya = robed; honoured.
MEANING:  I am a bard (singer of praise) of the Lord, Waheguru, and came to His Door (to beg).  Waheguru heard my loud request inside and granted me the “see” of Him.  He called me in and asked me the purpose of my visit.  I begged, “Please, give the gift of Your Naam so that I may recite it all the time, oh kind God.”  The kind Waheguru honoured me by giving me His Naam to recite, oh Nanak.   21.  Verified.

ਜਨਨੀ ਜਾਨਤ ਸੁਤੁ ਬਡਾ ਹੋਤੁ ਹੈ ਇਤਨਾ ਕੁ ਨ ਜਾਨੈ ਜਿ ਦਿਨ ਦਿਨ ਅਵਧ ਘਟਤੁ ਹੈ॥ ਮੋਰ ਮੋਰ ਕਰਿ ਅਧਿਕ ਲਾਡੁ ਧਰਿ ਪੇਖਤ ਹੀ ਜਮਰਾਉ ਹਸੈ॥ 1॥
