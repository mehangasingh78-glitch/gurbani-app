(PAGE 17): Huqam(u) soyee, Toodh(u) bhaavsi, hoar(u) aakhun(u) buhout apaar(u). Nanak Sacha Patshah(u), pooch(i) na kuray beechaar(u).  4. Baba hoar(u) sauna khooshi khooaar(u). Jit(u) sootaiy tun(u) peerriyaiy, mun(u) meh(i) chulleh(i) vikaar. 1. Ruhaou. 
Word Meaning: Rus = enjoyable tastes.  Saloanay = salty.    Khut Toorsi = sour and tangy.   Maarun = spices.   Naad = Shabad (Waheguru’s praise).   Chhuteeh Amrit = the 36 accepted kinds of delicious foods.      1.     Khooshi Khooaar(u) = destroying the enjoyment.  Peerriyaiy = discomfort; suffering.    Vikaar = vices; immoral thoughts.       Pause.    Rutta Pehnun = wearing red.   Rutta = imbued.   Soopaydi = white.  Sat(u) = high morality.   Neeli = blue.   Siyaahi = ink = darkness (filth) of the mind.  Kuda Kerni = finish; remove.  Pehrun(u) = coak.   Paiyr Dhiyaan = focus of mind with love.   Kumebund = waste-band.   Suntokh = contentment.    Jobun = youthfulness; beauty.         2.           Paakhur = saddles.  Saakhut(i) = an adornment tied to the tail of a horse, which is connected to the saddle.    Boojhun(u) = wisdom.  Tayri Vaat = path leading to union with You (Waheguru).  Terkush = quiver.  Teer = arrows.   Kmaan = bow.   Saang = spear.  Teghbund = scabbard.   Goon Dhaat(u) = efforts to acquire virtues.   Nayja = javelin.  Put Siyoun Pergut(u) = appear (be approved) with honour.  Kerm = benevolence.       3.           Churrhna = riding.       Pause.        Mundar = mansion.     Pervaar(u) = family.    Bhaavsi = liked.    Aakhun(u) = orders.    Apaar(u) = beyond limits; useless.     4. 
 
MEANING:  (People work hard all their life to enjoy foods of different tastes), but a devotee, who listens to Waheguru’s Naam, feels as if he has enjoyed all the salty tastes, and all the sweet tastes when he obeys the Teachings (Shabad) of the Guru (in Waheguru’s praise). He enjoys the sour and the tangy tastes when he recites Waheguru’s Naam with his tongue, and he enjoys all the spices when he sings His praise. He feels he has enjoyed all the 36 accepted varieties of food when he fosters love for the “One” (God) with His benevolence.       1.
Eating anything else (without Waheguru’s Naam), which causes discomfort in the body and generates immoral thoughts in the mind, kills one’s joy and zest, oh, wise man.      Pause.          (People wear outfits of different colours either for religious reasons or otherwise, to please their minds), But a devotee, whose mind is imbued red with Waheguru’s love, feels as if he is wearing a red outfit; and a devotee with high morality and with selfless service to humanity feels as if he is wearing a white (pure) outfit. If one kills the filth of the mind, he feels as if he is wearing a blue colour, and focusing one’s mind on Waheguru’s Feet (Naam) is like wearing a cloak.  One’s contentment is like wearing a waistband, and Waheguru’s Naam is his wealth and youthfulness.          2.               Wearing anything else (without Waheguru’s Naam), which causes discomfort, and which generates evil ideas in the mind, kills one’s joy and zest, oh, wise man.        Pause.         Comprehending the path leading to union with Waheguru is like riding horses with saddles and saddles of gold, and efforts to acquire virtues (by singing Waheguru’s praise) are like wearing a quiver full of arrows, a bow, a spear, and a sword in a scabbard, for a devotee.  Waheguru’s benevolence toward me feels like the high-caste earning of respect in society and the playing of music with the throwing of javelins (like the salute of guns) in my honour.             3.          Riding on anything else (without Waheguru’s Naam), which causes discomfort in the body, and generates evil thoughts in the mind, kills the joy and zest, oh, wise man.        Pause.             Waheguru’s Naam is the joy of home and mansions, and His kind sight is the family for a devotee.  Only those of my commands that are approved by Waheguru are meaningful; any other order is beyond my limits.  The True Lord does not consult anyone for His decisions, oh Nanak.         4.        Sleeping (enjoying comfort) of any other type (without Waheguru’s Naam), which causes discomfort, and generates evil ideas in the mind, kills the joy and zest, oh, wise man.  Pause. 4.7.

 

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 1॥ ਕੁੰਗੂ ਕੀ ਕਾਇਆ ਰਤਨਾ ਕੀ ਲਲਿਤਾ ਅਗਰਿ ਵਾਸੁ ਤਨਿ ਸਾਸੁ॥ ਅਠਸਠਿ ਤੀਰਥ ਕਾ ਮੁਖਿ ਟਿਕਾ ਤਿਤੁ ਘਟਿ ਮਤਿ ਵਿਗਾਸੁ॥ ਓਤੁ ਮਤੀ ਸਾਲਾਹਣਾ ਸਚੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਗੁਣਤਾਸੁ॥ 1॥ ਬਾਬਾ ਹੋਰ ਮਤਿ ਹੋਰ ਹੋਰ॥ ਜੇ ਸਉ ਵੇਰ ਕਮਾਈਐ ਕੂੜੈ ਕੂੜਾ ਜੋਰੁ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਪੂਜ ਲਗੈ ਪੀਰ ਆਖੀਐ ਸਭੁ ਮਿਲੈ ਸੰਸਾਰੁ॥ ਨਾਉ ਸਦਾਏ ਆਪਣਾ ਹੋਵੈ ਸਿਧੁ ਸੁਮਾਰੁ॥ ਜਾ ਪਤਿ ਲੇਖੈ ਨ ਪਵੈ ਸਭਾ ਪੂਜ ਖੁਆਰੁ॥ 2॥ ਜਿਨਿ ਕਉ ਸਤਿਗੁਰਿ ਥਾਪਿਆ ਤਿਨ ਮੇਟ ਨ ਸਕੈ ਕੋਇ॥ ਓਨਾ ਅੰਦਰ ਨਾਮੁ ਨਿਧਾਨ ਹੈ ਨਾਮੋ ਪਰਗਟੁ ਹੋਇ॥ ਨਾਉ ਪੂਜੀਐ ਨਾਉ ਮੰਨੀਐ ਅਖੰਡ ਸਦਾ ਸਚੁ ਹੋਇ॥ 3॥ ਖੇਹੂ ਖੇਹ ਰਲਾਈਐ ਤਾ ਜੀਉ ਕੇਹਾ ਹੋਇ॥ ਜਲੀਆ ਸਭਿ ਸਿਆਣਪਾ ਉਠੀ ਚਲਿਆ ਰੋਇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮਿ ਵਿਸਾਰਿਐ ਦਰਿ ਗਇਆ ਕਿਆ ਹੋਇ॥ 4॥8॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 1: Koongoo kee kaaya, ruttna kee lalita, agar(i) vaas(u), tun(i) saas(u). Utthsutth(i) teeruth ka mookh(i) tikka, tit(u) ghut(i) mut(i) vigaas(u). Oat(u) mutee salaahna, Sach(u) Naam(u) Goontaas(u). 1. Baba, hoar mut(i), hoar, hoar. Jay suou, vayr kmaayiyaiy, koorraiy koorra joar(u). 1. Ruhaou. Pooj luggaiy, peer aakhiyaiy, subh(u) milaiy sunsaar(u).  Naoun sudaayay apna, hovaiy Sidh(u) shoomaar(u). Ja put(i) laykhaiy na puvvaiy, subbha pooj khooaar(u).  2. Jinh(i) kuou Satgur(i) thaapiya, tinh mayt na sukaiy koyay. Ohna undar Naam(u) nidhaan haiy, Naamo pergut(u) hoyay. Naaoun poojiyaiy, Naaoun munniyaiy, akhund sudaa Sach(u) hoyay. 3. Khayhoo, khayh rulaaiyaiy, ta jeeou kayha hoyay. Juliyaan subh(i) siyaanpaan, ootthi chulliya royay. Nanak Naam(i) visaariyain, Dur(i) guyyaan kiyya hoyay.  4.8.
Word Meaning: Koongoo = (pure like) saffron.  Ruttna Kee = of virtues (Waheguru’s Naam).  Lalita = tongue.   Agar(i) Vaas(u) = sweet fragrance (selfless service).   Saas(u) = (politeness in the) beaths.  Mookh(i) Tikka = (honour of) anointment on the forehead.  Vigaas(u) = thrives.   Oat(u) Mutee = with that thinking.   Goontaas(u) = Waheguru.      1.     Koorraiy = of the deceitful.    Joar(i) = efforts.       Pause.
Kmaayiyaiy = to earn.   Peer = (Muslim) spiritual leader.   Milaiy = come to visit.   Naoun  Sudaayay = famous.   Shoomaar(u) = included.   Laykhaiy Na Puvaiy = not accredited.     2.          Thaapiya = established; exalted.     Mayt = erase; dismantle.   Pergut(u) = become famous.          Akhund = eternal.        3.       
Khayhoo = dust-like body.  Jeeou = soul.   Juliyaan = burned; destroyed.      4.
MEANING: The human body, which is pure like saffron (free from all vices), which recites Waheguru’s Naam with its tongue, and which has sweet fragrance in its breaths (is very polite to others in its dealings), and has been anointed by the 68 holy places (has no filth of jealousy or deceit as if it has been washed away by the holy places) acquires the pure wisdom to sing praise of Waheguru, which is the source of all virtues.       1.           Any other type of wisdom leads one’s mind in many different directions (related to Maya).  The efforts of a Maya-engrossed individual (to acquire Waheguru’s Naam) are all hypocritical, even if he tries them hundreds of times.        Pause.            If an individual begins to worship Waheguru (or Allah), people may start calling him a “Peer”, and the whole world may come to visit him (with their wishes to be fulfilled), and he may earn a good name to be included among the accomplished Sidh yogis, but, if his worship is not recognized in God’s Court, all his efforts become worthless.             2.          Nobody can undo the exaltation of the individuals who have been exalted by Satguru, because they have the treasure of Waheguru’s Naam in their hearts, because of which they become famous. One becomes an eternal part of Waheguru by worshipping Him, and by obeying His Huqam.          3.         One cannot guess what happens to his soul when the dust-like body of a Maya-engrossed individual becomes dust after death and mixes with the dust of the earth.   All types of his wisdom get destroyed, and he cries hard when he leaves the world. One cannot imagine what happens to the individual in Waheguru’s Court when he forgets Waheguru’s Naam, oh Nanak. 4.8.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 1॥ ਗੁਣਵੰਤੀ ਗੁਣ ਵੀਥਰੈ ਅਉਗਣਵੰਤੀ ਝੂਰਿ॥ ਜੇ ਲੋੜਹਿ ਵਰ ਕਾਮਣੀ ਨਹ ਮਿਲੀਐ ਪਿਰ ਕੂਰਿ॥ ਨਾ ਬੇੜੀ ਨ ਤੁਲਹੜਾ ਨਾ ਪਾਈਐ ਪਿਰੁ ਦੂਰਿ॥ 1॥ ਮੇਰੇ ਠਾਕੁਰ ਪੂਰੈ ਤਖਤਿ ਅਡੋਲੁ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਪੂਰਾ ਜੇ ਕਰੇ ਪਾਈਐ ਸਾਚੁ ਅਤੋਲੁ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਹਰਿ ਮੰਦਰੁ ਸੋਹਣਾ ਤਿਸੁ ਮਹਿ ਮਾਣਕ ਲਾਲ॥ ਮੋਤੀ ਹੀਰਾ ਨਿਰਮਲਾ ਕੰਚਨ ਕੋਟ ਰੀਸਾਲ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਪਉੜੀ ਗੜਿ ਕਿਉ ਚੜਉ ਗੁਰ ਹਰਿ ਧਿਆਨ ਨਿਹਾਲ॥ 2॥ ਗੁਰੁ ਪਉੜੀ ਬੇੜੀ ਗੁਰੂ ਗੁਰ ਤੁਲਹਾ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਉ॥ ਗੁਰੁ ਸਰੁ ਸਾਗਰੁ ਬੋਹਿਥੋ ਗੁਰੁ ਤੀਰਥੁ ਦਰਿਆਉ॥ ਜੇ ਤਿਸੁ ਭਾਵੈ ਊਜਲੀ ਸਤਸਰਿ ਨਾਵਣ ਜਾਉ॥ 3॥ ਪੁਰੋ ਪੂਰੋ ਆਖੀਐ ਪੂਰੈ ਤਖਤਿ ਨਿਵਾਸ॥ ਪੂਰੈ ਥਾਨਿ ਸੁਹਾਵਣੇ ਪੂਰੈ ਆਸ ਨਿਰਾਸ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਪੂਰਾ ਜੇ ਮਿਲੈ ਕਿਉ ਘਾਟੇ ਗੁਣ ਤਾਸ॥ 4॥9॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 1: Goonvunti goon veethraiy, augunvuntee jhoor(i). Jay loarreh(i) Vur kaamni, neh miliyaiy Pir, koor(i).  Na bayrri, na toolhurra, na paayiyaiy Pir(u), door(i). 1. Mayray Tthaakur, Pooraiy Tukhut adoal. Gurmukh(i), poora jay kuray, paayiyaiy Saach(i) atoal(u). Ruhaou. Prubh Hur(i) Mundar sohna, Tis(u) meh(i) maanuk laal. Moti, heera nirmula, kunchun koat reesaal. Bin paurri, gurrh kiyoun chuurrhoun, Gur Hur(i) dhiyan nihaal.  2. Gur paurri, bayrri Guru, Gur toolha Hur(i) Naaoun. Gur(u) sur(u) saagar bohitho, Gur(u) teeruth duriyaaou. Jay tis(u) bhaavaiy oojli, Satsur(i) naavun jaaou. 3. Pooro, Pooro aakhiyaiy, Pooraiy Tukhut(i) nivaas. Pooraiy thaan(i) soohaavnay, Pooraiy aas, niraas. Nanak, Poora jay milaiy, kiyoun ghaatay Goontaas. 4.9.
Word Meaning: Veethraiy = spreads; preaches.  Augunvuntee = with ignoble qualities.   Jhoor(i) = cries in repentance.    Loarreh(i) = wishes for.   Vur = husband; Waheguru.    Kaamni = woman.   Koor(i) = fake ness; deceit.   Toolhurra = crude raft.       1.           Adoal = with firmness; carefree.  Atoal = immeasurable.        Pause.        Maanuk Laal = pearls and rubies (noble qualities).  Moti = pearls.  Nirmula = pure.    Kunchun Koat = fort of gold.   Reesaal = source of all tastes.    Paurri = ladder.   Gurrh = fort.  Kiyoun = how?    Nihaal = blissful.    Sur(u) = pool = sea = Saagur.    Bohitho = ship.         Jay = which; if.   Oojli = clean; pure.   Satsur(i) = gathering of holy saints.    3.          Pooro = perfect.     Soohavnay = attractive; dignified.    Pooraiy = the perfect (omnipresent) Waheguru.    Niraas = detached from worldly attractions.   Taas = his. 
MEANING: A virtuous woman (human) shares her virtues with (preaches them to) others, while the one lacking them simply cries in repentance.  If she wishes to connect with Waheguru, she cannot do so through falsehood. The Maya-engrossed individual cannot have access to Waheguru, who resides far away on the other side of the sea of Maya (above its effects), and the individual has no boat or raft to swim across the sea to reach Waheguru.        1.             My Lord (Waheguru) sits majestically (carefree) on His throne; a devotee, who has been made perfect (has acquired noble qualities like Waheguru) in the shelter of Satguru, acquires (gets connected with) the immeasurable Waheguru.         Pause.
Waheguru’s Palace is beautiful as if it is a fort built of gold, and which is full of priceless qualities of purity like pearls, rubies, and diamonds; and which is the source of all tastes.  How can I reach the tall fort of Waheguru without the appropriate ladder?   But one can be blissful (to get connected with Him) by focusing one's mind on the Guru, Waheguru.      2.   
The Guru, which is the source of Waheguru’s Naam, is everything - the ladder, the boat, and the raft – He is also the ship in the sea of Maya.  In addition, the Guru is also the holy river, a dip in which washes away all one’s sins.  If Waheguru wishes, He connects His devotees with a gathering of holy saints, which is like a holy pool, and a dip in which purifies the devotees.          3.           Everybody says that Waheguru is perfect, and so is His throne where He resides.  All places are holy with His presence, and with hope (faith) in Him, His devotees become detached from worldly attractions.  If such devotees get connected with Waheguru, why would there be any loss in their noble qualities, oh Nanak?    4.9.
