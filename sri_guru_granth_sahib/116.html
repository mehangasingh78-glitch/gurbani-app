(ਅੰਗ 115)॥      ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਸੇਵੀ ਸਬਦਿ ਸੁਹਾਇਆ॥ ਜਿਨਿ ਹਰਿ ਕਾ ਨਾਮੁ ਮੰਨਿ ਵਸਾਇਆ॥ ਹਰਿ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਹਉਮੈ ਮੈਲੁ ਗਵਾਏ ਦਰਿ ਸਚੇ ਸੋਭਾ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 2॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਗੁਰ ਨਾਮੁ ਨ ਪਾਇਆ ਜਾਇ॥ ਸਿਧ ਸਾਧਿਕ ਰਹੇ ਬਿਲਲਾਇ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਗੁਰ ਸੇਵੇ ਸੁਖੁ ਨ ਹੋਈ ਪੂਰੈ ਭਾਗਿ ਗੁਰੁ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 3॥ ਇਹੁ ਮਨੁ ਆਰਸੀ ਕੋਈ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਵੇਖੈ॥ ਮੋਰਚਾ ਨਾ ਲਾਗੈ ਜਾ ਹਉਮੈ ਸੋਖੈ॥ ਅਨਹਤ ਬਾਣੀ ਨਿਰਮਲ ਸਬਦੁ ਵਜਾਏ ਗੁਰ ਸਬਦੀ ਸਚਿ ਸਮਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 4॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਕਿਹੁ ਨ ਦੇਖਿਆ ਜਾਇ॥ ਗੁਰਿ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਕਰਿ ਆਪੁ ਦਿਤਾ ਦਿਖਾਇ॥ ਆਪੇ ਆਪਿ ਆਪਿ ਮਿਲਿ ਰਹਿਆ ਸਹਜੇ ਸਹਜਿ ਸਮਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 5॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਹੋਵੈ ਸੁ ਇਕਸੁ ਸਿਉ ਲਿਵ ਲਾਏ॥ ਦੂਜਾ ਭਰਮੁ ਗੁਰ ਸਬਦਿ ਜਲਾਏ॥ ਕਾਇਆ ਅੰਦਰਿ ਵਣਜੁ ਕਰੇ ਵਾਪਾਰਾ ਨਾਮੁ ਨਿਧਾਨੁ ਸਚੁ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 6॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਕਰਣੀ ਹਰਿ ਕੀਰਤਿ ਸਾਰੁ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਪਾਏ ਮੋਖ ਦੁਆਰੁ॥ ਅਨਦਿਨੁ ਰੰਗਿ ਰਤਾ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਵੈ ਅੰਦਰਿ ਮਹਲਿ ਬੁਲਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 7॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਦਾਤਾ ਮਿਲੈ ਮਿਲਾਇਆ॥ ਪੂਰੈ ਭਾਗਿ ਮਨਿ ਸਬਦੁ ਵਸਾਇਆ॥ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮੁ ਮਿਲੈ ਵਡਿਆਈ ਹਰਿ ਸਚੇ ਕੇ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 8॥9॥10॥
Majh Mehla 3:  Satgur sayviyaiy vudi vudiyayee. Hur(i) Ji achint(u) vusaiy mun(i) aayee. Hur(i) Jeeo sufliyo birkh(u) haiy Amrit, jin(i) peeta tis(u) tikha luhaavniya.  1. Huoun vaari jeeo vaari, Sach(u) Sungat(i) mayl(i) milaavniya.  Hur(i) Sat Sungat(i) aapay maylaiy, Gur Shabadi Hur(i) goon gaavniya. 1. Ruhaou.   
(PAGE 115):                           Satgur(u) sayveen Shabad(i) soohaya. Jin(i) Hur(i) ka Naam(u) munn(i) vusaya. Hur(i) nirmul(u), haumein maiyl(u) guvaayay, Dur(i) Sachay sobha paavniya. 2. Bin(u) Gur Naam(u) na paaya jaayay. Sidh Sadhik ruhay bil-laayay. Bin(u) Gur sayvay sookh(u) na hoyee, pooraiy bhaag(i) Gur(u) paavniya. 3. Eho mun(u) aarsi koyee Gurmukh(i) vaykhaiy. Morcha na laagaiy ja haumein sokhaiy. Unhut Bani nirmul Shabad(u) vujayay, Gur Shabadi Sach(i) smaavniya. 4.  Bin(u) Satgur kihou na daykhiya jaayay. Gur(i) kirpa kur(i) Aap(u) ditta dikhayay. Aapay Aap(i) Aap(i) mil(i) ruhiya, sehjay sehj(i) smaavniya.  5. Gurmukh(i) hovaiy su ikkus(u) siyoun liv laayay. Dooja bherm(u) Gur Shabad(i) julayay.  Kaayaan undar(i) vunuj(u) kuray vaapara, Naam(u) nidhaan(u) Sach(u) paavniya. 6.  Gurmukh(i) kerni Hur(i) keerut(i) saar(u). Gurmukh(i) paayay mokh dooaar(u). Undin(u) rung(i) rutta goon gaavaiy, undar(i) Mehl(i) boolaavniya. 7.  Satgur daata milaiy milaaya. Pooraiy bhaag(i) mun(i) Shabad(u) vusaaya. Nanak Naam(u) milaiy vudiyayee, Hur(i) Sachay kay goon gaavniya.  8.9.10.   
Word Meaning:  Savyiyaiy = serving (obeying His Huqam).  Achint(u) = without any special effort.   Sufliyo = laden with fruit (noble qualities).   Birkh(u) = tree.   Tikha = thirst (desires).   Luhaavniya = quench; remove.        1.       Vaari = offer to sacrifice.      Mayl(i) = by connecting.        Pause.       Soohaaya = pleasant; comforting.     Nirmal(u) = pure (without any flaws).       2.
Bin(u) = without.    Sidh = accomplished yogis.     Sadhik = who purify their bodies to achieve the level of a Sidh yogi.   Bil=laayay = (trying hard to achieve success but badly frustrated to the extent of) crying.      Pooraiy bhaag(u) = with full luck.        3. Aarsi = mirror.   Morcha = rust (filth of evil desires).    Sokhaiy = absorb to dry up (remove).    Unhut Bani = Guru’s Shabad that leads to a higher mental state where it is believed that auto-playing Divine music is heard.     Sach(i) = in Waheguru.        4. Kihou Na = from none else.      Aapay Aap(i) = Waheguru Himself.   Sehjay Sehj(i) = higher spiritual state where mind is at peace and quits wandering after Maya.      5. Ikkus(u) = with One (God).  Liv Laayay = focuses the mind.   Bherm(u) = misbelief.    Kaayaan = body (mind).   Vunuj = Vaapar = deal in.   Nidhaan(u) = treasure.       6. Kerni = deed (worth performing).      Keerut = singing praise.    Saar(u) = supreme.   Mokh = salvation (freedom from desires).    Rung(i) Rutta = imbued in love.      7.Milaaya = connected (by Waheguru’s grace).      Vudiyayee = respect.      8.9.10.   
MEANING:  The great recognition (reward) of serving Satguru (acting on His Shabad) is that, Waheguru makes one’s heart a place for Him to reside (enlightens his mind and stays in his memory) without any special effort on the devotee’s part.  Waheguru is like a fruit tree which is laden with (Amrit-like) virtues.  Whosoever drinks Waheguru’s Naam-Amrit has his thirst for all worldly desires quenched (is left with no desires).       1. I wish to gladly sacrifice my life for Waheguru, which connects us with Him by first connecting us with the holy congregation.  He Himself connects with the holy congregation where His praise is sung with the Guru’s Shabad.        Pause.
We serve (worship) Satguru when we act on His blissful Shabad (Advice), which pleases Waheguru, who then comes to reside in our heart. The Pure Waheguru (without any feelings) destroys the haumein of the devotee, as a result of which, the devotee is honoured in His Court.            2.          One cannot acquire the Naam (connect with Waheguru) without the Guru; that’s why the accomplished Sidh yogis and the yogis suffer frustration to the point of crying, because their efforts are not succeeding. There is no comfort (mental peace) without the Guru, and one connects with the Guru only if one has great fortune (based on one's deeds in previous lives).           3.
By taking Guru’s shelter, some rare devotee realizes that the human mind is like a mirror in which he can see his good or bad intentions (the external physical appearances can be seen with the eyes in the mirror, but you need to analyze your mind to see your interior qualities).   When one’s haumein is controlled, his mind does not get the filth of evil thoughts (and deeds).  Guru’s holy Bani leads to the high spiritual state where the auto-playing Divine music is believed to be heard, and one merges into Waheguru with the Guru’s Shabad.          4          Without the Guru, one cannot “see” Waheguru; the Guru Himself kindly “showed” me Waheguru in my heart when I realized that He is actually hiding (present imperceptibly) in everyone’s heart, and one connects with Him in the high spiritual state (above the effects of Maya).         5.
When one comes to Guru’s shelter, he concentrates his mind only on Waheguru, and burns away (gets rid of) his misbeliefs about the other (Maya).  He deals only in (thinks about) activities that are related to Waheguru in his body and mind, and acquires the eternal treasure of Waheguru’s Naam.           6.         A devotee’s supreme task is to sing Waheguru’s praise in the shelter of the Guru, whereby he reaches the door to freedom from the bonds of Maya.  Imbued with Waheguru’s love, he sings His praise day and night when he is called into His Residence (permanently connected with Him).        7.              The provider Waheguru first connects the devotee with the Guru before the devotee gets connected with Him.  With perfect fortune, the devotee gets the Guru’s Shabad embedded in his heart.   One is respected if he has Waheguru’s Naam at heart, and sings His praise, oh Nanak!     8.9.10.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ ਆਪੁ ਵੰਞਾਏ ਤਾ ਸਭ ਕਿਛੁ ਪਾਏ॥ ਗੁਰ ਸਬਦੀ ਸਚੀ ਲਿਵ ਲਾਏ॥ ਸਚੁ ਵਣੰਜਹਿ ਸਚੁ ਸੰਘਰਹਿ  ਸਚੁ ਵਾਪਾਰੁ ਕਰਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਹਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਜੀਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਹਰਿ ਗੁਣ ਅਨਦਿਨੁ ਗਾਵਣਿਆ॥ ਹਉ ਤੇਰਾ ਤੂ ਠਾਕੁਰੁ ਮੇਰਾ ਸਬਦਿ ਵਡਿਆਈ ਦੇਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਵੇਲਾ ਵਖਤ ਸਭਿ ਸੁਹਾਇਆ॥ ਜਿਤੁ ਸਚਾ ਮੇਰੇ ਮਨਿ ਭਾਇਆ॥ ਸਚੇ ਸੇਵਿਐ ਸਚੁ ਵਡਿਆਈ ਗੁਰ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਤੇ ਸਚੁ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 2॥ ਭਾਉ ਭੋਜਨੁ ਸਤਿਗੁਰਿ ਤੁਠੈ ਪਾਏ॥ ਅਨ ਰਸੁ ਚੂਕੈ ਹਰਿ ਰਸੁ ਮੰਨਿ ਵਸਾਏ॥ ਸਚੁ ਸੰਤੋਖੁ ਸਹਜ ਸੁਖੁ ਬਾਣੀ ਪੂਰੈ ਗੁਰ ਤੇ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 3॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਨ ਸੇਵਹਿ ਮੂਰਖ ਅੰਧ ਗਵਾਰਾ॥ ਫਿਰਿ ਓਇ ਕਿਥਹੁ ਪਾਇਨਿ ਮੋਖ ਦੁਆਰਾ॥ ਮਰਿ ਮਰਿ ਜੰਮਹਿ ਫਿਰਿ ਫਿਰਿ ਆਵਹਿ ਜਮ ਦਰਿ ਚੋਟਾ ਖਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 4॥ ਸਬਦੇ ਸਾਦੁ ਜਾਣਹਿ ਤਾ ਆਪੁ ਪਛਾਣਹਿ॥ ਨਿਰਮਲ ਬਾਣੀ ਸਬਦਿ ਵਖਾਣਹਿ॥ ਸਚੇ ਸੇਵਿ ਸਦਾ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਾਇਨਿ ਨਉਨਿਧਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਮੰਨਿ ਵਸਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 5॥ ਸੋ ਥਾਨੁ ਸੁਹਾਇਆ ਜੋ ਹਰਿ ਮਨਿ ਭਾਇਆ॥ ਸਤ ਸੰਗਤਿ ਬਹਿ ਹਰਿ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਇਆ॥ ਅਨਦਿਨੁ ਹਰਿ ਸਾਲਾਹਹਿ ਸਾਚਾ ਨਿਰਮਲ ਨਾਦੁ ਵਜਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 6॥
