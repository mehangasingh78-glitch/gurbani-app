(ਅੰਗ 76)॥     ਅੰਤਿ ਕਾਲਿ ਪਛੁਤਾਸੀ ਅੰਧੁਲੇ ਜਾ ਜਮਿ ਪਕੜਿ ਚਲਾਇਆ॥ ਸਭੁ ਕਿਛੁ ਅਪੁਨਾ ਕਰਿ ਕਰਿ ਰਾਖਿਆ ਖਿਨ ਮਹਿ ਭਇਆ ਪਰਾਇਆ॥ ਬੁਧਿ ਵਿਸਰਜੀ ਗਈ ਸਿਆਣਪ ਕਰਿ ਅਵਗਣ ਪਛੁਤਾਇ॥ ਕਹੁ ਨਾਨਕ ਪ੍ਰਾਣੀ ਤੀਜੈ ਪਹਰੈ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਚੇਤਹੁ ਲਿਵ ਲਾਇ॥ 3॥ ਚਉਥੈ ਪਹਰੈ ਰੈਣਿ ਕੈ ਵਣਜਾਰਿਆ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਬਿਰਧਿ ਭਇਆ ਤਨੁ ਖੀਣੁ॥ ਅਖੀ ਅੰਧੁ ਨ ਦੀਸਈ ਵਣਜਾਰਿਆ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਕੰਨੀ ਸੁਣੈ ਨ ਵੈਣ॥ ਅਖੀ ਅੰਧੁ ਜੀਭ ਰਸੁ ਨਾਹੀ ਰਹੇ ਪਰਾਕਉ ਤਾਣਾ॥ ਗੁਣ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਨਾਹੀ ਕਿਉ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਾਵੈ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਆਵਣ ਜਾਣਾ॥ ਖੜੁ ਪਕੀ ਕੁੜਿ ਭਜੈ ਬਿਨਸੈ  ਆਇ ਚਲੈ ਕਿਆ ਮਾਣੁ॥ ਕਹੁ ਨਾਨਕ ਪ੍ਰਾਣੀ ਚਉਥੈ ਪਹਰੈ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਸਬਦੁ ਪਛਾਣੁ॥ 4॥ ਓੜਕੁ ਆਇਆ ਤਿਨ ਸਾਹਿਆ ਵਣਜਾਰਿਆ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਜਰੁ ਜਰਵਾਣਾ ਕੰਨਿ॥ ਇਕ ਰਤੀ ਗੁਣ ਨ ਸਮਾਣਿਆ ਵਣਜਾਰਿਆ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਅਵਗਣ ਖੜਸਨਿ ਬੰਨਿ॥ ਗੁਣ ਸੰਜਮਿ ਜਾਵੈ ਚੋਟ ਨਾ ਖਾਵੈ ਨਾ ਤਿਸੁ ਜੰਮਣੁ ਮਰਣਾ॥ ਕਾਲੁ ਜਾਲੁ ਜਮੁ ਜੋਹਿ ਨ ਸਾਕੈ ਭਾਇ ਭਗਤਿ ਭੈ ਤਰਣਾ॥ ਪਤਿ ਸੇਤੀ ਜਾਵੈ ਸਹਜਿ ਸਮਾਵੈ ਸਗਲੇ ਦੂਖ ਮਿਟਾਵੈ॥ ਕਹੁ ਨਾਨਕ ਪ੍ਰਾਣੀ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਛੂਟੈ ਸਾਚੈ ਤੇ ਪਤਿ ਪਾਵੈ॥ 5॥2॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 1:  Pehlaiy pehraiy raiyn(i) kaiy vunjaariya mittra, baaluk boodh(i) achayt(u).  Kheer(u) peeyaiy khailayiyaiy vunjaariya mittra maat pita soot hayt(u). Maat pita soot nayhou ghunayra, Maya moh(u) subaayee. Sunjogi aaya kirt(u) kmaaya kerni kaar kraayee. Ram Naam bin(u) mookt(i) na hoyee, boodi doojaiy hayt(i).  Kuhou Nanak praani pehlaiy pehraiy chootehinga Hur(i) chayt(i).     1. Word Meaning:  Baaluk = child.    Boodh(i) = wisdom.    Achayt(u) = unaware.    Kheer(u) = milk.    Soot = son.     Nayhou = affection.     Ghunayra = deep.    Moh(u) = attachment.    Subaayee = all; the whole world.    Sunjogi = by destiny.    Kirt(u) = the result of the deeds (done in the previous lives).    Kerni Kaar = deeds.   Mookt(i) = emancipation.   Boodi = drowned.     Doojaiy Hayt(i) = in the love of Maya.   Chhootehinga = will get freedom from the bonds of Maya.       1.
MEANING:  In the first quarter of life, the wisdom of a child is not developed yet, and he is unaware of the real objective of life.  He is fed milk, and his parents play with him affectionately. The mother and father love their son deeply, as everyone loves Maya (including family).  Man comes into this world as per his destiny based upon the deeds of his previous lives, and continues to perform similar deeds in this life as well.  But he does not realize that emancipation is not possible without Waheguru’s Naam, and the whole world is drowning in the sea of Maya.  (Guru) Nanak advises that the man must remember Waheguru in the first quarter of his life to cut the bonds of Maya.   1.  Doojaiy Pehraiy raiyn(i) kaiy vunjaariya mittra, bhur(i) jobun(i) maiy mut(i).  Ehinis(i) kaam(i) viyapiya vunjaariya mittra, undhulay Naam(u) na chit(i).  Ram Naam(u) ghut untar(i) naahi, hoar(i) jaanaiy rus kus meetthay. Giyan(u) dhiyaan goon sunjum(u) naahi, junum(i) murhougay jhootthay.  Teeruth vert sooch(i) sunjum(u) naahi, kerm(u) dherm(u) nuhee Pooja. Nanak bhaayay bhagat(i) nistaara, doobidha viyaapaiy dooja.  2. Word Meaning:  Bhur(i) Jobun(i) = in prime youth.  Maiy Mut(i) = inebriated state.   Ehinis(i) = day and night.   Kaam(i) = in lust.   Viyapiya = under the influence.  Undhulay = blind.   Chit(i) = in memory.   Ghut = heart.   Kus = sour.  Sunjum(u) = control on sensual desires.  Jhootthay = which is not true (everlasting; perishable.   Vert = vows (to do or not to do certain things).  Sooch(i) = purity (of mind).   Dherm Kerm = deeds of high morality.    Pooja = worship.   Bhaayay = affection.  Nistaara = salvation.   Doobidha = doubts (caught between love for Maya and God).  Viyaapaiy = influences.     Dooja = Maya.    2. MEANING:  In the second quarter of life, the man is in the prime of his youth, and is in the inebriated state of mind under the influence of Maya.  The trader in Waheguru’s Naam is possessed by lust day and night, and the blind of wisdom does not remember God. Waheguru’s Naam is not in his mind while he enjoys all other sweet and sour tastes.  He makes no connection with Waheguru, and does not ponder on His Naam; he makes no effort to control his sensual desires, and as a result of all these, the perishable man is caught in the rebirth cycle. (Even though they are worthless), But, he does not even perform Hindu religious rituals like pilgrimage, commitments to do or not to do certain things, purity, control of sensual desires, idol worship or other religious deeds.  However, the only way to find salvation is to remember Waheguru; otherwise, Maya exerts its powerful influence, and the man spends his life in misgivings.     2. Teejaiy pehraiy raiyn(i) kaiy vunjaariya mittra, sir(i) huns ooluthrray aayay.  Jobun(u) ghutaiy jurooaa jinaiy vunjaariya mittra, aav ghutaiy din(u) jaayay. 
(Page 76): Unt(i) kaal(i) puchhutaasi undhulay, ja Jum pukurr(i) chulaaya. Subh(u) kichh(u) aapuna kur(i) kur(i) rakhiya, khin meh(i) bhuyya praaya.  Boodh(i) visurji guyee siyaanup, kur(i) uvgun puchhtaayay.  Kuhou Nanak praani teejaiy pehraiy, Prubh(u) chaytou liv laayay.      3. Word Meaning:  Huns = swan (white hair).  Ooluthrray = landed.   Jobun(u) = youth.  Jurooa = old age.   Jinaiy = wins; takes over.    Aav = span of life.   Unt(i) Kaal(i) = at the end.    Jum(i) = the Angel of death.   Khin = in a moment.   Boodh(i) = wisdom.  Visurji = went away.   Uvgun = evil deeds.   Puchhtaayay= repents.    Chaytou = remember.  Liv Laayay = with full dedication. 

 

MEANING:  White hair grows on the head (the man becomes old) in the third quarter of life, oh the trader of Waheguru’s Naam. Youth begins to recede, and old age takes over; and the life becomes shorter with every day gone by.  At the end of life, the blind man repents (because he did not remember Waheguru) when the Angel of death controls him, and takes him away.  He had thought that everything belonged to him, but all those become strangers to him in a moment.  His wisdom leaves him, and all his shrewdness is lost (rendering him helpless in front of the Angel).  He repents for the evil deeds he had done.  (Guru) Nanak advises that the man should worship Waheguru with full dedication in the third quarter of his life.       3. Chauthaiy pehraiy raiyn(i) kaiy vunjaariya mittra, birdh(i) bhuyya tun(u) kheen(u). Ukheen Undh(u) na deesyee, vunjaariya mittra, kunni soonaiy na, vaiyn. Ukheen undh(u) jeebh rus(u) naahi, ruhhay praakou taana. Goon untar(i) naahi kiyoun sookh(u) paavaiy, munmukh aavun jaana. Khurr(u) pukki, koorr(i) bhujaiy, binsaiy, aayay chulaiy, kiya maan(u). Kuhou Nanak praani chauthaiy pehraiy, Gurmukh(i) Shabad(u) puchhaan(u).        4. Word Meaning:  Birdh(i) Bhuyya = becomes old.   Kheen(u) = weak.   Vaiyn = wails  loudly.   Ruhhay = left behind.    Praakou Taana = force; energy.  Khurr(u) = the dried stem of crops like wheat.  Koorr(i) = crunch.   Bhujaiy Binsaiy = broken and destroyed.   Maan(u) = pride.  Shabad(u) Puchhaan(u) = Recognize Guru’s Shabad.   4.
MEANING: In the fourth quarter of life, man gets old, and his body becomes weak. He is almost blind and cannot see with his eyes, and cannot hear with his ears, so he cries loudly (for help).   He is almost blind and cannot enjoy tastes with his tongue. He is left with no energy. How can he have comfort when he has no good quality (virtue) in him?   The follower of his mind is caught in the birth-death-birth cycle.  Like the stem of a wheat crop, which is ready for harvesting, and which is brittle and loses its crunch to be easily destroyed, the bones of an old man become brittle, which can break easily.  So, what can he take pride in?  One should recognize Guru’s Shabad (make connection with Him) in the fourth quarter of life, oh man, advises (Guru) Nanak.  4. Orruk(u) aaya tin sahiyaan vunjaariya mittra, jur(u) jervaana kunn(i).  Ikk rutti goon na smaaniya vunjaariya mittra, uvgun khurrsun(i) bunnh(i). Goon sunjum(i) jaavaiy choat na khaavaiy, na tis(u) jumun(u) merna. Kaal(u) jaal(u) Jum(u) joh(i) na saakaiy, bhaayay bhugut(i) bhaiy terna.  Put(i) saytee jaavaiy sehj(i) smaavaiy suglay dookh mitaavaiy. Kuhou Nanak praani Gurmukh(i) chhootaiy, Sachaiy tay put(i) paavaiy. 5.2.
Word Meaning:  Orruk(u) = end.    Sahiyaan = breaths.   Jur(u) = old age.   Jervaana= powerful (and cruel).     Kunn(i) = on the shoulder; close.  Rutti = miniscule.  Smaaniya = absorbed; acquired.   Uvgun = bad qualities; vices.  Khurrsun(i) = will take away.     Bunnh(i) = tied up.  Choat = punishment.  Joh(i) = watch.   Bhaiy = Bhuv = the world sea.   Put(i) = dignity.   Sehj(i) = highest spiritual state where Maya has no effect.   Suglay = all.   5.
MEANING:  The end of life (last moment) finally arrives in the form of powerful (and cruel) old age.  The man did not acquire even a minuscule of virtuous qualities during his lifetime. Tied up, his evil qualities take him away (accompany him to the next world).  If a man leaves this world with control over his sensual desires, he will not be punished and will never be reborn.  The net of Maya, the fear of death, or the Angel of death cannot even come close to him.  Having attained the highest spiritual level, such an individual is freed from all suffering and enters the next world with dignity.  Man can break the bonds of Maya only by taking shelter of the Guru, and is honoured by Waheguru, oh Nanak!  5.2.

ਸਿਰੀਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 4॥ ਪਹਿਲੈ ਪਹਰੈ ਰੈਣਿ ਕੈ ਵਣਜਾਰਿਆ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਹਰਿ ਪਾਇਆ ਉਦਰ ਮੰਝਾਰਿ॥ ਹਰਿ ਧਿਆਵੈ ਹਰਿ ਉਚਰੈ ਵਣਜਾਰਿਆ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਸਮਾਰਿ॥ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਜਪੇ ਆਰਾਧੇ ਵਿਚਿ ਅਗਨੀ ਹਰਿ ਜਪਿ ਜੀਵਿਆ॥ ਬਾਹਰਿ ਜਨਮੁ ਭਇਆ ਮੁਖਿ ਲਾਗਾ ਸਰਸੇ ਪਿਤਾ ਮਾਤ ਥੀਵਿਆ॥ ਜਿਸ ਕੀ ਵਸਤੁ ਤਿਸੁ ਚੇਤਹੁ ਪ੍ਰਾਣੀ ਕਰਿ ਹਿਰਦੈ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਬੀਚਾਰਿ॥ ਕਹੁ ਨਾਨਕ ਪ੍ਰਾਣੀ ਪਹਿਲੈ ਪਹਰੈ ਹਰਿ ਜਪੀਐ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਧਾਰਿ॥ 1॥
ਦੂਜੈ ਪਹਰੈ ਰੈਣਿ ਕੈ ਵਣਜਾਰਿਆ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਮਨੁ ਲਾਗਾ ਦੂਜੈ ਭਾਇ॥ ਮੇਰਾ ਮੇਰਾ ਕਰਿ ਪਾਲੀਐ ਵਣਜਾਰਿਆ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਲੇ ਮਾਤ ਪਿਤਾ ਗਲਿ ਲਾਇ॥ ਲਾਵੈ ਮਾਤ ਪਿਤਾ ਸਦਾ ਗਲ ਸੇਤੀ ਮਨਿ ਜਾਣੈ ਖਟਿ ਖਵਾਏ॥ ਜੋ ਦੇਵੈ ਤਿਸੈ ਨ ਜਾਣੈ ਮੂੜਾ ਦਿਤੇ ਨੋ ਲਪਟਾਏ॥ ਕੋਈ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਹੋਵੈ ਸੁ ਕਰੈ ਵੀਚਾਰੁ ਹਰਿ ਧਿਆਵੈ ਮਨਿ ਲਿਵ ਲਾਇ॥ ਕਹੁ ਨਾਨਕ ਦੂਜੈ ਪਹਰੈ ਪ੍ਰਾਣੀ ਤਿਸੁ ਕਾਲੁ ਨ ਕਬਹੂੰ ਖਾਇ॥ 2॥ ਤੀਜੈ ਪਹਰੈ ਰੈਣਿ ਕੈ ਵਣਜਾਰਿਆ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਮਨੁ ਲਗਾ ਆਲਿ ਜੰਜਾਲਿ॥ ਧਨੁ ਚਿਤਵੈ ਧਨੁ ਸੰਚਵੈ ਵਣਜਾਰਿਆ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮਾ ਹਰਿ ਨ ਸਮਾਲਿ॥ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮਾ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਕਦੇ ਨ ਸਮਾਲੈ ਜਿ ਹੋਵੈ ਅੰਤਿ ਸਖਾਈ॥
