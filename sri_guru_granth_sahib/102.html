(ਅੰਗ 101)॥    ਜੋ ਜੋ ਪੀਵੈ ਸੋ ਤ੍ਰਿਪਤਾਵੈ॥ ਅਮਰੁ ਹੋਵੈ ਜੋ ਨਾਮ ਰਸੁ ਪਾਵੈ॥ ਨਾਮ ਨਿਧਾਨ ਤਿਸਹਿ ਪਰਾਪਤਿ ਜਿਸੁ ਸਬਦ ਗੁਰੂ ਮਨਿ ਵੂਠਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਜਿਨਿ ਹਰਿ ਰਸੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਸੋ ਤ੍ਰਿਪਤਿ ਅਘਾਨਾ॥ ਜਿਨਿ ਹਰਿ ਸਾਦੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਸੋ ਨਾਹਿ ਡੁਲਾਨਾ॥ ਤਿਸਹਿ ਪਰਾਪਤਿ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮਾ ਜਿਸੁ ਮਸਤਕਿ ਭਾਗੀਠਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 3॥ ਹਰਿ ਇਕਸੁ ਹਥਿ ਆਇਆ ਵਰਸਾਣੇ ਬਹੁਤੇਰੇ॥ ਤਿਸੁ ਲਗਿ ਮੁਕਤੁ ਭਏ ਘਣੇਰੇ॥ ਨਾਮੁ ਨਿਧਾਨਾ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਪਾਈਐ ਕਹੁ ਨਾਨਕ ਵਿਰਲੀ ਡੀਠਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥15॥22॥
Majh Mehla 5:  Amrit Naam(u) suda nirmuliya. Sookhdaayee dookh bidaarun Huriya.  Uver(u) saad chukh(i) suglay daykhay, mun Hur(i) rus(u) subh tay meettha jeeo.  1.
(PAGE 101):          Jo, jo peevaiy so triptaavaiy. Amar(u) hovaiy jo Naam rus(u) paavaiy.  Naam nidhaan tis-hay praput(i), jis(s) Shabad Guru mun(i) voottha jeeo. 2.  Jin(i) Hur(i) rus(u) paaya so triput(i) aghaana. Jin(i) Hur(i) saad(u) paaya, so naahay doolaana. Tis-hay praput(i) Hur(i) Hur(i) Naama, jis(u) mustik(i) bhaageettha jeeo.  3. Hur(i) ikkus(u) huth(i) aaya, vursaanay buhoutayray.  Tis(u) lug(i) mookut(u) bhuyay ghunayray.  Naam nidhaana Gurmukh(i) paayiyaiy, kuhou Nanak virleen deettha jeeo.  4.15.22. 
Word Meaning: Nirmuliya = purifier.  Dookh Bidaarun Huriya = the destroyer of discomforts (worries), Waheguru.  Uver(i) = other.   Saad = tastes (n); delicious dishes.     Chukh(i) = taste (v).     1.       Triptaavaiy = becomes content.  Amar(u) = immortal.  Nidhaan = treasure.  Praput(i) = available, is acquired.     Voottha = reside.     2.     Triput(i) = Aghaana = contented.      Doolaana = lose faith (or self-control).    Mustuk(i) = on the forehead.     Bhaagittha = good luck.       3.        Ikkus(u) Huth(i) = in the hand of only one person (Guru).   Vursaanay = those who benefit from.    Ghunayray = very many.     Virleen = some rare person.     4.
MEANING:  Waheguru’s Amrit-like Naam always purifies one’s mind because the source of all comfort, Waheguru, is the destroyer of all troubles (evils).   I have tasted all other delicious dishes, but Waheguru’s Naam is the sweetest of all.         1.        Whosoever drinks Waheguru’s Naam-Amrit becomes fully content and becomes immortal (not bodily, but spiritually as he becomes one with Waheguru).  However, the treasure of Naam-Amrit (Waheguru) is available only to those who have made their heart the residence for Guru’s Shabad.        2.
Those who are able to acquire the essence of Waheguru’s Naam are fully contented, and their faith is never shaken.  However, only those who have acquired Waheguru’s Naam have good luck carved on their forehead. (It is a belief that one’s luck is revealed on one's forehead.      3.           Waheguru’s Naam is given only to one individual – the Guru – but there are countless who can benefit from that.  By obeying the Guru, many, many people have found salvation. The Naam-treasure can be had only through the Guru's benevolence, but only a few rare individuals actually realize it, oh Nanak.   4.15.22

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਨਿਧਿ ਸਿਧਿ ਰਿਧਿ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਮੇਰੈ॥ ਜਨਮੁ ਪਦਾਰਥੁ ਗਹਿਰ ਗੰਭੀਰੈ॥ ਲਾਖ ਕੋਟਿ ਖੁਸੀਆ ਰੰਗ ਰਾਵੈ ਜੋ ਗੁਰ ਲਾਗਾ ਪਾਈ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਦਰਸਨੁ ਪੇਖਤ ਭਏ ਪੁਨੀਤਾ॥ ਸਗਲ ਉਧਾਰੇ ਭਾਈ ਮੀਤਾ॥ ਅਗਮ ਅਗੋਚਰੁ ਸੁਆਮੀ ਅਪੁਨਾ ਗੁਰ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਤੇ ਸਚੁ ਧਿਆਈ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਜਾ ਕਉ ਖੋਜਹਿ ਸਰਬ ਉਪਾਏ॥ ਵਡਭਾਗੀ  ਦਰਸਨੁ ਕੋ ਵਿਰਲਾ ਪਾਏ॥ ਊਚ ਅਪਾਰ ਅਗੋਚਰ ਥਾਨਾ ਓਹੁ ਮਹਲੁ ਗੁਰੂ ਦੇਖਾਈ ਜੀਉ॥ 3॥ ਗਹਿਰ ਗੰਭੀਰ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਨਾਮੁ ਤੇਰਾ॥ ਮੁਕਤਿ ਭਇਆ ਜਿਸੁ ਰਿਦੈ ਵਸੇਰਾ॥ ਗੁਰਿ ਬੰਧਨ ਤਿਨ ਕੇ ਸਗਲੇ ਕਾਟੇ ਜਨ ਨਾਨਕ ਸਹਜਿ ਸਮਾਈ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥16॥23॥
Majh Mehla 5:  Nidh(i) sidh(i) ridh(i) Hur(i) Hur(i) Hur(i) mayraiy. Junum(u) pdaruth(u) Gehr Gumbheeraiy.  Laakh koat(i) khooshiyaan rung raavaiy, jo Gur laaga paayeen jeeo. Dershun paykhut bhuyyay pooneeta. Sugal oodharay bhaayee meeta. Agumm agochur (u) Soami apuna, Gur kirpa tay Sach(u) dhiyaayeen jeeo. 2.  Ja kuou khojeh(i) serb oopaayay. Vudbhaagi ko virla paayay. Ooch, apaar, agoachur thaana, oho mehl Guru daykhaayee jeeo. 3.  Gehr gumbhheer Amrit Naam(u) Tayra.  Mookt(i) bhuyya jis(u) ridaiy vusayra. Gur bundhun tin kay suglay kaatay, jun Nanak sehj(i) smaayee jeeo.  4.16.23.
Word Meaning:  Nidh(i) = (nine) treasures of the mythical god, Kuber.   Ridh(i) Sidh(i) = (eighteen) supernatural powers.   Junum(u) Pdaruth(u) = the objective of human life (Waheguru’s Naam).   Gehr = unfathomable.   Gumbheeraiy = unemotional; never changing in any way.  Laakh kot(i) = millions.   Rung Raavaiy = enjoys.     1.
Paykhut = on “seeing”.   Pooneeta = holy, pious.   Oodhaaray = salvaged.   Agumm = (physically) unreachable.       Agoachar(u) = incomprehensible.       2.
Ooch = High (above the effects of Maya).  Apaar = limitless.   Thaana = residence.  3. 
Mookut(i) bhuyya = became free from the bonds of Maya.   Vusayra = residence.   Bundhun = bonds of Maya.   Sehj(i) = spiritual state above the effects of Maya.     4.
MEANING:  I have Waheguru’s Naam in my heart, which is like having all the nine treasures of Kuber and all the 18 supernatural powers, because the unfathomable, and never-changing Waheguru, is the source of bliss, and connecting with Him is the objective of human life.   For this reason, an individual who takes shelter at the Guru’s feet feels blissful as if he is enjoying countless happy occasions.            1.         He becomes completely free of all evil thoughts on “seeing” (connecting with) the Guru, and not only that, but he also enables his brothers and friends (his companions) to find salvation. The physically unreachable and incomprehensible Waheguru can be worshiped (and realized) only by the benevolence of the Guru.           2.
Waheguru, which is searched for by all His creation, is realized only by some rare, lucky individual.  The residence of Waheguru, which is above the effects of Maya, is unreachable and incomprehensible, and is shown by the Guru. (The Guru connects with Him).       3.
Your Naam is the real life-sustaining liquid (of immortality), oh unfathomable and never-changing Waheguru!  Whosoever makes his heart a residence for Your Naam becomes free from the bonds of Maya because the Guru cuts his bonds, and the devotee attains the higher spiritual state which is above the effects of Maya, oh Nanak.  4.16.23.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਤੇ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਧਿਆਵਉ॥ ਪ੍ਰਭੂ ਦਇਆ ਤੇ ਮੰਗਲ ਗਾਵਉ॥ ਊਠਤ ਬੈਠਤ ਸੋਵਤ ਜਾਗਤ ਹਰਿ ਧਿਆਈਐ ਸਗਲ ਅਵਰਦਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਨਾਮੁ ਅਉਖਧੁ ਮੋ ਕਉ ਸਾਧੂ ਦੀਆ॥ ਕਿਲਬਿਖ ਕਾਟੇ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਥੀਆ॥ ਅਨਦੁ ਭਇਆ ਨਿਕਸੀ ਸਭ ਪੀਰਾ ਸਗਲ ਬਿਨਾਸੇ ਦਰਦਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਜਿਸ ਕਾ ਅੰਗੁ ਕਰੇ ਮੇਰਾ ਪਿਆਰਾ॥ ਸੋ ਮੁਕਤਾ ਸਾਗਰ ਸੰਸਾਰਾ॥ ਸਤਿ ਕਰੇ ਜਿਨਿ ਗੁਰੂ ਪਛਾਤਾ ਸੋ ਕਾਹੇ ਕਉ ਡਰਦਾ ਜੀਉ॥3॥ ਜਬ ਤੇ ਸਾਧੂ ਸੰਗਤ ਪਾਏ॥ਗੁਰ ਭੇਟਤ ਹਉ ਗਈ ਬਲਾਏ॥ ਸਾਸਿ ਸਾਸਿ ਹਰਿ ਗਾਵੈ ਨਾਨਕੁ ਸਤਿਗੁਰਿ ਢਾਕਿ ਲੀਆ ਮੇਰਾ ਪੜਦਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥17॥24॥
Majh Mehla 5:  Prubh kirpa tay Hur(i) Hur(i) dhiyaavoun. Prubhoo duyya tay mungal gaavoun. Ootthut baiytthut, sovut jaagut, Hur(i) dhiyayiyaiy sugal averda jeeo. 1.  Naam(u) aukhudh(u) mo kuou Sadhu deeya. Kilbikh kaatay nirmul(u) theeya. Anund bhuyya niksi subh peera, sugal binaasay durda jeeo. 2. Jis ka ung(u) kuray mayra Piyara. So mookta saagur sunsaara.  Sat(i) kuray jin(i) Guru puchhaata, so kaahay kuou durda jeeo. 3. Jub tay Sadhu sungat paayay. Gur bhaytut huoun guyee bulaayay. Saas(i) saas(i) Hur(i) gaavaiy Nanak(u), Satgur(i) dhaak(i) leeya mayra purrda jeeo. 4.17.24.
Word Meaning:  Mungal = songs of happiness (or of praise of Waheguru which produces bliss).      Averda = life.     1.       Aoukhudh(u) = cure; medicine.    Sadhu = Guru.   Kilbikh = sins.    Nirmul = clean (of all evil).    Theeya = became.    Anund = bliss.    Binasay = destroyed.      2.     Ung(u) = favour; support.    Mookta = free (from the bonds of Maya).   Sat(i) Kuray = acknowledge to be True (have full faith).   Puchhaata = recognized (made a connection).     3.
Jub Tay = since.   Bhaytut = on meeting.    Bulaayay = evil spirits; worries; troubles.   Purrda = secrets (weaknesses).     4.
MEANING: I recite Waheguru’s Naam and sing His blissful praise only because of His benevolence. One should recite Waheguru’s Naam all his life, no matter what he is doing – standing, sitting, asleep or awake.            1.            The Guru has given me Waheguru’s Naam (connected me with Him), which is the cure for all (mental problems, which also give birth to physical ailments).  Now all my sins are gone, and I am completely clean (of all evils).  I am blissful now, and all my ailments and troubles are gone.        2.
An individual, who has the support of my dear Waheguru, is salvaged in this world. Those who have recognized the Guru (get connected with Him) have full faith in the existence of Waheguru, and are not afraid of anyone or anything.          3.
Ever since I have been connected with the Guru, all my worries and troubles have disappeared at the sight of Him.  Now, I recite Waheguru’s Naam with every breath, and the Guru has covered (removed) all my weaknesses, oh Nanak!   4. 17. 24.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਓਤਿ ਪੋਤਿ ਸੇਵਕ ਸੰਗਿ ਰਾਤਾ॥ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਪ੍ਰਤਿਪਾਲੇ ਸੇਵਕ ਸੁਖਦਾਤਾ॥ ਪਾਣੀ ਪਖਾ ਪੀਸਉ ਸੇਵਕ ਕੈ ਠਾਕੁਰ ਕਾ ਹੀ ਆਹਰੁ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਕਾਟਿ ਸਿਲਕ ਪ੍ਰਭਿ ਸੇਵਾ ਲਾਇਆ॥ ਹੁਕਮੁ ਸਾਹਿਬ ਕਾ ਸੇਵਕ ਮਨਿ ਭਾਇਆ॥ ਸੋਈ ਕਮਾਵੈ ਜੋ ਸਾਹਿਬ ਭਾਵੈ ਸੇਵਕੁ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਬਾਹਰਿ ਮਾਹਰੁ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਤੂੰ ਦਾਨਾ ਠਾਕੁਰੁ ਸਭ ਬਿਧਿ ਜਾਨਹਿ॥
