(ਅੰਗ 53)॥           ਭਾਈ ਰੇ ਸਾਚੀ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਸੇਵ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਤੁਠੈ ਪਾਈਐ ਪੂਰਨ ਅਲਖ ਅਭੇਵ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਵਿਟਹੁ ਵਾਰਿਆ ਜਿਨਿ ਦਿਤਾ ਸਚੁ ਨਾਉ॥ ਅਨਦਿਨੁ ਸਚੁ ਸਲਾਹਣਾ ਸਚੇ ਕੇ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਉ॥ ਸਚੁ ਖਾਣਾ ਸਚੁ ਪੈਨਣਾ ਸਚੇ ਸਚਾ ਨਾਉ॥ 2॥ ਸਾਸਿ ਗਿਰਾਸਿ ਨ ਵਿਸਰੈ ਸਫਲ ਮੂਰਤਿ ਗੁਰੁ ਆਪਿ॥ ਗੁਰ ਜੇਵਡੁ ਅਵਰੁ ਨ ਦਿਸਈ ਆਠ ਪਹਰ ਤਿਸੁ ਜਾਪਿ॥ ਨਦਰਿ ਕਰੇ ਤਾ ਪਾਈਐ ਸਚੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਗੁਣਤਾਸਿ॥ 3॥ ਗੁਰੁ ਪਰਮੇਸਰੁ ਏਕੁ ਹੈ ਸਭਿ ਮਹਿ ਰਹਿਆ ਸਮਾਇ॥ ਜਿਨ ਕਉ ਪੂਰਬਿ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਸੇਈ ਨਾਮੁ ਧਿਆਇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਗੁਰ ਸਰਣਾਗਤੀ ਮਰੈ ਨ ਆਵੈ ਜਾਇ॥ 4॥30॥100॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 5:  Sunt junou soon(i) bhaayeeo chhootun Saachaiy Naayay. Gur kay Churn srayvnay teeruth Hur(i) ka Naoun. Aagaiy Durgeh munniyeh(i) milaiy nithaavaiyn thaaon. 1. 
(PAGE 53):  Bhaayee ray, Saachi Satgur sayv. Satgur tootthaiy payeeyaiy, Poorun Alukh Abhayv. 1. Ruhaou. Satgur vitoun vaariya, jin(i) ditta Sach Naoun. Undin(u) Sach(u) slaahna Sachay kay goon gaaoun. Sach(u) khaana, Sach(u) pehnuna, Sachay Sacha Naoun. 2. Saas(i) giraas(i) na visraiy suful moorut(i) Gur(u) aap(i).  Gur jayvud uver(u) na disyee, aatth pehr Tis(u) jaap(i).  Nudr(i) kuray ta payeeyaiy Sach(u) Naam(u) Goontaas(i). 3. Gur(u) Permayshur(u) aik(u) haiy, subh(i) meh(i) ruhiya smaayay. Jin kuou poorub(i) likhiya, sayee Naam(u) dhiyayay. Nanak Gur shenaagti, muraiy na aavaiy jaayay.  4.30.100.
Word Meaning:  Chhootun = freedom (from the bonds of Maya); emancipation.   Gur Kay Churn = feet of the Guru (His Shabad or Teachings).     Srayvnay = serve (obey Guru’s Teachings).   Teeruth = holy places (pilgrimage).   Durgeh = Waheguru’s Court.   Munniyeh(i) = acknowledged (honoured).    Nithaavaiyn = homeless (no support).       1.
Toothaiy = pleased to be kind.   Alukh = intangible.   Abhayv = enigmatic.      Pause.
Vitoun = on; for.     Vaariya = be a sacrifice.    Undin(u) = every day; all the time.   Slaahna = Goon Gaaoun = sing praise.   Pehnuna = wearing; dress up.        2. 
Giraas(i) = (with every) nibble.   Suful = successful.    Moorut(i) = persona.   Jayvud = as great.  Uver(e) = else.   Aatth Pehr = twenty-four hours.  Jaap(i) = recite (His Naam).     Goontaas(i) = treasure of noble qualities.         3.        Permayshur(u) = Supreme Being.    Smaayay = get completely absorbed in.    Poorub(i) Likhiya = predestined.    Dhiyayay = recite Naam.    Shernangti = in the shelter.  4
MEANING: Hey, lovers of God, hey, my brothers, listen carefully: the bonds of Maya can be broken only by fostering Waheguru’s Naam in your hearts.  Rather than visiting holy places, keep Waheguru’s Naam in your mind, because Naam is the real holy place; and rather than worshiping any god, worship the Feet of Satguru (obey His Word).  By doing this, you will be recognized in Waheguru’s Court, as a result of which, even a homeless (having no support) person finds a permanent home (becomes “one” with Waheguru).            1.               Service to the Guru is only True service (the sweetness of) which lasts forever.  When the Guru is pleased, he connects us with Waheguru, who is incomprehensible and enigmatic.      Pause.
I am a sacrifice to the Satguru who gifts us the Naam of the eternal God.  Waheguru should be praised, and His noble qualities should be discussed all the time.  The Naam of the eternal Waheguru is also eternal. So, His Naam should become our food and our dress (one should remember Him even while eating and dressing).           2.        One should not forget Waheguru, which should be remembered with every breath and every bite, because Waheguru (in the form of Satguru) is capable of fulfilling all your wishes.  Satguru should be remembered all twenty-four hours; there is no one else equal to Him.  If Satguru casts a kind sight on someone, he cultivates the Naam of Waheguru, which is the Treasure of all noble qualities, in his heart.         3.        Satguru and Waheguru are one and the same (Satguru is a manifestation of Waheguru), and both are present everywhere.  But only those who recite His Naam are predestined to do so.  I have come to the shelter of Waheguru/ Satguru, who is never born and never dies, oh Nanak.   4.30.100
ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਅਸਟਪਦੀਆਂ॥ ੴ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਪ੍ਰਸਾਦਿ
ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 1 ਘਰੁ 1 ਅਸਟਪਦੀਆ
            SIRI RAAG(U) ASHTPADIAAN:  EK ONKAR SATGUR PERSAD(I)
                                           Siri Raag(u) Mehla 1 Ghur(u) 1:
                                 
ਆਖਿ ਆਖਿ ਮਨੁ ਵਾਵਣਾ ਜਿਉ ਜਿਉ ਜਾਪੈ ਵਾਇ॥ ਜਿਸ ਨੋ ਵਾਇ ਸੁਣਾਈਐ ਸੋ ਕੇਵਡੁ ਕਿਤੁ ਥਾਇ॥ਆਖਣ ਵਾਲੇ ਜੇਤੜੇ ਸਭਿ ਆਖਿ ਰਹੇ ਲਿਵ ਲਾਇ॥ 1॥ ਬਾਬਾ ਅਲਹੁ ਅਗਮ ਅਪਾਰੁ॥ ਪਾਕੀ ਨਾਈ ਪਾਕ ਨਾਇ ਸਚਾ ਪਰਵਦਿਗਾਰੁ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਤੇਰਾ ਹੁਕਮੁ ਨ ਜਾਪੀ ਕੇਤੜਾ ਲਿਖਿ ਨ ਜਾਣੈ ਕੋਇ॥ ਜੇ ਸਉ ਸਾਇਰ ਮੇਲੀਅਹਿ ਤਿਲੁ ਨ ਪੁਜਾਵਹਿ ਰੋਇ॥ ਕੀਮਤਿ ਕਿਨੈ ਨ ਪਾਈਆ ਸਭਿ ਸੁਣਿ ਸੁਣਿ ਆਖਹਿ ਸੋਇ॥2॥ ਪੀਰ ਪੈਕਾਮਰ ਸਾਲਕ ਸਾਦਕ ਸੁਹਦੇ ਅਉਰੁ ਸਹੀਦ॥ ਸੇਖ ਮਸਾਇਕ ਕਾਜੀ ਮੁਲਾ ਦਰਿ ਦਰਵੇਸਿ ਰਸੀਦ॥ ਬਰਕਤਿ ਤਿਨ ਕਉ ਅਗਲੀ ਪੜਦੇ ਰਹਨਿ ਦਰੂਦ॥3॥ ਪੁਛਿ ਨ ਸਾਜੇ ਪੁਛਿ ਨ ਢਾਹੇ ਪੁਛਿ ਨ ਦੇਵੈ ਲੇਇ॥ ਆਪਣੀ ਕੁਦਰਤਿ ਆਪੇ ਜਾਣੈ ਆਪੇ ਕਰਣੁ ਕਰੇਇ॥ ਸਭਨਾ ਵੇਖੈ ਨਦਰਿ ਕਰਿ ਜੈ ਭਾਵੈ ਤੈ ਦੇਇ॥ 4॥ ਥਾਵਾ ਨਾਵ ਨ ਜਾਣੀਅਹਿ ਨਾਵਾ ਕੇਵਡੁ ਨਾਉ॥ ਜਿਥੈ ਵਸੈ ਮੇਰਾ ਪਾਤਿਸਾਹੁ ਸੋ ਕੇਵਡੁ ਹੈ ਥਾਉ॥ ਅੰਬੜਿ ਕੋਇ ਨ ਸਕਈ ਹਉ ਕਿਸ ਨੋ ਪੁਛਣਿ ਜਾਉ॥ 5॥ ਵਰਨਾਵਰਨ ਨ ਭਾਵਨੀ ਜੇ ਕਿਸੈ ਵਡਾ ਕਰੇਇ॥ ਵਡੇ ਹਥਿ ਵਡਿਆਈਆ ਜੈ ਭਾਵੈ ਤੈ ਦੇਇ॥ ਹੁਕਮਿ ਸਵਾਰੇ ਆਪਣੈ ਚਸਾ ਨ ਢਿਲ ਕਰੇਇ॥ 6॥ਸਭੁ ਕੋ ਆਖੈ ਬਹੁਤੁ ਬਹੁਤੁ ਲੈਣੈ ਕੈ ਵੀਚਾਰਿ॥ ਕੇਵਡੁ ਦਾਤਾ ਆਖੀਐ ਦੇ ਕੇ ਰਹਿਆ ਸੁਮਾਰਿ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਤੋਟਿ ਨ ਆਵਈ ਤੇਰੇ ਜੁਗਹ ਜੁਗਹ ਭੰਡਾਰ॥ 7॥1॥
Aakh(i) aakh(i) mun(u) vaavna jiyoun jiyoun jaapaiy vaayay. Jis nu vaayay soonayiyaiy, so kayvud(u) kit(u) thaayayn. Aakhun waalay jayturray subh(i) aakh(i) ruhay liv laayay. 1. Baba, Allhou agum apaar(u). Paaki naayee paak Naayay, Sucha Purvidgaar(u). 1. Ruhaou..  Tayra Huqam na jaapi kayturra, likh(i) na jaanaiy koyay. Jay suou shaayir mayliyeh(i) til(u) na poojaaveh(i) royay.  Keemut(i) kinaiy na paayiya, subh(i) soon(i) soon(i) aakheh(i) soyay. 2. Peer paikaamar saaliq saadiq, shohday aur shaheed.  Sheikh musaayik qazi mullah, Dur(i) durvaysh(i) ruseed. Berkut(i) tin kuou ugli, purrhday rehn Drood. 3. Poochh(i) na saajay, pooch(i) na dhaahay, pooch(i) na dayvaiy layay. Aapni Koodrut(i) aapay jaanaiy, aapay kern(u) kuray. Subhna vaykhaiy nudr(i) kur(i), jaiy bhavvaiy taiy dayay. 4. Thaavaan naav na jaaniyeh(i), naavaan kayvud Naoun. Jithaiy vusaiy mayra Patshah(u), so kayvud(u) haiy thaaoun. Umburr(i) koyay na sukyee ,huoun kis nu poochhan(i) jaaoun. 5. Vern-avern na bhaavni jay kisaiy vuda kuray. Vudday huth(i) vudyayiyaan, jaiy bhaavaiy taiy dayay. Huqam(i) svaaray Aapnaiy chusa na dhill kuray. 6. Subh ko aakhaiy buhout buhout, laiynaiy kaiy veechaar(i). Kayvud Daata aakhiyaiy, day kay ruhhiya shoomaar(i).  Nanak toat(i) na aavuyee Tayray joogeh joogeh bhundaar.  7.1.
Word Meaning:  Ashatpudi = a Shabad with eight stanzas.
Vaavna = agonize; torment.  Jaapaiy= understand; realize.  Vaayay = speak; describe.    Kayvud = how expansive.   Kit(u) = which; what.    Jayturray = however many.   Liv laayay = with full focus.     1.
Agum = physically unreachable.  Apaar = limitless (with unknowable qualities).   Paaki = holy.   Naayee = magnanimity.   Purvidgaar = raiser and nourisher.  Pause. 
Huqam = Command; Will; Laws.  Kayturra = how big.   Saou = hundreds.   Shaayir = poets.   Til(u) = the size of a sesame seed (very small).   Poojaaveh(i) = deliver.   Royay = cry (regret their inability).     2.
Paikaamar = prophets.   Saaliq = saints.     Saadiq = contented.    Shohday = ascetic.   Shaheed = witnesses; martyrs.  Musaayik = many sheikhs (learned persons).   Mullah = a Muslim priest.  Dervaysh = holy men who also go door to door and beg.   Dur(i) Raseed = reveived at the door (approved).  Berkut(i) = enhancement.    Ugli = much.   Drood = prayer after nivaj.      3
Saajay = creates.  Dhaahay = destroys.   Kern(u) = worth doing.   Bhaavaiy = likes. 4.
Thaavaan = places.  Naav = Huiqam; Laws.   Vusaiy = reside.   Umburr(i) = reach.  5.    
Vern-avern = high or low classes.   Bhaavni = likes.   Vudyayiyaan = honour.   Svaaray = grooms; embellish with (noble qualities).     Chusa = a moment.  Dhill = delay.     6.
Shoomaar(i) = limit.   Bayshoomaar(i) = unlimited.    Toat(i) = shortage.   Bhundaar = treasures.  7.1.
NOTE:  The use of many Muslim words in this holy Shabad suggests that Guru Nanak Sahib is addressing some Muslim. 
MEANING:  Allah is incomprehensible and physically beyond our reach, oh my friend. Allah is our real provider, and nourishes us all; His magnanimity is great, and so is His residence (we cannot describe them fully).      Pause.
So, saying (something about God without any knowledge) is simply to agonize one’s mind; and the more one says, the more he realizes that his utterings are meaningless. One does not realize how Great Allah is, and where He resides, which he is talking about.   All those who meditate and describe Allah (simply with the intent to describe Him) have failed badly in their efforts.     1. 
No one knows how expansive (indescribable) Your Huqam is, oh Allah. So, how can anyone write about You?  Even if hundreds of poets assemble to describe You, they cannot deliver any information at all – not even the size of a sesame seed – and they simply regret their inability. Everybody says only what they have heard from others, and nobody knows Your secrets, oh Waheguru.       2.
All the holy men, the prophets, the saints, the contented, the martyrs (who suffered in the name of Allah), learned people, qazies, and mullahs - all describe You - but only those are approved by You, who, rather than describing You, sing Your praise like a beggar at Your Door, oh, Allah.           3.            However, He (Allah) does not consult any of those to create, to destroy, to grant, or to withdraw anything.  He knows His nature, and Himself is the source and the creator.  He has His kind sight on everyone, but grants (His Naam) only to those whom He chooses.      4.
Nobody knows anything about His residences and His Huqam, and the magnanimity of His palace where He resides.  Whom should I approach to ask about this information, because nobody can reach Him.       5.
If Allah wants to honour someone, He does not care about their caste, whether it is high or low.  All types of honours are in His control; He can confer them on anyone of His choosing.  He can adorn anyone with noble qualities by His Command, and there is not a delay of even a short moment when He chooses to do so.        6.
With the intent to receive certain favours, everybody praises Him, and asks for more and more,  but how great and large the Provider must be that He keeps giving and yet (His treasures) are unlimited in capacity.  There is never a shortage in His treasures, which are full over the eons, oh Nanak.   7.1.
