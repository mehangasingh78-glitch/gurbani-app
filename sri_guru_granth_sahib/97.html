(ਅੰਗ 96)॥      ਧਨੁ ਧਨੁ ਹਰਿ ਜਨ ਜਿਨ ਹਰਿ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਜਾਤਾ॥ ਜਾਇ ਪੁਛਾ ਜਨ ਹਰਿ ਕੀ ਬਾਤਾ॥ ਪਾਵ ਮਲੋਵਾ ਮਲਿ ਮਲਿ ਧੋਵਾ ਮਿਲਿ ਹਰਿ ਜਨ ਹਰਿ ਰਸੁ ਪੀਚੈ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਦਾਤੈ ਨਾਮੁ ਦਿੜਾਇਆ॥ ਵਡਭਾਗੀ ਗੁਰ ਦਰਸਨੁ ਪਾਇਆ॥ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਰਸ ਸਚੁ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਬੋਲੀ ਗੁਰਿ ਪੂਰੈ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਲੀਚੈ ਜੀਉ॥ 3॥ ਹਰਿ ਸਤਸੰਗਤਿ ਸਤਪੁਰਖੁ ਮਿਲਾਈਐ॥ ਮਿਲਿ ਸਤਸੰਗਤਿ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਧਿਆਈਐ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਹਰਿ ਕਥਾ ਸੁਣੀ ਮੁਖਿ ਬੋਲੀ ਗੁਰਮਤਿ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਪਰੀਚੈ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥6॥
Majh Mehla 4: Huoun goon Govind Hur(i) Naam(u) dhiyayeen. Mil(i) Sungat(i) mun(i) Naam(u) vusayeen. Hur(i) Prubh agumm agoachar Soami, mil(i) Satgur Hur(i) rus(i) keechaiy jeeo. 1.  
(PAGE 96): Dhun(u) dhun(u) Hur(i) jun jin Hur(i) Prubh(u) jaata. Jaayay poochhaan jun Hur(i) kee baata.  Paanv mulovaan mul(i) mul(i) dhoavaan, mil(i) Hur(i) jun hur(i) rus(u) peechaiy jeeo. 2. Satgur Daataiy Naam(u) dirraaya. Vudbhaageen Gur dershun(u) paaya. Amrit rus Sach(u) Amrit boali, Gur(i) pooraiy Amrit(u) leechaiy jeeo. 3.  Hur(i) Satsungat(i) Sat Purakh(u) milayiyaiy. Mil(i) Satsungat(i) Hur(i) Naam(u) dhiyayiyaiy.  Nanak Hur(i) kutha sooni mookh(i) boali, Gurmat(i) Hur(i) Naam(u) preechaiy jeeo.  4.6.  
Word Meaning: Huoun = I; me.  Govind = Waheguru.   Agumm = beyond our physical reach.    Agoachar = incomprehensible.     Rus(i)  = sweetness.      1.      Dhun(u) = worthy of respect.    Jun = devotees.  Jaata = who have realized Waheguru.   Mulovaan = massage.   Mul(i)  = rub (gently).   Peechaiy = drink.      2.      Daataiy = donor; provider.       Dirraaya = reinforced.   Amrit = life-sustaining.       3.      Sat Purakh(u) = eternal Waheguru.   Kutha = description of virtues (of Waheguru).   Preechaiy = (come to) like.     4.
MEANING:  I recite Waheguru’s Naam, and sing praise of His virtues (kindness). In the company of the holy men, I have made my heart a residence for Waheguru. Even though Waheguru is beyond our reach (physically) and beyond our comprehension, one can enjoy the essence of His Naam in the shelter of Satguru.          1.        Those devotees who have made a connection with Waheguru are worthy of our respect. I wish to visit them and ask them about the noble qualities of Waheguru.  I wish to massage their feet and wash them, gently rubbing, because one relishes Waheguru’s Naam-essence in their company only.     2.
I have had the holy sight of the Guru because of my good luck; the kind Satguru has reinforced Waheguru’s Naam in my heart. Now, I enjoy the eternal life-sustaining Waheguru’s Naam and recite it all the time. One can acquire the Naam-Amrit only by the grace of the Perfect Guru.    3.             One can get connected with Waheguru only in the company of a holy congregation where one recites His Naam, listens to His virtues, and recites them with one's tongue. One comes to like (and love) Waheguru’s Naam only by following the advice of the Guru, oh Nanak.          4. 6.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 4॥ ਆਵਹੁ ਭੈਣੋ ਤੁਸੀ ਮਿਲਹੁ ਪਿਆਰੀਆ॥ ਜੋ ਮੇਰਾ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਮੁ ਦਸੇ ਤਿਸ ਕੈ ਹਉ ਵਾਰੀਆ॥ ਮਿਲਿ ਸਤਸੰਗਤਿ ਲਧਾ ਹਰਿ ਸਜਣੁ ਹਉ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਵਿਟਹੁ ਘੁਮਾਈਆ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਜਹ ਜਹ ਦੇਖਾ ਤਹ ਤਹ ਸੁਆਮੀ॥ ਤੂ ਘਟਿ ਘਟਿ ਰਵਿਆ ਅੰਤਰਜਾਮੀ॥ ਗੁਰਿ ਪੂਰੈ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਲਿ ਦਿਖਾਲਿਆ ਹਉ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਵਿਟਹੁ ਸਦ ਵਾਰਿਆ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਏਕੋ ਪਵਣੁ ਮਾਟੀ ਸਭ ਏਕਾ ਸਭ ਏਕਾ ਜੋਤਿ ਸਬਾਈਆ॥ ਸਭ ਇਕਾ ਜੋਤਿ ਵਰਤੈ ਭਿਨਿ ਭਿਨਿ ਨ ਰਲਈ ਕਿਸੈ ਦੀ ਰਲਾਈਆ॥ ਗੁਰ ਪਰਸਾਦੀ ਇਕੁ ਨਦਰੀ ਆਇਆ ਹਉ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਵਿਟਹੁ ਵਤਾਇਆ ਜੀਉ॥ 3॥ ਜਨ ਨਾਨਕੁ ਬੋਲੈ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਬਾਣੀ॥ ਗੁਰ ਸਿਖਾ ਕੈ ਮਨਿ ਪਿਆਰੀ ਭਾਣੀ॥ ਉਪਦੇਸੁ ਕਰੇ ਗੁਰੁ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਪੂਰਾ ਗੁਰੁ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਪਰਉਪਕਾਰੀਆ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥7॥ ਸਤ ਚਉਪਦੇ ਮਹਲੇ ਚਉਥੇ ਕੇ॥
Majh Mehla 4:  Aavhou bhaiyno tooseen milhou piyariyaan. Jo mayra Preetum(u) dussay,  tis kaiy huoun vaariyaan. Mil(i) Satsungat(i) ludha Hur(i) sujjan(u), huoun Satgur vit-houn ghoomaayiyaan jeeo. 1.  Jeh jeh daykhaan teh teh Soami. Tu ghut(i) ghut(i) ruvviya untarjaami. Gur(i) Pooraiy Hur(i) naal(i) dikhaliya, huoun Satgur vit-huoun sud vaariya jeeo. 2. Aiko puvun(u) maati subh aika, subh aika Jot(i) subayiya. Subh ikka Jot(i) vertaiy bhin(i) bhin(i), na rulyee kisaiy dee rulaayiya. Gur Persadi Ikk(u) nudri aaya, huoun Satgur vit-huoun vutaaya jeeo. 3.  Jun Nanak bolaiy Amrit Bani. Gur Sikhaan kaiy mun(i) piyari bhaani. Oopdaysh(u) kuray Gur(u) Satgur(u) Poora, Gur(u) Satgur(u) per-oopkaariya jeeo.  4.7.   Sat Chaupuday Mehlay Chauthay kay.  
Word Meaning:  Vaariyaan = glad to offer life.    Ludha = found.   Sujjan = friend. 
Vit-houn = for; from.    Ghoomaayiyaan = sacrifice life.      1.     Jeh Jeh = wherever.    Teh Teh = there.    Ghut(i) Ghut(i) = in every heart.   Ruvviya = present.   Untarjaami = one who knows everything.    Sud = always.      2.      Maati = soil; earth.   Jot(i) = light (of God).   Subayiya = in all.   Bhin(i) Bhin(i) = different.      Rulyee = mixes.    Vutaaya = sacrifice.     3.
Oopdaysh(u) = advise.    Peroopkaariya = one who does selfless favours to others.     4.
MEANING:  Let us join together (to sing praise of Waheguru), oh my dear friends! I will gladly sacrifice my life for the friend who explains to me the greatness of my dear (Waheguru).  I am a sacrifice to my Satguru, who connected with me in the holy congregation, where I realized my dear friend, Waheguru.          1. Wherever I look, I find the omnipresent Waheguru present there in every heart.  I am always a sacrifice for my Perfect Satguru who “showed” me Waheguru inside me.        2. Everybody is made from the same soil and same air with Waheguru’s light in them.  Even though the same light (of Waheguru) is lighting all the different bodies, yet one light does not match with the other, no matter how hard one tries (because everybody has a different degree of awareness and wisdom).   I wish to be a sacrifice for my Satguru, with whose benevolence one can still “see” the single light of Waheguru present in everybody.    3. Waheguru’s slave, Nanak, utters the life-sustaining Bani (praise of Waheguru), which is loved by all the Sikhs of the Guru. I am a sacrifice to the selfless Guru who gives the same advice to everyone and always performs acts of kindness for others.  4.7.
