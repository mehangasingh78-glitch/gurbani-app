NOTE:  Being present in everybody, it is Waheguru Himself who prompts people to do whatever they do. There is a lot of freedom to make choices, but the traits and wisdom to make them are gifts from Waheguru. 
MEANING: Waheguru Himself is (the source) of all noble qualities; He Himself describes, listens to, and ponders on them. Waheguru Himself is the precious stone (His Naam), its evaluator, and Himself the immeasurable worth of (His Naam). Waheguru Himself is the real pride (support) of everybody, such that they can depend on Him for all sorts of support; and He is the only provider.           1.          You are the creator and the source of everything, oh my Waheguru.  I pray to You to please protect me (from the attacks of Maya) in whatever way You wish and grant me Your Naam as the guide for my life, oh my Waheguru.       Pause.
God Himself is the pure diamond (without any filth of Maya), and Himself the colour of madder (which is representative of deep love or full devotion, and which is fostered by His kindness). He Himself is the shiny pearl (beacon of enlightenment), and Himself the mediator (as the Guru) between the devotees and Himself.  The otherwise invisible Waheguru, which is present in every heart, becomes visible (with mental eyes) when one sings His praise by the Guru’s Shabad.           2.             Waheguru Himself is the sea of Maya, Himself the ship to carry people across the sea, Himself, on the near shore of the sea, and Himself, the far side of the sea.  The perfectly wise Waheguru Himself is the True path, while Guru’s Shabad is the means for people to tread that path and swim across the sea of Maya. Without the Guru, people are in complete darkness, ignorant of the damaging effects of Maya, and fearless of Waheguru; but when the Guru enlightens their mind, they realize the truth of Maya and become fearful of Waheguru.           3.        Only God is immortal, while countless people take birth and die.  Only Waheguru is pure (without any effect of Maya), while everybody else is indulged in the filth of Maya.  Only those are salvaged who act on the Guru’s advice and always focus their mind on Waheguru (keep Him in mind).            4.
Waheguru is realized by Guru’s Shabad when a devotee gets imbued in Waheguru’s Naam.  The body (mind) of an individual who resides in Waheguru’s Palace (keeps Waheguru in mind all the time) never gets filthy (due to indulgence in Maya).  A devotee realizes Waheguru when He casts His kind sight on him; there is no other (permanent) relative in the world than Waheguru’s Naam.            5.
Those who realize the eternal Waheguru live in comfort in all the four eons (no matter what eon it is).  They overcome their haumein (selfishness and ego), and desires, and keep Waheguru always in their heart.  There is only one thing to earn in this world - and that is Waheguru’s Naam, which can be realized by pondering on (obeying) Guru’s Shabad.            6. 
When one deals in the true merchandise (Waheguru’s Naam), he earns the Naam as the benefit in his wealth (of noble qualities).  With honest prayer with devotion, one gets room (is honoured) in Waheguru’s Court.  The account (of good and bad deeds) of such a devotee is settled with dignity, as his heart is enlightened with Waheguru’s Naam.       7.
People say that God is higher and higher, but He cannot be seen just by saying that.  However, when Satguru shows Waheguru to someone with his mental eyes, He alone is visible in every direction.  One can realize continuous light (of Waheguru) with control over his mind and with affection, oh Nanak.    8.3.

Ruhaou.   Subh(u) jug(u) baadho kaal ko, bin(u) Gur kaal(u) afaar(u).  Such(i) ruttay say oobray, doobidha chhoad(i) vikaar.  Huoun tin kaiy bulihaarnaiy dur(i) Suchaiy suchiaar. 2. MEANING:  While enjoying life in the deep, salty ocean, the fish feels safe and forgets death. As a result, she fails to recognize the dangers of the net and gets trapped. The fish are considered very beautiful and wise, but why do they trust the net (that there is no danger there)?  Because of this unawareness, they get caught and killed.  The death looming over their heads cannot be avoided.           1. 
Remember, a similar death is looming over your head, oh my brother. Like the fish, the man is caught unaware by the net of death.            Pause.
Everybody is trapped in the net of (is a prisoner of) death, which is uncontrollable.  Only the Guru can enlighten our minds (so that our concept of death changes, and we look forward to the opportunity to be “one” with God).  The Guru clears all our doubts (about Waheguru and Maya), thereby making our mind stable and enabling us to control our sensual pleasures. The Guru connects us with Waheguru, and we are imbued with His Naam and are saved.  Guru Nanak says He gladly wants to sacrifice His life for the devotees who are honoured as the bearers of Truth (having noble qualities) in Waheguru’s Court.         2.
Just as a Seechanna (a bird of prey) or a hunter with a net in his hand is dangerous to birds, the net of Maya causes spiritual death for humans.  Those who are enlightened by the Guru are saved, but the others are trapped by Maya's bait.  Those who are without Waheguru’s Naam in their heart are selectively rejected (in Waheguru’s Court), and there is no friend to help them.          3.           One should always recite the Naam of the eternal Waheguru, whose throne is also eternal. Those who believe (have full faith) in the eternal Waheguru, always keep Him in mind. Also, those who have been enlightened by the Guru are connected to Waheguru and are pure in mind and in speech (they speak truth only).         4.
One should pray to the Guru, begging to connect him with the real Friend (God).  When one is connected with Waheguru, he enjoys all the comforts, and for him, the agent of death is dead as if he had taken poison.  I live in Waheguru’s Naam (always remember Him), and He lives in me, oh Nanak.          5.             Without the Guru, there is darkness of ignorance (caused by Maya), but one does not realize this until enlightened by the Guru.  When enlightened by the Guru, one stays focused on Waheguru’s Naam.  In that state, the human soul becomes “one” with Waheguru, and (the fear of) death does not reach there.          6.
You are my friend, are omnipotent, and You Yourself, connect people with You, oh Waheguru.  The inaccessible Waheguru can be praised only by the Guru’s Shabad.  The fear of death cannot reach there where the incomprehensible Guru’s Shabad is present (is internalized).     7.             All life is born with the Huqam (Laws) of Waheguru, and all humans spend their life as per the Huqam of Waheguru.  Some people are subject to death (for rebirth) while some become part of Waheguru as per His Huqam.  Only that happens in the world that Waheguru wishes; nothing is in the control of humans, oh Nanak.  8.4.
