(ਅੰਗ 15)॥  ਨਾਨਕ ਕਾਗਦ ਲਖ ਮਣਾ ਪੜਿ ਪੜਿ ਕੀਚੈ ਭਾਉ॥ ਮਸੂ ਤੋਟਿ ਨ ਆਵਈ ਲੇਖਣਿ ਪਉਣੁ ਚਲਾਉ॥ ਭੀ ਤੇਰੀ ਕੀਮਤਿ ਨ ਪਵੈ ਹਉ ਕੇਵਡ ਆਖਾ ਨਾਉ॥ 4॥2॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 1: Koat(i) koati mayri aarja, puvun peeun, apiyaaou. Chund, sooruj, doyay goofaiy na daykhaan, soopnaiy saun na thaaoun. Bhee Tayri keemut(i) na puvvaiy, huoun kayvud aakhaan Naoun. 1. Saacha Nirunkar(u) nij thaayayn. Soon(i), soon(i) aakhun(u) aakhna, jay bhaavaiy kuray tumaayay. 1. Ruhoau. Kusaan, kuttiaan vaar, vaar, peesun(i) peesaan paayay. Uggeen sayti jaaliyaan, bhusm sayti rul(i) jaaoun. Bhee Tayri keemut(i) na puvvaiy, huoun kayvud aakhaan Naoun. 2. Punkhi hoyay kaiy jay bhuvvaan, saiy asmaani jaaoun. Nedreen kisaiy na aavoun, na kichh(u) paayaan, na khaaoun. Bhee Tayri keemut(i) na puvvaiy, huoun kayvud aakhan Naoun. 3. 
(PAGE 15):        Nanak, kaagud lakh munaa, purrh(i) purrh(i) keechaiy bhaaou. Mussoo toat(i) na aavyee, laykhun(i) paun(u) chulaaoun. Bhee Tayri keemut(i) na puvvaiy, huoun kayvud aakhaan Naaoun.  4.2.
Word Meaning: Koat(i) = millions.  Aarja = age.   Peeun(u) = to drink.   Apiyaaou = food.       Goofaiy = in the cave.   Bhee = still.      1.    Nij Thaayayn = absorbed in self.  Tumaayay = wish; longing.        Pause.       Kusaan = kill mercilessly.   Kuttiaan = cut into pieces.   Peesun(i) = in the grinding mill.   Sayti = with.   Bhusm = ash.   Rul(i) = mix well.        2.        Punkhi = bird.     Saiy = hundreds.     Asmaani = skies.      3.
Kaagud = paper.   Munaa = 40 kg; 0.4 quintal.        Keechaiy Bhaaou = show love.  Mussoo = ink.   Toat(i) = shortage.   Laykhun(i) = pen.   Chulaaoun = move; write.  4.
MEANING: The eternal and the formless Waheguru stays worry-free, absorbed in His own self.  (Nobody knows the true worth of Waheguru). Listening to others, people describe Him in their own way, but only that individual acquires a longing for Him whom he exhorts.       Pause.               If I could live for multimillion years, and depended only on air as my drinks and my food (waste no time to prepare them), and locked myself in a cave so that I did not see the moon and the sun, and did not even dream about sleeping (and spent all that time to evaluate Waheguru), I still would not be able to find the greatness of Waheguru as to how large (powerful) His command (Huqam is).          1.          (Some people torture themselves physically to please some deity with that belief in mind), If I kill myself mercilessly, cut myself into small pieces, grind myself in the grinding mill, or burn myself to ashes, I will still not be able to find the limit of the greatness of Waheguru as to how powerful His Huqam is.        2.  
If I could become a bird, and fly to hundreds of skies, and be invisible to anyone, and did not have the need to eat or drink anything (and spend the whole time finding the limits of Waheguru), I would not be able to do so, no matter how hard I tried.     3.
If I had thousands of tons of paper to read about Waheguru, and foster love for Him, or if I had a similar amount of paper to write on, used air as my pen, and if there was no shortage of ink to write about the magnanimity of Waheguru, I would still not be able to describe His vastness and the power of His Command, oh Nanak.    4.2.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 1॥ ਲੇਖੈ ਬੋਲਣੁ ਬੋਲਣਾ ਲੇਖੈ ਖਾਣਾ ਖਾਉ॥ ਲੇਖੈ ਵਾਟ ਚਲਾਈਆ ਲੇਖੈ ਸੁਣਿ ਵੇਖਾਉ॥ ਲੇਖੈ ਸਾਹ ਲਵਾਈਅਹਿ ਪੜੇ ਕਿ ਪੁਛਣ ਜਾਉ॥1॥ ਬਾਬਾ ਮਾਇਆ ਰਚਨਾ ਧੋਹੁ॥ ਅੰਧੈ ਨਾਮੁ ਵਿਸਾਰਿਆ ਨਾ ਤਿਸੁ ਏਹੁ ਨ ਓਹੁ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਜੀਵਣ ਮਰਣਾ ਜਾਇ ਕੈ ਏਥੈ ਖਾਜੇ ਕਾਲਿ॥ ਜਿਥੈ ਬਹਿ ਸਮਝਾਈਐ ਤਿਥੈ ਕੋਇ ਨ ਚਲਿਓ ਨਾਲਿ॥ ਰੋਵਣ ਵਾਲੇ ਜੇਤੜੇ ਸਭਿ ਬੰਨਹਿ ਪੰਡ ਪਰਾਲਿ॥2॥ ਸਭੁ ਕੋ ਆਖੈ ਬਹੁਤੁ ਬਹੁਤੁ ਘਟਿ ਨ ਆਖੈ ਕੋਇ॥ ਕੀਮਤਿ ਕਿਨੈ ਨ ਪਾਈਆ ਕਹਣਿ ਨ ਵਡਾ ਹੋਇ॥ ਸਾਚਾ ਸਾਹਿਬ ਏਕੁ ਤੂ ਹੋਰਿ ਜੀਆ ਕੇਤੇ ਲੋਅ॥3॥ ਨੀਚਾ ਅੰਦਰਿ ਨੀਚ ਜਾਤਿ ਨੀਚੀ ਹੂੰ ਅਤਿ ਨੀਚੁ॥ ਨਾਨਕੁ ਤਿਨ ਕੈ ਸੰਗਿ ਸਾਥਿ ਵਡਿਆ ਸਿਉ ਕਿਆ ਰੀਸ॥ ਜਿਥੈ ਨੀਚ ਸਮਾਲੀਅਨਿ ਤਿਥੈ ਨਦਰਿ ਤੇਰੀ ਬਖਸੀਸ॥ 4॥3॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 1: Laykhaiy boalun(u) boalna, laykhaiy khaana khaaoun. Laykhaiy vaat chulaayiya, laykhaiy soon(i) vaykhaaoun. Laykhaiy saah luvaayiyeh(i), purrhay k(i) poochhun jaaou.  Baba, Maya ruchna dhoh(u). Undhaiy Naam(u) visaariya, na tis(u) eh(u) na Oh(u). 1. Ruhaou. Jeevun, merna jaayay kaiy, aithaiy khaajay kaal(i). Jithaiy beh(i) sumjhaayiyaiy, tithaiy koyay na chulliyo naal(i). Rovun vaalay jaytrray, subh(i) bunneh(i) pund praal(i). 2. Subh(u) ko aakhaiy buhout(u), buhout(u), ghutt(i) na aakhaiy koyay. Keemut(i) kinaiy na paayiya, kehn(i) na vudda hoyay. Saacha Sahib aik Tu, hoar(i) jeeyaan kaytay loe. 3. Neechaan undar(i), neech jaat(i), neechi hoon, ut(i) neech(u).  Nanak(u), tinh kaiy sung(i) saath(i) vuddiyaan siyoun kiyya rees. Jithaiy neech smaaliun(i) tithaiy nedr(i) Tayri bukhsheesh. 4.3.
Word Meaning:   Laykhaiy = in account; as per prewritten destiny.   Vaat Chulayiya = journey of life; his way of life.    Saah = breaths.   K(i) = what.   Baba = oh wise man.        1.        Ruchna = creation.  Dhoh = deceit; not forever.  Eh(u) = Maya.   Oh(u) = Waheguru.     Pause.   
Jeevun Merna = from birth to death.  Jaayay Kaiy = after taking birth.   Khaajay = about food.    Kaal(i) = (all) the time.   Jithaiy = where.   Tithaiy = there.     Pund Praal(i) = bay of paddy stalk; worthless.        2.          Loe = worlds.        3.       Neech = ignoble.    Rees = comparison.   Smaaiun = looked after.   Bukhsheesh = kindness.     4.
MEANING: Blinded by the gimmicks of Maya, the man does not realize that its creation by Waheguru is deceitful (short-lived); He is so captivated by it that he forgets Waheguru’s Naam, with the result that he neither enjoys the gimmicks of Maya forever, nor does he acquire Waheguru’s Naam.         Pause.        (Man may be captivated by Maya, which is his natural inclination – but in reality, he has no independent control over his actions, which are predetermined by Waheguru for Him). So, all his talking, his eating, his lifestyle, his listening, his explanations, and the number of breaths he takes (his lifespan) are predetermined for him. There is no need to consult any learned person on these issues.  (See Paurri 33 of Jap Ji).         1.          From his birth to his death, the man eats (and enjoys life) all the time, but none of the luxuries accompany him where his deeds are explained to him (in Waheguru’s Court). All the crying of his relatives at his death is useless to him.         2.
Everybody says that Waheguru’s creation, the universe, is very large, and nobody says any less than the others; but nobody can determine the actual size of the universe (of Waheguru’s largess), and simply by saying, it gets no bigger than its actual size.   Only Waheguru is the eternal Master, while there are countless perishable worlds and the living beings.        3. 
Among the low-castes, I also belong to the low-caste – in fact, lower than the lowest, oh Nanak.   I am from a lower caste and have no comparison with the higher castes.  I know, You cast a kind sight where the low castes are looked after, oh Waheguru.  4.3.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 1॥ ਲਬੁ ਕੁਤਾ ਕੂੜੁ ਚੂਹੜਾ ਠਗਿ ਖਾਧਾ ਮੁਰਦਾਰੁ॥ ਪਰ ਨਿੰਦਾ ਪਰ ਮਲੁ ਮੁਖਿ ਸੁਧੀ ਅਗਨਿ ਕ੍ਰੋਧੁ ਚੰਡਾਲੁ॥ ਰਸ ਕਸ ਆਪੁ ਸਲਾਹਣਾ ਏ ਕਰਮ ਮੇਰੇ ਕਰਤਾਰ॥ 1॥ ਬਾਬਾ ਬੋਲੀਐ ਪਤਿ ਹੋਇ॥ ਊਤਮ ਸੇ ਦਰ ਊਤਮ ਕਈਅਹਿ ਨੀਚ ਕਰਮ ਬਹਿ ਰੋਇ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਰਸੁ ਸੁਇਨਾ ਰਸੁ ਰੁਪਾ ਕਾਮਣਿ ਰਸੁ ਪਰਮਲ ਕੀ ਵਾਸੁ॥ਰਸੁ ਘੋੜੇ ਰਸੁ ਸੇਜਾ ਮੰਦਰ ਰਸੁ ਮੀਠਾ ਰਸੁ ਮਾਸੁ॥ ਏਤੇ ਰਸ ਸਰੀਰ ਕੇ ਕੈ ਘਟਿ ਨਾਮ ਨਿਵਾਸੁ॥2॥ ਜਿਤੁ ਬੋਲਿਐ ਪਤਿ ਪਾਈਐ ਸੋ ਬੋਲਿਆ ਪਰਵਾਣੁ॥ ਫਿਕਾ ਬੋਲਿ ਵਿਗੁਚਣਾ ਸੁਣਿ ਮੂਰਖ ਮਨ ਅਜਾਣ॥ ਜੋ ਤਿਸੁ ਭਾਵਹਿ ਸੇ ਭਲੇ ਹੋਰਿ ਕਿ ਕਹਣ ਵਖਾਣ॥ 3॥ ਤਿਨ ਮਤਿ ਤਿਨ ਪਤਿ ਤਿਨ ਧਨ ਪਲੈ ਜਿਨ ਹਿਰਦੈ ਰਹਿਆ ਸਮਾਇ॥ ਤਿਨ ਕਾ ਕਿਆ ਸਾਲਾਹਣਾ ਅਵਰ ਸੁਆਲਿਉ ਕਾਇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਦਰੀ ਬਾਹਰੇ ਰਾਚਹਿ ਦਾਨਿ ਨ ਨਾਇ॥ 4॥4॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 1: Lub(u) koota, koorr(u) choohrra, tthug khaadha moordaar(u). Per ninda, per mul(u) mookh(i) soodhi, ugun(i) kuroadh(u) chundaal(u). Rus, kus, aap(u) salaahna, eh kerm mayray Kartar. 1. Baba, boaliyaiy put(i) hoyay. Ootum say der ootum kuhhiyeh neech kerm beh royay. 1. Ruhoau. Rus(u) soyina, rus rooppa, kaamun(i), rus(u) permul kee vaas(u). Rus ghorray, rus(u) sayja, mundar, rus(u) meettha, rus(u) maas(u). Aitaiy rus sureer kay, kaiy ghut(i) Naam nivaas(u). 2. Jit(u) boaliyain put(i) paayiyaiy, so boaliya pervaan(u). Fikka boal(i) vigoochna, soon(i) moorukh mun ajaan.  Jo Tis(u) bhaaveh(i) say bhulay, hoar(i) k(i) kehn vukhaan.  3. Tinh mut(i), tinh put(i), tinh dhun pullaiy, jinh hirdaiy ruhhiya smaayay. Tinh ka kiyya salaahna, uver sooaaliyo kaayay. Nanak, nedri baahray raacheh(i) daan(i), na Naayay. 4.4. 
Word Meaning: Lub(u) = gluttony.   Koorru(u) = deceit; lies.  Choohrra = ignoble.  Moordaar(u) = usurping the rights of others.       Per = of others.    Ninda = vilification.   Mul(u) = filth.  Soodhi = entirely.  Chundaal(u) = merciless; cruel.    Rus Kus = enjoyable tastes.   Aap(u) Salaahna = self-praise.    Kerm = deeds.       1.       Baba = Oh, wise man.   Boaliyaiy = utter (words of praise for God).  Put(i) = dignity.  Ootum = superior.     Say = those.   Neech Kerm = with ignoble deeds.            Pause.          Rus(u) = habitually irresistible tastes.  Rooppa = silver.  Kaamun(i) = woman.   Permul = fragrant.   Vaas(u) = fragrance.   Sayja = bed.   Mundar = mansions.   Ghut(i) = heart.   Nivaas(u) = dwelling.       2.
Pervaan(u) = approved.    Fikka = impolite; rooted in Maya.   Vigoochna = suffer humiliation.   Ajaan = ignorant.   K(i) = what?     Vukhaan = describe.        3.
Pullaiy = in possession.    Smaayay = present.   Sooaaliyo = beautiful.   Nedri Baahray = devoid of Waheguru’s kind sight.    Raachay = absorbed; rapt.  Daan = largess.  4.
MEANING: I possess only ignoble qualities and perform ignoble deeds, oh my Waheguru. I have dog-like gluttony, and deceitfulness is another ignoble quality of mine.  I earn my living by cheating others and usurping their rights, which is like sucking their blood. Vilification of others is pure filth on my tongue, and merciless rage is the fire burning in my mind.  Enjoying different tastes of food and self-praise are my contemptuous deeds, oh my Waheguru.         1.     One should speak only those words which can earn him respect (in Waheguru’s Court), oh wise man.  Only those are superior who are considered so, in Waheguru’s Court. The individuals with ignoble deeds have to cry there.         Pause.          Wearing of gold and silver, love for women, fragrance of flower-extracts, riding horses, enjoying the comfort of luxurious beds and big mansions, enjoying the tastes of sweets and meats, are all habitually irresistible habits of the man; so, with all such bad habits, where is the room for Waheguru’s Naam in his heart?           2.            Only those spoken words are approved in Waheguru’s Court which earn one respect.  One suffers humiliation in Waheguru’s Court if his spoken words are impolite and cause hurt to others, oh my foolish and ignorant mind.  Only those are noble people who are liked by Waheguru; what else needs to be said?        3. 
Only those who have true wisdom, dignity, and wealth have Waheguru dwelling in their mind (are connected with Him).   How much can you praise such devotees, and who else has a more beautiful mind than those?   Those who are deprived of Waheguru’s kind sight stay absorbed in Waheguru’s largess rather than His Naam, oh Nanak.  4.4.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 1॥ ਅਮਲੁ ਗਲੋਲਾ ਕੂੜ ਕਾ ਦਿਤਾ ਦੇਵਣਹਾਰਿ॥ ਮਤੀ ਮਰਣੁ ਵਿਸਾਰਿਆ ਖੁਸੀ ਕੀਤੀ ਦਿਨ ਚਾਰਿ॥ ਸਚੁ ਮਿਲਿਆ ਤਿਨ ਸੋਫੀਆ ਰਾਖਣ ਕਉ ਦਰਵਾਰੁ॥1॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਸਾਚੇ ਕਉ ਸਚੁ ਜਾਣੁ॥ ਜਿਤੁ ਸੇਵਿਐ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਾਈਐ ਤੇਰੀ ਦਰਗਹ ਚਲੈ ਮਾਣੁ॥1।ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਸਚੁ ਸਰਾ ਗੁੜ ਬਾਹਰਾ ਜਿਸੁ ਵਿਚਿ ਸਚਾ ਨਾਉ॥
