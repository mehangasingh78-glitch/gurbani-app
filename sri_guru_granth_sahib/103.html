(ਅੰਗ 102)॥    ਠਾਕੁਰ ਕੇ ਸੇਵਕ ਹਰਿ ਰੰਗ ਮਾਣਹਿ॥ ਜੋ ਕਿਛੁ ਠਾਕੁਰ ਕਾ ਸੋ ਸੇਵਕ ਕਾ ਸੇਵਕੁ ਠਾਕੁਰ ਹੀ ਸੰਗਿ ਜਾਹਰੁ ਜੀਉ॥ 3॥ ਅਪੁਨੈ ਠਾਕੁਰਿ ਜੋ ਪਹਿਰਾਇਆ॥ ਬਹੁਰਿ ਨ ਲੇਖਾ ਪੁਛਿ ਬੁਲਾਇਆ॥ ਤਿਸੁ ਸੇਵਕ ਕੈ ਨਾਨਕ ਕੁਰਬਾਣੀ ਸੋ ਗਹਿਰ ਗੰਭੀਰਾ ਗਉਹਰੁ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥18॥25॥
Majh Mehla 5:  Oat(i) poat(i) sayvuk sung(i) raata. Prubh pritpaalay sayvuk Sookhdaata.  Paani pukkha peesoun sayvuk kaiy, Tthakur ka hee aahur(u) jeeo. 1. Kaat(i) silk Prubh(i) sayva laaya. Huqam(u) Sahib ka sayvuk mun(i) bhaaya.  Soyee kmaavaiy jo Sahib bhaavaiy, sayvuk(u) untar(i) baahur(i) maahur(u) jeeo. 2. Tu daana Tthakur(u) subh bidh(i) jaaneh(i). 
(PAGE 102): Tthakur kay sayvuk Hur(i) rung maaneh(i).  Jo kichh(u) Tthakur ka, so sayvuk ka, sayvuk Tthakur hee sung(i) jaahur(u) jeeo. 3.  Apunaiy Tthakur(i) jo pehiraaya. Bouhur(i) na laykha pooch(i) boolaaya. Tis(u) sayvuk kaiy Nanak koorbani, so gehr gumbheera gouhur(u) jeeo.  4.18.25.
Word Meaning = Oat(i) Poat(i) = the cross threads of fabric (weft and woof).    Raata = mixed; imbued.  Pritpaalay = looks after.   Sookhdaata = source of all comfort.   Paani, Pukkha, Peesoun = fetch water, fan (v), and grind wheat (all such services were needed before mechanization).   Aahur = task; service.       1.       Silk = bonds (of maya).  Bhaaya = liked.   Kmaavaiy = does (things).   Untar(i) Baahur(i) = inside and out (mentally and physically).    Maahur(u) = expert; alert.     2.      Daana = wise; respectable.  Bidh(i) = ways (n); possibilities; alternatives.  Maaneh(i) = enjoy.     Jaahur(u) = famous; revealed.      3.
Pehiraaya = robed; honoured.   Laykha = accountability; questions.   Gehr = deep (who does not disclose his secrets).    Gumbheera = not affected by conditions.  Gouhur(u) = a valuable stone (of great qualities). 
MEANING:  The source of all comfort, Waheguru, is mixed (blended) with His devotee just as the cross threads in fabric, and He looks after his devotee in every way.  I wish to serve such a devotee who is serving Waheguru all the time by fetching water for him, fanning him, and grinding flour for him.         1.         Waheguru cuts all the bonds of Maya for His devotee, and the devotee gladly obeys the Huqam of His Lord.  The devotee does only those things that are to the liking of the Lord, and he serves Him enthusiastically by mind and by body.        2.
You are wise and know every possibility (inner feelings of the devotee), oh Waheguru!  The devotees of Waheguru feel blissful under all conditions.  Everything that belongs to the Lord belongs to the devotees as well, and the devotees are made prominent (famous) by Waheguru.        3.          Once the Lord robes (honours) His devotee, He never questions him again.  I wish to offer my life to such a devotee who has become “one” with Waheguru, and like Him, which is unfathomable, never changes and is invaluable, oh Nanak!     4.18.25

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਸਭ ਕਿਛੁ ਘਰ ਮਹਿ ਬਾਹਰਿ ਨਾਹੀ॥ ਬਾਹਰਿ ਟੋਲੈ ਸੋ ਭਰਮਿ ਭੁਲਾਈ॥ ਗੁਰ ਪਰਸਾਦੀ ਜਿਨੀ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਪਾਇਆ ਸੋ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਬਾਹਰਿ ਸੁਹੇਲਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਝਿਮਿ ਝਿਮਿ ਵਰਸੈ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਧਾਰਾ॥ ਮਨੁ ਪੀਵੈ ਸੁਨਿ ਸਬਦੁ ਵੀਚਾਰਾ॥ ਅਨਦ ਬਿਨੋਦ ਕਰੇ ਦਿਨ ਰਾਤੀ ਸਦਾ ਸਦਾ ਹਰਿ ਕੇਲਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਜਨਮ ਜਨਮ ਕਾ ਵਿਛੁੜਿਆ ਮਿਲਿਆ॥ ਸਾਧ ਕ੍ਰਿਪਾ ਤੇ ਸੂਕਾ ਹਰਿਆ॥ ਸੁਮਤਿ ਪਾਏ ਨਾਮੁ ਧਿਆਏ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਹੋਏ ਮੇਲਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 3॥ ਜਲ ਤਰੰਗ ਜਿਉ ਜਲਹਿ ਸਮਾਇਆ॥ ਤਿਉ ਜੋਤੀ ਸੰਗਿ ਜੋਤਿ ਮਿਲਾਇਆ॥ ਕਹੁ ਨਾਨਕ ਭ੍ਰਮ ਕਟੇ ਕਿਵਾੜਾ ਬਹੁੜਿ ਨ ਹੋਈਐ ਜਉਲਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥19॥26॥
Majh Mehla 5: Subh kichh(u) ghur meh(i) baahur(i) naahi. Baahur(i) tolaiy so bherm(i) bhoolaayee. Gur persadi jinhi untar(i) paaya, so untar(i) baahur(i) soohayla jeeo. 1. Jhim(i) jhim(i) versaiy Amrit dhaara. Mun(u) peevaiy soon(i) Shabad(u) veechaara.  Anund binoad kuray din raati, suda suda Hur(i) kayla jeeo.  2. Junum, junum ka vichhurriya miliya. Sadh kirpa tay sooka huriya. Soomut(i) paayay Naam(u) dhiyayay, Gurmukh(i) hoyay mayla jeeo. 3.  Jul trung jiyoun juleh(i) smaaya. Tiyoun joti sung(i) Jot(i) milaaya.  Kuhou Nanak bherm kuttay kivarra, buhourr(i) na hoyiyaiy jaoula jeeo. 4.19.26.
Word Meaning:  Ghur = heart.     Tolaiy = searches.    Bherm(i) Bhoolaayee= lost in doubts and misbeliefs.   Soohayla = in happiness; content.      1.
Jhim(i) Jhim(i) = dropwise; drizzle.   Anund Binoad = enjoys bliss.   Kayla = detached; blissful.        2.             Sadh = Guru.  Sooka Huriya = dead (hopeless) and barren mind came alive.   Soomut(i) = noble wisdom.   Mayla = union (with Waheguru).          3.            Trung = wave.   Smaaya = became “one” with.   Kivaarra = doors; walls.  Jaoula = separate.           4. 
MEANING: Everything (Waheguru’s Naam) resides in man’s heart, and there’s nothing outside in the world. Those who search for Him in the world are lost in doubts and misbeliefs.  Those who have found (got connected with) Waheguru in the shelter (guidance) of the Guru are happy and content internally and in the outside world.        1.         The Naam-Amrit slowly drips into the minds of those who are connected with Waheguru (slow dripping is more effective than a flood).  They drink it tastefully when they ponder on Guru’s Shabad (i.e. pondering on Guru’s Shabad is equivalent to drinking Amrit).  Being connected with God, which is always blissful, such individuals also feel blissful and enjoy day and night.          2.
They have been united with Waheguru, from whom they had been disconnected for many lives (Sikhism strongly believes in reincarnation), and with the benevolence of the Guru, their barren mind (which never thought about Waheguru) came alive (begins to have holy thoughts).  With Guru’s noble wisdom, they begin to remember Waheguru, and thus get connected to Him.           3.                 Just as a wave on the surface of water mingles completely with water again, Waheguru’s Light in humans (human soul) becomes one with His own Light (Waheguru Himself).   All the walls of their doubts are smashed, and they never get separated from Him again, oh Nanak!   4.19.26.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਤਿਸੁ ਕੁਰਬਾਣੀ ਜਿਨਿ ਤੂ ਸੁਣਿਆ॥ ਤਿਸੁ ਬਲਿਹਾਰੀ ਜਿਨਿ ਰਸਨਾ ਭਣਿਆ॥ ਵਾਰਿ ਵਾਰਿ ਜਾਈ ਤਿਸੁ ਵਿਟਹੁ ਜੋ ਮਨਿ ਤਨਿ ਤੁਧੁ ਆਰਾਧੇ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਤਿਸੁ ਚਰਣ ਪਖਾਲੀ ਜੋ ਤੇਰੈ ਮਾਰਗਿ ਚਾਲੈ॥ ਨੈਨ ਨਿਹਾਲੀ ਤਿਸੁ ਪੁਰਖੁ ਦਇਆਲੇ॥ ਮਨੁ ਦੇਵਾ ਤਿਸੁ ਅਪੁਨੇ ਸਾਜਨ ਜਿਨਿ ਗੁਰ ਮਿਲਿ ਸੋ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਲਾਧੇ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਸੇ ਵਡਭਾਗੀ ਜਿਨਿ ਤੁਮ ਜਾਣੇ॥ ਸਭ ਕੈ ਮਧੇ ਅਲਿਪਤ ਨਿਰਬਾਣੈ॥ ਸਾਧ ਕੈ ਸੰਗਿ ਉਨਿ ਭਉਜਲੁ ਤਰਿਆ ਸਗਲ ਦੂਤ ਉਨਿ ਸਾਧੇ ਜੀਉ॥ 3॥ ਤਿਨ ਕੀ ਸਰਣਿ ਪਰਿਆ ਮਨੁ ਮੇਰਾ॥ ਮਾਣੁ ਤਾਣੁ ਤਜਿ ਮੋਹੁ ਅੰਧੇਰਾ॥ ਨਾਮੁ ਦਾਨੁ ਦੀਜੈ ਨਾਨਕ ਕਉ ਤਿਸੁ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਅਗਮ ਅਗਾਧੇ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥20॥27॥
Majh Mehla 5:  Tis(u) koorbani jin(i) Tu sooniya. Tis(u) bulihaari jin(i) rusna bhuniya. Vaar(i) vaar(i) jaayeen tis(u) vit-houn. jo mun(i) tun(i) Toodh(u) aaradhay jeeo.  1. Tis(u) churn pukhaaleen jo Taraiy maarug(i) chaalaiy. Naiyn nihaalee tis(u) Purakh(u) duyyalay. Mun(u) dayvaan tis(u) apunay saajun, jin(i) Gur mil(i) so Prubh(u) laadhay jeeo. 2. Say vudbhaagi jin(i) Toon jaanay. Subh kaiy mudhay, aliput nirbaanaiy. Sadh kaiy sung(i) oon(i) bhoujul(u) turiya, sugul doot oon(i) saadhay jeeo. 3. Tin kee shern(i) puriya mun(u) mayra. Maan(u) taan(u) tuj(i) moh(u) undhayra. Naam(u) daan(u) deejaiy Nanak kuou, tis(u) Prubh agumm agaadhay jeeo.  4.20.27. 
Word Meaning:  Koorbani = Bulihaari = Vaar(i) Jaayeen = to offer life.   Rusna = tongue.    Bhuniya = recited.   Vitoun = from; for.   Aaradhay = worship.    1.     Churn = feet.  Pukhaaleen = I (wish to) wash.   Maarug(i) = path.   Nihaaleen = see; have sight.    Purakh(u) Duyyalay  = kind Waheguru.  Laadhay = found; connected.     2.       Vudbhaagi = very lucky.   Mudhay = in the middle; among.   Aliput = unaffected by Maya.  Nirbaanaiy = having absolutely no worldly attachment or desire.   Bhoujul = world sea.  Doot = demons.   Saadhay = fixed (controlled).     3.      Shern = shelter.   Taan = force; power.   Tuj(i) = quit.   Moh undhayra = darkness of attachment.    Agumm = inaccessible.    Agaadhay = unfathomable.      4.
MEANING: I wish to offer my life to those who listen to You (obey Your Huqam), and to those who recite Your Naam by the tongue, and also to those who worship You by mind and body, oh Waheguru!           1.         I wish to wash the feet of those who follow the path leading to You.  I also wish to “see” the kind Waheguru who is pervading the whole universe.  I will gladly surrender my heart (mind) to the friend who is connected with Waheguru by taking shelter of the Guru.          2.          They are very lucky who have realized You, oh Waheguru!  Even though You are present in everybody, yet You are unaffected by Maya, and have no worldly attachment.  They have controlled all the demons (lust, rage, greed, attachment and conceit), and swim across the sea of Maya in the company of the Guru.        3.
I have taken refuge in such fortunate individuals and have gotten rid of all my ego, power, and the darkness of attachment.  (I beg of them only) for fostering of Waheguru’s Naam, who is inaccessible and unfathomable, oh Nanak!  4.20.27.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਤੂੰ ਪੇਡੁ ਸਾਖ ਤੇਰੀ ਫੂਲੀ॥ ਤੂੰ ਸੂਖਮੁ ਹੋਆ ਅਸਥੂਲੀ॥ ਤੂ ਜਲਨਿਧਿ ਤੂ ਫੇਨੁ ਬੁਦਬੁਦਾ
ਤੁਧੁ ਬਿਨੁ ਅਵਰੁ ਨ ਭਾਲੀਐ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਤੂੰ ਸੂਤੁ ਮਣੀਏ ਭੀ ਤੂੰ ਹੈ॥ ਤੂੰ ਗੰਠੀ ਮੇਰੁ ਸਿਰਿ ਤੂੰ ਹੈ॥ ਆਦਿ ਮਧਿ ਅੰਤਿ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਸੋਈ ਅਵਰੁ ਨ ਕੋਈ ਦਿਖਾਲੀਐ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਤੂੰ ਨਿਰਗੁਣ ਸਰਗੁਣੁ ਸੁਖਦਾਤਾ॥ ਤੂੰ ਨਿਰਬਾਣੁ ਰਸੀਆ ਰੰਗਿ ਰਾਤਾ॥ ਅਪਣੇ ਕਰਤਬ ਆਪੇ ਜਾਣਹਿ ਆਪੇ ਤੁਧੁ ਸਮਾਲੀਐ ਜੀਉ॥ 3॥ ਤੂੰ ਠਾਕੁਰੁ ਸੇਵਕੁ ਫੁਨਿ ਆਪੇ॥ ਤੂੰ ਗੁਪਤੁ ਪਰਗਟੁ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਆਪੇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਦਾਸੁ ਸਦਾ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਵੈ ਇਕ ਭੋਰੀ ਨਦਰਿ ਨਿਹਾਲੀਐ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥21॥28॥
Majh Mehla 5:   Toon payd(u), shaakh Tayri phooli. Toon sookhum(u) hoa asthooli. Tu julnidh(i) Tu phayn(u) boodbooda,  toodh(u) bin(u) uver(u) na bhaaliyaiy jeeo.  1. Toon soot(i), muniyay bhee Toon haiyn. Toon guntthi, Mayr(u) sir(i) Toon haiyn. Aad(i) mudh(i) unt(i) Prubh soyee, uver(u) na koyee dikhaaliyaiy jeeo.  2.  Toon Nirgoon, Surgoon, Sookhdaata. Toon nirbaan(u), rusiya rung(i) raata. Apnay kertub aapay jaaneh(i), aapay Toodh(u) smaaliyaiy jeeo.  Toon Tthaakur(u) sayvuk phoon(i) aapay. Toon goopt pergut(u) Prubh aapay. Nanak Dass(u) suda goon gaavaiy, ikk bhoari nudr(i) nihaaliyaiy jeeo.  4.21.28.
Word Meaning:   Payd(u) = tree.    Shaakh = branches.    Sookhum(u) = minuscule.  Asthooli = huge.   Julnidh(i) = treasure of water (ocean).  Phayn(u) = suds.   Boodbooda = bubble.  Uver(u) = other than.   Bhaaliyaiy = find.      1.       Soot(u) = (thread of) cotton.  Muniyay = beads.   Guntthi = knot.   Mayr(u) = supreme (bead in the centre).  Aad(i) = beginning.   Mudh(i) = middle.  Unt(i) = (in the) end.       2.        Nirgoon = formless (invisible).   Surgoon = The visible universe (is His visible form).   Nirbaan(u) = without any desire (above the effects of Maya).   Rusiya = source of all tastes.  Kertub = amazing feats.    Smaaliyaiy = look after; remember (worship).       3.      Tthaakur = Master; Lord.   Phoon(i) = again; too.   Goopt() = hidden.   Pergut(u) = visible.   Bhoari = a little.   Nudr(i) = kind sight.   Nihaaliyaiy = cast sight.4.   
NOTE: Mayr(u) is a mythical mountain of gold and all the precious stones. (Hindu Granths).

MEANING:  If You are compared to a tree, then the whole universe is like Your branches, oh Waheguru!  You have transformed from minuscule to huge (the universe).  You, Yourself, are the ocean, and Yourself the suds-bubbles on the surface; no one else can be seen like You.     1.             You are the thread, and also the beads in the rosary.  You are the knot, and you are the main supreme (Mayru) bead.  You were at the beginning of the universe, are there now, and will be there at the end; nobody else can be seen like You.        2.
You are formless, and You are in the whole universe, oh, the source of all comforts, Waheguru!  You are the source of all essences, imbued with love for Your Creation, yet You are completely detached from the world.  Only You know all Your amazing feats of how You look after Your Creation.        3         You are the Lord and also the devotee.  You are hidden, and You are visible (in Your Creation).  Please cast a kind sight on me, even if it is for a very short period of time, so that I may always sing Your virtues, begs Your slave, Nanak.  4.21.28.
