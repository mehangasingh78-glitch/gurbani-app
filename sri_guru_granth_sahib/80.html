Word Meaning:  Moondh(u) = young woman (human).  Iyaani = ignorant.   Payiurraiy = (in) parental house (this world).  Pikhaiy = see; have sight of.    Saahur-rraiy Kumm = responsibilities of in-laws’ house (of next world; Waheguru’s home).   Suhiyaan = (devotee) friends.   Soohayli = enjoying comfort.   Baanh loodayay = walk with arms swinging (with pride).   Laykha Baaki = account balance.   Kirkhaiy = pulls out.  
NOTE: Even though the holy Shabad is written in the first person, it applies to the whole world; therefore, the meaning will be explained in the third person. 
MEANING: Like a young girl, who is ignorant about her future responsibilities, and her behaviour in her in-laws’ house while she is still at her parents’ house; living in this world, human beings are ignorant about their responsibilities about Waheguru as to how to achieve union with Him who will be their future husband; so how can they have a glance of Waheguru in this life?  However, if Waheguru is kind to some devotee, he is connected to the Guru, and under His shelter, the devotee can learn the behaviour with which he can unite with Waheguru.  Such an individual (human bride) learns that reciting Waheguru’s Naam all the time is the way to get connected with Him.  She exudes confidence and enjoys comfort (blissfulness) among her devotee-friends and feels proud (is honoured) in Waheguru’s Court.  If there is any balance of bad deeds in her account with the Justice of deeds, she pulls it out (pays it off) by reciting Waheguru’s Naam.  By doing so (reciting Waheguru’s Naam) in the Guru’s shelter, the ignorant young woman can have a glance of Waheguru even in this world.    1.  Veaah(u) hoa mayray babola, Gurmukhay Hur(i) paaya. Word Meaning:   Gurmukhay = in the shelter of the Guru.   Perchund(u) = very bright.    Blaaya = lighted.   Binsiya = disappeared.   Rattan Pdaruth = highly valuable matter (Waheguru’s Naam).  Laadha = found; acquired.    Laatha = removed; receded.    Aap(u) = self (selfishness).  Abinaashi = immortal.     2. MEANING:  The ceremony of marriage (union) with Waheguru is completed with the guidance of Satguru, oh my father.  The darkness of ignorance is dispelled by the blaze of Divine Knowledge lighted by the Guru. In the bright light of Guru’s Knowledge, the darkness of ignorance is cleared, and the precious Naam of Waheguru is realized.  Along with the discomfort caused by it, the disease of haumein is gone, and one eats away (kills) his selfishness by the Guru’s advice. The immortal and omnipresent Waheguru-husband is realized, who never dies or goes away.  The marriage ceremony with Waheguru is completed by the Guru's kindness, oh my father.  2. MEANING: The marriage party of the immortal Waheguru-husband becomes very impressive (highly noble) with His supreme devotees joining Him, oh my father.  The human bride, who worships Waheguru, is successful in her mission in this world, and by virtue of her reciting Waheguru’s Naam, she looks very pretty (dignified) in the next world.  All those who are able to control their mind and perform noble deeds by taking refuge in the Guru make their life successful, just like a player at the game of dice who throws the dice with full concentration and achieves victory.  In the company of holy men, such devotees have their noble goal achieved, and acquire the source of bliss and omnipresent Waheguru as their husband (achieve union with Him).  The husband, Waheguru, is immortal, and His marriage party becomes very dignified with His supreme devotees joining Him, oh my father.   3. MEANING:  The human bride requests her father, Satguru, to give her Waheguru’s Naam in the dowry in her marriage with Waheguru as the husband.  She requests that her father give her Waheguru’s praise as clothes (to save her dignity), which will lead her to success (in marriage with Waheguru).  The dowry of Worship of Waheguru makes the task of (union with Waheguru) successful; however, this donation can be received only by the Guru's kindness.  Nothing else can match this dowry (Waheguru’s praise), and such a dowry is appreciated in this world and the whole universe.  Any other kind of dowry - like the religious symbols and garb - which the Maya-engrossed gurus give to their disciples publicly, is not only worthless, it is simply a display of ego and show off.  Please give me the dowry of Waheguru’s Naam in my marriage with Waheguru, oh my father, Satguru.    4.
NOTE: While discussing the dowry given by the parents, Guru Sahib is not talking about the dowry in worldly marriages, which people also display; here, Guru Sahib is discussing the dowry only in the marriage with Waheguru. So, He is talking only about the dowry that can be given in such a holy marriage with Waheguru. Here, a Sloak of Guru Nanak Sahib will be very appropriate.

ਮਨ ਪਿਆਰਿਆ ਜੀਉ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਗੋਬਿੰਦ ਨਾਮੁ ਸਮਾਲੇ॥ਮਨ ਪਿਆਰਿਆ ਜੀ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਹਰਿ ਨਿਬਹੈ ਤੇਰੈ ਨਾਲੇ॥ ਸੰਗਿ ਸਹਾਈ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਧਿਆਈ ਬਿਰਥਾ ਕੋਇ ਨ ਜਾਏ॥ ਮਨ ਚਿੰਦੇ ਸੇਈ ਫਲ ਪਾਵਹਿ ਚਰਣ ਕਮਲ ਚਿਤੁ ਲਾਏ॥ ਜਲਿ ਥਲਿ ਪੂਰਿ ਰਹਿਆ ਬਨਵਾਰੀ ਘਟਿ ਘਟਿ ਨਦਰਿ ਨਿਹਾਲੇ॥ ਨਾਨਕੁ ਸਿਖ ਦੇਇ ਮਨ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਮ ਸਾਧਸੰਗਿ ਭ੍ਰਮੁ ਜਾਲੇ॥ 1॥ਮਨ ਪਿਆਰਿਆ ਜੀ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਹਰਿ ਬਿਨੁ ਝੂਠਿ ਪਸਾਰੇ॥ ਮਨ ਪਿਆਰਿਆ ਜੀਉ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਬਿਖੁ ਸਾਗਰੁ ਸੰਸਾਰੇ॥ ਚਰਣ ਕਮਲ ਕਰਿ ਬੋਹਿਥੁ ਕਰਤੇ ਸਹਸਾ ਦੂਖੁ ਨ ਬਿਆਪੈ॥ ਗੁਰੁ ਪੂਰਾ ਭੇਟੈ ਵਡਭਾਗੀ ਆਠ ਪਹਰ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਜਾਪੈ॥ ਆਦਿ ਜੁਗਾਦੀ ਸੇਵਕ ਸੁਆਮੀ ਭਗਤਾ ਨਾਮੁ ਅਧਾਰੇ॥ ਨਾਨਕੁ ਸਿਖ ਦੇਇ ਮਨ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਮ ਬਿਨੁ ਹਰਿ ਝੂਠ ਪਸਾਰੇ॥ 2॥ਮਨ ਪਿਆਰਿਆ ਜੀਉ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਹਰਿ ਲਦੇ ਖੇਪ ਸਵਲੀ॥ ਮਨ ਪਿਆਰਿਆ ਜੀਉ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਹਰਿ ਦਰੁ ਨਿਹਚਲੁ ਮਲੀ॥ ਹਰਿ ਦਰੁ ਸੇਵੇ ਅਲਖ ਅਭੇਵੇ ਨਿਹਚਲੁ ਆਸਣੁ ਪਾਇਆ॥ ਤਹ ਜਨਮ ਨ ਮਰਣੁ ਨ ਆਵਣ ਜਾਣਾ ਸੰਸਾ ਦੂਖੁ ਮਿਟਾਇਆ॥ ਚਿਤ੍ਰ ਗੁਪਤ ਕਾ ਕਾਗਦੁ ਫਾਰਿਆ ਜਮਦੁਤਾ ਕਛੂ ਨ ਚਲੀ॥ ਨਾਨਕੁ ਸਿਖ ਦੇਇ ਮਨ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਮ ਹਰਿ ਲਦੇ ਖੇਪ ਸਵਲੀ॥ 3॥ ਮਨ ਪਿਆਰਿਆ ਜੀਉ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਕਰਿ ਸੰਤਾ ਸੰਗਿ ਨਿਵਾਸੋ॥ ਮਨ ਪਿਆਰਿਆ ਜੀਉ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਜਪਤ ਪਰਗਾਸੋ॥ ਸਿਮਰਿ ਸੁਆਮੀ ਸੁਖਹ ਗਾਮੀ ਇਛ ਸਗਲੀ ਪੁੰਨੀਆ॥
