(ਅੰਗ 111)॥     ਲਖ ਚਉਰਾਸੀਹ ਜੀਅ ਉਪਾਏ॥ ਜਿਸ ਨੋ ਨਦਰਿ ਕਰੇ ਤਿਸੁ ਗੁਰੂ ਮਿਲਾਏ॥ ਕਿਲਬਿਖ ਕਾਟਿ ਸਦਾ ਜਨ ਨਿਰਮਲ ਦਰਿ ਸਚੈ ਨਾਮਿ ਸੁਹਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 6॥ ਲੇਖਾ ਮਾਗੈ ਤਾ ਕਿਨਿ ਦੀਐ॥ ਸੁਖੁ ਨਾਹੀ ਫੁਨਿ ਦੂਐ ਤੀਐ॥ ਆਪੇ ਬਖਸਿ ਲਏ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਸਾਚਾ ਆਪੇ ਬਖਸਿ ਮਿਲਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 7॥ ਆਪਿ ਕਰੇ ਤੈ ਆਪਿ ਕਰਾਏ॥ ਪੂਰੇ ਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਸਬਦਿ ਮਿਲਾਏ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮੁ ਮਿਲੈ ਵਡਿਆਈ ਆਪੇ ਮੇਲਿ ਮਿਲਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 8॥2॥3॥
Majh Mehla 3:  Mayra Prubh Nirmul(u) Agumm Apaara. Bin(u) tukkrri toalaiy sunsaara. Gurmukh(i) hovaiy soyee boojhaiy, goon keh(i) Gooni smaavniya. 1.  Huoun vaari jeeo vaari, Hur(i) ka Naam(u) munn(i) vusaavuniya.  Jo Sach(i) laagay say undin(u) jaagay, dur Sachay sobha paavniya. 1. Ruhaou.  Aap(i) soonaiy taiy Aapay vaykhaiy.  Jis nu nudr(i) kuray soyee jun(u) laykhaiy. Aapay laayay luyyay so laagay, Gurmukh(i) Sach(u) kmaavniya. 2.  Jis(u) aap(i) bhoolaayay so kithaiy huth(u) paayay. Poorub(i) likhiya so maytna na jaayay. Jin Satgur miliya say vudbhaagi, pooraiy kerm(i) milaavniya. 3. Payiurraiy dhun undin(u) sooti. Kunt(i) visaari uvgun(i) mooti. Undin(u) suda phiraiy bil-laandi, bin(u) Pir neend na paavniya.  4. Payiurraiy Sookhdaata jaata. Haumein maar(i) Gur Shabad(i) puchhaata. Sayj soohaavi suda Pir(u) raavay, Sach(u) seegaar(u) bunaavniya. 5. 
(PAGE 111)   Lukh chauraasee jea oopaayay. Jis nu nudr(i) kuray tis(u) Guru milaayay. Kilvikh kaat(i) suda jun nirmul, Dur Sachaiy Naam(i) soohaavniya.   6. Laykha maangaiy ta kin(i) deeyaiy. Sookh(u) naahi phoon(i) dooyaiy teeyaiy.  Aapay bukhsh(i) luyay Prubh(u) Saacha, aapay bukhsh(i) milaavniya.  7.  Aap(i) kuray taiy Aap(i) kuraayay. Pooray Gur kaiy Shabad(i) milayay. Nanak Naam(u) milaiy vudiyayee, Aapay mayl(i) milaavniya.  8.2.3.
Word Meaning:   Nimul = pure (without prejudice).    Agumm = inaccessible.    Apaara = which has no end (limitless).    Tukkrri = weighing scale.    Toalaiy = weighs; assesses.    Boojhaiy = realizes.    Gooni = the source of virtues (God).       1. Vaari = offer self for sacrifice (or services).    Vusaavniya = make to reside in the heart; store in memory; connect with Him.  Undin(u) = day and night.   Jaagay = stay alert; watchful.   Dur Sachay = at the door of Waheguru.   Sobha = praise.         Pause.
Nudr(i) = kind sight.     Laykhaiy = is approved (declared successful).   Kmaavniya = earn Truth (connect with God).       2.      Bhoolaayay = misguides.   Huth(u) Paayay = lay hand; seek support.     Poorub(i) Likhiya = pre-written (fortune) based on the deeds of previous lives.   Maytna = erase.    Vudbhaagi = very lucky.    Kurm(u) = kindness.       3.      Payiurraiy = parents’ house (this life).   Dhun = woman (human being).   Sooti = unaware (about Waheguru).   Kunt = husband (Waheguru).  Vissari = forgotten; ignored.   Mooti = deceived (robbed of good qualities).    Bil-laandi = wail; complain; never content.   Pir = husband (Waheguru).         4.     Jaata = Puchhaata = recognized (connected with).    Sayj = bed.   Soohaavi = comforting.   Raavay = enjoys.    Seegaar(u) = adornment.        5.     Lukh Chauraasee = 8.4 million life forms (A Hindu belief).    Oopaayay = created.   Kilvikh = sins.   Dur Sachaiy = at Waheguru’s door.         6.      Laykha = account of good or bad deeds.     Phoon(i) = again; also.   Dooyaiy Teeyaiy = the other, (involving) Maya.       Bukhsh(i) = forgive.          7.
Kur(i)rayay = makes others do.     Vudiyaayee = respect.       8.
MEANING:  My Waheguru is pure (without prejudice), inaccessible and limitless. He weighs (evaluates) the world without using any scale.  But only that individual realizes this fact who has surrendered to the Guru, and is able (is qualified) to merge with the source of virtues (Waheguru) by singing His praise.            1.            I will sacrifice my life for those individuals who have made their hearts a place for Waheguru to reside (those who are connected with God).  Those who are connected with God are alert (against the attacks of evil desires) day and night, and are honoured in Waheguru’s Court.         Pause.             He Himself listens to and looks after the needs of His creation, but only that individual is successful on whom Waheguru casts His kind sight.  Only those individuals who put in effort to connect with Him are prompted by Waheguru Himself to do so.  Those who follow Guru’s advice earn (acquire) Waheguru’s Naam.         2.             What support (or shelter) can one have if he is misled by Waheguru Himself?  Pre-written destiny (based on the deeds of previous lives) cannot be erased (by the individual with his own efforts).  However, those are very fortunate who get connected with the Guru by the kindness of Waheguru.          3.          A woman (human bride), who is always unaware of Waheguru in her present life, is robbed (of her spirituality) by her ignoble qualities (due to worldly attractions) and ignores Waheguru.  She cannot enjoy a peaceful sleep (life) and wails (complains and suffers) all the time.       4.
(On the other hand), a woman (human being) who recognizes (make connection with) the source of all comfort, Waheguru, in this life, overcomes her haumein by acting on the Shabad of the Guru. She adorns herself with virtues to be connected with Waheguru, and the bed of her heart becomes a source of bliss all the time, as she enjoys her husband’s (Waheguru’s) company.          5.        Waheguru has created 8.4 million life forms, but He connects only that individual with the Guru, on whom He casts His kind sight. Such an individual gets rid of all his sins; becomes completely pure, and with Waheguru’s Naam at heart, he looks exalted (honourable) in Waheguru’s Court.          6.            Humans indulge in sensual pleasures, but there is no joy in such deeds (as they are not approved by Waheguru). So, who can (no one can) pass Waheguru’s test for His approval when the account of one’s deeds is examined? But the eternal Waheguru forgives some of us (who deserve it), and absorbs them into Him (connects them with Him).          7.         Waheguru Himself does, and makes His creation do, whatever He wishes it to do, and connects some with Him by making them act on the Guru’s advice.  Waheguru Himself connects individuals with Him by first connecting them with the Guru, who implants Waheguru’s Naam in them, thereby honouring them in His Court, oh Nanak! 8.2.3.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ ਇਕੋ ਆਪਿ ਫਿਰੈ ਪਰਛੰਨਾ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਵੇਖਾ ਤਾ ਇਹੁ ਮਨੁ ਭਿੰਨਾ॥ ਤ੍ਰਿਸਨਾ ਤਜਿ ਸਹਜ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਏਕੋ ਮੰਨਿ ਵਸਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਹਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਜੀਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਇਕਸੁ ਸਿਉ ਚਿਤੁ ਲਾਵਣਿਆ॥ ਗੁਰਮਤੀ ਮਨੁ ਇਕਤੁ ਘਰਿ ਆਇਆ ਸਚੈ ਰੰਗਿ ਰੰਗਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਇਹੁ ਜਗੁ ਭੂਲਾ ਤੈ ਆਪਿ ਭੁਲਾਇਆ॥ ਇਕੁ ਵਿਸਾਰਿ ਦੂਜੈ ਲੋਭਾਇਆ॥ ਅਨਦਿਨੁ ਸਦਾ ਫਿਰੈ ਭ੍ਰਮਿ ਭੂਲਾ ਬਿਨੁ ਨਾਵੈ ਦੁਖੁ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 2॥ ਜੋ ਰੰਗਿ ਰਾਤੇ ਕਰਮ ਬਿਧਾਤੇ॥ ਗੁਰ ਸੇਵਾ ਤੇ ਜੁਗ ਚਾਰੇ ਜਾਤੇ॥ ਜਿਸ ਨੋ ਆਪਿ ਦੇਇ ਵਡਿਆਈ ਹਰਿ ਕੈ ਨਾਮਿ ਸਮਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 3॥ ਮਾਇਆ ਮੋਹਿ ਹਰਿ ਚੇਤੇ ਨਾਹੀ॥ ਜਮਪੁਰਿ ਬਧਾ ਦੁਖ ਸਹਾਹੀ॥ ਅੰਨਾ ਬੋਲਾ ਕਿਛੁ ਨਦਰਿ ਨ ਆਵੈ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਪਾਪਿ ਪਚਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 4॥ ਇਕਿ ਰੰਗਿ ਰਾਤੇ ਜੋ ਤੁਧੁ ਆਪਿ ਲਿਵ ਲਾਏ॥ ਭਾਇ ਭਗਤਿ ਤੇਰੈ ਮਨਿ ਭਾਏ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਸੇਵਨਿ ਸਦਾ ਸੁਖਦਾਤਾ ਸਭ ਇਛਾ ਆਪਿ ਪੁਜਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 5॥ ਹਰਿ ਜੀਉ ਤੇਰੀ ਸਦਾ ਸਰਣਾਈ॥ ਆਪੇ ਬਖਸਿਹਿ ਦੇ ਵਡਿਆਈ॥ ਜਮਕਾਲੁ ਤਿਸੁ ਨੇੜਿ ਨਾ ਆਵੈ ਜੋ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਧਿਆਵਣਿਆ॥ 6॥ ਅਨਦਿਨੁ ਰਾਤੇ ਜੋ ਹਰਿ ਭਾਏ॥ ਮੇਰੈ ਪ੍ਰਭਿ ਮੇਲੇ ਮੇਲਿ ਮਿਲਾਏ॥ ਸਦਾ ਸਦਾ ਤੇਰੀ ਸਰਣਾਈ  ਤੂ ਆਪੇ ਸਚੁ ਬੁਝਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 7॥ ਜਿਨ ਸਚੁ ਜਾਤਾ ਸੇ ਸਚਿ ਸਮਾਣੇ॥ ਹਰਿ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਵਹਿ ਸਚੁ ਵਖਾਣੇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮਿ ਰਤੇ ਬੈਰਾਗੀ ਨਿਜ ਘਰਿ ਤਾੜੀ ਲਾਵਣਿਆ॥  8॥3॥4॥
Majh Mehla 3:  Ikko Aap(i) phiraiy perchhunna. Gurmukh(i) vaykhaan ta eho mun(u) bhinna.  Trishna tuj(i) sehj sookh(u) paayaa, Aiko munn(i) vusaavniya. 1. Huoun vaari jeeo vaari, Ikkus(u) siyoun chit(u) laavniya. Gurmat(i) mun(u) Ikut(u) ghur(i) aaya Sachaiy rung(i) rungaavniya.  1. Ruhaou.   Eho jug(u) bhoola Taiyn aap(i) bhoolaaya. Ikk(u) visaar(i) doojaiy lobhaaya.  Undin(u) suda phiraiy bherm(i) bhoola, bin(u) Naavaiy dookh(u) paavniya.  2. Jo rung(i) raatay kerm Bidhaatay.  Gur sayva tay joog chaaray jaatay. Jis nu Aap(i) dayay vudiyaayi, Hur(i) kaiy Naam(i) smaavniya. 3.  Maya moh(i) Hur(i) chaytay naahi. Jumpur(i) buddha dookh suhaahi.  Unnah bola kichh(u) nudr(i) na aavaiy, munmukh paap(i) puchaavniya.  4.  Ikk(i) rung(i) raatay jo Toodh(u) Aap(i) liv laayay.  Bhaayay bhugut(i) Tayraiy mun(i) bhaayay. Satgur(u) sayvun(i) suda Sookhdaata, subh ichhaan Aap(i) poojaavniya. 5. Hur(i) Jeeo Tayri suda shernaayee.  Aapay bukhsheh(i) day vudiyaayi.  Jumkaal(u) tis(u) nayrr(i) na aavaiy, jo Hur(i) Hur(i) Naam(u) dhiyaavniya. 6.   Undin(u) raatay jo Hur(i) bhaayay.  Mayraiy Prubh(i) maylay mayl(i) milaayay. Suda suda Tayri shernaayee, Tu Aapay Sach(u) boojhaavniya.  7.  Jin Sach(u) jaata say Sach(i) smaanay.  Hur(i) goon gaaveh(i) Sach(u) vukhaanay. Nanak Naam(i) ruttay baiyraagi, nij ghur(i) taarri laavniya.  8.3.4.
Word Meaning:  Perchhunna = hidden.    Bhinna = imbued.   Trishna = desires.   Sehj Sookh(u) = bliss of a higher spiritual state.       1.      Ikkus(u) siyoun = just with the One.     Rung(i) = in love.      Pause.      Bisaar(i) = forgetting.   Lobhaaya = captivated.  Bherm(i) = in doubts (misbeliefs).      2.        Kurm Bidhaatay = writer of future deeds (fortune) = Waheguru.   Jaatay = recognized, famed.   Vudiyaayee = honour; recognition.       3.
Chaytaiy = remembers (Naam).  Jumpur = in the city (court) of the angel of death.  Suhaayee = endures; suffers.    Puchaavniya = burn in suffering.         4.
Rung(i) raatay = imbued in love.    Bhaayay Bhugat(i) = in love with worship.   Ichhaan = wishes.    Pujaavniya = fulfills.        5.      Shenaayee = shelter.   Bukhsheh(i) = forgive.    Jumkaal(u) = angel of death.        6.      Undin(u) = every day (always).   Mayl(i) = connecting (with the Guru).         7.      Smaanay = merge; mix well.  Vukhaanay = discuss.   Bairaagi = minds turned away from Maya (renouncers of Maya).   Nij Ghur(i) =  in one's own mind.  Taarri = meditate with full concentration.         8. 
MEANING:  It is only Waheguru which operates hidden in the world.  My mind gets imbued in His love when I “see” Him in the shelter of the Guru.  One can realize Waheguru in his heart (which becomes Waheguru’s residence) by getting rid of all his desires, and attain the bliss of the higher spiritual state (above the effects of Maya).  1. 
             I wish to sacrifice my life for those who have only the “One” Waheguru in their mind. When one obeys the Guru’s advice, his mind comes to rest in itself (quits wandering) and he gets imbued in Waheguru’s love.        Pause.
This world has been misled by You (for being engrossed in Maya), oh Waheguru!  It has forgotten You, and has been captivated by Maya. It is lost in misbeliefs day and night, and suffers as a result.             2.           Those who are imbued with the love of the creator of our fortunes (Waheguru) come to be recognized all over the world due to their serving (obeying) the Guru.  Those who are honoured by Waheguru become “one” with Him.         3.
In the love for Maya, those who do not remember Waheguru suffer in the court of the Angel of justice.  They are blind and deaf as far as the worship of Waheguru is concerned, and cannot see the reality; such followers of their mind suffer because of their sins.          4.          Some, who are prompted by You, are imbued in Your love; and You like Your worship which is performed with love, oh Waheguru!  Those who serve Satguru, which is the source of all comfort, have all their wishes fulfilled by You, oh Waheguru!          5.         You forgive and honour (Your devotees), oh Waheguru! I am always in Your shelter. The Angel of death does not come even close to those who recite Your Naam.         6.         Those who are liked by Waheguru are always imbued with His love. Waheguru connects them with Him through the Guru.  I am always in Your shelter; You, Yourself, enlighten Your devotees, oh Waheguru!           7.
Those who recognize (get connected with) Waheguru become “one” with Him.  They discuss Waheguru’s virtues and recite His Naam.  Those who are imbued in Waheguru’s Naam are the real renouncers of Maya, and they meditate on Waheguru with full concentration in their own mind, oh Nanak.    8.3.4.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ ਸਬਦਿ ਮਰੈ ਸੁ ਮੁਆ ਜਾਪੈ॥ ਕਾਲੁ ਨ ਚਾਪੈ ਦੁਖੁ ਨ ਸੰਤਾਪੈ॥ ਜੋਤੀ ਵਿਚਿ ਮਿਲਿ ਜੋਤਿ ਸਮਾਣੀ ਸੁਣਿ ਮਨੁ ਸਚਿ ਸਮਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਹਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਜੀਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਹਰਿ ਕੈ ਨਾਇ ਸੋਭਾ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਸੇਵਿ ਸਚਿ ਚਿਤੁ ਲਾਇਆ ਗੁਰਮਤੀ ਸਹਜਿ ਸਮਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਕਾਇਆ ਕਚੀ ਕਚਾ ਚੀਰੁ ਹੰਢਾਏ॥ ਦੂਜੈ ਲਾਗੀ ਮਹਲੁ ਨ ਪਾਏ॥
