ੴ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਪ੍ਰਸਾਦਿ॥ ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 1 ਪਹਰੇ ਘਰੁ 1॥ 
        Ek Onkar Satgur Parsad(i): Siri Raag(u) Mehla Pehray Ghur(u) 1 :

ਪਹਿਲੈ ਪਹਰੈ ਰੈਣਿ ਕੈ ਵਣਜਾਰਿਆ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਹੁਕਮਿ ਪਇਆ ਗਰਭਾਸਿ॥ ਉਰਧ ਤਪੁ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਕਰੇ ਵਣਜਾਰਿਆ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਖਸਮ ਸੇਤੀ ਅਰਦਾਸਿ॥ ਖਸਮ ਸੇਤੀ ਅਰਦਾਸਿ ਵਖਾਣੈ ਉਰਧ ਧਿਆਨਿ ਲਿਵ ਲਾਗਾ॥ ਨਾ ਮਰਜਾਦੁ ਆਇਆ ਕਲਿ ਭੀਤਰਿ ਬਾਹੁੜਿ ਜਾਸੀ ਨਾਗਾ॥ ਜੈਸੀ ਕਲਮ ਵੁੜੀ ਹੈ ਮਸਤਕਿ ਤੈਸੀ ਜੀਅੜੇ ਪਾਸਿ॥ਕਹੁ ਨਾਨਕ ਪ੍ਰਾਣੀ ਪਹਿਲੈ ਪਹਰੈ ਹੁਕਮਿ ਪਇਆ ਗਰਭਾਸਿ॥ 1॥
(ਅੰਗ 75)॥      ਦੂਜੈ ਪਹਰੈ ਰੈਣਿ ਕੈ ਵਣਜਾਰਿਆ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਵਿਸਰਿ ਗਇਆ ਧਿਆਨੁ॥ ਹਥੋ ਹਥਿ ਨਚਾਈਐ ਵਣਜਾਰਿਆ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਜਿਉ ਜਸੁਦਾ ਘਰਿ ਕਾਨੁ॥ ਹਥੋ ਹਥਿ ਨਚਾਈਐ ਪ੍ਰਾਣੀ ਮਾਤ ਕਹੈ ਸੁਤੁ ਮੇਰਾ॥ ਚੇਤਿ ਅਚੇਤ ਮੂੜ ਮਨ ਮੇਰੇ ਅੰਤਿ ਨਹੀ ਕਛੁ ਤੇਰਾ॥ ਜਿਨਿ ਰਚਿ ਰਚਿਆ ਤਿਸਹਿ ਨ ਜਾਣੈ ਮਨ ਭੀਤਰਿ ਧਰਿ ਗਿਆਨੁ॥ ਕਹੁ ਨਾਨਕ ਦੂਜੈ ਪਹਰੈ ਵਿਸਰਿ ਗਇਆ ਧਿਆਨੁ॥ 2॥ ਤੀਜੈ ਪਹਰੈ ਰੈਣਿ ਕੈ ਵਣਜਾਰਿਆ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਧਨ ਜੋਬਨ ਸਿਉ ਚਿਤੁ॥ ਹਰਿ ਕਾ ਨਾਮੁ ਨ ਚੇਤਹੀ ਵਣਜਾਰਿਆ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਬਧਾ ਛੁਟਹਿ ਜਿਤੁ॥ ਹਰਿ ਕਾ ਨਾਮੁ ਨ ਚੇਤੈ ਪ੍ਰਾਣੀ ਬਿਕਲੁ ਭਇਆ ਸੰਗਿ ਮਾਇਆ॥ ਧਨ ਸਿਉ ਰਤਾ ਜੋਬਨਿ ਮਤਾ ਅਹਿਲਾ ਜਨਮੁ ਗਵਾਇਆ॥ ਧਰਮ ਸੇਤੀ ਵਾਪਾਰੁ ਨ ਕੀਤੋ ਕਰਮੁ ਨ ਕੀਤੋ ਮਿਤੁ॥ ਕਹੁ ਨਾਨਕ ਤੀਜੈ ਪਹਰੈ ਪ੍ਰਾਣੀ ਧਨ ਜੋਬਨਿ ਸਿਉ ਚਿਤੁ॥ 3॥ ਚਉਥੈ ਪਹਰੈ ਰੈਣਿ ਕੈ ਵਣਜਾਰਿਆ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਲਾਵੀ ਆਇਆ ਖੇਤੁ॥ ਜਾ ਜਮਿ ਪਕੜਿ ਚਲਾਇਆ ਵਣਜਾਰਿਆ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਕਿਸੈ ਨ ਮਿਲਿਆ ਭੇਤੁ॥ ਭੇਤੁ ਚੇਤੁ ਹਰਿ ਕਿਸੈ ਨ ਮਿਲਿਓ ਜਾ ਜਮਿ ਪਕੜਿ ਚਲਾਇਆ॥ ਝੂਠਾ ਰੁਦਨੁ ਹੋਆ ਦੁੋਆਲੈ ਖਿਨ ਮਹਿ ਭਇਆ ਪਰਾਇਆ॥ ਸਾਈ ਵਸਤੁ ਪਰਾਪਤਿ ਹੋਈ ਜਿਸੁ ਸਿਉ ਲਾਇਆ ਹੇਤੁ॥ ਕਹੁ ਨਾਨਕ ਚਉਥੈ ਪਹਰੈ ਲਾਵੀ ਲੁਣਿਆ ਖੇਤੁ॥ 4॥1॥
Pehlaiy pehraiy raiyn(i) kaiy vunjaariya mittra, Huqam(i) puyaa gerbhaas(i). Oordh tup(u) untar(i) kuray vunjaariya mittra, Khusm saytee ardaas(i).  Khusm saytee ardaas(i) vukhaanaiy, oordh dhiyaan(i) liv laaga.  Na merjaad(u) aaya kul(i) bheeter(i), bahourr(i) jaasi naanga. Jaisi kulm voorri hai mustuk(i), taisi jeeurray paas(i).  Kuhou Nanak praani pehlaiy pehraiy, Huqam(i) puyya gerbhaas(i).     1. NOTE: (1) As per Guru Granth Sahib, human life is an opportunity given to man to unite with Waheguru, but the enchantment of Maya is a big hurdle in the path to uniting with God. Humans struggle to overcome the allure of Maya, but in most cases, they lose the battle to its deceit. Guru Sahib compares the role of man in the world to a trader who trades his merchandise for money; a man mostly trades his virtuous qualities for the gimmicks of Maya. (2) Pehr is a unit of time and equals three hours. Man’s life is compared to the night, which he spends in darkness (of ignorance). On average, one per hour is a quarter of a night (or a quarter of a life).   
(3) It is a Hindu belief that the fetus does tup(u) (recites God’s Naam) in the womb.
Word Meaning:  Raiyn(i) = night (life).    Vunjaara = trader.   Gerbhaas(i) = (in the) womb.    Oordh = upside down.    Khusm = Lord.    Saytee = for.    Vukhaanaiy = says.   Liv Laaga = fully focused (on God’s Naam).  Na Merjaad = uncustomary (naked).   Kul(i) = (world of) of darkness (ignorance).     Bahourr(i) = again.    Kulm = pen.  Voorri = moved (wrote).   Mustuk(i) = forehead.      Praani = man.      1.
MEANING:  There is only one Supreme Being, which can be realized only by the benevolence of Satguru.  
Humans, who are here in this world as traders in Waheguru’s Naam, do not realize that the first quarter of their life starts in their mother’s womb as per the Huqam (Law) of Waheguru.  (As per the Hindu belief) They recite Waheguru’s Naam (they are connected with Him) while they are hanging upside down in the womb, and pray to the Lord.  Upside down, they are connected with God and pray to Him (to get them out of the unfavourable situation).  They come naked in the world (without any wealth), and will leave naked again. The individual inherits the disposition according to what has been inscribed on his forehead by Waheguru’s pen.  The first quarter of one’s life starts with conception in the mother’s womb as per Waheguru’s Laws, oh Nanak.       1.
(PAGE 75):    Doojaiy pehraiy raiyn(i) kaiy vunjaariya mittra vissar(i) guyya dhiyaan(u). Hutho huth(i) nuchaayiyaiy vunjaariya mittra, jioun Jashoodha ghur(i) Kahn(u). Hutho huth(i) nuchaayiyaiy praani, maat kuhaiy soot(u) mayra.  Chayt(i) achayt moorr mun mayray, unt(i) nuhee kuchh(u) tayra.  Jin(i) ruch ruchiya Tisheh(i) na jaanaiy, mun bheetar(i) dhur(i) Giyan(u).  Kuhou Nanak doojaiy pehraiy, vissar(i) guyya dhiyaan(u).      2. Word Meaning:  Vissar(i) = lost (from mind).    Dhiyaan(u) = focus.    Nuchaayiyaiy = toss around.  Jasoodha = the godmother of Krishna, who raised him in Gokul.  Kahn(u) = Krishna.   Praani = oh, man.  Soot(u) = son.   Chayt(i) = remember.   Achayt = unaware.   Moorr = foolish.   Bheetar(i) = inside.   Giyaan(u) = (Divine) Knowledge.  2.
MEANING:  In the second quarter of life, the trader of Waheguru’s Naam loses the focus on Waheguru’s Naam, which he was having in his mother’s womb.  His focus shifts to the love and attention he receives when he is tossed affectionately from one of his relatives to another, just as (Sri) Krishna was being raised by his godmother, Yashoda, in her house.  As the infant is quickly taken from one relative to another, the mother claims that he is her son (everyone makes the same claim as a sign of affection).  Remember (Waheguru), oh, my ignorant and foolish mind, because (despite all the love you receive now), nobody will be there (to help you) when your end comes.  The man does not make any connection with his Creator (Waheguru), which he must maintain.  In the second quarter of life, man loses his focus on Waheguru, oh Nanak.         2. Teejaiy pehraiy raiyn(i) kaiy vunjaariya mittra dhun jobun sioun chit(u). Hur(i) ka Naam(u) na chayt-hee vunjaariya mittra budha chhooteh(i) jit(u). Hur(i) ka Naam(u) na chaytaiy praani bikul(u) bhuyya sung(i) Maya.  Dhun syioun rutta jobun(i) mutta, ehila junum(u) guvaaya.  Dherm saytee vapaar(u) na keeto kerm(u) na keeto mitt(u). Kuhou Nanak teejaiy pehraiy praani, dhun jobun sioun chitt(u).  3. Word Meaning:  Jobun = youthfulness; beauty.    Chit(u) = mind.     Chayt-hee = remember.   Budha = shackled (in the chains of Maya).   Jit(u) = with which.    Bikul(u) = crazy; insane.   Rutta = imbued.    Mutta = intoxicated.    Ehila = precious.   Dherm saytee = of high moral values.    Kerm = (noble) deeds.    Mitt(u) = friend.       3.
MEANING:  In the third quarter of his life, the trader in Waheguru’s Naam gets attached to wealth and beauty.  He does not recite Waheguru’s Naam, which can cut him free from the bonds of Maya.  The man does not recite Waheguru’s Naam as he goes crazy after Maya.  Imbued in, and under the intoxication of wealth and beauty, the man wastes away his precious human life (the opportunity to unite with God).  He did not trade in the high moral values, and made no friendship with noble deeds (did not perform them). In the third quarter, the man is attached to wealth and beauty, oh Nanak.  
3. Chauthaiy pehraiy raiyn kaiy vunjaariya mittra, laavi aaya khayt(u).  Ja Jum pukarr(i) chulaaya vunjaariya mitttra, kisaiy na miliya bhayt(u). Bhayt(u) chayt(u) Hur(i) kisaiy na miliyo, ja jum(i) pukarr(i) chulaaya. Jhoottha roodun(u) hoa doaalaiy khin meh(i) bhuyya praaya. Saayee vust(u) praput(i) hoyee jis(u) sioun laaya hayt(u). Kuhou Nanak praani chauthaiy pehraiy, lava looniya khayt(u).  4.1. Word Meaning:  Laavi = harvester (Angel of death).   Khayt(u) = field (human body).   Jum = Angel of death.   Chayt(u) = mind; knowledge; information.   Roodan(u) = crying; wailing.    Khin = moment.     Bhuyya = became.    Praaya = stranger; disconnected.   Hayt(u) = attachment.    Looniya = harvested (finished off).     4.
MEANING:  In the fourth quarter of life, the Angel of death appears (to be) close.  When the Angel of Death takes control of the individual and takes him away, nobody knows the secret (where the soul of the individual is taken). Nobody gets any information about the secret place where the human trader is taken by the Angel of Death.  The fake crying and wailing (which does not produce any results) takes place around the dead body, which becomes stranger in a moment. One reaps the results of his deeds, performed during his lifetime, to achieve what he loves.  The Angel of Death harvests the field of the human body in the fourth quarter of life, oh Nanak.        4.1.

ਸਿਰੀਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 1॥ ਪਹਿਲੈ ਪਹਰੈ ਰੈਣਿ ਕੈ ਵਣਜਾਰਿਆ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਬਾਲਕ ਬੁਧਿ ਅਚੇਤੁ॥ ਖੀਰੁ ਪੀਐ ਖੇਲਾਈਐ  ਵਣਜਾਰਿਆ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਮਾਤ ਪਿਤਾ ਸੁਤ ਹੇਤੁ॥ ਮਾਤ ਪਿਤਾ ਸੁਤ ਨੇਹੁ ਘਨੇਰਾ ਮਾਇਆ ਮੋਹੁ ਸਬਾਈ॥ ਸੰਜੋਗੀ ਆਇਆ ਕਿਰਤੁ ਕਮਾਇਆ ਕਰਣੀ ਕਾਰ ਕਰਾਈ॥ ਰਾਮ ਨਾਮ ਬਿਨੁ ਮੁਕਤਿ ਨ ਹੋਈ ਬੂਡੀ ਦੂਜੈ ਹੇਤਿ॥ ਕਹੁ ਨਾਨਕ ਪ੍ਰਾਣੀ ਪਹਿਲੈ ਪਹਰੈ ਛੂਟਹਿਗਾ ਹਰਿ ਚੇਤਿ॥ 1॥ ਦੂਜੈ ਪਹਰੈ ਰੈਣਿ ਕੈ ਵਣਜਾਰਿਆ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਭਰਿ ਜੋਬਨਿ ਮੈ ਮਤਿ॥ ਅਹਿਨਿਸਿ ਕਾਮਿ ਵਿਆਪਿਆ ਵਣਜਾਰਿਆ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਅੰਧੁਲੇ ਨਾਮੁ ਨ ਚਿਤਿ॥ ਰਾਮੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਘਟ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਨਾਹੀ ਹੋਰਿ ਜਾਣੈ ਰਸ ਕਸ ਮੀਠੇ॥ ਗਿਆਨੁ ਧਿਆਨੁ ਗੁਣ ਸੰਜਮੁ ਨਾਹੀ ਜਨਮਿ ਮਰਹੁਗੇ ਝੂਠੇ॥ ਤੀਰਥ ਵਰਤ ਸੁਚਿ ਸੰਜਮੁ ਨਾਹੀ ਕਰਮੁ ਧਰਮੁ ਨਹੀ ਪੂਜਾ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਭਾਇ ਭਗਤਿ ਨਿਸਤਾਰਾ ਦੁਬਿਧਾ ਵਿਆਪੈ ਦੂਜਾ॥ 2॥ ਤੀਜੈ ਪਹਰੈ ਰੈਣਿ ਕੈ ਵਣਜਾਰਿਆ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਸਰਿ ਹੰਸ ਉਲਥੜੇ ਆਇ॥ ਜੋਬਨੁ ਘਟੈ ਜਰੂਆ ਜਿਣੈ ਵਣਜਾਰਿਆ ਮਿਤ੍ਰਾ ਆਵ ਘਟੈ ਦਿਨੁ ਜਾਇ॥
