(ਅੰਗ 88)॥   ਸਲੋਕ ਮ:3॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਸੇਵੇ ਆਪਣਾ ਸੋ ਸਿਰੁ ਲੇਖੈ ਲਾਇ॥ਵਿਚਹੁ ਆਪੁ ਗਵਾਇ ਕੈ ਰਹਨਿ ਸਚਿ ਲਿਵ ਲਾਇ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਜਿਨੀ ਨ ਸੇਵਿਓ ਤਿਨਾ ਬਿਰਥਾ ਜਨਮੁ ਗਵਾਇ॥ਨਾਨਕ ਜੋ ਤਿਸੁ ਭਾਵੈ ਸੋ ਕਰੇ ਕਹਣਾ ਕਿਛੂ ਨ ਜਾਇ॥1
(PAGE 88):           Sloak M:3: Satgur sayvay aapna, so sir(u) laykhaiy laayay. Vichoun aap(u) guvaayay kaiy, rehn(i) Sach(i) liv laayay. Satgur jinhi na sayviou, tinha birtha junum guvaayay. Nanak, jo Tis(u) bhaavaiy so kuray, kehna kichhoo na jayay.   1.
Word Meaning:  Laykhaiy Laayay = considered worthy.  Rehn(i) = stay.   Sach(i) = (with) Waheguru.  Liv Laayay = focused mind.   Birtha = empty; waste. 
MEANING:  Only the mind that serves Satguru is considered worthy.  Such individuals overcome selfishness and stay connected with Waheguru with a focused mind. Those who do not serve Satguru waste away their human life.  But they cannot be blamed completely, because they do only what the Will of Waheguru dictates, and nobody can do anything about that (change it), oh Nanak.

ਮ:3॥ ਮਨੁ ਵੇਕਾਰੀ ਵੇੜਿਆ ਵੇਕਾਰਾ ਕਰਮ ਕਮਾਇ॥ ਦੂਜੈ ਭਾਇ ਅਗਿਆਨੀ ਪੂਜਦੇ ਦਰਗਹ ਮਿਲੈ ਸਜਾਇ॥ ਆਤਮ ਦੇਉ ਪੂਜੀਐ ਬਿਨੁ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਬੂਝ ਨ ਪਾਇ॥ ਜਪੁ ਤਪੁ ਸੰਜਮੁ ਭਾਣਾ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੂ ਕਾ ਕਰਮੀ ਪਲੈ ਪਾਇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਸੇਵਾ ਸੁਰਤਿ ਕਮਾਵਣੀ ਜੋ ਹਰਿ ਭਾਵੈ ਸੋ ਥਾਇ ਪਾਇ॥2
M:3: Mun(u) vaykaareen vayhrriya, vaykaaraan kerm kmaayay. Doojaiy bhaayay ugiyani poojday, Durgeh milaiy sjaayay. Aatum Dayo poojiyaiy, bin(u) Satgur boojh na paayay. Jup(u) tup(u) sunjum(u) bhaana Satguru ka, kermi pullaiy paayay. Nanak sayva soort(i) kumaavni, jo Hur(i) bhaavaiy so thaayayn paayay.  2.
Word Meaning:  Vakaareen = in immoral affairs.  Vayhrriya = gripped by.  Ugiyani = ignorant.  Aatum Dayo = Waheguru.  Boojh = understanding.  Jup(u) = ritualistic recitation of some Mantra to please some god.  Tup(u) = self-torture to please some god.  Sunjum(u) = stubborn effort to control desires.  Pullaiy Paayay = receive; acquire. Thaayayn Paayay = is credited with; is rewarded.  
MEANING: Man’s mind is gripped by immoral deeds. The ignorant worships (some deity) for the love of Maya, and is consequently punished in Waheguru’s Court.  The only power to worship is Waheguru, but we do not get this realization without Satguru.  There is no need for any jup(u), tup(u) or sunjum(u) if one obeys Waheguru’s Huqam; but that happens only by the benevolence of Satguru.  One’s duty is to recite Waheguru’s Naam with full dedication, but only those are rewarded who are liked by Him, oh Nanak.    2.

ਪਉੜੀ॥ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਜਪਹੁ ਮਨ ਮੇਰੇ ਜਿਤੁ ਸਦਾ ਸੁਖੁ ਹੋਵੈ ਦਿਨੁ ਰਾਤੀ॥ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਜਪਹੁ ਮਨ ਮੇਰੇ ਜਿਤੁ ਸਿਮਰਤ ਸਭਿ ਕਿਲਵਿਖ ਪਾਪ ਲਹਾਤੀ॥ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਜਪਹੁ ਮਨ ਮੇਰੇ ਜਿਤੁ ਦਾਲਦੁ ਦੁਖ ਭੁਖ ਸਭ ਲਹਿ ਜਾਤੀ॥ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਜਪਹੁ ਮਨ ਮੇਰੇ ਮੁਖਿ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਿ ਲਗਾਤੀ॥ ਜਿਤੁ ਮੁਖਿ ਭਾਗੁ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਧੁਰਿ ਸਾਚੈ ਹਰਿ ਤਿਤੁ ਮੁਖਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਜਪਾਤੀ॥ 13॥
Paurri: Hur(i) Hur(i) Naam(u) jup-hou mun mayray, jit(u) suda sookh(u) hovaiy din(u) raati. Hur(i) Hur(i) Naam(u) jup-hou mun mayray, jit(u) simrut subh(i) kilvikh paap luhaati.  Hur(i) Hur(i) Naam(u) jup-hou mun mayray, jit(u) daalud(u) dookh bhookh subh leh(i) jaati. Hur(i) Hur(i) Naam(u) jup-hou mun mayray, mookh(i) Gurmukh(i) preet(i) lugaati. Jit(u) mookh(i) bhaag(u) likhiya dhoor(i) Sachaiy, Hur(i) tit(u) mookh(i) Naam(u) jupaati.  13
Word Meaning:  Simrut = recitation (of Naam).    Kikvikh = Paap = sins.   Luhaati = removed.   Daalud = poverty.    Bhookh = appetite; desires. 
MEANING: Recite Waheguru’s Naam, which produces comfort day and night, oh my mind.  Recite Waheguru’s Naam, which removes all the sins, all poverty, all discomforts, and all desires, oh my mind.  Recite Waheguru’s Naam by the tongue in Satguru’s shelter, which will produce love for Waheguru, oh my mind.  Only those who recite Waheguru’s Naam with the tongue for whom Waheguru has prewritten fortune (to recite His Naam).    13.   
ਸਲੋਕ ਮ:3॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਜਿਨੀ ਨ ਸੇਵਿਓ ਸਬਦਿ ਨ ਕੀਤੋ ਵੀਚਾਰੁ॥ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਗਿਆਨੁ ਨ ਆਇਓ ਮਿਰਤਕੁ ਹੈ ਸੰਸਾਰਿ॥ ਲਖ ਚਉਰਾਸੀਹ ਫੇਰੁ ਪਇਆ ਮਰਿ ਜੰਮੈ ਹੋਇ ਖੁਆਰੁ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਕੀ ਸੇਵਾ ਸੋ ਕਰੇ ਜਿਸ ਨੋ ਆਪ ਕਰਾਏ ਸੋਇ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਵਿਚਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਨਿਧਾਨੁ ਹੈ ਕਰਮਿ ਪਰਾਪਤਿ ਹੋਇ॥ ਸਚਿ ਰਤੇ ਗੁਰ ਸਬਦ ਸਿਉ ਤਿਨ ਸਚੀ ਸਦਾ ਲਿਵ ਹੋਇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਜਿਸ ਨੋ ਮੇਲੇ ਨ ਵਿਛੁੜੈ ਸਹਜਿ ਸਮਾਵੈ ਸੋਇ॥ 1॥
Sloak M:3: Satgur jinhi na sayvio, Shabad(i) na keeto veechaar(u). Untar(i) Giyaan(u) na aayo, mirtuk(u) haiy, sunsaar(i). Lukh chaurasee phayr(u) puyya, mer(i) jummaiy hoyay khooaar(u).  Satgur kee sayva so kuray jis nu Aap kurayay Soyay. Satgur vich(i) Naam(u) nidhaan(u) haiy, kerm(i) praput(i) hoyay. Sach(i) ruttay Gur Shabad siyoun, tin Sachi suda liv hoyay. Nanak jis nu maylay na vichhurraiy, sehj(i) smaavaiy soyay.  
Word Meaning: Lukh Chaurasee = the 8.4 million life forms (a Hindu belief).  Nidhaan(u) = treasure.    Kerm(i) = by the benevolence of the Guru.  Soyay = God.
MEANING: Those who do not serve Satguru (obey His advice), and do not ponder on His Shabad, are ignorant, and are (spiritually) dead in the world.  They suffer after death and go through the 8.4 million life forms. However, only those who serve Satguru are prompted to do so by Him.   Satguru is the treasure of Waheguru’s Naam, but only those who are kind to Waheguru have access to it.  Those who are imbued with the Guru’s eternal Shabad are truly connected with Waheguru.  Those who are connected to Him by the grace of God remain in a high spiritual state (above the effects of Maya), and they never get separated from Him. 1.
ਮ:3॥ ਸੋ ਭਗਉਤੀ ਜੁੋ ਭਗਵੰਤੈ ਜਾਣੈ॥ ਗੁਰ ਪਰਸਾਦੀ ਆਪੁ ਪਛਾਣੈ॥ਧਾਵਤੁ ਰਾਖੈ ਇਕਤੁ ਘਰਿ ਆਣੈ॥ ਜੀਵਤੁ ਮਰੈ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਵਖਾਣੈ॥ਐਸਾ ਭਗਉਤੀ ਉਤਮੁ ਹੋਇ॥ਨਾਨਕ ਸਚਿ ਸਮਾਵੇ ਸੋਇ॥2
M:3: So bhugouti jo Bhugvantaiy jaanaiy. Gur Persadi aap(u) puchhanaiy. Dhaavut(u) raakhaiy, ikutt(u) ghur(i) aanaiy. Jeevut(u) muraiy Hur(i) Naam(u) vukhaanaiy. Aisa bhugauti ootum(u) hoyay. Nanak Sach(i) smaavay soyay.  2.
Word Meaning:  Bhugouti = devotee.      Bhugvantaiy = God.   Dhaavut(u) = fast running - around mind.  Ikutt(u) Ghur(i) = stable mind.  Jeevut(u) muraiy = dead (for worldly desires).    Vukhaanaiy = explains; recites.  Ootum(u) = supreme.
MEANING:  A true devotee is the one who realizes Waheguru and recognizes the self by the benevolence of the Guru. He recites Waheguru’s Naam and controls his wavering mind so strongly that it seems to be dead from worldly desires. Such a devotee is supreme, and becomes “one” with God, oh Nanak.    2.

ਮ:3॥ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਕਪਟੁ ਭਗਉਤੀ ਕਹਾਏ॥ ਪਾਖੰਡਿ ਪਾਰਬ੍ਰਹਮੁ ਕਦੇ ਨਾ ਪਾਏ॥ ਪਰ ਨਿੰਦਾ ਕਰੇ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਮਲੁ ਲਾਏ॥ ਬਾਹਰਿ ਮਲੁ ਧੋਵੈ ਮਨ ਕੀ ਜੂਠਿ ਨ ਜਾਏ॥ ਸਤਸੰਗਤਿ ਸਿਉ ਬਾਦੁ ਰਚਾਏ॥ ਅਨਦਿਨੁ ਦੁਖੀਆ ਦੂਜੈ ਭਾਇ ਰਚਾਏ॥ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਨ ਚੇਤੈ ਬਹੁ ਕਰਮ ਕਮਾਏ॥ ਪੂਰਬਿ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਸੁ ਮੇਟਣਾ ਨ ਜਾਏ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਬਿਨੁ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਸੇਵੇ ਮੋਖੁ ਨ ਪਾਏ॥ 3॥
M:3: Untar(i) kupt(u), bhugauti kuhaayay. Pakhund(i) Parbraham kuday na paayay. 
Per ninda kuray, untar(i) mul(u laayay. Baahur(i) mul(u) dhovaiy, mun kee jootth(i) na jaayay. Satsungat(i) siyoun baad(u) ruchaayay.  Undin(u) dookheeya, doojaiy bhaayay ruchaayay. Hur(i) Naam(u) na chaytaiy, buhou kerm kmaayay. Poorub(i) likhiya, su maytna na jaayay. Nanak bin(u) Satgur sayvay moakh(u) na paayay.  3. 
Word Meaning:  Untar(i) = inside.   Kupt(u) = deceit; guile.   Pakhund(i) = by faking; by hypocrisy.    Ninda = unwarranted malicious criticism.   Mul(u) = filth.   Jootth(i) = slop; filth; treachery.  Baad(u) = destructive discussion.  Doojaiy Bhaayay = love for the other (Maya).  Ruchaayay = deal in.   kerm = rituals.  Moakh(u) = salvation.   3.
MEANING:  There are some who call themselves devotees, but have deceit in their minds.  They cannot realize Waheguru by such hypocrisy. They acquire filth in their minds by maliciously criticizing others.  They can't wash off their bodily filth by taking a dip in water or otherwise, but the filth of their mind cannot be washed away by water (even if it's holy water). Their dealings are for the love of Maya, so they engage in destructive discussions with the holy congregation and suffer day and night. They perform many rituals (to show off) but never remember Waheguru. However, the prewritten nature of individuals cannot be erased; without serving Satguru, one cannot get salvation, oh Nanak.  3.

ਪਉੜੀ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਜਿਨੀ ਧਿਆਇਆ ਸੇ ਕੜਿ ਨ ਸਵਾਹੀ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਜਿਨੀ ਧਿਆਇਆ ਸੇ ਤ੍ਰਿਪਤਿ ਅਘਾਹੀ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਜਿਨੀ ਧਿਆਇਆ ਤਿਨ ਜਮ ਡਰੁ ਨਾਹੀ॥
