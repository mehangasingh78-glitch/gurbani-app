(ਅੰਗ 49)॥          ਸੰਤਾ ਸੰਗਤਿ ਮਨਿ ਵਸੈ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਮ ਬਖਸਿੰਦੁ॥ ਜਿਨਿ ਸੇਵਿਆ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਆਪਣਾ ਸੋਈ ਰਾਜ ਨਰਿੰਦੁ ॥2॥ ਅਉਸਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਜਸੁ ਗੁਣ ਰਮਣ ਜਿਤੁ ਕੋਟਿ ਮਜਨ ਇਸਨਾਨੁ॥ ਰਸਨਾ ਉਚਰੈ ਗੁਣਵਤੀ ਕੋਇ ਨ ਪੁਜੈ ਦਾਨੁ॥ ਦ੍ਰਿਸਟਿ ਧਾਰਿ ਮਨਿ ਤਨਿ ਵਸੈ ਦਇਆਲ ਪੁਰਖੁ ਮਿਹਰਵਾਨੁ॥ ਜੀਉ ਪਿੰਡੁ ਧਨੁ ਤਿਸ ਦਾ ਹਉ ਸਦਾ ਸਦਾ ਕੁਰਬਾਨੁ॥ 3॥  ਮਿਲਿਆ ਕਦੇ ਨ ਵਿਛੜੈ ਜੋ ਮੇਲਿਆ ਕਰਤਾਰਿ॥ ਦਾਸਾ ਕੇ ਬੰਧਨ ਕਟਿਆ ਸਾਚੈ ਸਿਰਜਣਹਾਰਿ॥ ਭੂਲਾ ਮਾਰਗਿ ਪਾਇਓਨੁ ਗੁਣ ਅਵਗੁਣ ਨ ਬੀਚਾਰਿ॥ ਨਾਨਕੁ ਤਿਸੁ ਸਰਣਾਗਤੀ ਜਿ ਸਗਲ ਘਟਾ ਆਧਾਰੁ॥ 4॥18॥88॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 5:  Soyee Shaast(u), Saoun(u) soyay, jit(u) jupiyaiy Hur(i) Naaoun. Churn kumul(u) Gur(i) dhun(u) deeya, miliya nithaavayn thaaoun.  Saachi poonji, Sach(u) sunjumo, aatth pehr goon gaaoun. Kur(i) kirpa Prubh(u) bhaytiya, mern(u) na aavun(u) jaao. 1. Mayray mun, Hur(i) bhuj(u) suda ikk rung(i).  Ghut ghut untar(i) ruv(i) ruhiya suda suhayee sung(i). 1. Ruhaou. Sookhaan kee mit(i) kiya guneen, ja simreen Govind(u). Jin chaakhiya say triptaasiya, oh rus(u) jaanaiy jind(u).
(PAGE 49):       Suntaan sungat(i) mun(i) vusaiy, Prubh(u) preetum bukhshind(u). Jin(i) sayviya Prubh(u) aapna, soyee raj nurind(u). 2. Ausur(i) Hur(i) jus(u) goon rumun, jit(u) kot(i) mujan ishnaan. Rusna oochraiy goonvuti, koyay na poojaiy daan(u). Drisht(i) dhaar(i) mun(i) tun(i) vusaiy, duyyal Purakh(u) mehrvaan(u). Jeo pind(u) dhun(u) tis da, houn suda suda koorbaan(u).  3. Miliya kuday na vichhurraiy, jo mayliya Kartar(i).  Daasaan kay bundhun kuttiya, Sachaiy Sirjunhaar(i). Bhoola maarug payayon(u) goon uvgoon na beechaar(i). Nanak(u) tis(u) shernaagti j(i) sugal ghutaan aadhaar(u).    4.18.88
Word Meaning:   Shaast(u) = Hindu religious books.   Saoun = Another Hindu book written by Shaunak, which describes the results of good and bad rituals.   Soyay = same; that one.    Jit(u) = (by) which.    Churn Kumul = (Waheguru) whose feet are soft like a lotus flower.    Nithaavayn = homeless; supportless.   Poonji = capital; wealth.   Sunjumo = self-control.   Atth pehr = twenty-four hours.    Bhatiya = got connected.    Aavun(u) Jaao = birth and death.       1.           Ikk Rung(i) = in the love of the One (Waheguru).     Ghut = heart.    Suhaayee = helper.        Sung(i) = with; accompanies.            Pause.           Mit(u) = measurement.    Ja = when.    Govind(u) = Waheguru.    Chaakhiya = tasted.    Triptaasiya = became content.     Rus(u) = taste; enjoyment.   Jind(u) = soul.   Preetum = dear.    Bukhshind(u) = forgiver.   Sayviya = served.    Nurind(u) = king.          2.             Ausur(i) = time; opportunity.    Ruman = recite.   Kot(i) = millions.    Mujan = Ishnaan = baths (in recognized holy places.    Rusna = tongue.    Oochraiy = say (sing).   Goonvuti = full of noble qualities; fortunate.   Poojaiy = reach; match.     Drisht(u)  Dhaar(i) = by casting kind sight.    Duyyal = Mehrvaan(u) = kind.    Jeo = soul; life.   Pind(u) = body.     Koorbaan = sacrifice.            3.
Bundhan = chains.    Kartaar(i) = Sirjunhaar(u) = the Creator.    Bhoola = lost; misguided.   Maarug = (the right) path.   Uvgoon = ignoble qualities.    Shernaagti = in the shelter.   Sugul = all.    Ghutaan = hearts.    Aadhaar(u) = support.          4. 
MEANING:  Worship Waheguru with love, which is pervading every heart, is present everywhere, and is always with you to help you, oh my mind.         Pause. 
Only that book can be considered a Shastra or a Saoun by which one can sing Waheguru’s praise. The Guru has kindly given me the wealth of the Naam of Waheguru, which has Feet soft like a lotus flower, as a result of which, a homeless (support-less) person like me has found a home in Waheguru’s Palace (I am connected with Him).   Singing Waheguru’s praise all the time is the eternal wealth, and the control of your sensual pleasures (stability of mind).  The Guru has kindly connected me with Waheguru, and now I will not suffer the pain of reincarnation.        1.          When a person worships Waheguru, there is no limit to the comforts that he experiences. Only those people enjoy the bliss of Waheguru’s Naam-Amrit who taste it and feel contented.  The dear Waheguru is forgiving and takes residence in your heart when you are in the company of His devotees (to sing His praise). Those who serve Waheguru are the real rulers and kings.              2.            Human life is an opportunity to sing praise of Waheguru; even millions of dips in holy places do not match the praise of Waheguru.  And no ritualistic donation can match the fortunate tongue that sings the praise of Waheguru.  The kind Waheguru takes residence in the body and the soul of His devotee with His kind sight.  The life, the body, and the wealth belong to Waheguru (are given by Him), and I (Guru Nanak) am always a sacrifice for Him.          3.            Once Waheguru has connected someone to Him, that person does not get separated from Him. The eternal Creator cuts the bonds of Maya for His devotees.  After that, Waheguru does not consider the individual's good or bad qualities; instead, it sets him on the right (spiritual) path.  I am in the shelter of Waheguru, who is the support for every heart, oh, Nanak.      4.18.88.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ 

ਰਸਨਾ ਸਚਾ ਸਿਮਰੀਐ ਮਨੁ ਤਨੁ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਹੋਇ॥ ਮਾਤ ਪਿਤਾ ਸਾਕ ਅਗਲੇ ਤਿਸੁ ਬਿਨੁ ਅਵਰੁ ਨ ਕੋਇ॥ ਮਿਹਰ ਕਰੇ ਜੇ ਆਪਣੀ ਚਸਾ ਨਾ ਵਿਸਰੈ ਸੋਇ॥ 1॥ ਮਨ ਮੇਰੇ ਸਾਚਾ ਸੇਵਿ ਜਿਚਰੁ ਸਾਸੁ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਸਚੇ ਸਭ ਕੂੜੁ ਹੈ ਅੰਤੇ ਹੋਇ ਬਿਨਾਸੁ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਸਾਹਿਬੁ ਮੇਰਾ ਨਿਰਮਲਾ ਤਿਸੁ ਬਿਨੁ ਰਹਣੁ ਨ ਜਾਇ॥ ਮੇਰੈ ਮਨਿ ਤਨਿ ਭੁਖ ਅਤਿ ਅਗਲੀ ਕੋਈ ਆਣਿ ਮਿਲਾਵੈ ਮਾਇ॥ ਚਾਰੇ ਕੁੰਡਾ ਭਾਲੀਆ ਸਹ ਬਿਨੁ ਅਵਰੁ ਨ ਜਾਇ॥ 2॥ ਤਿਸੁ ਆਗੈ ਅਰਦਾਸਿ ਕਰਿ ਜੋ ਮੇਲੇ ਕਰਤਾਰੁ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਦਾਤਾ ਨਾਮ ਕਾ ਪੂਰਾ ਜਿਸੁ ਭੰਡਾਰੁ॥ ਸਦਾ ਸਦਾ ਸਾਲਾਹੀਐ ਅੰਤੁ ਨ ਪਾਰਾਵਾਰੁ॥ 3॥ ਪਰਵਦਗਾਰੁ ਸਾਲਾਹੀਐ ਜਿਸ ਦੇ ਚਲਤ ਅਨੇਕ॥ ਸਦਾ ਸਦਾ ਆਰਾਧੀਐ ਏਹਾ ਮਤਿ ਵਿਸੇਖ॥ ਮਨਿ ਤਨਿ ਮਿਠਾ ਤਿਸੁ ਲਗੈ ਜਿਸੁ ਮਸਤਕਿ ਨਾਨਕ ਲੇਖ॥ 4॥19॥89॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 5: Rusna Sacha simriyaiy, mun(u) tun(u) nirmul(u) hoyay. Maat pita saak uglay, tis(u) bin(u) uver(u) na koyay.  Mehr kuray jay Aapni chusa na visraiy soyay. 1. Mun mayray, Saacha sayv(i) jichur(u) saas(u). Bin(u) Sachay subh koorr(u) haiy, untay hoyay binaas(u). 1. Ruhaou.   Sahib(u) mayra nirmula, tis(u) bin(u) rehn(u) na jaayay. Mayraiy mun(i) tun(i) bhookh ut(i) ugli, koyee aan(i) milaavaiy maayay.  Chaaray koondaan bhaaliyaan, Sheh bin(u) uver(u) na jaayay. 2.  Tis(u) aagaiy ardaas(i) kur(i) jo maylay Kartar(u).  Satgur daata Naam ka poora jis(u) bhundaar(u). Suda suda salahiyaiy unt(u) na paaravaar(u). 3.  Pervudgaar(u) salahiyaiy jis day chult anayk. Suda suda aaradhiyaiy eha mut(i) visaykh.  Mun(i) tun(i) mittha tis(u) luggaiy, jis(u) mustuk(i) Nanak laykh.  4.19.89.
Word Meaning:  Rusna = tongue.   Sacha = eternal (Waheguru).   Simriyaiy = remember; worship.   Nirmul(u) = pure.   Saak = relatives.  Uglay = many.   Uver(u) = else; other   Chusa = very short period of time.  Visraiy = lose from mind.           1.          Jichur(u) = as long as.   Saas(u) = breath.   Koorr(u) = false; temporary.  Untay = in the end.   Binaas(u) = vanish.       Pause.          Bhookh = appetite (desire).  Ut(i) = extreme.  Ugli = much.   Maayay = oh, mother.   Chaaray Koondaan = four directions.  Bhaliyaan = searched for.   Sheh = husband (God).   Jaayay = place.          2.            Tis(u) = Him.   Kartar = Creator (Waheguru).   Daata = donor; source.  Bhundaar(u) = treasure.   Salahiyaiy = sing praise.   Paaravaar(u) = limitless.     3.           Pervudgaar(u) = one who nourishes and raises.   Chult = amazing (uncanny) acts.  Anayk = unlimited.     Aaradhiyaiy = worship.   Visaykh = special.   Mittha = sweet.  
Mustuk(i) = (on the) forehead.   Laykh = writing.          4.
MEANING: Serve the eternal Waheguru (worship Him) as long as you breathe, oh my mind.  Without Waheguru’s Naam, everything else is short-lived and vanishes at the end (of your life).           Pause.         One should recite Waheguru’s Naam with the tongue, as a result of which, his mind and body are both purified. There are many relatives in the world, including your mother and father, but nobody, other than Waheguru, can help you at the end.  When God is kind to someone, he does not lose God from his mind for even a moment.            1.
My Lord is perfectly pure (just and impartial); I cannot survive without Him.  I am very, very hungry for his sight, oh my mother; I pray to all if someone can connect me with Him. I searched everywhere in all four directions for peace of mind, but realized that there is no other place (for comfort) without my Lord, Waheguru.          2.
I pray before that individual who can connect me with the Creator (God).  (I know) Satguru is the only provider of Waheguru’s Naam whose treasure is full (there is never a shortage in His treasure, even if He distributes Waheguru’s Naam to millions of people).  We should always sing Satguru’s praise, who is a manifestation of the limitless Waheguru.     3.
We should sing praise of the Creator and the Provider (Waheguru), who performs countless amazing feats.  The only supreme wisdom is that by which we worship God all the time.  However, only those find Waheguru sweet to their body and mind, who have the good fortune prewritten on their foreheads, oh Nanak.    4. 19.89.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ 

ਸੰਤ ਜਨਹੁ ਮਿਲਿ ਭਾਈਹੋ ਸਚਾ ਨਾਮੁ ਸਮਾਲਿ॥ ਤੋਸਾ ਬੰਧਹੁ ਜੀਅ ਕਾ ਐਥੈ ਓਥੈ ਨਾਲਿ॥ ਗੁਰ ਪੂਰੇ ਤੇ ਪਾਈਐ ਆਪਣੀ ਨਦਰਿ ਨਿਹਾਲਿ॥ ਕਰਮਿ ਪਰਾਪਤਿ ਤਿਸੁ ਹੋਵੈ ਜਿਸ ਨੋ ਹੋਇ ਦਇਆਲੁ॥ 1॥ ਮੇਰੇ ਮਨ ਗੁਰ ਜੇਵਡ ਅਵਰੁ ਨ ਕੋਇ॥ ਦੂਜਾ ਥਾਉ ਨ ਕੋ ਸੁਝੈ ਗੁਰ ਮੇਲੇ ਸਚੁ ਸੋਇ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਸਗਲ ਪਦਾਰਥ ਤਿਸੁ ਮਿਲੇ ਜਿਨਿ ਗੁਰ ਡਿਠਾ ਜਾਇ॥ ਗੁਰ ਚਰਣੀ ਜਿਨ ਮਨੁ ਲਗਾ ਸੇ ਵਡਭਾਗੀ ਮਾਇ॥ ਗੁਰੁ ਦਾਤਾ ਸਮਰਥੁ ਗੁਰੁ, ਗੁਰੁ ਸਭ ਮਹਿ ਰਹਿਆ ਸਮਾਇ॥ ਗੁਰੁ ਪਰਮੇਸਰੁ ਪਾਰਬ੍ਰਹਮੁ ਗੁਰੁ ਡੁਬਦਾ ਲਏ ਤਰਾਇ॥ 2॥ ਕਿਤੁ ਮੁਖਿ ਗੁਰੁ ਸਾਲਾਹੀਐ ਕਰਣ ਕਾਰਣ ਸਮਰਥੁ॥ ਸੇ ਮਥੇ ਨਿਹਚਲ ਰਹੇ ਜਿਨ ਗੁਰਿ ਧਾਰਿਆ ਹਥੁ॥ ਗੁਰਿ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਨਾਮੁ ਪੀਆਲਿਆ ਜਨਮ ਮਰਨ ਕਾ ਪਥੁ॥ ਗੁਰੁ ਪਰਮੇਸਰੁ ਸੇਵਿਆ ਭੈ ਭੰਜਨੁ ਦੁਖ ਲਥੁ॥ 3॥
