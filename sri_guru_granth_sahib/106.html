(ਅੰਗ 105)॥     ਕਰਿ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਭਗਤੀ ਲਾਵਹੁ ਸਚੁ ਨਾਨਕ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਪੀਏ ਜੀਉ॥ 4।28॥35॥
Majh Mehla 5:  Aao saajun Sunt meet piyaaray.  Mil(i) gaaveh goon Agumm Apaaray. Gaavut soonut subhay hee mooktay, so dhiyayiyaiy Jin(i) hum keeyay jeeo. 1.  Junum junum kay kilbikh jaaveh(i). Mun chinday sayee ful paaveh(i).  Simer(i) Sahib(u) so Sach(u) Soami, rijuk subhus(u) kuou deeyay jeeo.  2.  Naam(u) juput serb sookh paayiyaiy.  Subh(u) bhuou binsaiy Hur(i) Hur(i) dhiyayiyaiy.  Jin(i) sayviya so paargiraami, kaaruj suglay theeyay jeeo. 3.  Aayay puyya Tayri shernaayi. Jiyoun bhaavaiy Tiyoun laih(i) milaayi. 
(PAGE 105):              Kur(i) kirpa Prubh(u) bhugti laavhou, Sach(u) Nanak Amrit(u) peeyay jeeo.  4.28.35.
Word Meaning:  Saajun = Meet = friend.   Agumm = (physically) inaccessible.    Apaaray = limitless.  Mooktay = free from the bonds of Maya.     1.       Kilbikh = sins.    Chinday = desired.       Rijuk = food and other necessities.     2.      Binsaiy = destroyed.   Paargiraami = able to swim across the sea of Maya.  Kaaruj = tasks; objectives.  Theeyay = became; completed.       3.
Shernaayi = shelter.    Bhaavaiy = (as You) deem appropriate.   Sach(u) = eternal.    4.
MEANING:  Come, all my devotee friends; let us sing praise of the unreachable, and the limitless Waheguru together.  All those who sing and all those who listen to the praise of our Creator, Waheguru, are emancipated.          1.          When one recites the Naam of Waheguru - who nourishes all His Creation, and fulfills all other needs - all his sins of many lives are destroyed, and all his conceived wishes are granted.          2.          One enjoys all the comforts, and all his fears go away when he recites Waheguru’s Naam. Whoever serves Waheguru can swim across the sea of Maya, and all his tasks are realized (his noble wishes are granted).       3.          I have come to Your shelter, oh Waheguru!   Please connect me with You in whatever way You please. Kindly enable me to serve You (recite Your Naam with dedication) so that I (Guru Nanak) may drink the True Naam - Amrit.    4.28.35.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਭਏ ਕ੍ਰਿਪਾਲ ਗੋਵਿੰਦ ਗੁਸਾਈ॥ ਮੇਘੁ ਵਰਸੈ ਸਭਨੀ ਥਾਈ॥ ਦੀਨ ਦਇਆਲ ਸਦਾ ਕਿਰਪਾਲਾ ਠਾਢਿ ਪਾਈ ਕਰਤਾਰੇ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਅਪੁਨੇ ਜੀਅ ਜੰਤ ਪ੍ਰਤਿਪਾਰੇ॥ ਜਿਉ ਬਾਰਿਕ ਮਾਤਾ ਸੰਮਾਰੇ॥ ਦੁਖ ਭੰਜਨ ਸੁਖ ਸਾਗਰ ਸੁਆਮੀ ਦੇਤ ਸਗਲ ਆਹਾਰੇ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਜਲਿ ਥਲਿ ਪੂਰਿ ਰਹਿਆ ਮਿਹਰਵਾਨਾ॥ ਸਦ ਬਲਿਹਾਰਿ ਜਾਈਐ ਕੁਰਬਾਨਾ॥ ਰੈਣਿ ਦਿਨਸੁ ਤਿਸੁ ਸਦਾ ਧਿਆਈ ਜਿ ਖਿਨ ਮਹਿ ਸਗਲ ਉਧਾਰੇ ਜੀਉ॥3॥ ਰਾਖਿ ਲੀਏ ਸਗਲੇ ਪ੍ਰਭਿ ਆਪੇ॥ ਉਤਰਿ ਗਏ ਸਭ ਸੋਗ ਸੰਤਾਪੇ॥ ਨਾਮੁ ਜਪਤ ਮਨੁ ਤਨੁ ਹਰੀਆਵਲੁ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਦਰਿ ਨਿਹਾਰੇ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥29॥36॥
Majh Mehla 5:  Bhuyay kirpaal Govind Gosayeen.  Maygh(u) versaiy subhni thaayeen. Deen Dyaal suda kirpaala, tthaandh(i) payee Kartaaray jeeo. 1. Apunay jea junt pritpaaray. Jiyoun baarik maata summaaray.  Dookh bunjun sookh saagar Soami, dayt sugal aahaaray jeeo.  2.  Jul(i) thul(i) poor(i) ruhiya Mehrvaana. Sud bulihaar(i) jaayiyaiy koorbaana.  Raiyn(i) dinus(s) tis(u) suda dhiyayeen, j(i) khin meh(i) sugal oodhaaray jeeo.  3. Raakh(i) leeyay suglay Prubh(i) aapay. Oottur(i) guyay subh soag suntaapay.  Naam(u) juput mun(u) tun(u) huriyavul(u), Prubh Nanak nudr(i) nihaaray jeeo.   4.29.36.
Word Meaning:  Bhuyay = became.    Govind = Gosayeen = provider for the earth = Waheguru.   Maygh(u) = clouds.   Tthaandh(i) = coolness; peace.      1.      Jea Junt = living beings.   Pritpaaray = provides for; looks after.   Bhunjun = destroyer.   Saagar = sea.   Aahaaray = food.      2.        Bulihaar(i) = Koorbaana = offer for sacrifice.   Raiyn(i) = night.   Khin = moment.     3.       Soag = grief; sorrow.  Suntaapay = troubles; hardships.   Huriyavul(u) = blooms; joyful.   Nihaaray = sees; looks kindly at.       4.
MEANING:  With the kindness of Waheguru, it is raining Naam everywhere.  Waheguru, who is always kind to the poor and the powerless, has brought calmness and peace to the world, which burns (suffers) with the heat of vices.         1.            He looks after His creation just the way a mother looks after her baby. The Lord Waheguru, who is the destroyer of all discomforts and is the source of all comfort, provides food and support to all living beings.        2.
The kind Waheguru is present everywhere – on the land, in the waters, and in the sky - we should sacrifice our lives for Him, and must worship Him day and night. He can salvage all of us in a moment (in the twinkling of an eye).           3.
Waheguru protects all of us, and all the sorrows and troubles are eliminated.  The body and mind bloom (feel blissful) when we recite Waheguru’s Naam, which happens only when He casts His kind sight on us, oh Nanak.   4.29.36.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਜਿਥੈ ਨਾਮੁ ਜਪੀਐ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਪਿਆਰੇ॥ ਸੇ ਅਸਥਲ ਸੋਇਨ ਚਉਬਾਰੇ॥ ਜਿਥੈ ਨਾਮੁ ਨ ਜਪੀਐ ਮੇਰੇ ਗੋਇਦਾ ਸੇਈ ਨਗਰ ਉਜਾੜੀ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਹਰਿ ਰੁਖੀ ਰੋਟੀ ਖਾਇ ਸਮਾਲੇ॥ ਹਰਿ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਬਾਹਰਿ ਨਦਰਿ ਨਿਹਾਲੇ॥ ਖਾਇ ਖਾਇ ਕਰੇ ਬਦਫੈਲੀ ਜਾਣੁ ਵਿਸੂ ਕੀ ਵਾੜੀ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਸੰਤਾ ਸੇਤੀ ਰੰਗੁ ਨ ਲਾਏ॥ ਸਾਕਤ ਸੰਗਿ ਵਿਕਰਮ ਕਮਾਏ॥ ਦੁਲਭ ਦੇਹ ਖੋਈ ਅਗਿਆਨੀ ਜੜ ਅਪੁਣੀ ਆਪਿ ਉਪਾੜੀ ਜੀਉ॥ 3॥ ਤੇਰੀ ਸਰਣਿ ਮੇਰੇ ਦੀਨ ਦਇਆਲਾ॥ ਸੁਖ ਸਾਗਰ ਮੇਰੇ ਗੁਰ ਗੋਪਾਲਾ॥ ਕਰਿ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਨਾਨਕੁ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਵੈ ਰਾਖਹੁ ਸਰਮ ਅਸਾੜੀ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥30॥37॥
Majh Mehla 5:  Jithaiy Naam(u) jupiyaiy Prubh piyaaray. Say usthul soyin chaubaaray.  Jithaiy Naam(u) na jupiyaiy mayray Goinda, sayee nugar oojaarri jeeo. 1.  Hur(i) rookhee roti khaayay smaalay. Hur(i) untar(i) baahur(i) nudr(i) nihaalay.  Khaayay khaayay Kur(i)ay budphaiyli, jaan(u) visoo kee vaarree jeeo. Suntaan sayti rung(u) na laayay.  Saakut sung(i) vikurm kmaayay. Doolubh dayh khoyee ugiyani, jurrh apunee aap(i) oopaarri jeeo.  3. Tayri shern(i) mayray deen Duyaala. Sookh saagur mayray Gur Gopaala. Kur(i) kirpa Nanak goon gaavaiy, raakhou sherm asaarri jeeo.  4.30.37.
Word Meaning:  Usthul = barren land; desert; deserted place.  Chaubaaray = mansion.   Oojaarri = uninhabited (deserted) place.      1.      Rookhee = uncoated chapati without any dish (subsistence food).   Smaalay = remembers Waheguru’s Naam.   Nihaalay = Nihaaray = sees.    Budphaiyli = evil deeds.   Visoo = poison.    Vaarree = garden.      2.
Rung(u) = love; respect.    Saakut = worshipper of wealth.   Vikurm = ignoble deeds.   Doolubh Dayh = priceless human life.   Ugiyani = ignorant.   Jurrh = root.     3.
Shern(i) = shelter.    Deen = powerless; weak.   Sherm = honour; self-respect.     4.
MEANING:  A deserted place feels like a mansion of gold where Waheguru’s Naam is recited; and a town where Waheguru’s Naam is not recited feels like a deserted place.        1.
If a poor person, who has very little to eat, recites Waheguru’s Naam, he has Waheguru’s kind sight on him everywhere; but a person, who has very rich food to eat, but does evil deeds, is growing a garden of poison (in Waheguru’s eyes).            2. 
An individual who develops no love or respect for the holy devotees (of Waheguru) and, on top of that, commits ignoble deeds in the company of lovers of money is an ignorant person who uproots (destroys) his own base and wastes away his precious human life.       3.
I have come to the shelter of the Waheguru, who is kind to the powerless, is the source of all comfort, is the Guru, and is the provider for the whole world.   Please be kind to me (Your slave Nanak), so that I may sing Your praise to save my honour in Your Court (to achieve my objective of being “one” with You), oh Waheguru.    4.30.37.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਚਰਣ ਠਾਕੁਰ ਕੇ ਰਿਦੈ ਸਮਾਣੇ॥ ਕਲਿ ਕਲੇਸ ਸਭ ਦੂਰਿ ਪਇਆਣੇ॥ ਸਾਤਿ ਸੂਖ ਸਹਜ ਧੁਨਿ ਉਪਜੀ ਸਾਧੂ ਸੰਗਿ ਨਿਵਾਸਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਲਾਗੀ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਿ ਨ ਤੂਟੈ ਮੂਲੇ॥ ਹਰਿ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਬਾਹਰਿ ਰਹਿਆ ਭਰਪੂਰੇ॥ ਸਿਮਰਿ ਸਿਮਰਿ ਸਿਮਰਿ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਵਾ ਕਾਟੀ ਜਮ ਕੀ ਫਾਸਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਵਰਖੈ ਅਨਹਦ ਬਾਣੀ॥ ਮਨ ਤਨ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਸਾਤਿ ਸਮਾਣੀ॥ ਤਿਪਤਿ ਅਘਾਇ ਰਹੇ ਜਨ ਤੇਰੇ ਸਤਿਗੁਰਿ ਕੀਆ ਦਿਲਾਸਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 3॥ ਜਿਸ ਕਾ ਸਾ ਤਿਸ ਤੇ ਫਲੁ ਪਾਇਆ॥ ਕਰਿ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਸੰਗਿ ਮਿਲਾਇਆ॥ ਆਵਣ ਜਾਣ ਰਹੇ ਵਡਭਾਗੀ ਨਾਨਕ ਪੂਰਨ ਆਸਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥31॥38
Majh Mehla 5:  Churn Tthakur(i) kay ridaiy smaanay. Kul(i) klaysh subh door(i) puyiyaanay.  Shaant(i) sookh sehj dhoon(i) oopji, Sadhu sung(i) nivaasa jeeo. 1.  Laagi preet(i) na tootaiy moolaiy. Hur(i) untar(i) baahur(i) ruhiya bhurpooray. Simer(i) simer(i), simer(i) goon gaavaan, kaati jum kee phaasa jeeo. 2.  Amrit verkhaiy unhud Bani. Mun tun untar(i) shaant(i) smaani. Tiput(i) aghaayay ruhay jun Tayray, Satgur(i) keeya dilaasa jeeo. 3.  Jis ka sa tis tay ful(u) paaya.  Kur(i) kirpa Prubh sung(i) milaaya. Aavun jaan ruhay vudbhaageen, Nanak poorun aasa jeeo.  4.31.38. 
Word Meaning:  Churn Tthaakur Kay = Lord’s feet (His Naam).   Ridaiy = (in) heart.   Kul(i) = Kulyug (an era of time considered to be unholy in Hindu Granths.  (Sikhism does not believe in any such stipulated time periods).    Klaysh = bitterness; hostility.   Door(i) Puyiyaanay = went away; removed.   Shaant(i) = peace.   Sehj = peace of mind.   Dhoon(i) = concentration.     Nivaasa = reside; in the company of.  1.      Na Moolay = absolutely not; not at all.    Bhurpooray = filling completely.   Simer(i) = remembering (Waheguru).   Jum kee Phaasa = noose of the angel of death.      2.      Verkhaiy = drops; rains.   Unhud = Divine music which plays automatically.    Shaant(i) = peace; calmness. Tiput(i) Aghaayay = contented completely.  Dilaasa = encouragement; support.       3.      Ful(u) = success; achieved the object.   Aavun Jaan = reincarnation.      4.
MEANING:   A man who stays in the company of the holy men (and recites Waheguru’s Naam) makes his heart the place for Waheguru’s Naam to stay.  All his worldly troubles and hostility towards him are eliminated, and he attains peace of mind, comfort, a higher spiritual state, and concentration of mind.          1.           Once one is connected to Waheguru through his love and devotion, his connection is never broken, and he “sees” Waheguru present everywhere.  I can also cut the noose of the angel of death by singing the virtues of Waheguru.      2.            The rain of the Divine music of Bani (words of Waheguru’s praise) is falling everywhere as a result of which peace and calmness prevail in one’s body and mind.  With Satguru’s support, the devotees acquire full contentment in the Naam-rain.        3.
When a man surrenders to Waheguru and becomes dependent upon Him (has full faith in Him), he achieves the objective of his life. With His kindness, Waheguru connects His devotee to Him as a result of which all his wishes are fulfilled, and his reincarnation comes to an end, oh Nanak!    4.31.38.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਮੀਹੁ ਪਇਆ ਪਰਮੇਸਰਿ ਪਾਇਆ॥ ਜੀਅ ਜੰਤ ਸਭਿ ਸੁਖੀ ਵਸਾਇਆ॥ ਗਇਆ ਕਲੇਸੁ ਭਇਆ ਸੁਖੁ ਸਾਚਾ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਸਮਾਲੀ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਜਿਸ ਕੇ ਸੇ ਤਿਨ ਹੀ ਪ੍ਰਤਿਪਾਰੇ॥ ਪਾਰਬ੍ਰਹਮ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਭਏ ਰਖਵਾਰੇ॥ ਸੁਣੀ ਬੇਨੰਤੀ ਠਾਕੁਰਿ ਮੇਰੈ ਪੂਰਨ ਹੋਈ ਘਾਲੀ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥
