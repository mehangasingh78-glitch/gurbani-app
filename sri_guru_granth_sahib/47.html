(ਅੰਗ 46)॥      ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ 

ਮਿਲਿ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਸਭੁ ਦੁਖੁ ਗਇਆ ਹਰਿ ਸੁਖੁ ਵਸਿਆ ਮਨਿ ਆਇ॥ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਜੋਤਿ ਪ੍ਰਗਾਸੀਆ ਏਕਸੁ ਸਿਉ ਲਿਵ ਲਾਇ॥ ਮਿਲਿ ਸਾਧੂ ਮੁਖੁ ਊਜਲਾ ਪੂਰਬਿ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਪਾਇ॥ ਗੁਣ ਗੋਬਿੰਦ ਨਿਤ ਗਾਵਣੇ ਨਿਰਮਲ ਸਾਚੈ ਨਾਇ॥ 1॥ ਮੇਰੇ ਮਨ ਗੁਰ ਸਬਦੀ ਸੁਖੁ ਹੋਇ॥ ਗੁਰ ਪੂਰੇ ਕੀ ਚਾਕਰੀ ਬਿਰਥਾ ਜਾਇ ਨ ਕੋਇ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਮਨ ਕੀਆ ਇਛਾ ਪੂਰੀਆ ਪਾਇਆ ਨਾਮੁ ਨਿਧਾਨੁ॥ ਅੰਤਰਜਾਮੀ ਸਦਾ ਸੰਗਿ ਕਰਣੈਹਾਰੁ ਪਛਾਨੁ॥ ਗੁਰ ਪਰਸਾਦੀ ਮੁਖੁ ਉਜਲਾ ਜਪਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਦਾਨੁ ਇਸਨਾਨੁ॥ ਕਾਮੁ ਕ੍ਰੋਧ ਲੋਭੁ ਬਿਨਸਿਆ ਤਜਿਆ ਸਭੁ ਅਭਿਮਾਨੁ॥2॥ ਪਾਇਆ ਲਾਹਾ ਲਾਭੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਪੂਰਨ ਹੋਏ ਕਾਮ॥ ਕਰਿ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਪ੍ਰਭਿ ਮੇਲਿਆ ਦੀਆ ਆਪਣਾ ਨਾਮੁ॥ ਆਵਣ ਜਾਣਾ ਰਹਿ ਗਇਆ ਆਪਿ ਹੋਆ ਮਿਹਰਵਾਨੁ॥ ਸਚੁ ਮਹਲੁ ਘਰੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਗੁਰ ਕਾ ਸਬਦੁ ਪਛਾਨੁ॥ 3॥ ਭਗਤ ਜਨਾ ਕਉ ਰਾਖਦਾ ਆਪਣੀ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਧਾਰਿ॥ ਹਲਤਿ ਪਲਤਿ ਮੁਖ ਊਜਲੇ ਸਾਚੇ ਕੇ ਗੁਣ ਸਾਰਿ॥ ਆਠ ਪਹਰ ਗੁਣ ਸਾਰਦੇ ਰਤੇ ਰੰਗਿ ਅਪਾਰ॥ ਪਾਰਬ੍ਰਹਮ ਸੁਖ ਸਾਗਰੋ ਨਾਨਕ ਸਦ ਬਲਿਹਾਰ॥ 4॥11॥81॥
(PAGE 46):           Siri Raag(u) Mehla 5:  Mil(i) Satgur subh(u) dookh(u) guyya, Hur(i) sookh(u) vusiya mun(i) aayay. Untar(i) jot(i) purgasiya, Aikus siyoun liv laayay. Mil(i) Sadhu mookh(u) oojla poorub(i) likhiya paayay. Goon Gobind nit gaavnay, nirmul Sachaiy Naayay. 1. Mayray mun, Gur Shabadi sookh(u) hoyay. Gur Pooray kee chaakri, birtha jaayay na koyay. 1. Ruhaou.   Mun keeyaan ichhaan pooriyaan, paaya Naam(u) nidhaan(u). Untarjaami suda sung(i), Kurnaiyhaar(u) puchhaan. Gur persadi mookh(u) oojla, jup(i) Naam(u), daan(u), ishnaan(u). Kaam(u) krodh lobh(u) binsiya, tujiya subh(u) abhimaan. 2. Paaya laaha laabh(u) Naam(u), poorun hoyay kaam. Kur(i) kirpa Prubh(i) mayliya deeya aapna Naam(u).  Aavun jaana reh(i) guyya, Aap(i) hoa mehrvaan(u). Such(u) mehl(u) ghur(u) paaya, Gur ka Shabad(u) puchhaan. 3. Bhugat juna kuou raakhda, Aapni kirpa dhaar(i). Hult(i) pult(i) mookh oojlay, Saachay kay goon saar(i). Aatth pehr goon saarday, ruttay rung(i) apaar. Parbrahm sookh saagro, Nanak sud bulihaar. 4.11.81.
Word Meaning:  Vusiya = took residence.   Jot(i) = Waheguru’s light (enlightenment).   Purgasiya = lighted up.   Liv = concentration; connection.   Sadhu = Satguru.  Oojla = gleam; shiny; pious.  Poorub(i) Likhiya = predestined.  Nit = every day.       1.
Chaakri = personal tending.   Birtha = empty; without.  Na Koyay = nobody.      Pause.
Ichhaan = desires; wishes.   Nidhaan(u) = treasure.  Untarjaami = omniscient.  Sung = with.    Kurnaiyhaar(u) = the Creator.  Daan(u) = service to humanity; giving the gift of Waheguru’s Naam.     Ishnaan = washing (the mind).    Kaam(u) = lust.  Lobh = greed.   Binsiya = disappeared.  Tujiya = quit.   Abhimaan = ego.        2.
Laaha = Laabh(u) = profit.   Poorun = complete.    Kaam = task; objective.  Aavun Jaana = birth-death-birth cycle.   Reh(i) Guyya = left behind (came to an end).  Mehl(u) = Ghur(u) = (Waheguru’s) residence; union with Him.  Puchhaan = recognizing.        3. 
Hult(i) = this world.   Pult(i) = the next world.   Saar(i) = taking care of; remembering (the noble qualities of Waheguru).  Aatth Pehr = 24 hours.   Ruttay = imbued.   Rung(i) 
Apaar = limitless.  Sookh Saagro = sea of comforts.  Bulihaar = sacrifice.       4.
MEANING:  You get the real comfort when you follow the Guru’s Teachings, oh my mind.  Nobody leaves empty (without Guru’s blessings) when you serve the Guru as His personal attendant (obey His Will).          Pause.            When one is connected with Satguru, all his discomforts go away, and the source of all comfort – Waheguru- takes residence in his heart. Concentrating on Waheguru, one’s mind is enlightened.  When one gets connected with the Guru, his face begins to glow (his mind becomes pure); but only those get connected with the Guru who are predestined to do so.  One’s mind becomes pure by singing His praise and reciting Waheguru’s Naam every day (all the time).       1.
All his wishes are fulfilled when one acquires the treasure of Waheguru’s Naam. The omniscient Waheguru is always with you, but you need to recognize the Creator.  One can do that (recognize Waheguru) when he recites His Naam by Guru’s grace, and gives the gift of Waheguru’s Naam to others (fosters the Naam in them). At that level of spirituality, his lust, rage, greed, and ego all disappear, and his mind becomes pure.      2.             One’s objective of life (union with Waheguru) is achieved as he internalizes Waheguru’s Naam – this is the benefit (wealth) he earns. Waheguru becomes very kind and fosters His Naam in the heart of the devotee. With the kindness of Waheguru, the individual's birth-death-birth cycle is terminated. With Guru’s Shabad, one’s mind is enlightened, and one reaches Waheguru’s Palace (merges into Him).         3.
As a result of the devotees’ singing His praise, Waheguru protects them (from the attacks of Maya) by His kindness, and they are honoured in His Court.  The devotees are imbued in Waheguru’s Naam and sing His praise twenty-four hours.  Waheguru is the sea (unlimited source) of all comforts; and (Guru) Nanak is always a sacrifice for Him.  4.11. 81.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ 

ਪੂਰਾ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਜੇ ਮਿਲੈ ਪਾਈਐ ਸਬਦੁ ਨਿਧਾਨੁ॥ ਕਰਿ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਆਪਣੀ ਜਪੀਐ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਨਾਮੁ॥ ਜਨਮ ਮਰਣ ਦੁਖੁ ਕਾਟੀਐ ਲਾਗੈ ਸਹਜ ਧਿਆਨੁ॥ 1॥ ਮੇਰੇ ਮਨ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਸਰਣਾਈ ਪਾਇ॥ ਹਰਿ ਬਿਨੁ ਦੂਜਾ ਕੋ ਨਹੀ ਏਕੋ ਨਾਮੁ ਧਿਆਇ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਕੀਮਤਿ ਕਹਣੁ ਨ ਜਾਈਐ ਸਾਗਰੁ ਗੁਣੀ ਅਥਾਹੁ॥ ਵਡਭਾਗੀ ਮਿਲੁ ਸੰਗਤੀ ਸਚਾ ਸਬਦੁ ਵਿਸਾਹੁ॥ ਕਰਿ ਸੇਵਾ ਸੁਖ ਸਾਗਰੈ ਸਿਰਿ ਸਾਹਾ ਪਾਤਿਸਾਹੁ॥ 2॥ ਚਰਣ ਕਮਲ ਕਾ ਆਸਰਾ ਦੂਜਾ ਨਾਹੀ ਠਾਉ॥ ਮੈ ਧਰ ਤੇਰੀ ਪਾਰਬ੍ਰਹਮ ਤੇਰੈ ਤਾਣਿ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਨਿਮਾਣਿਆ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਮਾਣੁ ਤੂ ਤੇਰੈ ਸੰਗਿ ਸਮਾਉ॥ 3॥ ਹਰਿ ਜਪੀਐ ਆਰਾਧੀਐ ਆਠ ਪਹਰ ਗੋਵਿੰਦੁ॥ ਜੀਅ ਪ੍ਰਾਣ ਤਨੁ ਧਨੁ ਰਖੇ ਕਰਿ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਰਾਖੀ ਜਿੰਦੁ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਸਗਲੇ ਦੋਖ ਉਤਾਰਿਅਨੁ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਪਾਰਬ੍ਰਹਮ ਬਖਸਿੰਦੁ॥ 4॥12॥82॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 5:  Poora Satguru jay milaiy, paayiyaiy Shabad(u) nidhaan(u). Kur(i) kirpa Prubh aapni, jupiyaiy Amrit Naam(u). Junum mern dookh(u) kaatiyaiy laagaiy sehj dhiyaan. 1. Mayray mun, Prubh shernaayee paayay. Hur(i) bin(u) dooja ko nuhi aiko Naam(u) dhiyayay. 1. Ruhaou.  Keemut(i) kehn(u) na jaayiyaiy saagur(u) gooni athaah(u. Vudbhaagi mil(u) Sungati, Sacha Shabad(u) visaah(u). Kur(i) sayva sookh saagraiy, sir(i) shaahaan Patshaah(u). 2. Churn kumul ka aasra, dooja naahi tthaaoun.  Mein dhur Tayri Parbrahm, Tayraiy taan(i) ruhaoun.  Nimaaniya Prubh maan(u) Tu, Tayraiy sung(i) smaaoun. 3. Hur jupiyaiy, aaraadhiyaiy, aatth pehr Govind(u). Jea praan tun(u) dhun(u) rukhay, kur(i) kirpa raakhi jind(u). Nanak suglay dokh ootariyun Prubh Parbrahm bukhshind(u). 4.12.82.
Word Meaning:  Nidhaan(u) = treasure.  Jupiyaiy = recite (with dedication).  Sehj = peaceful mind.      1.        Athaah(u) = unfathomable.    Visaah(u) = deal in; buy.   Saagraiy = sea of.   Sir(i) = above.   Shaahaan = kings.  Paatshah(u) = supreme King.       2. 
Churn Kumul = lotus-like soft feet (of Waheguru).   Tthaaon = place; refuge.    Dhur = support.  Parbrahm = Waheguru.   Taan(i) = power.   Nimaaniyaa = without any dignity; powerless.   Maan(u) = honour; respect.   Smaaoun = merge with.        3.
Aaraadhiyaiy = worship.   Aatth Pehr = 24 hours.  Govind(u) = Waheguru.  Jea = soul.   Praan = breath; life.    Jind(u) = life.   Suglay = all.  Bukhshind(u) = forgiver.   4. 
MEANING:  Come to the shelter of Waheguru, oh my mind.  Worship only the Naam of God, as there is nobody else (equal to Him).          Pause.       If one is able to connect with the Perfect Satguru, he acquires the treasure of the Shabad (fosters Waheguru’s Naam in his heart). (I pray to Waheguru that) He is kind to me so that I may worship His Naam, which is the real Amrit (otherwise a mythical liquid which makes a person immortal). (When one worships Waheguru’s Naam), his mind gets focused on the Naam (quits wandering), and the suffering of rebirth is eliminated.        1.       Waheguru is a fathomless sea of noble qualities, and nobody can evaluate Him fully (cannot describe Him). One can only deal in the noble qualities (acquire them) in the company of the devotees.  One needs to serve Waheguru (lead one's life according to the Guru’s teachings), which is the source of all comforts and the Supreme King above all worldly kings.       2.           There is no other support than Waheguru whose Feet are as soft as the lotus flower (an expression of devotee’s love for God). You are my only support, oh Waheguru, and I survive on Your power (support). Waheguru confers honour on the weak, and I pray that I may be fully absorbed in Him by singing His praise.        3.              One should always worship and remember Waheguru, who kindly supports our breathing, our bodies, our wealth, and our lives. Waheguru is kind and forgiving, and removes all our sins, oh Nanak.   4.12.82.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਿ ਲਗੀ ਤਿਸੁ ਸਚ ਸਿਉ ਮਰੈ ਨ ਆਵੈ ਜਾਇ॥ ਨਾ ਵੇਛੋੜਿਆ ਵਿਛੁੜੇ ਸਭ ਮਹਿ ਰਹਿਆ ਸਮਾਇ॥ ਦੀਨ ਦਰਦ ਦੁਖੁ ਭੰਜਨਾ ਸੇਵਕ ਕੈ ਸਤ ਭਾਇ॥ ਅਚਰਜ ਰੂਪੁ ਨਿਰੰਜਨੋ ਗੁਰਿ ਮੇਲਾਇਆ ਮਾਇ॥ 1॥ ਭਾਈ ਰੇ ਮੀਤੁ ਕਰਹੁ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਸੋਇ॥
