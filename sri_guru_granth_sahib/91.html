(ਅੰਗ 90)॥   ਮ:3॥ ਸਬਦਿ ਰਤੀ ਸੋਹਾਗਣੀ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਭਾਇ ਪਿਆਰਿ॥ ਸਦਾ ਰਾਵੇ ਪਿਰੁ ਆਪਣਾ ਸਚੈ ਪ੍ਰੇਮਿ ਪਿਆਰਿ॥ ਅਤਿ ਸੁਆਲਿਓ ਸੁੰਦਰੀ ਸੋਭਾਵੰਤੀ ਨਾਰਿ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮਿ ਸੋਹਾਗਣੀ ਮੇਲੀ ਮੇਲਣਹਾਰਿ॥ 2॥
(PAGE 90):          M:3: Shabad(i) rutti sohaagni, Satgur kaiy bhaayay piyaar(i).  Suda raavay Pir(u) aapna, Sachaiy praym(i) piyaar(i).  Ut(i) sooaaliyo soondri, sobhavunti naar(i). Nanak Naam(i) sohaagni mayli Maylunhaar(i).  2.
Word Meaning:  Rutti = imbued in.   Sohaagni = married; loved by the husband.  Raavaiy = enjoys (union).   Ut(i) = extremely.  Sooaaliyo = beautiful.   Sobhavunty = praiseworthy.   Maylunhaar(i) = the connector (Waheguru).
MEANING:  An individual, who is imbued in Satguru’s Shabad with deep love for Him, is the one who is truly married to Waheguru (connected with Him).  Such an individual always enjoys the union with Him because of his true love for Waheguru.  Such an individual is extremely beautiful and a praiseworthy wife.  With Waheguru’s Naam in the heart, she is connected with Waheguru with His grace, and is always loved by Him, oh Nanak.     2.
ਪਉੜੀ॥ ਹਰਿ ਤੇਰੀ ਸਭਿ ਕਰਹਿ ਉਸਤਤਿ ਜਿਨਿ ਫਾਥੇ ਕਾਢਿਆ॥ ਹਰਿ ਤੁਧ ਨੋ ਕਰਹਿ ਸਭਿ ਨਮਸਕਾਰੁ ਜਿਨਿ ਪਾਪੈ ਤੇ ਰਾਖਿਆ॥ ਹਰਿ ਨਿਮਾਣਿਆ ਤੂ ਮਾਣੁ ਹਰਿ ਡਾਢੀ ਹੂੰ ਤੂੰ ਡਾਢਿਆ॥ ਹਰਿ ਅਹੰਕਾਰੀਆ ਮਾਰਿ ਨਿਵਾਏ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਮੂੜ ਸਾਧਿਆ॥ ਹਰਿ ਭਗਤਾ ਦੇਇ ਵਡਿਆਈ ਗਰੀਬ
ਅਨਾਥਿਆ॥ 17.
Paurri: Hur(i) Tayri subh(i) kureh(i) oostut(i), jin(i) phaathay kaadhiya. Hur Toodh nu kureh(i) subh(i) numskaar(u), jin(i) paapaiy tay raakhiya. Hur(i) nimaaniya Tu maan(u) Hur(i), daadhi hoon Tu dadhiya . Hur(i) ahunkaariyaan maar(i) nivayay munmukh moorr sadhiya. Hur bhugtaan dayay vudiyayee greeb anaathiya.   17 
Word Meaning:  Oostut(i) = praise.  Phaathay = caught in the net (of Maya).  Kaadhiya = took out; cut free.  Nimaaniya = prideless; powerless.  Daadhi = tough; strict.  Dadhiyaan = oppressors; tyrants.    Moorr = foolish.   Sadhiya = set straight; fixed.  Anaathiya = orphans. 
MEANING: Everybody sings Your praise because You cut the bonds of Maya, and free the devotees, oh Waheguru.  Everybody bows to You because You protect the devotees from committing sins.   Waheguru bestows dignity on the powerless, but is tough with the tyrants. Waheguru forces the conceited to bow, and fixes the foolish followers of their mind.  God bestows honour on the poor and the supportless.  17.

ਸਲੋਕ ਮ:3॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਭਾਣੈ ਜੋ ਚਲੈ ਤਿਸੁ ਵਡਿਆਈ ਵਡੀ ਹੋਇ॥ ਹਰਿ ਕਾ ਨਾਮੁ ਉਤਮੁ ਮਨਿ ਵਸੈ ਮੇਟਿ ਨ ਸਕੈ ਕੋਇ॥ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਕਰੇ ਜਿਸੁ ਆਪਣੀ ਤਿਸੁ ਕਰਮਿ ਪਰਾਪਤਿ ਹੋਇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਕਾਰਣੁ ਕਰਤੇ ਵਸਿ ਹੈ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਬੂਝੈ ਕੋਇ॥ 1॥
Sloak M:3: Satgur kaiy bhaanaiy jo chullaiy, tis(u) vudiyayee vudi hoyay. Hur(i) ka Naam(u) ootum(u) mun(i) vusaiy, mayt(i) na sukaiy koyay. Kirpa kuray jis(u) Aapni, tis(u) kerm(i) praput(i) hoyay.  Nanak, kaarun(u) Kertaiy vus(i) haiy, Gurmukh(i) boojhaiy koyay.  1.
Word Meaning:  Bhaanaiy = as per His Will.   Vudiyayee = respect; praise.   Ootum(u) = supreme.   Mayt(i) = erase; remove.   Kerm(i) = benevolence.   Kaarun(u) = resources.      Vus(i) = (in His) control.
MEANING:  The individual, who leads his life as per the Will of the Guru, earns great respect because Waheguru’s supreme Naam(u) takes residence (is implanted) in his heart, and nobody can remove that respect for him.  But only those individuals earn such respect who are blessed by Waheguru with His benevolence.  Only a rare devotee realizes that Waheguru is the controller of all resources, oh Nanak.  1.

ਮ:3॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਜਿਨੀ ਆਰਾਧਿਆ ਅਨਦਿਨੁ ਹਰਿ ਲਿਵ ਤਾਰ॥ ਮਾਇਆ ਬੰਦੀ ਖਸਮ ਕੀ ਤਿਨ ਅਗੈ ਕਮਾਵੇ ਕਾਰ॥ ਪੂਰੈ ਪੂਰਾ ਕਰਿ ਛੋਡਿਆ ਹੁਕਮਿ ਸਵਾਰਣਹਾਰ॥ ਗੁਰ ਪਰਸਾਦੀ ਜਿਨਿ ਬੁਝਿਆ ਤਿਨਿ ਪਾਇਆ ਮੋਖ ਦੁਆਰੁ॥ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਹੁਕਮੁ ਨ ਜਾਣਨੀ ਤਿਨ ਮਾਰੇ ਜਮ ਜੰਦਾਰੁ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਜਿਨੀ ਅਰਾਧਿਆ ਤਿਨੀ ਤਰਿਆ ਭਉਜਲੁ ਸੰਸਾਰੁ॥ ਸਭਿ ਅਉਗਣ ਗੁਣੀ ਮਿਟਾਇਆ ਗੁਰੁ ਆਪੇ ਬਖਸਣਹਾਰੁ॥ 2॥
M:3:  Nanak Hur(i) Naam(u) jinhi aaradhiya, undin(u) Hur(i) liv taar.  Maya bundi Khusm kee, tin uggaiy kmaavaiy kaar.  Pooraiy poora kur chhodiya, Huqam(i) Svaarunhaar.  Gur persadi jin(i) boojhiya, tin(i) paaya mokh dooaar(u).  Munmukh Huqam(u) na jaan-ni, tin maaray Jum jundaar(u).  Gurmukh(i) jinhi aaradhiya, tinhi turiya bhoujul(u) sunsaar(u).  Subh(i) aougun gooni mitaaya, Gur(u) aapay bukhshunhaar(u).  2.
Word Meaning:  Aaradhiya = worshiped.   Liv Taar = (with full) concentration.   Bundi = slave.   Khusm = master; Waheguru.   Kmaavaiy Kaar = obeys command.   Pooraiy = perfect (Waheguru).  Svaarunhaar = perfectionist (Waheguru).   Boojhiya = realized.   Mokh = freedom from Maya.   Jum Jundaar(u) = unkind angel of death.  Bhoujul(u) = the world sea.   Aougun = ill qualities.   Bukhshunhaar = forgiving.  2.
MEANING:  Those who worship Waheguru’s Naam day and night with full concentration, enslave Maya, which obeys their command, oh Nanak.  With His Command, the Perfect Waheguru makes such devotees perfect too.  Those who realize this fact in the shelter of the Guru get freedom from the bonds of Maya.  The followers of their mind do not recognize Waheguru’s Will, and are punished by the unkind Angel of death. Those who worship Waheguru in the shelter of the Guru swim across the world-sea. All their bad qualities are replaced by noble qualities by the benevolence of the forgiving Waheguru.     2.

ਪਉੜੀ॥ ਹਰਿ ਕੀ ਭਗਤਾ ਪਰਤੀਤਿ ਹਰਿ ਸਭ ਕਿਛੁ ਜਾਣਦਾ॥ ਹਰਿ ਜੇਵਡ ਨਾਹੀ ਕੋਈ ਜਾਣੁ ਹਰਿ ਧਰਮੁ ਬੀਚਾਰਦਾ॥ ਕਾੜਾ ਅੰਦੇਸਾ ਕਿਉ ਕੀਜੈ ਜਾ ਨਾਹੀ ਅਧਰਮਿ ਮਾਰਦਾ॥ ਸਚਾ ਸਾਹਿਬੁ ਸਚੁ ਨਿਆਉ ਪਾਪੀ ਨਰੁ ਹਾਰਦਾ॥ ਸਾਲਾਹਿਹੁ ਭਗਤਹੁ ਕਰ ਜੋੜਿ ਹਰਿ ਭਗਤ ਜਨ ਤਾਰਦਾ॥18॥
Paurri:  Hur(i) kee bhugtaan purteet(i), Hur(i) subh kichh(u) jaanda. Hur(i) jayvud naahi koyee jaan(u), Hur(i) dherm(u) beecharda. Kaarra undaysha kiyoun keejaiy, ja naahi udherm(i) maarda. Sacha Sahib(u) Sach(u) Niyaoun, paapi nur(u) haarda. Slaahou bhugtou ker jorr(i) , Hur(i) bhugut jun taarda.  18.
Word Meaning:  Purteet(i) = faith.  Jaan(u) = knowledgeable; know-all.  Dherm(u) = justice.   Andaysha = doubt.   Kaarra = causing heart burn.  Udherm(i) = injustice.  Sacha = Perfect.  Niyaoun = judgment.   Slaahou = sing praise.  Kur = hands.  Jorr(i) = folded. 
MEANING:  The devotees have full faith that Waheguru is omniscient.  Nobody is as great as Waheguru is; He always delivers justice.  Why should anyone have heartburn due to any doubt, because Waheguru delivers no injustice. The perfect Lord delivers perfect judgment, and the sinners lose (are punished).  Sing praise of Waheguru with folded hands, oh devotees; He always salvages His devotees.   18.

ਸਲੋਕ ਮ:3॥ ਆਪਣੇ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਮ ਮਿਲਿ ਰਹਾ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਰਖਾ ਉਰਿਧਾਰਿ॥ ਸਾਲਾਹੀ ਸੋ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਸਦਾ ਸਦਾ ਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਹੇਤਿ ਪਿਆਰਿ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਜਿਸੁ ਨਦਰਿ ਕਰੇ ਤਿਸੁ ਮੇਲਿ ਲਏ ਸਾਈ ਸੁਹਾਗਣਿ ਨਾਰਿ॥ 1॥
Sloak M:3: Aapnay Preetum mil(i) ruhaan untar(i) rukhaan oor(i) dhaar(i).  Saalaheen so Prubh suda suda Gur kaiy hayt(i) piyaar(i).  Nanak jis(u) nedr(i)  kuray tis(u) mayl(i) luyay, saayee soohaagun(i) naar(i).  1.
Word Meaning:  Preetum = beloved.  Oordhaar(i) = hold in heart.  Hayt(i) = for.  Soohaagun = married; beloved.  
MEANING:  I wish to be connected with my beloved (Waheguru) and hold Him in my heart.  I wish to sing His praise constantly, with deep love for the Guru.  However, Waheguru connects only those individuals whom He is kind to; and such an individual is a beloved of Waheguru, oh Nanak.  1.

ਮ:3॥ ਗੁਰ ਸੇਵਾ ਤੇ ਹਰਿ ਪਾਈਐ ਜਾ ਕਉ ਨਦਰਿ ਕਰੇਇ॥ ਮਾਣਸ ਤੇ ਦੇਵਤੇ ਭਏ ਧਿਆਇਆ ਨਾਮੁ ਹਰੇ॥ ਹਉਮੈ ਮਾਰਿ ਮਿਲਾਇਅਨੁ ਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਸਬਦਿ ਤਰੇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਸਹਜਿ ਸਮਾਇਅਨੁ ਹਰਿ ਆਪਣੀ ਕ੍ਰਿਪਾ ਕਰੇ॥ 2॥
M:3:  Gur sayva tay Hur(I) paayiyaiy, ja kuou nudr(i) kuray. Maanus tay dayvtay bhuyay, dhiyaya Naam(u) Huray.  Haumein maar(i) milaayun(u), Gur kaiy Shabad turay. Nanak sehj(i) smaayayun(u) Hur(i) aapni kirpa kuray.     2.
Word Meaning:  Nudr(i) = kind sight.   Maanus = humans.  Huray = Hur(i) = Waheguru.  Sehj(i) = spiritual state above the effects of Maya.  Smaayayun = become one with. 
MEANING:  Those, on whom Waheguru casts His kind sight, serve the Guru and become “one” with Waheguru.  Those who recite Waheguru’s Naam(u) are transformed from humans to gods (Godlike). By following Guru’s Shabad, their selfishness and ego are destroyed, and they swim across the sea of Maya, thereby becoming “one” with Waheguru. Because of Waheguru’s benevolence, they attain a spiritual state above the effects of Maya, oh Nanak.  2.

ਪਉੜੀ॥ ਹਰਿ ਆਪਣੀ ਭਗਤਿ ਕਰਾਇ ਵਡਿਆਈ ਵੇਖਾਲੀਅਨੁ॥ ਆਪਣੀ ਆਪਿ ਕਰੇ ਪਰਤੀਤਿ ਆਪੇ ਸੇਵ ਘਾਲੀਅਨੁ॥
