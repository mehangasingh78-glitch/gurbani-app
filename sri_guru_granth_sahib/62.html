(ਅੰਗ 61)॥              ਸਾਚਿ ਸਹਜਿ ਸੋਭਾ ਘਣੀ ਹਰਿ ਗੁਣ ਨਾਮ ਅਧਾਰਿ॥ ਜਿਉ ਭਾਵੈ ਤਿਉ ਰਖੁ ਤੂ ਮੈ ਤੁਝ ਬਿਨੁ ਕਵਨੁ ਭਤਾਰੁ॥ 3॥ ਅਖਰ ਪੜਿ ਪੜਿ ਭੁਲੀਐ ਭੇਖੀ ਬਹੁਤੁ ਅਭਿਮਾਨੁ॥ ਤੀਰਥ ਨਾਤਾ ਕਿਆ ਕਰੇ ਮਨ ਮਹਿ ਮੈਲੁ ਗੁਮਾਨੁ॥ ਗੁਰ ਬਿਨੁ ਕਿਨਿ ਸਮਝਾਈਐ ਮਨੁ ਰਾਜਾ ਸੁਲਤਾਨੁ॥ 4॥ ਪ੍ਰੇਮ ਪਦਾਰਥੁ ਪਾਈਐ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਤਤੁ ਵੀਚਾਰੁ॥ ਸਾ ਧਨ ਆਪੁ ਗਵਾਇਆ ਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਸਬਦਿ ਸੀਗਾਰੁ॥ ਘਰ ਹੀ ਸੋ ਪਿਰੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਹੇਤਿ ਅਪਾਰੁ॥ 5॥ ਗੁਰ ਕੀ ਸੇਵਾ ਚਾਕਰੀ ਮਨੁ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਸੁਖੁ ਹੋਇ॥ ਗੁਰ ਕਾ ਸਬਦੁ ਮਨਿ ਵਸਿਆ ਹਉਮੈ ਵਿਚਹੁ ਖੋਇ॥ ਨਾਮੁ ਪਦਾਰਥੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਲਾਭੁ ਸਦਾ ਮਨਿ ਹੋਇ॥ 6॥ ਕਰਮਿ ਮਿਲੈ ਤਾ ਪਾਈਐ ਆਪਿ ਨ ਲਇਆ ਜਾਇ॥ ਗੁਰ ਕੀ ਚਰਣੀ ਲਗਿ ਰਹੁ ਵਿਚਹੁ ਆਪੁ ਗਵਾਇ॥ ਸਚੇ ਸੇਤੀ ਰਤਿਆ ਸਚੋ ਪਲੈ ਪਾਇ॥7॥ ਭੁਲਣ ਅੰਦਰਿ ਸਭੁ ਕੋ ਅਭੁਲੁ ਗੁਰੂ ਕਰਤਾਰੁ॥ ਗੁਰਮਤਿ ਮਨੁ ਸਮਝਾਇਆ ਲਾਗਾ ਤਿਸੈ ਪਿਆਰੁ॥ਨਾਨਕ ਸਾਚੁ ਨ ਵੀਸਰੈ ਮੇਲੇ ਸਬਦੁ ਅਪਾਰੁ॥8॥12॥

Word Meaning:  Tthaur = shelter.    Pdaruth = valuable article.    Khoya = lost.    Tthugiya = Moottha = defrauded; swindled.     1.      Baba = elderly (wise) man.   Bherm(i) = misled; deceived.   Dohaagni = disowned by husband.  Unk(i) = (in the) heart.  Smaayay= mixes completely; becomes one with.       Pause.       Disunited = foreign countries.   Grehou = home.  Tuj(i) = relinquish.   Doongur = hill.  Thul = desert; ground.   Dolaayay = uncontrolled; wavering (mind).   Dhooroun = right from the source.   Vichhooni = separated.    Gerb = ego.   Billayay= wails.      2.    Vichhoorriyaan = separated.  Rus(i) = with taste.  Sehj(i) = mental state above the attractions of Maya.   Sobha = fame; praise.    Ghuni = very much.   Adhaar(i) = support.   Bhutaar(u) = husband.       3.     Akkhur = letters; teachings of holy books.   Bhaykheen = symbols of different faiths. Abhimaan = Goomaan = conceit.   Sultan = a Muslim ruler.     4.
Pdaruth = valuable item.  Tut(u) = the root; source (Waheguru).   Sa Dhun = that woman.   Aap(u) = self.  Seegaar(i) = adornment.   Hayt(u) = (by) live.      5.
Chaakri = personal tending.   Nirmul = pure.   Haumein = selfishness and ego.  6.
Kerm(i) = by the grace.    Ruttiyaan = being imbued.   Pullaiy = in account.     7.
Abhool = infallible.  Apaar(u) = incomprehensible.    8.
MEANING:  (Maya’s effects on man are very powerful), and under its influence, the man is misled from the real path of life (to get connected with Waheguru), oh, wise man.  A misled human bride (man) is like a wife who has been disowned by her husband and cannot enjoy his love (Waheguru).        Pause.
The follower of his mind is misled (under the influence of Maya) from the real path of life and has no shelter (for spiritual gain).  Nobody, other than the Guru, can show him the real path (to connect with God), and the blind (ignorant man) leaves this world trapped in the birth-death cycle, because he has been cheated of the valuable Divine Knowledge by Maya.      1.
Misguided (but still desirous of connecting with Waheguru), the man leaves home (in search of God), and wanders in different territories, on the mountains, and on earth, but due to the non-clarity of his mission (caught between Maya and the search for God), his mind does not find peace.  But such men are not fully to blame because those who are pre-destined to be engrossed in Maya are deceived by their ego; they only wail about their misfortune.       2.
However, the Guru can connect such people to Waheguru by fostering in their minds the love and the sweet taste of Waheguru’s Naam. (Acting on Guru’s advice), When an individual becomes imbued in Waheguru’s Naam and makes Waheguru his only support, he attains a spiritual level above the attractions of Maya, remains connected to Waheguru, and earns praise in the world. Hey Waheguru, I pray to You to kindly protect me, too, from the attacks of Maya, in whatever way You please; I have no other husband (Lord) than You, begs Nanak.       3.               Some people read holy books, but (without the proper guidance of the Guru) they are misled and may join a faith group and wear its symbols, yet they still suffer from their false ego (of being religious).   Pilgrimage, too, does not serve any purpose if the mind has the filth of ego on it. Their mind is the ruler for them (controls all their actions); the mind can be enlightened only by the Guru, and nobody else.         4. 
By taking Guru’s shelter, one ponders on the Divine qualities of the root (Waheguru), and acquires love for Him. With the mind adorned by the Guru’s Shabad, such a human bride is able to kill her selfishness. With pure love for the Guru, she realizes the incomprehensible God in her heart (and does not need to leave home).         5.
Service to the Guru as a personal attendant purifies one’s mind, and one realizes and feels all comforts.  One’s ego is eradicated when the Guru’s Shabad takes residence in his mind, and he permanently earns the valuable Naam (it stays in his mind forever).         6.
However, nobody can acquire Waheguru’s Naam by their own efforts; only the kindness of the Guru makes that possible. Therefore, the man should always take Guru’s shelter to get rid of his selfishness.  When one is imbued in Waheguru’s Naam, he acquires Him (becomes “one” with Waheguru).          7.            Everybody is fallible; only the Guru and Waheguru are infallible. So, the individual, who takes shelter of the Guru, develops love for Waheguru, and his mind is enlightened. Guru’s Shabad connects one with the incomprehensible Waheguru, and he never lets the Naam slip from his mind, oh Nanak.   8.12.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 1॥ ਤ੍ਰਿਸਨਾ ਮਾਇਆ ਮੋਹਣੀ ਸੁਤ ਬੰਧਪ ਘਰਿ ਨਾਰਿ॥ ਧਨਿ ਜੋਬਨਿ ਜਗੁ ਠਗਿਆ ਲਬਿ ਲੋਭਿ ਅਹੰਕਾਰਿ॥ ਮੋਹ ਠਗਉਲੀ ਹਉ ਮੁਈ ਸਾ ਵਰਤੈ ਸੰਸਾਰਿ॥ 1॥ ਮੇਰੇ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਮਾ ਮੈ ਤੁਝ ਬਿਨੁ ਅਵਰੁ ਨ ਕੋਇ॥ਮੈ ਤੁਝ ਬਿਨੁ ਅਵਰੁ ਨਾ ਭਾਵਈ ਤੂ ਭਾਵਹਿ ਸੁਖੁ ਹੋਇ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਨਾਮ ਸਾਲਾਹੀ ਰੰਗ ਸਿਉ ਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਸਬਦਿ ਸੰਤੋਖੁ॥ ਜੋ ਦੀਸੈ ਸੋ ਚਲਸੀ ਕੂੜਾ ਮੋਹੁ ਨ ਵੇਖੁ॥ ਵਾਟ ਵਟਾਊ ਆਇਆ ਨਿਤ ਚਲਦਾ ਸਾਥੁ ਦੇਖੁ॥ 2॥ ਆਖਣਿ ਆਖਹਿ ਕੇਤੜੇ ਗੁਰ ਬਿਨੁ ਬੂਝ ਨ ਹੋਇ॥ ਨਾਮੁ ਵਡਾਈ ਜੇ ਮਿਲੈ ਸਚਿ ਰਪੈ ਪਤਿ ਹੋਇ॥ ਜੋ ਤੁਧੁ ਭਾਵਹਿ ਸੇ ਭਲੇ ਖੋਟਾ ਖਰਾ ਨ ਕੋਇ॥ 3॥ ਗੁਰ ਸਰਣਾਈ ਛੁਟੀਐ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਖੋਟੀ ਰਾਸਿ॥ ਅਸਟ ਧਾਤੁ ਪਾਤਿਸਾਹ ਕੀ ਘੜੀਐ ਸਬਦਿ ਵਿਗਾਸਿ॥ ਆਪੇ ਪਰਖੇ ਪਾਰਖੂ ਪਵੈ ਖਜਾਨੈ ਰਾਸਿ॥ 4॥ ਤੇਰੀ ਕੀਮਤਿ ਨ ਪਵੈ ਸਭ ਡਿਠੀ ਠੋਕਿ ਵਜਾਇ॥ ਕਹਣੈ ਹਾਥ ਨ ਲਭਈ ਸਚਿ ਟਿਕੈ ਪਤਿ ਪਾਇ॥ ਗੁਰਮਤਿ ਤੂ ਸਾਲਾਹਣਾ ਹੋਰੁ ਕੀਮਤਿ ਕਹਣੁ ਨ ਜਾਇ॥ 5॥ ਜਿਤੁ ਤਨਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਨ ਭਾਵਈ ਤਿਤੁ ਤਨਿ ਹਉਮੈ ਵਾਦੁ॥ ਗੁਰ ਬਿਨੁ ਗਿਆਨੁ ਨ ਪਾਈਐ ਬਿਖਿਆ ਦੂਜਾ ਸਾਦੁ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਗੁਣ ਕਾਮਿ ਨ ਆਵਈ ਮਾਇਆ ਫੀਕਾ ਸਾਦੁ 6॥ ਆਸਾ ਅੰਦਰਿ ਜੰਮਿਆ ਆਸਾ ਰਸ ਕਸ ਖਾਇ॥ ਆਸਾ ਬੰਧਿ ਚਲਾਈਐ ਮੁਹੇ ਮੁਹਿ ਚੋਟਾ ਖਾਇ॥ ਅਵਗਣਿ ਬਧਾ ਮਾਰੀਐ ਛੂਟੈ ਗੁਰਮਤਿ ਨਾਇ॥ 7॥
