(ਅੰਗ 83)॥                   ੴ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਪ੍ਰਸਾਦਿ॥
ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗ ਕੀ ਵਾਰ ਮਹਲਾ 4 ਸਲੋਕਾ ਨਾਲਿ॥
(PAGE 83):                       Ek Onkar Satgur Persad(i).  
                     Siri Raag kee Vaar Mehla 4, Sloakaan Naal(i)
VAAR:  Vaar is a type of lyric (Ballad) used to describe battles.  They aroused valour among people.  Vaars were very popular in Punjab at the time of Guru Sahiban. Since the human mind is also engaged in a battle between the good and the evil (between the worship of God and the attractions of Maya), Guru Nanak Sahib used vaars in the spiritual realm for the first time to stir feelings of Waheguru’s worship.   A typical vaar in Guru Granth Sahib has between 20 and 30 verses called paurris, and about twice as many sloaks. The vaars were written in paurris only to begin with, but later appropriate sloaks were also added. 
ਸਲੋਕ ਮ:3॥ ਰਾਗਾ ਵਿਚਿ ਸ੍ਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਹੈ ਜੇ ਸਚਿ ਧਰੇ ਪਿਆਰੁ॥ ਸਦਾ ਹਰਿ ਸਚੁ ਮਨਿ ਵਸੈ ਨਿਹਚਲ ਮਤਿ ਅਪਾਰੁ॥ ਰਤਨੁ ਅਮੋਲਕੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਗੁਰ ਕਾ ਸਬਦੁ ਬੀਚਾਰੁ॥ ਜਿਹਵਾ ਸਚੀ ਮਨੁ ਸਚਾ ਸਚਾ ਸਰੀਰ ਅਕਾਰੁ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਸਚੈ ਸੇਵਿਐ ਸਦਾ ਸਚੁ ਵਾਪਾਰੁ॥ 1॥
ਨੋਟ: ਮ:3 ਦਾ ਭਾਵ ਹੈ ਮਹਲਾ 3, ਅਤੇ ਇਹ ਭਾਵ ਇਸਤਰ੍ਹਾਂ ਹੀ ਚੱਲੇਗਾ॥
Sloak M:3: Raagaan vich(i) Siri Raag(u) haiy, jay Sach(i) dhuray piyaar. Suda Hur(i) Sach(u) mun(i) vusaiy, nihchul mut(i) apaar(u). Ruttan(u) amoluk paaya, Gur ka Shabad(u) beechaar(u). Jihva sachi mun(u) sacha, sacha sreer akaar(u). Nanak Sachaiy sayviyaiy , suda Sach(u) vaapaar(u). 1.     
NOTE: M:3 means Mehla 3, and this will continue throughout the rest of SGGS. 
Word Meaning:  Sach(i) = for the ternal (Waheguru).  Nihchul = stable; unwavering.  Mut(i) = wisdom; thinking.   Ruttan = precious like jewels (Waheguru’s Naam).  Vapaar(u )= business.     1.
MEANING: (Even though Siri Raag is an important Raag), it is meaningful only if it arouses love for Waheguru in the devotee’s mind (when he recites His Naam with Guru’s Shabad); if Waheguru takes permanent residence in the heart of the devotee, and he acquires a stable thinking (to stay connected with Waheguru).   But, Waheguru’s precious Naam can be realized only by pondering on Guru’s Shabad, as a result of which, Waheguru’s Naam is not only on the tongue of the devotee, but also in his mind, as well as in his whole body (with which he performs noble deeds acceptable to Him).  Serving Waheguru is the True business of the man, oh Nanak.  1.

ਮ:3॥ ਹੋਰਿ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਸਭ ਧਾਤੁ ਹੈ ਜਬ ਲਗੁ ਸਾਹਿਬ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਿ ਨ ਹੋਇ॥ ਇਹੁ ਮਨੁ ਮਾਇਆ ਮੋਹਿਆ ਵੇਖਣੁ ਸੁਨਣੁ ਨ ਹੋਇ॥ ਸਹ ਦੇਖੇ ਬਿਨੁ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਿ ਨ ਊਪਜੈ ਅੰਧਾ ਕਿਆ ਕਰੇਇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਜਿਨਿ ਅਖੀ ਲੀਤਿਆ ਸੋਈ ਸਚਾ ਦੇਇ॥ 2॥
M:3:  Hoar(i) birha subh dhaat(u) haiy,  jub lug(u) Sahib preet(i) na hoyay. Ehou mun(u) Maya mohiya, vaykhun(u) soonan(u) na hoyay. Sheh daykhay bin(u) preet(i) na oopjaiy, undha kiya kuray. Nanak Jin(i) ukheen leetiyaan, soyee Sacha dayay.   2.
Word Meaning:  Birha = pain of separation; pangs of love.  Dhaat(u) = attraction for Maya.  Mohiya = captivated.  Sheh = Shah = king = Waheguru.  Oopjaiy = arises.   2.
MEANING:  Love for anything else is not more than running around for Maya unless there is love for Waheguru.  Captivated by Maya, the human mind does not see or listen to (anything about Waheguru).  (Because Waheguru is formless, and one cannot see him), man is blind to His existence; so how can he love Him because love for Waheguru’s Naam does not take root in his mind unless he sees Him. However, the same Waheguru gives the “eyes” to the devotee (enlightens their mind), who had taken them away in the first place, by instilling love for Maya in their mind, oh Nanak.     2.

ਪਉੜੀ॥ ਹਰਿ ਇਕੋ ਕਰਤਾ ਇਕੁ ਇਕੋ ਦੀਬਾਣੁ ਹਰਿ॥ ਹਰਿ ਇਕਸੈ ਦਾ ਹੈ ਅਮਰੁ ਇਕੋ ਹਰਿ ਚਿਤਿ ਧਰਿ॥ ਹਰਿ ਤਿਸੁ ਬਿਨੁ ਕੋਈ ਨਾਹਿ ਡਰੁ ਭ੍ਰਮੁ ਭਉ ਦੂਰਿ ਕਰਿ॥ ਹਰਿ ਤਿਸੈ ਨੋ ਸਾਲਾਹਿ ਜਿ ਤੁਧੁ ਰਖੈ ਬਾਹਰਿ ਘਰਿ॥ ਹਰਿ ਜਿਸ ਨੋ ਹੋਇ ਦਇਆਲੁ ਸੋ ਹਰਿ ਜਪਿ ਭਉ ਬਿਖਮੁ ਤਰਿ॥1॥
Paurri:  Hur(i) ikko kerta Ikk(u), ikko deebaan(u) Hur(i). Hur(i) ikksaiy da haiy amar(u), ikko Hur(I) chit(i) dhur(i). Hur(i) tis(u) bin(u) koyee nahay, dur(u) bherm(u) bhaou door(i) kur(i). Hur(i) tisaiy nu salaahay, j(i) toodh(u) rukhaiy baahur(i) ghur(i). Hur(i) jis nu hoyay duyyal(u) so Hur(i) jup(i) bhuou bikhum(u) tur(i).  1.
Word Meaning:  Kerta = creator.   Deebaan(u) = court; judge.  Amar(u) = Huqam = command.    Chit(i) = in mind.     Bherm(u) = doubts.    Bikhum(u) = difficult (to swim the world-sea).
MEANING:  Waheguru is the only creator (of the universe), and He is the only judge.   Waheguru is the only King who is commanding everywhere; so, keep only Him in mind, oh man.  There is none other than Waheguru (that has powers like Him), so, clear all your fears and doubts about Him, oh man.  Praise only Waheguru, who protects you outside (in the world) and inside (in your mind).  Those who are kind to Waheguru swim across the difficult (sea of Maya) and are salvaged.     1.

ਸਲੋਕ ਮ:1॥ ਦਾਤੀ ਸਾਹਿਬ ਸੰਦੀਆ ਕਿਆ ਚਲੈ ਤਿਸੁ ਨਾਲਿ॥ ਇਕ ਜਾਗੰਦੇ ਨਾ ਲਹੰਨਿ ਇਕਨਾ ਸੁਤਿਆ ਦੇਇ ਉਠਾਲਿ॥ 1॥
Sloak M:1:  Daateen Sahib sundiyaan kiya chulaiy Tis(u) naal(i).  Ikk jaagunday na lehun(i), ikkna sootiyaan dayay ootthaal(i) .  1.
Word Meaning:  Daateen = gifts; largesse.   Sundiyaan = of.   Kiya Chulaiy = what power (one has).    Lehun(i) = receive; acquire.   
MEANING:  All the gifts (especially His Naam) are in the control of Waheguru (provided by Waheguru); man has no power to argue with Him (about receiving the gifts).  Some are awake (visibly) yet (mentally asleep), so they do not receive them (His Naam); while He awakens (enlightens) some sleeping (unaware of His gifts) individuals, and gives (the gift of His Naam) to them.    1.
ਮ:1 ॥ ਸਿਦਕੁ ਸਬੂਰੀ ਸਾਦਿਕਾ ਸਬਰੁ ਤੋਸਾ ਮਲਾਇਕਾ॥ ਦੀਦਾਰੁ ਪੂਰੇ ਪਾਇ ਸਾ ਥਾਉ ਨਾਹੀ ਖਾਇਕਾ॥2॥
M:1:  Siduk(u) suboori saadikaan, subur(u) toasa mlaayaykaan. Deedaar(u) Pooray payay sa, thaoun naahi khaayaykaan.  2.
Word Meaning:  Siduk(u) = faith.  Suboori = contentment.  Saadikaan = having faith.  Subur(u) = patience.   Toasa = food and money for journey.  Mlaayaykaan = devotees; holy men.  Sa = they.   Khaayaykaan = those who just talk big.
MEANING: Faith, contentment, and patience are the food and money for the journey (to union with Waheguru) for the faithful devotees; they get the sight of the Perfect (Waheguru) (achieve union with Him), whereas those who only talk big find no room in Waheguru’s Palace.     2.
ਪਉੜੀ ॥ ਸਭ ਆਪੇ ਤੁਧੁ ਉਪਾਇਕੈ ਆਪਿ ਕਾਰੈ ਲਾਈ॥ ਤੂੰ ਆਪੇ ਵੇਖਿ ਵਿਗਸਦਾ ਆਪਣੀ  ਵਡਿਆਈ॥ ਹਰਿ ਤੁਧਹੁ ਬਾਹਰਿ ਕਿਛੁ ਨਾਹੀ ਤੂੰ ਸਚਾ ਸਾਈ॥ ਤੂੰ ਆਪੇ ਆਪਿ ਵਰਤਦਾ ਸਭਨੀ ਹੀ ਥਾਈ॥ ਹਰਿ ਤਿਸੈ ਧਿਆਵਹੁ ਸੰਤ ਜਨਹੁ ਜੋ ਲਏ ਛਡਾਈ॥ 2॥
Paurri:  Subh aapay Toodh(u) oopayaykaiy, Aap(i) kaaraiy laayee. Toon aapay vaykh(i) vigsuda,, aapni vudiyayee. Hur(i) toodhoun baahur(i) kichh(u) naahi Toon Sacha Saayee. Toon aapay hee aap(i) vertda, subhnee hee thaayeen. Hur(i) Tisaiy dhiaavhou sunt junhou jo luyay chhudayee.  2.
Word Meaning:  Oopayaykaiy = having created.   Kaaraiy = in (worldly) business.   Vigsuda = feels elated.   Vertda = operates.   Luyay Chhudayee = frees from the bonds of Maya; salvages.  
MEANING: Having created the world, You put everybody in worldly business (involving Maya), oh Waheguru. You feel elated to watch the world, which is your wonderful creation!  There is nothing beyond Your control; You are the eternal Lord, oh Waheguru. You alone are operating everywhere.  Recite the Naam of Waheguru, who protects you from the bonds of Maya, (salvages you), oh holy men.  2.

ਸਲੋਕ ਮ: 1॥ ਫਕੜੁ ਜਾਤੀ ਫਕੜੁ ਨਾਉ॥ ਸਭਨਾ ਜੀਆ ਇਕਾ ਛਾਉ॥ ਆਪਹੁ ਜੇ ਕੋਈ ਭਲਾ ਕਹਾਏ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਤਾ ਪਰੁ ਜਾਪੈ ਜਾ ਪਤਿ ਲੇਖੈ ਪਾਏ॥1॥
Sloak M:1: Phukkurr(u) jaati phukkurr(u) naoun. Subhna jeaan ikka chhaoun.  Aap-houn jay koyee bhula kuhaayay. Nanak ta pur(u) jaapaiy, ja put(i) laykhaiy paayay.  1.
Word Meaning:  Phukkurr(u) = joke; baseless.   Naaoun = status in society.   Chhaoun = protection; support.   Put(i) = dignity.
MEANING:  The pride of caste, or of the status in society, is a joke (is baseless) because all human beings have the support (protection) of the same God.  Self - proclamation of being kind or respectable, is again a joke, because one is respectable only if he is recognized as such (in Waheguru’s Court), oh Nanak.  1.

ਮ: 2॥ ਜਿਸੁ ਪਿਆਰੇ ਸਿਉ ਨੇਹੁ ਤਿਸੁ ਆਗੈ ਮਰਿ ਚਲੀਐ॥ ਧ੍ਰਿਗੁ ਜੀਵਣੁ ਸੰਸਾਰਿ ਤਾ ਕੈ ਪਾਛੈ ਜੀਵਣਾ॥ 2॥
M:2:  Jis(u) piyaray siyoun nayhou, tis(u) aagaiy mur(i) chulliyaiy.  Dhrig(u) jeevun(u) sunsaar(i), ta kaiy paachhaiy jeevna.  2.
Word Meaning:  Siyoun = with.     Nayhou = Love.   Dhrig(u) = condemnable.  
MEANING:  The true test of love for someone is to offer everything, including one’s life, to the beloved.  It is condemnable for the person claiming love to live in the world without caring for the beloved.  
NOTE: It appears that Guru Angad Dev Ji is referring to Guru Nanak Sahib.

ਪਉੜੀ॥ ਤੁਧੁ ਆਪੇ ਧਰਤੀ ਸਾਜੀਐ ਚੰਦੁ ਸੂਰਜੁ ਦੁਇ ਦੀਵੈ॥ ਦਸ ਚਾਰਿ ਹਟ ਤੁਧੁ ਸਾਜਿਆ ਵਾਪਾਰੁ ਕਰੀਵੈ॥ ਇਕਨਾ ਨੋ ਹਰਿ ਲਾਭੁ ਦੇਇ ਜੋ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਥੀਵੈ॥ ਤਿਨ ਜਮਕਾਲੁ ਨ ਵਿਆਪਈ ਜਿਨ ਸਚੁ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਪੀਵੈ॥ ਓਇ ਆਪਿ ਛੁਟੇ ਪਰਵਾਰ ਸਿਉ ਤਿਨ ਪਿਛੈ ਸਭੁ ਜਗਤੁ ਛੁਟੀਵੈ॥ 3॥
Paurri:  Toodh(u) aapay dherti saajiyaiy, Chund(u) Sooruj(u) doyay deevaiy.  Dus- chaar(i) hut Toodh(u) saajiya, vapaar(u) kreevaiy. Ikkna nu Hur(i) laabh(u) dayay, jo Gurmukh(i) theevaiy.  Tin Jumkaal(u) na viyaapyee, jin Sach(u) Amrit(u) peevaiy. Oye aap(i) chhootay pervaar siyoun, tin pichhaiy subh(u) jugat(u) chhooteevaiy.   3. 
Word Meaning:  Saajiyaiy = has created.   Dus-chaar(i) = ten plus four = 14 (layers of the universe – a Muslim belief).   Hut = shops; business centres (worlds).  Kureevaiy = to do (business).   Theevaiy = became.   Jumkaal = Angel of death.  Viyaapyee = affects.   Pervaar = family (devotees in contact).   Siyoun = with.   3.
MEANING: You alone created the Earth, the moon and the sun, as two lamps, oh Waheguru. You created fourteen worlds as business centres (for humans) to do business in Waheguru’s Naam. Those whom You guide to take the Guru’s shelter earn the benefit (wealth of Your Naam).  Those who drink the Amrit of Your Naam are not tormented by the Angel of death.  They find salvation along with their families (devotees who sing Waheguru’s praise in their company). In fact, the whole world can benefit from them to find salvation.    3.
