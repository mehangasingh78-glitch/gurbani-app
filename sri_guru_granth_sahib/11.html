(ਅੰਗ 10)॥  ਜਿਨਿ ਦਿਨ ਕਰਿ ਕੈ ਕੀਤੀ ਰਾਤਿ॥ ਖਸਮੁ ਵਿਸਾਰਹਿ ਤੇ ਕਮਜਾਤਿ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਵੈ ਬਾਝ ਸਨਾਤਿ॥4॥3॥
Assa Mehla 1. Aakhaan jeevaan visraiy mur(i) jaaoun. Aakhun(i) aukhaa Saacha Naoun. Saachay Naam kee laagaiy bhookh. Oot(u) bhookaiy khaaayay chuliyay dookh .1. So kiyoun visraiy mayri maayay. Saacha Sahib(u) saachaiy Naayay. 1. Ruhaou. Saachay Naam kee til(u) vuddiyaayee. Aakh(i) thukkay keemut(i) nuhi payee. Jay subh(i) mil(i) kaiy aakhun(i) paah(i). Vudda na hovaiy, ghaat(i) na jaayay. 2. Na oh(u) muraiy na hovaiy soag(u). Daynda ruhaiy na chookaiy bhoag(u). Goon(u) ehou, hoar(i) naahi koyay. Na ko hoa, na ko hoyay.3.  Jay vudd Aap(i) tay vudd Tayri daat(i).   
(PAGE 10): Jin(i) din kur(i) kaiy keetee raat(i). Khusm(u) visareh(i) tay kumjaat(i). Nanak naavaiy baajh snaat(i). 4.3. 
Word meaning:  Visraiy = on forgetting.  Bhookh = appetite; urge; desire.  Oot(u) = bhookhaiy =  because of that urge.     Dookh = discomforts.      1 
Visraiy = lose from memory. Saache Sahib(u) = (union with) the True Master. 
Saachaiy Naayay= by (reciting) the True NaaM.       Pause. 
Til(u) = (the size of a) sesame seed. Aakh(i) tthukkay = exhausted describing.  Keemut(i) = value.  Ghaat(i) = loss (in greatness).       2 
Soag(u) = mourning (causing grief).  Chookaiy = miss (discontinue).   Bhoag(u) =  food.   Goon(u) = good quality; ability.  Ehou = (only in) Waheguru.  Hoar(i) nahi koyay = nobody else.      3 
Jay vudd = as large (great).  Tay vudd = the same size.   Daat(i) = largesse. 
Khusm(u) = Master.  Visareh(i) =  forget.  Kumjaat = Snaat(i) = low caste, ignoble.   4.   MEANING: Even though it is very hard to recite the True (Waheguru’s) Naam (with full concentration and dedication), I am spiritually alive only when I recite Waheguru’s Naam, and experience spiritual death when I forget it. I wish that I experienced the appetite (strong desire) for Waheguru’s Naam all the time, because this appetite eats away (kills) all the discomforts.       1.   
How can I (I cannot afford to) forget the True Naam of Waheguru, oh my mother!  The True Master can be realized only by reciting His Naam (singing His praise).     Pause.   
Many people have exhausted themselves in evaluating (describing) the splendour of God, but they have not been able to describe even a small part of Him (have been badly unsuccessful).  Even if everybody joined together to describe Him, there will be no change in Him – He will become neither bigger nor smaller.       2. 
The Master never dies, and thus does not cause any grief to anyone.  He never stops being the provider.  Only He has this unique ability; nobody else will ever have it.     3. His largesse is as great as He Himself is.  (It is His kindness) that He has created nights after days. Anybody who forgets such a benevolent Master is ignoble. Also ignoble is the person who does not recite His Name (sing His praise), oh Nanak.  4.3. 
     
ਰਾਗੁ ਗੂਜਰੀ ਮਹਲਾ 4॥ ਹਰਿ ਕੇ ਜਨ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਸਤਪੁਰਖਾ ਬਿਨਉ ਕਰਉ ਗੁਰ ਪਾਸਿ॥ ਹਮ ਕੀਰੇ ਕਿਰਮ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਸਰਣਾਈ ਕਰਿ ਦਇਆ ਨਾਮੁ ਪਰਗਾਸਿ॥1॥ ਮੇਰੇ ਮੀਤ ਗੁਰਦੇਵ ਮੋ ਕਉ ਰਾਮ ਨਾਮੁ ਪਰਗਾਸਿ॥ ਗੁਰਮਤਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਮੇਰਾ ਪ੍ਰਾਨ ਸਖਾਈ ਹਰਿ ਕੀਰਤਿ ਹਮਰੀ ਰਹਰਾਸਿ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ ॥ ਹਰਿ ਜਨ ਕੇ ਵਡਭਾਗ ਵਡੇਰੇ ਜਿਨ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਸਰਧਾ ਹਰਿ ਪਿਆਸ॥ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਮਿਲੈ ਤ੍ਰਿਪਤਾਸਹਿ ਮਿਲਿ ਸੰਗਤਿ ਗੁਣ ਪਰਗਾਸਿ॥2॥ ਜਿਨ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਰਸੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਨ ਪਾਇਆ ਤੇ ਭਾਗਹੀਣ ਜਮ ਪਾਸਿ॥ ਜੋ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਸਰਣਿ ਸੰਗਤਿ ਨਹੀ ਆਏ ਧ੍ਰਿਗੁ ਜੀਵੈ ਧ੍ਰਿਗੁ ਜੀਵਾਸਿ॥3॥ ਜਿਨ ਹਰਿ ਜਨ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਸੰਗਤਿ ਪਾਈ ਤਿਨ ਧੁਰ ਮਸਤਕਿ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਲਿਖਾਸਿ॥
ਧਨੁ ਧੰਨੁ ਸਤਸੰਗਤਿ ਜਿਤੁ ਹਰਿ ਰਸੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਮਿਲਿ ਜਨ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮੁ ਪਰਗਾਸਿ॥4॥4॥
Raag(u) Goojri Mehla 4.  Hur(i) kay jun Satgur Sat Purkha, binou kuroun Gur paas(i). Hum keeray kirm, Satgur shernaayee, kur(i) duyya Naam(u) purgaas(i). 1. Mayray meet Gurdayv, mo kuou Ram Naam(u) purgaas(i). Gurmut(i) Naam(u) mayra praan sukhaayee, Hur(i) keerut(i) humree rehras(i). 1. Ruhaou. Hur(i) jun kay vudbhaag vuddayray, jin Hur(i) Hur(i) sherdha Hur(i) piyaas. Hur(i) Hur(i) Naam(u) milaiy triptaaseh(i) mil(i) Sangut(i) goon pergaas(i). 2. Jin Hur(i) Hur(i) Hur(i) rus(u) Naam(u) na paayaa, tay bhaag-heen Jum paas(i). Jo Satgur shern(i) sungat(i) nuhi aayay, dhrig(u) jeevaiy dhrig(u) jeevaas(i). 3. Jin Hur(i) jun Satgur(u) sungat(i) paayee tin dhoor mustak(i) likhiya likhaas(i). Dhunn(u) dhunn(u) Satsungat(i) jit(u) Hur(i) rus(u) paaya mil(i) jun Nanak Naam(u) pergaas(i). 4.4.   
Word Meaning:  Hur(i) kay Jun = devotee.  Sat Purkha = Manifestation of God (Satgur).    Binou = humble request.   Keeray Kirm = worms (filthy; engrossed in Maya).  Shernaayee = shelter; refuge.  Kur(i) Duyya = be kind.   Naam(u) purgaas(i) = enlighten with Naam.         1         Meet = friend.   Gurmut(i) = advice (teachings) of the Guru.  Praan sukhaayee = friend (supporter) of life.  Hur(i) keerut(i) = praise of God.  Rehras(i) = prayer; routine.           Pause.           Vudbgaag = good fortune.   Sherdhaa = (respectful) wish.    Piyaas = thirst (longing).   Triptaaseh(i) = satisfied.       2 
Rus(u) = (sweet) taste.  Tay = those.  Bhag-heen = unlucky.  Jum paas(i) = prisoner of the agent of death.  Dhrig(u) = condemnable.   Jeevaas(i) = future life.        3.  
Mustuk(i) = on the forehead (luck).  Likhiya = written.  Dhunn(u) = great.       4. 
MEANING: Oh, the devotee of God, Satguru - the manifestation of Waheguru – I have 
a humble request to make.  We are like worms (who lead a filthy life), and seek your refuge. Please, be kind and enlighten our hearts with Waheguru’s Naam.     1.  
Please enlighten my mind with Waheguru’s Naam, oh my friend, Satguru.  The Naam is the support of my life, but can be realized only by the Guru’s Shabad (advice). I pray to You, that singing praise of Waheguru becomes my daily routine.       Pause.  
Those devotees are fortunate who have the thirst (longing) and wish for Waheguru (His Naam). They feel content only when they are enlightened with noble qualities and recite Waheguru’s Naam in the company of the holy men.         2. 
On the other hand, those who have not enjoyed the taste of Waheguru’s Naam are very unlucky. Such people become the subjects of the agent of death.  Their present and future lives are condemnable who do not take refuge in Satguru.          3.  
Only those people seek Satguru’s refuge and enjoy His company who have their fortune prewritten on their foreheads (are predestined) by Waheguru. The company of the holy men (Satguru) is great, in which an individual can enjoy the taste of Waheguru’s Naam. (Guru) Nanak says that He, too, seeks such company where His mind is enlightened.    4.4. 

ਰਾਗੁ ਗੂਜਰੀ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਕਾਹੇ ਰੇ ਮਨ ਚਿਤਵਹਿ ਉਦਮੁ ਜਾ ਆਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਜੀਉ ਪਰਿਆ॥ ਸੈਲ ਪਥਰ ਮਹਿ ਜੰਤ ਉਪਾਏ ਤਾ ਕਾ ਰਿਜਕੁ ਆਗੈ ਕਰਿ ਧਰਿਆ॥1॥ ਮੇਰੇ ਮਾਧਉ ਜੀ ਸਤਸੰਗਤਿ ਮਿਲੇ ਸੁ ਤਰਿਆ॥ ਗੁਰ ਪਰਸਾਦਿ ਪਰਮ ਪਦੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਸੂਕੇ ਕਾਸਟ ਹਰਿਆ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਜਨਨਿ ਪਿਤਾ ਲੋਕ ਸੁਤ ਬਨਿਤਾ ਕੋਇ ਨ ਕਿਸ ਕੀ ਧਰਿਆ॥ ਸਿਰਿ ਸਿਰਿ ਰਿਜਕੁ ਸੰਬਾਹੇ ਠਾਕੁਰੁ ਕਾਹੇ ਮਨ ਭਉ ਕਰਿਆ॥॥2॥ ਊਡੇ ਊਡਿ ਆਵੈ ਸੈ ਕੋਸਾ ਤਿਸੁ ਪਾਛੈ ਬਚਰੇ ਛਰਿਆ॥ ਤਿਨ ਕਵਣੁ ਖਲਾਵੈ ਕਵਣੁ ਚੁਗਾਵੈ ਮਨ ਮਹਿ ਸਿਮਰਨੁ ਕਰਿਆ॥3॥ ਸਭਿ ਨਿਧਾਨ ਦਸ ਅਸਟ ਸਿਧਾਨ ਠਾਕੁਰ ਕਰ ਤਲ ਧਰਿਆ॥ ਜਨ ਨਾਨਕ ਬਲਿ ਬਲਿ ਸਦ ਬਲਿ ਜਾਈਐ ਤੇਰਾ ਅੰਤੁ ਨ ਪਾਰਾਵਰਿਆ॥4॥5॥
Raag Goojri Mehla 5: Kaahay ray mun chitveh(i) oodum(u), ja aahur(i) Hur(i) Jeeo puriya.  Saiyl puththur meh(i) junt oopaayay, ta ka rijuk(u) aagaiy kur(i) dhuriya.  1. Mayray Madho Ji, Satsungut(i) milay s(i) turiya.  Gur persad(i) Perm pud(u) paaya, sookay kaasht huriya.  1. Ruhaou.  Junun(i), pita, loak, soot, bunita, koyay na kis kee dhuriya.  Sir(i), sir(i) rijuk(u) sumbaahay Tthaakur, kaahay mun bhou kuriya.  2. Oodaiy, ood(i) aavaiy saiy koasaan, tis(u) paachhaiy buchray chhuriya.  Oon kuvun(u) khulaavaiy, kuvun choogaavaiy, mun meh(i) simrun kuriaya.  3.  Subh nidhaan, dus usht sidhaan, Tthaakur ker tul dhuriya.  Jun Nanak bul(i), bul(i), sud bul(i)  jaayiyaiy, Tayra unt(u) na paaravuriya.   4.5. 
Word Meaning:   Chitveh(i) Oodum = think about putting in effort; worry about.   Aahur(i) = (has the) responsibility.    Saiyl = Puththur = rocks.   Junt = living beings.   
Rijuk(u) = food.    Aagaiy = before.  Dhuriya = placed; arranged.     1. 
Madho = God.   S(i) = that.   Turiya = salvaged.   Perm Pud(u) = spiritual state above Maya.   Sookay Kaasht = dry wood; dead trees.  Huriya = (became) green; came to life.         Pause.         Junun(i) = mother.   Loak = people; daughtes (because they leave their parents’ home and become members of other’s family).  Soot = sons.   Bunita =   wife.     Dhuriya = support; dependence.   Sir(i) Sir(i) = everybody.   Sumbaahay = delivers.    Rijuk = food; necessities of life.   Kaahay = why.   Bhuou = afraid of.        2.               Oodaiy = (Koonj) flies.  Saiy = hundreds.  Koas = a unit of length (1 koas = 2.2 km).    Pachhaiy Chhuriya = leaves behind.    Buchray = young ones.   Choogaavaiy = pick food.     Simrun = remember.        3. 
Nidhaan = trasures. Dus Usht = ten and eight = 18.  Siddhaan = supernatural powers.  Ker = hands.   Tul = palms.  Bul(i) = sacrifice life.   Paaravuriya = measure; beyond limits.       4.  
NOTE:  Koonj is a white bird which visits India from Russia in the winter months and returns in the summer.  It flies for several hundred km, leaving her young ones behind, with no one to feed them.  It is believed that when she picks food, she keeps her young ones in mind, and that is how they are fed by nature.  
MEANING:  Why should you worry about your food when Waheguru has that in His mind as His innate responsibility, oh man?  He has created life even in the rocks (where there is no exit or entrance), and has supplied food even before life began there.        1. 
(The more important thing for the man is to get detached from Maya, and unite with 
Waheguru).  Waheguru salvages the individual who attends the holy congregation of Satguru (and acts on His Advice).  There, the devotee attains the spiritual level above Maya by the grace of the Guru, and his spiritually dead mind comes to life.       Pause. Why should you be afraid of anything (worry about food, etc.) when Waheguru provides food (and other necessities of life to everybody?  You commit many immoral acts to earn wealth for your relatives), but nobody will be able to protect you from punishment for your immoral deeds in Waheguru’s Court), be it your mother, father, daughter, son, or wife.          2.          Look, the koonj travels several hundred km and leaves her young ones behind who have nobody to feed them.  But when the koonj picks food, she keeps her young ones in mind.  (This is a wonder of Waheguru that the food that the koonj picks, is fed to the young ones)!           3.   
All the treasures and the 18 supernatural powers lie in the palms of Waheguru (which He can gift to anyone).  I am a slave of Waheguru, and am always ready to sacrifice my life for Him. His wonders can never be known, oh Nanak.   4.5.
