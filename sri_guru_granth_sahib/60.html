(ਅੰਗ 59)॥            ਸਾਹਿਬੁ ਅਤੁਲੁ ਨ ਤੋਲੀਐ ਕਥਨਿ ਨ ਪਾਇਆ ਜਾਇ॥ 5॥ ਵਾਪਾਰੀ ਵਣਜਾਰਿਆ ਆਏ ਵਜਹੁ ਲਿਖਾਇ॥ ਕਾਰ ਕਮਾਵਹਿ ਸਚ ਕੀ ਲਾਹਾ ਮਿਲੈ ਰਜਾਇ॥ ਪੂੰਜੀ ਸਾਚੀ ਗੁਰੁ ਮਿਲੈ ਨਾ ਤਿਸੁ ਤਿਲੁ ਨ ਤਮਾਇ॥ 6॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਤੋਲਿ ਤੁੋਲਾਇਸੀ ਸਚੁ ਤਰਾਜੀ ਤੋਲੁ॥ ਆਸਾ ਮਨਸਾ ਮੋਹਣੀ ਗੁਰਿ ਠਾਕੀ ਸਚੁ ਬੋਲੁ॥ ਆਪਿ ਤੁਲਾਏ ਤੋਲਸੀ ਪੂਰੇ ਪੂਰਾ ਤੋਲੁ॥ 7॥ ਕਥਨੈ ਕਹਣਿ ਨ ਛੁਟੀਐ ਨਾ ਪੜਿ ਪੁਸਤਕ ਭਾਰ॥ ਕਾਇਆ ਸੋਚ ਨ ਪਾਈਐ ਬਿਨੁ ਹਰਿ ਭਗਤਿ ਪਿਆਰ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮੁ ਨ ਵੀਸਰੈ ਮੇਲੇ ਗੁਰੁ ਕਰਤਾਰ॥   8॥9॥

Sach(i) maanay Pir sayjurri goohrra hayt(u) piyaar(u). Gurmukh(i) jaan(i) sinjaaniyaiy Gur(i) mayli goon chaar(u). 2. Sach(i) milhou vur kaamni Pir(i) mohi rung(u) laayay. Ikut(u) taagaiy rul(i) milaiy, gul(i) motiun ka haar(u). Sunt subha sookh(u) oopjaiy Gurmukh(i) Naam adhaar(u). 4. Khin meh(i) oopjaiy, khin(i) khupaiy, khin(u) aavaiy, khin(u) jaayay. Shabad(u) puchhaniy ruv(i) ruhaiy na tis(u) kaal(u) suntaayay.

Word Meaning:  Dhun = woman (human bride).     Sheegariyaiy = make up; adornment.     Jobun(u) = youth.    Baad(i) = worthless.   Khooaar(u) = suffer without achieving anything.      Maanay = enjoy.     Sayjurri = sayj = bed.   Ghuno = too much.    Dohaagni = devoid of husband’s love.    Bhutaar(u) = husband.        1.
Jup-hou = recite.   Rung(u) = love; enjoyment.      Pause.    Vur(u) = husband.    Goohrra = dark; deep fast (colour).   Hayt(u) = love.   Jaan(i) = recognizing; connecting (with God).  Sinjaaniyaiy = develop an acquaintance (make a connection).   Chaar(u) = beautiful; noble (qualities).        2.       Kaamni = woman.     Mohi = captivated; enchanted.    Vigsiya = blooms.    3.       Munooa = mun = mind.   Raavaiy = enjoys.    Ikut(u) = same.   Taagaiy = thread; bond.   Rul(i) Milaiy = mix together.    Oopjaiy = is produced       4.
Khin = a moment.     Khupaiy = disappear; die.    Kaal(u) = (fear of) death.     Suntayaiy = causes trouble; hurts.    Sahib(u) = Lord.     Atool(u) = which cannot be weighed (measured).     Kuthan(i) = by describing.       5.       Vaapaari = Vunjaariya = traders; dealers.  Vujhou = income (fortune).      Kaar = business (work for living).    Laaha = profit; benefit.   Rujaayay = (according to) the Will.     Poonji = capital.    Til(u) = sesame seed.    Tmaayay = desire; greed.     6.         Tol(i) = amount; weight.    Tolayaysi = will enable you to have the needed amount.     Traaji = weighing scale.   Aasa = wishes; hopes.    Munsa = desires of the mind.   Mohni = captivating.    Tthaaki = stopped; controlled.     7.
Chhootiyaiy = freedom from the bonds of Maya.    Bhaar = huge amount.  Kaaya = body.  Soch = cleanliness.    Kartar = the Creator (Waheguru).    8.
MEANING:  If you want comfort (peace of mind), recite Waheguru’s Naam, oh my mind.  However, love for Waheguru cannot be fostered in the mind without the shelter of the Guru. One can connect with Waheguru only by following the Guru’s Shabad, for true enjoyment follows.         Pause.        A woman, who adorns herself with makeup, but has no union with her husband, fails to enjoy the comfort in the bed of her heart with her husband, and all her makeup goes to waste.  Just like that woman, a human bride who wears all the religious symbols without connecting with God, has her life going by worthlessly, and suffers a lot.  A wife, who has been disowned by her husband, suffers badly as she has no husband to enjoy on the bed of her heart.          1.           Serving the Guru enables the human bride to have the adornment of a high spiritual state above the attractions of Maya so as to get connected with God, and to enjoy the comfort of union with Him.  A human bride enjoys the bed (union) with Waheguru when she is connected with Him because of her full affection and devotion for Him. In Guru’s shelter, the human bride acquires noble qualities, as a result of which, she comes to recognize Waheguru (get connected with Him).        2.  
Captivated by Waheguru, when a human bride is connected with Him, she enjoys the bliss of His love.  Because of Waheguru’s light in her heart, her body and mind become pure and priceless, and nobody can evaluate her (can describe her) spiritual level.  The beloved bride has her Husband (Waheguru) in her mind all the time, and her heart blooms because of the Naam in her mind.        3.         If the mind of a human bride is dead towards Maya (has killed all her worldly desires), the Husband (Waheguru) enjoys such a bride (loves her), and they are close to each other like the pearls in a necklace that stick together on the same thread. For a devotee of the Guru, Waheguru’s Naam is his support, and he enjoys comfort in the company of the holy men (other devotees).          4.          Without the Guru’s shelter, a Maya-engrossed individual experiences joy at a birth in the family and grief at a death in the family; the same goes for a gain or a loss in his wealth. At one moment, his heart is buoyed, and the next, it sinks because of changes in his financial situation. On the other hand, an individual who is connected with the Guru’s Shabad is always content and feels blissful.  The fear of death never bothers him.  However, a devotee never claims that he has found the limits of the limitless Waheguru; he only realizes Him, which cannot otherwise be realized simply by claims.      5.        All human beings are traders here in this world (who trade in good or bad values), and come here with pre-determined qualities or traits (abilities).  Those who deal in Waheguru’s Naam earn the benefit of leading their life as per the Will of God.  However, only those who earn the capital (wealth) of Waheguru’s Naam are connected with the Guru, who has absolutely no greed for His fame, or any other desire for personal gain of any kind.      6.
When humans are weighed (evaluated or tested) for their success in Waheguru’s Court for their spiritual levels, the scale and the standard weight are only the Truth (Waheguru’s Naam). Those who have been enlightened by the Guru meet the requirements because the Guru has enabled them to control the captivating lure of hopes and wishes by making them recite Waheguru’s Naam. Waheguru’s requirements for human success never change, and He Himself enables individuals to meet those requirements.    7.
If someone believes that he can break the bonds of Maya simply by reading books or making claims, he is seriously mistaken because the mind’s purity (which is essential to break Maya’s bonds) cannot be achieved without worshiping Waheguru with love.  Only those get connected with the Guru and Waheguru, who never forget Waheguru’s Naam, oh Nanak.  8.9.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 1॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਪੂਰਾ ਜੇ ਮਿਲੈ ਪਾਈਐ ਰਤਨੁ ਬੀਚਾਰੁ॥ ਮਨੁ ਦੀਜੈ ਗੁਰ ਆਪਣੇ ਪਾਈਐ ਸਰਬ ਪਿਆਰੁ॥ ਮੁਕਤਿ ਪਦਾਰਥੁ ਪਾਈਐ ਅਵਗਣ ਮੇਟਣਹਾਰੁ॥ 1॥ ਭਾਈ ਰੇ ਗੁਰ ਬਿਨੁ ਗਿਆਨੁ ਨ ਹੋਇ॥ ਪੂਛਹੁ ਬ੍ਰਹਮੇ ਨਾਰਦੈ ਬੇਦ ਬਿਆਸੈ ਕੋਇ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਗਿਆਨੁ ਧਿਆਨੁ ਧੁਨਿ ਜਾਣੀਐ ਅਕਥੁ ਕਹਾਵੈ ਸੋਇ॥ ਸਫਲਿਓ ਬਿਰਖੁ ਹਰੀਆਵਲਾ ਛਾਵ ਘਣੇਰੀ ਹੋਇ॥ ਲਾਲ ਜਵੇਹਰ ਮਾਣਕੀ ਗੁਰ ਭੰਡਾਰੈ ਸੋਇ॥ 2॥ ਗੁਰ ਭੰਡਾਰੈ ਪਾਈਐ ਨਿਰਮਲ ਨਾਮ ਪਿਆਰੁ॥ ਸਾਚੋ ਵਖਰੁ ਸੰਚੀਐ ਪੂਰੈ ਕਰਮਿ ਅਪਾਰੁ॥ ਸੁਖਦਾਤਾ ਦੁਖ ਮੇਟਣੋ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਅਸੁਰ ਸੰਘਾਰੁ॥ 3॥ ਭਵਜਲੁ ਬਿਖਮੁ ਡਰਾਵਣੋ ਨਾ ਕੰਧੀ ਨਾ ਪਾਰੁ॥ ਨਾ ਬੇੜੀ ਨਾ ਤੁਲਹੜਾ ਨਾ ਤਿਸੁ ਵੰਝੁ ਮਲਾਰੁ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਭੈ ਕਾ ਬੋਹਿਥਾ ਨਦਰੀ ਪਾਰਿ ਉਤਾਰੁ॥ 4॥ ਇਕੁ ਤਿਲੁ ਪਿਆਰਾ ਵਿਸਰੈ ਦੁਖੁ ਲਾਗੈ ਸੁਖੁ ਜਾਇ॥ ਜਿਹਵਾ ਜਲਉ ਜਲਾਵਣੀ ਨਾਮੁ ਨ ਜਪੈ ਰਸਾਇ॥ ਘਟੁ ਬਿਨਸੈ ਦੁਖੁ ਅਗਲੋ ਜਮੁ ਪਕੜੈ ਪਛੁਤਾਇ॥ 5॥ ਮੇਰੀ ਮੇਰੀ ਕਰਿ ਗਏ ਤਨੁ ਧਨੁ ਕਲਤੁ ਨ ਸਾਥਿ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਨਾਵੈ ਧਨੁ ਬਾਦਿ ਹੈ ਭੂਲੋ ਮਾਰਗਿ ਆਥਿ॥ ਸਾਚਉ ਸਾਹਿਬੁ ਸੇਵੀਐ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਅਕਥੋ ਕਾਥਿ॥ 6॥ ਆਵੈ ਜਾਇ ਭਵਾਈਐ ਪਇਐ ਕਿਰਤਿ ਕਮਾਇ॥ ਪੂਰਬਿ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਕਿਉ ਮੇਟੀਐ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਲੇਖੁ ਰਜਾਇ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਨ ਛੁਟੀਐ ਗੁਰਮਤਿ ਮਿਲੈ ਮਿਲਾਇ॥ 7॥ ਤਿਸੁ ਬਿਨੁ ਮੇਰਾ ਕੋ ਨਹੀ ਜਿਸ ਕਾ ਜੀਉ ਪਰਾਨੁ॥ ਹਉਮੈ ਮਮਤਾ ਜਲਿ ਬਲਉ ਲੋਭੁ ਜਲਉ ਅਭਿਮਾਨੁ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਸਬਦੁ ਵੀਚਾਰੀਐ ਪਾਈਐ ਗੁਣੀ ਨਿਧਾਨੁ॥ 8॥10॥

Word Meaning:  Poora = perfect (“one” with God).    Rattan = (valuable like) precious stone (Waheguru’s Naam).  Mookt(i) = freedom from evil desires.   Pdaruth = valuable matter (Waheguru’s Naam).     Uvgun = bad qualities.   Maytunhaar(u) = destroyer.  1.
Naardaiy = from Narad (was a son of Brahma who was born out of Brahma’s thigh.  He was a musician and a messenger. He loved to create problems between gods and demons).   Biyasaiy = from Biyas. (He was a sage who is supposed to have reorganized the Vedas which are attributed to Brahma as their author).      Pause.     Ukuth(u) = indescribable (Waheguru).   Giyaan(u) = (to acquire) Divine Knowledge (connecting with God).   Dhiyan(u) = to focus thoughts (on Waheguru).   Dhoon(i) = sound; concentration of mind.
to meditate.  Sufliyo = fulfiller of your wishes.    Laal, Juvayhur. Maanki = all precious jewels or stones (represent Waheguru’s Naam).  Bhundaaraiy = (in) treasures.     2.
Saacho Vukkhur(u) = true merchandise (Naam).   Sunchiyaiy = accumulate.   Kerm(i) Apaar(u) = by kindness of the incomprehensible (Waheguru).   Asoor = demons.   Sunghaar(u) = killer; destroyer.     3.         Bhuvjul(u) = sea of life (Maya).   Bikhum(u) = difficult (to cross).   Kundhi = shore.  Toolhurra = raft; a crude float.  Vunjh(u) = oar.   Mulaar(u) = boatman.  Bohitha = ship.   Nedri = kind sight.     4.       Til(u) = sesame (a moment).   Visraiy = forget; drop from mind.   Jihva = tongue.  Rusayaiy = tastefully.   Ghut(u) = heart.   Binsaiy = gets destroyed; is dead.       5.        Klut(u) = wife.   Dhun(u) = woman (human bride).   Baad(i) = worthless.   Aath(i) = Maya.   Kaath(i) = recite; describe.        6.       Bhuvaayiyaiy = put through the cycle of birth and death.   Puyya Kirt(i) = mental inclination based on one’s deeds of previous lives.    Poorub(i) Likhiya = pre-destined.    Rujaayay = Waheguru’s Will (Huqam).    Gurmut(i) = guidance of the Guru.       7.      Jeo = life.   Praan(u) = breathing.   Haumein = selfidhnes and ego.   Mumta = motherly affection.    Abhimaan(u) = ego.    Gooni Nidhaan(u) = treasure of noble qualities (Waheguru).    8. 
MEANING:  You cannot acquire Divine Knowledge without the Guru, oh my brothers; (you will get the same response even if) you ask the wise people like Brahma, Nard, the Vedas, or (sage) Biyas.      Pause.          If you are lucky enough to connect with the Guru, only then will you learn to discuss the Divine qualities of Waheguru, which are priceless, like jewels.  If you offer your mind to the Guru (to acquire His wisdom), you can realize Waheguru, who loves everybody. Waheguru is the destroyer of all evil qualities and is the source of His Naam, which frees you from the bonds of Maya.           1.        The Guru enables you to acquire Divine Knowledge (connect with the source – Waheguru), to focus your mind, and to meditate on Him. The Guru is like an evergreen fruit tree which provides tasty fruit (fulfils all your wishes) and provides good shade (shelter) too.  All the noble qualities, which are priceless like some jewels and pearls, are in the treasure of the Guru.          2.        The love for the holy Naam (of Waheguru) can be acquired only from the treasure of the Guru. This everlasting merchandise (Waheguru’s Naam) can be had only by the kindness of the incomprehensible (Waheguru).  Satguru is the source of all comfort, the eliminator of all discomforts, and the destroyer of all evil.          3.         The sea of life (of Maya), which has no shore either on this side (this world), or on the far side (the next world), is very frightening and extremely difficult (to cross).  (Other than the Guru), There is no boat, not even a crude raft, no oar, and no boatman (to take across the sea).  Satguru is the ship which kills all your fears and ferries you across the sea, just by His kind sight.          4.          If I drop my dear Waheguru from my mind even for a moment, all my comforts disappear, and discomforts entrap me.  Such a tongue, which does not recite Waheguru’s Naam tastefully, deserves to be burnt, and must be burnt.  When the heart fails (the body takes its last breath), the Angel of Death takes the individual into his possession and causes him a great deal of misery; at that time, the individual feels remorse.     5.             The man lays claim to all his belongings, but when he dies, nobody - including his own body, his wealth, or even his wife – accompanies him to help his soul.  The man is misguided (by his desires) and pursues the path for Maya without realizing that worldly wealth is worthless without Waheguru’s Naam. The man should serve Waheguru by taking Guru’s shelter, who makes him recite the Naam of the indescribable God.         6.
However, the man is also helpless because he pursues the path of life for which he is predestined. His prewritten destiny cannot be erased, which has been written according to Waheguru’s Will. At the same time, the man cannot cut himself loose from the bonds of Maya without Waheguru’s Naam, which can be worshipped only by acting on the Guru’s advice.      7.            There is nobody else who is mine other than Waheguru, who is the Master of my life and breaths. I wish my selfishness, my attachment with the family, my greed, and my ego were burnt down, because to attain union with the source of all the Divine qualities (Waheguru), all the evil qualities must be gotten rid of by pondering (and acting) on Guru’s Shabad, oh Nanak.    8.10.
