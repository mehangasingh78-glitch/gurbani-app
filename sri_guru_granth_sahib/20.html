(ਅੰਗ 19)॥  ਦਰਿ ਘਰਿ ਢੋਈ ਨ ਲਹੈ ਦਰਗਹ ਝੂਠੁ ਖੁਆਰੁ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਆਪਿ ਸੁਜਾਣੁ ਨ ਭੁਲਈ ਸਚਾ ਵਡ ਕਿਰਸਾਣੁ॥ ਪਹਿਲਾ ਧਰਤੀ ਸਾਧਿ ਕੈ ਸਚੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਦੇ ਦਾਣੁ॥ ਨਉ ਨਿਧਿ ਉਪਜੈ ਨਾਮੁ ਏਕੁ ਕਰਮਿ ਪਵੈ ਨੀਸਾਣੁ॥ 2॥ ਗੁਰ ਕਉ ਜਾਣਿ ਨ ਜਾਣਈ ਕਿਆ ਤਿਸੁ ਚਜੁ ਅਚਾਰੁ॥ ਅੰਧੁਲੈ ਨਾਮੁ ਵਿਸਾਰਿਆ ਮਨਮੁਖਿ ਅੰਧੁ ਗੁਬਾਰੁ॥ ਆਵਣੁ ਜਾਣੁ ਨ ਚੁਕਈ ਮਰਿ ਜਨਮੈ ਹੋਇ ਖੁਆਰੁ॥ 3॥ ਚੰਦਨੁ ਮੋਲਿ ਅਣਾਇਆ ਕੁੰਗੂ ਮਾਗ ਸੰਧੂਰੁ॥ ਚੋਆ ਚੰਦਨੁ ਬਹੁ ਘਣਾ ਪਾਨਾ ਨਾਲਿ ਕਪੂਰੁ॥ ਜੇ ਧਨ ਕੰਤ ਨ ਭਾਵਈ ਤ ਸਭਿ ਅਡੰਬਰ ਕੂੜੁ॥ 4॥ ਸਭਿ ਰਸ ਭੋਗਣ ਬਾਦਿ ਹਹਿ ਸਭਿ ਸੀਗਾਰ ਵਿਕਾਰ॥ ਜਬ ਲਗੁ ਸਬਦਿ ਨ ਭੇਦੀਐ ਕਿਉ ਸੋਹੈ ਗੁਰ ਦੁਆਰਿ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਧੰਨੁ ਸੁਹਾਗਣੀ ਜਿਨ ਸਹ ਨਾਲਿ ਪਿਆਰੁ॥ 5॥13॥

Siri Raag(u) Mehla 1: Dhrig jeevun(u) dohaagni, mootthi doojaiy bhaayay. Kullur kayri kundh jiyoun ehinis(i) kir(i) dheh paayay. Bin(u) Shabadaiy sookh(u) na theeyaiy, Pir bin(u) dookh(u) na jaayay. 1. Moondhay, Pir bin(u) kiyya sheengaar(u).  
(PAGE 19):              Dur(i), ghur(i) dhoyee na luhhaiy, Durgeh jhootth(u) khooaar(u). Ruhaou.  Aap(i) Soojaan(u), na bhoollyee, Sacha vud kirsan. Pehlaan dherti saadh(i) kaiy, Sach(u) Naam(u) day daan(u). Nuou nidh(i) oopjaiy Naam(u) Aik(u), kerm(i) puvvaiy neeshaan(u).  2. Gur kuou jaan(i) na jaanyee, kiyya tis(u) chujj(u) achaar(u)?  Undhulaiy Naam(u) visaariya, munmukh(i) undh(u) goobaar(u).  Aavun jaan(u) na chookyee, mur(i) junmaiy hoyay khooaar(u).  3. Chundun(u) moal anaaya, koongu maang sundhoor(u). Choa chundun(u) buhou ghunaa, paana naal(i) kupoor(u).  Jay dhun Kunt na bhaavyee, ta subh adummbur koorr(u). 4. Subh rus bhogun baad(i) haiy, subh sheengar vikaar. Jub lug Shabad(i) na bhaydiyaiy, kiyoun sohaiy Gur Dooaar(i). Nanak, dhunn suhaagni, jinh Sheh naal(i) piyaar(u).   5.13.
Word Meaning: Dhrig =reprehensible.    Dohaagni = ditched by husband; disconnected with Waheguru.      Kullur Kayri = of saltpetre.   Kundh = wall.   Ehinis = day and night.  Dheh = collapse.   Theevaiy = happens.        1.         Moondhay = oh, young woman.   Sheengar = adornments.    Dhoyee = shelter.   Khooaar(u) = humiliation.          Pause.           Soojaan(u) = wise; know-all.   Kirsan = farmer.    Dherti = field (of heart).   Saadh(i) = make suitable.   Daan(u) = donation; seeding.    Nuou Nidh(i) = the nine treasures.  Neeshaan(u) = stamp of approval.          2.         Jaan(i) = recognize.   Chujj(u) Achaar(u) = manners; character.      Undhulay = blind.     Goobaar(u) = darkness.    Chookyee = ends.          3.
Chundun = sandalwood.    Anaaya = brought.   Maang = central part.    Sundhoor(u) = vermillion.   Choa = extract of flowers.    Paan = betel-leaf wrap.   Kupoor = camphor.  Adummbur(u) = decorations for show off.   Koorr(u) = false; worthless.       4.
Rus = tastes.   Bhogun = to enjoy.   Baad(i) = useless = Vikaar.    Bhaydiyaiy = pierced. 
MEANING: The living of the woman (human) who is robbed of her good qualities due to her love for worldly attractions, and as a result of which she is disconnected from God, is reprehensible. She is like a wall of saltpetre, which can crumble at any time, day or night.  There is no comfort without Guru’s Shabad, and one’s suffering does not go away without union with Waheguru-Husband.       1.       What is the use of any adornment without the (Waheguru) husband, oh young woman?     You cannot find shelter at Waheguru Door or at His court, where you will suffer humiliation due to your falseness.         Pause.        Waheguru is omniscient and is a great farmer who never forgets His responsibilities.  First, He prepares the field of His devotee’s heart with noble qualities to make it suitable (for His Naam to sprout), and then He seeds His Naam in there.  When Waheguru’s Naam, which is like the nine mythical treasures (source of all comforts), sprouts in the devotee’s heart, he receives Waheguru’s stamp of approval with His benevolence.           2.          What kind of (pathetic) manners does a woman have who, knowing well that there is no comfort without Waheguru’s Naam, still does not make any connection with Him?  Engrossed in Maya, the man lives in complete darkness in the realm of spirituality, and the blind man forgets Waheguru.  His cycle of births and deaths never gets broken, and he suffers humiliation due to his rebirths.          3.        
Such a man is like the bride who buys sandalwood extract, adorns herself immensely with the extract of sandalwood and flowers, with camphor and saffron, and puts vermilion in the central part of her hair, and chews wraps of betel leaves. But all the big show of her adornments goes to waste if her husband does not like her.        4.       All one’s enjoyments of different tastes in life, and all his adornments are worthless unless his mind is pierced with Guru’s Shabad to look dignified in Waheguru’s Court (to be honoured there).   Those women are highly fortunate who love their Waheguru-Husband, oh Nanak.    5.13.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 1॥ ਸੁੰਞੀ ਦੇਹ ਡਰਾਵਣੀ ਜਾ ਜੀਉ ਵਿਚਹੁ ਜਾਇ॥ ਭਾਹਿ ਬਲੰਦੀ ਵਿਝਵੀ ਧੂਉ ਨ ਨਿਕਸਿਓ ਕਾਇ॥ ਪੰਚੇ ਰੁੰਨੇ ਦੁਖਿ ਭਰੇ ਬਿਨਸੇ ਦੂਜੈ ਭਾਇ॥ 1॥ ਮੂੜੇ ਰਾਮੁ ਜਪਹੁ ਗੁਣ ਸਾਰਿ॥ ਹਉਮੈ ਮਮਤਾ ਮੋਹਣੀ ਸਭ ਮੁਠੀ ਅਹੰਕਾਰਿ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਜਿਨੀ ਨਾਮੁ ਵਿਸਾਰਿਆ ਦੂਜੀ ਕਾਰੈ ਲਗਿ॥ ਦੁਬਿਧਾ ਲਾਗੇ ਪਚਿ ਮੁਏ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਤ੍ਰਿਸਨਾ ਅਗਿ॥ ਗੁਰਿ ਰਾਖੇ ਸੇ ਉਬਰੇ ਹੋਰਿ ਮੁਠੀ ਧੰਧੈ ਠਗਿ॥ 2॥ ਮੁਈ ਪਰੀਤਿ ਪਿਆਰੁ ਗਇਆ ਮੁਆ ਵੈਰੁ ਵਿਰੋਧੁ॥ ਧੰਧਾ ਥਕਾ ਹਉ ਮੁਈ ਮਮਤਾ ਮਾਇਆ ਕ੍ਰੋਧੁ॥ ਕਰਮਿ ਮਿਲੈ ਸਚੁ ਪਾਈਐ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਸਦਾ ਨਿਰੋਧੁ॥ 3॥ ਸਚੀ ਕਾਰੈ ਸਚੁ ਮਿਲੈ ਗੁਰਮਤਿ ਪਲੈ ਪਾਇ॥ ਸੋ ਨਰ ਜੰਮੈ ਨ ਮਰੈ ਨ ਆਵੈ ਨ ਜਾਇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਦਰਿ ਪਰਧਾਨੁ ਸੋ ਦਰਗਹਿ ਪੈਧਾ ਜਾਇ॥ 4॥14॥
Soonjeen dayh duraavni, ja jeeou vichoun jaayay. Bhaah(i) bulundi vijhvi, dhooun na niksiya kaayay. Punchay roonnay dookh(i) bhuray, binsay doojaiy bhaayay. 1. Moorray, Ram(u) jupou goon saar(i). Haumain, mumta, mohni, subh mootthi uhunkaar(i). Ruhaou. Jinhi Naam(u) visaariya, dooji kaaraiy lug(i). Dooidha laagay puch(i) mooyay, untar(i) trishna ugg. Gur(i) raakhay say oobray, hoar mootthi dhundaiy tthug(i). 2. Mooyi preet(i), piyaar(u) guyya, mooaa vaiyr virodh(u). Dhundha thukka, huoun mooyi, mumta, Maya, kroadh. Kerm(i) milaiy Sach(u) paayiyaiy, Gurmukh(i) sudaa niroadh.  3. Sachi kaaraiy, Sach(u) milaiy, Gurmut(i) pullaiy paayay.  So, nur jummaiy, na muraiy, na aavaiy, na   jaayay. Nanak Dur(i) perdhaan(u) so, Durgeh(i) paiyndha jaayay.  4.14.
Word Meaning:  Soonjeen = empty.    Duraavni = scary; frightening.  Jeeou = soul.   Bhaayay Bulundi = burning fire (of life).   Vijhvi = put out.   Dhooun = smoke.   Punchay = the five (demons).       Roonnay = cried.   Binsay = destroyed.     Doojaiy Bhaayay = with love for sensual pleasures.            1.          Moorray = oh, foolish.   Saar(i) = by remembering.   Mohni = captivating.    Mootthi = robbed.         Pause.         Doobidha = in indecisiveness.   Puch(i) = in suffering.   Trishna = desires.    Oobray = salvaged.    Dhundaiy Tthug(i) = deceitful (short-lived) worldly affairs.         2.     
Viroadh = opposition.    Kerm(i) = with benevolence; fortune.    Nirodh(u) = unattached.  3.  Kaaraiy = deeds.    Pullaiy = acquire.    Nur = man.   Paiyndha = robe of honour.      4.  
MEANING:  The dead body, after the soul leaves it, becomes frightening to everybody.  The fire of life, which was alive in the body, is put out, and no smoke (sign of life) is visible.  All five demons (lust, greed, rage, etc.), which enjoyed sensual pleasures, do cry in suffering as their enjoyments are destroyed.         1.     You are being robbed of your noble qualities by the captivating Maya of selfishness, ego, worldly attractions, and your pride, oh, the foolish man. So, (to protect your noble qualities), recite Waheguru’s Naam remembering His virtues.           Pause.            Those who forget Waheguru because of the fire of desires in their mind, and work hard to acquire worldly comforts, suffer in that fire.  Only those who are protected by Satguru attain salvation; the others are robbed of their good qualities through their dealings in worldly affairs.       2.         (At the time of death) All one’s love and affection for the perishables, and his enmity and opposition against others, come to an end.  His worldly affairs exhaust (end), and his attachment to Maya and the resulting rage become dead. It is with the benevolence of Waheguru that one comes to know the reality and gets fully detached from the world in the shelter of the Guru.          3.         One acquires Waheguru’s Naam by following the path of truthfulness (high morality with Waheguru’s Naam at heart) with the advice of the Guru.  Such a devotee neither dies nor takes rebirths (becomes immortal by merging into Waheguru).  Such individuals become (spiritual) leaders and are honoured in Waheguru’s Court, oh Nanak.   4.14.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 1॥ ਤਨੁ ਜਲਿ ਬਲਿ ਮਾਟੀ ਭਇਆ ਮਨੁ ਮਾਇਆ ਮੋਹਿ ਮਨੂਰੁ॥ ਅਉਗਣ ਫਿਰਿ ਲਾਗੁ ਭਏ ਕੂਰਿ ਵਜਾਵੈ ਤੂਰੁ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਸਬਦੈ ਭਰਮਾਈਐ ਦੁਬਿਧਾ ਡੋਬੇ ਪੂਰੁ॥ 1॥ਮਨ ਰੇ ਸਬਦਿ ਤਰਹੁ ਚਿਤੁ ਲਾਇ॥ ਜਿਨਿ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਨ ਬੂਝਿਆ ਮਰਿ ਜਨਮੈ ਆਵੈ ਜਾਇ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਤਨੁ ਸੂਚਾ ਸੋ ਆਖੀਐ ਜਿਸੁ ਮਹਿ ਸਾਚਾ ਨਾਉ॥ ਭੈ ਸਚਿ ਰਾਤੀ ਦੇਹੁਰੀ ਜਿਹਵਾ ਸਚੁ ਸੁਆਉ॥ ਸਚੀ ਨਦਰਿ ਨਿਹਾਲੀਐ ਬਹੁੜਿ ਨ ਪਾਵੈ ਤਾਉ॥ 2॥ ਸਾਚੇ ਤੇ ਪਵਨਾ ਭਇਆ ਪਵਨੈ ਤੇ ਜਲੁ ਹੋਇ॥ ਜਲ ਤੇ ਤ੍ਰਿਭਵਣੁ ਸਾਜਿਆ ਘਟਿ ਘਟਿ ਜੋਤਿ ਸਮੋਇ॥ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਮੈਲਾ ਨ ਥੀਐ ਸਬਦਿ ਰਤੇ ਪਤਿ ਹੋਇ॥ 3॥ ਇਹੁ ਮਨੁ ਸਾਚਿ ਸੰਤੋਖਿਆ ਨਦਰਿ ਕਰੇ ਤਿਸੁ ਮਾਹਿ॥
