(ਅੰਗ 37)॥        ਬਿਨੁ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਕਿਨੈ ਨ ਪਾਇਓ ਕਰਿ ਵੇਖਹੁ ਮਨਿ ਵੀਚਾਰਿ॥ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਮੈਲੁ ਨ ਉਤਰੈ ਜਿਚਰੁ ਗੁਰ ਸਬਦਿ ਨ ਕਰੇ ਪਿਆਰੁ॥ 1॥ ਮਨ ਮੇਰੇ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਭਾਣੈ ਚਲੁ॥ ਨਿਜ ਘਰਿ ਵਸਹਿ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਪੀਵਹਿ ਤਾ ਸੁਖ ਲਹਹਿ ਮਹਲੁ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਅਉਗੁਣਵੰਤੀ ਗੁਣੁ ਕੋ ਨਹੀ ਬਹਿਣ ਨ ਮਿਲੈ ਹਦੂਰਿ॥ ਮਨਮੁਖਿ ਸਬਦੁ ਨ ਜਾਣਈ ਅਵਗੁਣਿ ਸੋ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਦੂਰਿ॥ ਜਿਨੀ ਸਚੁ ਪਛਾਣਿਆ ਸਚਿ ਰਤੇ ਭਰਪੂਰਿ॥ ਗੁਰ ਸਬਦੀ ਮਨੁ ਬੇਧਿਆ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਮਿਲਿਆ ਆਪਿ ਹਦੂਰਿ॥ 2॥ ਆਪੇ ਰੰਗਣਿ ਰੰਗਿਓਨੁ ਸਬਦੇ ਲਇਓਨੁ ਮਿਲਾਇ॥ ਸਚਾ ਰੰਗੁ ਨਾ ਉਤਰੈ ਜੋ ਸਚਿ ਰਤੇ ਲਿਵ ਲਾਇ॥ ਚਾਰੇ ਕੁੰਡਾ ਭਵਿ ਥਕੇ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਬੂਝ ਨ ਪਾਇ॥ ਜਿਸੁ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਮੇਲੇ ਸੋ ਮਿਲੈ ਸਚੈ ਸਬਦਿ ਸਮਾਇ॥ 3॥ ਮਿਤ੍ਰ ਘਨੇਰੇ ਕਰਿ ਥਕੀ ਮੇਰਾ ਦੁਖੁ ਕਾਟੈ ਕੋਇ॥ ਮਿਲਿ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਮ ਦੁਖੁ ਕਟਿਆ ਸਬਦਿ ਮਿਲਾਵਾ ਹੋਇ॥ ਸਚੁ ਖਟਣਾ ਸਚੁ ਰਾਸਿ ਹੈ ਸਚੇ ਸਚੀ ਸੋਇ॥ ਸਚਿ ਮਿਲੇ ਸੇ ਨ ਵਿਛੁੜਹਿ ਨਾਨਕ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਹੋਇ॥ 4॥26॥59॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 3:  Goonvunti Sach(u) paaya, trishna tuj(i) vikaar. Gur Shabadi mun(u) rungiya, rusna praym piyaar(i).
(PAGE 37):          Bin(u) Satgur kinaiy na paayiyo, kur(i) vaykhou mun(i) veechaar(i). Munmukh maiyl(u) na ootraiy, jichur(u) Gur Shabad(i) na kuray piyaar(u). 1. Mun mayray, Satgur kaiy bhaanaiy chul. Nij ghur(i) vuseh(i) Amrit peeveh(i), ta sookh luhay mehl(u). 1. Ruhaou. Augunvanti goon ko nuhee, behn na milaiy hudoor(i). Munmukh(i) Shabad(u) na jaanyee, uvgoon(i) so Prubh(u) door(i).  Jinhi Sach(u) puchhaaniya Such(i) ruttay bhurpoor(i). Gur Shabadi mun(u) baydhiya Prubh(u) miliya aap(i) hudoor(i). 2. Aapay rungun(i) rungiyoun, Shabaday luyyioun(u) milayay. Sacha rung(u) na ootraiy, jo Sach(i) ruttay liv laayay. Chaaray koondaan bhuv(i) thukkay munmukh boojh na paayay. Jis(u) Satgur(u) maylay so milaiy, Sachaiy Shabad(i) smaayay. 3. Mittar ghunayray kur(i) thukki, mayra dookh(u) kaataiy koyay. Mil(i) Preetum dookh(u) kuttiya, Shabad(i) milaava hoyay. Sach(u) khuttna Sach(u) raas(i) haiy. Sachay Sachee soyay. Such(i) milay say na vichhurreh(i), Nanak Gurmukh(i) hoyay.  4.26.59.
Word Meaning:  Goonvunti = having noble qualities.   Sach(u) = eternal truth (Waheguru).  Trishna = worldly desires.  Tuj(I) = by giving up (overcoming).   Vikaar = immoral thoughts and deeds.  Ootraiy =   goes away.     Jichur(u) = as long as; until.       1.       Bhaanaiy = as per the Will (Command).   Nij ghur(i) = in your own house (mind).  Vuseh(i) = resides.    Mehl(u) = woman (human being).        Pause.        Aougoonvunti = devoid of noble traits.   Hudoor(i) = near Waheguru; in His presence.  Puchhaaniya = recognized; made a connection.   Baydhiya = pierced; deeply affected.       2.        Rungun(i) = in (His) colour (love).   Liv Laayay = full concentration.   Koondaan = directions.    Bhuv(i) = by travelling.     Boojh = understand; enlightenment.        3.       Ghunayray = very many.   Khuttna = earn.   Raas(i) = capital.  Soyay = fame.     4. 
MEANING:  Individuals, possessing noble qualities, overcome their immoral thinking and deeds, and realize the source of all truth – Waheguru. By reciting Guru’s Shabads with love and respect, their minds are imbued with Guru’s Shabad.  If one thinks deeply, one will realize that no one ever realizes Waheguru without taking shelter of the Guru.  The filth (of immoral thinking) is not washed away from a person who follows his mind, until he ponders on the Guru’s Shabad.          1.          Lead your life as per the Will (Advice) of the Guru, oh my mind.  When you do that, you will drink Amrit (of Guru’s Shabad); your mind will quit wandering and will find peace.            Pause.           The individuals lacking noble qualities cannot enjoy union with Waheguru. The followers of their mind do not pay any attention to the Guru’s Shabad, and thus lack good qualities; as a result, Waheguru is far away from them.  Those who realize the Truth are imbued in Waheguru’s Naam and find Waheguru everywhere. Their mind is pierced (is deeply affected) by Guru’s Shabad, and Waheguru Himself connects them with Him.          2.          Waheguru Himself imbues their minds with His love and connects with them through the Guru’s Shabad.  The colour of (love for) Waheguru’s Naam is everlasting, and never wears off for those who are always connected with Him with full dedication. The follower of his mind runs in all four directions in pursuit of Maya; as a result, he never finds the Truth (never connects with the Guru or Waheguru).  Only those are connected with Waheguru who take the Guru’s shelter and stay focused on the Guru’s Shabad.        3.
I made many friends with the hope that someone would eradicate my pain (of separation from Waheguru), but my pain was gone only when I got connected with Waheguru through Guru’s Shabad. For the devotee, Waheguru’s Naam is his true capital and true wealth; he also earns an everlasting praise. Those, who are connected to Waheguru in Guru’s shelter, never suffer separation from Him, oh Nanak.       4.26.59

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ ਆਪੇ ਕਾਰਣੁ ਕਰਤਾ ਕਰੇ ਸ੍ਰਿਸਟਿ ਦੇਖੈ ਆਪਿ ਉਪਾਇ॥ ਸਭ ਏਕੋ ਇਕੁ ਵਰਤਦਾ ਅਲਖੁ ਨ ਲਖਿਆ ਜਾਇ॥ ਆਪੇ ਪ੍ਰਭੂ ਦਇਆਲੁ ਹੈ ਆਪੇ ਦੇਇ ਬੁਝਾਇ॥ ਗੁਰਮਤੀ ਸਦਾ ਮਨਿ ਵਸਿਆ ਸਚਿ ਰਹੈ ਲਿਵ ਲਾਇ॥ 1॥ ਮਨ ਮੇਰੇ ਗੁਰ ਕੀ ਮੰਨਿ ਲੈ ਰਜਾਇ॥ ਮਨੁ ਤਨੁ ਸੀਤਲ ਸਭੁ ਥੀਐ ਨਾਮੁ ਵਸੈ ਮਨਿ ਆਇ॥1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਜਿਨਿ ਕਰਿ ਕਾਰਣੁ ਧਾਰਿਆ ਸੋਈ ਸਾਰ ਕਰੇਇ॥ ਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਸਬਦਿ ਪਛਾਣੀਐ ਜਾ ਆਪੇ ਨਦਰਿ ਕਰੇਇ॥ ਸੇ ਜਨ ਸਬਦੇ ਸੋਹਣੇ ਤਿਤੁ ਸਚੈ ਦਰਬਾਰਿ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਸਚੈ ਸਬਦਿ ਰਤੇ ਆਪਿ ਮੇਲੇ ਕਰਤਾਰਿ॥ 2॥
ਗੁਰਮਤੀ ਸਚੁ ਸਾਲਾਹਣਾ ਜਿਸ ਦਾ ਅੰਤੁ ਨ ਪਾਰਾਵਾਰੁ॥ ਘਟਿ ਘਟਿ ਆਪੇ ਹੁਕਮਿ ਵਸੈ ਹੁਕਮੇ ਕਰੇ ਬੀਚਾਰੁ॥ ਗੁਰ ਸਬਦੀ ਸਾਲਾਹੀਐ ਹਉਮੈ ਵਿਚਹੁ ਖੋਇ॥ ਸਾਧਨ ਨਾਵੇ ਬਾਹਰੀ ਅਵਗੁਣਵੰਤੀ ਰੋਇ॥ 3॥ ਸਚੁ ਸਲਾਹੀ ਸਚਿ ਲਗਾ ਸਚੈ ਨਾਇ ਤ੍ਰਿਪਤਿ ਹੋਇ॥ ਗੁਣ ਵੀਚਾਰੀ ਗੁਣ ਸੰਗ੍ਰਹਾ ਅਵਗੁਣ ਕਢਾ ਧੋਇ॥ ਆਪੇ ਮੇਲਿ ਮਿਲਾਇਦਾ ਫਿਰਿ ਵੇਛੋੜਾ ਨਾ ਹੋਇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਗੁਰ ਸਾਲਾਹੀ ਆਪਣਾ ਜਿਦੂ ਪਾਈ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਸੋਇ॥ 4॥27॥60॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 3:  Aapay kaarun(u) Kerta kuray, srisht(i) daykhaiy Aap(i) oopaayay. Subh aiko Ikk(u) vertda, Alukh(u) na lukhiya jaayay. Aapay Prubhoo duyaal(u) haiy, Aaapay dayay boojhaayay. Gurmuti suda mun(i) vussiya, Such(i) ruhaiy liv laayay. 1. Mun mayray, Gur kee munn(i) laiy rujaayay. Mun(u) tun(u) seetul subh(u) theeyaiy, Naam(u) vusaiy mun(i) aayay. 1. Ruhaou.  Jin(i) kur(i) kaarun(u) dhaariya,  soyee saar kuray. Gur kaiy Shabad(i) puchhaaniyaiy ja aapay nudr(i) kuray. Say jun Shabday sohnay tit(u) Sachaiy Durbaar(i). Gurmukh(i) Sachaiy Shabad(i) ruttay, aap(i) maylay Kartar(i). 2.  Gurmuti Sach(u) salaahna, jis da unt(u) na paaravaar(u). Ghut(i) ghut(i) Aapay huqam(i) vusaiy huqmay kuray beechaar(u). Gur Shabadi salaahiyaiy, haumein vichoun khoyay. Sa dhun naavay baahri, uvgoonvunti royay. 3. Sach(u) slaaheen Such(i) luggaan, Sachaiy Naayay tript(i) hoyay. Goon veechaareen goon sungruhaan uvgoon kuddhaan dhoyay. Aapay mayl(i) milaayaynda, phir(i) vaychhorra na hoyay. Nanak Gur salaheen aapna jidoon paayeen Prubh soyay. 4.27.60.
Word Meaning:   Kaarun(u) = the source of all basic ingredients; make arrangements.   Kerta = the Creator (Waheguru).  Srisht(i) = world.   Daykhaiy = looks after.  Oopaayay = (after) creating.  Vertda = operates; controls.   Alukh(u) = formless; incomprehensible.    Lukhiya = understood; comprehended.  Duyaal(u) = kind.   Boojhaayay = enlightens.   Liv Laayay = full concentration.        1.        Rujaayay = Will; Command.  Seetal = calm; at peace.  Theeyaiy = become.         Pause.         Dhaariya = provided support; created.   Saar = take care.   Nudr(i) = (kind) sight.   Jun = devotees.    Sohnay = stand out; approved.   Ruttay = imbued.  Kartar = Creator.        2.           Gurmuti = (with the) advice of the Guru.  Salaahna = sing praise.    Paaravaar = without limits.   Ghut(i) = in the heart (mind).   Huqam(i) = (by His) Will.   Khoyay = eradicated; gets rid of.  Sadhun = that woman (human being).   Baahri = outside; without.    Uvgoonvunti = lacking noble qualities.   Royay = cries; laments.        3.          Tript(i) = satisfaction; contentment.    Sungruhaan = assemble; acquire.   Kuddhaan = pull out; kick out.    Dhoyay = by washing or cleansing.    Jidoon = from whom.         4.
MEANING:  Obey Guru’s Will (Teachings), oh my mind. As a result, Waheguru’s Naam will take up dwelling in you, and your body and mind will be at peace.         Pause. 
Waheguru, the source of all basic materials, Himself plans and creates the world, and looks after it.  He alone controls and provides for everybody, but the formless Waheguru is beyond comprehension.  Waheguru is kind and enlightens the world Himself. He takes dwelling (appears) in people’s hearts when they obey the Guru’s advice and stay focused on His Naam.       1.           Waheguru, which is the cause of support for the entire world, also looks after His creation. We realize His existence with His kindness, and by acting on the Guru’s Shabad.  Those who obey the Guru’s Shabad stand out (are recognized) in Waheguru’s Court. Such devotees are imbued with the Guru’s Shabad, and Waheguru Himself connects them with Him.            2.         Taking Guru’s shelter, one should sing Waheguru’s praise, which has no end to His limits.  With His own sweet Will, he resides in everybody’s heart and looks after the world.  Taking Guru’s shelter, if one sings praise of Waheguru, his selfishness and ego are eradicated. But those who lack noble qualities remain devoid of His Naam and suffer badly.        3.          By singing Waheguru’s praise, one gets connected with Him and attains contentment.  I pondered on His noble traits, accumulated (acquired) noble qualities for myself, and washed away the filth of my ignoble qualities. As a result, Waheguru Himself connected me with Him, and I never experienced separation from Him.  I sing praise of my Guru, who connected me with Waheguru, oh, Nanak.   4.27.60.
