(ਅੰਗ 113)॥    ਤੂ ਆਪੇ ਹੀ ਘੜਿ ਭੰਨਿ ਸਵਾਰਹਿ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮਿ ਸੁਹਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 8॥5॥6॥
Majh Mehla 3:  Undar(i) heera laal(u) bnaaya. Gur kaiy Shabad(i) perkh(i) perkhaaya. Jin Sach(u) pullaiy Sach(u) vukhaaneh(i), Sach(u) kusvutti laavniya. 1. Huoun vaari jeeo vaari, Gur kee Bani munn(i) vusaavniya. Unjun maah(i) Nirunjun paaya, joti Jot(i) milaavniya. 1. Ruhaou. Iss(u) kaaya undar(i) buhout(u) pusaara. Naam(u) Nirunjun ut(i) Agumm Apaara. Gurmukh(i) hovaiy soyee paayay, Aapay bukhsh(i) milaavniya. 2. Mayra Tthakur(u) Sach(u) drirryaayay. Gur persadi Sach(i) chit(u) laayay. Sacho Sach(u) vertaiy subhni thaayeen, Sachaiy Sach(i) smaavniya. 3.  Vaypervaah(u) Sach(u) mayra Piyara. Kilvikh uvgun kaatunhaara. Praym preet(i) suda dhiyaayiyaiy, bhaiy bhaayay bhugat(i) drirraavniya.  4.  Tayri bhugtee sachee, jay Sachay bhaavaiy. Aapay dayay na puchhotaavaiy. Subhna jeeaan ka aiko Daata. Shabaday maar(i) jeevaalniya. 5. Hur(i) Toodh(u) baajhoun mein koyee naahi. Hur(i) Toodh(u) sayveen taiy Toodh(u) salaaheen.  Aapay mayl(i) layho Prubh Sachay, pooraiy kerm(i) Tu paavniya. 6.  Mein hoar(u) na koyee Toodhaiy jayha. Tayri nudr(i) seejhus(i) dayha. Undin(u) saar(i) smaal(i) Hur(i) raakheh(i), Gurmukh(i) sehj(i) smaavniya. 7. Toodh(u) jayvud mein hoar(u) na koyee. Toodh(u) Aapay sirjee aapay goyee.

Word Meaning:  Undar(i) = in the heart (mind).   Heera, Laal(u) = (precious like) a diamond and ruby (noble qualities which connect with Waheguru) (see Paurri 6 of Japuji.    Perkh(i) = tested (assess the worth).   Pullaiy = in possession.    Vukhaaneh(i) = utter; recite.   Kusvutti = touchstone.        1. Vusaavniya = is harboured in mind.   Unjun = kohl (Maya).   Nirunjun = above the effects of Maya (God).   Joti = light (human soul).  Jot(i) = Light (Waheguru).      Pause. 
Kaaya = body.  Psaara = expansion.    Agumm = inaccessible.   Apaara = limitless.   Bukhsh(i) = by forgiving.    Milaavniya = is connected.         2. Tthaakur(u) = Lord.    Drirryaayay = reinforces.    Vertaiy = operates.       3. Vaypervaah(u) = carefree; indifferent.     Kilvikh = sins.   Uvgun = vices.   Bhaiy Bhaayay = (with respectful fear and respect).             4.       Bhaavaiy = is liked (approved).   Shabaday Maar(i) = (after) killing vices by Guru’s Shabad.     Jeevalniya = is given (spiritual) life.         5.        Baajhoun = without.      Savyeen = (I) serve (obey).    Kurm(i) = kindness.         6.       Jayha = like.  Seejhus(i) = succeeds.    Dayha = body.   Saar(i) = inquiring.   Smaal(i) = looking after.    Sehj(i) = in a higher spiritual state.         7.
Jayvud = as great.   Sirjee = created.    Goyee = destroyed.   Ghurr(i) = build artfully.  Bhunn(i) = break.   Svaareh(i) = refine.   Soohaavniya = is adorned.        8.
NOTE:  Maya’s effects are compared to the black colour of kohl because attraction for Maya creates darkness in the minds, as far as awareness about God is concerned.  Nirunjun = without (above) the effects of Maya = Waheguru.
MEANING:  Those who obey the Guru’s Shabad are able to assess (appreciate the worth of) the noble qualities, which connect them with Waheguru in the company of the devotees (holy saints). As a result, they enshrine Waheguru’s Naam, which is highly valuable like a diamond or a ruby, in their mind.  Those who possess (have acquired) Waheguru’s Naam in their heart after testing it in the company of the devotees as the touchstone, discuss Waheguru’s virtues all the time.       1.
I am a sacrifice for those who harbour Waheguru’s Naam in their mind. While living in the world of Maya, they acquire the Naam of Waheguru, which is above the effects of Maya, and become “one” with Him.          Pause.          Because of the presence of the inaccessible and limitless Waheguru in the human mind, the human body is also a home for Waheguru’s vast expansion, but only those who take the Guru’s shelter when the forgiving Waheguru connects them with Him.          2.           My Lord reinforces Truth (His Naam), and the devotee applies his mind to Waheguru’s Naam by the grace of the Guru. It is only Waheguru who operates everywhere; one can become “one” with Him by reciting His Naam.          3.           My dear Waheguru is carefree and is the destroyer of all sins and vices.  One should always recite His Naam with love and respect, because this is what the Guru preaches: worship Waheguru with love and some fear.           4.          Waheguru’s worship is true (successful) only if it is approved by Waheguru.  He Himself gifts such worship (to the deserving, and never has to repent (that He made a wrong choice).  There is only one Lord of all the living beings, and He grants the high spiritual life to His devotees by enabling them to kill the worldly desires by the Guru’s Shabad.            5.                I have none other than You (as my support), oh Waheguru!  I serve only You, and sing only Your praise.  Waheguru, Himself, connects His devotees with Him with His own benevolence.           6.
There is none like You for me.  Your kind sight makes my body (life) successful.  Waheguru always protects His devotees by being aware of their needs and looking after them.  The devotees take shelter of the Guru and attain a spiritual state beyond the effects of Maya.         7.             There is none other, as magnanimous as You are, oh Waheguru!  You create the world, and then destroy it Yourself.  You beautify the lives of Your devotees by destroying their vices and adorning them with virtues.  Waheguru’s Naam beautifies the life, oh Nanak!    8.5.6.                                        

(ਅੰਗ 113)॥     ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ ਸਭ ਘਟ ਆਪੇ ਭੋਗਣਹਾਰਾ॥ ਅਲਖੁ ਵਰਤੈ ਅਗਮ ਅਪਾਰਾ॥ ਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਸਬਦਿ ਮੇਰਾ ਹਰਿ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਧਿਆਈਐ ਸਹਜੇ ਸਚਿ ਸਮਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਹਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਜੀਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਗੁਰ ਸਬਦੁ ਮੰਨਿ ਵਸਾਵਣਿਆ॥ ਸਬਦੁ ਸੂਝੈ ਤਾ ਮਨ ਸਿਉ ਲੂਝੈ ਮਨਸਾ ਮਾਰਿ ਸਮਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਪੰਚ ਦੂਤ ਮੁਹਹਿ ਸੰਸਾਰਾ॥ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਅੰਧੇ ਸੁਧਿ ਨ ਸਾਰਾ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਹੋਵੈ ਸੁ ਆਪਣਾ ਘਰੁ ਰਾਖੈ ਪੰਚ ਦੂਤ ਸਬਦਿ ਪਚਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 2
॥ ਇਕਿ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਸਦਾ ਸਚੈ ਰੰਗਿ ਰਾਤੇ॥ ਸਹਜੇ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਸੇਵਹਿ ਅਨਦਿਨੁ ਮਾਤੇ॥ ਮਿਲਿ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਮ ਸਚੇ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਵਹਿ ਹਰਿ ਦਰਿ ਸੋਭਾ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 3॥ ਏਕਮ ਏਕੈ ਆਪੁ ਉਪਾਇਆ॥ ਦੁਬਿਧਾ ਦੂਜਾ ਤ੍ਰਿਬਿਧਿ ਮਾਇਆ॥ ਚਉਥੀ ਪਉੜੀ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਊਚੀ ਸਚੋ ਸਚੁ ਕਮਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 4॥ ਸਭੁ ਹੈ ਸਚਾ ਜੇ ਸਚੇ ਭਾਵੈ॥ ਜਿਨਿ ਸਚੁ ਜਾਤਾ ਸੋ ਸਹਜਿ ਸਮਾਵੈ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਕਰਣੀ ਸਚੇ ਸੇਵਹਿ ਸਾਚੇ ਜਾਇ ਸਮਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 5॥ ਸਚੇ ਬਾਝਹੁ ਕੋਈ ਅਵਰੁ ਨ ਦੂਆ॥ ਦੂਜੈ ਲਾਗਿ ਜਗੁ ਖਪਿ ਖਪਿ ਮੂਆ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਹੋਵੈ ਸੁ ਏਕੋ ਜਾਣੈ ਏਕੋ ਸੇਵਿ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 6॥ ਜੀਅ ਜੰਤ ਸਭਿ ਸਰਣਿ ਤੁਮਾਰੀ॥ ਆਪੇ ਧਰਿ ਦੇਖਹਿ ਕਚੀ ਪਕੀ ਸਾਰੀ॥ ਅਨਦਿਨੁ ਆਪੇ ਕਾਰ ਕਰਾਏ ਆਪੇ ਮੇਲਿ ਮਿਲਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 7॥ ਤੂ ਆਪੇ ਮੇਲਹਿ ਵੇਖਹਿ ਹਦੂਰਿ॥ ਸਭ ਮਹਿ ਆਪਿ ਰਹਿਆ ਭਰਪੂਰਿ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਆਪੇ ਆਪਿ ਵਰਤੈ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਸੋਝੀ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥  8.6.7.
(PAGE 113):             Majh Mehla 3:  Subh ghut Aapay bhogunhaara. Alukh(u) vertaiy Agumm Apaara. Gur kaiy Shabad(i) mayra Hur(i) Prubh(u) dhiyaayiyaiy, sehjay Sach(i) smaavniya.  1. Huoun vaari jeeo vaari, Gur Shabad(u) munn(i) vusaavniya.  Shabad(u) soojhaiy, ta mun siyoun loojhaiy, munsa maar(i) smaavniya. 1. Ruhaou.   Punch doot moheh(i) sunsaara. Munmukh undhay, soodh(i) na saara. Gurmukh(i) hovaiy su aapna ghur(u) raakhaiy, punch doot Shabad(i) puchaavniya.  2.  Ikk(i) Gurmukh(i) suda Sachaiy rung(i) raatay. Sehjay Prubh(u) sayveh(i) undin(u) maatay. Mil(i) Preetum Sachay goon gaaveh(i), Hur(i) Dur(i) sobha paavniya.  3. Aikum aikaiy Aap(u) oopaaya. Doobidha dooja tribidh(i) Maya. Chauthi paurri Gurmukh(i) oochi, Sacho Sach(u) kmaavniya.  4.  Subh(u) haiy Sacha, jay Sachay bhaavaiy. Jin(i) Sach(u) jaata so sehj(i) smaavaiy. Gurmukh(i) kerni Sachay sayveh(i), Saachay jaayay smaavniya.  5. Sachay baajhoun koyee uver(u) na dooa. Doojaiy laag(i) jug(u) khup(i) khup(i) mooa.  Gurmukh(i) hovaiy su Aiko jaanaiy, Aiko sayv(i) sookh(u) paavniya. 6. Jea junt subh(i) shern(i) Toomhaari. Aapay dhur(i) vaykheh(i) kutchi pukki saari. Undin(u) Aapay kaar kurayay, Aapay mayl(i) milaavniya. 7. Tu Aapay mayleh(i), vaykheh(i) hudoor(i). Subh meh(i) Aap ruhiya bhurpoor(i).  Nanak Aapay Aap(i) vertaiy, Gurmukh(i) sojhi paavniya.   8.6.7.
Word Meaning:  Ghut = heart.    Bhogunhaara = one who enjoys.    Alukh = inconceivable.    Agumm = inaccessible.    Apaara = limitless.   Sehjay = in a higher spiritual state.           1.        Vusaavniya = make heart a residence for Waheguru; is embedded (in mind).    Soojhaiy = understands (is connected with).     Siyoun = with.    Loojhaiy = fights.     Munsa = desires.       Pause.     Punch doot = five demons (lust, rage, greed, attachment, and conceit).     Moheh(i) = are deceiving (robbing people of spiritual qualities).    Soodh(i) = wisdom.    Saara = knowledge.   Ghur = (good qualities of) heart.     Raakhaiy = protects.       2.       Ikk(i) = many.    Raatay = imbued.   Maatay = intoxicated; imbued; focused.          3.      Aikum = firstly.    Aikaiy = only Him.   Oopaaya = created.     Doobidha = difficulty in making a decision.   Tribidh(i) = having three effects (Maya).    Chauthi Paurri = fourth (spiritual) level, where Maya has no effect = Sehj.         4.       Bhaavaiy = likes; approves.   Jaata = recognized (made a connection).    Kerni = performance of (worthy) deeds.       5.       Baajhoun = without.    Uver(u) = other.     Doojaiy = (in) Maya.    Khup(i) Khup(i) = try hard (suffer) without any success.    Sayv(i) = serve.        6.     Shern(i) = shelter (dependence on).    Saari = pawns in the game of dice.   Kaar = deeds.       7.     Vakheh(i) = sees; looks after.   Hudoor(i) = in the presence.   Bhurpoor = filling completely; omnipresent.    Vertaiy = operates.     8
MEANING:  The inconceivable, the inaccessible, and the limitless Waheguru operates everywhere and enjoys every heart.  Waheguru is worshipped with Guru’s Shabad when one attains a higher spiritual state above the effects of Maya; he then becomes “one” with Waheguru.          1.          I offer my life for sacrifice for all those who make their hearts a place for Waheguru (His Naam) to reside.   An individual who recognizes the value of the Guru’s Shabad (makes a connection with Him), fights with his mind (evil desires), and becomes “one” with Waheguru after killing (overcoming) his desires.          Pause.           The five demons (lust, rage, greed, attachment, and ego) are cheating the world (robbing it of spiritual wealth).  The followers of their mind are blind (ignorant) and do not have any wisdom or knowledge to realize this.  However, those who obey and act on the Guru’s Shabad are able to overpower the demons and thus protect their house (wealth of virtues).           2.         Some devotees of the Guru are always imbued in Waheguru’s love, and serve Waheguru (recite His Naam) in a high spiritual state, and are always intoxicated with His Naam.  They sing the virtues of Waheguru in the company of their dear (Guru), and as a result are honoured in Waheguru’s Court.         3.
First of all, Waheguru created Himself, and then He created the second (Maya) with three (accepted) effects, which result in dichotomy and cause difficulty for people in choosing between the captivating Maya and Waheguru’s Naam.   Above the three effects of Maya is the highest spiritual state, which is called the Fourth Level (state).  Waheguru’s devotees deal in pure Truth (Waheguru’s Naam) and attain the Fourth Level.          4.         Anything is true (everlasting) if it is liked (or approved) by Waheguru. Those who recognize the real Truth (Waheguru) merge into Him in the Fourth State.  The real deed worthy of performing for the devotees is to serve Waheguru so as to become “one” with Him.        5.           There is none other than Waheguru (worthy of worship).  People indulge in Maya, and are suffering badly (spiritually) because they do not gain any spirituality (and no peace of mind). A devotee recognizes only one Waheguru, serves only Him, and enjoys all the comforts.         6.
All living beings are in Your shelter (depend on You), oh Waheguru! You alone examine each of them like the pawns in a game of dice, and decide which pawn (human being) will succeed (in merging with You), and which will not – at least not just yet.  Waheguru always makes them perform the deeds He wishes them to perform, and connects them with Him through the Guru.       7.             Waheguru is present everywhere (is omnipresent), and He observes the living beings for their deeds in His own presence (under His supervision) and connects the deserving with Him.  He operates alone, and one is enlightened by the grace of the Guru, oh Nanak.   8.6.7.
