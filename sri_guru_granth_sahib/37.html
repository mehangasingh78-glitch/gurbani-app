(ਅੰਗ 36)॥    ਸਭੁ ਕਿਛੁ ਸੁਣਦਾ ਵੇਖਦਾ ਕਿਉ ਮੁਕਰਿ ਪਇਆ ਜਾਇ॥ ਪਾਪੋ ਪਾਪੁ ਕਮਾਵਦੇ ਪਾਪੇ ਪਚਹਿ ਪਚਾਇ॥ ਸੋ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਨਦਰਿ ਨ ਆਵਈ ਮਨਮੁਖਿ ਬੂਝ ਨ ਪਾਇ॥ ਜਿਸੁ ਵੇਖਾਲੇ ਸੋਈ ਵੇਖੈ ਨਾਨਕ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਪਾਇ॥ 4॥23॥56॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 3:  Aapna bhuou tin paayoun(u) jinh Gur ka Shabad beechaar(i). Satsungti suda mil(i) ruhaay Sachay kay goon saar(i). Doobidha maiyl(u) chookaayiun(u) Hur(i) raakhiya oordhaar(i). Sachi Bani, Sach(u) mun(i), Sachay naal(i) piyaar(i).  1. Mun mayray, haumein maiyl(u) bhurnaal(i). Hur(i) nirmul(u) suda sohna, Shabad(i) svaarunhaar(u). 1. Ruhaou. Sachaiy Shabad(i) mun(u) mohiya Prubh(i) luyay milayay. Undin(u) Naamay ruttiyaan joti Jot(i) smaayay. Joti hoo Prubh(u) jaapda, bin(u) Satgur boojh na paayay. Jin kuou poorub(i) likhiya, Satgur(u) bhaytiya tin aayay. 2. Vin(u) Naavaiy subh doommni, doojaiy bhaayay khooayay. Tis(u) bin(u) ghurri na jeenvdi. dookhi raiyn(i) vihayay. Bherm(i) bhoolaana undhula, phir(i) phir(i) aavaiy jaayay. Nudr(i) kuray Prubh(u) aapni, aapay luyay milayay.     3. 
(PAGE 36):        Subh(u) kichh(u) soon-da vaykhda kiyoun mooker(i) puyyiya jaayay. Paapo paap(u) kmaanvday paapay pucheh(i) puchaayay. So Prubh(u) nudr(i) na aavuyyi munmukh(i) boojh na paayay. Jis(u) vaykhaalay soyee vaykhaiy, Nanak Gurmukh(i) paayay.  4.23. 56.
Word Meaning:  Bhuou = fear; respect.  Saar(i) = by remembering, by pondering on.   Doobhida = duality; indecisiveness.    Chookaayiun = cleared.    Oordhaar(i) = fostered in mind.      1.         Bhurnaal(i) = in the sea of Maya.   Nirmul(u) = pure.   Svaarunhaar(u) = one who grooms (Waheguru).        Pause.        Mohiya = captivated.   Ruttiyaan = imbued.   Joti Jot(i) Smaayay = merge your light (soul) in Waheguru (Joti).   Jaapda = is discernible; visible.   Poorub(i) = predestined.   Bhaytiya = met; connected.        2.        Vin(u) Naavaiy = without Waheguru’s Naam.   Doojaiy Bhaayay = in love with the other (Maya).   Khooayay = suffer in worthless efforts.  Vihayay = is spent.  Bherm(i) bhoolaana = lost (misguided) in doubts.  Aavaiy Jaayay = takes rebirths.         3.         Mooker(i) = refuse to admit; disavow.  Pucheh(i) = suffers.  Boojh = comprehend.       4.
MEANING: (Waheguru) instils respectful fear for Him in the minds of those who ponder on (act on) Guru’s Shabad, and remember it. By keeping Guru’s Shabad in mind, they stay connected with Waheguru in the company of the holy saints, and wash away the filth of duality (whether to pursue Waheguru or Maya). Acting on the True (everlasting) Shabad of the Guru, their minds are purified, and they cultivate love for the eternal (Waheguru).    1.                          The world sea is full of the filth of Maya, oh my mind.  (To wash away this filth) Obey Guru’s Shabad to acquire Waheguru in your heart, who is always pure, beautiful, and who fulfils all your needs and wishes.        Pause.        Waheguru connects all those with Him whose minds have been captivated by the Guru’s Shabad.  Imbued in Waheguru’s Naam day and night, their soul (joti) merges with the Supreme Soul of Waheguru (Jot(i).  God is discernible from His soul (in nature), but one cannot recognize that without the Guru’s shelter.  The Guru connects only those with Him who are predestined for it.          2.         Everybody is caught in duality without the Guru and suffers because of their love for the other (Maya).  I cannot survive without Waheguru even for a short moment, and the night of my life is spent in discomfort.  (Without the Guru), the ignorant man is blind, and is lost in doubts and indecisiveness, and is trapped in reincarnation.  But, when Waheguru is kind, He Himself connects individuals with Him.         3.          The man hears and watches everything (about the generosity of Waheguru), so, how can he refuse to admit His benevolence and His largesse?  (He still does not believe in Him)?  The man continues to commit sins and suffers constantly because of them.   The follower of his mind cannot discern and recognize Waheguru.  Only that individual “sees” Him, to whom He shows “Himself” by the grace of the Guru, oh Nanak.    4.23.56.

ਸਿਰੀਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਗੁਰ ਰੋਗੁ ਨ ਤੁਟਈ ਹਉਮੈ ਪੀੜ ਨ ਜਾਇ॥ ਗੁਰ ਪਰਸਾਦੀ ਮਨਿ ਵਸੈ ਨਾਮੇ ਰਹੈ ਸਮਾਇ॥ ਗੁਰ ਸਬਦੀ ਹਰਿ ਪਾਈਐ ਬਿਨੁ ਸਬਦੈ ਭਰਮਿ ਭੁਲਾਇ॥1॥ ਮਨ ਰੇ ਨਿਜ ਘਰਿ ਵਾਸਾ ਹੋਇ॥ ਰਾਮ ਨਾਮੁ ਸਾਲਾਹਿ ਤੂ ਫਿਰਿ ਆਵਣ ਜਾਣੁ ਨ ਹੋਇ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਹਰਿ ਇਕੋ ਦਾਤਾ ਵਰਤਦਾ ਦੂਜਾ ਅਵਰੁ ਨ ਕੋਇ॥ ਸਬਦਿ ਸਾਲਾਹੀ ਮਨਿ ਵਸੈ ਸਹਜੇ ਹੀ ਸੁਖੁ ਹੋਇ॥ ਸਭ ਨਦਰੀ ਅੰਦਰਿ ਵੇਖਦਾ ਜੈ ਭਾਵੈ ਤੈ ਦੇਇ॥ 2॥ ਹਉਮੈ ਸਭਾ ਗਣਤ ਹੈ ਗਣਤੈ ਨਉ ਸੁਖੁ ਨਾਹਿ॥ ਬਿਖੁ ਕੀ ਕਾਰ ਕਮਾਵਣੀ ਬਿਖੁ ਹੀ ਮਾਹਿ ਸਮਾਹਿ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਨਾਵੈ ਠਉਰੁ ਨ ਪਾਇਨੀ ਜਮਪੁਰਿ ਦੂਖ ਸਹਾਹਿ॥ 3॥ ਜੀਉ ਪਿੰਡੁ ਸਭੁ ਤਿਸ ਦਾ ਤਿਸੈ ਦਾ ਆਧਾਰੁ॥ ਗੁਰ ਪਰਸਾਦੀ  ਬੁਝੀਐ ਤਾ ਪਾਏ ਮੋਖ ਦੁਆਰੁ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮੁ ਸਲਾਹਿ ਤੂ ਅੰਤੁ ਨ ਪਾਰਾਵਾਰੁ॥ 4॥24॥57॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 3.  Bin Gur roag(u) na tootyee, haumein peerr na jaayay. Gur persadi mun(i) vussaiy, Naamay ruhhaiy smaayay. Gur Shabadi Hur(i) paayiyaiy, bin Shabadaiy bherm(i) bhoolayay. 1. Mun ray, nij ghur(i) vaasa hoyay. Ram Naam(u) salaahay tu, phir(i) aavun jaan(u) na hohay. 1. Ruhaou. Hur(i) ikko daata vertda dooja uver(u) na koyay. Shabad(i) saalahee mun(i) vussaiy sehjay hee sookh(u) hoyay. Subh nudri undar(i) vaykhda jaiy bhaavaiy taiy dayay. 2. Haumein subha gunut haiy, guntaiy nuou sookh(u) naah(i). Bikh(u) kee kaar kmaavni, bikh(u) hee maah(i) smaahay. Bin(u) Naavaiy tthaur(u) na paayayni, Jumpur(i) dookh suhaahay. 3. Jeo pind(u) subh(u) Tis(u) da, Tissaiy da adhaar(u). Gur persadi boojhiyaiy, ta paayay mokh dooaar(u). Nanak Naam(u) slaahay tu unt(u) na paaravaar(u).  4.24.57. 
Word Meaning:  Tootyee = breaks; disappears.  Haumein = selfishness and ego.  Peerr = pain.   Smaayay = absorbed; merged.  Bherm(i) = duality; doubtfulness     1.
Nij Ghur(i) = in one's own mind.   Vaasa = take residence.         Pause.         Vertda = controls; operates.    Uver(u) = else; other than.   Sehjay = mental state where there is no effect of Maya.   Nedri = kind sight.    Bhaavaiy = likes.  Taiy = to him.         2.          Gunut = calculations; worries.   Bikh(u) = poison (Maya).    Kaar = job; service.  Smaahay = absorbed.   Tthaur(u) = stability; peace.   Jumpoor(i) = in the city of the angel of death (in his prison).   Suhaahay = endures.     3.         Jeo Pind(u) = soul and body.   Aadhaar(u) = support.   Boojhiyaiy = recognize; comprehend.    Mokh = freedom from the bonds of Maya; salvation.  Paaravar = limits.       4.
MEANING:  The disease of haumein, and the accompanying suffering, do not go away without the Guru.  Because Waheguru takes residence in one’s heart by the grace of the Guru, the individual stays connected with Waheguru with the Guru’s grace (and this is the cure for the disease of haumein).  One realizes Waheguru when he obeys Guru’s Shabad; without Guru’s Shabad, people are lost in doubts and superstitions.         1.           Praise Waheguru so that His Naam is implanted in your heart, and your birth-death-birth cycle is broken, oh my mind.           Pause.           Waheguru is the only provider, and He alone controls the world – there is nobody else. He purifies the hearts of those who praise Him by obeying the Guru’s Shabad in His shelter, and as a result, they enjoy bliss (with no effect of Maya).  Even though He casts His kind sight on everyone, but Waheguru confers His Naam only on some chosen people.         2.          The man performs some accepted rituals with the belief to please some god, and keeps account of his so-called noble deeds, but there is no peace of mind in such calculations (because Waheguru is not pleased with them). When one engages in tasks involving the poisonous Maya, he stays engrossed in that, and without Waheguru’s Naam, he does not find peace and endures suffering at the hands of the Angel of death.         3.           The human body and the soul belong to Waheguru, and He is our only support.  The individual, who recognizes this fact by the grace of the Guru, is salvaged at Waheguru’s door.  Acting on Guru’s Shabad, sing praise of Waheguru whose limits can never be determined, oh Nanak!        4.24.57.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ ਤਿਨਾ ਅਨੰਦੁ ਸਦਾ ਸੁਖੁ ਹੈ ਜਿਨਾ ਸਚੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਆਧਾਰੁ॥ ਗੁਰ ਸਬਦੀ ਸਚੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਦੂਖ ਨਿਵਾਰਣਹਾਰੁ॥ ਸਦਾ ਸਦਾ ਸਾਚੇ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਵਹਿ ਸਾਚੈ ਨਾਇ ਪਿਆਰੁ॥ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਕਰਿ ਕੈ ਆਪਣੀ ਦਿਤੋਨੁ ਭਗਤਿ ਭੰਡਾਰੁ॥ 1॥ ਮਨ ਰੇ ਸਦਾ ਅਨੰਦੁ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਇ॥ ਸਚੀ ਬਾਣੀ ਹਰਿ ਪਾਈਐ ਹਰਿ ਸਿਉ ਰਹੈ ਸਮਾਇ॥1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ਸਚੀ ਭਗਤੀ ਮਨੁ ਲਾਲੁ ਥੀਆ ਰਤਾ ਸਹਜਿ ਸੁਭਾਇ॥ ਗੁਰ ਸਬਦੀ ਮਨੁ ਮੋਹਿਆ ਕਹਣਾ ਕਛੂ ਨਾ ਜਾਇ॥ ਜਿਹਵਾ ਰਤੀ ਸਬਦਿ ਸਚੈ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਪੀਵੈ ਰਸਿ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਇ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਏਹੁ ਰੰਗੁ ਪਾਈਐ ਜਿਸ ਨੋ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਕਰੇ ਰਜਾਇ ॥2 ਸੰਸਾ ਇਹੁ ਸੰਸਾਰੁ ਹੈ ਸੁਤਿਆ ਰੈਣਿ ਵਿਹਾਇ॥ ਇਕਿ ਆਪਣੇ ਭਾਣੈ ਕਢਿ ਲਇਅਨੁ ਆਪੇ ਲਇਓਨੁ ਮਿਲਾਇ॥ ਆਪੇ ਹੀ ਆਪਿ ਮਨਿ ਵਸਿਆ ਮਾਇਆ ਮੋਹੁ ਚੁਕਾਇ॥ ਆਪਿ ਵਡਾਈ ਦਿਤੀਅਨੁ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਦੇਇ ਬੁਝਾਇ॥3॥ ਸਭਨਾ ਕਾ ਦਾਤਾ ਏਕੁ ਹੈ ਭੁਲਿਆ ਲਏ ਸਮਝਾਇ॥ ਇਕਿ ਆਪੇ ਆਪਿ ਖੁਆਇਅਨੁ ਦੂਜੈ ਛਡਿਅਨੁ ਲਾਇ॥ ਗੁਰਮਤੀ ਹਰਿ ਪਾਈਐ ਜੋਤੀ ਜੋਤਿ ਮਿਲਾਇ॥ ਅਨਦਿਨੁ ਨਾਮੇ ਰਤਿਆ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮਿ ਸਮਾਇ॥ 4॥25॥58॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 3:  Tinha anund suda sookh(u) haiy, jinha Sach(u) Naam(u) aadhar(u). Gur Shabadi Sach(u) paaya, dookh nivaarunhaar(u). Suda suda Saachay goon gaaveh(i), Sachaiy Naayay piyaar(u). Kirpa kur(i) kaiy Aapni, dit-on(u) bhugut(i) bhundaar(u). 1. Mun ray, suda anund(u) goon gaayay. Suchi Bani Hur(i) paayiyaiy Hur(i) siyoun ruhaiy smaayay. 1. Ruhaou.   Sachi bhugti mun(u) laal(u) theeya, rutta sehj(i) soobhaayay. Gur Shabadi mun(u) mohiya, kehna kuchhoo na jaayay. Jihva rutti Shabad(i) Sachaiy, Amrit(u) peevaiy, rus(i) goon gaayay. Gurmukh(i) eho rung(u) paayiyaiy, jis no kirpa kuray rujaayay. 2. Sunsa ehu sunsaar(u) haiy, sootiyaan raiyn(i) vihaayay. Ikk(i) Aapnay bhaanaiy kudh(i) luyyiun, Aapay luyion(u) milaayay.  Aapay hee Aap(i) mun(i) vussiya, Maya moh(u) chookayay. Aap(i) vudayee dittiun(u), Gurmukh(i) dayay boojhaayay. 3. Subhna ka daata Aik haiy, bhoolliyaan luyay sumjhaayay. Ikk(i) Aapay Aap(i) khooyaayiun, doojaaiy chhuddiyun laayay. Gurmati Hur(i) paayiyaiy, joti Jot(i) milaayay. Undin(u) Naamay ruttiyaan, Nanak Naam(i) smaayay. 4.25.58.
Word Meaning: Anund = blissfulness.  Aadhaar(u) = support.  Sach(u) = Truth (God).   Dit-on(u) = He has given.   Bhundaar(u) = treasure.        1.        Laal(u) = red colour (represents a deep relationship).   Theeya = became.  Sehj(i) = in the state of a stable mind.   Mohiya = captivated.  Jihva = tongue.   Rus(i) = extract; essence; Amrit.  Rujaayay = by His own Will.      2.          Sunsa = doubts; duality.   Vihaayay = spends.    Kudh(i) = pull out.   Vudayee = honour.  Boojhaayay = given knowledge; enlightened.        3.        Daata = provider.  Bhulliyaan = misled; gone astray.   Khooyaayiun = misled; cause to suffer.  Doojay = (in) Maya.  Laayay = attached to.  Jot(i) = Source of enlightenment (Waheguru).  Undin(u) = day and night.        4.
MEANING:  Those who have made Waheguru’s Naam their support are always blissful and enjoy all the comforts. Following on Guru’s Shabad, they realize Waheguru, who is the destroyer of all discomforts.  They love Waheguru’s Naam, and sing His praise all the time. Waheguru, kindly confers upon them all the treasures of bhakti (devotion).        1.
Waheguru’s praise is the source of all bliss, oh my mind. Waheguru is realized through reciting the Guru’s Shabad, and one always remains connected to Him.        Pause.  
With true devotion, one’s mind is coloured red (meaning the mind is imbued permanently in Waheguru’s love) in the mental state above the effects of Maya.   When one’s mind gets captivated by the Guru’s Shabad, he becomes speechless (has no words to describe his blissful state of mind).  One’s tongue is also imbued with the recitation of the eternal Shabad, and he enjoys the taste of Amrit when singing Waheguru’s praise. The individual, to whom Waheguru is kind, has his mind coloured with His affection by the grace of the Guru.       2.
The world is caught in duality (doubt and superstition), and their lives slip away in sleep (unaware of the Truth). However, some are pulled out of their slumber (by Waheguru), and are connected with Him.  Waheguru takes up residence in their mind, thereby ending their attraction to Maya. Having been enlightened by the Guru, they are honoured by Waheguru.       3.           The provider for all is the same Waheguru; He guides the ones who are on the wrong path (of Maya). But also misguides some to the allurement of Maya.  Waheguru can be realized only by taking the Guru’s advice, as a result of which the soul (light) of the individual merges with the source of light (Joti) – Waheguru.  Those who are imbued in Waheguru’s Naam all the time become “one” with Him, oh Nanak.       4. 25.58.
