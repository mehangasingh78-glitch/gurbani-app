(ਅੰਗ 25)॥      ਜੇਹੀ ਸੁਰਤਿ ਤੇਹਾ ਤਿਨ ਰਾਹੁ॥ ਲੇਖਾ ਇਕੋ ਆਵਹੁ ਜਾਹੁ॥ 1॥ ਕਾਹੇ ਜੀਅ ਕਰਹਿ ਚਤੁਰਾਈ॥ ਲੇਵੈ ਦੇਵੈ ਢਿਲ਼ ਨ ਪਾਈ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਤੇਰੇ ਜੀਅ ਜੀਆ ਕਾ ਤੋਹਿ॥ ਕਿਤ ਕਉ ਸਾਹਿਬ ਆਵਹਿ ਰੋਹਿ॥ ਜੇ ਤੂ ਸਾਹਿਬ ਆਵਹਿ ਰੋਹਿ॥ ਤੂ ਓਨਾ ਕਾ ਤੇਰੇ ਓਹਿ॥ 2॥  ਅਸੀ ਬੋਲ ਵਿਗਾੜ ਵਿਗਾੜਹ ਬੋਲ॥ ਤੂ ਨਦਰੀ  ਅੰਦਰਿ ਤੋਲਹਿ ਤੋਲ॥ ਜਹ ਕਰਣੀ ਤਹ ਪੁਰੀ ਮਤਿ॥ ਕਰਣੀ ਬਾਝਹੁ ਘਟੇ ਘਟਿ॥ 3॥ ਪ੍ਰਣਵਤਿ ਨਾਨਕੁ ਗਿਆਨੀ ਕੈਸਾ ਹੋਇ॥ ਆਪੁ ਪਛਾਣੈ ਬੂਝੈ ਸੋਇ॥ ਗੁਰ ਪ੍ਰਸਾਦਿ ਕਰੇ ਬੀਚਾਰੁ॥ ਸੋ ਗਿਆਨੀ ਦਰਗਹ ਪਰਵਾਣੁ॥ 4॥30॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 1, Ghur(u) 4:  Aika soort(i) jaytay haiyn jea. Soort(i) vihoona koyay na kea. 
(PAGE 25):                 Jayhi soort(i), tayha tinh raah(u). Laykha ikko, aavhou, jaahou. 1.  
Kaahay jea kureh(i) chaturaayi. Lavaiy, Dayvaiy, dhill na paayi. 1. Ruhaou.  Tayray jea, jeaan ka Toh(i). Kit kuou Sahib, aaveh(i) roh(i). Jay Tu Sahib, aaveh(i) roh(i). Tu ohna ka, Tayray oh(i).  2. Useen boal-vigaarr, vigaarreh boal.  Tu nedri undar(i) toleh(i), toal. Jeh kerni, teh poori mut(i). Kerni baajhoun ghutay ghut(i).  3. Prunvut(i) Nanak(u) Giyani kaiysa hoyay. Aap(u) puchhaanaiy, boojhaiy soyay.  Gur persad(i) kuray beechaar(u). So Giyani Durgeh pervaan(u).  4.30.
Word Meaning: Soort(i) = awareness.  Vihoona = without; devoid of.   Raah(u) = lifestyle.  Laykha Ikko = the same account (rules).         1.        Kaahay = what for?   Chuturaayi = play smart.       Layvaiy = takes away.    Dhill = delay.         Pause.     Roh(i) = anger.    2.
Boal-Vigaarr = bad-mouthed.   Boal = words.    Toleh(i) Toal = weigh; evaluate.   Kerni = (noble) deeds.     Baajhoun = without.   Prunvut(i) = suggests humbly.    Aap(u) Puchhaanaiy = self-analysis.    Boojhaiy = understands; unlocks (the secrets).      3. 
MEANING: Waheguru has infused awareness in every living being – albeit to varying degrees and in different forms – and there is no one without awareness.  However, everyone chooses their lifestyle based on the level of awareness they have.  Waheguru keeps account of everybody’s deeds using the same principles, as a result of which, they come to the world and leave (take birth, and die).           1.         Why do you use your smartness to acquire worldly things, when Waheguru gives His gifts, and takes them away according to your deeds, and does not delay even for a short moment in doing so, oh man?          Pause.
Everybody belongs to Waheguru, and Waheguru belongs to them, so how can the Lord be angry at them?   Waheguru is forgiving, so if He does get angry at someone, it is for their benefit, because even he has been created by Him.           2.         On the other hand, we (humans) are bad-mouthed, and we harm our interests by our foul language, whereas the munificent Waheguru evaluates us with His kind sight.  An individual whose performs noble deeds (as per the Advice of the Guru), has perfect (noble) wisdom (to attain high spirituality); whereas, there is always a loss in spirituality) without noble deeds.         3.
Who is considered to be a giyani (enlightened devotee), asks Nanak humbly.  (The answer is) That a giyani is a devotee who analyses the self (to determine his objective in life and his strengths and weaknesses) and connects with Waheguru.  Such a giyani ponders on Guru’s Shabad, and is approved in Waheguru’s Court.    4.30.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 1 ਘਰੁ 4॥ ਤੂ ਦਰੀਆਉ ਦਾਨਾ ਬੀਨਾ ਮੈ ਮਛੁਲੀ ਕੈਸੇ ਅੰਤੁ ਲਹਾ॥ ਜਹ ਜਹ ਦੇਖਾ ਤਹ ਤਹ ਤੂ ਹੈ ਤੁਝ ਤੇ ਨਿਕਸੀ ਫੂਟਿ ਮਰਾ॥ 1॥ ਨਾ ਜਾਣਾ ਮੇਉ ਨ ਜਾਣਾ ਜਾਲੀ॥ ਜਾ ਦੁਖੁ ਲਾਗੈ ਤਾ ਤੁਝੈ ਸਮਾਲੀ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਤੂ ਭਰਪੂਰਿ ਜਾਨਿਆ ਮੈ ਦੂਰਿ॥ ਜੋ ਕਛੁ ਕਰੀ ਸੁ ਤੇਰੈ ਹਦੂਰਿ॥ ਤੂ ਦੇਖਹਿ ਹਉ ਮੁਕਰਿ ਪਾਉ॥ ਤੇਰੈ ਕੰਮਿ ਨ ਤੇਰੈ ਨਾਇ॥ 2॥ ਜੇਤਾ ਦੇਹਿ ਤੇਤਾ ਹਉ ਖਾਉ॥ ਬਿਆ ਦਰੁ ਨਾਹੀ ਕੈ ਦਰਿ ਜਾਉ॥ ਨਾਨਕੁ ਏਕ ਕਹੈ ਅਰਦਾਸਿ॥ ਜੀਉ ਪਿੰਡੁ ਸਭੁ ਤੇਰੈ ਪਾਸਿ॥ 3॥ ਆਪੇ ਨੇੜੈ ਦੂਰਿ ਆਪੇ ਹੀ ਆਪੇ ਮੰਝਿ ਮਿਆਨੁੋ॥ ਆਪੇ ਵੇਖੈ ਸੁਣੈ ਆਪੇ ਹੀ ਕੁਦਰਤਿ ਕਰੇ ਜਹਾਨੁੋ॥ ਜੋ ਤਿਸੁ ਭਾਵੈ ਨਾਨਕਾ ਹੁਕਮੁ ਸੋਈ ਪਰਵਾਨੋ॥  4॥31॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 1, Ghur(u) 4. Tu duriyaayo daana, beena, mein muchhuli kaiysay unt(u) luhaa? Jeh jeh daykhaan, teh, teh Tu haiyn, Toojh tay nikseen phoot(i) muraan. 1. Na jaana mayo, na jaana jaali.  Ja dookh laagaiy, ta Toojhaiy smaaleen.  1. Ruhaou. Tu bherpoor(i), jaaniya mein door(i). Jo kuchh(u) kureen, su Tayray hudoor(i). Tu daykheh(i), huoun mookkur(i) paaoun. Tayraiy kumm(i), na, Tayraiy Naayay. 2.  Jayta dayh(i), tayta huoun khaaoun. Biyya dur(i) naahi, kaiy dur(i) jaaoun. Nanak(u) aik kuhhaiy ardaas(i). Jeou pind(u) subh(u) Tayray paas(i).  3. Aapay nayrraiy, door(i) Aaapay hee, Aapay munjh(i) miyaano. Aapay vaykhaiy, soonaiy Aapay hee, koodrut(i) kuray jahaano. Jo Yis(u) bhaavaiy Nanaka, Huqam(u)  soyee pervaano.  4.31.
Word Meaning: Daana = wise.   Beena = know-all.     Muchhuli = fish.   Nikseen = come out.      Phoot(i) Muraan = explode (squirm) to die.        1.      Mayo = boatman; fisherman.   Jaali = net.    Smaali = remember.       Pause.      Bhepoor(i) = omnipresent.    Hudoor(i) = in Your presence; under Your eye.   Mookkur(i) = deny; disavow.    Kumm(i) = deeds.  2.  Biyya = other.   Dur(u) = shelter; support.    Pind(u) = body.   Paas(i) = under control.        3.     Munjh(i) Miyaano = in the middle.   Koodrut(i) Kuray = with powers.   Jahaano = world.  4.
MEANING: You are perfectly wise, omniscient, and are vast like an ocean (source of all life), whereas I am like a small fish in that ocean, so how can I find Your limits, oh Waheguru?  (Like the water in the ocean), You are present everywhere I look, and I squirm to death if I get out of You (forget You), oh Waheguru.         1.         I am not aware of any fisherman and his net; I simply remember You whenever I am in trouble, oh Waheguru.           Pause.
I think of You as being somewhere far away, while You are omnipresent; so, whatever I do is known to You.  You watch all my deeds, but (ignorant me), I deny all my evil deeds.  I do not perform deeds according to Your expectations, and do not recite Your Naam.         2.
(But I know one thing for sure), I have no other shelter to go to. I consume only that which You give me.  I humbly admit that my body and soul are controlled by You.         3.
You are close to some, while far away for some others, and also in-between for some.  You have created the world with Your powers, and watch and listen to everything (that we do).   I accept (obey) everything that happens according to Your Huqam (Will), oh Nanak.  4.31.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 1 ਘਰੁ 4॥ ਕੀਤਾ ਕਹਾ ਕਰੇ ਮਨਿ ਮਾਨੁ॥ ਦੇਵਣਹਾਰੇ ਕੈ ਹਥਿ ਦਾਨੁ॥ ਭਾਵੈ ਦੇਇ ਨ ਦੇਈ ਸੋਇ॥ ਕੀਤੈ ਕੈ ਕਹਿਐ ਕਿਆ ਹੋਇ॥ 1॥ ਆਪੇ ਸਚੁ ਭਾਵੈ ਤਿਸੁ ਸਚੁ॥ ਅੰਧਾ ਕਚਾ ਕਚੁ ਨਿਕਚੁ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਜਾ ਕੇ ਰੁਖ ਬਿਰਖ ਆਰਾਉ॥ ਜੇਹੀ ਧਾਤੁ ਤੇਹਾ ਤਿਨ ਨਾਉ॥ ਫੁਲੁ ਭਾਉ ਫਲੁ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਪਾਇ॥ ਆਪਿ ਬੀਜਿ ਆਪੇ ਹੀ ਖਾਇ॥ 2॥ ਕਚੀ ਕੰਧ ਕਚਾ ਵਿਚਿ ਰਾਜੁ॥ ਮਤਿ ਅਲੂਣੀ ਫਿਕਾ ਸਾਦੁ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਆਣੇ ਆਵੈ ਰਾਸਿ॥ ਵਿਣੁ ਨਾਵੈ ਨਾਹੀ ਸਾਬਾਸਿ॥ 3॥32॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 1, Ghur(u) 4: Keeta kuhha kuray mun(i) maan(u). Dayvunhaaray kaiy huth(i) daan(u). Bhaavaiy dayay, na, dayee Soyay. Keetaiy kaiy kuhhiyaiyn kiyya hoyay. 1. Aapay Sach(u), bhaavaiy Tis(u) sach(u).  Undha kucha, kuch(u), nikuch(u). 1. Ruhaou. Ja kay rookh, birkh aaraaou. Jayhi dhaat(u), tayha tinh naaoun. Phool bhaaou, phul(u) likhiya paayay. Aap(i) beej(i), aapay hee khaayay. 2. Kuchi kundh, kucha vich(i) raaj(u). Mut(i) alooni, fikka saad(u). Nanak aanay, aavay raas(i). Vin(u) Naavaiy naahi shabaash(i).  3.32.
Word Meaning: Keeta = created.   Kuhaa = where; how?   Maan(u) = take pride (credit).   Dayvunhaaray = the Provider.   Daan(u) = gifts.   Bhaavaiy = whether.         1.
Sach(u) = eternal; source of all virtues.     Kucha = weak in qualities.          Pause.     
Rookh Birkh = trees; vegetation.   Aaraaou = adornment.    Dhaat(u) = basic nature.    Bhaaou = affection; intent.    Phul = result; reward.  Likhiya = in fortune.         2.       
Kundh = wall; body; human character.   Raaj(u) = (the mind architect).   Alooni = without salt (distasteful).   Fikka Saad = tasteless.     Aanay = (if He) brings (causes).    Raas(i) = successful.  Shabaash(i) = praise; approval.          3.
MEANING:  The eternal Waheguru is the creator of the universe, and is the source of all virtues, and he likes virtues (purity of mind) only. The Maya-engrossed individual is blinded by its attractions and lacks virtues; as a result, he is totally unreliable.           Pause.
So, how can the man created by Waheguru (and with many weaknesses) take credit for something?  All gifts (natural resources) are controlled by Waheguru.  What can the man, who was created by Waheguru, acquire by saying (wishing for that)?  It is completely up to Waheguru to grant or not to grant him his wishes.          1.         The Guru has adorned the world with trees and vegetation, and they are named according to their nature (sweet, sour, bitter, fragrant, distasteful, medicinal, or harmful, etc.). Like the trees, the man bears different kinds of flowers and fruit (good or bad nature) as per his predetermined intent (by Waheguru).   One sows the seeds of his choice and reaps their resulting rewards.        2. 
One’s mind is the architect of his body house.  If the mind (thinking) is impure, so will be his body (his deeds).  With tasteless thinking, his deeds will also be tasteless (useless for others, and for himself too).   One achieves spiritual success only if Waheguru is kind to him (he recites His Naam).  One earns no praise in Waheguru’s Court without His Naam, oh Nanak.  3.32.
