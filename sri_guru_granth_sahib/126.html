(ਅੰਗ 125)॥           ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਜੀਵੈ ਮਰੈ ਪਰਵਾਣੁ॥ ਆਰਜਾ ਨ ਛੀਜੈ ਸਬਦੁ ਪਛਾਣੁ॥ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਮਰੈ ਨ ਕਾਲੁ ਨ ਖਾਏ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਸਚਿ ਸਮਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 2॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਹਰਿ ਦਰਿ ਸੋਭਾ ਪਾਏ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਵਿਚਹੁ ਆਪੁ ਗਵਾਏ॥ ਆਪਿ ਤਰੈ ਕੁਲ ਸਗਲੇ ਤਾਰੇ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਜਨਮੁ ਸਵਾਰਣਿਆ॥ 3॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਦੁਖੁ ਕਦੇ ਨ ਲਗੈ ਸਰੀਰਿ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਹਉਮੈ ਚੂਕੈ ਪੀਰ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਮਨੁ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਫਿਰਿ ਮੈਲੁ ਨ ਲਾਗੈ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਸਹਜਿ ਸਮਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 4॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਮਿਲੈ ਵਡਿਆਈ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਵੈ ਸੋਭਾ ਪਾਈ॥ ਸਦਾ ਅਨੰਦਿ ਰਹੈ ਦਿਨੁ ਰਾਤੀ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਸਬਦੁ ਕਰਾਵਣਿਆ॥5॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਅਨਦਿਨੁ ਸਬਦੇ ਰਾਤਾ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਜੁਗ ਚਾਰੇ ਹੈ ਜਾਤਾ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਵੈ ਸਦਾ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਸਬਦੇ ਭਗਤਿ ਕਰਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 6॥ ਬਾਝੁ ਗੁਰੂ ਹੈ ਅੰਧ ਅੰਧਾਰਾ॥ ਜਮਕਾਲਿ ਗਰਠੇ ਕਰਹਿ ਪੁਕਾਰਾ॥ ਅਨਦਿਨੁ ਰੋਗੀ ਬਿਸਟਾ ਕੇ ਕੀੜੇ ਬਿਸਟਾ ਮਹਿ ਦੁਖੁ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 7॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਆਪੇ ਕਰੇ ਕਰਾਏ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਹਿਰਦੈ ਵੁਠਾ ਆਪਿ ਆਏ॥ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮਿ ਮਿਲੈ ਵਡਿਆਈ ਪੂਰੇ ਗੁਰ ਤੇ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 8॥25॥26॥
Majh Mehla 3:  Gurmukh(i) milaiy, milaayay Aapay. Kaal(u) na johaiy, dookh(u) na suntaapay. Haumein maar(i) bundhan subh toarraiy, Gurmukh(i) Shabad(i) soohaavniya. 1. Huoun vaari jeeo vaari, Hur(i) Hur(i) Naam(u) soohaavniya. Gurmukh(i) gaavaiy, Gurmukh(i) naachaiy, Hur(i) sayti chit(u) laavniya.  1. Ruhaou.
(PAGE 125):          Gurmukh(i) jeevaiy muraiy pervaan(u). Aarja na chheejaiy Shabad puchhaan(u). Gurmukh(i) muraiy na, kaal(u) na khaayay, Gurmukh(i) Sach(i) smaavniya.  2. Gurmukh(i) Hur(i) Dur(i) sobha paayay. Gurmukh(i) vichoun aap(u) guvaayay. Aap(I) turaiy kool suglay taaray, Gurmukh(i) junum(u) suvaarniya.  3. Gurmukh(i) dookh(u) kuday na luggaiy sreer(i). Gurmukh(i) haumein chookaiy, na peer. Gurmukh(i) mun(u) nirmul(u) phir(i) maiyl(u) na laagaiy, Gurmukh(i) sehj(i) smaavniya. 4. Gurmukh(i) Naam(u) milaiy vudiyayee. Gurmukh(i) goon gaavaiy sobha paayee. Suda anund(i) ruhaiy din(u) raati, Gurmukh(i) Sahabad(u) kraavniya. 5. Gurmukh(i) undin(u) Shabaday raata. Gurmukh(i) joog chaaray haiy jaata. Gurmukh(i) goon gaavaiy suda nirmul(u), Shabaday bhagat(i) kraavniya. 6.  Baajh(u) Guru haiy undh undhaara.  Jumkaal(i) grutthay kureh(i) pookaara. Undin roagi, bishta kay keerray, bishta meh(i) dookh(u) paavniya.  7. Gurmukh(i) Aapay kuray kuraayay. Gurmukh(i) hirdaiy voottha Aap(i) aayay. Nanak Naam(u) milaiy vudiyaayee, Pooray Gur tay paavniya.    8.25.26. 
Word Meaning:  Gurmukh(i) = in the shelter of the Guru; a Sikh (spiritually connected with the Guru). Kaal(u) = death.    Johaiy = warns.    Suntaapy = cause suffering.  
Bundhan = bonds.   Soohaavniya = is decorated (with virtues).     1.     Sayti = with.     Chit(u) = mind.        Pause.       Muraiy = kills mind (turns away from Maya).    Arja = (the effect of) age.   Chheejaiy = weakens (spirituality).       2.      Sobha = praise; respect.    Aap(u) = selfishness.     Turaiy = swims (across the sea of Maya).   Suglay = all.    Kool = clans (associates).       3.      Chookaiy = is lifted (gone); is eradicated.    Sehj(i) smaavniya = attains the spiritual state above Maya.       4. Vudiyaayee = respect; praise.    Anund(i) = (in) bliss.        5. Raata = imbued.      Joog Chaaray = (in) all the four (believed) eons – Satyug, Trayta, Doaapar, and Kulyug.    Jaata = recognized; honoured.    Nirmul(u) = pure.       6. Baajh(u) = without.    Jumkaal(u) = angel of death.   Grutthay = gripped.    Pookaara = cries for help.    Bishta = feces; Maya (ignoble deeds).  Keerray = worms.       7. Aapay = (Waheguru) Himself.    Hirdaiy Voottha = takes residence in heart.        8.  
MEANING:  Waheguru can be realized only by the grace of the Guru, but one is connected with the Guru only by the Will of Waheguru, oh my friends.  When one connects with the Guru, fear of death and discomfort can no longer affect them.  His life is adorned (with noble qualities) with Guru’s Shabad, and by controlling his haumein, he breaks all the bonds of Maya.        1.         I am a sacrifice for those who adorn their life with Waheguru’s Naam.  The mind of the individual, who applies his mind to Waheguru’s Naam in the shelter of the Guru, sings His praise, and (his mind) dances in high spirits.         Pause.           An individual, who kills his mind (controls it) from worldly attractions, and leads a life of purity, is approved by Waheguru. He recognizes (evaluates) himself with Guru’s Shabad, and advancing age does not weaken his spirituality.   In the shelter of the Guru, the devotee becomes “one” with Waheguru, and he never dies or becomes a victim of death.          2. A devotee of the Guru overcomes his selfishness and ego, and is honoured in Waheguru’s Court.  He adorns his life with virtues, and not only does he swim across the sea of Maya himself, but he also ferries his associates across the sea.        3. A devotee’s suffering of haumein is eradicated, and he never suffers any mental ailment. His pure mind never catches filth (of sensual pleasures), and he stays absorbed in the spiritual state above the effects of Maya.       4. A devotee of the Guru is honoured because of Waheguru’s Naam in his heart, and he is respected for singing praise of Waheguru’s virtues. (While working for an honest living), A devotee’s only task is to sing Waheguru’s praise day and night, as a result of 
which he stays blissful.          5.         A devotee is imbued in the Guru’s Shabad day and night, and no matter what the eon, he is always recognized (respected).  The devotee is always pure because of his singing of Waheguru’s praise, which he does by Guru’s Shabad.          6.        It is complete darkness (no awareness of Waheguru) without the Guru.  Gripped by the Angel of death (the fear of death), the world cries for help.  The people are (mentally) sick, and like the worms in feces, they suffer day and night due to the problems caused by Maya.        7.         
Waheguru Himself comes to reside in the heart of the Guru’s devotee, who realizes that it is Waheguru Himself who does everything, and makes the people do (whatever they do).   Respect is accorded to Waheguru’s Naam, which can be realized only by the grace of the Guru, oh Nanak.      8.25.26.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ਏਕਾ ਜੋਤਿ ਜੋਤਿ ਹੈ ਸਰੀਰਾ॥ ਸਬਦਿ ਦਿਖਾਏ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਪੂਰਾ॥ ਆਪੇ ਫਰਕੁ ਕੀਤੋਨੁ ਘਟ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਆਪੇ ਬਣਤ ਬਣਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਹਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਜੀਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਹਰਿ ਸਚੇ ਕੇ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਵਣਿਆ॥ ਬਾਝੁ ਗੁਰੂ ਕੋ ਸਹਜੁ ਨ ਪਾਏ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਸਹਜਿ ਸਮਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਤੂ ਆਪੇ ਸੋਹਹਿ ਆਪੇ ਜਗ ਮੋਹਹਿ॥ ਤੂ ਆਪੇ ਨਦਰੀ ਜਗਤ ਪਰੋਵਹਿ॥ ਤੂੰ ਆਪੇ ਦੁਖੁ ਸੁਖੁ ਦੇਵਹਿ ਕਰਤੇ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਹਰਿ ਦੇਖਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 2॥ ਆਪੇ ਕਰਤਾ ਕਰੇ ਕਰਾਏ॥ ਆਪੇ ਸਬਦੁ ਗੁਰ ਮੰਨਿ ਵਸਾਏ॥ ਸਬਦੇ ਉਪਜੈ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਬਾਣੀ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਆਖਿ ਸੁਣਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 3॥ ਆਪੇ ਕਰਤਾ ਆਪੇ ਭੁਗਤਾ ॥ ਬੰਧਨ ਤੋੜੇ ਸਦਾ ਹੈ ਮੁਕਤਾ॥ ਸਦਾ ਮੁਕਤੁ ਆਪੇ ਹੈ ਸਚਾ ਆਪੇ ਅਲਖੁ ਲਖਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 4॥ ਆਪੇ ਮਾਇਆ ਆਪੇ ਛਾਇਆ॥ ਆਪੇ ਮੋਹੁ ਸਭੁ ਜਗਤੁ ਉਪਾਇਆ॥ ਆਪੇ ਗੁਣਦਾਤਾ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਵੈ ਆਪੇ ਆਖਿ ਸੁਣਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 5॥ ਆਪੇ ਕਰੇ ਕਰਾਵੇ ਆਪੇ॥ ਆਪੇ ਥਾਪਿ ਉਥਾਪੇ ਆਪੇ॥ ਤੁਝ ਤੇ ਬਾਹਰਿ ਕਛੂ ਨ ਹੋਵੈ ਤੂੰ ਆਪੇ ਕਾਰੇ ਲਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 6॥ ਆਪੇ ਮਾਰੇ ਆਪਿ ਜੀਵਾਏ॥ ਆਪੇ ਮੇਲੇ ਮੇਲਿ ਮਿਲਾਏ॥ ਸੇਵਾ ਤੇ ਸਦਾ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਸਹਜਿ ਸਮਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 7॥
