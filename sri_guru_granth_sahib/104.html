(ਅੰਗ 103)॥   ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਸਫਲੁ ਸੁ ਬਾਣੀ ਜਿਤੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਵਖਾਣੀ॥ ਗੁਰ ਪਰਸਾਦਿ ਕਿਨੈ ਵਿਰਲੈ ਜਾਣੀ॥ ਧੰਨੁ ਸੋ ਵੇਲਾ ਜਿਤੁ ਹਰਿ ਗਾਵਤ ਸੁਨਣਾ ਆਏ ਤੇ ਪਰਵਾਨਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਸੇ ਨੇਤ੍ਰ ਪਰਵਾਣੁ ਜਿਨੀ ਦਰਸਨੁ ਪੇਖਾ॥ ਸੇ ਕਰ ਭਲੇ ਜਿਨੀ ਹਰਿ ਜਸੁ ਲੇਖਾ ॥ ਸੇ ਚਰਣ ਸੁਹਾਵੇ ਜੋ ਹਰਿ ਮਾਰਗਿ ਚਲੇ ਹਉ ਬਲਿ ਤਿਨ ਸੰਗਿ ਪਛਾਣਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਸੁਣਿ ਸਾਜਨ ਮੇਰੇ ਮੀਤ ਪਿਆਰੇ॥ ਸਾਧ ਸੰਗਿ ਖਿਨ ਮਾਹਿ ਉਧਾਰੇ॥ ਕਿਲਵਿਖ ਕਾਟਿ ਹੋਆ ਮਨੁ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਮਿਟਿ ਗਏ ਆਵਣ ਜਾਣਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 3॥ ਦੁਇ ਕਰ ਜੋੜਿ ਇਕੁ ਬਿਨਉ ਕਰੀਜੈ॥ ਕਰਿ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਡੁਬਦਾ ਪਥਰੁ ਲੀਜੈ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਕਉ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਭਏ ਕਿਰਪਾਲਾ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਨਾਨਕ ਮਨਿ ਭਾਣਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥22॥29॥
Majh Mehla 5: Suful(u) su Bani jit(u) Naam(u) vukhaani.  Gur persad(i) kinaiy virlaiy jaani. Dhunn(u) so vayla jit(u) Hur(i) gaavut soon-na ,aayay tay pervaana jeeo.  1. Say naytur pervaan(u) jinhi dershun(u) paykha. Say ker bhulay, jinhi Hur(i) jus(u) laykha. Say churn soohaavay jo Hur(i) maarug(i) chulay, houn bul(i) tin sung(i) puchhaana jeo. 2.  Soon(i) saajun mayray meet piyaaray. Sadh sung(i) khin maah(i) oodhaaray. Kilvikh kaat(i) hoa mun(u) nirmul(u), mit(i) guyyay aavun jaana jeeo. 3. Doyay ker joarr(i) ikk(u) binou kreejaiy. Kur(i) kirpa doobda puthur(u) leejaiy. Nanak kuou Prubh bhuyay kirpaala, Prubh Nanak mun(i) bhaana jeeo.  4.22.29.
Word Meaning:   Suful(u) = successful; fruitful.    Bani = language; words (of praise).   Vukhaani = utter; recite.  Virlaiy = some rare (individual).    Dhunn(u) = worthy of respect; great.    Pervaana = acceptable.     1.      Say = those.  Naytur = eyes.   Dershun = sight.   Paykha = saw.   Ker = hands. Bhulay = good; noble.  Jus(u) = praise.    Soohaavay = pretty; successful.    Maarug(i) = path.  Bul(i) = sacrifice.    Sung(i) = in the company.   Puchhana = realize.       2.      Saajun = Meet = friend.    Khin = moment.     Oodhaaray = salvages.   Kilvikh = sins.  Mit(i) = ended.    Aavun Jaana = coming and going (rebirths).       3.
Ker = hands.   Binou = prayer; imploring.    Puthur(u) = rock-like heavy (with evil deeds).   Leeajaiy = save.    Bhuyay = became.    Bhaana = likes.      4.
MEANING:  Only those words are worthwhile which are in Waheguru’s praise.  But only a few rare individuals have realized (made a connection with) such Bani by the Guru’s grace.  Wonderful is the time when one sings, or listens to the praise of Waheguru; such individuals are recognized in Waheguru’s Court.         1.           Those eyes are lucky (worthy of respect) which have had the “sight” of Waheguru.  Those hands are lucky which write praise of Waheguru. Those feet are pretty (lucky) which walk on the path leading to Waheguru. I offer my life to those in whose company I may get connected with Waheguru.         2.
Listen to me, my dear friend; Waheguru salvages individuals in the company of the Guru in a moment. Their minds become pure, and all their sins are destroyed, and they are never born again.        3.          With folded hands, I beg You, oh Waheguru, “save me - the stone-like heavy rock (with the weight of my sins); I am sinking in the sea of Maya (Worldly attractions)”.  The kind Waheguru listened to my prayer, and now Waheguru is my most favourite friend, oh Nanak.  4.22. 29.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਬਾਣੀ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਤੇਰੀ॥ ਸੁਣਿ ਸੁਣਿ ਹੋਵੈ ਪਰਮ ਗਤਿ ਮੇਰੀ॥ ਜਲਨਿ ਬੁਝੀ ਸੀਤਲੁ ਹੋਏ ਮਨੂਆ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਕਾ ਦਰਸਨੁ ਪਾਏ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਸੂਖੁ ਭਇਆ ਦੁਖੁ ਦੂਰਿ ਪਰਾਨਾ॥ ਸੰਤ ਰਸਨ ਹਰਿਨਾਮੁ ਵਖਾਨਾ॥ ਜਲ ਥਲ ਨੀਰਿ ਭਰੇ ਸਰ ਸੁਭਰ ਬਿਰਥਾ ਕੋਇ ਨ ਜਾਏ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਦਇਆ ਧਾਰੀ ਤਿਨਿ ਸਿਰਜਨਹਾਰੇ॥ ਜੀਅ ਜੰਤ ਸਗਲੇ ਪ੍ਰਤਿਪਾਰੇ॥ ਮਿਹਰਵਾਨ ਕਿਰਪਾਲ ਦਇਆਲਾ ਸਗਲੇ ਤ੍ਰਿਪਤਿ ਅਘਾਏ ਜੀਉ॥ 3॥ ਵਣੁ ਤ੍ਰਿਣੁ ਤ੍ਰਿਭਵਣੁ ਕੀਤੋਨੁ ਹਰਿਆ॥ ਕਰਣਹਾਰਿ ਖਿਨ ਭੀਤਰਿ ਕਰਿਆ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਨਾਨਕ ਤਿਸੈ ਅਰਾਧੇ ਮਨ ਕੀ ਆਸ ਪੁਜਾਏ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥23॥30
Majh Mehla 5: Amrit Bani Hur(i) Hur(i) Tayri. Soon(i) soon(i) hovaiy perm gut(i) mayri. Juln(i) boojhee seetul(u) hoyay munooa, Satgur ka dershun(u) paayay jeeo. 1. Sookh(u) bhuyya dookh(u) door(i) praana. Sunt rusn Hur(i) Naam(u) vukhaana. Jul thul neer(i) bhuray sur soobhur, birtha koyay na jaayay jeeo. 2. Duyya dhaari tin(i) Sirjunhaaray.  Jea junt suglay pritpaaray. Mehrvaan kirpal duyyala, suglay triput(i) aghaayay jeeo. 3.  Vun(u), trin(u), tirbhuvun(u), keeton(u) huriya. Kernhaar(i) khin bheetur(i) kuriya.  Gurmukh(i) Nanak tisaiy araadhay, mun kee aas poojaayay jeeo. 4.23.30.
Word Meaning:  Amrit Bani = Amrit-like (spiritual life-sustaining) words of praise.   Perm Gut(i) = supreme spiritual state.   Juln(i) = burning of heart (suffering of mind).   Seetul(u) = calm; peaceful.       Munooa = mind.      1.     Prana = moved away.   Rusn = tongue.   Vukhaana = utter; recite.  Neer(i) = (with) water (Waheguru’s Naam-Amrit).    Sur = pools; lakes.  Soobhur = full to the brim.   Birtha = without; empty.     Jaayay = place.       2.
Duyya = kindness.    Sirjunhaar = the Creator (Waheguru).   Pritpaaray = looks after; raises.  Mehrvaan = Kirpal = Duyyala = kind; sympathetic.   Triput(i) Aghaayay = content.     3.
Vun(u) = forest.  Trin(u) = grass; bushes.   Tirbhuvun = three-layered universe.   Kernhaar(i) = the Creator (Waheguru).  Khin Bheetur(i) = in a moment.   Araadhay = worships.    4.
MEANING: The words of Your praise are the real Amrit, oh Waheguru!  By listening to them, I attain the supreme spiritual state (above the effects of Maya). On seeing the Satguru (connecting with Him), the burning (restlessness) of my mind has calmed down, and my mind has become peaceful.         1.          With the grace of the Guru, I am reciting Waheguru’s Naam with my tongue as a result of which, all my troubles have gone away, and it is all comforts for me now.  I feel as if all the waters, the land, and the lakes are full to the brim with the Naam-Amrit, and there is no place without it.           2.         With the kindness of the Creator, all the living beings are being looked after well, and they are fully contented.         3.
All the life in the forests, in the grass, the bushes, and in the whole universe has bloomed, and it did not take the Creator even a moment to do so. Taking shelter of the Guru, I worship Waheguru, which fulfills the wishes of every mind, oh Nanak! 4.23.30

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 5: ਤੂ ਮੇਰਾ ਪਿਤਾ ਤੂ ਹੈ ਮੇਰਾ ਮਾਤਾ॥ ਤੂ ਮੇਰਾ ਬੰਧਪੁ ਤੂ ਮੇਰਾ ਭਰਾਤਾ॥ ਤੂ ਮੇਰਾ ਰਾਖਾ ਸਭਨੀ ਥਾਈ ਤਾ ਭਉ ਕੇਹਾ ਕਾੜਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਤੁਮਰੀ ਕ੍ਰਿਪਾ ਤੇ ਤੁਧੁ ਪਛਾਣਾ॥ ਤੂ ਮੇਰੀ ਓਟ ਤੂ ਹੈ ਮੇਰਾ ਮਾਣਾ॥ ਤੁਝ ਬਿਨੁ ਦੂਜਾ ਅਵਰੁ ਨ ਕੋਈ ਸਭੁ ਤੇਰਾ ਖੇਲੁ ਅਖਾੜਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 2॥ ਜੀਅ ਜੰਤ ਸਭਿ ਤੁਧੁ ਉਪਾਏ॥ ਜਿਤੁ ਜਿਤੁ ਭਾਣਾ ਤਿਤੁ ਤਿਤੁ ਲਾਏ॥ ਸਭ ਕਿਛੁ ਕੀਤਾ ਤੇਰਾ ਹੋਵੈ ਨਾਹੀ ਕਿਛੁ ਅਸਾੜਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 3॥ ਨਾਮੁ ਧਿਆਇ ਮਹਾ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਾਇਆ॥ ਹਰਿ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਇ ਮੇਰਾ ਮਨੁ ਸੀਤਲਾਇਆ॥ ਗੁਰਿ ਪੂਰੈ ਵਜੀ ਵਾਧਾਈ ਨਾਨਕ ਜਿਤਾ ਬਿਖਾੜਾ ਜੀਉ॥ 4॥24॥31॥
Majh Mehla 5:  Tu mayra pita Tu haiy mayra maata. Tu mayra bundhup(u) Tu mayra bhraata.  Tu mayra raakha subhni thaayeen, ta bhuou kayha kaarra jeeo. 1.  Toomri kirpa tay Toodh(u) puchhaana. Tu mayri oat, Tu haiy mayra maana. Toojh bin(u) dooja uver(u) na koyee, subh(u) Tayra khayl(u) akhaarra jeeo.  2.  Jea junt subh(i) Toodh(u) oopaayay. Jit(u) jit(u) bhaana, tit(u) tit(u) laayay. Subh kichh(u) keeta Tayra hovaiy, naahi kichh(u) asaarra jeeo. 3.  Naam(u) dhiyaayay maha sookh(u) paaya. Hur(i) goon gaayay mayra mun seetlaaya. Gur(i) Pooraiy vuji vadhaayee, Nanak jita bikhaarra jeeo.  4.24.31.
Word Meaning:  Bundhup(u) = relative; friend.  Bhraata = brother.  Raakha = protector; guard.   Bhuou = fear.   Kaarra = worry; irritation.      1.        Puchhana = I recognize; get connected.     Oat = support; shelter.    Maana = pride.  Uver(u) = other; else.     Akhaarra = ring (for sports.      2.      Jea Junt = living beings.    Oopaayay= created.   Jit(u) = as.   Bhaayay = as (You) wish.   Tit(u) = the same.   Asaarra = ours.       3.     Maha = great.   Seetlaaya = became calm (peaceful).   Vadhaayee = joy; bliss.   Bikhaarra = very difficult task (to fight against the evils).4.       
MEANING:   You are my father, and You are my mother, oh Waheguru!  You are my friend, and You are my brother; when You are my guard everywhere, why should I be afraid of anything, and why should I have any worries or irritations!          1.
I realize You (connect with You) with Your kindness.  You are my shelter, and You are my pride.  There is no other power than You, and the whole world is Your sport, and the sports ring (You enjoy Your Creation just as a sportsman enjoys his sports), oh Waheguru!      2.
You have created all the living beings, and they do exactly what You wish them to do, oh Waheguru.  Whatever happens in the world happens according to Your Huqam; we have no control over it.           3.          I attain great comfort by reciting Your Naam (remembering You), and my mind finds peace when I sing Your virtues.  Because of the kindness of the Perfect Guru, my mind is overjoyed, and I have won the most difficult battle (fighting against the demons and the attractions of Maya) of my life, oh Nanak.   4.24.31

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 5॥ ਜੀਅ ਪ੍ਰਾਣ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਮਨਹਿ ਅਧਾਰਾ॥ ਭਗਤ ਜੀਵਹਿ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਇ ਅਪਾਰਾ॥ ਗੁਣ ਨਿਧਾਨ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮਾ ਹਰਿ ਧਿਆਇ ਧਿਆਇ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਜੀਉ॥ 1॥ ਮਨਸਾ ਧਾਰਿ ਜੋ ਘਰ ਤੇ ਆਵੈ॥ ਸਾਧਸੰਂਗਿ ਜਨਮੁ ਮਰਣੁ ਮਿਟਾਵੈ॥
