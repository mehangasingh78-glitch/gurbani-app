Majh Mehla 3:  Aithaiy sachay su aagaiy saachay. Mun(u) sucha, Sachaiy Shabad(i) raachay. Sacha sayveh(i) Sach(u) kmaaveh(i), Sacho Sach(u) kmaavniya.  1. Huoun vaari jeeo vaari, Sacha Naam(u) munn(i) vusaavniya. Sachay sayveh(i) Sach(i) smaaveh(i), Sachay kay goon gaavniya. 1. Ruhaou.  Pundit purrheh(i) saad(u) na paaveh(i). Doojaiy bhaayay Maya mun(u) bhermaveh(i). Maya moh(i) subh soodh guvaayee, kur(i) uvgun puchhotaavniya. 2.  Satgur(u) milaiy ta Tut(u) paayay. Hur(i) ka Naam(u) munn(i) vusaayay.

Word Meaning:  Aithaiy = here (in this world).    Sachay = pure; detached from Maya.    Aagaiy = there (in the next world).    Raachay = absorbed.   Kmaaveh(i) = earn; acquire.     1.              Munn(i) Vusaavniya = make mind the home (for Waheguru).       Smaaveh(i) = merge with; become “one” with.          Pause.       Saad(u) = (enjoy) taste.    Doojaiy Bhaayay = in the love for the other (Maya).   Bhermaveh(i) = captivates.   Moh(i)=   in attachment.    Soodh(u) = awareness; wisdom.     Uvgun = ignoble deeds.    Puchhotaavniya = repent.       2.       Tut(u) = reality (Waheguru’s Naam).   Muraiy = die (as far as Maya is concerned); kill desires.         3.         Kilvikh = sins.     Krodh = rage.    Nivaaray = get rid of.    Oordhaaray = bear in heart.  Ruttay = imbued.     Bairaagi = detached from Maya.      4.     Ruttan = precious stone (Waheguru’s Naam).   Tribhidh(i) = having three qualities.   Munsa = desires.    Moani = ascetics who stay quiet for long periods of time.    Chautha Pud = the fourth (spiritual) state above Maya, which has three effects.    Saar = knowledge; value.        5.      Rungay = dyed.   Say Jun = those devotees.    Raatay = imbued.   Ut(i) Apaara = extremely dark (fast).    Rus(i) = while relishing.        6.       Ridh(i) Sidh(i) = supernatural powers.     Sunjum = efforts to control desires.    Soyee = the same (Waheguru).     Kaar = business; deeds.      7.
Thaapay = creates.   Oothaapay = destroys.   Put(i) = respect; dignity.     8.
MEANING:  Those whose minds have been purified (detached from Maya) in this world are also pure in the next world.  They are imbued with the love for Guru’s Shabad, and have the eternal Waheguru in their heart.  They serve Waheguru, acquire His Naam, and stay connected with Him by singing His praise.          1. 
I am a sacrifice for those who keep Waheguru’s Naam in mind all the time. They serve the eternal Waheguru, merge in Him (get fully connected), and sing His virtues.   Pause.  
             The pundits do read (their religious books), but find no taste for Waheguru because Maya, which they love, captivates them.  With attachment to Maya, they lose all their wisdom and awareness (of Waheguru) and repent for their unholy deeds.      2.
One realizes truth (becomes pure) only if he is connected with the Guru, who enables him to have Waheguru appear in his heart (mind).  One overcomes his worldly desires by following the Guru’s Shabad and controls his mind, whereby he finds the door to emancipation.          3.          An individual who bears the Guru’s Shabad in mind washes away his sins and gets rid of his rage.  Those who are imbued in Waheguru’s love are always detached from Maya and merge in Waheguru by overpowering their haumein.         4.                  Waheguru’s highly valuable Naam is always there in the human mind, but one can access it only by connecting with Him.  The human mind always craves the pleasures of Maya, which has three effects.  The Pundits and the sages exhaust themselves by reading their holy books, but do not enjoy them because their minds are always wandering around the three effects of Maya; so, they have no knowledge of the Fourth spiritual state, which is above the three effects of Maya.         5.
Waheguru Himself colours His devotees' minds with His love.   Following Guru’s Shabad, they get imbued in Waheguru’s love.  The colour of Waheguru’s love on their mind is so strong that they sing His praise, enjoying its full taste.           6. 
A devotee, who takes the Guru’s shelter, realizes that Waheguru is everything for him, including the supernatural powers, truthfulness, and control over sensual pleasures.   Such a devotee realizes that it is the Divine Knowledge acquired through Waheguru’s Naam that results in his freedom from Maya.  So, the devotee’s routine is to stay connected with Waheguru to become “one” with Him.         7.
Such a devotee realizes that it is Waheguru who creates, and then destroys (the world).  For the devotee, Waheguru is his caste (status in society), his dignity, and everything else.   The devotee recites Waheguru’s Naam all the time, whereby he is completely absorbed in Him, oh Nanak.    8.12.13

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ ਉਤਪਤਿ ਪਰਲਉ ਸਬਦੇ ਹੋਵੈ॥ ਸਬਦੇ ਹੀ ਫਿਰਿ ਓਪਤਿ ਹੋਵੈ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਵਰਤੈ ਸਭੁ ਆਪੇ ਸਚਾ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਉਪਾਇ ਸਮਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਹਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਜੀਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਗੁਰੁ ਪੂਰਾ ਮੰਨਿ ਵਸਾਵਣਿਆ॥ ਗੁਰ ਤੇ ਸਾਤਿ ਭਗਤਿ ਕਰੇ ਦਿਨੁ ਰਾਤੀ ਗੁਣ ਕਹਿ ਗੁਣੀ ਸਮਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਧਰਤੀ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਪਾਣੀ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਪਵਣੁ ਬੈਸੰਤਰੁ ਖੇਲੈ ਵਿਡਾਣੀ॥ ਸੋ ਨਿਗੁਰਾ ਜੋ ਮਰਿ ਮਰਿ ਜੰਮੈ ਨਿਗੁਰੇ ਆਵਣ ਜਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 2॥ ਤਿਨਿ ਕਰਤੈ ਇਕੁ ਖੇਲੁ ਰਚਾਇਆ॥ ਕਾਇਆ ਸਰੀਰੈ ਵਿਚਿ ਸਭੁ ਕਿਛੁ ਪਾਇਆ॥ ਸਬਦਿ ਭੇਦਿ ਕੋਈ ਮਹਲੁ ਪਾਇ ਮਹਲੇ ਮਹਲਿ ਬੁਲਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 3॥ ਸਚਾ ਸਾਹੁ ਸਚੇ ਵਣਜਾਰੇ॥ ਸਚੁ ਵਣੰਜਹਿ ਗੁਰ ਹੇਤਿ ਅਪਾਰੇ॥ ਸਚੁ ਵਿਹਾਝਹਿ ਸਚੁ ਕਮਾਵਹਿ ਸਚੋ ਸਚੁ ਕਮਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 4॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਰਾਸੀ ਕੋ ਵਥੁ ਕਿਉ ਪਾਏ॥ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਭੂਲੇ ਲੋਕ ਸਬਾਏ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਰਾਸੀ ਸਭ ਖਾਲੀ ਚਲੇ ਖਾਲੀ ਜਾਇ ਦੁਖੁ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 5॥ ਇਕਿ ਸਚੁ ਵਣੰਜਹਿ ਗੁਰ ਸਬਦਿ ਪਿਆਰੇ॥ਆਪਿ ਤਰਹਿ ਸਗਲੇ ਕੁਲ ਤਾਰੇ॥ ਆਏ ਸੇ ਪਰਵਾਣੁ ਹੋਏ ਮਿਲਿ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਮ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 6॥ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਵਸਤੁ ਮੂੜਾ ਬਾਹਰ ਭਾਲੇ॥ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਅੰਧੇ ਫਿਰਹਿ ਬੇਤਾਲੇ॥ ਜਿਥੈ ਵਥੁ ਹੋਵੈ ਤਿਥਹੁ ਕੋਇ ਨ ਪਾਵੈ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਭਰਮਿ ਭੁਲਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 7॥ ਆਪੇ ਦੇਵੇ ਸਬਦਿ ਬੁਲਾਏ॥ ਮਹਲੀ ਮਹਲਿ ਸਹਜ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਾਏ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮਿ ਮਿਲੈ ਵਡਿਆਈ ਆਪੇ ਸੁਣਿ ਸੁਣਿ ਧਿਆਵਣਿਆ॥  8॥13॥14॥
Majh Mehla 3: Ootput(i) perlou Shabaday hovaiy.  Shabday hee phir(i) oput(i) hovaiy. Gurmukh(i) vertaiy subh(u) aapay Sacha, Gurmukh(i) oopaayay smaavniya.  1. Huoun vaari jeeo vaari, Gur(u) Poora munn(i) vusaavniya. Gur tay shaant(i) bhugut(i) kuray din(u) raati, goon keh(i) Gooni smaavniya. 1. Ruhaou.   Gurmukh(i) dhurti Gurmukh(i) paani. Gurmukh(i) puvun(u) baiysuntar(u) khaylaiy vidaani.  So nigura jo mur(i) mur(i) jummaiy, niguray aavun jaavniya. 2. Tin(i) Kertaiy ikk(u) khayl(u) ruchaaya. Kaayaan sreeraiy vich(i) subh(u) kichh(u) paaya.  Shabad(i) bhayd(i) koyee Mehl(u) paayay, Mehlay Mehl(i) boolaavniya.  3. Sacha Shah(u) Sachay vunjaaray. Sach(u) vunjeh(i) Gur hayt(i) apparay. Sach(u) vihaajheh(i) Sach kmaaveh(i), Sacho Sach(u) kmaavniya. 4. Bin(u) raaseen ko Vuth(u) kiyoun paayay. Munmukh bhoolay loak subaayay. Bin(u) raaseen subh khaali chullay, khaali jaayay dookh(u) paavniya.  5. Ikk(i) Sach(u) vunjeh(i) Gur Shabad piyaaray. Word Meaning: Ootput(i) = Oput(i) = production; creation. Perlou = destruction.  Shabaday = Waheguru’s Huqam.   Vertaiy = operates.   Smaavniya = merge into.  1. 
Shaant(i) = peace of mind.  Gooni = the source of virtues (Waheguru).       Pause.
Baiysuntar(u) = fire.    Khalaiy Vidaani = does amazing things.    Nigura = without Guru.    Aavun Jaavniya = comes and goes (takes birth and dies).       2.
Tin(i) = that.   Kertaiy = creator.   Khayl(u) Ruchaaya = Created world like a play (amazing thing).  Kaayaan = body.   Bhayd(i) = piercing of the mind so as to unlock secrets.    Mehlay = the Lord of the mansion.    Mehl(I) = in the mansion (of Waheguru).        3.       Shah(u) = money lender; ruler (Waheguru).    Vunjaaray = traders.   Vunjeh(i) = (deal in).  Hayt(i) = for.    Kmaaveh(i) = do business.   Sacho Sach(u) = pure Truth.        4.       Raas(een) = capital; investment; hard work (of singing Waheguru’s praise).  Ko = anyone.   Vuth = (precious) article (Waheguru’s Naam).    Kiyoun = why; how.    Khaali = empty (handed).      5.     Ikk(u) = some.   Vunjeh(i) = deal in.   Tureh(i) = swim across (the sea of Maya).  Kool = clan (associates).    Say = those.    Pervaan = approved; honoured.       6.  
Vust = Vuth= (precious) article (Waheguru’s Naam).   Moorra = foolish.   Bhaalay = searches for.   Baytaalay = out of step (lost).   Tithoun = from there.        7.
Boolaayay = calls in (connects with).    Mehli = the Lord of the residence (Waheguru).   Sehj Sookh(u) = the bliss of the higher spiritual state.   Vudiyaayee = respect.       8.
MEANING:  Waheguru creates and destroys the universe with His Huqam (Laws) and recreates it with His Huqam again.  A true devotee of the Guru believes that Waheguru is everywhere and present in His creation.         1.
I am a sacrifice for all those who make their hearts a place for the Perfect Guru to reside in (are connected with Him).   They acquire mental bliss in the Guru’s shelter, and worship Waheguru day and night. One merges with Waheguru, which is the source of all virtues, only by singing the praise of His virtues.       Pause.
The devotee of the Guru has the firm belief that the earth, the water, the air, and the fire are under the control of Waheguru, which enables them to perform amazing feats. (In addition to doing all the favourable things for life, they also do some unusual things. For example, the Earth experiences earthquakes and undergoes major changes; water causes floods and huge waves in the oceans, etc.  A non-believer in the Guru and in the amazing feats of Waheguru is born again, and again, and will continue to do so.           2.                The creator has done an amazing thing: he has put everything in the human body (the ability to know the secrets of the universe).  Someone who is able to unlock these secrets by deeply pondering on Guru’s Shabad gets access to Waheguru’s Mansion (gets connected with Him) when the Lord Himself calls him inside His Mansion         3.               The eternal Waheguru is like a banker (owner of a business), and all the human beings are like traders who trade in His eternal Naam.  They do so with love for the Guru (in His shelter).  They do business in the eternal Truth (Waheguru’s Naam), and eventually, merge in Him.          4.         How can anyone acquire the precious thing (Waheguru’s Naam) without the right investment (reciting Waheguru’s Naam and obeying His Will)?  All the followers of their minds are completely misguided.  Without the right investment (recitation of Waheguru’s Naam), everybody leaves this world empty-handed (without any spirituality) and suffers as a result.           5.
Some deal in Waheguru’s Naam by following the Shabad of the dear Guru. They swim across the sea of Maya (find salvation), and also ferry all their associates across. They are approved by Waheguru and enjoy bliss in their union with their Dear (Lord).       6.
Waheguru is present in one’s mind, but the foolish looks for Him outside (in isolated places).  The followers of their mind are blind (completely ignorant), and spend their lives like a singer who is completely out of tune.  The follower of his mind is lost in misbeliefs, and does not look for the desired thing (Waheguru’s Naam) where it is present (inside his mind).         7.          Waheguru Himself invites (inspires) the individuals of His choice, and bestows His Naam in the form of Guru’s Shabad.  They enjoy the bliss of the high spiritual state in their union with Waheguru.  Waheguru Himself listens (pays attention) to His devotees when they sing His Naam, and honours them, oh Nanak!     8.13.14.
