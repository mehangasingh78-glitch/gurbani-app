Word Meaning: Surkaar = governance. Kaar Kmaavni = do the deeds. Ghut(i) = in the house (heart). Purgut(u) = appears. Vusaiy = resides. Soyay = fame. Vichhurreh(i) = separated. Nij = own. Ghur(i) = house (heart). Vaasa = residence. 1. Ram (u) = a name for God. Uver(u) = other. Nirmula = clean. Milaava = united. Pause. Doojay = other (Maya). Bhaayay = in love. Aavaiy jaayay = comes and goes (reborn). Vertda = operates; serves. Smaayay = present (mixed uniformly). Duyaal = kind. 2. Jotki = fortune teller. Vaad = debate. Boodhi = wisdom. Bhuvee = turned around. Boojhyee = figure out. Lobh = greed. Vikaar(u) = evil thoughts. Lukh chauraasee = 8.4 million (Hindus believe that there are 8.4 million life forms in the world). Bhurmday = go through 8.4 M life forms. Khooaar(u) = suffer humiliation without any success. Poorub(i) Likhiya = prewritten, predestined. Kmmavna = perform deeds. Maytunhaar(u) = eraser; remover. 3. Gakhrri = difficult. Aap(u) guvaayay = get rid of selfishness. Paarus(i) = with touchstone. Pursiyaiy = by touching. Jot(i) = Waheguru’s light. 4. Bhookha = hungry. Mut = warn against doing something. Siri = created. Adhaar(u) = support (food). Nirbhou = fearless (Waheguru). Dyaal(u) = kind. Saar = looks after. Mokh = freedom from Maya. Dooaar(u) = door. 5. MEANING: Everybody belongs to Waheguru, who governs the universe (nobody is outside His control). (So, the only thing to do is) to act on Guru’s advice, as a result of which Waheguru appears in one’s heart. The individuals who harbour Waheguru in their hearts become like Him (Sachay), and their fame becomes real (everlasting). Those who are connected to Waheguru never experience separation from Him because Waheguru makes their heart His dwelling. 1. I have no faith in anybody other than You, oh my Waheguru. A pure (pious) Satguru is a manifestation of Waheguru, who can be realized through His Shabad. Pause. Waheguru Himself connects the individuals with Him who use Guru’s Shabad (sing Waheguru’s praise) to connect with Him, and they always stay connected. There is no other way to realize Waheguru – people who use other means are never successful in attaining freedom from Maya and take rebirths again and again. God alone looks after everyone, and He alone is omnipresent. The individual, whom Waheguru is kind to, recites His Naam by taking refuge in the Guru, and becomes “one” with Him. 2. Pundits and fortune tellers read the holy books only to participate in discussions and debates. Their twisted wisdom and intelligence cannot find Truth because their minds are full of greed and evil thoughts. They go through the 8.4 million life forms and suffer in vain. However, they cannot be fully blamed because they are doing just what has been predestined for them, and which nobody can erase. 3. It is very difficult to serve Satguru because one has to get rid of his selfishness and his wisdom first, and offer his head (thinking) to Satguru. If one seeks shelter of Satguru and obeys His Shabad (Teachings), he realizes Waheguru, and all his service to the Guru becomes successful. By touching the touchstone (taking shelter of Satguru), one also gets transformed into a touchstone (becomes like Him), and his soul becomes “one” with the Light Source – Waheguru. But one connects with Satguru only if it is predestined for him. 4. Do not ever cry (complain) about your hunger, and do not ever pray for food; Waheguru, who has created 8.4 million life forms, provides food to each and every one (without asking). The fearless Waheguru is kind and looks after everyone. But one realizes this fact only after taking the Guru’s refuge, when he also gets rid of the shackles of Maya, oh Nanak. 5.3.36. ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ ਜਿਨੀ ਸੁਣਿ ਕੈ ਮੰਨਿਆ ਤਿਨਾ ਨਿਜ ਘਰਿ ਵਾਸੁ॥ ਗੁਰਮਤੀ ਸਾਲਾਹਿ ਸਚੁ ਹਰਿ ਪਾਇਆ ਗੁਣਤਾਸੁ॥ ਸਬਦਿ ਰਤੇ ਸੇ ਨਿਰਮਲੇ ਹਉ ਸਦ ਬਲਿਹਾਰੈ ਜਾਸੁ॥ਹਿਰਦੈ ਜਿਨ ਕੈ ਹਰਿ ਵਸੈ ਤਿਤੁ ਘਟਿ ਹੈ ਪਰਗਾਸੁ॥ 1॥ਮਨ ਮੇਰੇ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਧਿਆਇ॥ ਧੁਰਿ ਮਸਤਕਿ ਜਿਨ ਕਉ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਸੇ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਰਹੇ ਲਿਵ ਲਾਇ॥1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਹਰਿ ਸੰਤਹੁ ਦੇਖਹੁ ਨਦਰਿ ਕਰਿ ਨਿਕਟਿ ਵਸੈ ਭਰਪੂਰਿ॥ ਗੁਰਮਤਿ ਜਿਨੀ ਪਛਾਣਿਆ ਸੇ ਦੇਖਹਿ ਸਦਾ ਹਦੂਰਿ॥ ਜਿਨ ਗੁਣ ਤਿਨ ਸਦ ਮਨਿ ਵਸੈ ਅਉਗੁਣਵੰਤਿਆ ਦੂਰਿ॥ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਗੁਣ ਤੈ ਬਾਹਰੇ ਬਿਨੁ ਨਾਵੈ ਮਰਦੈ ਝੂਰਿ॥ 2॥ ਜਿਨ ਸਬਦਿ ਗੁਰੂ ਸੁਣਿ ਮੰਨਿਆ ਤਿਨ ਮਨਿ ਧਿਆਇਆ ਹਰਿ ਸੋਇ॥ਅਨਦਿਨੁ ਭਗਤੀ ਰਤਿਆ ਮਨੁ ਤਨੁ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਹੋਇ॥ ਕੂੜਾ ਰੰਗੁ ਕਸੁੰਭ ਕਾ ਬਿਨਸਿ ਜਾਇ ਦੁਖੁ ਰੋਇ॥ ਜਿਸੁ ਅੰਦਰਿ ਨਾਮ ਪ੍ਰਗਾਸੁ ਹੈ ਓਹੁ ਸਦਾ ਸਦਾ ਥਿਰੁ ਹੋਇ॥ 3॥
