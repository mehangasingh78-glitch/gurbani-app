(ਅੰਗ 126)॥     ਆਪੇ ਊਚਾ ਉਚੋ ਹੋਈ॥ ਜਿਸੁ ਆਪਿ ਵਿਖਾਲੇ ਸੁ ਵੇਖੈ ਕੋਈ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮੁ ਵਸੈ ਘਟ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਆਪੇ ਵੇਖਿ ਵਿਖਾਲਣਿਆ॥ 8॥26॥27॥
Majh Mehla 3:   Aika Jot(i), Jot(i) haiy sreera. Shabad(i) dikhaayay Satgur(u) Poora.  Aapay ferq(u) keeton(u) ghut untar(i), Aapay bunut bunaavniya. 1. Huoun vaari jeeo vaari, Hur(i) Sachay kay goon gaavniya. Baajh Guru ko sehj(u) na paayay, Gurmukh(i) sehj(i) smaavniya. 1. Ruhaou.  Tu Aapay soheh(i) Aapay jug moheh(i). Tu Aapay nudri jugat proveh(i). Tu Aapay dookh(u) sookh(u) dayveh(i) Kertay, Gurmukh(i) Hur(i) daykhaavniya.  2. Aapay Kerta kuray kuraayay. Aapay Shabad(u) Gur munn(i) vusaayay.  Shabadaiy oopjaiy Amrit Bani, Gurmukh(i) aakh(i) soonaavniya.  3. Aapay Kerta Aapay bhoogta. Bundhan torray suda haiy mookta. Suda mookt(u) Aapay haiy Sacha, Aapay Alukh(u) likhaavniya. 4.  Aapay Maya Aapay chhaaya.  Aapay moh(u) subh(u) jugat(u) oopaaya. Aapay Goondaata goon gaavaiy, Aapay aakh(i) soonaavniya.  5.  Aapay kuray kuraavay aapay. Aapay thaap(i) oothaapay Aapay.  Toojh tay baahur(i) kuchhoo na hovaiy, Tu Aapay kaaray laavniya.  6.  Aapay maaray, Aap(i) jeevaayay. Aapay maylay, mayl(i) milaayay. Sayva tay suda sookh(u) paaya, Gurmukh(i) sehj(i) smaavniya.  7. 
(PAGE 126):  Aapay oocha oocho hoyee. Jis(u) aap(i) vikhaalay su vaykhaiy koyee.  Nanak Naam(u) vusaiy ghut untar(i) Aapay vaykh(i) vikhaalniya.    8.26.27.
Word Meaning:  Jot(i) = Waheguru’s light.    Keeton(u) = (He has) done.    Ghut = hearts.     Bunut = creation; design.     1.     Baajh = without.   Sehj(u) = spiritual state above the effects of Maya.       Pause.        Soheh(i) = look impressive (majestic).   Moheh(i) = captivate.   Proveh(i) = string together (like beads in a necklace).           2.
Kerta = Creator.  Munn(i) Vusaayay = embeds in mind.     Bani = words in praise of Waheguru.      3.       Bhoogta = the one who enjoys; receive the rewards or punishment.    Bundhan = bonds.   Mookta = free from bonds.   Alukh(u) = inconceivable.   Likhaavniya = shows; enlightens.      4.        Chhaaya = shadow (effect on living beings); darkness (ignorance).     Jugat(u) = world.    Oopaaya = created.    Goondaata = source of all virtues (Waheguru).        5.        Thaap(i) = (having) created.   Oothaapay = destroy.    Kaaray = (put to) work.      6.        Sayva = service (obey Huqam).   Sehj(i) = (in) higher spiritual state.    7.    Oocha Oocho = higher and higher (above Maya).    Ghut = heart.8.       
MEANING:  Only Waheguru’s Light is the light (soul) in everybody; the Perfect Guru shows this Light with His Shabad.  (Even though, it is the same Light in everybody, yet everybody has his different characteristics). Waheguru Himself has created such differences among human beings.            1.             I am a sacrifice for all those who sing praise of Waheguru. Nobody can attain the spiritual state above the effects of Maya without the Guru; only those devotees attain such a state who take shelter of the Guru.         Pause.            You look very majestic and captivate the whole world, oh Waheguru! Like the beads of a necklace that stay attached to a common string, You keep the world under Your control with the single thread of Your Huqam.  You, Yourself, cause comforts and discomforts for the living beings, and “show” Yourself to the devotees who take the Guru’s shelter.          2.        The Creator Himself does everything and makes the living beings do whatever He wishes. He implants Guru’s Shabad Himself in devotees’ minds.  Guru’s Shabad is the word of praise of Waheguru, which the devotee recites and shares with others.          3.         You, Yourself, are the creator of the universe, and Yourself enjoys it (by being present in them).  At the same time, You are always free from all the bonds because You are above the effects of Maya, and have no bonds to break.  As you are always above the effects of Maya, it is impossible to discern You, but You reveal Yourself (to the devotees through the Guru), oh eternal Waheguru!           4.         Waheguru, Himself is Maya, and Himself its effect on living beings.  He, Himself, has implanted feelings of attachment in the world.  He is the source of all virtues, and (being present in living beings), Himself sings His praise and recites to the people.          5.         He does everything himself and makes the living beings do whatever He wishes them to do.  He creates the universe Himself, and destroys it too.  There is nothing that happens outside Him (which He is not in His control), and He Himself puts the world to business (worldly chores).        6.
Waheguru Himself gives and takes away life. He Himself connects individuals to the Guru and then to Himself.  A true devotee of the Guru always enjoys comfort by serving Him and Waheguru, and attains the state of blissfulness.        7.        Waheguru is higher and higher (above the effects of Maya), so it is impossible to “see” Him; some rare person “sees” Him to whom He “shows” Himself.  He, Himself, implants His Naam in the heart of His devotees and “shows” Himself for them to see, oh Nanak!      8.26.27.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 3:  ਮੇਰਾ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਭਰਪੂਰਿ ਰਹਿਆ ਸਭ ਥਾਈ॥ ਗੁਰ ਪਰਸਾਦੀ  ਘਰ ਹੀ ਮਹਿ ਪਾਈ॥ ਸਦਾ ਸਰੇਵੀ ਇਕ ਮਨਿ ਧਿਆਈ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਸਚਿ ਸਮਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਹਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਜੀਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਜਗਜੀਵਨੁ ਮਨਿ ਵਸਾਵਣਿਆ॥ ਹਰਿ ਜਗਜੀਵਨੁ ਨਿਰਭਉ ਦਾਤਾ ਗੁਰਮਤਿ ਸਹਜਿ ਸਮਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਘਰ ਮਹਿ ਧਰਤੀ ਧਉਲੁ ਪਤਾਲਾ॥ ਘਰ ਹੀ ਮਹਿ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਮੁ ਸਦਾ ਹੈ ਬਾਲਾ॥ ਸਦਾ ਅਨੰਦਿ ਰਹੈ ਸੁਖਦਾਤਾ ਗੁਰਮਤਿ ਸਹਜਿ ਸਮਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 2॥ ਕਾਇਆ ਅੰਦਰਿ ਹਉਮੈ ਮੇਰਾ॥ ਜੰਮਣ ਮਰਣੁ ਨ ਚੂਕੈ ਫੇਰਾ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਹੋਵੈ ਸੁ ਹਉਮੈ ਮਾਰੈ ਸਚੋ ਸਚੁ ਧਿਆਵਣਿਆ॥ 3॥ ਕਾਇਆ ਅੰਦਰਿ ਪਾਪੁ ਪੁੰਨੁ ਦੁਇ ਭਾਈ॥ ਦੁਹੀ ਮਿਲਿ ਕੈ ਸ੍ਰਿਸਟਿ ਉਪਾਈ॥ ਦੋਵੈ ਮਾਰਿ ਜਾਇ ਇਕਤੁ ਘਰਿ ਆਵੈ ਗੁਰਮਤਿ ਸਹਜਿ ਸਮਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 4॥ ਘਰ ਹੀ ਮਹਿ ਦੂਜੈ ਭਾਇ ਅਨੇਰਾ॥ ਚਾਨਣੁ ਹੋਵੈ ਛੋਡੈ ਹਉਮੈ ਮੇਰਾ॥ ਪਰਗਟੁ ਸਬਦੁ ਹੈ ਸੁਖਦਾਤਾ ਅਨਦਿਨੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਧਿਆਵਣਿਆ॥ 5॥ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਜੋਤਿ ਪਰਗਟੁ ਪਾਸਾਰਾ॥ ਗੁਰ ਸਾਖੀ ਮਿਟਿਆ ਅੰਧਿਆਰਾ॥ ਕਮਲੁ ਬਿਗਾਸਿ ਸਦਾ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਜੋਤੀ ਜੋਤਿ ਮਿਲਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 6॥ ਅੰਦਰਿ ਮਹਲ ਰਤਨੀ ਭਰੇ ਭੰਡਾਰਾ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਪਾਏ ਨਾਮੁ ਅਪਾਰਾ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਵਣਜੇ ਸਦਾ ਵਾਪਾਰੀ ਲਾਹਾ ਨਾਮੁ ਸਦ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 7॥ ਆਪੇ ਵਥੁ ਰਾਖੈ ਆਪੇ ਦੇਇ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਵਣਜਹਿ ਕੇਈ ਕੇਇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਜਿਸੁ ਨਦਰਿ ਕਰੇ ਸੋ ਪਾਏ ਕਰਿ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਮੰਨਿ ਵਸਾਵਣਿਆ॥   8॥27॥28॥
Majh Mehla 3:  Mayra Prubh(u) bhurpoor(i) ruhiya subh thaayeen. Gur persadi ghur hee meh(i) payee. Suda srayveen, ikk mun(i) dhiyaayee, Gurmukh(i) Sach(i) smaavniya. 1.  Houn vaari jeeo vaari, Jugjeevun(u) mun(i) vusaavniya. Hur(i) Jugjeevun(u) Nirbhou Daata, Gurmut(i) sehj(i) smaavniya.  1. Ruhaou.  Ghur meh(i) dherti dhaul(u) putaala. Ghur hee meh(i) Preetum(u) suda haiy baala. Suda anund(i) ruhaiy Sukhdaata, Gurmut(i) sehj(i) smaavniya. 2.  Kaayaan undar(i) haumein mayra. Jumun mern(u) na chookaiy fayra.  Gurmukh(i) hovaiy su haumein maaraiy, Sacho Sach(u) dhiyaavniya. 3. Kaayaan undar(i) paap(u) poonn(u) dooyay bhaayee. Dooheen mil(i) kaiy srisht(i) oopaayee. Dovaiy maar(i) jaayay Ikutt(u) ghur(i) aavaiy, Gurmut(i) sehj(i) smaavniya. 4.  Ghur hee meh(i) doojaiy bhaayay anayraa. Chaanun hovaiy chhodaiy haumein mayra. Pergut(u) shabad(u) haiy Sookhdaata, undin(u) Naam(u) dhiyaavniya.  5.  Untar(i) Jot(i) pergut(u) paasaara. Gur Saakhi mitiya undiyaara. Kumul(u) bigaas(i) suda sookh(u) paaya, joti Jot(i) milaavniya. 6.  Undar(i) mehl ruttani bhuray bhundaara. Gurmukh(i) paaya Naam(u) Apaara.  Gurmukh(i) vunjay suda vaapaari, laaha Naam(u) sud paavniya. 7.  Aapay Vuth(u) raakhaiy, aapay dayay. Gurmukh(i) vunjeh(i) kayee kayay. Nanak jis(u) nudr(i) kuray so paayay, kur(i) kirpa munn(i) vusaavniya.     8.27.28.
Word Meaning:  Bhurpoor(i) = omnipresent.    Ghur(i) = in the heart.  Srayveen = serve with devotion.      Ikk mun(i) = with full concentration.      Sach(i) = in God.   1.
Jugjeevun(u) = life of the world (Waheguru).   Nirbhou = fearless; perfectly Just.   Sehj(i) = in a mental state above the effects of Maya.      Pause.       Dhaul(u) = the mythical ox which supports the earth.   Ptaala = the underworld.   Baala = child–like; pure–minded.   Anund(i) = in bliss.      2.     Kaayaan = body.    Mayra = mine (ego).   Na Chookay Fayra = the rebirth cycle is not broken.   Sacho Sach(u) = pure Truth (Waheguru only).       3.      Poonn(u) = virtuous deeds.   Srisht(i) = world.   Ikutt(u) = One (Waheguru).   Ghur(i) = in the heart.        4.      Doojaiy Bhaayay = in the love of the other (Maya).   Anayraa = Hunayra = darkness.   Chaanun = light.    Chhodaiy = quit.   Mayra = mine (selfishness).   Pergut = visible.     5.     Jot(i) = Waheguru’s light.     Paasaara = expanse.   Saakhi = Teaching.   Kumal = lotus flower; heart.   Bigaas(i) = blooming.  Joti = light (soul).        6.       Mehl = mansion; heart.    Ruttani Bhuray = full of precious virtues (Guru’s Shabad).    Bhundaara = treasures.   Apaara = limitless (Waheguru).     Vunjay = deals in.    Laaha = profit; benefit.    Sud = always.      7. Vuth = desired precious item (Waheguru’s Naam).     Raakhaiy = retains; does not share.    Kayee Kay = only a few.     Nudr(i) = kind sight.        8.
MEANING:  (Even though) Waheguru is omnipresent, He can be realized only by the grace of the Guru.  Taking Guru’s shelter, one should serve Waheguru (sing His praise) all the time with full devotion, as a result of which, he will merge into Him.       1.  
I offer my life to those who make their heart home for Waheguru to reside there (embed 
Him in mind).  One can unite with Waheguru, which is the life of the whole world, is fearless, and is the provider for the whole world, in the high spiritual state above the effects of Maya (with a peaceful mind).         Pause.
Once an individual has Waheguru’s Naam in his heart, he comes to know the secrets of the earth, the mythical ox that supports it (a Hindu belief), and the underworld (which they believe is controlled by Waheguru). The devotee attains the spiritual state above Maya through the Guru’s advice and always remains in a blissful state.       2.
The man also has big haumein and ego in his mind because of which his reincarnation cycle never breaks.  A devotee overcomes his haumein in Guru’s shelter, and recites the Naam of Waheguru only.         3.         The mind also contemplates virtuous and ignoble deeds, which are like brothers (living in the same house – human mind), and both are the cause of reproduction (reincarnation) of the whole world.   A true devotee overcomes both of them with the grace of the Guru, and enshrines Waheguru’s Naam in his mind, and attains the spiritual state above the effects of Maya.        4.
Because of the love for Maya, there is complete darkness in the human mind; but once the mind is enlightened (by the Guru), it gets rid of all its selfishness and ego.  With the enlightenment by Guru’s Shabad, which is the source of all comfort, the individual recites Waheguru’s Naam day and night.           5.         With Guru’s Teachings, the human mind is enlightened, whereby all its darkness is dispelled, and the individual can “see” the whole expanse of the universe.  His mind blooms like a lotus flower and merges into Waheguru to stay blissful all the time.        6.
There are invaluable hidden treasures of virtues (seeds of Waheguru’s Naam) in the human mind, but Waheguru’s Naam can be realized only by the Guru’s grace.   A devotee of the Guru always deals in the virtuous qualities and realizes Waheguru’s Naam as the benefit (spiritual wealth).          7.
Waheguru keeps the invaluable thing (His Naam) away from some, and gives it to others (as per His Will).   But only a few devotees, who have the kind sight of Waheguru, deal in His Naam, and get it enshrined in their mind by His grace, oh Nanak!        8.27.28.

ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ ਹਰਿ ਆਪੇ ਮੇਲੇ ਸੇਵ ਕਰਾਏ॥ ਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਸਬਦਿ ਭਾਉ ਦੂਜਾ ਜਾਏ॥ ਹਰਿ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਸਦਾ ਗੁਣਦਾਤਾ ਹਰਿ ਗੁਣ ਮਹਿ ਆਪਿ ਸਮਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਹਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਜੀਉ ਵਾਰੀ ਸਚੁ ਸਚਾ ਹਿਰਦੈ ਵਸਾਵਣਿਆ॥ ਸਚਾ ਨਾਮੁ ਸਦਾ ਹੈ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਗੁਰ ਸਬਦੀ ਮੰਨਿ ਵਸਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਆਪੇ ਗੁਰ ਦਾਤਾ ਕਰਮਿ ਬਿਧਾਤਾ॥ ਸੇਵਕ ਸੇਵਹਿ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਹਰਿ ਜਾਤਾ॥ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਨਾਮਿ ਸਦਾ ਜਨ ਸੋਹਹਿ ਗੁਰਮਤਿ ਹਰਿ ਰਸੁ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 2॥ ਇਸੁ ਗੁਫਾ ਮਹਿ ਇਕੁ ਥਾਨੁ ਸੁਹਾਇਆ॥ ਪੂਰੈ ਗੁਰਿ ਹਉਮੈ ਭਰਮੁ ਚੁਕਾਇਆ॥ ਅਨਦਿਨੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਸਲਾਹਨਿ ਰੰਗਿ ਰਾਤੇ ਗੁਰ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਤੇ ਪਾਵਣਿਆ॥ 3॥
