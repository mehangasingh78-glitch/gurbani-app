(ਅੰਗ 71)॥     ਚਿਤਿ ਨ ਆਇਓ ਪਾਰਬ੍ਰਹਮੁ ਤਾ ਖੜਿ ਰਸਾਤਲਿ ਦੀਤਿ॥ 7॥ ਕਾਇਆ ਰੋਗੁ ਨ ਛਿਦ੍ਰੁ ਕਿਛੁ ਨਾ ਕਿਛੁ ਕਾੜਾ ਸੋਗੁ॥ ਮਿਰਤੁ ਨ ਆਵੀ ਚਿਤਿ ਤਿਸੁ ਅਹਿਨਿਸਿ ਭੋਗੈ ਭੋਗੁ॥ ਸਭ ਕਿਛੁ ਕੀਤੋਨੁ ਆਪਣਾ ਜੀਇ ਨ ਸੰਕ ਧਰਿਆ॥ ਚਿਤਿ ਨ ਆਇਓ ਪਾਰਬ੍ਰਹਮੁ ਜਮ ਕੰਕਰ ਵਸਿ ਪਰਿਆ॥ 8॥ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਕਰੇ ਜਿਸੁ ਪਾਰਬ੍ਰਹਮੁ ਹੋਵੈ ਸਾਧੂ ਸੰਗੁ ॥ਜਿਉ ਜਿਉ ਓਹੁ ਵਧਾਈਐ ਤਿਉ ਤਿਉ ਹਰਿ ਸਿਉ ਰੰਗੁ॥ ਦੁਹਾ ਸਿਰਿਆ ਕਾ ਖਸਮੁ ਆਪਿ ਅਵਰੁ ਨ ਦੂਜਾ ਥਾਉ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਤੁਠੈ ਪਾਇਆ ਨਾਨਕ ਸਚਾ ਨਾਉ॥ 9॥1॥26॥
Ja kuou mooshkul(u) ut(i) bunaiy, dhoyee koyay na dayay. Laagoo hoyay dooshmna, saak bh(i) bhuj(i) khulay. Subho bhujay aasra chookay subh(u) usraao. Chit(i) aavaiy oas(u) Parbraham(u) lugaiy na tutti vaao. 1. Sahib(u) nitaaniyaan ka taan(u). Aayay na jaayee thir(u) suda, Gur Shabadi Sach(u) jaan(u). 1. Ruhaou.  Jay ko hovaiy doobla nung bhookh kee peer. Dumrra pullaiy na puvaiy na ko dayvaiy dheer. Sooaarth(u) sooaao na ko kuray, na kichh(u) hovaiy kaaj(u). Chit(i) aavaiy oas(u) Parbraham(u), ta nihchul(u) hovaiy raaj(u). 2.  Ja kuou chinta buhout(u) buhout(u), dayhee viaapaiy roag(u). Grist(i) kootumb(i) playtiyaa, kuday hurkh(u) kuday soag(u). Gaoun kuray chuhoun koont ka, ghurri na baisun(u) soyay. Pothi geet kvit kichh(u), kuday na kurn(i) dhuriya. Chit(i) aavaiy oas(u) Parbraham(u) ta nimkh simrut turiya. 4.  Shast Simrit(i) Bayd chaar(i) mookhaagur bichray. Tupay tupeesur yogia, teeruth(i) guvun(u) kuray. Khut(u) kerma tay doognay pooja kerta naayay. Rung(u) na luggi Parbraham, ta surpur nerkay jaayay. 5.  Raaj milk sikdaariyaan rus bhogun bisthaar. Chit(i) na aayo Parbraham(u), ta serp kee joon(i) guyya. 6.  Buhout(u) dhunaad(i) achaarvunt(u), sobha nirmul reet(i). Maat pita soot bhaayiyaan, saajun sung(i) preet(i).  Lushkur turkush bund bund jeeo jeeo sugli keet.

Word Meaning:  Ut(i) Mooshkul(u)= extremely difficult situation (trouble).   Dhoyee = shelter; support.   Laagoo = on the attack.    Saak = relatives.   Chookaiy = ended.     Parbraham = God; Waheguru.  Tutti Vaao = hot air (India, being a hot country, experiences a very hot climate that becomes unbearable in the summer months, especially at the time of Guru Sahiban, when no fans or air-conditioning were available).  So, hot air was synonymous with big discomfort and has been used as a metaphor for troubles.     1.           Taan = force; power.    Nitaaniyaan = powerless; downtrodden.   Thir(u) = uniform; never changing.              Pause.
Doobla = weak.   Peer = Peerr = pain; discomfort.    Dumrra = a very small unit of money.   Pullaiy = in possession.   Dheer = comfort; support.   Sooaaruth(u) =  Sooaao =  needs.   Kaaj(u) = task.   Nihchul(u) = stable; everlasting.   Raj = rule.       2.       Viyapaiy = happens; attacks.    Grist(i) = Kootumb(i) = in family (affairs).   Hurkh(u) = happiness.   Soag(u) = grief.    Gaoun = travel.    Koont = directions.   Seetal(u) = calm; peaceful.       3.
Kirpun = money-minded.   Chaaray Kilvikh (Ugh) = all the four recognized sins (killing of a Brahman, and a cow; killing or selling a daughter; and donations from an immoral person).  Asoor = demon.   Sunghaar(u) = deserving death.  Pothi = religious book.  Kvit = (religious) poem.   Na Kurn(i) dhuriya = turned no ear; paid no attention. Nimkh = a moment.   Turiya = swam across (the sea of Maya); salvaged.       4.
Shast, Simrit(i), Bayd (Vedas) = Hindu religious books.     Mookhaagur = from memory.   Bichray = explains.   Tupay = Tupeesur = self=torturers.   Teeruth Guvun(u) = pilgrimage.    Khut(u) Kerma = six accepted deeds (for Brahmans: receive and deliver education; perform yugna for self and for others; give and receive donations.  For other Hindus: bathing; recitation of some mantras; peforming huvans; idol worship; pilgrimage; and meditation).   Rung = love.    Surpur = for sure.     5.
Milk = Milkh = estate.   Sikdariyaan = leadership.   Bisthaar = expansion.    Afaar = (of the) conceited.     Bidhi = of different kinds.   Chaayay = gusto; zest.       6.
Dhunaadh(i) = rich.    Achaarvunt(u) = disciplined; strict follower of expected norms.  Lushkur = soldiers.   Turkush = quiver (for arrows).   Bund = Bundna = to bow before. 
Khurr(i) = take to.    Rusatul(i) = underworld (hell).   Deet(i) = is sent; thrown.     7. 
Kaayan = bodily.   Chhider = shortcoming; weakness; bad habit.  Kaarra = suffering. Soag(u) = grief.   Mirt(u) = death.   Ehinis = day and night.   Bhoagaiy Bhoag(u)  = enjoys luxuries.  Shunk = doubt.  Jum Kunkur = deputies of the angel of death.     8.
Rung(u) = love; affection.  Khusm(u) = Lord; Master.   Uver(u) = other.   Tootthaiy = pleased to be kind.  9.
MEANING:  The Lord is the power behind the powerless (downtrodden), oh my friend.  (Not only that), He is eternal, and never comes or goes (dies or leaves); one can realize Him by the Guru’s Shabad.         Pause.      If someone is in big trouble, and has no shelter or support, his enemies are on the attack, all his relatives have left him, and all the doors of support are shut on him; he does not suffer even a slight trouble if he remembers Waheguru with full faith.     1.              If someone is weak, is suffering the pain of poverty and hunger; has no money, and there is nobody to solace him; nobody helps him in his needs, and he is unable to achieve anything; he will attain (a spiritual state where he feels that he has) an eternal reign if he thinks of Waheguru (prays to Him) with full faith.           2            If someone is suffering from some bodily disease, and is overwhelmed by several worries; and he is so engrossed in the family affairs, and experiences some happiness and some grief; and runs around in all the four directions to meet his needs, and cannot rest even for a moment; he will find bodily and mental peace if he thinks of God (prays to Him) with full faith.          3.          If someone is fully controlled by lust, rage, worldly attachments, greed, and love for money; and the demon has committed all the four big sins for which he deserves death; and he has never paid any attention to the praise of Waheguru in the form of a religious book, a song, or a poem; he can be salvaged in a moment if he remembers Waheguru (prays to Him) even for moment with full faith.          4.            (On the other hand) If someone is able to read the Shastra, the Simritis, and the four Vedas from memory, and undertakes pilgrimage like the well-known yogis, tortures himself, or performs twice as many as the six recognized rituals, takes early morning baths, and performs idol worship, he is bound to go to hell if he has no love for Waheguru.            5.           If someone owns huge tracts of land, is a popular leader, or even a ruler, has a huge expanse of enjoyable luxuries, has beautiful orchards, and the conceited can issue big orders, can afford too many different and colourful activities which he enjoys zestfully, he will be incarnated in a snakelike form if he does not remember God.        6.
If someone is very rich, leads a disciplined life as per societal norms, and enjoys fame because of his clean behaviour; and his mother, father, sons, brothers and friends all love him; and if a number of soldiers with quivers loaded with arrows bow to him, and obey his orders; he is still thrown in hell if he does not remember God.        7. 
If someone does not suffer from some bodily disease, has no shortcoming, or no suffering due to some grief, has never thought about death, and enjoys luxuries of life day and night; has control on everything, and never doubted his ownership of all his properties; becomes a prisoner of the deputies of the Angel of death if he has not remembered God.       8.
If Waheguru is kind to someone, he gets connected with the Guru, and the more he stays in touch, the more he is imbued in Waheguru’s love. Waheguru is the Lord of both the worlds (present and the next after death), and there is no place beyond His control.  I have realized such Waheguru’s Naam by the benevolence of Satguru, oh Nanak.     9.1.26

ਸਿਰੀਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 5 ਘਰੁ 5॥ ਜਾਨਉ ਨਹੀ ਭਾਵੈ ਕਵਨ ਬਾਤਾ॥ ਮਨ ਖੋਜਿ ਮਾਰਗੁ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਧਿਆਨੀ ਧਿਆਨੁ ਲਾਵਹਿ॥ ਗਿਆਨੀ ਗਿਆਨੁ ਕਮਾਵਹਿ॥ ਪ੍ਰਭੂ ਕਿਨ ਹੀ ਜਾਤਾ॥ 1॥ ਭਗਉਤੀ ਰਹਤ ਜੁਗਤਾ ॥ਜੋਗੀ ਕਹਤ ਮੁਕਤਾ॥ ਤਪਸੀ ਤਪਹਿ ਰਾਤਾ॥ 2॥ ਮੋਨੀ ਮੋਨਿ ਧਾਰੀ॥ ਸਨਿਆਸੀ ਬ੍ਰਹਮਚਾਰੀ॥ ਉਦਾਸੀ ਉਦਾਸਿ ਰਾਤਾ॥ 3॥ ਭਗਤਿ ਨਵੈ ਪਰਕਾਰਾ॥ ਪੰਡਿਤੁ ਵੇਦੁ ਪੁਕਾਰਾ॥ ਗਿਰਸਤੀ ਗਿਰਸਤਿ ਧਰਮਾਤਾ॥ 4॥ ਇਕਸਬਦੀ ਬਹੁਰੂਪਿ ਅਵਧੂਤਾ॥ ਕਾਪੜੀ ਕਉਤੇ ਜਾਗੂਤਾ॥ ਇਕਿ ਤੀਰਥਿ ਨਾਤਾ॥ 5॥ ਨਿਰਹਾਰ ਵਰਤੀ ਆਪਰਸਾ॥ ਇਕਿ ਲੂਕਿ ਨ ਦੇਵਹਿ ਦਰਸਾ॥ ਇਕਿ ਮਨ ਹੀ ਗਿਆਤਾ॥ 6॥ ਘਾਟਿ ਨ ਕਿਨ ਹੀ ਕਹਾਇਆ॥ ਸਭ ਕਹਤੇ ਹੈ ਪਾਇਆ॥ ਜਿਸੁ ਮੇਲੇ ਸੋ ਭਗਤਾ॥ 7॥ ਸਗਲ ਉਕਤਿ ਉਪਾਵਾ॥ ਤਿਆਗੀ ਸਰਨਿ ਪਾਵਾ॥ ਨਾਨਕੁ ਗੁਰ ਚਰਣਿ ਪਰਾਤਾ॥ 8॥2॥27॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 5 Ghur(u) 5:  Jaanoo nuhi, bhaavaiy kuvun baata. Mun khoj(i) maarg(u). 1. Ruhaou. Dhiyani dhiyan(u) laaveh(i).  Giyani giyan(u) kmaveh(i). Prubhoo kin hee jaata. 1. Bhugaouti reht joogta. Yogi keht mookta. Tupsee tupeh(i) raata. 2.  Moani moan(i) dhaari. Sanyasi brahmchaari.  Oodaasi oodaas(i) raata. 3. Bhugut(i) nuvaiy perkaara. Pundit Vayd(u) pookaara. Gristi grist(i) dhermaata. 4. Ikk-shabadi buhouroop uvdhoota. Kaaprri kautay jaagoota. Ikk teeruth(i) naata. 5.  Nirhaar verti aapursha. Ikk(i) look(i) na dayveh(i) dersha. Ikk(i) mun hee giyaata. 6. Ghaat(i) na kin hee kuhaaya. Subh kehtay haiy paaya. Jis(u) maylay so bhugta.  7.  Sugul ookt(i) oopaava. Tiyaagi shern(i) paava. Nanak(u) Gur churn(i) praata.   8.2.27. 
Word Meaning:  Bhaavaiy = likes. Baata = wording; deeds.  Khoj(i)= search.  Pause. 
Dhiyani = meditator.  Kmaveh(i) = earns; works toward.   Kin = some rare person.    1.
Bhugaouti = devotee.   Joogta = (in) discipline.   Mookta = free from the bonds of Maya.  Tupsee = self- torturer.   Raata = imbued.      2.        Moani = an ascetic who stays silent for a long time.   Sanyasi = an ascetic who renounces the world.   Brahmchaari = celibate.  Oodaasi = turned away from Maya.    3. 
Worship of Nuvaiy Parkaara = Hindu books have listed nine forms of worship as follows:
Listen to the praise of one’s deity.  2. Singing praise. 3. Remembering the deity. 4. Wash the feet.  5. Offering flowers etc.  6.  Kneeling before.   7.  Friendly behaviour.  8. Personal tending.  9. Offer self.     Gristi = family man.  
Vayd(u) = Vedas.    Pookaara = reads aloud.   Gristi = family man.   Dhermaata = discharges his responsibilities.     4.        Ikk-sahabadi = ascetics who utter only one word at the doors to collect food.   Buhouroop = who changes appearances frequently.   Uvdhoota = naked sadhus.   Kaaprri = one who covers his entire body with clothes.   Kautay = poets; entertainer.  Jagoota = who does not sleep.     5.     Nirhaar = who does not eat.   Verti = those who take vows not to do certain things.  Aapursha = who don’t touch others.   Look(i) = keep hiding. Giyaata = knowledgeable.      6.       Ookut(i) = logic.   Oopaava = arrangements; use different techniques.   Praata = fallen (on feet).      8.
NOTE: In this holy Shabad, Guru Arjun Dev Ji explains the different approaches people follow to connect with God. Everybody believes that their approach is successful (pleases Waheguru).  But, worshipping Waheguru by taking Guru’s shelter is the only way to please or connect with Him.
MEANING:  I don’t know which religious path (approach) pleases (is approved by) Waheguru for connecting with Him. So, search for the right approach, oh my mind.       Pause.
The meditators believe in meditation alone; the (divine) knowledge seekers believe in acquiring knowledge, but no one has realized Waheguru (or made a connection with Him) using such approaches.          1.          The devotees of god Vishnu worship as per their belief - a special anointment on the forehead, wearing a necklace of basil, and pilgrimage, etc.  The Yogis believe that their path leads to freedom from Maya, and the people who torture themselves believe that their approach is right.          2.         The ascetics who stay quiet for long periods of time practise their belief; the Sanyasi and the celibates believe in their respective approaches, and the people who believe in turning their minds away from Maya, follow their own path.       3.         Some believe in the nine types of worship; the pundits read the Vedas aloud to the people, and the family man is busy with his family obligations.         4.
There are some who go from door to door to collect food, and utter only one word (Aulukh, Nirunjun, etc.) to draw the attention of the families. Some believe in wearing no clothes at all, while some others keep changing their appearances.  On the other hand, some believe in covering their whole body with clothes; some people are poets, and some entertain people with different acts, and there are some who believe in having no sleep. There are still others who believe in pilgrimage.          5.           Some eat no food, some live by certain self-imposed restrictions, while others do not let people touch them. Some stay in hiding and remain invisible, while others consider themselves highly knowledgeable.           6.         Nobody believes that his path is less effective than others, and everybody claims that they have realized God, but only he is the real devotee whom Waheguru Himself connects with Him.       7.         I have relinquished all these logics and approaches and have fallen at the Guru’s feet (taken His shelter) to be connected with Waheguru, oh Nanak(u).  8.2.27.
