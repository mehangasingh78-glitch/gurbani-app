(ਅੰਗ 131)॥   ਤੂ ਵਡਾ ਤੂ ਊਚੋ ਊਚਾ॥ ਤੂੰ ਬੇਅੰਤੁ ਅਤਿ ਮੂਚੋ ਮੂਚਾ॥ ਹਉ ਕੁਰਬਾਣੀ ਤੇਰੈ ਵੰਞਾ ਨਾਨਕ ਦਾਸ ਦਸਾਵਣਿਆ॥  8॥1॥35॥
Majh Mehla 5:  Untar(i) Alukh(u), na jaayee lukhiya.  Naam(u) ruttan(u) laiy goojha rukhiya. Agumm(u) Agoachur(u), subh tay oocha, Gur kaiy Shabad(i) lukhaavniya. 1. Huoun vaari jeeo vaari, kul(i) meh(i) Naam(u) soonaavniya.  Sunt piyaaray, Sachaiy dhaaaray, vudbhaagi dershun(u) paavniya. 1. Ruhaou. Sadhik Sidh jisaiy kuou phirday. Brahmay Inder dhiyaayin hirday. Kot(i) tayteesa khojeh(i) Ta kuou, Gur mil(i) hirdaiy gaavniya.  2. Aatth pehr toodh(u) jaapay puvna. Dherti sayvuk paayik churna. Khaani baani serb nivaasi, subhna kaiy mun(i) bhaavniya.  3. Sacha Sahib(u) Gurmukh(i) jaapay. Pooraiy Gur kaiy Shabad(i) sinjaapay. Jin peeya sayee triptaasay, Sachay Sach(i) aghaavniya. 4. Tis(u) ghur(i) sehja soyee soohayla. Anund binod kuray sud kayla. So dhunvunta, so vud shaha, jo Gur Churni mun(u) laavniya. 5.  
Pehlon day Taiyn rijuk smaaha. Pichhon day Taiyn junt(u) oopaaha. Toodh(u) jayvud daata uver(u) na Soami, luvaiy na koyee laavniya. 6.  Jis Tu toottha so Toodh(u) dhiyaayay. Sadh junaa ka muntar kumaayay. Aap(i) turaiy suglay kool taaray, tis(u) Durgeh tthaak na paavniya.  7. 
(PAGE 131):       Tu vuda Tu oocho oocha, Tu bayunt(u) ut(i) moocho moocha. Houn koorbaani Tayray vunjaan, Nanak daas dusaavniya.  8.1.35.
Word Meaning:  Alukh(u) = invisible; indiscernible.    Lukhiya = “seen”; understood. 
Ruttan = precious stone.   Goojha = hidden.   Agumm(u) = (physically) inaccessible.   Agoachar(u) = inconceivable.      Lukhaavniya = is understood.       1.
Kul(u) = Kulyug (considered the worst eon in Hinduism); the world.   Sunt = saintly people; devotees.   Dhaaray = supported.     Vudbhaagi = highly lucky.    Pause.
Sadhik = those who purify their bodies by Yogic exercises.  Brahmay = many Brahmas.  Hirday = (in) heart.   Kot(i) Tayteesa = 330 million gods of Hindus.    Khojeh(i) = search for.   Ta Kuou = Him.       2.     Aatth Pehr = twenty-four hours.    Toodh(u) = You.    Jaapay = recites (Naam).   Puvna = air.   Paayik = servant.      Churna = (of) feet.   Khaani = sources of reproduction.    Baani = languages; means of communication.  Serb Nivaasi = present in all of them.    Bhaavniya = is liked.       3.
Sinjaapay = recognizes; gets connected.    Triptaasay = contented.   Aghaavniya = fully satisfied.         4.      Tis(u) Ghur(i) = in that heart.    Sehja = peace of mind due to a higher spiritual state.   Soohayla = enjoying comfort; blissful.    Anund = bliss.   Binod = zest; gusto.   Kayla = alone; detached.    Dhunvunta = rich.    Shaha = money-lender.        5.       Pehlon = before.    Rijuk = food.    Smaaha = made arrangements.   Pichhon = after.  Junt(u) = living being.   Jayvud = as great.    Luvaiy = equal; at par.       6.
Toottha = pleased to be kind.  Sadh = Guru.  Muntar = Shabad (Teachings).     Kmaayay = work towards.   Traiy = finds salvation.    Kool = associates.   Tthaak = restraint.        7.
Bayunt(u) = without limits.   Moocha = large.    Vunjaan = (i) go; I do.   Daas Dusaavniya = slave of slaves.         8.  
MEANING:   The invisible God in the form of His Naam, resides hidden in every heart, but cannot be seen with our eyes.  The physically inaccessible and the incomprehensible Waheguru is the highest of all (much above the effects of Maya) but can be “seen” or realized only with the Guru’s Shabad.            1.
I offer my life to all those who recite Waheguru’s Naam in this world.  The supreme devotees are dear to God, and are supported by Him; but only the highly lucky people get to see them (make a connection with them).           Pause. 
Waheguru, which is being searched for by the accomplished Sidh yogis and the yogis and whom gods like Brahma and Indra worship, and the 330 million other gods search for Him, can be worshiped only by connecting with the Guru (in His shelter).          2.
The air worships Waheguru (is at His Command) all the twenty-four hours, and the earth is a slave at His feet.  Waheguru resides in the hearts of, and is liked by, all the living beings of the four sources of reproduction, and by those which have different ways to communicate.          3.          The True Lord is revealed in the shelter of the Guru, and can be recognized (can be accessed) by the Shabad of the Perfect Guru.  Whoever tastes Waheguru’s Naam-Amrit becomes fully content and can achieve complete satisfaction by being “one” with Him.         4.
An individual who attains the state of high spirituality above the effects of Maya enjoys all comforts. (Even while living in the family) He stays detached from worldly attractions, is always zestful and blissful.  That individual is rich in the real sense, and is a huge banker (mine of virtues), who stitches his mind to the Feet (Shabad) of the Guru.           5.            Waheguru makes arrangements for the food for each kind of living being before He creates them.  Nobody else is as great as the Lord Waheguru, and no one can equal Him.          6.           Only those individuals worship You whom You are pleased to be kind to, and they act on the Shabad of the Guru, oh Waheguru!   They swim across the sea of Maya (get emancipated), and find salvation for their associates as well, and there is no restriction for them in Waheguru’s Court.        7.        You are huge, and higher and higher; You are limitless and larger and larger, oh Waheguru.  I offer my life to (serve) You, and I (Nanak) am a slave of Your slaves, oh Waheguru!     8.1.35

Majh Mehla 5:  Kaun(u) su mookta, kaun(u) su joogta. Kaun(u) su giyaani, kaun(u) su bukta. Kaun(u) girhee, kaun(u) oodaasi, kaun(u) su keemut(i) paayay jeeo. 1. Kin(i) bidh(i) bandha, nin(i) bidh(i) chhoota.  Kin(i) bidh(i) aavun jaana toota.  Kaun(u) kerm, kaun(u) nihkerma, kaun(u) su kuhaiy kuhaayay jeeo. 2. Kaun su sookhiya, kaun(u) su dookhiya. Kaun(u) su sunmukh(u) kaun(u) vaymookhiya. Kin(i) bidh(i) miliyaiy kin(i) bidh(i) bichhuraiy, eho bidh(i) kaun(u) pergtaayay jeeo. 3. Kaun(u) su akkhur(u) jit(u) dhaavut rehta. Kaun(u) oopdaysh(u) jit(u) dookh(u) sookh(u) sum sehta. Kaun(u) so chaal jit(u) Parbrahm dhiyaayay, kin(i) bidh(i) keertun gaayay jeeo. 4.  Gurmukh(i) mookta, Gurmukh(i) joogta. Gurmukh(i) giyaani, Gurmukh(i) bukta. Dhunn girhee, oodaasi Gurmukh(i), Gurmukh(i) keemut(i) paayay jeeo. 5.  Haumein baandha, Gurmukh(i) chhoota.  Gurmukh(i) aavun(u) jaavun(u) toota. Gurmukh(i) kerm Gurmukh(i) nihkerma, Gurmukh(i) kuray su soobhaayay jeeo. 6. Gurmukh(i) sookhiya, munmukh(i) dookhiya.  Gurmukh(i) sunmukh(u) munmukh(i) vaymukhiya. Gurmukh(i) miliyaiy, munmukh(i) vichhuraiy, Gurmukh(i) bidh(i) pergtaayay jeeo.  7. Gurmukh(i) akkhur(u) jit(u) dhaavut(u) rehta. Gurmukh(i) oopdaysh(u) dookh(u) sookh(u) sum sehta.  Gurmukh(i) chaal jit(u) Parbrahm diyaayay, Gurmukh(i) keertun gaayay jeeo. 8. Sugli bunut bunaayee Aapay. Aapay kuray kraayay thaapay. Ikkus(u) tay hoyo Anunta, Nanak Aikus(u) maaheh(i) smaayay jeeo.  9.2.36.
Word Meaning:   Mookta = free from all bonds.    Joogta = connected (bonded).  Giyaani = having Divine Knowledge.    Bukta = orator; lecturer.   Girhee = family man.  Oodaasi = detached from worldly affairs (living in isolation).  Keemut(i) Paayay = evaluate (understands the magnanimity of Waheguru).      1.      Bidh(i) = means; ways.   Baandha = attached (to Maya).   Aavun Jaana = birth and death.   Toota = (cycle is) broken.    Kerm = (noble) deeds.    Nihkerma = detached from Maya.         2.
Sunmukh(u) = one who obeys the Guru.   Vaymookhiya = one who disregards the Guru.    Bichhuraiy = get separated.   Pergtaayay = clarify.        3.
Akkhur(u) = Mantra (words to please or control).   Dhaavut = wandering fast; unsettling.    Rehta = restraint.    Sum = the same; equal.   Sehta = endures; accepts.  Chaal = rites; customs; way of life.        4.       Gurmukh(i) = supreme devotee of the Guru.    Dhunn = worthy of respect.       5.      Haumein = selfishness and ego.    Soobhaayay = with love and respect.        6.       Bunut = creation.    Thaapay = establish.    Ikkus(u) Tay = from One.   Anunta = unlimited.      Smaayay = merge in.        9.
MEANING: Q: Who is free (from the bonds of Maya), and who is connected (with Waheguru)? Who has Divine Knowledge (connected to Waheguru) and who is a good orator (to describe Waheguru’s virtues)? Who is a real family man, and who is detached from Maya? Who can evaluate Waheguru (know His magnanimity)?         1.
Q:  How does a man get bonded to Maya, and how can one get freedom from it?  How can a man break the cycle of reincarnation?  What are the noble deeds, and who is detached from Maya (while living in the family)?  Who sings the praise of Waheguru and makes others do the same?            2.        Q:  Who enjoys comfort (bliss), and who is in discomfort (is worrisome)?   Who keeps his face towards the Guru (eager to obey Him), and who faces away from the Guru (disregards Him)?  How can I connect with Waheguru, and how can one get disconnected from Him? Who can explain such means (or causes)?          3.         Q: What mantra is effective to restrain the fast-wandering mind, and what is the advice by which one can accept comfort and discomfort the same way (be a stoic)?  What lifestyle is most effective to remember Waheguru, and to sing His praise?      4.
Answers to these questions: 
It is the supreme devotee of the Guru (a Gurmukh) who is free from the bonds of Maya, and he is the one who is connected with Waheguru. The Gurmukh is the bearer of the Divine Knowledge, and he is the perfect orator to describe Waheguru’s virtues.  A Gurmukh is worthy of respect whether he is a family man or lives alone.  A Gurmukh knows the virtues (magnanimity) of Waheguru.           5.
Man’s haumein is the cause of his bonds with Maya, whereas the Gurmukh is free from all such bonds.  The reincarnation cycle is broken for the Gurmukh.  The deeds of a Gurmukh are noble because all his deeds are selfless, and whatever he does is with love and respect for the Guru.         6.          The Gurmukh is blissful, while the follower of his mind is worrisome.  The Gurmukh faces towards the Guru (obeys His Command), while the follower of his mind faces away from the Guru.  The Gurmukh is connected with Waheguru, while the follower of his mind is disconnected.  Gurmukh’s lifestyle illustrates the path (to connect with God).        7.
Obeying the Guru is the mantra by which one controls one's fickle mind, and one accepts comforts and discomforts the same way, by the advice of the Guru.  The lifestyle of the Gurmukh is the one to copy to remember Waheguru and to sing His praise.        8.         The whole creation is carried out by Waheguru Himself. He alone does everything, makes the humans do whatever He wishes, and establishes them (in their lifestyles). From being One, Waheguru transforms Himself into countless, and everything eventually merges into Him, oh Nanak!     9.2.36.
