(ਅੰਗ 85)॥   ਨਾਨਕ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਉਬਰੇ ਸਾਚਾ ਨਾਮੁ ਸਮਾਲਿ॥ 1॥
Sloak M:3: Huoun, Huoun kerti subh mooyee, sumpou kisaiy na naal(i). Doojaiy bhaayay dookh(u) paaya, subh johi Jumkaal(i).
(PAGE 85) Nanak Gurmukh(i) oobray, Sacha Naam(u) smaal(i).   1. 
Word Meaning: Houn, Houn = I and only I (selfishness).   Sumpou = wealth.  Doojaiy Bhaayay = love for the other (Maya).  Johi = warned.  Jumkaal(i) = Angel of death.
MEANING: The whole world is dying in selfishness, yet wealth accompanies nobody.  The world suffers because of its love for the other (Maya), and is being warned (being watched closely) by the Angel of Death.  The devotees are saved because of their worship of Waheguru’s Naam in the Guru’s shelter.   1.

ਮ:1॥ ਗਲੀ ਅਸੀ ਚੰਗੀਆ ਆਚਾਰੀ ਬੁਰੀਆਹ॥ ਮਨਹੁ ਕੁਸੁਧਾ ਕਾਲੀਆ ਬਾਹਰਿ ਚਿਟਵੀਆਹ॥ ਰੀਸਾ ਕਰਹਿ ਤਿਨਾੜੀਆ ਜੋ ਸੇਵਹਿ ਦਰੁ ਖੜੀਆਹ॥ ਨਾਲਿ ਖਸਮੈ ਰਤੀਆ ਮਾਣਹਿ ਸੁਖਿ ਰਲੀਆਹ॥ ਹੋਦੈ ਤਾਣਿ ਨਿਤਾਣੀਆ ਰਹਹਿ ਨਿਮਾਨਣੀਆਹ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਜਨਮੁ ਸਕਾਰਥਾ ਜੇ ਤਿਨ ਕੈ ਸੰਗਿ ਮਿਲਾਹ॥ 2॥
M:1:  Gulleen useen chungiyaan, aachaareen booriyaan.  Munhou kusoodha kaaliyaan, baahur(i) chitviyaan.  Reesaan kureh(i) tinnarriyaan, jo sayveh(i) Dur(u) khurriyaan. Naal(i) Khusmaiy ruttiyaan, maaneh(i) sookh(i) ruliyaan. Hoanday taan(i) nitaaniyaan, ruhay nimaananiyaan.  Nanak junum(u) skaartha, jay tin kaiy sung(i) milaah.  2.
Word Meaning:  Aachaareen = character wise.  Kusoodha Kaaliyaan = impure; black; evil-minded.  Chitviyaan = polished; noble-looking.  Reesaan = emulate.   Tinaarriyaan = of those.  Dur(u) khurriyaan = waiting at the door (with dedication).  Ruttiyaan = imbued.  Nitaaniyaan = powerless.  Nimaananiyaan = without ego.   Skaartha = successful. 2.
MEANING:  We are good at conversation, but are bad character-wise.  We look noble from the outside, but are evil at heart.  We try to emulate those devotees who serve (Waheguru) with dedication.  Imbued in the love of the Lord, the devotees enjoy the comfort of being with Him.  The devotees have all the power, but act as if they are powerless, and stay humble (have no ego). I, too, can make my life successful if I can have their company, oh Nanak.   2.

ਪਉੜੀ॥ ਤੂੰ ਆਪੇ ਜਲੁ ਮੀਨਾ ਹੈ ਆਪੇ ਆਪੇ ਹੀ ਆਪਿ ਜਾਲੁ॥ ਤੂੰ ਆਪੇ ਜਾਲੁ ਵਤਾਇਦਾ ਆਪੇ ਵਿਚਿ ਸੇਬਾਲੁ॥ ਤੂੰ ਆਪੇ ਕਮਲੁ ਅਲਿਪਤ ਹੈ ਸੈ ਹਥਾ ਵਿਚਿ ਗੁਲਾਲੁ॥ ਤੰ ਆਪੇ ਮੁਕਤਿ ਕਰਾਇਦਾ ਇਕ ਨਿਮਖ ਘੜੀ ਕਰਿ ਖਿਆਲੁ॥ ਹਰਿ ਤੁਧਹੁ ਬਾਹਰਿ ਕਿਛੁ ਨਹੀ ਗੁਰ ਸਬਦੀ ਵੇਖਿ ਨਿਹਾਲੁ॥7
Paurri: Toon Aapay jul(u), meena haiyn aapay, aapay hee aap(I) jaal(u).  Toon Aapay jaal(u) vutaayaynda, aapay vich(i) saybaal(u). Toon aapay Kumal(u) ulipt haiyn, saiy huthaan vich(i) goolaal(u). Toon aapay mookt(i) kraayaynada ikk nimukh ghuree kur(i) khiyaal(u). Hur(i) toodhoun baahur(i) kichh(u) nuhi, Gur Shabadi vaykh(i) nihaal(u).  7.
Word Meaning:  Meena = fish.  Vutaayaynda = cast; throw.   Saybaal(u) = green algae (bait for the fish).  Ulipt = unaffected.   Saiy Huthaan = in hundred and fifty feet (in deep waters); among countless attractions of Maya.  Goolaal(u) = beautiful flower.   Mookt(i) = free (from the bonds of Maya).  Nimukh= ghurree = a moment.  Nihaal(u) = blissful.
MEANING: (Being present in each and every thing) You are the water, the fish, and the net, oh Waheguru.  You cast the net Yourself, and Yourself are the green algae (bait for the fish), oh Waheguru.  Like the lotus flower, which stays unaffected by many other living things in deep water, You stay majestically unaffected by countless attractions of Maya.  You Yourself cut the bonds of Maya for those who think of You even for a moment, oh Waheguru.  There is nothing beyond Your control. One can “see” you only by the Guru’s Shabad, and attain bliss, oh Waheguru.  7.

ਸਲੋਕ ਮ:3॥ ਹੁਕਮੁ ਨ ਜਾਣੈ ਬਹੁਤਾ ਰੋਵੈ॥ ਅੰਦਰਿ ਧੋਖਾ ਨੀਦ ਨ ਸੋਵੈ॥ ਜੇ ਧਨ ਖਸਮੈ ਚਲੈ ਰਜਾਈ॥ ਦਰਿ ਘਰਿ ਸੋਭਾ ਮਹਲਿ ਬੁਲਾਈ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਕਰਮੀ ਇਹ ਮਤਿ ਪਾਈ॥ ਗੁਰ ਪਰਸਾਦੀ ਸਚਿ ਸਮਾਈ॥ 1॥
Sloak M:3: Huqam na jaanaiy buhouta rovaiy. Undar(i) dhokha, neend na sovaiy. Jay dhun Khusmaiy chullaiy rujayee. Dur(i) ghur(i) sobha Mehal boolayee. Nanak, kermeen eh mut(i) paayee. Gur Persadi Sach(i) smaayee. 
Word Meaning: Huqam = Will; Command; Laws.  Dhokha = dhookh dhookha = unrest.  Dhun = human bride.   Rujaayee= according to the Will.  Dur(i) = Ghur(i) = Mehl = Waheguru’s residence.  Kermeen = kindness; blessing. 
MEANING:  Those who do not recognize the Will of Waheguru suffer a lot.  There is unrest in their mind, and they cannot sleep well.  If the human bride leads her life by the Will of God, she is invited to Waheguru’s residence (gets connected with Him), and is praised (loved) by Him.  But one acquires such wisdom (to obey Waheguru) only by His benediction.  With the Guru's blessings, the individual becomes “one” with Waheguru. 1.
ਮ:3॥ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਨਾਮ ਵਿਹੂਣਿਆ ਰੰਗੁ ਕਸੁੰਭਾ ਦੇਖਿ ਨ ਭੁਲੁ॥ ਇਸ ਕਾ ਰੰਗੁ ਦਿਨ ਥੋੜਿਆ ਛੋਛਾ ਇਸ ਦਾ ਮੁਲੁ॥ ਦੂਜੈ ਲਗੈ ਪਚਿ ਮੁਏ ਮੂਰਖ ਅੰਧ ਗਵਾਰ॥ ਬਿਸਟਾ ਅੰਦਰਿ ਕੀਟ ਸੇ ਪਇ ਪਚਹਿ ਵਾਰੋ ਵਾਰ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮ ਰਤੇ ਸੇ ਰੰਗੁਲੇ ਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਸਹਜਿ ਸੁਭਾਇ॥ ਭਗਤੀ ਰੰਗੁ ਨ ਉਤਰੈ ਸਹਜੈ ਰਹੈ ਸਮਾਇ॥ 2॥
M:3:  Manmukh Naam-vihooniya, rung(u) kusoombha daykh(i) na bhooll(u). Iss da rung(u) din thorriya, chhochha is da mooll(u).  Doojaiy luggaiy puch(i) mooyay, moorukh undh(u) guvaar.  Bishta undar(i) keet say, paiy pucheh(i) vaaro vaar. Nanak Naam ruttay say rungulay, Gur kaiy sehj(i) soobhayay. Bhugati rung(u) na ootraiy sehjaiy ruhaiy smaayay.  2.
Word Meaning:  Manmukh = follower of mind; Maya-engrossed.   Vihooniya = devoid of.  Kusoombha = an attractive, colourful flower which dies out quickly (it is used as a metaphor for Maya, which is also attractive but short-lived).   Chhoccha = of no value.  Puch(i) = suffering.   Bishta = crap.   Keet = tiny worms.  Vaaro Vaar = again and again.  Rungulay = pretty; lovable.  Sehj(i) = high spiritual state.  Soobhayay = in love.
MEANING:  Do not make the mistake of falling in love with the attractive kusoombha flower (Maya), oh, the follower of your mind, and devoid of Waheguru’s Naam, man.   Its beauty lasts only for a short while, and it has no value (other utility). (Maya is just like the ksoombha flower, which lasts for a short time) So, those who indulge in Maya are foolish, ignorant, unrefined, and suffer again and again like the tiny worms in crap.  Those who are imbued in the Naam are truly pretty people (in the mind); in the love of the Guru, they enjoy the bliss of a high spiritual state (above the effects of Maya).  Their love of devotion never dies, and they always stay in a high spiritual state.     2.

ਪਉੜੀ॥ ਸਿਸਟਿ ਉਪਾਈ ਸਭ ਤੁਧੁ ਆਪੇ ਆਪੇ ਰਿਜਕੁ ਸੰਬਾਹਿਆ॥ ਇਕਿ ਵਲੁ ਛਲੁ ਕਰਿ ਕੈ ਖਾਵਦੇ ਮੁਹਹੁ ਕੂੜੁ ਕੁਸਤੁ ਤਿਨੀ ਢਾਹਿਆ॥ ਤੁਧੁ ਆਪੇ ਭਾਵੈ ਸੋ ਕਰਹਿ ਤੁਧੁ ਓਤੈ ਕੰਮਿ ਓਇ ਲਾਇਆ॥ ਇਕਨਾ ਸਚੁ ਬੁਝਾਇਓਨੁ ਤਿਨਾ ਅਤੁਟ ਭੰਡਾਰ ਦੇਵਾਇਆ॥ ਹਰਿ ਚੇਤਿ ਖਾਹਿ ਤਿਨਾ ਸਫਲੁ ਹੈ ਅਚੇਤਾ ਹਥ ਤਡਾਇਆ॥ 8॥
Paurri:  Sisit(i) oopaayee subh Toodh(u) aapay, aapay rijuk(u) sumbhaaya. Ikk vul(u) chhul(u) kur(i) kaiy khaanvday, moohoun koorr(u) koosutt(u) tinhi dhaaya. Toodh(u) aapay bhaavaiy so kureh(i), Toodh(u) oataiy kumm(i) oye laaya.  Ikkna Sach(u) boojhayayon(u), tinha atoot bhundaar dayvaaya. Hur(i) chayt(i) khaahay tinha sufl(u) haiy,  achayta huth tudaaya.  8.
Word Meaning:  Sist(i) = world.  Rijk(u) = daily needs.  Sumbhaaya = delivers.  Vul(u) Chul(u) = by deceitful methods; by hook or by crook.  Koosutt(u) = lies.   Dhaaya = controlled; made their business.  Bhaavaiy = as per (His) wish.  Ootaiy = the same.  Atoot Bhundaar = inexhaustible resources.  Chayt(i) = remember.   Achayta = do not remember.  Tudaaya = spread hands to beg.
MEANING: You have created the world all by Yourself, and You deliver the necessities to fulfil the daily needs of Your creation, oh Waheguru. Many people acquire wealth by deceitful means and by telling lies, which has become their routine business. However, they do only those things for which they are predestined by Your Will (based on the deeds of their previous lives). Those who have been enlightened (by the Guru) enjoy unlimited resources (they reach a state of contentment where they feel the bliss of every comfort).  Those who lead their lives keeping Waheguru in mind all the time have a successful life (are approved by Waheguru), while those who forget Waheguru are always begging (never achieve contentment and keep asking for more and more).     8.

ਸਲੋਕ ਮ:3॥ ਪੜਿ ਪੜਿ ਪੰਡਿਤ ਬੇਦ ਵਖਾਣਹਿ ਮਾਇਆ ਮੋਹ ਸੁਆਇ॥ ਦੂਜੈ ਭਾਇ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਵਿਸਾਰਿਆ ਮਨ ਮੂਰਖ ਮਿਲੈ ਸਜਾਇ॥ ਜਿਨਿ ਜੀਉ ਪਿੰਡੁ ਦਿਤਾ ਤਿਸੁ ਕਬਹੂੰ ਨ ਚੇਤੈ ਜੋ ਦੇਦਾ ਰਿਜਕੁ ਸੰਬਾਹਿ॥ ਜਮ ਕਾ ਫਾਹਾ ਗਲਹੁ ਨ ਕਟੀਐ ਫਿਰਿ ਫਿਰਿ ਆਵੈ ਜਾਇ॥ ਮਨਮੁਖਿ ਕਿਛੂ ਨ ਸੂਝੈ ਅੰਧੁਲੇ ਪੂਰਬਿ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਕਮਾਇ॥ ਪੂਰੈ ਭਾਗਿ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਮਿਲੈ ਸੁਖਦਾਤਾ ਨਾਮੁ ਵਸੈ ਮਨਿ ਆਇ॥ ਸੁਖੁ ਮਾਣਹਿ ਸੁਖੁ ਪੈਨਣਾ ਸੁਖੇ ਸੁਖਿ ਵਿਹਾਇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਸੋ ਨਾਉ ਮਨਹੁ ਨ ਵਿਸਾਰੀਐ ਜਿਤੁ ਦਰਿ ਸਚੈ ਸੋਭਾ ਪਾਇ॥ 1॥
Sloak M: 3: Purrh purrh pundit Bayd vukhaaneh(i) Maya moh sooayay. Doojaiy bhaayay Hur(i) Naam(u) visariya, mun moorukh milaiy sujaayay. Jin(i) jeeo pind(u) ditta, tis kubhoon na chaytaiy, jo daynda rijuk(u) sumbhahay. Jum ka phaha gulhoun na kuttiyaiy, phir(i) phir(i) aavaiy jaayay. Munmukh(i) kichhoo na soojhaiy undhulay, poorub(i) likhiya kmaayay. Pooraiy bhaag(i) Satgur(u) milaiy sookhdata, Naam(u) vusaiy mun(i) aayay. Sookh(u) maaneh(i) sookh(u) pehn-na, sookhay sookh(i) vihaayay. Nanak so Naoun munhou na visaariyaiy, jit(u) Dur(i) Sachaiy sobha paayay. 
Word Meaning:  Vukhaneh(i) = explain.   Sooayay = for the purpose of.   Doojaiy bhaayay = in love for the other (Maya).   Visariya = dropped from mind.   Pind(u) = body.   Kubhoon Na = never.  Phaha = noose.   Aavaiy Jaayay = comes and goes (is born and dies).  Soojhaiy = realize.   Poorub(i) Likhiya = predestined.  Vihaayay = is spent.
MEANING: The pundit studies and explains the Vedas for the sole purpose of acquiring money.   He drops Waheguru’s Naam from his mind in his love for Maya, as a result of which, the foolish follower of his mind is punished.  He never remembers Waheguru, who has given him life and body, and who provides him with food and other necessities.  The noose of the Angel of Death is never cut from around his neck, and he is reborn again and again. The mentally blind follower of his mind does not understand reality and keeps following the predestined path of life. However, with good fortune, one is connected with Satguru, who is the source of all comfort, and with whose grace, Waheguru’s Naam takes residence in his heart.  Then he enjoys all the comforts in every situation, so much so that he feels comfortable even in wearing low-quality clothes, and his whole life is spent in such comfort.  I pray that I should never forget Waheguru’s Naam, which earns respect in Waheguru’s Court, oh Nanak.   1.
