(ਅੰਗ 66)॥       ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ 

ਪੰਖੀ ਬਿਰਖਿ ਸੁਹਾਵੜਾ ਸਚੁ ਚੁਗੈ ਗੁਰ ਭਾਇ॥ ਹਰਿ ਰਸੁ ਪੀਵੈ ਸਹਜਿ ਰਹੈ ਉਡੈ ਨ ਆਵੈ ਜਾਇ॥ ਨਿਜ ਘਰਿ ਵਾਸਾ ਪਾਇਆ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮਿ ਸਮਾਇ॥ 1॥ ਮਨ ਰੇ ਗੁਰ ਕੀ ਕਾਰ ਕਮਾਇ॥ਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਭਾਣੈ ਜੇ ਚਲਹਿ ਤਾ ਅਨਦਿਨੁ ਰਾਚਹਿ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਇ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਪੰਖੀ ਬਿਰਖ ਸੁਹਾਵੜੇ ਊਡਹਿ ਚਹੁ ਦਿਸਿ ਜਾਹਿ॥ ਜੇਤਾ ਊਡਹਿ ਦੁਖ ਘਣੇ ਨਿਤ ਦਾਝਹਿ ਤੈ ਬਿਲਲਾਹਿ॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਗੁਰ ਮਹਲੁ ਨ ਜਾਪਈ ਨ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਫਲੁ ਪਾਹਿ॥ 2॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਬ੍ਰਹਮੁ ਹਰੀਆਵਲਾ ਸਾਚੈ ਸਹਜਿ ਸੁਭਾਇ॥ ਸਾਖਾ ਤੀਨਿ ਨਿਵਾਰੀਆ ਏਕ ਸਬਦਿ ਲਿਵ ਲਾਇ॥ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਫਲੁ ਹਰਿ ਏਕੁ ਹੈ ਆਪੇ ਦੇਇ ਖਵਾਇ॥ 3॥ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਊਭੇ ਸੁਕਿ ਗਏ ਨਾ ਫਲੁ ਤਿਨਾ ਛਾਉ॥ ਤਿਨਾ ਪਾਸਿ ਨ ਬੈਸੀਐ ਓਨਾ ਘਰੁ ਨ ਗਿਰਾਉ॥ ਕਟੀਅਹਿ ਤੈ ਨਿਤ ਜਾਲੀਅਹਿ ਓਨ੍ਹਾ ਸਬਦੁ ਨ ਨਾਉ॥ 4॥ ਹੁਕਮੇ ਕਰਮ ਕਮਾਵਣੇ ਪਇਐ ਕਿਰਤਿ ਫਿਰਾਉ॥ ਹੁਕਮੇ ਦਰਸਨੁ ਦੇਖਣਾ ਜਹ ਭੇਜਹਿ ਤਹ ਜਾਉ॥ ਹੁਕਮੇ ਹਰਿ ਹਰਿ ਮਨਿ ਵਸੈ ਹੁਕਮੇ ਸਚਿ ਸਮਾਉ॥ 5॥ ਹੁਕਮੁ ਨ ਜਾਣਹਿ ਬਪੁੜੇ ਭੂਲੇ ਫਿਰਹਿ ਗਵਾਰ॥ ਮਨ ਹਠਿ ਕਰਮ ਕਮਾਵਦੇ ਨਿਤ ਨਿਤ ਹੋਹਿ ਖੁਆਰੁ॥ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਸਾਤਿ ਨ ਆਵਈ ਨਾ ਸਚਿ ਲਗੈ ਪਿਆਰੁ॥ 6॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀਆ ਮੁਹ ਸੋਹਣੇ ਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਹੇਤਿ ਪਿਆਰਿ॥ ਸਚੀ ਭਗਤੀ ਸਚਿ ਰਤੇ ਦਰਿ ਸਚੈ ਸਚਿਆਰ॥ ਆਏ ਸੇ ਪਰਵਾਣੁ ਹੈ ਸਭ ਕੁਲ ਕਾ ਕਰਹਿ ਉਧਾਰੁ॥ 7॥ ਸਭ ਨਦਰੀ ਕਰਮ ਕਮਾਵਦੇ ਨਦਰੀ ਬਾਹਰਿ ਨ ਕੋਇ॥ ਜੈਸੀ ਨਦਰਿ ਕਰਿ ਦੇਖੈ ਸਚਾ ਤੈਸਾ ਹੀ ਕੋ ਹੋਇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮਿ ਵਡਾਈਆ ਕਰਮਿ ਪਰਾਪਤਿ ਹੋਇ॥ 8॥3॥20॥

Word Meaning:   Birkh(i) = (on the) tree.   Soohaavrra = pretty (having high moral values).   Choogaiy = picks.  Gur Bhaayay= (to the) liking of the Guru.    Sehj(i) = (in the) metal state above Maya.   Nij = own.   Ghur(i) = in the heart.     1.    Kaar Kmaayay = act as per (Guru’s advice).   Bhaanaiy = as per the Will.   Raacheh(i) = get imbued.    Pause.
Chuhou Dis(i) = all four directions.   Ghunay = way too many.   Daajheh(i) = burns (suffers).   Billaheh(i) = wails; repents.    Mehl(u) = (God’s) palace.   Amrit = liquid of immortality (life-sustaining Guru’s Shabad).   Paaheh(i) = receives.     2.      Braham = (like of) God.   Soobhaayay = good nature (intentions).    Shaakhaan Teen(i) = the effects (of Maya: Tumo = darkness (no awareness); Rujo = normal; balanced (some awareness), Suto = top state (more awareness).    Nivaariyaan = removed; gotten rid of.      3.      Oobhay = stalled (no improvement).   Sook(i) = dried up.   Giraaoun = village (shelter).   Tai = and.   Nit = every day.     4.      Kerm = deeds.    Puyaiy Kirt(i) = the results of deeds done in previous lives.  Phiraaou = running around.    Huqamay = as per (God’s Will).   Dershun(u) = (holy) sight.   5.
Bupurray = ignorant.    Gvaar = unrefined; crude.  Mun Hutth(i) = obstinacy of mind.   Khooaar(u) = suffer because of no success; discomfiture.    Shaant(i) = peace.       6.
Muh = moonh = faces.   Hayt(i) = for.  Ruttay = imbued.    Sachiyaar = bearers of Truth.   Pervaan(u) = approved.    Kool = clan; ancestry.   Oodhaar(u) = salvation.     7.
Nudri = sight.   Vudaayiyaan = praise.   Kerm(i) = kindness.  Praput(i) = receiving.    8.
MEANING: Lead your life as per the Teachings of the Guru, oh my mind. If you obey Guru’s advice, you will stay connected with Waheguru’s Naam all the time.      Pause.
(Human mind is unstable, and takes flights like) a bird perched on a tree, which can fly freely in any direction to pick food. A human mind is pretty (has noble qualities) if it picks the food of Truth (Waheguru’s Naam) with love for the Guru. He enjoys the juice of Waheguru’s Naam and attains the spiritual level above the effects of Maya, as a result of which he is not caught in the rebirth cycle. Such an individual’s mind becomes stable, and he stays connected with Waheguru’s Naam.          1.          But the human mind, which takes flights in every direction in pursuit of Maya, does not become stable, and as a result, he suffers severe discomfort, and his mind burns (becomes restless and suffers), and he wails in suffering. Without the Guru, he cannot acquire the Amrit–fruit (life-sustaining Waheguru’s Naam), and cannot reach Waheguru’s Palace.          2.          In His love, a true devotee of the Guru attains the spiritual level above the effects of Maya and becomes “one” with Waheguru. He becomes like an evergreen tree, providing shade and fruit (for the needy) in every season. By being imbued in the Guru’s Shabad, he overcomes the three known effects of Maya.  Waheguru is the only source of the Amrit-fruit, and He Himself feeds it to His devotees.         3.
The followers of their mind become like leafless trees which bear no fruit, and have no shade (they have nothing positive to offer to anyone). One should not join a company because they have no home (stable mind, and definite objective).  Since they do not act on Guru’s Shabad, they do not have Waheguru’s Naam; as a result, they always suffer like a dead tree that is cut and burned.        4.             But they are not fully to blame because (based on their deeds of previous lives) they are predestined to perform deeds of haumein (selfishness and ego).  They have to obey Waheguru’s Huqam whether they are allowed to have a “sight” of Waheguru, or to do other deeds.  It is with Waheguru’s Huqam that a man makes his heart Waheguru’s residence, and merges with Him (or not).           5.          The ignorant do not understand Waheguru’s Will, and the unrefined wander around, misguided. They perform deeds guided by their mind and suffer all the time without any success (spiritual gain).   They do not develop any affection for Waheguru, and as a result, find no peace of mind.         6.
In the love for Guru, His devotees have pretty faces (internal peace because of high moral values). With true devotion, they are imbued in Waheguru’s Naam, and are honoured in Waheguru’s Court as the bearers of Truth.  Their deeds are approved by Waheguru, and along with them, their clan (other devotees in their company) is also salvaged.          7.
Everybody performs deeds under Waheguru’s supervision, and nobody can escape His supervision. A person acquires the character as it is moulded under Waheguru’s supervision. Waheguru’s Naam earns respect, which one can acquire only by the benevolence of Waheguru, oh Nanak.    8.3.20

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ 

ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਧਿਆਈਐ ਮਨਮੁਖਿ ਬੂਝ ਨ ਪਾਇ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਸਦਾ ਮੁਖ ਊਜਲੇ ਹਰਿ ਵਸਿਆ ਮਨਿ ਆਇ॥ ਸਹਜੇ ਹੀ ਸੁਖ ਪਾਈਐ ਸਹਜੇ ਰਹੈ ਸਮਾਇ॥ 1॥ ਭਾਈ ਰੇ ਦਾਸਨਿ ਦਾਸਾ ਹੋਇ॥ ਗੁਰ ਕੀ ਸੇਵਾ ਗੁਰ ਭਗਤਿ ਹੈ ਵਿਰਲਾ ਪਾਏ ਕੋਇ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਸਦਾ ਸੁਹਾਗੁ ਸੁਹਾਗਣੀ ਜੇ ਚਲਹਿ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਭਾਇ॥ ਸਦਾ ਪਿਰੁ ਨਿਹਚਲੁ ਪਾਈਐ ਨਾ ਓਹੁ ਮਰੈ ਨ ਜਾਇ॥ ਸਬਦਿ ਮਿਲੀ ਨ ਵੀਛੁੜੈ ਪਿਰ ਕੈ ਅੰਕਿ ਸਮਾਇ॥ 2॥ ਹਰਿ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਅਤਿ ਊਜਲਾ ਬਿਨੁ ਗੁਰ ਪਾਇਆ ਨ ਜਾਇ॥ ਪਾਠੁ ਪੜੈ ਨ ਬੂਝਈ ਭੇਖੀ ਭਰਮਿ ਭੁਲਾਇ॥ ਗੁਰਮਤੀ ਹਰਿ ਸਦਾ ਪਾਇਆ ਰਸਨਾ ਹਰਿ ਰਸੁ ਸਮਾਇ॥ 3॥ਮਾਇਆ ਮੋਹੁ ਚੁਕਾਇਆ ਗੁਰਮਤੀ ਸਹਜਿ ਸੁਭਾਇ॥
