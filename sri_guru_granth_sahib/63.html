Mayray Preetma, mein Toojh bin(u) uver(u) na koyay. Mein Toojh bin(u) uver(u) na bhaavyee, Tu bhaaveh(i) sookh(u) hoyay. 1. Ruhaou.  Naam(u) salaaheen rung siyoun, Gur kaiy Shabad suntokh(u). Jo deesaiy so chulsee koorra moh(u) na vaykh(u). Vaat vutayoo aaya, nit chulda saath(u) daykh(u). 2. Aakhun(i) aakheh(i) kaytrray Gur bin(u) boojh na hoyay. Naam(u) vudayee jay milaiy Sach(i) rupaiy put(i) hoyay. Jo toodh(u) bhaaveh(i) say bhulay, khota khura na koyay. 3.  Gur shernaayee chhootiyaiy manmukh khoti raas(i). Asht dhaat(u) Patshah kee, ghurriyaiy Shabad(i) vigaas(i). Aapay purkhay Paarkhoo, puvaiy khjaanaiy raas(i). 4. Tayri keemut(i) na puvaiy subh ditthi tthok(i) vujaayay. Aasaan undar(i) jummiya, aasaan rus kus khaayay. Aasa bundh(i) chlaayiyaiy, moohay mooh(i) choataan khaayay. Uvgun(i) buddha maariyaiy chhootaiy Gurmut(i) Naayay. 7.

Word Meaning:  Trishna = desire for Maya.  Mohni = captivating.   Soot = sons.   Bundhup = relatives.    Tthugiya = defrauded; deceived; duped.    Lub = lobh = greed.  Tthugaouli = a substance that causes temporary unconsciousness (usually used to rob people).    Houn = I; me.      1.     Uver(u) = other; else.   Bhavyee = like.      Pause.       Rung = affection.    Siyoun = with.  Suntokh = contentment.    Koorra = false; temporary.   Vaat = path.   Vutayoo = visitor.      2.         Aakhin(i) = just to quote.   Vudayee = praise.   Ruppaiy = imbued.   Khota = fake; impure.    Khura = real; pure.      3.       Shernaayee = (in the) shelter.  Raas(i) = capital; investment.  Asht Dhaat(u) = eight ingredients that make up human body (blood, bones, veins, skin, fat, muscles, semen and hair). MEANING: I have no other support than You, oh My dear (Waheguru).  I do not like anybody else; I feel blissful to like You.      Pause.            Sons, relatives, wife at home, and desires are all forms of Maya, which is captivating. The world is being defrauded by wealth, youthfulness, greed, and ego.  I am also a victim of the deceiving worldly attachments (which induce temporary unconsciousness); in fact, the whole world is their victim.      1.
(To escape from the captivating Maya), I sing praise of Naam (Waheguru), taking Guru’s shelter because of which I find contentment. One must realize that everything visible is perishable, and love for them is false (short-lived).  So, one should not pay any attention to them.  Watching people leaving this world all the time, one can realize that he is here on this earth like a visitor, and life is just a short journey.         2.
If we were to count, there are countless people who talk (about God), but they get no enlightenment without the Guru.  If one gets enlightened about the greatness of the Naam, he earns respect by getting imbued in Waheguru. We cannot judge people and label them as good or bad; only those are good whom You like, oh Waheguru.      3.
One can get freedom from the bonds of Maya only by the grace of the Guru; the capital (spiritual investment) of a follower of his mind is fake (not acceptable to Waheguru).  The eight-elemental human body belongs to Waheguru, and it feels blissful when it is moulded in Guru’s Shabad. The adjudicator Waheguru Himself examines individuals, and those who have the investment in spirituality, succeed and become part of Waheguru’s Treasure.     4.
People have tried to examine You from every angle, and no one can evaluate You, oh Waheguru. (Let alone evaluating You), Nobody can achieve anything spiritually simply by saying things about You; only those are honoured who keep You in mind all the time.  You can only be praised in the shelter of the Guru; other than that, nobody is qualified to say anything about Your magnanimity.         5.          The mind, which has no liking for the Naam, is a home for haumein and tussle (lack of peace). No one can get enlightenment without the Guru, and the love for Maya is like a poison (which kills spirituality).  Without noble qualities, nothing is acceptable (in Waheguru’s Court) as Maya has no place in spirituality.       6.
The man is born with some hopes and desires, and he lives his life experiencing tasty and sour foods (good and bad results of his deeds).  Engrossed in hopes and desires, he leaves this world and suffers continuous punishment on his face (mental torture).  Man receives punishment because of his ill qualities, but is salvaged in the shelter of the Guru when he worships (Waheguru’s) Naam.        7.
You are omnipresent; please protect me (from the attacks of Maya) in whatever way You please, oh Waheguru.  When the True Naam takes residence in the heart of a person in the Guru’s shelter, goodness and respect automatically come with it. By reciting the Naam of the only Truth – Waheguru – by Guru’s Shabad, the disease of haumein is cured (and man becomes “one” with Waheguru).          8.        You are present everywhere - in the sky, in the underworld, and in the whole universe – oh Waheguru.  You yourself are the love for devotion, and You connect with people through the Guru.  Please, Waheguru, be kind to me so that I may not forget Your Naam, and may live my life as per Your Huqam (Will), oh Nanak.    9.13.

Shabad soort(i) sookh(u) oopjaiy Prubh raatou sookh saar(u).  Jiyoun bhaavaiy tiyoun raakh(u) Tu, mein Hur(i) Naam(u) adhaar(u). 1.  Mun ray, Saachi Khusm rujaayay. Jin(i) tun(u) mun(u) saaj(i) seegaariya, Tis(u) saytee liv laayay. 1. Ruhaou. Tun(u) baisuntar(i) homiyaiy, ikk rutti toal(i) kutaayay. Tun(u) mun(u) smudha jay kureen, undin(u) ugn(i) jlaayay. Hur(i) Naamaiy tool(i) na poojyee subh ditthi tthok(i) vujaayay.  3. Kunchan kay kot dut(u) kuree, buhout haiyvur gaiyvur daan(u). Bhoom(i) daan(u) guooaan ghuni, bhee untar(i) gerb(u) goomaan(u). Word Meaning: Baydhiya = pierced.    Uver(u) = else.  Soort(i) = concentration. Oopjaiy = is produced.   Raatou = imbued.    Saar(u) = supreme.  Adhaar(u) = support.     1.

NOTE: In this holy Shabad, Guru Nanak Sahib discusses the futility of self-torture, donations to undeserving people, and other prevalent rituals to please Waheguru. He emphasizes the important role of the Guru in the union with God. 
MEANING:  Leading one’s life according to the Will (of Waheguru) is the only true path to connect with Him, oh my mind. Concentrate on the Naam (of God) who has not just given the (human) body and mind, but has also refined it.       Pause.
When my heart is pierced with Waheguru’s Naam, I do not need to think about anyone else. Acting on Guru’s Shabad and concentrating on it produces comfort in one’s mind, and imbuing the mind with Waheguru’s Naam results in the supreme comfort (mental bliss). Please, Waheguru, protect me in whatever way You like, because Your Naam is my sole support.         1.             If someone burns himself alive, or cuts his body into small pieces, the size of a Rutti, or offers his mind and body as fuel for a yug day and night, or performs millions of other prevalent rituals (to please some god), there is no spiritual gain, because all such acts of self-torture are fruitless as nothing comes even close to Waheguru’s Naam as far as union with God is concerned.           2.
If someone cuts his body into two pieces, or has his head cut by the saw (in Banaras- now Varanasi), or exposes his body to the ice on Himalayas for freezing (again to please some god), all such gory acts are useless because one’s ego does not go away (in fact it is enhanced) (the destruction of which is a prerequisite to the union with God).  We have tested from every angle, and nothing comes close to the recitation of Waheguru’s Naam.     3.
If someone donates forts (mountains) of gold, many excellent horses and elephants, or gives land, and many, many cows, such donations are useless because they produce more ego and conceit. The real donation (that counts for union with Waheguru) is getting one’s mind pierced with His Naam, which is given (fostered in mind) by the Guru.      4.
Many people use obstinacy (continuous but misdirected effort), or their wisdom, or ponder on the Vedas, or put constraints on their mind (to please gods); but all such approaches are useless to please Waheguru, who is pleased only when people take the Guru’s shelter, and cut the bonds of Maya by His advice.  Every other approach is lower than the worship of God, which is higher than even high living.            5.           We should treat everybody as belonging to the high class, and nobody is lower, because the same God has created everybody as equal, and it is His light which is present in all the three worlds (three-layered universe).  One receives His Naam (gets connected to Him) only by His mercy, and nobody can erase His gift, which He gives to individuals right from the beginning.        6.
Holy men keep company with other holy men only, and the contentment is achieved when one lives their life as per the Guru’s Dictum. That way, the man becomes “one” with the Guru, and ponders on the story of the indescribable Waheguru (His traits) as enlightened by the Guru. One achieves complete contentment in Guru’s shelter as he drinks Waheguru’s Naam- Amrit, and is honoured in Waheguru’s Court.         7.  
In love with Guru’s Shabad, each heart feels as if soothing music of the fife is playing there day and night. But only a rare person is enlightened in the Guru’s shelter who can educate (control) his mind.  One should not forget Waheguru’s Naam because the bonds of Maya can be broken only by following the Guru’s Teachings, oh Nanak.      8.14.
