(ਅੰਗ 87)॥   ਗੁਰਮਤੀ ਜਮੁ ਜੋਹਿ ਨਾ ਸਾਕੈ ਸਾਚੈ ਨਾਮਿ ਸਮਾਇਆ॥ ਸਭੁ ਆਪੇ ਆਪਿ ਵਰਤੈ ਕਰਤਾ ਜੋ ਭਾਵੈ ਸੋ ਨਾਇ ਲਾਇਆ॥ ਜਨ ਨਾਨਕੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਲਏ ਤਾ ਜੀਵੈ ਬਿਨੁ ਨਾਵੈ ਖਿਨੁ ਮਰਿ ਜਾਇਆ॥ 2॥
M:3:  Gur(i) Pooraiy Hur(i) Naam(u) drirraaya, tin vichoun bherm(u) chookaaya. Ram Naam(u) Hur(i) keerut(i) gaayee, kur(i) chaanun(u) mug(u) dikhaaya. Haumein maar(i) Aik liv laagi, untar(i) Naam(u) vusaaya. 
(PAGE 87):          Gurmatee Jum(u) joh(i) na saakaiy, Sachaiy Naam(i) smaaya. Subh(u) Aapay aap(i) vertaiy Kerta, jo bhaavaiy so Naayay laaya. Jun Nanak(u) Naam(u) luyay ta jeevaiy, bin(u) Naavaiy khin(u) mer(i) jaaya.  2.
Word Meaning:  Drirraaya = reinforced.   Bherm(u) = doubt; uncertainty.  Chookaaya = removed.   Keerut(i) = praise; adoration.   Mug(u) = path.   Liv = connection; concentration on.  Joh(i) = watch (critically).   Khin(u) = quickly.
MEANING:  The Perfect Guru reinforces Waheguru’s Naam among the devotees, whereby all their doubts are cleared.  The Guru lightens up the path (for union with Waheguru) by singing praise of Waheguru’s Naam.  He kills selfishness and ego from the minds of His Sikhs, as a result of which, they get connected with Waheguru, and the Naam takes residence in their hearts. Following the advice of the Guru, the devotees get absorbed in Waheguru’s Naam, and the Angel of Death cannot keep a watchful eye on them.  The Creator, Waheguru, is everywhere, and He makes His favourites recite His Naam.  I live (spiritually) only as long as I recite Waheguru’s Naam; the moment I am without His Naam, I experience (spiritual) death, oh Nanak.    2

ਪਉੜੀ॥ ਜੋ ਮਿਲਿਆ ਹਰਿ ਦੀਬਾਣੁ ਸਿਉ ਸੋ ਸਭਨੀ ਦੀਬਾਣੀ ਮਿਲਿਆ॥ ਜਿਥੈ ਓਹੁ ਜਾਇ ਤਿਥੈ ਓਹੁ ਸੁਰਖਰੂ ਓਸ ਕੈ ਮੁਹਿ ਡਿਠੈ ਸਭ ਪਾਪੀ ਤਰਿਆ॥ ਓਸੁ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਨਿਧਾਨੁ ਹੈ ਨਾਮੋ ਪਰਵਰਿਆ॥ ਨਾਉ ਪੂਜੀਐ ਨਾਉ ਮੰਨੀਐ ਨਾਇ ਕਿਲਵਿਖ ਸਭ ਹਿਰਿਆ॥ ਜਿਨੀ ਨਾਮੁ ਧਿਆਇਆ ਇਕ ਮਨਿ ਇਕ ਚਿਤਿ ਸੇ ਅਸਥਿਰੁ ਜਗਿ ਰਹਿਆ॥ 11॥
Paurri:  Jo miliya Hur(i) deebaan(u) siyoun, so subhnee deebaani miliya. Jithaiy oh(u) jaayay, tithaiy oh(u) soorkhroo, oas kaiy moonh(i) ditthaiy, subh paapi turiyaa. Oas untar(i) Naam(u) nidhaan(u) haiy, Naamo pervuriya. Naaoun poojiyaiy, Naaoun munniyaiy, Naayay kilvikh subh hiriya. Jinhi Naam(u) dhiyayaa ikk mun, ikk chit(i), say 
usthir(u) jug(i) ruhiyya. 
Word Meaning:  Deebaan(u) = court; ruler.   Siyoun = with.  Miliya = honoured.   Soorkhroo = clear; cannot be questioned for anything.  Pervuriya = transformed.  Kilvikh = sins.  Hiriya = removed.   usthir = immortal
MEANING: The individual, who is honoured in Waheguru’s Court, is honoured in all 
other courts. Nobody questions him, no matter where he goes, and the sinners are salvaged by his holy sight (they are so enchanted by his preaching that they start to recite Waheguru’s Naam).  He is completely transformed into a holy man because of the treasure of Waheguru’s Naam inside him.  We, too, should worship Waheguru’s Naam and obey Him so as to get rid of all our sins. Those who recite Waheguru’s Naam with full concentration become immortal in the world.  11.

ਸਲੋਕ ਮ:3॥ ਆਤਮਾ ਦੇਉ ਪੂਜੀਐ ਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਸਹਜਿ ਸੁਭਾਇ॥ ਆਤਮੇ ਨੋ ਆਤਮੇ ਦੀ ਪ੍ਰਤੀਤਿ ਹੋਇ ਤਾ ਘਰਿ ਹੀ ਪਰਚਾ ਪਾਇ॥ ਆਤਮਾ ਅਡੋਲੁ ਨ ਡੋਲਈ ਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਭਾਇ ਸੁਭਾਇ॥ ਗੁਰ ਵਿਣੁ ਸਹਜੁ ਨ ਆਵਈ ਲੋਭੁ ਮੈਲੁ ਨ ਵਿਚਹੁ ਜਾਇ॥ ਖਿਨੁ ਪਲੁ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਮਨਿ ਵਸੈ ਸਭ ਅਠਸਠਿ ਤੀਰਥ ਨਾਇ॥ ਸਚੇ ਮੈਲੁ ਨ ਲਗਈ ਮਲੁ ਲਾਗੈ ਦੁਜੈ ਭਾਇ॥ ਧੋਤੀ ਮੂਲਿ ਨ ਉਤਰੈ ਜੇ ਅਠਸਠਿ ਤੀਰਥ ਨਾਇ॥ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਕਰਮ ਕਰੇ ਅਹੰਕਾਰੀ ਸਭ ਦੁਖੋ ਦੁਖੁ ਕਮਾਇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਮੈਲਾ ਊਜਲੁ ਤਾ ਥੀਐ ਜਾ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਮਾਹਿ ਸਮਾਇ॥ 1॥
Sloak M:3:  Aatma Dayo poojiyaiy, Gur kaiy sehj(i) soobhaayaiy. Aatmay nu aatmay dee perteet(i) hoyay, ta ghur(i) hee percha paayay. Aatma udoal, na doalyee, Gur kaiy bhaayay soobhaayay. Gur vin(u) sehj(u) na aavyee, lobh(u) maiyl(u) na vichoun jaayay. Khin(u) pul(u) Hur(i) Naam(u) mun(i) vusaiy, subh utth-sutth(i) teeruth naayay. Sachay maiyl(u) na lugyee, mul(u) laagaiy doojaiy bhaayay. Dhoti mool(i) na ootraiy, jay utth-sutth(i) teeruth naayay. Munmukh kerm kuray ahunkaari, subh dookho dookh(u) kmaayay. Nanak maiyla oojul(u) ta theeyay, ja Satgur maah(i) smaayay. 1.
Word Meaning:  Aatma Dayo = light of soul (Waheguru).  Sehj (i) = blissfulness state of mind; high spiritual state.    Soobhayay = with love.  Perteet(i) = full faith.  Ghur(i) = in the heart.  Percha = gets attracted to.  Udoal = unwavering.   Utth-sutth(i) = sixty-eight (Hindu hoy places).  Doojaiy Bhaayay = in the love for the other (Maya).  Oojul(u) = clean. 
MEANING:  One should worship God with an unwavering mind and with love for the Guru. When one acquires full faith in the Supreme Soul (Waheguru), he gets attracted to (and makes a connection with) Waheguru in his own mind.  With love for the Guru, one’s soul (mind) becomes blissful, and one's mind does not waver. One’s filth of greed does not go away without the Guru, and his mind does not acquire peace.  If Waheguru’s Naam is implanted in one’s mind even for a short moment, he does not need to take dip in any of the sixty-eight Hindu holy places.  The human mind becomes filthy only when it has love for Maya; a devotee who is “one” with Waheguru never has the filth of greed on his mind. And the filth of the mind can never be washed by pilgrimage, even if one takes a dip in all the holy places.  The follower of his mind performs deeds under the influence of the ego, thereby earning discomfort all the way.  A filthy mind can be cleansed only if one surrenders to the Guru, oh Nanak.  1.

ਮ:3॥ ਮਨਮੁਖੁ ਲੋਕੁ ਸਮਝਾਈਐ ਕਦਹੁ ਸਮਝਾਇਆ ਜਾਇ॥ ਮਨਮੁਖੁ ਰਲਾਇਆ ਨਾ ਰਲੈ ਪਇਐ ਕਿਰਤਿ ਫਿਰਾਇ॥ ਲਿਵ ਧਾਤੁ ਦੁਇ ਰਾਹ ਹੈ ਹੁਕਮੀ ਕਾਰ ਕਮਾਇ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਆਪਣਾ ਮਨੁ ਮਾਰਿਆ ਸਬਦਿ ਕਸਵਟੀ ਲਾਇ॥ ਮਨ ਹੀ ਨਾਲਿ ਝਗੜਾ ਮਨ ਹੀ ਨਾਲਿ ਸਥ ਮਨ ਹੀ ਮੰਝਿ ਸਮਾਇ॥ ਮਨੁ ਜੋ ਇਛੇ ਸੋ ਲਹੈ ਸਚੈ ਸਬਦਿ ਸੁਭਾਇ॥ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਨਾਮੁ ਸਦ ਭੁੰਚੀਐ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਕਾਰ ਕਮਾਇ॥ ਵਿਣੁ ਮਨੈ ਜਿ ਹੋਰੀ ਨਾਲਿ ਲੁਝਣਾ ਜਾਸੀ ਜਨਮੁ ਗਵਾਇ॥ ਮਨਮੁਖੀ ਮਨ ਹਠਿ ਹਾਰਿਆ ਕੂੜੁ ਕੁਸਤਿ ਕਮਾਇ॥ ਗੁਰ ਪਰਸਾਦੀ ਮਨੁ ਜਿਣੈ ਹਰਿ ਸੇਤੀ ਲਿਵ ਲਾਇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਸਚੁ ਕਮਾਵੈ ਮਨਮੁਖਿ ਆਵੈ ਜਾਇ॥ 2॥
M:3:  Munmukh(u) loak(u) sumjhayiyaiy, kud-houn sumjhayaa jaayay. Munmukh(u) rulaaya na rulaiy, puyya kirt(i) phirayay. Liv dhaat(u) doyay raah haiy, Huqami kaar kmaayay. Gurmukh(i) aapna mun(u) maariya, Shabad(i) kusvutti laayay. Mun hee naal(i) jhuggrra, mun hee naal(i) suth, mun hee munjh smaayay. Mun(u) jo ichhay so luhaiy, Sachaiy Shabad(i) soobhayay. Amrit Naam(u) sud bhoonchiyaiy, Gurmukh(i) kaar kmaayay.  Vin(u) munaiy j(i) hoari naal(i) loojhna, jaasi junum(u) guvaayay. Munmukhi mun hutth(i) haariya, koorr(u) koosut(i) kmaayay. Gur Persaadi mun(u) jinaiy, Hur(i) sayti liv laayay. Nanak Gurmukh(i) Sach(u) kmaavaiy, munmukh(i) aavaiy jaayay.   2.

Word Meaning:  Kud-houn = when? (never).    Rulaiy = mingle with.    Puyya Kirt(i) = character based on previous deeds.    Liv = focus (on Waheguru’s Naam);   Dhaat(u) = love for Maya.   Kusvutti = touchstone.    Jhuggrra = struggle.     Suth company; co-operation.    Munjh = in.    Luhaiy = acquires.   Soobhaayay = with good intentions.   Bhoonchiyaiy = enjoy.        Loojhna = fight. Hutth(i) = stubbornness.  Koorr(u)  Koosut(i) = lies; mundane stuff.   Jinaiy = win over.   Sayti = with.
MEANING: If a follower of his mind is given advice (to do noble deeds), he will never accept the advice.  Even if you try hard, such a man will not mingle with Waheguru’s devotees and will keep following the predestined path of life.  There are two ways to live – connecting with Waheguru and running around for Maya. Everybody follows the path as per the Will of God for them.  A devotee controls his mind by following the Guru’s Shabad, which serves as a touchstone for his mind.   A devotee struggles with his mind, seeks its co-operation, and gets absorbed in it (i.e., conquers it.)   Following on Guru’s Shabad with a clean mind, a man can acquire anything he wishes for. If a man spends his life as per the Guru’s advice, he always enjoys the taste of immortalizing Waheguru’s Naam.  An individual who fights over mundane things rather than using his mind wastes away his precious life.  The followers of their mind lose because of their stubbornness, and deal only in lies and mundane stuff.  The only way to win over the mind (control it) is to concentrate on Waheguru’s Naam in the shelter of the Guru.  A devotee deals in Truth (and becomes “one” with Waheguru), while the follower of his mind is reborn again, and again, oh Nanak.  2.

ਪਉੜੀ॥ ਹਰਿ ਕੇ ਸੰਤ ਸੁਣਹੁ ਜਨ ਭਾਈ ਹਰਿ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਕੀ ਇਕ ਸਾਖੀ॥ ਜਿਸੁ ਧੁਰਿ ਭਾਗੁ ਹੋਵੈ ਮੁਖਿ ਮਸਤਕਿ ਤਿਨਿ ਜਨਿ ਲੈ ਹਿਰਦੈ ਰਾਖੀ॥ ਹਰਿ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਕਥਾ ਸਰੇਸਟ ਉਤਮ ਗੁਰ ਬਚਨੀ ਸਹਜੇ ਚਾਖੀ॥ ਤਹ ਭਇਆ ਪ੍ਰਗਾਸੁ ਮਿਟਿਆ ਅੰਧਿਆਰਾ ਜਿਉ ਸੂਰਜੁ ਰੈਣਿ ਕਿਰਾਖੀ॥ ਅਦਿਸਟੁ ਅਗੋਚਰੁ ਅਲਖੁ ਨਿਰੰਜਨੁ ਸੋ ਦੇਖਿਆ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਆਖੀ॥ 12॥
Paurri: Hur(i) kay sunt soonhou jun bhaayee, Hur(i) Satgur kee ikk saakhi. Jis(u) dhoor(i) bhaag(u) hovaiy mookh(i) mustik(i), tin(i) jun(i) laiy hirdaiy raakhi.  Hur(i) Amrit kutha srayshut ootum, Gur Buchni sehjay chaakhi. Teh bhuyya pergaas(u), mitiya undhiyara, jiyoun sooruj(u) raiyn(i) kiraakhi.  Udisht(u) Agochar(u) Alukh(u) Nirunjun(u), so daykhiya Gurmukh(i) aankhi.  12.
Word Meaning:  Sunt = saint; holy people.  Saakhi = advice.  Dhoor(i) Bhaag(u) = predestined fortune.   Mustik(i) = forehead.  Amrit = immortalizing.  Srayshut = Ootum = supreme.  Sehjay = with a peaceful mind.    Chaakhi = tasted.   Kiraakhi = pulls out.  Udisht(u) = invisible.  Agochar(u) = unreachable.  Alukh(u) = incomprehensible.   Nirunjun = beyond the effects of Maya. 
MEANING: Listen to the advice of the Guru, oh holy men - the devotees of Waheguru.  Those who have fortune prewritten on their forehead keep the advice of the Guru safely in their mind. The immortalizing story of Waheguru (His praise) is supreme, and one can taste it with a peaceful mind (above the effects of Maya) in the form of Guru’s Shabad.  The darkness of such a mind is pulled out (dispelled) by enlightenment just as the sun pulls out the darkness of night.  A true devotee can “see” the otherwise invisible, unreachable, and incomprehensible Waheguru, which is beyond the effects of Maya.    12.
