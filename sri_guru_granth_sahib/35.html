(ਅੰਗ 34)॥                ਸਬਦਿ ਮੰਨਿਐ ਗੁਰੁ ਪਾਈਐ ਵਿਚਹੁ ਆਪੁ ਗਵਾਇ॥ ਅਨਦਿਨੁ ਭਗਤਿ ਕਰੇ ਸਦਾ ਸਾਚੇ ਕੀ ਲਿਵ ਲਾਇ॥ ਨਾਮੁ ਪਦਾਰਥੁ ਮਨਿ ਵਸਿਆ ਨਾਨਕ ਸਹਜਿ ਸਮਾਇ॥ 4॥19॥52॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 3:  Kirpa kuray Gur(u) paayiyaiy, Hur(i) Naamo dayay drirraayay. Bin(u) Gur kinaiy na paayo, birtha junum(u) guvaayay. Munmukh kerm kmaavnay Durgeh milaiy sjaayay. 1. Mun ray dooja bhaao chookayay. Untar(i) tayraiy Hur(i) vusaiy, Gur sayva sookh(u) paayay. Ruhaou.   Sach(u) Bani Sach(u) Shabad(u) haiy, ja Such(i) dhuray piyaar(u). Hur(i) ka Naam(u) mun(i) vusaiy haumein krodh(u) nivaar(i). Mun(i) nirmul Naam(u) dhiyayiyaiy, ta paayay mokh dooaar(u).2. Haumein vich(i) jug(u) binusda, mur(i) jummaiy aavaiy jaayay. Munmukh Shabad(u) na jaan-nee jaasun(i) put(i) guvaayay. Gur sayva Naaoun paayiyaiy, Sachay ruhaiy smaayay. 3. 
(PAGE 34):            Shabad(i) munniyaiy Gur(u) paayiyaiy, vichoun aap(u) guvaayay. Undin(u) bhugut(i) kuray suda, Saachay kee liv laayay. Naam(u) pdaruth mun(i) vussiya, Nanak sehj(i) smaayay. 4.19.52.
Word Meaning:  Drirraayay = reinforces; emphasizes.  Kinaiy na = nobody.   Birtha = without achieving the objective.   Guvaayay. Wastes.   Munmookh = follower of the mind.       1.
Dooja Bhaao = love for the other (Maya).   Chookayay. = end.        Pause.        Bani = Guru’s Word.    Ja = if; when.   Krodh(u) = rage.    Nivaar(i) = overcoming; getting rid of.     Nirmul = pure; holy.      Mokh = freedom from (Maya).         2.        Jug(u) = world.  Binusda = vanishes; wastes life.  Jaasun(i) = will leave.  Put(i) = dignity.   Naaoun = (Waheguru’s) Naam.   Smaayay = merged.        3.         Munniyaiy = obeying.   Aap(u) = self (selfishness).    Liv = full concentration.  Pdaruth = (valuable) article.    Smaayay = merge (becomes one with)
MEANING: When Waheguru is kind, He connects you with the Guru who instills and reinforces Waheguru’s Naam in your mind. Nobody ever connects with Waheguru without the Guru’s mediation, and disconnected people waste away their lives. They perform deeds following their own mind, because of which, they are punished in Waheguru’s Court.        1.
Serve the Guru, and renounce your love for Maya to enjoy bliss, because then Waheguru (the source of bliss) makes your heart His abode, oh my mind.          Pause.
When one fosters love for Waheguru in His mind, he realizes that Guru’s Bani, Guru’s Shabad, are eternal like Waheguru.  Acting on Guru’s Shabad, one overcomes his haumein and rage, and Waheguru takes residence in his mind.  When one recites Waheguru’s Naam with a pure mind, he breaks all the bonds of Maya and enters Waheguru’s door (becomes “one” with Him).          2.             The world vanishes because of haumein, and keeps taking rebirths. The follower of his mind does not realize the importance of the Guru’s Shabad and leaves this world, losing his dignity.  Waheguru’s Naam can be realized only by serving the Guru, and one also stays connected with Waheguru.          3.        One connects with the Guru when he obeys His Shabad and rids himself of his selfishness.  Such an individual always keeps Waheguru in mind and recites His Naam with full concentration.  The priceless Naam takes residence in his heart, and he attains the state of bliss through his union with God, oh Nanak.        4.19.52.

ਸਿਰੀਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ ਜਿਨੀ ਪੁਰਖੀ ਸਤਗੁਰੁ ਨ ਸੇਵਿਓ ਸੇ ਦੁਖੀਏ ਜੁਗ ਚਾਰਿ॥ ਘਰਿ ਹੋਦਾ ਪੁਰਖੁ ਨ ਪਛਾਣਿਆ ਅਭਿਮਾਨਿ ਮੁਠੇ ਅਹੰਕਾਰਿ॥ ਸਤਗੁਰੂ ਕਿਆ ਫਿਟਕਿਆ ਮੰਗਿ ਥਕੇ ਸੰਸਾਰਿ॥ ਸਚਾ ਸਬਦੁ ਨ ਸੇਵਿਓ ਸਭਿ ਕਾਜ ਸਵਾਰਣਹਾਰੁ॥ 1॥ ਮਨ ਮੇਰੇ ਸਦਾ ਹਰਿ ਵੇਖੁ ਹਦੂਰਿ॥ ਜਨਮ ਮਰਨ ਦੁਖੁ ਪਰਹਰੈ ਸਬਦਿ ਰਹਿਆ ਭਰਪੂਰਿ॥1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਸਚੁ ਸਲਾਹਨਿ ਸੇ ਸਚੇ ਸਚਾ ਨਾਮੁ ਅਧਾਰੁ॥ ਸਚੀ ਕਾਰ ਕਮਾਵਣੀ ਸਚੇ ਨਾਲਿ ਪਿਆਰੁ॥ ਸਚਾ ਸਾਹੁ ਵਰਤਦਾ ਕੋਇ ਨ ਮੇਟਣਹਾਰੁ॥ ਮਨਮੁਖ ਮਹਲੁ ਨ ਪਾਇਨੀ ਕੂੜਿ ਮੁਠੇ ਕੂੜਿਆਰ॥2॥ ਹਉਮੈ ਕਰਤਾ ਜਗੁ ਮੁਆ ਗੁਰ ਬਿਨੁ ਘੋਰ ਅੰਧਾਰੁ॥ ਮਾਇਆ ਮੋਹਿ ਵਿਸਾਰਿਆ ਸੁਖਦਾਤਾ ਦਾਤਾਰੁ॥ ਸਤਗੁਰੁ ਸੇਵਹਿ ਤਾ ਉਬਰਹਿ ਸਚੁ ਰਖਹਿ ਉਰਧਾਰਿ॥ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਤੇ ਹਰਿ ਪਾਈਐ ਸਚਿ ਸਬਦਿ ਵੀਚਾਰਿ॥ 3॥ ਸਤਗੁਰੁ ਸੇਵਿ ਮਨੁ ਨਿਰਮਲਾ ਹਉਮੈ ਤਜਿ ਵਿਕਾਰ॥ ਆਪੁ ਛੋਡਿ ਜੀਵਤ ਮਰੈ ਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਸਬਦਿ ਵੀਚਾਰ॥ ਧੰਧਾ ਧਾਵਤ ਰਹਿ ਗਏ ਲਾਗਾ ਸਾਚਿ ਪਿਆਰੁ॥ ਸਚਿ ਰਤੇ ਮੁਖ ਉਜਲੇ ਤਿਤੁ ਸਾਚੈ ਦਰਬਾਰਿ॥ 4॥ ਸਤਗੁਰੁ ਪੁਰਖੁ ਨ ਮੰਨਿਓ ਸਬਦਿ ਨ ਲਗੋ ਪਿਆਰੁ॥ ਇਸਨਾਨੁ ਦਾਨੁ ਜੇਤਾ ਕਰਹਿ ਦੂਜੈ ਭਾਇ ਖੁਆਰੁ॥ ਹਰਿ ਜੀਉ ਆਪਣੀ ਕ੍ਰਿਪਾ ਕਰੇ ਤਾ ਲਾਗੈ ਨਾਮਿ ਪਿਆਰੁ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮੁ ਸਮਾਲਿ ਤੂ ਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਹੇਤਿ ਅਪਾਰਿ॥ 5॥20॥53॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 3:  Jinhi purakheen Satgur(u) na saviyo, say dookhiyay joog chaar(i). Ghur(i) hoanda Purakh(u) na puchhaniya, abhimaan(i) mootthay ahunkaar(i). Satgur(u) kiyaan phitkiyaan mung(i) thukkay sunsaar(i). Sacha Shabad(u) na saviyo, subh(i) kaaj svaarunhaar(u). 1. Mun mayray suda Hur(i) vaykh(u) hudoor(i). junum murn dookh(u) purhuraiy, Shabad(i) ruhhiya bhurpoor(i). Ruhaou. Sach(u) slaahun(i) say Sachay Sacha Naam(u) adhaar(u). Sachi kaar kmaavni Sachay naal(i) piyar(u). Sacha Shahou vertda koyay na maytunhaar(u). Munmukh Mehl(u) na payayni koorr(i) mootthay koorriaar. 2. Haumein kerta jug(u) mooaa, Gur bin(u) ghor undhaar(u). Maya moh(i) visaariya sookhdaata dataar(u). Satgur saveh(i) ta oobreh(i) Sach(u) rukhiya oordhaar(i). Kirpa tay Hur(i) paayiyaiy Such(i) Shabad(i) veechaar. 3. Satgur sayv(i) mun(u) nirmula haumein tuj(i) vikaar. Aap(u) chhoad(i) jeevut muraiy Gur kaiy Shabad(i) veechaar. Dhunda dhaavut reh(i) guyay laaga Saach(i) piyar(u). Sach(i) ruttay mookh oojlay tit(u) Saachaiy  Durbaar(i) 4. Satgur(u) Purakh(u) na munniyon, Shabad(i) na lugo piyar(u). Ishnaan(u) daan(u) jayta kureh(i) doojaiy bhaayay khooaar(u).  Hur(i) jeo aapni kirpa kuray ta laagaiy Naam(i) piyar(u).  Nanak Naam(u) smaal(i) tu Gur kaiy hayt(i) apaar(i).  5.20.53.
Word Meaning:   Purakheen = men (people).   Saviyo = serve (worship).    Ghur(i) = in mind.    Purakh = Waheguru.    Mootthay = robbed.    Phitkiyaaan = condemned.       1.
Hudoor(i) = present close by.   Purhuraiy = remove; dispel.    Bhurpoor(i) = full to capacity; present everywhere.        Pause.          Slaahun(i) =   sing praise.     Adhaar = support.    Kaar = work; service.   Shahou = banker; (source of everything - Waheguru).    Vertda = operates. fulfills His responsibility.  Maytunhaar(u) = eraser; destroyer.   Koorr(i) = short-lived worldly attractions.        2.         Ghor = dense; deep.  Vissariya = forgotten.   Sookhdaata = source of all comforts (Waheguru).    Oobreh(i) = safely swam across the sea of Maya; salvaged.   Oordhaar(i) = acquired in the heart.             3.                 Nirmula = clean; pure.    Tuj(i) = quit.   Vikaar = evil thoughts or deeds.  Dhunda = worldly business.    Dhaavut = running around.   Ruttay = imbued.   Oojlay = shiny; clean (blame-free).   Durbaar(i) = in the Court.       4.
Munniyon = believed in; obeyed.  Doojaiy Bhaayay = in love with the other (Maya).  Khooaar(u) = suffering humiliation.   Smaal(i) = take care of; foster.  Hayt = love.  5. 
MEANING: The people who do not serve the Guru (do not obey His Command) suffer in each eon (no matter which eon it is).  They do not recognize the Waheguru residing in their heart, and are robbed (of their spiritual wealth) because of their ego and conceit.  Condemned by the Guru, they exhaust themselves wandering around for their worldly needs, but do not obey the Will of the Guru, who can fulfil all their wishes and needs.        1.         Feel Waheguru always around you, which is pervading the whole universe in the form of Shabad (His Command or Laws), and end your suffering of the birth-death-birth cycle, oh my mind.       Pause.           Those who sing praise of Waheguru and make His True Naam the basis of their life become “one” with Him.  They love Waheguru, and all their deeds are based on honesty and truth.  The True Lord controls the world (looks after His creation), and nobody can erase (or undo) His largesse.  The followers of their mind do not have access to Waheguru’s residence (cannot connect with Him), and the perishable are robbed (of their spiritual wealth) in their pursuit of the mundane.           2.         Without the Guru, there is dense darkness, and the world is dying in selfishness and ego.  In the love for Maya, the world has forgotten the Provider and the Source of all comforts (Waheguru).  Those who serve the Satguru and foster Waheguru’s Naam in their heart are salvaged. But one can acquire Waheguru only by pondering on the Guru’s Shabad - and that too only because of His benevolence.           3.        Serving the Satguru purifies one’s mind, and he abandons his haumein and evil deeds.  By pondering and acting on the Guru’s Shabad, one kills his selfishness and his attachment with Maya (this is called dying alive).  All his efforts to earn more wealth become a matter of the past, and he develops love only for Waheguru.  Such individuals are imbued in Waheguru’s Naam, and their faces shine (they are honoured) in Waheguru’s Court.  Those who do not recognize (obey the Will of) the Satguru, and develop no love for His Shabad, suffer humiliation in the love for Maya, no matter how much pilgrimage they undertake, and how much wealth they donate (to the Brahmans).  However, if Waheguru is kind to them, they foster love for Waheguru’s Naam in their heart. You too foster Waheguru’s Naam with love, oh Nanak!    5.20.53.

ਸਿਰੀਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ ਕਿਸੁ ਹਉ ਸੇਵੀ ਕਿਆ ਜਪੁ ਕਰੀ ਸਤਗੁਰ ਪੂਛਉ ਜਾਇ॥ ਸਤਗੁਰ ਕਾ ਭਾਣਾ ਮੰਨਿ ਲਈ ਵਿਚਹੁ ਆਪੁ ਗਵਾਇ॥ ਏਹਾ ਸੇਵਾ ਚਾਕਰੀ ਨਾਮੁ ਵਸੈ ਮਨਿ ਆਇ॥ ਨਾਮੈ ਹੀ ਤੇ ਸੁਖੁ ਪਾਈਐ ਸਚੈ ਸਬਦਿ ਸੁਹਾਇ॥ 1॥ ਮਨ ਮੇਰੇ ਅਨਦਿਨੁ ਜਾਗੁ ਹਰਿ ਚੇਤਿ॥ ਆਪਣੀ ਖੇਤੀ ਰਖਿ ਲੈ ਕੂੰਜ ਪੜੈਗੀ ਖੇਤਿ॥ 1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਮਨ ਕੀਆ ਇਛਾ ਪੂਰੀਆ ਸਬਦਿ ਰਹਿਆ ਭਰਪੂਰਿ॥ ਭੈ ਭਾਇ ਭਗਤਿ ਕਰਹਿ ਦਿਨੁ ਰਾਤੀ ਹਰਿ ਜੀਉ ਵੇਖੈ ਸਦਾ ਹਦੂਰਿ॥ ਸਚੈ ਸਬਦਿ ਸਦਾ ਮਨੁ ਰਾਤਾ ਭ੍ਰਮੁ ਗਇਆ ਸਰੀਰਹੁ ਦੂਰਿ॥ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਸਾਹਿਬੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਸਾਚਾ ਗੁਣੀ ਗਹੀਰੁ॥ 2॥ ਜੋ ਜਾਗੈ ਸੇ ਉਬਰੇ ਸੂਤੇ ਗਏ ਮੁਹਾਇ॥ ਸਚਾ ਸਬਦੁ ਨ ਪਛਾਣਿਓ ਸੁਪਨਾ ਗਇਆ ਵਿਹਾਇ॥ ਸੁੰਞੇ ਘਰ ਕਾ ਪਾਹੁਣਾ ਜਿਉ ਆਇਆ ਤਿਉ ਜਾਇ॥
