(ਅੰਗ 35)॥   ਮਨਮੁਖ ਜਨਮੁ ਬਿਰਥਾ ਗਇਆ ਕਿਆ ਮੁਹੁ ਦੇਸੀ ਜਾਇ॥3॥ ਸਭ ਕਿਛੁ ਆਪੇ ਆਪਿ ਹੈ ਹਉਮੈ ਵਿਚਿ ਕਹਨੁ ਨ ਜਾਇ॥ ਗੁਰ ਕੈ ਸਬਦਿ ਪਛਾਣੀਐ ਦੁਖੁ ਹਉਮੈ ਵਿਚਹੁ ਗਵਾਇ॥ ਸਤਗੁਰੁ ਸੇਵਨਿ ਆਪਣਾ ਹਉ ਤਿਨ ਕੈ ਲਾਗਉ ਪਾਇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਦਰਿ ਸਚੈ ਸਚਿਆਰ ਹਹਿ ਹਉ ਤਿਨ ਬਲਿਹਾਰੈ ਜਾਉ॥ 4॥21॥54॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 3:  Kis(u) houn sayveen, kiya jup(u) kureen, Satgur poochhoun jaayay. Satgur ka bhaana munn(i) luyee, vichoun aap(u) guvaayay. Aiha sayva Chakri, Naam(u) vusaiy mun(i) aayay. Naamaiy hee tay sookh(u) paayiyaiy Sachaiy Shabad(i) soohayay. 1. Mun mayray undin(u) jaag(u) Hur(i) chayt(i). Aapni khayti rukh(i) laiy, koonj purraiy gee khayt(i). 1. Ruhaou.  Mun keeyaan ichhaan pooriyaan Shabad(i) ruhiya bhurpoor(i). Bhaiy bhaayay bhugut(i) kureh(i) din(u) raati, Hur(i) jeo vaykhaiy suda hudoor(i). Sachaiy Shabad(i) suda mun raata, bherm(u) guyya sreeroun door(i). Nirmul(u) Sahib(u) paaya Saacha gooni guheer(u). 2. Jo jaagaiy say oobray, soottay guyay moohaayay. Sacha Shabad(u) na puchhaaniyo, soopna guyya vihaayay. Soonjay ghur ka paahoona jiyoun aaya tiyoun jaayay. 
(PAGE 35):                    Munmukh junum(u) birtha guyya, kiya moonh(u) daysi jaayay. 3. Subh kichh(u) aapay aap(i) haiy, haumein vich(i) kehn(u) na jaayay. Gur kaiy Shabad(i) puchhaaniyaiy, dookh(u) haumein vichoun guvaayay. Satgur(u) sayvun aapna houn tin kaiy laagoun paayay. Nanak dur(i) Sachaiy suchiyaar heh(i) houn tin bulihaaraiy jaaoun. 4.21.54.
Word Meaning:   Houn = I; me.   Jup(u) = recite.   Bhaana = Will; Huqam.   Aap(u) = selfishness.   Chaakri = free service.      Soohayay = pleasing; blissful.       1.       Chayt = remember; meditate on.   Khayti = crop; spiritual wealth.  Koonj = a bird that feeds on grain; will rob spiritual wealth.       Pause.           Ichhaan = wishes; desires.    Bhurpoor(i) = full to capacity.   Bhaiy = respectful fear.  Bhaayay = with love.    Hudoor(i) = present close by.   Raata = imbued.   Bherm(u) = doubtfulness.    Nirmul(u) = clean; pure.   Guheer(u) = deep; unfathomable.        2.              Oobray = salvaged.    Moohaayay = robbed.   Vihaayay = gone by.   Soonjay = empty; deserted.   Paahoona = guest; visitor.    Birtha = empty (without any achievement).    Daysi = show.         3.           Haumein = selfishness and ego.  Kehn = say; praise (Waheguru).  Laagoun Paayay = touch feet (show deep respect).   Suchiyaar = fosters  Truth.       4.
MEANING:  Stay alert, and worship Waheguru (remember Him) day and night, oh my mind, and thus save the crop of (your spirituality) in your mind-field, otherwise this crop will be attacked by the crop-destroying bird of Maya.          Pause.         (If you do not know) Whom to serve and whose name to recite and enshrine in mind, go and seek the Guru’s advice, oh my mind.  But you must overcome your selfishness, and obey Guru’s Will. (Obeying the Guru) is the real service to Him; besides, Waheguru’s Naam takes root in your heart.  You will enjoy all the comforts by fostering Waheguru’s Naam in your heart by obeying the blissful Shabad of the Guru.        1.        By obeying the Guru’s Shabad, one finds that Waheguru is pervading the whole universe and fulfils all your wishes.  A man who worships Waheguru day and night with respectful fear and love finds Him close (everywhere).   Such a man is always imbued with Waheguru’s Naam, and all his doubts (and superstitions) are cleared.  He internalizes the pure Waheguru, who is very deep (unknowable), and is the source of all noble qualities.        2.             Those who stay alert (against the attacks of Maya), swim across the sea of Maya (to be “one” with God), but those who are unaware and careless are robbed (of their spirituality) by Maya.  They do not recognize the Guru’s True Shabad, and their life is spent like a dream (without achieving any objective), and like a person who visits a deserted house.  The follower of his mind wastes away his life (without any spiritual gain); so, what kind of face will he show to Waheguru (he will not be able to face Waheguru)?          3.
Those who think only about themselves are victims of haumein (selfishness and ego) and cannot sing Waheguru’s praise.  But, when one ponders on Guru’s Shabad, he recognizes the Truth, and is able to overcome his suffering from haumein.  I touch their feet (respect them) who serve the Guru, oh Nanak. But, only the bearers of Truth become “one” with Waheguru; I wish to sacrifice my life for them, oh Nanak.  4.21.54.

ਸਿਰੀਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ ਜੇ ਵੇਲਾ ਵਖਤੁ ਵੀਚਾਰੀਐ ਤਾ ਕਿਤੁ ਵੇਲਾ ਭਗਤਿ ਹੋਇ॥ ਅਨਦਿਨੁ ਨਾਮੇ ਰਤਿਆ ਸਚੇ ਸਚੀ ਸੋਇ॥ ਇਕੁ ਤਿਲੁ ਪਿਆਰਾ ਵਿਸਰੈ ਭਗਤਿ ਕਨੇਹੀ ਹੋਇ॥ ਮਨੁ ਤਨੁ ਸੀਤਲ ਸਾਚ ਸਿਉ ਸਾਸੁ ਨ ਬਿਰਥਾ ਕੋਇ॥1॥ ਮੇਰੇ ਮਨ ਹਰਿ ਕਾ ਨਾਮੁ ਧਿਆਇ॥ ਸਾਚੀ ਭਗਤਿ ਤਾ ਥੀਐ ਜਾ ਹਰਿ ਵਸੈ ਮਨਿ ਆਇ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਸਹਜੇ ਖੇਤੀ ਰਾਹੀਐ ਸਚੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਬੀਜੁ ਪਾਇ॥ ਖੇਤੀ ਜੰਮੀ ਅਗਲੀ ਮਨੂਆ ਰਜਾ ਸਹਜਿ ਸੁਭਾਇ॥ ਗੁਰ ਕਾ ਸਬਦੁ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤੁ ਹੈ ਜਿਤੁ ਪੀਤੈ ਤਿਖੁ ਜਾਇ॥ ਇਹੁ ਮਨੁ ਸਾਚਾ ਸਚਿ ਰਤਾ ਸਚੇ ਰਹਿਆ ਸਮਾਇ॥2॥ ਆਖਣੁ ਵੇਖਣੁ ਬੋਲਣਾ ਸਬਦੇ ਰਹਿਆ ਸਮਾਇ॥ ਬਾਣੀ ਵਜੀ ਚਹੁ ਜੁਗੀ ਸਚੋ ਸਚਿ ਸੁਣਾਇ॥ ਹਉਮੈ ਮੇਰਾ ਰਹਿ ਗਇਆ ਸਚੈ ਲਇਆ ਮਿਲਾਇ॥ ਤਿਨ ਕਉ ਮਹਲੁ ਹਦੂਰਿ ਹੈ ਜੋ ਸਚਿ ਰਹੇ ਲਿਵ ਲਾਇ॥ 3॥ ਨਦਰੀ ਨਾਮੁ ਧਿਆਈਐ ਵਿਣੁ ਕਰਮਾ ਪਾਇਆ ਨ ਜਾਇ॥ ਪੂਰੈ ਭਾਗਿ ਸਤਸੰਗਤਿ ਲਹੈ ਸਤਗੁਰੁ ਭੇਟੈ ਜਿਸੁ ਆਇ॥ ਅਨਦਿਨੁ ਨਾਮੇ ਰਤਿਆ ਦੁਖੁ ਬਿਖਿਆ ਵਿਚਹੁ ਜਾਇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਸਬਦਿ ਮਿਲਾਵੜਾ ਨਾਮੇ ਨਾਮਿ ਸਮਾਇ॥ 4॥22॥55॥
Siri Raag(i) Mehla 3.  Jay vayla vukhut(u) veechaariyaiy, ta kit(u) vayla bhugut(i) hoyay.  Undin(u) Naamay ruttiyaan Sachay suchee soyay. Ikk(u) til(u) Piyara vissraiy bhugut(i) kunayhee hoyay. Mun(u) tun(u) seetul Saach siyoun, saas(u) na birtha koyay. 1. Mayray mun Hur(i) ka Naam(u) dhiyayay. Saachi bhugut(i) ta theeyaiy, ja Hur(i) vusaiy mun(i) aayay. 1. Ruhaou.  Sehjay khayti raahiyaiy Sach(u) Naam(u) beej(u) paayay. Khayti jummi ugli, munooa rujja sehj(i) soobhayay. Gur ka Shabad(u) Amrit haiy, jit(u) peetaiy tikh(u) jaayay. Eho mun(u) saacha Such(i) rutta, Sachay ruhhiya smaayay. 2. Aakhun(u), vaykhun(u), bolna, Shabaday ruhhiya smaayay. Bani vujji ch-houn joogeen Sucho Such(i) soonaayay. Haumein mayra reh(i) guyya, Sachaiy luyya milaayay. Tin ko Mehl(u) hudoor(i) haiy, jo Such(i) ruhay liv laayay. 3. Nudri Naam(u) dhiyayaiy vin(u) kerma paaya na jaayay. Pooraiy bhaag(i) Sutsungat luhhaiy Satgur(u) bhaytaiy jis(u) aayay. Undin(u) Naamay ruttiyaan dookh(u) bikhiya vichoun jaayay. Nanak Shabad(i) milaavrra Naamay Naam(i) smaayay. 4.22.55.
Word Meaning:   Vayla Vukht(u) = time.   Kit(u) = what; how?  Ruttiyaan = imbued.   Soyay = fame.  Til(u) = the size of a sesame seed (very small).  Knayhee = what kind of?    Seetul = cool (peaceful).   Birtha = empty; useless.        1.         Sehjay = with a controlled mind.  Raahiyaiy = sow the seed.  Ugli = very much; healthy.  Munooa = mind.   Rujja = satisfied.  Sehj(i) Soobhaayay = with a peaceful mind and love.   Tikh(u) = thirst.  Smaayay = merged; become one with.        2.         Bani = Guru’s Shabad.  Vujji = is heard; paid attention to.    Ch-houn = in all four.  Joogeen = eons.   Mayra = selfishness.  Hudood(i) = present close by.  Liv = deep concentration.           3.           Nudri = kind sight.   Vin(u) = without.   Kerma = kindness; luck.   Luhhaiy = acquires; gets.    Bhaytaiy = meets; connects.    Bikhiya = poison (Maya).  Milaavrra = union.  4.
MEANING: If one believes that Waheguru can be worshipped only during particular times (a Hindu belief), then what time will one worship Him (there will be no appropriate time)?  Those who are imbued in His Naam day and night are the Truth bearers (have pure minds, and are “one” with God), and true (everlasting) is their fame (reputation).  What kind of worship is that in which the Dear (Waheguru) is forgotten even for a short moment?  One’s mind and body are blissful when they are connected with Waheguru with every breath.        1.
Recite Waheguru’s Naam, and always remember Him, oh my mind!  The worship is real only if Waheguru’s Naam is embedded (enshrined) in your heart.        Pause.        (In order for Waheguru to reside in your heart), You need to sow the seed of truthfulness (Waheguru’s Naam) in the ground of your mind, which needs to be at peace (and not wandering after the mundane).  (As a result), a healthy crop (of Waheguru’s Naam) sprouts, and your mind achieves contentment without any special effort.  The real Amrit (immortalizing water) is the Guru’s Shabad, which quenches your thirst (for worldly things).  When the mind is imbued with Waheguru’s Naam, it connects with Him and remains connected all the time, oh Nanak.       2.            When one is merged with Waheguru, all his talk, his looking around, and his conversation are about Waheguru.  Guru’s Shabad is heard (is spread) over all the four eons as it disseminates nothing but the Truth. (When one obeys the Guru’s Shabad), he overcomes his selfishness and feeling of possessiveness, and is connected with Waheguru. Those who meditate on Waheguru with full dedication have easy access to His Palace (get united with Him).       3.         But one worships Him only when Waheguru casts His kind sight on him; without His benevolence, no one can worship Him.  With good fortune, one finds the company of holy men where he is connected with the Guru.  When one is imbued in Waheguru’s Naam, his suffering caused by the poisonous Maya is dispelled. Remember, the union with Waheguru is achieved through the Guru’s Shabad, and the individual stays connected with Waheguru’s Naam all the time,     oh Nanak.    4.22.55.

ਸਿਰੀਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 3॥ ਆਪਣਾ ਭਉ ਤਿਨ ਪਾਇਓਨੁ ਜਿਨ ਗੁਰ ਕਾ ਸਬਦੁ ਬੀਚਾਰਿ॥ ਸਤਸੰਗਤੀ ਸਦਾ ਮਿਲਿ ਰਹੇ ਸਚੇ ਕੇ ਗੁਣ ਸਾਰਿ॥ ਦੁਬਿਧਾ ਮੈਲੁ ਚੁਕਾਈਅਨੁ ਹਰਿ ਰਾਖਿਆ ਉਰਧਾਰਿ॥ ਸਚੀ ਬਾਣੀ ਸਚੁ ਮਨਿ ਸਚੇ ਨਾਲਿ ਪਿਆਰੁ॥ 1॥ ਮਨ ਮੇਰੇ ਹਉਮੈ ਮੈਲੁ ਭਰਨਾਲਿ॥ ਹਰਿ ਨਿਰਮਲੁ ਸਦਾ ਸੋਹਣਾ ਸਬਦਿ ਸਵਾਰਣਹਾਰੁ॥1॥ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਸਚੈ ਸਬਦਿ ਮਨੁ ਮੋਹਿਆ ਪ੍ਰਭਿ ਆਪੇ ਲਏ ਮਿਲਾਇ॥ ਅਨਦਿਨੁ ਨਾਮੇ ਰਤਿਆ ਜੋਤੀ ਜੋਤਿ ਸਮਾਇ॥ ਜੋਤੀ ਹੂ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਜਾਪਦਾ ਬਿਨੁ ਸਤਗੁਰ ਬੂਝ ਨ ਪਾਇ॥ ਜਿਨ ਕਉੇ ਪੂਰਬਿ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਸਤਗੁਰੁ ਭੇਟਿਆ ਤਿਨ ਆਇ॥ 2॥ ਵਿਣੁ ਨਾਵੈ ਸਭ ਡੁਮਣੀ ਦੂਜੈ ਭਾਇ ਖੁਆਇ॥ ਤਿਸੁ ਬਿਨੁ ਘੜੀ ਨ ਜੀਵਦੀ ਦੁਖੀ ਰੈਣਿ ਵਿਹਾਇ॥ ਭਰਮਿ ਭੁਲਾਣਾ ਅੰਧੁਲਾ ਫਿਰਿ ਫਿਰਿ ਆਵੈ ਜਾਇ॥ ਨਦਰਿ ਕਰੇ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਆਪਣੀ ਆਪੇ ਲਏ ਮਿਲਾਇ॥ 3॥
