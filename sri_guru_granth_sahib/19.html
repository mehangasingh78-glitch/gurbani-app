(ਅੰਗ 18)॥     ਕੇਤੀਆ ਤੇਰੀਆ ਕੁਦਰਤੀ ਕੇਵਡ ਤੇਰੀ ਦਾਤਿ॥ ਕੇਤੇ ਤੇਰੇ ਜੀਅ ਜੰਤ ਸਿਫਤਿ ਕਰਹਿ ਦਿਨੁ ਰਾਤਿ॥ ਕੇਤੇ ਤੇਰੇ ਰੂਪ ਰੰਗ ਕੇਤੇ ਜਾਤਿ ਅਜਾਤਿ॥ 3॥ ਸਚੁ ਮਿਲੈ ਸਚੁ ਉਪਜੈ ਸਭ ਮਹਿ ਸਾਚਿ ਸਮਾਇ॥ ਸੁਰਤਿ ਹੋਵੈ ਪਤਿ ਉਗਵੈ ਗੁਰ ਬਚਨੀ ਭਉ ਖਾਇ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਸਚਾ ਪਾਤਿਸਾਹੁ ਆਪੇ ਲਏ ਮਿਲਾਇ॥ 4॥10॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 1: Avhou bhaiynay gul mile hunk(i) suhaylurriyaan. Mil(i) kaiy kureh kahaaniyaan Sumruth Kunt keeyaah. Saachay Sahib subh(i) goon, augun subh(i) usaah. 1.  Kerta, subh(u) ko Tayraiy joar(i). Aik Shabad(u) beechaariyaiy, ja Tu, ta kiyya hoar(i). 1. Ruhaou. Jaayay poochhou sohaagni, tooseen raaviya kinhi gooni. Sehj(i) suntokh(i) seegaariyaan, mittha boalni. Pir(u) reesaaloo ta milaiy, ja Gur ka Shabad(u) sooni.  2.
(PAGE 18):             Kaytiyaan Tayriyaan koodruteen, kayvud Tayri daat(i). Kaytay Tayray  jea junt, sift(i) kureh(i) din(u) raat(i). Kaytay Tayray roop rung, kaytay jaat(i), ajaat(i). 3. Sach(u) milaiy, Sach(u) oopjaiy, subh meh(i) Saach(i) smaayay. Soort(i) hovaiy, put(i) oogvaiy, Gur Buchni bhuou khaayay. Nanak Sacha Patshah Aapay luyyay milaayay. 4.10.    
Word Meaning: Unk(i) Suhaylurriyaan = bosom (dear) friends.  Sumruth = omnipotent. Kunt = husband (Waheguru).  Augun = ignoble qualities.      1.  
Joar(i) = under control.   Shabad(u) = Naam.         Pause.         Sohaagni = married to (connected with) Waheguru.   Raaviya = enjoyed the love of.   Sehj(i) = in high spiritual state.  Suntokh(i) = with contentment.   Seegaariya = adorned with.   Pir(u) = husband.     Resaaloo = source of all tastes.       2.        Koodruteen = powers.   Daat(i) = largess.   Sift(i) = praise.  Ajaat(i) = low caste.              3.             Sach(u) = Waheguru.    Oopjaiy = appears.    Soort(i) = awareness.   Buchni = words.  Bhuou Khaayay = kills his fears.    4.
MEANING:  Everyone is under Your control, oh, the Creator (Waheguru). When we ponder on Your Shabad (in Your praise), we realize that there is no one else who has the ability to support us when You are there (to support us).       Pause. 
The True Lord possesses all virtues, and we possess only ignoble qualities. So, let all of us dear friends join with affection, and discuss the stories of the omnipotent Waheguru (sing His praise).        1.         If we ask the devotees who are married to Waheguru (are connected with Him), with what qualities they have enjoyed union with the Lord, their response is that one must be adorned with the high spiritual state, contentment, and sweet language. Besides, one must listen to Guru’s Shabad (and obey that) to get connected with the Dear Husband (Waheguru), which is the source of all enjoyable tastes in life.           2. 
Nobody knows the countless powers, the unlimited gifts of Waheguru, and the countless living beings which sing His praise. Nobody has any idea about the countless forms and colours of the Lord, and His high and low castes.          3.        Waheguru is present in every living being, and Waheguru appears in the heart of the devotee who gets connected with Him.   If one acquires the awareness (becomes enlightened) with the Guru’s Words (His Shabad), he is able to kill his worldly fears and earn respect in Waheguru’s Court.  The eternal Waheguru Himself connects His devotees with Him, oh Nanak.   4.10.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲਾ 1॥ ਭਲੀ ਸਰੀ ਜਿ ਉਬਰੀ ਹਉਮੈ ਮੁਈ ਘਰਾਹੁ॥ ਦੂਤ ਲਗੇ ਫਿਰਿ ਚਾਕਰੀ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਕਾ ਵੇਸਾਹੁ॥ ਕਲਪ ਤਿਆਗੀ ਬਾਦਿ ਹੈ ਸਚਾ ਵੇਪਰਵਾਹੁ॥ 1॥ ਮਨ ਰੇ ਸਚੁ ਮਿਲੈ ਭਉ ਜਾਇ॥ ਭੈ ਬਿਨੁ ਨਿਰਭਉ ਕਿਉ ਥੀਐ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਸਬਦਿ ਸਮਾਇ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਕੇਤਾ ਆਖਣੁ ਆਖੀਐ ਆਖਣਿ ਤੋਟਿ ਨ ਹੋਇ॥ ਮੰਗਣ ਵਾਲੇ ਕੇਤੜੇ ਦਾਤਾ ਏਕੋ ਸੋਇ॥ ਜਿਸ ਕੇ ਜੀਅ ਪਰਾਣ ਹੈ ਮਨਿ ਵਸਿਐ ਸੁਖੁ ਹੋਇ॥ 2॥ ਜਗੁ ਸੁਪਨਾ ਬਾਜੀ ਬਨੀ ਖਿਨ ਮਹਿ ਖੇਲੁ ਖੇਲਾਇ॥ ਸੰਜੋਗੀ ਮਿਲਿ ਏਕਸੇ ਵਿਜੋਗੀ ਉਠਿ ਜਾਇ॥ ਜੋ ਤਿਸੁ ਭਾਣਾ ਸੋ ਥੀਐ ਅਵਰੁ ਨ ਕਰਣਾ ਜਾਇ॥ 3॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਵਸਤੁ ਵੇਸਾਹੀਐ ਸਚੁ ਵਖਰੁ ਸਚੁ ਰਾਸਿ॥ ਜਿਨੀ ਸਚੁ ਵਣੰਜਿਆ ਗੁਰ ਪੂਰੇ ਸਾਬਾਸਿ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਵਸਤੁ ਪਛਾਣਸੀ ਸਚੁ ਸਉਦਾ ਜਿਸੁ ਪਾਸਿ॥ 4॥11॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehla 1: Bhulee suree j(i) oobri, haumein mooyi ghuraah(u). Doot luggay phir(i) chaakri, Satgur ka vaysaah(u). Kulp tiyaagi, baad(i) haiy, Sacha vaypervaah(u). 1. Mun ray, Sach(u) milaiy, bhuou jaayay. Bhaiy bin(u) nirbhuou kiyoun theeyaiy, Gurmukh(i) Shabad(i) smaayay. 1. Ruhaou. Kayta aakhun(u) aakhiyaiy, aakhun(i) toat(i) na hoyay. Mungun vaalay kayturray, Daata aiko soyay. Jis kay jea praan haiyn, mun(i) vussiyain sookh(u) hoyay. 2. Jug(u) soopna, baaji bunee, khin meh(i) khayl(u) khaylaayay. Sunjogi mil(i) aiksay, vijogi ootth(i) jaayay. Jo Tis(u) bhaana, so theeyaiy, uver(u) na kerna jaayay. 3. Gurmukh(i) vust(u) vaysaahiyaiy, Sach(u) vukkhur Sach(u) raas(i). Jinhi Sach(u) vunjiya, Gur Pooray shaabaas(i). Nanak vust(u) puchhaansi, Sach(u) sauda jis(u) paas(i).
Word Meaning: Bhulee Suree = it was great.   Oobri = salvaged.    Ghuraah(u) = from home (the root).     Doot = the five demons.   Chaakri = in service as slaves.  Vaysaah(u) = full faith in.   Kulp = imaginary; not real (useful).   Baad(i) = worthless.     1.        Theeyaiy = become.      Pause.         Aakhun(u) = pleas to beg.  Daata = provider.         2.        Baaji = drama.   Sunjogi = with predetermined union.  Aiksay = one (family).   Vijogi = separation.    Bhaana = is liked by.          3.         Vust(u) = thing (Waheguru’s Naam).    Vaysaahiyaiy = buy; deal in.    Vukkhur = merchandise.      Raas(i) = capital.    Vunjiya = dealt in.    Shabaash(i) = accolades.    Puchhaansi = will recognize.    Sauda = merchandise.        4.        
MEANING: With full faith in Satguru, it was great for me that my haumein (selfishness and ego) was destroyed from my mind (from the root), and I was salvaged with His benevolence.  Now, the five demons (lust, rage, worldly attachments, etc.) are serving me (are under my control).  Realizing that religious debates carry no worth in Waheguru’s Court, as He does not care about them, I have abandoned them as my imaginary (ill-conceived) beliefs.        1.
All one’s worldly fears disappear when one gets connected with Waheguru. One cannot become fearless without being afraid for Waheguru in mind.  The Guru’s Sikhs stay absorbed in His Shabad in His shelter.          Pause.          There are countless people who beg for their needs, but there is only one Provider (Waheguru). It does not matter how many times people beg for their necessities, Waheguru fulfils the just needs of everybody, and there is never a shortage in His largess. However, one feels blissful (content) only when one enshrines Waheguru’s Naam in one's heart, who has given us the body and the life.          2.
Like a dream, the world is a short-lived drama, in which the living beings are actors who complete their roles in a short time as per the Will of Waheguru.  With the predestined union, they come together to become one family, while they leave the world with their predestined separation.  Only what Waheguru likes happens, and there is nothing else anyone can do.     3.         Guru’s Sikhs deal in the eternal Naam of Waheguru, which becomes their eternal wealth. Those who deal in the eternal Naam earn accolades from the Guru, and Waheguru recognizes their true merchandise of His Naam, oh Nanak. 4.11.

ਸਿਰੀ ਰਾਗੁ ਮਹਲੁ 1॥ ਧਾਤੁ ਮਿਲੈ ਫੁਨਿ ਧਾਤੁ ਕਉ ਸਿਫਤੀ ਸਿਫਤਿ ਸਮਾਇ॥ ਲਾਲੁ ਗੁਲਾਲੁ ਗਹਬਰਾ ਸਚਾ ਰੰਗ ਚੜਾਉ॥ ਸਚੁ ਮਿਲੈ ਸੰਤੋਖੀਆ ਹਰਿ ਜਪਿ ਏਕੈ ਭਾਇ॥ 1॥ ਭਾਈ ਰੇ ਸੰਤ ਜਨਾ ਕੀ ਰੇਣੁ॥ ਸੰਤ ਸਭਾ ਗੁਰੁ ਪਾਈਐ ਮੁਕਤਿ ਪਦਾਰਥੁ ਧੇਣੁ॥ 1॥ ਰਹਾਉ॥ ਊਚਉ ਥਾਨੁ ਸੁਹਾਵਣਾ ਊਪਰਿ ਮਹਲ ਮੁਰਾਰਿ॥ ਸਚੁ ਕਰਣੀ ਦੇ ਪਾਈਐ ਦਰੁ ਘਰੁ ਮਹਲੁ ਪਿਆਰਿ॥ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਮਨੁ ਸਮਝਾਈਐ ਆਤਮ ਰਾਮੁ ਬੀਚਾਰਿ॥ 2॥ ਤ੍ਰਿਬਿਧਿ ਕਰਮ ਕਮਾਈਅਹਿ ਆਸ ਅੰਦੇਸਾ ਹੋਇ॥ ਕਿਉ ਗੁਰ ਬਿਨੁ ਤ੍ਰਿਕੁਟੀ ਛੁਟਸੀ ਸਹਜਿ ਮਿਲਿਐ ਸੁਖੁ ਹੋਇ॥ ਨਿਜ ਘਰਿ ਮਹਲੁ ਪਛਾਣੀਐ ਨਦਰਿ ਕਰੇ ਮਲੁ ਧੋਇ॥ 3॥ ਬਿਨੁ ਗੁਰ ਮੈਲੁ ਨ ਉਤਰੈ ਬਿਨੁ ਹਰਿ ਕਿਉ ਘਰ ਵਾਸੁ॥ ਏਕੋ ਸਬਦੁ ਵੀਚਾਰੀਐ ਅਵਰ ਤਿਆਗੈ ਆਸ॥ ਨਾਨਕ ਦੇਖਿ ਦਿਖਾਈਐ ਹਉ ਸਦ ਬਲਿਹਾਰੇ ਜਾਸੁ॥ 4॥12॥
Siri Raag(u) Mehl(u) 1: Dhaat(u) milaiy phoon(i) dhaat(u) kuou Sifti sift(i) smaayay. Laal(u) gulaal(u) gehbura, sacha rung churraaou. Sach(u) milaiy suntokhiyaan, Hur(i) jup(i) Aikaiy bhaayay. 1.   Bhaayi ray, Sunt junaa kee rayn(u). Sunt subha Gur(u) paayiyaiy, mookt(i) pdaaruth dhayn(u). 1. Ruhaou. Oochou thaan(u) soohaavna, oopur(i) Mehl Mooraar(i). Sach(u) kerni day paayiyaiy, Dur(u), Ghur(u) Mehl(u), piyaar(i). Gurmukh(i) mun(u) sumjhaayiyaiy, Aatum Ram(u) beechaar(i). 2. Tribidh kerm kmaayiyaiy, aas undaysha hoyay. Kiyoun Gur bin(u) tirkooti chhootsi, sejh(i) miliyaiyn sookh(u) hoyay. Nij ghur(i) Mehl(u) puchhaaniyaiy, nedr(i) kuray mul(u) dhoyay. 3. Bin(u) Gur maiyl(u) na ootraiy, bin(u) Hur(i) kiyoun ghur vaas(u). Aiko Shabad veechaariyaiy, uver tiyaagaiy aas. Nanak, daykh(i) dikhaayiyaiy, huoun sud bulihaaray jaas(u). 4.12.
Word Meaning:  Dhaat(u) = metal.     Phoon(i) = again.   Sifti = worthy of praise; God.   Gulaal = red like a rose.       Gebura = dark; deep; never fading.   Suntokhiyaan = the content.  Aikaiy Bhaayay = love for the only “One.”         1.         Rayn(u) = dust of the feet.   Mookt Pdaaruth = emancipator; Waheguru.     Dhayn = Kamdhenu.          Pause.         Oochou Thaan = the high place (above the effects of Maya).    Soohaavna = blissful.    Mooraar(i) = Waheguru.    Sach(u) Kerni = holy deeds.    Aatum Ram = God.        2.        Tribidh Kerm = deeds under the influence of the three effects of Maya.   Undaysa = worries.   Tirkooti = three effects of Maya.   Chhootsi = get rid of.  Sehj(i) = in a high spiritual state.  Nedr(i) = kind sight.     Mul(u) Dhoyay = wash filth of.        3.        Vaas(u) = dwelling.    Tiyaagaiy = abandon.    Bulihaari Jaas(u) = offer to sacrifice.           4.
MEANING: Just like a metal which melts, and mixes uniformly with another metal having the same properties, a devotee, who sings praise of Waheguru, acquires the same qualities as Waheguru, and his mind is dyed with the deep red colour (which never fades) with Waheguru’s love, and he merges with Him.    With the recitation of the Naam of the “One” (Waheguru), the content devotees acquire Waheguru (His Naam) in their hearts.     1.                   Become humble like the dust of the feet of the holy men, oh my brother. The gathering of the holy saints is like the mythical cow, Kamdhenu, which is believed to grant all wishes, and one acquires the Guru (Waheguru’s Naam) in the holy gathering and attains emancipation.         Pause.        The spiritual state above the effects of Maya is highly blissful, and so is the Palace of Waheguru; so, the devotee, with deeds of high morality and with love for Him, reaches the Door and the Palace of Waheguru (gets connected with Him) in that state.   So, one needs to teach (purify) his mind in the shelter of the Guru by pondering over Waheguru (His Naam).           2.             To fulfil his hopes, the man performs deeds under the influence of the three effects of Maya, but suffers from worries. So, how can one rise above the effects of Maya to attain a high spiritual state where he can enjoy real bliss by connecting with the Guru? One recognizes the dwelling (presence) of Waheguru in his own heart in that state when he washes away the filth of his mind with His kind sight.           3.        One’s filth of his evil thinking does not come off without the Guru, and he cannot implant Waheguru’s Naam in his heart. So, abandoning all other hopes, one should ponder only on the Guru’s Shabad.  I am a sacrifice for the devotee who “sees” Waheguru, and “shows” Him to others, oh Nanak. 4.12.
