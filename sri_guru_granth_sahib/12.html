MEANING:  The Living Supreme Being, Waheguru, is above the effects of Maya. The limitless Waheguru is beyond limits.  Everybody thinks of you (recites Your Naam), oh, the perfect creator, Waheguru! All living beings are Yours (created by You), and You are their provider. Always remember Waheguru, which is the destroyer of all discomforts, oh holy men!  (Being present in all life) Waheguru Himself is the Lord, and Himself the server. The living being is helpless (has no control).       1. 
You are present in each and every body all the time, and thus are mixed with the entire universe (everything is a part of Yours). But it is Your amazing (strange) ways, oh Waheguru, that some people are providers (limited donors), while some others are beggars.  (Being present in everybody) You Yourself are the provider and yourself the user, oh Waheguru! I do not recognize anybody other than you (who can play these roles), oh my Lord!  You are the greatest of all, oh limitless Waheguru!  I am unable to describe all Your noble qualities. I simply wish to sacrifice my life for those who remember You (sing Your praise).          2. 
Those who sing Your praise lead a comfortable life in this world, oh my Lord! They are able to break the bonds of Maya, thereby escaping the noose of the Angel of death (they become free from reincarnation).  Those who recite the Naam (sing praise) of the fearless Lord get rid of all their fears, and they are absorbed in Waheguru to become “one” with Him. They are worthy of praise, and I wish to sacrifice my life for them.    3. 
There are countless treasures full of Your worship (there are countless devotees who have been enabled by You for Your worship), oh my Lord!  And there are countless devotees who sing Your praise for Your countless forms.   And there are countless who worship you in various ways, meditating and self-afflicting (enduring hardships). There are countless others who study the Hindu holy books and perform the six recognized rituals – to study the Vedas and teach; to perform yagna and motivate others to do the same; to donate and to accept donations. However, only those devotees are approved by my Lord whom He likes.           4.         You are the origin of everything, and are the creator, oh my limitless Waheguru! Nobody else can rival You. You are the only one who has always been there through the eons, and You are the everlasting creator. Only those events take place in the universe which you desire, and only that happens, which You cause (to happen).  You create the entire universe, and You destroy it at Your Will. The devotee, Nanak, simply sings the praise of the Creator who knows everything.     5.
