NOTE: It is the same holy Shabad as Paurri number 27 in Jap(u) Ji with very minor changes in spellings of some words with no change in the meaning as a whole. So, for more detail, see the meaning of that Paurri.  
Word Meaning:  Dur(u) = door; entrance.  Smaalay = looks after. vaajay Naad = music produced by musical instruments.  vaavanhaaray = musicians.  Raag Purie = varieties of music.  Baisunter(u) = fire.   Raja Dherm(u) = Dhermraj = Angel of justice.  
Suvaaray = decorated.   Jutee = celibate.  Sutee = with high moral character.  Suntokhi = content.  Veer Kraaray. = gallants.   Mohiniyaan = mind-captivating; pretty. Muchh = this world.   Pyaalay = worlds beneath the earth.  Ruttan = jewels; noble qualities.   Khaani chaaray = the four sources of production of life (live birth, eggs, vegetation, from air under ripe conditions).  Khund, Mundal Verbhunda = parts of universe.  Ruttay = imbued.  Ruchna = creation.    Rungi Rungi Bhaanti = of different kinds.  
MEANING:  (Guru Nanak says), Oh Waheguru! People wonder what kind of entrance   Your Residence has, and what kind of Residence You reside in, where You look after Your whole Creation. Even though I am unable to describe the exact nature of Your Residence, You are omnipresent, and there are countless types of musical instruments, and countless musicians who play on them to produce many different types of lilting tunes (naad). There are many different types of lyrics and their subcategories (purees), and countless singers to sing them.  (The gods of) air, water, and fire all sing Your praise, and so does the Angel of justice on Your doorsteps.  Chit(u) and Goopt(u), who are believed to keep written records of the deeds of humans, sing Your Praise with their recordings being analyzed by Dherm Raj, the Angel of justice at Your door.   Many Shivas, and many Brahmas, etc., and many Parbatis sing Your praise, as a result of which, they are decorated (find respect) in Your Court.  Sitting on their thrones, many Indras (Ind), along with other gods, also sing Your praise at Your door.  The Sidh yogis sing Your praise in their meditation, and the holy men (sadh) sing by pondering on Your magnanimity. The celibate, men with high character, and the content (santokhi), the gallant, all sing Your praise. In all eons (yugs), the learned (pundits), the great sages (rukheesur), and the Vedas, sing Your praise.  Even the beautiful fairies of the paradise, of the earth (muchh) and of the underworld (payaal), having charming beauties, sing Your praise. Gods of jewels (Kuber) created by You, sing Your praise, and so do the sixty-eight holy places believed to be made holy by the gods. The powerful valiant (jodh) and the intrepid (maha bal soora), sing Your praise, and so do the living beings from all the four sources (mammals, eggs, vegetation, and the ones which grow under optimum conditions) of reproduction. The skies, the galaxies, and the whole universe, supported by You, sing Your praise. Only those devotees sing Your Praise (are approved by You) whom You like, and who are imbued with Your affection, and enjoy singing it. I do not know, and cannot imagine, how many more sing Your praise. Only the Master, Waheguru, is eternal (Such Sahib Saacha) and so is His magnanimity, or justice (naayee). The Creator has always existed, will exist in future, and will never go away (will live forever). He has created the universe with countless varieties of different living beings and bodies, and has created attractions of different types (Maya) for them. He enjoys and looks after His Creation as He pleases.  He will do whatever He chooses to do, and nobody has the power to suggest any modifications (huqam na kerna jaayee).  
(Guru Nanak Sahib advises) That rather than wasting time and energy to find the secrets and the limits (because nobody will ever be able to do so) of Waheguru, who is the real Emperor of emperors, the man should simply sing His praise and obey His Will (rajaayee).     27.

MEANING:  After hearing from different sources, everybody says that Waheguru 
(God’s creation) It is very large. If someone were actually able to see Him (His creation), how large would he find Him!!  Nobody could actually evaluate (find the size) of His creation, and thus nobody can describe His vastness.  Those who try get simply absorbed in You (are captivated by and lost in your magnanimity, and still cannot find any limits), oh Waheguru! 1.  
Your splendour is unfathomable. You are a treasure of all good qualities and never change; nobody knows how large Your creation is, oh, my great Master!         Pause.  All the meditators meditated (to find Your ends), and all the evaluators tried to evaluate You, and find your limits (but to no avail). The scholars, the people with deep concentrations, and the recognized gurus tried, but could not describe even a little amount of Your greatness.      2.  
All the noble deeds, all sorts of self-afflicted suffering to please You, and other religious rituals (do not result in any success to find Your limits).  All good qualities, including the glory (due to limited supernatural powers) of the Sidh yogis, come from You, by Your kindness. If You do not want someone to have those, they will stay undelivered.       3.   
You are the treasure of all the praiseworthy qualities, oh Waheguru! And nobody can describe them. Everybody is helpless.  If you impart Your good qualities to someone, nobody can match him.  Only You have the ability to adorn individuals (of Your choice with noble qualities), oh Nanak.  4.2.
