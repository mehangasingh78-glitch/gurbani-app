MEANING: There are six Hindu Shastras, and six are their authors with six different philosophies. However, all of them have the same Great Guru – the Supreme Lord, who has countless forms.       1.  
But you follow the philosophy (teachings) of that book or faith in which the praise of the Lord is written; that will earn you honours, oh my friend.          Pause. 
The amazing thing is that, even though there is only one Sun, there are so many different seasons and measures of time (that make up the seasons). Just in terms of time and seasons, it is wondrous that the Creator has many different forms, oh Nanak.2

Kaiysi aarti hoyay, Bhuvkhundna Tayri aarti! Anhuta shabad vaajunt bhayri. 1. Ruhaou. Sehs tuv naiyn, nun naiyn haiyn toh(i) kuou, sehs moorut(i), nuna aiyk Tohi. Sehs pud bimul, nun aiyk pud, gundh bin(u), sehs tuv gundh, ivv chult mohi. 2. Subh meh(i) jot(i) jot(i) haiy soyay. Tis daiy chaanun(i) subh meh(i) chaanun(u) hoyay. Word Meaning:  Thaal(u) = platter.  Gugganmaiy = sky-like.  Ruv(i) = sun.  Chund(u) = moon.  Taarika Mundul = galaxy, full of stars.  Junuk = take for; imagine.  Moti = large pearls.  Dhoop = incense.  Mulyaanlo Puvun(u) = wind from the Mullya Mountain.  Chuvro Kurray = fanning respectfully.  Sugal bunraayay = the whole vegetation.  Phoolunt Joti = light (fragrance) of flowers.        1. 
Kaisi = how wonderful!  Aarti = a Hindu ritual of prayer.  Bhuv-Khundna = the destroyer of the birth-death-birth cycle.   Unhuta Shabad = the never-changing Shabad (a manifestation of Waheguru).   Vaajunt = is playing.  Bhayri = clarinets.      Pause. 
Sehs = thousands.  Naiyn = eyes.  Nun = none.  Toh(i) kaou= yours.   Moorut(i) = forms.  Bimul pud = clean (holy) feet.  Gundh = noses.  Ivv chult = such enchanting feats.   Mohi = charmed (to be His devotee).          2.        Jot(i) = light (a part of the Lord); consciousness.  Soyay = same.  Chaanun(i) = with the light.   Saakhi = Teachings.   
Purgut(u) hoyay = appears; is realized.  Bhaavaiy = is liked by.  3. 
Churn Kunvul = lotus-like soft feet.   Mukrund = pollen dust.  Lobhit = Greedy.  Undin(ou) = day and night.  Pyaasa = thirst (desire).   Saaring = rainbird: a bird that is believed to drink the first drop of falling rain that it receives in its open beak.  
Kirpa – jul(u) = Kindly give the special water to this rainbird.   Jaa Tay = by which. Naaye = in the Naam.  Vaasa = make home.       4.
Aarti = a Hindu ritual in which a platter containing incense, flowers, a few earthen lamps, vermilion, and a coconut is rotated in a vertical circle in front of a deity or anybody else worthy of respect.  Guru Nanak Sahib visited a famous Hindu shrine (the Temple of Jagannath) in Odisha (southeastern India) and joined in the Aarti that the priest performed for the deity. After the ritualistic aarti, Guru Sahib recited this holy Shabad to educate the people about the true Aarti of the Supreme Lord. The ritualistic aarti is performed before an idol with a face, bust, arms, and hands, which the performer covers. When the priest asked Guru Sahib about the various body parts of the Supreme Lord who is formless, Guru Sahib replied as above. (The meanings of the holy Shabad follow).  
MEANING: (The ritualistic aarti of gods is fruitless, oh my friend!) because the real Aarti of the Lord is taking place all the time.  (You have a platter in which you place earthen lamps (and may be some pearls), incense, and flowers for the ritualistic aarti), But look, the sky is like the platter on which the sun and the moon are two lamps, with millions of bright stars replacing your artificial pearls.  The wind from the Mallya Mountain, which is filled with the fragrance of sandalwood, acts as incense, and the entire vegetation serves as flowers. (Mallya Mountain is in southern India, and has sandal trees grown 
on it, which are the source of a very sweet fragrance, thereby serving as incense).    1.  
The Divine music of never-changing Waheguru’s Shabad (in His praise) serves as the music of clarinets. So, how wonderful Aarti of the Lord – the destroyer of the birth-death-birth cycle – is taking place in nature!          Pause. 
(If you ask about the body parts of the formless Lord, even though He has no formal eyes, body, nose, or feet, yet being present in all living beings, He has thousands of eyes, bodies, noses, and perfectly clean feet. Such enchanting feats of Waheguru spell charm on me.          2.          The Lord is present in all living beings, and everybody is enlightened by His Light.  He reveals Himself through the Guru’s Teachings. Only that aarti is approved by Him which is to His liking.      3. 
Just like the bumblebee which loves flowers for their pollen-dust, I long for the lotus like soft Feet of the Lord (His Naam); and just like the rainbird, which waits for the first rain drop (and does not drink water from anywhere else no matter how thirsty he is), I am thirsty day and night for Lord’s water of kindness, and humbly request that He bestows me that so that I may reside in His Naam (always worship Him ), oh Nanak.   4.3.

Raag(u) Gaurri Poorbi Mehla 5: Kuroun baynti, sunhou mayray meeta, Sunt tehl kee bayla. Eehaan khaat(i) chulou Hur(i) laaha, aagaiy bussun(u) soohayla.1. Aoudh ghutaiy dinus(u) raina ray. Word Meaning:  Baynti = request.   Meeta = friends.  Sunt = Guru; holy men.  Tehl = serve.  Bayla = time (human life).  Eehaan = in this world.  Khaat(i) = earn.  Laaha = profit; benefit.  Aagaiy = next world.   Bussun(u) = lifving.  Soohayla = comfortable; pleasant.        1.       Aoudh = life span.   Kaaj = task; purpose; objective.    Gur Mil(i) = taking refuge of the Guru.  Svaaray = improve (achieve the objective).    Pause.

Brahm Giyaani. = knowledgeable about Waheguru (connected to Waheguru).  Piyavaiy = feeds.  Ehou rus(u) = the sweet taste (of Naam).  Ukuth = indescribable.  Katha = story (facts).     2.

MEANING:  I make a request; listen to it carefully, oh my friend!  It (the human life) is the opportunity for you to serve the Guru.  Earn Waheguru’s favour in this life, so that your next life becomes comfortable.           1. 
Your life is slipping away by day and night, oh my mind!  Get connected to the Guru so as to achieve your objective (of being “one” with Waheguru).          Pause. 
This world (life) is passing by in vain, consumed by meaningless worries and hesitations.  Only a staunch devotee finds salvation. Only those who enjoy the sweetness of Waheguru’s Naam are awakened by Him, and only they realize the secrets of the indescribable Waheguru.         2. 
Engage only in that business for which you have come into this world (to get connected to Waheguru).  The Guru can connect you with Waheguru, and you will be able to make your heart the house for Him (internalize Him).  Thus, you will have everlasting comfort in your mind and will not be reborn.         3.               I, Nanak, always long for the dust of the Guru’s Feet (devotion to Him); please fulfil my wish (of connecting with You), oh omniscient, omnipresent, and the architect of my fate, Waheguru!       4. 5.
